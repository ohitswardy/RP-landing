POWER
FGEN 1Q26 core profit down 25% to P3.4bn on natgas sale; EDC profits rebound
  • FGEN reported its net income fell 24% YoY to P3.63bn in 1Q26. On a recurring basis, profits were lower by 25% YoY to P3.38bn
    • The profit decline reflects the 60% stake sale of its natgas portfolio to Prime Infrastructure, as well as lower output from the Casecnan hydro plant
  • Geothermal unit, EDC, recorded a 16% YoY improvement in core earnings to P1.4bn
    • EDC’s Leyte, Bacman, and Mindanao plants sold higher kilowatt hours on better plant availability
    • Its three (3) BESS projects, which started commercial operations in Sep-Dec 2025, drove ancillary revenues higher
    • To the downside, its Burgos wind plant experienced outages and lower wind yield, while higher interest expenses also weighed
  • Profits from hydro fell 38% YoY to P382mn
    • Due to lower output from the 165MW Casecnan plant, as well as higher acquisition-related interest expenses. Casecnan posted a net loss of P181mn from a net income of P242mn in 1Q25
    • Its 132MW Pantabangan-Masiway plants saw profits rise to P560mn from P373mn, on higher water output
  • On a like-for-like basis, FGEN said that the natgas plants grew profits by P150mn (+4% YoY) to P3.9bn in 1Q26
MACRO
Unmilled rice output drops to 6-year low in 1Q26; Dept. of Agriculture projects rice output to hit 10-year low in FY26
  • Philippine production of palay (unmilled rice) in 1Q26 amounted to 4.4 MMT, down 6% YoY from 4.7 MMT in 1Q25
    • This is the lowest quarterly harvest since 2020
  • The Dept. of Agriculture (DA) revised its total palay output projection in 2026 from 20.3 MMT previously to 18.8 MMT (-5% YoY from 19.7 MMT in 2025) by year-end if fertilizer and fuel prices remain elevated
    • The 18.8 MMT palay output figure would translate to 11.8 MMT of rice based on the 63% milling recovery rate reported by the Philippine Statistics Authority
      • This would be 5.8 MMT short of the USDA's projected rice consumption for 2026
  • The DA stated that the production slump was due to low farmgate prices which discouraged farmers from planting
    • Farmgate prices in Oct 2025 fell to as low as P10-12/kg, below the average production cost of P14.5/kg
  • The department is hopeful for a recovery in local rice production as palay prices have improved to P22-P27/kg as of late
    • To help sustain production, the DA will provide fertilizer and fuel subsidies as well as expanded financial assistance
  • Recall that President Marcos reduced the rice tariff to 15% from 35% back in June 2024, leading to an influx of imports and the decline in farmgate prices
    • Marcos banned rice imports for four months from September to December 2025 in an effort to protect local farmers from low farmgate prices
    • Following the Middle East conflict, Marcos in end-March 2026 approved the P50/kg price cap on imported rice but has yet to issue an executive order as of late
