REGIS REPORT

26 January 2026

The auto industry association CAMPI released its full-year sales figure for FY25 of 464k, which is practically unchanged YoY (-0.8%). The derived unit sales for Dec of 42,870 was up 2%. More interestingly, sales of Toyota rose +18% to 23.9k YoY - certainly a strong number despite the overall market. We attribute this jump to the aggressive promotions offered for hybrid vehicles during the month.

 

For the year, we estimate Toyota’s market share would have been around 47%, up slightly from the previous year. GT Capital (GTCAP PM) is the majority shareholder of Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) and TMP accounts for around 1/4th of profits. GTCAP is trading at 44% discount to its market-based NAV and on <5x PER.

 

+++

 

The government is retaining its target fiscal ratios for this year, despite the downgrading of GDP growth estimates (and thus revenue target). This implies some spending will need to be shaved to keep the budget deficit target (5.3%) unchanged.

 

+++

 

ICTSI (ICT PM) is seeking to borrow US$300m from the AIIB, the bank backed by the Chinese government. The money will be used for capex at its existing Philippine ports. Management is of the view that debt levels are low right now (net debt/EBITDA of 2.3x) so there could be room to take on more leverage.

 

+++

 

Water utility Maynilad (MYNLD PM) is spending P30bn for capex this year. This is a chunky amount but is unlikely to allow the company to fully catch up with its commitment under its five-year plan (which ends Dec 2027). The company has spent P71bn as of Sept 2025 compared to its P163bn commitment.

CONGLOS
CAMPI says vehicle sales (ex-BYD) up 2% YoY in December; Toyota sales up 18% YoY
  • CAMPI data showed that car sales rose 2% YoY to 42,870 units in December
    • Brings FY25 unit sales of CAMPI members down by 0.8% YoY to 463,646 units
    • Note that BYD is not a member of CAMPI and BYD previously reported its FY25 rose more than five-fold to 26,122 units (~5% market share)
    • Including other industry data, CAMPI said that vehicle sales stood at 491,395 units in FY25, up 3.7%
  • Sales of electric vehicles (including other available industry data) reached 58,905 units in FY25, accounting for 12% of demand
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) sold 229,447 units in FY25, up 5.2% YoY but fell a tad short of our forecast of 231k units (+6%)
    • Derived Dec 2025 volumes stood at 23,895 units, up 17.9% YoY
    • Toyota’s market share (incl. BYD) may have reached 47% in FY25, up from ~46% in FY24
    • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 23% of FY26E profit
    • GTCAP is trading at a 44% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,173/sh and at 4.7x FY26E PER

 

 

 

 

MACRO
PH gov't retains fiscal deficit targets
  • Finance Secretary Frederick Go stated that it will retain its fiscal deficit targets
    • This is despite the recent downward revision for the 2026 revenue target from P4.98trn to P4.82trn
    • Recall that the DBCC trimmed the GDP growth targets for 2026 to 5-6% and 2027 to 5.5-6.5% from 6-7% initially
  • The fiscal deficit targets for 2026/27/28 are P1.65trn (5.3% of GDP), P1.6trn (4.8%), and P1.55trn (4.3%), respectively
  • The Bureau of Treasury's Dec 2025 and FY25 cash operations report will be released on Mar 3
    • As of 11M25, revenues amounted to P4.15trn (+1% YoY) while expenses were P5.41trn (+2.5% YoY)
      • The YTD fiscal deficit stood at P1.26trn (+7% YoY)
      • The gov't projects the FY25 deficit to be P1.56trn (+4% YoY)

 

 

TRANSPORT
ICTSI seeks US$300mn AIIB loan
  • ICTSI is seeking a US$300mn senior unsecured corporate loan from the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB)
    • Proceeds will be used to fund the expansion and upgrade of its Philippine ports (i.e. Bauan International Port, Manila International Container, and Mindanao International Container Port)
    • The project is currently “under preparation”, with AIIB estimating financing approval by March 23, 2026
  • With leverage ratios now well below peak levels, ICTSI’s balance sheet has sufficient headroom to finance future expansions
  • ICTSI is trading at 11x FY26E EV/EBITDA and 18.6x PER vs. peer average (12.6x EBITDA / 18x PER) vs. historical valuation (11x EBITDA / 19x PER)

 

 

UTILITIES
Maynilad plans up to P30bn capex this year
  • Maynilad Water Services, Inc. plans to spend between P26-P30bn in 2026
    • According to President and CEO Ramoncito S. Fernandez
    • Note that Maynilad has spent P70.6bn on capex from 2023-9M25, trending below its committed capex of P163bn
    • Maynilad will need to spend a cumulative amount of ~P92bn from 4Q25-2027 to catch up on commitment
    • Accelerating investments will boost its concession value (ie. OCP), which was estimated at P145bn in end-2024
    • We estimate Maynilad’s EV today at P202bn
MACRO
BSP uncertain on another rate cut this year
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona guided that another rate cut this year is uncertain given current economic conditions
    • Recall that Remolona previously said that a rate cut in Feb was "on the table"
  • He stated that a cut during the next Monetary Board meeting "… is still a maybe" and not certain
    • Remolona highlighted that if inflation and 4Q growth are benign and weaker-than-expected, respectively, it may prompt another cut
  • The first out of six Monetary Board meetings for the year will be held on Feb 19
    • For 2026, consensus and Regis expect one 25bp cut to 4.25%
  • The BSP's GDP growth forecasts for 2025/26/27 are 4.6/5.4/6.0%, respectively
    • For 2025/26/27, consensus and Regis expectations for GDP growth are at 4.9/5.3/5.8% and 4.8/5.5/5.7%, respectively
  • Consensus and Regis Inflation expectations for 2026 are 2.6% and 3.0%, respectively
