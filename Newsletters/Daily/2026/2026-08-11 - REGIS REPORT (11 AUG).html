REGIS REPORT

11 August 2026

We have a slew of results, mainly from the property names today. The latest numbers from the three largest listed real estate companies show two clear trends: 1) the residential segment remains weak – every developer saw a YoY decline in reservation sales; 2) consumption spending slowed down – same-mall revenue growth of the three major mall operators decelerated in 2Q26

 

  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) (-) - 2Q26 net profit fell 16% YoY to P6.1bn, well behind REGIS and consensus forecasts, as development revenue declined 16% YoY (flat QoQ). Meanwhile, 2Q26 residential reservation sales fell 21% YoY as sales of mid-income projects halved. Despite reducing unsold inventory by 17% QoQ and 30% YTD, management only intends to launch P5bn worth of residential projects for the full year. We believe this highlights management’s cautious stance on the residential segment.

For Philippine-based investors>>Ayala Land - 2Q26 earnings fall 16% YoY; management remains cautious on residential

 

  • SM Prime (SMPH PM) (=) – 2Q26 and 1H26 earnings were both flat, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. 2Q26 mall revenue rose 8% YoY on the back of 6% SSSG, slightly slower than the 7% in 1Q26. On the other hand, the value of unsold residential inventory grew 10% QoQ and 15% YoY to P200bn, equivalent to about 4years’ worth of reservation sales

  • Robinsons Land (RLC PM) (=) – 1H26 earnings increased 5% to P7.2bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus FY forecasts. 2Q26 mall revenue growth slowed to 5% YoY, from 7% in 1Q26. Meanwhile, 2Q26 reservation sales of both standalone and JV projects fell substantially YoY..

 

  • AREIT (AREIT PM) (+) - 1H26 net profit rose 36% to P5.8bn, slightly ahead of REGIS and consensus FY forecasts. AREIT will acquire P20bn worth of assets from Ayala Land for P2.6bn in cash and 462.5mn new AREIT shares. We estimate that the Ayala Group would need to sell c. 80mn AREIT shares in order for the latter to meet minimum public ownership requirements.  

 

  • Globe (GLO PM) (=) – Core profit for 1H26 was P10.2bn, down slightly (-2%) YoY. The core telecom business is doing quite well, with revenues rising +6% for 1H and +8% for 2Q alone. Mobile revenues were a positive surprise, despite the weak macro environment.

TELCOS
Globe Telecom 2Q26: Core operations defy weak macro

Solid results from telecom operations

Globe reported core income of P10.2bn for 1H26, marginally down (-2%) YoY, due to higher depreciation charges and financing costs but within expectations. Underlying operations of the core telecom business, however, provided a positive surprise. 2Q service revenue jumped 8% to P43.4bn, with corporate data (14% of revenue) leading at +15% growth. Mobile and broadband revenues were both up +6% YoY. Overall, 1H revenue and EBITDA grew a healthy +6%.

 

Mobile segment surprise

For a business that’s been growing <3% annually, the +6% YoY growth in mobile was a pleasant surprise. Notable is the fact that revenue was up +2% QoQ despite the apparently weak macro environment. We reckon the period of high oil (and transport) prices likely drove more work-from-home (WFH) arrangements, leading to greater mobile usage.

 

Another good quarter for corporate data and broadband

 

Corporate data continued to do well, growing 15% for both 2Q and 1H due to strong underlying demand. Meanwhile, broadband revenue was again up +6% YoY as the push into prepaid fiber continues to pay dividends. Net subscriber additions, which should be a leading indicator of revenue, remained firm at 130k for 2Q, broadly the same as the previous five quarters.

 

Data center ops ramping up

Globe’s data center (DC) business under the 50%-owned joint venture with STT GDC delivered P57m in earnings for 2Q, a modest drop YoY. The business is set to exceed 30MW of operational capacity by end of 2026. Management is optimistic that underlying DC demand will be augmented by a recent mandate for government-linked data to be housed within the country.

 

Capex to stay below US$1bn

Capital expenditures amounted to P26.3bn for 1H26, ~31% of revenue and up +39% YoY. Management says it intends to keep capex for FY26 below US1bn (~P61bn). Capex for FY25 was P46bn.
