REGIS REPORT

30 June 2026

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) issued a new regulation that mandates that person-to-person e-payment fees across institutions should not be “materially different” from fees within institutions. Since transfers within banks are often free of charge, the effect of this regulation will be to reduce (or eliminate) transfer fees across institutions (i.e., banks, e-wallets).

 

The BSP’s aim is, clearly, to reduce friction costs relating to digital payments for consumers and thereby increase digital adoption. This move will likely spur greater use of inter-bank digital transfers. While those holding bank accounts could be more inclined to use their bank apps to send payments, the Philippine population remains largely unbanked or functionally unbanked and thus reliant on e-wallets that, among other things, don’t require any minimum balances.

The power regulator (ERC) has extended the suspension of the collection of the Green Energy Auction Allowance (GEA-All) from consumers (up to Aug). The GEA-All is imposed on consumers to pay for renewable energy contracted by the government.  While the GEA-All amount is small (P0.037 per kwh or 1/4th of 1% of residential Meralco rates), this action by the ERC is only one example of what it’s doing to hold back power rates, even if temporarily.

Monetary Board member Benjamin Diokno offered his opinion that the Philippines is at risk of a ratings downgrade by the credit ratings agencies because of the corruption scandal. Two of the main credit ratings firms downgraded the outlook for the country (but not the ratings themselves) recently.

BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
BSP amends e-payments framework
  • The BSP issued Circular No. 1238, which states that fees for person-to-person e-payments across banks, e-wallet operators, and other payment service providers (PSPs) should not be materially different from fees within the same institution
  • Since transfers within one bank or e-wallet are often free, any difference in pricing should mainly reflect fees paid to the network switch operator
  • These institutions must also have an analysis of their costs for delivering electronic payment products and services
    • The BSP may require this in the conduct of its oversight activities
  • InsiderPH said that BPI will permanently waive fees for fund transfers made through InstaPay and PESONet across its digital banking platforms beginning July 1
    • This will allow over 9mn BPI app users to transfer funds to other banks and e-wallets without paying transaction fees
MACRO
Monetary Board member sees the Philippines at risk of losing investment-grade ratings
  • Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Monetary Board member Benjamin Diokno in an interview stated that the country is at risk of losing its investment-grade sovereign credit ratings given the threats of the oil crisis and a prolonged flood-control corruption scandal
    • Diokno warned that the corruption scandal may be more damaging than external shocks due to the potential loss of confidence and credibility as well as a stoppage in infra spending
  • Recall that Fitch Ratings recently affirmed the country's BBB rating but downgraded the outlook to 'negative' from 'stable'
    • S&P retained its BBB+ rating for the Philippines but cut its outlook to “stable” from “positive”
    • Moody's retained its "Baa2 stable" investment-grade rating for the Philippines
  • BSP Governor Eli Remolona, however, previously said in a separate interview that the negative outlook does not automatically lead to a rating downgrade
    • Remolona added that securing an A credit rating is still possible despite Fitch's outlook downgrade as the revision was "not due to domestic weaknesses" but due to the external environment

 

 

POWER
DOE to issue show-cause orders to 175 power firms over plant outages
  • Energy Secretary Sharon S. Garin said the department will issue show-cause orders (SCOs) to 164 generation companies operating in on-grid areas and 11 in off-grid areas
    • SCOs will be issued over alleged prolonged unplanned outages and other violations that have strained the country’s power supply
    • The agency cited that some power firms failed to meet the minimum fuel reserve requirement
    • In Luzon alone, about 60-80 generation companies will receive SCOs while 20/40 firms will be issued SCOs in Visayas/Mindanao
  • However, Secretary Garin declined to identify the companies to avoid “complications”
POWER
ERC extends suspension of GEA-ALL collection for July-August 2026
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) has approved the extension of the temporary suspension of the collection of the Green Energy Auction Allowance (GEA-All) for the July and August 2026 billing months
    • Ruling covers the GEA-All rate of PhP 0.0371 per kilowatt-hour (kWh) (~0.3% of MER’s end-user rate)
    • Cited that the GEA-All Fund balance of P707mn remains financially sufficient to support expected disbursements
  • GEA-All is a uniform charge collected from consumers to fund payments to participants of the government’s Green Energy Auction Program
