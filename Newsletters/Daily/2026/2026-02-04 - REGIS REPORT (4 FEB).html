REGIS REPORT

04 February 2026

Globe (GLO PM) issued a press release last night on its FY25 (and 4Q) results. Core income for the year was P20.9bn, down slightly (-3% YoY). Stripping out preferred dividends, we estimate core profit to common shares was P19.4bn, down 4%. This is better than our expectations but appears mildly weaker than the Street’s.

 

Core telecom operations were actually quite robust in 4Q, with service revenues up +6% YoY for the quarter. This took service revenue growth from -2% in 9M to practically zero (flat) for the year. Meanwhile, profit contributions from Mynt for FY25 were P6.1bn, which was up around 60% for the year. This implies, however, that 4Q contributions were down ~40% QoQ.

The two main container ports in Manila, including ICTSI’s (ICT PM) Manila International Container Terminal (MICT), are operating at full yard capacity, with congestion exceeding normal seasonal levels given the extended gap between Christmas holidays and Chinese New Year. We expect a temporary YoY boost in MICT’s 1Q26 storage revenues, which should help offset any possible sustained volume weakness. We estimate MICT accounts for ~20% of volumes and revenues.

TELCOS
Globe 4Q/FY25 results -- initial comment
  • Core income, as reported, for FY25 was down slightly (-3%) at P20.9bn.  After preferred share distributions, we estimate core profit to common shareholders at P19.4bn (-4% YoY), which is above expectations

 

  • Core operations were unexpectedly strong but contributions from Mynt fell again QoQ

 

  • Full-year service revenue was practically unchanged YoY but they were actually quite strong for 4Q alone (+6% YoY, +5% QoQ).  This is true particularly for mobile.

 

  • Mynt contributed P6.1bn for FY25 to Globe’s bottom line.  This up around 60% YoY. This, however, implies 4Q contribution was around P800m, down by ~40% QoQ.

 

  • Wireline broadband subs increased by 107k in the last quarter – still a pretty solid outcome.  This means Globe has added over 100k wireline subs every quarter in FY25. The company now has 1.795m wireline broadband subs.

 

  • Capex for FY25 was P46bn (~US$800m), 28% of revenue and management is guiding for capex to be “below US$1bn” for FY26

 

  • The company also declared a dividend of P25/share for 1Q26, unchanged from the prior years.  This puts it on track to pay P100 for the year.

 

 

TRANSPORT
Limited withdrawal of empties causes congestion at Manila ports
  • Congestion has built up at the two container ports in Manila, following limited withdrawal of import containers during the holiday season
    • ICTSI’s (ICT PM) Manila International Container Terminal (MICT) yard utilization rates for reefers was at 106% and overall utilization at 82% as of February 2
    • Asian Terminal’s (ATI PM) South Harbour has a utilization rate of 64% of reefers and an overall utilization rate of 89%
  • ICTSI attributed the congestion to the following:
    • High cargo volume from December to pre-Chinese New Year;
    • Limited withdrawal of import containers during the holidays;
    • Lack of trucks to return empty containers to full utilized depots, limiting the ability of trucks to collect laden containers from the ports
  • The high yard utilization rate of South Harbor of 89%, even after its 40% capacity expansion in May 2025, suggests that market dynamics will remain favorable
  • While yard utilization usually peaks at this time of year, conditions this year appear more severe than normal and should result in a temporary YoY boost in MICT’s storage revenues
    • We estimate MICT accounts for 20% of consolidated volumes and 20% of revenues
  • ICTSI trades at 11.6x FY26E EV/EBITDA and 20x PER vs. peer average (13x EBITDA / 19x PER) and historical average (11x EBITDA / 19x PER)
MACRO
Dept. of Finance eyes P101bn revenues from sale of gov't assets
  • The Department of Finance said the government is targeting the sale of three major real estate assets this year, which—together with the privatization proceeds from the Caliraya-Botocan-Kalayaan (CBK) complex last year—could generate a combined P101bn in revenues
    • Note that P101bn was the initial privatization target revenue last year which was cut down to P5bn as the gov't cited "slight delays" in selling properties
  • The three properties targeted to be sold this year are the Food Terminal, Inc. (FTI) property in Taguig (P40.4bn), Mile Long Complex in Makati (P12.3bn), and the Atrium of Makati (P449mn)
  • The privatization of the 744 MW CBK hydroelectric power complex garnered an offer of P36.3bn from the Thunder Consortium (Aboitiz Renewables, Inc., Sumitomo Corp., and Electric Power Development Co.)
  • The gov't targets P350bn in nontax revenues for 2026, revised upwards from the previous P249bn target

KKR, Singtel consortium to buy STT GDC for SGD6.6bn
