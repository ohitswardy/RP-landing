The statistics agency reported Feb CPI inflation was 2.4%, which would be in line with consensus expectations (=2.4%).  The inflation number is expectedly higher than the Jan inflation figure (=2.0%) on base effects.

 

This print is unlikely to influence the monetary policy authorities much ahead of the next Monetary Board meeting.  What’s probably going to weigh more is the impact of the ongoing conflict in the Persian Gulf.

TRANSPORT
ICTSI FY25 profits up 27% YoY to US$1.03bn – Ahead of Regis and Street estimates

ICTSI reported FY25 profits after perps of US$1.03bn, up 27% YoY and comfortably beating both Regis and consensus estimates. Excluding new and discontinued operations, the company said that its recurring profit before prefs rose 26% YoY. The beat was broad based, but the Americas region stood out with revenues well above expectations.

 

The company declared regular dividends of P17.85/sh, up 26% YoY and higher than forecast. The dividend is equivalent to the company’s min. payout ratio of 60% and yield of 2%. It will be paid on March 27 (ex-div: Mar 18). 

 

4Q25 highlights:

  • 4Q25 profits up 11%QoQ/37% YoY to US$293mn.  Derived 4Q25 profits were up 11% QoQ and 37% YoY to US$293mn, accelerating further from the 9M pace of 27%. 

 

  • Volume growth moderates in 4Q. Volumes in the quarter rose 10%, just a tad slower than 9M trend of 11%, due to a volume contraction at the EMEA region and a mild slowdown at the Americas (+17% in 4Q from +27% in 9M). Asia volumes rose 11% during the quarter, lifted by the new port in Batam. On a like-for-like basis, volume growth in Asia slowed to 6% in FY25, after rising +9% in 9M25. 

 

  • Revenues jump 24% YoY in 4Q, with Americas region up 41%. 4Q25 revenues rose 8% QoQ / 24% YoY to a new quarterly high of US$897mn. Revenues from the Americas region surprisingly grew 41% YoY in 4Q25, even faster than the 9M25 rate of +31%.  We believe the strength likely came from its port in Rio, Brazil, where volumes surged by more than 70% in 4Q, while yields were likely lifted further by the spike in commodity prices (tariffs are linked to the CIF value of goods).

JFC eyes Highlands Coffee IPO in Vietnam by 1Q27
  • JFC, in a press release, elaborated its planned standalone IPO of Highlands Coffee in Vietnam by 1Q27
    • JFC highlighted Highlands Coffee position as the #1 coffee chain in Vietnam with the proposed IPO to support the chain's "next phase of growth"
  • Highlands Coffee was said to currently be engaged with international and local advisors to define the structure, process, and timing for the potential IPO
  • As of 9M25, Highlands Coffee contributed to 2.4% of JFC's total SWS on +6.5% SSSG
    • EBITDA for the same period amounted to P1.9bn (~6% of global EBITDA)
    • The coffee chain has 928 stores, with a majority (800) being company owned stores
  • Recall that Jollibee acquired a 50% stake in Highlands Coffee for US$25mn back in 2012, then increased its stake to 60% in 2017
GAMING
Bloomberry sells Korean casino business
  • Bloomberry Resorts (through a subsidiary) consummated the sale of its Jeju casino business
    • This transaction was previously announced in October 2025
  • The Korean casino business was spun off to a new company, which was subsequently sold to the buyer, now named Blue One
  • Total value of KRW10bn (c. P400mn)
  • Blue One has paid KRW7bn and will pay another KRW3bn on 27 February 2027
  • Bloomberry will continue to own the gaming area and will receive rental payments from Blue One
  • BLOOM will also continue to operate the hotel business (including F&B) in Jeju
  • In 9M25, BLOOM’s Jeju operations recorded a net loss of P183mn
  • Bloomberry’s 9M25 recurring net loss was c. P2.7bn
  • Since BLOOM’s Korean gaming license has been fully impaired, the company could recognize a gain on the sale of its Korean gaming business
GAMING
IEC issues first tranche of convertible notes to DigiPlus
  • International Entertainment Corporation (1009 HK) issues HK$800mn of convertible notes to DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM)
  • IEC, through its subsidiaries, owns and operates New Coast Hotel Manila, an integrated hotel and casino complex
  • The gaming floor of New Coast Hotel Manila completed a renovation in 1Q26, which increased its gaming tables from 100 to 115 and its slot machines from 520 to 665
    • The hotel renovation will finish in 3Q26
    • Under its provisional gaming license, IEC is required to spend at least US$1bn to develop the integrated resort. The resort complex will have at least 250,000sqm of GFA and at least 800 5-star hotel rooms
  • The subscription to HK$1.6bn (c. P12bn) convertible notes will be issued in two tranches
  • PLUS has the option to convert the notes and could hold c. 53.9% of IEC’s issued capital stock, based on the conversion price of HK$1.00/share (higher than the last price of HK$1.06/share)
    • The notes carry an interest rate of 3% per annum
    • If PLUS will not convert, the notes will be redeemable at 108% at the end of their five-year maturity
    • The notes can be converted beginning 31 days after issuance of the second tranche
  • For the year ending June 2025, IEC reported a net loss of HK$282mn (c. P2.1bn)
  • Integrated resort operators are charged c. 9ppt less on their e-casino GGR than pure online operators
    • If this were to be applied to DigiPlus Interactive’s trailing 12 months operations (including 54% of the net loss of IEC), we estimate that net profit would have been about P19.4bn (c. 40% higher than the actual trailing 12M profit of P14.0bn)
  • PLUS is trading at 5.8X FY26E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
POLITICS
House rules complaints against VP Sara Duterte sufficient in substance; Duterte asked to respond to charges
  • The House Committee on Justice has ruled that the third and fourth impeachment complaints filed against Vice President Sara Duterte were "sufficient in substance, with 54 voting in favor of the motion and only one voting against
  • The committee will then issue the respondent (Duterte) with a copy and a notice to respond to the complaints within 10 calendar days
    • Both sides must submit affidavits/counter-affidavits and documentary evidence along with their pleadings
    • The committee will conduct hearings and submit a recommendation whether to approve or dismiss the complaints
      • Note that the committee has 60 session days since referral of the verified complaint to submit a recommendation to the House
    • The House would then need a one-third vote to approve and elevate the impeachment complaint against Duterte to the Senate for trial
MACRO
Government debt rises to P18.13trn in end-Jan
  • The government's total debt stock went up by 2.4% MoM and 11% YoY to P18.13trn as of end-Jan
    • The increase was driven by "front-loading domestic and external issuances" ahead of global market uncertainties that may raise interest costs
  • The current debt mix of the country's total obligations has domestic debt accounting for 68.0% and 32.0% being external borrowings
    • Domestic debt increased by 1.7% MoM to P12.32trn
    • External debt rose by 3.9% MoM to P5.81trn
  • The Bureau of Treasury (BTr) stated that the debt level is within the P19.06trn FY26 borrowing program
