Our results tally is skewed more to the “miss” column today.

 

  • Ayala Corp (AC PM) (-) – Profits was down 5% at P12bn while ‘core’ profit dipped 1% to P11.2bn for 1Q26, a little short of expectations due to disappointments at BPI (BPI PM), Ayala Land (ALI PM) and ACEN (ACEN PM). The bright spots this were quarter were in Globe (GLO PM) and AC Mobility. The latter’s unit volumes have been soaring on the back of a structural shift to “new energy vehicles” (NEVs).  More below.

 

  • Converge ICT (CNVRG PM) (-) – Profits for 1Q26 were practically unchanged YoY at P3.02bn, which would be disappointment relative to Regis estimates. Revenues grew only 4%, which is a surprise given that it’s even slower than Globe’s (GLO PM) pace. The company added 105k subscribers in the quarter, an improvement from 4Q25 but lagging Globe’s net additions.

 

  • Cebu Air (CEB PM) (=) – Underlying operations were robust in 1Q26, with core profit tripling to P1.5bn. However, the full impact of the spike in jet kero prices in March has yet to flow through, with management warning of losses ahead.

The House of Representatives transmitted the articles of impeachment against Vice President Sara Duterte-Carpio to the Senate, even as reports (videos) of gunshots in the Senate premises emerged.

 

The transmittal to the Senate sets the stage for the impeachment trial of the Vice President. The likelihood of Duterte’s conviction and disqualification from office was always slim because conviction requires a 2/3rds majority in the Senate, and Duterte allies number more than 1/3rd. Recent changes in the Senate leadership make that even slimmer. We expect the new Senate majority to move to dismiss the impeachment case quickly after the Senate nominally convenes as an impeachment court.

1Q26 core profit slips to P11.2bn, trailing Regis, Street estimates

Reported 1Q26 net profit before pref dividends declined by 5% YoY to P12bn while core profit slipped by 1% to P11.2bn. The results are trending behind both Regis and Street forecasts, as the solid performance of Globe (GLO PM), narrower losses at several portfolio investments, as well as lower parent expenses only partly cushioned the shortfalls at ALI (here), BPI (here), and ACEN (here).

AC Mobility unit sales up 34% YoY; BYD sales double

AC Mobility’s net loss narrowed 73% QoQ/35% YoY to P109mn, partly due to the 12% QoQ/34% YoY increase in unit sales to 12.3k units, with BYD sales doubling. The divestment of loss-making businesses was also a key factor in the earnings improvement. Relative to the broader industry, AC estimates its market share improved by 1.2ppts to 8.5%, with management targeting a 12.5% share by year-end. However, the influx of new brands has reduced its share of the New Energy Vehicle (NEV) segment to 64% from 85% previously. We are not concerned about the NEV share drop given the structural industry shift, with the ongoing oil crisis likely to accelerate demand further.

Bonuses lead to EBITDA drop at AC Health; IMI core profit up 21%

AC Health posted a wider net loss of P143mn. Even though revenues rose 19%, EBITDA fell 21% YoY to P209mn due to the late accrual of last year’s employee bonuses. Despite lower revenues, lMI’s core profit rose 21% YoY to US$4.5mn due to lower opex and financing costs. Cost rationalization initiatives at AC Logistics continued to gain traction, with losses narrowing to P167mn in 1Q.

Capital allocation

Management remains committed to supporting the growth of ACEN and the capital requirements the latter needs to achieve its targets. The group however says it does not plan to stray from the prudent path amidst the prevailing macroeconomic challenges. AC’s expanded buyback program will be continued, but in a calibrated manner to avoid adversely affecting stock liquidity and index weightings.

TRANSPORT
Philippine port regulator implements fuel surcharge
  • The Philippine Ports Authority (PPA) has issued guidelines (here) on the implementation of fuel surcharge adjustment (FSA) that port operators may impose as a temporary cost-recovery measure due to the increase in fuel prices
    • The FSA will be applied to stevedoring and arrastre tariffs, and the formula will be based on the Department of Energy’s published fuel prices
    • FSA will not be part of the PPA’s revenue share, concession fees, or similar charges
  • This will help relieve margin pressure for ICTSI’s (ICT PM) ports in the Philippines, which account for ~30% of revenues and 26% of profits
  • ICTSI trades at 12x EV/EBITDA and 21x PER (vs. peer average: 11x EBITDA/16x PER and historical average of 11x EBITDA/19x PER)

House transmits articles of impeachment vs VP Duterte to Senate
  • The House of Representatives announced that it had formally transmitted the committee report that contains the articles of impeachment against VP Sara Duterte to the Senate, two days after she was impeached by the House
    • The House stated that the transmittal marks the start of the next constitutional phase of the impeachment process and paves the way for Senate trial
  • Recall that the newly installed Senate President Alan Peter Cayetano stated that he vows to convene the impeachment court after transmittal to start the trial "forthwith"
  • An impeached official is "convicted" if at least two-thirds of the Senate find the official guilty of the charges filed by the House
MACRO
Marcos sets P50/kg price cap on imported rice
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. set the price ceiling for imported rice at P50/kg, he highlighted the need to protect consumers by containing prices given the national energy emergency and by deterring market manipulation (e.g., profiteering, unjustified price increases)
  • Executive Order (EO) 118 puts a cap on the price of 5% broken imported rice for an initial period of 30 days and takes effect immediately
    • The National Price Coordinating Council is tasked to review the cap every 15 days for possible adjustment or discontinuation
  • Rice prices are currently at P50.3/kg and P48/kg as of May for local and imported well-milled rice, rising from the low of around P42/kg each back in Oct-25
