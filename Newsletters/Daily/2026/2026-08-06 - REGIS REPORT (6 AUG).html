REGIS REPORT

06 August 2026

Semirara’s (SCC PM) 1H26 recurring profit edged up 1% YoY to P8.4bn, following a 19% QoQ and 14% YoY improvement in 2Q26 core profits to P4.6bn.  Profit from the power segment rebounded fourfold QoQ and doubled YoY to P3.9bn in 2Q26, buoyed by stronger power spot prices and fewer plant outages. The strong performance of the power unit negated the very weak performance of the coal mining unit, where 2Q earnings collapsed 73% QoQ/65% YoY to a mere P771mn, as persistent water seepage issues at its new mine led to a sharp drop in sales volumes. For further detail, please see our comments below.  More to follow after management’s briefing this afternoon. 

 

+++

 

The deceleration in headline CPI inflation to 6.2% in July was largely due to softening Transport costs. We note that Food prices are visibly creeping up with inflation for the Food basket now at 5.2% and rice inflation at 17%. We remain wary about the direction of inflation given (1) the chunky minimum wage hike (suspended for now) for Metro Manila and (2) the threat from El Nino. Note the government projects that palay (unmilled rice) output this 3Q will be down by 13% due to a decline in hectarage.

 

The Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) Monetary Board meets on Aug 27 to decide on policy rates. We think a 25bp hike is likely.

POWER
Semirara: Higher power spot prices lift 2Q26 profit up 14% YoY, despite challenging mining ops
  • Strong 2Q26 earnings beat from power unit. Semirara reported its net income rose 17% YoY to P4.76bn in 2Q26. Removing a one-off reversal of non-cash charges at its power unit, as well as FX adjustments, we estimate 2Q26 core profit rose 14% YoY to P4.56bn, underpinned by (a) strong power prices and (b) fewer plant outages, which more than compensated for the drop in sales volumes of the coal mining unit. The strong 2Q results for the power unit brought its cumulative 1H26 profit to P8.4bn (+1% YoY), comfortably on track to beat both Regis and consensus full-year estimates.   

 

  • Coal mining production collapses in 2Q due to persistent water seepage issues at new Acacia mine. Despite a 14% QoQ and 17% YoY increase in ASPs to P2,833/MT, coal mining profits plunged 73% QoQ and 65% YoY to P771mn in 2Q26, as production more than halved QoQ/YoY. Management had previously expected the new Acacia mine to fully ramp up in 2Q26 while its Narra mine underwent pre-stripping activities, but the transition progressed more slowly than anticipated due to persistent water seepage issues in Acacia. As a result, production from Acacia remained limited, leading to an 11% QoQ/13% YoY drop in sales volumes to 4mn MT.  Sales volumes reached 8.5mn MT in 1H, down 9% YoY but remains on track to hit management’s 14-15mn MT guidance.

 

  • Power profits double YoY in 2Q on high spot prices. Profits from the power segment doubled to P3.9bn in 2Q26 while EBITDA improved 65% YoY to P5.2bn. The earnings expansion was supported by (a) a 29% jump in ASPs and (b) a higher plant availability factor of 94% (vs. 87% in 2Q25), as well as a 27% YoY drop in non-cash costs following an extension of estimated useful lives of some of its assets. Its realized spot prices surged 70% YoY to P6.80/kwh in 2Q26, which more than offset the 9% drop in contracted power pirces. 

 

  • Net cash position at P12.8bn, equivalent to 14% of market cap. Following the deferral of dividend payments unit the coal operating contract bidding is over, Semirara’s net cash position has ballooned to P12.8bn in end-June, equivalent to 14% of its market capitalization. 

 

  • Management will hold a briefing this afternoon, Aug 6, at 4pm (registration link).  Beyond management’s near-term guidance, we also hope to get an update on their views regarding the upcoming coal contract bidding process.

 

 

 

MACRO
Inflation moderates further to 6.2% in July
  • Headline inflation moderated to 6.2% in Jul from Jun's 6.4%
    • This was slightly lower than consensus expectations of 6.4% and within the BSP's forecast range of 5.6-6.6% for Jul
    • The softer inflation print is the third consecutive month of deceleration following five consecutive months of accelerating inflation
  • This brings 7M26 inflation to 5.0%, above the upper limit of the BSP's long-term target of 3% (± 1ppt) but still below their forecast of 6.4% for the year
  • Inflation in Jul decelerated given primarily driven by the slower YoY increase in fuel prices vs Jun
    • Food prices overall rose 5.2% YoY,  unchanged relative to the previous month, as lower prices of meat were offset by higher rice prices
      • Rice prices climbed 17.0% YoY in Jul, picking up from the 15.0% YoY increase in Jun
      • Rice prices are currently at P48.5/kg and P47.9/kg as of end-Jul for local and imported well-milled rice
    • Transport prices rose 11.9% YoY, slower than +12.8% the previous month
      • The increase in diesel prices was relatively steady at +38.5% YoY from +39.0% YoY, while the gasoline price increase moderated to +34.1% YoY from +39.2% YoY in Jun
  • Core inflation, which excludes volatile food and energy items, eased to 4.2% in Jul from 4.4% in Jun, as the moderation in core non-food (4.4% from 4.6% in Jun) offset the acceleration in core food (3.6% from 3.3%)
  • For 2026, the BSP forecasts inflation to settle at 6.4%
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to be 5.9% and 6.7%, respectively
  • The fourth out of the six Monetary Board meetings scheduled for the year will be held on Aug 27
    • The current policy rate is 4.75%
      • For 2026, consensus and Regis expectations for rate hikes are +100bp total to 5.25% by year-end
