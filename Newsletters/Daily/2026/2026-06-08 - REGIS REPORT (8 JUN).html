REGIS REPORT

08 June 2026

Jollibee (JFC PM), according to a media report, is considering moving the location of the listing of its international operations to HK (vs the US). Moving the listing to HK would bring it closer to investors more familiar with its key brands, in our view.

 

Nevertheless, we think the spin-off plan is still in its early stages and many things are still likely to change.

 

+++

 

A drop in food price inflation coupled with sharp MoM falls in pump prices helped to trim overall CPI inflation to 6.8% in May. We think this slowdown is a temporary reprieve and inflation would again resume its climb, especially with the impending return of a “severe” El Nino.

 

The Monetary Board (MB) of the Bangko Sentral (BSP) will meet on June 18 to decide on policy rates. We expect an upward move to come out of the meeting.

 

+++

 

SM Prime (SMPH PM) announced the opening of its first hotel in China, a businessman’s hotel under the “Voco” brand. The group’s hotel segment, with 2,600 rooms in the Philippines, accounts for just 3% group operating profit.

 

+++

 

The country’s international reserves inched down 0.3% MoM to US$104bn in May. This is equivalent to 6.9 months import cover.

 

+++

 

Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) signed an agreement to develop as much as 5GW worth of power capacity (RE and gas) with a Saudi Arabian firm. This would contradict previous pronouncements about it limiting its exposure to this sector (power).

CONSUMER
Jollibee is said to mull moving US listing of int'l business to HK
  • Jollibee is reportedly considering moving the potential listing of its international business from the US to Hong Kong after it is spun off
    • Considerations are preliminary and plans are still subject to change
  • Recall that JFC earlier this year in January stated that it plans to spin off and list its international operations in the US by latter part of 2027
    • The spin-off will be accomplished by giving existing JFC shareholders a proportionate ownership in the new spun-off entity (Jollibee Foods Corp International or JFCI)
MACRO
Inflation moderates to 6.8% in May
  • Headline inflation moderated to 6.8% in May from Apr's 7.2%
    • This was lower than consensus expectations of 7.8% and below the BSP's forecast range of 7.1-7.8% for Apr
    • The softer inflation print ends five consecutive months of accelerating inflation
    • The third out of the six Monetary Board meetings for the year will be held on Jun 18
      • For 2026, consensus expects two more rate hikes to 5.00% by year-end
        • Regis expects four more hikes to 5.50% by end-2026
  • This brings 5M26 inflation to 4.5%, above the upper limit of the BSP's long-term target of 3% (± 1ppt) but still below their forecast of 6.3% for the year
  • Inflation in May decelerated given slower YoY increases vs Apr in food as well as utilities (electricity, gas, fuel), and transport prices
    • Food prices overall rose 5.7% YoY, decelerating from the 6.0% pace in Apr, driven by lower prices of meat and lower YoY increases in fish and vegetables which offset higher rice prices
      • Rice prices jumped 15.6% YoY in May, picking up from the 13.7% YoY increase in Apr, marking the second straight month of acceleration
      • Rice prices are currently at P49.7/kg and P48.0/kg as of end-May for local and imported well-milled rice, rising from the low of around P42/kg each back in Oct-25
    • Utilities and transport prices climbed 15.9% YoY and 16.2% YoY, slower than +16.9% and +21.4% the previous month
      • The increase in diesel prices slowed to 58.5% YoY from 122.7% YoY, while the gasoline price increase moderated to 51.5% YoY from 59.6% YoY in Apr
  • Core inflation picked up to 4.1% in May from 3.9% in Apr, driven by core non-food inflation which rose by 4.4% in May from 4.0% in Apr
  • For 2026, the BSP forecasts inflation to settle at 6.3% (previously revised from 5.1% and 3.6%)
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to be 6.3% and 7.5%, respectively

 

 

 

 

 

PROPERTY
SM Prime opens first hotel in China
  • SM Prime opened its first hotel in China
    • 325 rooms in SM Prime’s 10-hectare Xiamen complex in Fujian province
    • It offers accommodations for RMB700/night (P6,300/night)
    • The hotel is under the label Voco, a four-star hospitality brand under InterContinental Hotels Group
  • The company previously said that its future expansion in China would only be in Fujian province, where it has stronger brand equity
  • The hotel segment accounts for c. 3% of SM Prime’s operating profit
  • The company has 2,600 hotel rooms in the Philippines  
  • SMPH is trading at 59% discount to NAV, 1.0X FY26E P/B, and 10.5X FY26E P/E
MACRO
PH dollar reserves at $104bn as of end-May
  • The Philippines' gross international reserves (GIR) at end-May was flat (-0.3% MoM) at US$104.0bn relative to the previous month (US$104.3bn)
    • The May GIR figure is a 16-month low
    • The slight MoM drop in the GIR was primarily due to central bank's foreign exchange operations given its continued effort to support the peso
      • The USD-PHP rate depreciated to P61.4 in end-May from P60.3 in end-Apr
    • The level of dollar reserves was enough to cover about 3.6x the country's short-term external debt and equivalent to 6.9x months' worth of imports of goods and payments
  • For 2026, the BSP expects the GIR to settle at US$111bn

 

 

 

POWER
Nickel Asia teams up with ACWA Power for 5GW of RE, gas-fired projects
  • Nickel Asia’s power unit, Emerging Power Inc. (EPI), signed an agreement with ACWA Power Phils. to explore up to 5GW of renewable energy and gas-fired generation capacity across the Philippines
    • ACWA Power Phils is a subsidiary of KSA-based ACWA Power (ACWA AB), a firm with a portfolio of power generation and water desalination assets across 15 countries
    • EPI and ACWA plan to participate in the government’s Green Energy Auction Program
  • This partnership would be a departure from the company’s recently stated plans of limiting its RE portfolio to 1GW (vs. current operating gross capacity at 458MW)
    • The move suggests NIKL’s growing ambition to develop larger-scale renewable energy projects
