REGIS REPORT

09 June 2026

A strong earthquake (magnitude 7.8) struck southern Mindanao yesterday morning.  Its epicenter was located 13km southwest of the city of General Santos, in the region of Soccksargen (Region 12). As of the latest count, fatalities from the earthquake number 35. That number is likely to climb in the coming days.

 

Soccksargen is a relatively underdeveloped region and has a population of 4.5m (vs PH population of 110m+). Among the key industries are agriculture (coconut, pineapple, banana) and fisheries (tuna). In terms of economic output, it accounts for 2.5% of GDP. General Santos, its largest city, has a population of ~700,000 and is informally called the “Tuna Capital of the Philippines”.

 

A few companies have provided an initial assessment of the damage from the quake, including Century Pacific (CNPF PM) whose core business, as one can guess, is closely tied to the region. Check it out below.

Citicore REIT (CREIT PM) expects several asset injections this year which will boost its portfolio. Its biggest shareholder, Citicore Renewable Energy (CREC PM), is spending heavily this year to expand capacity. Injections of RE projects by CREC into CREIT and the accompany sale of CREIT shares by CREC are one way to raise money for CREC.

The Customer Xperience Association of the Philippines (CXAP), formerly the Contact Center Association of the Philippines, wants to debunk the notion that AI will kill the contact center industry. CXAP, which is the umbrella group for contact (call) centers, is in fact claiming AI will create jobs.

MACRO
Magnitude 7.8 earthquake hits Southern Mindanao
  • A magnitude 7.8 earthquake hit the southern island of Mindanao at 7:37am on June 8
    • The origin of the earthquake was located 13 kilometers southwest of General Santos City, a coastal city in the province of South Cotabato
  • Soccksargen (Region 12), which includes four provinces—South Cotabato, Cotabato, Sarangani, Sultan Kudarat, was directly affected being the closest region to the epicenter
    • General Santos City experienced the strongest reported intensity at "Intensity VII" with buildings exhibiting significant structural damage or total collapse
    • Soccksargen contributed to 2.5% of the Philippines' GDP in 2025
  • The quake was also felt in other areas at varying intensities such as: Davao Region (i.e., Davao City) and Northern Mindanao
  • Century Pacific Food (CNPF PM) has two processing plants for tuna and coconut located in General Santos City
    • Operations are temporarily suspended with a target to resume within one to two weeks but subject to damage assessment and clearance from local authorities
    • Two fatalities were reported after a portion of a warehouse wall collapsed
    • The company stated that partner retailers currently hold ample inventory to meet near-term demand
    • The facilities are covered by insurance
    • As of FY25, the Branded Tuna and OEM (tuna and coconut) segments contributed to 31% and 20% of group-wide revenues, respectively
  • Wilcon Depot (WLCON PM) has two stores that suspended operations with no structural damage reported but some damage to inventory
    • The stores will remain closed until clearance is given by the local gov't units (LGUs)
  • Puregold Price Club (PGOLD PM) stated that one store remains closed after the earthquake
  • Jollibee Foods Corp (JFC PM) is still coordinating with teams in Mindanao to assess the impact to operations
    • According to various news reports, a multi-level building housing a Jollibee store reportedly collapsed but all employees were reported to be safe and accounted for
  • SM Prime (SMPH PM) temporarily closed its mall in General Santos
    • We estimate that the mall in General Santos comprises 1% of mall floor area and less than 1% of the company’s operating profit
    • All of SM Prime’s malls are insured for damages as well as lost revenue

Citicore REIT expects asset infusions within the year
  • Citicore REIT (CREIT PM) president and CEO Oliver Tan expects several solar asset infusions within the year
    • It is in the process of securing regulatory approvals for the planned infusions
  • Last May 15, CREIT’s board approved the infusion of ~1.7mn sqm of land and 860MWp of solar assets in the provinces of Pampanga, Pangasinan, Batangas, Quezon, and Negros Occidental
    • Upon completion, CREIT’s gross leasable area is expected to expand by 19% to 8.8mn sqm
    • The third-party valuation for the proposed asset infusion is currently underway
  • Citicore Renewable Energy (CREC PM) currently owns 32.8% of CREIT
    • CREC plans to commission 2.2GW of solar and 45MW of wind projects by year-end, expanding its operating asset base of 1.2GW as of end-March
    • CREC’s net debt stood at P39bn in end-2026, with net debt-to-equity ratio at 1.6x
PROPERTY
CXAP expects AI to create new contact center jobs
  • The Customer Xperience Association of the Philippines (CXAP), formerly the Contact Center Association of the Philippines, said it wants to debunk the notion that AI is negatively affecting the contact center industry
  • It expects AI to create new types of jobs
  • CXAP is targeting to grow industry revenue to US$35.7bn this year (+5% from US$33.9bn in 2025) and targeting to expand industry headcount to 1.73mn (+3% from 1.68mn in 2025)
  • CXAP is a sub-organization under the IT-BPO Association of the Philippines (IBPAP), which currently expects the industry to achieve revenue of US$42bn and employ 1.97mn Filipinos by the end of 2026
    • These represent 4%-5% growth from the 2025 revenue (US$40bn) and employee (1.9mn) figures
  • BPO firms occupy 70%-80% of the office space of the developers we cover
