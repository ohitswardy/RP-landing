REGIS REPORT

31 March 2026

Wilcon (WLCON PM) reported FY25 revenues and profit that were slightly ahead of our forecasts. As with the rest of the consumer sector, management reported that strong growth momentum carried into 1Q26. For Wilcon, March was even stronger, as there was some evidence of “panic buying” from customers wary of either price increases, or supply constraints delaying home construction or improvements. That said, the company warned of margin pressure in 2Q26 from increased logistics costs, as nearly all of Wilcon’s products are imported. The company declared a P0.40/sh cash dividend, for a yield of c7%.

 

+ + +

 

MTerra Solar, the solar farm + battery project of Meralco (MER PM) and SP New Energy (SPNEC PM) began supplying 250MW to the Luzon grid. It has also switched on its battery energy storage system, capable of delivering 450 MWH at night. The project should help ease supply constraints, especially as we enter the peak demand summer months of April-June. Upon full completion in 2027/28, the project should have a capacity of 3,500 MW and 4,500 MWH of battery storage.

McDonald’s plans to open 75 stores this year, on top of its existing 850 network, with a plan to open its 1,000th store in early 2028. For reference, the main Jollibee brand alone had 1,341 stores in the Philippines as of end-2025. Alliance Global (AGI PM) owns 49% of McDonald’s Philippines.

Wilcon Depot - 4Q25: Healthy top line momentum, uncertain runway due to global turmoil
  • FY25 profit -3%, top line +4%. WLCON’s FY25 net profit amounted to P2.4bn (-3% YoY) slightly ahead of expectations, while FY25 revenues (P35.4bn, +4% YoY), were in line with estimates. In 4Q25 alone, net profit climbed 41% to P580mn as EBIT surged 53%. Profitability improved on higher sales and margin expansion, with operating leverage amplifying growth off a low base in 4Q24. 4Q25 exhibited continued top line recovery as revenues rose 7% YoY on higher sales volumes.

 

  • Positive top line momentum. 4Q25 net sales rose 7% to P9.1bn, accelerating from the +3% growth in 9M25. SSSG was +3.8% on higher ticket size as in-store optimizations bore fruit. Management guided that sales likely improved further in 1Q26, with March ADS stronger than Jan-Feb amid customers reportedly accelerating purchases and “panic buying” due to the Middle East conflict. Although sustainability of this trend is uncertain as higher-priced inventory and inflationary pressures set in.

 

  • Margins expand but headwinds emerge. Gross margin in the quarter expanded 1.7ppt YoY on more disciplined discounting and sale of old inventory, while controlled opex growth (+3% YoY) further supported EBIT margin expansion (+2.8ppt YoY). However, margins were said to already be under pressure given global developments, particularly through higher logistics and inventory costs.

 

  • Higher dividends; strong start to the year but outlook unclear. The company declared dividends of P0.40/sh (record as of Apr 17, payable May 12), higher than the P0.36/sh dividends declared last year and implies a ~7% yield on the current share price. Adjustments in the payout ratio was said to be dependent on future inventory needs and working capital requirements. Overall, we view the sustained operational improvements as positive, but the path for continued growth momentum is less clear due to external risks.

MTerra Solar adds 250MW to capacity
  • The MTerra Solar project of SP New Energy Corporation (SPNEC PM) has begun delivering 250MW to the Luzon grid
    • SPNEC is 57% owned by Meralco (MER PM), through its subsidiary, MGEN Renewable Energy 
  • It has also switched on the first tranche of its battery energy storage system (BESS), allowing the facility to inject up to 450 megawatt hours (MWh) of energy to the grid system at night
  • The MTerra Solar project is slated for full commercial operations in 2027
  • Upon full completion, it will have a capacity of 3,500 MWp of solar power, supported by 4,500 MWh BESS
