REGIS REPORT

03 June 2026

The chairman of the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) has, not to our surprise, admitted that the final outcome of Meralco’s (MER PM) rate reset will not be completed this month.

 

ERC chairman Saturnino Juan says the tariff review results will be delayed one or two months beyond July. Under the ERC’s own timeline, it is supposed to release its decision this month (June), in time for implementation by next month (July).

 

The delay is not altogether surprising, given the ERC’s track record as an institution. The last time Meralco actually completed a tariff review (due every four years) was in 2010. We reckon a further delay for this current review beyond Sept is plausible.

POWER
ERC Chairman says decision on Meralco’s rate reset may be deferred to September
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC)’s ruling on Meralco’s rate reset submission may be delayed by 1-2 months beyond July to allow a more thorough review
    • According to Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Francis Saturnino C. Juan
    • Recall that MER is applying for a 73% increase in distribution tariffs to P2.34/kwh, primarily to fund its 5-year capex program of P272bn
    • Although MER’s tariff reviews are supposed to occur every four years, its last tariff reset process was completed in 2010
  • Outside of Meralco, the ERC expects to release its ruling for the first entry group by July, later than the original target of June
    • The first entry group includes Meralco, Cagayan Electric Power and Light Co. Inc., Cotabato Light and Power Co. Inc. and Dagupan Electric Corp.
UTILITIES
Maynilad appoints new Chief Legal Counsel
  • Maynilad has appointed Atty. Mercedita L. Ona-Saldua as its Chief Legal Counsel effective June 1
    • She will replace Atty. Lourdes Marivic K. Punzalan-Espiritu, who has served the role since 2008 and is set to take an early retirement
  • Atty. Saldua has been with Maynilad since 2012 and has been a key part of multiple rate rebasing exercises and arbitration proceedings
  • We believe Maynilad has a strong legal team, as evidenced by its success in several arbitration proceedings

MACRO
Government debt inches down to P18.49tn in end-Apr
  • The government's total debt stock went down by 0.1% MoM (P17.6bn) to P18.49tn as of end-Apr
    • This was due to the repayment of domestic securities which offset the impact of depreciation of the PHP against the USD relative to the previous month of Mar
    • As of end-Apr, the USD-PHP depreciated to P60.3, from P59.4 at end-Mar
  • Domestic debt declined by 1.0% (P119.2bn) MoM to P12.41tn due to the impact of net redemption (P121.6bn) with debt maturities exceeding new issuances
  • External debt increased by 1.7% (P101.6bn) MoM to P6.06tn due to unfavorable currency movements
  • The debt-to-GDP ratio rose to 65.2% as of 1Q26 from 63.2% end-2025
    • The 65.2% ratio is the highest since hitting 65.7% back in 2005
  • The Dept. of Finance's (DoF) forecasts as of Aug 2025 projected the debt-to-GDP ratio to hit 61.8% in 2026, before gradually declining to 61.3% in 2027 and 60.3% in 2028
    • By 2029, the DoF expects the ratio to fall below 60% to 59.5%
