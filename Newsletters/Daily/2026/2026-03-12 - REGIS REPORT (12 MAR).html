The FY25 profits of Jollibee Foods (JFC PM) came in at P10.5bn (after pref divs), 6% higher YoY. This would be short of the estimates of both Regis and the rest of the Street. Note the YoY growth was already lifted by the chunky (>P800m pretax) FX loss in 2024. Overall, we view the top line growth (+13% for FY25, +10% for 4Q) positively. Much of that growth, however, was negated by the time it reached the bottom line.

TRANSPORT
Volumes at ICTSI’s Mexico port up 18% in February, +11% in 2M26
  • Data from Mexico’s port authority showed that throughput at Contecon Manzanillo (CMSA) jumped 18% YoY to 138k TEUs in February
  • This brings 2M26 volumes to 280k TEUs, up 11% YoY
    • We anticipate CMSA’s volumes will rise by 8% to 1.66mn TEUs in 2026
    • To reach our forecast, volumes for the rest of the year would have to average 138k TEUs per month
    • Every 5% increase in CMSA’s volumes will raise our EBITDA forecast by 1% and profit forecast by 2%
    • Note that CMSA is expanding capacity from 1.7mn TEUs to 2mn TEUs (~167k TEUs per month) by 3Q26
  • We estimate CMSA accounts for 10% of group-wide volumes, 20% of revenues, and 23% of EBITDA
  • ICTSI trades at 12x EV/EBITDA and 20x PER vs. peer average (15x EBITDA / 21x PER) vs. historical average (11x / 19x)

 

 

 

MACRO
House approves fuel excise tax adjustment on second reading; DOTr announces P5,000 fuel subsidy to PUV drivers
  • The House of Representatives approved on second reading the measure authorizing President Marcos, until Dec 31, 2028, to suspend or reduce the excise tax on fuel products, while the counterpart measure in the Senate is up for plenary approval
    • Senator Pia Cayetano stated that a consolidated bill is eyed to be discussed by Monday next week
  • Under the draft bill, the President may adjust the fuel excise tax only if one of the two conditions are present:
    • (a) Dubai crude oil price reaches or exceeds US$80/bbl for one month immediately before the order to suspend or reduce is issued
    • (b) any order to adjust excise taxes due to emergencies or calamities must be certified by the Energy secretary to confirm that pump prices surged "extraordinarily"
  • The measure sets a duration limit of up to six months unless Congress extends or terminates earlier through a joint resolution
    • It also requires the adjustment to be lifted if extraordinary conditions no longer exist and automatic reinstatement of the excise tax after the period ends without need for further gov't action
  • Dubai crude prices as of Mar 11 are at US$113.5/bbl up 66% from US$68.4/bbl end-Feb
  • The Dept. of Transportation (DOTr) announced that the gov't will start distributing a P5,000 fuel subsidy to public utility drivers (PUV) in Metro Manila starting March 17
    • Toll operators were also said to provide discounts to buses and trucks to reduce costs and offset the impact of higher fuel prices
    • DOTr Secretaary Giovanni Lopez, however, guided that there will be a fare adjustment that will impact commuters but it is currently under evaluation
CONGLOS
Metro Pacific FY25 core profit up 15% to P27bn
  • Metro Pacific Investments (MPIC) announced its FY25 core net income rose 15% to P27.1bn while headline profit rose 5% due to a one-off gain in 2024
    • By segment, the power unit contributed P22.1bn (+12%), water unit at P7.2bn (+16%), and tollroad unit at P6.1bn (-3%)
    • At the parent level, MPIC’s net debt was reduced to P52.5bn in end-2025 from P61.5bn, with gross cash at P7.9bn
  • Derived 4Q25 core profit rose 24% YoY (-59% QoQ) to P3.5bn
  • MPIC is a 19.6%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounted for 18% of GTCAP’s 9M25 profits
MINING
Department of Energy says no change in coal mining royalty structure
  • The Department of Energy will maintain the royalty structure for coal miners, even as the coal operating contract covering 10 blocks on Semirara Island is set for re-bidding
    • According to Energy Undersecretary Alessandro Sales
    • Cited the royalty structure is legally defined in the Coal Development Act of 1976
  • Submission of bids is scheduled on April 28
