15 April 2026

Jollibee (JFC PM) issued a press release citing improvements at its US-based burger chain Smashburger. Same-store sales reached “positive double-digit growth” by March. SSS had been contracting for all of the previous four quarters.

 

Is this a sign of a turnaround? Too early to say at this point. It sounds like a major price-led campaign was behind the sales improvement and it’ll take time for one to see if this can last.

 

+++

 

Throughput at ICTSI’s (ICT PM) port in Mexico rose 3% YoY in March, decelerating from the 18% pace in February due to congestion issues. Cumulative 1Q26 volumes for this port, which we estimate accounts for 20% of revenues, are up 8%. 

 

+++

 

The Development Budget Coordination Committee (DBCC) has come out opposing the suspension of the excise taxes on gasoline and diesel. It said the cut “would not likely provide meaningful relief, as any reduction in retail pump prices would be marginal and largely offset by prevailing market dynamics". The excise tax is currently equivalent to around 11-12% of the pump price of gasoline and 4-5% of diesel.

 

Instead, the DBCC is proposing subsidies targeting public utility vehicles. We are, however, not convinced about the effectiveness of such targeted subsidies to address issues of transport and logistics costs, not just for people but also for goods.

 

+++

 

Moody’s retained its Baa2 stable rating for the Philippines in its latest report, similar to the recent assessment of S&P.

CONSUMER
Jollibee provides update on Smashburger
  • JFC, in a press release, provided an update on Smashburger stating that SSSG for company-owned stores improved from negative mid-teen levels in 3Q25 to positive double-digit growth by Mar-26 on higher transaction volumes following its "Summer of Smash" campaign
    • The company noted improvements in "menu innovations and value offerings" as the drivers for the recovery with company-owned stores seeing high teens transaction and average daily sales growth
  • Smash targets to open 10-12 new stores in 2026 through franchise expansion
    • Recent new store openings in both traditional (in malls or standalone) and non-traditional (e.g., sports arenas, airports, universities) formats saw healthy customer demand and attractive returns
    • Smashburger had 192 stores as of end-2025, down from 214 in end-2024
      • In 2025, 62% of stores are company-owned while 38% are franchised stores
  • The brand contributed to 3.0% of total SWS in FY25, down from 4.0% in FY24
    • For 2025, growth in SWS and SSS was -12.9% and -8.2%, respectively
TRANSPORT
ICTSI’s Mexico port volumes rise 3% in March; 1Q26 volumes up 8%
  • Mexico’s port authority reported that throughput at Contecon Manzanillo (CMSA) rose 3% YoY to 137k TEUs in March
    • Growth slowed from the 18% print in February due to congestion issues
    • Management previously indicated that the congestion has led to elevated yields
  • This brings CMSA’s 1Q26 volumes up 8% to 417k TEUs
    • We anticipate CMSA’s volumes will rise by 8% to 1.66mn TEUs this year
    • To reach our forecast, volumes for the rest of the year would have to average 138k TEUs per month
    • Every 5% increase in CMSA’s volumes will raise our EBITDA forecast by 1% and profit forecast by 2%
    • Note that CMSA is expanding capacity from 1.7mn TEUs to 2mn TEUs (~167k TEUs per month) by 3Q26
  • We estimate CMSA accounts for 10% of group-wide volumes, 20% of revenues, and 23% of EBITDA
  • ICTSI trades at 11.7x EV/EBITDA and 20x PER vs. peer average (11x EBITDA / 15x PER) vs. historical average (11x / 19x)

 

 

 

MACRO
DBCC opposes gas and diesel excise tax suspension
  • The Development Budget Coordination Committee (DBCC) has determined that the suspension of gas and diesel excise taxes "would not likely provide meaningful relief, as any reduction in retail pump prices would be marginal and largely offset by prevailing market dynamics"
  • Finance Secretary Frederick Go highlighted that the suspension of excise taxes on kerosene and LPG directly provides relief on the most vulnerable
    • The Philippine Statistics Authority survey exhibited that 48% of total kerosene usage is from the bottom 30% of households, while 56% of LPG users are from the bottom 70%
    • The reduction for kerosene and LPG is equivalent to P5.60/liter and P3.36/kg (P37 per tank)
  • The gov't anticipates P4.1bn in foregone revenue for the next three months due to the suspension but would be offset by P13bn in additional VAT collections assuming crude stays at US$100/bbl
  • If the suspension on gas and diesel excise tax were approved, the gov't stated that it would have incurred P43.6bn in foregone revenues
    • Excise taxes are at P10/liter for gas and P6/liter for diesel
MACRO
Moody's retains "Baa2 stable" investment rating for the Philippines
  • Moody's retained its "Baa2 stable" investment-grade rating for the Philippines
  • The ratings agency noted key strengths such as a stable banking system, ample foreign-currency reserves, pipeline of PPP projects, and resilient OFW remittances
    • Credit challenges include low capita per income, "weak rule of law and control of corruption", high climate and natural disaster risk
  • A ratings upgrade could be considered if there is rapid improvement in fiscal and gov't debt metrics but a downgrade may occur if such metrics deteriorate
MACRO
Government rejects unsolicited Iloilo airport proposal
  • The Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP) rejected the P21bn unsolicited proposal of Prime Asset Ventures to upgrade and operate the Iloilo international airport
    • Cited that the the group’s proposal lacked an endorsement to the Department of Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev)
    • The government is opting to privatize the airport through a solicited mode instead
  • Prime Asset Ventures is a company owned by the Villar family
