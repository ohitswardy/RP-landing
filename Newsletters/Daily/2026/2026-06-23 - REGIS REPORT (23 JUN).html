REGIS REPORT

23 June 2026

The Dept of Economic Planning (DEPDev) has cut the government’s official GDP growth target to 3.5-4.5% from 5.0-6.0%. The reduction comes after the surprisingly weak 1Q26 GDP growth of 2.8%, compounded by the continuing impact of the attack on Iran.

 

This adjustment in the official GDP numbers should precede changes in other government targets, including on tax revenue and the fiscal balance.

 

+++

 

PLDT (TEL PM) has filed the IPO application for its data center business, which will be separately listed as a REIT. Based on the draft prospectus, the VITRO REIT will hold eight DCs with a combined capacity of 24MW (IT load).

 

The filing also shows PLDT intends to sell up to 2.2bn secondary shares in VITRO, equivalent to 49% of the equity, at a maximum price of P11/share. The maximum price (P11/share) would translate to a projected dividend yield of 6.2% on the target dividend of P0.68/share for FY27.  That yield (6.2%) would be lower than the other listed Philippine REITs, which are largely real estate REITs.

 

The value enhancement to PLDT could be as much 10% at the maximum price of VITRO, we estimate.

 

More details below.

 

+++

 

The US government, this time through the Federal Communications Commission, is again raising proposals that could make it more difficult for American firms to outsource contact/call center work to countries like the Philippines. Like in the past, we expect American firms to lobby hard against such proposed measures.

MACRO
Gov't cuts 2026 GDP growth target to 3.5-4.5%; expects recovery in 2H26
  • Dept. of Economy, Planning and Development (DEPDev) Secretary Arsenio Balisacan stated that the GDP growth target for 2026 has been reduced to 3.5-4.5% from 5.0-6.0% previously given the impact of the Middle East conflict and gov't corruption crackdown
    • 1Q26 GDP growth was 2.8%, the slowest since 2009 excluding the pandemic
    • To reach the lower and upper ends of the FY26 growth target band, GDP growth needs to average 3.7% and 5.1%, respectively, for the rest of the year
  • Balisacan added that he expects government spending to rebound in 2H26, noting that the gov't is seeing an improving fiscal outlook in the second half of this year
  • He remains optimistic that the Middle East crisis did not erode the country's growth narrative with household consumption and services expected to continue to anchor growth
    • He also added that the return to high growth can be done through mitigating external risks and inflationary pressures, prioritizing high-impact infra projects, as well as restoring public trust
  • The Development Budget Coordination Committee (DBCC) is expected to announce the official recalibrated macro assumptions and fiscal targets within the week
  • For 2026, consensus and Regis expect GDP to grow 4.0% and 3.8%, respectively
PROPERTY
PLDT files papers for the REIT IPO of its data center business
  • PLDT announced that it has filed the draft prospectus for the spin-off and listing of its data center (DC) business, VITRO, as a REIT
  • VITRO REIT’s initial portfolio is comprised of eight data center assets with an aggregate Total IT Ready Capacity of approximately 24MW
  • 1.91bn secondary common shares with an over-allotment option of up to 287mn shares
    • Total offer of up to 2.2bn secondary common shares
  • The offer shares (assuming full exercise of the over-allotment option) represent 48.95% of total outstanding shares
  • Offer price of up to P11/share
    • Maximum amount to be raised (including over-allotment option) – P24.2bn
    • Maximum market capitalization of P49.4bn
  • Use of proceeds:
    • P6.7bn – invest in PLDT Group’s Data Center, telecommunications equipment, and network infrastructure
    • P3.8bn – to be infused as equity into VITRO Sta. Rosa
    • P12.8bn – to pay debt used for the construction of VITRO Santa Rosa
  • Target offer period – September 25 to October 1
  • VITRO REIT is targeting to pay DPS of P0.68/share in FY27
    • This represents FY27 dividend yield of 6.2% (at the maximum offer price of P11/share)
  • It is targeting to pay DPS of P0.16/share in 4Q26
    • This represents 4Q26 annualized dividend yield of 5.8% (At the maximum offer price of P11/share) 
  • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 7.0% 
  • PLDT previously said it was planning to raise money for debt reduction through a number of initiatives, including a DC REIT IPO
  • The data center business comprised c. 6% of PLDT’s 2025 earnings

 

 

PROPERTY
US FCC proposes onshoring of call centers
  • The US Federal Communications Commission (FCC) is seeking comment on proposals that would encourage businesses to bring call center jobs back to the US
  • The FCC alleges that outsourcing:
    • “…took jobs away from communities across the country…”
    • Led to poor customer service
    • Poses risks related to data protection and national security
  • The proposals include:
    • Empowering consumer to transfer calls to a US based location
    • Requiring calls that involve certain types of sensitive information be handled domestically
    • Requiring covered providers to disclose the location of the call center
    • Requiring call center workers to be proficient in American Standard English and to be trained appropriately for resolving issues with US customers
  • The IT-BPO Association of the Philippines has submitted its position to the FCC and is coordinating with Philippine government agencies
  • Philippine ambassador to Washington Jose Manuel Romualdez said the Philippine government is working with lawmakers and industry stakeholders to seek exemptions for the country
  • Contact center outsourcing generated US$33.9bn in revenue and employed 1.68mn Filipinos in 2025
  • About 70% of the revenue of the BPO sector is from North America
  • BPO firms occupy 70%-80% of the office space of the developers we cover
GAMING
DigiPlus aims to grow VIP business
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) aims to attract more high-spending players
  • VIP players account for a significant portion of revenues even though majority of the company’s 10mn customers belong to the mass market 
  • PLUS’ VIP customers generate a minimum monthly turnover of P3mn/month
  • VIP segments are broken down into multiple categories: “Crown,” “Imperial,” and the highest being “Maharlika”
  • Based on these categories, the company provides benefits such as trips to local and overseas destinations. The company also holds birthday parties for VIP clients and provides shopping sprees where it closes out signature stores
  • PLUS is trading at 4.9X FY26E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
TRANSPORT
Air Asia Philippines may begin replacing older aircraft in end-2028
  • Air Asia Philippines (AAP) may receive its first A220 aircraft by end-2028
    • According to AirAsia Group Executive Officer Bo Lingam
    • The move is part of the group’s fleet renewal program, although the allocation of aircraft to each country will still be finalized by 1Q28
  • Note that AirAsia Group has been deploying aircraft to other markets, except the Philippines, since the pandemic ended
    • AAP currently has 13 operating aircraft, down from 24 pre-covid
    • AAP has a market share of 14% on domestic routes and 4% on international routes
