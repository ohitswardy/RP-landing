REGIS REPORT

23 February 2026

FY25 recurring profit of Ayala Land (ALI PM) grew 8% to P30.6bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts if the gain from the sale of its stake in Alabang Tower Center were excluded. 4Q25 residential reservation sales fell 30% QoQ and 8% YoY to P24.4bn, bringing the full-year figure to P125bn (down 1% YoY). The company appears cautious on the residential segment, only guiding for a minimum of P120bn in reservation sales in 2026. Hopes for a strong rebound in residential demand seem to have been doused by recent numbers.

 

In contrast, it is targeting double-digit mall revenue growth this year (from 5% in 2025), as the redevelopment of the two remaining flagship malls is completed and the company expands leasable area by 9% (200k sqm).

MACRO
Finance Secretary expects new 10% Trump tariff to have minimal impact on Philippine goods
  • Dept. of Finance Secretary Frederick Go stated that US President Donald Trump's recent declaration to impose a 150-day 10% global duty (effective Feb 24) will have minimal impact on Philippine goods
    • Go highlighted that majority of Philippine exports were already exempted (i.e., semiconductor and key agricultural exports such as coconut and banana)
  • The new global tariff declaration by Trump follows the US Supreme Court's decision to void the earlier imposed Trump tariffs
    • Recall that the prior US-imposed tariff on Philippine goods was 19%
  • In 2025, Philippine exports to the US amounted to US$13.3bn (+11% YoY) and comprised 16% of total exports
CONGLOS
Toyota Motor Philippines sees flat-to-low single-digit sales volume growth in 2026
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) expects sales volumes to remain flat to up 5% this year, reflecting subdued private spending
    • According to TMP Chairman Alfred Ty
    • The market is likely on a “wait-and-see” stance before purchasing activity regains momentum
    • We are forecasting sales volumes of 247k units in 2026, up 7% from last year’s sales of 229k units
    • Every 5% reduction in our sales volume forecast will cut TMP’s profit estimate by 2% and GTCAP’s profit by <1%
  • TMP is targeting growth for its affordable hybrid vehicles, with provincial markets seen as a key driver
    • TMP sold 19,516 units of electric models in 2025, up 40% YoY and accounting for 33% of industry-wide EV sales (note that BYD’s market share of EV market stood at 44% in 2025)
  • For 2026, TMP plans to introduce five new models, including three electrified vehicles:
    • the Urban Cruiser battery electric vehicle (EV),
    • the RAV4 hybrid EV, and
    • the Land Cruiser 300 hybrid EV
    • Other new launches will include the Land Cruiser FJ and the all-new Hilux
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 23% of FY26E profit
    • GTCAP trades at a 47% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,208/sh and at 4.6x PER
TRANSPORT
Air passenger volumes rise 4% YoY in FY25; 4Q25 pax volumes soften
  • Data from airline regulator, Civil Aeronautics Board (CAB), showed that domestic and international passenger volumes rose by 4% YoY to 62mn in FY25
    • Domestic passenger volumes rose 3.5% YoY to 33.2mn
    • International passenger volumes rose 5% YoY to 29.1mn
    • By airline, Cebu Air sustained market share gains, up +2pts to 40% (vs. 34% pre-Covid); Philippine Airlines retained market share at 26% (vs. 27% pre-Covid) while Air Asia Philippines stood at 9% (vs. 13% pre-Covid)
  • Derived 4Q25 systemwide passenger volumes dipped by 2% YoY, reversing 9M25 trend of +6%
    • Both domestic and international pax volumes dropped by 2% YoY in 4Q25
    • CEB was the only airline, which grew volumes in 4Q
