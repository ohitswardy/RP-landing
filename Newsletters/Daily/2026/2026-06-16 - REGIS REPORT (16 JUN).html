REGIS REPORT

16 June 2026

President Marcos signed a proclamation calling for a special session of Congress tomorrow (June 17). The special session will allow Congress to pass some pieces of priority legislation (including a rather weak Anti-Political Dynasty bill) as well as approve military promotions. This would have otherwise been delayed past July (if not longer).

 

The special session could also settle lingering issues relating to the leadership of the Senate. Settling the leadership at the Senate could have important ramifications for the impeachment proceedings against Vice President Sara Duterte (and thus the 2028 elections).

 

+++

 

ACEN (ACEN PM) announced it is selling up to 49% of its wholly-owned 420MW Tejorupa solar project in India to an affiliate of Mitsubishi Corp’s wholly owned unit, Diamond Generating Asia (DGA). The partnership should ease ACEN’s funding requirements for the project, which is currently under construction and is set to be completed in 2027. ACEN has partnered with DGA on other renewable energy projects, including North Luzon Renewables and the Capa wind project in the Philippines.

 

+++

 

Overseas remittances reached US$2.72bn for April, up 2% YoY. While this growth figure appears to be on the low side, the YoY increase in monthly remittances has been relatively (maybe even suspiciously) consistent at between 2-4% every single month over a number of years.

 

Meanwhile, collections by the tax bureau (BIR) were up 14% in May as tax payments spilled over into that month following the extension of the filing of tax returns.

 

+++

 

The AUMs of BSP-regulated Trusts surged 29% YoY to P10.2trn (c$170mn) in 1Q26. However, roughly 2/3 of the growth came from “Other Fiduciary Services”, which we believe to be banks starting to offer custody services. To be fair, this is relevant for banks’ fee incomes. But for investments, if we exclude these, the Trust and Agency businesses were still up a decent 9.8% YoY to a very respectable P7.5trn, or about $125bn. The frustration remains – despite the continued growth in overall AUMs, the domestic equity markets don’t seem to be getting much love.

 

+++

 

The merger between the toll road operations of San Miguel (SMC PM) and Metro Pacific (MPI) is about to be finalized, if some media reports are to be believed. SMC is expected to emerge as the majority shareholder in the combined operations.

  • President Marcos issued Proclamation No. 1318 for the Congress of the Philippines to hold a special session on June 17 (Wednesday) in order to address urgent measures and pending appointments
  • This follows delays in the passage of priority legislative measures and appointments in the military and foreign service due to the ongoing Senate leadership dispute
    • Priority measures include numerous social welfare bills (e.g., Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations Act, Government Assistance to Students and Teachers in Private Education Act, Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act, among others) as well as the Anti-Political Dynasty Act
  • It has reportedly been said that the special session may also pave the way for Senator Sherwin Gatchalian's election as Senate President with a handful of members of the minority being persuaded to join the new majority bloc
    • Recall that Senator Gatchalian was recently elected as Senate President pro tempore, effectively serving as Acting Senate President following the support of 12 senators which formed the new majority
    • Note that the chamber needs at least 13 votes to elect a new Senate chief

BANKS
Trust AUMs surge again in 1Q26, driven again by... others?

The Philippines’s “Trust and Other Fiduciary Business” spiked again in 1Q26, up 29% YoY and 11% QoQ to P10,179bn (just under $170bn). However, as with 4Q25, the driver has been the “others” account within the banking sector. In 4Q, Metrobank (MBT PM) and Landbank (gov’t owned, not listed) saw their numbers double YoY. MBT said during their briefing that it was because they had started to offer custody services. It is unclear if that was the same reason for Landbank. In 1Q26, MBT’s numbers didn’t change that much. It could be that the big jump again came from Landbank.

 

Excluding the “other fiduciary services” line, Trust and Agency AUMs would still be up a respectable 9% YoY for banks, and 13% YoY for non-banks. The data for the individual “non-banks” are not out yet.

 

By bank, BDO is still by far the largest, with consolidated AUM of P2,611bn (about $43bn), up 12% YoY and 1% QoQ. However, BPI is fast catching up, at P1,947bn, growing at a much faster clip of +20% YoY and 6% QoQ.
