REGIS REPORT

14 July 2026

The government is considering as much as P69bn (US$1.1bn) worth of tax incentives for the domestic manufacture (assembly) of vehicles. Up to P60bn will be for electrified vehicles (including hybrids) under the proposed Electric Vehicle Incentive Strategy (EVIS) program. The remaining P9bn will be for traditional ICE vehicles under the proposed Revitalizing the Automotive Industry for Competitiveness Enhancement (RACE) program.

 

+++

 

Foreign ownership of Philippine listed stocks inched up 0.54% to 20.9% in June. The increase was driven by the relative outperformance of stocks that are heavily foreign owned (e.g. ICTSI (ICT PM)) and despite net foreign selling for the month.

 

+++

 

Megaworld (MEG PM) sold another 38m shares of MREIT (MREIT PM). This takes the Alliance Global (AGI PM) group’s stake down 0.8pt to 54.5%. The sell-down creates more room for another share-for-asset injection as, by regulation, the AGI group cannot own more than 2/3rds of MREIT.

 

The yield of MREIT at the selling price was 7.7%.

MACRO
Government eyeing P69bn worth of incentives for domestic car manufacturers
  • Trade Undersecretary Ceferino Rodolfo said the two proposed incentive programs could provide a combined P69bn in tax incentives for qualified vehicle manufacturers
  • The proposed Electric Vehicle Incentive Strategy (EVIS) would allocate up to P60bn in incentives to as many as four qualified EV manufacturers
    • The Board of Investments (BOI) is currently drafting the implementing rules for EVIS before opening a three-month application period
    • According to BOI executive director Ma. Corazon Dichosa, two local vehicle manufacturers are also in talks with the agency and are planning to partner with foreign manufacturers to qualify for the program
  • Meanwhile, the proposed Revitalizing the Automotive Industry for Competitiveness Enhancement (RACE) program would provide P9bn in incentives to three internal combustion engine (ICE) manufacturers over three years
    • Toyota Motor Philippines Corp. (TMP) previously indicated its intention to apply the Tamaraw under RACE

    • Note that TMP is still eligible for ~P3bn worth of tax credits but has not received government approval to use this incentive

MARKET
Foreign ownership of Philippine market rises in June
  • We estimate that foreign ownership of the overall market rose 54bps MoM to 20.9% (in June) while foreign ownership of the PSE Index increased 60bps MoM to 24.5%
  • Net foreign selling fell to US$22mn, from US$150mn in May
    • The increase in foreign ownership appears to have been thanks to the outperformance of high foreign-owned stocks, such as ICT and GLO
  • Among the PSEi constituents, 11 saw increases in foreign ownership while 18 experienced declines
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were:
    • ALI – Foreign ownership declined 1.17 percentage points MoM to 20.9%, as net foreign selling hit US$31.7mn, the highest for the month.
    • MER – foreign ownership fell 52bps MoM to 4.9%. It also saw the third highest net foreign selling for the month (at US$21.4mn).   
    • PLUS – foreign ownership climbed 30bps MoM to 30.3%, as foreign buying reached US$3.4mn. 
    • ICT – foreign ownership increased 20bps MoM to 37.5%, as foreign buying hit US$53.9mn, the highest for the month. 

 

 

PROPERTY
Megaworld sells MREIT shares
  • Megaworld sold 38mn MREIT shares at P13.70/share
    • Total proceeds of P516mn
    • This represents a 1.4% discount to the previous closing price
    • At that price, MREIT offers an FY26E dividend yield of 7.7% (based on consensus forecast)
  • MREIT previously announced that it would acquire 300k sqm of mall, office, and hotel assets from related parties
    • The acquisition will increase its portfolio by 47% to 950,000sqm
  • MREIT will issue up to 1.8bn new MREIT shares
    • This would expand shares outstanding by up to 38%
  • MREIT said that the acquisition will be DPS accretive
  • MREIT currently offers 7.6% dividend yield (based on consensus forecast)
    • The 10-year government bond yield is 7.3%
  • MEG currently offers 4.3% dividend yield and is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY26E P/B, and 3.3X FY26E P/E
