25 June 2026

In light of higher borrowing costs and uncertainties surrounding the conflict in the Middle East, the BSP expects bank lending to grow at a more measured pace for the rest of the year. This could mean that loan growth for the bank we cover will be slower than our projections of 8%-13%.

 

+++

 

After recently completing the 2,500MW first phase of its MTerra Solar project, SP New Energy Corp (SPNEC PM) aims to complete the remaining 1,000MW by 1Q27. At full completion, we estimate that the MTerra Solar project could generate c. P15bn in annual profits, 60% (P9bn) of which would be attributable to SPNEC. The project could also contribute P5bn in annual profits to Meralco (MER PM), which has a 57% economic stake in SPNEC.

 

+++

 

Lance Gokongwei will acquire a stake in online gaming solutions provider PhilWeb Corporation (WEB PM). He will subscribe to 159.5mn new common shares and 93.8mn convertible preferred shares at P8/share, giving him an initial 10% stake in the company (15% assuming full conversion of the preferred shares). Although he is purchasing shares at 45% below the last closing price, a P2bn personal investment by one of the country’s top business magnates is arguably a strong vote of confidence on the company.

 

+++

 

Shakey's (PIZZA PM) and Emperador (EMI PM) both held their annual stockholders' meetings yesterday and each gave their respective insights on sales trends and consumer sentiment as of late.

 

The common theme was that value-for-money products such as Potato Corner (for PIZZA) and CLVB Emperador brandy (for EMI) continue to provide an uplift to sales from the price-conscious consumer.

BANKS
BSP expects subdued lending growth this year
  • In an emailed statement to BusinessWorld, The BSP said that “Credit growth is projected to remain positive but at a more measured pace, reflecting tighter financial conditions and more selective lending”
  • This is due to higher borrowing costs and uncertainties surrounding the war in the Middle East
  • It also cited that banks have begun to tighten credit standards for sectors exposed to higher fuel costs, logistics pressures, and foreign exchange volatility
  • We forecast loan growth of 8%-13% for the banks that we cover
    • Banking sector loan growth hit 12% in April 
POWER
MGEN eyes 1Q27 start for second phase of MTerra Solar project
  • Meralco’s power generation unit, MGen, is eyeing to complete the second phase (1,000 MW) of the 3,500MW MTerra Solar project by 1Q27, following the completion of the first phase of 2,500MW
    • Securing sufficient land remains a key requirement to achieve this target
    • According to MGen president and CEO Emmanuel Rubio
  • Under its contract with Meralco, MTerra Solar has committed to provide 650MW of mid-merit capacity by Feb 2026 and the entire 850MW mid-merit capacity by Feb 2027
  • At full build, we estimate the project’s standalone annual profits could reach P15bn
    • Meralco effectively owns 34% of Terra Solar, through its 57% economic ownership of SPNEC PM (SPNEC owns 60% of Terra Solar)
GAMING
Lance Gokongwei acquires stake in PhilWeb
  • Lance Gokongwei will subscribe to 159.5mn common shares and 93.8mn redeemable preferred shares of PhilWeb Corporation (WEB PM) at P8/share
    • total subscription price of P2.03bn
    • Mr. Gokongwei’s subscription price is 45% lower than the last closing price (of P14.48/share)
  • Mr. Lance Gokongwei is President and CEO of JG Summit Holdings (JGS PM)
  • The shares will be created pursuant to the proposed increase in the company’s authorized capital stock from P2.6bn to P3.6bn, which is subject to shareholder approval
  • The 159.5mn common shares would represent 10% of the company’s common shares outstanding (upon issuance)
  • Assuming full conversion of all the 93.8mn preferred shares, Mr. Gokongwei would own 15% of the company’s expanded common shares
  • PhilWeb is a B2B online gaming solutions provider which has agreements with Travellers, Okada, Hann Resorts, and NUSTAR (of the Gokongwei group)
CONSUMER
Shakey's to continue store network rationalization
  • Shakey's Pizza Asia Ventures Inc. (PIZZA PM) chairman Christopher Po in its annual stockholders' meeting guided that the company will continue to rationalize its store network this year
    • 15-20 stores (mostly Peri-Peri restaurants) have been shuttered YTD with a few more set to close towards year-end
    • As of 1Q26, PIZZA had 3,039 stores globally and domestically
      • 2,151 Potato Corner kiosks, 301 Shakey's, 82 Peri-Peri, 15 domestic emerging brands, and 490 international stores
  • The company has also guided that it will reduce costs while implementing "measured" price increases in an effort to improve margins
    • Price increases were calibrated to be lower than inflation in the low single-digits to mitigate impact on demand
  • Po added that the kiosk format (Potato Corner) continues to deliver strong growth, partially offsetting the weakness from the full-service brands (Shakey's, Peri-Peri)
    • Management was optimistic that there may be a gradual improvement in consumer spending in the second half of the year if geopolitical tensions do not re-escalate
  • In 1Q26, PIZZA's revenue and SWS rose 13% and 14%, respectively YoY on flat (+0%) SSSG with core net income declining 17% YoY to P152mn
CONSUMER
Emperador sees sustained growth momentum for 2026
  • Emperador Inc (EMI PM) expects to sustain growth in 2026 as management cites a resurgence in the brandy business and improvements in the whisky segment
  • EMI President and CEO Glenn Manlapaz during the company's annual stockholders' meeting stated that the company's business segments have exhibited positive developments as of late
    • Brandy is gaining traction amid the shift towards value-oriented products (i.e., CLVB Emperador)
    • Whisky saw improved demand in international markets with "the market slowly going back to premium products"
  • In 1Q26, EMI revenue rose 1% YoY to P13.4bn while net profit rose 4% YoY to P1.9bn
    • The brandy and whisky segments saw 1% and 2% revenue growth, respectively
