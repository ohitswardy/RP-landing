REGIS REPORT

12 February 2026

Gaming agency PAGCOR says it tightened KYC protocols as well as advertising rules for online gaming firms beginning in 4Q.

 

Among other things, PAGCOR now requires a valid government ID and a real-time selfie before an initial deposit to online gambling accounts can be made. We believe the added step/s will lead to a further dip in online gambling, at least for legal, licensed operators including Digiplus (PLUS PM).

 

Online gambling has grown sharply over the last two years and has had an arguably meaningful impact on other forms of consumption. Curbs on it, if successful, should help these other forms of consumption. It would, however, also reduce government revenue in the form of license fees to PAGCOR.

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) issued a draft circular that would tighten restrictions on rural banks that engage in online banking. This appears aimed at some entities that have used a loophole allowing the more lightly regulated rural banks to engage in online banking services.

 

+++

 

Globe (GLO PM) raised P25bn from a preferred share sale that offered relatively high yields (>6%), considering the coupons are not tax deductible. We expect this is a partial refinancing exercise for the perp that it is callable in Aug later this year.

GAMING
PAGCOR tightens regulations on online gambling
  • In a Senate hearing, PAGCOR Chairman Alejandro Tengco said that the regulator has tightened know-your-customer (KYC) protocols and advertising regulations on online gambling
  • PAGCOR Electronic Gaming Licensing Department officer in charge Jessa Mariz Fernandez said the updated KYC process requires a valid government ID and a real-time selfie before the initial deposit can be made
  • TV and radio ads during primetime are no longer allowed
    • PAGCOR might expand the ban to the entire day
  • Other implemented measures include online self-exclusion tools on gaming platforms, accreditation of treatment and rehabilitation centers for problem gamblers, stricter KYC enforcement, and the removal of gambling-related billboards and other outdoor advertisements
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) has been implementing these tighter restrictions since 3Q25 so the full-quarter impact should be felt in 4Q25
  • PLUS is trading at 4.8X FY26E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
BANKS/DIGITAL BANKS
BSP tightens regulations on rural banks with online platforms
  • The BSP issued a draft circular requiring rural banks using online platforms to maintain certain thresholds
  • Accounts of customers whose registered addresses are outside the bank’s physical areas of operations are to be capped at 30 percent of total customer accounts
  • The BSP “reserves the right to deploy appropriate enforcement actions” against rural banks whose out-of-area customer accounts exceed 30 percent
    • The bank may be required to comply with regulations applicable to a digital bank, including meeting a minimum capital of P1 billion within one year and maintaining a supervisory assessment framework rating of “3” or higher
  • The BSP is accepting feedback on the draft circular until February 25
TELCOS
Globe raises P25bn from pref share sale
  • Globe issued P25bn worth of non-convertible, non-voting perpetual preferred shares
    • Series “A” prefs’ dividend rate at 6.1179%
    • Series “B” prefs’ dividend rate at 6.7631%
  • Note that the company has US$600mn senior perpetual capital securities (4.2% distribution rate) that is callable on August 2, 2026
MACRO
BSP sees ongoing but slower-than-expected business confidence rebound
  • Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona noted that the economy is on track to recover by 2H26 given that business confidence has started to show signs of a rebound
    • He noted, however, that the recovery in sentiment is "not as fast we would like, but it's coming back"
    • Remolona cited the following as indicators that sentiment may be improving:
      • The Philippines’ manufacturing PMI rising to a nine-month high of 52.9 in Jan
      • The easing of 10-year government bond yields to below 6%
      • The PSEi’s recovery from sub-6,000 levels in Nov 2025
  • Remolona also noted that the potential revision to their 2026 GDP growth forecast of 5.4% is still under review
  • The BSP's GDP growth forecasts for 2026/27 are 5.4/6.0%
    • For 2026/27, consensus and REGIS expectations for GDP growth are at 5.2/6.0% and 5.5/5.7%, respectively
  • The first out of six Monetary Board meetings for the year will be held on Feb 19
    • For 2026, consensus and Regis expect one 25bp cut to 4.25%
