REGIS REPORT

07 September 2026

The Dept of Agriculture (DA) says the government’s subsidized rice program will be funded through the end of the term of President Marcos. The funding for the program, which buys rice from local producers and sells these at P20 (US$0.32) per kg, is P23bn (~US$400m) for this year. Bringing rice prices down to P20 per kg was a key campaign promise of Marcos when he was running for the Presidency, so we had expected this program to continue.

 

Following the constriction of infrastructure spending over the last year due to the corruption scandal/s, the administration has chosen to increase spend more aggressively on subsidies, partly due to price increases sparked by the US war on Iran.

 

+++

 

Speaking of rice: the inflation data showed rice price inflation rising to 19.4%, though inflation for the broader food basket, remarkably, showed a deceleration to 4.6%. Given (1) the low CPI base over 4Q25, (2) lower domestic rice output and (3) rising regional export prices even as the PHP weakens, we expect YoY rice inflation to climb higher (to >20%) in succeeding months. This should, we expect, drive CPI inflation up which could add pressure for the Bangko Sentral (BSP) to raise policy rates.

 

More detail below.

 

+++

 

Vice President Sara Duterte posted bail after a local court issued an arrest warrant against her. The warrant arose from charges filed due to “grave threats” she issued at an online press conference against the lives of the President, his wife, and the former Speaker of the House.

 

Those same “grave threats” are also among the issues being litigated at Sara Duterte’s ongoing impeachment trial. The impeachment trial is a political process that could remove her from office and bar her from holding future political office. The charges in the local court, on the other hand, are part of a judicial process to determine criminal culpability.

 

The impeachment trial clearly remains the more important of the two processes as it could have a direct impact on the 2028 elections.

MACRO
Sufficient funding in place to sustain P20/kg subsidized rice program—DA
  • Dept. of Agriculture (DA) Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel stated that President Marcos' P20/kg subsidized rice program has sufficient funding until the end of his term (June 2028)
    • The subsidized rice program is targeted for vulnerable consumers and is expected to reach as many as 60mn Filipinos till June 2028
    • The pilot program was launched in May 2025 in select areas with a P5bn budget from the President’s contingency fund
    • For 2026, the P20/kg rice program has been allocated P23bn, a much higher allocation than 2025’s for a broader rollout of the initiative
  • The agriculture secretary, however, noted that President Marcos is aware that the P20/kg market price for rice, which was one of his key campaign promises in 2022, could no longer be attained
    • Urea prices have risen to almost P2,600/sack (+62% YoY) and is 3.7x higher than the P700/sack price levels when Marcos made the promise

 

 

 

MACRO
Inflation inches down to 6.1% in August
  • Headline inflation moderated slightly to 6.1% in Aug from Jul's 6.2%
    • This was in line with consensus expectations of 6.1% and within the BSP's forecast range of 5.5-6.5% for Aug
    • The softer inflation print is the fourth consecutive month of deceleration following five consecutive months of accelerating inflation
  • This brings 8M26 inflation to 5.2%, above the upper limit of the BSP's long-term target of 3% (± 1ppt) but still below their forecast of 6.1% for the year
    • The FY26 inflation forecast implies that inflation could average 7.9% for the rest of the year
  • Inflation in Aug decelerated slightly as the moderation in the YoY increase in food prices was offset by the reacceleration of fuel prices
    • Food prices overall rose 4.6% YoY,  moderating relative to the previous month's increase of +5.2%, %, as higher rice prices were offset by the slower YoY increase of fish and other items
      • Rice prices climbed 19.4% YoY in Aug, picking up from the 17.0% YoY increase in Jul
      • The YoY increases in rice prices were propelled by the low base as rice prices in Aug 2025 fell 17% YoY
    • Transport prices rose 13.5% YoY, faster than +11.9% the previous month
      • The increase in diesel prices reaccelerated to +55.7% YoY from +38.5% YoY, while the gasoline price picked up slightly to +36.2% YoY from +34.1% YoY in Jul
  • Core inflation, which excludes volatile food and energy items, eased a tad to 4.1% in Aug from 4.2% the prior month, as the moderation in core non-food (2.6% from 3.6%) offset the slight acceleration in core food (4.5% from 4.4%)
  • The central bank inflation estimates for 26/27E are 6.1%/5.4%
  • For 2026, consensus and REGIS expectations for 26/27E inflation are 5.5%/3.9% and 6.7%/4.5%, respectively
    • Policy rate expectations for both consensus and REGIS are for one more 25bp hike to 5.25% by year-end
  • The last two of the six Monetary Board meetings scheduled this year will be held on Oct 22 and Dec 17, respectively
