18 June 2026

Century Pacific (CNPF PM) provided the public with an update on its operations following the 7.8 magnitude earthquake off the southern coast of Mindanao. The quake affected some of the facilities of CNPF near the area and the statement said that those facilities are covered by insurance and have resumed partial operations.

 

We don’t expect the calamity to have a meaningful impact on the prospects of the company beyond a short-term hiccup.

Senator Sherwin Gatchalian was officially elected as Senate President yesterday after a thirteenth senator joined his bloc, allowing it to form a constitutional majority for the purpose of electing a Senate President. Gatchalian was previously just “acting” Senate President with another bloc, allied with Vice President Sara Duterte, opposing his election.

 

Control of the Senate will be crucial in the upcoming impeachment trial of the Vice President. The conduct and outcome of that process will have significant ramifications on the national elections in 2028.

CONSUMER
CNPF provides update on tuna and coconut facilities post-earthquake
  • Century Pacific Food in a disclosure provided an update on its tuna and coconut manufacturing facilities as operations have partially resumed a week after the 7.8 magnitude earthquake hit General Santos City
  • CNPF assured that the facilities and inventory are covered by insurance, it also affirmed that it "maintains sufficient inventory… to continue serving customer requirements"
  • The company expects operations to be gradually restored over the next one to two weeks after further safety assessments and repairs are conducted
  • As of FY25, the Branded Tuna and OEM (tuna and coconut) segments contributed to 31% and 20% of group-wide revenues, respectively
POLITICS
Senator Gatchalian formally elected as Senate President
  • Senator Sherwin Gatchalian was elected as Senate President as the majority bloc was able to garner 13 votes following the addition of Senator Joel Villanueva to the majority bloc
    • Gatchalian replaced Senator Alan Peter Cayetano who was removed as leader on June 3
  • Senator Tito Sotto III was elected as Senate pro tempore while Senator Migz Zubiri took oath as the Senate majority leader
    • Senator Cayetano, despite being absent, formally assumed the role as the minority leader following Senator Loren Legarda's move to nominate him
  • Senator Cayetano alongside his allies in the minority bloc, previously petitioned the Supreme Court to nullify the election of the Gatchalian leadership, arguing that the 12 votes that elected Gatchalian as acting Senate President was illegitimate
    • This petition is now moot given the subsequent vote by 13 senators
  • The Senate majority bloc also reportedly agreed to elect Senator Chiz Escudero as the presiding officer of the Senate impeachment court once it convenes on July 6
    • Escudero was said to be chosen as he is a lawyer and has experience with the impeachment process

PROPERTY
Ayala Corp purchases ALI shares
  • Ayala Corp purchased 35mn ALI shares at an average price of P13.07/share
    • Total value of P457mn
  • This is the first time since 2022 that AC has bought shares of ALI
  • We estimate that Ayala Corp’s ownership in ALI rose from 53.2% to 53.5%  
  • AC is trading at 64% discount to NAV and 4.6X FY26E P/E
  • ALI is trading at 75% discount to NAV, 0.6X FY26E P/B, and 6.3X FY26E P/E
GAMING
PAGCOR orders out-of-home gambling advertisements to be replaced with helpline information
  • PAGCOR ordered all gaming firms to replace existing gambling advertisements with materials promoting the newly launched 24/7 National Problem Gambling Helpline
  • The directive covers billboards, digital displays, and other out-of-home (OOH) advertising platforms currently carrying responsible gaming messages
  • The replacements must be completed on or before July 15 and should remain in place until September 15, 2026
  • We do not expect significant medium-term impact due to the temporary nature of the directive as well as the forms of advertising covered (online gambling advertisements are unaffected)
  • PLUS is trading at 4.2X FY26E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
