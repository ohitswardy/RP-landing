REGIS REPORT

24 August 2026

After months of speculation, Dominion Holdings (DHI PM) finally announced it was acquiring 100% of Sagittarius Mines, which holds the contract (FTAA) to develop the huge Tampakan Copper-Gold project in Mindanao. DHI also disclosed it was increasing its authorized capital to P30bn (which includes 26.75bn common shares, the maximum shares it can have in issue).

 

DHI’s share price has been climbing the past months in anticipation of such an injection/acquisition. However, it’s uncertain (undetermined) how much DHI will pay (in number of shares) to acquire Sagittarius Mines. Hence, we can’t say at this time whether DHI shares are already too expensive. What we do know, however, is that DHI’s book equity was P3.47bn (most of which is in cash) and book value per share was P1.6 as of June 2026. These are roughly 1/10th of DHI’s market cap (P33.7bn) and share price (P15.6).

 

DHI also already previously disclosed plans to acquire 54% of Atlas Consolidated (AT PM) in two separate deals, but did not say how it would be paying for the shares (or how much).

 

+++

 

 

Senator Francis Escudero filed Senate Bill No. 2347 (SB 2347) or the proposed Gambling Advertising Prohibition Act. While PAGCOR prohibited gambling advertisements on print media, SB 2347 would ban practically all forms of gambling advertisements.

 

According to gaming consultant Adren Consult, over 60% of online gaming operators are generating less than P30mn in GGR per month (or less than P360mn/year). Should SB 2347 become law, it would become even more difficult for the smaller gaming operators to gain meaningful market share from the market leaders.

 

+++

 

The country’s international reserves dropped US$1.4bn to US$103.3bn in July, likely reflecting the Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) efforts to defend the PHP.

MINING
Dominion Holdings set to take over Tampakan copper-gold project
  • Dominion Holdings (DHI PM) announced that it is merging with Indophil Resources Phils and Sonar Hldgs. with DHI as the surviving entity
    • Indophil and Sonar together hold 100% voting rights in Sagittarius Mines, Inc., the owner of the FTAA contract for the Tampakan Copper-Gold project
    • DHI’s Board has authorized management to determine, fix, and finalize the exchange ratio for the merger
  • DHI is also seeking SEC approval to increase its authorized capital to P30bn (26.75bn common shares @ P1/sh and 2.5mn preferred shares @ P100) from P3.4bn (3.4bn common and 200k prefs)
  • It is also amending its articles to deny pre-emptive rights of shareholders
  • Tampakan Copper-Gold greenfield project has an estimated 2.9bn MT of resources, containing an estimated 15mn MT of copper and 17.6mn ounces of gold
    • Sagittarius Mines has planned a phased development for Tampakan, targeting an initial annual mill production rate of 15mn MT (see here), which is similar to Atlas Mining (AT PM)
    • Assuming a higher copper grade of 1% (vs. Atlas at 0.20%) and a gold grade of ~3 grams/tonne, we estimate Tampakan could produce ~124k MT of copper metal-equivalent and 12k oz of gold annually
    • At today’s LME copper price of US$14,050/MT and gold price of US$4,500/oz, this could translate to annual revenues of ~US$1.8bn
    • The project is expected to start commercial operations in two to three years, or as early as 2028
GAMING
Senator files Bill to ban gambling advertisements
  • Senator Francis Escudero filed Senate Bill No. 2347 (SB 2347) or the proposed Gambling Advertising Prohibition Act
    • Senator Escudero compared the Bill to the Tobacco Regulation Act of 2003
  • SB 2347 would prohibit gambling advertisements across television, radio, newspapers, magazines, billboards, websites, online platforms and social media
    • Gambling advertisements are currently prohibited on print media but are allowed on television and social media
  • It would also ban celebrity and influencer endorsements, product placements, promotional giveaways, branded merchandise appealing to people under 21 and gambling sponsorships involving sports, concerts and cultural events
  • The proposal provides a one-year transition period after the law takes effect, allowing operators, advertisers, broadcasters and platforms to wind down existing agreements. No new advertising or sponsorship agreement could be entered into during that period except where necessary to conclude existing obligations
GAMING
Over 60% of online gaming operators not meeting PAGCOR benchmarks
  • According to Arden Consult, over 60% of Philippine online gaming operators are underperforming relative to benchmarks set by PAGCOR
  • A Gaming System Administrator (GSA) offering electronic casino games must pay 30% of GGR or P9mn/month, whichever is higher
    • This implies a minimum GGR benchmark of P30mn/month
  • A GSA that does not offer electronic casino games must pay 20% of GGR or PP3mn/month
    • This implies a minimum GGR benchmark of P15mn/month
  • The minimum guaranteed fees will increase in beginning January 2027:
  • A GSA that offers electronic casino games will pay 30% of GGR or P10.5mn/month, whichever is higher
    • This implies a minimum GGR benchmark of P35mn/month
  • A GSA that does not offer electronic casino games will pay 20% of GGR or P4mn/month, whichever is higher
    • This implies a minimum GGR benchmark of P20mn/month
MACRO
BOP posts US$1.5bn deficit in July; GIR settles at US$103.3bn
  • The country's overall balance of payments (BOP) for the month of July posted a deficit of US$1.5bn
    • A reversal from the US$3.4bn surplus in the previous month of June but wider than the US$167mn deficit in July 2025
      • Recall that the gov't raised US$2.5bn through a US dollar bond issuance in June
    • This brings the 7M26 deficit to US$5.4bn, narrower than the US$5.8bn deficit in the same period last year
    • For 2026, the BSP projects a BOP deficit of US$10.7bn (2.1% of GDP), much wider than the US$5.7bn deficit (1.2% of GDP) in 2025
  • Meanwhile, the revised GIR level for end-July stood at US$103.3bn, -1.4% MoM
    • The BSP attributed the decline to the central bank's net foreign exchange operations and the national gov't's drawdowns on foreign currency deposits for external debt payments
      • The peso weakened to P61.4/USD at end-July from P61.2/USD at end-June
    • The GIR level is reflective of an external liquidity buffer equivalent to 6.7 months’ worth of imports of goods and payments of services and primary income
    • For 2026, the GIR is expected to settle at US$104.0bn, down from US$110.8bn in 2025
