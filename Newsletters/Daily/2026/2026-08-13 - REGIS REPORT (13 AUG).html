REGIS REPORT

13 August 2026

Here’s the results tally for today:

 

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (+) – Profits for 1H26 came in at P46bn, +8% higher YoY.  With profits at its two biggest units, BDO (BDO PM) and SM Prime (SMPH PM) practically unchanged, growth was delivered mainly by its smaller “Portfolio Investments”, chiefly Atlas Mining (AT PM), which management indicated SM will be divesting. SM Retail, meanwhile, produced P8.9bn in profit for 1H, +5% YoY on revenue growth of 6%. Growth in 2Q was just slightly slower than 1Q. More below.

 

  • Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) (+) – Core income of P5.5bn for 1H is +16% higher YoY but this is off a weak 1H25 base. Revenue grew +7% but this was lifted partly by FX translation of revenues at Quorn. For 2Q alone, both APAC BFB and Quorn saw 3% revenue growth, but the weaker PHP magnified the growth of the latter. Overall, the 2ppt advance in gross margins were able to lift profits YoY. We remain cautious, however, given tepid growth and continued margin pressures.

  • Megaworld (MEG PM) (=) - 2Q26 net profit inched up 1% YoY to P5.7bn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. However, 2Q26 EBIT grew at a faster pace of 7% YoY, thanks to stronger-than-expected residential GPM and rental revenue growth. Furthermore, 2Q26 reservation sales rose 12% QoQ and 20% YoY to P33.3bn, equivalent to c. 90% of the 2019 average.

 

+++

 

Private equity firm KKR is offering to buy part of First Gen (FGEN PM) from its principal shareholder First Philippine Holdings (FPH PM) and also from the public. This was confirmed by First Gen. The supposed (but unconfirmed) bid price is P35/share.

 

While we are doubtful that an agreement will be struck at that price, we believe the current situation of the Lopez group is very fluid and thus unstable. Many parties are indirectly and directly invested in the group, interested in taking a stake or in partnership with some part/s.  Among them: Enrique Razon, Ramon Ang, KKR, Macquarie + GIC, and Barito (BREN IJ).

 

A major shakeup is, in our view, brewing.

 

+++

 

The Dept of Energy (DOE) is deferring the bidding for the Semirara coal mining concession to re-write contract terms. An increase in the government’s share is likely. Semirara Mining (SCC PM) is facing the major uncertainty of whether it can keep operating the mine on the island.

 

+++

 

Ayala Land (ALI PM) is set to be downgraded to the MSCI Small Cap Index, which should prompt near-term selling of this name.

CONGLOS
SM Investments 1H profit up 8% to P46bn
  • Income grows 8%. SM reported net income of P45.9bn for 1H26, up 8% YoY, likely mildly ahead of Street expectations.  Banking delivered 47% of profits for 1H, while property and retail brought in 27% and 15%. Its two biggest subsidiaries, BDO and SMPH, saw earnings largely flat YoY but “Portfolio Investments” saw a significant jump.

 

  • SM Retail revenues up 5% in 1H. Overall retail revenues grew 6% for 1H, while profit grew at a similar pace (+5%).  For 2Q alone, top line was up +5% but income was flat (-0.6%)

 

  • Slight dip in SSSG. SSSG for the overall retail segment was +2.9% in 1H, compared  to 3.4% in 1Q.  This implies 2Q alone was close to around +2.4%.

 

  • Contributions from Portfolio investments up,  The share of income derived from SM’s “Portfolio Investments” grew from 7% to 11%. This was driven largely by the improvement in Atlas (AT PM), up 9x, and, to a lesser extent, 2GO.

SM Investments to divest Atlas stake in 2027
  • SM Investments President Frederic DyBuncio said that the company would sell its 34% stake in Atlas Mining (AT PM) to another listed company in 2027
  • Mr. DyBuncio is also a Director of Dominion Holdings (DHI PM), which will purchase a 20% stake in Atlas from Anglo Philippines (APO PM)
PROPERTY
Megaworld 2Q26 reservation sales rise 12% QoQ and 20% YoY
  • Megaworld’s 2Q26 net profit inched up 1% YoY to P5.7bn
    • 1H26 net profit rose 2% to P10.9bn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts
  • 2Q26 EBIT grew at a faster pace of 7% YoY, thanks to stronger-than-expected residential GPM (+4.1ppt YoY to 54.3%) and rental revenue growth (of 6%)
    • 1H26 EBIT increased 6% on the back of 3.7ppt expansion in residential GPM (to 53.9%) and 6% rental revenue growth
  • Mall revenue rose 7% YoY on the back of a 2ppt YoY expansion in mall occupancy (to 95%)
  • Office revenue increased 2% QoQ (+6% YoY), possibly signaling a sequential improvement in occupancy
  • 2Q26 residential reservation sales rose 12% QoQ and 20% YoY to P33.3bn, equivalent to c. 90% of the 2019 average
    • As a result, 1H26 reservation sales increased 15% to P63bn
    • The company said that projects in Ilocos and Palawan sold well
  • MEG currently offers 3.9% dividend yield and is trading at 65% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY26E P/B, and 3.6X FY26E P/E

 

 

POWER
KKR offers to acquire FPH’s 8.4% stake in FGEN, paving way for delisting
  • KKR offered to acquire an additional 8.43% stake in First Gen (FGEN PM) from First Philippine Holdings (FPH PM)
    • Offer is preliminary, non-binding and remains subject to due diligence, regulatory approvals, etc
    • Media reports indicated a price of P35/sh for FGEN (vs. closing price of P19.60/sh on Aug 10)
  • In the proposal, KKR will launch a voluntary tender offer for FGEN’s entire public float of 11.67%, which will pave the way for a de-listing
  • Given recent market valuations for the natgas plants and its eventual buy-in valuation for Prime Hydro, we estimate the P35/sh offer price is at a 26% discount to FGEN’s NAV of P47/sh
    • FGEN’s NAV of P47/sh values EDC at US$2bn (or 20x trailing PER and 12.5x trailing EV/EBITDA)
  • Recall that KKR, through Valorous Asia Hldgs, acquired an initial 11.9% economic stake (8.4% voting stake) in FGEN when it undertook a tender offer at P22.50/sh in 2020
    • KKR undertook another tender offer for FGEN in September 2021 at an offer price of P33/sh
    • KKR today holds a 19.9% economic interest and 14.1% voting stake in FGEN
    • First Philippine Holdings, meanwhile, owns a 67.84% economic stake in FGEN

 

 

POWER
Government defers Semirara coal auction to 4Q to rewrite contract terms
  • Energy Secretary Sharon Garin told reporters that the bidding for the Semirara coal block will be delayed by at least two (2) months from the original September target
    • The government is currently revising the terms of reference, specifically on the royalty and domestic delivery mandates
  • Under the current coal operating contract, the operator would have to pay 30% of revenues less allowable expenses (i.e. pre-tax income), subject to an effective minimum of 3% of annual revenue
    • Meanwhile, metal miners pay a 4% excise tax, on top of royalties (5% of revenues or up to 5% of pre-tax income) and windfall profit taxes
PROPERTY
Ayala Land to be downgraded to MSCI small cap index
  • MSCI announced that Ayala Land would be removed from the MSCI Standard Index but would be added to the MSCI Small Cap Index
    • Effective at the close of August 31 
  • No other Philippine companies will be removed from the MSCI Standard Index
  • ALI currently offers 3.2% dividend yield and is trading at 71% discount to NAV and 0.7X FY26E P/B
MACRO
Government reduces 2027 overall infra program allocation
  • The gov't infrastructure program (which includes other infra projects ex-DPWH [Dept. of Public Works and Highways] and DoTr [Dept. of Transportation]) for 2027 has been lowered to P1.34trn (+5% YoY, 4.0% of GDP) from P1.56trn (+22% YoY, 4.6% of GDP), previously
    • The Dept. of Budget and Management (DBM) released details on the gov't's overall infra allocation in the National Gov't Fiscal Program for 2025-2029 which was published alongside the 2027 National Expenditure Program
  • Despite the significant reduction in the gov't infra program, 2027 allocations for the DPWH and DoTr were increased to P644bn (+21% YoY, 1.9% of GDP) and P303bn (+113% YoY, 0.9% of GDP), respectively
