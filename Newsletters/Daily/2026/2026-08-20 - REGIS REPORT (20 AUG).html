REGIS REPORT

20 August 2026

Total AUMs of BSP-regulated entities grew 31% YoY in 2Q26 to P10.4trn (roughly $168bn). Of this, about P7.7trn ($125bn) was in Trust and Agency accounts, up 11% YoY. The balance of “others” was low-fee custody businesses, which surged 172%. BDO remained the leader by far, with AUM of P2.65trn, or 26% market share, though BPI is fast catching up at P2.05trn. Frustratingly, despite the size and growth of AUMs, the local stock market isn’t getting much love. Anecdotally, majority of the inflows go either to fixed income, or to global equities/ETFs.

 

+ + +

 

The BSP reported that retail digital transactions grew sharply in 2025, up 69% YoY, and now account for 65% of all retail transactions. The biggest driver of growth was payments made by individuals (P2X) jumped to 74.9% of total, up from 72.2% previously. While we have some questions on the methodology (ie what is the denominator?) the direction of travel is consistent with the InstaPay and PesoNet data, which was up 42% YoY by value in 2025, and up 217% by transaction count. The continued digitalization of the economy is great news for improved financial inclusion, and offers massive opportunity for e-commerce companies, fintechs, wallets and online lenders.

The Dept of Finance says it is open to reducing some taxes (e.g. VAT) as long as other taxes can offset the revenue losses. Among the proposed new taxes is a “billionaires” tax on income above a threshold. This proposal(s) could be politically popular, but we have doubts about how workable it could be. Conceptually, this proposal(s) would be contrary to the recent thrust of switching the tax burden from income to consumption.

The Bureau of Customs (BOC) says it is confident of meeting its full-year revenue collection target of P1.01trn (+8% YoY, tweaked slightly since the start of the year). The weakness of the PHP (-5% YTD) is probably a factor in the BOC’s confidence

BANKS
Trust Assets jump 31% to P10.4trn, but...

Assets under management (AUMs) of the Trust and Fiduciary Business segments of banks and non-banks regulated by the BSP jumped 31% YoY and 2.4% QoQ to P10,420bn ($168.4bn). However, this was driven largely by the “Other fiduciary services”, which include low-revenue custody, etc, which exploded +172% YoY to P2,702bn. The Trust and Agency businesses – more relevant to capital markets watchers – still grew a respectable 11% YoY and 2.5% QoQ to P7,718bn (about $125bn).

 

  • BDO remained the clear leader, with P2,653bn in AUM, up 12% YoY, with a market share of 25.5%. Its growth was driven by its BDO PB subsidiary, up 19% YoY.
  • BPI kept its #2 spot, but growing at a faster 22% clip, with AUM breaching P2trn for the first time at P2,047bn.
  • Metrobank AUMs surged 41% YoY to P1,075bn, but this was largely to their custody business, which they took on board at end-2025. The custody business brings in volume, but is not a massive fee generator, as seen in the drop in MBT’s average trust fee from 21 bps in 2024 to just 14bps in 2025.
  • Other notable up-moves include SECB, up 15% YoY, and East West, up 15% YoY. At the other end was Chinabank, whose AUMs dipped 0.3% YoY

 

We note, though, that the data for the non-banks – such as ATRAM and the insurance companies – and for the government banks have not yet been posted.

 

While there are no hard numbers, we note that the growth in Trust+Agency AUMs has largely been driven by demand for “global” assets, including white-labeled ETFs (US, tech, AI, etc). Anecdotally, we understand that the domestic equity portfolios are not seeing significant inflows, except maybe into the higher dividend paying stocks.

 

 

 

 

BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
Digital payments comprise 65% of 2025 retail payments
  • Digital payments rose to 64.7% of retail payments in 2025, from 57.4% in 2024
    • The BSP’s target is for digital payments to be 60%-70% of retail payments
  • There was a 69.4% growth in the number of digital payment accounts
  • There was also a 36.3% increase in business outlets that accept digital payments
  • QR Ph transactions also exceeded debit and credit card transactions for the first time in 2025
    • 2.47 billion QR Ph transactions worth PHP1.16 trillion were processed in 2025
MACRO
Customs on track to meet P1tn revenue goal; expects port congestion order approval by August
  • The Bureau of Customs (BoC) is confident that it can meet its P1tn (+8%) revenue goal for 2026 and its P1.1tn (+6%) revenue target for 2027
    • The ongoing agency modernization program as well as the expected rise in import volumes are expected to drive the growth in revenue
  • Customs Commissioner Ariel Nepomuceno is hopeful that a joint administrative order targeted to ease port congestion and regulate cargo-handling charges will be signed this month before the expected year-end surge in imports
    • Nepomuceno stated that the order can help raise gov't revenues on improved movement of goods and better logistics efficiencies
    • Finance Undersecretary Rolando Ligon said that the agency is looking to have the order signed as soon as possible prior to the "ber" months
  • The "Guidelines on Yard Utilization and Transparency on Charges in the Handling of Cargoes by Customs Third-Parties and Other Logistics Providers" seeks to give the BoC broader authority to oversee customs third parties, formulate policies, expedite accreditation of container yards and terminals, streamline empty container returns, among others
    • The proposed order eyes to address high logistics costs, non-transparent ancillary charges, and recurring port congestion

 

 

MACRO
Dept of Finance open to reduction of some taxes, imposition of wealth tax
  • Dept. of Finance (DoF) Secretary Frederick Go stated that the gov't is open to proposals to reduce or repeal some taxes, such as the VAT, granted that alternative sources will offset the resulting revenue losses
  • The gov't expects VAT and related sales tax collections to amount to P762bn in 2026, 12% higher YoY from P680bn (+6%) in 2025
    • Recall that the BIR missed the 2025 VAT target (P707bn, +10%) due to reduced consumer spending and downtrading to lower-priced alternatives
    • The DoF is hopeful for a 2H recovery in the overall economy and in infrastructure spending
  • A consulting group stated that the country's VAT collection efficiency needs to be improved as it is only at 35-40%, much lower than the 57% average in ASEAN
  • Secretary Go stated that the agency is studying a proposal to impose a billionaire's tax equivalent to 1% for every P1bn in taxable income
    • This proposal is separate from the PROGRESS bill which includes   higher tax rates on sin products with annual increases as well as a 75% (vs. 50% previously) automobile excise tax for vehicles worth more than P8mn
