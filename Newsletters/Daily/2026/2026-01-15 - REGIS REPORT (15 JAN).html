REGIS REPORT

15 January 2026

Following the lead of ICTSI (ICT PM), Asian Terminals (ATI PM) is applying, this early, to renew its contract to operate Manila South Harbor (MSH) beyond 2038. Until recently, there appeared to have been a legal impediment against a renewal.  Changes in regulations have since allowed the possibility of a contract renewal for both ICTSI’s Manila International Container Terminal (MICT) as well as ATI’s MSH.

 

The Philippine sovereign wealth fund Maharlika Investment Corp is launching a tender offer to buy up to 5% of ATI at P36/share. The price values ATI at 7x EBITDA and 13x PER on a trailing basis. The action, if successful, would lead to a delisting of ATI.

 

+++

 

Foreign ownership of companies in the benchmark PSE Index (PSEi aka “PCOMP”) declined 17bp for the month of Dec to 23.4%. This drop reflects the net foreign selling that persisted throughout all of 2025.

 

The foreign ownership of the entire market as a whole, however, appears to have increased over the month. We caution against drawing inferences from this, though, as this “increase” is only because of the sharp drop in the price of a single stock (HVN PM) with zero foreign ownership.

 

+++

 

Megaworld (MEG PM) is expanding its office property portfolio in a few key provincial areas, diversifying beyond Metro Manila.

TRANSPORT
ATI seeks contract renewal for Manila South Harbor beyond 2038
  • Asian Terminals Inc. (ATI PM) has applied to renew its contract with the Philippine Ports Authority (PPA) to operate Manila South Harbor (MSH) beyond 2038
  • This follows its competitor, ICTSI (ICT PM), who also applied to renew its contract to operate the Manila International Container Terminal (MICT) (see here)
  • Recall that the Maharlika Investment Corp. is launching a tender offer to buy up to 101mn (5%) of ATI’s shares at P36/sh, a move that will delist ATI from the PSE
    • Maharlika Investment Corp said that it expects to hold approx. 11.2% of ATI’s outstanding capital stock (after completion of tender offer, subject to level of tenders received)
    • ATI’s public float is currently at 15.7%
    • We estimate the offer price values ATI at just 7x trailing EV/EBITDA, 13x PER, and ~US$520 EV/TEU, a discount to ICTSI (12x trailing EV/EBTDA, 22x PER, and US$1,200 EV/TEU)
PROPERTY
Megaworld to expand office portfolio in key provincial areas
  • Megaworld formed a new office group, called Megaworld Global Offices, which will collaborate with the existing office leasing team, to accelerate growth of the company’s portfolio in key provinces: Ilocos Region, Pampanga, Cavite, Bacolod, and Cagayan de Oro
  • These provinces currently comprise less than 5% of Megaworld’s office portfolio
  • The company also seeks to attract more Fortune 500 companies
  • Megaworld Global Offices will be led by Francisco Roxas, a former Ayala Land executive
  • MEG currently offers 4.1% dividend yield and is trading at 66% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY26E P/B, and 3.4X FY26E P/E
MARKET
Foreign ownership of PSE Index declines in December
  • We estimate that foreign ownership of the PSE Index fell 17bps MoM to 23.4% (in December) while foreign ownership of the overall market rose 57 bps MoM to 19.5%
    • The increase in foreign ownership of the overall market was largely due to the 45% MoM share price decline of Villar Land/Golden MV Holdings (HVN PM), which has zero foreign ownership (and a market cap of P644bn as of end-December 2025)
  • Foreign activity reversed to net selling of US$202mn in December, from net foreign buying of US$78mn in November
  • Among the PSEi constituents, 5 saw increases in foreign ownership while 25 experienced declines
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were:
    • URC – Foreign ownership fell 1.26 percentage points MoM to 19.0%, as net foreign selling hit US$8.2mn, the tenth highest for the month. Furthermore, we reckon foreign selling in late November was only recorded in the December foreign ownership figure.   
    • AGI – foreign ownership dropped 1.09 percentage points MoM to 15.8%, as net foreign selling reached US$17.6mn, the fifth highest for the month.   
    • ALI – foreign ownership decreased 65bps MoM to 23.1%, as net foreign selling hit US$20.7mn, the fourth highest for the month. 
    • BDO – foreign ownership declined 59bps MoM to 27.2%, as it saw the highest net foreign selling for the month (at US$40.2mn).
    • AREIT – foreign ownership climbed 48bps MoM to 5.0%
    • GTCAP – foreign ownership increased 22bps MoM to 23.1%, as net foreign buying hit US$4.5mn, the second highest for the month
    • ICT – foreign ownership expanded 17bps MoM to 35.9%, as it saw the highest net foreign buying for the month (US$15.5mn)

 

MARKET
Congressman files bill to increase rights of minority shareholders
  • House Banks and Financial Intermediaries Committee Vice Chairman Raymond Adrian Salceda of Albay filed House Bill 7120, the Shareholder Bill of Rights, which will increase protections for minority shareholders of both listed and non-listed corporations
  • For listed companies:
    • Material related party transactions will require board approval with conflicted directors abstaining
    • Independent fairness opinions would be required for transactions exceeding 10 percent of total assets
    • Transactions exceeding 25 percent of total assets or resulting in a change of control would require approval of the majority of minority shareholders
