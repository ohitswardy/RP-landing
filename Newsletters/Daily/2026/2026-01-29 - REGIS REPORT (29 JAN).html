REGIS REPORT

29 January 2026

The BPO sector expressed cautious optimism about the industry’s prospects for FY26. The head of the industry association, Jack Madrid, said the group was expecting growth of 4-5% in both employment and revenue. he final numbers for FY25 are still not out but expectations are for growth rates similar to the FY26 projections.

 

Madrid also raised the two main factors affecting the growth of the industry in the country (ease of doing business and supply of labor), which conspicuously omitted the one factor most are worried about: Artificial Intelligence (AI).

 

+++

 

The CEO of BPI (BPI PM), TG Limcaoco, says he expects the bank to show stronger growth in 2026, with high single-digit growth in income and mid-teen growth in loans. Regis Partners is projecting 8% growth in profits and 12% increase in loans for BPI for 2026.

 

+++

 

With Finance Secretary Go saying that the government is sticking to its budget deficit targets despite lower revenue projections, this only means expenditures will have to be reduced (by around 2% to 5%). There is, however, one lever that the government still has available --- interest rates, so that primary spending (ex- debt service) may be affected less.

 

The GDP data out today and the CPI numbers out next week are, in our view, likely to support a BSP decision to reduce policy rates again on Feb 19.

 

+++

 

Although Hann Group’s planned P12bn IPO did not push through in 2025, it is still pursuing its expansion plans. In addition to world-class golf courses, it is targeting to complete its second integrated resort in Clark by 2030. Post-expansion, we estimate that Hann’s gaming capacity will grow by over 40% to c. 380 gaming tables and 3,000 slot machines, making it larger than City of Dreams Manila or Okada Manila.

PROPERTY
IT-BPO Association “cautiously optimistic” about growth in 2026
  • Jonathan Madrid, President of the IT-BPO Association of the Philippines, said that the association was “cautiously optimistic” about growth in 2026
    • He said that two factors influencing the growth were: ease of doing business and supply of employable talent  
  • The IT-BPO Association of the Philippines (IBPAP) expects the industry to achieve revenue of US$42bn and employ 1.97mn Filipinos by the end of 2026
    • These represent 4%-5% growth from the 2025 revenue (US$40bn) and employee (1.9mn) figures
  • Mr. Madrid previously said that the industry’s growth will still be driven by core segments – financial services and healthcare
  • He also said that he expects Global Capability Centers (GCC) to grow faster
    • These are offshore units established by multinational corporations to provide specialized services such as finance, IT and customer support to their global operations
    • IBPAP previously reported that American and Australian companies had plans to expand in the Philippines 
  • BPO firms account for 70%-90% of the occupied office space of the developers we cover
  • MEG currently offers 4.2% dividend yield and is trading at 66% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY26E P/B, and 3.4X FY26E P/E
BANKS
BPI eyes stronger growth in 2026
  • BPI President and CEO Jose Limcaoco stated that expectations for 2026 are for stronger growth than in 2025 with high single-digit growth in net income and mid-teens growth in its loans
    • He elaborated that there is "a bit of hesitancy on the borrowing side" given global tensions and local noise but he expects that sentiment for both the corporates and consumer can recover this year
  • As of 9M25, BPI's NPAT rose 5.2% YoY to P50.5bn with loan growth of 13.3% YoY
    • For 2025, REGIS expects net profit to grow 10% YoY to P68.2bn and forecasts 13% loan growth
    • For 2026, REGIS expects net profit to grow 8% YoY to P73.7bn and forecasts 12% loan growth
MACRO
PH gov't revises revenue targets for 2026 onwards
  • The Development Budget Coordination Committee has reduced the gov't's revenue targets for 2026 onwards
    • Recall that Finance Secretary Frederick Go recently stated that it will retain its fiscal deficit targets, which implies lower gov't spending moving forward
  • The revised gov't revenue forecasts are as follows:
    • 2026: P4.82trn from P4.98trn
    • 2027: P5.12trn from P5.37trn
    • 2028: P5.57trn from P5.91trn
  • Note that the macro assumptions are still subject to change as the results of the DBCC meeting have yet to be fully disclosed

 

 

GAMING
Hann to complete second integrated resort by 2030
  • Hann Group plans to complete its second integrated resort in Clark by 2030
  • The company is currently developing the 450-hectare Hann Reserve, which is about 15 minutes away from the original Hann Casino Resort
  • Hann Reserve phase 2 will include championship golf courses, public parks, retail space, and an international school
  • Hann Casino Resort currently has 275 gaming tables and 1,800 slot machines/ETG
    • Solaire Entertainment City has 350 gaming tables and 2,700 slot machines/ETG
MACRO
Imported rice tariff to stay at 15% until March
  • The Dept. of Agriculture (DA) stated that the imported rice tariff will remain at 15% until the end of 1Q26 but will increase by 5ppt to 20% if the average price of 5% broken Vietnam rice falls between US$350/MT and US$367/MT
    • The average price of the benchmark variety was US$387/MT as of Dec 2025
  • Recall that the rice import tariff was reduced from 35% to 15% back in July 2024 under EO 62, which is subject to review every four months
    • The tariff will be reviewed in April with March serving as the reference price point
