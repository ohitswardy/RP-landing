REGIS REPORT

27 January 2026

The results of the twice-yearly review of the PSEi (“PCOMP”) are out and, as predicted, RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM), subsidiary of Robinsons Land (RLC PM), will be added to the main index. RCR will replace Alliance Global (AGI PM) at the start of the business day Feb 2 (Monday) so we can expect much of the trading activity on the previous trading day (Jan 30). We estimate inflows of over US$30m into RCR from passive funds.

 

Meanwhile RLC disclosed that it sold 946m shares of RCR, equivalent to 4.8% of RCR’s capital, overnight. The placement seems to have been timed to take advantage of the announcement that RCR will be added to the PSEi. The RCR shares were sold at P7.40/share, thus raising P7.0bn for RLC. At the placement price of P7.40/sh, RCR would be on 14.5x PER and dividend yield of 6.7%, based on consensus forecasts.

 

Post-placement, RLC’s stake in RCR would now drop to 55.9% from 60.7%.

 

More detail below.

 

+++

 

BDO (BDO PM) raised P100bn in another mammoth bond issue for the PHP bond market. The ASEAN Sustainability bonds will have a tenor of three years and will carry a coupon of 5.7125%.  

 

Both BDO and BPI (BPI PM) have been raising funding through bonds in recent months, possibly a reflection of tight LDRs and efforts to raise alternative (non-deposits) funding. BDO’s bond issues, in particular, have been quite large by Philippine standards.

 

+++

 

Maynilad (MYNLD PM) reported an average non-revenue water (NRW, aka water losses) of 34.9% for FY25, down from 40% the prior year.  While the figure does not meet the stated goal of 34.0%, the shortfall is within the allowable limits and Maynilad will not incur any financial penalties. Maynilad is committed to lower NRW further to 25% by 2027.

 

+++

 

Ayala Corp (AC PM) subsidiary AC Logistics is acquiring an 84% interest in a cold chain logistics firm as part of its push into that industry. AC has been trying to turn around the logistics business with new management and the entry of a new partner, AP Moller.

 

 

+++

 

President Marcos signed the National Digital Connectivity Plan (NDCP) which is supposed to be the government’s blueprint to improve digital connectivity for the country. Among other things, the plan aims to reduce internet access costs by 40-80%. We believe the cost of fixed broadband in the Philippines is still high, but mobile internet costs are already comparable with, if not cheaper than, most places globally.

CONGLOS
AC Logistics acquires majority stake in a cold chain business
  • AC Logistics signed an Investment Agreement for the acquisition of up to an ~84% stake in Glacier Megafridge, Inc. (GMI)
    • GMI is one of the leading cold storage operators in the country by capacity
    • It currently operates 12 cold storage facilities with 74k pallet position
  • Transaction is subject to the satisfaction of certain closing conditions by GMI as well as regulatory approval
  • Acquisition is in line with its target to shift into a comprehensive logistics solutions provider with cold chain logistics among its key verticals
  • AC Logistics is one of Ayala Corp’s (AC PM) underperforming business units
    • AC is in the process of divesting a 40% stake in AC Logistics to AP Moller
    • AC trades at a 31% discount to its market-based NAV of P760/sh and at 7x FY26E PER
TELCOS
Marcos approves national broadband connectivity plan
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. during an Economy and Development (ED) Council meeting has approved the National Digital Connectivity Plan (NDCP), an initiative expected to fast-track the rollout of critical broadband infrastructure and lower internet costs
    • A draft Executive Order (EO) for the NDCP was said to be released in the future to strengthen the implementation of the plan
  • The Dept. of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) elaborated in a press release that the plan was stated to be the gov't's strategic roadmap for every Filipino to have access to fast, reliable, and affordable internet by 2028
    • The gov't targets to accomplish the creation of 8mn digital jobs, a 40-80% reduction in internet costs, and higher average broadband speeds of 225 Mbps for mobile and 275 Mbps for fixed broadband
  • The DICT also listed down priority initiatives such as:
    • Completion of the National Fiber Backbone
    • Expansion of submarine cable landing sites
    • Acceleration of next-generation mobile technologies
    • Improving connectivity for isolated areas
    • Cybersecurity and climate-resilient infra measures
PROPERTY
Majority of Ayala Land offices have green certifications
  • Ayala Land said that it has 1.5mn sqm of office GFA that have received the EDGE Zero Carbon Certification
    • We estimate this represents c. 75% of Ayala Land’s office portfolio 
  • This means Ayala Land has the largest portfolio of EDGE Zero Carbon Certified buildings globally
  • EDGE Zero Carbon is a global green building certification developed by the International Finance Corp. (IFC)
    • To qualify, buildings must first achieve EDGE Advanced certification, demonstrating at least 40 percent reduction in energy plus at least 20 percent savings in water use and embodied carbon in materials
  • Most international firms consider green certifications in their decisions on office location
  • ALI is trading at 60% discount to NAV, 0.9X FY26E P/B, and 10.0X FY26E P/E
