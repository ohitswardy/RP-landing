REGIS REPORT

23 April 2026

We cut our forecasts for BPI (BPI PM) by 6-8%, and reduce our target price by 6.3% to P150, as we bake in increased stress in the bank’s consumer/MSME lending book. The new NPL formation in 1Q26 was surprisingly led by corporates, which the bank expects to resolve either by foreclosure or restructuring by 2Q. However, the loan portfolio is showing early signs of increased delinquencies (30 days). BPI has started adjusting its ECL parameters, resulting in 87bps in credit costs in 1Q, compared to c80bps guidance at the start of the year. At the briefing yesterday, management opined that 90-100bps for the FY would not be unreasonable, given the impact of the Iran conflict on inflation and GDP growth. The bank remains committed to the customer growth trajectory, both on the loan and deposit sides, but is tightening loan standards and collections near-term. Despite the forecast and TP cuts, we maintain our Buy call on valuations, with the stock now trading at 1.0x P/bk on our 2026E, and paying a 5.4% dividend yield.

In a very limited press release, Chinabank (CBC PM) reported headline 1Q26 NPAT of P6.8bn, up 4% YoY slightly lagging consensus of P30.6bn for FY26E. Loan growth accelerated to 16% YoY (13% in 4Q), and deposits +13% YoY (from 8.5% in 4Q). While NII was up 14% YoY, we estimate that non-interest income was a loss of about P1.6bn, likely on trading/fx losses as part of its rate swaps. NPL ratio was flat at 1.6% as was NPL cover at 110%. The stock currently trades at about 0.9x P/bk on 1Q26 numbers.

+++

Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) has agreed to acquire a 20% stake in an operating copper mine in Kazakhstan..  Although valuation details were not provided, the deal supports the company’s diversification efforts. 

+++

The power regulator (ERC) has asked Meralco (MER PM) to shorten the period over which it was paying refunds to 12 months (from 36 months). This would increase the monthly refund and thus hopefully trim electricity bills short-term.  The ERC has been actively trying to mitigate the impact of soaring energy costs by directives such as this.

+++

The Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) Monetary Board is set to meet today, and consensus is leaning slightly for a rate hike.  BSP Governor Remolona previously raised the possibility of an increase in policy rates due to the direction of local inflation.

BANKS
Chinabank 1Q26 net profit rises 4%
  • Chinabank’s 1Q26 net profit rose 4% to P6.8bn, below consensus forecast
  • Net interest income grew 14% to P19.5bn, supported by higher revenue and lower interest expense
    • Net interest margin improved 12bps to 4.61%
  • Opex increased 5% to P8.8bn
  • Gross loans climbed 16% to P1.1trn
    • The bank saw strong demand across both corporate and consumer segments
  • Deposits increased 13% to P1.5trn
    • CASA grew 20%, bringing the CASA ratio to 48%, from 46% a year ago
  • The bank said that asset quality remained steady, with NPL ratio at 1.6% and NPL coverage at 110%
POWER/UTILITIES
Energy regulator shortens Meralco’s remaining P14bn refund period to 12 months
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission ordered Meralco to shorten the period of the remaining P14.2bn unrefunded amount to 12 months from 36 months previously
    • As a result, the average refund rate will increase to P0.25/kwh starting in May from P0.12/kwh previously
    • This is to mitigate the increase in electricity rates due to higher spot prices
  • Note that the refund will not affect Meralco’s P&L
MINING
Nickel Asia acquires 20% stake in Kazakhstan copper mine
  • Nickel Asia entered into an agreement with Silk Road Resource Ltd. to acquire the latter’s 20% “membership” stake in East Copper
    • East Copper owns 100% of GRK MLD LLP (GRK), which holds subsoil use rights for the Karchiga copper mine located in Kazakhstan
    • This transaction is subject to the completion of the due diligence on East Copper and GRK and other closing conditions, including the necessary regulatory approvals
    • No other details disclosed
    • As of end-2025, NIKL had a net cash position of P1.97bn
  • GRK’s annual production capacity is 8.5kt of copper sulfide concentrate at an average grade of 1.8% and 2kt of copper cathode at an average grade of 1.0%
    • For perspective, the Philippines produced 232kt of refined copper in 2025 while Kazakhstan produced 471kt (see here)
  • Acquisition is in line with the company’s strategy “to expand market capitalization and earnings beyond nickel into a diversified natural resources platform”
  • NIKL trades at 9x FY26E PER and 4x EV/EBITDA (vs. Indo nickel miners: 11x PER / 7x EBITDA)
