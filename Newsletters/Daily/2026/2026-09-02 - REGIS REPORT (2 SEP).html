Jollibee (JFC PM) provided an update to its plan to spin off and list its international business (“JFC International”/ “JFCI”). Among the information provided was the decision to list the international business in Hongkong, instead of the previously announced venue (the U.S.). JFC also disclosed that the current group CFO, Richard Shin, will be CEO of the spun-off JFCI.

 

Listing in HK (as opposed to the U.S.) would likely give JFCI a better chance of avoiding the fate of becoming an “orphaned” stock. The Asian region is where the bulk of JFCI’s business is still derived and the name is still more recognized in this part of the world. Beyond just location, JFCI will still need a large enough and profitable enough business to avoid becoming overlooked and ignored in a ‘foreign’ marketplace.

The former controlling shareholder of SPNEC (SPNEC PM) sold another 4.8% of the company’s stock to an undisclosed buyer. Rep Leandro Leviste would still hold ~11% of SPNEC after the sale. Meralco (MER PM) currently holds 57% of SPNEC.

Revenue of land-based casinos inched up QoQ and YoY, thanks to an increase of both VIP and mass GGR. On the other hand, revenue of the e-games segment was flat QoQ and down 37% YoY. Interestingly, the market share of Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) expanded while the market share of DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) contracted.

CONSUMER
Jollibee shifts plan to list international operations to Hong Kong stock market
  • Jollibee Foods Corporation (JFC PM), in a disclosure, provided updates on its previously announced spinoff of the international business (Jollibee Foods Corporation International [JFCI])
  • The company has decided to pursue the proposed listing of its int'l business in the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (HKEX) instead of the initially planned U.S. listing
    • Management highlighted that Hong Kong is the market that is "best positioned to recognize the value of its distinctive business profile and growth potential"
  • Richard Shin will serve as the CEO of JFCI
    • He will continue to serve his current role as the CFO of JFC until the completion of the proposed separation, at which point he will be CEO of JFCI on a full-time basis
  • The company said it has made "substantial progress" since announcing the planned spinoff in Jan, with restructuring underway to prepare JFCI for its proposed listing and transition into a standalone public company
    • This includes strengthening JFCI’s financial, organizational, and governance capabilities to support sustainable growth
  • Jollibee Foods Corporation, which will include all Philippine operations will remain listed in the Philippines
  • Upon implementation, JFC shareholders will receive share in JFCI corresponding to their prevailing interest in JFC
  • The proposed plan has yet to be finalized and is still subject to change with further details to be provided as the transaction progresses
    • The disclosure did not mention the implementation date but the previously stated target execution date was late 2027
  • As of 1H26, JFC's net profit to common amounted to P4.7bn (-14% YoY), with the domestic and international business contributing to 118% (P5.5bn) and -18% (-P0.8bn) of profit, respectively
  • JFC is trading at 16.1x 26E PE

POWER/UTILITIES
San Miguel, Aboitiz submit lowest bids for Meralco’s 600MW CSP
  • Meralco is set to evaluate the three lowest bids for its 600MW baseload supply requirement
    • San Miguel’s (SMC PM) Mariveles Power Gen Corp. offered a total levelized cost of P5.36/kwh (incl. VAT) for 200MW
    • SMC’s Sual Power Inc offered P5.44/kwh (incl. VAT) for another 200MW
    • Aboitiz Power’s GN Power Mariveles Energy Center submitted a bid of P5.58/kwh (incl. VAT) for 600MW but later reduced its capacity to 200MW
  • First Gen’s (FGEN PM) Energy Development Corp. also submitted an offer, but its price was higher than those offered by SMC and AP’s plants
  • After the evaluation, the 15-year power supply agreement will be submitted to the ERC for review and approval before implementation
    • An initial 300MW of baseload supply is targeted for deliver by Feb 26, 2028 while the remaining 300MW is due to be supplied by Feb 26, 2029
