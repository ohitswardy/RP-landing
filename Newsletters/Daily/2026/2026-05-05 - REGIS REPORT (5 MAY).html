REGIS REPORT

05 May 2026

The government statistics agency delivered a jolt this morning, announcing that CPI inflation for April was 7.2%, well above the 4.1% in March and also consensus guestimates.

This print is likely to spur change/s in the thinking of the Bangko Sentral (BSP), leading to higher inflation expectations there and, ultimately, tighter monetary policy near-term.  Moves in domestic fixed income markets are also likely to reflect such quickly.

+++

We have a few heavyweights reporting results yesterday.

  • ICTSI (ICT PM) (+) – ICTSI delivered another robust performance, with core profits rising 29% YoY to US$305mn in 1Q26, trending ahead of Regis and Street estimates.  The improvement was driven by (a) sustained strong yields of US$230 and (b) a modest but accretive contribution from recent acquisitions in Batam and Durban.  These gains helped offset a sharp deceleration in organic volume growth, which slowed to 1.4% in 1Q26 from the over 6% pace in 4Q25, driven by the poor performance of its port in Iraq, as well as ports in Europe.  Notably, management’s indication that yields could remain at current levels stood out as a potential upside surprise, underpinned by tariff hikes and fuel surcharges implemented in select ports, particularly those in the Americas, to offset the impact of higher oil prices.  Read more below.

  • Meralco (MER PM) (-) – Core profit for Meralco rose 2% to P11.4bn for 1Q26, which appears short of Street expectations. Notably, volumes for its distribution utility business were down 2%, leading to a 21% earnings contraction there.  The generation side was able to offset this with a 51% jump in contributions.  The key issue for Meralco, however, is the tariff review. Management expressed its optimism (?) that the reset will be done by June (for implementation by July 1) but we can’t say we share in this optimism. More below.
  • Semirara (SCC PM) (+) – At yesterday’s briefing, management highlighted a sharp improvement in plant availability, rising to 96% in 2Q26 so far from 68% in 1Q, signaling a reversal from the 10% YoY decline in 1Q26 net income of P3.9bn. This, as well as firmer benchmark thermal coal prices, whose impact was only partially reflected in 1Q and is expected to be more evident in 2Q, could support sequential earnings growth. While the postponement of the coal contract bidding originally scheduled for April 28 was a setback, management remains optimistic that the government will move forward with the process by the latter part of June.

  • Megaworld (MEG PM) (=) – 1Q26 net profit rose 4% YoY and QoQ to P5.3bn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. Unlike the other developers that have reported results so far, Megaworld’s residential profits did not decline. Residential revenue was flat YoY (+15% QoQ) while residential GPM expanded 3.2ppt YoY to 53.4%. Meanwhile, office revenue grew 4% YoY on expansion of floor area while mall revenue increased 9% YoY (flat QoQ), thanks in part to a 2ppt YoY improvement in occupancy (to 95%).

POWER/UTILITIES
Meralco 1Q core profits rise 2%
  • Core income up 2% to P11.4bn.  Meralco reported headline income of P10.8bn, +4% YoY, with core income of P11.4bn (+2%).  We believe the results are trending behind Street estimates. While the generation business was quite strong, with core profit rising 51%, the distribution utility (DU) business saw segment core profit fall 21%.

 

  • DU electricity sales down 2%. Electricity sales for the DU decline 1.8% in 1Q26, which management blamed on cooler weather and various energy conservation schemes promoted by the government (shorter mall hours, WFH arrangements, etc.).  This is a sharp contrast to the growth reported by other DUs.

 

All customer classes (residential, commercial, industrial) saw YoY drops in demand, with residential users seeing the biggest dip (-3.4%). This drop, coupled with higher opex and lower interest income, led to core income dropping 21% to P5.3bn (=46% of group core income).

 

  • Generation core income up 51%. The generation business saw a 51% leap in core income to P5.1bn, as Meralco saw a full quarter contribution from the LNGPH, the umbrella holding for its gas-fired assets.  Its landmark solar project MTerra Solar is, management claims, on track to begin delivering 600MW of power under its contract with its own parent Meralco by Aug. While this is six months delayed, MTerra Solar will not suffer penalties for that period of delay.

 

  • Demand weakness continues into early 2Q.  Indications show demand contracting more in April, with usage falling as much as 4%. Management added that demand is picking up over the last few days and are hopeful that the 1Q decline can become be reversed.

 

  • Tariff reset decision due next month …. Meralco has presented all its witness for its 4-year tariff reset application (under “1RP” or a new First Regulatory Period) and says it expects a decision from the regulator (ËRC) by June. Delays however should surprise no one.  The last full tariff reset process was completed in 2010.

 

PROPERTY
Megaworld 1Q26 net profit rises 4%, in line with REGIS and consensus
  • Megaworld’s 1Q26 net profit rose 4% YoY and QoQ to P5.3bn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts
  • Operating profit rose 4% YoY to P29.8bn, slightly ahead of REGIS forecast
    • The outperformance seems to have come from higher residential GPM, which expanded 3.2ppt YoY to 53.4%
  • Residential revenue was flat YoY (+15% QoQ) at P13.3bn
  • Office revenue grew 4% YoY (to P3.8bn), about the same pace as the expansion of floor area
    • However, during the MREIT briefing, Megaworld said that on a net basis, it saw mild negative rental reversions in 1Q26. Although most areas saw flat to slightly higher rental rate renewals, Eastwood City dragged the average rental reversions to negative territory
  • Mall revenue increased 9% YoY (flat QoQ) to P1.8bn, partly thanks to a 2ppt YoY improvement in occupancy (to 95%)
  • The company will hold its briefing on May 7
  • MEG is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY26E P/B, and 3.3X FY26E P/E

DigiPlus reportedly offers to purchase Ramon Ang’s Diamond Hotel
  • According to Insiderph.com, DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) made an offer to acquire Ramon Ang’s Diamond Hotel

  • PLUS is trading at 5.5X FY26E P/E (based on consensus forecast)

 

MARKET
PSEi May 2026 rebalancing: EMI, PLUS, RCR to see upweights
  • The PSE announced a rebalancing of the PSE Index at the open of Monday, May 11 (close of Friday, May 8)
  • There will be an increase to the public float levels of EMI, PLUS, and RCR, which should lead to corresponding increases to their weighting
  • Other PSE Index constituents should experience outflows 
  • A rebalancing of the PSE Index occurs in May when public float of a PSEi constituent moves by at least 5ppt from end-December to end-March
    • The public float of PLUS, increased 6ppt QoQ in 1Q26, while the public float figures of RCR and EMI rose 5ppt each
