REGIS REPORT

21 May 2026

Port operator ICTSI (ICT PM) secured a US$300m loan from the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) to finance the capacity expansion of its ports in the Philippines, including its flagship in Manila as well as ports in Southern Luzon and Mindanao.

 

+++

 

The Dept of Trade and Industry (DTI) is reportedly reviewing adjustments for the suggested retail prices of a number of goods, including canned sardines and instant noodles. It may be recalled that the DTI called for a freeze in prices for some goods after the outbreak of the current hostilities in the Gulf until May 10.

 

We understand that many manufacturers have already raised prices earlier this month. The higher prices should be passed on by retailers (e.g. supermarkets, etc) very soon. We expect its impact on CPI to show up by next month.

 

+++

 

The Dept of Transportation says it will extend its fuel subsidy program for public utility vehicles (PUV) until the end of this year. It is currently extending a P10/liter subsidy to PUVs accessible through accredited service stations. For perspective, the current price of diesel in Manila is around P80+ per liter.

 

Based on previous pronouncements, the budget for this program was P1.5bn (US$25m) for the period until July. This program has allowed the government to restrain transport fare increases in the meantime.

ICTSI secures US$300mn loan from AIIB
  • ICT secured a US$300mn loan from Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) to finance capacity expansion and technology upgrades at three Philippine container terminals
  • The capacity of Manila International Container Terminal (MICT) is slated to expand to 3.7mn TEUs by 2027 (from 3.6mn TEU currently)
    • We are forecasting MICT’s capacity to expand to 3.7mn TEU by 2028
  • Mindanao Container Terminal’s capacity is targeted to hit 1mn TEUs by 2028 (from 300,000 currently)
    • This is higher than our forecast of 600,000 TEU by 2028
  • The capacity of South Luzon Container Terminal is slated to reach 800,000 TEUs by 2028 (from 400,000 TEU currently)
    • This is in line with our forecast
CONSUMER
Dept. of Trade and Industry reviewing potential price increases for canned sardines and instant noodle products
  • The Dept. of Trade and Industry (DTI) Secretary Cristina Roque stated that it is currently reviewing proposed price adjustments for canned sardines and instant noodles before issuing an updated suggested retail price list
    • Roque elaborated that several manufacturers of such goods have petitioned for price increases of less than 10% of prewar prices
    • She added that manufacturers for other basic goods such as canned and processed meat, processed milk and bread have yet to propose plans for price adjustments
  • Recall that the DTI previously secured commitments from manufacturers to defer price increases until May 10
  • Century Pacific Food's (CNPF PM) branded business (marine, meat, milk) posted P17.9bn (+11% YoY) of revenues for 1Q26, which comprises ~78% of CNPF's total revenue
  • Monde Nissin's (MONDE PM) APAC BFB revenues amounted to P19.1bn (+9% YoY) in 1Q26, the segment accounts for ~84% of total revenue
    • Noodles contribute to 41% of APAC BFB's sales or ~34% of MONDE's consolidated revenue
  • Universal Robina's (URC PM) domestic BCF revenues were P22.0bn (+10% YoY) in 1Q26, the business accounts for ~46% of URC’s total revenue
MACRO
Dept. of Transportation extends fuel support for public utility vehicles until December
  • The Dept. of Transportation (DOTr) stated that it will extend its Recalibrated Transport Support Package for public utility vehicle (PUV) drivers and operators till the end of this year
    • The program was initially slated to run for three months on a P1.5bn budget from April to July
    • 151,605 PUV units are expected to benefit from the fuel subsidy program
  • The Dept. of Budget and Management (DBM) said that funding has been secured to sustain UPLIFT (Unified Package for Livelihoods, Industry, Food, and Transport) programs for the rest of the year
  • The gov't has launched a website which includes details on support programs and key statistics on food prices and the country's fuel supply
