REGIS REPORT

16 April 2026

Ahead of the Monetary Board (MB) meeting next week, Bangko Sentral (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona says the BSP has room to raise policy rates given rising inflationary pressures from second and third-order effects of surging energy prices.

 

March inflation jumped to 4.1% and is likely to rise further in succeeding months. This appears to be nudging the BSP to tighten monetary policy. The efficacy of any rate hike on inflation (whether first, second or nth order), however, is questionable.

Casino operator Okada Manila generated P6.6bn of gross gaming revenue (GGR) in 1Q26. The figure may be down YoY (-17%) but is actually 9% up QoQ. There was some improvement in the VIP business, but the mass segment fell both on a YoY and QoQ basis.

Overseas remittances into the country inched up 2.6% to US$2.8bn in February.

MACRO
BSP governor sees room for rate hikes
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona stated that the central bank has room to raise policy rates given mounting pressures on inflation and economic growth due to the Middle East conflict and the lingering effects from the flood control scandal
    • According to Remolona, the BSP will focus on tempering second-order effects of the oil price shock but will avoid any excessive tightening
  • March inflation was 4.1%, higher than BSP's 3.1-3.9% forecast and above the 2-4% target band
    • For 2026, the BSP forecasts inflation to settle at 5.1% (previously revised from 3.6%)
      • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to be 3.0% and 4.0%
      • Inflation has averaged 2.8% as of end-March
  • The second out of six scheduled Monetary Board meetings for the year will be held on Apr 23
    • For 2026, consensus and Regis expect rates to be held steady at 4.25%

 

 

MACRO
BSP gives banks flexibility to extend loan payments
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), in a statement, announced that it has extended regulatory relief measures to BSP-supervised financial institutions (BSFIs) to support consumers and businesses amidst rising costs due to the energy crisis
  • The BSP has permitted banks to give borrowers more time to settle loans
    • Subject to bank assessments, grace periods of up to six months (up to one year for agricultural loans) can be granted according to the BSP
    • The loans may also temporarily not be classified as past due or non-performing by banks for up to one year
  • The central bank also urged banks to temporarily suspend online transaction fees to ease costs for consumers and businesses
GAMING
Okada Manila 1Q26 GGR improves sequentially
  • After declining for four consecutive quarters, the 1Q26 GGR of Okada Manila rose 9% QoQ (down 17% YoY) to P6.5bn, thanks to an improvement in the VIP segment
  • After declining for five consecutive quarters, VIP rolling chip climbed 82% QoQ (+ 9% YoY) to P57.6bn
  • However, volumes of both (live) mass tables and slots fell on a QoQ and YoY basis
  • Mass table volume fell 4% QoQ and 29% YoY to P7.4bn, the lowest level since 4Q23
  • Slot handle declined 7% QoQ and 4% YoY to P46.4bn, the lowest level since 2Q22

 

 

 

GAMING
Philippine gaming industry feeling strain from higher fuel prices
  • PAGCOR Chairman Alejandro Tengco said that elevated fuel prices have begun to weigh on the Philippine gaming industry
  • “At PAGCOR, we will adjust what we need to do. We have to be in tune with the times and ensure that responsible gaming remains at the center of what we do.”
  • PAGCOR previously deferred the implementation of Minimum Guaranteed Fees (MGF) of P9mn/month on e-casino operators
    • The implementation was deferred from April 1 to June 1
MACRO
Remittances rise by 2.6% in February
  • Cash remittances amounted US$2.8bn in Feb, 2.6% higher YoY—decelerating from the 3.5% growth in Jan
    • Remittance growth was primarily driven by Singapore (+3.8%), Saudi Arabia (+5.1%), and the UAE (+5.2%)

 

 

 

MACRO
BIR net collections for March increase by 7%
  • The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) guided that collections net of tax refunds in March amounted to P187bn, up 7% YoY
    • BIR Commissioner Charlito Mendoza noted that the performance is encouraging "especially at this time of economic strain"
  • Following President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.'s order to extend the income tax return filing deadline by one-month from the original deadline of April 15—the BIR may potentially miss its April collection target of P499bn
  • The BIR targets to collect P3.43trn (+10% YoY) for 2026
TRANSPORT
Cebu Air’s pax volumes rise 11% YoY in March; 1Q26 up 8%
  • Cebu Air reported its passenger volumes rose 11% to 2.5mn in March, accelerating from the 8% pace in February
    • Cited the start of summer school breaks and sustained momentum in the international segment
    • This was despite the >20% increase in base fares starting from the second week of March, after the surge in jet fuel prices
  • Brings 1Q26 pax volumes up 8% YoY to 7.5mn, trending below our FY26E forecast of 31mn (+17% YoY)
    • Every 5% cut to our pax volume forecast reduces EBITDA by 18% and profit by 90%, holding other things constant
  • For 2Q26, management said that they are adopting a cautious and deliberate strategy, adjusting flight frequencies as needed and prioritizing routes with higher demand
    • Recall that fuel surcharges, which we estimate account for <10% of CEB’s fares, more than doubled beginning April 1 so the real impact on demand will become apparent then
    • Fuel availability is a larger concern, in our view. CEB previously indicated that its fuel buffer is likely sufficient until end-May
