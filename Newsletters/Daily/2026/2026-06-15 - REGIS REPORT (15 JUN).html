REGIS REPORT

15 June 2026

The Dept of Energy (DOE) aims to hold the pre-bid conference for the auction of the coal blocks on Semirara island within the month and receive bid submissions sixty (60) days later. This means we’ll probably find out by Aug if Semirara Mining (SCC PM) will retain control of the mining concessions beyond July next year.

 

There are likely to be only two bidders for the concession – SCC and San Miguel (SMC PM).

 

+++

 

Telecom operator Dito Telecommunity (Dito Tel), a subsidiary of Dito CME (DITO PM), aims to grow revenue by 20-30% this year from last year’s P20bn. All other things constant, we reckon DITO has to reach revenue of >P30bn just to break even on an EBIT basis and needs to exceed P50bn to be break even on a profit basis.  In the mobile sector, we estimate it has ~8% revenue market share.

 

We estimate that Dito Tel has a capital deficit (i.e. negative shareholder equity) of ~P100bn (US$1.6bn) as of end-2025.  The company is, in our view, holding out as long as it can in hopes of finding a new controlling shareholder.

 

+++

 

Electricity spot prices jumped sharply in May to P7/kwh (+66% YoY) following the lifting the trading suspension and the administered prices in April.

 

+++

 

Our updated estimates suggest that it is now even more likely that Converge ICT (CNVRG PM) will be replaced by Maynilad Water Services (MYNLD PM) in the August 2026 PSEi rebalancing, which will be based on the 12-month period from July 2025 to June 2026. While DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) was previously a candidate for removal from the index, we now consider that highly unlikely.

 

+++

 

Despite net foreign selling of US$150mn in May, we estimate that foreign ownership of the market rose 20bps MoM (to 20.3%) while foreign ownership of the PSEi increased 28bps MoM (to 23.9%). This odd situation occurred because of the outperformance of high foreign-owned stocks such as ICTSI (ICT PM) and Globe (GLO PM) .

POWER
Department of Energy plans to set pre-bid auction for Semirara coal contract within June; bid submission by August
  • The Department of Energy (DOE) said it hopes to schedule the pre-bid conference for the Semirara coal block auction within the month, with the bid submission deadline set 60 days after or by August
    • According to DOE Secretary Sharon Garin
    • Recall that the DOE deferred the bid submission deadline originally set on April 28 to ensure that all issues raised by the bidders are fully considered
    • There are currently only two (2) groups vying for the Semirara coal contract: Semirara (SCC PM) and the group of San Miguel (SMC PM)
  • The DOE plans to have the final consultation on the terms of reference
    • She also noted that the agency intends to offer a 25-year contract term, departing from its earlier proposal to align the contract duration with the reserve life, subject to renewal based on additional reserves
  • While financial strength remains a consideration in the selection process, Secretary Garin emphasized that greater weight will be given to a bidder’s technical expertise and operational capability
    • “They must demonstrate that they know what they are doing. That is what we are still trying to determine”
TELCOS
DITO expects 20-30% revenue growth for 2026
  • Dito Telecommunity Corp. (Dito Tel) CEO Ernesto Alberto stated that he expects the company's revenue to grow between 20-30% to P24-P27bn from P20.5bn in 2025 driven by subscriber acquisition despite softer market conditions due to inflationary pressures
  • Dito Tel targets to grow its subscriber base to 20mn by year-end from the current 17mn, including users of its fixed wireless access (FWA) service
    • Alberto said that the company registers an average of 23,000 to 24,000 new subscribers daily and sees growing traction in its 5G user base
  • Alberto also elaborated that Dito Tel has a population reach of 86.4% as of late on an infrastructure backbone of 7,347 towers
    • He said that the ideal goal is to hit 90% but this would require an additional US$1bn to build more cellular towers
  • In 1Q26, DITO CME Holdings Corp (DITO PM), parent company of Dito Tel, posted a wider net loss of P6.8bn from P1.7bn a year earlier
    • Revenue in the quarter grew 24% YoY to P5.8bn from P4.7bn
POWER
Power spot prices spike in May
  • Wholesale Electricity Spot Market (WESM) prices surged in May, after the ERC lifted the WESM’s suspension on May 1
    • Recall that on March 26, the ERC suspended the WESM to address supply risks and price volatility triggered by the Middle East conflict
  • Spot prices in Luzon jumped to P7.02/kwh in May, up 66% YoY and 25% MoM
  • Companies with net long exposures to the spot market are key beneficiaries
    • ACEN’s PH operations (c. 43% of FY25 core att. EBITDA) have ~33% of capacity exposed to the spot market
    • SCC’s power unit has 51% of capacity exposed to the spot market
    • AP has ~10% of capacity un-contracted

 

 

MARKET
MYNLD likely to be added to PSE Index during August 2026 rebalancing
  • Based on our estimates, Maynilad Water Services (MYNLD PM) will likely be added to the PSE Index during the August 2026 rebalancing
  • If MYNLD will enter the PSE Index, then there are a few candidates for removal from the PSE Index
    • We believe the most likely to be removed is Converge Information and Communications Technology Solutions (CNVRG PM)
  • The PSE Index rebalancing in August 2026 will be based on the 12 months from July 2025 to June 2026
  • Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap
    • For companies that have been listed for less than 12 months (such as MYNLD), the stocks must be among the top 20% in terms of median daily trade per month for at least six months or 75% of the total number of months it has been listed, whichever is higher 
  • Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when
    • 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or
    • 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35 
  • Based on our estimates, MYNLD is ranked within the top 25
    • Thus, the lowest ranked PSE Index constituent will be removed
  • Our preliminary estimates suggest that MYNLD could see inflow of US$28.8mn while CNVRG could see outflow of US$13.6mn

 

 

 

MARKET
Foreign ownership of Philippine market rises in May
  • We estimate that foreign ownership of the overall market rose 20bps MoM to 20.3% (in May) while foreign ownership of the PSE Index increased 28bps MoM to 23.9%
  • Net foreign selling fell to US$150mn, from US$209mn in April
    • The increase in foreign ownership appears to have been thanks to the outperformance of high foreign-owned stocks, such as ICT and GLO
  • Among the PSEi constituents, 10 saw increases in foreign ownership while 20 experienced declines
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were:
    • JFC – Foreign ownership declined 45bps MoM to 18.5%. It also saw the fifth highest net foreign selling for the month, at US$12.1mn.
    • CNPF – foreign ownership fell 44bps MoM to 21.5%.   
    • PGOLD – foreign ownership climbed 56bps MoM to 17.7%.
    • ICT – foreign ownership increased 36bps MoM to 37.3% as foreign buying hit US$50mn, the highest for the month.
