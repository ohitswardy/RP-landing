REGIS REPORT

14 September 2026

In an unexpected turn of events, private equity firm KKR sold its entire ~20% economic stake in First Gen (FGEN PM) to another private equity firm, Gateway Partners, at P36/sh. This comes after its foiled attempt to acquire an 8% stake in FGEN from First Philippine Holdings’ (FPH PM) at P35/sh, which would have been subsequently followed by a tender offer to buy out minority shareholders last month. 

 

The latest transaction drove FGEN’s share price down ~11% last Friday, as hopes for a tender offer faded. We nonetheless believe Gateway Partners is acquiring FGEN at a meaningful discount to its underlying value of ~P48/sh, which still conservatively values Energy Development Corp. at its 2018 tender offer valuation of US$2bn. If we were to value EDC at US$5bn, which was the offer made by Barito Renewables (BREN IJ) in July, then our FGEN valuation would rise to ~P72/sh.

Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) has completed the purchase of a 20% stake in a copper miner in Kazakhstan for US$30mn.  While we await the valuation details, management previously indicated that the deal will be earnings accretive. The strategic value of gaining exposure to copper is the most compelling aspect of the deal, in our view, as the metal is likely to remain in structural deficit over the medium term. 

 

+++

 

In its August Monetary Policy Report, The BSP stated that further rate hikes this year might still be necessary to contain high inflation but “…a policy reversal in 2027 could become possible...”

 

REGIS (and consensus) expects a 25bp rate hike later this year (to 5.25%) and a 25bp rate cut next year (to 5.0%).

POWER
KKR sells entire 19.9% stake in FGEN to global PE firm for P36/sh
  • KKR sold 715.8mn shares in FGEN for P36/sh to Dubai-based Gateway Partners
    • Share were sold at a 34% premium to FGEN’s previous close of P26.90/sh and at a 35% premium to KKR’s blended buy-in cost of P26.72/sh
  • Recall that KKR, through Valorous Asia Hldgs, acquired an initial 11.9% economic stake (8.4% voting stake) in FGEN when it undertook a tender offer at P22.50/sh in 2020
    • KKR undertook another tender offer for FGEN in September 2021 at an offer price of P33/sh
MINING
Nickel Asia completes 20% purchase of Kazakh-based copper miner
  • Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) completed the purchase of a 20% membership interest in East Copper Production LLP from Silk Road Resources, after satisfying all closing conditions
    • East Copper is a Kazakhstan-based entity that owns 100% of GRK MLD LLP, which holds subsoil use rights for the Karchiga copper mine
    • On a standalone basis, GRK generated US$70mn in revenues and US$40mn in EBITDA as of 1H26, after producing 5.24mn kilotons of copper
    • GRK's annual production capacity is 8,500 tons of Copper Sulfide concentrate at an average grade of 1.8% and 2,000 tons of Copper Cathode at an average grade of 1.0%
  • Recall that NIKL paid the total consideration of US$30mn in August, which values the stake a 100% equity value of ~US$150mn
    • We await further details from management on the basis for the valuation, but management previously said that the transaction is earnings accretive
    • However, we note that global copper miners trade at ~17x FY26E PER and 8x EV/EBITDA, while NIKL trades at 8x FY26E PER and 3x EV/EBITDA
MACRO
Easing may start in 2027 if inflation falls faster than expected—BSP
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), in its August Monetary Policy Report, stated that further rate hikes this year remain "on the table" but easing may begin next year if inflation moderates faster than expected in order to support economic growth
    • BSP guided the possibility of rate cuts next year if inflation moves closer to its 3% (± 1ppt) long-term target by 2Q27
  • The central bank inflation estimates for 26/27E are 6.1%/5.4%
    • With 8M26 inflation at 5.2%, the FY26 inflation forecast implies that inflation could average 7.9% for the rest of the year
  • For 2026, consensus and REGIS expectations for 26/27E inflation are 5.5%/3.9% and 6.7%/4.5%, respectively

    • Policy rate expectations for both consensus and REGIS for 26/27E are for one more 25bp hike to 5.25% in 2026 and one 25bp cut to 5.00% in 2027

  • The last two of the six Monetary Board meetings scheduled this year will be held on Oct 22 and Dec 17, respectively

 

 

 

MARKET
Foreign ownership of Philippine market declines in August
  • We estimate that foreign ownership of the overall market declined 35bps MoM to 21.3% (in August) while foreign ownership of the PSE Index fell 56bps MoM to 24.4%
  • Foreign activity reversed to net foreign selling of US$223m, from net foreign buying of US$66mn in July
  • Among the PSEi constituents that remained, 15 saw increases in foreign ownership while 14 experienced declines
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were:
    • SMPH – foreign ownership fell 82bps MoM to 21.2% as it experienced net foreign selling of US$13.3mn, the fifth highest for the month
    • ALI – foreign ownership declined 49bps MoM to 20.2%. It also saw the highest net foreign selling for the month (at US$33.7mn). However, we suspect that the foreign ownership figure does not yet reflect the foreign selling on the last trading day of the month, when ALI was removed from the MSCI Standard Index
    • PLUS – foreign ownership rose 46bps MoM to 30.8%, as it saw net foreign buying of US$2mn, the fifth highest for the month
