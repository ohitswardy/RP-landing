REGIS REPORT

20 July 2026

The government, through the Dept of Energy (DOE), has demanded that Semirara Mining (SCC PM) hand over a detailed list of assets related to its coal mining activities on Semirara island, with the aim of sharing such with the only other bidder for the coal operating contract. The DOE is also asserting ownership of the company’s assets ahead of the bidding.  SCC, expectedly, is resisting the DOE’s demands. It contends that the DOE demand has no legal basis for the claims and has filed a suit against it.

The fact that the DOE is resorting to such … “measures” supports our view that the status quo ahead of the bid is favorable to SCC.  Not only does SCC have all the equipment operational in place on the island, it also has the workers and the experience (knowledge) associated with running the mines there for decades. A new operator will have to hire workers and buy equipment, among other things.

In a subsequent interview, DOE Secretary Garin said that the government still intends to proceed with the bid process for the coal mine operation by next month. Assuming the bid goes ahead without the DOE’s demands on SCC being met, SCC will likely have a decisive advantage.

On the latest share price (~P20/share), SCC is practically pricing in the loss of its coal mining concession. If it does emerge successful in securing a renewal, we reckon it could be worth as much as P33/share.

The government announced a new program to hand out cash assistance to as many as 7.5m low-income households. Some of these households will be given P2,000 (US$33) per month over six months.

This latest program could cost as much as P30bn to the government, adding to the ~P20bn already being spent in other populist initiatives.  These exclude subsidies for the affordable rice program.

The latest approval rating polls show a slight uptick in Vice President Sara Duterte’s ratings to +31 (net), from +29. President Marcos’ net satisfaction rating was -7 from -15.  VP Duterte is currently facing impeachment proceedings that could remove her from office and bar her from running for the presidency in 2028.

POWER
Semirara asks court to rule on government’s demand for data disclosure; Gov’t says bidding will proceed despite dispute
  • Semirara (SCC PM) filed a petition for Declaratory Relief before a local court, following repeated demands from the Department of Energy (DOE) to disclose proprietary information of its assets
    • The DOE directed the company to submit detailed info of its assets, including geological and technical data, and a detailed list of equipment
    • The DOE stated that the information will be made available to prospective bidders of the Semirara Coal Operating Contract (COC)
    • DOE contends that asset ownership will vest to the government once SCC has recovered its costs; It is also prematurely asserting ownership of these assets even though the contract has yet to expire
  • Semirara is challenging the DOE’s demands, given the following:
    • Its COC, which is governed by Presidential Decree (PD) 972, does not contain any provision that vests the title of the assets to the government upon cost recovery
    • PD 972 also states that ownership of movable assets within the exploration and production area transfers to the government only if they remain unremoved one year after the contract expires on July 14, 2027
  • Meanwhile, DOE Secretary Sharon Garin said that the bidding remains on track for July or August, despite the legal dispute
    • DOE is still working out the auction structure, given that rules for brownfield projects have not been crafted yet

MACRO
7.5mn poor households to receive new cash-aid amid rising oil prices
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. announced that the gov't will provide another round of cash assistance to 7.5mn low-income households affected by the rising oil prices given the renewed tensions in the Middle East
    • 1.5mn low-income workers and their families will receive P2,000 per month from July to December or P12,000 in total for six months
    • A one-time additional cash aid of up to P2,000 will be given to 3.5mn beneficiaries of the Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) and the Walang Gutom Program
    • 2.5mn poor and near-poor households identified through the Community-Based Monitoring System (CBMS) will also be extended assistance
  • This will be implemented through the Uplift Assistance program by the Dept. of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) with 37.5mn Filipinos set to benefit

Vice President Sara Duterte’s net satisfaction rating inches up in June SWS survey
  • Vice President Sara Duterte’s net satisfaction rating (% satisfied minus % dissatisfied) in the latest Social Weather Stations Survey (SWS) inched up to +31 points in June from +29 points in March
    • Recall that President Marcos’ net satisfaction rating rebounded from his record low "poor" -15 rating in March to "neutral -7" in June
  • Duterte's gross satisfaction rating rose from 54% in Mar to 58% in Jun
    • Gross dissatisfaction inched up from 25% to 26% for the same period
  • SWS cited the following movements in Marcos’ net satisfaction rating in all four major areas:
    • -2 points in Metro Manila (from “neutral” +5 to "neutral" +3)
    • +5 points in Balance Luzon (from “neutral” +5 to "moderate" +10)
    • unchanged in the Visayas (steady at "good" +36)
    • +2 points in Mindanao (from “excellent” +82 to "excellent" +84)
  • Survey was conducted via face-to-face interviews with 1,200 adults from Jun 20-29, 2026
