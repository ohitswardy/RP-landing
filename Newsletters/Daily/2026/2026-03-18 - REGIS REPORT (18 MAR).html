When it rains it pours…

 

In our latest strategy report, we highlight that Philippine consumers are due for a massive inflation shock:

 

  • Gasoline prices surged 60% MoM and YoY
  • Diesel prices more than doubled MoM and YoY, prompting a 19% increase in public transport prices
  • The Department of Energy estimated that electricity prices could rise by 16% in April, when the peak season for electricity demand begins
  • The Department of Agriculture has also warned of higher rice prices, which represent 9% of broad CPI and a heavier 18% weight for the “bottom 30%” of households

 

In light of all of these, the government is doing what it can to provide some relief:

 

  • Congress is rushing a bill that would temporarily suspend excise taxes on gasoline (P10/liter or c11% of this week’s pump price), and diesel (P6/liter or c5% off the pump).
  • The Department of Transportation plans to provide free or discounted transport fares by mid-April.
  • The government continues to provide subsidized rice at P20/kg, less than half the retail price

 

As we noted last week, consumer wallets will be squeezed badly by rising prices, crimping discretionary spending, and prompting down-trading.

 

To the upside, coal producer Semirara (SCC PM) and renewable energy companies like ACEN (ACEN PM) stand to book windfall gains. High petrol prices will also accelerate the shift to hybrids and EVs, benefitting Ayala Corp (AC PM), the distribution partner for BYD cars and the majority shareholder of ACEN

We have results from conglomerate DMCI Holdings (DMC PM), online gaming leader DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM), and gold mining firm Apex Mining (APX PM)

 

DMCI Holdings’ (DMC PM) 4Q25 recurring income fell 20% YoY to P3.1bn, bringing FY25 core profit down 21% to P14.9bn. The results missed consensus expectations, given the disappointment at Semirara (SCC PM), as well as persistent operational challenges at the property unit. Provided that Semirara can secure the coal contract next month, most of DMCI’s businesses are poised for improvement this year, supported by Indonesia’s production cuts on nickel and thermal coal, as well as higher sales volumes. More below. 

 

DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) reported that 4Q25 revenue fell 9% QoQ and 27% YoY due to the full-quarter impact of the delinking of e-wallets. On the other hand, 4Q25 net profit climbed 43% QoQ to P2.45bn, as margins expanded from the unusually low levels in 3Q25. As a result, FY25 net profit remained flat at P12.6bn, in line with consensus forecast. Looking ahead, the street is projecting FY26 net profit to hit P13.5bn. While this is only 7% higher than the FY25 figure, it represents a 35% increase from the annualized 4Q25 performance.  

 

Apex Mining (APX PM) reported FY25 net income rose 77% YoY to P7.66bn, driven largely by soaring gold prices.

Meralco (MER PM) confirmed it is undertaking its due diligence to assess a potential partnership with SCC. We believe a joint venture involving SCC’s coal mining asset would likely be value accretive. Given its decades-long track record of independently operating the mine, SCC is unlikely to pursue any arrangement that does not deliver clear operational, financial, or strategic benefits.

CONGLOS
DMCI 4Q25: Continued weakness at Semirara, property unit

4Q25 profit falls 20% to P3.1bn. Reported 4Q25 profit fell 14% to P3.3bn. Removing the one-off settlement claim from an abandoned railway project, core profit dropped 20% to P3.1bn. Operations remained weak at Semirara (here) and the property unit, which overshadowed operational improvements at other smaller units. FY25 core profit reached P14.9bn, down 21% YoY and falling short of consensus estimate of P15.9bn. Management is also deferring paying dividends until after the bidding of the Semirara coal contract is over. 

 

Wider 4Q EBITDA loss at property unit; Reservation sales drop 36% in FY25.  Despite a 2% rise in revenue bookings, the property segment recorded a wider EBITDA loss of P84mn in 4Q25 (vs. -P27mn in 4Q24) due to higher opex from RFO projects. Profits rose 51% to P421mn on higher forfeiture income. Reservation sales (net of cancellations) were lower by 36% YoY to P21.4bn in FY25 due to the absence of new launches, which was partly offset by higher RFO sales. With unsold inventory exceeding four years’ worth of sales, the company is prioritizing launching leisure developments, including a new project in Baguio that will be done in partnership with its parent firm, Dacon.

 

Start-up costs from new nickel mine weigh on 4Q25. Even though revenues jumped 26% in 4Q25, start-up costs at its new mine in Palawan dragged profits for its nickel mining arm down 25% YoY to P198mn. Nevertheless, profit contributions rose more than threefold to P924mn in FY25, buoyed by elevated ore prices and a 31% increase in sales volumes. Given its strong nickel resource base of over 200mn MT, management is upbeat, targeting a 50% increase in sales volumes to 3mn MT in 2026 and a further increase to 8-10mn MT in the next five years.

 

Construction, cement units improve but... The construction unit is hopeful it can sustain double-digit revenue growth alongside margin improvements this year. However, the recent spike in oil prices poses risks of project delays due to logistical challenges. Meanwhile, its cement arm, Concreate (CHP PM), narrowed EBITDA losses by 80% to P133mn in FY25, largely due to cost reductions. It is aiming to achieve cash break-even this year, on the back of a 20% improvement in sales volumes to 4.8mn MT as well as cost efficiencies.

 

 

GAMING
DigiPlus FY25 net profit remains flat, in line with consensus
  • DigiPlus Interactive’s FY25 net profit remained flat at P12.6bn, in line with consensus forecast
  • FY25 EBITDA rose 2% to P14.2bn, also in line with consensus forecast
  • FY25 revenue grew 12% to P84bn, behind consensus forecast
  • 4Q25 revenue fell 9% QoQ and 27% YoY to P17.3bn, due to the full-quarter impact of the BSP directive prohibiting e-wallets from redirecting to online gaming platforms
  • However, 4Q25 EBITDA jumped 52% QoQ (down 33% YoY) to P3.1bn
    • EBITDA margin expanded 7.2 percentage points QoQ (down 1.5 ppt YoY) to 17.9%
  • 4Q25 net profit climbed 43% QoQ (down 36% YoY) to P2.45bn
    • Net margin expanded 5.2 percentage points QoQ (down 2.1ppt YoY) to 14.2%
  • The company declared a cash dividend of P0.83/share, representing a dividend yield of 4.8%
  • PLUS is trading at 6.0X FY26E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
    • The street is projecting revenue to rise 4% (to P87.3bn) and net profit to grow 7% (to P13.5bn)

 

 

MINING
Apex Mining FY25 net income jumps 77% to P7.66bn; Declares P0.615/sh dividend
  • In a press release, Apex Mining disclosed its FY25 net income rose 77% YoY to P7.66bn, which appears below consensus’ estimate of P7.88bn
  • Revenues rose 41% to P21bn, on the back of 45% increase in realized gold prices
  • Gold sales volumes fell 4% YoY to 100k ounces while silver sales volumes rose 4% to 365k ounces
  • The company amended its dividend policy to a minimum payout of 20% from 10% previously
  • The company declared a regular dividend of P0.246/sh and a special dividend of P0.369/sh, totaling P0.615/sh (vs. 2025 divs of P0.139/sh)
    • Dividend is equivalent to a payout ratio of 45% (up from 20% in 2024) and translates to a yield of 4%
    • Ex-div date on April 14; Payment of April 27
  • APX trades at 7.8x consensus’ FY26E PER

  • Note that ICT has 2.2bn of authorized but unissued common shares and has 24.59mn shares under treasury

MACRO
LTFRB approves higher jeepney, bus, TNVS fares
  • The Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) Chairman Vigor D. Mendoza II stated that the board has approved fare hikes for public utility vehicles effective March 19 (Thursday)
    • The minimum fare for traditional jeepneys has been raised to P14 from P13 while modern jeepneys' fares rose to P17 from P15
    • For non-air-conditioned and air-conditioned buses, the minimum fare will be raised to P15 and P18 from P13 and P15, respectively
      • Point-to-point bus fees will rise 15% based on the existing fare route
    • The base fare for transportation network vehicle services (TNVS) has rose to P65 from P45 for sedans, and P165 from P145 for premium TNVS
  • Passenger road transport has a weight of 4.5% in the CPI basket
MACRO
Suspension of mandatory biofuel blending bill approved by House, reaches Senate plenary
  • A bill seeking the temporary suspension of mandatory biofuel blending in order to ease oil prices has been approved by the Lower House, while the Senate is still finalizing its counterpart version of the bill
    • The bill seeks to amend the Biofuels Act of 2006, in order to allow the President to suspend the mandatory use of locally sourced biofuels for up to one year if the prices of blended fuel are at least 5% higher than pure gasoline or diesel
  • The bill was tagged as urgent by President Ferdinand Marcos, alongside the fuel excise tax adjustment bill which has been recently approved by both the House and Senate
