CONSUMER
SEVN 1Q26 net profit P629mn (+5% YoY), EBIT P1.1bn (+8%)
  • Philippine Seven Corporation [PSC] (SEVN PM) reported 1Q26 net profit of P629mn (+5% YoY), tracking behind estimates
  • EBIT and revenue were within expectations, rising 8% and 14% to P1.1bn and P24.8bn, respectively
  • 1Q26 SSSG was +4.4%, an acceleration from +3.1% in 4Q25 on a faster increase in average ticket size
  • Management noted "improved consumer demand" and "stronger store-level execution across the network" with all regions posting positive SSSG in the quarter
    • Growth was supported by a "sales recovery" in non-alcoholic beverage and tobacco categories as well as the continued rollout of card payment terminals
    • The company also noted growth in market share of its ready-to-eat (RTE) products and highlighted the strength of its Luzon stores despite strong competition in the region
  • Both EBIT and net profit lagged revenue growth due to expansion-related expenses on higher opex (e.g., utilities, logistics, manpower) and higher lease liabilities interest expense, respectively
    • Rising fuel costs also contributed to margin pressure but the company cited implemented mitigation measures
  • More after the briefing on May 14 (Thursday, 3:35pm). Register here: https://events.pse.com.ph/pse-star/

 

 

CONSUMER
Voluntary delisting of RRHI from PSE approved by stockholders during ASM
  • RRHI announced in a press release the results of its ASM held on May 12, the company stated that the proposed delisting from the PSE has been approved by stockholders
    • 82.82% of the total outstanding shares of the company voted to approve the delisting proposal, with 1.97% voting against, less than 0.01% abstained, and 15.21% did not cast their vote
  • Note that stockholders' approval for the voluntary delisting of RRHI is separate from the decision of the stockholders to tender their shares
  • Terms and mechanics of tender offer will be included in the tender offer report which will be disseminated to shareholders "in due course after the May 12, 2026 ASM"
    • The tender offer will commence on May 25, 2026, with the tender offer report to be given likely within the next few days
  • Recall that JE Holdings, a Gokongwei family holdco, back in end-March announced its intention to conduct a tender offer to bring RRHI private at P48.30/sh (8x FY26E PER)
GAMING
PAGCOR sets caps on rebate and cashback programs on e-games
  • PAGCOR instituted caps on rebate and cashback programs on e-games
    • These caps will be effective beginning May 15
  • Rebates based on player turnover or deposits:
    • For slot machine games, electronic bingo, numeric games and sports betting, operators may offer cash rebates of up to 1.5% based on player turnover or deposits
    • Casino table games and arcade-type games are excluded from the turnover-based rebate provision
  • Cashbacks based on player net losses:
    • Cashback programs based on player net losses may be offered across all types of electronic games, capped at a maximum rate of 15%
  • DigiPlus currently offers cashback/rebates that exceed these limits (in some cases)
  • However, management said that these promotions have not delivered meaningful results in terms of revenue generation and user acquisition, and they are already scheduled to be discontinued this month
  • PLUS is trading at 4.5X FY26E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
BANKS
Commercial banks’ loan growth picks up to 10.7% in March
  • Preliminary data showed that commercial bank loan growth accelerated to 10.7% in Mar from 9.6% in Feb
    • Loans to productive activities picked up to 9.7% vs 8.6% the previous month
    • Consumer loans grew at a still robust pace of 20.5%, a tad slower than Feb's 20.8% rate
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) accelerated to 12.0% in Mar from 10.3% in Feb, the fastest since Sep 2020 (12.2%)

Yield on 5-year Philippine government bond rises
  • The Philippine government issued P30bn of 5-year bonds at an average yield of 7.249%
    • The bid-to-cover ratio was 1.28X, the lowest level since 2013
  • The 5-year bond yield in the previous auction (April 14) was 6.328%
  • The 5-year Philippine government bond yield in the secondary market (BVAL) was 7.01%
