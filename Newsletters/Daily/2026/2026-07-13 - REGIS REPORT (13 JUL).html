REGIS REPORT

13 July 2026

Throughput at ICTSI’s (ICT PM) terminal in Mexico remained strong into June, with volumes for the month up 24% to 137k TEUs.  The June figure means YTD volumes were up 15% while 2Q26 volumes were up 23%. This puts Contecon Manzanillo SA (CMSA) firmly on track to exceed our FY26 volume estimates by over 10%.

 

The CMSA terminal is one of the two largest operations globally for ICTSI and contributes almost 1/4th of group-wide EBITDA.

Gaming firm Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) relaunched its online gaming platform under a new name (“FUNaloMAX”). FUNaloMAX is currently available only through web browsers and is yet to be available through a mobile app. BLOOM expects the initiative lose money to post losses initially, with a possible profit by next year (2027) at the earliest.

 

+++

 

The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) is proposing new rules to give it more direct oversight on power supply auctions conducted by distribution utilities (DU). DUs contract power to sell to captive customers and we suspect the ERC is trying to prevent DUs from unduly favoring affiliate gencos in the process (and thus passing on higher costs to consumers). We shall see if the ERC’s proposals will have any real-world impact in the future.

 

+++

 

Ayala Corp (AC PM) subsidiary AC Health says it aims to complete the acquisition of at least one hospital within the year.  Beyond that, it remains on the lookout for more hospitals and is planning a handful of new clinics every year.

TRANSPORT
Throughput at ICTSI’s Mexico port rises 24% YoY in June; 2Q26 volumes up 23%
  • Data from Mexico’s port authority showed that throughput at ICTSI’s port, Contecon Manzanillo SA (CMSA), rose 24% YoY to 137k TEUs in June
    • We note, however, that volumes softened by 10% MoM and that the YoY growth was flattered by easy comps from last year's customs-related disruptions
  • Brings CMSA’s 2Q26 volumes up 23% YoY to 437k TEUs and 1H26 volumes up 15% YoY to 854k TEUs
    • This is trending well ahead of our full-year forecast (1.66mn TEUs, +8%)
    • Every 10% increase in CMSA’s topline will raise consolidated FY26E EBITDA by 3% and profit by 4%
  • We estimate CMSA accounts for 10% of ICTSI’s consolidated FY26E volumes, 20% of revenues, and 23% of EBITDA
  • ICTSI trades at 15x FY26E EV/EBITDA (vs. peer average of 11x EBITDA and historical average of 10x EBITDA)

POWER
DOE suspends green energy auction for offshore wind projects
  • The Department of Energy (DOE) has temporarily put on hold the fifth round of the Green Energy Auction Program (GEA-5) to recalibrate the auction framework
    • GEA-5 involves offshore wind projects
    • The recalibration seeks to better match auction requirements with actual project development conditions, including infrastructure readiness, regulatory processes, transmission capability and overall project deliverability
  • ACEN Corp. has a definitive agreement to acquire a 25% stake in the proposed 1GW offshore wind project in the coast of San Miguel Bay, Camarines Sur
    • Project is estimated to cost US$3bn and will be done in partnership with Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners (CIP)
    • ACEN and CIP previously intended to participate in GEA-5
    • Our forecasts and valuation do not incorporate this project
POWER
ERC to tighten oversight on power supply auctions
  • Under its proposed regulatory framework, the ERC intends to review the terms of reference (TORs) for competitive selection processes (CSPs) prior to bidding launch
  • The proposed rules would mandate power distribution utilities and the system operator to first submit their TORs to the ERC before publishing invitations to bid
    • Power utilities will be required to submit a sworn least-cost justification statement, attesting that the TOR parameters neither exclude legitimate bidders nor favor certain suppliers
  • All concerned energy stakeholders may submit their comments on the proposed rules until July 24, ahead of a virtual public consultation on July 31
CONGLOS
AC Health targets acquisitions, plans to open 3-5 clinics annually
  • AC Health said it has several hospital acquisition opportunities in the pipeline, with at least one transaction expected to close within the year
    • The company is targeting Level 2 hospitals with at least 100 beds located in densely populated urban areas
    • According to AC Health Chief Strategy and Investment Officer Rafael Jaime V. Recio
  • The company also plans to expand its clinic network by opening three to five new clinics annually over the next three to five years
    • While its clinics have traditionally been located in shopping malls, the company is also exploring alternative formats, including standalone roadside locations.
  • Over the medium term, AC Health aims to grow its network to 1,150 retail pharmacies, 300 clinics, and 10 hospitals
    • As of end-2025, AC Health had a network of 900+ pharmacies, 18 multi-specialty clinics and 300 corporate clinics, as well as 7 hospitals (including 1 cancer center)
    • AC Health generated revenues of P12.8bn, EBITDA of P1.5bn and net income of P34mn in 2025
  • AC hold an 84% economic stake in AC Health, with the balance owned by Temasek-backed fund, ABC Impact
    •  AC trades at a 29% discount to its market-based NAV of P667/sh and a 5x FY26E PER
