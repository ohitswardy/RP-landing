In a press release BPI (BPI PM) reported headline 1H26 NPAT of P32.8bn, down 0.4% YoY, and bang in line with REGIS FY26 forecast of P65.34bn (consensus P66.26bn). For 2Q26 alone, NPAT was down 3% YoY. Loan growth slowed to 12.4% (from 13.4%), with corporate loans +8.7% and consumer/MSMEs +21.2%. Management estimates 1H26 NIMs gained 5bps YoY. Revenues (+12%), opex (+14%), and PPOP (+11%) were in line. Provision expense surged 85% to P13.3bn, with credit costs jumping to 120bps for 2Q (vs 86bps in 1Q), despite NPL ratio being flat QoQ at 2.42%, due to higher ECL with the deterioration of economic conditions, which was flagged during the 1Q26 briefing. NPL cover popped back up to 93% from 87% in 1Q. The stock is on 8.4x PE and 1.1x P/bk on our FY26F. Management will hold its briefing on Wed, 29 July at 4pm.

BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
Digital payments up 45.4% YoY in June; set to re-surge with zero-fees

Digital transactions jumped to a new all-time high of P2,909bn ($47.2bn) in June, up 45.4% YoY, accelerating from the 41.0% pace in May. That took the 1H26 total to P16,090bn, also up 44.6%, faster than the 42.0% growth in FY2025.

 

The surprise driver was PesoNet – the rail used for larger transaction values – which surged to a new record of P1,491bn, growing 37.5% YoY, compared to 26.4% in May. The 6M figure was P8,113bn, up 31.9% YoY, a shade faster than the 30.9% clocked for the whole of 2025. Meanwhile, transactions over InstaPay – real-time payments up to P50,000 – grew 54.8% YoY to P1,419bn, a deceleration from the 57.4% pace the previous month. That said the year-to-date figure of P7,977bn was still up 60.3% YoY, faster than the FY25 growth of 57.3%. The volume of transactions over InstaPay continued to surge, up 96% YoY.

 

We expect volumes to surge again, especially for InstaPay, with the recent announcement of many banks dropping P2P transfer fees to zero, and with the two major e-wallets slashing fees from P15 to P10. We believe the InstaPay fees were a big hurdle to adoption for really small-ticket payments. Prior to the price drops, banks and e-wallets charged on average P10-15, with some as high as P25-35 per transaction. For perspective, the average ticket size for InstaPay in 1H26 was just P1,927. Clearly the fixed fees had discouraged much smaller-value transfers.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

GAMING
Okada Manila 2Q26 GGR falls QoQ and YoY
  • Okada Manila’s 2Q26 GGR fell 6% QoQ and 15% YoY to P6.1bn
  • The decline was led by the VIP business
    • VIP GGR fell 30% QoQ and 56% YoY to P1.0bn
    • VIP rolling chip shrank 30% QoQ and 23% YoY to P40.6bn
  • On the other hand, the gaming machine segment saw an improvement
    • Gaming machine GGR rose 5% QoQ and 3% YoY to P2.86bn
    • Gaming machine handle rose 9% QoQ and YoY to P50.7bn

 

 

MACRO
Remittances rise by 2.0% in May
  • Cash remittances amounted US$2.7bn in May, 2.0% higher YoY—steady relative to the 2.0% growth in April, which was the lowest YoY growth figure since May 2022 (+1.8%)
    • Remittance growth since March has been below 2.3%, below the typical 3-4% range
  • Remittance growth was primarily driven by Japan (+4.0%), Saudi Arabia (+3.6%), and the UAE (+4.0%)

 

 

 

MACRO
Gov't mulls measures to cushion impact of rising fuel prices
  • Palace Press Officer Claire Castro stated that the President alongside the concerned agencies are assessing possible measures to mitigate the impact of rising oil prices given renewed tensions in the Middle East
    • The Dept. of Energy (DOE) recently said that it is weighing the implementation of fixed weekly fuel price adjustments over the current prescribed fuel price ranges as oil firms were consistently implementing adjustments at the higher end of the range
    • The Dept. of Transportation (DoTr) is evaluating potential assistance for affected sectors as well as studying pending fare hike petitions
      • Recall that the DoTr alongside other gov't agencies previously issued fuel subsidies and cash aid during the height of the war
    • The gov't is also still studying the removal/suspension of excise taxes for gasoline and diesel
      • If the suspension of the excise tax on gasoline and diesel is approved, previous government estimates suggest it would result in P43.6bn in foregone revenues
        • Excise taxes are at P10/liter for gas and P6/liter for diesel

 

 

 

 

TRANSPORT
Cebu Air’s pax volumes up 3% in June but load factors still soft
  • Cebu Air reported its pax volumes rose 3% YoY to 2.3mn in June, the first positive growth since the Iran conflict
    • Domestic pax volumes climbed 6% YoY to 1.8mn on an 18% increase in seat capacity, with seat load factor down 9ppts YoY to 83.1%
    • International pax volumes declined 9% YoY to 510k as capacity was cut by 19%
  • Jet kero prices fell ~40% in June from the peak in April but remain materially above pre-conflict levels
    • Cost pressures remain, limiting the pace of margin recovery, particularly during the lean travel season
