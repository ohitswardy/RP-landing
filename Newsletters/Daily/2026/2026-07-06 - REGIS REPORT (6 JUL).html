REGIS REPORT

06 July 2026

The BSP in June issued new guidelines meant to reduce InstaPay and PesoNet fees for individuals. BPI (BPI PM) surprised the market last week, permanently waiving all fees across all its digital platforms. RCBC (RCB PM) quickly followed with zero fees, but with conditions. Over the weekend, the two major e-wallets GCash and Maya both cut their InstaPay fees from P15 to P10 effective 04 and 06 July respectively. The BSP expects other banks and wallets to announce big cuts soon. BPI estimates that outbound (“off-us”) transfer fees account for about P2.2bn on an annualized basis, or roughly 1.1% of its 2025 total revenues, and 2.6% of pre-tax profit. It is unclear how large these fees are for other banks/e-wallets, either in absolute PHP terms or in % of profits. For BPI, though, management believes that leading the “race to zero” would result in increased usage among existing customers (more, potentially increasing its customer count, which would generate enough CASA, loans, fees, etc. to make up for foregone payment fees. Digital adoption has exploded in recent years, with InstaPay and PesoNet transactions hitting P2.8trn in May, tracking over 100% of nominal GDP. Further acceleration in financial inclusion opens up a vast market for institutions ready to capitalize on it.

 

+++

 

Meralco’s (MER PM) electricity volumes inched up by a mere 0.6% for 2Q26, despite what feels like a warmer-than-usual period. The clear softness in demand is, we believe, a symptom of (1) self-generation through rooftop solar and (2) high unit power costs for captive customers.

 

+++

 

Ayala Corp (AC PM) disclosed it has set aside P5bn for the purchase of shares in subsidiary Ayala Land (ALI PM), in an announcement that we would view as a signaling move. AC still has P9.6bn left in its buyback war chest – a war chest that can be used to buy its own shares or shares of its various operating units, including ALI.

 

+++

 

A new (sort of) player is getting into the broadband business. Bitstar Telecom, a small broadband operator, has apparently registered under the new rules for the Konektadong Pinoy Act. We expect the broadband sector to be significantly more viable and thus attract more new competition than the mobile business.

 

+++

 

The impeachment trial of Vice President Sara Duterte is set to begin today for, among other things, corruption, misuse of public funds and threats to … harm… the President. Conviction will mean not just removal from office but also be perpetually disqualified from holding public office. The latter penalty could mean she would not be able to run for President in 2028.

 

The impeachment trial is likely to dominate news flow on the political front for the next few months and also provide an unnecessary distraction from policy matters.

TELCOS
New broadband player enters under Konektadong Pinoy Act
  • Bitstar Telecom Inc. announced its entry into the Philippine telco industry as a new internet service provider (ISP) for businesses
    • The company plans to penetrate enterprise demand for broadband services as a lower-cost alternative in Metro Manila then eventually to the provinces
    • It eyes to utilize the Konektadong Pinoy Act in order to combine its own infrastructure with network assets from existing telco companies to roll out services without building a new network from scratch
  • Bitstar Telecom is the telecommunications and broadband venture of MRC Allied Inc. (MRC PM)
    • The company is partnered with affiliate PT&T which provides the underlying network in Metro Manila, while Bitstar will handle sales, onboarding, and customer support
TELCOS
PLDT, DITO sign network-sharing deal
  • PLDT's mobile arm, Smart Communications, and DITO Telecommunity have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) to share network infrastructure (e.g., cell tower sites, in-building telco systems, submarine cable capacity) without charging for lease, colocation or capacity fees
  • The companies stated that they expect to deploy capital more efficiently while delivering better service
POLITICS
VP Sara Duterte impeachment trial starts today
  • The proceedings for the impeachment trial of Vice President Sara Duterte will begin this afternoon with the outcome determining whether she will be acquitted or be perpetually disqualified from holding public office and ending her 2028 presidential bid
    • Conviction requires the votes of at least two-thirds (16) of the Senate
  • The first day of trial will focus on opening statements and procedural matters with no witnesses or presentation of evidence yet
    • The proceedings will start with allegations of Duterte's threats against President Marcos then charges involving confidential funds, bribery and unexplained wealth
  • The prosecution team stated that they are expecting and preparing for the defense's possible bid to dismiss the case
    • Spokesperson of the prosecution panel, Robert Ace Barbers, said that the team would also seek Duterte's perpetual disqualification from public office if she resigns before the end of the trial
