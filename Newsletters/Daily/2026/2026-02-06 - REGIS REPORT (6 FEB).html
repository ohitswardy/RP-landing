REGIS REPORT

06 February 2026

The Jan CPI print announced yesterday (2.0%) was, arguably, a mildly negative surprise but we reckon it is unlikely to derail expectations of a policy rate reduction by the Bangko Sentral (BSP) two weeks from now. The GDP growth figure of 3.0% for 4Q had also previously laid a strong case for more monetary policy loosening at the next Monetary Board (MB) meeting.

 

Beyond the next MB meeting, however, prospects for further easing are looking more uncertain.  Core inflation accelerated to 2.8% in Jan and is now approaching the mid-point of the BSP’s inflation range. The flattering effect of a higher base for rice prices will also fade soon, thus leading to higher headline inflation into 2Q26.

 

+++

 

ICTSI (ICT PM) secured an extension of its contract for its port in Melbourne to 2066 from 2040. Given that Melbourne represents approximately 5% of our NAV, the 26-year extension results in a 3% (+P20/sh) uplift to our NAV.

 

+++

 

RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM) reported that its FY25 revenue rose 35% to P11.1bn, which appears to be 5% above consensus estimates. Before investors get too excited, we should caution them that the large outperformance is almost certainly because of varying assumptions on asset injections rather than anything attributable to operations. The dividend declared (P0.1125/share) from 4Q25 earnings to be paid next month is otherwise in line with Street estimates (P0.46/share for FY26).

 

+++

 

Property consultancy Colliers noted the recent improvement in Metro Manila condominium sales and attributed it to an improvement for the mid-income segment. Net take-up of Metro Manila condominiums rose c. 10% to 10,000 units, with mid-income units comprising 70% of total sales (from only 35% in 2024). This was thanks to aggressive promotions on mid-income ready-for-occupancy (RFO) units, such as cash discounts (ot as much as 50%), rent-to-own schemes (of up to 10 years), free parking, etc.

MACRO
Inflation inches up to 2.0% in January
  • Headline inflation picked up to 2.0% in Jan from Dec's 1.8%
    • This was a tad higher than consensus expectations of 1.8% but still within the BSP's forecast range of 1.4-2.2% for Jan
    • This is the first time inflation has hit 2.0% since Feb 2025 (2.1%) as the CPI has been hovering below BSP's 2-4% long-term inflation target for the previous ten months
  • Inflation in Jan accelerated slightly given faster YoY increases vs Dec in electricity, gas, fuel as well as restaurants and accommodation services prices
    • Food prices overall rose 1.1% YoY, decelerating from the 1.4% pace in Dec—as vegetable prices normalize following the impact of strong rains during 2H25
    • Notably, rice prices were up MoM by 3.4%—marking the third consecutive MoM price increase
      • Rice prices are down 8.5% YoY in Jan, the thirteenth consecutive YoY price decline since Jan 2025
  • Core inflation accelerated to 2.8% in Jan from 2.4% in Dec, driven by core non-food inflation which rose by 2.7% in Dec from 2.1% in Jan
  • For 2026, the BSP forecasts inflation to settle at 3.2%
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to be 2.6% and 3.0%
  • The first out of six Monetary Board meetings for the year will be held on Feb 19
    • For 2026, consensus and Regis expect one 25bp cut to 4.25%

ICTSI extends contract expiry for Melbourne port by 26 years
  • Victoria International Container Terminal (VICT), wholly-owned unit of ICTSI, and the Port of Melbourne Operations signed an agreement to extend the expiration of its contract to 2066 from 2040 previously
    • An ongoing investment program due for completion in late 2026 will increase estimated capacity to 1.6mn TEUs
    • No significant capital expenditure for further capacity expansion is anticipated under the terms of the extended contract
  • We estimate VICT accounts for ~7% of group-wide volumes, revenues, and EBITDA, as well as 5% of our NAV
    • Our model already assumes capacity will expand gradually to 1.8mn TEUs by 2030 from 1.3mn TEUs today
  • We estimate the contract extension will add +P20/sh (+3%) to our NAV
  • ICTSI trades at 11x FY26E EV/EBTDA and 19x PER vs. peer average (13x EBITDA, 18x PER) vs. historical average (11x EBITDA/19x PER)
PROPERTY
RCR FY25 revenue rises 35% YoY, above consensus forecast
  • RCR’s FY25 revenue rose 35% to P11.08bn, 5% above consensus forecast
    • 4Q25 revenue climbed 49% YoY
  • The increase in revenue was largely due to asset infusions in 2H25, which expanded its portfolio by c. 40% to 1.1mn sqm
  • Occupancy stood at 96%, flat YoY and QoQ
  • The company declared a dividend of P0.1112/share for 4Q25, which will be paid in March
    • Ex-date: Feb 19
    • We estimate RCR is on track to meet the FY26E DPS forecast of P0.464/share  
  • RCR currently offers 6.2% dividend yield (based on consensus forecast)
PROPERTY
Colliers: 2025 condominium take-up improves thanks to RFO promotions
  • Colliers reported that Metro Manila condominium net take-up improved to 10,000 units in 2025
    • From 9,000 units in 2024
  • This was largely thanks to aggressive promotions on mid-income ready-for-occupancy (RFO) units
  • Mid-income units comprised c. 70% of 2025 net take-up, from only 35% in 2024
    • This implies that sales of mid-income units more than double
  • Developers have been offering aggressive promotions on RFO units, including:
    • cash discounts of as much as 50%
    • rent-to-own schemes of up to 10 years
    • free parking spaces
    • fully furnished units
  • Colliers said that developers would have to retain these aggressive promotions since there are still 79,000 unsold condominium units in Metro Manila
    • This represents c. 8 years of sales (down from 13 years in 2Q25, flat from 4Q25)
