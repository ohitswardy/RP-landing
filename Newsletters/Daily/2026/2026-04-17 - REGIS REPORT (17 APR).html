MARKET
PSEi May 2026 rebalancing predictions: AREIT, EMI, PLUS, RCR to see upweights
  • Based on our estimates, AREIT, EMI, PLUS, and RCR, will see inflow during the May 2026 PSE Index rebalancing
    • Due to increases in their public float figures
    • Other PSE Index constituents should experience outflows
  • The principal shareholders of AREIT (AREIT PM), Emperador (EMI PM), and RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM) sold shares of the respective companies
  • A major shareholder of DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) sold shares of the company 
  • A rebalancing of the PSE Index occurs in May when public float of a PSEi constituent moves by at least 5ppt from end-December to end-March
    • The public float of PLUS, increased 6ppt QoQ in 1Q26, while the public float figures of RCR and EMI rose 5ppt each

 

 

TRANSPORT
ICTSI raises tariffs at some ports to offset fuel costs
  • ICTSI chairman and president Enrique Razon Jr. said that they have already implemented tariff adjustments to make up for the differential in diesel and fuel prices across its terminals
    • In the Philippines though, MICT in particular, any change in tariff will require approval from the port authority
    • We note that its Mexico port has not yet raised tariffs, but we expect it will likely do so in May, as this is typically when tariffs are increased
  • He further noted that there is so far no disruption on (fuel) deliveries, but noted that prices are very high
    • ICTSI is “constantly working” with the government and other agencies throughout the world “to make sure that the Philippines has a continuous (fuel) supply.”
    • The impact of the shortage with the closure of the Strait of Hormuz is mostly felt in Asia, unlike Latin America, which has big suppliers like the United States, Canada, Mexico, and Brazil
  • Power and fuel collectively comprise roughly 10% of ICTS’s cash costs
CONSUMER
Jollibee provides update on Highlands Coffee and Compose Coffee
  • JFC, in two separate press releases, provided updates on Highlands Coffee and Compose Coffee elaborating on Highlands' strong performance as of 1Q26 and Compose' expansion outside of Korea
  • Highlands posted high-single digit in SSSG for 1Q26 and high double-digit revenue growth in FY25 exhibiting "sustained demand and disciplined execution"
    • JFC noted the solid store-level profitability and the continued growth of digital channels
  • Compose saw a strong pre-opening debut for its first store in Taiwan with "one cup sold every 20 seconds" and wait times stretching up to two hours
    • Management highlighted the brand's "unlimited potential to become a leading global brand" with quality coffee at accessible prices
    • The company also reiterated its plans to enter the Philippine market this year
  • Store counts for Highlands Coffee and Compose Coffee are at 2,972 and 985, up from 2,629 and 850 at end-2024, respectively
  • Highlands and Compose comprise 2.4% and 7.4% of JFC's FY25 consolidated SWS
CONSUMER
Jollibee secures antitrust regulatory approval for South Korean hot pot brand, Shabu All Day
  • JFC via its 70%-owned subsidiary Jolli-K, secured Korea Fair Trade Commission approval for its US$87mn (~P5.1bn) acquisition of 100% of All Day Fresh Co., Ltd., operator of Shabu All Day
    • According to JFC, the acquisition price implies a 4x EV/EBITDA multiple
      • Haidilao International Holding Ltd. (6862 HK), global Chinese hot pot chain, currently trades at 8.9x FY26E EV/EBITDA
  • Upon completion of the transaction, Shabu All Day will be immediately consolidated into JFC's financial statements and the company estimates that it will add +2% to revenues, +8% to Global EBIT, and +1% (170 stores) to store count in 2026
    • The consolidation impact is based on All Day Fresh's annualized FY25 results vs JFC's inhouse FY26 forecast
GAMING
Bloomberry online gaming platform could generate profit in 2027
  • During the company’s stockholders’ meeting, Bloomberry Chairman Enrique Razon Jr. said its online gaming platform will relaunch in 2Q26
  • Rebranding from “MegaFUNalo” to “FUNaloMAX”

  • He also said that on premise gaming will remain challenging this year, especially VIP
GAMING
DigiPlus receives license to operate in Western Cape, South Africa
  • DigiPlus Interactive received licenses to operate from the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB) in South Africa
  • The South African online gaming market is estimated to have generated US$5bn in revenue in 2025
    • 31% (c. US$1.5bn) came from Western Cape
  • DigiPlus generated P84bn in revenue in 2025 (in the Philippines)
  • Philippine online gaming revenue (including bingo) grew 30% to P200bn (c. US$3.3bn) in 2025
    • 4Q25 industry GGR fell 12% to P40.4bn
    • We estimate that DigiPlus market share rose 2ppt QoQ to 43%
POWER/UTILITIES
Meralco’s P4bn cost recovery cleared by regulator
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) allowed Meralco to collect Excellent Energy’s (EERI) fuel cost recovery, amounting to P3.67bn, from consumers
    • Cost can be billed for 12 months, starting in September, translating to a rate hike of P0.1099/kwh
    • EERI is a 40%-owned unit of Meralco, 33% of San Miguel (SMC PM), and 27% of Aboitiz Power (AP PM)
    • The ERC said it “recognizes” EERI’s commercial operations date
MACRO
Foreign ownership in telco, retail sectors and gov't infra projects eased
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr., through Executive Order (EO) 113, issued the 13th Foreign Investment Negative List which provides updates on the guidelines on specific sectors where foreign ownership is restricted or prohibited
    • This will take into effect 15 days after its publication
  • Full foreign ownership is allowed for firms involving the operation and management of telecommunications provided that there is reciprocity from the investor's home country
    • Without such reciprocity, only 50% foreign equity is allowed for such firms
  • Retail trade enterprises with paid-up capital of less than P25mn are now able to have up to 40% foreign equity, previously foreigners were completely prohibited
  • 75% foreign ownership is now permitted for gov't infrastructure projects but limited to projects that require special skills or technologies that local companies lack
  • Up to 40% foreign equity is allowed in the exploration, development, and utilization of natural resources, including the use of water from natural sources, subject to existing laws and regulations
