REGIS REPORT

23 July 2026

The latest survey for the 2028 Presidential and Senatorial candidates show VP Sara Duterte and her allies strengthening their positions among voters. According to PulseAsia’s June survey, if the Presidential elections were held today, VP Duterte would handily win a majority, regardless of who runs against her in a head-to-head. More telling, if the field were split into 3 or more candidates, Duterte’s numbers tend to hold, while the other candidates’ votes get split. Remember that there is NO runoff in the Philippines. Meanwhile, for the 12 Senate seats up for grabs, at least 3 of the top 5 are broadly allied with Duterte, with former actor and fierce loyalist Robin Padilla in the top spot.

 

Ms Duterte’s numbers were strong across geographies, a perhaps more importantly, across income groups.

 

That said, the survey was conducted before the start of the ongoing impeachment trial against the Vice President. Her conviction is highly unlikely – in our view – as her allies have enough votes (around 10) to block the required 2/3 vote (out of 24) for a guilty verdict. However, a protracted hearing, with more allegations and evidence brought against her could erode her popularity enough to make another candidate viable.

 

It is still unclear, at this stage, what the VP’s economic agendas are, making it exceedingly difficult to predict policy direction should she win.

POLITICS
Sara Duterte leads in Pulse Asia's 2028 presidential preference survey
  • The Pulse Asia survey on voter preferences for the May 2028 presidential election showed Vice President Sara Duterte leading with 49% support, followed by former Vice President Leni Robredo at 26% and Senator Raffy Tulfo at 14%
    • The remaining potential candidates each garnered 2% or less
    • Note that the survey was conducted June 28-July 3 & 6, while the impeachment trial of VP Sara Duterte started on July 6
  • Duterte posted commanding leads across all major island groups, with her strongest support in Mindanao (87%), followed by the Visayas (53%), while she also led in Balance Luzon (31%) and Metro Manila (39%)
    • Robredo ranked second nationally, drawing her strongest support from Metro Manila (38%) and Balance Luzon (31%), while posting more modest support in the Visayas (27%) and limited backing in Mindanao (8%)
    • Tulfo placed third with relatively broad but lower support across regions, performing best in Balance Luzon (20%) and Metro Manila (16%), followed by the Visayas (10%) and Mindanao (2%)
  • Across socioeconomic classes, Duterte led all segments, topping Class E with 59% support, while Robredo's strongest showing was among Class ABC (34%) and Tulfo's support was relatively consistent across income groups at 10-15%
  • For the senatorial preferences survey, the results found that the five strongest contenders are all incumbent senators—Robin Padilla (Rank 1–2), Tito Sotto (Rank 1–3), Raffy Tulfo (Rank 2–5), Alan Peter Cayetano (Rank 3–5), and Loren Legarda (Rank 3–5)
  • Other incumbent senators that are within the top 12 are DepEd Secretary Sonny Angara (Rank 8–9), Senator Mark Villar (Rank 8–9), Senator Jinggoy Estrada (Rank 10–17), and Senator Chiz Escudero (Rank 11–19) all remained within or around the projected winning circle
  • Several candidates remained within striking distance of the Top 12, led by Philip Salvador (Rank 13–25), Jimmy Bondoc (Rank 13–25), and Senator JV Ejercito (Rank 14–26)

 

 

 

POWER/UTILITIES
Meralco eyes Albay expansion
  • Meralco said that local officials in Albay is seeking its assistance in addressing power reliability issues in the province
    • According the Meralco chief external and government affairs officer Arnel Casanova
  • The Albay Electric Cooperative (Aleco) currently serves as the province’s power distributor
  • Meralco has been eyeing joint venture opportunities with electric cooperatives across the country (e.g. Batangas and South Cotabato)
    • It plans to enter as an investor with an effective control of the board, while the electric cooperative will continue to manage day-to-day operations
  • Meralco holds distribution franchises across Luzon, serving areas that represent ~70% of the Luzon grid’s power demand
    • Note that Albay province is part of Region V
    • Power demand in Region 5 stood at 2k GWH in 2025 vs. MER’s franchise area demand of 55k GWH
  • Meralco’s distribution arm accounted for 58% of 2025 profits
