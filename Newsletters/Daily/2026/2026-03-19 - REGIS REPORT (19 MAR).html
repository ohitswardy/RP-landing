REGIS REPORT

19 March 2026

Vehicle sales for CAMPI members dropped 8.5% YoY in the month of Feb. Unit sales for Toyota declined 3%. While some of the weakness is attributable to a market share shift to non-CAMPI brands (e.g. BYD), the overall market still appears soft. Even if BYD were to be included, we believe vehicle sales would still be marginally lower YoY.

 

+++

 

The mammoth Terra Solar project under SP New Energy (SPNEC PM) is starting to deliver some power to Meralco (MER PM), albeit less so far than what was promised. SPNEC is also controlled by Meralco.

 

Terra Solar began sending 85MW of electricity, which should ramp up gradually over the next few months. Terra Solar is delivering less than the 650MW of mid-merit power it committed to provide by last month (Feb 2026). However, it is not obligated to compensate MER for the shortfall until August.

 

+++

 

President Marcos suspended the average 19% fare hike for public utility vehicles only hours before it was supposed to take effect (today March 19).  The action postpones the impact of the fare hike and the second-order effects from it. However, we view it as an action that only delays the inevitable -- the government won’t be able to avoid significant fare increases without massive fuel subsidies (which it can ill afford).

 

+++

 

Our estimate of aggregate foreign ownership of listed Philippine companies is up 83bp to 20.5% for the month of Feb. While there was some net foreign buying in the market, around half of the increase was in fact due to a drop in the nominal market cap of one firm – Villar Land (VLC PM). More below.

CONGLOS
Vehicle sales (ex-BYD) drop 8.5% YoY in February; Toyota unit sales drop 3%
  • CAMPI data showed car sales fell 8.5% YoY to 35,842 units in February (+6.4% MoM)
    • Note that BYD is not a member of CAMPI; BYD’s market share in 2025 stood at 5% (here)
    • Brings 2M26 unit sales down 9.4% YoY to 69,538 units
  • Sales of electric vehicles (ex-BYD) surged 71% YoY to 3,098 units, accounting for 9% of car sales
    • Brings 2M26 EV sales up 67% YoY to 5,708 units, accounting for 8% of YTD sales
    • Hybrids accounted for 80% of sales while PHEVs and BEVs each accounted for 10%
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) sold 17,954 units, down 3% YoY but up 10% MoM
    • Brings TMP’s 2M26 unit sales down 6.3% YoY to 34,300 units, trending below our FY26E forecast of 247k units (+7%)
    • Every 5% reduction in our sales volume forecast will cut TMP’s profit estimate by 2% and GTCAP’s profit by <1%
    • TMP is targeting unit sales to grow “up to 5%” (~229k-240k units) this year
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 23% of FY26E profit
  • GTCAP trades at a 50% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,119/sh and at 4x PER

 

 

 

POWER
Terra Solar delivers 85MW of power to Meralco, falling short of commitment
  • Meralco PowerGen Corp. (MGEN) announced that its affiliate, Terra Solar Philippines, delivered its first 85MW of mid-merit power to Meralco
    • The project will continue to ramp up output in the coming months in coordination with the National Grid Corp of the Philippines (NGCP)
    • Under its contract with Meralco, Terra Solar has committed to provide 650MW by Feb 2026; However, replacement power obligations only starts in August
  • At full build-out, Terra Solar will have a capacity of 3,500MWdc
    • We estimate the project’s standalone annual profits could reach P15bn once completed
    • Meralco effectively owns 37% of Terra Solar, through its 61% ownership of SPNEC PM (SPNEC owns 60% of Terra Solar)
MACRO
Marcos suspends PUV fare hike
  • President Ferdinand Marcos suspended the fare increase for public utility vehicles (PUVs) which was supposed to be implemented today (Mar 19)
    • Marcos stated that "this may not be the right time to increase fares for our fellow citizens"
    • The suspended hike was supposed to increase fares by 19% on average across all modes of land transportation
  • Dept. of Transportation Secretary Giovanni Lopez stated that they are looking to rollout more programs and initiatives to support drivers and commuters, such as expansion of fuel subsidies and free rides
  • Passenger road transport has a weight of 4.5% in the CPI basket
MARKET
Foreign ownership of Philippine market rises in February
  • We estimate that foreign ownership of the overall market rose 83bps MoM to 20.5% in February, as the market cap of some low foreign-owned stocks fell, leading to higher average foreign ownership of the market   
    • The increase was partly due to the P227bn decline in the market cap of Villar Land (VLC PM), which has zero foreign ownership
    • If not for the reduction in Villar Land’s market cap, we estimate that the market’s foreign ownership would only have risen 50bps MoM to 20.2%
  • Meanwhile, we estimate that foreign ownership of the PSE increased 22bps MoM to 23.7%
    • The increase in foreign ownership of the PSE Index was weighed by the index rebalancing
    • RCR, which has foreign ownership of 6.6%, replaced AGI, which has foreign ownership of 16.8%
  • Net foreign buying declined to US$143mn, from US$226mn in January
  • Among the 29 PSEi constituents that remained, 12 saw increases in foreign ownership while 16 experienced declines
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were:
    • PLUS – Foreign ownership declined 1.09 percentage points MoM to 21.2%.
    • CNPF – foreign ownership fell 32bps MoM to 22.7%.   
    • MBT – foreign ownership climbed 47bps MoM to 20.4%, as net foreign buying hit US$24.4mn, the third highest for the month
    • ICT – foreign ownership rose 37bps MoM to 36.4%, as it saw the highest net foreign buying for the month (US$65.4mn)
    • AC – foreign ownership increased 36bps MoM to 26.9%, as net foreign buying hit US$27.5mn, the second highest for the month

 

 

GAMING
Key takeaways from DigiPlus briefing
  • Here are our key takeaways from the DigiPlus Interactive briefing:
  • 2M26 revenue is similar to 4Q25 performance
    • In the first 2 months of the year, revenue was about 30% lower than the pre-delinking period
  • Management expects margins to expand in FY26 (after declining in FY25)
    • A&P will remain high in 1H26 as the company spends to recover customers that it lost in 2H25. However, this should decline (as a percentage of revenue) beginning 2H26
    • The company is achieving operational efficiencies thanks to AI, which also allows the company to customize the user experience 
  • DigiPlus President Andy Tsui expects the industry to grow in the low double-digit levels in FY26
    • There are coordinated efforts by PAGCOR, BSP, e-wallets, and licensed operators against illegal online gambling
  • PLUS is trading at 6.2X FY26E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
MACRO
BSP releases statement on Middle East conflict, peso intervention
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) stated that it is closely monitoring the impact of the Middle East conflict on inflation and the economy (e.g., current account, remittances, trade, financial markets) ahead of the monetary policy meeting on Apr 23
  • The BSP also emphasized that it is intervening in the foreign exchange market to "smooth excess volatility" and temper significant swings that could impact inflation rather than defending a specific level
    • As of Mar 18, the USD-PHP depreciated to P59.74, from P57.55 at end-Feb
  • BSP Deputy Governor Zeno Abenoja, in a separate interview, recently stated that the central bank is evaluating whether the inflation shock will be temporary or persistent
    • Abenoja highlighted that persistent oil prices may prompt hawkish policy action from the Monetary Board (for the first time since Oct 2023)
  • The second out of six Monetary Board meetings for the year will be held on Apr 23
    • For 2026, consensus and Regis expect one 25bp cut to 4.25%
