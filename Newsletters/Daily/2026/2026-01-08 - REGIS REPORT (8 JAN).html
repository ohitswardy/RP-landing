REGIS REPORT

08 January 2026

Revenue collection by the Bureau of Customs (BOC) inched up by 2% to P934bn last year. This is 3% short of the Bureau’s target. The chronic underperformance of revenue collections should have implications on the deficit and national debt numbers.

 

Meanwhile, government debt for Nov reached P17.65trn, which is 0.5% higher MoM.  Government debt to GDP rose to 63.1% as of end-3Q but the government is hopeful this ratio will dip as it pays down some debt before year-end.

 

+++

 

Ayala Corp (AC PM) has received clearance from the competition authorities to sell 40% of its logistics business to AP Moller. This is part of AC’s efforts to cut its losses on its smaller, underperforming businesses. AC has already sold/written off many of these investments.

 

+++

 

ACEN (ACEN PM) revealed plans to invest battery storage capacity with the addition of 1,325Mwh of BESS at its solar farm in Zambales.

 

+++

 

Electricity spot market prices fell below P3.0/kwh for Dec (LWAP P2.98/kwh) in Luzon as power demand for the grid looked notably soft. Recall the news on Meralco’s FY25 volume implies that 4Q volumes for its core franchise area were slightly down YoY.  Surprisingly weak power demand is compounding the impact of new capacity additions, contributing to a larger supply-demand gap.  For FY25, electricity spot prices are down 28% to P3.72/kwh for Luzon.

 

More than half (~60%) of Semirara’s (SCC PM) capacity is uncontracted while we estimate the proportion for ACEN is ~44% for its Philippine business.

MACRO
Customs falls short of 2025 target
  • The Bureau of Customs (BoC) stated that revenue collections rose to P934.4bn (+2% YoY), falling short of the P958.7bn (+5% YoY) FY25 target
  • Challenges during the year were lower import volumes, rice importation suspension, increased illicit trade, and global commodity price volatility
    • Rice tariff collection amounted to P13.7bn, 60% lower than the P34bn in 2024
      • Lower rice import volume (-26% YoY) and a lower rice tariff rate at 15% from 35% initially contributed to the decline in collections
    • Confiscated smuggled goods from illicit trade amounted to P62bn in 2025 which is 27% lower than the P85bn seized in 2024
  • For 2026/27/28, the bureau targets revenues of P1.01trn (+6%), P1.07trn (+6%), and P1.14trn (+6%), respectively

 

MACRO
Government debt inches up to P17.65trn in end-Nov
  • The government's total debt stock went up by 0.5% MoM to P17.65trn as of end-Nov
    • The increase was driven the net issuance of domestic and external debt
  • The current debt mix of the country's total obligations has domestic debt accounting for 68.7% and 31.3% being external borrowings
    • Domestic debt increased by 0.6% MoM to P12.12trn
    • External debt rose by 0.3% MoM to P5.53trn
  • The P17.65trn figure exceeds the P17.36-trn end-2025 projection by 1.7%
    • Despite this, the Bureau of Treasury previously stated that the debt stock is seen to ease by year-end with the gov't set to repay P814bn in domestic bonds by Dec and as fundraising activities wind down
  • The gov't debt-to-GDP ratio as of 3Q25 stood at 63.1% of GDP unchanged vs 2Q25 but up from 60.7% in 4Q24
  • The gov't targets the debt-to-GDP ratio to decline to 61.3% by end-2025
    • The ratio is expected to increase in 2026 to 61.8%, then decrease to 61.3% in 2027 and 60.3% by 2028

Philippine Competition Commission clears AP Moller’s 40% stake in AC Logistics
  • The Philippine Competition Commission (PCC) approved AP Moller’s purchase of a 40% stake in AC Logistics
  • AC Logistics, a unit of Ayala Corp. (AC PM), has been incurring losses

POWER
ACEN to increase capacity of solar project in Zambales
  • In a filing with the Department of Environment and Natural Resources, ACEN will add a 1,325MWh battery energy storage system (BESS) to its existing solar plant in Zambales
    • As a result, the plant’s capacity could increase to to 420MWp from 346MWp upon completion
  • Construction of the additional solar capacity, along with the BESS component, is slated to start in the first quarter, with completion and operations targeted for early 2027
POWER
Luzon spot prices remain soft in December
  • Spot prices (load weighted average price or LWAP) in the Luzon grid showed continued weakness in December, falling by 15% MoM, 9% YoY to P2.98/kwh
    • Brings 4Q25 LWAP down 8% YoY (+2% QoQ) to P3.49/kwh
    • For FY25, LWAP is lower by 28% YoY to P3.72/kwh
  • The following gencos are net sellers to the spot market:
    • Semirara (SCC PM) – 60% of capacity is un-contracted
    • ACEN Corp. (ACEN PM) – 44% of capacity in PH is un-contracted
    • Aboitiz Power (AP PM) – 10% of capacity is un-contracted

 

POWER
15 firms vie for Meralco’s 200MW power contract
  • Meralco said that 15 generation companies are interested to participate in the Competitive Selection Process (CSP) for 200MW baseload contract
    • The four-year PSA resulting from this auction will help Meralco meet its obligations under the Renewable Portfolio Standards
  • A pre-bid conference is set on Jan 15, while the deadline for submission is scheduled on Feb 16
MACRO
Joint congressional oversight body to be created to monitor budget use
  • Senate Finance Committee chairperson Sen. Sherwin Gatchalian stated that a Joint Congressional Oversight Committee on Public Expenditures will be created to monitor budget utilization and project implementation, especially infra projects
    • Gatchalian stated that the creation of the oversight body is already mandated under the General Appropriations Act (GAA) but was not implemented in previous years
  • The committee is to focus on key issues such as the underutilization of agency budgets as well as the implementation of infra and other key gov't projects
    • The oversight body will also scrutinize projects included in the National Expenditure Program (NEP) in order to prevent questionable project insertions
  • The oversight committee will be co-chaired by the chairpersons of the Senate Committee on Finance and the House Committee on Appropriations
