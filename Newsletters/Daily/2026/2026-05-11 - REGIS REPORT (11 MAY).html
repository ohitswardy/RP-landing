Here’s our results round-up for today:

 

  • Puregold (PGOLD PM) (+) – Sales growth of 12% (to P59b) and profit growth of 24% (P3.3bn) are definitely robust numbers. Among the notable highlights from the earnings call: (1) 1Q margins were boosted by supplier support, which may not persist to 2Q, (2) SSSG slowed down to “low single digit” in April for Puregold. It’s retailers like PGOLD who’ll see the first sign of broad consumption slowdown so investors will need to watch this space.

  • DMCI (DMC PM) (+) – DMCI’s 1Q26 profit fell 2% to P4.9bn, driven largely by the dilution from Maynilad’s IPO. Despite the profit decline, the results are trending ahead of Street estimates.  Operations for its smaller units (nickel, off-grid, cement) posted better sales volumes while higher RFO sales and lower cancellations boosted the property unit’s profits, as well as led to lower inventory levels.  More below. 

 

  • Manila Water (MWC PM) (-) – Manila Water reported its recurring profit declined 1% YoY to P3.54bn, weighed down by higher financing costs from acquisition loans, which offset the 12% tariff hike at its core East Zone (EZ) unit. The results are trending below consensus’ full-year P15bn profit estimate.  More importantly, cash spending at the East Zone dropped 25% YoY to P4.4bn, taking cumulative spending to P69bn, trailing its P175bn 2023-2027 regulatory commitment.

CONGLOS
DMCI 1Q26: Nascent operational improvements eclipsed by Maynilad IPO dilution
  • 1Q26 profit dips to P4.9bn – Ahead of Street estimates.  DMCI reported its net income fell by 2% YoY to P4.9bn in 1Q26. While the dilution impact from Maynilad’s (MYNLD PM, here) IPO, as well as higher plant outages at Semirara (SCC PM, here), weighed, the smaller units posted encouraging operational improvements, driven by higher commodity prices and output.

 

  • Property RFO inventory drops. The property segment contributed profits of P1.3bn, up 3% YoY, as revenues improved by 5% on lower cancellations and higher qualified accounts. Reservation sales of P6.3bn was a 13% improvement relative to the trailing four-quarter average, but remains below pre-Covid levels. Given strong RFO sales, RFO inventory dropped 14% QoQ to P21bn in end-March, aiding in further leverage decline.

 

  • Nickel: Pre-development costs at new mine drag. Contributions from nickel were 8% YoY higher due to a lower tax rate. Even though revenues rose 19%, EBITDA was flat at P714mn, as pre-development costs from its Long Point mine weighed. Sales volumes were up 24% YoY to 784k MT, but management expressed confidence about hitting its 3mn MT (+50%) sales volume target with operations at Long Point now in full swing.  

 

  • Cement sales volumes up 22% QoQ/33% YoY; Off-grid power income up 56%.  Its cement unit, Concreat (CHP PM), posted EBITDA of P220mn, driven by market share gains that lifted sales volumes by 22% QoQ and 33% YoY, alongside continued declines in cash costs. The off-grid power unit saw profits improve 56% YoY, underpinned by capacity additions and market share gains in Palawan. Profits from construction were lower by 6% on slower completions across client segments.

 

  • Declares P0.30/sh regular dividend. After dividend inflows from units outside SCC, DMCI declared a regular dividend of P0.30/sh. Management likewise committed to declare a special dividend, as soon as the coal contract bid is completed and SCC can declare dividends.

 

 

TRANSPORT
Throughput volumes at ICTSI’s Mexico port jump 21% YoY in April; 4M26 volumes up +11%
  • Data from Mexico’s port authority showed that volumes at ICTSI’s port, Contecon Manzanillo (CMSA), grew throughput volumes by 21% YoY to 147k TEUs in April
    • Growth accelerated from the +3% pace in March 2026 and +14% in April 2025
  • This brings CMSA’s 4M26 volumes up 11% to 565k TEUs
    • We anticipate CMSA’s volumes will rise by 8% to 1.66mn TEUs this year
    • To reach our forecast, volumes for the rest of the year would have to average 137k TEUs per month
    • Every 5% increase in CMSA’s volumes will raise our EBITDA forecast by 1% and profit forecast by 2%
    • Note that CMSA is expanding capacity from 1.7mn TEUs to 2mn TEUs (~167k TEUs per month) by 3Q26
  • We estimate CMSA accounts for 10% of group-wide volumes, 20% of revenues, and 23% of EBITDA
  • ICTSI trades at 12x EV/EBITDA and 21x PER vs. peer average (11x EBITDA / 16x PER) vs. historical average (11x / 19x)

 

 

MACRO
PH dollar reserves at $104.1bn end-Apr
  • The Philippines' gross international reserves (GIR) at end-Apr declined 2.4% MoM to US$104.1bn from US$106.6bn the previous month
    • The April GIR figure is a 15-month low
    • The MoM drop in the GIR was primarily due to central bank's foreign exchange operations
      • The USD-PHP rate depreciated to P60.3 in end-Apr from P59.4 in end-Mar
    • The level of dollar reserves was enough to cover about 3.8x the country's short-term external debt and equivalent to 6.9x months' worth of imports of goods and payments
  • For 2026, the BSP expects the GIR to settle at US$110bn
