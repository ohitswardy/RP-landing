11 September 2026

ICTSI (ICT PM) continues to gain market share at its port in Mexico, Contecon Manzanillo (CMSA), which saw throughput volumes rise by 21% YoY in August while combined volumes at other ports in Manzanillo fell 3%. We have highlighted in our latest TP upgrade (see report linked below) that the strong growth has been driven by CMSA’s ~20% capacity expansion. CMSA is the only port in Manzanillo with available land for further expansions, allowing it to capture the market’s growing demand as additional capacity comes online.

The change in control of the Lopez family’s unlisted holding company, Lopez Inc., appears to be underway, with San Miguel (SMC PM) chairman Ramon Ang reportedly close to increasing his stake to 71% from the initial 25.7% stake acquired last month.  This would give Mr. Ang a controlling stake in Lopez Holdings (LPZ PM) and could trigger a mandatory tender offer.

 

Recall that previous reports indicated a P45bn purchase price for 100% of Lopez Inc., implying an LPZ valuation of P83bn, >3x LPZ’s current market capitalization of P25bn. 

 

Lopez Inc and LPZ holds controlling stakes for many of the Lopez group’s listed companies, First Philippine Holdings (FPH PM), ABS-CBN (ABS PM), Rockwell Land (ROCK PM), and First Gen (FGEN PM). 

 

The remaining Lopez branch, led by First Gen chairman and CEO Federico "Piki" Lopez, still holds roughly 29% of Lopez Inc.

TRANSPORT
ICTSI’s Mexico port volumes rise 21% YoY in August
  • Data from Mexico’s port authority showed that ICTSI’s port, CMSA, grew throughput volumes by 21% YoY to 164k TEUs in August
    • Note that combined throughput at other ports in Manzanillo, Mexico, declined 3% YoY in August
    • CMSA continues to gain market share following its ~20% capacity expansion this year
    • CMSA is now the only terminal in Manzanillo capable of handling ultra-large container vessels (up to 24k TEUs)
  • Brings CMSA’s 8M26 throughput to 1.19mn TEUs, up 20% YoY and tracking mildly ahead of our full-year forecast of 1.8mn TEUs (+18% YoY)
  • We estimate CMSA accounts for 11% of group FY26E volumes, 23% of revenues, and 27% of EBITDA
  • ICTSI trades at 12.7x FY27E EV/EBITDA, above peers (12x) and historical mean (11x)

 

 

MINING
Dominion Holdings to base Tampakan project injection exchange ratio on book value
  • Dominion Holdings (DHI PM) announced that it will fix the exchange ratio for the planned merger with Indophil and Sonar at “book-to-book value”
    • Book values of DHI, Indophil, and Sonar will be based on their audited end-Aug 2026 financial statements
    • The number of shares to be issued to the shareholders of Indophil and Sonar will be disclosed in due course
  • Note that Indophil’s book value per share (BVPS) stood at P21 as of end-2025, ~13x larger than DHI’s end-June 2026 BVPS of P1.6
    • At end-June 2026, DHI’s book value stood at P3.5bn
    • At end-Dec 2025, Indophil’s book value stood at P23.6bn
  • Given that DHI was listed in the PSE in 1997, it will only be required to maintain a minimum public float of 10% following the backdoor listing (see PSE rule here)
    • DHi is currrently owned by the Sy family (70%), the Consunji family (9%), and the public (21%)
    • Meanwhile, the Sy family owns 88% of Indophil and 100% of Sonar while the Consunji family and Alcantara family own 10% and 2%, respectively, of Indophil
    • For DHI to maintain a 10% public float, we estimate the exchange ratio must not exceed 1.5x DHI shares for every Indophil share
  • The merger is contingent on approval of stockholders (DHI’s stockholder’s meeting is on Sep 14), Philippine Competition Commission, and SEC
    • Effective date of the merger will be the first calendar day of the month immediately following the date of receipt of SEC’s issued Certificate of Merger
    • DHI previously announced that it plans to set the exchange ratio prior to its annual shareholder’s meeting on September 14
  • Indophil and Sonar hold 56% and 44% voting interests, respectively, in Sagittarius Mines, Inc. (SMI), which holds the FTAA for the largest undeveloped Tampakan copper-gold project
    • Based on DHI’s disclosures, Indophil and Sonar hold 40% and 31.6% economic interests, respectively, in SMI, with the remaining 28.4% held by Tampakan Mining Corp. and Southcot Mining Corp.

MACRO
Philippines to bear brunt of El Niño next year—DA
  • Dept. of Agriculture (DA) spokesperson Arnel de Mesa stated that the brunt of the incoming drought from the upcoming El Niño may likely be felt during the dry season harvest in 1H27
  • He added that the DA sees minor impact from El Niño this year on overall rice supply
    • The DA's estimated impact of El Niño on palay (unmilled rice) output this year was a 700,000 MMT (~440,000 MMT of rice) decline but the recent torrential rains cushioned the impact to a 400,000 MMT (~250,000 MMT) decline instead
  • The country's palay (unmilled rice) output is projected to be 19 MMT (-3.5% YoY) for FY26,
    • Using the PSA's official milling recovery rate of 63%, the estimated palay output equates to approximately 12 MMT of rice
      • The country's annual rice demand amounts to ~15-16 MMT
    • Palay output as of 1H26 amounted to 9.1 MMT (-0.6% YoY)
  • According to the state weather bureau PAGASA, a moderate to strong El Niño is currently present in the Tropical Pacific, which is slated to last till 1H27 and may develop to a "very strong" state before year-end
    • The impact of the expected drought next year will depend on the severity and the length of the El Niño
  • Total agricultural damage during El Niño in 2024 reached P15bn, with a 3% decline in the country's agriculture and fisheries output
