The Philippine SEC is reducing the minimum public float for future IPO to 15% for companies with a market cap of at least P50bn (~US$870m). The public float threshold could go to as low as 12% for those even larger (>P200bn market cap).

 

+++

 

Collections by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) for the month of January are likely to be barely up (<1%) YoY, according to Commissioner Charlito Mendoza.

 

+++

Mining firm Philex (PX PM) reported profits of P1.08bn for FY25, up 44%. Given (1) where gold prices are today and (2) the imminent operation of its long-delayed Silangan project, profits this year are likely to be up very sharply.

 

+++

 

Data from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) shows that Trust Assets of banks and non-banks jumped 24.6% YoY to P9,183bn ($160bn) as of end-2025 (Fig 1). However, the numbers were a bit… odd. The AUMs of Bank trusts surged 28% YoY – and even more oddly, up 17.0% QoQ – to P5,696bn (Fig 2). The QoQ figure was unusual in that none of the major banks that have reported showed QoQ growth anywhere close to 17% QoQ – Metrobank (MBT PM) being the closest at +13% QoQ and 64% YoY (Fig 4).

 

After digging into the aggregated Bank AUM data, we noticed that the “Other Fiduciary Services” account shot up 80% QoQ to P1,424bn, from just P792bn the previous quarter (Fig 3). At their FY25 earnings call, Metrobank management explained that the jump in their AUM was driven largely by them opening custodian services for their clients, which used to be lodged in other (presumably non-Trust) entities. We suspect that a similar thing happened with other banks (either the government banks, or foreign banks).

 

Without that anomaly, Bank AUMs would have been up a still-decent 13.8% YoY and 4.0% QoQ, with Non-Bank AUMs up 19.4% YoY and 4.7% QoQ. Total AUMs would have been up 16.0% YoY and 4.3% QoQ. This would still be a decent acceleration from 3Q25’s growth rate of 13.7% YoY and 2% QoQ.

 

BDO (BDO PM) continues to be by far the market leader, with P2,588bn ($45bn) in AUMs, up 12.2% YoY, with a market share of 28%. BPI (BPI PM) is comfortably in second place, at P1,830bn, with a 20% share. That said, BPI grew AUMs 19.5% YoY and is (slowly) closing the gap. Metrobank’s (MBT PM) numbers jumped 64% YoY to P844bn, due in large part to the aforementioned offering of custodian services. Other notable moves came from Philippine National Bank (PNB PM), up 18.7% to P226bn, and from Security Bank (SECB PM), up 14.9% YoY to P179bn.

 

The Non-Bank Trusts have yet to report their individual numbers (or at least the BSP has not yet put it up on their website).

MARKET
SEC reduces public float requirement for large IPOs
  • The SEC reduced the minimum public float for the IPO of firms with a market cap of at least P50bn
    • From 20% to 15%
  • “Exceptionally large” issuers with market capitalizations of at least P200bn may request for a lower float from the PSE
    • However, the public float cannot go below 12%
  • Other tiers were retained:
    • 33% minimum public float for market cap below P500mn
    • 25% minimum float for market cap between P500mn and P1bn
    • 20% minimum float for market cap between P1bn and P50bn
MACRO
BIR collections flat in January
  • Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) Commissioner Charlito Mendoza guided that preliminary collections for January amounted to P358bn, +0.8% YoY
    • The January figure is around 10% of the agency's P3.58trn (+15% YoY) revenue goal for 2026
    • In 2025, the BIR fell short of its initial P3.22trn (+13% YoY) target as FY revenue amounted to P3.11trn (+9% YoY)
  • Mendoza expects revenue collections to be driven by the rebound in GDP growth to 5-6% this year from 4.4% in 2025 as well as from the acceleration of gov't infra projects
    • Recall that Public Works Secretary Vince Dizon previously guided for infra spending to rebound to ~P250bn in 1Q26
      • Derived infra spending for the month of Nov 2025 was P69bn down 22% MoM and 35% YoY
MINING
Strong metal prices lift Philex Mining’s FY25 core profit up 44%; Silangan project on track for end-1Q26 start
  • In a press release, Philex Mining (PX PM) disclosed FY25 core profits of P1.08bn, up 44% YoY and beating consensus forecast of P800mn
    • Profits were boosted by higher metal prices, with gold and copper output lower by 21% YoY to 24k ounces and 8% YoY to 18mn lbs, respectively, due to ageing of Padcal mine
  • The company doubled its cash dividend to P0.04/sh, which will be paid on March 25 (ex-div March 9)
  • Management expects to complete the repair of Padcal’s mill plant within the year, with throughput expected to steadily improve by 2Q26
  • Its greenfield Silangan project remains on schedule to begin commercial operations by the end of Q1 2026, with an estimated mill production of 2k MT per day
    • Based on the project’s technical report, Silangan will produce ~15mn lbs/year of copper; 47k ounces/year of gold; and 58k ounces of silver on the first five years of operations
    • PX trades at 15x consensus’ FY26E PER (FY26E profit of P5.8bn) vs global peer average of 20x and 14x for Philippine gold/copper miners
