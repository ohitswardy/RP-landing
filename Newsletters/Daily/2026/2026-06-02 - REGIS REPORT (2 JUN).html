The BPO industry association (IBPAP) says it is reviewing its projections for the industry due to the rapid change brought about by advances in artificial intelligence (AI).

 

The most recent projections from IBPAP forecast annual growth of 7% in revenue and 3% in headcount up to 2028 under its base case scenario. Its “bear case” scenario forecasts a 1-2% annual decline in both.

 

The prevailing conventional wisdom would say that both these scenarios are too optimistic. Based on near-term expectations, however, AI does not seem to be having a significant impact (yet) on BPO employment in the Philippines.  It would be interesting to see, from the ground, how the industry and its outlook are being affected by global trends like AI.

In reaction to the war in Iran, the government is considering the creation of a strategic petroleum reserve program through the government-controlled Philippine National Oil Co (PNOC). The war and its effect on energy supplies illustrated how vulnerable the country is to supply disruptions. This program is too late for the current crisis, but maybe it will be there in time for the next one (if and when that happens).

PROPERTY
IT-BPO Association to review industry targets
  • The IT-BPO Association of the Philippines said that it will update its projections for its industry roadmap due to the rapid pace of change brought about by artificial intelligence
  • The IT-BPO Association of the Philippines (IBPAP) currently expects the industry to achieve revenue of US$42bn and employ 1.97mn Filipinos by the end of 2026
    • These represent 4%-5% growth from the 2025 revenue (US$40bn) and employee (1.9mn) figures
  • The bull case 2028 targets are – US$59bn in revenue and 2.5mn employees
    • These represent 14% revenue CAGR and 10% employment CAGR from the 2025 levels
  • The base case 2028 targets are – US$48.5bn in revenue and 2.1mn employees
    • These represent 7% revenue CAGR and 3% employment CAGR from the 2025 levels
  • The bear case 2028 targets are – US$38.5bn in revenue and 1.8mn employees
    • These represent a compounded annual decline of 1%-2% from the 2025 levels
  • BPO firms occupy 70%-80% of the office space of the developers we cover
MACRO
Philippines targets to build strategic petroleum reserves
  • Dept. of Energy (DOE) Secretary Sharon Garin stated that the gov't is working on establishing a strategic petroleum reserve program, which involves building new stockpiling facilities that are "sufficient enough to weather any effects or disruptions" in global oil production
  • Garin elaborated that the DOE is working with state-run firms Philippine National Oil Co. (PNOC) and Maharlika Investment Corp. (MIC) to evaluate options to build at least 30 days' worth of additional reserves
    • PNOC will lead the effort to secure reserves
    • MIC, the country's sovereign wealth fund, will manage funding for the program
  • She also stated that Japan during the President's recent state visit affirmed to support the program through conducting feasibility studies and providing capacity building
  • According to the DOE, the country's current fuel supply as of May 29 is equivalent to 46 days of inventory
