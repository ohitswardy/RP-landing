REGIS REPORT

01 June 2026

The trade deficit widened 50% to US$6bn for the month of April, due principally to higher oil import costs (which doubled). YT-April, the trade deficit has now reached US$19.3bn.  A wider trade deficit will put pressure on PHP/USD and oil is the single biggest factor in the trade deficit swings.

 

+++

 

ACEN (ACEN PM) has secured a project in Australia that comes with revenue support (and revenue caps) from the government. The project, with a capacity of 600MW +2,400MWh BESS, will be operational in 2030, if all goes according to plan. This project is not yet part of ACEN’s publicized project pipeline of ~7,000MW.

 

+++

 

Both the online gaming segment as well as the (land-based) licensed casinos saw gaming revenue largely flat QoQ, and down YoY. The e-games segment continues to suffer from the BSP directive delinking e-wallets from online gambling platforms, while the licensed casinos continue to hurt from the POGO ban, lower tourist arrivals from China and Korea, as well as the flood control corruption scandal. 

 

Looking ahead, we anticipate more intense competition among land-based casinos after the opening of a new integrated resort in 3Q26. On the other hand, PAGCOR is preparing a position paper to persuade the BSP to reverse its order prohibiting e-wallets from redirecting to online gambling platforms.

MREIT (MREIT PM) will acquire 300,000sqm of malls, offices, and hotels from its sponsor, Megaworld (MEG PM), as well Travellers International, the gaming arm of Alliance Global (AGI PM). The acquisition would expand its floor area by 47% to c. 950,000sqm. While pricing terms have not been set, MREIT will issue up to 1.8bn new shares, which would increase its shares outstanding by 38%.

With the trading data for the month of May in, we refine our prediction(s) for the upcoming PSEi (“PCOMP”) rebalancing. Maynilad (MYNLD PM) remains a virtual shoo-in for the PSEi in Aug.What remains less certain is which stock will be removed a result of MYNLD’s entry. Given the latest prices (and the average of the last eleven months), we think Converge (CNVRG PM) is the more likely casualty but the exit of Digiplus (PLUS PM) remains a possibility.

MACRO
BSP expects May inflation to settle within 7.1-7.9%
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) in its month-ahead inflation forecast expects May inflation to fall within 7.1-7.9%, likely accelerating further from the 7.2% print in April
    • BSP expectations for May inflation are well above the BSP's 2-4% long-term target range
      • For 2026, BSP expectations for inflation this year is 6.3% (previously revised from 5.1%)
    • May inflation data will be released by the PSA (Philippine Statistics Authority) on Jun 5 (Friday)
  • Upward price pressures stem from higher prices of rice, vegetables, and meat, as well as the depreciation of the peso
    • These may have been partially offset by lower prices of domestic petroleum, fish, and electricity
    • As of end-May, the USD-PHP depreciated to P61.4, from P60.3 at end-Apr
  • Inflation has averaged 3.9% so far as of 4M26
    • For 2026, consensus expectations are at 6.3%
  • The next Monetary Board meeting is on Jun 18 (third out of six scheduled this year)
    • For 2026, consensus expects the BSP to conduct two more 25bp rate hikes to 5.00%

 

 

 

 

MACRO
Trade deficit widens 50% YoY to $6.0bn in April
  • The April trade deficit widened to US$6.0bn (+50% YoY) as imports picked up while exports growth slowed—this is the highest deficit recorded since Aug 2022
    • This brings the 4M26 trade deficit to US$19.3bn, 17% wider YoY
  • Imports for the month climbed 22% YoY to US$13.2bn, accelerating from the 12% increase in Mar
    • The growth in imports was primarily led by the 106% surge in fuel imports as oil prices soared which offset the 17% decline in consumer goods
  • Exports growth decelerated to 6% YoY (US$7.2bn) from 20% YoY the previous month
    • This marks the sixteenth consecutive month of YoY growth in exports
    • Growth in electronics exports significantly slowed to 1% YoY to US$3.4bn, moderating from the 33% YoY growth in Mar
    • Meanwhile, non-electronics exports were up 11% to US$3.8bn, picking up from 6% the previous month

 

 

 

 

 

POWER
ACEN gets state-backed revenue support for new 600MW + BESS solar project in Australia
  • ACEN Corp.’s 600MW Birriwa solar + 2,400 MWH battery storage (BESS) project in New South Wales has secured a long-term government-backed revenue support agreement
    • Under the agreement, revenue below a set floor is covered by the Australian government, while earnings above a ceiling are shared with the government
    • The project was among 19 renewable energy projects selected under the latest Capacity Investment Scheme round that are expected to deliver 7.8GW of capacity by 2030
  • This project is not included in our forecasts and valuation; It is also not part of ACEN’s 7,083MW RE portfolio of operating and prospective projects as of May 12
    • We estimate the Birriwa project could cost ~US$650mn
  • We estimate projects in Australia account for 23% of ACEN’s att. RE operating capacity and 11% of FY26E core att. EBITDA
PROPERTY
MREIT to acquire assets from related parties
  • MREIT signed an MOU to acquire 303,000sqm of mall, office, and hotel assets from Megaworld and Travellers International
    • 160,000sqm of malls
    • 117,000sqm of offices
    • 26,500sqm – Holiday Inn Express Manila in Newport
  • The acquisition will increase its portfolio by 47% to 950,000sqm
  • MREIT will issue up to 1.8bn new MREIT shares
    • This would expand shares outstanding by up to 38%
  • The assets have a combined occupancy rate of 92%
    • MREIT’s current occupancy is 90%
  • The assets to be acquired have a combined WALE of 5.8 years
    • MREIT’s current WALE is 3.1 years
  • MREIT said that the acquisition will be DPS accretive
  • We estimate that the AGI Group would need to sell c. 320mn MREIT shares in order for the latter to meet minimum public ownership requirements
  • MREIT currently offers 7.6% dividend yield
    • The 10-year government bond yield is 7.5%
  • MEG currently offers 4.4% dividend yield and is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY26E P/B, and 3.2X FY26E P/E
GAMING
DigiPlus Interactive appoints new President
  • DigiPlus Interactive appointed Mr. Ping Chen as President of the company
  • Prior to his appointment, Mr. Chen served as Chief Financial Officer, Chief Investment Officer, and Executive Committee member of Digital China Group, one of Asia’s largest publicly listed technology and services conglomerates
  • Before that, he spent two decades in global investment banking in Hong Kong and London, as Senior Managing Director at Moelis & Company, and in Bank of America Merrill Lynch, Citi, and Morgan Stanley
  • Mr. Chen holds a Master of Philosophy in Financial Engineering from the University of Cambridge and a Master of Engineering in Electrical and Electronic Engineering with Management Studies from Imperial College London, where he graduated at the top of the 1st Class
  • He replaces Mr. Andy Tsui, who has been President of DigiPlus for four years
  • Mr. Tsui said that the company has implemented measures to improve profit margins, so the 2Q26 performance should be better than 1Q26
    • We estimate that DigiPlus’ 1Q26 recurring profit fell 47% YoY and 9% QoQ to P2.2bn
    • 1Q26 headline profit fell 33% YoY (up 15% QoQ) to P2.8bn, thanks to non-recurring gains
  • PLUS is trading at 3.9X FY26E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
MARKET
MYNLD likely to be added to PSE Index during August 2026 rebalancing
  • Based on our estimates, Maynilad Water Services (MYNLD PM) will likely be added to the PSE Index during the August 2026 rebalancing
  • If MYNLD will enter the PSE Index, then there are a few candidates for removal from the PSE Index
    • We believe the most likely to be removed is Converge Information and Communications Technology Solutions (CNVRG PM)
    • Given its current market cap, we consider DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) to be the next in line for removal 
  • The PSE Index rebalancing in August 2026 will be based on the 12 months from July 2025 to June 2026
  • Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap
    • For companies that have been listed for less than 12 months (such as MYNLD), the stocks must be among the top 20% in terms of median daily trade per month for at least six months or 75% of the total number of months it has been listed, whichever is higher 
  • Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when
    • 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or
    • 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35 
  • Based on our estimates, MYNLD is ranked within the top 25
    • Thus, the lowest ranked PSE Index constituent will be removed
