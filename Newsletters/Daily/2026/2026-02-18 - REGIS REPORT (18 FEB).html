SM Prime (SMPM PM) reported that its 4Q25 net profit fell 1% YoY, as the mall and residential segments moved in opposite directions. 4Q25 mall revenue grew 7% YoY (the same pace as 9M25) while margins expanded. On the other hand, residential revenue and EBIT margin both declined markedly on a QoQ and YoY basis. Residential demand was weak, as evidenced by the 17% YoY (and 44% QoQ) decline in reservation sales. For the full year, net income rose 7% to P48.8bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts.

Speaking of weak residential demand, Ayala Land (ALI PM) said that sales of its high-end Makati project, Laurean, hit P10.4bn. This implies that 4Q25 sales of about P2.5bn or less than 10% of the project. Since Laurean’s launch in June, less than 40% of the project has been taken up.

We published two reports on Semirara (SCC PM), discussing (1) the news on the government’s intention to re-bid the company’s coal operating contract, as well as (2) management’s commentary on the issue during yesterday’s briefing. 

 

The government’s decision to turn down SCC’s request to extend the existing contract and to conduct an auction that will involve other parties raises the level of uncertainty and risk over SCC’s shares. While the Department of Energy has yet to release official tender terms, management tried to assuage concerns by emphasizing its expectation that the bid selection will be driven by technical and operational qualifications, not purely on the financial offer. It also illustrated substantial financial and operating barriers a new entrant will have to overcome to make the investment viable. 

 

Meralco’s (MER PM) Chairman Manuel V. Pangilinan, when asked about a possible interest in the coal mine, said the firm would consider it, though his remarks of a possible mine-mouth power plant investment seemed off-the-cuff. Any new operator for the mine, including possibly Meralco, would need to consider the relatively short residual mine life, possible hurdles on permits as well as other operational issues that only the current operator (SCC) fully comprehends and appreciates.

 

Nevertheless, we believe the downside risk appears to be not fully factored in the current share price, which already fell over 22% last Monday. Without knowing the final terms of the bidding, management confirmed our view that the outlook for special dividends this year could be dim.  We sharply reduced our FY26E profit and dividend estimates, as we wait for more detail on the tender.

Meralco may look into the bidding of Semirara’s coal contract
  • Meralco PowerGen Corp. is open to participating in the government bidding for the coal operating contract of Semirara island
    • According to Meralco Chairman Manuel V. Pangilinan during an ambush interview
  • MER can operate a mine-mouth project, a power station located adjacent to a coal mine that allows the direct transport of fuel from the mine to the plant through a conveyor belt
    • Note that Semirara island is not connected to any of the three main power grids so we reckon any investment of this nature will likely require transmission
CONSUMER
JFC acquires South Korean hot pot brand, Shabu All Day for P5.1bn
  • Jollibee Foods Corp. (JFC PM), through its 70% owned subsidiary Jolli-K Co., Ltd. (Jolli-K), has signed an agreement to acquire South Korean hot pot brand, Shabu All Day through the full acquisition of "All Day Fresh Co., Ltd. for US$87mn (~P5.1bn)
    • According to JFC, the acquisition price implies a 4x EV/EBITDA multiple
      • Haidilao International Holding Ltd. (6862 HK), global Chinese hot pot chain, currently trades at 9.7x FY26E EV/EBITDA
  • It was not stated whether the deal will be done through cash or debt financing
    • As of 9M25, JFC's cash and cash equivalents amounted to P33.7bn
  • Shabu All Day is the number one hot pot chain in South Korea with US$285mn (~P16.5bn) in system-wide sales annually and 169 stores as of Jan 2025
    • JFC highlights Shabu All Day's "highly franchisable platform" given its payback period of 2-3 years as well as high double-digit EBITDA and EBIT margins
  • Upon completion of the transaction, Shabu All Day will be immediately consolidated into JFC's financial statements and the company estimates that it will add +2% to revenues, +8% to Global EBIT, and +1% to store count (+169 stores) in 2026
    • The consolidation impact is based on All Day Fresh's annualized FY25 results vs JFC's inhouse FY26 forecast

POWER/UTILITIES
San Miguel offers best bid for Meralco’s 200MW baseload supply
  • San Miguel Global Power (SMGP) has emerged as the apparent winner in the auction for Meralco’s 200-megawatt (MW) four-year baseload supply
    • SMGP’s Sual Power Inc., which operates a 1,200MW coal-fired plant, submitted the lowest offer price of P4.2955/kwh for 200MW
  • Other bidders were:
    • SMGP’s Mariveles Power Generation Corp. (coal fired) – P4.3805/kwh for 200MW
    • FGEN’s BacMan Geothermal – P4.5187/kwh for 35MW
    • San Manuel Solar – P4.5787/kwh for 46MW
    • ACEN Corp. – P5.2087/kwh for 25MW
    • FGEN Hydro – P5.4906/kwh for 25MW
  • This competitive selection process (CSP) is supposed to help Meralco meet its obligations under the Renewable Portfolio Standards
    • Even companies operating conventional plants were allowed to participate, but they would be required to offer financial guarantees in the form of renewable energy certificates
