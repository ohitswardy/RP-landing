26 August 2026

The vehicle sales figure reported by CAMPI for July showed a slight drop (-2.5%) YoY to 37.3k units for the month of July. For Toyota, unit sales were practically unchanged YoY at almost 18k units. YT-July, volumes are still down 10% for CAMPI members.

 

Note the CAMPI tally does not include sales of BYD. If those are included, overall unit volumes would be up ~5% YoY. We estimate BYD now has close to 7% of the market.

Non-bank fintech firms are asking the Bangko Sentral (BSP) for more “leniency” in the latter’s push for lower (or total elimination) of digital transfer fees. The BSP asked all parties to reduce, if not eliminate, fees on digital transfers (e.g. Instapay). While the BSP has not explicitly mandated that all such transfers be “free” for consumers, it is reportedly unsatisfied with the quantum of the fee reductions implemented by some fintech firms.

CONGLOS
Vehicle sales (ex-BYD) drop 2.5% YoY in July; Toyota’s unit sales flattish
  • CAMPI reported new car sales reached 37,319 units in July, down 2.5% YoY (+0.2% MoM)
    • Brings 7M26 car sales down 10% YoY to 241,872 units
    • Note that the BYD brand is not included in CAMPI’s data; We estimate BYD’s market share stood at ~7% in 1H26
  • We reckon industry-wide car sales (including BYD) likely returned to positive YoY growth of ~+5% in July 2026
    • Recall that the implementation of new excise taxes for trucks under the CMEPA law took effect on July 1, 2025, prompting pull-forward demand in prior months
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) sold 17,979 units in July, slipping by 0.4% YoY but higher by 2% MoM
    • Brings TMP’s 7M26 car sales to 118,888 units, down 8% YoY and tracking below our FY26E of 227k units (-1% YoY) but ahead of TMP’s guidance of 205k units (-9% YoY)
    • To reach Regis’ FY26 sales forecast, sales for the rest of the year will need to average 22k units
    • To reach TMP’s FY26 target, sales for the rest of the year will only need to average 17k units
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 22% of FY26E profit and 23% of NAV
    • GTCAP trades at a 54% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,092/sh and at 3.7x PER

 

 

BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
Fintech firms ask regulators for leniency on transfer fees
  • In a panel discussion during the ASEAN Tech Summit Manila, fintech executives expressed their concerns over policies that seem to favor only the interests of consumers
  • Barbie Dapul, chief operations officer of GCash’s operator G-Xchange Inc., cited as example the BSP Circular No. 1238, which encourages reducing digital transaction fees 
    • She said GCash could only afford to cut transfer fees to P10, as it has to pay for cybersecurity and reliability services to keep the e-wallet running
    • Ms. Dapul said making regulations fairer for e-wallets would improve their viability
    • She also noted that e-wallets like GCash cater primarily to unbanked Filipinos by lowering barriers to financial access
  • The BSP previously clarified that Circular No. 1238, which was implemented on July 4, does not require financial institutions to waive digital transfer fees 
BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
GCash IPO timetable adjusted
  • Mynt (operator of GCash), in its latest prospectus, adjusted its IPO timetable:
    • IPO pricing date was moved to Oct 1 from Sep 28
    • Notice of final offer price is on Oct 2 instead of Sep 30
    • Offer period is now scheduled for Oct 6 to 12 from Oct 5 to 9 previously
    • Listing date is eyed to be on Oct 20 instead of Oct 19
  • Dates are subject to regulatory approval and change due to market conditions
