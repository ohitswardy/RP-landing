Commercial bank loan growth dipped to +9.2% in Dec, breaking a streak of nineteen (19) consecutive months of double-digit YoY growth. Loans to the manufacturing sector contracted 9% while consumer lending growth remained quite brisk (+21%).

 

This deceleration does appear to conform with the government’s report of slower GDP growth in the last quarter.

BANKS
Commercial banks’ loan growth slows to 9.2% in Dec
  • Preliminary data showed that commercial bank loan growth slowed to 9.2% in Dec from 10.3% in Nov, breaking a 19-month streak of double-digit growth and marking the slowest pace since Feb 2024 (8.6%)
    • Loans to productive activities decelerated to 8.0% vs 9.0% in Nov
    • Consumer loans still grew at a robust pace of 21.4%, though slower than Nov's 22.9% rate
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) moderated to 7.0% in Dec from 7.6% in Nov

 

 

 

MACRO
Marcos establishes new economic affairs body
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr., through the signing of Executive Order (EO) No. 108, transferred the functions of the Office of the Special Assistant to the President for Investment and Economic Affairs (OSAPIEA) to the new established Office of Economic Affairs (OEA) in order to streamline the administration's investment and economic initiatives
  • OEA, the new investment and economic policy body, will be headed by Executive Secretary Ralph Recto
  • Recall that the OSAPIEA was previously under Finance Secretary Frederick Go prior to his appointment to the Dept. of Finance (DOF)
