For today’s 1Q results tally, we have SM Investments (SM PM) and Maynilad (MYNLD PM).

  • Maynilad (MYNLD PM) (+) - At yesterday’s briefing, management indicated that it expects the impact of the oil crisis and the potential onset of El Niño to be manageable. While its 1Q26 profit of P4.0bn (+10%) was trending ahead of our forecast (In line with Street), we remain cautious that earnings pressures stemming from the oil crisis could dampen its growth momentum and have trimmed our profit forecast accordingly. We nonetheless maintain our BUY rating and TP of P24/sh, as the value of the concession, as well as the company’s dividend prospects, remain intact.

CONGLOS
SM Investments 1Q earnings rise 7%
  • 1Q26 earnings rise 7% to P21.5bn. SM Investments delivered 1Q26 profits of P21.5bn, 7% higher than a year ago and just a tad below expectations. Earnings from two of its biggest subsidiaries, BDO and SM Prime, were mildly disappointing. The retail operations, meanwhile, delivered respectable profit growth of 13%, in line with expectations.

 

  • Banking and property seeing some pressure. The results for the latest quarter for both the banking and property business showed modest but visible pressure from separate issues. Higher provisions for loan losses capped profit growth  at 2% for BDO despite loan growth of 16%. Meanwhile, 1Q earnings at SM Prime were virtually unchanged YoY. The mall business maintained its steady growth pace but contributions from the residential property side dropped sharply.

 

  • Profits from SM Retail rise 13%. Revenues at SM Retail grew +6% for 1Q26, while profits expanded 13%. Group-wide SSSG was 3.4%, a tad lower than last year’s 4.1%. The largest contributor to the Retail business, Food Retail, reported revenue growth of 6.7% with SSSG of +3.8% and profit growth of 9%. 

 

Management also mentioned that Alfamart ended the quarter with 2,337 stores, implying a net addition of 28 stores in 1Q26 (gross additions of 61). The group intends to add 200-250 stores for this year. SSSG for Alfamart was +3% in the quarter.

 

  • Dividends raised 31%. The company declared a dividend of P17/share for this year, roughly 1/4th of last year’s EPS. This is 31% higher than the dividends paid last year and is equivalent to ~2.7% dividend yield. We see this as the company’s attempt to improve returns and lift the share price given the market investors’ focus on dividends in recent years. Note SM has an active share buyback program and has bought back over P10bn worth of its own shares.
PROPERTY
Colliers: 1Q26 residential net take-up improves thanks to affordable housing
  • Colliers reported that 1Q26 Metro Manila residential vacancy was relatively flat QoQ at 25%
  • Net take-up of Metro Manila condominium units rose c. 60% QoQ (up tenfold) to 2,000 units
  • Most of the sales came from units below P7mn/unit
  • Many of the units sold were below P4mn/unit and were part of the government 4PH program, which offers subsidies and incentives for low-cost housing
    • The developers we cover likely did not benefit from sales of such projects
  • Metro Manila office vacancy also remained flat QoQ at 19%     
    • Makati CBD and BGC vacancy rates also stayed flat at the 9%-10% level
  • Net office take-up fell 22% QoQ and 6% YoY to 73,000sqm
  • Office rental rates in most parts of Metro Manila were generally flat QoQ
    • They inched up modestly in Makati CBD
  • Construction costs have generally increased 10%-15%
    • Some contractors have invoked force majeure clauses
MACRO
1Q26 GDP growth could fall below 3%—DepDev
  • Dept. of Economy, Planning, and Development (DepDev) Secretary Arsenio Balisacan stated that GDP growth in 1Q26 could fall below 3% given the lingering impact from last year's corruption scandal and the external shocks from the Middle East conflict
    • This implies that 1Q26 GDP growth would be lower than the 5.4% and 3.0% growth in 1Q25 and 4Q25, respectively
  • Balisacan stated that the Development Budget Coordination Committee (DBCC) will review its growth assumptions and likely revise its 5-6% GDP growth target for 2026
    • He added that the gov't is working to mitigate the impact of negative shocks on the economy with financial support being extended to the most vulnerable sectors
  • 1Q26 GDP data will be released on May 7
    • Consensus expects 1Q26 GDP growth to be 4.1%
  • FY26 GDP growth expectations for the BSP/consensus/Regis are   4.8/4.5/4.4%
POLITICS
House justice committee finds probable cause to impeach VP Sara Duterte
  • The House justice committee, in a unanimous vote, declared that there is probable cause in the two impeachment complaints against Vice President Sara Duterte
    • The complaints accuse VP Duterte of graft, bribery, culpable violation of the Constitution, betrayal of public trust, and other high crimes
  • The committee is expected to submit its report on the proceedings and corresponding Articles of Impeachment to the plenary for consideration, where a one-third vote of all House members will be required to impeach VP Duterte and to send the case to the Senate for trial
    • A two-thirds vote of the Senate impeachment court will then be required for a conviction, which will remove Duterte from office and perpetually disqualify her from any future gov't position
    • Recall that VP Sara Duterte back in Feb announced her intention to run for president in the 2028 election
PROPERTY
SM Prime could reduce capex by 30%-35%
  • According to Bilyonaryo.com, SM Prime could reduce its capex budget from P100bn to P65bn-P70bn this year
    • The company spent P82bn in capex in 2025
  • The revised strategy would halt land acquisitions and new projects and focus on strategic assets, including its Manila Bay reclamation
  • During the SMPH stockholders’ meeting, the company said it would defer “non-critical capex”
  • SMPH is trading at 56% discount to NAV, 1.1X FY26E P/B, and 11.2X FY26E P/E
