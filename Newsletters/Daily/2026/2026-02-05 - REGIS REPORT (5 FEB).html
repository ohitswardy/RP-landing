REGIS REPORT

05 February 2026

The statistics office reported that CPI inflation for January 2026 was 2.0% compared to 1.8% in Dec 2025. The figure is a little higher than consensus expectations (1.8%).

 

The increase in reported inflation could arguably be blamed on Utility costs (3.3%) and Restaurants and Miscellaneous (4.0%). Food price inflation (1.1%) remained tame, largely because of rice prices (-8.5%), which are still lapping a high base period. The benefits of the high base for rice prices should, however, fall away in a couple more months. 

 

Despite the uptick, we reckon the Bangko Sentral (BSP) is still likely to move on policy rates two weeks from now.

 

 

+++

 

Luzon spot prices were at P3.25/kwh for January, which is up +9% YoY. The increase was attributed to a large chunk of capacity (4,811MW) being offline during the month.

 

More interesting than the spot price, however, was the fact that demand for the month of Jan was down 2% YoY. While some of the demand weakness may be due to cooler temperatures, our concern is that it might be indicative of economic activity in general.

 

+++

 

The Lower House Justice Committee dismissed yesterday the first two impeachment complaints filed against President Marcos. The outcome was not really a surprise. Unless the decision is overturned by the House plenary, it becomes final and no new impeachment cases against Marcos can be taken up for one year.

 

In the meantime, polling firm Social Weather Stations (SWS) says the net satisfaction rating of Marcos was -3ppt in Nov, largely unchanged from Sept (-5ppt). Notable, however, was the deterioration in Metro Manila which was offset by the improvement in the Visayas.

 

+++

 

The capex and residential launch targets of Megaworld Corporation (MEG PM) suggest that the company is generally upbeat about the state of the property sector. It is targeting to spend P65bn in capex, a record level and 30% higher than last year. It also plans to launch P65bn worth of residential projects, much higher than last year’s P20bn.

 

+++

 

The BSP said that it would issue the regulations on digital marketplaces by 1H26. The draft regulations published last year prohibit gambling products and services from being offered or presented in the digital marketplace operated by banks and electronic money issuers. However, since the BSP already prohibits banks and electronic wallets from redirecting to gambling platforms, the release of the official regulations is unlikely to have any meaningful impact on the online gaming market.

POWER
Luzon spot prices rise 9% YoY in January on forced outages, demand stays soft
  • The Independent Electricity Market Operator of the Philippines (IEMOP) reported that spot prices in Luzon rose 9% YoY to P3.25/kwh in January
    • Cited forced outages from coal and gas-fired plants, which took 4,811MW out of commission
    • Demand declined 2% YoY to ~8,600MW
  • The following gencos are net sellers to the spot market:
    • Semirara (SCC PM) – 60% of capacity is un-contracted
    • ACEN Corp. (ACEN PM) – 44% of capacity in PH is un-contracted
    • Aboitiz Power (AP PM) – 10% of capacity is un-contracted

Megaworld to launch P65bn of residential projects in 2026
  • Megaworld said it will launch P65bn worth of residential projects in 2026
    • This is higher than the P20bn launch target in 2025
  • Launches in 2026 will predominantly be in provincial areas
    • Ilocos, Cavite, Batangas, Laguna, Palawan, Iloilo, Cebu, Cagayan de Oro, Pasig, Taguig, and Manila City
  • Megaworld had P140bn in unsold residential inventory as of 3Q25
  • The company generated P88.8bn in reservation sales in 9M25
  • We estimate that as of 3Q25, unsold residential inventory represented c. 20 months’ worth of net reservation sales
  • Megaworld’s capex target also increased 30% YoY to P65bn, a record level
  • The company aims to complete 470,000sqm of offices, 480,000sqm of malls, and 3,600 hotel rooms by 2030
    • It currently has 1.5mn sqm of offices, 520,000sqm of malls, and 5,400 hotel rooms  
  • MEG currently offers 4.1% dividend yield and is trading at 66% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY26E P/B, and 3.5X FY26E P/E
BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
BSP to issue rules on digital marketplaces in 1H26
  • The BSP aims to issue regulations on digital marketplaces by the end of 1H26
  • BSP Deputy Governor Lyn I. Javier said that it will be open to big banks or electronic money issuers opening their platforms to other financial service providers
  • In 2025, the BSP issued draft regulations on digital marketplaces
  • The draft rules would prohibit banks and electronic money issuers from offering products linked to online gambling activities
  • In August 2025, the BSP issued a directive prohibiting banks from redirecting to online gaming platforms
POLITICS
House panel dismisses Marcos impeachment complaints
  • The House Committee on Justice dismissed the two impeach complaints filed against President Marcos, which accused him of corruption, violating the constitution, and betraying public trust, as they were deemed "insufficient in substance"
  • The House is expected to convene for a plenary vote to either uphold or override the committee’s findings
    • If upheld, the junking of the complaints will trigger the one-year ban rule given the Constitutional rule that an official can only undergo one impeachment proceeding per year
POLITICS
President Marcos’ net satisfaction rating remains neutral in November SWS survey
  • President Marcos’ net satisfaction rating remained "neutral" at -3 in Nov 2025 from "neutral" -5 in Sep 2025
    • Note that Marcos’ net satisfaction rating declined to -5 in Sep from "moderate" +10 in Jun, with the drop occurring after his flood control scandal expose during his SONA last July
  • Marcos' gross satisfaction rating inched up 1% to 40% from Sep to Nov
    • Gross dissatisfaction dropped a tad to 43% from Sep’s 44%
    • Gross undecided was stable at 17%
  • SWS cited the following movements in Marcos’ net satisfaction rating in all four major areas:
    • -15 points in Metro Manila (from “neutral” -2 to “poor” -17)
    • +5 points in Balance Luzon (from “neutral” +8 to “moderate” +13)
    • +10 points in the Visayas (from “neutral” -8 to “neutral” +2)
    • +1 point in Mindanao (from “poor” -28 to “poor” -29)
  • Marcos' average net satisfaction was zero in 2025, down from +25 in 2024 and +51 in 2023
  • Survey was conducted via face-to-face interviews with 1,200 adults from Nov 24-30, 2025
