REGIS REPORT

10 July 2026

The government tweaked its revenue targets for the year, trimming it by just 0.3% following the reduced GDP growth target (3.5% - 4.5%). Lower targets for the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) were broadly offset by higher ones for the Customs bureau.

 

Overall, the government is hoping to generate P4.8trn of revenue, up 7.9% from a year earlier.

 

+++

 

Electricity spot prices in Luzon rose to P7.95/kwh in June, up 13% MoM and more 2x YoY. Power demand in the quarter was only up slightly (+2% YoY) but there were more plant outages during the period.

 

+++

 

The chairman of the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) is hoping to have the tariff decision for Meralco’s (MER PM) distribution utility (DU) business’ new First Regulatory Period (July 2026 to June 2030) released within 3Q. Meralco applied for a tariff of P2.34/kwh, which represents a 73% increase from the current rate of P1.35/kwh.

 

The rate decision has been delayed multiple times with the last full review completed as far back as 2011. The tariff outcome would be, by far, the single most consequential event for the company over the near-term. Over 50bn kwh runs through the wires of Meralco’s DU business annually.

 

+++

 

Digiplus (PLUS PM) extended its buyback program by another twelve months. The program, which still has P5.36bn unutilized, was supposed to expire this month.

MACRO
Gov't adjusts revenue targets for 2026
  • The Development Budget Coordination Committee (DBCC) trimmed its tax revenue forecasts for this year by 0.3% to P4.81trn (+7.9% YoY) from P4.82trn (+8.3% YoY) following the recently lowered 2026 GDP growth target of 3.5-4.5% from 5.0-6.0%
  • The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) lowered its projection by 1% to P3.39trn (+9% YoY) from P3.43trn (+ 10% YoY)
    • Growth is expected to moderate given elevated domestic and external uncertainties
    • In 2025, the BIR collected P3.11trn (+9% YoY) in revenues
  • The Bureau of Customs' (BoC) target was slightly raised by 0.7% to P1.01trn (+8% YoY) from P1.00trn (+7% YoY)
    • The adjustment reflects the agency's efforts to boost collections and the weaker peso against the US dollar
    • In 2025, the BoC collected P933bn (+2% YoY) in revenue
  • The DBCC increased its nontax revenue forecast by 4.3% to P365.1bn (-11% YoY) from P349.9bn (-15% YoY) previously

 

 

 

POWER
Power spot prices jump further in June
  • Spot prices in Luzon jumped to P7.95/kwh in June, up 13% MoM and 103% YoY
    • According to IEMOP Trading Operations Department’s Rica Cagnayo
    • Cited that the MoM increase was due to more frequent plant outages
    • Brings 2Q26 spot prices to P6.87/kwh (+62% YoY, +105% QoQ)
  • Average demand in Luzon rose 7% YoY (-2% MoM) in June, taking 2Q26 demand up 2% YoY, 21% QoQ

 

 

POWER
ERC says Meralco rate decision likely out by Q3
  • ERC chairman Francis Saturnino Juan said it is targeting to release its decision on Meralco’s rate application by August or September
    • “Right now, we are already starting the evaluation and informal deliberation and discussions,” he said
    • Juan said the regulator must first conduct an inspection of the company’s assets within the month lasting until next month
  • Recall that MER is applying for a 73% increase in distribution tariffs to P2.34/kwh, primarily to fund its 5-year capex program of P272bn
    • Although MER’s tariff reviews are supposed to occur every four years, its last tariff reset process was completed in 2011

MACRO
PH dollar reserves at $104.8bn as of end-Jun
  • The Philippines' gross international reserves (GIR) at end-Jun increased 0.8% MoM to US$104.8bn
    • The June GIR figure is a three-month high
  • The MoM increase in the GIR was primarily due to the national gov't's higher net foreign currency deposits with the BSP which offset the downward valuation adjustments to the BSP's gold holdings
    • The significant increase in foreign exchange (foreign currency and deposits) was likely related to the gov't's recent US$2.5bn global bond issuance in June
  • The level of dollar reserves was enough to cover about 3.7x the country's short-term external debt and equivalent to 6.8x months' worth of imports of goods and payments
  • For 2026, the BSP expects the GIR to settle at US$104bn, lower than the US$110.8bn in 2025
