REGIS REPORT

22 May 2026

The 1Q26 core attributable profit of Alliance Global (AGI PM) rose 4% YoY to P5.2bn, ahead of consensus forecast. The stronger profit contribution from Megaworld offset the decline from Travellers.

 

  • Megaworld (MEG PM) 1Q26 EBIT rose 4% YoY on the back of 3% revenue growth and a 3.2ppt YoY expansion in residential GPM
  • Travellers’ EBIT fell 50% QoQ and 27% YoY due to a sharp deterioration in its VIP business
  • Emperador (EMI PM) net profit rose 4% YoY to P1.9bn (a reversal from P813mn net loss in 4Q25) as revenue and margins of both the whisky and brandy businesses improved
  • Profit contribution from Golden Arches, which holds the master franchise of McDonald’s, also rose c. 5% to P200mn

The ripple effects of the big macro event this year (i.e. the attack on Iran) are very much being felt.

 

Bangko Sentral (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona says the BSP is considering another hike in policy rates at the next Monetary Board meeting on June 18 but was not ruling out an “off-cycle” hike even before that. The CPI inflation data for May will be released on June 5 and that should have a material influence on the MB’s decision then. A rate hike is looking very likely at this point.

 

Meanwhile, the regional wage board for Metro Manila (National Capital Region or NCR) will begin holding consultations soon on increases in the minimum wage. The earliest any new minimum wage for NCR can be implemented is one year after the last one (July 18, 2025). Given the soaring inflation due to the energy crisis, we reckon an increase of around 10% is in the cards.

The SEC has finally imposed term limits on directors of the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE PM). The rule is being imposed as governance measure to allow new people to come in and introduce new thinking and ultimately improve the local equity market.

CONGLOS
AGI 1Q26 core attributable profit rises 4% YoY
  • AGI’s core attributable profit for 1Q26 rose 4% YoY to P5.2bn, ahead of consensus forecast
  • The higher profit contribution from Megaworld offset the declines from Emperador and Travellers
    • Emperador’s contribution fell because AGI’s ownership shrank from 79% in 4Q25 to 75% in 1Q26
  • Megaworld 1Q26 EBIT rose 4% on the back of 3% revenue growth and a 3.2ppt YoY expansion in residential GPM (brought about by better product mix and “disciplined cost management”)
  • Travellers’ EBIT fell 50% QoQ and 27% YoY to P800mn
    • This was largely due to the 33% QoQ and 43% YoY decline in VIP GGR (to P2.0bn), which was brought about by lower VIP rolling chip
    • Meanwhile, mass GGR fell 8% QoQ (up 2% YoY) to P4.5bn
  • Emperador's 1Q26 profit rose 4% YoY and reversed from a P813mn net loss in 4Q25 to P1.9bn—ahead of consensus estimates
    • Profit growth was driven by improvements in topline and margins for both the brandy and whisky segments
      • Brandy (65% of total revenue) reverted to positive revenue growth (+1% YoY following two consecutive quarters of YoY decline as sales performance across all brands both int'l (Terry, El Presidente) and local (Emperador) were "very encouraging" during the quarter
      • Whisky likewise returned to positive sales growth (+2% YoY) after four consecutive quarters of YoY decline led by Dalmore
      • Margins improved for both segments on primarily due to a favorable product mix for Brandy and improvements in operational efficiencies for Whisky
  • The profit contribution from Golden Arches rose c. 5% YoY to P200mn
  • AGI is trading at 3.8X FY26E P/E

 

 

 

 

MACRO
BSP considering off-cycle rate hike
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona in an interview stated that the Monetary Board is considering a second straight hike prior to its June 18 meeting (3rd out of 6 this year)
  • Remolona elaborated that an off-cycle hike is not likely but the board is considering it
  • He also added that the board may wait until after May inflation data is released on June 5 before making the next monetary policy decision
  • The BSP Governor also stated that the central bank can likely tolerate the peso hitting P63.5 to the dollar given that it moves in a "measured" way which would not stoke inflation
    • The USD-PHP rate has depreciated from P58.8 at the start of the year to P61.7 as of May 21
  • The BSP and consensus expects inflation to average 6.3% for 2026
    • Consensus expectations for the policy rate are for two more 25bp hikes to 5.00% by year-end

 

 

MACRO
NCR wage board consultations to start on May 22
  • The National Capital Region (NCR) Regional Tripartite Wages and Productivity Board (RTWPB) will begin its consultation process on May 22, first with the labor sector, then with employers on May 28, and a public hearing on June 18
  • The last minimum wage adjustment that took effect was last July 18, 2025, where a P50 (+8%) minimum wage hike was approved, which then increased the NCR daily minimum wage to the current P695
    • Relative to the past decade, the P50 wage hike is the highest percentage increase granted by the NCR regional wage board
  • The hike was estimated to directly benefit 1.2mn minimum wage workers as well as indirectly benefit 1.7mn full-time wage workers from correction of wage distortions

 

 

MARKET
SEC imposes term limits on broker directors of exchanges
  • The SEC will impose a term limit for broker directors of exchanges
    • This should include the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE PM)
  • The maximum cumulative term limit is ten years
  • After serving a cumulative five years, a broker director would need to observe a one-year cooling off period
  • The SEC has provided a two-year transition period for incumbent broker directors, who may complete their current term and remain eligible for election in the next two annual elections
  • After this transition period, four of the current five broker directors would no longer be eligible
  • At least 51 percent of the 15-member board must be non-brokers-- five independent directors and four directors representing the interests of issuers, investors and other market participants, with each sector having at least one representative
