REGIS REPORT

02 January 2026

The BPO industry association IBPAP says it is on track to reach US$40bn of revenue and have headcount of 1.9m for the year 2025. These would still represent +5% and +4% growth, respectively even if the pace has slowed from the >10% a decade ago.

 

For FY26, IBPAP is forecasting approximately the same growth rate of 4-5%, notwithstanding fears surrounding the impact of AI.

 

+++

 

Based on the available trading data on the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE PM), it is looking almost certain that RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM) will become part of the PSEi benchmark by February. RCR is, of course, the REIT spin-off of Robinsons Land Corp (RLC PM) that’s quickly become bigger than the parent company itself.

 

We expect the entry of RCR into the PSEi to be at the expense of Alliance Global (AGI PM). An announcement of changes to the PSEi should be expected by early Feb.

 

+++

 

Based on media reports, BDO (BDO PM) management says it is expecting loan growth to slow and margins to narrow for FY26. Regis is forecasting loan growth of 12.6% and 11.7% in FY25 and FY26, respectively.

PROPERTY
BPO sector on track to hit 2025 targets
  • The IT-BPO Association of the Philippines (IBPAP) said it was on track to hit its 2025 targets: US$40bn in revenue and 1.9mn employees
  • In 2026, IIBPAP expects the industry to achieve revenue of US$42bn and employ 1.97mn Filipinos (c. 4%-5% growth)
  • IBPAP President Jonathan Madrid previously said that the industry’s growth will still be driven by core segments – financial services and healthcare
  • He also said that Global Capability Centers (GCC) are growing faster
    • These are offshore units established by multinational corporations to provide specialized services such as finance, IT and customer support to their global operations
  • BPO firms account for 60%-80% of the occupied office space of the developers we cover
  • MEG currently offers 4.4% dividend yield and is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY26E P/B, and 3.2X FY26E P/E
MARKET
PSE Index prediction: RCR (+), AGI (-)
  • Based on our estimates, RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM) will be added to the PSE Index during the February 2026 rebalancing while Alliance Global (AGI PM) will be removed
  • The PSE Index rebalancing in Feb 2026 will be based on the 12 months from Jan 2025 to Dec 2025
  • Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap
  • Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when
    • 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or
    • 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35 
  • Based on our estimates, RCR is ranked within the top 25 and AGI is ranked outside the top 35

 

 

BANKS
BDO expects slower loan growth and margin pressure in 2026
  • BDO Unibank (BDO PM) mentioned in a news article that key challenges for the local banking industry in 2026 include slower credit expansion amid the broad economic slowdown from political uncertainty as well as pressured margins brought about by recent rate cuts
    • REGIS is forecasting 12.6% loan growth in 2025 and 11.7% growth in 2026
  • BDO is optimistic on certain segments such as consumer lending which continues to post robust growth on the back of OFW remittances, BPO growth, outsourcing industry, low unemployment levels, and low inflation
    • The bank also highlighted upside on the corporate side, specifically the energy sector given the "pipeline of new power generation projects awaiting regulatory approval"
  • The company noted that the banking sector was still able to post modest income growth despite declining rates, slowing credit and GDP growth
    • As of 9M25, BDO's net income rose 4% YoY to P63.1bn 
MACRO
BSP expects December inflation to settle within 1.2-2.0%
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) in its month-ahead inflation forecast expects Dec inflation to fall within 1.2-2.0%
    • BSP expectations for Dec inflation are around the lower-end of the BSP's 2-4% long-term target range
      • For 2025, BSP expectations for inflation this year is 1.7%
    • Consensus expects Dec inflation to be 1.8%
  • Upward price pressures are higher prices of food due to strong holiday demand and lingering adverse weather effects, as well as higher LPG and gasoline prices
    • These may have been partially offset by lower prices of electricity
  • Inflation has averaged 1.6% so far for 11M25, while inflation in Nov alone was 1.5%
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect inflation to average 1.7% and 1.8%, respectively
    • For 2026, consensus and Regis expectations are at 2.6% and 3.0%, respectively
  • Dec inflation data will be released by the PSA (Philippine Statistics Authority) on Jan 6 (Tue)
