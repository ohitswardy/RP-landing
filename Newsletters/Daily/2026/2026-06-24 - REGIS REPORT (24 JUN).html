RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM) will acquire 160,000sqm of provincial malls from its sponsor, Robinsons Land (RLC PM), in exchange for 1.29bn new RCR shares. The acquisition would expand RCR’s portfolio and shares outstanding by 7% (to 1.3mn sqm and 20.8bn shares, respectively).

 

We expect the transaction to be mildly DPS accretive for RCR and fundamentally neutral for RLC.

 

However, the transaction could be positive for RLC’s share price. Once the transaction is completed, the value of RLC’s 12.2bn RCR shares would be P86bn (at the RCR closing price of P7.09/share), higher than RLC’s current market cap of P79bn. This highlights how undervalued RLC shares are, particularly since the company still has 1.1mn sqm of malls (and 300,000sqm of offices) that can be infused into RCR in the future 

 

RLC also offers a dividend yield of 6.1%, not much lower than RCR’s FY27E dividend yield of 6.7%.

APM Terminals announced the completion of a new port in Suape, Brazil with a capacity of 400k TEUs annually. This is a terminal that would compete directly with ICTSI’s (ICT PM) there. ICT’s Suape terminal has a capacity of 700k annual TEUs and is currently a practical monopoly in that area. We are already factoring in a 30% decline in volumes at this terminal next year in anticipation of the new competitor. On our numbers, the Suape terminal would account for 3% of ICT’s FY27 volumes.

Ayala Land (ALI PM) is seeking to extend its lease agreement with the government over the land upon which the Market! Market! Mall is located. The lease expires next year and that mall accounts for approximately 5% of ALI’s mall revenue.
