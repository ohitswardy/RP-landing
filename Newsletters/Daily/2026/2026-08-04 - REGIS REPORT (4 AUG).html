The Dept of Finance (DOF) is pushing a new tax reform package whose acronym spells “PROGRESS” (sort of). This tax package is arguably an extension of the “TRAIN” Law passed under the Duterte administration. “PROGRESS” proposes, like “TRAIN”, to reduce personal income taxes and shift the burden to consumption taxes.

 

Among the proposals is the elimination of income taxes for all those making up to P350,000 per year. To compensate for the revenue losses, the DOF is proposing increases in taxes on sweetened beverages, tobacco (including e-cigarettes), motor vehicles, and alcoholic beverages.

 

Among listed firms, those who could be affected by tax changes for sweetened beverages are: Universal Robina (URC PM), Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM) and LT Group (LTG PM). For tobacco and related products, it will be LTG and possibly even Philippine Seven (SEVN PM). For alcoholic beverages, it will be the likes of LTG, San Miguel Food and Beverage (FB PM) and Emperador (EMI PM). For motor vehicles, companies like GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and Ayala Corp (AC PM) could see some impact.

 

We expect fierce lobbying from those with interests in any of those industries (and related businesses), including the politically power sugar lobby. Up to very last second until any bill is signed (or vetoed) by the President, anything can change with regard to the tax proposals. Recall, for example, that final version of TRAIN ended up with a reduction in taxes on luxury vehicles.
