24 February 2026

Water utility Maynilad (MYNLD PM) announced it saw a net profit of P15.2bn for FY25, up 19% YoY and well ahead of Street and REGIS estimates.  The company also disclosed that the regulatory value of its West Zone concession (a.k.a. interim “cash position”) was P164bn, +13% YoY and +63% from end-2022. This means the regulatory value has been growing at a compounded rate of 18% over the current regulatory period.

 

The company also declared a dividend of P1.14/share, which should be a positive surprise for dividend-centric watchers. That dividend translates to 5.4% yield on yesterday’s price and 7.6% on the IPO subscription price.

During its 4Q25 earnings briefing, AREIT (AREIT PM) revealed that for FY25, office contracts were renewed at rental rates that were 6% lower than the previous contracts. While this is an improvement from the 10%-15% declines in 1H25, these negative office rental reversions have persisted in 1Q26), with one tenant being renewed at a rental rate that was 17% lower than the previous contract. How the situation in the office rental market evolves bears watching.

The impeachment complaints against Vice President Sara Duterte-Carpio have been officially initiated with their referral to the House Justice Committee. The likelihood of her removal from office via impeachment, however, is quite remote. Should the complaints advance to a Senate impeachment trial, Duterte-Carpio appears to have more than enough allies in the Senate to block a conviction.

 

Meanwhile, over on the other side of the world, a different trial began. In their opening arguments, the prosecution as well as the defense counsel in the trial of Duterte-Carpio’s father Rodrigo Duterte for crimes against humanity offered contrasting narratives of his actions while he was the leader of both the country and of the city of Davao. The conduct and outcome of his trial is likely to have implications on the political landscape in the country.

Maynilad reports end-FY25 OCP of P163.9bn, declares P1.14/sh dividend
  • In a press release, Maynilad announced its interim cash position stood at P163.9bn in end-FY25, up 63% from its end-2022 P100bn validated OCP and broadly in line with our forecast

  • Company declared dividends of P8.44bn (P1.14/sh), above our forecast of P0.97/sh and equivalent to a yield of 5%
  • Management will hold a briefing this Friday, Feb 27, at 4pm (email: investor.relations@mayniladwater.com.ph to register)
POLITICS
House initiates impeachment proceedings against VP Duterte
  • Impeachment proceedings against Vice President Sara Duterte have been formally initiated as the four filed complaints were sent to the House Committee on Justice
  • Members of the committee have 60 days to determine if the complaints are sufficient in form and in substance
    • The committee will come up with a recommendation whether to approve or dismiss the complaints
    • The House would then need a one-third vote to approve and elevate the impeachment complaint against Duterte to the Senate
  • Recall that Duterte recently announced in a press briefing that she will run for president in the 2028 election
TRANSPORT
Bill to scrap travel tax progresses at House Committee
  • The House Committee on Tourism approved a bill proposing the removal of travel tax imposed on Filipino travelers flying overseas
    • Bill’s passage would mean travel tax imposed under Presidential Decree No. 1183 and the Tourism Act of 2009 would be repealed
    • Exempt from travel tax are overseas Filipino workers, Filipino permanent residents overseas who stayed less than a year in the Philippines, and children aged two years and below
    • The travel tax amounts to P1,620 (US$28) for economy passengers and P2,700 (US$47) for first class
  • The government estimates annual revenue loss of P8bn (<1% of total tax revenues) from the removal of the tax
  • The removal of the tax will be positive for airlines
    • International routes respectively account for ~25% and ~40% of Cebu Air’s (CEB PM) and Philippine Airlines’ (PAL PM) passenger volumes
