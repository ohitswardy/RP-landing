REGIS REPORT

31 July 2026

The latest statements by the presidential spokesperson suggest the administration is gradually walking back its “demand” that distribution utilities (DU) absorb electricity systems losses so consumers “do not pay” for it (See https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2274619/palace-open-to-talks-on-scrapping-system-loss-billing)

 

We are of the belief that compelling the DUs to absorb the entire systems loss is not implementable and will have widespread, damaging, unintended consequences. We expect the administration to eventually come around to this realization.

 

Despite this, we believe the conversation over why power rates in the Philippines are so high will lead to … “discoveries” that could significantly alter regulations. Amending these regulations to reduce what consumers pay will likely lead to changes in the economics of the industry.

 

+++

 

A restraining order was apparently issues by a local court holding back the implementation of the minimum wage increase on July 25 for the National Capital Region (NCR). Recall the government announced the wage hike (P85/day or +12%) a few weeks ago and the first tranche of that (P60/day) was supposed to be enforced on July 25.

 

A local court however issued a temporary restraining order (TRO) against the increase with a hearing is set for Monday (Aug 3). We believe the TRO to be lifted shortly thereafter.

 

This wage increase is the largest such in recent memory and is likely to lead to margin squeezes for many companies (especially those employing many low-skilled workers). Second and third-order effects on inflation are also probable consequences. The wage hike will also, however, have positive effects on consumption spending.

 

+++

 

As widely expected, ICTSI (ICT PM) signed a 25-year renewal for its flagship port, Manila International Container Terminal (MICT), extending the validity of the concession to May 2063. The disclosure lacked detail on the renewal terms so we hope management will provide clarity during its 2Q briefing on Monday, August 3.  

 

+++

 

PAGCOR reported that its 1H26 revenue fell 27%, with revenue from e-games falling 42% and revenue from licensed (on-premise) casinos declining 4%. From these figures, we estimate that 2Q26 industry e-games GGR could have fallen c. 30% QoQ and c. 55% YoY. This is not a good omen for the 2Q numbers of DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM).

 

On the other hand, we reckon that 2Q26 GGR of licensed casinos was likely flattish QoQ and YoY.

The 600MW hydro project owned by the Razon group has attracted Kansai Electric as a new investor. Recall First Gen (FGEN PM) is taking a stake in this project. No valuation first Kansai Electric’s investment was disclosed.  A valuation would have helped to dispel or confirm views that FGEN overpaid for its share (28% effective interest).

MACRO
DOLE confirms court suspension of P85 NCR minimum wage hike
  • The Dept. of Labor and Employment (DOLE) confirmed that the implementation of the first tranche of the P85 minimum wage increase in Metro Manila was suspended due to a court order issuance (Temporary Restraining Order [TRO]) following the legal challenge by two construction companies
  • Readycon Trading and Construction Corp. and R-II Builders Inc. filed a petition to temporarily halt the wage hike, questioning whether the Regional Wage Board adequately considered the factors under Article 124 of the Labor Code, particularly the "fair return of the capital invested and capacity to pay of employers"
    • The companies cited that the hike was issued "without adequate regard to compounding economic conditions"
  • Dept. of Labor Secretary Francis Tolentino stated that he is "saddened" by the court decision but notes that the agency fully respects the judicial process
  • The current NCR minimum wage prior to the suspension was set to increase to P780 which will be implemented in two tranches
    • +P60 effective July 25, 2026 and the remaining +P25 effective January 20, 2027

Japan’s Kansai Electric Power Co. investing in 600MW Wawa Pumped Storage Hydro project
  • Kansai Electric Power Company (9503 JP) has agreed to buy a 14% stake in PMJVCo Hldgs. (see here)
    • PMJVCo holds an 87% stake in Olympia Violago Water and Power Inc, which is the entity that owns the 600MW Wawa Pumped Storage Hydro project in Rizal
    • Financial details on the transaction were not disclosed
  • Recall that First Gen (FGEN PM) has a 33% stake in Prime Hydropower Energy Inc. (PHEI), which prior to this transaction owned 100% of PMJVCo. 
    • Upon deal completion, FGEN’s effective stake in the Wawa hydro project will be 24.69% (vs. 28.4%)
    • Apart from the Wawa hydro project, FGEN also holds an effective 33% stake in the 1,400MW Pakil Pumped Storage Hydroelectric Power Project in Laguna
    • Note that FGEN originally bought its effective stakes in these two projects for P61bn
MACRO
Marcos approves incentives to attract EV investments
  • President Marcos Jr. signs Executive Order (EO) 21 establishing the Electric Vehicle Incentive Strategy (EVIS) Program
    • Program provides fiscal incentives for the local manufacture of hybrid and battery electric vehicles and their parts and components
    • Total fiscal support for the EVIS will be capped at P60bn, with up to P15bn allocated for each enrolled EV model
  • Qualified manufacturers may register up to two (2) EV models under the EVIS program
    • To qualify, companies must undertake a capex of at least P5bn and introduce such models to the domestic and export markets within a three-year period
  • In exchange, qualified participants will receive the following fiscal incentives:
    • A Fixed Investment Support that will be equivalent to 40% of capex for battery EVs and 30% for hybrid EVs, plug-in hybrid EVs and fuel cell EVs
    • A Production Volume Incentive of up to 12% of the ex-factory unit price but not exceeding P200,000 per unit for domestically manufactured or assembled EVs once production hits at least 10,000 EV units
  •  Participants will be entitled to the FSI and PVI for a maximum of 10 years from the start of production of the enrolled EV models
    • Registered participants will receive tax payment certificates
    • Companies that fail to introduce locally manufactured EV models or components within three years of registration may face cancellation of their registration, monetary fines, or the refund of fiscal incentives
  • Mitsubishi Motors Philippines Corp. (MMPC) said it will participate in the EVIS program through its P7bn investment to produce hybrid EVs

MREIT 2Q26 distributable income remains flat QoQ
  • MREIT’s 2Q26 distributable income fell 1% QoQ (up 33% YoY) to P1.24bn
    • 1H26 distributable income rose 34% to P2.5bn, in line with consensus forecast
  • 2Q26 revenue fell 2% QoQ (up 24% YoY) to P1.7bn
    • Contracts were renewed at slightly lower rental rates (down low single-digit)
    • Negative rental reversions in Eastwood were in the low teens
    • Other areas saw flat to slightly positive rental reversions (contracts were renewed at slightly higher rental rates)
  • 2Q26 EBITDA margin improved 1.1ppt YoY to 81.2%
    • Management attributed this to “disciplined cost management”, including the transition to 100% renewable energy
  • The company said that the upcoming share-for-property swap will cause DPS accretion of 3%
    • MREIT will acquire 304,000sqm of mall, office, and hotel assets from Megaworld and Travellers International in exchange for 1.64bn new MREIT shares
    • The transaction will expand MREIT’s floor area by 47% and its shares outstanding by 35%
  • MREIT currently offers FY26E dividend yield of 7.5%
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 7.4%

 

 

MACRO
Trade deficit widens 12% YoY to $4.9bn in Jun
  • The June trade deficit widened to US$4.9bn (+12% YoY)
    • This brings the 6M26 trade deficit to US$30.8bn, 26% wider YoY
  • Imports for the month climbed 20% YoY to US$13.7bn, a tad slower than the 22% increase in May
    • The increase in imports was supported by strong growth across the categories such as raw and intermediate goods (+53%) as well as consumer goods (+14%), which offset the decline in capital goods (-6%)
      • Imports of electronics was the primary growth driver, surging 83% YoY vs non-electronics which was relatively flat (+1%) for the month
  • Exports growth accelerated to 24% YoY (US$8.8bn) from 8% the previous month
    • This marks the eighteenth consecutive month of YoY growth in exports
    • Growth in electronics exports picked up to +35% from +12% in May
      • These accounted for 60% of total exports in the month
    • Meanwhile, non-electronics exports were up +11%, accelerating from +3% the previous month

 

 

 

 

 

POLITICS
Marcos authorizes release of BIR tax records for VP impeachment trial
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. has authorized the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) to release the tax records of Vice President Sara Duterte to the Senate impeachment court
    • Senate Secretary Renato Bantug noted that the submission of the VP's tax records would not have been accepted without presidential approval
    • Several bank documents were also submitted to the Senate impeachment court
  • House prosecutors requested for the documents as the records are expected to shed light on Duterte's unexplained wealth
  • Palace press Officer Claire Castro denied claims that the Marcos' action was politically motivated as she noted that the President's decision was in line with what the law required and the proper legal process with the approval being "necessary in the interest of establishing the truth"
