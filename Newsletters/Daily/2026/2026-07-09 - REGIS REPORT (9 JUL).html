REGIS REPORT

09 July 2026

Crisis? What crisis? Loan growth accelerated again in May to 12.5% YoY, marking a new 16-month high growth rate. The improvement was driven by corporate/commercial loans, expanding 11.4% YoY, compared to 10.5% the previous month. This offset the slight slowdown in consumer (ex-mortgage) loans, which grew 19.0% YoY in May, from 19.9% in April. NPL ratio deteriorated 8bps to 3.63%; however, this was unlikely to have been due to the Iran conflict, being just about 90 days since the war’s beginning. The NPL uptick was still led by the construction sector, which is still reeling from the “flood control” corruption scandal from 2H25. Surprisingly, Past Due (PD) rates improved MoM, dipping 8bps MoM to 4.60%. Strong loan growth, higher interest rates, and (surprisingly) benign asset quality, and cheap valuations should put the spotlight back on banks.

BANKS
Loan growth accelerates in May; NPLs up, but PDs improve

Another month of oddly conflicting data points in the banking system in May. Despite being smack in the middle of the Iran conflict, loan growth accelerated to 12.5%, a 16-month high. And while NPL ratios ticked up MoM, the more indicative Past Due (PD) numbers fell. As before, all the numbers we quote exclude interbank loans (IBLs) and reverse repos (RRPs) which tend to distort the reported numbers on a MoM basis.

 

The continued loan growth is counter-intuitive, considering the sharp slowdown in GDP in 1Q25, and the spike in inflation. The May acceleration was led by “loans for production” – ie corporate/commercial loans – which jumped 11.4% YoY. By sector, we saw large jumps in Mining (+48%), Utilities (+30-33%), and in Transport (+23%). Anecdotally, banks have told us that companies that had postponed capex plans were signing now, also to head off the prospects of higher benchmark interest rates.  Consumer loans (ex-Mortgages) slowed to +19% YoY.

 

NPL ratio blipped up 8bps MoM to 3.63%, and up 5bps YoY. Note, though, that this would not yet capture any impact from Iran (just about 90days from the start of the conflict). Interestingly, Past Due (PD) ratios actually dopped 8bps MoM, to 4.60%, though this was still up 12bps YoY. Restructured Loans (RLs) were flat at 2.10%, despite the BSP allowing banks to restructure loans without classifying them as NPL. NPL Cover, though dropped to 89%, dipping below 90% for the first time since March 2022.

 

By sector, the major segment that continues to see asset quality deterioration was Construction sector, with NPLs at 14.55% and PD ratio at 16.47%, as the government still reels from the “flood control” infrastructure corruption scandal from 2H25. Other sectors that posted higher delinquencies were Trade, Real Estate, and Hotels/Restaurants. Within the Consumer sector, Credit Card NPLs and PDs continued their slow deterioration, with NPLs up 17bps MoM and 55bps YoY to 5.32%, while PDs were up 14bps MoM and 54bps YoY to 7.85%.

 

By bank type, PD rates went up for Thrift and Digital banks – plus 12bps and 30bps respectively – while the larger Universal/Commercial banks had PDs down 5bps MoM. This would suggest to us that households are bearing the brunt of the Iran conflict, while corporates seem broadly ok. It will be interesting to see how the trend behaves into June/July as oil prices have abated.

Metrobank and PNB to waive InstaPay and PESONet fees
  • Metrobank (MBT PM) and Philippine National Bank (PNB PM) will waive InstaPay and PESONet fees for domestic fund transfers made through PNB Digital

PROPERTY
LPC: 2Q26 Metro Manila residential rental rates fall further
  • Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) reported that Metro Manila 2Q26 condominium sales (before cancellations) fell 2% QoQ (up 2% YoY) to 6,768 units
  • Unsold condominium inventory rose 2% QoQ (flat YoY) to 82,900 units
    • We estimate this is equivalent to about 37 months of reservation sales (based on trailing 12M reservation sales before cancellations)
  • Residential condominium rental rates in most parts of Metro Manila fell 1%-4% QoQ (1%-7% YoY)
    • Condominium rental rates in Manila Bay area fell 7% QoQ and 18% YoY
  • Meanwhile, Metro Manila office vacancy was flat QoQ and YoY at 18%
    • The Fort Bonifacio office vacancy rate is 8%
  • Office rental rates in most parts of Metro Manila were generally flat QoQ
  • 2Q26 nationwide net office take-up fell 39% QoQ to 111,000sqm

 

 

 

 

 

 

CONGLOS
Ayala Corp completes 40% stake sale of AC Logistics to AP Moller Capital
  • AP Moller Capital completed the acquisition of a 40% stake in Ayala Corp. unit, AC Logistics
    • We understand that AP Moller Capital bought primary shares but the valuation was not disclosed
  • In a complementary development, AC Logistics earlier completed its acquisition of an 84% stake in Glacier Megafridge (GMI)
    • GMI is one of the leading cold storage operators in the country by capacity
    • It currently operates 12 cold storage facilities with 74k pallet position
  • The investment proceeds from AP Moller will support AC Logistics’ expansion plans, including organic growth initiatives and strategic acquisitions such as the GMI deal
  • AP Moller Capital’s entry could bring operational expertise that could support AC Logistics’ operational turnaround
  • AC trades at a 28% discount to its market-based NAV of P667/sh and at 5.5x PER
MACRO
Gov't lifts excise tax suspension on kerosene and LPG
  • The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) said excise tax rates on kerosene and LPG have automatically reverted to their original rates of P5.60/liter and P3.36/kg, respectively, as the average Dubai crude oil price for June was below the US$80/pb threshold
    • This was made effective on July 8 and came ahead of the July 17 lapse of the tax suspension
  • Recall that Executive Order (EO) 114 was issued on April 16 to temporarily suspend excise taxes on specific petroleum products for three months to mitigate the impact of rising oil prices
  • As of end-June, kerosene and LPG prices have fallen to around P98.5/liter and P1,150-1,300 per 11-kg cylinder, respectively, from P154.6/liter and P1,600 per cylinder as of April 16
TRANSPORT
Philippine Airlines raises US$300mn from 7.75% five-year bond
  • PAL Holdings’ wholly-owned unit, Primero Agila Ltd., priced its US$300mn fixed-rate five-year senior notes at a 7.75% coupon on July 7
    • Offer received more than US$1.4bn in investor orders or about 4.5x oversubscribed
    • The notes, which will be unconditionally and irrevocably guaranteed by Philippine Airlines and Air Philippines Corp., are expected to settle on July 16 and will be listed on the Singapore Exchange Securities Trading Ltd. 
  • The carrier is also expecting the delivery of about five Airbus A350-1000 aircraft this year and is refurbishing 18 Airbus A321ceo aircraft as part of its fleet modernization program
    • The company operates a fleet of 82 aircraft and has a market share of 29% on domestic routes and 23% on international routes
    • Given that the proceeds will be used to replace aging narrow-body aircraft, we believe that this will not disrupt the industry’s favorable demand-supply dynamics
  • Prior to this fund raising, PAL had P73bn of net debt and generated P36bn of EBITDA in 2025, equivalent to a net debt-to-EBITDA ratio of ~2x
    • PAL Holdings’ market capitalization is at P70bn
MACRO
Ex-DILG Chief Benhur Abalos appointed as Cabinet Secretary
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. has appointed former Dept. of Interior and Local Government (DILG) Secretar Benhur Abalos Jr. as Cabinet Secretary
    • Abalos previously served as DILG Secretary from 2022-2024 as he stepped down from his post to run for senator during the midterm elections, he was replaced by current Interior Secretary Jonvic Remulla
    • Abalos' responsibilities as Cabinet Secretary include assisting the President with "timely, relevant, and strategic advice on emerging national, local, and international issues affecting the political landscape"
    • He will also serve as Marcos' liaison to national gov't agencies, LGUs, political organizations, sectoral groups of on matters of political concern, among others
  • Recall that President Marcos previously abolished the Office of the Cabinet Secretary back in July 2022 to remove "duplicated and overlapping official functions"
