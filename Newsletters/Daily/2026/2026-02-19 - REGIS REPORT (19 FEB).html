REGIS REPORT

19 February 2026

Metrobank (MBT PM) reported FY25 NPAT of P49.7bn up 3% YoY, coming in slightly behind REGIS forecast of P50.9bn and consensus P50.1bn. For 4Q alone, NPAT was P12.4bn, flat YoY and QoQ. Revenues and PPOP were broadly in line with expectations. However, bottomline was dragged by higher provision and tax expenses. Most importantly for some investors, the bank maintained its P5.00/sh cash dividend for FY26 (P3 regular, P2 special), for a 6.7% yield. The bank will hold a briefing on Monday, 23 February at 4pm. Ahead of that, some highlights:

 

  • Gross loans grew 7.4%, slowing sharply from the 11.3% in 3Q, and clocking the slowest pace since 1Q22. Consumer loans were up 13.9% YoY, though slower than the 15.8% pace in 3Q25.
  • NIMs dropped 9bps QoQ, likely on sharply lower policy interest rates. Loan yields tend to be wonky in 4Q, as there is some reclassification, but even YoY yields were down 15bps from 4Q24. Overall asset yields were down 13bps QoQ while funding costs only dipped 7bps QoQ.
  • Fee incomes were up 6% YoY, while trading/fx gains surged 47% YoY.
  • Opex was kept under control, up just 3% YoY, leading to a 16% jump in PPOP for FY25, and +29% YoY for 4Q alone. Note, though, that 4Q24 was a trading/fx loss, which helped boost 4Q25 PPOP.
  • Provisions jumped 87% YoY to P11.9bn (in line) from a low base in 2024. Credit cost was 65bps in 4Q However, we note that NPL Cover dipped to 141%, from 147% in 3Q and from 164% at end-2024, suggesting provision expense would have been higher (and NPAT lower) were it not for running down of reserves. Tax expenses were up 34%, also on a low base.  

 

Overall, a mixed set of results. More after the briefing on Monday.

The Dept of Energy (DOE) will launch the auction for the various areas as soon as Feb 27.  These areas with verified reserves include areas within Semirara island currently under the coal operating contract of Semirara Mining (SCC PM).

 

This announcement is in line with the DOE’s previous declaration that it will hold an auction for the right to mine coal on the island.  The rather short lead time was a bit of a surprise, however.  This suggests we will have greater clarity sooner rather than later.

 

+++

 

In prepared remarks, Vice President Sara Duterte-Carpio declared yesterday her intention to run for the Presidency in 2028.  While her intention comes as no surprise to anyone, her declaration more than two years before the election is.  We are inclined to view the announcement as part of her efforts to battle fresh impeachment charges against her.

 

+++

 

The Monetary Board (MB) of the Bangko Sentral (BSP) will meet to decide on the direction of monetary policy rates today.  REGIS (and the market) expects the MB to decide on a 25bp cut to policy rates, which would bring it to 4.25%.

POWER
Department of Energy set to launch auction for coal areas, including Semirara sites, on Feb 27
  • The Department of Energy is set to launch the auction for 18 coal areas with verified reserves on Feb 27; Sites include the following
    • 10 blocks on Semirara island in Caluya, Antique
    • Five blocks across the municipalities Benito Soliven, Naguilian, Cauayan in Isabela
    • Three blocks across the municipalities of Amulung and Iguig in Cagayan province
  • From the launch date of Feb 27, a pre-submission conference will be held 20 days after the launch (~March 19)
    • Interested bidders will be given sixty (60) calendar days to submit application documents (or on April 28 at 11am)
    • Opening of applications will be held on the same day as the deadline (April 28)
  • Note that the blocks on Semirara island are covered by the existing Coal Operating Contract (COC) of Semirara Mining & Power Corp. (SCC PM)
    • We await the bidding’s terms of reference, which we expect to be released on Feb 27
