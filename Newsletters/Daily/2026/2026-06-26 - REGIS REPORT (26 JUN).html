CAMPI members’ vehicle sales declined 16% YoY but recovered 23% MoM to 33.5k units in May. Including estimated sales of non-CAMPI brands BYD and Denza, total industry sales reportedly rose 6% MoM to 35,200 units, suggesting that BYD sales may be normalizing following the surge in EV purchases triggered by the spike in fuel prices in March/April. Sales of Toyota Motor Philippines also bounced 20% MoM (-13% YoY), broadly maintaining its market share at ~48%.

The Philippine government’s budget deficit grew 37% YoY to P198bn. This is despite weak primary spending, which only grew 1% in May. For the YTD period, both tax collections and primary spending have been weak, rising only 5% and 3%, respectively.

CONGLOS
Vehicle sales (ex-BYD) fall 16% YoY in May; Toyota unit sales down 13%
  • CAMPI reported vehicle sales dropped 16% YoY to 33,532 units in May (+24% MoM)
    • Brings YT-May industry unit sales down 12% YoY to 167k units
    • Note that BYD’s sales are not captured by CAMPI’s data
  • However, various news outlets reported that industry sales reached 35,200 units in May (+6% MoM) if estimates of BYD and Denza sales are included in the tally
    • This suggests that BYD and Denza brands combined accounted for 5% of the market in May
    • It further suggests that on a month-on-month basis, derived sales of the BYD brand fell to 1.7k units in May from almost 6k units in April
    • BYD/Denza is the third best-selling car brand after Toyota (48.5%) and Mitsubishi (15.4%), besting other brands, such as Suzuki (4.1%), Ford (3.6%), and Isuzu (2.6%)
  • Meanwhile, sales of xEVs (including BYD and Denza sales estimates) reached 7,695 units in May, accounting for 22% of car sales
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) sold 17,076 units in May, down 13% YoY but up 20% MoM
    • Brings 5M26 sales to 83,282 units, down 9% YoY
    • To reach our full-year forecast of 227k units (-1% YoY), sales for the rest of the year should average 20.5k units per month
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 22% of FY26E profit and 23% of NAV
    • GTCAP trades at a 55% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,061/sh and at 3.5x PER

 

 

MACRO
Government posts P198bn budget deficit in May
  • The Philippine government recorded a budget deficit of P198bn in May, 37% wider YoY but a reversal from the P31bn surplus in April
    • This brings the 5M26 deficit to P522bn, flat (-0.3%) vs the same period last year
  • Revenue collections in May declined 7% YoY, a reversal from +3% YoY growth in April as the 12% growth in tax collections were offset by the 64% dip in non-tax revenues
    • Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) collections accelerated to +15% from +0.4% the previous month
      • The huge swing can be attributed to the extension of the filing/payment deadline of the Annual Income Tax Return from April to 15 to May 15 following the declaration of the State of National Energy Emergency
    • Customs collections moderated to 6% growth from +16% the previous month
      • The temporary suspension of excise taxes on LPG and kerosene contributed to the slowdown in growth
    • Non-tax revenues plunged 64% due to the difference in timing of dividend remittances as well as one-off receipts booked from the Bases and Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA)
      • Recall that dividends from GOCCs were remitted early this year in Feb
  • Gov't spending was up 4% YoY, moderating from the 11% growth in April
    • Primary spending for the month slowed to 1% from 8% the month before
      • The flattish YoY growth still marks the fourth straight month of YoY growth after six consecutive months of YoY declines in gov't spending
    • Interest payments rose 21% YoY

 

 

MACRO
Dept. of Agriculture pushes to revive shift to 5% coconut-biodiesel fuel blend
  • The Dept. of Agriculture (DA) is seeking to revive the mandated shift to 5% coco-biodiesel blend given "more favorable market conditions"
  • DA Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. endorsed a resolution which seeks to implement a B5 biodiesel blend and relayed the proposal to the Senate and the Dept. of Energy (DOE)
    • Tiu Laurel endorses the shift citing efforts to improve the country's energy security and lessen reliance on imported fuels
  • Recall that the transition was put on hold by the National Biofuels Board  (chaired by the Energy Secretary) after international coconut prices spiked in 2025
    • The Biofuels Act of 2006 mandates that liquid fuels are to be blended with biofuels
      • The nationwide diesel biodiesel blend remains at B3 or 3% coconut methyl ester (CME), with the scheduled transition to B4 in October 2025 and B5 in October 2026 currently suspended
  • D&L Industries' (DNL PM) Chemrez Group, the company's wholly-owned subsidiary which produces petrochem products and other products, contributed to 48% of DNL's net profit in FY25
