Two more consumer names released results yesterday: convenience store operator Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) and the manufacturer of canned food (and others) Century Pacific (CNPF PM).

 

CNPF reported FY25 income rising 11% P7.07bn, which is below our (admittedly high) estimate. Among the things worth highlighting for FY25/4Q25 results: (1) OEM revenues reversed the big declines over 9M25, (2) Branded revenue growth showed a modest, volume-driven acceleration and (3) the capex budget for FY26 is much larger than anticipated. Have a look at our comments here:

There was a visible, albeit small recovery in same store sales (SSS) which moved to positive growth (+3%). Management also indicated that 1Q26 numbers are looking encouraging. President Marcos suspended the collection of excise taxes for LPG and kerosene yesterday but held off suspending those for gasoline and diesel. The move will result in small price reductions for both LPG and kerosene. 

 

We have to wonder why the excise taxes on gasoline (P10/liter) and diesel (P6/liter) were not suspended at the same time. Because of the huge pump price increases, we estimate the aggregate increase in VAT (12%) collections for gasoline and diesel would be able to offset the revenue losses if the excise tax collections from gasoline and diesel were suspended.

Marcos suspends excise tax on kerosene and LPG; gas and diesel unchanged
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. approved the suspension of excise taxes on kerosene and liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) but did not remove the levies on gasoline and diesel as it was still up for discussion
  • The reduction for kerosene and LPG would be equivalent to P5.60/liter and P3.36/kg (P37 per tank), respectively
    • Kerosene and LPG prices are currently around P154-P177.2/liter and P1,000-P1,600/tank
  • Marcos stated that the Unified Package for Livelihoods, Industry, Food, and Transport (UPLIFT) Committee will meet to discuss and decide on the possible reduction or suspension of gasoline and diesel excise taxes
    • Excise taxes are at P10/liter for gas and P6/liter for diesel
  • The President stated that he was not supportive of calls to cut or suspend the VAT on oil as revenues are needed to fund aid programs for the public
    • However, Marcos mentioned that he remains open for a future review if necessary
      • Note that there currently is no law permitting the President to suspend the VAT on fuel, so a measure needs to be passed into law through Congress
    • The Dept. of Finance (DOF) estimated a revenue loss of P120bn should the VAT on fuel be reduced or removed
