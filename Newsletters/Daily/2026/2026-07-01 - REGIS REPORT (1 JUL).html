REGIS REPORT

01 July 2026

The National Capital Region (NCR) Regional Tripartite Wages and Productivity Board (RTWPB) approved an P85 (+12%) daily minimum wage hike in Metro Manila, increasing the daily minimum wage to P780/day. This will be implemented in two tranches: P60 beginning July 19 and the remaining P25 beginning January 20, 2027.

 

The Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) estimates that the wage hike will directly benefit 1.1mn minimum wage workers in Metro Manila and indirectly benefit another 1.9mn workers earning slightly above the minimum wage.

 

While this wage hike only covers Metro Manila, it sets the stage for other regions to implement their own large wage hikes in the coming months. DOLE estimates that there are 4.7mn minimum wage workers nationwide and there are another 8.8mn workers earning slightly above minimum wage.  

 

The 12% wage hike is the highest percentage increase in the past 20 years and is likely to be taken negatively in the near-term. However, consumer companies targeting lower-income customers could see a more moderate impact thanks to the higher purchasing power of their buyers.

His hesitance to provide stronger guidance might mean there is risk to the consensus forecast of 13% DPS growth this year. Recall that in 1Q26, Meralco’s profit rose 2% while its distribution volume fell 2%.

BSP expects June inflation to settle within 6.0-7.0%
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) in its month-ahead inflation forecast expects June inflation to fall within 6.0-7.0%, implying a potential deceleration from the 6.8% print in May
    • BSP expectations for June inflation are well above the BSP's 2-4% long-term target range
      • For 2026, BSP expectations for inflation this year is 6.4% (previously revised from 6.3%)
    • June inflation data will be released by the PSA (Philippine Statistics Authority) on July 7 (Tuesday)
  • Upward price pressures stem from higher prices of electricity and vegetables
    • These may have been partially offset by lower prices of domestic petroleum, rice, and meat
  • Inflation has averaged 4.5% so far as of 5M26
    • For 2026, consensus expectations are at 6.0%
    • Regis expects inflation to average 7.5% this year
  • The next Monetary Board meeting is on Aug 27 (fourth out of six scheduled this year)
    • For 2026, consensus expects the BSP to conduct one more 25bp rate hike to 5.00%
    • Regis expects three more 25bp hikes to 5.50% by year-end

 

 

 

MACRO
Trade deficit widens 51% YoY to $5.5bn in May
  • The May trade deficit widened to US$5.5bn (+51% YoY) as imports growth continued to outpace exports growth
    • This brings the 5M26 trade deficit to US$25.2bn, 26% wider YoY
  • Imports for the month climbed 22% YoY to US$13.4bn, steady from the 22% increase in Apr
    • The increase in imports was supported by strong growth across the categories such as raw and intermediate goods (+33%), capital goods (+23%), and fuel (+36%), which offset the 5% decline in consumer goods
  • Exports growth picked up to 8% YoY (US$7.9bn) from 6% the previous month
    • This marks the seventeenth consecutive month of YoY growth in exports
    • Growth in electronics exports rebounded to +12% from +1% in Apr
    • Meanwhile, non-electronics exports were up +3%, moderating from +11% the previous month

CNPF reaffirms capex spend and double-digit growth targets for 2026
  • Century Pacific Food (CNPF PM) in its annual stockholders' meeting stated that the company is maintaining its P8-9bn capex program as well as its double-digit growth targets for both revenue and profit this year
  • CNPF President and CEO Teodoro Alexander T. Po stated that the capex spend for expanded capacity for all its segments across the board is "necessary and required" with a significant portion allocated to expand OEM production capacity with results expected to show beginning next year
  • Po also emphasized that despite 2Q being "quite challenging", the company is still seeing double-digit growth on both the branded and OEM business segments
    • He expects 2H26 to be similar or slightly better than 1H26 driven by increased brand investments and alleviating geopolitical pressures
  • CNPF in 1Q26 saw consolidated revenue and net profit grow 15% YoY and 10% YoY, respectively
    • The branded and OEM business posted YoY growth of 11% and 32%

    • According to Meralco Chairman and CEO Manuel V. Pangilinan

MARKET
PSEi August 2026 predictions: CNVRG (-), MYNLD (+)
  • Based on our estimates, Converge Information and Communications Technology Solutions (CNVRG PM) will be removed from the PSE Index during the August 2026 rebalancing
    • We expect Maynilad Water Services (MYNLD PM) to take its place
  • The PSE Index rebalancing in August 2026 will be based on the 12 months from July 2025 to June 2026
  • Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap
    • For companies that have been listed for less than 12 months (such as MYNLD), the stocks must be among the top 20% in terms of median daily trade per month for at least six months or 75% of the total number of months it has been listed, whichever is higher 
  • Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when
    • 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or
    • 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35 
  • Based on our estimates, MYNLD is ranked within the top 25
    • Thus, the lowest ranked PSE Index constituent will be removed
  • Our preliminary estimates suggest that MYNLD could see inflow of US$24.7mn while CNVRG could see outflow of US$13.7mn
