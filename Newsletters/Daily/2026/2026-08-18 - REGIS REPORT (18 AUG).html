Jollibee (JFC PM) held an earnings briefing yesterday to discuss 2Q results. Plenty of data (minutiae) was provided but the underlying story is that margins (and profits) improved QoQ following price adjustments implemented during the period (with more hikes looming). Can the consumer take more of the increases? 2Q SSSG already shows some of the impact on consumers, so we are apprehensive.

 

Management also outlined its new (reduced) guidance for various operating/financial metrics for this year. It did not address the question of whether it’s keeping (or abandoning) its aim of reaching P24bn profit by 2028. More in the note.

CONGLOS
First Philippine Holdings rejects KKR offer on valuation
  • First Philippine Holdings (FPH PM) has rejected KKR’s proposal to buy the former’s 8.4% stake in First Gen (FGEN PM) for P35/sh
    • KKR, which already owns 19.9% of FGEN, submitted a non-binding proposal to FPH on July 10
    • KKR’s proposal included a subsequent offer to launch a tender offer for FGEN’s public float at P35/sh
  • FPH has determined that KKRs proposal does not represent FGEN’s true value
  • FPH holds a 67.84% economic stake in FGEN
  • Recall that KKR, through Valorous Asia Hldgs, acquired an initial 11.9% economic stake (8.4% voting stake) in FGEN when it undertook a tender offer at P22.50/sh in 2020
    • KKR undertook another tender offer for FGEN in September 2021 at an offer price of P33/sh
    • KKR’s offer price of P35/sh is at a 26% discount to FGEN’s NAV of P47/sh, which values EDC at its 2018 tender offer valuation of ~US$2bn
MACRO
Remittances rise by 1.7% in June
  • Cash remittances amounted US$3.0bn in Jun, 1.7% higher YoY—a deceleration from the 2.0% growth in May,
    • Remittance growth was primarily driven by Japan (+2.8%) and Singapore (+3.7%)
  • The June growth rate is the lowest YoY growth figure since Feb 2022 (+1.3%)
    • Remittance growth since March has been around 2.0%, below the typical 3-4% range
    • Growth in remittances from countries in the Middle East slowed down to 2-3% from 3-4% in May

 

 

 

PROPERTY
Legislator seeks two-year suspension of property valuation law
  • House Ways and Means Committee Chairman Romero Quimbo filed House Bill No. 10808 (HB 10808), which seeks to suspend the adoption of new or updated Schedules of Market Values (SMVs) and general property assessments, which are key components of the Real Property Valuation and Assessment Reform Act (RPVARA)
    • President Marcos signed the RPVARA into law in 2024
  • Under HB 10808, current SMVs, zonal values, assessed values and other valuation bases will remain in effect during the two-year suspension for purposes of national and local taxation, assessments, fees, charges and government transactions
  • Mr. Quimbo said the suspension would allow government agencies to continue preparatory work for the implementation of RPVARA
  • The RPVARA transfers the responsibility of updating the “Schedule of Market Values” (from which real property taxes are based) from Local Government Units (such as city governments) to the national government
  • While raising real property taxes would expand the coffers of a city government, it could also lead to the mayor (and other officials) losing the next election. As a result, local governments in many places are hesitant to regularly update their Schedule of Market Values. As a result, there are cities/municipalities that might not have updated it for over 10 years
  • RPVARA is slated to take full effect in 2028
POWER
ACEN signs agreement to partially divest a 49% stake in India solar project
  • ACEN completed the execution of a securities subscription and purchase agreement, which will allow it to sell its 49% stake in the 420MW Tejorupa solar project in India (project is under “Avana Renewables”)
    • Buyer is Dutch firm, Diamond India Renewables One BV (DIRO), a unit of Diamond Generating Asia (DGA), who is ACEN’s partner in other wind projects in Philippines (i.e. North Luzon Renewables and Capa Wind)
  • The valuation for this deal was not disclosed; Deal is subject to contractual and customary conditions precedent
    • Transaction is expected to close in stages, with DIRO acquiring an initial 10% voting interest in Avana
  • ACEN currently owns 100% of the Tejorupa project, which is expected to be completed in 2027
    • (Project completion rate stood at 26% as of Aug 11)
    • Tejorupa’s capacity is largely contracted under a 25-year fixed price offtake contract
