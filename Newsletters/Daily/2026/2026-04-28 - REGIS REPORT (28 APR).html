REGIS REPORT

28 April 2026

Aboitiz Power (AP PM) reported headline profit of P7.9bn (+71%) for 1Q26, with “core income” (ex- exceptional items) amounting to P7.6bn (+61%).

 

While the YoY bounce looks large, this is principally due to the low 1Q25 base, which was affected by outages. We reckon this profit result is actually trailing Street estimates slightly. Among the interesting things to note was the +6% growth in distribution utility sales volume. This stands in stark contrast to what Meralco (MER PM) is likely to report for 1Q26 (i.e. a YoY decline).

 

The company holds its briefing on May 11.

 

+++

 

The revenue share of Belle Corp (BEL PM) in the operations of City of Dreams Manila (CODM) amounted to P486m (US$8m) in 1Q26, down 16% QoQ but up 12% YoY. This is likely to be an indicator of the performance of the rest of the domestic gaming sector (e.g. Bloomberry (BLOOM PM)) for this quarter.

POWER
Aboitiz Power 1Q26 core income up 61% to P7.6bn
  • In a limited press release, Aboitiz Power disclosed headline 1Q26 net income rose 71% YoY to P7.9bn
    • After adjusting for FX and other non-recurring items, reported “core” profit was up 61% YoY to P7.6bn, which represents 22% of consensus’ FY26E (P34.4bn)
  • Beneficial EBITDA increased by 35% YoY to P20.3bn, driven by higher plant availability, contracted capacity, and the full quarter contribution of Chromite Gas
    • Note that Chromite Gas’ subsidiary, Excellent Energy, commenced commercial operations in 2Q25
  • Generation and RES EBITDA rose 41% YoY to P18.5bn
    • Energy sales rose 30% to 11,243 GWH, of which 9% was sold to the spot market
  • EBITDA for the distribution segment rose 13% to P2.3bn on the back of a 6% increase in electricity sales, which picked up from the 4% pace in 4Q25 and 5% in 1Q25
  • Its net debt to equity ratio was broadly stable QoQ at 1.3x in end-March 2026 but remained above end-2024 levels of 0.84x
  • Management is holding a briefing on May 11, at 4PM
  • AP trades at 9x FY26E consensus PER and 8x EV/EBITDA
