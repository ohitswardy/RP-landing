CONSUMER
Jollibee buys out partner in U.S. Tim Ho Wan for US$5mn, transfers Japan stores
  • Jollibee Foods Corp. (JFC PM) and WDI Corp. (3068 JP) have agreed to split the franchise rights for Tim Ho Wan, with JFC to take complete ownership of the brand's US stores while handing full control of the Japan stores to WDI
    • Tim Ho Wan, a fully owned Jollibee subsidiary, will acquire WDI's 30% stake in their North American joint venture for US$5mn (~P300mn)
    • WDI will purchase Tim Ho Wan's 30% interest in the Japanese joint venture for 166mn JPY (~P64mn)
    • The transaction is subject to regulatory approvals and is expected to be completed within 3Q26
  • Jollibee, in a disclosure, elaborated that the realignment will enable each partner "to focus its resources and expertise" in their respective markets
  • JFC plans to expand Tim Ho Wan's footprint in the US to 20 stores by 2028 from five currently
  • As of 1H26, Tim Ho Wan contributes to 2.0% of JFC's total SWS and is ~0.9% of global EBITDA
    • THW's global store network consists of 83 stores
TELCOS
VITRO REIT IPO could be delayed
  • According to a media outlet, PLDT could defer the VITRO REIT IPO to later in the fourth quarter of 2026 in order to give more time to secure cornerstone investors
    • Previously announced offer period: September 25 to October 1
    • Previously announced listing date: October 12
  • The Maynilad Water Services (MYNLD PM) IPO in 2025 was also delayed by a few months in order to give cornerstone investors more time
  • VITRO REIT’s initial portfolio is comprised of eight data center assets with an aggregate Total IT Ready Capacity of approximately 24MW
  • 1.91bn secondary common shares with an over-allotment option of up to 287mn shares
    • Total offer of up to 2.2bn secondary common shares
  • The offer shares (assuming full exercise of the over-allotment option) represent 48.95% of total outstanding shares
  • Offer price of up to P11/share
    • Maximum amount to be raised (including over-allotment option) – P24.2bn
    • Maximum market capitalization of P49.4bn
  • Use of proceeds:
    • P6.7bn – invest in PLDT Group’s Data Center, telecommunications equipment, and network infrastructure
    • P3.8bn – to be infused as equity into VITRO Sta. Rosa
    • P12.8bn – to pay debt used for the construction of VITRO Santa Rosa
  • VITRO REIT is targeting to pay DPS of P0.68/share in FY27
    • This represents FY27 dividend yield of 6.2% (At the maximum offer price of P11/share)
MARKET
Foreign ownership of Philippine market rises in July
  • We estimate that foreign ownership of the overall market rose 60bps MoM to 21.5% (in July) while foreign ownership of the PSE Index increased 43bps MoM to 25.0%
  • Foreign activity reversed to net foreign buying of US$66m, from net foreign selling of US$22mn in June
    • The increase in foreign ownership appears to have been further boosted by the outperformance of high foreign-owned stocks, such as MONDE and GLO
  • Among the PSEi constituents, 15 saw increases in foreign ownership while 15 experienced declines
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were:
    • CNVRG – foreign ownership expanded 87bps MoM to 15.8%.
    • CNPF – foreign ownership increased 43bps MoM to 21.6%. It also saw the fifth highest net foreign buying for the month (at US$5.6mn).   
    • ICT – foreign ownership rose 39bps MoM to 37.9%, as it saw the highest net foreign buying for the third consecutive month (at US$85.5mn). 

 

  • RCR – foreign ownership declined 29bps MoM to 6.1%.
  • ALI – foreign ownership fell 20bps MoM to 20.7%, as net foreign selling hit US$17.8mn, the highest for the month
  • SM – foreign ownership decreased 20bps MoM to 32.0%, as net foreign selling reached US$8.9mn, the fourth highest for the month
