REGIS REPORT

05 June 2026

The statistics agency reported a surprise drop in headline CPI inflation to 6.8% for the month of May. This is lower than the 7.2% registered for April and also lower than the Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) range as well as expectations by the Street and Regis.

 

Will this “lower than expected” inflation print alter the BSP’s policy direction, especially in relation to the impending Monetary Board meeting (on June 18)? We think not. While it may give BSP policy makers a pause, we don’t think there will be a change in the near-term direction. Pump prices have indeed come off from their highs. But with many big consumer companies already raising prices in May (after being “persuaded” to hold back until last month), we expect inflation to still head higher.

Gaming agency PAGCOR is expecting industry-wide gaming revenue (GGR) of P320bn to P350bn (US$5.2bn to US$5.7bn) for the year. This would represent a decline of 12-19% over the previous year. With 1Q26 GGR at P88bn, the PAGCOR forecast would also mean that quarterly revenue for FY26 from hereon will be broadly unchanged, if not slightly lower. 

 

We note that a new casino, being completed by the Alliance Global (AGI PM) group, is expected to open in the Entertainment City area in the next few months. This will mean the industry gaming pie will be cut into more (and smaller) slices.

 

+++

 

Online gaming firm Digiplus (PLUS PM) completed its investment of HKD1.6bn into convertible notes of International Entertainment Corporation (1009 HK). IEC operates the New Coast Hotel Manila, which has a casino. The investment will allow PLUS to reduce the fees it pays the government (PAGCOR).  Purely online gaming operators pay higher license fees compared to the online business of integrated casinos (i.e. those with physical casinos).

 

+++

 

Air Asia Philippines (AAP) avoided being banned from domestic airports after it paid overdue fees yesterday. AAP has a market of 14% for domestic routes and 4% for international routes.

GAMING
PAGCOR expects gaming revenue to decline in 2026
  • PAGCOR Chairman Alejandro Tengco said that industry GGR is likely to be between P320bn and P350bn in 2026
  • This range reflects a 12%-19% decline from the 2025 figure of P396bn
  • It also implies GGR could decline further from 1Q26 levels
  • He cited the delinking of e-wallets from online gambling platforms as well as the Middle East Crisis as the reasons for the forecasted decline
  • 1Q26 industry GGR fell 16% YoY (up 1% QoQ) to P87.6bn
    • GGR of bingo + e-games fell 22% YoY (down 1% QoQ) to P39.9bn
    • GGR of (land-based) licensed casinos declined 10% YoY (up 2% QoQ) to P44.5bn
    • GGR of PAGCOR operated casinos decreased 8% YoY (up 10% QoQ) to P3.2bn
GAMING
DigiPlus Interactive completes subscription to convertible notes of integrated resort operator
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) acquired the second and final tranche of convertible notes from International Entertainment Corporation (1009 HK)
  • Second tranche – HK$0.8bn
  • Total – HK$1.6bn
  • IEC, through its subsidiaries, owns and operates New Coast Hotel Manila, an integrated hotel and casino complex
  • New Coast Hotel Manila currently offers 203 guest rooms and operates 96 gaming tables, and 495 slot machines
    • Under its provisional gaming license, IEC is required to spend at least US$1bn to develop the integrated resort. The resort complex will have at least 250,000sqm of GFA and at least 800 5-star hotel rooms
  • PLUS has the option to convert the notes and could hold c. 53.9% of IEC’s issued capital stock, based on an agreed initial conversion price of HK$1.00/share (higher than the last price of HK$1.24/share)
    • The notes carry an interest rate of 3% per annum
    • If PLUS will not convert, the notes will be redeemable at 108% at the end of their five-year maturity
    • The notes can be converted beginning 31 days after issuance
  • For the year ending June 2025, IEC reported a net loss of HK$282mn (c. P2.1bn)
  • Integrated resort operators are charged c. 9ppt less on their e-casino GGR than pure online operators
    • DigiPlus has been availing of the lower fees to PAGCOR since 4Q25, when it first announced the convertible notes agreement with IEC
TRANSPORT
AirAsia Philippines pays CAAP obligations, avoids shutdown
  • The Civil Aviation Authority confirmed that AirAsia Philippines (AAP) has complied with the directive to settle its obligations amounting to P272mn
    • CAAP further noted that the settlement remains “subject to ongoing standard reconciliation processes”
  • AAP has a market share of 14% on domestic routes and 4% on international routes
MARKET
PSE publishes draft amendments to market making rules
  • The PSE published draft amendments to its market making rules
    • Stakeholders can send their comments to the PSE until June 23
  • The current market making regulations only apply to ETFs but the PSE seeks to expand the scope of products that can be subject to market making, including Global Philippine Depositary Receipts
  • Obligations of a market maker:
    • A Market Maker must ensure two-way quotes (buy and sell orders) with a maximum spread
    • Market making orders posted by a Market Maker must have a minimum quantity of five board lots
    • Market Makers shall maintain Market Making Orders of at least 50% of the time for any given trading day
