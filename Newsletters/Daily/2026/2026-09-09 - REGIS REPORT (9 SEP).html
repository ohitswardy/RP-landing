REGIS REPORT

09 September 2026

Dominion Holdings (DHI PM) announced that the planned injection of the mammoth Tampakan copper-gold project is expected to take place in 4Q26, with the exchange ratio likely to be determined ahead of the shareholder vote scheduled on September 14.

 

+++

 

ABS-CBN Corp (ABS PM) will issue 1.64bn new shares at P3.65/share, raising P6bn and increasing its shares outstanding by 150%. The shares will be issued to Lopez Inc, various holding companies of the Lopez family, and I&C, a firm run by investment bankers. After the issuance, I&C will own 27% of voting stock (38% of common shares) while Lopez Inc’s ownership will fall from 78% to 44% of voting stock.  

 

+++

 

Preliminary data showed that commercial bank loan growth accelerated to 10.4% in July, from 9.8% in June. Although consumer loan growth decelerated to 17.1% (from 17.8% in June), loans to productive activities picked up to 9.8% (from 9.2%). Meanwhile, M3 growth slowed to 10.3%, from 10.7% in June.

MINING
Dominion Holdings sets Tampakan copper-gold project takeover in 4Q
  • Dominion Holdings said that the merger with Indophil Resources and Sonar Holdings is expected to be fully implemented by 4Q26, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals
    • DHI shareholders are scheduled to vote on the transaction at its annual shareholder’s meeting on September 14, with the exchange ratio set to be finalized prior to that date
  • Indophil and Sonar own 100% voting interest in Sagittarius Mines Inc. (SMI), which is the entity that owns the FTAA for the Tampakan Copper-Gold project
    • Indophil is 87.93%-owned by the Sy family, 10% by the Consunji family (through DFC Holdings), and ~2% by the Alcantara family
    • Sonar is an investment holding company of the Sy family
    • SMI’s amended FTAA (1st 25-year term) is set to expire on March 22, 2038, suggesting that the FTAA can still be extended by another 25 years
    • Tampakan is one of the largest undeveloped copper-gold deposits in South-East Asia, with an estimated 15mn MT of copper and 17.9mn ounces of gold
    • Its resource/reserve estimates have not been updated since 2012; However, with the Philippine Mineral Reporting Code compliance deadline set for January 2027, we reckon the company will need to update its estimates in accordance with the new reporting code by then
  • Exchange ratio will be based on the latest audited financial statements of the constituent companies, while an independent third-party valuator will issue a fairness opinion
    • Indophil’s book equity stood at P23.6bn in end-2025
    • DHI’s book equity stood at P3.5bn in end-June 2026
MEDIA
ABS-CBN to issue new shares
  • ABS-CBN Corp (ABS PM) will issue 1.64bn new shares at P3.65
    • Total value of P6bn
    • P3.65/share was also the last closing price
    • This will increase shares outstanding by c. 150% 
  • I&C will subscribe to 960mn shares (P3.5bn)
  • Lopez family holding companies will subscribe to c. 600mn shares (P2.2bn)
  • Lopez Inc will subscribe to c. 80mn shares (P300mn)
  • After the transaction,
    • I&C will hold 27% of voting stock (38% of common shares)
    • Lopez Inc’s ownership fill fall from 78.4% to 44.3% of voting stock
    • Lopez family holding companies will hold 17% of voting stock
  • The P6bn will be used for working capital and to pay down debt
  • ABS recorded a net loss of P1.6bn in 1H26, higher than the P715mn loss in 1H25
  • I&C is run by Fortman Cline founders Daniel D. Ibasco and Gary Emerson P. Cheng, along with corporate secretary Clarisse Darlene Rose Tan
BANKS
Commercial banks’ loan growth accelerates to 10.4% in July
  • Preliminary data showed that commercial bank loan growth accelerated to 10.4% in Jul from 9.8% in Jun
    • Loans to productive activities picked up to 9.8% vs 9.2% the previous month
    • Consumer loans grew 17.1%, moderating from Jun's 17.8% rate
      • This marks the slowest pace of growth since Jul 2022 (14.7%)
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) growth eased to 10.3% from 10.7% the previous month

Meralco seeks ERC approval for 20-year Cagbalete microgrid power supply agreement
  • In a regulatory filing, Meralco is seeking the Energy Regulatory Commission’s approval of its microgrid system 20-year provider service contract with National Power Corp.
    • It is also seeking the issuance of an authority to operate its 2.8MWp solar + 6.69MW BESS + 1MW diesel backup unit, which were energized in December
    • It is proposing a full cost recovery rate of P39.17/kwh, of which P12/kwh will be collected from residential customers and the balance would be covered by the universal charge subsidy
