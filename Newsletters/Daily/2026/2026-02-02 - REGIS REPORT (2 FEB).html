REGIS REPORT

02 February 2026

Volumes at the ICTSI’s (ICT PM) Manila International Container Terminal (MICT) declined 2% in 4Q25, compared to +8% growth over 9M25.  The dip appears symptomatic of the slowdown experienced across large parts of the economy in recent months following the massive corruption scandal 3Q of last year.

 

+++

 

Government infrastructure spending plunged 35% YoY in Nov to P69bn, in no small due to the controversies that erupted. As a result, YT-Nov infrastructure spend is now down 13%

 

In response, the administration is hoping to perk up activity by 1H of this year and is earmarking a 17% increase in government primary spending to P1.4trn within 1Q26.

 

+++

 

 

PAGCOR reported that the fees it collected from licensed land-based casinos fell 5% YoY in 2025. While a decline in revenue is generally not welcome news, we reckon this actually signals an improvement in 4Q25. Based on our preliminary estimates, 4Q25 GGR of the licensed casinos rose 10%-15% QoQ and was flattish YoY, from a 7% decline in 9M25. If so, this would bode well for Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM), the largest land-based casino operator.

 

+++

 

Monde (MONDE PM) issued a press release saying it expects to report 4Q results in line with its guidance (mid-single digit growth, with sequential improvement in gross margins). This implies slight improvement in 4Q revenue growth from what was reported over 9M (+3% YoY).  Also of note was that Monde also raised the possibility of a “modest reversal” of previously recorded impairment losses.

 

+++

 

The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) extended the power supply contract between Meralco (MER PM) and the 1000MW Sta Rita natgas plant by another five months to June 2026.  Sta Rita is now controlled by the Razon group after 60% was sold by First Gen (FGEN PM).

TRANSPORT
ICTSI’s MICT port volumes dip 2% YoY in 4Q25; Nationwide foreign container volumes flat
  • Data from the Philippine Ports Authority showed that ICTSI’s MICT saw TEU volumes slip 2% YoY to 776k units in 4Q25, reversing the 8% growth rate in 9M25
    • In contrast, Asian Terminals (ATI PM) South Harbour saw volumes rise 5% YoY in 4Q25, decelerating sharply from +16% pace in 9M
    • Market share loss due to South Harbour’s headline 40% capacity increase in May (here)
    • We believe ATI’s market share gains will be short-lived, as MICT’s Berth 8 Phase 2 expansion should be completed in 2026
    • Brings MICT’s FY25 volumes up 5% to 3.07mn TEUs, just a tad short of our estimate (3.1mn TEUs, +6% YoY)
    • We estimate MICT accounts for 22% of volumes, 23% of revenues, and 20% of EBITDA
  • Nationwide foreign container volumes were YoY/QoQ flat at 1.5mn TEUs in 4Q25, bringing FY25 volumes up 8% YoY to 6.0mn TEUs
  • ICTSI trades at 11x FY26E EV/EBTDA and 19x PER vs. peer average (13x EBITDA, 18x PER) vs. historical average (11x EBITDA/19x PER)

 

 

 

 

 

GAMING
PAGCOR license fees from land-based casinos fall 5% in 2025
  • PAGCOR reported that the fees it collected from licensed (land-based) casinos fell 5% to P31.4bn in 2025
    • PAGCOR did not disclose the figures for 2Q25 and 3Q25
  • 9M25 GGR of licensed (land-based) casinos fell 7% to P139bn
  • From these figures, we estimate that GGR of licensed (land-based) casinos might have been flattish YoY (c. P52bn) in 4Q25
  • If this is the case, then 4Q25 GGR likely rose 10%-15% QoQ
    • Okada Manila reported that 4Q25 GGR fell 15% QoQ and 34% YoY to P5.9bn

 

 

MACRO
Government infrastructure spending plunges by 35% YoY in November; YTD infra spend down 13% YoY
  • Overall infrastructure spending (i.e. disbursements from national government, LGUs, and GOCCs) fell 13% YoY to P1.2tn in 11M25
    • Derived infrastructure spending in November stood at P69bn, down 22% MoM, 35% YoY
    • Cited delays in settlement of progress billings for public work’s projects amid the flood control corruption scandal
  • Note that the government recently reduced its infra spend target for 2026 to ~P1.3tn (~4.3% of GDP) from P1.56tn previously (~5.1% of GDP)

 

MACRO
Gov't allocates P1.4trn primary spending for 1Q26
  • Finance Secretary Frederick Go stated that the gov't has set aside P1.4trn in primary spending for 1Q26 to support economic recovery
    • This follows the recent disappointing 4Q25 and FY25 GDP prints of 3.0% and 4.4%, respectively
    • The P1.4trn targeted primary spending is 17% higher YoY than 1Q25's P1.2trn figure
    • As of 11M25, primary spending is up only 0.8% to P4.2trn
  • Go highlighted that he is constantly coordinating with the Dept. of Budget and Management (DBM) on the release of funds in order for the gov't to "get back on track this year"
  • Recall that the Development Budget Coordination Committee (DBCC) has reduced the gov't's GDP growth targets for 2026-2027
    • Revising the 2026-2028 6-7% target to 5-6% in 2026 and 5.5-6.5% in 2027, while keeping the 2028 6-7% target unchanged
    • For 2026/27, consensus and Regis expectations for GDP growth are at 5.2/6.0% and 5.5/5.7%, respectively
CONSUMER
Monde Nissin guides that 4Q25 results are consistent with previous guidance
  • MONDE in a disclosure stated that 4Q25 topline growth was inline with previous guidance of mid-single digit topline growth with gross margin improving sequentially
  • The company also highlighted that ongoing annual impairment review results imply a potential modest reversal of previously recorded impairment losses in its Meat Alternative business
  • MONDE also emphasized the possibility for a "potentially meaningful dividend distribution in FY 2026"
  • As of 9M25, MONDE topline grew 3% to P63.2bn
    • REGIS for 2025 forecasts MONDE topline to grow 5% YoY to P87.0bn
  • MONDE is trading at 8.8X FY26E P/E
POWER
ERC approves 5-month extension of Meralco-First Gas power supply contract
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) has approved a second interim extension of a power purchase agreement between Manila Electric Co, (Meralco) and First Gas Power Corp. (FGPC)
    • The extension allows the 1,000-megawatt Santa Rita natural gas power plant in Batangas to continue supplying Meralco until June 25, 2026
    • Note that this is the second extension granted since the original contract expired on Aug 28
    • Regulators said the extension maintains the same terms and conditions as the previous agreement
  • FGPC is 60%-owned by Razon-led Prime Infrastructure and 40% by First Gen (FGEN PM)

CONGLOS
DMCI set to break ground on Kalayaan, BGC subway stations this year
  • The construction arm of DMCI Holdings (DMC PM) said that it expects to break ground on contract package 105 (CP 105)
    • CP 105 contract amounts to P21.7bn (ex-VAT) and will cover a 0.66-kilometer tunnel, a 242.2-meter station in Kalayaan and a 436.05-meter station in Bonifacio Global City
    • DMCI previously said that it may take at least 67 months to complete the construction
  • Meanwhile, it expects to purse another subway project and expects the award decision as early as next month
    • Bidding for the said contract was completed about 18 months ago
  • DMCI’s order book stood at P32.8bn in end-September and doesn’t yet include CP105, in which it will own <50% stake
MACRO
Negotiations ongoing for P16bn regional airports development package
  • The Dept. of Transportation stated that it is under negotiation with proponents on the terms, scope, and coverage of the unsolicited proposal to develop the P16bn bundled development projects for the airports in Davao, Siargao, and Bicol
    • The unsolicited proposal was submitted by the Philippine Regional Airports Consortium which includes Filinvest Infra-Solutions Ventures, Inc. (subsidiary of FDC PM), JG Summit Infrastructure Holding Corp. (subsidiary of JGS PM), and Asian Infrastructure and Management Corp.
    • Once negotiations finish and the consortium gains Original Proponent Status, the Swiss Challenge process will begin where other parties may submit their respective counterproposals
  • The P16bn development project includes the development, operation, and maintenance of the Davao Int'l Airport, and Bicol Int'l Airport over a 30-year concession period, and Siargao over a 15-year concession period

SEC files criminal cases against Villar Land principals and officers

  • The following were named as respondents:
    • Manuel B. Villar, Jr. (Chairman)
    • Paolo Villar (Director)
    • Senator Camille Villar (Director)
    • Senator Mark Villar (Director)
    • Cynthia Alvarez (President)
    • Ana Marie Pagsibigan (Independent Director)
    • Garth Castaneda (Independent Director)
  • The SEC also charged related firms Infra Holdings Corp and MGS Construction, including its officers
  • The SEC alleges that Infra Holdings Corp and MGS Construction engaged in trades that artificially boosted the Villar Land share price
    • Virgilio B. Villar, who is also Chairman of Medilines Distributors (MEDIC PM) and brother of Manuel B. Villar Jr.
    • Josephine R. Bartolome
    • Jerry M. Navarrete
    • Joy J Fernandez
  • The SEC also accused Camille A. Villar of insider trading after allegedly buying 73,600 shares in December 2017 shortly before a disclosure that lifted the stock price
    • The company, which was called Golden Haven at the time (and sold memorial homes), disclosed that it would acquire its affiliate, Bria Homes (which developed mass housing) for about P3bn in shares
  • In 2025, the SEC imposed a fine of P1mn on Villar Land as well as its senior officers and directors
  • Furthermore, on 28 March 2025, HVN issued a disclosure stating that the company’s board had “approved and authorized the release of financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2024,” which reported fair value gains of P1.3trn, total assets of P1.4trn, and net profit of P1trn
    • However, the 2024 audited financial statements only showed P36bn in assets and P1.4bn in net profit
