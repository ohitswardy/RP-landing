REGIS REPORT

07 August 2020

The country’s statistics office declared that the Philippine economy shrank 16.5% in 2Q, which is much worse than the Street was expecting and just slightly off our own predictions. This is the larger GDP decline since most of us here have been alive, if not ever.

The impact of the pandemic and the lockdowns was clearly quite severe with nearly all segments posting sharp declines.  Mathemetically, net exports gave a lift because of the collapse in imports.

Near-term, we can draw (cold) comfort that the contraction in succeeding quarters is likely to be less severe.  Longer-term, there should be plenty of hand-wringing over how much damage this inflicts on the balance sheets of both individuals and businesses as that will likely determine the trajectory of recovery if and when the virus is contained.

The results we cover today all reinforce our views on the respective companies.  Both PLDT (TEL PM) and ICT (ICT PM) did better than we expected and the underlying performances suggest upside risk to Street numbers.  San Miguel F&B, on the other hand, did worse than anticipated.

PLDT (TEL PM)(+) - Core income (including losses at Voyager) came in P13bn for 1H (+5%) and P6.6bn for 2Q (+15%), trending better than our expectations.  The outperformance can be attributed to the resilience of service revenue, which did not dip as feared.  We estimate PLDT gained over 1ppt of market share in wireless revenue over the quarter.

ICTSI (ICT PM) (+) - 1H20 profit after perps fell 11% YoY to US$85mn, trending well above our forecast.  2Q20 profit remained flat at US$40mn, as higher ancillary revenues and lower costs compensated for the 11% drop in volumes due to the pandemic-induced global lockdowns.  With volumes already recovering from the trough in 2Q, we reckon the direction of earnings should already be firmly up. 

San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) (-) – FB’s profit in 2Q20 fell 86%yoy to P542mn. Its food segment incurred an estimated loss of P298mn in 2Q as poultry demand and prices collapsed on the closure of restaurants and hotels. Beer volumes plunged more than 60%, while gin volumes were surprisingly resilient, down just single digits.

MACRO
DBCC cuts GDP outlook on pandemic impact
 
  • The Dept. of Budget Coordination Committee (DBCC) downgraded its GDP forecast to -5.5% for FY20 from -2% to - 3.4% previously
    • Cited updated indicators on the COVID-19 pandemic on tourism, trade, and remittances
  • GDP forecasts for 2021 and 2022 were also lowered to 6.5%-7.5%
    • Previous forecast was +8-9% for 2021 and 6-7% for 2022
  • GDP forecasts already take into account the effect of the recent two-week MECQ
MACRO
BIR, BOC collections down nearly 30% YoY, 8% YoY in July; Collections exceed government targets for the month
  • Collections by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) in July reached P126.72bn, c. 30% lower than the P180bn collected last July 2019
    • However, this was 2% higher than the agency’s revised goal of P124bn
    • Cited tax gains from both excise and value-added taxes
  • Meanwhile, the Bureau of Customs (BOC) collected P50.07bn in July, down by 8% from last year’s collection of P54.6bn
    • BOC collections were higher than target of P47.67bn
INFRASTRUCTURE
Eight flagship infrastructure projects worth P370bn shelved
  • The government has shelved eight major infrastructure projects worth P370bn under its flagship program
  • According to Presidential adviser for flagship infrastructure projects, Vivencio B. Dizon
  • Projects shelved were:
    • P175.66bn Bataan-Cavite Interlink Bridge
    • P13.94bn New Zamboanga International Airport
    • P6.94bn New Dumaguete Airport
    • P9.5bn Dalton Pass East Alignment Alternative Road
    • the Guimaras-Negros segment of the P189.526 bn Panay-Guimaras Negros Bridge
    • P56bn Kanan Dam project
    • P31.22 bn Kabulnan-2 Multipurpose Irrigation and Power Project in Mindanao
    • P19.36 bn Panay River Basin Integrated Development Project
  • These projects are still under feasibility studies and will be revisited next quarter to see the progress of the studies
  • It also added 13 new projects that it deems crucial for post-pandemic recovery.  These projects are “ready to go and shovel-ready”
    • No amount given for the replacement projects, which comprise four IT-related projects, five water projects, 3 transport projects, and 1 health sector project
  • Government has retained 92 infrastructure projects worth P4.1 tn, out of its original list of 100 projects worth P4.4 tn
  • All projects under the revised list will be started before President Rodrigo R. Duterte ends his six-year term by 2022
RETAIL
Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) posts P494mn net loss in 2Q20
 
  • Philippine Seven reported a net loss of P390mn in 1H20, and a net loss of P494mn in 2Q20 alone.
  • Same store sales dropped 26% YoY during the quarter, largely due to the temporary store closure
    • SEVN temporarily shut as much as 30% of its store network at the onset of the lockdown in April
    • 95% of its stores have resumed operations as of July
  • Apart from the temporary closures, not all stores were able to operate 24/7 given the curfews imposed
  • The SSS decline continued to track c.20% in July, though sales did pick up during its 7/11 Day (July 11), where many discounts and promotions were offered
