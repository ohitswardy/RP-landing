REGIS REPORT

19 October 2020

Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM), the retail operation of the Gokongwei group, announced that it is acquiring the leading pharmacy chain the Visayas region, Rose Pharmacy. RRHI will acquire 100% of Rose Pharmacy from Dairy Farm (D01 SP) for P4.5bn (US$93m) in cash. Dairy Farm is the retail arm of the Jardine group, and owns 12% of RRHI.

The transaction will add 300 stores to RRHI’s network, concentrated mostly in the Visayas region, with some in Mindanao. We view the transaction as a significant one for RRHI’s pharmacy business, adding c.50% to its segment's revenue, further consolidating a fragmented industry and narrowing the gap between RRHI and the market leaders. At 0.5x sales, the deal appears reasonably priced (even it is barely making money today), assuming it can reach 4% net margin soon. And this is before any possible synergies and the strategic value that can be created from its network in areas where RRHI is less present.

The government is easing Covid-19-related restrictions further in an effort to revive the economy as we approach the holiday season. The ban on non-essential foreign travel is being lifted and domestic tourism within the country is being allowed. These measures are on top of a shorter curfew in Metro Manila and more people being allowed on the rail lines.

Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP), majority owned by GT Capital (GTCAP PM), modestly increased its FY20 expectation for vehicle sales in a sign of improving confidence.

Property broker and consulting firm Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) is warning of further POGO closures over the next few months. This is likely to affect not just office rentals but also the broader property market as well. Property values have risen sharply because of the POGOs, most visibly in the areas adjacent to Manila Bay.

PH Resorts (PHR PM), controlled by the Davao Dennis Uy, priced its follow-on offer at P1.68/share, hoping to raise as much as P756m to help fund its casino-resort in Cebu. PHR is developing Emerald Bay on Mactan Island, an integrated resort casino, after it was granted a gaming license by the administration. It is unlikely the amount to be raised from the offering will be nearly enough to complete the US$600m project, however. PHR already has over P6bn of debt on its books and aims to complete the initial phase of Emerald Bay by 2022.

PROPERTY
Leechiu Property Consultants expects further POGO closures
  • David Leechiu of Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) said that he expects POGO office vacancy to reach 17% in 2020 and 20-30% in 2021
  • This is broadly in line with our estimates
  • We estimate that POGOs occupied about 1.3m sqm of Metro Manila office space in 2019 and employed 300,000-400,000 Chinese employees
    • They occupied c. 11% of Metro Manila office stock and 60% of Manila Bay area offices
  • We estimate that POGOs will shrink their Metro Manila office space by
    • 200,000sqm in 2020 (about 100,000sqm were vacated in 9M19)
    • 200,000sqm in 2021
    • 100,000sqm in 2022
  • PAGCOR previously reported that 10 out of 60 POGOs have shut down and only 32 of the remaining 50 have resumed operations
  • About 70% of the office space occupied by POGOs is not PEZA accredited and would generally not be used by BPOs
  • POGOs occupy about 25% of FLI’s office portfolio and 15% of MEG’s office portfolio
  • Office leasing accounted
  • FLI is currently trading at 4.2X FY21E P/E, 0.3X FY21E P/B, and 72% discount to NAV
  • MEG is currently trading at 6.5X FY21E P/E, 0.5X FY21E P/B, and 60% discount to NAV
  • About 40% of SM Prime’s NAV is comprised of land in the Manila Bay area
  • SMPH is currently trading at 25.5X FY21E P/E, 2.6X FY21E P/B, and 19% discount to NAV
