REGIS REPORT

10 December 2020

The Philippine banking sector NPL ratio increased 22bps MoM to 3.80% in October, up from 3.58% in September (revised upwards from 3.50% as previously reported). In nominal terms, NPLs jumped 5.6% MoM to P391bn. Restructured loans (RLs) also rose 4.8% MoM, with the ratio rising to 1.28% of loans. This takes the “stressed” loan level to 5.1%.

Worryingly, NPL coverage continued to slip, now below 90% at 88.8%. Meanwhile loan growth slowed further to 0.8% YoY, with loan balances now down 4.3% year-to-date.

+++

Congress passed the 2021 national budget, which amounted to P4.5trn worth of spending (vs P4.1trn for 2020). Among the departments receiving the biggest budget increases were Health (+62% vs 2020) and Public Works (+58%). The allocation for the DOH includes a large lump sum for the procurement of vaccines, while the increase for DPWH is likely reflective of the administration’s view that infrastructure spending is the best way to boost the economy.  Also notable was the large cut in allocation for the Transportation department (-52%).

With this approval, the government will hopefully avoid a repeat of the 2019 experience which led to severe underspending.

We also note the plan by Congress to allow the budget allocations for 2020, including those under the Bayanihan Acts, to be carried over into next year. Assuming the government is actually able to deploy the cash (which is a big IF), we may get a substantial lift from government spending next year. Experience, however, has already taught us to rein in our expectations.

+++

In an online forum yesterday, PAGCOR Chairman Andrea Domingo talked about the recently approved online-betting platform for land-based casinos. “Liveshots”, as she calls it, which will initially involve a video stream between a live dealer and a VIP player, and is aimed to recoup some of the industry’s lost revenues due to restrictions on physical play.

She added that PAGCOR will be collecting a fee of 25% on GGR, similar to the current fee structure for the mass segment of bricks and mortar casinos. Only those with land-based casinos will be allowed to apply/engage, and they will have to facilitate payments (players should physically go to deposit/collect from the property).

Domingo said that Liveshots will initially be available only to Philippine-based VIP players, who accounted for 5-10% of pre-pandemic GGR. However, it is not a stretch to imagine that this could, eventually, morph into something else entirely.

Banks' bad loans hit record-high in October

    • Highest on record since 2013
    • Higher than the 3.58% in September 2020
  • Gross non-performing loans rose 69.9% YoY and 5.56% MoM to P391.4bn
  • Allowance for credit losses have been trending below NPL over the past 2 months at P347.7bn as of October
    • NPL coverage ratio sank to their record-low of 88.83%
  • Non-performing assets surged 48.8% YoY to P504.83bn
    • Including restructured loans, total distressed assets now account for 5.72% of banks’ real estate properties
