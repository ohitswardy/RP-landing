REGIS REPORT

13 October 2020

In a (legally questionable) show of force, congressmen backing Rep Lord Velasco voted to install him as speaker. While there might be doubt as to the legality of the session yesterday which removed Alan Cayetano and elected Velasco, it does appear that Velasco has the numbers. 186 congressmen (of 299) voted in the ‘session’ held in a sports club 5km from the halls of the Batasan.

One would normally expect a parliamentary leader who no longer commands a majority to quickly step down. Cayetano, however, still refuses to concede and insists on pushing through with the deliberations on and approval of the 2021 budget.

The apparent numbers behind Velasco suggest that the bitter fight over the Speakership will be over quickly. The House can then approve the budget during the special session and hopefully avoid a repeat of the 2019 budget delay.

+++

The government is allowing more jeepneys, the public utility vehicles ubiquitous in the Philippines, to get back on the road.  We estimate the number of jeepneys allowed back on the road are now close to half of what they were pre-pandemic (check out the table below). The absence of public transport has been the key reason holding back the gradual return to a semblance of “normalcy”.

Meanwhile, speaking of transport, vehicle sales are showing continued MoM improvement with Sept unit sales (from CAMPI-TMA) climbing 37% from Aug. Sales of Toyota vehicles grew 32% MoM, and appear on track to meet our FY20 assumptions (90k unit sales).

+++

Infrastructure spending by the government dropped 25% YoY and 15% MoM in August. The budgets for infrastructure this year have been cut and diverted to address the pandemic so the fall is not a surprise.

+++

Foreign ownership levels (%) in the Philippine stock market fell another 30bp in Sept. At 22.7%, this is the lowest it’s been since we first started tracking the data.

MACRO
Infra spend drops further in August
  • Infrastructure spending (excluding subsidies to GOCCs and LGU transfers) dropped further by 25% YoY, 15% MoM in August to P44.3bn
  • Brings YT-August infra spend down 11.5% to P394.5bn

    • This target is c. 7% higher than what was spent in 2019 for infrastructure (P1.05tn)

Car sales rise 37% MoM in September
  • Industry car sales reached 24,523 units in September, down 23% YoY but up 37% MoM
    • According to the Chamber of Automotive Manufacturers of the Philippines (CAMPI) and the Truck Manufacturers Assoc.
    • Brings 9M20 industry car sales down 45% YoY to 148,012 units

  • Toyota accounts for 23% of GTCAP’s FY20 profit estimate and 30% of NAV

 

CONGLOS
San Miguel gets Senate OK on airport franchise
  • With 22 affirmative votes and zero negative, the Senate approved House Bill No. 7507, which grants San Miguel Aerocity a franchise for the Bulacan airport project
    • Franchise exempts the grantee from direct and indirect taxes during the 10-year construction period after which an income tax and real estate tax exemption will be granted
    • The exemption, however, will expire once the Bureau of Internal Revenue finds the investment cost of the airport and airport city has been recovered
  • Upon expiration, San Miguel Aerocity will then be entitled to a revenue share worth 12% of the internal rate of return (IRR) annually
    • The amount in excess of the 12% IRR will be remitted to the government
  • The franchise provided that San Miguel Aerocity should be able to commence construction within one year from the signing of the franchise into law. Otherwise, the franchise may be revoked
