REGIS REPORT

05 July 2020

With profits up 17% to P1.8bn, Puregold (PGOLD PM) results were just a tad below expectations. Margins for its two brands heading in divergent directions. Manufacturers appear to be holding off on support and incentives for the trade in this environment, leading to a margin dip. Meanwhile, Shakey’s (PIZZA PM) disclosed a 34% profit fall to P114m resulting from the temporary store closures implemented in March. More notably, the company is also warning about its numbers in 2Q.

In this new report from Aaron, we refresh our numbers and maintain our Buy rating for LT Group (LTG PM) with a revised target price of P13.50/share. We are keenly aware that tobacco (and this stock) is out of favor because of ESG concerns. But at 4x PER, we think this name deserves a second look. The dividend yield of ~6% is predicated on a payout of only 20%, which is current policy. With minimal cash needs and parent net debt after this year, and a steady inflow of dividends from its tobacco unit PMFTC, the groundwork for an increase in dividends is being laid.

Rafa provides us a little more color and insight on the announcement by BDO (BDO PM) that it was preemptively setting aside an additional P20bn in loan loss provisions this year due to the pandemic. Read more of his thoughts here.

More from Carissa (carissa.mangubat@regis.ph), Aaron (aaron.salvador@regis.ph), and Rafa (rafa.garchitorena@regis.ph)

Confirmed Covid-19 cases added to the official tally were over 500 yesterday, still more than double what we had been seeing the previous month. While this is partly a result of catch-up test results, it is still disconcerting considering the eased lockdowns across the country.

As this is going on, the administration appears bent on passing, apart from its planned stimulus packages, a new anti-terrorism bill.

POLITICS
Duterte certifies Anti-Terror Bill as urgent
  • President Duterte certified the proposed Anti-Terror Bill as urgent, which would fast-track its approval in the House of Representatives
  • In a letter to House Speaker Alan Peter Cayetano, Duterte said immediate enactment of the bill is necessary “to address the urgent need to strengthen the law against anti-terrorism in order to adequately and effectively contain the menace of terrorist acts for the preservation of national security and promotion of general welfare”
  • Bills certified as urgent can be passed on second and third reading on the same day
  • Rights groups have warned that the bill’s enactment would make the human rights situation in the Philippines worse
  • Senate Bill 1083 was approved on third and final reading authorizing police and military to detain persons suspected of committing terrorist acts without charges for up to 24 days

OIL AND GAS
Phoenix Petroleum books P215mn net loss, names new President
  • Phoenix Petroleum (PNX PM) reported it booked P215mn in net loss in 1Q20, a reversal from the P415mn net income the prior year
    • Company attributes the weak performance to volatile oil prices and the Covid-19 pandemic which hurt total volumes (-5% YoY)
    • The 10% drop in commercial volumes were cushioned by strong volume growth from LPG (+39% YoY) and retail (+10% YoY)
  • Phoenix also recently named Henry Albert “Bong” Fadullon as the company’s new President, succeeding Founder Dennis Uy who will now assume the role of Chairman of the Board and Chief Strategy Officer
    • Former Chairman Domingo Uy will remain a board director and chairman emeritus
  • Fadullon, 52 years old, has been PNX’s COO since 2017 with 25 years of experience in the industry having held various roles at Pilipinas Shell (SHLPH PM), a decade of which overseas (Malaysia, Oman, London)
    • Prior to joining PNX, Fadullon was General Manager for Global Business of Shell Shared Services
