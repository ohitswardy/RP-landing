REGIS REPORT

02 December 2020

The long-awaited restructuring of the Lopez group companies takes a step forward with the announced tender offer of Lopez Holdings (LPZ PM) shares by its own subsidiary First Philippine Holdings (FPH PM).

FPH intends to buy at least 20% and up to 45% of LPZ that’s not owned by the Lopez family at a price of P3.85/share. This is a 25% premium to LPZ’s last traded price and a 27% discount to its estimated underlying market-price NAV (~P5.3/share), which is composed mostly of shares in FPH and its under-siege media subsidiary ABS-CBN (ABS PM).

If the tender offer is successful and LPZ is delisted, FPH could end up owning as much as 45% of an unlisted company that itself owns 50% of FPH. Yes, that’s right: the transaction would result in cross-shareholdings between them and what could be termed a “circular reference” in your usual spreadsheets. Nonetheless, if one were to cut through this knot of cross-shareholdings, the deal should be significantly enhancing to FPH’s NAV. We estimate this transaction could raise FPH’s underlying NAV/share by as much as 22% from the current P169/share to P206/share. The NAV accretion should come as no surprise since FPH is buying LPZ at a discount to NAV.

Minority investors can tender their shares from 22 Jan to 19 Feb 2021. 

For years, the listed Lopez group firms have traded at large discounts compounded at up to four levels – Lopez Holdings, First Philippine Holdings, First Gen (FGEN PM) and Energy Development Corp (EDC PM). The tender offer and delisting of EDC removed one layer in 2018. This planned tender offer and delisting of LPZ should remove another.

CONGLOS
First Philippine Holdings to conduct tender offer for Lopez Holdings
  • First Philippine Holdings (FPH PM) will make a tender offer for 20% to 45.6% of its parent, Lopez Holdings (LPZ PM) at P3.85/share
    • Represents 25% premium over last closing price and 41% premium over 3-month VWAP
  • 908mn to 2.07bn LPZ shares – P3.5bn to P8.0bn
  • The ultimate parent company, Lopez Inc, which owns 54.4% of Lopez Holdings, will not tender any of its shares
  • Tender offer period – 22 January 2021 to 19 February 2021     
  • If the tender offer is successful, LPZ will be delisted from the Philippine Stock Exchange
  • We estimate that LPZ has a market-based NAV of P5.30/share
  • Before the transaction, we estimate that FPH’s market-based NAV is P169/share
TRANSPORT
PPA estimates port traffic 6-10% below normal levels
  • The Philippine Ports Authority (PPA) estimates port traffic to have declined at between 6% and 10% over the course of the pandemic, though it expects an uptick for the yearend holidays
    • According to PPA General Manager Jay Daniel Santiago
    • The estimate refers to both passenger volume and cargo throughput
  • In 9M20, container volumes at the key ports in Luzon: MICT, South Harbour, and Batangas fell by 17%, 22% and 18%, respectively
    • MICT is wholly owned by ICTSI (ICT PM) and accounts for 52% of container throughput in the Philippines
    • MICT accounts for 35% of ICT’s group-wide revenues and ~50% of profit
  • ICTSI is trading 9.7x FY21E EV/EBITDA, a discount to peers (14x) and historical levels (12x)
POWER
Semirara halts mining operations in Molave pit
  • Semirara has voluntarily deferred mining operations in North Block 7 (NB7), one of four mining blocks in Molave Mine
    • Deferral is due to water build-up in the sump of NB7, which was caused by water seepages and aggravated by heavy rainfall from the recent typhoons
  • Coal shipments will not be affected, given that it has 3.4mn MT of coal inventory as of Dec 1
    • However, the company also flagged that it may not be able to meet the coal quality specifications of a “few” customers
  • Semirara sold 8.45mn MT in 9M20, accounting for 84% of our FY20E (10.1mn MT)
  • In 2021E, our estimates assume coal sales volumes of 15mn MT
  • Every 10% reduction in coal sales volumes reduces FY21E profit forecast by 12%
CONSUMER
Emperador sees further expansion of brandy biz in US
  • In a press release, Emperador (EMP) said that that volumes of Don Pedro and Presidente brandy products in the US have been growing at “double digits” amid the pandemic
  • Both brands are part of the Case Pedro Domecq portfolio in Mexico owned by Grupo Emperador Spain
  • They also saw sales of Fundador in the US grow by “around 23%” from January to September
BANKS
BDO expects banking industry NPL to peak in 2Q21
  • BDO President and CEO Nestor Tan expects the banking industry’s nonperforming loans to peak in 2Q21
  • This assumes the availability of a COVID vaccine and an economic rebound
  • The banking sector’s NPL ratio increased to 3.5% in September 2020 (from 2.93% in August 2020)
