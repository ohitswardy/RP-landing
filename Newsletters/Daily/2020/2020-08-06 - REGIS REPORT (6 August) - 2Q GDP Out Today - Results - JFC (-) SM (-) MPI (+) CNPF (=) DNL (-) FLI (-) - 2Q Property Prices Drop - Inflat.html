REGIS REPORT

06 August 2020

A day after the official 1Q GDP figure was revised to -0.7%, the country’s 2Q GDP numbers are set to be released this morning and the crippling impact of the pandemic / lockdowns on the economy will be laid bare.

Consensus expectations of a -7.6% contraction for the quarter are way too optimistic, in our view. We believe the pace of contraction could be as much as twice that.  While the 2Q GDP figure is indeed a backward-looking number, it will still show how widespread the damage was and gives one a foreshadowing of bad things could still be in succeeding periods.

+++

The results season continues with companies generally posting bad numbers (albeit not totally unexpected). SM Investments (SM PM) and Jollibee (JFC PM) both registered net losses in 2Q as the pandemic and the ensuing lockdowns took their toll.  One company that bucked the trend was canned food company Century Pacific Foods (CNPF PM), which saw pantry-stocking driving up sales.

Our key highlights:

  • Jollibee (JFC PM) (-) – 2Q20 recurring losses were estimated to be P3.5bn as operations bore the full brunt of various lockdown measures across its global store network. SSS dropped 41% as stores were temporarily shut and dine-in services limited/disallowed. Store rationalization efforts are underway, with the company guiding to permanently close c.400 stores in 2020.

 

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (-) – 1H20 profit came in at P7.1bn (-69% YoY), which would imply a net loss of P1.9bn for 2Q alone (vs 12.4bn profit the prior year).  SM Prime (SMPH PM) saw earnings contract by 80% in the quarter while both BDO (BDO PM) and SM Retail fell to net losses.

 

  • Century Pacific Foods (CNPF PM) (=) - Branded revenue growth accelerated to +39% YoY in 2Q20 (+24% in 1Q20) given heightened demand for shelf-stable products during the lockdown period. EBITDA margin contracted (-40bps YoY) due to a mild uptick in skipjack tuna prices. Top line growth has moderated since the Apr/May highs, though the company experienced some pantry-stocking ahead of the Aug 4 MECQ.

 

  • Metro Pacific (MPI PM) (+) - 1H20 core profit of P5.34bn (-38% YoY) was a mild beat due to lower costs at the water unit and at the parent.  2Q core profit fell 63% YoY, as demand fell across its operating segments during the lockdown.  Despite showing decent recovery in July, the re-imposition of MECQ this month, however, has dampened the recovery for its rail and tollways units. 

 

  • D&L industries (DNL PM) (-) – DNL’s 2Q profit fell 57%yoy to P287mn as its food segment contribution plunged 87%. Volumes of high margin products fell 25% in the period covering the lockdowns, while gross margins of the commodity business fell to just 1.5%.

 

  • Filinvest Land’s (FLI PM) (-) 2Q20 net profit fell 27% to P952m, bringing the 1H20 figure down 26% to P2.3bn. While this appears in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts, weak residential revenue was flattered by an unusually low tax rate, unusually high equitized earnings, and a sharp drop in opex.

 

+++

Property broker and consultancy firm Leechiu Property Consultants reports that prices of residential property in 2Q20 are down 5-10% (up to as much as 15%) from 1Q20.  Even office demand weakened with a net reduction of 53,000 sqm in 2Q as POGOs packed up.

These are clear signs of the emerging cracks in the sector and the economy (with implications on the banking sector as well).

MACRO
House plenary approves Bayanihan II
 
  • The House plenary passed on second reading House Bill 6953 or Bayanihan II
    • Bayanihan II extends the measures of Bayanihan I (RA 11469) until Sept 30
  • Bayanihan II also allocates another P162bn for COVID-19 response
    • In contrast, Senate Bill 1564 allocated only P140bn 
  • A third approval of the bill will formalize the passage of the bill by the House, for reconciliation with the Senate version
Inflation creeps up to 2.7% in July
 
  • Headline inflation increased to 2.7% in July, up from 2.5% in June
    • Within BSP’s expected range of 2.2-3.0% 
    • Inflation driven by transport (due to limited public transportation), alcohol and tobacco
    • Food inflation (fish, fruits, vegetables), however, eased further, more than offsetting meat inflation
  • Core inflation was higher at 3.3% YoY in July (vs. 3.0% in June 2020)
External trade plunges in June
 
  • The Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) reported the trade deficit stood at US$1.3bn in June, down 51% YoY
    • Imports fell 24% YoY (vs -41% in May) while exports dropped 13% YoY (vs. -27% in May)
    • Slower decline in trade performance reflects resumption of economic activities
  • Brings 1H20 trade deficit to US$10.6bn, down 48% YoY
PROPERTY
LPC: 2Q20 residential prices fell 5-15%; POGO office footprint shrank
  • Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) said that 2Q20 residential prices generally fell 5-10%, though some sellers offered discounts of up to 15%
    • This matches our channel checks
  • LPC estimates that 89,000sqm of Metro Manila office space was vacated in 2Q20 (out of 12m sqm total stock)
    • 48,000sqm was from POGOs (out of 1.3m sqm occupied in Metro Manila)  
  • This resulted in Metro Manila 2Q20 net office take-up of negative 53,000sqm 
  • This brought 1H20 net office take-up to 50,000sqm, from 667,000sqm in 1H19

  • Rental revenue was flat as the office segment compensated for lower mall revenues 
  • FLI is trading at 3.0X FY21E P/E, 0.2X FY21E P/B, and 78% discount to NAV
