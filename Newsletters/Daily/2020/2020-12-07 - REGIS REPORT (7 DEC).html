REGIS REPORT

07 December 2020

The bicameral committee has approved the Financial Institutions Strategic Transfer Bill, which provides fiscal incentives for banks to sell NPLs and foreclosed assets to SPVs (called Fist Companies or FIST-Cs). The key feature, in our view, is that the tax-exempt sale of assets from the bank to the FIST-C, as well as from the FIST-C to any 3rd party buyer. One major friction cost for selling foreclosed real estate, for example is the 6% tax on the gross value of the transaction. Without the tax incentives, the sale of the asset from bank to FIST-C, then onto a 3rd party would be at least 12% of the asset’s gross value. We have yet to see the reconciled version. But earlier version gave banks two years to sell assets to FIST-Cs, extendable by two years at the government’s discretion. The FIST-C was given 5 years (extendable to 10) within which to dispose of the NPAs.

The FIST Bill is a huge potential positive for banks. Offloading of bad assets would free up capital, as well as management time in dealing with collections, legal proceedings, etc. Selling bad asses could have banks quickly re-focus on re-growing the business. For banks that have provisioned aggressively, asset sales could even generate a small profit.

As with the SPV Law of 2002, the FIST-Cs are intended to be private companies. Though in theory, nothing would stop government institutions from investing in FIST-Cs. It is unclear if the FIST Bill will allow banks to partly own FIST-Cs themselves.

Dito Telecommunity, the telecom operator indirectly owned by the listed Dito CME (DITO PM), is beginning friendly user tests ahead of its technical audit next month. Under the auction conducted in 2018, Dito committed to deliver 37% population coverage and average wireless data speed of 27mpbs by July 7, 2020 but this was rescheduled to Jan 7, 2021.

Meanwhile, PLDT (TEL PM) subsidiary Smart says it will upgrade 40% of its cell sites to 5G by end of next year (2021).

MACRO
Inflation jumps to 3.3% in November
  • Headline inflation rose to 3.3% in November from 2.5% the previous month
    • This was slightly higher than BSP’s expectations (2.4-3.3%)
    • Due to higher food inflation (e.g. fish, fruits, and vegetables) due to weather-related supply disruptions while the African Swine Fever (ASF) continued to adversely affect pork supply
    • Inflation for alcoholic beverages and tobacco also accelerated
  • Core inflation rose to 3.2% from 3.0% in October
  • Brings YT-November inflation to 2.6%, within the government’s target range of 2-4%

BANKS
Bicameral panel approves FIST bill
  • The Bicameral Conference Committee approved the Financial Institutions Strategic Transfer (FIST) bill
    • FIST bill will allow financial institutions to offload bad loans to asset management companies
    • Bill will likely be ratified by Congress ahead of the Christmas break (Dec 19)
PROPERTY
Robinsons Land office REIT could raise as much as US$500mn
  • Bloomberg reported that Robinsons Land could list an office REIT in 1H21
  • The REIT IPO could raise as much as US$500mn (P24bn)
  • This implies a market capitalization of up to P72bn
    • We estimate this would value the office properties at 5-6% cap rate
  • Robinsons Land previously said that it would spin off over 400,000sqm of its 615,000sqm office portfolio into a REIT next year
  • We estimate that spinning off RLC’s entire office portfolio into a REIT could raise its NAV by 5-12% (from the current P35/share)
  • For comparison, AREIT has an office portfolio of 170,000sqm (245,000sqm when the 30th is acquired) and a market cap of P28.2bn
  • RLC is trading at 12.0X FY21E P/E, 0.9X FY21E P/B, and 45% discount to NAV
POWER
SN Aboitiz Power is looking to scale up floating solar portfolio after its pilot project over Magat Dam has proven its viability
  • SNAP switched on its first 200-kw floating solar project over Magat Dam in June 2019
    • The facility provides internal power to SNAP-Magat’s facilities
  • The SNAP board has approved the project to proceed to engineering design for 67MW and is looking at other locations for floating solar expansion
  • SN Power was acquired by Scatec Solar from Norfund last October 2020
    • Scatec Solar is is an independent solar power producer based in Norway
TELCOS
Dito begins friendly user tests
  • Dito, the telecom firm jointly owned by the Davao Dennis Uy and China Telecom, is beginning ‘friendly user tests’ ahead of its technical audit
  • Tests are being conducted via selected participants and in selected areas
  • Dito’s technical audit is supposed to be done in a month’s time (Jan 7)
  • The company is committed to deliver 37% population coverage and 27mbps average wireless data speed by that time
TELCOS
Smart to upgrade 40% of sites to 5G by end of next year
  • Smart Communications, the wireless arm of PLDT, plans to upgrade 40% of its sites to 5G by end of 2021
  • In a press release, it said it will upgrade 4,000 of its bas stations to 5G next year, along with deploying an additional 4,000 LTE bas stations nationwide
  • The company rolled out 279 base stations in 3Q to support its 5G network
