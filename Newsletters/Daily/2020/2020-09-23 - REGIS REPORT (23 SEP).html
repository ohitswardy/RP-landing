REGIS REPORT

23 September 2020

As the country braces for an announcement of possible changes to lockdown restrictions next week, a look at the Covid-19 statistics (new infections, deaths and hospital occupancy) seems to show the situation is gradually being contained, at least as much as it can be. Hospital occupancy figures are down sharply from their peaks, both in Metro Manila (NCR) as well as nationwide. Check out the charts below.

This could provide justification for the government to further ease restrictions and allow the economy and businesses to recover. Until cases surge again. It is, of course, entirely possible that President Duterte will overrule data-based recommendations from the Cabinet and choose not to ease.

For investors looking at re-opening plays, we reckon property companies and restaurant operators are the better bets. Those who are skeptical about the worst of the plague issues being behind us and wary of tighter restrictions being re-imposed ought to stay with the grocery and telco names.

Three Philippine banks were supposedly named in a report by the US FinCEN (Financial Crimes Enforcement Network) as being conduits of remittance firms to send money in suspicious transactions. The banks named were BDO (BDO PM), Metrobank (MBT PM) and Rizal Commercial (RCB PM). In their defense, the Philippines has one of the tightest banking secrecy laws in the world, so local banks have their hands tied in their attempts to KYCC (know your clients’ client).

It aims to raise a modest amount (up to P1.1bn or US$23m) to complete its casino-resort on Mactan Island in Cebu.

BANKS
Philippine banks implicated in FinCEN Files
  • A (rather long) piece by the Philippine Center for Investigative Journalism (PCIJ) examining the FinCEN files -- https://pcij.org/article/4297/ph-money-remitters-flagged-in-millions-of-dollars-in-suspicious-transactions -- details how Philippine banks and remittance outfits sent “more than $1billion” worth of “suspicious” wire transfers abroad. Two remittance firms feature prominently in the report – Philrem and Werquick. The two sent money from their accounts in local banks – BDO Unibank (BDO PM), Metrobank (MBT PM), and Rizal Commercial (RCB PM). Philrem and RCBC were part of the Bangladesh heist in February 2016.
  • These transfers were called “suspicious” as the documents did not have the names of the ultimate sender, and/or that the counterparties were “shell-like  or unverifiable entities”.
  • In defense of the banks, the Philippines is one of a very few countries in the world that still has absolute bank secrecy on deposits. Hence banks are constrained in attempts at KYCC (know your clients’ client). The PCIJ article includes comments from BDO and RCB, but MBT reportedly “was unable to participate” in the inquiry.
  • BDO management says that the bank has “complied with AMLA rules”, referring to the Anti Money Laundering Act.
  • It is unclear what kind of liability the listed banks might have. As mentioned, the Bank Secrecy Law does tie their hands a bit. One possible upside is that this could add momentum to pass a Senate bill filed last week that seeks to amend the secrecy rules (https://www.bworldonline.com/senate-bill-seeks-to-allow-bsp-to-look-into-deposits/).

RETAIL
WLCON targets 63 stores by end-2020
  • In a media briefing, CEO Lorraine Belo-Cincochan said WLCON is slated to open 2 more new stores before the end of the year, bringing total stores to 63 (Regis estimate: 61 stores)
    • Down from the original target of 65 due to construction delays resulting from the lockdown
  • WLCON expects to open 9 new stores in 2021, including the 2 stores originally planned for 2020 (Regis estimate: 6 stores)
  • COO Rosemarie Bosch Ong indicated that 3Q performance is “encouraging”, citing pent up demand from its customers
GAMING
SEC approves PH Resorts’ follow-on offer
  • The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) approved the application of PH Resorts Group Holdings for a P1.125bn follow-on offering
    • Based on previous disclosures, the offer will involve up to 300mn common shares (plus 150mn over-allotment) at a price range of P1-P2.50
  • The follow-on offer will raise money for the construction of The Emerald in Mactan, Cebu –
    • US$300mn – target opening 2022
    • 270 rooms, 122 tables, 600 slot machines
  • PH Resorts also has a license to build a casino hotel in Clark
