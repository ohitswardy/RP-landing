REGIS REPORT

02 October 2020

Two property consultants / brokers reported that online gaming firms (aka POGOs) vacated over 100,000 sqm of office space YTD. The figures from both Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) and Colliers imply POGOs ceased leasing at least 55,000sqm of offices within 3Q alone.

Restrictions on the operations (travel bans, and limits on maximum utilization of their office premises) compounded by increases taxes are likely to have been the reasons for the contraction in POGO operations.

While POGOs still occupy at least 1.2m of office space, cracks have appeared and the concern now is that this leak becomes a flood. Just as this “industry” emerged practically overnight, so it might disappear just as quickly. Beyond the direct impact on the office rental market, POGOs packing up would also have implications on other segments of the property market (residential property), consumption spending and even the balance of payments (BOP).

+++

Meralco (MER PM) issued a new invitation to bid to supply up to 1,800MW of capacity by 2024/2025. Depending on who actually wins the competitive selection process (CSP), it would determine if there would be new capacity to be built.

PLDT (TEL PM) revealed that its fiber network has reached 8.1m homes passed as of Aug, up from 7.8m as of June. The number of available ports for connection is now at 3.78m, from 3.67m as of mid-year.

Metro Pacific (MPI PM) is reviving its share buyback program with a P5bn budget beginning today. It wasn’t able to buy much under the previous one which began in Feb 2020.

+++

In a largely anticipated decision, the Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) Monetary Board kept policy rates at 2.25% at yesterday’s meeting. The BSP’s monetary stimulus is being undertaken in other ways now. Not least of these ways are the purchases of government debt paper both directly from the Treasury as well as in the secondary market.

+++

Down that slippery slope we go? The ink has barely dried on the BSP’s order capping credit card rates and fees, and the SEC Chairman Emilio Aquino is already asking for rate caps on salary loans and payday loans. To be fair, the SEC has been advocating for a cap since October 2019, saying that some payday lenders charge as much as 2.5% PER DAY, on top of other fees and charges. Two weeks ago, a Congressman filed a House Resolution urging the BSP to impose caps on pawnshops’ rates and fees during the Covid crisis.

It is unclear if the BSP will act on either request. But the trend globally seems to be towards more rate regulation of banks/fincos. With the Covid-19 as an easy excuse, we believe it will be difficult for central banks to relinquish the increased regulatory power post-pandemic.

MACRO
SEC wants cap on salary loan interest
  • The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) wants to put a limit on interest and penalty charges that lending companies charge for salary and payday loans
  • The SEC has been lobbying for the payday loan limit since October last year in a bid to stop “predatory” lending practices
  • “We are hopeful that the Monetary Board will likewise consider soon the Commission’s proposal for similar interest rates, fees and other charges imposed by lending and financing companies on consumer and payday loans”, said SEC Chairperson Emilio Aquino
  • He cited the recent cap on credit card charges as timely and much-needed to promote responsible lending
PROPERTY
POGOs vacate more office space in 3Q20
  • Colliers reported that POGOs vacated 123,000sqm of Metro Manila office space YTD
  • Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) said that POGOs vacated 103,000sqm YTD
    • Implies that POGOs vacated 55,000sqm in 3Q20 alone
  • PAGCOR previously reported that 10 out of 60 POGOs have shut down
  • Only 32 of the remaining 50 POGOs have resumed operations
  • We believe the main reasons for shutting down (not resuming operations) are:
    • Travel bans – some Chinese employees left the Philippines and cannot return
    • Cap on operations – POGOs are only permitted to operate at a maximum of 30% of capacity
    • Higher taxes – POGOs are now required to pay 7% of GGR as taxes/fees, from 2% previously
  • LPC expects more POGO closures in 4Q20 since several POGOs are not operating
  • In 2019, we estimate that the POGO industry (including service providers) occupied about 1.3m sqm of office space in Metro Manila and likely employed 300,000-400,000 mainland Chinese
    • POGOs occupy about 11% of Metro Manila office stock and 60% of Manila Bay area office stock
  • About 70% of the office space occupied by POGOs is not PEZA accredited and would generally not be used by BPOs
  • POGOs occupy about 25% of FLI’s office portfolio and 15% of MEG’s office portfolio
  • FLI is currently trading at 3.3X FY21E P/E, 0.3X FY21E P/B, and 75% discount to NAV
  • MEG is currently trading at 5.4X FY21E P/E, 0.5X FY21E P/B, and 65% discount to NAV
  • About 40% of SM Prime’s NAV is comprised of land in the Manila Bay area
  • SMPH is currently trading at 22.6X FY21E P/E, 2.5X FY21E P/B, and 30% discount to NAV

UTILITIES
Meralco issues invitation for power supply bids
  • Meralco issued bid terms for the competitive selection process to supply 1,800MW of capacity
    • 1,200MW by Dec 2024 and 600MW by May 2025
  • Contract term will be for 20 years and bids must be for a minimum of 150MW capacity
  • Unlike in the previous terms, there is no explicit requirement that the plant be a new facility when it begins supplying power
    • However, the terms do specify that the supplying plants must not be in operation before Jan 2020
    • There are at least two facilities under construction / expansion today that have yet to be operation
    • Depending on who wins the bid, it may spur new capacity building in a grid with no meaningful capacity is being built after Aboitiz Power’s (AP PM) Dinginin
  • The deadline for the submission of bids is Jan 25, 2021
