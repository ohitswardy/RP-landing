REGIS REPORT

15 December 2020

The suspension of the business permit of the NLEX will stay for now, and with it the “toll fee holiday”, pending resolution of issues surrounding its RFID system.

The mayor of Valenzuela City suspended the business permit of NLEX after long queues emerged with the full implementation of wholly cashless (RFID only) toll collection system. The suspension will not be lifted, according to the city chief executive, until the system is improved.

We estimated the net profit impact to Metro Pacific (MPI PM) will be approximately P2m for each day this goes on. So a 60-day suspension will cut close  to 1% from our P13bn profit forecast for the company.

+++

Credit card delinquencies rose 1ppt MoM in Sept to 11.5%, clearly showing the strains the pandemic and lockdowns are imposing on incomes. For perspective, the ratio was only 4.4% as of end of last year. Many individuals are clearly struggling to make their debt payments.

 

+++

The Lower House approved the bill extending the validity of national budget appropriations made for FY20 as well as those under Bayanihan 2. The Dept of Finance estimates around P213bn of spending will be carried over as a result of this bill’s passage, over 4% of the FY21 spending target.

+++

Based on surveys conducted by the Bangko Sentral (BSP), both consumer and business confidence are improving following positive news flow on the pandemic ahead of the holiday season. While consumer sentiment remains deeply negative, businesses are, on balance, already optimistic. Intriguingly, businesses are also now looking to add employment.

    • A counterpart measure has also been filed at the Senate
  • Last night, President Duterte certified as urgent the extension of two spending laws: Bayanihan II and the 2020 national budget
    • With the certification, the Lower House can proceed with the bills' second and third readings in two succeeding days, instead of waiting for three days in between
  • The government has not fully spent the P140bn stimulus fund under Bayanihan II
    • As of Dec 11, the government has only released P105.78bn to key agencies
MACRO
Business confidence turns optimistic in 4Q20; consumer confidence less pessimistic
  • The business confidence index reverted to positive territory of 10.6% in 4Q20 from -5.3% in 3Q20
    • Positive reading indicates that respondents with optimistic views increased and outnumbered those with pessimistic views
    • Data from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas
    • Cited (a) reopening of businesses and adapting to the “new normal,” (b) easing of community quarantines nationwide, (c) seasonal factors such as uptick in demand during the holiday season and start of milling season, and (d) increase in volume of sales and orders.
  • The employment outlook index returned to the positive territory for 1Q21 and the next 12 months, suggesting that firms are looking forward to hiring people in Q1 2021 and the next 12 months
  • Meanwhile, consumer sentiment was is less pessimistic at -47.9% in 4Q20 from -54.5% in 3Q20
    • Cited availability of more jobs and less community restrictions, among others
  • For the next quarter's outlook, consumer confidence turned positive to 4.3% from -4.1% in 3Q20

Credit card delinquency rate rises to 11.5% in September
  • The Credit Card Association of the Philippines (CCAP) said that the credit card delinquency rate rose to 11.5% in September
  • From 10.5% in August, 4.36% in December 2019
  • CCAP said the increase was primarily due to loss of income and unforeseen expenses
INFRASTRUCTURE
NLEX suspension order, toll holiday stay in Valenzuela City
  • The suspension order over NLEX in Valenzuela city will remain until it completes the system upgrade in the implementation of its RFID system by Jan. 30, 2021
    • NLEX must also be able to provide answers to the city's proposal to completely remove toll barriers
  • Both parties have also agreed to restore cash lanes, relocate the RFID installation, and increase the RFID sensor wattage from the current four watts to eight watts within two weeks
  • Last Dec 7, the local government of Valenzuela city suspended the business permit of NLEX
  • Every day’s suspension suggests opportunity losses of c. P2mn
