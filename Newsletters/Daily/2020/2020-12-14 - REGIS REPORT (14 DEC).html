REGIS REPORT

14 December 2020

PLDT (TEL PM) disclosed that is planning to increase capex to as much as P92bn (US$1.9bn) next year, a chunky increase from what it’s likely to spend this year (P70bn).

The increased spend comes after the enactment of the Bayanihan 2, which reduced the processing time for permits to build telecom towers. Previous efforts to cut red tape had not been particularly successful despite regulations and orders signed. The firm says that is now has 1,600 permits to be able to increase its tower count of over 10,000 (still low relative to the size and population density of the country).

+++

Meanwhile, with the deal now closed, we take a closer look at the previously reported sale for PLDT to sell one of its office towers (Smart Tower) to the Consunji family, the principals behind DMCI (DMC PM) and Semirara (SCC PM). The sale involves a five-year leaseback arrangement by PLDT (Smart).

The price for the building, with GFA of 38,000sqm on Ayala Avenue in Makati, was P6.14bn (US$128m), which is slightly higher than we would have estimated. This translates to, we estimate, P180k to 200k per sqm of leasable space and likely an implied cap rate of close to 5%. Note that Smart Tower is not a PEZA building (thus no tax incentives) and the only listed REIT in the Philippines is trading at 6.8% cap rate.

+++

Foreign ownership in the Philippine equity market continued to trend down in November. We estimate total foreign ownership dropped 43bp to 22.1% while foreign ownership of the PSEi components dipped 19bp to to 26.2%.
