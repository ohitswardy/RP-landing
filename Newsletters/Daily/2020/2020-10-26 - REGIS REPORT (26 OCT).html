REGIS REPORT

26 October 2020

We raise our estimates and target prices for both PLDT (TEL PM) and Globe (GLO PM) on expectations of better revenue outlook as demand for data has surged. We adjust our profit forecasts for PLDT by 23% in FY20 and 17% in FY21, and lift our target price to P1,700 (+17%). For Globe, our profit estimates were raised by 16% for FY20 and 20% for Fy21. We also raise our target price for GLO to P2,300 (+21%) and upgrade it to BUY.

The pandemic and the associated lockdown/s have been a major catalyst spurring demand for data, particularly on broadband. Broadband adoption has effectively been accelerated by ~2 years by the lockdown with the industry seeing record net subscriber additions due to work-from-home and study-from-home phenomena.

Meanwhile, on the mobile side, things are not looking as dismal as we previously thought. Top-ups are not falling as we feared. Longer-term, we remain wary of the third operator but note that its emergence as a meaningful threat is subject to political winds, both global and domestic.

At <5x EBITDA with dividend yields over 5% for FY21, both PLDT and Globe are looking attractive. Recent price weakness, we reckon, is not fundamentally driven and presents an opportunity to get exposure. Check out the note from Gio (gio.delarosa@regis.ph) via the links below.

In a press release, BDO (BDO PM) reported that 3Q20 NPAT surged to P12.3bn, up 3% yoy to a record high profit for a quarter, and reversing the loss in 2Q. For 9M20, NPAT was P16.6bn, down 48% yoy, and in line with to slightly ahead of our forecast of P26.9bn (consensus P27.5bn). The highlight was that NPLs were flat qoq to 1.97% compared to 1.95% in 2Q. Recall that NPLs jumped in 2Q largely because the bank continued to book PD and NPLs for its consumer book despite the first grace period under Bayanihan 1. As a result, provision expense for the quarter plunged to just P1.4bn. Some other highlights:

  • Loan growth was steady at 6% yoy (was 7% in 2Q), with loans flat qoq.
  • Net interest income was up 13% in 9M20, though up just 6% yoy in 3Q (flat qoq). The bank noted that its CASA ratio hit an all-time high of 79% from 77% in 2Q and 73% at end-2019.
  • Fee incomes were still down 23% yoy in 3Q. However, fees showed a remarkable rebound, +27 qoq , likely on the combination of recovering volumes, and of re-imposition of fees waived at the height of the pandemic.
  • Provision expense plunged in 3Q to just P1.3bn, following the massive P20bn put through in 2Q. NPLs were surprisingly steady at 1.97% in 3Q (was 1.95% in 2Q) and NPL cover was 138% (was 139% in 2Q).
  • CAR improved to 14.3% from 13.8% in 2Q, while CET-1 was up to 13.2% from 12.7% in 2Q.

The bank will hold an earnings call (exclusive to sell-side) at 2pm this afternoon. We will send updates after that.

AREIT (AREIT PM), the REIT company controlled by Ayala Land (ALI PM), is expanding its asset portfolio with the announced acquisition of The 30th, a mall-cum-office complex owned by the latter. AREIT will buy the asset for P5.1bn in cash in what is looking like part of a steady program asset injections by ALI into AREIT. We believe the purchase will likely result in a mild accretion to AREIT earnings and dividends.

The government reported a budget deficit of P138.5bn for Sept, bringing the cumulative deficit to P879bn. At this pace, reaching the programmed full-year deficit of P1.8trn is looking unlikely.

Hard liquor firm Emperador (EMP PM) reported net profit of P5.9bn for 9M20. This is up 11% YoY and is ahead of Street estimates as the international business seems to be outperforming. Could it be that people are turning to alcohol to drown their pandemic-induced sorrows?

MACRO
Budget deficit narrows to P138.5bn in September on lower expenditures
  • The National Government recorded a budget deficit of P138.5bn in September, 22% lower than the same period last year
    • Revenues contracted by 10% YoY, with BIR collections dropping 6.6% YoY and BOC collections dropping 13.7% YoY
    • Expenditures, however, declined by 15% YoY due to the timing of subsidy releases and high base effects from higher infra spend last year
    • Full impact of Bayanihan II (P166bn), which was approved last Sept 11, is expected to be felt in succeeding periods
  • This brings YT-September budget deficit to P879.2bn, up 194% YoY and 32% below the revised program of P1.298tn for the same period
    • Gov’t is targeting a budget deficit of P1.815tn in FY20
    • So far, revenue collections for 9M20 are down 7.9% YoY to P2.14tn, tracking ahead of FY20 target
    • Meanwhile, expenditures for 9M20 reached P3.0tn, up 15% YoY and accounting for 70% of FY20 target

PROPERTY
AREIT acquires office and mall complex from Ayala Land
  • AREIT will acquire the 30th (office and mall complex) along Meralco Avenue, Pasig City, for P5.1bn (VAT inclusive)
    • To be funded with debt
    • The transaction will be completed in 1Q21
  • The land will continue to be owned by a third party
    • the land lease agreement (until 2050) will be transferred from Ayala Land to AREIT
  • 76,000sqm – 48,000sqm office, 28,000sqm mall
    • This will grow AREIT’s leasable area by 45% to 246,000sqm
  • The office building is PEZA accredited and has no POGO tenants
  • Ayala Land will lease the mall from AREIT at a fixed rental rate
  • We estimate that the transaction was likely done at 6.5%-7.5% 2021E cap rate
    • We estimate that AREIT’s current market cap implies a cap rate of 6.5%-7.0% for AREIT’s existing assets
  • Our initial estimates suggest that the acquisition could raise 2021E DPS by 7% to P1.57/share
  • This could increase AREIT’s dividend yield from 5.7% to 6.1%
  • AREIT plans to establish credit facilities with banks amounting to P12bn
RETAIL
MerryMart opens 17th store
  • MerryMart opened its 17th store at Artonstrip by Rockwell, along Katipunan avenue, in Quezon City
  • This is the first store under the “market” format
  • MerryMart has retail formats include:
    • MerryMart Store – small format mini grocery, personal care shop, pharmacy
    • MerryMart Market – medium size
    • MerryMart Grocery – full supermarket
    • Dark Grocery – online
  • MerryMart is targeting 25 stores by end-2020 and 100 stores by end-2021
CONSUMER
Emperador’s 3Q profit jumps 26% to P2.5bn - ahead

    • The 3Q profit is also higher by 32%qoq
  • This brings 9M net income higher by 11% to P5.9bn – well ahead of estimates
  • “Emperador’s global business saw double-digit growth as it adapted well to new consumption trends”
  • EMP cited strong growth numbers of Fundador in several areas for 9M20 - +23% in US, +185% in UK, +15% in Italy
  • Earnings call will be on October 29, 2020 at 3PM (jtuquero@allianceglobalinc.com)
