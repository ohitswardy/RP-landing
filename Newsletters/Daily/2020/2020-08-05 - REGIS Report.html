REGIS REPORT

05 August 2020

The drop in OFW remittances continues and is, in fact, accelerating.  Remittances for May fell 19% to US$2.1bn.  This brings YT-May remittances to US$11.6bn, down 6% YoY.  The Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) forecast of a 5% drop for this year now looks way too optimistic.

We have results from two big caps today, SM Prime (SMPH PM) and Globe (GLO PM) today:

  • SM Prime (SMPH PM) (=) 2Q20 net profit fell 80% to P2.1bn due to the hard lockdown until the end of May. This brought 1H20 net profit down 46% to P10.4bn, slightly ahead of our forecasts, largely thanks to strong residential revenue.  2Q20 Philippine mall revenue fell 80% (to P3.1bn) because for most of the period, only essential services, which occupy c. 20% of space, were permitted to operate.

While earnings are within expectations, the recently-imposed harder lockdown (“MECQ” effective today) could change its near-term outlook if it continues.  More from Carl (carl.sy@regis.ph) in the attached note.

  • Globe (GLO PM) (+) – Core income for 1H came in at P11.1bn, which is ahead of Regis estimates but likely in line with consensus. For 2Q alone, core income was P4.5bn, down 15% YoY. Service revenue dipped but did not fall as much as feared.  We reckon the two key operating highlights for 2Q were (1) the drop in mobile revenue as a result of the lockdown and (2) the surge in home broadband take-up.  More detail below.

We update our numbers for SM Investments (SM PM), cutting our target price to P760/share (-11%).  The current crisis is leaving no company unscathed and we expect SM to be affected across all three of its main business segments (property, banking and retail).  Having said, we also expect the group to emerge from this crisis better than most.

YTD, the stock has, surprisingly, outperformed not just the PSEi but, even more surprisingly, even its two key listed subsidiaries BDO (BDO PM) and SM Prime (SMPH PM).  As a result, SM is now trading at 20% premium to our estimated value of its assets (NAV).  While something can be said about “flight to quality”, its current price is difficult to justify fundamentally.  Maintain HOLD.

OFW remittances drop 19% in May
 
  • OFW remittances fell 19% YoY to US$2.106bn in May
    •                 In PHP terms, remittances fell 22%
    •                 This is the biggest decline seen in nearly two decades
  • Biggest drops seen from: USA (-13%), Middle East (-39%), and sea-based (-12%)
  • More than 115k OFWs have been repatriated from Feb-Aug 1 due to the pandemic, according to the Dept. of Foreign Affairs
  •                 Of which, 68k are land-based while 47k are sea-based
  • Brings YT-May remittances down 6.4% to US$11.6bn
  • BSP forecasting remittances to drop by 5% this year

TELCOS
Globe core income down 8% to P11.1bn
 
  •  Core income declined 8% to P11.1bn in 1H as service revenue dipped 1%
    •  Despite drop, core income is better than our downgraded expectations
    •  We believe 1H/2Q was slightly below (or roughly in line) with consensus
