REGIS REPORT

29 December 2020

President Duterte signed the 2021 national budget of P4.5trn yesterday. This is a 10% increase over this year’s budget but the carryover from 2020 will likely provide a small boost to that figure. As noted previously, the Health and Public Works departments are getting big increases. The budget also includes a P72bn (US$1.5bn) provision to procure vaccines for Covid.

There’s been no word yet if “FIST”, the SPV law for banks’ bad assets, has also been approved. The other important piece of legislation, the corporate tax reform package called “CREATE”, remains pending with Congress.

+++

The manager of AREIT (AREIT PM), effectively Ayala Land (ALI PM), outlined its medium-term plans for the REIT (REIC) with an objective to produce returns of 10-12% for shareholders. Among the highlights of the plan were (1) a diversification into logistics and industrial properties and (2) an equity offering in 2022. Also of note was the target dividend of P1.62/share for FY21, a 24% increase from this year and equivalent to 5.5% yield.

+++

The country recorded a BOP surplus of US$1.47bn for Nov, taking the YT-Nov surplus to US$11.8bn. The BOP surpluses continue despite the net outflows in the equity markets while record-low US$ interest rates are prompting overseas borrowings.

  • President Duterte extended GCQ status in Metro Manila and other areas throughout January 2021
    • Areas under GCQ include: Davao City , Tacloban City, Santiago City, Iligan City, Iloilo City, Isabela, Batangas, Lanao Del Sur, Davao del Norte
  • The rest of the country will be under the more lenient modified GCQ
MACRO
BOP surplus at US$1.47bn in November
  • The country’s overall balance of payments (BOP) position posted a surplus of US$1.47bn in November
    • This is up 172% YoY but lower than the US$3.4bn surplus in October 2020
  • Brings YT-November BOP surplus to US$11.79bn, higher than the US$6.27bn in the same period last year
    • Due to higher government foreign borrowings and a lower trade deficit
    • Reflects the increase in GIR to US$105bn, sufficient to cover 11.2 months’ of imports
MACRO
Foreign portfolio investments yield net inflow in November
  • Foreign portfolio investments (i.e. “hot money”) registered a net inflow of US$227mn in November
    • This is lower than the US$439mn net inflow recorded in October 2020
  • YT-November net outflow at US$3.72bn, up 135% YoY
PROPERTY
AREIT targeting a 24% increase in 2021 dividends
  • AREIT Fund Managers Inc (AFMI), revealed its three-year investment strategy for AREIT:
  • Its investment objectives are:
    • 10-12% shareholder return through organic growth and new acquisitions
    • Grow and diversify AREIT’s asset portfolio – including logistics and industrial properties
    • Maintain AREIT’s leadership (in the Philippine REIT sector) in terms of asset size and market capitalization
  • AREIT expects its 2021 DPS to increase 24% to P1.62/share
    • 5.5% dividend yield
  • The acquisition of the 30th (mall and office complex) from Ayala Land will expand AREIT’s leasable area by 45% to 246,000sqm
  • The company plans to have a second equity offering in 2022 and a combination of debt and equity funding in 2023 – to maintain an overall gearing level of 30-35%
  • We estimate that after the acquisition of the 30th from Ayala Land, AREIT’s gearing will reach c. 15% (based on value of properties)
