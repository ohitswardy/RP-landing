REGIS REPORT

22 April 2022

In a press release, BPI (BPI PM) reported headline 1Q22 NPAT of P8.0bn, up 60% YoY. Operations showed solid improvements, though the large YoY growth was driven by a drop in provisions and tax expense. Loan growth accelerated to 7.1% YoY (from 4.5% in 4Q) driven by high-margin credit cards (+12%). NIMs were up 11bps YoY, on loan growth and a high CASA ratio of 81%. Non-interest income fell 14.5% YoY due to high bases for both trading gains and fees in 1Q/2Q21. NPL ratio improved to 2.38% (-11bps QoQ) and NPL Cover jumped to 149.5% (from 136% in 4Q21), despite a 31% drop in provision expense to P2.5bn. All around, a solid set of results. We would caution, though, against annualizing the 1Q22 NPAT – which seems well ahead of Regis and consensus forecasts of P30bn. Opex growth in 1Q22 was just 6%, while management has guided a 20% jump for the full-year as the bank ramps up its IT spend. An earnings call will be held on 04 May.

+ + +

BDO Unibank (BDO PM) reported headline NPAT of P11.7bn for 1Q22, up 13% YoY, beating Regis forecast of P40.3bn, but slightly lagging consensus of P48.3bn. Details were scant, as the data was out on new wires, but not on the PSE disclosure page. Loans grew 7%, up slightly from the 6.5% in 4Q21, driven by mid-market demand. Net interest income was up 6% YoY (flat QoQ) suggesting broadly flat NIMs. Other income was up 9% YoY, on better fees and insurance income. No opex data was given. Asset quality improved, with NPL ratio down to 2.72% from 2.81% at end-2021, while NPL cover rose to 121% from 107%. The headline figures look solid, though we will wait for further details to give a more thorough comment.

+++

Philip Morris (PM US) reported results overnight and its sales volumes in the Philippines (under its joint venture with LT Group (LTG PM) bounced 18% YoY (+11% QoQ) to 9.6bn units (sticks). While this may appear strong relative to our estimates, we have to caution against being too excited about a return of the smoking habit. The sales figures look good due to the timing of price hikes, which lifted 1Q22 and depressed 1Q21 due to distributors stocking up.

+++

We refresh our forecasts and TP (+20% to P/12sh) for leading chained restaurant operator Shakey’s Pizza (PIZZA PM). Our new forecasts factor in 1) a more meaningful recovery in same-store sales as dine-in restrictions are lifted and 2) the recent acquisition of leading food cart/kiosk operator Potato Corner. We are forecasting EPS CAGR of +105% over 2022-24E as operating leverage from the SSS improvement is likely to overshadow any potential margin pressure from the surge in prices of key inputs (e.g. cheese and potatoes).

At 18x PE, the stock trades at a discount to peers and to its historical average. We believe the company will be among the key beneficiaries of the economy’s reopening this year, as it bounces back from two years of pandemic-related lockdowns.

BANKS
BPI 1Q22 surges 60% to P8bn, ahead; but mind future opex growth
  • In a press release, BPI (BPI PM) reported headline net profit of P8.0bn, up 60% YoY and ahead of consensus and Regis estimates of P30bn for FY2022. It is also ahead of management’s (soft) guidance of P28bn for the full year. Profit was driven by NII, which grew 13% YoY on the back of accelerating loan growth (7.1% YoY) and expanding NIMs (+11bps YoY to 3.42%, by BPI’s estimate). Provisions also fell 31% to P2.5bn, as NPLs improved to 2.38% (vs 2.49% in 4Q21). Trading gains and fees were down YoY, as both were coming off high bases (including IPOs in 1Q21). NPAT got a big boost from lower tax expense, as the bank took a large hit in 1Q21 as they wrote off deferred tax assets (DTA) as a result of CREATE.
  • Some other highlights:
    • Loan growth accelerated to 7.1% YoY from 4.9% in 4Q21. The 1Q22 growth was driven by credit cards, which was up 12.2% YoY, followed by corporates (+7.7%) and mortgages (+6.6%). Deposits were up 13.1% YoY, slowing slightly from 13.9% the previous quarter. CASA ratio improved QoQ to 81% from 77% in 4Q21.
    • NIMs improved 11bps YoY to 3.42% by BPI’s estimates (different computation from ours), as a result of improving loan growth, loan mix, and deposit mix. Net interest income (NII) jumped 13% YoY.
    • Non-interest income was down 15% YoY, due to very high base of trading gains in 1Q21. Fees were also coming off a high base given the strong IPO/FOO pipeline in 1H21.
    • Opex was up just 6% YoY. We suspect this will jump sharply in coming quarters, as the bank has guided for a 20% increase in full-year 2022 opex as the bank ramps up its IT spend.
    • Tax expense also plunged; we estimate tax+minorities were down some 40% YoY. Recall that BPI took a big one-time hit in 1Q21, writing down deferred tax assets (DTA) as a result of the CREATE law, which also lowered the statutory corporate tax rate from 30% to 25%.
  • As mentioned, we believe the future quarters could be softer NPAT wise, as the bank’s opex ramps up on its planned IT spend. We hope to hear more during the analyst briefing, though this won’t be until 04 May.

PMFTC volumes rebound in 1Q22 to 9.6bn sticks (+18%YoY, +11%QoQ)
  • Philip Morris International disclosed overnight that cigarette volumes in the Philippines in 1Q22 came in at 9.6bn sticks, +18%YoY and +11%QoQ
    • Industry volumes hit 15.6bn sticks (+19%YoY, +10%QoQ)
    • Unit market share climbed slightly to 61.3%, after having lost market share in 4Q21
  • The recovery in volume was driven by Marlboro and Chesterfield brands, offsetting the decline of mid-priced Fortune
  • Prices were increased by 17-21% for high, low end brands last March 21
  • PMFTC – FY22E profit - P38bn (+10%)
  • LTG is trading at 3.5x FY22E PE and a 70% discount to market NAV
