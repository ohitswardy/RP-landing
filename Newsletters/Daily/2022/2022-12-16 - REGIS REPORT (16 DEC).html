REGIS REPORT

16 December 2022

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) raised its policy rate by 50bp yesterday, matching the rate hike announced by US Federal Reserve hours earlier. The BSP policy rate is now 5.5%, the highest in 14 years and ~125bp higher than the Fed’s.

Market expectations are for the Fed raising rates by another 50-75bp next year but BSP Governor Medalla pointed out he sees less “urgency” to match the Fed. With the PHP now under less pressure, there is not much need to raise rates. The rise in inflation has largely been a result of higher import prices, driven by conditions elsewhere and not under the BSP’s control.

+++

PLDT (TEL PM) disclosed it was selling another 650 telecom towers to a group backed by Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM). The aggregate sale price for this new tranche amounts to P9.2bn, which would bring the total proceeds from tower sales to P86bn.

The terms of the leaseback arrangements for this portfolio are similar to the previous tranches, so this sale (and the associated transactions) will be meaningfully accretive to PLDT, like the previous ones. PLDT’s EBIT will be little changed but its finance costs will fall due to the cash receipts.

+++

Overseas remittances for Oct amounted to US$2.9bn, up 3.5%. Cumulative remittances as of Oct were US$26.7bn, +3.1% higher YoY.

The House of Representatives approved the bill creating a sovereign wealth fund to be funded by two government banks as well as the BSP. As it appears in the proposed bill, the Maharlika Investment Fund (MIF), as it will be called, will be little more than a vehicle to fund projects outside the national budget. Its disbursements will thus not form part of the budget deficit.

+++

We have another new report today, this time on Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) with Aaron reiterating his BUY call on the stock with a TP of P11.70/share.

The relaxation of Covid controls by the Chinese government has created some excitement for Macau names on the prospects of players finally returning to the gaming hub. While our numbers factor in an eventual return of travel from China this year, more than 80% of our GGR estimates now come from locals, South Koreans and other non-Chinese tourists. China is clearly not the revenue driver anymore, but a strong return of Chinese tourists could provide upside to our numbers. On our base case, we already have GGR/EBITDA growing at CAGR of 22%/21% up to FY24E.

At 7.8x EV/EBITDA on next year’s estimates, multiples are below both historical (10x) and Macau (27x). This discount was justifiable and reasonable in the past, but we see it as no longer warranted.  Not only is BLOOM’s regulatory situation more “certain” than that of the Macau operators, it also has clearly better earnings visibility and outlook.

MACRO
BSP raises rates by 50 bps, signals more tightening
  • The BSP raised its benchmark interest rate by 50bps to 5.5%
    • Within consensus expectations
    • Move followed the 50bp rate hike of the Fed
    • Overnight borrowing rate of 5.5% at the highest since November 2008
  • Cited the further uptick in headline and the sharp rise in core inflation in November amid pent-up demand
    • Recall headline and core inflation accelerated to 8% and 6.5%, respectively, in November
  • BSP raised its 2023 average inflation target to 4.5% from 4.3%, up from 5.8% in 2022 (unch)
    • Due to faster-than-expected Nov 2022 inflation and the looming impact of approved water rate hikes beginning Jan 2023
    • Latest forecast point to inflation peaking in December
  • BSP expects inflation to return to the 2-4% target band by 2H23
    • It lowered its 2024 average inflation target to 2.8% from 3.1% previously
  • BSP Governor Medalla expects the pace of rate increases to slow down next year, depending on the data
    • He sees less urgency to match the US Federal Reserve’s monetary tightening next year as bringing inflation back to the 2-4% target remains their top priority

Cash remittances rise 3.5% YoY in October
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks rose 3.5% YoY to US$2.9bn in October
    • In PHP terms, remittances are up 20% YoY
    • 3-month average growth rate at +3.9% YoY
    • Driven by higher inflows from Europe, Middle East, HK, Singapore
    • MoM, Oct remittances were higher by 2.5%
  • Brings YT-October remittances to US$26.7bn, up 3.1% YoY
    • Driven by inflows from USA, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Qatar
    • To reach the BSP’s 22E of +4% YoY, remittances for the balance of the year should average US$2.97bn per month

MACRO
Lower House approves Maharlika Investment Fund bill
  • The House of Representatives approved on third and final reading the bill creating the controversial Maharlika Investment Fund
    • Voting 279-6, the House of Representatives overwhelmingly approved House Bill (HB) No. 6608, which was just filed two weeks ago
    • President Ferdinand R. Marcos, Jr. on Wednesday certified the MIF bill as urgent
  • A mix of public funds and dividends from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) will be used as seed money for the MIF, based on the current iteration
    • Initial funding will come from the Land Bank of the Philippines (P50bn), Development Bank of the Philippines (P25bn) and BSP
    • The BSP will initially put in the MIF 100% of its dividends to be declared as income
    • Subsequent annual contributions will come from BSP dividends, and part of the gaming revenues of the PAGCOR. and other state-owned gaming operators, among others
    • According to a BSP official, the BSP’s projected dividends for 2022 is P30-35bn, which is equivalent to 50% of its income (see here)
    • Recall that the original bill involved public funds of P275bn, which included contributions from SSS (P50bn) and GSIS (P125bn)

Meralco signs emergency supply deal with AP’s GNPower Dinginin
  • Manila Electric Co (Meralco) executed an emergency power supply agreement (EPSA) with GNPower Dinginin Ltd for the supply of 300-MW baseload capacity
  • The EPSA starts today and will last until January 25, 2023
  • This will partially replace the 670MW capacity under Meralco’s power supply agreement with South Premiere Power Corp (SPPC)
  • The EPSA has a rate of P5.96/kWh
