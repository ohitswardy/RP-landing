03 June 2022

Ports-and-casinos tycoon Enrique Razon is acquiring the stake/s in the operator of Malampaya gas field held by Dennis Uy, the businessman closely identified with Rodrigo Duterte. Dennis Uy has been on an apparent selling spree of assets acquired during the Duterte administration with announced divestments of 2GO (2GO PM), casino firms and property assets in just the past few months.

The Malampaya gas field is the fuel source for 3,000MW of power generation in the Luzon grid but its output has declined in the past years. On the face of it, there seems to be little value to acquiring the operator since the contract over Malampaya ends in a mere two years (in 2024). An extension to the operating contract (Service Contract 38) would be necessary to (2) extract every last drop from the existing field and (2) explore adjacent areas for more hydrocarbons.

The Dept of Energy (DOE) didn’t act on the extension proposals when Shell and Chevron were the main shareholders, before Uy negotiated to buy them out. Maybe things will be different when majority ownership of SC-38 changes hands.

President-elect Ferdinand Marcos, Jr has directed the incoming Budget Department to keep his priorities in mind in the crafting of the national budget for next year (2023). Among the priorities are (a) agriculture and food security, (b) climate change and (c) renewable energy. Also included are the more generic, ‘motherhood’ priorities like “economic recovery” and “sustainable development”.

No longer in this list are the outspoken (and/or unspoken) priorities of the Duterte administration: the fight against the insurgencies (communist and otherwise) and the “war” against the alleged drug menace.

Following the minimum wage increase granted in Metro Manila (NCR), more than half of all the country’s regions have also since announced wage hikes that were much higher (%) than the one in NCR (+6%).

How big a deal is the minimum wage increase? While labor is indeed a big proportion of costs and also pretax earnings for many companies (we have a table for that in the report below), the actual impact is much more modest. One has to take a more nuanced look: How many employees are actually on minimum wage? How many firms have already programmed wage hikes? Or can pass on the higher cost to their customers?

Razon firm to acquire controlling stake in Malampaya
  • Razon-led Prime Infrastructure Holdings said on Thursday that it is “poised to acquire a controlling stake” in the Malampaya gas-to-power project

    • The deal announced in October 2019 was approved by the DOE in March 2021

    • PNOC EC holds the remaining 10%

  • The Razon group is also in talks to gain control of Udenna’s casino projects in Cebu and Clark
