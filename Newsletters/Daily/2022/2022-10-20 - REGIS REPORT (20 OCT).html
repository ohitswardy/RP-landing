REGIS REPORT

20 October 2022

BPI (BPI PM) released 3Q results this morning, with solid numbers that are not just a prelude to what the outlook will be like in the coming quarters for itself, but also for the rest of the banking sector.

Profits for 3Q doubled to P10bn, lifting 9M earnings to P30.5bn (+75%) --- on track to handily beat full-year estimates of the Street and Regis (P35bn). Note that 2Q earnings were boosted by a one-off gain on the sale property amounting to over P3bn. Loans as of 3Q were 15% higher YoY and the NPL ratio was at 1.94%. 

The impact of rising rates is evident in the bank’s net interest margins (NIMs), which rose by 23bp to 3.53% by its reckoning. With more rate hikes coming, NIMs have only one way to go.

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) revised the country’s international reserves figure for end-Sept to US$93bn, a downward adjustment of US$2bn. The result is that the gross international reserves (GIR) showed a US$4.4bn (-5%) decline MoM. This is the largest single month drop in at least the last decade.

Disclosures provided by Converge (CNVRG PM) showed that its founders, Dennis and Grace Uy, were the buyers of the 3% stake in Converge transacted on Monday. The number of shares corresponded to the stake held by private equity firm Warburg Pincus after its last sell-down in May. The transaction means the public float of Converge will stay at 31%.

Starlink is reportedly coming to the Philippines in partnership with a company owned by SM (SM PM) director Henry Sy Jr. Operations are expected to begin by the end of 2023 but we don’t expect much impact on the overall broadband market. Given its upfront cost (US$500) and monthly fee (US$100), its services will likely be a niche but complementary addition to the broadband market.

MACRO
GIR drops to US$93bn in September; BOP deficit balloons to US$2.3bn
  • The BSP revised the end-September GIR to a lower figure of US$93.0bn (from a preliminary data of US$95.0)
    • The GIR dropped by US$4.4bn (-5%) MoM, the largest sequential drop in the past 10 years
    • Equivalent to 7.4 months’ worth of imports and is also 6.6x the country’s short-term external debt
  • The balance of payments (BOP) posted a deficit of US$2.3bn in September, larger than the US$572mn deficit in Aug 2022 and the US$412mn in Sep 2021
    • Brings YT-September BOP deficit to US$7.8bn, higher than the US$665mn deficit in the same period a year ago
    • The BSP is targeting a BOP deficit of US$8.4bn by year-end, equivalent to 2% of GDP

BANKS
BPI 9M22 net profit jumps 75%, ahead
  • BPI’s 9M22 net profit jumped 75% to P30.5bn on both higher revenue and lower provisions
  • The loan portfolio rose 15% (to P1.6trn) while net interest margin expanded 23 basis points to 3.53%
    • Credit card receivables +29.1%
    • Corporate/SME +16.4%
    • Auto loans +12.1%
  • Deposits grew 13% to P2.0trn while CASA ratio stood at 76%
  • Despite the drop in provisions, asset quality continued to improve
    • NPL ratio stood at 1.94% while NPL coverage was 177%
  • Excluding the impact of a one-off property sale in 2Q22 and adjustments due to the CREATE law, net income would have risen to P26.8bn
  • 3Q22 net profit rose 78% YoY to P10.1bn
    • Excluding the gain on property sale in 2Q22, earnings would have risen 15% QoQ
    • 3Q22 net interest income grew 9% QoQ and 29% YoY

TELCOS
Converge founders acquire Pincus' 3% stake
  • Converge (CNVRG PM) disclosed today that it was the Uy couple that acquired 3% of the company the other day
    • The number of shares correspond to the shares held by Warburg Pincus (Coherent Cloud) so we surmise that the Uys acquired those shares from Pincus
    • The price of the purchases was P12.5/share, half of the price at the last placement by Pincus
    • At the time of the placement (in May), it was said that the last bit of Pincus’ stake would also be “distributed” at P25/share.
  • Note that this also means that there will be no change in the official ‘public float’ of Converge, contrary to our expectations when Coherent Cloud last sold its stake in May. It should now stay at 31%.

TELCOS
Henry Sy Jr. to bring Elon Musk’s Starlink by end 2023
  • Sy, through his new company Data Lake, has been picked as SpaceX’s reseller and integrator to hook up Filipinos with its high-speed satellite internet service, Starlink
  • Data Lake, which is partly owned by Anthony Almeda, president of Sy-controlled National Grid Corp of the Philippines, has been conducting technical demonstrations of Starlink in the Philippines since early 2022
  • Sy and Almeda signed the agreement with SpaceX on October 18
  • Starlink subscribers need to pay $499 for the starter kit and a monthly fee of $99
