REGIS REPORT

07 February 2022

Today, we cover the re-composition of two indices.

The first one is the re-composition (or “rebasing”) of the consumer price index tracked by the statistics office.  The Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) now uses a 2018 base for the official CPI compared to the 2012 base used until Dec 2021.

The new base now features a lower weight for the Food basket and also for Rice specifically. “Food and Non-Alcoholic Beverages” is now 37.8% of the CPI, down from 38.3%.  Rice itself is now 8.9% , down 60bp.  On the other hand, “Alcoholic Beverages and Tobacco” saw a 60bp increase.  The CPI weight of “Education” also dropped, presumably because of the introduction of free tertiary education in states colleges.

Among the CPI components that saw increases were: Transport (+30bp) and Communication (+50pm). The “Miscellaneous goods and services category” was also expanded to break out “Financial services” and “Personal care”.

The immediate consequence of the CPI rebasing is what appears to be a lower inflation rate. Reported CPI inflation using the 2018 was 3.0% in January, down from 3.2% in Dec.  While no information was provided on what Jan 22 CPI inflation would have been under the old 2012 base, the previously reported Dec 21 inflation was 3.6% -- a difference of 40bp.  This could have implications on policy, possibly reducing pressure on the monetary authorities to tighten money supply given its stated inflation target of 2-4%.

+++

The second index re-composition is the change in the Philippine Stock Exchange Index (PSEi or PCOMP in the Bloomberg).  The Exchange announced on Friday that Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) and Emperador (EMP PM) will be added to the PSEi while Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) and Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM) will be removed.  Contrary to expectations of some, but in line with our own view, Dito CME (DITO PM) was not added to the PSEi.

We estimate a rather outsized inflow into MONDE of US$64m as a result, with inflow into EMP a smaller but still chunky US$41m.  Our estimate of outflows from RRHI and BLOOM are US$21m and US$16m respectively. The weight of recent PSEi addition Converge (CNVRG PM) is also increasing because of a higher float, so we expect a moderate inflow on the rebalancing day.

The changes will take effect at the close of business on Friday, Feb 11.  More detail on the index re-composition below.

+++

Low-cost housing developer 8990 Holdings (HOUSE PM) is planning a follow-on offering of its shares which will involve the sale of the holdings of two large investors, Khazanah Nasional of Malaysia and TPG.  Given its size (up to 1.25bn shares or P15bn at current price), an international tranche is likely to be needed to place the target amount/s.

MACRO
Inflation slows to 3.0% in January
  • Headline inflation slowed down to 3.0% in January 2022 from 3.2% in December 2021 and 3.7% in January 2021
    • The Philippine Statistics Authority re-based the CPI series to 2018
    • Inflation settled at the midpoint of the government’s target range of 2-4%
  • Slowdown due to lower electricity prices and LPG prices, as well as easing inflation for alcoholic beverages and tobacco
    • Restaurants and accommodation services inflation also moderated
  • Using the re-based series, the annual headline inflation for 2021 stood at 3.9% (vs 4.5% for the 2012-based CPI)

 

INFRASTRUCTURE
Gov’t infrastructure spending rises in November
  • Government infrastructure spending (excluding LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) stood at P60bn in November
    • This is up 49% YoY but was slightly down (-1%) MoM
  • YT-November overall infra spending (including LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) to P967bn, up 33% YoY
    • Implied November infra spend at P83bn, up 52% YoY and 8% MoM
    • YT-November infra spend is ahead of the government’s FY21 target of P1.095tn (+26% YoY)
    • For 2022, the government is targeting to spend P1.27tn (+16% YoY)

PROPERTY
8990 Holdings to conduct follow-on offering of up to P26.6bn
  • 8990 Holdings said that it would conduct a follow-on offering of up to 1.25bn common shares at a maximum price of P18.99/share
    • This represents 23% of current shares outstanding
    • At the current HOUSE price of P11.88/share, the offering could be as large as P16.6bn
  • TPG Rafter and Pasir Salak (SPV of Nasional Berhad) will sell a combined 800mn HOUSE shares (15% of current shares outstanding)
  • IHoldings will sell up to 450mn HOUSE shares (8% of current shares outstanding)
    • This assumes that the overallotment option will be exercised
  • After the share sale, up to 450mn new common shares will be issued to IHoldings
    • This would increase shares outstanding by 8%
    • At P11.88/share, 8990 Holdings could raise as much as P5.3bn
  • IHoldings is currently the largest shareholder of 8990 Holdings
  • After all the transactions, IHoldings will still hold 2.2bn HOUSE shares  (unchanged)
    • However, its ownership in HOUSE will be diluted from 39.6% to 37.4% due to the increase in shares outstanding
  • Funds from the primary share issuance will likely be used to pay down debt
    • As of 3Q21, 8990 had net debt of P28.9bn
