REGIS REPORT

26 May 2022

According to media reports, the incoming administration of Ferdinand Marcos, Jr has chosen Benjamin Diokno, current Governor of the Bangko Sentral (BSP), to become Secretary of Finance.  Talk of his appointment as Finance Secretary has already been percolating in recent days and the markets are likely to have half-anticipated it.

Diokno would be a relatively known quantity for the markets and a familiar pair of hands to which the country’s finances would be entrusted. His stint as part of the Duterte economic team (as Budget Secretary) would likely provide some measure of continuity for the current policies. In terms of policy leanings, he is perceived to be more inclined towards deficit spending (and less concerned about budget deficits).

+++

Speaking of Diokno, he also practically guided for another hike in policy rates in June, when he will preside over what is likely to be his last Monetary Board (MB) meeting as BSP Governor.  The BSP raised rates by 25bp last week, its first such hike since 2018.  The US Fed has raised rates by 75bp so far this cycle.

+++

Two more names announced their 1Q results yesterday – Emperador (EMP PM) and parent company Alliance Global (AGI PM). They are not yet the last among our coverage as Vista Land (VLL PM) has yet to release results.

  • Emperador (EMP PM) (-) – 1Q22 profit of P2.1bn was flat YoY and -23% QoQ. The softness was largely driven by a 12% decline in the profit contribution of brandy, which saw volumes and margins impacted by logistics constraints. We believe EMP’s market share in 1Q is now to the low 20s, implying it lost further market share in the quarter.

 

  • Alliance Global (AGI PM) (-) - AGI’s 1Q22 profit hit P3.9bn (+52%YoY), driven by higher contributions from Megaworld and Golden Arches, and a smaller net loss from Travellers. Liquor profits on the other hand were flat, weighed down by a 7% decline in brandy revenues.

+++

On the topic of delayed disclosure of results, the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE PM) suspended trading in eleven companies for failing to submit reportorial requirements, which include their annual results filings for FY21. Among the notable names whose trading will be suspended are 8990 Holdings (HOUSE PM) and Dito CME (DITO PM).

+++

Government infrastructure spending dipped by 3% in 1Q22. This a delayed data point, frankly, given that 1Q GDP and even cement volumes of some listed companies are already out. It does, however, corroborate our view that the touted election spending bump isn’t happening this time around,

MACRO
Infra spend drops 3% in 1Q22
  • Infrastructure spending (excluding LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) grew by 14% YoY to P100bn in March
    • Reversing the 16% YoY drop in 2M22
    • Cited payments of completed projects of Dept. of Public Works, Dept. of National Defense, and Education
    • Brings 1Q22 infra spend down 3% YoY to P190bn
  • Overall infra spend (including LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) in 1Q22 amounted to P253bn, up 4% YoY
    • The government is targeting to spend P1.27tn on infra this year (+13% YoY)

 

MACRO
DoF urges next government to impose new taxes
  • Finance Secretary Carlos G. Dominguez III said in a press conference that the government could cover existing debt by borrowing more or reducing spending by P249bn each year
  • Instead, he said the government has to raise revenues, improve tax administration and cut unnecessary spending
  • The DoF proposed three tax reform packages for 2023-2025, which include -
    • Thee-year deferment of reduction of personal income tax under TRAIN, which will have an estimated average revenue impact of P97.7bn
    • Expansion of VAT base and possible reduction in VAT rate
    • Intensified implementation of income tax on social media influencers
    • Mandatory casino admission charge at P3,500 and 5% tax on gross gaming revenues of electronic betting machines, which is expected to contribute P13.1bn annually
    • Health tax reform that involves increasing taxes on cigarettes, e-cigarettes, alcopops and sweetened beverages estimated to bring in P91.4bn a year
    • Increasing petroleum tax by P1/liter for a minimum of three years, excise tax on domestic coal and imported coal
    • Tax on cryptocurrencies
    • Tax on carbon emissions
  • In total, the projected revenue impact of all packages combined is seen to be an average of P349bn a year between 2023 and 2027

CONSUMER
EMP 1Q22 profit hit P2.1bn (flat YoY, -23%QoQ) - behind
  • Emperador said in a limited press release that its net profit to owners amounted to P2.1bn in 1Q22.
    • The figure is flat vs 1Q21 and down 23%QoQ – behind estimates
  • Profit appears to be tracking behind estimates for the year (cons P11bn)
  • Revenues reached P12.3bn (+2%YoY, -30%QoQ), with the press release citing “challenges posed by global logistics and the Omicron variant”
  • The brandy segment’s profit contribution fell 12%YoY to P1.2bn, dragged by a 6.5% decline in revenues and 2.7ppt contraction in GPM.
    • Without citing volumes, the disclosure attributes the demand weakness to logistics constraints and lockdown restrictions
    • We estimate that volumes may have been down by high single digits, compared to a growth of 6% for Ginebra and 2% for Tanduay
    • We estimate that Emperador’s market share in domestic liquor has dropped to the low 20s vs Tanduay (26%) and Ginebra (47%)
      • Emperador’s market share peaked at 48% in 2013 but has gone down steadily since then
    • Recall in the previous briefing, management warned of cost pressures and logistics issues that have resulted in a number of SKUs not being available.  
  • Profit from whisky meanwhile grew 25% to P893mn. This was driven by a 20% jump in revenues. GP also grew 18% as margins were stable at c.40.3%.
    • Single malt sales continued to grow in “double digits” on back of “strong sales in Europe, Americas and APAC”
  • EMP is trading at 26x FY22E PE and EV/EBITDA of 19x

CONGLOS
AGI 1Q profit +52%YoY to P3.9bn - behind
  • AGI reported a 1Q22 profit of P3.9bn (-20%QoQ, +52%YoY) – tracking behind estimates (vs P17.7bn consensus)
    • The income in the quarter is 52% higher YoY, largely on account of higher contributions from MEG and Golden Arches and a smaller loss from Travellers.
  • Travellers incurred a smaller loss of P207mn (vs -P1.1bn in 1Q21) as GGR increased 43%YoY to P6.5bn
    • Non-gaming revenues also jumped 46%YoY due to higher hotel occupancy rates
  • Meanwhile, GADC’s income jumped to P258mn (+253%YoY) on revenues of P7.2bn (+27%YoY)
    • GPM for the quarter was slightly lower at 6.3% (vs 6.9%), but net margin was markedly higher at 3.6% (vs 1.3%)
  • AGI is trading at 5.7x FY22E PE and a 68% discount to market-based NAV
