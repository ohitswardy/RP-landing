REGIS REPORT

09 June 2022

The Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) has approved a P1.00 (+11%) increase in the minimum fares for jeepneys in Metro Manila and the surrounding regions. Jeepneys remain the main mode of transportation of many and the doubling of diesel prices has made operating them arguably unviable. The fare hike is unlikely to be adequate but drivers will likely “survive” only because of the subsidies being handed out.

The transport fare increase should lead to more ripple effects on prices and inflation over the next few months.

+++

The BPO industry association IBPAP said that the number of workers employed by the industry grew 9% last year, adding 120,000 employees. The headcount growth under pandemic conditions is a surprise and a sign that demand for outsourcing from the West remains robust.

What’s also noteworthy is that incremental demand for office space from the industry was only ~100,000sqm last year, suggesting that workers are not back in the office as much yet (if they ever will be). The big structural issue for the office property sector is how much remote work (work-from-home) will affect demand for office space in the future.

+++

Prime Infra, controlled by ICTSI (ICT PM) and Bloomberry’s (BLOOM PM) Enrique Razon, will build 3,500MW of solar power capacity, with accompanying battery storage. No timetable was disclosed for the entire rollout but of that capacity, 850MW will be for Meralco (MER PM), with 600MW available by 2026.

The fact that only 600MW will be available (and only) in 2026 illustrates our point that rolling out renewables to replace coal-fired capacity isn’t so simple and that the moratorium on new coal power plants in 2020 does not appear to have been well-planned. We expect power supply in Luzon to become progressively tighter over the next few years, with power outages to become more frequent.

+++

The Jefferies commodities analyst raised his coal price estimates by 36% to US$320/MT and 89% to US$255/MT for this year and next year. His long-term coal price assumption was also increased to US$100/MT from US$80/MT.  Note that the US$320/MT price estimate is still 20% below spot prices.

Plugging in those numbers to this year’s assumptions for Semirara (SCC PM) would result in a doubling of our profit forecast for the year (currently at P24bn, equivalent to EPS of P5.6).

+++

The local cement manufacturers are lobbying to have the US$4/MT safeguard duty on imports extended. The safeguard duty is supposed to expire in Oct.

MACRO
LTFRB approves P1 jeepney fare provisional hike in NCR, Central Luzon, and Calabarzon
  • The Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board approved a P1 provisional fare increase on minimum fares for public utility jeepneys (PUJs)
    • Minimum fares for the first four kilometers will rise to P10 beginning June 9
    • There’s no increase in fares for succeeding kilometers
    • Fare increase will cover PUJs in NCR, Region III, and Region IV
  • Transport fares have a weight of 4.54% in the CPI basket
PROPERTY
BPO sector headcount grew 9% in 2021
  • The number of BPO employees grew 9% to 1.44mn in 2021
    • This is already higher than the target of 1.43mn employees in 2022
    • The industry added 120,000 employees in 2021
  • BPO revenue expanded 11% to US$29.5bn in 2021
  • However, the industry only took up c. 100,000sqm of office space in Metro Manila in 2021 (net of space given up)
    • The annual pre-COVID take-up of the BPO industry was 400,000-450,000sqm
  • Our channel checks suggest that a number of BPO firms are looking to expand their office footprint, particularly since the government mandate requiring at least 70% of BPO employees to work in the office
  • However, demand does not appear to be at pre-COVID levels
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 57% of Filinvest Land’s NAV
    • 47% of Megaworld’s NAV
    • 32% of Robinsons Land’s NAV
  • FLI is trading at 69% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY22E P/B, and 5.6X FY22E P/E
  • MEG is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY22E P/B, and 6.3X FY22E P/E
  • RLC is trading at 51% discount to NAV, 0.8X FY22E P/B, and 13.5X FY22E P/E

POWER
Prime Infra unit plans world’s largest solar farm
  • Razon-led Prime Infrastructure Hldgs plans to build a solar farm with a capacity of up to 3,500 MW plus a battery energy storage that can hold up to 4,500 MWH
    • According to president and CEO Guillaume Lucci
  • Project will be under Terra Solar Philippines Inc, a unit of Terra Renewables
    • Terra Renewables partnered with Solar Philippines Power Project Hldgs
    • Terra Solar will supply 850 MW to Meralco from power generated by the facility, of which 600 MW will be available by 2026 and the rest will be delivered by 2027
POWER
Jefferies coal price upgrades
  • Jefferies’ global commodities analyst, Chris Lafemina, published a very bullish report on thermal coal (see report link here)
    • He upgraded seaborne thermal coal price forecasts for 22E/23E/24E by 36%/89%/78% to US$320/255/178 per MT
    • He also upgraded his long-term coal price forecast by 25% to US$100/MT
    • These are lower than the current spot price of US$412/MT
  • If we assume his revisions to Semirara (SCC PM), our DCF-based TP will go up to P41/sh (+64%) while our 2022E/2023E profit forecasts could more than double to P53bn/P36bn, holding other things constant
  • At SCC’s current price and assuming higher coal price estimates, SCC’s FCF for 22F/23F could be as high at P11/sh (=32% yield) and P8/sh (23% yield)

INFRASTRUCTURE
Cement manufacturers seek extension of safeguard duty
  • Cement Manufacturers Association of the Philippines (CeMAP) is seeking to extend the safeguard duty on imported cement, which will expire in October
    • The safeguard duty is currently at P200 per MT (or P8 per 40-kg bag)
  • We estimate the safeguard duty currently accounts for 5% (US$4/MT) of an importer’s total cost (US$77/MT)
  • The safeguard duty is also c. 5% of the ASP of local manufacturers, such as Cemex Philippines (CHP PM)
