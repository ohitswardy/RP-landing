REGIS REPORT

31 January 2022

Dito CME (DITO PM) announced it was “deferring” its P8bn rights issue days after the offer period already lapsed. DITO is the controlling shareholder (54%) of Dito Telecommunity (Dito Tel), the new mobile operator partly owned by China Telecom.

This is despite the fact the deal supposedly had a “firm” underwriting commitment from its sole underwriter China Bank (CHIB PM) and an undertaking from its main shareholder to buy all shares left unsubscribed in the rights issue.

DITO blamed the “less than ideal market conditions” for the collapse of the fund-raising exercise. It’s apparent that (1) not enough minorities were willing to subscribe to the rights issue and (2) the main shareholder of DITO, Udenna Corp, was also unwilling or unable to infuse more money into the company.

Frankly, we think P8bn (<US$160m) was never nearly enough to fund Dito Tel for more than a few months anyway. It might have been enough to tide DITO over, however, until the local regulations were revised to allow China Telecom to take over Dito Tel fully -- IF the administration could get favorable amendments to the Public Service Act enacted.

In the near-term, DITO could face sanctions from the regulators and the Exchange due to the “deferral” of the rights issue days after it was supposed to have been completed. With this incident, we reckon the likelihood of DITO becoming part of the PSEi (“PCOMP”), already unlikely in our view, becomes even more remote.

+++

Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) raised prices for a select number of vehicles this January. This comes after selective price increases implemented in Nov as well. This should help ease the margin pressure arising from the weakness of the PHP. TMP is 51% owned by GT Capital (GTCAP PM).

Incidentally, GTCAP’s property arm Federal Land also revealed a joint venture with Nomura Real Estate. The latter will invest US$324m for a 34% stake in the joint venture, while GTCAP will infuse an undisclosed amount of cash and property assets. The venture will initially develop ~250 hectares of land in Metro Manila, Cavite and Cebu.

+++

The government lowered the Covid-19 Alert level for Metro Manila (NCR) and seven other provinces to Level Two. This will allow businesses to accommodate more people (50% capacity vs 30%) in their establishments.

In addition, and possibly more crucially, the government also announced a relaxation of rules for inbound travelers. The government will now allow fully vaccinated travelers to enter the country without them having to undergo facility-based quarantine. This should pave the way for the gradual normalization of travel, especially air travel, which will help tourism-related businesses, including airlines like Cebu Air (CEB PM).

TOURISM
Gov’t scraps facility quarantine for fully vaccinated inbound international travelers
  • The Philippines is suspending the COVID-19 risk classification for incoming international travelers beginning Feb 1
    • Removal of the red, yellow, green list classification means there will be no distinction regardless if a traveler arrived from a country that is high, moderate, or low risk for COVID-19 transmission
  • There will instead be different requirements for arriving passengers based on their vaccination status; All travelers are required to present a negative RT-PCR test done within 48 hours before departure
    • Fully vaccinated travelers are no longer required to undergo facility-based quarantine. They only have to monitor themselves for symptoms at home for 7 days
    • Unvaccinated and partially vaccinated travelers have to undergo facility-based quarantine until they get a negative test result taken on the 5th day, then complete their 14-day quarantine at home
    • Outbound travelers, regardless of vaccination status, must present a negative RT-PCR test taken within 48 hours before their departure
  • The country will once again accept fully vaccinated foreign tourists from visa-free countries starting Feb 10
  • We believe this development will benefit tourism-related sectors such as airlines (e.g. Cebu Air (CEB PM)) and airports (Megawide’s (MWIDE PM) Mactan Cebu airport)
