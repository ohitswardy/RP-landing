REGIS REPORT

04 August 2022

The earnings results released yesterday all outperformed expectations as consumer spending appears to be roaring back. Both SM (SM PM) and Century Pacific (CNPF PM) showed remarkably robust revenue figures lifted by consumption spending, while Metro Pacific’s (MPI PM) toll road operations also appear to be shrugging off the impact of higher fuel prices and even toll fees.

Overall, early 2Q figures are painting a picture of a solid rebound. The question now is how sustainable the current pace of activity is in the face of higher inflation and personal balance sheets that are still being repaired.

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (+) – 1H earnings came in at P25.5bn, up +27% YoY which implied 2Q profit was P13.5bn (also +27%). The return of consumer spending is evident, even if it hasn’t quite reached pre-pandemic levels. Revenue for SM Retail is now almost the level of 2Q19. Income, in fact, was already higher than 2019 though we suspect that was a result of rental concessions from sister firm SM Prime (SMPH PM). Given the expected contributions of the geothermal field operator PGPC (retroactive to the start of 2022), SM’s FY22 profit is likely to far exceed Street estimates.

  • Century Pacific (CNPF PM) (+) – The margin contraction in 2Q22 was not bad as initially expected despite (1) raw material prices peaking, and (2) more gradual pricing action relative to its peers (e.g. URC and Monde). Implied volume growth (>10%) was healthy despite the mid-single digit price increase, while emerging categories (pet food, Unmeat, etc) provided a boost to top line growth. The next 1-2 quarters is will continue to be a balancing act as it winds down high-cost inventory and implements further price adjustments, but it appears CNPF is weathering the challenging cost environment relatively well.

  • Metro Pacific (MPI PM) (+) – Recurring profit jumped 25% YoY to P7.6bn in 1H22, on track to beat Regis and consensus FY22E. 2Q22 profit alone rebounded by 43% QoQ/27% YoY, with (a) strong profits from Meralco’s generation unit (see MER results recap) and (b) better-than-expected organic revenues from its toll road unit. Its water business could also see significant upside from its ongoing rate review. Valuations are attractive, trading at a 48% discount to its market-based NAV.

SM Prime (SMPH PM) CFO John Ong addressed questions on the issue of rising cancellations of residential property purchases yesterday, saying that there was about P6bn worth of revenue reversals in 1H22, mostly in 2Q. This is around 1/4th of what should have been its residential revenue and a fairly large proportion. We note, however, that this is likely an accumulation of cancellations over a few quarters but booked only in 2Q.

The cancellation figures suggest issues for the residential property segment, particularly due to the decline in POGO presence. Filinvest Land (FLI PM) just reported its residential sales figures for 2Q/1H. which are up +31% YoY. The fact that the figures look strong and don’t appear to be affected by cancellations much should merit closer scrutiny.
