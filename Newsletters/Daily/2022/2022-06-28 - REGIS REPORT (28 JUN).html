REGIS REPORT

28 June 2022

Property consultancy Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) claims that POGOs are trickling back, with take-up of around 20,000sqm so far in 2Q22. Apparently, this is the first new take-up of office space by POGOs in over two years (i.e., since 1Q20).

Is this a sign that POGOs are returning? Property firms and property brokers probably hope so but we have yet to be convinced. Office vacancies (19%) are at their highest in recorded memory and rents are down 20-30%, so a return of POGOs could have a big impact there.

+++

Metro Pacific (MPI PM) disclosed it was acquiring 51% of dairy producer, Carmen’s Best Group, in a deal worth P198m (less than $4m). The Carmen’s Best Group is an integrated dairy firm producing and selling premium ice cream and milk products. The transaction is being justified as a way of diversifying out of highly regulated industries, but the purchase, to use the tired cliché, will not move the needle for MPI.

+++

Union Bank (UBP PM) is infusing PhP2bn into its digital bank subsidiary UnionDigital, which will be launching operations as soon as next month. UnionDigital is one of six entities granted digital banking licenses by the Bangko Sentral (BSP). These entities were given one year from when they were awarded licenses to begin operations.

+++

The anti-dummy case against Megawide (MWIDE PM) officers was dismissed by a local court, which cited the recent passage of the Amended Public Service Act. The newly-enacted law lifted foreign ownership limits on, among other industries, airport operations. MWIDE and its partner GMR were being accused of circumventing these restrictions in the anti-dummy case.

+++

The PHP continued its slide yesterday, breaching the psychological PhP55/USD threshold before snapping back before closing. The PHP is now the worst performing among ASEAN currencies YTD (-7%), but that should not come as a big surprise given soaring commodity prices and the country’s position as a net importer of most of them. The impact of expanding trade and current account deficits is also being compounded by the interest rate differentials (Fed hikes vs. BSP hikes), which have aggravated / accelerated the fundamental trends behind the depreciation.

PROPERTY
POGOs start taking up office space again
  • Property Consultant David Leechiu said that POGOs recently took up c. 20,000sqm of office space
  • This is the first (take-up) transaction since 1Q20
  • In 2019, POGOs occupied about 1.4mn sqm of office space in Metro Manila but have since vacated about 600,000sqm
  • Metro Manila office vacancy rose from 4% in 2019 to 19% currently
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 57% of Filinvest Land’s NAV
    • 47% of Megaworld’s NAV
    • 32% of Robinsons Land’s NAV
  • FLI is trading at 71% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY22E P/B, and 5.2X FY22E P/E
  • MEG is trading at 72% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY22E P/B, and 5.4X FY22E P/E
  • RLC is trading at 57% discount to NAV, 0.7X FY22E P/B, and 11.9X FY22E P/E
CONGLOS
MPIC enters strategic partnership with Carmen’s Best Group
  • Metro Pacific (MPI PM) bought a 51% stake in The Laguna Creamery (TLC) for P198mn
    • Transaction is a combination of primary and secondary shares
    • Pre-money valuation at P288mn for 100% equity; Post-money valuation at P388mn
    • Shares acquired from Carmen’s Best Group, the group behind premium ice cream brand “Carmen’s Best” and locally pasteurized and homogenized fresh milk, “Holly’s milk”
  • Under the partnership, TLC will be consolidating the assets and business of the Carmen’s Best Group
  • Transaction is conditional upon customary regulatory confirmation and approvals
  • Latest development is part of MPI’s plans to diversify out of highly regulated businesses
    • Last April, MPI revived its ambitions into real estate/hospital sector, after taking full ownership of niche property developer, Landco
  • As at end-March 2022, MPI’s parent-level gross cash stood at P17bn and net debt stood at P66bn
  • MPI is trading at a 53% discount to its market-based NAV of P7.50/sh

INFRASTRUCTURE
Court dismisses anti-dummy law case against Megawide
  • The Regional Trial Court of Lapu-Lapu City dismissed the criminal case against the directors and officers of Megawide (MWIDE PM) for the alleged violation of the anti-dummy law
    • Directors accused include MWIDE CEO and President Edgar Saavedra and three others
    • Omnibus Order cited the enactment of RA 11659 or “Amended Public Service Act” last March 2022, which lifted foreign ownership limits of airports
  • We estimate the airport unit accounts for c. 53% (P3.9/sh) of MWIDE’s NAV of P7.2/sh (vs. current share price of P3.17/sh)
    • Pre-pandemic, the airport unit accounted for c. 50% of MWIDE’s EBITDA and c. 60% of profit
