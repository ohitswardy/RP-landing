REGIS REPORT

21 July 2022

BPI (BPI PM) kicks off the results season with pretty solid numbers. Recurring NPAT for 1H22 was P16.7bn, up 42% YoY and ahead of REGIS full year forecast of P28.5bn (Bberg consensus P31.3bn). Headline NPAT of P20.4bn included one-off sale of real estate in Makati for an after-tax gain of P3.7bn. The outperformance was driven by opex, which was up just 7% YoY, well behind management guidance of +20% for the full-year. We believe this will accelerate sharply in 2H as the bank ratchets up its IT spend. Otherwise, the story is going according to script. Loans surged 14.4% YoY, NIMs were up 15bps YoY in 1H, and NPLs were down 39bps QoQ to 1.99%. We maintain our BUY call on BPI, and our positive stance on the sector in general.

The bank will hold a briefing at 4:30pm tomorrow.

+ + + +

The economic reopening / recovery story remains in full swing. Cebu Air (CEB PM) has increased its seat capacity to 86% of pre-Covid levels as of the first two weeks of July, up from 81% in June. The company has resumed some international routes, and has also added domestic flights from the Cebu airport. The company also increased its fuel surcharges effective August.

+ + + +

And now for the bad news. The country’s Balance of Payments (BOP) clocked in at a $1.57bn deficit in June, taking the 1H22 figure to a $3.1bn deficit. This was due to the government’s payment of external debt, as well as the rapidly expanding trade deficit. The weak external position will continue to weigh on the PHP.

BANKS
BPI 1H22 recurring NPAT P16.7bn, ahead

In a press release, BPI (BPI PM) reported headline NPAT for 1H22 of P20.4bn, up 73% YoY. However, this figure included roughly P3.7bn worth of net gain from the sale of a Makati property. Without this, recurring NPAT was P16.7bn, up 42% YoY, ahead of REGIS full-year forecast (P28.5bn) and consensus (P31.3bn). The beat came mostly from opex, which was up just 7%, well below management guidance of +20% for the year. We believe this will accelerate in 2H as the bank ramps up its IT spend.

The story remains consistent with the thesis laid out in our recent report: loan growth is accelerating (14.4%), NIMs are expanding (+15bps), and asset quality is improving (NPL ratio 1.99%, NPL cover 171%). We have a BUY on the stock.

Some key highlights from the PR:

  • Loan growth accelerated to 14.4% YoY, from 7.1% in 1Q22. Corporate loans jumped 16.3% YoY, driven in part by BPI’s funding of the sale of PLDT’s cell towers. Credit cards also jumped 16.5%, while auto loans turned positive (5.9%). Deposits jumped 18.3% YoY, with CASA up 12.6%.
  • NIMs widened 15bps YoY by the bank’s estimate to 3.46%, taking NII up 16.6% YoY.
  • Non-interest income surged 32% YoY due in large part to the sale of property in Makati, resulting in a P3.7bn gain after tax. Trading/fx gains dropped 36% YoY.
  • Opex was up 7.3% YoY, well behind management guidance of 20% YoY for the full-year. We suspect that the IT spend will accelerate into 1H22, particularly as some of the 3rd-party contracts may come with milestone payments.
  • Asset quality improved significantly. NPL ratio was 1.99%, down 39bps QoQ and 95bps YoY. NPL cover increased to 170.7%, compared to 149.6% in 1Q22. This led to provision expense dropping 23% YoY to P5.0bn.

Overall, a solid set of results. The bank will hold a briefing tomorrow, 22 July, at 4:30pm

Cebu Air sustains capacity recovery momentum
  • Jefferies’s airline capacity data showed Cebu Air’s (CEB PM) capacity has reverted to 86% of pre-pandemic levels in the first two weeks of July

    • We reckon capacity improvements this time around were driven by the resumption of international flights, as well as more domestic flights mounted from Cebu airport

    • This is up from Level 11 in July and reflects jet kero prices
    • Represents a 10% increase MoM in the fuel surcharge
