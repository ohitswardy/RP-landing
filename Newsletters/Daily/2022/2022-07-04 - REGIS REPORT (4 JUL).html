REGIS REPORT

04 July 2022

In an eyebrow-raising move, one of President Marcos, Jr’s first official acts was to veto a bill that would have established the Bulacan Airport Special Economic Zone and Freeport (House Bill 7575). The bill would have been a huge boost to the property values around the Bulacan Airport being built by San Miguel (SMC PM). In the veto letter, Marcos said that the bill, if passed into law, would “pose substantial fiscal risk to the country”, and had other provisions that “conflict with other agencies’ mandates”. It also noted that the CREATE Act rendered the creation of new Special Economic Zones (SEZs) moot, as it allowed eligible enterprises to apply for fiscal incentives even if they are outside existing SEZs. Also, the Bulacan airport is quite close to two other SEZs in Subic and Clark.

The previous Department of Finance (DOF) was vehemently opposed to HB 7575 for the exact same reasons, and had recommended strongly for outgoing President Duterte to veto the bill. However, the law was passed by the House in 2020, but was only approved by the Senate on 26 May 2022, and was transmitted to the Office of the President only on 03 June. Had President Marcos not vetoed the bill, it would have lapsed into law on 03 July. The veto has generated a surprising (or not) amount of chatter on business news sites, considering a) the beneficiary of the bill, b) the Congressmen and Senators pushing the bill, c) the timing of the approval/transmittal, and d) the swiftness and firmness of the veto. For some entertaining reading, click here and here; popcorn recommended.

However surprising the veto was, we believe it was entirely appropriate. The new administration will need all the tax revenues it can get if it is to get the government finances in order post-Covid. The country’s government debt to GDP ratio jumped from 40% at end-2019 to 60.1% at end-2021, and is expected to peak at 67% in 2023. In all likelihood, it will take at least a decade to get back to pre-Covid levels. (See related news below.) The country needs to grow out of the debt, which will require a continuation of the “Build, Build, Build” infra spending. The government will likely need to find new revenue (tax) sources, and granting new ecozones will not help.

Metro Pacific (MPI PM) disclosed that its subsidiary MPTC has agreed to purchase a 40% stake in the Jakarta-Cikampek (Elevated) Toll Road in Indonesia for P16.2bn, for an implied valuation of 0.7x Price/book. However, since the acquisition will be funded mostly with debt, we reckon it will have a minimal impact to our NAV estimate, against which by the way MPI already trades at a 54% discount.

Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) has signed a JV with Shell to develop at least 1GW of renewable energy (RE) projects by 2028. The JV will initially focus on solar, but is looking into other technologies including offshore wind and energy storage systems.

MACRO
Government debt dips in May
  • The government’s debt stock decreased by P267bn (-2%) MoM to P12.5tn in May
    • Cited settlement of P300bn in provisional advances from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas
  • Compared with end-2021, the national government’s debt was P768bn (+6%) higher
    • Of the P12.5tn debt stock, 69% were domestic borrowings while 31% were sourced externally
  • The government expects its debt-to-GDP ratio will peak in 2023 at 67% before falling to 66% in 2026
    • Former Finance Secretary Dominguez previously said that the country’s debt-to-GDP ratio may not return to pre-pandemic levels (40%) for at least 10 years
    • To reach this ratio by 2031, a median annual primary balance (revenues minus non-interest expenditures) increase of 2.42% of GDP would be needed
      • Based on the most optimistic scenario that assumes a GDP growth rate of 7% and a real interest rate of 2%

INFRASTRUCTURE
President Marcos vetoes bill creating San Miguel’s Bulacan airport an ecozone
  • President Ferdinand "Bongbong" Marcos Jr. vetoed a proposed bill (House Bill 7575) to build the Bulacan Airport City, a project of San Miguel Corp. (SMC PM), as a special economic zone and freeport
    • Cited provisions that "pose substantial fiscal risks to the country" and its "infringement on or conflict with other agencies' mandates and authorities
    • Both chambers of Congress passed the Bill on May 31
    • Veto of HB 7575 is among the first official acts of Marcos Jr
  • The P740bn Bulacan Airport City project nonetheless secured a 50-year legislative franchise last December 2020
    • Franchise allows for a 10-year tax holiday
CONGLOS
MPI tollroad unit buys 40% stake in Indonesian tollroad
  • PT Margautama Nusantara (MUN), a 72%-owned unit of Metro Pacific Tollways Corp. (MPTC), is acquiring a 40% stake in Jasa Marga Jalanlayang Cikampek (JJC)
    • JJC holds the concession for the 38-km elevated tollroad called Jakarta-Cikampek Elevated (“Japex”)
    • Japex serves as an entry/exit gate from Jakarta to West, Central and East Java; Began operations on Dec 2019
    • JJC is an 80%-owned unit of Jasa Marga (JSMR IJ)
    • Transaction is subject to certain conditions
    • Payment will be in tranches: 18% (July 31, 2022), 73% (Dec 20, 2022), 9% (Dec 31, 2024)
  • MUN is buying the 40% stake for IDR4.4tn (P16.2bn)
    • Valuation implies 0.7x PBV, which is lower than valuation of JSMR IJ (1x)
  • Acquisition will be funded with short-term debt initially at MUN level
    • MUN will effectively be funding the acquisition with cash through a combination of sales of its tollroad assets and additional cash infusion from MPTC/MPI
    • As at end-March 2022, MPI’s parent-level gross cash stood at P17bn and net debt stood at P66bn
  • MPI is trading at a 54% discount to its market NAV of P7.58/sh
MINING
Nickel Asia ties up with Shell to develop 1GW of renewable energy
  • Nickel Asia Corporation disclosed that its renewable energy unit Emerging Power Inc (EPI) signed a joint venture with Shell Overseas Investments B.V> to develop 1GW of renewable energy projects in the Philippines by 2028
  • The JV between Emerging Power Inc would “potentially increase” to 3GW
  • The venture will focus on utility-scale solar photovoltaic but will also consider onshore wind and energy storage systems
  • NIKL owns 87% of Emerging Power Inc
  • EPI currently operates a 100MW solar farm in Mt. Sta. Rita in Subic Bay Freeport Zone in Zambales
    • It recently secured a second 100MW solar service contract with the DOE for the Mt. Sta. Rita site, with development slated to begin this year
    • It also developing pilot geothermal projects in Mindoro and Biliran
