REGIS REPORT

05 August 2022

We have another batch of 2Q results today, and this set continues the streak of profit upside surprises (mostly) since this earnings season started. So far, ten (10) out of the 15 firms that have reported 2Q numbers delivered better-than-expected results.

  • PLDT (TEL PM) (+) – 1H core income, inclusive of the attributable losses of Voyager, amounted to P15.5bn, +9% higher YoY and ahead of expectations. This is despite the rather uninspiring revenue numbers in 2Q and 1H. Wireless service revenue remains lower YoY and management seems concerned about the near-term prognosis amidst the soaring cost of living. For home broadband, revenue growth still seems healthy (+21% YoY in 2Q) but the net subscriber adds was the lowest since 1Q20 and is something to watch out for.

  • ICTSI (ICT PM) (+) - 1H22 profit after perps jumped 70% YoY to US$276mn while EBITDA rose 26% YoY to US$672mn, trending ahead of estimates largely due to better yields and a lower tax rate. Throughput volumes rose +6% YoY in 2Q22, slightly accelerating from 1Q (+5%). Operations remain robust, with MoM trends in July improving. Yield per TEU remained high at US$179 in 2Q and US$181 in 1H22, with more tariff hikes expected in 2H.

  • Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM) (=) – 1H22 profit outperformance was largely driven by a lower-than-expected tax rate, but operating profit (+64% YoY) is tracking in line. 2Q22 profit more than doubled YoY driven by operating leverage from the strong SSSG. Consolidated SSSG accelerated to +15% in 2Q (+5% in 1Q), with majority of formats posting double-digit growth. Supermarkets SSSG came in at +9%, outperforming figures reported by SM Retail (food) and Puregold (ex-S&R). Meanwhile, non-essentials (Dept Stores, DIY, Specialty Stores, CVS) saw a sharp recovery in SSS levels, but these are still tracking ~20% below pre-pandemic. There is likely room for these formats to improve further as we move into the seasonally strong 4Q.

  • Puregold (PGOLD PM) (=) – We received further detail on PGOLD’s SSSG trends for the quarter. The core Puregold brand reported a 6% contraction in average tickets as walk-in customers return to the stores. These customers account for 70-75% of Puregold’s store traffic, but generate only a third of the ticket size vs bulk buyers under its Aling Puring loyalty program.

  • Semirara (SCC PM) (+) – 2Q22 core profit was surprisingly strong at P10.07bn, buoyed by lower-than-expected cash and non-cash costs at the coal mining unit. FCF generation in 1H was strong, with end-June cash levels already at P26bn, suggesting the company is ripe to declare a hefty special div (forecast: P21bn or P5/sh, ~12% yield) in 2H. The shares, however, have already outperformed and have breached our TP of P38/sh. We downgrade our rating to Hold.
