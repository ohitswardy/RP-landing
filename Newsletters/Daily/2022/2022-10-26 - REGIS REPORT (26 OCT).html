We initiate coverage (finally) on Converge ICT (CNVRG PM), with a Buy rating and a target price of P16.50/share, which represents +32% upside from current levels.

Broadband services provider Converge has seen its share price plunge over the past few months, as the market cast off unrealistic expectations about its growth prospects. Despite many investors throwing in the towel on this name (or maybe because of it), we are initiating with a BUY on Converge with the shares just a tad above its all-time low.

We believe there is a fundamental case for the stock. First, there is secular growth potential with broadband penetration at only 25% even if the price points going forward will be lower than what the market previously expected. Second, the company has proven itself to be a nimble operator, and has managed to practically match if not sometimes exceed PLDT’s (TEL PM) fiber rollout over the past few years. Third, Converge is a low-cost operator with EBITDA margins of over 55%, allowing it produce ROICs of ~20%. It helps that the company is run by its entrepreneur-founders.

The share price is down over 70% from its peak last year as expectations of continued 40-50% growth met with reality. Results of the past two quarters have injected sobriety into estimates, which also led to capitulation of many holders. We think current levels now offer attractive value, as the stock is only <10x PER and 5x EBITDA, on our estimates of +15% EPS CAGR.

Hardware retailer Wilcon (WLCON PM) is the second firm to release 3Q results and, like BPI (BPI PM) before it, it delivered better-than-expected numbers, with net profit surging 77% YoY. SSSG accelerated to +23.5%, albeit coming from a low base, but top line momentum continues to be strong given the healthy pipeline of private construction projects that were delayed by the pandemic. Strong demand, coupled with a benign competitive environment, has given Wilcon more flexibility in pricing, resulting in continued margin expansion despite the challenging cost environment.

The Philippine Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) approved the distribution by Ayala Corp (AC PM) of some of its ACEN (ACEN PM) shares to shareholders as a property dividend. AC shareholders will receive a 4.7% direct stake in ACEN as a result. The final timing of when investors will receive the ACEN shares, however, has still not been determined.

Meanwhile, ACEN also disclosed it intends to buy 43MW of power from the GN Power Dinginin coal plant controlled by Aboitiz Power (AP PM). The power supply deal should help cap ACEN’s short position in the spot market.

President Marcos is set to issue an Executive Order (EO) making the indoor use of face masks optional / voluntary, in a bid to further ease pandemic-related regulations.  Masks will still be required in public transportation and medical facilities. The easing of rules should pave the way for the further normalization of activity and thus the economy.

Philippines to make indoor face mask use voluntary
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. is set to issue an executive order making the wearing of face masks indoor areas voluntary, according to Tourism Secretary Christina Frasco

  • Masks will still be required in public transportation, medical transportation and medical facilities
  • According to Frasco, the direction of the Marcos administration is to lift the remainder of COVID-related travel restrictions
