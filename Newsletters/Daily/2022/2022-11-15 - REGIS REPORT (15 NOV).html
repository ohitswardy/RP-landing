REGIS REPORT

15 November 2022

Another big batch of 3Q/9M results in our round-up today, as the earnings season begins drawing to a close. The results are still generally leaning towards ‘beats’ rather than ‘misses’.

  • Ayala Corp (AC PM) (=) – Net income for 9M22 came in at P24bn, up 23% inclusive various exceptional items both at its operating companies and the holding company level. AC’s disclosure revealed another one-off item in 3Q. Excluding various one-off items, AC says net income would have been P21.8bn, up +13% YoY. AC will hold its briefing this afternoon.
  • GT Capital (GTCAP PM) (=) - 9M22 core profit after preferred dividends of P10.89bn (+43% YoY) was within expectations, as the shortfalls in the auto and property segments in 3Q22 were offset by the outperformances of Metrobank and Metro Pacific. GP margins for the auto unit contracted sharply in 3Q22 due to unfavorable FX, negating better-than-expected sales volumes and price hikes. Meanwhile, profits from the property unit dropped in 3Q22, as sales cancellations in one location remained elevated.

  • Megaworld (MEG PM) (=) – 9M22 operating profit rose 30%, thanks in large part to the 3ppt improvement in residential GPM. However, headline profit was up only 3% (to P8.4bn) due to forex losses on US$ debt. 3Q22 reservation sales jumped 102% YoY (+24% QoQ) as sales to international buyers (about 90% of whom are OFWs) rose to 20% of 9M22 sales, from only 14% as of 1H22. Furthermore, presales have not slowed down in early 4Q22.

  • San Miguel Food & Bev (FB PM) (+) – FB’s 3Q22 profit fell 29%QoQ as profit from the food segment contracted by more than 50%. EBIT margins for food fell more than 5ppts to just 5.0% in 3Q22, succumbing to higher prices of inputs. Gin volumes continued to inch up (8%QoQ, 10.6mn cases), while beer volumes dipped 8%QoQ to 48mn cases after the reopening-induced jump in 2Q. Margins for beer, in particular, held particularly steady compared to its other segments.

  • First Gen (FGEN PM) (=) – 3Q22 profits dipped 5%QoQ to $63.8mn, after management reported a reduction in natural gas earnings due to derating of its San Gabriel plant (derated by 210MW out of 420MW). The decline was partially offset by a recovery in contribution from EDC which saw the return of Unified Leyte and higher revenues from repriced contracts/WESM sales.

  • Alliance Global (+) – The QoQ decline from Emperador’s (EMI PM) profits (-38%QoQ to P2.0bn) dragged AGI’s profits sequentially lower (-15%QoQ, P3.7bn). The 9M22 number, however, is still tracking mildly ahead of estimates due to lower-than-expected losses from Travellers. The group will hold a briefing tomorrow on its results.
  • Manila Water (MWC PM) (+) – 9M22 profit before the share of participating prefs rose 29% YoY to P4.5bn, trending ahead of forecasts due to non-operating items related to the concession agreement. The 1% YoY decline in 9M22 EBITDA is within expectations. Operations are doing well but the completion of the rate rebasing is the key event to watch out for in the next few weeks, with the company proposing a pretty large tariff hike (+P20 per cubic meter or +75%).

  • Shakey’s (PIZZA PM) (-) – 3Q22 NPAT rose 17% QoQ as the strong top line momentum continued into the quarter. Gross margins compressed sequentially (likely due to input cost inflation), but was cushioned by proportionately lower opex. This led to a mild expansion (+30bps QoQ) in EBITDA margin. While 9M22 NPAT (P454mn) appears to be lagging our FY22E, we believe there is room to catch up during the seasonally strong 4Q. We expect to get further detail after the company’s briefing tomorrow.

We upgrade our Metrobank (MBT PM) 2022-24E earnings forecasts by 20-25%, and raise our target price by 17% to P76/sh, maintaining our Buy call. Our estimates were raised after the 9M22 NPAT grew 45% YoY (3Q22 +77%), on accelerating loan growth, wider NIMs, and slower opex growth. We are wary, though, of the 20bps QoQ increase in NPLs. That said, NPL cover was still high at 172% in 3Q22. At our target price, the stock would trade at just 1x book value, but would already give a potential total return of close to 50%.

    • AC cited a one-off loss recorded in 3Q22 as well as a gain from the sale of power assets in the prior year
  • Most of AC’s main subsidiaries performed in line with or better than expectations for 9M/3Q (BPI, Ayala Land and Globe)

AGI 9M22 profit at P11.9bn, flat YoY
  • AGI disclosed that its net income for 9M22 was flat at P11.9bn (-1%YoY)
    • Taking out a one-time gain by Travellers last year, the YoY growth would have been 29%
  • The 9M figure implies a 15%QoQ drop in profits to P3.7bn in 3Q22
  • Emperador’s attributable profit for 9M was flat at P7.2bn, which implies 3Q was down 38%QoQ
    • The company cited higher input costs, logistics, A&P and increased on-trade activities
    • These tempered revenue growth from whisky (16%) and brandy (8%)
  • GADC’s profits fell to P232mn in 3Q22 from P510mn in 2Q22; 9M22 income of P1.0bn was nevertheless more than 5x the 9M21 income of P180mn
  • Travellers was back in the red, incurring a loss of P234mn in 3Q
    • The loss for 9M of P209mn is trending lower than estimates
  • At P9/share, AGI is trading at a 75% discount to market-based NAV

CONSUMER
PIZZA 9M22 NPAT at P454mn, behind Regis as margins lag expectations
  • PIZZA’s 9M22 NPAT came in at P454mn, a turnaround from the P35mn loss in 9M21. This is tracking behind our FY22E, but broadly in line with consensus. While revenues (+80% YoY to P6.8bn) are ahead of expectations, margins are lagging.
  • 3Q22 NPAT was P204mn, also a turnaround from the P49mn loss in the same period last year. Revenues surged over 2x YoY on the back of 60-70% SSSG (estimated), coming from a low base. Operating leverage drove EBITDA to P479mn during the quarter.
  • On a QoQ basis, GPM contracted 30bps, likely due to input cost inflation. Despite this, EBITDA margin was lifted by proportionately lower opex
  • The company’s briefing is on 16 Nov
  • PIZZA currently trades at 14x 2023E PE
