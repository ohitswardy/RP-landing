REGIS REPORT

04 May 2022

The preferences barely changed in the last major opinion poll before the elections to be held next week. Ferdinand “Bongbong” Marcos, Jr maintained his commanding lead with over 50% of preferences. If that holds on election day (May 9), Marcos will be the first candidate to be elected president with an absolute majority under the 1986 Constitution.

+++

Data from the statistic office show, unsurprisingly, that wholesale prices have been rising at an accelerated pace. Prices were up 5.6% in Feb, the fastest pace in 11 years. This is likely to be indication of where consumer prices are heading in the next few months. CPI inflation for April will be released tomorrow, with consensus expecting inflation at 4.6% for the month, higher than the Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) target band of 2-4%.

+++

President Duterte ordered a halt to online cockfight betting operations (“e-sabong”) following an outcry over its social effects and the “disappearance” of people connected to the business. E-sabong generates around P60bn in bets monthly, and the government’s cut has been >P600m/month this year. Globe’s (GLO PM) e-wallet GCash has been among the main ways to top up e-sabong wallets, but the revenue loss if it’s halted will be quite small.

+++

The weighting of Monde (MONDE PM) in the PSEi (“PCOMP”) is expected to increase to 2.5% (from 2.0%) in the upcoming rebalancing next week as its PSE-recognized public float was raised to 38% (from 31%). All other  components will see a small dip to make room.

POLITICS
Marcos Jr, Robredo barely move in final Pulse Asia survey
  • Bongbong Marcos Jr. maintained his lead in the latest presidential preference survey conducted by Pulse Asia
    • VP Leni Robredo remained at a distant second
    • Poll was conducted between April 16-21 on a sample size of 2400 adults
  • In the VP race, Davao city mayor Sara Duterte is still the frontrunner

MACRO
Bulk prices surge to over 10-year high
  • Nationwide bulk prices of general goods rose to their highest level in more than a decade in February due to higher fuel costs, preliminary data from PSA showed
  • The country’s general wholesale price index (GWPI) jumped 5.6% in February, from 4.6% in January and 2.5% in February 2021
  • This was the highest reading in more than 10 years or since the 5.8% growth recorded in December 2011
  • YTD, the national GWPI averaged 5.1%, faster than the 2.3% in the same period in 2021
  • The GWPI is u sed to monitor the wholesale trade sector and serves as a basis for price adjustments in business contracts and projects
  • The heavily weighted food index, which contributed 36.8% in the theoretical wholesale basket of goods, went up 4.9%YoY in February from 3.9% the previous months
    • Manufactured goods classified by materials increased 6.6% from 6.4% the previous month
    • Minerals fuels, lubricants, and related materials surged 37.7% in February, from 30.4% the previous month and 2.1% in February 2021
    • Crude materials, inedible except fuels, also jumped 26.7% from 16.3% in January
  • Luzon outpaced the national level’s GWPI with 5.9% in February, faster than the 4.7% in January and 2.8% in Feb 2021
PROPERTY
Wholesale prices of building materials rise 6.6% in March
  • According to the Philippine Statistics Authority, wholesale prices of building materials in Metro Manila rose 6.6% in March
    • From 5.2% in February  
  • The impact of rising material costs on residential earnings will be larger than the effect on rental earnings
  • We expect this development to have only minor impact to our earnings estimates so far, partly because of the increase in the proportion of residential sales from horizontal projects (mostly lots)  
  • ALI is trading at 43% discount to NAV, 2.0X FY22E P/B, and 25.2X FY22E P/E
    • Horizontal projects comprised 47% of FY21 reservation sales (26% in 2019)
  • MEG is trading at 66% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY22E P/B, and 6.6X FY22E P/E
    • Horizontal projects comprised 32% of FY21 reservation sales (9% in 2019)
GAMING
Duterte stops e-sabong operations
  • President Duterte said Monday he will order the termination of online cockfighting operations in the Philippines, citing the impact on families and values
  • Duterte gave Interior Secretary Ano until May 3 to finalize the order to terminate e-sabong operations
  • PAGCOR earlier estimated collections from e-sabong licensees averaged P400mn/month from April to December in 2021 and rose to P640mn/month since January 2022
  • In a senate hearing in March, Atong Ang of Lucky 8 Star Quest Inc (one of the 8 gaming companies given a license to operate e-sabong by PAGCOR) said that total monthly bets from e-sabong amount to “more or less P60bn a month” from which his firm gets 5% or around P3bn
    • From this amount, Ang said that agents get P2-2.5bn
  • In a letter to the Senate, G-Xchange AVP Gilbert Escoto claimed GCash only earned P600k in average net revenues from Pitmasters in 2021
    • He also clarified that GCash does not earn revenue from e-sabong but from the top-up of funds from GCash to Pitmasters wallet
INFRASTRUCTURE
Megawide, Japanese partners bag P13bn subway deal
  • A joint venture composed of two Japanese construction companies and Megawide (MWIDE PM) bagged the P13.26bn contract to build two stations of the Metro Manila subway
    • The two Japanese firms are Tokyu Construction, Tobishima Construction
    • The contract involves the construction of the Ortigas North and South subway stations and tunneling work
  • The 36-km Metro Manila subway is estimated to cost P357bn and is expected to link key areas within the capital district starting from Valenzuela up to Manila’s Ninoy Aquino International Airport (Naia) Terminal 3
    • Project is estimated to be completed partially in 2025 (previously 2023); Full completion by 2027 (previously 2025)
    • The Department of Transportation is bidding out 13 contracts, six of which have been awarded
    • Once operational, the project will reduce travel time to 35 mins from 1 hour and 10mins
  • MWIDE’s order book stood at P60bn in end-2022
  • MWIDE is trading at 12.9x FY22E EV/EBITDA
OTHERS
MONDE weight in PSEi will increase due to increase in free float
  • The PSE reported that the free float of MONDE increased from 31% to 38% and this will be reflected in the PSEi rebalancing on (the open of) May 16 
  • Our initial estimates suggest that MONDE’s weighting in the PSEi will increase from 2.0% to 2.5%
  • We estimate that MONDE will experience inflow of US$11.8mn and all other PSEi constituents will see a combined outflow of US$11.8mn
