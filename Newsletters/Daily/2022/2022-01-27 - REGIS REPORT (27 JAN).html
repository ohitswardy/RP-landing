REGIS REPORT

27 January 2022

The government, through the Dept of Energy (DOE), is inviting bids for as much as 2,000MW of renewable energy supply under its Green Energy Auction Program (GEAP). The program aims to encourage new RE supply by providing an off-taker for what might still be financially uncompetitive RE power.

While the aims appear to be laudable, we suspect any new supply it will encourage will not be enough or not arrive in time to avert what we reckon will be a very tight supply situation in the next two to three years. The government has a stated goal of having RE supply 35% of the country’s electricity needs by 2030, from the 21% last year.

+++

E-commerce firm Growsari raised US$45m from KKR, based on a news report yesterday. The firm, partly owned by Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM), operates an online B2B platform that allows  mom-and-pop shops (“sari-sari stores”) to purchase goods from it at lower prices. We reckon it is potentially a threat to a portion of Puregold’s (PGOLD PM) business as the latter generates as much as 15-20% of revenue from sari-sari stores under its Aling Puring program.

+++

The Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) issued a revision to its previously announced 3Q GDP growth number ahead of the release of the initial 4Q GDP estimate. The revised growth figure for 3Q is 6.9%, from 7.1%.

+++

DM Wenceslao (DMW PM) disclosed it sold another parcel of land within the area near Manila Bay, apparently to the same buyer who bought it in the transaction a few weeks ago. As we previously noted, the price of the land sale was higher than we thought likely given the collapse in demand for property in that vicinity. The departure of online gaming operations (“POGOs”) has caused vacancies to rise acutely over the past year.

A higher valuation for property in that area has the greatest impact on SM Prime’s (SMPH PM) NAV since a disproportionately large part is derived from land (both actual and future) beside Manila Bay.

+++

Data from Google shows personal mobility is recovering a bit as new Covid-19 cases fall, particularly in Metro Manila.

POWER
DOE solicits green energy bids for 2,000MW
  • The Department of Energy (DoE) said it is inviting companies to supply 2,000MW of renewable energy (RE) in its first round of its Green Energy Auction Program (GEAP)
  • In the Notice of Auction, the Doe Said the power will be supplied to Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao at the following volumes: 1,400MW, 400MW and 200MW, respectively
  • For Luzon bidders, the total capacity for auction is 900MW of solar, 360MW of wind, 80MW hydro and 60MW biomass
  • For Visayas, the breakdown is 120MW biomass, 260MW solar and 20MW wind
  • For Mindanao, the targets are 100MW solar, 50MW biomass and 50MW hydro
  • The GEAP aims to boost RE as a source of energy, with RE generators participating in an auction for their output
  • The GEAP features the Green Energy Tariff, which influences the commercial value of power generated from qualified RE facilities, and sets a benchmark price for DUs under an opt-in mechanism
  • DU can procure power from the GEAP pool of winning bidders to reduce Feed-in Tariff-All charges to the end-users
