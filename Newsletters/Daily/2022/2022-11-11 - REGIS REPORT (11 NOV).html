REGIS REPORT

11 November 2022

The government statistics office says the country’s GDP grew 7.6% in 3Q. That figure is not only much higher than Street expectations, but is also an acceleration from the +7.5% (revised) growth in 2Q.

Private consumption appeared to have been the main engine for growth and the corporate results seem to conform with this. The consumer-related names have all been reporting strong underlying demand, notwithstanding the inflationary environment.

More below.

+++

We have results from Converge (CNVRG PM), Cebu Air (CEB PM) and Emperador (EMI PM), which are a little more downbeat than what’s been the usual case this season.

  • Converge (CNVRG PM) (=) – Revenue was up +30% to P24.5bn, and while EBITDA (as reported by the company) appeared to be better-than-expected at P14.4bn (+39%), we note that figure includes one-off items like FX gains.  Without those, EBITDA would have been within expectations, while net profit (reported at P6.1bn for 9M22, +17% YoY) would have been a tad short.  Notably, net subscriber additions came in at 28k for the quarter, a disappointing figure due to churn.  The company also revealed plans to introduce packages at lower monthly tariffs (below P1,500) which we believe is needed in order to reinvigorate subscriber adds.

  • Cebu Air (CEB PM) (-) – 3Q22 EBITDA contracted QoQ and net losses widened, as the lean season adversely affected system-wide pax volumes, which dropped 2% QoQ. Even though domestic pax volumes remained above pre-pandemic levels in 3Q, the company struggled to raise domestic fares to fully offset input cost pressures, given excess domestic supply, which is in turn a consequence of the slow recovery in the international market. With system-wide utilization rates still 10-15% below pre-pandemic levels, management now expects profits to recover in 1Q23, later than its initial guidance of 4Q22.

  • Emperador (EMI PM) (-) - 3Q22 profit fell 38%QoQ and 10%YoY to P1.962bn as revenues slowed (-7%QoQ). Margins appear to have been squeezed, with management citing higher inputs, logistics as well as marketing activities. We expect growth to continue tapering off, as the home consumption phenomenon that propped sales during the pandemic unwinds with the reopening.

MACRO
3Q22 GDP growth surprises, accelerating to 7.6%
  • 3Q22 GDP rose 7.6% YoY, slightly faster than the revised +7.5% (7.4% initially) in 2Q22
    • This beat Bloomberg consensus median estimate of +6.1%
    • To achieve the government’s FY22E GDP target of +6.5 to +7.5%, 4Q22 GDP should grow by +3.3% to +6.9%
  • Demand-side drivers were private consumption, investments, and moderating net imports
    • Due to further easing of mobility and the (partial) resumption of in-person classes
    • On capital formation, inventory build-up (i.e. change in stocks) remained evident, as it grew at a faster pace in 3Q22 vs. 2Q22
    • Net import growth slowed to 32% and partly lifted overall GDP
  • On the supply side, the service sector sustained momentum, +9.1% YoY
    • Industry sector moderated slightly to +5.8%, as public construction slowed to +13% YoY in 3Q22 from +23% YoY in 2Q22

CONSUMER
EMI 3Q profit falls 38%QoQ to P2.0bn; 9M22 down 1% - behind
  • In a limited press release, Emperador said that its net profit for 9M22 was P7.2bn, down 1%YoY
    • The 9M figure implies 3Q22 income fell 38%QoQ and 10%YoY to P2.0bn, citing higher input costs, logistics, A&P and increased on-trade activities
  • The 9M numbers is tracking behind Regis and consensus forecasts for FY22
  • Revenues for 9M grew 11% to P42.6bn
    • It said revenues were driven by whisky (+16%), while brandy grew 8%
    • Implies 3Q revenue slowed by 7%QoQ to P14.5bn
  • EMI trades at 28x PE and 21.8x EV/EBITDA for FY23E
