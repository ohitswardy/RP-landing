REGIS REPORT

10 May 2022

Based on unofficial results out of the Commission on Elections (Comelec), Mr Ferdinand R Marcos, Jr (aka “Bongbong”, or “BBM”) will be the 17th President of the Philippines. As of 8:17am, reports show Marcos received 30.3mn votes, roughly 56% of total votes cast, with more than 95% of the precincts tallied. This would make him the first President elected by an outright majority under the 1987 Constitution. Ms Sara Duterte, who got slightly more votes (30.7mn) will be VP.

* as of 8:17am, with 95% of votes tallied

Investors should now focus on announcements of Mr Marcos’s economic policies, and his appointments, particularly the Department of Finance which is the unofficial head of the Cabinet’s economic cluster. Throughout the campaign, Marcos declined to participate in debates, and gave very few interviews, making his economic priorities quite opaque. His public statements, included nostalgic references to his late father’s policies such as subsidized prices for oil, rice, etc. It is unclear whether or how much of the campaign rhetoric will make it to actual policy.

Mr Marcos will inherit myriad challenges, both domestic and global. While the economic recovery / reopening is well under way, sharply higher inflation could dampen consumer demand, particularly once election spending fades. Covid spending in 2020/21 saw the fiscal deficit balloon to >8% of GDP, and government debt up to 61% of GDP. Meanwhile, high global commodity prices are also weighing on the Current Account deficit (CAD), with March posting a trade deficit of $5bn, 81% wider YoY.

Our base case is still that cooler heads will prevail, and that economic policy will be broadly orthodox, and fiscally responsible. The questions is, who will be the proverbial grown-up in the room?

Back to your regular programming. Results! Results! Unsurprisingly, 1Q22 was a blowout quarter for Semirara (SCC PM). Meanwhile Globe (GLO PM) and Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM) posted decent numbers too.

  • Semirara (SCC PM) – ahead – Semirara reported record profits in 1Q22 of P15bn, up 552% YoY and very close to approximating its full-year 2021 profit. The outperformance seems to be largely due to a stellar performance from its coal mining unit, which benefited from elevated global thermal coal prices and strong sales volumes. The power unit also gained from a tight power market, with power ASPs up 50% YoY.
  • Globe (GLO PM) – ahead – Core income of P5.1bn for 1Q was, as noted Friday, just a tad higher YoY and mildly ahead of expectations. The main source of outperformance was Mynt (GCash) which continues to show remarkable growth. On its core operations, we note the market share advance of its mobile business in the quarter.

During the MREIT (MREIT PM) briefing, Megaworld (MEG PM) management said that its tenants have not indicated any desire to reduce office space in order to implement work-from-home. In fact, tenants even expanded their office footprint in 1Q22. This is consistent with what other developers have said recently – that net office take-up remains positive.

The Light Rail Manila Corp (LRMC) which operates the LRT-1 line has formally filed for arbitration to recoup some P2.67bn ($51mn) worth of lost revenues from fare increases that it was not allowed to implement in 2016, 2018, and 2020. LRMC is owned by Metro Pacific (36%), Ayala Corp (35%), Sumitomo (19%), Macquarie Infra (10%). Ayala Corp has disclosed it is looking to dispose of its stake. As we understand it, Metro Pacific has the right of first refusal on the shares.

Trade deficit expands further in March
  • Preliminary data showed the trade deficit widened further to US$5bn in March
    • Up from a revised US$4.2bn deficit in Feb 2022 and US$2.8bn deficit in March 2021
  • Due to steady growth in imports (+28% YoY) and a deceleration of export growth (+6% YoY from +16% YoY in Feb 2022)

POWER
Semirara’s 1Q22 profit surges to record P15bn
  • In a press release, Semirara reported its 1Q22 profit stood at P15bn, up 552% YoY and the highest quarterly figure on record
    • 1Q22 profit alone almost reached its full-year 2021 profit of P16bn
    • This is tracking way ahead of Regis (P23.8bn) and street full-year estimates (P18.7bn)
    • We reckon the outperformance largely came from the coal mining unit’s higher ASPs and volumes
    • Management cautioned against annualizing 1Q due to market volatility and unfavorable weather conditions typically in 2H
  • Cited record-high coal production and shipments, soaring global thermal coal prices, and higher spot electricity sales at elevated prices
  • On coal mining, the company sold 5.1mn MT (104% QoQ, 31% YoY) in 1Q21 at an average price of P5,125/MT (15% QoQ/180% YoY)
    • Both are tracking ahead of our estimates on full-year volumes (15.2mn MT) and ASP (P3,190/MT)
    • Note that Newcastle thermal coal prices rose 44% QoQ / 203% YoY in 1Q22 to US$265/MT
  • Its power unit sold 908 GWH (+14% YoY), bulk of which was to the spot market (57%)
    • Power ASPs improved by 17% QoQ / 50% YoY to P5.29/kwh, tracking below forecast
  • The company is holding a briefing today at 4:30pm: Register

INFRASTRUCTURE
LRMC files arbitration case against Republic of the Philippines
  • Light Rail Manila Corp. (LRMC) filed a Request for Arbitration with the International Chamber of Commerce in respect of its disputes with the Republic of the Philippines
  • The request pertains to the adjustment of the approved fare for the years 2016, 2018 and 2020 and LRMC’s claims for compensation for:
    • (1) the difference between the stipulated fare and the approved fare based on the schedule provided in the concession agreement
    • (2) the losses, costs and expenses incurred by LRMC for the government’s failure to deliver the required number of light rail vehicles that meet the stipulated technical requirements and the structural defects on the existing LRT 1 system
  • As of March 31, 2022, LRMC’s monetary claims for the abovementioned matters amounted to approximately P2.67bn, before interest, in addition to its application for the implementation of the long-overdue fare adjustments for 2016, 2018, and 2020
  • LRMC holds the P65bn 32-year concession agreement to operate and extend the LRT-1 railroad
  • LRMC is owned by Metro Pacific (36%), Ayala Corp (35%), Sumitomo (19%), Macquarie Infra (10%)
  • MPI is trading at a 48% discount to its market-based NAV of P7.34/sh
  • AC is trading at a 33% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,083/sh
