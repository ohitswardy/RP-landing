REGIS REPORT

11 July 2022

Finance Secretary Benjamin Diokno laid out the admin’s plan for fiscal consolidation. Among the measures being backed include the imposition of 12% value added tax (VAT) on online purchases of things like video and music services (eg Netflix, Spotify, etc), apps (Apple, Google), advertising, subscription services, etc. The measure does NOT include goods sold on online platforms such as Lazada or Shopee as the merchants are (presumably) already paying VAT and other taxes. This tax could add P13bn (roughly 0.6% of 2022E GDP) to government revenues.

Mr Diokno however added that the DOF will not recommend additional taxes on consumption. It will instead focus on improving tax collection, ie implementation of new tax laws passed by the previous administration. The government’s tax effort stood at 14.1% of GDP in 2021, down from the peak of 14.5% in 2019. The government aims to improve this ratio by 30-40bps annually. This will be critical in bringing the fiscal deficit from the 7.6% target this year, back down to the 3% range by the end of the current administration in 2028.

The economic team’s goal seems more to promote economic growth, ie expanding the denominator (GDP) to improve “xxx-do-GDP” ratios.

+ + +

Speaking of growth, the reopening/recovery theme seems to be continuing despite the headwinds. Metro Pacific (MPI PM) says that traffic at its toll road network continues to rise, despite the surge in fuel prices. The company claims that traffic in June was running at 5-6% above the average levels in 2019, though this slowed a bit in early July due to seasonal factors.

+ + +

The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) dismissed a motion to temporarily suspend the fixed price of the power supply contract between the San Miguel (SMC PM) group and Meralco (MER PM). The SMC group has commitments to sell 1,000MW of electricity to Meralco at P4.93/kwh. However, with global energy prices where they are, this contract is now hugely unprofitable. For reference, the WESM spot prices for electricity are currently in the P10/kwh range.

MACRO
Marcos admin open to digital tax, tax on plastics
  • The government supports imposing 12% VAT on digital services
    • Bill will be tackled by the Senate in the upcoming 19th Congress
    • Estimated to generate P13bn in incremental revenues per year
    • Digital services include online licensing or software, updates and add-ons, website filters and firewalls, mobile applications, video games and online games, and webcasts and webinars
    • It also includes the provision of digital content, such as music, files, images, text, and information; online advertising spaces; electronic marketplaces; search engine services; social networks; database and hosting; and online training
    • The bill exempts small businesses (i.e. sales below P3mn, net income below P250k) and books and other printed materials that are sold electronically or online
  • It also plans to impose a levy on single-use plastic bags, which would raise P1bn per year in additional tax revenues
  • However, the Marcos admin is cold to the previous admin’s proposal to to slap new or higher taxes slapped on consumption plus a three-year deferral of scheduled personal income tax cuts
    • It is also not keen on slashing public spending on non-priority projects
  • Instead, Finance Secretary Benjamin Diokno said they will focus on improving tax administration
    • Targets to raise tax effort by 0.3-0.4% annually
    • Tax revenues to GDP stood at 14.1% in 2021, down from the peak of 14.5% in 2019

 

INFRASTRUCTURE
MPI tollroad unit yet to feel impact of high fuel prices; exceeds pre-pandemic traffic in June
  • Metro Pacific Tollways Corp. (MPTC) has not felt the impact of fuel price increase
    • According to MPTC CFO Daniel Lizo
  • Across its network, they are above 2019 traffic volumes by 5-6% in June
    • However, it has seen traffic drop by 6% in the first five days of July compared with June due to seasonality
  • MPTC accounts for c. 20% of MPI’s profit and NAV
  • MPI is trading at a 51% discount to its market-based NAV of P7.48/sh

 

POWER
ERC urged to junk motion of 3 firms to get out of power deal
  • The United Filipino Consumers and Commuters (UFCC) called on the Energy Regulatory Commission to dismiss the join motion filed by three leading power corporations to relieve them from the power supply agreements that set a fixed cost of electricity
  • Meralco, South Premier Power Corp and San Miguel Electric Corp filed the motion in a hearing conducted by the ERC on June 9
  • South Premier (670MW) and San Miguel Energy Corp (330MW) are contracted to Meralco at fixed prices (P4.93/kwh) from Dec 2019 to Dec 2029
