REGIS REPORT

26 August 2022

The Bureau of Customs (BOC) continues to perform well, already exceeding its collection target for Aug by the 23rd.  This is the 8th consecutive month that it’s beaten its target. While the outperformance is being attributed to improved systems, rising imports (partly from elevated commodity prices) and a stronger USD are probably the bigger factor.

For better perspective, BOC collections are up 32% for YT-June, while imports are up +27% over the same period. Note the PHP has also weakened ~10% vs the USD over the period.

From a big picture perspective, the improved collection is a good sign for the fiscal position. But if it’s rising imports (and thus outflows) driving it, then it also means a worsening current account.

+++

Speaking of outflows, the Bangko Sentral (BSP) reported a net outflow of hot money in July amounting to US$103m, lower than the net outflow in June (US$342m). Bulk of the net outflow can be traced to the equity market, which saw US$80m of hot money exiting.

MACRO
Hot money exit continues in July
  • Data released by the BSP showed that total foreign investments registered through authorized agent banks logged a net outflow amounting to US$103.14mn in July
    • However, this is lower than the US$342mn outflow in June 2022 and US$340mn outflow in July 2021
  • Gross inflows amounted to US$681mn in July, lower than the US$1.0bn recorded in June 2022
    •   Majority of investments (or 64.8%) registered were in PSE-listed securities
  • Gross outflows amounted to US$784mn in July, lower than the US$1.4bn outflow in June 2022
  • YT-July hot money remains at a net inflow of US$625mn, a reversal from the US$446mn net outflow in the same period last year
MACRO
Customs exceeds collection target in latest data for August
  • The Bureau of Customs (BOC) collected P60.52bn from August 1 to Aug 23
    • This is already 2.8% above its original target of P58.85bn for the month
    • This is the eighth consecutive month that the BOC exceeded its monthly targets
  • Brings cumulative collections at P541.66bn for the January to Aug.23 period
    • Accounting for 74% of its full-year target
  • Customs has been attributing its success in exceeding its target goals to the improved valuation of the bureau, as well as its digitized and modernized systems
