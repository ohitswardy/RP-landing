REGIS REPORT

02 March 2022

The government registered a budget deficit of P338bn in Dec, taking the full year deficit to P1.67trn – a record level and equivalent to 8.6% of GDP. The budget deficit for FY22 projected by the government is little changed from last year (P1.65trn) but should dip slightly as a share of GDP.

It should take a few years for the government’s fiscal health to be restored to what it was before the pandemic, when national debt and the deficit were 40% and 3% of GDP, respectively.

+++

We have FY21 results from four names today, with three of the beating market expectations.

  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) (+) – Thanks to the surge in 4Q mall and commercial lot revenue, FY21 net profit jumped 40% to P12.2bn, ahead of REGIS forecast (in line with consensus). 4Q21 mall revenue doubled (YoY and QoQ) as tenant sales reached 78% of the pre-COVID level, from 44% in 3Q21 (50% in FY20). Meanwhile, FY21 reservation sales grew 13% to P92.2bn, led by high-end lots. Demand for horizontal projects has surged during the pandemic and they now comprise 47% of 2021 residential sales, from only 26% in 2019. The company plans to launch P100bn of residential projects this year, 33% higher than in 2021, suggesting that it expects a recovery in residential sales.

  • Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM) (+) – Non-essential formats drove much of the SSSG improvement in 4Q21 as mobility restrictions eased in Nov-Dec. This, coupled with the continued improvement in Rose Pharmacy’s margins, led to a +50% QoQ/YoY jump in operating margins during the quarter. SSSG momentum is likely to continue in 2022 as Metro Manila moves into Alert Level 1.

  • Semirara (SCC PM) (+) – 4Q21 core profit soared to a record high of P5.8bn, bringing FY21 profit to P15.8bn, beating Regis and consensus forecasts. 4Q21 profit was lifted by (a) higher coal ASPs, (b) lower coal mining costs, and (c) better plant availability factors.  Given the recent strength in thermal coal prices and power spot prices, we raise our 22E/23E profit forecasts by 40%/60% and TP by 20% to P25/sh. While we expect SCC to report strong earnings in 1H22 as commodity prices remain elevated, earnings will also likely drop thereafter once the global coal market normalizes.  We maintain our Hold rating.

  • Manila Water (MWC PM) (-) – FY21 net profit after the share of participating prefs massively underperformed, declining by 18% YoY to P3.2bn. Even though 4Q profit contracted QoQ/YoY on soft billed volumes and a surge in costs, we believe the ramp up in FY21 capex to P17bn (+40% YoY) is a more important positive factor in determining the regulatory value of its core East zone asset. MWC is about to enter another rate rebasing process by month-end, which management hopes to be completed by year-end.

Alliance Global (AGI PM) announced it was infusing P7.8bn into its gaming unit Travellers. Travellers was previously listed but AGI and its partner Genting HK took it private and each now owns 50%.  With Genting HK now in liquidation, we reckon AGI is trying to dilute the stake of Genting HK and take a controlling majority before liquidators hand over the 50% stake in Travellers to a third party.

Terra Solar, a joint venture between the Razon group and Solar Philippines, is facing competition for its unsolicited proposal to supply Meralco (MER PM) with 850MW of power. There are a couple things of note in the supply proposal: (1) the price of P6.08 (US$0.12) per kwh, which looks high and (2) when the power will be available – 600MW in 2026 and 250MW in 2027. These two items suggest Luzon will be in for a period of tight supply and high prices over the next few years.

MACRO
Budget deficit expands to P1.67tn in FY21, P338bn in December 2021
  • The National Government recorded a budget deficit of P338bn in December 2021
    • Due to a contraction in revenues (-3% YoY) and higher expenses (+5%)
  • Brings FY21 budget deficit to P1.67tn, equivalent to 8.61% of GDP
    • Broadly in line with the government’s target (P1.61tn, or 8.2% of GDP)
    • Tax effort of 14.13% in 2021 rose from last year’s 13.96%
    • Total expenditures was 24.1% of GDP, slightly higher than in 2020 (23.57%)
  • For 2022, the government is targeting a deficit-to-GDP ratio of P1.65tn (7.7% of GDP)

63mn Filipinos are fully vaccinated
  • The Department of Health said that “more than” 63mn Filipinos are fully vaccinated against COVID
    • This represents 58% of the total population
    • From 59mn as of January 2022
  • The government is unlikely to meet its goals of fully inoculating
    • 77mn Filipinos by end-1Q22
    • 90mn Filipinos by end-2Q22
CONGLOS
AGI to infuse P7.8bn into Travellers
  • AGI disclosed that it has agreed to infuse P7.8bn into Travellers International’s paid-up capital
  • The infusion of fresh capital “will afford AGI’s entertainment and hotel business the opportunity to keep current with its obligations and catch the window of business recovery”
  • AGI said that it has already informed Genting to infuse their share of the capital increase and awaiting their response or the liquidators’
  • We estimate AGI’s market-based NAV is P34.2/share, with the share price currently at 61% discount
  • Travellers accounts for c.7% of its NAV
POWER
Solar firms eye Meralco supply deal
  • SunAsia Energy Inc has formed a consortium of solar power developers to challenge Terra Solar’s unsolicited bid to supply 850MW of mid-merit renewable energy to Meralco
  • Terra Solar is the special purpose vehicle of Prime Infrastructure Capital Inc and Solar Philippines Power Project Holdings
    • It proposed a P6.0800/kWh headline rate and levelized cost of electricity
    • Offer includes 600MW to be made available by February 26, 2026 while additional 250MW is expected to be delivered starting February 26, 2027
    • Terra Solar said it will source power from its planned power plants with energy storage system in Batangas-Cavite, Bulacan, Nueva Ecija, Tarlac and Zambales
  • Apart from SunAsia, SMC Global Light and Power Corp of the San Miguel Group has also expressed interest to challenge the bid
