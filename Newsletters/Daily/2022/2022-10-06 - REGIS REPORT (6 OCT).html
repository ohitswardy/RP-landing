REGIS REPORT

06 October 2022

Inflation jumped to 6.9% in September, accelerating sharply from the 6.3% in August. The move may seem counter-intuitive, given the sharp drop in global commodity prices. Historically, though, there has been a 2-3 month lag between the two. Companies typically are unable or unwilling to pass through large price hikes in one go, for fear of a collapse in demand. Anecdotally, retail prices were still being adjusted upwards to make up for companies’ lost margins.

Further complicating matters is that the government had held public transport fares down by temporarily directly subsidizing bus and jeepney operators. With those subsidies ending, fares have been allowed to rise by as much another 9-20% starting October, which would take the YTD hikes to 15-30%.

The “good news” is that the CPI base was already rising as of 4Q2021, so the YoY base effects should start to fade.

Until then, though, the BSP may be pressured to raise policy rates, maybe even ahead of their next meeting scheduled for 17 November, especially if the US Fed raises again two weeks before that during their own meetings from 1-2 Nov.

+++

Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) Chair Monalisa Dimalanta said in a briefing that the commission has begun the process of resetting transmission rates which were last reviewed for the 2011-2015 period. The interim rate that is currently being charged by the grid operator National Grid Corp (controlled by Synergy Grid and Development, SGP PM) is still based on last approved rate (that was based on a WACC of 15.04%). Initial figures point to a lower rate applicable for the fourth regulatory period, according to Dimalanta, which could mean a 1) potential refund and 2) prospectively lower earnings for the fifth regulatory period (2023-2029) for SGP.

+++

Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM) and Ayala Land subsidiary ALLHC (ALLHC PM) announced that they would each enter the data center business with foreign partners. Several business groups have been entering this segment over the past few years due to the substantial growth potential. PLDT previously said that the market size (revenue) could grow from US$120mn in 2021 to US$412mn in 2027 as countries like the Philippines grab market share from Hong Kong and Singapore.

MACRO
Headline inflation jumps to 6.9% in September
  • Headline CPI for the month of September rose by 6.9% YoY, faster than in August 2022 (6.3% YoY)
    • Came within street expectations (6.9%) and the BSP’s target range of 6.6%-7.4%
    • Food was the biggest driver, after last year’s low base for prices of meat, fish, vegetables, sugar, and oils/fats
    • Electricity prices also grew at a faster rate of 16.7% from 15.7% in August
    • Transport decelerated slightly to 14.5% from 14.6% in August, as the YoY growth in regional prices of diesel/gasoline (in PHP terms) decelerated to 78%/31% from 100%/47% in August
  • On a MoM basis, headline CPI increased by 0.4%, broadly unchanged from August (+0.4%)
    • Due largely to higher prices of food (i.e. flour, vegetables, and sugar) and electricity prices (+1.7% MoM)
    • Offset by lower fuel prices; regional prices of diesel/gasoline (in PHP terms) dropped by 6%/9% MoM
    • We estimate headline CPI will rise >0.4% MoM in October, given that transport fares (i.e. jeepneys) were raised by another 9% beginning Oct 3
  • Core CPI (ex-food and fuel) rose by 4.5% YoY in September, slightly moderating from 4.6% in August
    • On a MoM basis, core CPI was flat for the first time since December 2021
  • Brings 9M22 average inflation rate to 5.1% (vs BSP’s target of +5.6% for 2022)
  • Next monetary board meeting is on November 17; policy rate currently at 4.25% (+225bps since end-2021)
    • Fed meeting on Nov 3; US Fed funds rate at 3%-3.25% (+300bps since end-2021)

PROPERTY
Ayala Land subsidiary to enter data center business
  • AyalaLand Logistics Holdings Corp (ALLHC PM) entered a JV partnership with FLOW Digital Infrastructure to develop and operate carrier-neutral data centers across the Philippines
  • The first data center facility will be in a hyperscale campus, which will eventually have a capacity of 36MW
  • The initial roll-out of 6MW will be ready by end-2024
  • ALLHC is 71% owned by Ayala Land
  • ALI is trading at 49% discount to NAV, 1.5X FY22E P/B, and 20.0X FY22E P/E

Aboitiz InfraCapital partners with EdgeConnex to develop data center platform
  • Aboitiz InfraCapital said on Wednesday that it partnered with EdgeConneX to develop a data center in NCR
  • It said the partnership is meant to address the rising data usage in the country
  • It described EdgeConnex as a global provider of full-range sustainable data center solutions
  • After paring down its stake in power, AEV has been redeploying capital to other industries (banking, infrastructure)
POWER
Lower power rates from NGCP review
  • Sen. Sherwin Gatchalian, vice chairman of Senate Committee on energy said a reset of power transmission rates by the ERC, which has been delayed for 10 years, could provide some respite to consumers, as it is likely to lead to lower transmission rates
  • While the current power transmission rates take into account a weighted average cost of capital for NGCP at 15.04%, a 2018 study by the NTC said that WACC for NGCP should be at 6.4%
  • Recently, the ERC promulgated Resolution No. 8 amending the rules for setting transmission wheeling rates, which triggers the process of resetting transmission rates of the NGCP
    • A new RTWR means that current WACC of the NGCP will be revised after more than 10 years
  • ERC Chair Monalisa Dimalanta, in a briefing last Monday, said that initial figures point to a lower transmission rate applicable for the fourth regulatory period
  • Commission is targeting to complete the review for the fourth regulatory period covering 2016-2022 within the year, while hearings and review for the fifth regulatory period will be conducted next year
