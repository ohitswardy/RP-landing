REGIS REPORT

25 April 2022

BDO (BDO PM) reported net profit of P11.7bn for 1Q22, up +13% YoY, trending slightly behind Street estimates.  Among the other highlights were that loan growth was +7.5% and NPLs inched down to 2.72% (2.81% in 4Q). Provisions for the quarter were at P3.7bn, which may have been up +28% YoY but is down 43% QoQ.  More detail below.

+++

The asset injections for the REITs continue with AREIT (AREIT PM) shareholders approving a share-for-asset swap with parent Ayala Land (ALI PM) and RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM) completing its own such transaction with Robinsons Land (RLC PM).

This latest deal involving AREIT will add +23% to its rental portfolio while RCR’s will add +13%.  AREIT’s leasable area has grown four-fold since its listing due to such asset injections while this is the first such for RCR since its own IPO last year.

We don’t think current price levels for both AREIT and RCR are attractive given that their dividend yields are below the 10-year sovereign’s (6.1%) and interest rates appear set to rise further.  Their parent companies (sponsors), Ayala Land and RLC clearly offer superior value.

+++

Among the last (but not the very last) to report FY21 results were Megawide (MWIDE PM) and Emperador (EMP PM).

For MWIDE, we estimate FY21 net loss after minorities and pref divs stood at P864mn, wider than Regis and street estimates with all segments falling short of estimates. The derived 4Q21 net loss was wider QoQ/YoY, after (1) the construction unit booked an impairment charge and cost overruns while (2) the landport unit’s revenues dropped. To fund rising receivables, management is taking on more debt (short-term and a P3bn bond).  Meanwhile, a hearing is set today on the dismissal of the anti-dummy case.  More with the note (link below):

For foreign investors >> Megawide – All segments disappoint in 4Q21

EMP, meanwhile, claimed that “normalized income” came in at P10.6bn (+34%) with headline income at P10bn (+25%) for the year.  The company didn’t provide much more detail but said its Fundador brandy product “did very well” in a number of countries.

+++

The deal for Cerberus to buy the former shipyard of Hanjin in Subic has been completed.  Many of the major banks had exposure to the bankrupt Hanjin and this sale would go to paying creditors.  Among the banks exposed are: RCBC (RCB PM), BDO (BDO PM), BPI (BPI PM) and Metrobank (MBT PM).

BANKS
BDO 1Q22 NPAT up 13% to P11.7bn, ahead on revenue recovery, cost controls
  • BDO Unibank (BDO PM) reported NPAT of P11.7bn, up 13%, ahead of Regis (P40.3bn) but slightly behind consensus (P48bn). The outperformance (vs our forecasts) were largely due to opex, which came in lower than we expected. Overall revenues were up 7% YoY, while opex was up just 3% YoY, taking PPOP up 15% YoY. Loan growth accelerated slightly to 7.5% YoY from 6.5% in 4Q, though NIMs were flat/down slightly QoQ. Asset quality improved, with NPLs down a tad to 2.72%; BDO used strong operating results to rebuild its NPL cover to 121% (from 111% in 4Q).
  • Some highlights:
    • Loan growth was 7.5% YoY, driven by the mid-market segment. Deposits were also up 7.1% YoY, but CASA grew faster at 11%, taking CASA ratio to a new high of 86%.
    • NIMs, though were down slightly (-5bps QoQ) to 3.91%, driven by lower loan yields (down 8bps QoQ), while deposit costs were broadly flat, -2bps QoQ from already extremely low levels (was 0.21% in 1Q22).
    • Fees grew 6% YoY, sustaining levels that are well above pre-Covid levels. The growth was driven by service charges, which surged 18% YoY. Trading/fx gains were up 55% YoY from a low base.
    • Opex was kept surprisingly benign, up just 2.5% YoY. Compensation was up just 4% YoY, with insurance policy reserves and insurance benefits down 6%.
    • Provisions were up 28% to 3.7bn, despite an improvement in NPL ratio to 2.72% from 2.81% in 4Q21. The bank seems to be using strong operations to rebuild its NPL coverage, which improved to 121% from 111% in the previous quarter. We also estimate that Restructured Loans (RL) improved to 5.2% of loans, down from 5.7% at end-2021.
  • Overall a solid set of results. The bank will not be holding a briefing for 1Q22.

PROPERTY
AREIT shareholders approve share-for-asset swap with Ayala Land
  • AREIT will acquire 124,000sqm of Cebu offices from Ayala Land
    • This will expand AREIT’s rental portfolio by 23% to 673,000sqm
    • The offices have a combined occupancy rate of 97%
    • eBloc Towers 1 to 4 at Cebu IT Park, ACC Tower and Tech Tower at Ayala Center Cebu
  • The offices are valued at P11.257bn
    • AREIT’s AUM will grow 21% to P64bn
  • AREIT will issue 252.1mn AREIT shares to Ayala Land
    • The AREIT shares would effectively be priced at P44.7/share
    • AREIT’s shares outstanding will expand 17%
  • We estimate that this would reduce public float from 33.64% to 28.8%
    • Thus, we estimate that AREIT would have to conduct a share offering of at least 74mn shares - c. P3.2bn at the last price of P43.85/share
  • AREIT currently offers a dividend yield of 4.6%
    • Based on consensus forecast
    • The interest rate on the 10-year government bond is 6.1%
  • ALI is trading at 38% discount to NAV, 2.1X FY22E P/B, 27.4X FY22E P/E
