11 March 2022

Vehicle sales for Feb under CAMPI/TMA amounted to 23,304 – which is 17% higher MoM, an encouraging sign even it is still lower YoY.

Also of note was that Toyota sold 12,234 units for the month putting its relative market share for that period at over 50% -- the highest it’s been for a number of years.  Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) is majority owned by GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for a quarter of its earnings.

+++

We refresh our numbers for Globe (GLO PM) in a new report and reduce our rating to Hold.

This year is likely to be a recovery year for mobile revenue as personal mobility begins to bounce back after two years of Filipinos being practically confined in their homes. While the impact of Dito (DITO PM) on the telecom market this year will likely rise, its long-term threat appears to be diminishing.  Growth in earnings, however, is likely to be restrained by record capex spend and the resulting rise in depreciation and financing cost. Our new target price now also reflects a valuation of US$2.5bn for Mynt (GCash), where Globe has a minority interest.  This is already ~25% above the valuation after latest funding round and is much lower than valuations implied by Globe’s share price over much of 2H last year.

+++

Ayala Land (ALI PM) is injecting another 124,000 sqm of office space into AREIT (AREIT PM) and the injection is expected to be followed by a sale of shares in AREIT.  The tactic of asset injections followed by share sales takes advantage of the tax and valuation arbitrage opportunity provided by REITs.

One company, Citicore Energy REIT (CREIT PM), has taken it further by combining it with solar farms at above-market lease rates for the underlying land. We should expect other sectors to follow suit as long as rules are not revised.  Telecom tower REITs, anyone?

+++

Management of San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) gave more color on its results in the call yesterday. Although full-year profit was up 58% YoY, the food segment dragged what would have been an impressive recovery for the beer. Beer volumes grew 18%QoQ to 51.1mn cases, a level that was just c.20% lower than pre-pandemic volumes (vs. >30% for most of last year). As in the past, swings in the prices of commodities dragged the food segment, just barely avoiding losses in the quarter. Movement in the prices of wheat, soybean meal and chicken are likely to put pressure on margins in the coming months. More below.

CONSUMER
Vehicle sales rise 17% MoM but drops 7% YoY; Toyota’s market share inches up
  • Vehicle sales in February rose by 17% to 24,304 units, according to data from CAMPI/TMA
    • MoM improved attributed to better consumer confidence as COVID cases drop and mobility restrictions eased
    • Six of seven vehicle categories showed double-digit growth, with trucks/buses alone up 31% MoM
    • February industry sales were 21% below 2019
    • YT-Feb vehicle sales reached 45,069 units, down 9.2% YoY, 27% below 2019
  • Toyota sold 12,243 units (+24% MoM, -6% YoY), 9% below 2019
    • Toyota further solidified market position, with market share improving to 50.4%, vs 48% in end-2021 and 44% in end-2019
    • YT-Feb sales for Toyota reached 22,121 units (-7% YoY), 18% below 2019
  • Toyota is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for c. 20% of GTCAP’s profit/NAV
  • GTCAP is trading at an 18% discount to its market-based NAV (P653/sh), wider than historical average (4%)

CONSUMER
FB 4Q down -10%QoQ, -19%YoY dragged by food segment collapse
  • 4Q21 profit down 10%QoQ as food contribution collapsed. FB’s net income to equity holders was P19.8bn for FY21 (+58%), ahead. However, the full-year result implies 4Q21 profit fell 10%QoQ and 19%YoY to P3.8bn, dragged by a food segment that barely stayed above water. Food dropped sharply in the quarter, but has been trending lower since 2Q on rising commodity prices and increasing chicken inventory levels. Beer nevertheless, jumped, as margins widened on the first price increase in 18 months and volumes jumped on reopening.

  • Beer volumes up 18%QoQ in 4Q, price increase helps margins. Profits of SMB jumped 41%QoQ to P6.4bn. Domestic beer volumes grew 18%QoQ to 51.1mn cases as mobility rebounded towards the latter part of last year, but is still >20% lower than typical pre-pandemic volumes. For the year, domestic beer volumes were flat at 182.0mn cases, c.34% off from the 2019 level. A price increase last October (+5-6%) was the first in 18 months, and helped  beer margins climb from 22 to 25.5%.
  • Ginebra volumes +14%QoQ; margins down a bit. Gin volumes continued to climb, posting another 14%QoQ growth in 4Q to 11.6mn cases. That brought the FY21 volume to a record 41.9mn cases (+8%). Profit contribution was slightly down QoQ, however, as margins corrected (EBIT 14.0% - 10.3%). 3Q was unusually high because of better bottle retrieval rate (90% vs 70% normally).
  • Commodities crimp food contribution. Food was barely above water in 4Q. It posted a profit of just P37mn, down 97%QoQ. Management cited rising prices of commodities like wheat and soybean meal, key raw materials for its feeds and flour businesses. We suspect that poultry pricing was also impacted by higher overall inventory levels in the industry due to frozen imports. Food operating margin collapsed to just 2.3% from (9.7% in 9M21).
  • FB trades at 17.5x FY22E
AIRLINES
PAL to boost flights by 52% this month
  • PAL will add more than 1500 international and domestic flights this month, representing a 52% increase
    • It will step up flights serving North America (+24%), Southeast Asia (+98%), Japan (+11%), the Middle East (+16%) and Australia (+146%)
    • It will also bolster domestic service (+56%), including connections between Manila and Cebu, Davao and Boracay
  • In 4Q21, CEB/PAL/AAP domestic market shares stood at 56% / 30% / 13% (vs. pre-pandemic: 51%/ 29% / 18%)
  • We believe CEB’s cost advantage over PAL (i.e. high cabin density, lower lease/borrowing rates, high utilization, high labor efficiency, low overhead) will unlikely be eroded anytime soon
  • While we reckon this recent announcement could raise competition risks in domestic flights originating outside NAIA, the impact of which will unlikely be material in our view
  • CEB is trading at 5.3x FY22E EV/EBITDA, below peers: AirAsia (20x), Vietjet (29x), Spring Airlines (19x)
TELCOS
GCash introduces ‘Buy Now, Pay Later’ feature
  • Mobile wallet GCash unveiled their newest app feature ‘GGives’, which allows qualified users to pay for items up to P30k under 24 installments or ‘gives’ twice monthly within 12 months
  • GGives will be available to users with higher GScore on the GCash app
  • ‘GLoan’ is another GCash financial service that allows eligible users to borrow funds for up to P25k
    • Funds get in the form of actual cash in their GCash wallet whereas approved users have repayment option from 9-12 months
  • Eligible users can avail in GGives in over 27k stores via Scan QR
PROPERTY
Ayala Land to sell 124,000sqm of Cebu offices to AREIT
  • AREIT will acquire 124,000sqm of Cebu offices from Ayala Land
    • This will expand AREIT’s rental portfolio by 23% to 673,000sqm
    • The offices have a combined occupancy rate of 97%
    • eBloc Towers 1 to 4 at Cebu IT Park, ACC Tower and Tech Tower at Ayala Center Cebu
  • The offices are valued at P11.257bn
    • AREIT’s AUM will grow 21% to P64bn
  • AREIT will issue 252.1mn AREIT shares to Ayala Land
    • The AREIT shares would effectively be priced at P44.7/share
    • AREIT’s shares outstanding will expand 17%
  • We estimate that this would reduce public float from 33.64% to 28.8%
    • Thus, we estimate that AREIT would have to conduct a share offering of at least 74mn shares - c. P3.7bn at the last price of P49.40/share
  • AREIT currently offers a dividend yield of 4.1%
    • Based on consensus forecast
    • The interest rate on the 10-year government bond is 5.4%
  • ALI is trading at 34% discount to NAV, 2.2X FY22E P/B, 28.8X FY22E P/E
