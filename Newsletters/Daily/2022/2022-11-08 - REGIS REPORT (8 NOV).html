REGIS REPORT

08 November 2022

We have 3Q results from ICTSI (ICT PM) and SM Prime (SMPH PM) this morning, both of whom delivered better-than-expected numbers. The former delivered good numbers despite widespread concerns about the outlook for trade globally. The latter, on the other hand, showed mall rental revenue that finally exceeded pre-pandemic levels both on aggregate as well as on a like-for-like basis.

  • ICTSI (ICT PM) (+) – 3Q22 profit to common improved 14% QoQ and 55% YoY to US$163mn, lifted by (a) a sustained rebound in organic volumes (+2% QoQ, +6% YoY) and (b) surprisingly strong organic yield per TEU (+2% QoQ, +9% YoY). While all-in costs also went up, these increases were lower than we had anticipated.  9M22 profit of US$439mn (+64% YoY) is on track to beat our recently upgraded, Street-high estimates and consensus forecasts. Despite management’s muted 2023 sales volume growth guidance of “low-single digits”, the company reiterated its view that yields will likely hold up even next year.

Although 3Q is seasonally weaker than 2Q, 3Q22 Philippine mall revenue rose 7% QoQ (+163% YoY) and is already 95% of the 3Q19 figure. The country’s gross international reserves (GIR) rose by US$1.1bn to US$94.1bn in Oct. The bond issue by the government in Oct boosted GIR, despite what has appeared to be very active intervention by the Bangko Sentral (BSP) to keep the PHP from depreciating past PHP59/USD.

Meanwhile, ACEN Corp (ACEN PM), the renewable energy platform of Ayala Corp (AC PM), completed the Energy Transition Mechanism (ETM) relating to the 246MW SLTEC coal plant. The ETM will allow SLTEC to be retired early and for ACEN to divest from it in favor of financial investors. Despite the divestment, ACEN will still hold bulk of the economics related to SLTEC, and will be responsible for selling its output and retain exposure to fuel cost.

Dollar reserves rise by 1.2% MoM to US$94bn in October
  • Preliminary data from the BSP showed gross international reserves (GIR) reached US$94.1bn in October
    • A US$1.07bn (+1.2%) increase MoM
    • Cited proceeds from the issuance of ROP Global Bonds, and upward valuation adjustments in foreign currency-denominated reserves (or non-gold reserves)
  • Represents 7.5 months’ worth of imports and 6.7x the country’s short-term external debt
  • The BSP is targeting an end-2022 GIR level of US$99bn

POWER
ACEN completes world’s first Energy Transition Mechanism for 246MW SLTEC coal plant; announces investment decision for 520MWdc Stubbo Solar project in Australia
  • ACEN disclosed today the full divestment of the 246MW SLTEC coal plant using the energy transition mechanism, a concept developed by ADB which aims to leverage low-cost funding geared towards early coal retirement and reinvestment of proceeds to enable renewable energy projects
  • The ETM for the SLTEC plant involved P13.7bn in debt financing provided by BPI and RCBC as well as P3.7bn in equity investments from GSIS, Insular Life and ETM Philippines for a total deal value of P17.4bn
    • GSIS invested P2.2bn in redeemable preferred shares issued by SLTEC
    • InLIfe anchored the equity placements with a P1bn investment
  • ACEN received P7.2bn from the transaction for reinvestment in the company’s renewable energy projects
  • Balance was used to refinance debt
  • ACEN also disclosed that ACEN Australia has executed key contracts and announced that a final investment decision on the 520MWdc Stubbo Solar project has been made, following the signing of connection agreement with Lumea and the execution of a PV module supply contract and the commitment of UAD800mn by parent company ACEN Corp
  • ACEN Australia has more than 1.5GW of projects under construction
