REGIS REPORT

02 August 2022

We have 2Q results from two companies of the SM group today, BDO (BDO PM) and SM Prime (SMPH PM).

  • BDO Unibank (BDO PM) (=) - BDO 1H22 NPAT came in at P23.9bn, up 12% YoY and bang in line with our (and consensus) FY22E estimates. Loan growth accelerated to 9% (vs 7% in 1Q), and NIMs widened 8bps QoQ, even without the benefit of the 75bps off-cycle rate hike in July. Asset quality improved dramatically, with NPL ratio down to 2.39%, down 33bps QoQ, and Restructured Loan ratio down to 4.9% (from 5.2% in 1Q). Despite this, BDO kept provisions high, bringing NPL cover to 138%. During the briefing, management took pains to ease investor concerns over its P36bn loan exposure to the Udenna group, saying that collateral values, plus existing provisions, were equivalent to 130-160% of the loan value. The bank expects the strong trends to carry into 2H22.

  • SM Prime (SMPH PM) (+) – 2Q22 net profit jumped 29% YoY to P6.7bn, slightly ahead of our expectations. Two things stood out: 1) on the positive side, despite some tenants still enjoying rental concessions, Philippine mall revenue jumped 51% QoQ (+156% YoY) to P12.4bn, already 85% of 2Q19 revenue. Malls should improve even further given SM Prime’s plan to remove all concessions by end-3Q22. 2) On the other hand, residential revenue fell 50% QoQ and YoY due to sales cancellations.

Meanwhile, Metrobank (MBT PM) also announced its own 1H/2Q numbers this morning. The bank reported 1H22 NPAT of P15.6bn in 1H22, up 33% YoY, and well ahead of our P27.2bn FY22E forecast (Bloomberg consensus P28.4bn). The outperformance was driven largely by trading and FX gains on the revenue side, and provision on the expense side. Loans jumped 9% YoY (from 5.2% in 1Q22), driven by corporates (+12%) and credit cards (+16%). Asset quality improved further, with NPL ration dropping to 1.9%, down from 2.16% in 1Q22, while NPL cover surged to 196% (was 179% in 1Q22). Both ratios are by far the best among the Big Three banks.

MBT will hold a briefing tomorrow, 3 Aug at 4pm.

The sale of another batch of towers has been completed by PLDT (TEL PM). Another 1,013 towers were transferred to the two buyers, with a corresponding P13.2bn changing hands. To date, PLDT has received P52bn from ISOC edotco and Comworks Infratech, a subsidiary of Edgepoint from the transfer of 4,025 towers. This is two-thirds of the P77bn it expecting to receive from the sale.

+++

Prime Infra, controlled by port and casino tycoon Enrique Razon, signed a sale and purchase agreement to buy 45% of the Malampaya field operator from the Udenna group. The deal will bulk up Prime Infra ahead of its anticipated listing. For Dennis Uy’s Udenna, it will raise funds for the highly leveraged group and allow it to meet its obligations to creditors worried about the possibility of default.

+++

San Miguel (SMC PM) revealed a few details about its pending application with the regulator to revise its power supply contracts to recoup huge losses from the sky-high fuel prices. Its subsidiary SMC Global Power signed contracts to sell power at fixed prices to Meralco (MER PM) but is now seeking to raise tariffs (close to 20% for Ilijan and almost double for Sual) for the period up to May 2022, citing the prices of coal and gas. Approval by the regulator could set an important precedent that would allow future power agreements to revise tariffs if fuel prices move against the gencos.
