REGIS REPORT

08 August 2022

Today, we have 2Q prints from Ayala Land (ALI PM) and Bloomberry (BLOOM PM). The former’s closely watched not only because of its size but also because it’s results are a bellwether of the direction of the residential property market.

  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) (-) – While 1H22 earnings of P8.1bn (+34%) only appears slightly behind REGIS and consensus forecasts, we believe investors should focus on the drop in residential revenue brought about by sales cancellations. 2Q22 residential revenue fell 12% YoY due to cancellations from the middle-income and affordable brands. While cancellations were lower in 2Q22 than in 1Q22, management hinted that they would remain relatively elevated for the remainder of the year. On a more positive note, the rental segments performed well, led by the mall business. 2Q22 mall revenue jumped 168% YoY (34% QoQ) to P3.9bn, already 75% of the 2019 figure.

  • Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) (+) - 2Q22 profit jumped to P1.805bn (163%QoQ) on the continued recovery of gaming. Total GGR jumped nearly 50% from 1Q22, helped by robust increases from slots (89%QoQ) and VIP (67%QoQ). The GGR level of P13.1bn is close to 90% of the 2019 quarterly average.

+++

The CPI inflation figure for July hit 6.4%. This is an acceleration for 6.1% the previous month, with most categories seeing an uptick. Among the notable exceptions was Utilities, as the government put through “refunds” to reduce electricity bills.

With the latest CPI print where it is, and headline 2Q GDP figures (out tomorrow) likely to look strong, the BSP is almost certainly going to raise rates again when the Monetary Board meets on Aug 18. The question now is by how much but +50bp appears to consensus.

+++

Two other telling pieces of macro data were released over the weekend: (1) the latest gross international reserves (GIR) level and (2) national government debt. The two numbers are reflective of the deteriorating direction of the country’s twin deficits (current account and budget deficits).

The GIR dropped US$2bn to below US$100bn in July. While the BSP said nothing on it, we can probably guess the drop was due to the BSP defending the PHP over that time.

Meanwhile, national government debt rose 2.4% MoM to P12.79bn as of June. At the current rate, debt-to-GDP is likely to top 65% (if not higher) by year-end. That figure also means total government debt doubled in the past six years under the Duterte government.

MACRO
Inflation accelerates further to 6.4% in July
  • Headline inflation continued its uptrend, rising to 6.4% in July, from 6.1% in June
    • Fastest recorded inflation rate since October 2018
    • Came at the upper-end of the BSP’s forecast range of 5.6-6.4% and above consensus (6.1%)
  • Almost all categories saw an uptick in inflation, except for some moderation in utilities, operation of transport equipment, and health
    • Food inflation driven by fish, meat, sugar; offset by lower inflation of vegetables
    • Prices of transport services jumped by 7%, faster than June’s 3%, due to nationwide increase in jeepney fares
  • YT-July inflation now at 4.7%, above the government’s average inflation target of 2-4%
  • Monetary Board meeting on Aug 18

MACRO
End-July GIR level drops 2% MoM to US$98.8bn
  • The country’s GIR level, based on preliminary data, dropped by US$2.0bn (-2%) MoM to US$98.8bn in July
    • Down from US$108.8bn in end- 2021 and US$110.1bn in end-2020
    • However, this is still above end-2019 GIR of US$87.8bn (~7.7 import cover)
  • July level represents 8.3 months’ worth of imports

MACRO
NG debt rises to P12.79tn in end-June
  • The national government’s outstanding debt reached P12.79tn in end-June
    • This is P296bn (2.4%) higher MoM and P1.063tn (9.1%) higher than in end-December 2021
    • Of the total debt stock, 31.5% was sourced externally while 68.5% were domestic borrowings
  • The government is targeting to lower the debt-to-GDP ratio to below 60% only by 2025

GAMING
BLOOM 2Q profit jumps 163%QoQ to P1.8bn - ahead
  • Bloomberry posted a net profit of P1.805bn in 2Q22, more than double the income of P687mn in 1Q22
    • The 2Q profit compares to a net loss of P1.158bn in the same period last year
  • The result brings 1H22 profit to P2.492bn – half our FY22E profit estimate
    • We think it is on track to exceed this figure given the expected ramp-up in succeeding quarters
    • Consensus FY22E profit is P2.7bn
  • 2Q22 EBITDA was P3.865bn (35%QoQ, 277%YoY)
    • 1H EBITDA reached P6.7bn (174%YoY) – 49% of Regis, 54% of consensus
  • 2Q GGR jumped to P13.115bn (47%QoQ, 131%YoY)
    • 1H GGR was P22.0bn (76%YoY) – 60% of Regis FY22E
    • The absolute GGR is nearing the 2019 average of P15bn/quarter
    • Slot revenues recovered from a soft 1Q, with slot GGR jumping 89%QoQ to P4.4bn
      • This was despite slots win rate being lower at 44% (from 55% in 1Q22 and 60% in 2Q21)
    • VIP revenues also jumped 67%QoQ to P4.4bn
      • Despite the jump, VIP accounted for 34% of GGR – vs 50% pre-pandemic
  • Cash stood at P36.8bn
