REGIS REPORT

14 September 2022

Vehicle sales for the month of Aug, as reported by CAMPI/TMA, amounted to 30,185 units. This is up 90% from the low base in the prior year but, more tellingly, also up 9% MoM. The Aug figure now is virtually equal to the monthly pre-pandemic average. YT-Aug, volumes are now up +25% to 212k units.

Volumes for Toyota are not yet available but we expect the brand to have close to ~50% share. This would put it on track to more than meet its full-year target to sell 162k vehicles (+25%). GT Capital (GTCAP PM) owns 51% of Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) and also a few dealerships.

+++

President Marcos moved to stop sugar exports by declaring that production be earmarked only for the local market, at least for the current crop year. At the same time, Marcos also authorized the importation of 150,000MT of sugar to augment supply. That amount is just around enough to match the projected shortfall for the crop year (which began Sept).

The twin actions will probably keep domestic demand-supply in balance, which is likely to tame prices a bit. Sugar prices are up 65-80% YoY and are contributing to the rise in CPI inflation prints.

For decades, the country’s sugar industry was in a weird situation where it was an exporter of sugar to the U.S. under historical quota rules, despite domestic prices being exceptionally high compared to regional prices (roughly 2x). With domestic sugar prices being where they are now (almost 3x global prices), there should be little damage to the local sugar industry interests (of which Universal Robina (URC PM) is one player) if they sold all production domestically.

+++

The government raised P45bn from the sale of 10-year Treasury bonds at a yield of 6.7%, a slight increase from the secondary market yields. Bond yields are up sharply this year after the global surge in inflation. Short-term interest rates are also poised to rise further with the US Fed expected to raise policy rates by 75bp at next week’s meeting and the BSPs Monetary Board likely to follow suit.

+++

Metro Pacific (MPI PM) is reportedly contemplating a US$500m IPO for its toll road unit by next year if not 2024. Based on our estimates, the toll road business accounts for around 1/4th of MPI’s gross assets.

Gov’t raises P45bn from 10-year treasury bonds
  • The government raised a total of P45bn from an original issue of 10-year T-bonds
    • T-bonds carry a coupon of 6.75% but fetched a yield of 6.703% (9.4bps higher than the previous auction; BVAL: 6.6%)
  • The Bureau of the Treasury fully awarded P35bn through a regular auction and, subsequently, offered an additional P10bn through a tap facility
    • Investors tendered almost 3x the volume offered
  • The street expects the Fed to raise policy rates by another 75bps on its Sep 21 meeting; BSP’s Monetary Board also expected to raise 50bps on Sep 22
INFRASTRUCTURE
Concerns remain over draft rules for BOT Law
  • The government plans to remove some provisions under the draft IRR of the Build-Operate-Transfer Law
    • According to Public-Private Partnership Center Deputy Executive Director Jeffrey Manalo
  • Provision on “acts and decisions of regulators shall not be subject to arbitration” will likely be removed, as this absolves the government of liability
  • There were also concerns on “availability payments” and “viability gap funding”, as these can be construed as direct government subsidies
  • The Marcos administration is looking to attract more investments in infrastructure through PPPs amid the fiscal challenges it is facing

CONSUMER
Philippines to import 150,000 MT of sugar, entire sugar output now for domestic use 
  • The Philippines will import 150,000 MT of refined sugar in order to manage prices
  • Half of the imports will be for industrial users while the other half will be for consumers
  • Separately, the Sugar Regulatory Administration (SRA) issued Sugar Order No. 1, requiring 100% of sugar production in the coming crop year to be classified as “B” or domestic sugar supply
  • The crop year is from 1 September 2022 to 31 August 2023
  • The SRA said that total sugar production in the crop year is estimated at 1.88mn MT, while demand is expected to reach 2.03mn MT
  • As of September 2:
    • The average retail price of refined sugar in wet markets was P97.43/kg, (+85%) vs P52.71/kg a year ago
    • Raw sugar in wet markets is now priced at P72.43/kg (+60% YoY)
CONSUMER
Vehicle sales rebound strongly in August
  • Vehicle sales jumped 90% YoY, 9% MoM to 30,185 units in August
    • According to data from CAMPI/TMA, as reported by Bloomberg
    • Data for the month of August alone reverted to 98% of 2019 average monthly sales
    • Brings YT-August industry sales to 212,872 units, up 25% YoY
    • To reach CAMPI’s year-end target of 336k units (+25%), the industry would need to sell 30.5k units per month for the rest of the year
  • Assuming Toyota retained its market share of c. 50% in August, this would imply that Toyota’s sales for the month would have been c. 15k units (+90% YoY, 8% MoM)
    • At this level, Toyota will only need to sell 13k units per month for the rest of the year to reach its 162k target (+25% YoY) by year-end and only 12k units per month to reach Regis’ FY22E of 158k (+22%)
  • Toyota accounts for c. 20% of GTCAP’s NAV/profit
  • GTCAP is trading at a 46% discount to its market NAV of P928/sh
