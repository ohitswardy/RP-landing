REGIS REPORT

09 August 2022

ACEN (ACEN PM), formerly AC Energy, released 2Q profit figures early today. Profit rebounded to P1.778bn (339%QoQ, 25%YoY) in the quarter, following an improvement in plant availability that allowed for some excess capacity (and net long WESM exposure). The company also cited contributions from new plants that came online, particularly wind capacity in Vietnam and solar capacity in India. 1H earnings, however, remain below trend to meet FY22E profit expectations, both Regis’ and the Street’s.

The company also announced a lofty 20GW (20,000MW) target for renewable energy capacity by 2030, roughly 6x the level today. Mathematically, that implies CAGR of 25% over an eight-year period --- an ambitious pace that will challenge ACEN management’s ability to execute its plans.

++

Mobile operator Dito Telecommunity, subsidiary of Dito CME (DITO PM), filed anti-competition complaints PLDT (TEL PM) and Globe (GLO PM). Dito Tel is accusing the latter two of making it difficult for its subscribers to make voice calls to their networks. Without commenting on the merits of the complaints, we note that voice is a declining part of mobile revenue (<15%) as calls are increasingly done through the mobile data network. Any remedies on voice interconnect (if the Competition Commission so orders) will likely have minimal longer-term impact.

+++

With the appointment of Aboitiz Power (AP PM) legal counsel and compliance officer, Atty Monalisa Dimalanta, as head of the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) and talk that another Aboitiz Power executive will become president of the power sector agency PSALM, the perception of a pro-Aboitiz regulatory environment cannot be avoided. This could be tricky to manage in the years ahead as the administration and the regulators will have to navigate public perception on what is arguably the most politically charged among utility services.

+++

We raise our earnings estimates and target price (TP) for Wilcon (WLCON PM) following robust operating results in 2Q.

WLCON has had continuous margin expansion over the past six quarters, benefitting largely from cost and scale advantages even amidst tightness in the global supply chain. This scale advantage, in our view, has conferred on it a high degree of pricing and buying power relative to smaller players, who have struggled to keep up with the surge in demand. While there are talks of larger players potentially entering the market, we believe the barriers to entry have been raised further by WLCON.

We upgrade our 2022-24E EPS forecast by 5-16% following the consensus-beating 1H22 results. We are now expecting +19% EPS CAGR over the forecast period to be driven by (1) the continued recovery of private home construction, which will in turn drive SSSG, (2) store expansion as the company meets its 100-store target by 2025, and (3) operating leverage as the business further scales up. Our TP values WLCON at 31x 2023E, which is a premium to peers. However, we believe the premium is justified given faster EPS growth and higher ROE relative to peers.

MACRO
Employment improves in June
  • The Philippine Statistics Authority reported that the number of employed improved MoM/YoY by 508k/1.5mn
    • MoM improvement due to agriculture sector, which created 1.3mn jobs in June vs. May
    • Brings employment rate to 94%, higher than in June 2021 but still below pre-Covid levels (94.7%)
  • The number of unemployed rose slightly MoM but was significantly lower than in June 2021
    • June unemployment rate at 6.0%, down from last year’s 7.7% but still above pre-Covid levels of ~5%

POWER
ACEN 2Q profit recovers to P1.778bn
  • ACEN reported that its 2Q22 income amounted to P1.778bn, more than 4x the level in 1Q and up 25%YoY
  • The press release attributed to recovery to fresh contributions from new Philippine and International plants, as well as easing of curtailment issues in Visayas
  • Improvements in plant availability also allowed ACEN some excess capacity, enabling the company to benefit from strong spot prices
  • This brings 1H22 net income to P2.183bn, down 19%YoY
  • Attributable output grew 11% to 2,482GWh with increased operating capacity from Vietnam wind farms and India solar plants
  • ACEN trades at PE 44x for FY22E and attributable EV/EBITDA of 22.2x

POWER
ACEN targets 20GW renewable energy capacity by 2030
  • ACEN Corp announced on Monday that it has adopted a new corporate vision and strategy that targets 20GW of attributable renewables capacity by 2030
  • The company’s aspiration to boost its growth plan by six times from its current renewable energy capacity of 3.4GW is achievable by 2030
  • Solar and wind will remain its core energy technologies, complemented by new technologies such as battery energy storage, floating solar and offshore wind
  • The Philippines will remain as its core market which currently accounts for 40% of total capacity but plans to aggressively expand investments in Australia
POWER
Aboitiz Power counsel appointed ERC chair
  • President Marcos Jr has appointed lawyer Monalisa Dimalanta as chairperson of the Energy Regulatory Commission
  • Prior to her appointment, Dimalanta served as the legal counsel and compliance officer of Aboitiz Power Corporation, according to Press Secretary Trixie Angeles-Cruz
    • She also chaired the National Renewable Energy Board prior to joining AP
    • Dimalanta is also senior partner of Puyat Jacinto & Santos law firm
  • Last month, Marcos appointed Raphael Lotilla, who served as independent director of Aboitiz Power and Aboitiz Equity Ventures, as Secretary of Department of Energy
TELCOS
Dito files competition complaint against PLDT, Globe
  • New mobile operator Dito Telecommunity filed complaints against PLDT and Globe for “anti-competitive” behavior
    • Complaints relate to voice interconnection
    • Dito claims only 30% of calls from its network get through to PLDT and Globe
  • Regular voice calls are becoming less frequent and important already as calls are increasingly being done through the data network
    • Voice revenue represents a decreasing share of total mobile revenue (~15%)
  • The government-mandated removal of mobile interconnect/termination fees also results in little incentive to improve voice
CONGLOS
DMCI 2Q22 profit doubles YoY; Property and construction units rebound, along with SCC
  • DMCI reported 1H22 profit of P20.3bn. Stripping out one-offs and FX, we estimate core income jumped 142% YoY to P19.9bn, representing 59% of consensus FY22E
  • 2Q profit more than doubled YoY to P8.7bn
  • Apart from Semirara, there were notable QoQ/YoY improvements in construction, property, nickel units
    • DMCI Homes’ profit jumped 79% YoY largely on lower costs (2Q revenues only up by 6% YoY); Reservation sales dropped 30% QoQ to P5.7bn (~60% of 2019 average). 
    • Construction recovered strongly on lower provisions (vs 2Q21).  QoQ improvement also looks driven largely by lower costs.
  • The company is holding a briefing at 2PM (registration link). We hope to get more detail then, particularly on the sustainability of margins for property and construction
