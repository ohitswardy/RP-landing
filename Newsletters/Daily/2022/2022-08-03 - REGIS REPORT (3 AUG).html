REGIS REPORT

03 August 2022

Metrobank’s (MBT PM) 1H22 NPAT of P15.6bn roundly beat REGIS and consensus forecasts. Core operations were broadly in line – loan growth accelerated to 10.2%, NIMs expanded 9bps QoQ, and fees were strong (+18% YoY). The outperformance came chiefly from surprise trading/FX gains, and from a sharp drop in provisions. MBT booked P3.4bn worth of trading/fx gains in 1H22; however, we note that they also took a huge P17bn mark-to-market hit in 1H22 on their FVOCI/AFS book on higher interest rates. To the upside, industry-best asset quality (NPL ratio 1.01% and NPL cover of 196%) allowed the bank do drop 1H provisions 46% YoY. Despite the headline beat, we retain our forecasts, acknowledging the volatility of trading gains, and of provisions particularly given the current macro challenges

Apart from Metrobank, we also have earnings press releases from Puregold (PGOLD PM) and Semirara (SCC PM) before their respective briefings.

For Puregold (PGOLD PM), the 2Q results are looking just on trend for our full-year estimates. Operating profit in 2Q22 jumped 17% YoY on the back of a 14% growth in net sales. We estimate SSSG accelerated during the quarter, albeit coming from a low YoY base, but underlying trends suggest soft demand for the core Puregold customer. The sequential contraction in margins is likely being driven by (1) the timing of supplier/trade support recognition and/or (2) the depreciation of the PHP (for S&R). Further details should be available after the company hosts its earnings briefing.

On the other hand, Semirara (SCC PM) produced another exceptional quarter, unsurprising with coal prices where they are. The company’s headline 2Q22 profit of P10.8bn surprised to the upside due largely to lower costs at the coal mining unit. Coal revenues were not as high as one might have thought, however, because ASPs did not rise as much. Profits from power generation, meanwhile, recovered strongly QoQ, YoY and were within expectations. Management will hold a briefing tomorrow (registration link here). 

+++

The purchase by SM Investments (SM PM) of the company operating the Tiwi-Makban geothermal fields has been approved by the SEC. SM will be issuing 17.4m shares (+1.4% of share capital) to acquire 81% of AllFirst Equity, which owns Philippine Geothermal Production Company (PGPC), which sells “steam” used to run power plants run by Aboitiz Power (AP PM). Because PGPC’s tariff are linked to coal prices, FY22 will be a much better year earnings-wise. The acquisition of PGPC appears to have been driven by a desire to burnish SM’s ESG credentials.

+++

Reported Covid-19 infections were up 21% WoW the past week, though the actual infections are likely to be much higher. Hospital occupancy numbers are now at 27% (ICU) and 40% (non-ICU), not high yet, relative to what they were in January.

RETAIL
PGOLD 1H22 net profit +5% YoY to P4.2bn – in line with Regis, mildly ahead of consensus
  • PGOLD reported 1H22 net profit of P4.2bn, up 5% YoY. For 2Q22 alone, net profit rose 4% YoY but operating income jumped 17% YoY
  • Net sales grew 14% YoY to P43.7bn in 2Q22, we estimate SSSG accelerated during the quarter, but the YoY base was also lower because of the lockdown last year
  • Margins were largely stable YoY, but contracted QoQ. The sequential contraction is likely driven by the (1) timing of supplier/trade support recognition and/or (2) the deprection of the PHP (eg S&R)
  • PGOLD currently trades at 10x 2022E PE

CONGLOS
SM Investments receives SEC approval to acquire Philippine Geothermal Production Company 
  • SM Investments received SEC approval to acquire the 81% stake of related parties in Allfirst Equity Holdings in exchange for shares of SM
  • Allfirst is the holding company for Philippine Geothermal Production Company (PGPC)
    • PGPC operates geothermal fields in Tiwi, Albay and Makban (Makiling-Banahaw), Laguna
    • The geothermal output is sold to Aboitiz Power’s power plants which produce electricity from it
    • Tariffs are linked to coal prices
  • The transaction will bring SM’s ownership in PGPC to 100%
  • SM Investments will issue 21.5mn SM shares to:
    • SM Investments – 4.1mn SM shares
    • Hans Sy – 5.8mn SM shares
    • Elizabeth Sy – 5.8mn SM shares
    • Harley Sy – 5.8mn SM shares
    • Adelia Cua – 26,914 SM shares
    • Asuncion Yap – 26,914 SM shares
  • The acquisition will increase SM’s shares outstanding by 17.4mn shares (or 1.4%)
POWER
Semirara 2Q22 profit up almost 3x YoY to P10.8bn
  • In a press release, headline 2Q22 profit stood at P10.8bn, up 171% YoY but down 28% QoQ
    • Brings 1H22 profit to P25.8bn, trending AHEAD of Regis (P38bn) and street (P35bn) estimates
    • We suspect the beat largely came from lower-than-expected costs at the coal mining unit
  • The coal segment’s profit dropped c. 30% QoQ to P9.8bn (+171% YoY), as volumes fell 27% QoQ
    • ASP in 2Q alone were up 5% QoQ to P5,399/MT, brings 1H22 ASP to 5,262/MT, tracking below our FY22E (P5,648/MT)
    • 1H22 volumes were flat at 8.8mn MT, in line with management’s guidance of 15mn MT
  • The power unit’s profits jumped 68% QoQ, 107% YoY, thanks to higher spot sales volumes and prices
    • 1H22 stand alone profit for the power unit came in at P2bn, in line with forecast
  • SCC’s cash stood at P25.7bn in end-June, after royalty payments (P8.8bn), capex (P2.5bn), debt servicing (P2.1bn), and dividends payments (P6.2bn)
