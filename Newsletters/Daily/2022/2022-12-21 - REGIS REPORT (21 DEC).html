REGIS REPORT

21 December 2022

The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Felipe Medalla said the regulator may consider cutting the banks’ deposit regulatory reserve requirement (RRR) by June 2023. Mr Medalla, though, said that the BSP wants to see inflation moderating first, as a RRR cut would run counter to the prevailing monetary tightening stance. The financial system is also quite liquid, with banking industry LDRs at 72% as of October, making the RRR cut less urgent.

The current RRR for universal and commercial banks (UKBs) is 12.0% on banks’ PHP-denominated deposits. The BSP has had a long-term commitment to bring the RRR for UKBs down to 10.0%.

Any cut in RRR would be immediately positive for the banks, as assets held in RRR do not earn any interest. We estimate that, on a full-year basis, every 100bps cut to the RRR would add around 1% to pre-tax profit for banks we cover (see table below). We assume simplistically that banks are able to invest the released funds at rates close to the BSP’s policy rate at a minimum.

It is now looking very likely that DMCI (DMC PM) will be displacing Megaworld (MEG PM) in the semi-annual game of musical chairs that is the PSEi rebalancing. What’s also looking likely (if only a little less probable) is that Union Bank (UBP PM) will unseat Robinsons Land (RLC PM) in the index roster.

Among the other interesting conclusions we draw but might be overlooked by others are the weight increases for Emperador (EMI PM) and San Miguel (SMC PM), who have somehow managed to report increases in their free float. This will also lead to fund inflows into the two names.

MACRO
GIR revised up to US$95.1bn in end-Nov; BOP posts US$756mn deficit in November
  • The BSP revised the end-November 2022 GIR level to US$95.1bn from a previously reported figure of US$93.95bn
    • GIR rose by 1.1% MoM (c. +US$1.1bn) but down from end-2021 GIR level of US$108.79bn
    • Represents adequate external liquidity buffer of 7.2 months’ worth of imports and 5.8x short-term external debt
  • Balance of payments recorded a deficit of US$756mn in November
    • Reversing the BOP surplus of US$711mn in October 2022
    • On a YoY basis, deficit was higher than the US$123mn deficit recorded in Nov 2021
    • Brings YT-November BOP deficit to US$7.9bn (BSP FY22E target at US$11.2bn, equivalent to 2.8% of GDP)
BANKS
BSP to cut reserve ratio at end June 2023
  • The BSP plans to further reduce banks’ reserve requirement ratio before the end of the first half
    • Cut will coincide with monetary authority’s plan to wind down from tightening
    • It has pledged to cut the reserve requirement ratio to below 10% by 2023 from 12% currently
  • BSP will keep raising interest rates in the next two meetings
    • According to BSP Governor Felipe Medalla
    • The BSP has raised key policy rates by 350bps so far this year to catch up with the Fed’s 425bps rate hike
TRANSPORT
ICTSI signs new 30-year lease for container port in Poland
  • ICTSI and the Port Authority of Gdynia SA signed a new 30-year lease that will run until 2053
    • Our valuation assumes contract will end in 2043
  • Baltic Container Terminal (BCT) has an estimated handling capacity of 1.2mn TEUs
    • We estimate the port accounts for c. 1% of revenues/EBITDA
    • BCT’s market reach includes all major destinations in Poland and extends along the increasingly important trans-European Baltic – Adriatic Corridor
  • ICTSI trades at 12.3x FY23E PER and 7.6x FY23E EV/EBITDA, below historical valuations (22x PER/11x EV/EBITDA) and peer average (14x/12x)
RETAIL
PGOLD raises dividend payout policy
  • PGOLD raised its stated dividend policy to at least 30% of prior year’s net income from at least 15% previously
  • In the past five years, PGOLD has paid out 17-19% of prior year’s net income
  • The Board declared a dividend of P0.86/sh (payable on Jan 20, 2023)
    • Equivalent to ~25% of Regis FY22E net profit
    • Implies a yield of 2.6% as of last closing price
  • PGOLD currently trades at 9x FY23E PE
OTHERS
DMC and UBP could enter PSE Index during the February 2023 rebalancing
  • Based on our estimates, DMC is highly likely to enter the PSE Index during the February 2023 rebalancing
    • We expect this to lead to the removal of MEG
  • Meanwhile, we consider the likelihood of UBP entering the PSE Index to be moderate
    • This should lead to the removal of RLC from the index
  • The PSEi rebalancing in February will be based on the 12 months from January 2022 to December 2022
  • Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap
  • Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when
    • 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or
    • 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35 
  • Based on our estimates, DMC and UBP have reached the top 25 (as of 11M22)
  • In addition to DMC and UBP, we also expect relatively large inflow to SMC and EMI due to increases in their public float
  • In addition to the PSEi constituents to be removed, we also expect relatively large outflow from ALI due to the reduction in its public float
  • We estimate US$114mn in total flows (US$57mn in each direction)
  • Notes:
    • EMI disclosed that its public float increased to 20%.
    • The SSS stake in UBP is about 19%. We assume that the SSS stake in excess of two board seats (3.5%) will be treated as public float.
