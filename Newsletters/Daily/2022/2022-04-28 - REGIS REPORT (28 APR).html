REGIS REPORT

28 April 2022

Building materials retailer Wilcon (WLCON PM) started the year strong with a robust set of results. While the same cannot be said of Aboitiz Power (AP PM), we remain optimistic about the outlook of the latter given the demand-supply situation for power.

  • Wilcon (WLCON PM) (+) – 1Q22 net profit grew sharply (+41%) on strong margins. SSSG (+8.6%) was healthy despite Omicron surge in January, leading to a sharp improvement in operating margins. There is likely still some room for margins to expand as the contribution from higher-margin in-house brands increases (now at c51%), but near term headwinds from supply chain issues could persist.

  • Aboitiz Power (AP PM) (-) – AP’s 1Q22 earnings came in lower at P2.9bn (-44%QoQ, -53%YoY) but the YoY/QoQ declines looked worse than the actual underlying operations because of insurance claims booked last year. Income from the generation and distribution segment declined due to outages caused by Typhoon Odette in the early part of 1Q.  The recovery from Odette and the growing contribution from GNPD would mean profits have potential to catch up to our estimate over the succeeding periods.

Here are another two Johnny-Come-Lately’s on FY21 results releases: Alliance Global (AGI PM) and Puregold (PGOLD PM).

  • Alliance Global (AGI PM) (+) – AGI’s FY21 profit hit P16.9bn (+92%), ahead of estimates, helped by tax benefits booked by MEG and the outperformance of EMP’s brandy unit in 4Q. Although the press release did not provide profit numbers for Golden Arches and Travellers, we think there may have been non-operating gains that contributed to the group’s outperformance.
  • Puregold (PGOLD PM) (=) – 4Q21 SSSG improved sequentially across both brands as mobility restrictions eased. Margins on a YoY basis appear weak, but we highlight that 4Q20 was a high base due to proportionately higher supplier support. Recall that suppliers/vendors held back on spending at the peak of the 2020 lockdowns, but made up for the shortfall during the last few months of the year. On our 2022 forecasts, PGOLD trades at 10x PE on 15% EPS growth.

+++

Ayala Land (ALI PM) continues to cash in on its share-for-asset injections into AREIT (AREIT PM) by selling more shares in the latter.  ALI raised another P3.5bn (US$70m) after selling another 87m shares of AREIT at P39.70/share. The REIT listing and the tax-free exchanges between ALI and AREIT have allowed ALI to effectively monetize its lease revenue streams at attractive valuations without having to give up its land.

+++

The government reported a decent month for revenues in March with tax take rising by 28%, albeit off a low base.  This brought the budget deficit down 2% to P188bn.  For 1Q22, the cumulative budget deficit was P317bn, also down by a small figure (-1%).

+++

During our REGIS CEO Roundtable Series, Robinsons Land (RLC PM) President Frederick Go revealed that the rental segments were recovering nicely and he expects them to perform even better over time. However, the residential business is currently facing challenges from cancellations and higher input costs.

MACRO
Budget deficit narrows in March, as revenues pick up pace
  • Data from the Bureau of Treasury showed the budget deficit shrink by 2% YoY to P187.7bn in March
  • Revenues picked up pace, with tax revenues up 29% YoY while non-tax revenues surged 88% YoY due to higher dividends from state-run firms
  • Primary spending also reversed the slight decline in 2M22, rising by 18% YoY in March
    • Cited release of funds for scholarship programs and capital outlay projects of the Dept. of Public Works and Dept. of National Defense
  • Brings 1Q22 budget deficit to P317bn, down 1.4% YoY

RETAIL
PGOLD FY21 net profit +2% to P8.2bn - in line with Regis and consensus estimates
  • PGOLD reported FY21 net profit of P8.2bn (+2% YoY). This implies 4Q21 net profit of P2.4bn (-19% YoY)
  • Net sales in 4Q21 grew 3% YoY, while consolidated SSS declined by 2.5%
    • SSS decline was an improvement vs 3Q21 as same-store traffic trends improved
  • Gross margin contracted >300bps YoY during the quarter, but note that 4Q20 was an exceptionally high base
    • In 2020, bulk of trade spending was booked in 4Q due to the various lockdowns earlier in the year
  • For 2022E, PGOLD currently trades at 10x PE and 5x EV/EBITDA on +14% EPS growth

PROPERTY
Ayala Land sells 87mn AREIT shares
  • Ayala Land sold 87.4mn AREIT shares at P39.70/share
    • Total value of P3.5bn
    • Lower than the previous closing price of P42.70/share
  • This is to comply with the minimum public ownership requirement of 33% (on REITs)
  • AREIT will issue 252.1mn AREIT shares to Ayala Land in exchange for six Cebu office buildings
    • We estimate that, if not for the sale of AREIT shares, this would have reduced AREIT’s public float from 33.6% to 28.8%
  • With the concluded share sale, we estimate that AREIT’s public float will be 34%
  • We estimate AREIT currently offers a dividend yield of 5.3%
    • The interest rate on the 10-year government bond is 6.1%
  • ALI is trading at 39% discount to NAV, 2.1X FY22E P/B, and 26.7X FY22E P/E
