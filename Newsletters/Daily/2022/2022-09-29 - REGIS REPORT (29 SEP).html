REGIS REPORT

29 September 2022

Jollibee (JFC PM) is investing an additional SGD90m (PHP3.7bn) in the international Tim Ho Wan franchise, with an eye to increase its footprint to 100 stores over the next four years, from the current 67. JFC has close to 6,300 stores globally across its many brands, of which slightly less than half are in locations outside the Philippines.

+++

San Miguel Corp (SMC PM) filed paperwork to raise at least P40bn through a bond issuance, mainly to refinance debt maturities. This follows the recent bond issue of subsidiary SMC Global Power for a similar amount (P40bn). Group-wide, SMC has P89bn (US$1.5bn) of maturities this year (FY22) and a huge P178bn (US$3bn) next year. The maturities appear to be coming at a rather challenging time, as the group will have to contend with not just rising rates but also a strong USD.

+++

Metro Pacific’s (MPI PM) Cebu toll road, the Cebu-Cordoba Link Expressway (CCLEX), is now generating P1m of revenue per day on traffic of 12.5k/day. This, however, remains a far cry from what it should generate in order to produce an economic return on its P31bn capex investment. We expect losses for CCLEX in the near-term. MPI is trading at 48% discount to its market-based NAV.

+++

Cezar Consing has been officially appointed as President and CEO of Ayala Corp (AC PM), replacing Fernando Zobel who has now resigned his post. Consing become President in an acting capacity when Zobel took a medical leave a number of weeks back. Consing was previously CEO of Bank of the Philippine Islands (BPI PM).

Apart from that appointment, there was also a switching of positions at the Aboitiz group. Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM) CFO Manuel Lozano is moving to Union Bank of the Philippines (UBP PM) as its CFO, while Jose Emmanuel Hilado of UBP is taking over Lozano’s position.

CONSUMER
JFC announces additional investment in Tim Ho Wan operator
  • JFC, through its wholly-owned subsidiary, Jollibee Worldwide Pte Ltd announced that it is making an additional SGD90mn (PHP3.7bn) investment in Titan Dining LP
    • In conjunction with the increase in the fund size of Titan to SGD350mn from the current SGD250mn
    • JFC currently has a 90% stake in Titan
  • Titan is the private equity fund that operates the Tim Ho Wan brand and company-owned stores
    • Total 67 outlets in Asia (mostly franchised), of which 11 are in Mainland China
    • JFC is targeting to hit 100 Tim Ho Wan stores in Mainland China in the next four years
  • Titan also has other investments in F&B (Tiong Bahru Bakery and Common Man Coffee Roasters) and non-F&B (Strip, Browhaus)
  • JFC currently trades at 2023E multiples of 24x PE and 12x EV/EBITDA
INFRASTRUCTURE
Metro Pacific’s Cebu tollroad’s daily revenue at P1mn
  • Cebu-Cordova Link Expressway (CCLEX) is currently generating revenues of c. P1mn per day from the passage of at least 12.5k motorists
    • According to Renato Ticzon, vice president and marketing consultant at MPT Mobility, a unit of Metro Pacific Tollways Corp.
  • The company expects traffic volumes to surge to 20k by December as a result of the holiday influx and for revenues to hit P2mn a day
  • To boost traffic, MPTC plans to build an P800mn Drive&Dine complex to boost traffic
  • CCLEX was opened to the public last April 30
  • Note that our forecasts assume daily traffic and revenues of 36k and P4mn, respectively, in 2023; We expect CCLEX to incur a net loss of P1.4bn next year
  • CCLEX is an 8.5-km elevated tollroad that cost P27bn to build.  It has a daily capacity of 100k.
  • CCLEX is a 99%-owned unit of Metro Pacific (MPI PM) and accounts for c. 1% of gross asset value
  • MPI trades at a 48% discount to its market-based NAV of P6.45/sh
CONGLOS
SMC files registration for P60bn bonds
  • Ang-led San Miguel Corp (SMC) filed the registration statement and preliminary prospectus for its P60bn fixed rate bonds to be used for redemption of preferred shares and bonds, and debt repayments
  • The base offer amounts to P40bn with an oversubscription of up to P20bn and will be issued at 100% face value
  • The prospectus stated the net proceeds for the offer will be used for the optional redemption of the company’s series 2H preferred shares and up to P13.15bn for final redemption and payment of the company’s series E bonds
  • Up to P17bn will be used to repay company’s $300mn facility with MUFG Bank set to mature in 2Q23
  • In July, SMC subsidiary SMC Global Power Holdings raised P40bn from the issuance of bonds
CONGLOS
Cezar Consing officially appointed as Ayala Corp president, CEO
  • Ayala Corp announced the official appointment of Cezar “Bong” Consing as its president and chief executive officer, officially taking over the post vacated by Fernando Zobel de Ayala
  • Consing was first appointed as interim chief on August 15 after Zobel de Ayala took a medical leave of absence
  • Zobel de Ayala has since resigned from his position in the conglomerate and other companies under the group
  • Prior to his appointment, Consing served as president and CEO of the Bank of the Philippine Islands from 2013 to 2021
  • He also worked with JP Morgan in Hong Kong and Singapore from 1985 to 2004 and headed the firm’s investment banking business in Asia Pac from 1997 to 2004
  • Consing also served as partner at the Rohatyn Group from 2004 to 2013
BANKS
BSP may relax single-borrower limit to promote ESG
  • The BSP said it may relax the single borrower’s limit and rules for directors, officers, stakeholders and related interests for ESG lending
    • According to the BSP’s Twitter account, citing Governor Felipe Medalla
  • There is currently a cap on the loans, credit accommodations and guarantees that may be extended by a bank to any individual or corporation at 25% of its net worth
