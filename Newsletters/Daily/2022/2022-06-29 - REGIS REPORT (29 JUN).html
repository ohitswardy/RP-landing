REGIS REPORT

29 June 2022

There will be no change in the pandemic Alert level of the government for Metro Manila until at least July 15, based on the announcement yesterday. We note that the government also revised the bases for determining Alert levels, putting greater emphasis on hospital utilization, reflective of the view that Covid-19 is less deadly now because of vaccinations.

+++

We take a few minutes to highlight that how commodity prices have come off recent highs and corrected the past few weeks (oil being one of the few exceptions).

A check of the widely followed index of a commodity basket by the Commodity Research Bureau (“CRY Index”) shows that it’s retraced slightly in recent days, giving hope that we are already at an inflection point for inflation. 

While oil remains sky-high, two key soft commodities (wheat and palm oil) have come off sharply from recent peaks. This should provide relief for firms like Monde (MONDE PM) and Universal Robina (URC PM). For palm oil specifically, it should also be positive for D&L (DNL PM).

Given that the Philippines is net importer of nearly every important commodity, the CRY Index has historically been a good leading indicator of where CPI inflation is heading. With commodity prices only starting to come off, we could see its impact on inflation a few months from now. The near-term inflation prints, however, are likely to continue rising.

+++

The Bureau of Treasury (BTr) rejected all bids for the 7-year Treasury Bond yesterday because it felt the resultant yields would have been too high. Had the BTr given the full award, yields for the 7-year paper would have risen to 6.947%. This would have been 52bp higher than the auction last month and ~30bp higher than secondary market yields.

MACRO
Metro Manila, most areas stay under Alert Level 1 until July 15
  • Alert Level 1 will remain in Metro Manila from July 1-15
    • 85 of 121 provinces, highly urbanized cities and independent component cities and 166 of 744 other component cities and municipalities are also under Alert Level 1
    • 37 areas from across the Philippines “shall be placed” under the alert system’s level 2
  • Gov’t also redefined alert level classifications to be based “on the revised cross tabulation of total beds utilization rate and average daily attack rate”
    • New classification removes the two-week growth rate in determining case-risk classification
  • Nationwide cases (7-day average) are now at 711 (up from 479 the previous week), with NCR at 342 (vs 238 the previous week)
  • Nationwide weekly covid bed occupancy rates are now at 14.9% (ICU) from 14.6% the previous week

MACRO
Treasury rejects all bids for 7-year bonds
  • The Bureau of Treasury rejected bids for reissued 7-year T-bonds
    • Had the offer been fully awarded, yield would have reached 6.947%, 52bps higher from the previous auction in May (6.428%)
    • Yield would have also been above the 6.627% for 7-year BVAL and 45bps higher than its 6.5% coupon
    • Total bids reached P62bn, higher than the P35bn program
  • National Treasurer Rosalia V. de Leon said that bids were rejected bids as investors asked for too high a risk premium, given anticipated aggressive rate hikes from the Fed and BSP
  • For July, the Bureau of Treasury plans to raise P200bn, of which P60bn will be via T-bills and P140bn via T-bonds
    • It is set to offer longer bond tenors next month, with maturities as long as 14 years
MACRO
Wholesale prices of building materials in Metro Manila rise by 8.3%, fastest since 2011
  • Wholesale prices of building materials in Metro Manila rose by 8.3% in May
    • +6.9% in April
    • +2% in May 2021
  • This is the fastest pace of growth since the 8.6% figure in November 2011
  • The Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) said that supply chain issues also delayed the delivery of materials from abroad, which pushed up costs onshore  
  • Minimum wages have risen 6% in Metro Manila and c. 15% in most parts of the Philippines
MACRO
Gov’t hopes to contain rising poultry prices by easing import bans
  • The Department of Agriculture (DA) said it is relaxing import bans, allowing vaccine imports, and exploring new types of feed to help contain the rising prices of poultry products, including eggs.
  • Bureau of Animal Industry Director Reildrin G. Morales said producers are holding back production to minimize losses and are experiencing increased competition as hog raisers shifted to poultry and egg production after their herds were reduced by African Swine Fever.
  • The DA will also soon allow the import of poultry from non-infected parts of Spain, Denmark and the Czech Republic to bolster the breeding stocks

SEC clears Philex’s P2.65-B share sale
  • The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has conditionally approved a P2.65-billion stock rights offering (SRO) planned by Philex Mining (PX PM) to raise funds for its Silangan copper-gold project in Surigao del Norte.
  • Under this SRO, which will run from July 14 to July 25, existing shareholders are entitled to buy one share for every 5.8674 common shares owned.
