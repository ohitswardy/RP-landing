REGIS REPORT

18 February 2022

We published a new note on ICTSI (ICT PM), raising our earnings forecasts by 11-22% and TP by 25% on a more robust outlook. ICT’s market shares across a number of locations improved over the course of 2021, which led them to surprisingly raise tariffs in some key ports such as Melbourne, Manila, and Mexico. We expect this year to see a return to the growth path, as vaccination levels improve in most of the areas in its portfolio, which we believe will propel their respective economic and trade recoveries. We also believe yields will stay healthy due to recent tariff adjustments in Melbourne and Manila, with another looming for Manila. 

Other things we highlighted in the note were (1) the company’s de-leveraging initiatives, which we believe will propel earnings growth further, and (2) the inelasticity of import demand/container volumes to modest weakness of local currencies relative to the USD.

While the stock has outperformed the index massively over the past year, we believe the shares remain attractive, trading at 9x FY22E EV/EBITDA, still a discount to historical valuations (11x) and peers (12x).

The Bangko Sentral (BSP), as expected, kept policy rates unchanged at yesterday’s Monetary Board meeting. BSP has maintained the record low 2% policy rate since last year after cutting during the early part of the pandemic. Worth highlighting, however, is that the BSP’s inflation forecast for 2022 was increased to 3.7% (vs. 3.4%), not far from the upper end of its inflation target band of 2% to 4%. Note that this likely already considers the CPI rebasing which resulted in a lower headline inflation number (in Dec 2021).

While we believe a rate hike from the BSP within 1H22 is unlikely, there will be even more pressure for higher rates towards the latter half of the year as (1) the markets anticipate an increasing number of rate hikes from the Fed and (2) local CPI numbers creep up in the coming months.

+++

Another IPO of a solar farm operator is in the pipeline, as these RE firms rush to take advantage of the generous valuations. Raslag Corp, which operates 23MW of solar capacity, plans to raise up to P805m (US$16m) in new money from its listing.

Solar Philippines Nueva Ecija Corp (SPNEC PM) listed early this year and Citicore REIT, a solar farm disguised as a REIT to take advantage of tax incentives, will be listing next week.

MACRO
Monetary Board maintains policy rates
  • For a 10th straight meeting, the BSP maintained its overnight reverse repurchase rate at a historic low of 2%
    • Within market expectations
    • Cited a manageable inflation environment and emerging uncertainty surrounding domestic and global growth prospects
  • However, BSP Governor Diokno hinted at an “eventual normalization” of policy once recovery is sustained or inflation risks rise
    • They see the economy reaching pre-pandemic GDP levels by 3Q22
    • The risks to the inflation outlook continue to lean slightly towards the upside for 2022 but remain broadly balanced for 2023
    • Upside risks are linked mainly to the continued shortage in domestic pork and fish supply and the possible impact of higher oil prices on transport fares
  • The BSP raised its average inflation forecast for 2022 to 3.7% (from 3.4%) and for 2023 to 3.3% (from 3.2%)
    • Inflation is seen to decelerate in early 2022 due to base effects
    • But inflation could accelerate towards the upper end of the target band (2-4%) in the second quarter on elevated oil and nonoil prices
    • By 2H22, inflation is expected to move back to within target range

Raslag Corp plans P805mn IPO
  • Raslag Corp is planning to list on the stock market next month with an P805mn IPO to fund its solar projects
  • The company is planning to sell 350mn primary common shares for up to P2/share and an overallotment option of 52.5mn secondary common shares owned by J Ten Equities, Inc.
    • The company may raise up to P700mn from the sale of primary shares
  • Net proceeds will be used to fund the balance of its Pamapanga Solar Power Project Phase 4 (35MW)
  • It currently operates 2 solar farms with 23MW of capacity in Pampanga
  • 53MW under construction and another 60MW being planned
  • Market capitalization at the offer price is P3bn
  • Founded by Peter Nepomuceno of Pampanga in 2013
