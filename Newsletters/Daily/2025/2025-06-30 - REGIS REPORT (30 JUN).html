REGIS REPORT

30 June 2025

The Dept of Finance (DOF) has practically given up on any privatization sales this year, slashing its P105bn privatization target to virtually nothing. This reduction accounts for bulk of the reduction in the government’s overall revenue target this year. The government cut its FY25 revenue target by P124bn to P4.52trn.

 

+++

 

Century Pacific (CNPF PM) says it is reducing the price of some of its sardine products by P1/can effective July 1. A typical 155g can of sardines sells for a retail price of over P20 so the price reduction would be around 4-5%. Sardines are part of the branded “marine” segment which also includes tuna. It (marine) forms the largest part of CNPF’s business.

 

+++

 

Double Dragon (DD PM) subsidiary Hotel101 is listing on NASDAQ under the ticker HBNB. Hotel101 operates something akin to what one would call a condotel business model. It sells units in a property that is meant to be leased to tenants and for which it receives a revenue share.

 

Successfully monetizing at least part of its 70% stake in Hotel101 would help ease the debt burden that DD is laboring under.

 

+++

 

The trade deficit contracted by 30% to US$3.29bn in May as exports grew 15%. YT-May, the trade deficit stood at US$19.7, down 5% YoY.

MACRO
Dept. of Finance slashes privatization goal to P5bn for 2025
  • The Department of Finance (DoF) cut its privatization goal for this year by 95% to P5bn from P101bn
    • This is due to delays in selling gov't properties given legal issues
    • The majority of the properties are instead expected to be sold by 2026, raising the privatization goal for 2026 to P100bn from P1bn previously
  • The Development Budget Coordination Committee (DBCC) recently hiked its 2025-2028 fiscal deficit targets largely due to lower revenue targets
    • For 2025 alone, it has lowered revenue projections by P124bn (-3%) to P4.52tn
CONSUMER
Century Pacific to rollback canned sardines prices by 1 peso
  • Century Pacific Food, Inc. (CNPF PM) announced a price rollback of P1 (~4-5%) per can on all Ligo and 555 sardines starting July 1
    • The suggested retail of a 155g can of sardines is over P20
  • The company stated that continuous efforts to maximize the value chain allow CNPF to give "maximum value" to its customers with hundreds of millions of cans of 555 and Ligo sardines sold annually
  • The branded business (marine, meat, milk) posted P59.3bn (+7% YoY) of revenues for FY24, which comprises ~80% of total revenue
    • The marine segment as of 1Q25 had 82% market share, -120 bps lower than in Dec 2024

MACRO
Trade deficit narrows to $3.3bn in May
  • The May trade deficit narrowed to US$3.3bn, contracting 30% YoY, reflecting the jump in exports and the decline in imports
    • This brings the 5M25 trade deficit to US$19.7bn, 5% narrower YoY
  • Imports for the month declined by 4% YoY to US$10.6bn, a slower decline relative to the 7% decrease in April
    • Primarily driven by the decrease in fuel and raw & intermediate goods imports
  • Exports jumped by 15% YoY to US$7.3bn, the fifth consecutive month of growth
    • Electronics exports rose by 8% to $3.8bn, a reversal from the 5% YoY decrease in April
      • These made up the bulk of exports at 53% of the total
    • Meanwhile, non-electronics exports surged by 24% YoY to US$3.4bn
      • Growth in non-electronics exports was led by other manufactured goods, gold, copper concentrates, as well as machinery and transport equipment

ACEN, UPC divesting stake in India project?
  • Bloomberg reported that ACEN and UPC plan to sell a significant stake in its upcoming 1GW project in India
    • Deal could potentially involve a sale of up to 74%
    • Deal could potentially be valued at US$600mn and at an equity value of US$200mn
  • A 100% divestment at US$600mn implies an EV/MW of US$600k, higher than replacement cost in India for solar project of US$300-400k per MW
    • A sale at this valuation is likely to generate significant gains and could also improve NAV
    • Our model currently includes 1,300 MW of standalone solar projects (630 MW operational) in India at an investment cost of US$400k/MW
    • ACEN has an 80% economic stake in each of these projects
MACRO
Current consumer expectations remain negative, future expectations positive—BSP survey
  • The latest Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Consumer Expectations Survey (CES) results exhibited that current sentiment or the overall consumer index (CI) fell to -14.0% in 2Q25 from -13.0% the previous quarter
    • Concerns over rising prices, declining wages, and fewer job opportunities were cited as the main reasons
    • “Political noise” was cited as a reason given respondents’ growing concerns on good governance and the gov’t’s ability to deliver their services amidst political turbulence
    • A negative CI implies more respondents are pessimistic than optimistic
  • Notably, consumer sentiment for the next quarter reverted to positive 0.6% from -0.5% the previous quarter
  • Consumers expect economic prospects to improve in the next 12 months, with a positive CI at 11.8%, slightly down from 12.4% the previous quarter
    • The positive consumer expectations for the future stem from the improvement of the aforementioned concerns on prices, wages, and jobs
  • The CES results are taken into account in the BSP's decision-making for monetary policy
