REGIS REPORT

06 August 2025

With yesterday’s inflation print of 0.9% for July, BSP Governor Remolona reiterated his stance that there was room for two more policy rate cuts this year. Bloomberg reported that Mr Remolona said a cut was “more likely” at the Monetary Board meeting on 28 August. He added that the BSP “can still cut some more”, if inflation stays soft the rest of the year. Two more cuts could see policy rates fall to 4.75%, lower than our expectation of 5.0%.

Results! Results! We have results comments on ACEN (=), ICT (-), MEG (+), WLCON (=), GLO (-)

 

  • ACEN Corp (ACEN PM) (=) - ACEN incurred a headline net loss of P1.2bn in 2Q25, after taking a chunky impairment charge on its wind farm investment in Vietnam. Excluding that, recurring profit for the quarter declined 38% YoY to P1.3bn, as operational issues (i.e. lower spot prices, lower solar irradiance, extended outage of wind facilities in Luzon) persisted and more than offset fresh contributions from Stubbo solar (Australia) and Stockyard wind (USA) facilities. The results are broadly in line with our expectations but are well below consensus forecasts. The good news is that management is on track with its target of energizing 800MW of new capacity this year, as well as the completion of north Luzon wind farm repairs by 4Q. PROPERTY
Megaworld 1H25 net profit rises 25%, ahead of REGIS and consensus
  • Megaworld’s 1H25 headline net profit grew 25% to P10.7bn, ahead of REGIS and consensus forecasts
    • Stripping off forex gains in 1H25 (and forex losses in 1H24), we estimate that 1H25 recurring profit rose 10% to P10.4bn, which is still ahead of REGIS and consensus forecasts
  • 1H25 operating profit climbed 9% to P15.2bn, ahead of REGIS and consensus forecasts
  • Revenue of every segment outperformed our expectations
  • Residential revenue rose 10% YoY (+7% QoQ) to P14.0bn, a record level
    • This likely implies that cancellations remain low
  • Office revenue grew 18% YoY (+1% QoQ) to P3.7bn
    • This likely means that office occupancy was flat QoQ (at 17%)
  • Mall revenue rose 9% YoY (+1% QoQ) to P1.7bn 
  • The company is targeting to have an office portfolio of 2mn sqm and a mall portfolio of 1mn sqm by 2030
    • From 1.5mn sqm and 520,000sqm as of 4Q24/1Q25
    • This implies CAGR of 5% for the office portfolio and 12% for the mall portfolio
  • MEG is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 3.5X FY25E P/E
  • The company will host its earnings briefing on August 7

 

 

MACRO
Inflation rate dips to 0.9% in July
  • Headline inflation fell to 0.9% in July from 1.4% in the previous month of June
    • This is slightly ahead of the consensus forecast of 1.1% and stands well within the BSP's forecast range of 0.5-1.3%
    • This is the lowest inflation print since Oct 2019 (0.6%)
    • Inflation has fallen from 2.8% in Jan and has been hovering below BSP's 2-4% target for the past five months
  • Primary drivers for the continued benign inflation were YoY price declines in rice (-15.9%) and transport (-2.0%) as well as the slower price acceleration in electricity, gas, fuel (+1.5% from +5.5% in June), which offset rising prices of meat (+8.8%) and fish (+6.3%)
    • Notably, rice prices are on their thirteenth consecutive MoM price decline since Jul 2024
      • According to the Dept. of Agriculture (as of Aug 2), prices for local and imported well-milled rice range from P37-52/kg and P38-50/kg, respectively vs. P42-52/kg and P40-54/kg back in Jan 2
  • Core inflation inched up to 2.3% in July from 2.2% in June, while core non-food inflation was unchanged relative to the previous month at 2.0%
  • This brings the YT-Jun headline inflation rate to 1.7%, below the lower end of the 2-4% BSP target for this year
    • Consensus expects 2025 inflation to be 2.0%
      • Regis expects the full year average to be 1.8%
  • The next Monetary Board meeting (4th out of 6 this year) will be on Aug 28
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expects 75bp total worth of cuts to 5.00%

 

 

 

 

 

MACRO
Rate cut likely this month; BSP Governor sees room for more cuts beyond 2025
  • Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona stated that the central bank has more leeway to continue the easing cycle next year after two more 25bp cuts for the rest of 2025
  • Remolona guided for a possible rate cut in later this month followed by another cut in 4Q is "more likely" given the six-year low inflation print in July
    • This implies 100bp total rate cuts for 2025, bringing the key policy rate to 4.75% by year-end from the current 5.25%
  • He also added that the BSP does not necessarily follow US rate moves given the peso's relative strength despite not closely matching the Fed's rate path
  • The next Monetary Board meeting is scheduled for Aug 28 (fourth out of six this year)
    • For 2025, Bloomberg median consensus and REGIS expects 75bp total worth of cuts to 5.00%

 

 

BANKS
Commercial banks’ loan growth 12.1% in June; M3 growth at 6.3%
  • Loans of commercial banks rose by 12.1% YoY in June, an acceleration from the 11.3% rate in May
    • Loans to productive activities increased 11.1% YoY, growing faster than the 10.2% in May due to increased lending in key industries such as
      • Electricity, gas, steam, air conditioning supply (+29.2% from +25.6%), transpo, storage (+15.9% from +14.0%), finance, insurance (+12.0% from +9.6%), and real estate (+9.9% from +8.7%)
    • Consumer loans grew 24.0%, slightly faster than the 23.7% pace in May
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) picked up to 6.3% in June, accelerating from the 5.5% growth in May

 

 

 

POWER
Energy regulator probes Ilijan operator for alleged anti-competitive behavior
  • In an order dated July 24, the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) issued a show-cause order to South Premiere Power Corp. (SPPC) for an alleged anti-competitive behavior in June 2022
    • SPPC was placed on “open breaker status” for 21 days in June 2022, which coincided with a 30% MoM jump in WESM prices due to power supply deficiencies
    • Phil. Electricity Market Corp. (PEMC), operator of WESM, notified the ERC on Sept. 13, 2022 of a possible anti-competitive act committed by SPPC
  • ERC directed SPPC to submit its verified explanation within 15 days from receipt of order
  • SPPC is the operator of the 1,200 MW Ilijan natural gas facility
    • SPPC is 40%-owned by Meralco (MER PM), 33% by San Miguel (SMC PM), and 27% by Aboitiz Power (AP PM)
