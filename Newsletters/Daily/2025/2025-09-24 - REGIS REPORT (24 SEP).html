REGIS REPORT

24 September 2025

GT Capital (GTCAP PM) disclosed that it increased its stake in Metrobank (MBT PM) with the purchase of 113mn MBT shares, equivalent to 2.5% interest in the bank. The shares were transacted at P72.55/share, which would be on 6x FY25 PER and 0.8x P/B. At those valuations, the acquisition would be immediately accretive to both earnings and NAV.

 

From publicly available information, we believe the MBT stake was likely acquired from a holding firm controlled by the Ty family, the controlling shareholders of both MBT and GTCAP.

 

+++

 

Robinsons Land (RLC PM) sold 1bn shares of RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM) at P7.75/share, raising a total of P7.75bn. Given the 40% YTD price appreciation of RCR (prior to yesterday), RLC likely deemed it a good time to liquidate some shares in order to make room for next year’s share-for-property swap. With the completed share sale, we estimate that RCR can acquire c. P24bn worth of rental assets from its sponsor.

 

+++

 

The BPO industry association BPAP says it’s expecting to have 1.97m employees and to generate US$42bn in revenue in 2026. Those numbers would represent a 4% and 5% increase from the numbers the association expects for this year (2025).

 

These predictions suggest that BPAP is yet to feel the adverse impact that AI is predicted to bring to the industry. BPOs account for close to half of all office space in Metro Manila.

 

+++

 

Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) is signing up with a tech firm for the latter to provide AI-assisted solutions for its supply chain and inventory management systems.

House committee approves 2026 national budget bill; starts plenary deliberations
  • The House Committee on Appropriations approved House Bill No. 4058 or the General Appropriations bill on a vote of 54 for, 6 against, 4 abstaining—moving the budget bill to plenary level deliberations
    • This comes after 37 days of committee-level vetting and the recent finalization of budget amendments after the reallocation of the P255bn flood control budget from the Dept. of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) to the education, health, and agricultural sectors
  • After passing the committee level, the budget bill will be deliberated through the House plenary level, then to the Senate, the bicam, and finally to President Marcos Jr. for approval
    • The House targets to approve the budget bill on second reading before Oct 10, and by final reading after sessions resume a month after in Nov
  • The proposed 2026 national budget amounts to P6.79trn, 7% higher YoY than this year's P6.33trn budget
