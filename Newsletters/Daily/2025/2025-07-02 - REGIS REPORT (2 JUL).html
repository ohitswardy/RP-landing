REGIS REPORT

02 July 2025

Several legislators have rushed to file their pet bills immediately after assuming office under the 20th Congress.

 

Among the more notable pieces of proposed legislation are those calling for another minimum wage increase, amendments to the law/s on rice imports, and an expansion of the conditional cash transfer program (“4Ps”).

 

What’s grabbing the most headlines, however, are the proposals on online gambling. While some have called for an outright ban, possibly the most realistic proposal is the imposition of restrictions, particularly on financial transfers. For example, imposing a minimum transfer of P10,000 (US$180) into online gambling apps, as has been proposed, could dramatically alter the dynamics of the business.

 

We highlighted the explosive growth of online gaming over the last three years in a recent note. This growth has, in our view, come at the expense of “regular” consumption – thus affecting consumer firms. A government response is now looking very likely, and its response could reverse this trend.

MACRO
Senators and congressmen file personal priority bills for the 20th Congress
  • Several senators and congressmen have started filing their personal priority bills and resolutions for the 20th Congress which is scheduled to convene on July 28
  • Some notable bills that have been filed were as follows:
    • Across-the-Board Wage Hike bills which vary from a P150 increase to meeting the "estimated average family living wage of P1,200"
    • Expansion of the 4Ps (Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program) Act, which currently provides conditional cash transfers to poor households
    • Stricter regulation or a complete ban on online gaming
    • Amendments to the Rice Tariffication Law and restoration of National Food Authority (NFA) powers for more affordable rice
  • Note that a bill becomes law only when it is passed by both houses of Congress, then processed through a bicameral conference committee, and approved by the President
GAMING
Senator wants stricter regulation of online gambling
  • Senator Sherwin Gatchalian filed a bill that would further regulate online gambling
  • The bill seeks to increase the minimum cash-in to P10,000
    • Some gaming platforms currently have a minimum cash-in of P25
  • The bill would also ban the linking of e-wallets to gambling sites and would restrict advertising of online gambling
  • Meanwhile, Senator Joel Villanueva filed a bill that would ban all online gambling
  • Earlier this year, Senators Alan Peter Cayetano and Francis Escudero called for a review of online gambling
  • 1Q25 GGR of e-games (including bingo) grew 88% YoY (flat QoQ) to P51bn
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) is the market leader with about 45% market share
    • PLUS is trading at 12.6X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
PROPERTY
Ayala Land acquires New World Hotel Makati
  • Ayala Land acquired New World Hotel in Makati for an undisclosed amount
    • Ayala Land only acquired the building since it already owns the land
  • The acquisition expands Ayala Land’s hotel portfolio by 598 rooms (14%) to 4,862 rooms
    • The hotel business accounts for about 2% of operating profit
  • ALI is trading at 48% discount to NAV, 1.3X FY25E P/B, and 13.2X FY25E P/E
TELCOS
DITO Tel eyes 1mn fixed wireless subscribers by 2026
  • DITO Telecommunity Corporation targets to have at least one million users of its 5G powered, plug-and-play fixed wireless access (FWA) by 2026
    • DITO Tel President and CEO Eric Alberto states that the telco's infrastructure can accommodate 3.6mn FWA subscribers
    • Alberto also elaborated that the home broadband market remains underpenetrated with only 8-9mn out of 24mn households connected
  • Since the service's launch of "DITO Home WOWFi" back in October 2024, the subscriber count as of end-June is 250,000
    • The company aims to have 300,000 FWA subscribers by end-2025
INFRA
Election ban tempers government infrastructure spending in April
  • Overall infrastructure spending (i.e. including spending of LGUs and GOCCs) rose by 2.4% YoY to P419bn in 4M25
    • Implies that infrastructure spending for the month of April alone fell 29% YoY, 24% MoM to P102bn
    • Cited frontloaded and accelerated spending in prior months ahead of the election ban from March 28 to May 11
  • The government has set a target of P1.54tn on infrastructure spending this year, down 0.5% YoY and equivalent to 5.4% of GDP
INFRA
Supreme Court issues Writ of Kalikasan on Davao-Samal bridge
  • The Supreme Court (SC) issued a Writ of Kalikasan against the proponents of the 4-km Samal Island-Davao City Connector Project
    • Proponents are the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) and the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR)
    • Petition was filed by various labor groups over alleged serious and irreversible damage to the coral reefs
  • SC referred the prayer for a Temporary Environmental Protection Order (TEPO) to the Court of Appeals-Cagayan De Oro for action
  • Construction on the P23bn Chinese ODA-funded project is ongoing; Completion by 2028
    • DPWH recently reported “a 38% accomplishment rate” on the bridge
