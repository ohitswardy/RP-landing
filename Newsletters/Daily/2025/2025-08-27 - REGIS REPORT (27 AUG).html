REGIS REPORT

27 August 2025

Manila North Harbour (MNHPI), a port controlled by ICTSI (ICT PM), has applied for a 20% increase in tariffs. We estimate MNHPI contributes 4% of ICT’s group revenues and 5% of EBITDA. MNHPI is runs a terminal that handles domestic cargo in the Port of Manila.

 

+++

 

With the 12% digital services tax now being collected, the Philippines is potentially at risk of being in President Trump’s crosshairs. Trump has threatened to impose additional tariffs on countries that impose such taxes, since many of the firms directly affected are American (e.g. Google, Netflix, Amazon, etc). Trump has aggressively tried to use tariffs as an instrument of foreign policy in order to bludgeon countries into submission. What long-term impact it will have on the US and the global order remains to be seen.

 

+++

 

The Dept of Agriculture (DA) is again seeking to recover its power to import rice. The DA, through the National Food Authority, used to be allowed to import rice until that power was removed by the Rice Tariffication Law.

 

While there are logical arguments in favor of restoring the DA’s ability to import rice (e.g. to serve as counterweight to any cartel-like behavior by the local rice trade), the arguments against it remain strong (e.g., a long history of corruption and mismanagement, market distortions, etc.).

 

+++

 

The cumulative amount of loans disbursed by GCash since it began operations has reached P287bn as of end-June. This figure implies it disbursed P34bn of new loans within 2Q alone (roughly the same pace as the previous two quarters).

 

What’s also interesting is that Maya appears to have disbursed almost the same amount in 2Q (P32bn).

TRANSPORT
ICTSI’s Manila North Harbour seeks 20% tariff hike
  • ICTSI’s domestic port, Manila North Harbour (MNHPI) has applied for a 20.32% increase in cargo-handling tariff, passenger terminal fee, and porterage fees
    • It also proposed 13 new tariff items, including reefer charges and admin fees
    • Stakeholders have until September 1 to submit their position papers on the proposal
  • MNHPI’s last tariff increase of 10% was implemented in 2022
  • Our forecasts already bake in a 20% tariff hike that will be implemented in two tranches, beginning 2026
  • We estimate MNHPI accounts for 9% of consolidated volumes, 4% of revenues, and 5% of EBITDA
    • ICTSI owns 50% of MNHPI
MACRO
Philippines may face more tariffs after Trumps' threats on countries with digital taxes
  • In a social media post, US President Donald Trump has threatened countries that have digital services taxes with "subsequent additional tariffs" and export restrictions for advanced technology and semiconductors if corresponding legislations are not removed
    • The US tariff on Philippine goods was pegged at 19%
  • Since June 2025, The Philippines under Republic Act (RA) No. 12023 has imposed a 12% Value-Added Tax (VAT) on nonresident digital service providers (i.e., Netflix, Amazon, and Google)
    • The Dept. of Finance (DOF) previously projected around P7.3bn in revenues for 2025 at 50% compliance, while from 2025–2029, the measure is expected to generate roughly P102bn
  • Eight of the ten ASEAN member countries have implemented digital VAT tax rates to varying degrees, ranging from 7-12%, with Philippines being the highest
MACRO
Dept. of Agriculture pushes for return of rice-importing powers to the gov't
  • The Dept. of Agriculture (DA) has proposed to legislators for Food Terminal, Inc. (FTI) and Planters Products, Inc. to be given the power to import rice on behalf of the gov't
    • This follows efforts to amend the Rice Tariffication Law which removed the National Food Authority's (NFA) ability to import rice solely giving the role to private traders
  • DA Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. stated that "Rice is a commodity imbued with too much public interest to leave entirely to the private sector."
    • Laurel proposed that private importers should cooperate with the gov't in maintaining the country's rice buffer stock, which will ensure more stable supply and reduce gov't costs in maintaining the rice reserve
  • He highlighted that the proposed changes would seek to balance ensuring affordable rice for consumers while protecting the livelihoods of Filipino rice farmers
  • According to the Dept. of Agriculture (as of Aug 23), prices for local and imported well-milled rice range from P38-52/kg and P40-50/kg, respectively vs. P42-52/kg and P40-54/kg back in Jan 2

 

 

BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
GCash has disbursed P287bn in loans as of end-June since launching its lending arm
  • GCash has disbursed P287bn loans to 9.5mn borrowers as of end-June since launching its lending arm
    • These were disbursed through the company's lending arm, Fuse, through products such as GLoan, GCredit, and GGives
  • Fuse Lending had loans outstanding of P38.9bn gross, P34.7bn net, as of end-2024, almost 3x (+182% YoY) from the P13.8bn gross, and (+190%) from the P11.9bn net levels in 2023
  • G-Xchange Inc. President and CEO Oscar Enrico Reyes Jr. stated that they have improved banking penetration to 65% from 29% and also increased access to formal credit to 8% from 2%
  • Globe (GLO PM) has a 34% interest in Mynt, which owns the operator of GCash, G-Xchange, Inc.
    • Globe’s interest was diluted slightly by the entry of MUFG as a shareholder
    • Globe’s share in Mynt’s profit climbed 78% to P3.8bn, comprising 35% of 1H25 profit
