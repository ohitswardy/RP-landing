REGIS REPORT

25 November 2025

SM Prime (SMPH PM) is pushing through with the launch of its luxury residential project in Susana Heights under its Signature Series and pricing details are emerging. Lots in the development, which is just a bit further south of Alabang and just near the southern edge of what’s still Metro Manila, will start at P100m (US$1.6m). With no parcels below 750sqm, the implied price per sqm would be around at least P130k, which would be ~40% lower than Ayala Alabang and more than 2x the price in Nuvali further south.

 

+++

 

Passenger volumes at Cebu Air (CEB PM) dipped 1% in Oct, taking the YT-Oct passenger volume growth to +12%. Overall seat load factor slid to 79% for the month.

 

+++

 

Meralco’s (MER PM) planned P26-30bn distribution utility (DU) capex for 2026 is a bit higher than the P25bn expected for this year. Because the rate-setting mechanism for DUs is broadly based on a return on asset formula, MER’s capex would be a key input in rate determination.

 

+++

 

Another reduction in policy remains in the cards, as Bangko Sentral (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona mentioned for the nth time. The most important data point ahead of the BSP Monetary Board meeting in mid-Dec will be the CPI inflation print for Nov (due next week).

PROPERTY
SM Prime to launch luxury residential project
  • Despite concerns regarding the flood control corruption scandal, SM Prime said that it would still launch its luxury residential project in Susana Heights
  • It will be the maiden project under the luxury line, The Signature Series
  • Lots will start at P100mn with no parcel smaller than 750sqm
    • This implies a price of c. P133,000/sqm
  • The company will spend P25bn to develop the initial phase of the 284-hectare property
  • SM Prime’s 9M25 reservation sales fell 23% to P40.7bn
  • SMPH is trading at 50% discount to NAV, 1.3X FY26E P/B, and 12.9X FY26E P/E
TRANSPORT
Cebu Air pax volumes, load factors dip in October
  • Cebu Air reported its pax volumes dipped by 1% YoY to 2.1mn in October
    • Seat load factors also declined 1.2ppts YoY to 2.1mn
    • Management cited ongoing capacity challenges related to Pratt & Whitney, as well as weather-related disruptions
    • It also said that moderating capacity growth during the lean season was intended to ensure operational resilience ahead of the peak travel season (Dec/Jan)
  • Brings YT-October pax volumes up 12% to 22mn, lagging our full-year forecast of 28.5mn (+16%)
    • During its 3Q briefing, management indicated that stronger fares would help offset softer volumes and hinted at the potential resumption of common-share dividend payments by March/April

 

 

 

UTILITIES
Meralco hikes distribution unit capex to P26-30bn in 2026
  • Meralco is set to spend P26-30bn for its distribution business next year
    • According to Meralco executive VP and COO Ronnie Aperocho in an ambush interview
    • Meralco plans to spend P25bn for the distribution segment in 2025 and as of 9M25, it has spent P19.8bn
  • Around P8bn of the 2026 capex program relates to the roll-out of advanced metering infrastructure
MACRO
BSP considering 25bp cut in Dec; RRR cut not urgent
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona stated that the central bank will consider another 25bp rate cut in Dec given that "demand is affected, just like investor sentiment"
    • He noted that a jumbo 50bp cut is unlikely
  • Remolona also elaborated that a reserve requirement ratio (RRR) cut is "on the table… but there's no urgency in adjusting it"
    • The RRR is currently at 5.0%
    • The ratio was 9.5% at the start of Remolona's term in 2023
  • On forex intervention, the BSP Governor stated that the central bank lets the market determine the exchange rate as it does not have a specific target peso level for it to intervene
    • Intervention was guided to only be done for "large swings in the peso that could lead to inflation"
    • The peso depreciated from P58.15/USD on Oct 1 to P58.98/USD as of Nov 24
  • The last Monetary Board meeting for the year will be held on Dec 11
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect 125bp total worth of cuts to 4.50%
    • For 2026, consensus expects a 25bp cut to 4.25%, while Regis expects 50bp cuts total to 4.00%
