REGIS REPORT

07 July 2025

More legislators are lining up to propose laws banning or restricting online gambling. Senators Alan and Pia Cayetano are the latest to join the growing list. We believe it’s very likely now that some restrictions, if not an outright ban, will be put in place against online gambling. In fact, we wouldn’t be surprised if the Bangko Sentral (BSP) will be the first to move by prohibiting e-wallets from having a direct link on their apps to online gambling platforms.

 

Meanwhile, Digiplus (PLUS PM), the market leader in the online gambling market, announced a P6bn (US$106m) share buyback program in response to its plunging share price. The price of PLUS has halved in only a few days after media reports of proposed bans or restrictions on online gambling.

 

+++

 

The consortium led by Aboitiz Power (AP PM) submitted the highest bid in the auction for the CBK hydro assets. The consortium, which includes AP, Sumitomo Corp and J-Power, offered P36bn (US$640m) for the 797MW hydropower complex in southern Luzon, implying EV/MW of US$800k. The bid is 85% higher than the bid of the consortium led by First Gen (FGEN PM).

 

+++

 

Dito Telecommunity (Dito Tel), a subsidiary of Dito CME (DITO PM), says it expects revenue to top P20bn this year. This would imply growth of over 20% and market share of ~9% (vs 7% in FY20). We estimate that Dito Tel would have to capture at least 20% market share in order to break even (after interest payments).

 

+++

 

The low inflation number for June (1.4%) was, as expected, led by the continuing softness in food prices, specifically rice prices. The drop in food/rice prices and thus headline inflation has been the major factor in allowing the BSP to push through with its monetary easing. Another benign inflation print next month could coax the BSP to cut rates again at its next Monetary Board (MB) meeting on Aug 28.

GAMING
More senators file bills banning online gambling
  • Senators Pia and Alan Peter Cayetano filed a bill that seeks to ban online gambling
  • Operators of online gambling platforms would face fines of at least P10mn and imprisonment of 10 years while users may be fined up to P100,000 and spend up to six months in prison
  • Senator Juan Miguel Zubiri also filed a bill that would ban online gambling
  • Earlier last week, Senator Joel Villanueva filed his own bill seeking to ban online gambling while Senator Sherwin Gatchalian filed a bill that would place restrictions on online gambling
  • Among the restrictions Senator Gatchalian desired was to raise the minimum cash-in to P10,000
    • Some gaming platforms currently have a minimum cash-in of P25
GAMING
DigiPlus announces share buyback program
  • The Board of Directors of DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) approved a P6bn share buyback program
  • This is equivalent to 4.6% of current market cap
  • PLUS is currently trading at 7.4X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)  
POWER
Aboitiz Power-led consortium bags 797MW CBK hydro plant complex
  • Thunder consortium submitted the highest bid of P36.27bn for the 797MW Caliraya-Botocan-Kalayaan (CBK) hydro plant complex
    • Thunder consortium’s offer was 85% higher than the P19.6bn offer of the consortium of First Gen (FGEN PM) and Korea Water Resources Corp. (FGKW Consortium)
    • Offer was also higher than the government’s transaction adviser, Asian Development Bank’s, upper estimate of US$600mn (~P33.8bn)
    • Thunder Consortium is composed of Aboitiz Power’s wholly-owned unit, Aboitiz Renewables, Inc., Sumitomo Corp., and Electric Power Development Co.
  • Offer implies an EV/MW valuation of ~US$800k
    • Offer is below FGEN’s valuation of >US$3mn per MW for the 165MW Casecnan hydro plant last year
    • CBK’s power supply contract will expire by Feb 2026
  • State-run Power Sector Assets and Liabilities Management Corp. (PSALM) plans to give the official notice of award by mid-July
    • The complex consists of the 39.37-MW Caliraya HEPP in Lumban; the 22.91-MW Botocan HEPP in Majayjay, and the 366-MW Kalayaan I and 368.36-MW Kalayaan II pumped-storage power plants
TELCOS
DITO expects 2025 revenue to be over P20bn
  • DITO Telecommunity Corp. expects to generate more than P20bn in revenue this year, up 22% from P16.35bn in 2024
  • DITO President and CEO Eric Alberto stated that the higher revenue growth expectations will be driven by the continued growth of data
  • The P10-15bn capex allocated for this year (vs. P20bn last year), will be mostly allocated to expand its 5G network
  • As of 1Q25, gross revenues hit P4.69bn (+24% YoY)
MACRO
Inflation rate inches up to 1.4% in June
  • Headline inflation inched up slightly to 1.4% in June from 1.3% in the previous month of May
    • This is in line with the consensus forecast of 1.5% and stands well within the BSP's forecast range of 1.1-1.9%
    • Inflation has fallen from 2.9% in Jan and has been hovering below 1.5% for the past three months
  • Primary drivers for the continued benign inflation were YoY price declines in rice (-14.3%) and transport (-1.6%), which offset rising prices of meat (+9.1%) and housing utilities (+3.2%)
    • Notably, rice prices are on their twelfth consecutive MoM price decline since Jul 2024
      • According to the Dept. of Agriculture (as of Jul 2), prices for local and imported well-milled rice range from P37-48/kg and P38-48/kg, respectively vs. P42-52/kg and P40-54/kg back in Jan 2
  • Core inflation was flat at 2.2% in Jun vs. May, core non-food inflation also remains unchanged relative to the previous month at 2.0%
  • This brings the YT-Jun headline inflation rate to 1.8%, below the lower end of the 2-4% BSP target for this year
    • Consensus expects 2025 inflation to be 2.1%
      • Regis expects the full year average to be 1.8%
  • The next Monetary Board meeting (4th out of 6 this year) will be on Aug 28
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expects 75bp total worth of cuts to 5.00%
