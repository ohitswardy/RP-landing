REGIS REPORT

10 February 2025

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) released data putting the country’s international reserves (“GIR”) as of end-Jan at US$103bn. Assuming there are no large revisions in the number, this would imply a chunky US$3bn MoM drop in the GIR.

 

We are unaware of any big maturities of government US$ debt last month so it’s probable there are other reasons for the drop. Pending a possible revision in the GIR figures in the coming weeks, we reckon a decline in the value of its foreign investments could be a reason.  At worst, it could mean the BSP expended a lot trying to prop up the PHP.

 

+++

 

Globe (GLO PM) results for 4Q/FY24 were, even at first glance, disappointing. FY24 core income rose 13% to P21.5bn, with 4Q core income down 34% QoQ to P3.9bn. For the quarter, mobile revenue was unusually soft (-1% QoQ) while contributions from Mynt plunged 74% QoQ.

 

Despite this, we are inclined to think that the main causes of the shortfall were temporary / one-off. Worse-than-usual disruptions from the 4Q storms and the asset clean-up at Mynt ahead of its target listing are items that are unlikely to recur this year. Check out our published comments:

+++

 

The Court of Appeals (CA) has reportedly ruled against Megaworld (MEG PM) with regard to the company deviating from original plans approved by the housing regulator. The CA ruled that MEG must install the requisite number of elevators as specified in its approved plan for a condominium project completed over two decades ago. More than any financial cost, the bigger impact from such an event would be reputational.

 

+++

 

The country’s sovereign wealth fund, Maharlika, signed an MOU to co-invest with the CP group of Thailand. Given the CP’s expertise in agribusiness, it would be good if the investments were made to boost the agriculture sector.

MACRO
PH Dollar reserves down 3.0% at end-January
  • The Philippines' gross international reserves (GIR) at end-Jan saw a 3.0% MoM drop to $103bn from $106.3bn at the end-Dec
    • Primarily reflecting the BSP's net foreign exchange operations and drawdowns used in payments for foreign debt obligations
    • The level of dollar reserves by end-Jan was enough to cover about 3.6x the country's short-term external debt and also equivalent to 7.3x months' worth of imports of goods and payments
  • Reserve position in the IMF increased 0.6% MoM to $671mn
  • Buffers in the form of gold were valued at $11.8bn, which jumped 6.8% MoM
  • Foreign investments declined by 3.7% MoM to $86.1bn
  • Foreign currency deposits dropped 46.3% MoM to $734mn
  • Note that these are preliminary figures from the BSP and can be subject to changes
  • The BSP expects the country's GIR to be at $110bn by end-2025

DIGITAL FINANCE/TELCOS
Eased public float requirement for Mynt IPO proposed
  • The chairman of Mynt, fintech platform of Globe Telecom (GLO PM), called for the PSE and SEC to ease the minimum public float requirement of 20% for its potential IPO
    • Ernest Cu also commented that their focus remains on a local listing and is not currently looking at other venues to list outside the PH
  • Finance Secretary Ralph Recto, who also oversees the SEC, commented that they are open to the request to lower public float but also states that it may not be needed
    • He cited that a feasible option would be for GCash to conduct follow-on offering in order to reach 20% float
  • The Mynt IPO is expected to take place in the 2H25 or 1H26
  • In separate news, management at Ayala Corp said that the company does not intend to sell down its stake in Mynt
    • AC has a 6.5% indirect interest in Mynt
PROPERTY
Court of Appeals orders Megaworld to add elevators to condominium building
  • According to abogado.com.ph, in a decision on 27 January 2025, The Court of Appeals (CA) found Megaworld (MEG PM) guilty of unsound real estate business practice because it only provided two parking elevators in Paseo Parkview Suites
    • The original plan approved by the Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board had four parking elevators
  • The CA ordered Megaworld to add operational elevators
  • The CA decision reversed a previous ruling that absolved Megaworld of liability
  • Should this ruling become widespread in the media, then Megaworld’s reputation might be damaged, making it more difficult to sell residential units
  • Paseo Parkview was completed in 2002
  • MEG is trading at 72% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 3.0X FY25E P/E
MACRO
Maharlika signs MOU with Thai-based CP Group for US$1bn multi-sectoral private equity fund
  • The Maharlika Investment Corp. (MIC) and Thailand-based Charoen Pokphand Group (CP Group) signed an MOU to establish a private equity fund with a target of up to US$1bn (see here)
    • Fund will invest in agriculture, food production, digital innovation, and renewable energy
    • Funding for an undisclosed initial capital is expected to close within nine to 12 months
