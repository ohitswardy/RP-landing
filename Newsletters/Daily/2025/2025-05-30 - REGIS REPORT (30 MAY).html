MACRO
GOCC officials also called to resign by Marcos
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. has extended the call for courtesy resignations to gov't-owned and -controlled corporations (GOCCs), more than a week after calling the resignation of his Cabinet secretaries
  • State-run firms' non ex-officio chairpersons, chief executive officers (CEOs), as well as all appointive directors/trustees/members of their respective GOCCs' governing boards were instructed to immediately submit their courtesy resignations to the President
  • Notable GOCCs include, among others, Social Security System (SSS), Government Service Insurance System (GSIS), Land Bank of the Philippines, Development Bank of the Philippines (DBP), Philippine Economic Zone Authority (PEZA), Philippine Reclamation Authority (PRA), Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation (PAGCOR), National Power Corporation (NPC), Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth),
  • Similar to the "bold reset" of Marcos' Cabinet, the President will have the final say on the performance review, the acceptance, reassignment, or replacement of GOCC heads
    • So far, Commission on Higher Education (CHEd) Chairman Prospero De Vera III has been replaced by the CHEd commissioner Shirley Agrupis
    • Darlene Berberabe will be the new solicitor general in place of Menardo Guevarra
    • Heads of PAGCOR, PRA, Landbank have submitted their courtesy resignations but no changes have been made regarding their positions as of late
CONGLOS
Supreme Court rejects challenge to LRT-1 concession
  • The Supreme Court denied a petition by Bagong Alyansang Makabayan, Train Riders Network, and others, contesting the validity of the Concession Agreement of Light Rail Manila Corp. (LRMC)
    • Petitioners claimed the periodic tariff adjustments provided in the concession agreement violate due process due to the absence of a procedure for giving notice and conducting hearings
    • Plaintiffs also challenged the government’s assumption of real property tax liabilities for rail project asset
  • The court found that the Concession Agreement does not violate due process rules of the Public Service Act and the Administrative Code of 1987, which require notice and hearings for rate-fixing
    • Concession agreement provides a mechanism for fare increase applications, statutory notice and hearing requirements
    • Court upheld the government’s assumption of real property taxes to entice private participation
  • Note that since taking over the concession in 2015, LRMC was only granted tariff adjustments in 2023 (+21%) and 2025 (+21%)
    • LRMC is entitled to fare hikes of 10.25% every two years, which is aligned with an annual 5% rate adjustment that was initially due in 2016
  • LRMC is the operator of LRT-1 and is owned by Metro Pacific (35.8%), Ayala Corp (35%), Sumitomo (19.2%), and Macquarie Infra (10%)
CONGLOS/PROPERTY
DMCI Homes says take up of condotel project in Baguio at 84% since May 2024 launch
  • DMCI Homes said 84% of ~700 condotel units of the first building (“Blanca”) of the Moncello Crest condotel project in Tuba, Benguet has been reserved
    • Project was launched in May 2024
    • This translates to P4.9bn in reservation sales
    • Project will be turned over by November 2028 (Blanca) and August 2029 (Silva)
  • We estimate DMCI’s unsold inventory levels are at P85bn, equivalent to almost three (3) years’ worth of sales
  • DMCI Homes is a wholly-owned unit of DMCI (DMC PM) and accounts for 28% of profit and 19% of NAV
    • DMCI is trading at an 18% discount to its market-based NAV of P13/sh

PAL’s new A350s set to arrive in latter part of 2025
