REGIS REPORT

26 November 2025

Ayala Land (ALI PM) was reported to have sold 100m shares of AREIT (AREIT PM) overnight, at a discount to yesterday’s close. It would mean ALI raised ~P4.2bn and reduced its stake in AREIT to an estimated 58.5% (from 61.2%). We believe the shares would have been sold at just under 6.0% yield.

 

Following this divestment, we reckon ALI would still need to sell ~50m AREIT shares in order for AREIT to comply with minimum public ownership rules. AREIT is set to complete the acquisition of more assets (two malls) from ALI in exchange for 441m new shares by next year, necessitating the reduction of ALI’s stake in AREIT.

 

+++

 

ICTSI’s (ICT PM) port in Melbourne, Australia is raising landside charges by 10% effective January 2026. This is not unexpected and is already embedded in our forecasts. ICTSI’s port in Australia accounts for 7% of its EBITDA.

 

+++

 

The semiconductor industry has “re-revised” its outlook for the industry upwards and is now predicting 5-7% growth. The industry association previously predicted zero growth because of the Trump tariff threat. The semiconductor sector is among the larger exporting industries in the country.

 

+++

 

As has been traditionally the case, the Finance Secretary, Frederick Go, will soon take a seat at the seven-member Monetary Board. Ralph Recto, the former Finance Secretary who was just appointed as Executive Secretary will stay on for one more Monetary Board meeting (in Dec) where there will likely be a push for another policy rate cut.

PROPERTY
Ayala Land sells AREIT shares
  • According to Bloomberg, Ayala Land sold 100mn AREIT shares at P41.50/share-P42.35/share
    • This is 3%-5% lower than the previous closing price (of P43.65/share)
    • At those prices, we estimate that AREIT would offer FY26E dividend yield of 5.9%-6.0%
    • Total of c. P4.2bn
  • We estimate that AREIT’s public ownership will rise from 33% to 36%
  • We estimate that the Ayala Group would need to sell another c. 50mn AREIT shares (over the next 12 months) in order for AREIT to meet minimum public ownership requirements
    • At the last closing price, this would be equivalent to c. P2.1bn
  • AREIT will acquire P19.5bn worth of assets from Ayala Land in exchange for 441mn new AREIT shares
    • This will increase AREIT’s AUM by 15% to P158bn
    • AREIT’s shares outstanding will increase by 12% to 4.16bn shares
  • This implies that the AREIT shares will be priced at P44.15/share
    • This is 1% higher than the last closing price of P43.65/share
  • AREIT will acquire two malls with a combined GLA of 375,000sqm
    • Ayala Center Cebu and Ayala Malls Feliz
  • The companies aim to complete the transaction in 2H26
  • ALI is trading at 61% discount to NAV, 0.9X FY26E P/B, and 9.9X FY26E P/E
  • AREIT currently offers 5.7% FY26E dividend yield
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 5.9%
TRANSPORT
ICTSI’s port in Melbourne raises landside port charges; domestic port in Manila adds new quay cranes
  • ICTSI is raising landside port charges (including storage) at its port in Melbourne, Australia (“Victoria International Container Terminal” or VICT) by ~10%
    • New charges will take effect by January 1, 2026
    • In 2026, VICT will embark on Phase 3B expansion (capacity increase of over 20%) with an investment of >AUD$100mn
  • Meanwhile, its domestic port, Northport, has acquired two new harbor cranes as part of its capacity expansion
    • Recall that Northport has applied for a 20% tariff increase with the Philippine Ports Authority in August
  • We estimate VICT and Northport account for 7% and 5%, respectively, of EBITDA
    • Tariff hikes and capacity expansions are baked into our estimates and valuation
  • ICTSI trades at 10x EV/EBITDA, below peer average of 12x and historical average of 11x
MACRO
Semiconductor exports expected to grow 5-7% this year
  • The Semiconductor and Electronics Industries in the Philippines Foundation Inc. (SEIPI) expects electronic exports to grow 5-7% this year
  • The 5-7% growth rate was revised from flat growth as the threat of US semiconductor tariffs has subsided
    • Reuters reported that Trump's semiconductor tariff plan was likely delayed, with no set date for implementation
    • SEIPI President Danilo Lachica stated that semiconductors are still exempt from tariffs under the US Trade Expansion Act of 1962
  • As of 9M25, semiconductor exports amounted to US$25.1bn (+8% YoY)
  • Semiconductor exports declined 8% and 6% in 2023 and 2024, respectively
    • In 2024, total PH exports to the US were $12.1bn
      • Semiconductors and electronics make up around 53% of Philippine exports to the US
        • Most locally made semiconductors are manufactured by US companies and exported
MACRO
Recto to resign from Monetary Board by end-Dec; Go named as successor
  • Executive Secretary Ralph Recto indicated that he will step down from the Monetary Board (MB) by end-Dec and will recommend Finance Secretary Frederick Go as his replacement
  • The Secretary of Finance is traditionally the Cabinet member sitting as representative of the administration on the MB
    • To ensure a smooth transition, Recto said he will participate in the final MB meeting this year, which will take place on Dec 11
