REGIS REPORT

12 September 2025

President Marcos signed an Executive Order (EO) creating an independent commission to investigate the massive corruption scandal involving infrastructure funds. The independent commission will have three members and will be tasked to investigate the corruption allegations over the last ten years.

 

How far the commission can go and how credible it can be will depend much on who will take on the job/s. There will be constraints on the powers of the commission, however. It’s clear the commission will have to investigate members of Congress. And Congress is, on paper, co-equal to the Executive branch and will have powers to block some actions.

 

The fact that the commission is being tasked to look through transactions over the past ten years also opens up the likelihood that offences committed during the last administration will be dug up.

Ayala Land (ALI PM) said that it has presold P8bn worth of units of Laurean, its latest high-end Makati project. In 2Q25, Laurean accounted for 17% (P6bn) of Ayala Land’s residential reservation sales (of P34.5bn). With only P2bn coming from Laurean in 3Q (so far), it is looking like the company could report weaker reservation sales for the quarter.

 

+++

 

Home Credit Philippines (HCPH) – 25% owned by Security Bank (SECB PM) – says it aims to grow its loan book to P100bn by end-2025, up around 35% from the P74bn (net) in 2024. As of 1H25, the bank had loans of P85bn. HCPH specializes in loans for household appliances and mobile phones, through its 18,000 partner stores. In 2024, the company booked NPAT of P3.2bn, for an ROE of around 18%. SECB completed its 25% purchase of HCPH earlier this year for P10.4bn, or around 2.1x HCPH’s end-2024 book value. SECB management claims the acquisition will be accretive within the first year.

 

 

 

+++

 

The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) approved the original power rates submitted by ACEN (ACEN PM) and San Miguel (SMC PM), which were the best bids in the auction held last year to supply power to Meralco (MER PM). The ERC previously approved a provisional tariff of P5.19/kwh, much lower than the P8/kwh and P7/kwh bids by ACEN and SMC, respectively.

 

The approval does not come as a surprise. What’s surprising to us is the relative speed with which the case/s were decided.

POLITICS
Marcos forms independent probe commission to investigate corruption in public works
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. has implemented Executive Order (EO) No. 94 which establishes a three-member Independent Commission for Infrastructure (ICI), they are tasked with investigating "anomalies, irregularities, and misuse of funds" concerning gov't flood control and other infrastructure projects nationwide
    • The EO states that the investigation will "prioritize" flood control and other infrastructure projects "within the last 10 years"
    • Marcos stated that the House and Senate can continue its inquiries as the ICI investigation will not impede with the ongoing investigations by the legislative branch
  • Some of the key functions and powers of the ICI mentioned in the EO are as follows:
    • The ICI can act on its own initiative, and on information against all gov't officials and employees as well as private individuals, who may be involved
      • Criminal, civil, and administrative charges can be held against those found liable for violating various laws
    • All departments, bureaus, agencies, and offices in the executive branch are ordered to assist the ICI
    • The commission also has the power to obtain documents such as contracts and bank statements from the BSP and BIR (Bureau of Internal Revenue)
    • The ICI will continue its work until it has been accomplished or it is dissolved by the president
  • A number of high profile personalities have reportedly been tapped to be part of the ICI, including former Senior Associate Justices Antonio Carpio and Estela Perla-Bernabe, and Baguio Mayor Benjamin Magalong

BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
Home Credit expects loan portfolio to hit P100bn this year
  • Home Credit Philippines (HCPH) Chief Marketing Officer Sheila Paul stated that they hope to hit P100bn (+35% YoY) in loan receivables within the year—driven by a growing customer base, expanding partner stores, manageable NPLs, and a boost from the Christmas season
    • Loan receivables as of 6M25 amounted to P85bn, well above end-2024's P74bn
      • Smartphone financing is projected to reach P20bn this year, double from last year's P10bn
        • Other top-selling products are televisions, air conditioners, refrigerators, e-bikes, motorcycles, cellphone insurance
    • The company had 12mn customers as of July, already exceeding the 11mn from October last year
    • Home Credit has 18,000 partner stores alongside 10,000 sales associate network which is expected to grow further
  • Home Credit's NPL ratio remains below 10%, with Ms Paul noting that "… as long as we're able to manage it within that threshold, I think we are fine"
  • Security Bank (SECB PM) has a 25% stake in Home Credit Philippines
POWER
ERC approves higher PSA rates for San Miguel, ACEN
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) has approved the higher tariffs offered by San Miguel (SMC PM) and ACEN (ACEN PM) to Meralco (MER PM)
    • SMC’s San Roque Hydropower offered a tariff of P7.10/kwh for 340 MW
    • ACEN’s Gigasol 3 unit offered P8.18/kwh for 139MW
    • Prices are higher than the provisional tariff of P5.1908/kwh, which was given last December
  • Power supply agreement (PSA) covered 500MW 10-year mid-merit supply (350MW by Feb 2025 and 150 MW by Feb 2026)
  • The approved final tariffs are comparable to Terra Soar’s tariff of P7.87/kwh
MACRO
Semiconductor exports guided to see moderate growth
  • Semiconductor and Electronics Industries in the Philippines Foundation Inc. (SEIPI) President Danilo Lachica stated that they maintain their earlier projection of relatively flat to modest export growth despite the possible imposition of tariffs on semiconductor exports to the US
    • Semiconductor exports declined 8% and 6% in 2023 and 2024, respectively
  • Lachica stated that operations were "business as usual" among semiconductor companies as they are still exempt from tariffs under the US Trade Expansion Act of 1962
    • He further elaborated that "Even with the tariff, if and when it happens, the demand will still be there, …you can’t replace that [technology] overnight"
  • In 2024, total PH exports to the US was $12.1bn
    • Semiconductors and electronics make up around 53% of Philippine exports to the US
      • Most of the semiconductors are manufactured by US companies in the Philippines and exported
