REGIS REPORT

16 January 2025

The parent company of casino operator Okada Manila disclosed its results, and the numbers are looking quite bad for the gaming firm. Gross gaming revenue for the last quarter was P5.9bn, down 15% QoQ and 34% YoY. GGR for the year was the lowest since 2021, when the country (and the world) was in the middle of the pandemic.

                                                                                                               

Okada Manila’s numbers don’t bode well for the other operators including Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) and Travelers, the gaming arm of Alliance Global (AGI PM). It’s probable that the eruption of the corruption scandal may have played a part in the drop.

 

+++

 

Jollibee (JFC PM) issued another press release essentially saying 4Q results for the group are looking solid and consistent with guidance. This comes a month after statements which amounted to broadly the same thing. Based on 9M25 results, the FY25 guidance was already looking very likely.

 

+++

 

In the hopes of improving tourism flows, the government announced that Chinese nationals will be allowed visa-free entry into the Philippines beginning today (Jan 16). Chinese tourists last year were literally just a fraction (21%) of what they were pre-pandemic (2019) and have been low for years (though one might argue that a lot of the “tourists” then were POGO workers). Malaysia seems to have gotten a bump on Chinese tourism recently at the expense of Thailand. The Philippine government appears to be trying to get in on the action.

 

+++

 

Remittances for Nov last amounted to US$2.91bn, which is 3.6% higher YoY. YT-Nov, remittances are US$32.1bn, 3.2% higher compared to the period in 2024.

 

+++

 

Cebu Air (CEB PM) reported that passenger volumes for Dec were up +5%, an improvement from the declines in Oct and Nov. The implied volumes for 4Q are down 2% YoY. For FY25, volumes were up a respectable +9.5%.

CONSUMER
JFC reports continued solid operating momentum since Dec update
  • JFC stated in a press release that recent business conditions have been consistent with the "solid operating momentum" guided in its previous guidance disclosure back in December
    • The company stated that since its last Dec update, results are still consistent with FY25 guidance:
      • 8-12% SWS growth
      • 4-6% SSSG
      • 4-8% Store network growth
  • Customer demand was noted to be stable across key markets given steady domestic performance and resilient international operations
    • Domestically, Jollibee's Champion Brands (Jollibee, Chowking, and Mang Inasal) were said to continue to significantly contribute to domestic SWS and SSSG with overall improving demand in-line with 4Q seasonality
    • In the int'l space, Jollibee Vietnam was said to sustain its high double-digit SWS and SSS growth alongside positive performance from the Tim Ho Wan brand
      • Jollibee Vietnam as of 9M25 posted SWS and SSS growth of 37% and 21%, respectively
  • The company's asset light growth strategy was said to progress well as network expansion continues given franchise-led expansion
  • JFC emphasized that the company remains confident in its growth trajectory and reiterated its aspiration to triple NIAT to P24bn and reach 20% ROIC by 2028
  • We note that there was no mention of or update on Smashburger and the China brands (Yonghe King, Hong Zhuang Yuan) which are the primary concerns of the int'l business
    • Improvements in terms of operations and profitability of these brands remain key to the overall profitability of the int'l business and management's triple NIAT target to P24bn by 2028
MACRO
Chinese nationals allowed visa-free entry to PH starting Jan 16
  • The Dept. of Foreign Affairs (DFA) announced that Chinese nationals will be allowed to enter the Philippines with no visa requirement for a stay of up to 14 days for tourism or business purposes
    • The visa-free 14-day stay is non-extendable and non-convertible to any other type of PH visa category
    • Requirements for Chinese nationals are a passport valid for at least six months, confirmed accommodation/booking, and a return ticket to next country of destination
  • The visa-free privilege is only valid through Ninoy Aquino International Airport (NAIA) in Metro Manila and Mactan-Cebu International Airport (MCIA) in Cebu
  • The visa-free entry will be in effect for one year and will be reviewed prior the expiry of the arrangement
  • As of 11M25, Chinese tourists account for 5% of total tourist arrivals, much lower than the 21% contribution to total back in 2019

Remittances rise by 3.6% in November

TRANSPORT
Cebu Air’s pax volumes up 5% YoY in December; FY25 pax volumes miss, rising only 10%
  • Cebu Air reported it flew a record 2.7mn passengers in December, up 5% YoY and reversing declines in Oct/Nov
    • However, systemwide seat load factor (SLF) slipped 140bps YoY to 83.8% due to soft international demand (International SLF fell 520bps to 79.4%)
  • This brings 4Q25 pax volumes down 2% YoY to 6.9mn; FY25 volumes up 9.5% YoY to 26.9mn, well below our expectations of 28.5mn (+16%)
    • Seat capacity grew 10% YoY in FY25, below management’s downgraded seat growth guidance of 11-13%
    • We attribute the volume shortfall to the lingering global aircraft/engine supply issues
  • CEB trades at 4.5x FY26E EV/EBITDA, below peer average of 7x
