REGIS REPORT

20 November 2025

A draft on the proposed revisions for REITs has been released by the SEC. Arguably the most significant proposal is the expansion of what can be call “income-generating real estate” to cover power, telecoms and other infrastructure assets. This expansion would allow, in theory, telcos like PLDT (TEL PM), Globe (GLO PM) and Converge (CNVRG PM) to spin off and list some of their assets (e.g. fiber backbone, data centers, telco towers) though one can argue this can already be done even today. Power firms like Meralco (MER PM), Aboitiz Power (AP PM), ACEN (ACEN PM) and First Gen (FGEN PM), can also do the same, especially with assets that have long-term supply agreements (or government-guaranteed feed-in-tariffs).

 

REITs as a subset of equities have outperformed in recent periods as investors focus on apparent dividend yield.

PROPERTY
SEC issues draft revisions on REIT regulations
  • The SEC issued a draft memorandum circular proposing revisions to REIT regulations
    • The draft circular is open for public comments until December 3
  • Among the proposals is to broaden the definition of “income-generating real estate”
    • The SEC proposes to allow power, infrastructure, and telecom assets to qualify as REIT-able assets  
  • Reinvestment of REIT proceeds must be completed within two years (instead of one year)
  • The proposed amendments would also require detailed disclosures on leases, related-party transactions, and fees
  • We do not expect the proposed revisions to significantly affect the existing REITs
BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
InstaPay surges +66% in Oct, leads digital payments +41% in Oct

Digital payments continue to power away, up 41.4% YoY in October to P2,291bn (roughly $39bn). Year-to-date, that’s P19,749bn, also up 41% YoY. That’s an acceleration over the 35% posted in FY2024 Again, remember that these only count transactions between platforms, and do NOT include payments done on the same system (eg, GCash to GCash, BDO to BDO, etc).

 

Transactions over InstaPay – real-time, up to P50,000 – surged to P1,123bn, up 66.1% YoY, the fastest monthly YoY growth since late 2021. The October figure is also up an impressive 10% MoM over September. For 10M25, InstaPay payments were up 55.8% YoY to P9,069bn. The 10M growth is much faster than the already-quick 46% pace set in FY24. More notable, though, is that the volume of InstaPay transactions continued to explode, up 327% YoY in October, bringing the average ticket size down to P1,983 (under $34), crossing below P2,000 for the first time, and further reinforcing the broadening of use cases for the function.

 

For larger, batched payments over PesoNet, October came in at P1,167bn, up 23.7% YoY, slowing from the 36% pace set in September. For the first ten months, PesoNet transactions totaled P10,680bn, up 30.7% YoY, a shade faster than the 28.5% growth in FY24.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

MACRO
Customs collections moderate in October
  • The Bureau of Customs (BOC) stated that collections in Oct were only up ~2% YoY, a slowdown from the 8% growth in Sep
    • Collections were said to have been hampered by a 3% drop in import volumes and fewer working days due to poor weather
      • Consumption-driven imports (which usually accounts for a large share of dutiable goods) fell sharply by 20%
  • Despite the challenging month, Customs Commissioner Ariel Nepomuceno stated that they are retaining their P959bn (+4.5% YoY) revised revenue goal for 2025
    • The BOC's initial target set for this year was P1.06trn (+16% YoY)
  • Customs also seeks to fully digitalize its operations through PPP in an effort to mitigate corruption and streamline collections

 

 

GAMING
DigiPlus plans soft launch in South Africa in early 2027
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) plans to soft launch its operations in South Africa in early 2027
  • It hopes to secure a gaming license in South Africa within eight months
    • The draft circular is open for public comments until December 3
  • Sports betting gross gaming revenue (GGR) in South Africa reached approximately US$1.9bn in 2024
    • Online sports betting contributed about US$1.6bn
    • In comparison, the Philippines’ electronic games GGR (online casino + online sports betting) totaled US$2.3bn (P136bn) in 2024
  • South Africa currently does not allow online casino games but there is Remote Gambling Bill in South Africa’s parliament which could expand the market by legalizing additional formats including online casino gaming
