REGIS REPORT

10 October 2025

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) surprised the market with a 25bp rate cut, trimming the policy rate to 4.75% yesterday. This comes not long after the BSP Governor said that rates were already in the “sweet spot” -- a statement which dampened hopes for another rate reduction.

 

This time around, Remolona said that the sweet spot has moved closer to 4% (from 5%) in view of a “favorable inflation outlook” and “moderating domestic demand.” The BSP highlighted the impact of the corruption scandal currently engulfing the government. We also highlight the BSP reduced inflation estimates for 2026 to 3.1% (from 3.3%) and 2027 to 2.8% (from 3.4%). The reduced inflation forecast should be a leading indicator of the BSP’s leanings on the direction of monetary policy.

 

There will be one last Monetary Board policy setting meeting before year-end (Dec 11) and Remolona said a rate cut then was a “possibility.”

 

+++

 

Meanwhile, the BSP is also considering curbs on digital fund transfers as a complement to limits on cash withdrawals from banks.  The BSP recently imposed a limit of P500,000 (<US$9,000) on withdrawals of cash as a measure against suspicious transactions.

 

+++

 

ICTSI’s (ICT PM) Mexico port recorded a 23% YoY increase in September volumes to 148k TEUs, the strongest growth rate and highest throughput level in nearly a year, underpinned by the completion of a new berth. Even though cumulative 9M25 throughput of 1.14mn TEUs (+7%) trail our full-year expectations on customs-related disruptions in mid-year, we remain positive on the port’s medium-term outlook given the full completion of its expansion by year-end. 

 

 

+++

On the online gaming side, Digiplus (PLUS PM) signed up to be merchant with Bayad, which would allow Bayad to receive cash-in payments into Digiplus’ online gambling platforms. We don’t think this will move the needle, though.

 

Also, there’s a disclosure that Gregorio Araneta is selling his controlling 57% stake in Philweb (WEB PM) to parties who include WEB’s current management. We believe WEB is likely being transformed to focus on online gambling similar to PLUS.

MACRO
BSP cuts rates by 25bp to 4.75%; sees lower inflation and moderating demand
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) cut the key policy rate by 25bp to 4.75%, the lowest since Sep 2022
    • This is the fourth consecutive 25bp cut this year (100bp total) since previously cutting rates in Apr, Jun, and Aug
    • The BSP has lowered rates by a total of 175bp since the start of the easing cycle in Aug 2024
  • The BSP stated that the outlook on GDP growth has softened in the near term, highlighting dampened business sentiment from "governance concerns" on public infra spending and moderating demand due to external uncertainties
    • The BSP kept its GDP growth forecast for 2025 and 2026 at 5.5% and 6.0%, respectively, but the central bank highlighted that there is a greater possibility for a miss
      • These are at the lower end of the gov't's 5.5-6.5% and 6-7% growth targets for the same periods
    • 3Q25 GDP data will be released on Nov 7
  • BSP Governor Eli Remolona noted that the "sweet spot" has moved closer to 4% now vs the initial assessment of 5% especially with the favorable inflation outlook and moderating domestic demand
    • Another cut this year was said to be a possibility as "the Monetary Board sees scope for a more accommodative monetary policy stance"
  • The inflation outlook was noted to be benign and well within the target range with expectations well-anchored
    • The BSP still expects inflation to settle at 1.7% for 2025 but revised its inflation forecasts for 2026 and 2027 to 3.1% and 2.8% from 3.3% and 3.4%, respectively
    • Primary risks were said to be potential electricity rate adjustments and increased rice tariffs
    • Remolona also guided that peso defense will be done if depreciation is sharp and large enough to be inflationary
  • The last Monetary Board meeting for the year will be held on Dec 11
  • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect 100bp total worth of cuts to 4.75% and inflation to average 1.8%
    • For 2026, consensus and Regis expect one 25bp cut to 4.5% and inflation to settle at 2.8% and 3.0%, respectively

 

 

BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
BSP eyes tighter rules on digital/cash transfers to curb fund misuse, protect banks
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas is deliberating on new thresholds not just for large cash withdrawals but also for digital and other fund transfers in a continued effort to prevent the misuse of public funds and strengthen detection of suspicious transactions
    • For now, the proposed thresholds will likely apply to government transactions, especially transfers from the state to private individuals or contractors
  • BSP Governor Eli Remolona noted that banks were uneasy about releasing funds from gov't agencies that are suspicious but could not refuse since the money came from the Treasury
    • The central bank plans to clearly define when banks can refuse transactions suspected of corruption
    • Uniform rules are sought to be applied across institutions to prevent depositors from simply moving funds elsewhere
  • Recall that the BSP recently required financial institutions to cap large-value cash transactions at P500,000 per banking day unless the amount is processed through traceable channels
TRANSPORT
ICTSI’s Mexico port volumes jump 23% YoY in September
  • Data from Mexico’s port authority showed that container volumes at ICTSI’s port, Contecon Manzanillo (CMSA), stood at 148k TEUs, up 23% YoY and the fastest pace in 11 months
    • CMSA accounted for all the 9% YoY increase in total port volumes at Manzanillo for the month, as competitor volumes slipped 1% YoY
  • Brings 9M25 volumes at CMSA up 7% YoY to 1.14mn TEUs, lagging our full-year forecast of 1.63mn TEUs (+12% YoY)
    • To reach our forecast, volumes for the rest of the year will need to average 165k TEUs per month
    • Despite the shortfall, we remain upbeat on the port’s medium-term outlook, supported by the anticipated completion of its ~20% capacity expansion by year-end
    • CMSA’s capacity will increase to “over 2mn TEUs” (~170k TEUs/month) by year-end from 1.7mn TEUs (~140k per month)
    • We estimate CMSA accounts for 12% of volumes, 20% of revenues and 24% of EBITDA
  • ICTSI trades at 10x FY26E EV/EBITDA, below peer median of 11x, peer average of 12x and historical average of 11x

 

 

 

GAMING
DigiPlus partners with Bayad for OTC cash-in
  • DigiPlus signed an agreement with Bayad, a bill payment service unit of Meralco
  • Customers of DigiPlus can now make deposits (cash-in) to their accounts through Bayad Centers
    • There are over 800 Bayad Centers nationwide
  • Customers can also make deposits in BingoPlus stores
    • There are over 130 BingoPlus stores nationwide 
  • We expect only minor impact from the partnership with Bayad as we expect most customers to continue making deposits/cash-in through e-wallets
  • PLUS is trading at 6.5X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
GAMING
Gregorio Araneta sells out of PhilWeb
  • Gregorio Araneta III sold his 57% stake in PhilWeb (WEB PM) for P1.8bn
    • 829mn shares at P2.17/share, 47% below the previous closing price
  • The shares were purchased by Nexora Holdings and Velora Holdings
  • PhilWeb’s President, Mr. Brian Ng, is also Nexora’s Chairman and President
  • PhilWeb’s Vice Chairman, Mr. Crisanto Roy Alcid, is also Nexora’s Treasurer and Director
  • The foreign ownership of WEB could rise from 5% to 40%
    • Since PhilWeb does not own land, there are no foreign ownership restrictions
  • There will be a tender offer for PhilWeb shares
