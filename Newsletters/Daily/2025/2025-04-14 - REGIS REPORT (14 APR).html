Puregold (PGOLD PM) saw a substantial improvement in the fourth quarter, as revenue growth accelerated while margins expanded. 4Q24 revenue grew 12% YoY, with Puregold sales growing 11% and S&R revenue rising 15%. Puregold saw SSSG of 7% in the fourth quarter, while S&R had an even faster 12%.

 

Management also indicated that operating results remained healthy in 1Q25.  So far, SSSG for Puregold has been in the “5-7%” range while SSSG for S&R is “positive”, albeit lower than 2024.

On the other hand, Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) did not fare as well in the fourth quarter. 4Q24 net profit was flat YoY at P1.2bn while revenue growth slowed to 7% YoY, from 16% in 9M24.

 

4Q24 SSSG dropped to -2.3%, the first quarterly decline since the pandemic. The unusual level of disruption from the 4Q typhoons affected foot traffic and sales for the company more than others, as it is disproportionately geared towards office workers and students. The company also noted that system outages in 4Q from a key e-wallet partner negatively impacted foot traffic and likely exacerbated the dip in SSS.

 

Due to the weakness in the fourth quarter, SEVN’s FY24 profit rose 9% to P3.8bn, behind both REGIS and consensus forecasts.

CONSUMER
SEVN 4Q/FY24 Results

FY24 net profit P3.8bn (+9% YoY). The full year net profit figure is behind consensus and our estimates. For 4Q alone, profits were unchanged at P1.2bn (+1% YoY) and revenues were only up 7% YoY to P23.8bn, which is a significant slowdown from the 16% YoY growth seen in the 9M24. The earnings miss stems from the rather soft second half of the year, with SSS actually contracting in 4Q.

 

SSSG falters due to bad weather. 4Q24 SSSG dropped to -2.3%, the first such quarterly decline since the pandemic. The unusual level of disruption from the 4Q typhoons affected foot traffic and sales for the company more than others as it is disproportionately geared towards office workers and students. The company also noted that system outages in 4Q from a key e-wallet partner (which is probably GCash) negatively impacted foot traffic and likely exacerbated the dip in SSS.

 

Margins. Operating margins in the 4Q were up 1.9 ppts QoQ as supplier rebates usually kick-in during year-end, while YoY, operating margins were only up 30 bps as operating leverage was offset by higher utility and labor costs as well operational efficiency investments. Net margins for the FY were down 30 bps YoY due to a higher effective tax rate.

 

Store expansion. The total store network for 2024 stood at 4,130, up by 362 or 10% from the previous year. The company targets to open ~500 (+12%) stores this year with the focus areas being Visayas and Mindanao as 73% of its current stores are located in Luzon.

Jollibee comments that Trump tariff impact will be 'indirect'
  • Jollibee Foods Corp. (JFC PM) downplayed the impact of Trump's global tariffs on its business as the company stated that any effect would be 'indirect'
  • JFC stated that most of the raw materials for its US products were sourced from North America but did not specify which raw materials were sourced locally
  • Although the tariffs were put on a 90 day pause, the company continues to assess the potential impact of the reciprocal tariffs on the business
  • The company stated that they are prepared to adapt their sourcing strategies as needed to bolster supply chain stability and operational efficiency
  • JFC out of its 9,766 global store network has 369 stores total in North America which include the Jollibee, Red Ribbon, Chowking, and Smashburger brands
    • These four contribute 11.9% of JFC's SWS in 2024 which is down from 12.5% the previous year due to weakness in Smashburger
      • 6.5% from Jollibee, 4.0% from Smashburger, 0.9% from Red Ribbon, 0.5% from Chowking
  • For 2025, management guided for 8-12% SWS growth, 4-6% SSSG, 700-800 (+4-8%) new stores

 

 

MACRO
BSP to review 2-4% inflation target
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona stated that the current 2-4% inflation target band may be revised next year
  • Remolona stated that the midpoint [3%] of the range might be "a bit high" and that 2.5% may be a "better" midpoint for the BSP's target band
    • He cited that the possible revision stems from the reduced need for a wide scope of relative price adjustments, as prices of key inflation items (i.e., rice, vegetables, oil) have continued to ease
  • Inflation for March posted at 1.8%, bringing the YT-Mar inflation rate is at 2.2%, which is situated in the lower-end of the inflation target range
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to be at 3.0% for 2025
