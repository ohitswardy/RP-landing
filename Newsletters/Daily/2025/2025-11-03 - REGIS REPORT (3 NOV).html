REGIS REPORT

03 November 2025

The BSP estimates the inflation print for October will come in between 1.4% and 2.2%. Consensus is smack in the middle at 1.8%, slightly higher than the September figure of 1.7%. Upward price pressures are expected from rice due to the import ban, and from vegetables due to inclement weather. The October inflation report will be out on Wednesday, 05 November.

 

Meanwhile, Finance Secretary Ralph Recto is warning of slower GDP in 2H25 on government under-spending as a result of the ongoing corruption scandal. The 3Q25 GDP numbers follow shortly on Friday 07 November

 

Tame inflation and a weak GDP print could prompt the BSP to cut policy rates by another 25bps at its final Monetary Board meeting on 11 December (not in our forecasts).

 

+ + +

 

Aboitiz Power (AP PM) is doubling down on coal power, pushing into Vietnam with the acquisition of a minority interest in a coal-fired power plant there.  AP will pay US$220m for the 25% stake in a 1,320MW facility selling power to the national utility.

 

Meanwhile, a fire broke out at AP’s Pagbilao facility, which will halt operations for the entire Pagbilao complex (700MW + 388MW).

 

+ + +

 

The PSEi Index will conduct a mild rebalancing at the close of trading on Friday 07 November, with AREIT (AREIT PM) seeing a small decline, and Digiplus (PLUS PM) a slight increase in their respective weightings.

MACRO
BSP expects October inflation to settle within 1.4-2.2%
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) in its month-ahead inflation forecast expects Oct inflation to fall within 1.4-2.2%
    • BSP expectations for Oct inflation are around the lower-end of the BSP's 2-4% target range
    • Consensus expects Oct inflation to be 1.8%
  • Upward price pressures are higher prices of rice, vegetables, electricity as well as the depreciation of the peso
    • The peso depreciated to P59 per US dollar by end-Oct from P58.15 in the beginning of the month
    • These may have been partially offset by lower prices of oil, meat, and fruits
  • Inflation has averaged 1.7% so far for 9M25, while inflation in Sep alone was 1.7%
  • For 2025, the BSP expects 2025 inflation to be 1.7%
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to average 1.8%
  • Oct inflation data will be released by the PSA (Philippine Statistics Authority) on Nov 5 (Wed)

 

 

MACRO
Finance Secretary expects lower 2025 GDP growth due to gov't underspending
  • Dept. of Finance Secretary Ralph Recto stated that gov't underspending in 3Q due to the corruption crackdown will likely drag overall GDP growth this year
    • The gov't disbursed P1.46trn in 3Q, P141.7bn (9%) less than the 1.6trn program for the period primarily due to lower spending in the Dept. of Public Works and Highways (DPWH)
  • Recto, however, stated that he expects faster growth in the medium term given that they are "plugging those leaks (inefficient capital expenditures) and reallocating funds to high-impact investments" such as education, healthcare, agriculture, and digitalization
  • The gov't is targeting 5.5-6.5% GDP growth in 2025
    • Economic Secretary Arsenio Balisacan and Budget Secretary Amenah Pangandaman previously said that the 2025 GDP growth target is still attainable
    • 1H25 GDP growth was 5.4%
    • 3Q GDP data will be released by the PSA on Nov 7 (Fri)
  • For 2026-2028, the gov't's target growth rate is 6-7%
    • Consensus and Regis expect GDP growth in 2026 to be 5.7% and 5.8%, respectively

 

 

POWER
Aboitiz Power eyes 25% stake in Vietnamese coal-fired plant
  • Aboitiz Power is acquiring a 25% stake in Van Phong Power Co. Ltd. (VPCL) from Sumitomo Corp. for US$220mn
    • VPCL owns and operates a 1,320 NW high-efficiency, low emission (HELE) coal-fired power plant in Khanh Hoa Province, Vietnam
    • Plant has a 25-year power supply agreement with Vietnam’s national utility, Vietnam Electricity (EVN)
  • We estimate the acquisition values VPCL at 8x trailing PER, broadly in line with AP’s 2024 PER of 8x
    • Note that Sumitomo announced its intention to sell 50% of its stake in VPCL (here)
  • Deal is subject to customary conditions precedent (e.g. regulatory and third-party approvals)
    • Deal is estimated to close sometime in 2026
  • AP is currently operating an attributable 4,636 MW of capacity, of which ~3,500 are from thermal plants
    • It has a pipeline of 421MW of renewable energy projects that are under construction
    • The company is targeting an expansion of its total attributable net sellable capacity to 9.2 GW by 2030, with a 50:50 balance between renewable and thermal energy sources
POWER
Fire hits Aboitiz Power’s Pagbilao coal-fired plant; One dead, nine injured
  • A fire broke out at Unit 3 of the Pagbilao Energy Corp. (PEC) on Oct 31, resulting in the death of one worker and injuries to nine others
    • PEC is a 50%-owned unit Aboitiz Power (50/50 JV with TeaM Energy) and has a net sellable capacity of 388MW
    • Before the incident, Pagbilao Unit 3 was operating at 131 MW and subsequently tripped at 9:32 p.m
MARKET
PSE Index rebalancing to take place at the close of November 7
  • The PSE announced a rebalancing of the PSE Index at the close of November 7 (open of November 10)
  • An index rebalancing occurs in November when the public float of a PSEi constituent changes by at least 5ppt between 2Q and 3Q
  • The public float of AREIT declined by 5ppt QoQ (to 33%) due to the recently concluded share-for-property swap between Ayala Land and AREIT
  • The public float of PLUS increased by 7ppt QoQ (to 37%) due to the disposal of shares by Mr. Alfredo Benitez as well as the reclassification of shares held by XII Capital to public float
  • Based on our preliminary estimates, AREIT will experience US$5.3mn of outflow while PLUS will see US$4.5mn of inflow
    • All of the other PSEi constituents should also experience modest amounts of inflow

GAMING
Belle Corp’s share of gaming revenue rises 60% QoQ in 3Q25
  • Belle Corp’s share of Melco Resorts Philippines’ revenue rose 61% QoQ (-2% YoY) to P545mn
  • This is a contrast to the experience of Okada Manila, whose GGR fell 2% QoQ and 15% YoY
    • Although mass GGR of Okada Manila improved QoQ, those gains were eroded by the decline in VIP GGR
