REGIS REPORT

16 September 2025

Online gambling firm Digiplus (PLUS PM) is reportedly in talks to acquire a brick-and-mortar casino in order to reduce the fees it pays the government. 

 

The fees paid by online gambling firms are lower by ~9ppt (as a share of revenue) if they have an investment in physical casinos of up to US$200mn. For the likes of PLUS, the fee reduction means annualized savings of ~P7bn (~US$130mn), based on 1H results.

 

The talks for the acquisition of a casino have stalled for now, given the recent outcries against online gambling. The financial incentive for PLUS to acquire a physical casino, however, will likely revive such if the rules on fees don’t change.

Overseas remittances for July rose 3.0% to US$3.2bn. Cumulative YT-July remittances are up 3.1%.

The Dept of Agriculture tweaked its rice harvest FY25 forecast to 20.3mn MT, down (-0.7%) from 20.45mn MT. The 20.3m figure would be 6% higher than last year’s 19.1mn MT harvest of unmilled rice. The better harvest would reduce the country’s need to import by around 800k MT.

Passenger volumes at Cebu Air (CEB PM) dipped 0.4% to 2.1mn in Aug. The airline already reduced capacity in anticipation of the lower demand. YT-Aug passenger volumes are at 18.1mn, which is up 15% YoY.

GAMING
DigiPlus reportedly in talks to acquire physical casino
  • According to an article in the Manila Standard, DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) is in talks to acquire a physical casino
  • However, talks have stalled due to “recent developments”
    • The BSP recently prohibited e-wallets from redirecting to online gaming platforms
    • There have been several other proposals to tighten regulations on e-gaming
  • Integrated resort operators (that have spent minimum capex of US$200mn) are charged only 29.2% of e-casino gross gaming revenue vs 38% of GGR for pure online firms
  • We estimate that if the lower fees/taxes were applied to 1H25, then the net profit of PLUS would have been c. 40% higher – c. P11.8bn vs the actual 1H25 net profit of P8.4bn
  • PLUS is trading at 4.9X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
GAMING
Anti-Money Laundering Council to station personnel in casinos
  • The Anti-Money Laundering Council (AMLC) said it might station personnel in casinos in order to prevent money laundering
  • Senate Committee on Games and Amusement Chairman Erwin Tulfo believes that casinos were aware that government officials gambled in their premises
  • He plans to plan to file a resolution to investigate casinos which are turning a blind eye on questionable transactions and refusing to report such to the AMLC
MACRO
OFW Remittances up 3.0% YoY in July
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks grew 3.0% YoY to US$3.2bn in July, decelerating from the 3.7% growth seen in June
    • Land-based remittances from the USA, Singapore, and Saudi Arabia were cited to be the primary drivers in the increase in remittances given their % contribution to the total
    • Sea-based remittances growth moderated to 3.1% YoY from 3.5% the previous month
    • In PHP terms, remittances were unchanged YoY

 

 

 

MACRO
Dept. of Agriculture revises unmilled rice output forecast to 20.3 MMT
  • The Department of Agriculture (DA) revised downwards its palay (unmilled rice) production forecast this year to 20.3 MMT (-0.7%) from 20.45 MMT previously
    • The 20.3 MMT figure is higher than both last year's 19.1 MMT and 2023's record output of 20.1 MMT
  • Unmilled rice production in 1H25 is up 6% YoY to 9.1 MMT
    • This is on track to reach the 20.3 MMT full-year target, assuming 1H continues to account for ~45% of annual output, which has typically been the case in past years
  • The United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) expects total rice demand to be 17.3 MMT this year as they estimate rice imports to hit 5.5 MMT
    • Assuming a 63% milling recovery rate, the DA's palay output forecast of 20.3 MMT is equivalent to ~12.8 MMT of rice
TRANSPORT
Cebu Air: August pax volumes flat, but load factors higher
  • Cebu Air reported that its systemwide pax volumes dipped 0.4% YoY to 2.1mn in August
    • Underpinned by a 3.2% reduction in seat capacity due to unscheduled engine removals, removal of two aircraft under wet-lease, and scheduled maintenance activities
    • Note that 3Q is a lean season
    • Seat load factors rose 240bps YoY to 83.7%, suggesting stronger fares
  • Brings 8M25 pax volumes to 18.1mn, up 15% YoY and trending slightly below our full-year forecast of 28.5mn (+16%)
    • To reach our forecast, pax volumes will need to average 2.6mn per month for the rest of the year
