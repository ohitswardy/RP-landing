REGIS REPORT

24 June 2025

The Dept. of Energy (DOE) announced that the local oil retailers have agreed to phase in the rather chunky (~10%) increase in pump prices. Over two to three days. This appears a rather feeble attempt to cushion the impact of global oil prices that skyrocketed following the eruption of conflict in the Middle East.

 

What should have a greater impact, though, is the reversal in oil prices due to still-unverified claims that the current conflict is over.

 

 

+++

 

A local telecom operators group is asking the President not to sign a recently passed bill allowing telecom firms to operate even without securing a congressional franchise. The bill (“Kontektadong Pinoy Bill”) will allow operators to offer connectivity services like broadband, etc. 

 

On paper, it allows new operators to come in with less oversight (congressional). This would make it easier for new entrants (especially on broadband) and thus introduce greater competition into the market. Opponents of the bill argue it puts national security at risk if new players are not vetted, especially given the geopolitical climate.

 

While the Marcos administration did certify this bill as “urgent”, we still think there’s a decent possibility that the bill will be vetoed.

 

+++

 

The Dept of Trade and Industry (DTI) say it is expecting the incentive scheme it is crafting for EV producers will help create as many as 680,000 jobs. The challenges to setting up EV production in the Philippines, however, are quite formidable. Most importantly, there’s virtually no vehicle manufacturing ecosystem today (in contrast to Thailand and Indonesia) and costs (labor and power) are not lower than other places in SE Asia.

MACRO
DOE and oil companies agree to staggered two-day fuel price hikes
  • The Department of Energy (DOE) announced that they have reached an agreement with oil companies to implement the P3-5 per liter price adjustment in two tranches
    • Oil firms stated that they will implement two rounds of price increases, with the first on June 24 and second on June 26 or 27
  • DOE projected significant price increases in gasoline (P2.50-P3.00 per liter, ~+5% WoW) and diesel (P4.30-P4.80, ~+9% WoW) for this week
  • Targeted subsidies will also be given for public transport drivers and farmers if the average price of crude oil exceeds the $80/barrel threshold
    • As of June 23, the average price of Dubai crude oil is $75.16/barrel

 

 

 

TELCOS
PCTO warns against the Konektadong Pinoy Act
  • The Philippine Chamber of Telecommunication Operations (PCTO) called on President Marcos to think twice before signing the Konektadong Pinoy Act into law
    • The law allegedly favors the technology of satellite providers, which are mostly foreign companies, given the cost of deploying and managing space-based assets
  • The country’s biggest telcos, who’ve largely invested in terrestrial assets, warned that the Philippines would be the only country that permits satellite providers to come in without regulation
    • New foreign operators, under the bill, will not be required to build infrastructure
    • They will also not be required to service or subsidize underserved rural areas, nor support government in times of national emergency
  • The Konektadong Pinoy bill eliminates barriers to entry in the telecommunications sector, removing the need for legislative franchises for connectivity services in the hopes of attracting more foreign investment in digital infrastructure
MACRO
DTI aiming to roll out electric vehicle incentive program soon
  • The Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) has completed the EV incentive framework called “Electric Vehicle Incentive Strategy” (EVIS)
    • The Fiscal Incentives Review Board will deliberate and finalize EVIS next month
    • Once the review is over, the DTI is expected to start the rollout of fiscal incentives
  • The DTI aims to implement tax breaks, duty exemptions & investment support under EVIS
    • 0% excise tax on fully built-up EVs imported as CBUs (Completely Built Unit) through 2028 
    • 15% excise tax on hybrids versus 50% under the TRAIN law
    • Duty-free importation of CKD EV units and EV parts upon BOI approval and Zero-tariff rates on batteries, motors, and charging-station equipment
    • Up to seven-year Income Tax Holiday for new EV manufacturing/assembly Post-ITH option to pay a 5% special corporate income tax on gross income in lieu of all national and local taxes
    • Zero-rating of VAT on EVs, parts, and batteries sold to end-users with and exemption from motor vehicle user’s charge and other local levies during the incentive period
    • One-stop-shop BOI processing for permits, environmental clearances, and import licenses 
    • Priority LTO registration and issuance of special EV license plates
  • The DTI expects EVIS to create 680,000 jobs and spur P120bn in investments
    • Program is expected to produce P11.4 T in output and P400bn in additional tax revenues
  • The DTI aims to produce 9mn EVs from 2028-2040 under this program and mandates the installation of 400k charging stations
MACRO
Lowering of imported rice MSRP delayed
  • The Department of Agriculture (DA) is postponing the plan to lower the maximum suggested retail price (MSRP) of imported rice on July 1 by one or two months
    • This is due to heightened geopolitical risk given the conflicts in the Middle East which drove the recent surge in oil prices
    • DA Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. stated that the delayed rollout of lower MSRP will enable them to get a clearer picture on the movement of global rice prices
  • The initial plan was to lower the MSRP of imported rice from the current P45/kg to P43/kg starting July 1
    • The MSRP was initially P58/kg at end-Jan, which was lowered to P49/kg by end-Feb, and P45/kg by end-March
  • The prices for local and imported well-milled rice as of June 21 range from P38-52/kg and P38-48/kg, respectively, compared to P40-55/kg and P40-52/kg on Jan 21
