REGIS REPORT

19 June 2025

The auto industry association CAMPI / TMA reported vehicle sales of 39,775 for the month of May, down slightly (1%) from the year before. The May volumes were up 18% MoM from the lower base in Apr (due to Easter Week).

 

Sales of Toyota vehicles for the month reached 19,725, up 6% YoY and 20% MoM. These figures imply a market share gain for Toyota, at least relative to the other CAMPI / TMA members.

 

Among the other highlights in the data was the fact that electrified vehicles accounted for 9% of vehicles sold in May. Of the total 3,600 units sold, more than 20% were pure battery electric vehicles (BEV). Note that BYD data is not yet included in the CAMPI stats. If one were to include BYD, it is reasonable to expect that electrified vehicles were already close to 15% of sales in May.

 

+++

 

The Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) Monetary Board (MB) is meeting today and the market is expecting a rate reduction of 25bp to be announced. Such a reduction would cut the rate to 5.25%, 125bp below its recent peak (6.5%).

 

The reported CPI inflation numbers have been quite benign in the past few months due to the fall in food costs as well as the prices of other commodities, including oil. The raging war in the Middle East is already upsetting hopes of continued weakness in energy prices. A likely increase in jeepney fares is already expected as early as next week.

 

+++

 

Digital payments made through the National Retail Payments System (NRPS) jumped 38% in May, accelerating from the +31% pace in April. This rise in digital transactions implies that the use of cash is declining, not just as a share of total payments but also in absolute amounts.

 

+++

 

The government finalized the 10-year contract extensions for Manila Water (MWC PM) and Maynilad with respect to their concessions in Metro Manila. Both contracts will now expire in 2047.

 

Note that the regulator has already been working on the assumption that the contracts end in 2047 so it will not have an impact on their regulatory outcomes. Our valuations for both MWC and Maynilad also already assume a 2047 contract termination.

CONGLOS
Vehicle sales (ex-BYD) dip in May; Toyota sales up 6%
  • CAMPI/TMA reported vehicle sales fell 1.2% YoY to 39,775 units in May
    • On a MoM basis, vehicle sales were up 18% due to the low base effects from Easter break
    • Note that CAMPI/TMA data does not capture BYD’s sales
    • Brings 5M25 sales up 1.7% YoY to 190,429 units; If we include BYD, we estimate industry car sales would have grown over 5%
  • Sales of electrified vehicles (ex-BYD) hit 3,613 units in May, up 139% MoM
    • EVs accounted for 9.1% of new car sales in May
    • Brings 5M25 EV sales to 10,433 units, accounting for 5.5% of the market
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) sold 19,725 units in May, up 5.6% YoY and 20% MoM
    • Brings TMP’s 5M25 sales volumes to 91,652 units (+6.3% YoY)
    • TMP is targeting to sell 235k units this year (+8% YoY). To reach this target, sales should average 20.5k units per month for the rest of the year
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 32% of NAV (25% of gross asset value) and 26% of profit before parent expenses
  • GTCAP is trading at a 56% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,291/sh and 5x FY25E PER

 

 

MACRO
P1 jeepney fare hike likely next week—LTFRB
  • The Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) chairman Teofilo Guadiz III stated there may be a P1 increase across the board for jeepney fares given rising fuel prices
    • He stated that it will not be a per-kilometer hike but only an increase to the flat rate
    • The fare hike is much lower than the P2 petitioned fare increase early this year
  • The minimum fare for traditional jeepneys is P13 while P15 for modern jeepneys
  • Recall that the government hiked minimum jeepney fares by P1 in October 2023
  • Passenger road transport has a weight of 3.5% in the CPI basket
DIGITAL FINANCE/TELCOS
Digital payments rise 38% YoY in May
  • Payments made digitally over the National Retail Payments System (NRPS) rose by 38% YoY to P1.98tn in May, accelerating from the 31% pace in April
    • Bringing the 5M25 transaction value to P9.1tn, up 37% YoY
  • Payments through InstaPay, which handles real-time payments of up to P50k, rose 54% YoY to P930.6bn in May
    • By volume, InstaPay payments surged 217% YoY, even faster than the 190% pace in April
  • Large-ticket payment transactions that go through PesoNet, rose 27% YoY to P1.05tn in May, faster than the 18% growth seen in April

 

 

 

 

 

 

UTILITIES
Government formalizes contract extensions of Manila Water, Maynilad
  • The Economy and Development (ED) Council, formerly known as the NEDA Board, has approved the 10-year extension of the two water concessionaires in Metro Manila
    • Extension to Jan 21, 2047 (from July 31, 2037) aligns the contracts with the legislative franchises of both concessionaires
  • This extension will not affect our valuation and profit forecasts for the water utilities
    • The water regulator, MWSS, already assumed the extension during the 2022 rate rebasing exercise
    • The auditors of both water companies have also assumed the extension in the amortization of concession assets
