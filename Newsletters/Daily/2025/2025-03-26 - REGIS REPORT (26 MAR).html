26 March 2025

 

FY24 profits at GT Capital (GTCAP PM) came in P28.4bn after preferred divs, down 2% YoY. We estimate “recurring” profit to common shareholders was P27.9bn, and reckon this is slightly below Street estimates, even if it’s above Regis forecasts.

 

There was a drop in 4Q contributions from associate Metro Pacific (MPI) and the auto business. Note the auto business showed an improvement in quarterly gross profit margins despite the PHP depreciation over the period.

 

The company will hold its earnings briefing this afternoon.

 

 

 

+++

 

Growth at what’s now the most watched port of ICTSI (ICT PM) slowed to 2% YoY in Feb. The CMSA terminal of ICTSI handled 117k TEUs in Feb, lower than January (see chart). This terminal in Mexico is estimated to be the largest EBITDA contributor for ICTSI and concerns over it have risen due to the erratic trade policies being espoused by the current US administration.

 

+++

 

Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) is aiming to add 500 new stores this year from the ~4,100 as of end-2024. SEVN added over 400 new stores last year and is hoping to sustain this year’s (2025) pace over the medium term.

 

+++

 

Converge (CNVRG PM) has signed up to be a reseller of Starlink, the provider of satellite-based internet services. CNVRG CEO Dennis Uy says he expects the agreement will add 5-10% to CNVRG’s enterprise revenue. The enterprise segment accounts for ~15% of CNVRG’s revenue so this could mean an incremental ~1% to total top line.

TRANSPORT
Volume growth at ICTSI’s Mexico port moderates to 2% YoY in February
  • Data from the port authority showed volumes at ICTSI’s port, CMSA, rose by 2% YoY to 117k TEUs in February 2025, slower than the +11% rate in January
    • Volumes across Mexico’s ports (excluding CMSA) grew by +1% YoY in February 2025, broadly the same pace as in January 2025
    • On Feb 1, President Trump signed an order imposing a 25% across-the-board tariff on Mexico but on Feb 3, he suspended the same order for 30 days
  • CMSA’s 2M25 cumulative volumes grew by 7% YoY to 253k TEUs, lagging our FY25E of +16% (1.69mn TEUs)
    • Note that our forecasts assume the US-Mexico-Canada (USMCA) treaty will be cancelled only by July 2026
    • Every 5% decrease (increase) in CMSA’s FY25E volumes will shave off (increase) EBITDA by 1.6% and profit by 2.4%
  • USMCA status:
    • On March 6, Trump deferred the imposition of a 25% across-the-board tariff from March 4 to April 2
    • On March 12, Trump imposed a 25% tariff on imports of steel and aluminum (raw material steel and aluminum exports only ~US$4bn out of Mexico’s total exports to the US of US$506bn)
    • To date, Mexico has not imposed retaliatory tariffs and has hinted at matching the USA’s tariffs against China to placate President Trump
    • Note that China accounts for 60% of CMSA’s volumes; USA currently has a 35% tariff on Chinese imports and Trump threatened to increase this by another 10% on April 2
    • We estimate CMSA accounts for 12% of consolidated volumes, 21% of revenues, and 25% of EBITDA
  • ICTSI trades 7.5x Regis’ FY25E EV/EBITDA (consensus: 8.1x), lower than peer average of (11x) and historical average (11x)

 

 

 

 

CONSUMER
Philippine Seven Corp. to open 500 stores this year; gives guidance on FY24 results
  • Philippine Seven Corp. (SEVN PM) targets to open 500 branches this year, which would be the most in the company's history and is also higher than the 400 stores they opened in 2024
  • SEVN President Jose Victor Paterno elaborated that most of the store network growth would be from Visayas and Mindanao, citing continued strong growth from the two areas post-COVID
  • Paterno is optimistic that 500 new stores annually will be the direction of the company moving forward
    • He also commented that they already have a database of potential new locations
    • Note that the company as of 9M24 last year had 4,022 stores, 52% being company-owned and with 1,000+ of total stores located in Visayas and Mindanao
  • SEVN has yet to release its FY24 results but Paterno guided higher profit and sales as the company saw growth but not as good as 2023's growth
    • SEVN's FY23 net income was at P3.5bn (+69% YoY) with SSSG at 13%, while 9M24 net income posted at P2.6bn (+13.7% YoY) and SSSG at ~6.3%
    • Consensus estimates FY24 net profit to be P3.95bn (+13.5% YoY) while we expect it to be at P4.07bn (+17 % YoY)

TELCOS
Converge to become Starlink reseller
  • Converge ICT Solutions Inc. (CNVRG PM) has partnered with satellite internet provider Starlink and US-based tech firm Ribbon Communications to bolster its enterprise offerings and bring revenues up by as much as 10%
  • CNVRG CEO Dennis Anthony Uy guided for a 5-10% revenue boost to its enterprise segment from the partnerships, citing Starlink's high-speed, low-latency satellite internet, which enables new offerings to enterprise clients such as a hybrid connectivity package with its fiber plans
    • He also cited other new potential customers such as vessels, cargoes, ferries, gov't coastal units, and the like
    • The monthly fee for the Starlink service in the Philippines is as low as P2,700 (US$47) but the upfront cost is quite high at P28,000 (US$490)
  • CNVRG's partnership with Ribbon is aimed at enhancing the fiber network with Ribbon's AI-enabled Multilayer Automation platform which will automate and optimize the company's network management
    • This was initially rolled out in Luzon but will be deployed nationwide to improve data transmission capabilities and operational efficiencies
