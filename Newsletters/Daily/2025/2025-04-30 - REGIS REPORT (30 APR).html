REGIS REPORT

30 April 2025

The results are trickling in with 1Q earnings season ramping up. We have results from SM Prime (SMPH PM) and Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM) this morning:

 

  • SM Prime (SMPH PM) (=) – Operating profit rose 13% to P16.7bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. Systemwide mall revenue grew 7% while 1Q25 residential reservation sales rose 45% QoQ (down 57% YoY) to P11.6bn. Looking ahead, the company plans to launch high-end residential projects and to start selling commercial lots in its new townships. These would mark strategic shifts for the company, which has not been known for commercial lots or high-end residential projects.

  • Robinsons Retail (RRHI) (=) – 1Q25 headline net profit was P760m, down 85% YoY from a high 1Q24 base lifted (due to a P4.4bn one-time gain from the BPI-RBC merger). Operationally, revenue and EBIT are trending within expectations at P47.8bn (+4%) and P1.9bn (+3%), respectively. 1Q consolidated SSSG improved to +3.0% versus +0.9%.

MACRO
March budget deficit at P376bn; 1Q budget deficit widens 76% YoY to P479bn
  • The government recorded a budget deficit of P374.6bn which is wider both 91.8% YoY and 119.2% MoM
    • This brings the 1Q25 deficit to P478.8bn, 75.6% wider YoY
  • Revenue collections in Mar inched down 3.0% YoY to P279.3bn, as non-tax revenues dipped 71.0% YoY
    • BIR (Bureau of Internal Revenue) collections jumped 20.9% to P175.7bn mainly due to ongoing revenue enhancement measures such as the crackdown on fake receipts, illicit trade, as well as improvements in digitalization and tax payment facilities
    • Customs saw higher growth, up 7.3% to P80.4bn in collections from higher VAT and excise collections
    • Non-tax revenues dropped by 71.0% to P19.6bn due to the earlier timing of remittances from Government Owned and Controlled Corporations (GOCCs) last year. The gov't expects this to improve in the succeeding months as dividends from various GOCCs are set to be remitted to the National Treasury starting May
  • Expenditures surged 35.4% YoY in Mar to P655bn mainly driven by higher primary spending
    • Primary spending for the month jumped 36.3% YoY to P652.9bn, likely due to accelerated spendings prior to the midterm elections
      • The increase in primary spending was attributed to the (a) capital expenditures of the Dept. of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), (b) health and social protection programs
  • The Bureau of Treasury commented that with the upcoming dividend remittances and other non-tax revenues expected in the coming quarters, and spending continuing to be on track, the 2025 fiscal deficit is seen to stay within the ₱1.5 trillion target
    • The gov't for 2025 targets the budget deficit to narrow to 5.3% of GDP from 5.7% in 2024, and the government aims to further reduce this to 3.7% in 2028

 

 

MACRO
BPO sector to hit US$40bn in revenue this year
  • The IT-BPO Association of the Philippines (IBPAP) said that it would hit its US$40bn revenue target this year
    • This represents c. 5% growth from US$38bn in 2024
  • It is also projecting the sector to employ 2mn Filipinos in 12 to 18 months
    • The sector had 1.82mn employees at the end of 2024
  • BPO firms account for 60%-80% of the occupied office space of the developers we cover
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 49% of Filinvest Land’s NAV - FLI is trading at 64% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 5.3X FY25E P/E
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV - MEG is trading at 72% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 3.0X FY25E P/E
    • 28% of Robinsons Land’s NAV - RLC is trading at 74% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY25E P/B, and 4.1X FY25E P/E
MACRO
Department of Finance withdraws proposal to hike capital gains tax
  • Finance Secretary Ralph G. Recto has withdrawn his department’s proposal to increase capital gains tax, donor’s tax and estate tax, citing higher revenue collections in the first quarter
    • These tax increases were previously sought to be included in the Capital Markets Efficiency Promotion Act (CMEPA)
    • Secretary Recto cited these amendments in a letter to House Ways and Means Committee Chairperson and Albay Rep. Jose Ma. Clemente S. Salceda
  • The DoF’s withdrawal of its proposed tax hikes is considered moot since the CMEPA was already ratified by Congress last February
    • The bill is set to be transmitted to Malacañang for the President’s signature
    • Among the CMEPA bill’s key measures include (a) a reduction of stock transaction tax from 0.6% to 0.1% and (b) a reduction in the dividend tax on foreign entities from 25% to 10%
POWER
ACEN sees robust growth in 2026, 2027, despite slower growth expectations this year
  • ACEN Corp. expects its earnings growth in 2025 to be “a little bit slower” but should accelerate sharply in 2026 and 2027 as it completes new projects
    • According to ACEN CFO Jonathan Back
    • ACEN expects some moderation in its 2025 earnings growth, as it does not have as much new capacity coming online
    • Recall that ACEN’s core profit before pref divs jumped 39% YoY to P6.6bn in 2024, after completing 1,632 MW worth of attributable RE capacity
  • ACEN president and CEO Eric Francia said the proceeds of its planned rights issue (see here) would be used to bankroll the company’s projects and finance its maturing obligations
  • ACEN is currently constructing 2,315 MW and has committed to build another 1,379 MW of capacity
    • Its operational capacity currently stands at 3,284 MW
