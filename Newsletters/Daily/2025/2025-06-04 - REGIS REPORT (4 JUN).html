04 June 2025

President Marcos has appointed Francis Lim, corporate lawyer and former CEO of the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE PM), as the new chairman of the Securities and Exchange Commission.

 

Lim’s background bodes well for possible reforms relating to the Philippine capital markets. He’s remembered to be among the main proponents of key pieces legislation to help boost the domestic equity/capital markets, including the REIT Law.

Meanwhile, it was also reported that President has declined the courtesy resignations of most members of his Cabinet. This means the revamp affected only a handful of Cabinet members, most notably: the Secretaries of Foreign Affairs, Energy, Environment, and Housing as well as the Solicitor General.

Government debt inches up 0.4% MoM to P16.75trn in April, despite a decrease in foreign liabilities due to the appreciation of the PHP.

Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) CFO Jesse Teo discussed the firm’s efforts revive its Meat Alternatives business, Quorn, which has struggled since Monde first acquired it a decade ago. Teo highlighted improvements in the business, which still saw a decline in 1Q revenue, but did not rule out a possible divestment.

MARKET
Francis Lim appointed as the new SEC chair
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. has corporate appointed Francis Edralin Lim as the new chairperson of the SEC, replacing SEC Chair Emilio Aquino
  • Formerly the president and CEO of the PSE, Lim spearheaded initiatives like the Real Estate Investment Trust Act, the Personal Equity Retirement Account, and the Financial Rehabilitation and Insolvency Act
  • PSE president and CEO Ramon Monzon welcomed the move stating his confidence that the incoming chair will support the initiatives of the PSE
  • Executive Secretary Lucas Bersamin highlighted Lim's decades of experience in corporate law, governance, and capital markets regulation
MACRO
Marcos Cabinet sees minimal changes after 'bold reset'
  • After previous calling for courtesy resignations, President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. has kept most of his Cabinet Secretaries, including Agriculture (DA), Education (DepEd), Labor (DOLE), Health (DOH), Social Welfare (DSWD), Transport (DOTr), Public Works (DPWH), and the entire core economic team
  • The few departments which saw changes in leadership were the Department of Energy (DOE), Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), Foreign Affairs (DFA), and Housing (DHSUD) as well as the Solicitor General
  • Performance reviews were signaled to still be under way and that announcements on changes for other officials and key GOCCs such as PAGCOR, Landbank, GSIS, PEZA will be unveiled soon
MACRO
Government debt inches up to P16.75trn in end-April
  • The government's total debt stock went up by 0.4% MoM to P16.75tn as of end-April
    • This was due to additional borrowings which was offset by the appreciation of the PHP against the USD (from P57.38 end-Mar to P56.25 end-Apr)
  • Domestic debt rose by 1.8% (P211bn) MoM to P11.59trn due to the impact of net borrowings
  • External debt decreased by 2.7% (P142.3bn) MoM to P5.16trn given a decrease in the peso equivalent of USD debt (P124.7bn) due to local currency appreciation, and net repayments (P58.3bn)
  • The current debt mix of the country's total obligations has domestic debt accounting for 69.2% and 30.8% being external borrowings
  • The year-end debt target for 2025 is pegged at P17.35trn which is 8% higher YoY than end-Dec's P16.1tn (see here)
    • This 2025 target is equivalent to 60.4% of GDP vs. 2024's debt-to-GDP ratio of 60.7%
    • The ratio is expected to further decline below 60% by 2027 (58.4%); by the end of the Marcos admin's term in 2028, it expects the debt-to-GDP ratio to fall to 56.9%

 

 

CONSUMER
Monde continues Quorn overhaul but keeps options open
  • In an interview with Bilyornaryo, Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) CFO Jesse Teo stated that the company is focused on revitalizing its meat alternatives business segment, Quorn Foods
  • Teo highlighted that there have been improvements made in the segment with losses down to P58mn in 1Q25 (from P216mn in 1Q24) and gross profit up 11% YoY to P759mn, as well as Quorn's improved cash generation with the recent repayment of a GBP£12mn loan
    • Net sales, however, was still in a decline, down 4% YoY to P3.3bn in 1Q25
    • He said that the turnaround will take time as it has only been two years but also mentioned that "…If it really doesn't work out, we can always sell."
    • Teo reiterated management's commitment as the Ang family and CEO Henry Soesanto pledged up to 2.16bn of their Monde shares to protect the company from further impairment losses in the meat alts business for the next 10 years from Dec 2023
  • Recall that Monde acquired Quorn back in 2015, but post-pandemic the segment has been posting losses given elevated input costs and tighter consumer wallets
    • In FY24, the meat alternatives segment posted P13.6bn (-4% YoY) in sales, gross profit of P2.9bn (-3% YoY), hit EBITDA breakeven, and a core net loss of P804bn
    • For FY25, Monde is guiding for mid-single-digit topline growth and broadly in line margins for APAC BFB, while the company targets positive core EBITDA despite continued topline challenges for the meat alts business
POWER
Electricity spot prices drop in May
  • The Independent Electricity Market Operator of the Philippines (IEMOP) said that nationwide average spot prices fell by 11% MoM to P4.01/kwh in May
    • Data covers April 26 to May 25 and represents Load Weighted Average Prices (LWAP)
    • Note that nationwide LWAP in May 2024 stood at P8.12/kwh
  • For Luzon, LWAP in May fell 7.9% MoM to P4.23/kwh, after supply rose 4.1% MoM while demand grew 3.6% MoM
    • 2Q25-to-date, LWAP in Luzon averaged at P4.41/kwh, up 18% QoQ but down by 37% YoY
  • The following gencos are net sellers to the spot market:
    • Semirara (SCC PM) – 60% of capacity is un-contracted
    • ACEN Corp. (ACEN PM) – 53% of capacity in PH is un-contracted
    • Aboitiz Power (AP PM) – 30% of capacity is un-contracted
