REGIS REPORT

18 June 2025

At least a couple of senators are raising concerns over online gaming and are urging action on the matter. While POGOs are now, on paper, banned, the online gaming activities targeting locals have grown exponentially. This has led to social ills and other unforeseen issues, with implications for other economic activities.

 

Gaming revenue for PIGOs (Philippine Inland Gaming Operators) has skyrocketed over the past three years. Digiplus (PLUS PM), the biggest participant in that space, has seen its share price do the same, and that has brought it to the cusp of becoming part of the 30-member PSE Index (PSEi or “PCOMP”).

 

+++

 

ACEN (ACEN PM) disclosed that it was selling its oil-fired power plants to its parent firm ACEIC, a subsidiary of Ayala Corp (AC PM). We expect the sale to be largely a cosmetic deal so that ACEN will no longer legally own those assets. However, the economics relating to those assets will largely remain with ACEN.

 

+++

 

PLDT (TEL PM) has reduced the price of its prepaid fiber broadband service to P699 for 30 days (from P999 previously), finally matching the price of Globe (GLO PM) and Converge (CNVRG PM).

 

PLDT had previously been reluctant to compete on prepaid fiber except in select areas, citing economics. On the other hand, Globe and Converge have been aggressively enticing subscribers to their prepaid fiber service, contributing to the higher subscriber adds over the past few quarters.

GAMING
Senator wants further probe of online gambling
  • Senator Alan Peter Cayetano urged the incoming 20th Congress to continue investigating online gambling in the country
  • He lamented that while an anti-POGO Bill has been passed, Philippine Inland Gaming Operators (PIGO) have grown 
  • Earlier this year, Senate President Francis Escudero called for a review of Philippine Inland Gaming Operators, which offer e-games to Filipinos
  • Gross Gaming Revenue of e-games (including bingo) grew 88% YoY (flat QoQ) to P51bn in 1Q25
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) is the market leader, with 45% market share
  • PLUS is trading at 15.6X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
POWER
ACEN sells diesel assets to parent
  • ACEN Corp. is selling its three diesel plants with a combined capacity of 189MW to its parent, AC Energy and Infrastructure Corp. (ACEIC)
    • Total consideration is less than 10% of ACEN’s assets
    • Divestment is subject to the satisfaction of agreed conditions precedent and the execution of definitive documentation
    • Company cited that the divestment would help it achieve its goal of reaching 100% RE generation by end-2025
  • ACEIC is a 100%-owned unit of Ayala Corp. (AC PM) and owns 58% of ACEN
  • Similar to its “exit” from the 246MW coal-fired plant SLTEC, we expect ACEN to maintain the contracts of diesel plants so that revenue/income streams will still accrue to ACEN
TELCOS
PLDT cuts price for prepaid fiber
  • PLDT (TEL PM) just cut the price of its 30-day prepaid service by 30% to P699 (from P999)
  • This matches the 30-day price of GFiber Prepaid of Globe (GLO PM) and Surf2Sawa of Converge (CNVRG PM)
  • PLDT’s net adds for wireline broadband have been lagging because it has avoided competing in prepaid fiber, possibly because it has a higher cost structure
