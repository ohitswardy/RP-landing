REGIS REPORT

24 March 2025

First Gen (FGEN PM) issued a press release saying its recurring profit declined 12% to US$245m in FY24. The drop looks largely attributable to the lower numbers from the geothermal business, which saw reduced output and higher costs.

 

FGEN faces a key risk this year with the expiry of the power supply agreement between the 1000MW Sta Rita and Meralco (MER PM).

 

+++

 

The tax bureau (Bureau of Internal Revenue, BIR) says it is confident of reaching its collection target of P3.23trn, which would represent an increase of 13% from last year. Two newly passed tax laws will have some impact on this year’s revenue: the law imposing VAT on digital services and the “CREATE MORE” law that rationalizes other existing tax regulations.

 

Contrary to the mistaken interpretations of some, CREATE MORE does not reduce the corporate tax rate by 5ppt to 20% across the board. The reduction is only applicable to those registered with the investment promotion agencies like BOI or PEZA, or those whose incomes doesn’t exceed P5mn and whose assets don’t exceed P100m.

 

+++

 

Home improvement retailer Wilcon (WLCON PM) intends to open another 8 to 10 outlets this year. This would be roughly the same pace as its store openings over the last three years.

 

+++

 

Mining firm Philex (PX PM) says it is close to agreeing to a deal for a foreign partner to take a stake in the Silangan mining project. Silangan is expected to  finally begin commercial production by early next year.

POWER
FGEN FY24 core profit drops 12% to US$245mn – Below Regis FY24E
  • In a press release, FGEN reported FY24 attributable recurring profit dropped 12% YoY to US$245mn, below Regis FY24E\
    • The principal factor in the profit decline for FY24 was the weaker operating results from EDC
  • Profits from the geothermal business saw profits drop 37% due to decreased output and spending on drilling
  • Derived 4Q24 recurring profit rebounded by 40% YoY after declining 17% YoY in 9M24
    • We suspect the YoY rebound was partly due to better operating numbers for its LNG terminal
    • On a QoQ basis, recurring profits fell 29% which may have been partly due to seasonality and the repair of Avion

 

MACRO
BIR confident on hitting 2025 collection target of P3.23tn
  • The Bureau of Internal Revenue is confident in meeting its collection goal of P3.23tn
  • 2025's target of P3.23tn represents a 13% YoY increase which is the same as the 13% YoY growth seen from the P2.85tn in collections for 2024
    • The breakdown of this year's collections is that 53% or P1.71tn is set to come from net income taxes. Followed by value-added tax (VAT) of P710bn, excise taxes (P343bn), other taxes (P298bn), and percentage taxes (P178bn)
  • BIR Assistant Commissioner Jethro Sabariaga stated that a downside risk would be the lower collections seen from the CREATE MORE law (Corporate Recovery and Tax Incentives for Enterprises to Maximize Opportunities for Reinvigorating the Economy)
    • As for registered business enterprises (RBEs) specifically, the new law lowers the corporate income tax to 20% from 25% and it also expands their VAT incentives coverage
      • The reduced tax rate (to 20%) covers only a small subset of corporate taxpayers; among them are those (a) registered with investment promotion agencies (IPA) or (b) whose income is P5million (or lower) with assets less than P100m
    • Recall that the CREATE MORE Act IRR was signed last Feb
  • However, the BIR also sees a boost from upcoming measures which could generate P22bn more revenues from VAT on digital services, windfall tax and royalty on mining, and single-use tax plastics
CONSUMER
WLCON set to open up to 10 stores in 2025
  • Wilcon Depot Inc. (WLCON PM), after opening its 100th store last year, targets to open eight to 10 new stores for 2025
  • The new stores will be a mix of small and medium-sized formats mostly in Luzon except for one in Cebu
    • With new smaller format stores, it aims to be more accessible to underserved areas
  • Existing stores that are old and dated will undergo renovation
  • WLCON as of late has not reported its FY24 earnings yet
    • But for 9M24, the company saw a 22% YoY drop in net income to P2.1bn after seeing a 1% decline in sales to P25.7bn with -5.9% SSSG and higher opex for the period
  • The company will hold its briefing on Mar 28, Friday
MINING
Philex nears deal with new foreign investor in Silangan
  • Philex Mining Corp. (PX PM) is in talks with potential foreign investors, which will take in a minority stake in its wholly-owned unit, Silangan Mindanao Mining. Co. Inc.
    • According to Philex Chairman Manuel V. Pangilinan
  • Last year, Mr. Pangilinan indicated that Philex may raise an additional US$400mn to finance the second phase of the Silangan gold and copper project
    • Project is expected to start commercial production by 1Q2026
    • Philex plans to produce 700k MT of copper and gold annually in the first five years, ramping up to 1.3mn MT by year 9 (vs. 2024A Padcal mine production of 6.8mn MT)
  • The Silangan Copper and Gold Project is touted as one of the big three mining projects in the country
    • The project, situated in Surigao del Norte, consists of the following deposits: Boyongan, Bayugo-Silangan and Bayugo-Kalayaan
    • The first phase of the project focuses on the Boyongan deposit, which contains an estimated 81mn tons of mineable (high grade) material
TELCOS
Converge building more fiber ports in rural areas
  • Converge ICT Solutions Inc. plans to expand its fiber network to 11 million ports by 2027 with the aim to scale its subscriber base in rural areas
    • According to Converge CEO and co-founder, Dennis Anthony Uy, the company is establishing more fiber lines in third and fourth class municipalities over the next two years for network expansion
    • Dennis Anthony Uy mentioned the strategy of capitalizing on the lack of broadband services in the farthest towns in Visayas and Mindanao to increase subscriber count to four million by 2027
  • To date, Converge’s fiber network has reached 8.96 million lines, with 2.56 million being utilized by residential and enterprise customers
  • For 2025, Converge is raising its capex to a range of P20 billion to P25 billion, with P 12 billion allocated for infrastructure expansion. Converge is also investing P7 billion for the completion of two subsea cables and P5 billion for the construction of two data centers
  • Converge’s subscriber base rose by 20% in 2024 to 2.56 million, driven by flexible service offerings like FiberX, BIDA Fiber, and Surf2Sawa
  • The company aims to sustain growth through expanded coverage and strategic partnerships
  • Converge’s net income increased by 19% to P10.81 billion in 2024, supported by a 15% revenue growth to P40.61 billion, with potential plans to re-enter the bond market if interest rates become favorable
