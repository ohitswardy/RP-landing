REGIS REPORT

24 November 2025

The deal for ICTSI (ICT PM) to take over the Durban terminal is likely to be signed by next, according to the chairperson of Transnet, the entity controlled by the South African government that holds the port today. With the expiry of the deadline for APM Terminals to appeal the ruling allowing the Durban deal to be awarded to ICTSI, there should be no impediment for it to proceed.

 

ICTSI will need to pony up ZAR11.1bn (~US$630m) to complete the acquisition of 50% of the terminal.

 

+++

 

BusinessWorld reported that BPI (BPI PM) expects consumer loan growth of 10-15% in 2026, sharply lower than the 27% in 9M25 owing to the weaker GDP growth forecasts for 2026. This is in stark contrast to the bank’s messaging during the 3Q earnings call, and in our subsequent meetings, where management guided for continued rapid expansion in the “non-institutional” segment of the book. Granted, those meetings were made before the 4.0% 3Q25 GDP came out. Still, we would take the news with a healthy helping of salt. Another news outlet reported on the same event with a much more bullish slant (no growth forecasts, though).

 

We have contacted BPI management, and while there is no official guidance on consumer loan growth, they still expect consumer/MSME to be key loan growth drivers in 2026, outpacing institutional loan growth.

 

+++

 

The fallout from what’s arguably the biggest corruption scandal in the country ever is being felt in the economic numbers. Government infrastructure spending for the month of Sept dropped 35% YoY. The new Finance Secretary Frederick Go is also signaling downward revisions in government revenue targets and expenditures as a result of diminished economic activity.

 

+++

 

Meralco’s (MER PM) plan to inject its renewable energy (RE) projects into SP New Energy (SPNEC PM) is likely to wait until its huge solar farm project, Terra Solar, is up and running.  The two phases of Terra Solar are supposed to be operational by early 2027, though a few months delay would not be surprising.

ICTSI to sign Durban deal next month; Takeover in April
  • Transnet said that it plans to sign the concession agreement for Durban Container Terminal 2 (DCT-2) with ICTSI next month
    • According to Transnet Chairman Andile Sangqu
    • ICTSI will be “onboarded: at the facility from April 2026
  • We have not incorporated DCT-2 in our estimates and valuation
    • However, our preliminary calculations indicate a potential P30/sh uplift to our NAV of P560/sh (here)
  • ICTSI trades at 10x FY26E EV/EBITDA, below peer average (12x) and historical average of 11x
BANKS
BPI expects consumer loan growth to ease in 2026
  • The Bank of the Philippine Islands (BPI PM) is expecting consumer lending growth to slow next year as a weaker macroeconomic backdrop and geopolitical risks temper demand
  • For 2026, BPI expects its consumer loan book to grow by 10-15%, a slowdown from the 28% growth so far this year
    • As of 9M25, BPI's total noninstitutional loan portfolio is at P750bn
      • The bank plans to keep its loan mix at 30% for this segment next year
  • BPI Consumer Banking head Maria Cristina Go stated that growth could have been stronger but noted that the bank is taking a cautiously optimistic stance after the softer-than-expected 3Q GDP print of 4.0%
    • She said that the BSP's rate cut cycle could spur loan activity but she also noted that consumer confidence will be critical which will impact their appetite to spend (e.g., travel, new house/car, credit card spending)
MACRO
Finance Secretary expects gov't revenue target revisions and budget adjustments
  • Dept. of Finance Secretary Frederick Go said revenue target revisions and budget adjustments need to be made, as the ongoing corruption probe has weighed on economic activity and put collection goals at risk
    • Recall that the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) and Bureau of Customs (BOC) have been guided to likely to miss their targets due to lower remittances from the Dept. of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) and slower import growth
      • BIR's total collection as of 9M25 reached P2.32trn, +11% YoY, lagging the +13% 2025 growth target
      • Total customs collections as of 9M25 amounted to P704bn, 2% YoY, behind the +4.5% target growth for the year
  • Go emphasized that corrective actions need to be made (i.e., optimized spending, identifying areas where savings can be achieved) to address current gov't fiscal challenges
  • Executive Secretary Ralph Recto stated that the Budget Committee will review its macroeconomic targets and assumptions in Dec

 

 

MACRO
Infrastructure spending slump persists in September
  • Overall infrastructure spending (including LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) fell 9% YoY to P1.04tn in 9M25
    • Implies that infrastructure spending contracted by 35% YoY, 5% MoM to P100bn for the month of September alone
    • Cited suspension of payments for public works amid ongoing validation and audit related to corruption concerns
    • DPWH Secretary Vince Dizon lifted the suspension of procurement last September 16
  • Cumulative 9M25 spending of P1.04tn (-9% YoY) lags full-year target of P1.5tn (-2%)

GAMING
DigiPlus sees gradual recovery
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) said that its customer service team has been reaching out to its high value players to try to bring them back to PLUS’ platform
  • 3Q25 revenue of DigiPlus fell 23% QoQ (flat YoY) to P19.1bn, while net profit declined 59% QoQ (down 51% YoY) to P1.7bn
    • The BSP prohibited e-wallets from redirecting to online gaming platforms (beginning August 17)
  • DigiPlus President Andy Tsui said that the company was “…seeing a gradual recovery from the last two months”
  • Industry data from PAGCOR showed that September GGR of Bingo + e-games was P12.2bn, a 45% drop from the June figure
    • If the September GGR level were to be maintained in 4Q25, then GGR for the quarter would amount to P36.6bn, another 20% lower than the 3Q25 level
