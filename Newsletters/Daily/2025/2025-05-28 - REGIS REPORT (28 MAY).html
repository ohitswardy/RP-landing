REGIS REPORT

28 May 2025

The national government reported revenue collections of P522bn in April, down 3% due to the drop in non-tax revenue. Expenditures also dropped 8% to P455bn, resulting in a surplus of P67bn for the month. The government does expect non-tax revenue take to rise in the coming months as dividends from government corporations come in.

 

YT-April, the budget deficit amounted to P412bn, +79% over the previous year. 

 

The government is still aiming for a FY25 budget deficit of P1.5trn, equivalent to 5.3% of GDP.

 

+++

 

Total air passenger volumes (domestic and international) grew 11% to 16m in 1Q25, domestic traffic growing 14%.  Cebu Air (CEB PM) increased its market share to 40% in the quarter from 38% for FY24.

 

+++

 

The latest poll data from Pulse Asia shows the public “trust” rating of President Marcos, Jr inching up slightly to 32% in early May (vs 29% in April).  The comparable rating for Vice President Duterte dipped to 50% (vs 57%).

 

This most recent poll shows an extreme polarization of the public between those in Mindanao and those elsewhere.  Marcos’ trust rating in Mindanao under the survey is 3% while Duterte’s is at 97%.

MACRO
Gov't posts P67bn budget surplus in April
  • The government recorded a budget surplus of P67.3bn in April which is higher 57% YoY and a reversal from the P376bn deficit in March
    • This brings the 4M25 deficit to P411.5bn, wider 79% YoY
    • The Bureau of Treasury stated the higher cumulative deficit was due to "faster expansion in public spending to fuel economic activity and support priority programs of the Marcos administration"
  • Revenue collections in Apr inched down 2.8% YoY to P522.1bn, as non-tax revenues dipped 69% YoY
    • BIR (Bureau of Internal Revenue) collections rose 11% YoY to P420.5bn mainly due to higher corporate income tax collections as well as increased personal income tax and VAT collections on the back of the enhancement of digital services
    • Customs collections fell 7% to P74.7bn given fewer working days due to Holy Week and lower import volumes given global trade uncertainties
    • Non-tax revenues dropped by 69% to P24.1bn due to the earlier timing of remittances from Government Owned and Controlled Corporations (GOCCs) last year
      • The gov't expects this to improve in the succeeding months as dividends from various GOCCs are set to be remitted to the National Treasury soon
  • Expenditures declined 8% YoY in Apr to P454.8bn mainly driven by lower primary spending and interest payments
    • Primary spending for the month was down 4% YoY to P408.3bn
      • The decrease in primary spending can likely be attributed to the election ban on public spending which started late March till election day on May 12
    • Interest payments dipped 31% to P46.4bn as lower debt repayments in the month was due to the shift of payment timings due to the Lenten holidays
  • The gov't targets for the 2025 budget deficit to narrow to 5.3% (1.5trn) of GDP from 5.7% in 2024, and the government aims to further reduce this to 3.7% in 2028
     

TRANSPORT
Air pax volumes up 11% YoY in 1Q25
  • The Civil Aeronautics Board (CAB) reported that the country’s systemwide air passenger volumes rose 10.9% YoY to 15.98mn in 1Q25
    • Growth rate has normalized after the Covid rebound
    • Cebu Air (CEB PM) grew its market share to 40% in 1Q25 from 38% in FY24 and 34% in FY19
    • Cebu Air’s market share gains have been at the expense of PAL (market share at 25% in 1Q25 vs. 27% pre-Covid) and Air Asia Philippines (9% in 1Q25 from 13% pre-Covid)
    • Note that CAB data does not capture ticketed passengers that do not show up for their flights (i.e. no-show)
  • Domestic pax volumes rose 14% YoY to 8.5mn while international pax volumes grew 7% YoY to 7.5mn

 

 

POLITICS
Marcos' trust rating inches up 3ppt to 32% in May
  • In the latest Pulse Asia Research Survey, President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.'s trust rating improved slightly to 32% from 29% in April
    • Notably, this is still much lower than the 82% trust rating at the start of his term as well as his 42% trust rating in Feb prior to dropping to 25% in March following the arrest of Former President Rodrigo Duterte
    • Marcos' trust rating in Luzon stands at 41% in May from 34% in April, Visayas at 39% from 38%, Mindanao at 3% from 10%
  • Vice President Sara Duterte's trust rating saw a 7ppt dip to 50% in May from 57% in April
    • Duterte's trust rating in Luzon was at 29% from 37%, Visayas at 46% from 70%, Mindanao at 97% from 95%

 

 

POWER
Meralco seeking DOE nod to proceed with 800MW CSP bidding
  • Meralco is awaiting the issuance of certifications from the Department of Energy (DOE) to proceed with the competitive selection process (CSP) to procure 800MW of electricity
    • 200MW RE baseload starting Jan 26, 2026
    • 600MW baseload starting Feb 26, 2028
  • These two proposed CSPs are part of Meralco’s 2025 power supply procurement plan to secure over 2.1 GW of capacity spanning 2026-2028
    • Apart from the two CSPs, Meralco also plans to conduct CSPs for 450-MW mid-merit and 900-MW baseload within the year
    • It also received an unsolicited proposal for the supply of 1.43-MW RE baseload power
