REGIS REPORT

22 July 2025

Finance Secretary Ralph Recto said that he sees room for two more 25bp policy interest rate cuts this year, for a total of 100bps. This is more than the consensus (and REGIS) forecast of a total of 75bps for the whole of 2025E. He added that the cuts could continue even if the US Fed holds its own rates steady, citing low inflation in the Philippine (at 1.4% in June), while inflation could pick up in the US. The BSP’s next policy meeting is set for 28 August.

 

+ + +

 

Digital payments keep surging, up 52% YoY in June, and crossing P2 trillion for the first time to a new monthly record high. The YoY figure was helped by a relatively low base in 2024, with the June 2025 figure up just 1% Mom. That said, the absolute figure is still quite impressive. Transactions over PesoNet jumped 47% YoY to P1.1trn, while smaller-ticket InstaPay grew 58% YoY. More intriguingly, the number of transactions over InstaPay continues to explode, up 227% YoY. This is the third straight month of exceptionally strong volume growth, with little indication for the catalyst in April (apart maybe from elections??).

First Gen (FGEN PM) raised P15bn in loans for its Casecnan hydro plant. The loan will be lodged at the operating entity, and proceeds will be used to pay down loan at the parent FGEN level used for the Casecnan acquisition.

MACRO
Finance Secretary sees two more rate cuts this year
  • Department of Finance Secretary Ralph Recto guided that he expects 50bp more worth of cuts this year
    • This implies 100bp total rate cuts for 2025, bringing the key policy rate to 4.75% by year-end from the current 5.25%
  • Recto stated that even if the Fed remains steady with rates, it is still possible to proceed with monetary easing but it would depend on external developments
  • The next Monetary Board meeting is scheduled for Aug 28 (third out of six this year)
    • Recto added that the next cut could happen as early as next meeting
  • For 2025, Bloomberg median consensus and REGIS expects 75bp total worth of cuts to 5.00%

 

 

BANKS/DIGITAL BANKS
Digital payments rise 52% YoY in June
  • Payments made digitally over the National Retail Payments System (NRPS) rose by 52% YoY to P2trn in June, accelerating from the 38% pace in May
    • Bringing the 6M25 transaction value to P11.1trn, up 39% YoY
  • Payments through InstaPay, which handles real-time payments of up to P50k, rose 58% YoY to P916.6bn in June
    • By volume, InstaPay payments surged 227% YoY, even faster than the 217% pace in May
  • Large-ticket payment transactions that go through PESONet, climbed 47% YoY to P1.1trn in May, accelerating from the 27% growth seen in May
    • In terms of volume, PESONet transactions increased by 21% YoY to 9.6mn, faster than the 14% growth in May
POWER
First Gen secures P15bn loan for Casecnan
  • Fresh River Lakes Corp. (FRLC), the operator of the 165-megawatt (MW) Casecnan hydroelectric power plant (HEPP), has secured a P15bn loan
    • Loan agreements signed with BDO, BPI, and RCBC
    • FRLC is a wholly-owned unit of First Gen (FGEN PM)
  • Loan will be used to pay off the P10bn loan acquired by the parent to finance the Casecnan acquisition and for general corporate purposes
  • As of end-March 2025, FGEN’s consolidated net debt stood at US$1.4bn, with net debt-to-equity at 0.40x
