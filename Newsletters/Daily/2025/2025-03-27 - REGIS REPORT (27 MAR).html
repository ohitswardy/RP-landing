During yesterday’s briefing on FY24/4Q results, GT Capital (GTCAP PM) management attributed the surprise GPM expansion of Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP PM) in 4Q24 to the surge in sales of higher-margin Japan-sourced vehicles. Management sounded upbeat about the prospects of its key units – Metrobank (MBT PM), TMP, and Metro Pacific - but was less so about its property unit, given the very apparent challenges industry-wide.

 

We continue to like the company, given its exposure to the banking sector and the rising motorization trend in the country, despite the clear challenges posed by the industry shift to EVs. Valuations are now even more compelling with the stock now trading at an even wider 62% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,268/sh and at 4.3x PER.

MACRO
MSRP of imported rice to be cut to P45/kg
  • The Department of Agriculture (DA) stated that it will reduce the maximum suggested retail price (MSRP) for imported rice to P45/kg effective March 31
    • The current MSRP is P49/kg
    • The reduction was due to the continued decline in global rice prices which has been on a downtrend since India lifted its year-long export ban of non-basmati white rice
      • For context, Vietnam is the primary overseas rice supplier for the country and Vietnam export prices for rice have gone down significantly to $367/MT as of Mar 2025 from as high as $620/kg in Dec 2023
  • Recall that the DA first implemented an MSRP on imported rice of P58/kg back in Jan 20 and staggered the reductions to the upcoming P45/kg MSRP
    • An MSRP was implemented due to stubbornly high retail prices as much as P64/kg "despite rice prices softening, tariff reductions, and a stronger peso."
  • According to the DA, well-milled rice prices as of Mar 25 for local and imported commercial rice are at P40-54/kg and P43-46/kg, respectively, vs. P40-55/kg and P40-52/kg back in Jan 25

 

 

POLITICS
SWS survey for senatorial preferences sees 9 from the Marcos-admin slate
  • Based on the Social Weather Stations (SWS) poll conducted in March, 13 out of 64 senatorial candidates have a statistical chance of securing one of the 12 available seats in the upcoming midterm elections on May 12, 2025
  • This is the first survey conducted and published after the arrest of Former President Rodrigo Duterte and turnover to the International Criminal Court (ICC) in the Hague, Netherlands
    • Bong Go (42%) and Erwin Tulfo (42%) secured the top spots, ranking 1st-2nd
    • Ben Tulfo (34%), Tito Sotto (34%), and Lito Lapid (33%) followed closely, ranking 3rd-4th
    • Bong Revilla (32%) and Pia Cayetano (31%) placed 6th-7th
    • Ping Lacson (31%), Bato Dela Rosa (30%), and Willie Revillame (28%) ranked between 7th-10th
    • Abby Binay (27%), Manny Pacquiao (27%), and Camille Villar (27%) secured places 11th-13th
  • Only four of the probable 13 candidates are not part of the Marcos administration’s slate—Go and Dela Rosa are known Duterte allies, while Ben Tulfo, and Revillame are independent
  • Notably, comparing the March survey vs. the one conducted in February (before Duterte arrest), the composition of the top 13 remain unchanged but exhibited some changes in rankings such as Bong Go moving to the top spot and Camille Villar moving to 11-13th from 13th previously
BANKS
Edwin Bautista named new PNB CEO
  • The banking arm of LT Group (LTG PM)—Philippine National Bank (PNB PM), disclosed on Wednesday that Edwin R. Bautista, former president of Union Bank of the Philippines (UBP PM) will be the new company president and CEO
    • This comes after the resignation of president and director Florido Casuela (effective April 29); he will take on a less active role of board adviser in the bank to focus on personal business
  • Bautista joins PNB after his 27-year stint at UnionBank, where he helped drive the bank's digital transformation and retail banking expansion efforts (i.e., acquisition of Citibank Philippines' consumer business)
