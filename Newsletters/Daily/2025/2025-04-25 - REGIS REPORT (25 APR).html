REGIS REPORT

25 April 2025

Convenience store operator Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) announced that retirement of its chairman Jose “Titoy” Pardo by July. More importantly, Victor Paterno will take over as chairman of the board and also chairman of the executive committee. Paterno will relinquish his current role as president of SEVN and will be replaced by the COO, Lee Ying-Jung, in that role. Lee is, of course, an appointee of SEVN’s Taiwanese controlling shareholder, President Chain Store.

 

It’s unclear how much Paterno’s role could change beyond the change in his official title. He is young (at 58) and his active role has arguably been a key factor in SEVN’s growth over the years. We suspect Paterno will remain active in the day-to-day operations, even in his new position.

 

+++

 

The government announced a new food stamp program (dubbed “REFUEL”) that will provide e-vouchers for poor families that can be used to purchase food items. The three-year program is estimated to cost P75bn (US$1.3bn).

 

Together with the recently announced subsidized rice initiative, this new program will enhance food security and arguably help improve consumption. This would reinforce our view that the consumer companies will do well this year.  All of that will, however, come with a fiscal cost.

 

+++

 

ICTSI (ICT PM) chairman Enrique Razon says it’s too early to tell what the impact of the unilateral tariff hikes by the US government will be. Meanwhile, data from Mexico shows that container volume growth slowed to +8% in 1Q25.

 

+++

 

Bloomberry (BLOOM PM), also controlled by Razon, says it will launch its new online gaming platform by May or June. BLOOM wants a piece of the online gaming pie which is now bigger than the business of the integrated resorts (IR). Operators of the IRs have an advantage when it comes to government fees, as they are only charged 29% of gaming revenue (vs 38% for pure online gaming firms).

 

+++

 

Ayala Land (ALI PM) plans to launch another ultra luxury residential project in the Makati CBD. No timetable was disclosed but we note that the most expensive residential project ALI is currently selling, Park Villas, is likely still less than half sold today.

CONSUMER
Philippine Seven announces organizational changes
  • Philippine Seven Corporation's (PSC) Board of Directors approved organizational changes, effective July 17, 2025, to be addressed during the annual stockholders' meeting on the same day
    • Jose T. Pardo will retire as Chairman of the Board and Independent Director of PSC and assume the role of Chairmen Emeritus
    • Jose Victor P. Paterno, President and CEO of PSC, will transition to be the Chairman of the Board and Chairman of the Executive Committee
    • Ying-Jung Lee, Chief Operating Officer (COO) of PSC, will be elected as Director of PSE and appointed as the President of PSC
      • He was COO of PSC since 2020 and was the former Vice President for Supply Chain of PSC from 2014 to 2019
      • Prior to joining PSC back in 2010, he held various positions in President Chain Store Corporation, which is the 7-Eleven area licensee in Taiwan
    • Jose T. Pardo, Jorge L. Araneta, and Maria Cristina P. Paterno will be members of the advisory board of PSC
MACRO
NEDA approves enhanced P74.6bn e-voucher food stamp program
  • The National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA), chaired by President Ferdinand Marcos Jr., has approved the Reducing Food Insecurity and Undernutrition with Electronic Vouchers (REFUEL) Project
  • The program will be implemented from July 2025 to July 2028 and costs an estimated P74.6bn, to be funded by the Asian Development Bank, Agence Française de Développement, and the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries Fund for International Development
  • The REFUEL program is an enhancement of the pilot Philippine Food Strategic Transfer and Alternative Measures Program (STAMP) of the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), rolled out late last year
  • REFUEL targets to benefit around 750,000 food-insecure households, which is higher than STAMP's 300,000 household beneficiaries last year
    • These food-insecure households will be given an electronic benefit transfer (EBT) card with monthly food credits worth P3,000 that can be used to buy high-nutrition food items from DSWD's partner retailers
    • STAMP's beneficiaries had to fulfill two conditions to receive the food vouchers, 1) attend nutrition education sessions once a month and 2) participate in employment promotion activities at gov't job fairs or classes conducted by TESDA (Technical Education and Skills Development Authority)
TRANSPORT
ICTSI Chairman says too early to tell impact on operations from Trump’s tariffs; 1Q25 port volumes at Mexico port up 8%
  • During its annual stockholders’ meeting, ICTSI Chairman and President Enrique K. Razon said that it is too early to tell how President Donal Trump’s “reciprocal” tariffs will impact its business
    • The group has a diverse portfolio, with US trade accounting for only 3% of the total business
    • However, Mr. Razon acknowledged that “the only major impact that could be possible or potential would be our Manzanillo terminal in Mexico but so far it’s still wait and see.”
  • For 2025, Mr. Razon said that he is excited about “how we can further outdo ourselves” following ICTSI’s “best year ever” last year.
  • Data from the Mexican port authority showed that volumes at ICTSI’s port, Contecon Manzanillo (CMSA), grew 8% YoY in 1Q25 to 387k TEUs, trailing our FY25E of 1.69mn TEUs (+16% YoY)
    • 1Q25 volume growth for CMSA sharply moderated from the +16% pace in 4Q24 and +14% in FY24
    • Meanwhile, volume growth across all container ports in Mexico also sharply moderated to 3% YoY in 1Q25, after rising +12% in FY24
    • Excluding CMSA, combined volumes of ports in Mexico rose by just +2% YoY in 1Q25 from +12% in FY24
  • We estimate CMSA accounts for 12% of consolidated volumes, 21% of revenues, and 25% of EBITDA
    • Note that our forecasts assume the US-Mexico-Canada (USMCA) treaty will be cancelled only by July 2026
    • Every 5% decrease (increase) in CMSA’s FY25E volumes will shave off (increase) EBITDA by 1.6% and profit by 2.4%
  • ICTSI trades 7.2x Regis’ FY25E EV/EBITDA (consensus: 8.0x), lower than peer average of (10x) and historical average (11x)

GAMING
International Entertainment to expand Manila casino operations
  • International Entertainment (1009 HK) said it spend US$1bn to expand its hotel and casino operations in Manila
    • Its subsidiary, Marina Square Properties, acquired Casino Filipino New Coast in Manila City in 2024
  • New Coast Hotel will be refurbished and the number of hotel rooms will be expanded from 200 to 800
  • The gaming space will also be expanded
    • The number of gaming tables will increase from 80 to 110
    • The number of slot machines will also increase from 500 to 920
  • For reference, Solaire Entertainment City has about 800 hotel rooms, 400 gaming tables, and 2,700 gaming machines
  • New Coast Hotel is about 10km from PAGCOR Entertainment City
