REGIS REPORT

19 November 2025

DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) signed a HK$1.6bn (c. P12bn) convertible notes agreement that could allow it to acquire a 54% stake in International Entertainment Corporation (1009 HK), which owns and operates an integrated resort in Manila. DigiPlus’ is likely making the acquisition because integrated resort operators are charged 9ppt less on their e-casino business (as a % of GGR) than pure online gaming operators. Based on our preliminary estimates, this transaction could boost PLUS’ annual profit by c. P5bn (an increase of 40%). However, this higher profit could come at a steep price. Under its provisional gaming license, International Entertainment Corporation is required to spend at least US$1bn to develop the integrated resort, which could in result in losses that PLUS would need to partly bear.

 

+++

 

Manila Water (MWC PM) announced a management shake-up yesterday, with a new CEO taking over at the start of 2026.  Roberto Locsin will become CEO on Jan 1, 2026, replacing Jose Victor Emmanuel De Dios, who become CEO of Prime CoreGen.  Prime CoreGen is natgas power operation of the Razon group, of which MWC is also a part. Locsin is the current COO for the international operations of MWC.

 

+++

 

Bangko Sentral (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona floated the possibility of another policy rate cut in Dec when the last rate-setting meeting of the Monetary Board (MB) for the year will be held. A rate cut then would not be unexpected, as the market (and Regis) seems to become more convinced that the BSP will act in light of the poor 3Q GDP print and current issues surrounding public governance.

GAMING
DigiPlus Interactive subscribes to convertible notes of integrated resort operator
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) signed a convertible notes agreement to acquire a controlling stake in International Entertainment Corporation (1009 HK)
    • The transaction is subject to approvals by IEC shareholders, the Securities and Futures Commission of HK, and the HKEx (388 HK)
  • IEC, through its subsidiaries, owns and operates New Coast Hotel Manila, an integrated hotel and casino complex
  • New Coast Hotel Manila currently offers 203 guest rooms and operates 96 gaming tables, and 495 slot machines
    • The complex is currently under renovation – the casino will be completed in January 2026 and the hotel renovation will finish in 3Q26
    • Under its provisional gaming license, IEC is required to spend at least US$1bn to develop the integrated resort. The resort complex will have at least 250,000sqm of GFA and at least 800 5-star hotel rooms
  • The subscription to HK$1.6bn (c. P12bn) convertible notes will be issued in two tranches
    • One of the conditions for the first tranche (HK$800mn) is that minority shareholders will approve a “whitewash waiver” (PLUS will not be required to issue a tender offer after conversion of the notes)
  • PLUS has the option to convert the notes and could hold c. 53.9% of IEC’s issued capital stock, based on an agreed initial conversion price of HK$1.00/share (lower than the last price of HK$1.20/share)
    • The notes carry an interest rate of 3% per annum
    • If PLUS will not convert, the notes will be redeemable at 108% at the end of their five-year maturity
    • The notes can be converted beginning 31 days after issuance
  • For the year ending June 2025, IEC reported a net loss of HK$282mn (c. P2.1bn)
  • Integrated resort operators are charged c. 9ppt less on their e-casino GGR than pure online operators
    • If this were to be applied to DigiPlus Interactive’s trailing 12 months operations (including 54% of the net loss of IEC), we estimate that net profit would have been about P19.4bn (c. 40% higher than the actual trailing 12m profit of P14.0bn)
UTILITIES
Manila Water appoints new CEO amid parent’s reorganization
  • Manila Water appointed Mr. Roberto Jose Locsin as president and CEO effective Jan 1, 2026
    • Mr. Locsin has been the company’s Chief Administrative Officer and COO for International Business since 2021
    • Prior to MWC, Mr. Locsin served as the chief executive officer of Philippine growth terminals of ICTSI (ICT PM)
    • He also held senior consulting roles with Capgemini and Ernst & Young Advisory in New York, where he focused on strategy, business transformation and mergers and acquisitions
  • Mr. Locsin will replace Mr. Jose Victor Emmanuel de Dios, who will assume the role of president and CEO at Prime CoreGen
    • Prime CoreGen is a subsidiary of Prime Infrastructure that holds the latter’s gas assets
MACRO
BSP Governor says rate cut possible in December
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona stated that another 25bp rate cut is 'possible' in December
    • Another cut in Dec would mark the fifth consecutive cut since April
    • Rates have been lowered by a total of 175bp since the start of the easing cycle back in Aug 2024
  • Meanwhile, a cut in the reserve requirement ratio (RRR) from 5.% was said to still being deliberated
  • Remolona also commented that the BSP is not worried about the peso's recent movement but noted that minimal intervention has been done
  • The last Monetary Board meeting for the year will be held on Dec 11
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect 125bp total worth of cuts to 4.5%
    • For 2026, consensus expects a 25bp cut to 4.25%, while Regis expects 50bp cuts total to 4.00%
