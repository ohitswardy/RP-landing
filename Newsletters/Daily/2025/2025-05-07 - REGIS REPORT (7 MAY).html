REGIS REPORT

07 May 2025

We have a slew of 1Q results comments today, leading off with Ayala Land (ALI PM), whose numbers corroborate our views on continuing softness in the residential segment (very evident in the results of Robinsons Land (RLC PM)). The top line numbers of grocery retailer Puregold (PGOLD PM), on the other hand, are looking solid. 

 

Here are our brief summaries:

 

  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) (=) – 1Q25 net profit rose 10% to P6.9bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. Commercial lot presales grew 233% YoY to P5bn, cushioning the decline in residential presales, which fell 6% YoY to P31.2bn. The YoY drop came from (“core”) mid-income Metro Manila vertical projects while premium projects performed better on a QoQ and YoY basis.

  • Puregold (PGOLD PM) (=) – Profits in 1Q amounted to P2.64bn, up 6.5%, on revenue growth of 11% (to P52bn).  SSSG was +6% for the Puregold stores and +4% for S&R, respectable if not solid numbers.  We view the double-digit top line expansion as a sign of the company gaining share in the market despite the rise of small format operators as well as hard discounters.

 

  • Semirara (SCC PM) (-) – Semirara’s 1Q25 core profit of P4.27bn (-34% YoY) disappointed due to a sharper-than-expected decline in mining ASPs At yesterday’s briefing, management explained that apart from the drop in benchmark coal prices, they also sold more low-quality coal. They expect to start extracting coal from another mine, which has higher-grade coal, by 2H26.

  • First Gen (FGEN PM) (=) – Recurring income came in at 78m for 1Q25, down 4%. The decline was attributed to a fall in profits at its geothermal business (EDC).  Its natgas operations saw a modest 7% rise in contributions while contributions from hydro jumped 38% due to the acquisition of Casecnan.

 

  • Robinsons Land (RLC PM) (=) – During the briefing, RLC revealed that the weak residential performance was partly due to cancellations, particularly for the JV projects, which saw a mere P47m of net presales in 1Q25.

  • Wilcon Depot (WLCON PM) (-) – The weak results (in 1Q and over the last year) prompt us to cut our profit estimates by 44-45% (to P2.4bn in FY25E and P2.7bn in FY26E) and target price by 55% to P10/share.  The near-term outlook remains difficult, but we maintain our positive view on the long-term trajectory of the business. As was noted in our comments yesterday, the CPI inflation figure of 1.4% for April was a positive surprise for the market – a surprise that will prompt downward revisions in inflation expectations for the year.

 

Food (especially rice) prices have been declining and are likely to remain low (if not go lower) in the coming months. Weakness in the USD and energy prices should also ease apprehensions about further rate reductions by the Bangko Sentral (BSP). The odds of a cut in policy rate in June are shorter now.

POWER
FGEN 1Q25 core profit dips to US$77.5mn
  • Headline 1Q25 profit fell 8% YoY to US$82mn while recurring profit dipped 4% YoY to US$78mn
    • Decline was attributed largely to lower profits at its geothermal unit, Energy Development Corp. (EDC)
  • Profit contributions from its natgas assets rose by 7% to US$46mn
    • FGEN LNG contributed US$7mn to core profit in 1Q25 from US$6mn in 1Q24 and declared commercial operations in January
    • Also cited lower financing costs following debt payments
    • San Gabriel’s core net loss widened on lower spot prices
  • EDC’s contributions dropped 23% YoY on lower WESM prices and sales volumes, as well as higher operating costs and expansion-related financing expenses
  • Hydro earnings jumped 38% due to the full-quarter contribution of 165MW Casecnan (US$4mn).
    • The 132MW Pantabangan-Masiway plant contributed US$7mn, flat YoY as higher output was offset by lower spot prices

 

 

MACRO
Inflation rate decelerates further to 1.4% in April
  • Headline inflation eased to 1.4% in April from 1.8% in the previous month of Mar, marking the lowest print since Nov 2019 (1.2%)
    • This is lower than the consensus forecast of 1.8% and stands at the lower-end of the BSP's forecast range of 1.3-2.1%
    • Inflation has continued to be on a downtrend since the start of the year, falling from 2.9% in Jan to 1.4% as of Apr
  • Primary drivers for easing inflation were MoM price declines in key food staples—rice -3.1%, meat -0.3%, and vegetables -5.4%
    • Notably, rice prices are on their tenth consecutive MoM price decline since Jul 2024
      • Rice prices are now down 10.9% YoY in Apr
      • According to the Dept. of Agriculture (as of May 3), prices for local and imported well-milled rice ranged from P37-54/kg and P38-48/kg, respectively vs. P40-52/kg and P40-54/kg back in Jan 3
  • Core inflation was unchanged at 2.2% in Apr vs. Mar. While core non-food inflation in Apr inched up slightly to 2.1% from 2.0% in Mar
  • This brings the YT-Apr headline inflation rate to 2.0%, at the lower-end of the 2-4% gov't target for this year
    • Consensus expects 2025 inflation to be 2.8%
      • Regis expects the full year average to be 3.0%
  • The next Monetary Board meeting (3rd out of 6 this year) is on Jun 19, Thursday
    • For 2025, consensus expects 75 bps total worth of cuts to 5.00%
    • We expect 50 bps worth of cuts to 5.25% but more could come if inflation stays benign or if the US Fed cuts rates more than 75 bps

 

 

 

 

 

CONSUMER
Alfamart targets 300 store openings this year
  • Alfamart Philippines Chief Operating Officer Harvey T. Ong stated that they plan to open around 300 new stores this year
  • Ong also mentioned that they may rationalize some of the older stores in its network and expects to have 250 net new store openings, 300 new stores and 50 stores that will be rationalized
  • He emphasized that Alfamart's diverse product offerings and more competitive prices [compared to convenience stores] will sustain growth
  • Alfamart had 2,092 stores as of end-2024
    • Recall that Alfamart opened ~400 stores in 2024
  • Alfamart, part of the SM Group’s (SM PM) retail food segment, operates as a joint venture with Indonesia-based PT Sumber Alfaria Trijaya Tbk
