It’s looking like Maynilad’s IPO will finally be pushing through, with the help of cornerstone investors International Finance Corp (IFC) and Asian Development Bank (ADB).

 

The IFC and ADB may reportedly subscribe to as much as US$245mn worth of shares at a subscription price of up to P15/share. Assuming the two actually sign up for that amount (US$245mn) at P15/share, the size of the IPO available to other investors would only be around ~US$255mn – a figure we expect would be quite manageable to place.

 

At the P15/share mark, however, the implied pre-IPO valuation of Maynilad would be less than half of our valuation estimate for the company. Maynilad represents 24% of our NAV estimate for DMCI Holdings (DMC PM).

The government extended the rice import ban by another month to the end of Nov. The ban is supposed to help protect rice farmers who are facing low prices due to falling international prices as well as much improved harvests this year. Low rice prices should keep CPI inflation tame over the next few months.

 

+++

 

Notwithstanding announcements of job cuts by Accenture hitting business headlines, our channel checks point to the firm actually increasing its office footprint. BPOs/outsourcing firms are the key source of demand for office in the Philippines and, as such, the health of their business will have a significant influence on office vacancies and rents.

UTILITIES
Maynilad says IFC, ADB may subscribe to IPO shares at up to P15/sh
  • International Finance Corp. (IFC) and the Asian Development Bank (ADB) plan to invest up to US$245mn in Maynilad Water’s IPO
    • IFC agreed to subscribe to US$100mn worth of shares in Maynilad at a subscription price of up to P15/sh
    • ADB is also considering participating as a cornerstone investor for up to US$145mn or up to P15/sh
    • According to Maynilad’s latest prospectus filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission
    • We estimate IFC and ADB could end up owning ~5% and 7%, respectively, of Maynilad
  • ADB is in the process of obtaining approval from its Board of Directors
  • At P15/sh and assuming full subscription of the IPO shares (1.9bn primary and 355mn secondary), Maynilad’s market capitalization could reach P113bn, with a public float of 30%
    • We estimate that an IPO price of P15/sh implies valuations of 1.1x EV/OCP, ~8x FY25E PER, and a ~6% div yield
    • In contrast, Manila Water (MWC PM) is trading at ~5x EV/OCP, 8x FY25E consensus’ PER, and a ~4% div yield (consensus)
  • The offer period for the IPO is scheduled from Oct. 23 to 31, 2025, with the listing date set for Nov. 7, 2025
MACRO
Gov't extends rice import ban for 30 days
  • The Dept. of Agriculture (DA) stated that the current two-month rice import ban was extended for at least 30 days till end-Nov
    • Recall that a 60-day suspension of rice imports from Sep 1 to Oct 30 was approved by the President to boost farmgate prices given the current peak harvest season of palay (unmilled rice)
  • DA Secretary Tiu Laurel noted that palay farmgate prices have dropped back to P8-10/kg after having previously improved to P13-17/kg
    • A significant number of rice farmers remain unprofitable given the P14/kg production cost of palay and difficulties in drying their harvest amid heavy rains
  • Tiu Laurel guided that the ban could last until end of the year depending on market conditions
  • He also stated that a floor price for palay and a higher rice import tariff may be implemented soon or by the end of the import ban
    • The original duty on imported rice was 35% but reduced to 15% after EO No. 62 in Jul 2024 to reduce retail prices of rice

MREIT aims to double its portfolio by 2027

PROPERTY
Accenture cuts 22,000 jobs globally in six months
  • During its quarterly earnings call, Accenture reported that it had 779,273 employees globally as of August 2025
    • May 2025 – 790,692
    • February 2025 – 801,099
    • August 2024 – 774,303
  • Accenture CFO Angie Park said that the reduction in headcount was due to “talent rotation” related to its AI push as well as the divestiture of two acquisitions “that are no longer aligned with our strategic priorities”
  • The company expects headcount to grow in 2026
  • In March 2025, Accenture reported that it lost contracts with the US government
  • Accenture is one of the largest BPO firms in the Philippines and employs over 50,000 Filipinos
  • Our channel checks suggest that Accenture seeks to expand its office footprint in the Philippines, implying that the company intends to increase its headcount
  • We estimate that Accenture occupies c. 5% of RLC’s office portfolio and 3% of MEG’s office portfolio
  • MEG is trading at 66% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 3.4X FY25E P/E
  • RLC is trading at 66% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY25E P/B, and 5.2X FY25E P/E
TELCOS
ePLDT launches first locally hosted AI platform—Pilipinas AI
  • ePLDT, Inc., ICT service provider and data center subsidiary of PLDT (TEL PM), has launched Pilipinas AI, which is the country's first "sovereign" artificial intelligence (AI) platform that allows enterprises to build and deploy AI models within the country using locally hosted infrastructure and data
    • The platform was developed with Dell Technologies and Katonic AI, hosted at ePLDT’s 50-MW VITRO Sta. Rosa data center in Laguna
  • This expands ePLDT's GPU-as-a-Service offering, an on-demand computing model which gives businesses access to advanced graphics processing units (GPUs) for AI applications
  • ePLDT President and CEO Victor Genuino stated that Pilipinas AI will provide enterprises access to computing power and AI tools without heavy infrastructure investment
    • He stated that the AI platform is expected to support multiple industries such as banking, BPO, healthcare, public services, and academia with potential use cases such as fraud detection, weather forecasting, customer service, medical diagnostics
