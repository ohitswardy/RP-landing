REGIS REPORT

07 August 2025

Here is today’s tally of 2Q/1H results, with majority chalking up disappointing numbers:

 

  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) (-) – 1H25 net profit rose 8% to P14.2bn, slightly behind REGIS and consensus forecasts, due to weak development revenue in 2Q. However, residential reservation sales saw a sequential improvement of 11%, with sales of core (mid-income) projects showing a 39% QoQ (+11% YoY) increase. Management said that cash discounts on ready-for-occupancy units in Metro Manila generated sales, which remained strong in July.

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (=) – Income for 1H rose 6% to P43bn, which is likely in line with Street estimates. Profits for SM Retail grew 10% for 1H on the back of 10% top line growth. While 1H earnings for BDO (BDO PM) were a little short, profits for SM Prime (SMPH PM) came ahead of expectations. Some more detail below.

 

  • Globe (GLO PM) (-) – Globe’s recurring income was down 11% to P10.4bn in 1H as revenue for the core telecom business shrank 2%. As has happened in previous periods, it was the non-telco businesses which help mitigate the drop. Another bright spot was the strong net adds for the wireline broadband business.

The APAC BFB business delivered 5% sales growth in 2Q25 but the Meat Alt segment (“Quorn”) was still shrinking in the latest quarter.

+++

 

President Marcos suspended all rice imports for a period of 60 days from Sept 1. This effective ban (albeit temporary) on rice imports is arguably bigger than any nominal increase in rice tariffs, which are currently at 15%. The move was announced in order to help local farmers. We should expect rice prices to creep up as a result. 

 

+++

 

The Philippine Senate voted (19-4) to “archive” the impeachment case against Vice President Sara Duterte-Carpio. The move is an effective dismissal of the impeachment case following the recent Supreme Court ruling declaring the proceedings “unconstitutional” due to a technicality.

CONGLOS
SM Investments 1H profits rise 6% to P42.6bn
  • Earnings rise 6% to P42.6bn.  Profits for 1H25 rose 6% to P42.6bn, with bulk of earnings still from banking (50%) and property (28%). For 2Q alone, profits were P22.5bn, +3% YoY.  We would characterize the net income for the half as within Street estimates.  Retail segment income rose 10% to P8.4bn and accounted for 15% of profit.

 

  • Retail revenues rose 8%. Revenues of SM Retail rose 8% for 2Q25, the same pace as in 1Q25. We estimate group SSSG was 6.7% for 2Q, an acceleration from the 4.1% in 1Q.

 

Within the retail business, it was the department stores (SM Store) which saw a temporary bump due to the shift in the school year (June vs July), which should reverse in 3Q.  See tables below.

 

Food retail (i.e. groceries) revenue saw +9% growth for 2Q, +8% for 1H.  SSSG for Food retail was approximately +5.1% in 2Q.  The top line expansion is a creditable performance and is likely to have been led by Alfamart. To compare, the Food segment of RRHI saw 4% revenue growth in 2Q on 4% SSSG.

 

Overall profit for SM Retail was P8.4bn for 1H, up +10%. For 2Q alone, earnings were P4.8bn (+5%).

 

  • Alfamart update.  Alfamart aims to add 200 stores in FY25.  SM’s Alfamart operation ended the period with 2229 stores, implying a net addition of 137 for the half (+85 for the quarter).  It intends to add around 200 stores for the year (~+10%). SSS for Alfamart was +1% growth in 2Q and roughly flat for 1H. While not directly comparable, the CVS segment of RRHI saw SSS decline 3.5% in 2Q and 3.3% in 1H.

 

  • SM Prime update. Property subsidiary SM Prime says sales cancellations for 1H25 improved and were “25% lower” without giving specific numbers.  The company also says it currently has unsold inventory of ~27,000 units.

 

 

CONGLOS
DMCI 2Q25: Cancellations pull down profits of property unit
  • DMCI reported 1H25 net income of P9.1bn, down 18% YoY
    • Excluding one-offs and FX, we estimate recurring income fell 18% to P9.0bn
  • 2Q25 profit dropped 19% QoQ, 28% to P4.0bn due to revenue declines at Semirara (SCC PM, results comment), as well as the property and cement units
    • Property unit saw profits decline 51% QoQ and 8% YoY on higher sales cancellations (MD&A did not provide reservation sales figures)
    • Despite a 20% YoY increase in revenues, profit contributions from the construction unit fell 93% YoY to P18mn due to higher provisions
    • Nickel unit sustains profit turnaround in 2Q25, with ASPs up 70% YoY to US$39/MT and sales volumes jump 62% YoY  
  • Management will hold a briefing today (Aug 7) at 4pm (registration link)

 

 

TRANSPORT
Cebu Air: Fares surprisingly jump 12% in 2Q25; books P5bn in extraordinary income
  • Headline 1H25 net income more than doubled to P8.97bn
    • The headline figure includes a net P4.9bn extraordinary income related to the value of engines CEB secured from Pratt & Whitney
    • These engines were given to CEB for free as part of their compensation for the ongoing aircraft-on-ground (AOG) issues
  • 1H25 core profit up 34% to P4.23bn, trending ahead of Regis (P7.5bn) and consensus (P5.6bn) estimates
  • In 2Q25 alone, recurring profit grew 214% YoY to P3.72bn, underpinned by a 26% jump in revenues
    • Pax volumes grew 16% YoY on seat growth of 17%; Seat load factors were solid at 86%
    • Average fares jumped 12% YoY, reversing the declines since 3Q24, when CEB introduced new routes and expanded to new hubs
    • EBITDA jumped 61% to P10.7bn, as jet kero prices declined and operating leverage kicked in
  • Management is holding a briefing today (Aug 7) at 3pm (registration link)

 

 

MINING
NIKL posts profit turnaround in 2Q25 but 1H25 recurring profit of P1.3bn trails REGIS estimates
  • Reported 1H25 profit of P2.1bn (+88%) included an P800mn gain from the sale of its HPAL plants
    • Stripping out the gain, we estimate recurring profit was up 16% YoY to P1.3bn, trending behind REGIS estimates
    • We attribute the shortfall to lower-than-expected profits at its power unit and larger-than-expected equitized losses from the HPAL plants
  • In 2Q25, profit rebounded to P1.6bn, up 75% YoY and reversing the loss in 1Q25 (bad weather affected 1Q25)
    • Mining ASPs jumped 51% YoY to US$27/MT, taking 1H25 ASPs to US$24, trending well above forecast
    • However, sales volumes remained weak in 2Q25, dipping by 3% YoY to 5.4mn MT
  • NIKL will hold a briefing on Aug 14, 2:30pm (registration link)

 

 

MACRO
Rice imports to be suspended but no decision yet on imported rice tariff hike
  • President Marcos Jr. ordered the suspension of all rice importation for 60 days (effective Sept 1, 2025) to protect local farmers from low palay (unmilled rice) prices
  • Recall that the Dept. of Agriculture (DA) recently proposed for an import freeze on rice and a gradual increase of the imported rice duties back to 35% from the current level of 15%
  • No decision has been made yet regarding the tariff increases on imported rice as Marcos and the DA is looking to "strike the right balance" in protecting both local famers and consumers
    • DA Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel stated that if the import ban brings higher farmgate prices and income for farmers, they may no longer need to raise the tariff

 

 

POLITICS
Senate votes to archive impeachment complaint against VP Sara Duterte
  • Nineteen senators voted in favor of Senator Joel Villanueva's motion to archive the complaint against Duterte while four voted against with one abstention
    • Villanueva's motion was an amendment to the original proposal of Senator Rodante Marcoleta to have the case dismissed
    • Marcoleta reiterated the Supreme Court's (SC) ruling on July 25 which stated that the impeachment of Vice President Sara Duterte was "unconstitutional, is void ab initio (void from the beginning), is violative of due process"
  • According to Senate President Chiz Escudero, a motion to bring the impeachment case out of the archives may be made if the SC decides to reverse its decision
    • The Lower House filed a motion for reconsideration on Aug 4
GAMING
GCash to add responsible gaming features
  • Globe CFO Juan Carlo Puno said that GCash will roll out a few more features that actively promote responsible gaming
  • It will soon include notifications and informational materials about responsible gaming practices. These prompts will appear upon entering GCash’s GLife section

  • Users will also have the ability to set top up limits in terms of amounts or frequency
