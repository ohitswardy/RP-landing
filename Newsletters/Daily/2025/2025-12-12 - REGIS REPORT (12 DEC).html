REGIS REPORT

12 December 2025

The Monetary Board (MB) of the Bangko Sentral (BSP) again cut policy rates 25bp at yesterday’s meeting, hours after the US Fed cut its target Fed funds rate by a similar amount.  The policy rate is now at 4.50%, the lowest since Sept 2022.

BSP Governor Eli Remolona signaled that the BSP is at or close to the end of this easing cycle.  However, we expect headline inflation to remain benign into early 2026, and this might help the BSP hold on to its dovish outlook for a little longer.  The MB will hold its next meeting in Feb.

MACRO
BSP cuts rates further to 4.50%; PH economy expected to recover in 2H26
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) cut the key policy rate by 25bp to 4.50%, the lowest since Sep 2022
    • This is the fifth consecutive 25bp cut this year (125bp total) since the decision to resume easing back in Apr 2025
    • The BSP has lowered rates by a total of 200bp since the start of the easing cycle in Aug 2024
  • BSP Governor Eli Remolona emphasized that the possibility of further cuts next year will remain hinged on the data and would be more likely "when things are worse than we thought" (e.g., 3Q GDP growth worse-than-expected)
  • On the economic growth outlook, it was noted to have weakened further as business sentiment remains negative given the lingering impact of the corruption scandal
    • Remolona also commented that "the new data suggests that even 4Q will see weak growth" 
    • Domestic demand is expected to rebound slowly with the lag effect of easing alongside improvement in the "pace and quality of public spending"
      • The recovery was noted to likely take place in 2H26 vs 1H26 as previously expected
    • The inflation outlook was said to still be benign with expectations well-anchored
      • The BSP still expects below target inflation for this year (1.6%) but slightly revised its inflation forecasts for 2026 and 2027 to 3.2% and 3.0% from 3.1% and 2.8%, respectively
    • The first out of the six Monetary Board meetings in 2026 will be likely be held during the 2nd week of Feb
      • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect 125bp total worth of cuts to 4.50% and inflation to average 1.7% and 1.8%, respectively
      • For 2026, consensus expectations are one 25bp cut to 4.25% and inflation at 2.6%, while Regis expects two 25bp cuts to 4.00% and inflation to settle at 3.0%

 

TRANSPORT
ICTSI Mexico port volumes drop 6% YoY in November but expansion prospects still firmly positive
  • Data from Mexico’s port authority showed that container volumes at ICTSI’s Mexico port, Contecon Manzanillo (CMSA), fell 6% YoY to 124k TEUs
    • In contrast, volumes in other ports in Manzanillo rose 23% YoY, indicating market share loss
    • Combined volumes across all ports in Manzanillo rose 10% YoY to 324k TEUs
  • Brings YT-November volumes at CMSA to 1.4mn TEUs, up 4% YoY
  • Despite the volume softness in 4Q25 so far, we remain positive on CMSA’s 12-month outlook, supported by the anticipated completion of its ~20% capacity expansion by year-end
  • We estimate CMSA accounts for 12% of volumes, 20% of revenues, and 24% of EBITDA
  • ICTSI trades at 10.8x FY26E EV/EBITDA and 18x PER vs. peer average (12.6x EV/EBITDA / 18x PER) and historical average (11x EV/EBITDA / 20x PER)

TRANSPORT
Mexican government approves higher levies on Asian imports
  • Mexico’s Senators voted in favor of the bill that imposes tariffs of between 5% and 50% on more than 1,400 products from Asian nations that don’t have a trade deal with Mexico
    • New levies will take effect starting next year, affecting products from clothing to metals and auto parts, with Chinese goods the most affected
  • ICTSI’s port in Mexico is estimated to generate ~30% of throughput from imports of Chinese goods (i.e. 48% of total throughput are imports, of which ~60% originate from China)
PROPERTY/REITS
AREIT shareholders approve share-for-property swap
  • AREIT shareholders approved a previously announced share-for-property swap
  • AREIT will acquire P19.5bn worth of assets from Ayala Land in exchange for 441mn new AREIT shares
    • This will increase AREIT’s AUM by 15% to P158bn
    • AREIT’s shares outstanding will increase by 12% to 4.16bn shares
  • This implies that the AREIT shares will be priced at P44.15/share
    • This is 4% higher than the last closing price of P42.50/share
  • AREIT will acquire two malls with a combined GLA of 375,000sqm
    • Ayala Center Cebu and Ayala Malls Feliz
  • The companies aim to complete the transaction in 2H26
  • We estimate that the Ayala Group would still need to sell another c. 50mn AREIT shares in order for the latter to meet minimum public ownership requirements
  • ALI is trading at 61% discount to NAV, 0.9X FY26E P/B, and 10.0X FY26E P/E
  • AREIT currently offers 5.8% FY26E dividend yield
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 6.0%
GAMING
PAGCOR believes industry GGR could hit low P400bn level in 2026
  • PAGCOR Chairman Alejandro Tengco said that industry GGR could hit the low P400bn level in 2026
    • Mr. Tengco’s statement suggests that he expects a recovery from the 3Q25 level
  • 9M25 industry GGR grew 16% to P309bn
  • 3Q25 GGR fell 15% QoQ (flat YoY) to P94.5bn
    • 3Q25 GGR of licensed casinos grew 3% QoQ (-10% YoY) to P45.6bn
    • 3Q25 GGR of bingo + e-games fell 28% QoQ (+14% YoY) to P45.7bn
      • September GGR of bingo + e-games fell 45% (from June) to P12.2bn
  • Mr. Tengco also said that the sector has only recovered 10%-15% of the revenues that were lost since the e-wallets were ordered to delink from gaming platforms
