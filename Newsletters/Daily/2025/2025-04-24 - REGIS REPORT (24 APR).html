The Dept of Agriculture (DA) will begin a pilot program selling rice for P20 (US$0.35) per kg. This pilot program will be conducted in the Visayas region before it is extended nationally. We reckon P20/kg is around 35-40% below market price. This pilot program is supposed to cost the government around P3.5bn – P4.5bn, so rolling it out nationally would likely cost multiples of that.

The sale of rice at P20/kg is among the key election promises of President Marcos. From an electoral perspective, this program comes a little late as it is now less than three weeks before the mid-term elections.

We release a new note updating our numbers for Jollibee (JFC PM), trimming our EPS estimates by 8%/2% for FY25/FY26E.

 

Our positive view on the outlook for JFC’s domestic business has only been reinforced by recent results and we reckon this segment can be reasonably expected to deliver >10% profit CAGR over the medium. What we are apprehensive about is the international business. Undoubtedly, there are wins, including Joliibee in North America. But two of its bigger overseas operations saw things deteriorate in 2H and management seems resolute about not cutting losses there.

 

We are adjusting our target price for JFC to P250/sh (-17%), which offers modest upside. We believe there’s value at current levels, but realizing this value will depend much on how management is able to deal with the struggling businesses.

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) is set to release a draft circular to cut fees on electronic fund transfers. A mandate to reduce such fees has been discussed before as a means of improving financial inclusion.

 

Reducing such transfer fees will have implications on non-bank e-wallets like GCash. Cutting such fees will reduce friction costs of using electronic payments, including the transfer of funds from banks into e-wallets (and vice versa). It will simultaneously reduce the cost of using bank apps to make payments directly, without having to go through e-wallets. How that will affect the competitive landscape is unclear.

 

What’s arguably clearer is that (1) the margins made on payments will be squeezed and (2) the costs to consumers will be reduced. Digital adoption (and financial inclusion) will be the winners.

MACRO
DA to sell rice at P20/kg starting in Visayas
  • Agriculture Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. stated that the Department of Agriculture (DA) will start to sell rice at P20/kg in Visayas as a pilot run before implementing the program nationwide
    • Details of the launch and points of sale have not been disclosed as of late
    • The DA estimates that it will spend around P3.5bn-P4.5bn for the pilot program
  • Factors that allowed the launch of the program were easing global rice prices as well as the National Food Authority's (NFA) currently abundant rice buffer stock due to ongoing harvest season
  • The program was initially set until Dec 2025 but President Marcos requested an extension until Feb 2026
    • Laurel also added that Marcos directed the DA to draft a roadmap to sustain the program until 2028
  • As of April 23, prices of local well-milled rice ranges from P37-54/kg while imported is P42-46/kg
BANKS/DIGITAL BANKS
BSP to set new pricing scheme for lower fund transfer fees
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas is set to release a draft this week on a new pricing mechanism targeted at lowering fees for electronic fund transfers
  • BSP Deputy Governor Mamerto Tangonan did not disclose full details but stated that the pricing mechanism will apply to all transaction sizes and is to be straightforward as well as easy to implement
    • Tangonan said fees will be low but may not be zero, as the rates will hinge on "competitive forces"
    • Current InstaPay fees hovers around P25 per transaction
  • He emphasized that lowering fees will be key in converting non-users to expand the country's digital payments base
  • The central bank has a goal for online payments to make up 60-70% of the country's total retail transaction volume by 2028
    • Note that the share of digital payment transactions to total monthly retail payments in the country grew from 42% in 2022 to 53% percent in 2023 (from a BSP report dated July 2024)
    • The value of electronic fund transfers through PESONet and InstaPay surged 39% YoY to P5.29trn as of end-Mar 2025
