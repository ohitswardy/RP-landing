REGIS REPORT

07 November 2025

The government statistics agency announced that GDP growth for the 3Q was 4.0%. This is well below the 5.2% Street expectations and is likely to be a shock for the market, which was already expecting a deceleration (but not to this level). We expect the fingers to be pointed at the ongoing corruption scandal and the ancillary effects from it.

 

+++

 

Maynilad Water (MYNLD PM), the operator of the West Zone water concession in Metro Manila, debuts in the PSE today, listing 1.9bn primary and 355mn secondary shares at an IPO price of P15/sh, valuing the company at P113bn and resulting in a 30% public float. 

 

Valuations are undemanding at just 1.1x its concession value (or Opening Cash Position, OCP) and with an attractive yield of ~6%.  It is also inexpensive even on less relevant valuation metrics, at 8x PER and 6x EV/EBITDA

 

+++

 

We have plenty of results in today’s round-up.  Port operator ICTSI (ICT PM) continues to deliver, as expected, ahead of the likely award of the Durban concession. Meanwhile, one can’t help but wonder at the contrast between the two consumer bellwether firms, Universal Robina (URC PM) and Century Pacific (CNPF PM). Finally, two of the three telcos have also announced results: Converge (CNVRG PM) and Globe (GLO PM). Revenue growth for Converge saw a visible slowdown while for Globe, the most watched number would be the profit contribution of Mynt (down QoQ).

 

 

  • ICTSI (ICT PM) (=) - 9M25 recurring profit rose 24% to US$752mn, with 3Q25 profit up 27% to US$264mn. Profits for the quarter benefited from storage revenues at its Mexico port, which will unlikely repeat.  Nevertheless, underlying operations remain robust and near-to medium-term prospects remain exciting, with FY25E-FY27E EPS CAGR of 16% and additional upside from acquisitions.

  • Universal Robina Corp (URC PM) (-) – Core income for 9M was up 1% to P9bn , on top line growth of +5%.  For 3Q alone, core income of P2.1bn was down 5% despite the low base the previous year. The international BCF business also saw a dip in the quarter even as overall margins declined because of stratospheric coffee prices and increased spend on brand-building.

  • Century Pacific Food (CNPF PM) (=) – 3Q revenues and profit growth accelerated to +15%, on the back of increased volumes. The branded business alone saw revenues jump +18% in the quarter, helping lift consolidated revenue to P22bn and profit to P1.9bn. For the 9M period, revenue are now up +8% while profits are up +10%.

  • Converge ICT (CNVRG PM) (-) – Net profit for 9M rose +8% to P8.9bn, on the back of revenues of P33bn (+10% YoY). A slowdown in 3Q was notable with revenue growth hitting +7%. Net income for 3Q alone (P2.95bn, +1% YoY) would have been down YoY if not for a chunky “Other income” item, which was mostly from FX gains.

  • Globe (GLO PM) (=) – Core income for 9M came in at P15.5bn, down 12% YoY.  3Q core income was P5.0bn, 15%. Service revenue was -1% in 3Q and -2% in 9M. Possibly the most anticipated number is the Globe’s share of Mynt’s profits, and this was P5.3bn for 9M.  This implies 3Q contribution was P1.5bn, compared to P2.0bn in 2Q.  The company will not be holding its briefing until Tuesday.

 

 

  • Finally, we don’t cover DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) but it is a name many watch. For PLUS, 3Q25 declined 23% QoQ, due to tighter restrictions which prohibit e-wallets from redirecting to online gaming platforms (beginning August 17). As a result, 3Q25 earnings fell 59% QoQ (down 51% YoY) to P1.7bn. Since the delinking of e-wallets and online gaming platforms only began in the middle of the third quarter, 4Q25 figures could be even lower.

GAMING
DigiPlus Interactive 3Q25 net profit falls over 50% YoY and QoQ
  • 3Q25 net profit of DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) fell 59% QoQ and 51% YoY to P1.7bn
    • 9M25 net profit rose 16% to P10.1bn, behind consensus forecast
  • 3Q25 revenue declined 23% QoQ (flat YoY) to P19.1bn, due to tighter regulations which prohibit e-wallets from redirecting to online gaming platforms (beginning August 17)
    • 9M25 revenue grew 30% to P66.8bn
  • 3Q25 EBIT margin declined 7.5ppt QoQ and 9.9ppt YoY to 9.2%, the lowest level since 4Q22, when fees to PAGCOR were about 17.5 ppt higher
  • The company will hold a briefing on November 14

 

TELCOS
Globe 3Q – Initial comments
  • Core income for 9M was P15.5bn, down 12% with 3Q core income at P5.0bn (-15% YoY).  Bottomline is in line with, if not slightly better than expectations.

 

  • Service revenue is still down 2% but the dip in mobile narrowed to -1%. Broadband revenue, however, seems to have finally grown (+5% YoY in 3Q).

 

  • Mynt’s profit contributions amounted to P5.3bn for 9M25. This implies 3Q contributions were ~P1.5bn, down from P2.0bn in 2Q.

 

 

BANKS
Commercial banks’ loan growth slows to 10.5% in September
  • Preliminary data showed that loans of commercial banks rose 10.5% in September, moderating further from the 11.2% pace in August
    • Loans to productive activities decelerated to 9.1% from 9.9% the previous month
      • Driven by a steeper 10% contraction in manufacturing loans (vs. -5.5% YoY in Aug) and a sustained slowdown in real estate loan growth (+9.2% in Sep from +11% in Aug)
    • Consumer loans still grew at a heady pace of 23.5%, though a tad slower than August’s 23.9% rate
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) picked up further to 7.3% in September from 6.6% in August
