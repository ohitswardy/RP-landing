REGIS REPORT

18 March 2025

For today’s results round-up, we have URC (URC PM), Converge (CNVRG PM) and DMCI (DMC PM).

 

  • Universal Robina (URC PM) (=) – URC delivered net profit of P11.4bn for FY24, down 5% YoY, including losses from its discontinued China operations. Overall, the results were within expectations as the declines in the sugar business dragged down what would have otherwise been a very respectable +20% EBIT growth from the rest. However, 4Q numbers continue to show what is, without doubt, the biggest issue: weak top line for the domestic branded (BCF) business.

  • Converge (CNVRG PM) (=) – Full-year profits for Converge reached P10.8bn, up +19% as revenues expanded 15% on the back of solid subscriber growth.  Converge again added over 100k subs in 4Q.  We note, however, the drop in 4Q profits due to a surprising jump (+76%) in G&A expenses.  The company will hold its earnings call later this week.  In the meantime, some more detail below.

 

  • DMCI Holdings (DMC PM) (=) - The 11% YoY drop in DMCI’s 4Q24 profits was largely expected, but we think there are other, important things to highlight from the briefing: (a) the large residential revenue reversals and very weak pre-sales of its property unit, (b) extended delays in construction biddings and (c) the quarry suspension at its newly acquired cement business.  Management also sounded tentative about prospects of a near-term rebound for these units, given ongoing macroeconomic and regulatory headwinds. One silver lining is the material rebound in nickel production and prices, as well as a possible increase in Semirara’s annual coal production limit of 16mn MT.

The planned listings of two firms are in the headlines today.

 

Mynt, the holding company for e-wallet firm GCash, is asking regulators to lower the minimum public float for new listings from the current 20%.  The 20% minimum is a big hurdle considering what is believed to be Mynt’s target valuation (>US$8bn). Globe (GLO PM) is the single biggest shareholder of Mynt, with ~34% ownership.

 

Maynilad, the water utility operating the West Zone concession in Metro Manila, officially filed the paperwork for an IPO.  At its maximum, Maynilad will sell up to 30% of its equity.

 

+++

 

UK-based Actis completed its purchase of 40% of MTerra Solar for US$600m yesterday. MTerra Solar is a subsidiary of SPNEC (SPNEC PM), which itself is a subsidiary of Meralco (MER PM). MTerra Solar is currently developing a giant 3,500MW solar farm whose output will be sold to Meralco.

 

+++

 

Overseas remittances were up 2.9% for January, reaching US$2.92bn. Growth for remittances has stayed at roughly 3% for most months over the past few years.

  • Profit for 4Q alone, however, was P2.6bn -- down 11% QoQ and 5% YoY.  There appears to have been a large jump in G&A expenses (+76% or + P1.2bn).  Might be big bonuses paid to employees and/or basketball team

  • Company declared a dividend of P0.43/share.  This is CNVRG’s second dividend (after P0.18/sh in Sept 2024).  Adding both would be = P0.61/sh, which is 3.6% of current share price.

DIGITAL FINANCE/TELCOS
GCash eyeing 10-15% public float for IPO in end-2025
  • Ernest Cu, chairman for Mynt which is the fintech arm of Globe Telecom Inc. (GLO PM), stated in an interview that a 10-15% range for the GCash IPO will be more suitable than the mandated minimum 20% public float requirement for IPOs in the PSE
    • The company will still need approval for this from the PSE and Philippines' SEC
  • He stated that the IPO may take place by the end of 2025 but cites that the company is in no rush to go public
  • Cu also mentioned discussions with "long-only funds" that are interested in bringing 2-3 cornerstone investors for the IPO, i.e., US, EU, JP funds
  • GCash saw its valuation rise to $5bn in its latest funding round as Mitsubishi Financial Group, Inc. took an 8% stake
UTILITIES
Maynilad targets July 10 for IPO
  • Maynilad officially filed its initial listing application with the SEC and PSE, targeting a July 10 listing
    • Offer period is set for June 25-July 2
  • IPO will involve up to 2.46bn common shares at a maximum offer price of P20/sh, representing 30.5% of Maynilad’s total issued and outstanding capital stock
    • Offer consists of 1.78bn primary ‘firm” shares, with an overallotment option of up to 266.3mn primary shares
    • Upsize option of up to 379.29mn secondary shares
    • Preferential offer of up to 36.3mn reserved shares, subject to the exercise of an upsize option, which will be subscribed by First Pacific (142 HK)
  • Under its franchise, Maynilad needs to offer up to 30% of its shares to the public by Jan 2027
  • Maynilad is owned by Metro Pacific (53.7%), DMCI (25.7%), and Marubeni (20.3%)
  • We value Maynilad at a 100% equity value of P166bn
POWER
UK-based Actis completes 40% buy-in in SPNEC’s Terra solar project
  • Global investment firm Actis Rubyred (Singapore) Pte. Ltd. has completed a US$600mn transaction to acquire a 40% equity stake in MTerra Solar
    • MTerra Solar is the largest single site solar-battery project (3500 MW solar + 4,000 MWh BESS) in the world, with a project cost of P200bn (over US$3bn)
  • Meralco (MER PM) has a 47% effective economic stake in SPNEC, which gives it a 28% economic stake in MTerra Solar post-deal
POWER
ERC: San Miguel tops power generation market share in 2024
  • Based on the Energy Regulatory Commission’s data, the San Miguel (SMC PM) group held a market share of 22.4% (6.1 GW) at the national level
    • This topped Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM), which held a market share of 21.8% or 5.9GW
    • First Gen (FGEN PM) came in third, with a market share of 13.2%, followed by Meralco (MER PM) (5.4% share, 1.5GW) and Ayala group (5.3%, 1.4GW)
  • In the Luzon grid, SMC held a market share of 28.4%, followed by AEV at 24.7%, and First Gen (13.8%
  • In the Visayas grid, First Gen held a market share of 23%, Meralco and AEV, respectively, accounted for 20.4% and 13.5%
  • In the Mindanao grid, AEV held a market share of 14.9% while SMC trailed at 8.9%
  • Under the Electric Power Industry Reform Act, the market share and installed generating capacity (IGC) of any company or related entity are capped at 30% per grid and 25% for the national grid
MACRO
OFW Remittances up 2.9% YoY in January
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks grew 2.9% YoY to US$2.92bn, but down 13.7% MoM given Dec's seasonal strength
    • Land-based remittances from the USA, Singapore, Saudi Arabia and the UAE were the primary drivers in the increase in remittances posting significant YoY growth
    • Sea-based remittances growth picked up to 0.9% YoY from 0.6% in Dec
    • In PHP terms, remittances grew by 7.3%, slower relative to the months of Nov and Dec but faster than the growth seen in Sep and Oct
  • This year's BSP's remittances target growth rate has yet to be disclosed as of late
    • Recall that last year's growth of 3.0% was in-line with the BSP target of 3.0%
