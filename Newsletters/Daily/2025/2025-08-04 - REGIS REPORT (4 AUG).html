REGIS REPORT

04 August 2025

Metrobank (MBT PM) held a briefing last Friday to discuss 2Q/1H results that were remarkably uncontroversial. 1H25 profits rose 5% to P24.8bn on loan growth of 13.5%. The NPL ratio was at 1.5% and NPL cover remained a very safe 154%.

+++


ICTSI (ICT PM) disclosed it is taking over a new port on Batam island in Indonesia. ICT secured a 30-year license to run Batu Ampar Container Terminal (BACT) on the island, which is a special economic zone. BACT has an estimated capacity of 1mn TEUs annually and ICT will have 75% interest in the port operations.

 

BACT will be the 30th operating terminal of ICT, and should add 4% to consolidated volumes and 1% to revenue.

 

+++

 

The gaming revenue (GGR) of City of Dreams (CODM) amounted to US$111mn in 2Q25 based on disclosures by its parent firm Melco Resorts (200 HK). This suggests that GGR for 2Q was flattish QoQ in PHP terms (P~6.2bn) for the property.

 

The CODM number implies GGR for all the other properties that have yet to report (e.g. Bloomberry (BLOOM PM). Travellers, et al) amounted to ~P30.7bn. This would be down around 13% QoQ – certainly not a great sign for 2Q results.

 

+++

 

First Gen (FGEN PM) subsidiary Energy Development Corp (EDC) is planning to spend as much as US$540mn to boost the capacity of its Bacon-Manito (BacMan) complex by 90MW. This project, however, could take more than half a decade.

 

+++

 

Semirara’s (SCC PM) 1H25 net profit of P8.4bn (-33% YoY) came in weaker than we had anticipated due to lower-than-expected mining revenues. Profits for 2Q25 alone dipped 6% QoQ (-33% YoY) to P4.1bn, after coal mining shipments dropped 2% QoQ and mining ASPs fell 10% QoQ to P2,223/MT.  Profit contributions from the power unit improved QoQ, thanks to better sales volumes and ASPs, bringing 1H25 contributions down 7% YoY to P3.5bn (In line).

TRANSPORT
ICTSI acquires port in Indonesia
  • ICT secured a 30-year license to operate Batu Ampar Container Terminal (BACT) in Indonesia
    • BACT is the main port in Batam Island, with an estimated ~80% market share
    • Batam island is a prominent special economic zone in Southeast Asia and handled 673k TEUs in 2024
    • BACT has recently completed its expansion and is estimated to have a capacity of 1mn TEUs (4% of ICT’s estimated att. capacity of 17.6mn TEUs)
  • ICT will be partnering with PT Interport Sarana Infrastruktur Indonesia, a wholly owned subsidiary of PT Indika Energy TBK (INDY IJ)
    • JV entity will be called PT Batu Ampar Container Terminal and will be owned 75% by ICTSI and 25% by PT Interport
  • We estimate BACT could add ~4% to ICT’s consolidated volumes but only 1% of revenues upon takeover due to a lower yield per TEU
    • We understand that the upfront cost will be very minimal
    • We estimate at full utilization and US$100 yield per TEU, the port could contribute over US$60mn (3%) to consolidated EBITDA
  • ICT trades at 9x 25E EV/EBITDA (consensus: 10x EV/EBITDA), at par with peer median (9x) but below peer average (12x)
GAMING
City of Dreams Manila reports flat GGR QoQ
  • Melco Resorts (200 HK) reported that City of Dreams Manila’s 2Q25 GGR hit US$111mn
    • Up 2% QoQ from US$109mn in 1Q25
    • Down 8% YoY from US$121mn
  • We estimate that GGR (in peso terms) was flat on a QoQ and YoY basis, at P6.2bn
  • VIP GGR improved 24% QoQ (thanks to higher rolling chip) but slot GGR fell 12% QoQ
  • Based on the industry figures reported by PAGCOR, as well as the reports of CODM and Okada Manila, we estimate that GGR of the other licensed casinos (in aggregate) fell 13% QoQ

 

POWER
Semirara 2Q25 net profit falls 33% YoY, 6% QoQ to P4.1bn
  • Semirara reported its 1H25 net profit fell 33% to P8.4, trailing Regis full-year estimates (P19.5bn) but within consensus forecast of P16.7bn
    • In 2Q25 alone, net profit fell 33% YoY, 6% QoQ to P4.1bn due to soft revenues at the coal mining unit
  • We attribute the weak 1H results to (a) lower coal mining ASPs, which averaged P2,352/MT in 1H (vs. FY25E: P2,400) and (b) lower coal production guidance of 18mn MT (vs. FY25E: 19mn MT)
  • More after management’s briefing at 4pm today
POWER
Energy Development Corp. investing US$540mn to raise capacity of Bac-Man plant by 90MW
  • Energy Development Corp. (EDC) could potentially increase the capacity of its Bacon-Manito (Bac-Man) geothermal plant by 90MW
    • Upgrading the facility could cost US$6mn per MW or a total of US$540mn
    • Expansion plan may be pursued in the next five or six years, as EDC still needs to conduct exploration activities
  • EDC is a 47.5%-owned unit of First Gen (FGEN PM)
POWER
MGEN aims to double net saleable capacity to over 10GW by 2030
  • MGen president and CEO Emmanuel Rubio said the company expects to double its net saleable capacity to 10,346 megawatts by 2030, from the current 5,068 MW
    • Cited projects such as Terra Solar (3,500 MW), Atimonan (1200 MW), and Toledo coal (73MW)
TRANSPORT
NAIA passenger volume up 6% in 1H25
  • Passenger volume at Ninoy Aquino International Airport (NAIA) increased 6% YoY to 26.7mn in 1H25
    • For the same period, Domestic passengers rose by 6% YoY to 14.3mn, while international passenger traffic was 7% higher YoY to 12.5mn
    • In 2Q alone, total passenger volume at NAIA increased by 6% YoY to 13.7mn
  • Projected FY25 passenger volume is 53.4mn (+6% YoY), with 1H typically accounting for ~50% of full-year traffic
    • Recall that in 2024, NAIA saw passenger volume climb to 50.3mn, up 11% YoY and 5% higher than 2019
  • The New NAIA Infra Corp. (NNIC), a consortium composed of San Miguel Holdings Corp. (SMC PM), RMM Asian Logistics Inc., RLW Aviation Development Inc., and Incheon International Airport Corp., started operating and maintaining NAIA in Sept 2024
    • The consortium seeks to boost NAIA's annual capacity to 62mn passengers vs. the current 35mn within four to five years
