REGIS REPORT

23 October 2025

During a congressional hearing on online gambling, PAGCOR said that revenue in September was down by nearly half. While it partly blamed the prohibition of e-wallets from redirecting to online gaming platforms (implemented in mid-August), it also said that it has begun to implement a minimum initial deposit of P500.

 

This new requirement, which only applies to new users, would likely deter low-income Filipinos from starting to gamble online. This would arguably be more detrimental for new platforms that are still trying to build a user base, such as MegaFUNalo of Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM). While the requirement would limit the growth of the online gaming segment, DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM), which already has over 40mn registered users, is likely to remain the market leader.

 

The decline in online gaming will have second order effects on the e-wallets but we don’t expect the impact to be as severe as some might have feared.

The reported departure of Anastacio Martirez, COO of PLDT (TEL PM) mobile subsidiary Smart, will further contribute to the perception of instability at the group’s top management. PLDT has seen lots of changes at the C-suite level (COO, CFO, CEO and Chief Revenue Officer) over the last few years, and this doesn’t look like it will help the underlying business.

 

+++

 

One unanticipated consequence of the surge in global gold prices is that it has breathed new life into the Philippine mining sector. In the distant past, the Philippines was one of the world’s largest mineral producers (especially gold/copper) but the industry has dwindled to nothing today. With gold now at ~$4,000/oz, whatever is left of the gold mining industry is suddenly much more profitable, encouraging more investment and development in the sector.

 

 

Production at a number of new mines is already set to begin over the next three years. If things go according to plan, one of these new mines could be the giant Tampakan project in southern Mindanao. Hopes for the mining sector becoming a source of hard currency may yet be realized if mineral prices hold up.

 

+++

 

Government infrastructure spending was down 16% YoY in Aug, a clear reaction to the corruption scandal that’s swamped the public works department.

GAMING
PAGCOR requires initial minimum deposit of P500 for online gambling
  • During a congressional hearing, PAGCOR AVP Jessa Fernandez said the agency's revenues have dropped by as much as 49 percent in September
  • Ms. Fernandez said that the drop might be due to the prohibition on e-wallets to redirect to online gaming platforms as well as the implementation of an initial minimum deposit of P500
    • The initial minimum deposit of P500 will likely deter low-income Filipinos from starting to gamble online
  • During the hearing, The Department of Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev) also said that it supports either tighter regulation or a complete prohibition of online gambling in the Philippines
  • Socioeconomic Planning Director Desiree Joy O. Narvaez said “Given its minimal contribution to the economy and vis-à-vis the significant social cost, we do support either its complete prohibition or stricter regulation”
  • The BSP prohibited e-wallets from redirecting to online gambling platforms (effective August 17)
  • There have been several other proposals to restrict or ban online gambling
  • DigiPlus Interactive is the market leader in online gambling, with c. 40% market share as of 2Q25
  • PLUS is trading at 5.7X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)

Government infrastructure spending extends decline in August
  • Overall infrastructure spending (incl. LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) fell 16% YoY, 8% MoM to P105bn in August
    • Cited suspension of payments for public works amid ongoing validation and audit related to corruption concerns
