REGIS REPORT

05 September 2025

CPI inflation for Aug came in at 1.5%, an acceleration from the 0.9% in July. We suspect that Aug CPI will be a jolt to the Street which might have been lulled into the belief that the unusually low July figure would be carried over. Core inflation, as defined by the statistics agency, rose to 2.7% from 2.3% in July.

 

The largest swing factor this month was food inflation, which was 0.9% in Aug from a negative figure (-0.2%) in July. Vegetable prices rose sharply, likely due to the unusual severity of the rains the last few weeks.

SM Prime (SMPH PM) says it is spending P150bn to build 12 new malls and redevelop 16 existing malls over the next five years. Among the ones scheduled for redevelopment are two out of its three biggest malls: SM North EDSA and SM Megamall. The redevelopment of SM Mall of Asia was just completed.

 

Ayala Land (ALI PM) is also currently redeveloping its flagship malls.

The President signed into law a reform of the tax regime governing large-scale metallic miners. Although the measure imposes additional taxes that may initially reduce profits of existing miners, the law also promotes better transparency, which could (hopefully) help attract more investments in the sector.

MINING
Marcos signs law reforming tax system for large-scale metallic mining
  • President Marcos signed Republic Act 12253 or the "Enhanced Fiscal Regime for Large-Scale Metallic Mining Act"
  • Key provisions include:
    • Retaining the 5% royalty rate on contracts within mineral reservations
    • Imposing a five-tiered margin-based royalty rate (0.1% to 5%) on mining contracts outside mineral reservations
    • Imposing a five-tiered windfall profits tax (1% to 10%) for all types of mining contracts
    • Limiting the amount of tax-deductible borrowing costs by implementing a 2:1 debt-to-equity ratio
    • Adopting a ring-fencing rule on a project-by-project basis to prevent the consolidation of income and expenses of all mining project by the same taxpayer
  • The government expects to generate additional P6bn in revenues annually once the law is implemented
    • Government collected P33bn in mining taxes (royalties, excise, and others) in 2024, equivalent to <1% of overall government revenues
  • Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) estimates the law could cut its profits by ~3%

POWER
GEA-4 falls short of target
  • The Department of Energy noted weaker investor participation in the fourth round of Green Energy Auction (GEA-4)
    • Committed offers reached 9,424 MW or 88% of its goal of 10,653 MW
    • Shortfall attributed to unsubscribed installation targets for floating solar, onshore wind, and Iress (integrated solar with energy storage systems)
  • Notable participants included listed companies:
    • ACEN Corp. (ACEN PM)
    • Aboitiz Power (AP PM)
    • San Miguel Power (SMC PM)
    • First Gen (FGEN PM)
    • Citicore Renewable Energy (CREC PM)
    • Alternegy Hldgs (ALTER PM)
  • Recall that GEA-4 involved 3,940MW of ground-mounted solar; 48MW of rooftop solar; 3,175 MW of floating solar; 2,390MW of onshore wind; and 1,100 MW of solar + BESS
    • Projects are targeted to begin from 2026-2029
  • List of winning bidders will be released on Oct 22
  • The Green Energy Auction program aims to promote investments in renewable energy sources
    • GEA allows RE gencos to sell energy to the WESM at their winning (guaranteed) bid prices
    • Any difference between the bid price and the WESM price will be settled through the Feed-in-Tariff system, which is a full pass-through to end users
