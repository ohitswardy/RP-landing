REGIS REPORT

22 September 2025

Simultaneous rallies were held in various locations across the country protesting the massive corruption relating to infrastructure projects (flood control) recently uncovered. The estimates are for more than 100,000 participants across multiple sites. While there were reports of violence in one area, the protests appear to have been generally peaceful.

 

The President has ordered the creation of an independent commission to investigate the matter and ultimately file the appropriate charges. The big question now is how far-reaching the investigation will go and if any “big fish” will actually be caught in the nets. An unsatisfactory outcome could risk igniting greater public outrage that can ultimately unsettle the administration.

 

+++

 

Ayala Corp (AC PM) is ending its distribution agreement with the Volkswagen (VW) group after more than a decade. We view this action as part of AC’s continuing efforts to prune its underperforming businesses. The VW distribution business never really took off as pricing was never quite competitive against Japanese and Korean vehicles. AC’s auto business now appears focused on BYD, which has seen a significant jump in demand.

 

+++

 

Media reports say that the International Finance Corp (IFC) and the Asian Development Bank (ABD) will become cornerstone investors in the IPO of water concessionaire Maynilad Water. Maynilad is aiming to sell up to 30% of its capital in its listing. If both parties do sign up, we reckon it would be a significant boost to the listing.

 

+++

 

Both Meralco (MER PM) and First Gen (FGEN PM) are considering plans to expand capacity not just within the country. Meralco seems to be setting its sights on Malaysia, given the data center boom there.

 

FGEN, meanwhile, is hoping to grow capacity to as much as 13GW after announcing a sale of a controlling interest in its ~2,000MW gas-fired plants. It recently signed a deal to enter Indonesia’s geothermal power generation sector.

POLITICS
Marcos vows accountability amid anti-corruption protests, says he is confident of finishing his term
  • Palace Press Officer Claire Castro stated that President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. is not concerned about any destabilization attempts as the anti-corruption protests are not targeted towards him
    • Castro stated that "the Filipinos are not mad at the President or at his administration, but to all the people who are involved in the anomalous flood control projects"
    • Rally organizers were noted to not have formally called for President Marcos' resignation but for accountability of gov't officials involved in the anomalous flood control projects
  • Castro emphasized the President's promise that "no one will be spared; those who must be held accountable will face the consequences "
  • The number of participants in the anti-corruption rallies in Luneta Park, Manila and EDSA were estimated to be over 100,000 people, marking one of the biggest protests in years
    • Protests were also held in other regions such as Bulacan, Cebu City, Davao City, and Baguio City
CONGLOS
Ayala group, Volkswagen AG end distribution partnership
  • Ayala Corp.’s Automobile Central Enterprise, Inc. (ACEI) and Volkswagen AG have agreed to conclude the distribution of Volkswagen vehicles in the country
    • ACEI, a wholly owned unit of ACMobility, is the exclusive importer and distributor of VW cars in the Philippines
    • It became the official importer and distributor of VW cars in October 2012, and relaunched the VW brand in 2013
  • ACMobility carries the Honda, Isuzu, Kia, and BYD automobile brands
    • As of the end of 2024, ACMobility managed a network of 27 BYD dealerships, 40 Kia dealerships, and 4 Volkswagen dealerships nationwide
    • ACMobility plans to expand its BYD dealerships to 77 by year-end
UTILITIES
Media sources say IFC, ADB in talks to back Maynilad’s IPO
  • The International Finance Corp. and Asian Development Bank are set to become cornerstone investors in the IPO of Maynilad Water
    • According to media sources
    • Both are reportedly subscribing to nearly 40% of IPO shares
  • Maynilad’s IPO timetable:
    • Pricing date: Oct 13
    • Final offer price: Oct 14
    • Offer period: Oct 16-22
    • Listing date: Oct 30
  • Maynilad is offering 2.289bn (1.9bn primary and 355mn secondary) shares to the public at a maximum offer price of P20/sh
    • At the max. offer price and full subscription of offer shares, Maynilad’s market cap could be P151bn, and public float will be 30%
    • Under its franchise, Maynilad is required to offer at least 30% of its outstanding capital stock by Jan 21, 2027
POWER
Meralco power unit eyes 1,500MW expansion in Malaysia
  • Meralco PowerGen Corp. (MGEN) is exploring opportunities to develop up to 1,500 MW of power capacity in Malaysia
    • According to MGEN president Emmanuel Rubio
    • Cited Malaysia’s data center investments, which would require 8GW of new grid capacity
    • MGEN plans to have a local partner in this venture
  • MGEN is aiming to double its net saleable capacity to 10,346 MW by 2030 from the current 5,068 MW
POWER
First Gen sets US$20bn plan to grow capacity to 13GW
  • First Gen expects to spend US$20bn over the next five years to expand its power portfolio to 13GW within and outside the Philippines
    • First Gen President and Chief Executive Officer Francis Giles B. Puno
    • Expansion will focus on renewables – geothermal, solar, and hydro
  • While there is momentum toward its target, Mr. Puno said achieving 13 GW by 2030 remains challenging due to the long development timelines of power projects
  • The company currently has a total generating capacity of 3,688 megawatts (MW) from geothermal, wind, hydropower, solar, and natural gas plants
    • FGEN is selling 60% of its natgas assets to Razon-led Prime Infrastructure for P50bn
MACRO
Dept. of Agriculture seeks rice import ban extension
  • The Dept. of Agriculture (DA) stated that it will likely recommend extending the ongoing rice import ban by 15-30 days
    • Recall that a 60-day suspension of rice imports from Sep 1 to Oct 30 was approved by the President to boost farmgate prices given the current peak harvest season of palay (unmilled rice)
  • DA Secretary Tiu Laurel noted that palay farmgate prices improved to P13-17/kg from P8-10/kg but noted that farmers in certain provinces remain unprofitable given the P14/kg production cost of palay
  • The Dept. will submit its recommendation to extend or lift the import ban on Sept. 30
  • Finance Secretary Ralph Recto and other economic officials are also set to meet to discuss the proposal's impact on rice prices and inflation expectations

 

 

 

MACRO
BOP shifts to US$359mn surplus in Aug; GIR settles at US$107.1bn
  • The country's overall balance of payments (BOP) for the month posted a surplus of US$359mn
    • Wider than the US$88mn surplus in Aug 2024 but a reversal from July 2025's US$167mn deficit
  • The August surplus reflected the BSP's net income from its investments abroad
  • This brings the YT-Aug deficit at US$5.4bn, reversing from the US$1.6bn surplus in the same period last year
    • The ballooning deficit stems from the continued trade in goods gap
  • The BSP projection for the overall BOP position for 2025 is a US$6.3bn deficit (-1.3% of GDP)
    • The BOP projection for 2025 reflects expectations of slower global trade and subdued investor confidence given uncertainties from trade policy and geopolitical developments
  • Meanwhile, the final GIR level for end-Aug stood at US$107.1bn, down from the $105.4bn in end-Jul
    • The GIR level is reflective of an external liquidity buffer equivalent to 7.2 months’ worth of imports of goods and payments of services and primary income
    • GIR is expected to decrease from $106bn in 2024 to $104bn in 2025
