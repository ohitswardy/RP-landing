REGIS REPORT

28 July 2025

In a unanimous 13-0 vote, the Philippine Supreme Court declared the current impeachment proceedings against Vice President Sara Duterte-Carpio as unconstitutional. The decision was based on the provision in the Constitution prohibiting an impeachment complaint from being “initiated” within one year after any previous one. The House of Representatives only voted to proceed with a fourth impeachment complaint on Feb 5, 2025, after refusing to act on three previous ones filed late 2024. The Court chose to consider the previous impeachment complaint/s in Dec as having been properly “initiated”, thus invalidating the fourth complaint as it falls within the one-year prohibition period. 

 

While we expect an appeal/s against the decision to be filed, the Court decision is unlikely to be reversed given the unanimity of the vote. Some of the details of the Court decision, however, could still be amended.

 

In any case, the removal of Duterte-Carpio from office through the impeachment trial is already very unlikely. Her allies have enough votes in the Senate to block a conviction, which requires 2/3rds vote in the 24-seat chamber.

BDO (BDO PM) reported 1H25 NPAT of P40.6bn, up 3% YoY, and tracking behind Regis (P90bn) and Street (P89bn) forecasts for FY25E. Loan growth accelerated to 14% (from 12% in 1Q), but NII was up just 8% YoY suggesting NIMs were flat-to-down. (Recall that the bank changed accounting presentation in 3Q24, making some ratios difficult to compare YoY. Non-interest income was strong, up 15% YoY, despite weak trading/fx gains (-11%). While revenues were up 10%, opex grew 15%, faster than our expectations. This saw PPOP grow just 2% YoY in 1H25. Asset quality was steady, with NPL ratio at 1.75% (vs 1.77% in 1Q) and NPL cover at 140% (vs 143%), though credit costs popped to c50bps in 2Q25 from 37bps in 1Q.

 

The bank will host a briefing at noon today.

POLITICS
Supreme Court rules VP Sara Duterte impeachment as unconstitutional; Lower House to appeal SC ruling
  • The Supreme Court (SC) ruled that the impeachment of Vice President Sara Duterte was unconstitutional, halting the trial that was supposed to take place early July
  • Four impeachment complaints were filed against Duterte, with the final one that was lodged back in Feb 2025 being the only one approved by the Lower House
  • The SC argued that failing to properly process the first three filings effectively dismissed these prior complaints
    • The high court argues that the dismissal of these cases, triggered the Constitutional rule of the one-year ban on filing new cases
    • This implies that another complaint has to be refiled by Feb 2026, should the Lower House pursue the impeachment of VP Duterte
  • Senate impeachment court spokesperson Regie Tongol said the SC ruling affirms the Senate's decision to act in line with the Constitution and uphold the rule of law, emphasizing their duty to respect the finality of high court decisions
  • Princess Abante, the Lower House spokesperson, stated that they will file a motion of reconsideration on the impeachment after it was declared unconstitutional by the SC
CONSUMER
Robinsons Retail acquires motorcycle retailing business
  • Robinsons Retail Holdings Inc. (RRHI PM), through subsidiary Robinsons Supermarket Corp. (RSC), has signed a share purchase agreement to acquire 100% of Premiumbikes Corp., an affiliate of JG Summit Holdings (JGS PM) for P146mn
  • Premiumbikes is a motorcycle retailer that currently operates 214 stores nationwide and was founded back in 2009 by John Gokongwei
    • In 2024, Premiumbikes' revenues climbed by 15% YoY to P4.2bn
  • RRHI President and CEO Stanley Co stated that the acquisition is in line with its strategy to diversify revenue by entering "a new and fast-growing category that is also profitable"
    • Premiumbikes General Manager Joselito Pojol also highlighted that the Philippines has low motorcycle penetration vs. other Southeast Asian markets
  • RRHI's FY24 net sales were up 4% YoY to P199bn, the company posted EBIT of P9.8bn (+9% YoY)
  • RRHI will hold its 2Q25 briefing tomorrow, July 29 at 4:00pm—register here

GAMING
DigiPlus applies for gaming license in South Africa
  • DigiPlus plans to enter South Africa’s online sports betting and fixed odds market
    • South Africa currently does not allow online casino games
  • The company will apply for three licenses with the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB): National Manufacturer License, Bookmaker License, and Bookmaker Premises License
    • The process is expected to take at least six months from filing
  • Sports betting gross gaming revenue (GGR) in South Africa reached approximately US$1.9bn in 2024
    • Online sports betting contributed about US$1.6bn and is projected to grow at around 5% YoY
    • In comparison, the Philippines’ electronic games GGR (online casino + online sports betting) totaled US$2.3bn (P136bn) in 2024
  • There is Remote Gambling Bill in South Africa’s parliament which could expand the market by legalizing additional formats including online casino gaming
  • South Africa’s total GGR hit US$3.3bn in 2024, a 25% jump YoY
    • The Philippines gaming industry GGR was US$6.4bn (P373bn)
    • There are currently 350 licensed gaming operators in South Africa (land-based and online combined)  
  • DigiPlus will launch its Brazil operations in September 2025
