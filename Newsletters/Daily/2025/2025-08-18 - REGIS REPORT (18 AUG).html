REGIS REPORT

18 August 2025

Jollibee (JFC PM) reported good operating results for 1H/2Q, with top line jumping +15% for both 1H/2Q and operating profit growing +18% in 1H (+19% in 2Q). Financing costs, however, took away most of those gains and net income for 1H was practically unchanged (-1%) at P5.6bn.

 

Among the key operating highlights: (1) group SSSG was +5.5% in 2Q, the same as 1Q, (2) the make-up of the +5.5% SSSG is slightly different this time, as the domestic business slowed down and the China business lifted the international side; (3) Smashburger is still struggling.

 

Management also delivered a statement that it plans to increase leverage as a way of reducing average cost of capital, presumably to raise returns to equity shareholders.

AREIT (AREIT PM) reported 1H25 net profit (excluding revaluation gains) grew 45% to P4.3bn, thanks in large part to asset infusions in 3Q24. While the earnings are notionally above REGIS and consensus forecasts, the outperformance came from accounting adjustments, which will not affect our dividend forecasts. Expiring office contracts (which comprised c. 2% of total floor area) were renewed at rental rates that were 10%-15% lower than the previous contracts, which were mostly signed in when rents were at their peak. However, beginning next year, we would expect any negative rental reversions to be far more modest. In fact, we believe positive rental reversions would be likely in Makati, where nearly half of AREIT’s office space is located.

Online gambling operators appear to be using alternative channels more aggressively following the action by e-wallets to sever the direct links between the e-wallet apps with online gambling apps. The Bangko Sentral (BSP) ordered all e-wallet apps to de-link by yesterday.

 

We should see over the next few months how severe (or not) the impact of this measure by the BSP will be. Even if this action fails to dramatically reduce online gambling activity, the politicians are likely to continue piling on pressure for more restrictions.

MACRO
OFW Remittances up 3.7% YoY in June
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks grew 3.7% YoY to US$3.0bn, accelerating from the 2.9% growth seen in May
    • Land-based remittances from the USA, Singapore, and Saudi Arabia were the primary drivers in the increase in remittances given their % contribution to the total
    • Sea-based remittances growth picked up to 3.5% YoY from 3.1% in May
    • In PHP terms, remittances declined by 0.5% YoY given the appreciation of the peso

 

 

 

TRANSPORT
Cebu Air July passenger volumes flat YoY
  • Cebu Air reported its passenger volumes stood at 2.1mn in July, unchanged from the same period last year
    • Seat capacity rose 4% YoY in July, resulting in seat load factors (SLF) falling by 320bps to 85.6%
    • Cited the earlier start of the academic calendar (June from July last year)
  • Brings 7M25 pax volumes to 16mn, up 18% YoY
    • We’ve baked in a +16% YoY increase in 25E pax volumes to 28.5mn
  • The seasonal adjustment is not a cause for concern, as all airlines similarly scale back capacity during the lean period
    • We note that SLF in July is still higher than the load factors in 3Q24, suggesting fares/yields may have gone up

CONSUMER
Jollibee to be investigated by DTI for suspected AI-generated raffle winners
  • Jollibee Foods Corp. (JFC PM) has faced scrutiny from social media users as the latest winners to its "Burger Blowout" raffle promo were flagged to be suspicious and AI-generated
    • Some of the listed winners included “Alayna Pfannerstill,” “Celestine Gaylord,” “Finn Hackett,” and “Hobby Dynamics.” 
  • The promo involves joining a raffle through the purchase of the "Burger Blowout" meal which gives consumers the chance to win a ticket to a concert and other minor raffle prizes
  • The company has released a statement that they are "reviewing the matter" with the DTI and is temporarily postponing succeeding raffle draws
  • The Dept. of Trade and Industry (DTI) stated that they have no comment as of late given that the issue is still under investigation
    • JFC may face a maximum fine of P300,000 if found to have violated the approved mechanics of its "Burger Blowout" promotion
PROPERTY
PEZA to exempt administrative workers from work-from-home cap
  • The Philippine Economic Zone Authority (PEZA)plans to exclude administrative and finance functions from the 50% work-from-home (WFH) cap
  • It intends to come out with the new guidelines within a month
  • Most BPO firms are registered with PEZA 
  • PEZA’s plan would allow administrative and finance workers of BPO firms to work from home without limit
  • While this might reduce BPO demand for office space, we expect the impact to be modest
  • BPO firms account for 60%-80% of the occupied office space of the developers we cover
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 49% of Filinvest Land’s NAV - FLI is trading at 61% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 5.6X FY25E P/E
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV - MEG is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY25E P/B, and 3.6X FY25E P/E
    • 28% of Robinsons Land’s NAV - RLC is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY25E P/B, and 5.1X FY25E P/E

MACRO
BIR eyes to increase excise tax collection to P360bn in 2026
  • The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) targets a 9% increase of excise tax collections to P360bn in 2026, driven primarily by tobacco products
    • P167bn or close to half of the target collections will be from tobacco products, P132bn from alcohol products, P42bn from sugar-sweetened beverages, P12bn from mining, P7bn from automobiles
  • As of 1H25, excise tax collection on tobacco was up 34% to P59bn
    • In 2024 and 2023, P135bn in tobacco excise tax was collected, while P161bn was collected in 2022
    • Tobacco excise collection targets for the respective years were 21%, 45%, and 24% below target respectively
  • The Philippine Tobacco Institute (PTI) estimates that 20% of cigarette sticks sold in the country are illicit
  • The BIR has been estimated to have lost P114bn in lost revenues in 2024 due to illicit tobacco trade
