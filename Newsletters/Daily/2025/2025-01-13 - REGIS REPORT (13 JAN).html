REGIS REPORT

13 January 2025

The Jefferies team released two pieces over the last few days we wanted to highlight.

In the first one, Chris Wood talks about the repudiation of “woke-ism” across many Western countries, not just in politics but also in the corporate world. More relevant to the Philippines, however, he expresses his skepticism about a rapid adoption of AI.  He reminds us about the dotcom boom in the late 90s and early 2000s and that, even if the predictions about the real-world impact eventually came true, share prices of many internet names collapsed in the aftermath.  AI will come but just not as fast as many hope or fear.

The other piece, from Thomas Simons, discuss his view on Fed policy action for the rest of this year. Simons is of the belief that the Federal Reserve will cut rates by 75bp this year, making him possibly among the most dovish of Fed watchers.  For perspective, following the latest US jobs data, the market is now pricing Fed rate reductions of less than 50bp.

+++

We are also tweaking our view on some key macro metrics, adjusting our prediction on the Bangko Sentral (BSP) policy rate to 5.25% by year-end. This implies 50bp of rate reductions this year, as opposed to 75bp previously.  Note, however, that we expect the BSP to pursue more monetary easing through further cuts in bank reserve requirement ratios.

Our GDP growth estimate for FY24 and FY25 have also been trimmed (to 5.9% and 6.0%), partly due to the lower GDP figures for 3Q and partly due to expectations of less aggressive easing.

+++

The latest data point commercial bank loans showed a modest uptick in growth to 11.1% in Nov. Notable too is the data on M3 growth, which at 7.7%, is still below what we reckon would be accommodative for an economy that’s growing 6% in real terms.

+++

Citicore Renewable Energy (CREC PM) is issuing 2.23bn new shares to a subsidiary of Indonesian state-owned energy firm Pertamina. CREC will issue the shares at P3/share, thus raising over USD110m.  CREC’s ambitious project pipeline will likely require it to raise more in the future.

+++

Tourist arrivals rose 9% in FY24 to 5.95m but fell short of the government’s goals. Arrivals from China are still far lower (~80%) than they were immediately before the pandemic.

BANKS
Commercial banks' loan growth picks up to 11.1% YoY in November
  • Preliminary data showed the outstanding commercial bank loans rose by 11.1% YoY in November, a tad faster than October's 10.6% rate
    • Loans to productive activities rose 9.8% in Nov from 9.1% the previous month
      • Due to faster loan growth in retail, motor vehicle repair, and electricity/gas/steam supply industries
    • Consumer loans growth decelerated slightly to 23.3% in Nov from 23.6% as growth momentum continued by credit card and vehicle loans
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) growth accelerated to 7.7% in Nov from 5.5% in Oct

 

 

 

POWER
Indonesia-based Pertamina NRE takes 20% stake in CREC
  • Pertamina New & Renewable Energy (NRE) is subscribing to 2.23mn unissued common shares of CREC for P3/sh
    • Total buy-in valuation for a 20% stake in CREC will be P6.7bn (US$115mn)
    • Pertamina NRE is a subsidiary of Indonesia’s state-owned enterprise PT Pertamina Power Indonesia
    • Proceeds will be used to fund CREC’s renewable energy project pipeline
  • Upon deal completion, CREC’s total shares outstanding will rise by 25% to 11.16bn from 8.93bn
    • Parent Citicore Power’s stake in CREC will be diluted to 63% from 79% while its public float will be reduced to 17% from 21%
  • “The partnership with Pertamina NRE presents limitless opportunities for Indonesia and the Philippines to collaborate on innovative technologies and practices in renewable energy”
    • According to CREC President and CEO Oliver Tan
  • CREC currently owns 8 operating power plants with a gross installed capacity of 285MW
    • The company is constructing 1,583 MW of solar projects over the next 12 months
    • CREC has a project pipeline of 6,716MW (gross) of solar and 813MW (gross) of onshore wind, and 26MW of hydro plants, which will take overall gross installed capacity of 7,570MW by 2028
MACRO
Tourism arrivals up 9% YoY to 5.95mn but fell short of arrival target in 2024
  • Data from the Department of Tourism (DoT) exhibited that tourist arrivals for 2024 has increased by 9% YoY to 5.95mn from 5.5mn in 2023
    • However the 2024 figure fell short of its arrival target of 7.7mn for the year
    • Although tourist arrivals of the top sources of tourism for the country all improved from 2023—relative to 2019 levels, the full year figure was about 72% of 2019 levels
      • With tourist arrivals from China being the glaring laggard at only 18% of 2019 levels
  • Tourism revenues have surpassed pre-pandemic levels at 126% of 2019 at approx. P756bn~ arrivals being behind at 72%
  • The DoT has not yet set its tourist arrival targets for 2025

INDUSTRIALS
D&L sees faster growth in net earnings this year
  • D&L Industries Inc. (DNL PM), is positive on its outlook for this year as the company expects faster earnings growth vs. last year
    • The company president, Alvin Lao, stated that the company's profitability in the last two years has been hampered by tight consumer wallets
    • But this year may be better due to easing inflation and interest rates, alongside higher spending with election season
  • He expects a strong 4Q24 which will help the company surpass its 2023 net profit of P2.3bn
    • For 9M24, DNL's net profit was flat—up 1% YoY to P1.8bn with increased expenses due to newly commissioned lines in its Batangas plant
    • Lao highlighted strong demand in its biodiesel business as it has been operating at a higher capacity from 40% to above 60%~
      • This is as the DOE (Department of Energy) has mandated downstream oil industry participants to implement a higher % of coco methyl ester (CME) blend in all diesel fuel sold nationwide
      • The mandated increase will be done gradually from 2 to 3% last Oct 2024, to 4% by Oct 2025, and to 5% by Oct 2026
