REGIS REPORT

27 May 2025

1Q25 net profit of Vista Land (VLL PM) rose 6% YoY to P3.0bn, ahead of REGIS and consensus forecasts. Despite the c. 40% YoY decline in combined revenue of AllDay Marts (ALLDY PM) and AllHome (HOME PM), which occupy c. 50% of Vista’s mall space, Vista’s rental revenue grew 4% YoY (due to straight-line revenue recognition). Meanwhile, reported reservation sales were flat QoQ and YoY (at P21.1bn), though the absolute figures are gross of cancellations, which are likely to be well north of 50%.

 

Vista Land says it will only launch housing projects in provincial areas this year due to the widely reported oversupply of Metro Manila condominiums.

 

+++

 

The Dept of Agriculture (DA) is proposing to retain the lower (15%) duty on rice imports for now in order to restrain domestic rice prices. Rice import tariffs were cut to 15% (from 35%) by Executive Order, which also mandates a periodic review.

 

Domestic rice prices have fallen since the tariff was cut and after international prices tumbled. The decline, however, hasn’t been quite as sharp as one would logically surmise under free market conditions.

 

+++

 

Digital payments slowed slightly in April, as expected, given the timing of the Easter holidays. Transaction values were up 31% YoY, down from 44.5% YoY in March. However, adjusting for the Easter week, the combined March-April figure was up 37% YoY, slightly faster than the 36% YoY pace in Jan-Feb. Most interesting, though, was the 190% YoY surge in the number of transactions for InstaPay in April, the digital rail used for small-ticket items. By value, InstaPay was up 51% YoY, just slightly slower than the 53% YoY clip in March. This took the average value per transaction over InstaPay down 33% MoM to just P2.826. We suspect this was due to “election spending” ahead of the midterms that were held on 12 May. If we’re right, then the same trend should be seen in May, with a return to “normal” in the June numbers.

 

Globe’s (GLO PM) GCash/Mynt remains the dominant e-wallet to play digitalization trends. The figures also support our investment thesis for 2025, ie that we expect consumption spending to rise, particularly for lower-income households. We wouldn’t be surprised to see better numbers for URC, MONDE and PGOLD in 2Q25.

MACRO
DA backs 15% rice tariff retention; gradual hike under review
  • The Department of Agriculture (DA) will keep the 15% rice import tariff through 2Q25 to prevent price spikes during the lean season (July–September), but noted that a gradual hike back to the previous 35% rate is under review
    • The 15% tariff, reduced from 35% in July 2024 under EO 62, is subject to review every four months
    • The next review is in June 2025, a year after the cut was enacted
  • Agriculture Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. stressed that any tariff hike should be gradual and aligned with harvest cycles of exporting countries to avoid disruptive supply shocks
    • Laurel cautioned that abruptly reverting to the previous 35% rate could destabilize markets and instead proposed a "slight, phased adjustment"
    • DA officials warned that a tariff increase during lean months, when local output declines by 20-30%, may increase rice prices
  • DepDev (Dept. of Economy, Planning, and Dev't) Secretary Arsenio Balisacan supported exploring a seasonal tariff scheme
    • Where import duties would rise during times of low global prices and fall during price spikes, to help stabilize farmer incomes without fueling inflation

 

 

BANKS/DIGITAL BANKS
Digital payments rise 31% YoY in April
  • Payments made digitally over the National Retail Payments System (NRPS) rose by 31% to P1.85tn in April, moderating from the 44.5% pace in March
    • Adjusting for the timing of Easter break, combined digital payment transactions for the months of March and April alone rose 37% YoY, mildly accelerating from the +36% growth rate in 2M25
    • Brings 4M25 transaction value to P7.15tn, up 37% YoY
  • Payments through InstaPay, which handles real-time payments of up to P50k, rose 51% YoY to P841bn in April
    • By volume, InstaPay payments surged 190% YoY, even faster than the blistering 97% pace in March
  • Large-ticket payment transactions that go through PesoNet, rose 18% YoY to P1.011tn in April, slower than the 38% pace in March
  • Globe Telecom’s (GLO PM) GCash/Mynt remains the dominant player in this space
  • PLDT (TEL PM) recently announced that its own wallet/Digibank Maya broke into profitability in 1Q25. BPI (BPI PM) said they were competing aggressively for the payments pool.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

CONSUMER
Jollibee sells stake in C-Joy Realty
  • JFC in a disclosure, stated that it will sell its 30% stake in C-Joy Poultry Realty Inc. to Agrotex Commodities Inc. for P33.9mn
    • C-Joy Realty currently leases land to Cargill Joy Poultry Meat Production Inc., which operates a poultry processing plant in Batangas
    • Cargill Inc. owns the remaining 70% of C-Joy Realty
  • The chicken production plant is a partnership between JFC and US-based food company Cargill Inc.
    • The facility supplies chicken to Jollibee Group restaurants
    • JFC said it will keep its 30% stake in C-Joy Poultry Meats Production
  • Rationale for the divestment is the company's "…strategic shift towards an asset-light business model, enabling greater capital efficiency and sharper focus on scalable, high-return investments."
