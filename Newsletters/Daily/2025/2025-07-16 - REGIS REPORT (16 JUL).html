President Ferdinand Marcos, Jr’s approval ratings bounced back strongly in June, according to survey firm Social Weather Stations. His net trust rating (“much trust” minus “little trust”) jumped 19 points to +18% in June, after languishing in negative territory from March to May following the arrest and extradition of former President Duterte (see our previous report here). The sharp recovery, we believe, was due in large part to recently announced populist policies such as rice subsidies and food vouchers. Despite the bounce, Mr Marcos’s ratings are still well below where they were at the start of his term. His ratings were also below those of Vice President Sara Duterte, at +38%, though her figures only eked out a 1%age point gain MoM. Her high “much trust” rating (+61%) could be a factor in her approaching impeachment trial.

 

+ + +

 

More bad news for the gaming sector – both online and offline. Senator Rafael Tulfo became the 6th Senator (by our count) to publicly call for a total ban on online gaming. At least two others seek to impose varying restrictions on operators and/or payments platforms.

 

Back in the physical world, Okada Manila reported pretty dire 2Q25 numbers, with GGR plunging 9% QoQ and 20% YoY, according to disclosures by its parent Universal Entertainment. Volumes were also down 4% QoQ and 27% YoY. We believe the continued softness is due to the POGO ban that became effective end-2024. The Okada numbers do not bode well for Bloomberry’s (BLOOM PM) Solaire Entertainment City property, which we assume will follow a similar trajectory. Prospects might be even bleaker, as weak demand will be met with more competition when the new integrated resort of Suntrust Resort Holdings (SUN PM) opens in the Manila Bay area next year.

 

+ + +

 

SM Prime (SMPH PM) has put its plans to list a REIT for its mall assets in the back burner.

 

+ + +

 

Cebu Air (CEB PM) reported that passenger volume growth slowed sharply to +8% in June, from +24% in the first 5 months of the year. This was likely due to the early start of school openings, which was moved to June, compared to July last year, cutting the peak summer travel season short. This still puts the 1H25 volume up 21% YoY, compared to our FY25 forecast of +16% YoY. We note, though, that CEB’s operating leverage is extremely high, and that any continued weakness in monthly numbers could pose outsized impact on our bottom line forecasts.

 

+ + +

 

OFW remittances grew 2.9% YoY in May to $2.66bn, normalizing from the 4.0% growth in April. However, in PHP terms, the May figure was down 1% YoY due to the strong PHP / weak USD for the period.

POLITICS
Marcos' net trust rating improves by 19ppt to 18% in June
  • In the latest Social Weather Stations Survey, President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.'s trust rating improved—jumping to an 18% net trust rating in June from -1% in May
    • Net figures are computed by % "much trust" rating minus % "little trust" rating
    • President Marcos' "much trust" rating went up to 48% in June from 38% in May, while those who answered "little trust" in the President fell to 30% from 39% of respondents in the same period
    • Notably, the 18% net trust rating is still much lower than Marcos' 63% net trust rating at the start of his term
      • But marks a stark improvement from his -3% net trust rating in Mar 2025 following the arrest of Former President Rodrigo Duterte
  • Vice President Sara Duterte's net trust rating inched up 1ppt to 38% in June from 37% in May
    • VP Duterte's "much trust" rating went up slightly to 61% in June from 60% in May, while those who answered "little trust" in the President was steady at 23% of respondents
    • The 38% net trust rating of the VP is much lower than her 73% net trust rating at the start of her term
      • But reflects an improvement for her 23% rating when she was impeached by the Lower House in Feb 2025

GAMING
Okada Manila volumes fall QoQ and YoY in 2Q25
  • Okada Manila’s 2Q25 volumes fell YoY and QoQ across every major category
    • As a result, 2Q25 GGR declined 9% QoQ and 20% YoY to P7.1bn
  • Mass table drop decreased 29% QoQ and 31% YoY to P7.5bn
    • Mass table GGR fell 33% QoQ and YoY to P2.0bn
  • Gaming machine handle declined 4% QoQ and 17% YoY to P46.6bn
    • Gaming machine GGR decreased 7% QoQ and 6% YoY to P2.8bn 
  • VIP rolling chip fell 1% QoQ and 33% YoY to P52.4bn
    • However, VIP GGR rose 29% QoQ (down 21% YoY) to P2.3bn, thanks to an unusually high win rate of 4.4%

TRANSPORT
Cebu Air grows pax volumes by 8% YoY in June; Load factors dip but remain high
  • Cebu Air (CEB PM) reported that systemwide pax volumes rose 8% YoY to 2.2mn in June
    • Growth was markedly slower than the 24% pace in 5M25 due to onset of the leaner season
    • Seat capacity rose 9% YoY and load factors dipped 73bps YoY to 87.5% due to the earlier start of school calendars (from July to June)
  • Domestic pax volumes rose 7% YoY to 1.7mn with load factors up 42bps to 92.1%
  • International pax volumes grew 10% YoY to 558k with load factors down by ~3ppts to 76.0%
  • As of 1H25, pax volumes are up 21% YoY to 13.9mn, trending mildly behind our full-year forecast of 28.5mn (+16%)
    • To reach this, pax volumes for the rest of the year would need to average 2.4mn per month
    • Every 1% reduction in pax volumes could reduce EBITDA/profit forecasts by 3%/17%, holding other things constant
    • We are encouraged by robust load factors, which spells strength in fares

 

 

 

MACRO
Marcos approves P6.8tn 2026 budget
  • President Ferdinand Marcos. Jr. approved the proposed P6.8tn national budget for 2026 which will be submitted to Congress later this month
  • The P6.8tn budget is equivalent to 22% of GDP and 7% higher than 2025's P6.33 budget
    • Priority allocations are the education, agriculture, and transportation sectors, which will see increases in their respective budgets for next year
