Here are the quick summaries of the most recent results:

 

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (=) – Profits rose 6% to P64bn for 9M, with 3Q income at P22bn (+5%), broadly in line with expectations. There weren’t any surprises for its biggest parts (BDO and SM Prime), but there was a little bit of weakness for SM Retail. Revenues for SM Retail were up +1% in 3Q, with Food Retail up +6%.  Non-Food Retail did not do as well.

  • Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) (-) – The 3Q25 net loss widened to P1.7bn, form P1.4bn in 2Q25 and P470mn in 3Q24. Although GGR grew 4% QoQ to P14.5bn, EBITDA fell to P1.9bn (down 24% QoQ and 53% YoY), behind REGIS and consensus forecasts, due to higher cash costs (QoQ). The company also said that the full launch of its online gaming platform, MegaFUNalo, would be later than the original plan of 1Q26.

  • Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) (+) – Soaring nickel ore prices boosted NIKL’s 9M25 recurring profit up 72% YoY to P4.4bn, running well ahead of REGIS estimates and exceeding the Street’s full-year forecast.  3Q25 profits more than doubled to P3.1bn on the back of a 33% improvement in ASPs, as well as a strong 12% rebound in sales volumes. The company announced another special dividend of P0.07/sh (ex-date: Nov 27), taking total dividends this year to P0.18/sh, equivalent to a payout ratio of 81% and a dividend yield of 5%. 

 

Meanwhile, Alliance Global (AGI PM) said that its 9M25 core attributable profit rose 8% to P13.9bn, which would be in line with consensus forecast. However, we estimate that 3Q25 core attributable profit fell c. 40% QoQ and 20% YoY, largely due to the revenue decline of the brand and whisky businesses of Emperador (EMI PM).

MINING
Nickel Asia (NIKL PM): Strong nickel ore prices lift 9M25 core profit up 72%

3Q25 profits up >2x

NIKL reported 9M25 profits doubled to P5.2bn. Removing the FX adjustment relating to the divestment of Coral Bay HPAL in 1Q, we estimate recurring profit stood at P4.4bn, up 72% YoY and trending well ahead of our forecast of P4.7bn and exceeding consensus full-year estimate of P4.3bn. 

 

In 3Q25 alone, core profit more than doubled to P3.1bn, driven solely by a 48% YoY improvement in mining revenues. EBITDA jumped 71% YoY to P6.3bn, a new quarter high.  Management will hold a briefing on Friday, November 14 at 2PM (registration link).

 

Mining ASPs up 33%, sales volumes rebound in 3Q25

The mining unit maintained its recovery momentum after weather-related disruptions in 1Q, with sales volumes up 12% YoY in 3Q25, reversing the 4% drop in 1H.  ASPs also strengthened by 12% QoQ, 33% YoY to US$31/WMT, bringing 9M25 ASPs up 38% to US$27/WMT.  While ore export prices (largely saprolite) fell 10% QoQ (+19% YoY) to US$36/WMT, prices of lower-grade limonite ore sold to the HPAL plants surprisingly jumped 19% QoQ (+9% YoY) to US$12/WMT.   

 

Power profits drop on soft spot prices

EBITDA for the power segment (including non-RE facilities) fell 39% YoY to P110mn in 3Q25, bringing 9M25 EBITDA down 20% YoY to P518mn.  The company began commissioning another 120MW (att. 72MW) solar plant this month, which will add to the current capacity of 172MW (att. 147MW).   

 

Reveals initial results for gold-silver-copper prospect

The company disclosed initial exploration results for its Cordon project in Isabela (northern Philippines), which indicated grades of 15.5 g/t Au, 85 g/t Ag, and 0.42% Cu. The Cordon project is held under Cordillera Exploration Co., a joint venture between NIKL (71%) and Sumitomo Metal (29%).

 

Declares P0.07/sh special dividend

The company declared another special dividend of P0.07/sh (ex-date: Nov 27), payable on Dec 15. Together with the regular dividend of P0.066/sh and the special dividend of P0.044/sh earlier this year, NIKL’s payout ratio has risen to 81% from 48% the year before.  This year’s dividend equates to a yield of ~5%. 

 

 

CONGLOS
AGI 3Q25 profit falls QoQ and YoY
  • Alliance Global said that its 9M25 core attributable profit grew 8% to P13.9bn, in line with consensus
  • We estimate that 3Q25 core attributable profit fell c. 40% QoQ and 20% YoY, largely due to Emperador
  • Megaworld’s 3Q25 operating profit rose 9% YoY (-2% QoQ) to P7.4bn, largely thanks to the increase in rental revenue
  • Travellers’ 3Q25 operating profit grew 25% YoY (flat QoQ) to P1.6bn, thanks to largely to lower cash costs as well as higher mass GGR
    • 3Q25 GGR rose 7% QoQ, thanks to the 10% increase in mass GGR
  • Emperador's 3Q25 EBITDA fell 18% YoY (-40% QoQ) to P1.8bn, primarily due to declining topline and lower gross margins for both the brandy and whisky segments 
    • Both segments saw a drop in sales in the quarter with brandy sales down 6% YoY to P7.8bn while whisky revenues fell 17% YoY to P5.2bn
    • Gross margins for both segments contracted mainly due to product mix with brandy's continued value brands push and whisky's continued softness in single-malt whiskeys
  • The company said that proceeds from the 2.2bn warrants (P0.50/warrant) as well as the potential issuance of new shares (at P12/share exercise price) would be used to fund capex and reduce debt
    • Total proceeds could reach P27.5bn if all the warrants are exercised
    • AGI is exploring new businesses and is also looking to infuse equity into its existing subsidiaries

 

 

 

MACRO
New BIR chief appointed by Marcos
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. appointed Finance Undersecretary Charlito Mendoza as the new commissioner of the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR), replacing Romeo Lumagui
  • Mendoza, prior to his current appointment, heads the Dept. of Finance's supervisory group that oversees the BIR and Bureau of Customs (BOC)
    • He also served as customs district collector for Cebu and was a practicing lawyer prior to joining the gov't
  • Lumagui was appointed as BIR Chief back in Nov 2022 by President Marcos
    • A day prior to being replaced, he had ordered a "major personnel overhaul" across the BIR's regional offices
    • Last month, the BIR under Marcos' orders had begun conducting lifestyle checks on gov't officials
