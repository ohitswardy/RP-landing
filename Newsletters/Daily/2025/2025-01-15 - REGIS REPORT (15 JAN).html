REGIS REPORT

15 January 2025

Vehicle sales reported by CAMPI rose 7% YoY in Dec to 42,044 units, taking full year unit sales to 467k (+8.7%).  Toyota sales amounted to 20.263 units for the month (+3.6% YoY and 4.8% MoM). For the year 2024, Toyota sales reached 218k units, which is 9% higher YoY and ahead of Regis assumptions. GT Capital (GTCAP PM) is the 51% owner of Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP).

Note that the CAMPI figures do not include data from some Chinese auto firms, most notably BYD, which is becoming increasingly prominent as the market gradually shifts to EVs. The distributor of BYD in the Philippines is controlled by AC Mobility, a subsidiary of Ayala Corp (AC PM).

+++

Thailand appears to want in on the gambling action with the Cabinet approving a draft bill to allow the development and operation of casinos. The issuance of new casino licenses in Thailand can be both a threat and an opportunity for the likes of Bloomberry (BLOOM PM). But given that BLOOM has its plate full now, it is more likely to be a potential threat, especially in terms of attracting overseas (Chinese) gamblers.

+++

Semirara (SCC PM) announced it achieved record coal mining sales volumes in 2024 of 16.5mn MT, exceeding our forecast and its own guidance of 16mn MT.

+++

The prospects for the legislated wage hike which alarmed quite a few people now appear dead in the water. The House leadership does not expect to entertain any action on the matter before the current session of Congress ends.

MACRO
House freezes work on bills seeking legislated wage hikes
  • Wage hike bills seeking to impose a legislated national wage hike have become secondary at the House of Representatives
    • According to Rizal Rep. Juan Fidel Felipe F. Nograles, the House Labor Committee Chairman
  • Recall bills seeking to increase wages of private sector workers by between P150 and P750 were filed at the Lower House last year
    • The Senate approved a bill increasing the daily minimum wage in the private sector by P100
  • The wage hike bill will be revisited with the 20th Congress, which will open on July 28, to provide the opportunity to review existing wage orders before holding the wage hike hearing
MACRO
Bill reorganizing NEDA into a department gets final House nod
  • The Lower House unanimously voted to approve on third reading House Bill 11199 or the proposed Economy, Planning and Development Act
  • Bill seeks to reorganize and institutionalize NEDA into the Department of Economy, Planning and Development (DEPDEV), expanding its functions and capacity
  • The measure’s counterpart bill in the Senate was approved on third and final reading last month
CONGLOS
Vehicle sales rise 7% YoY in December; FY24 sales up 8.7%
  • Industry car sales grew 7.4% YoY, 2.8% MoM to 42,044 units in December
    • According to the Chamber of Automotive Manufacturers (CAMPI) and Truck Manufacturers Assoc. (TMA)
    • Brings FY24 vehicle sales to 467,252 units, up 8.7% YoY and broadly in line with CAMPI’s target of 468,300 units
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) sold 20,263 units in December, up by 3.6% YoY and 4.8% MoM
    • Brings FY24 retail sales higher by 9% YoY to 218,019 units, above management’s target of 215k units (+7%) and Regis’ forecast of 204k units (+3%)
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 32% of NAV (25% of gross asset value) and 26% of profit before parent expenses
    • GTCAP is trading at a 54% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,243/sh and 5x FY25E PE

 

 

GAMING
Thailand’s Cabinet approves draft casino bill
  • Thailand’s Cabinet approved the draft “Entertainment Complex Business Act”
  • The draft will proceed to the House of Representatives
  • The draft bill would allow casinos to operate within a complex that includes a hotel, convention center, mall, or theme park
  • People below 20 years old will not be allowed to enter the casinos
    • Foreigners can enter for free but Thai citizens must pay THB5,000 (US$148) to enter
  • Should there be Integrated Resorts in Thailand in the future, they could compete with Philippine casinos for foreign players, including VIPs
  • Foreigners comprise c. 55% of the GGR of Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM)
    • VIP comprises c. 25% of BLOOM’s GGR
MINING
Semirara 2024 sales volumes reach 16.5mn MT
  • In a press release, Semirara said it achieved record high coal shipments of 16.5mn MT in 2024, up 4% YoY and surpassing our forecast of 16mn MT
    • Exports rose by 3% YoY to 8.4mn MT, with sales to China rising by 46% YoY to 7.6mn MT
    • Domestic sales volumes grew by 4% 8mn MT, driven by higher sales to its own power plants and cement plants
    • Company hit its maximum annual production limit of 16mn MT for the third consecutive year
    • SCC’ coal mining unit accounts for ~70% of profits
  • For 2025, we expect Semirara to generate profits of P18bn (vs. FY24E: P20bn), underpinned by coal mining sales volumes of 15.5mn MT
    • SCC trades at 4.3x FY25E PER
    • We forecast the company could pay dividends of P4.5/sh this year (~13% yield), lower than last year’s P6/sh
