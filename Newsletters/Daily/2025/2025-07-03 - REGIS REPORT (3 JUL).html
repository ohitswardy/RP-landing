Aboitiz Power (AP PM) is planning to build a 351MWdc solar farm with storage (338MWh) in Ilocos Norte. If all goes according to plan, the project will begin commercial operations in 2029. AP currently has attributable capacity of 4,636MW.

Online gaming firm Digiplus (PLUS PM) says it intends to launch its Brazil gaming platform in Sept, where it will be one of around 70 operators. The Brazil market is said to be worth around US$4.6bn.

 

+++

 

Speaking of PLUS, the company is likely to become part of the PSE’s (PSE PM) benchmark PSEi next month, assuming there’s no change in the criteria for index inclusion/exclusion. If it does get included, it will likely be at the expense of Bloomberry (BLOOM PM).

The newly appointed head of the Customs bureau is aiming to make up for the disappointing collections at the bureau. Customs collections are virtually flat YoY as of May, well behind this year’s goal of +8%.

DigiPlus to launch Brazil e-games operations in September

MARKET
PSE Index rebalancing prediction: BLOOM (-), PLUS (+)
  • Assuming there are no changes to the PSE Index inclusion criteria, we would expect Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) to be removed from the PSE Index during the August 2025 rebalancing
    • We forecast DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) to take its place
  • The PSE Index rebalancing in August 2025 will be based on the 12 months from July 2024 to June 2025
  • Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap
  • Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when
    • 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or
    • 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35 
  • Based on our estimates, BLOOM is ranked outside the top 35 and PLUS is ranked within the top 25
  • Based on our initial estimates, there could be c. US$39mn of inflow into PLUS
    • There could also be inflow to JGS and SMC due to higher public float
  • We also estimate c. US$11mn of outflow from BLOOM and about US$30mn of outflow from all other PSE Index constituents
  • Note that weighting on the PSE Index is based on free-float market cap
  • Thus, estimates on inflow/outflow change as free-float market cap of PSE Index constituents change
    • This can be due to movements in share price or public float

 

 

 

MACRO
New customs chief eyes catch up for 2025, raises 2026 collection target
  • Ariel Nepomuceno has been appointed as the new Customs Commissioner, replacing Bienvenido Rubio
    • Nepomuceno is one out of the five other new appointments in an effort by President Ferdinand Marcos. Jr. to overhaul the Bureau of Customs (BOC)
  • This follows the lower-than-expected revenue collection YT-May, which is flat YoY (+0.2%) at P382bn—lagging behind the 8% YoY growth target for the full year
    • The lackluster collection stems from the impact of reduced tariffs on rice and EVs
  • The newly appointed commissioner aims to catch up the recently slashed 2025 customs collection target of P990bn (+8% YoY) from P1.06tn (+16% YoY) previously
    • The agency however raised the revenue target for 2026 to P1.1tn (+11% YoY), eyeing to breach P1tn in revenues for the first time
  • Nepomuceno stated ramping up digitalization efforts should boost revenue collection given higher efficiency and transparency in customs processes
  • The Philippine gov't targets P4.5tn (+16% YoY) in revenues this year and seeks to narrow the fiscal deficit to 5.5% of GDP
    • The fiscal deficit as of 1Q25 is at 6.8% of GDP
BANKS
Cariaso appointed as new RCBC president and CEO
  • Rizal Commercial Banking Corp. (RCBC PM) has appointed Reginaldo Anthony B. Cariaso as its president and CEO, succeeding Eugene S. Acevedo (who will remain part of the RCBC's board of directors)
    • Cariaso was the EVP and operations group head before becoming deputy CEO following Acevedo's retirement back in January
  • Prior to joining RCBC, Cariaso took on senior leadership roles at Bank of the Philippine Islands and JPMorgan Hong Kong
  • Cariaso aims to continue efforts in the company's digital push and commitment to innovation
