REGIS REPORT

30 October 2025

Aboitiz Power’s (AP PM) 3Q25 EBITDA rose 12% YoY, marking a turnaround after two quarters of contraction.  The company attributed the recovery to stronger hydro generation and reduced exposure to depressed spot prices.  Although higher non-cash and financing costs from GN Power Dinginin (GNPD) continued to drag the bottom line, core profit edged up 3% YoY to P10.4bn in the quarter, narrowing the 9M25 profit (P23.1bn) decline to -15%, from -25% in 1H25. We hope to know more during management’s briefing on Nov 6, 4pm (registration link).

+++

Colliers said that the Metro Manila residential and office segments both showed stronger demand in 3Q25. Office net take-up grew about 75% QoQ while residential net take-up doubled on a YoY and QoQ basis. Although the flood control corruption scandal dampened the residential luxury segment, sales of mid-income units improved thanks to the aggressive promotions on ready-for-occupancy units.

+++

AREIT (AREIT PM) will acquire 375,000sqm of malls from its sponsor, Ayala Land (ALI PM), by issuing 441mn new AREIT shares to the latter. The issuance will increase shares outstanding by 12% and we estimate that the Ayala Group would need to sell about 140mn AREIT shares in order for the latter to meet the minimum public ownership requirement  

+++

Universal Robina Corporation (URC PM) halted the operations of its Bais Distillery Plant after the collapse of its wastewater lagoon wall. The Bais distillery is one of two ethanol distillery plants of URC’s renewables business, which accounts for 4% of EBIT.

  • Offshore gaming operations are prohibited while work permits and visas issued to people involved in such activities are revoked

PROPERTY
Colliers: Office and residential net take-up improve in 3Q25
  • Colliers reported that Metro Manila office vacancy remained flat QoQ at 20% in 3Q25        
    • Makati CBD vacancy was flat QoQ at 8.2%
    • BGC vacancy fell c. 2ppt QoQ to 12.4%
  • Net office take-up rose 75% QoQ to 105,000sqm
    • This was largely due to higher take-up from traditional tenants as well as fewer POGO closures
  • Office rental rates in most parts of Metro Manila were generally flat QoQ
  • Residential vacancy in Metro Manila was also relatively flat QoQ at 25%
  • Net take-up of Metro Manila condominium units doubled QoQ and YoY to 5,800 units, the highest level in eight quarters
  • The improvement in net take-up was driven by stronger take-up of mid-income units as well as lower cancellations
  • Colliers said that aggressive promotions (such rent-to-own schemes of up to 10 years) and cash discounts of up to 60% on select ready-for-occupancy units continued to entice buyers
  • On the other hand, Colliers Head of Research Joey Bondoc said that “…the flood control mess has had some impact on the more expensive condo segments – luxury and ultra luxury…”

  • MEG is trading at 69% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY26E P/B, and 3.1X FY26E P/E

POWER
Aboitiz Power posts 12% EBITDA recovery in 3Q25
  • In a press release, Aboitiz Power reported its core income (excluding FX and derivative gains) reached P23.1bn in 9M25, lower by 15% YoY
    • This is in line with the Street’s downgraded forecast of P30.6bn
    • Headline 9M25 profit was also lower by 15% YoY at P23.3bn
  • Implied 3Q25 core profit stood at P10.4bn, up 29% QoQ and 3% YoY
    • Beneficial EBITDA in 3Q25 rebounded by 12% YoY to P22.2bn, marking a recovery after two quarters of contraction
    • Generation/RES unit EBITDA jumped 12% YoY to P20bn, owing to (a) better hydro output and (b) reduced spot exposure
    • EBITDA for the distribution unit (DU) improved 5% YoY to P2.5bn, on the back of a 6% improvement in volumes
  • Management is holding a briefing on November 6, 4pm (registration link)

CONSUMER
URC ethanol distillery halts operations due to waste spill
  • Universal Robina Corporation (URC PM) has halted the operations of its Bais Distillery Plant after the collapse of its wastewater lagoon wall due to damages from the recent earthquake and heavy rains, which resulted in the spillage of wastewater to the Tañon Strait (between Negros Island and Cebu)
  • The distillery is located near the shoreline of Bais City, Negros Oriental and produces high-grade anhydrous ethanol for fuel blending using the molasses by-product sourced from the nearby URC sugar milling plant
  • The company is coordinating with affected communities to extend assistance and mitigate the impact of the spill
    • The Dept. of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) ordered URC to fast-track repairs to halt further contamination
    • Bais City Mayor Lugi Goñi said operations of the plant will only resume when URC gives "a more concrete, reliable, and long-term solution to this problem" as it has been recurring for the several years already
      • Previously reported incidents include "intentional" waste discharge as well reports of fish kill which led to the temporary closure of the plant back in 2015
  • The Bais distillery is one of two ethanol distillery plants part of the renewables segment of the URC's SURE (Sugar and Renewables) business, the renewables business alone was ~4% of consolidated topline and EBIT in 2024
