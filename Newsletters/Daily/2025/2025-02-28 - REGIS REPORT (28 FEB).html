REGIS REPORT

28 February 2025

PLDT (TEL PM) reported results yesterday, with core income reaching P34bn (+6% YoY), inclusive of losses at fintech Maya.  We’d describe the results as broadly within expectations, but note the surprising bounce in 4Q mobile revenue. This arguably raises questions about the relative softness in Globe’s (GLO PM) 4Q mobile results. Meanwhile, the net adds for wireline broadband seem to be a little disappointing, especially in comparison to competition. 

 

Management also shared their thoughts on the ~20% stake in Maya that KKR is reportedly selling.

Meanwhile, Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) (-) reported that recurring FY24 profit fell 18% YoY to P3.07bn, below Regis’ FY24E but ahead of consensus estimates. For 4Q24 alone, profits rose 5x YoY to P518mn, buoyed by lower losses at the HPAL facilities, which ran in contrast to the sustained weakness in LME nickel prices during the quarter. Ore mining revenues improved in 4Q (+4% YoY), ending six consecutive quarters of declines, thanks to more stable ore prices and the depreciation of the PHP.  The bad news is the revenue improvement during the quarter was more than offset by higher cash costs. Management will hold a briefing on March 5.     

 

 

+++

 

The government’s reported budget deficit for FY24 came in at P1.51trn, practically unchanged YoY. This is equivalent to 5.7% of GDP, which is worse than target (5.6%) but better than the previous year (6.1%). 

 

We need to highlight, however, that non-tax revenues were up sharply due to the transfers from government corporations (e.g. Philhealth, PDIC, etc). These are unlikely to be repeatable.

 

+++

 

Ayala Land (ALI PM) announced that it would purchase from ABS-CBN (ABS PM) a 30,000sqm parcel of land on which its facilities sit. ABS will then consolidate its facilities onto an adjacent 14,000sqm parcel. The P6.24bn purchase price will be payable over 10 years, which implies the NPV of the purchase price is likely to be well below the headline P208k/sqm.

 

The transaction is small for Ayala Land, representing just 1% of the company’s NAV. However, it will help ABS pay down its debts to BDO (BDO PM), Bank of the Philippine Islands (BPI PM), Security Bank (SECB PM), and UnionBank (UBP PM), as the former media giant has been registering losses since it lost its franchise in 2020.

MINING
NIKL: Profits rebound in 4Q
  • Headline FY24 profit of P1.52bn included a P1.55bn provision for its geothermal investment in Mindoro
    • Reported core profit stood at P3.07bn (-18% YoY), beating consensus forecast of P2.4bn but fell short of our estimate of P4.2bn
    • FY24 EBITDA stood at P8.8bn (-20% YoY) fell a tad short of our forecast (P8.9bn) but was above consensus forecast (P8.6bn)
    • This suggests that the key swing factor to the miss/beat was the loss of the HPAL plants
  • Implied 4Q24 core profit rose five-fold YoY to P518mn, primarily driven by lower equitized losses at its HPAL facilities
    • On a sequential basis, earnings fell 64% due to monsoon rains, which typically hit Palawan/Surigao in 1Q-4Q
    • EBITDA marginally improved (+2% YoY) to P2.1bn in 4Q24 on the back of a mild improvement in ore prices, which we suspect was offset by higher operating costs
  • NIKL declared a dividend of P0.15/sh (ex-div: Mar 12), equivalent to a yield of 6%. The dividend is equivalent to 68% of NIKL’s core income.   
  • Management is holding a briefing on March 5, 2pm (registration link)
MACRO
Gov't records budget deficit of P330bn in December (-17.8% YoY); P1.51tn for FY24 (-0.4% YoY)
  • The government recorded a budget deficit of P330bn in Dec, narrower than Dec 2023's deficit of P401bn
    • Bringing the FY24 deficit to 1.51tn, which narrowed by P5.7bn (0.4% YoY)
      • The FY deficit is equivalent to 5.7% of GDP—much lower than the 6.2% seen in 2023 but short of the 5.6% of GDP target set by the gov't
  • Revenue collections in Dec jumped 21.0% YoY to P315.bn, primarily driven by non-tax revenues which surged 333% YoY as tax revenues were flattish only up 2% YoY
    • The full-year revenue figure rose 15.6% YoY to P4.419tn as BIR collections were on target alongside better-than-expected non-tax revenues but was offset by customs' missed FY24 goal
      • Non-tax revenues surged due to windfall collections from Public-Private Partnership (PPP) concession fee (P30bn) and fund balance transfers (P167bn) from the Philippine Health Insurance Corp. (PhilHealth) and Philippine Deposit Insurance Corp. (PDIC)
        • The Bureau of Treasury (BTr) also saw higher dividend remittances and PAGCOR profits
      • Customs were short P23bn of their P940bn target given the implemented tariffs reductions on rice, meat products, and electric vehicles
  • Expenditures went down slightly by 2.6% in Dec as both primary expenses and interest payments were down for the month
    • Full-year disbursements however were up 11% YoY to P5.93tn and 3% higher than the FY24 program of P5.75tn due to higher-than-expected primary spending
    • Primary spending for the FY24 rose 9.6% YoY to P5.16tn
      • The increase in primary spending was attributed to the (a) infrastructure/other capital outlays of the Dept. of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) (b) maintenance and other opex for health and social protection programs, (c) salary adjustments of qualified gov't employees
    • Interest payments went up 21.5% given elevated interest rates alongside less favorable forex fluctuations
  • The gov’t targets the deficit to GDP ratio to narrow further to 5.3% by year-end 2025

 

 

PROPERTY
Ayala Land to purchase ABS-CBN property
  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) signed an MOA to acquire 30,000sqm of land from ABS-CBN (ABS PM)
  • The parcel will be acquired for P6.24bn (inclusive of VAT) to be paid over 10 years
    • This implies a transaction value of P208,000/sqm, lower than a transaction done at c. P250k/sqm in the 2-3 years ago
    • The value of the transaction represents c. 1% of ALI’s NAV
  • The property currently houses several buildings, including production studios
  • ABS will consolidate its facilities in the remaining 14,000sqm it holds (adjacent to the 30,000sqm)
  • ALI is trading at 58% discount to NAV, 1.1X FY25E P/B, and 10.6X FY25E P/E
  • ABS engaged in this deal in an effort to repay its debts which the company has been struggling to do since losing its legislative franchise back in 2020
    • As of 9M24, ABS reported a net loss of P2.59bn while its total consolidated bank loans stood at P16.3bn. Banks mentioned in the loan section include BDO, BPI, UBP, and SECB
      • Of the loans, parent ABS was P11.8bn, while SkyCable's was P4.5bn
