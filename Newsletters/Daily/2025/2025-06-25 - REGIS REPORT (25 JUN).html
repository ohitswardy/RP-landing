REGIS REPORT

25 June 2025

Stock market darling Digiplus (PLUS PM) has certainly caught the attention of investors in the Philippine market after rising a blistering 40x (~3,900%) over the past three years. This gain has been on the back of the explosive growth in online gaming (gambling) over the same period, which has not gone unnoticed.

 

For FY25, the “industry” is likely to reach almost US$4bn in revenue -- larger than the combined domestic branded revenues of the likes of URC (URC PM), Monde (MONDE PM) and Century Pacific (CNPF PM). It’s also now almost the same size as the entire mobile sector with revenues not far from the combined wireless revenues of PLDT (TEL PM), Globe (GLO PM) and Dito Tel.

 

Despite its newfound prominence, what’s been less noticed (or discussed) is the impact online gambling is having elsewhere, including on the traditional consumer names. Online gambling has been, in our view, a key reason why the consumption rebound we anticipated has been less pronounced.

 

These less benign, effects of online gambling have now been pushed to the public view and are now attracting attention from politicians. This scrutiny, we reckon, is likely to result in some restriction/s being imposed to curtail the online gaming industry.

GAMING
Some members of Congress want online gambling to be inaccessible on e-wallets
  • Some members of the House of Representatives are drafting a bill that seeks to remove gambling apps from e-wallet platforms
  • Representative Jonathan Keith Flores said that e-wallets have become “…channels of addiction”
  • About 80% DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) players fund their gaming via GCASH
  • We estimate that over 95% of players fund online gaming via e-wallets/online banking
  • Meanwhile, Representative Rolando Valeriano believes that the government should prohibit all online gambling
  • Gross Gaming Revenue of e-games (including bingo) grew 88% YoY (flat QoQ) to P51bn in 1Q25
    • DigiPlus is the market leader with c. 45% market share
  • PLUS is trading at 13.8X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
MACRO
Wage hike deliberations by NCR board to start this week
  • The National Capital Region (NCR) wage board is set to meet this week to deliberate on possible wage adjustments
    • This follows after the 19th Congress adjourned earlier this month without passing the proposed P100-200 across-the-board wage hike bill
    • With no law passed before the end of the legislative cycle, wage adjustments will primarily be decided by the Regional Tripartite Wages and Productivity Boards (RTWPBs)
  • Labor Secretary Bienvenido E. Laguesma stated that the last wage board order increase of P35 (from P610 to P645) took effect on July 17 last year, so the NCR board is within schedule to issue a new wage order
    • Laguesma stated that a new wage order for NCR workers can be expected—if any—by next month
  • The current minimum wage in NCR stands at P645, while the average minimum wage nationwide is P474

POWER
ACEN signs deal to acquire a 25% stake in a 1GW offshore wind project
  • ACEN has signed a deed that will allow the company to acquire a 25% stake in CI GMF II Camarines Offshore Wind Energy Corp. for P2.95mn
    • Shares were acquired from Danish firm CI NMF Philippines CV
  • CI GMF II is developing an offshore wind project that could potentially have an installed capacity of up to 1GW
    • Project will be in San Miguel Bay in Camarines Sur and was previously estimated to cost US$3bn
  • Project is currently in its pre-development stage in anticipation of the Department of Energy’s 5th round of the Green Energy Auction (GEA-5) and will be subject to relevant regulatory approvals
