REGIS REPORT

04 November 2025

BPI (BPI PM) held its briefing last Thursday. To recap, 9M NPAT was up 5% to P50.5bn. Loans were up 13% YoY, with consumer/MSME/microfinance loans surging 27% and are now over 30% of total loans. NIMs continued to climb, taking PPOP up 16% YoY. Provision expense was up 145% from an artificially low base in 2024, but credit costs have stabilized QoQ at around 75bps. During the earnings call, management took great pains to detail how, the risk-adjusted returns of the consumer/MSME push were much better even after higher provisions. Despite the in-line earnings, the stock is near its 22-month low and is now trading at 1x P/bk, and pays a 4.8% dividend yield on our FY26E forecasts. Buy. Metrobank (MBT PM) released headline NPAT of P37.3bn for 9M25, up 4% YoY in line with our FY25E of P50.9bn (consensus P50.2bn). Loans were up 11%, with consumers up 16%. The bank also booked large trading/fx gains of P6.6bn. Opex was kept in check, up just 2% YoY. This took PPOP up 12% YoY. However, asset quality deteriorated slightly, with NPL at 1.7% (from 1.5%), with NPL cover at 147.4% (from 153.9%). More when full FS is out later this am. Briefing this afternoon 430pm.

 

 

Semirara (SCC PM) net profit (ex-FX) for 3Q fell 53% to P1.47bn, bringing 9M profit to P9.76bn (-38%). The power segment delivered better than expected numbers while non-cash costs trimmed profits for the coal mining side. More in the report (link below)

Gaming agency PAGCOR reported revenue of P84bn for 9M25, which would imply 3Q revenue of P25bn – down 19% QoQ and 10% YoY. These PAGCOR numbers are an early indicator of performance of the gaming firms, both online and on-site. We suspect that the decline was led by e-games, as the BSP prohibited e-wallets from redirecting to online gaming platforms beginning mid-August.

GAMING
PAGCOR 3Q25 revenue falls YoY and QoQ
  • PAGCOR revealed that its 9M25 revenue rose 6% YoY to P84bn
  • From this, we estimate that 3Q25 revenue fell 19% QoQ and 10% YoY
  • The BSP prohibited e-wallets from redirecting to online gaming platforms beginning August 17
  • PAGCOR previously said that online gaming revenue fell c. 50% after this BSP directive

 

 

MACRO
DEPDev less optimistic on 3Q GDP growth
  • Dept. of Economy, Planning and Development (DEPDev) Secretary Arsenio Balisacan stated that 3Q growth "may not be as rosy as we expected"
    • He said that some of the primary factors that hampered growth were the flood control controversy which affected gov't spending, consecutive typhoons that impacted the industry and service sectors, as well as external risks from global trade uncertainties
  • Balisacan, however, mentioned that these setbacks are expected to be temporary
    • He highlighted that potential GDP growth remains "quite high, 6% and above" but realizing the potential is affected by instability and uncertainties
  • Recall that Balisacan previously said that the 2025 GDP growth target of 5.5-6.5% is still attainable
    • 1H25 GDP growth was 5.4%
    • 3Q GDP data will be released by the PSA on Nov 7 (Fri)
  • For 2026-2028, the gov't's target growth rate is 6-7%
    • Consensus and Regis expect GDP growth in 2026 to be 5.7% and 5.8%, respectively
