REGIS REPORT

11 July 2025

Gaming regulator PAGCOR has ordered gambling firms to remove advertising on billboards, buses, taxis, etc. While this would be negative for gambling firms overall, it would be more detrimental to new operators and brands, such as the new online gaming platform of Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM), MegaFUNalo.

 

Speaking of gaming, Development Secretary Aresenio Balisacan is the latest government official to propose higher taxes on online gambling.

 

We expect more restrictions on online gaming to be imposed in the coming months, if not weeks, even before any legislation in Congress is approved.

 

+++

 

While the Metro Manila residential and office segments are not strong, figures from the 2Q25 briefing of Leechiu Property Consultants showed some modest improvements in demand. Net office take-up improved sequentially, thanks to lower POGO closures as well as demand from both BPO firms and traditional tenants. Meanwhile, residential sales grew 2% QoQ.  

 

+++

 

Significant changes in the regulation and policies of the power sector could be coming due to changes at the top of the regulatory bodies.

 

President Marcos appointed officer-in-charge Sharon Garin as the Secretary of the Dept of Energy (DOE). Garin became OIC after the previous DOE, Secretary Raphael Lotilla, was appointed DENR Secretary. Garin was a former member of the House of Representatives and comes from a politically influential family in the Visayas.

 

In addition, two commissioners of the regulatory body for the power sector, the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC), retired effective yesterday. The replacement of the two members could have a significant impact on future direction of the ERC, which has been rendering crucial decisions on a 3-2 majority vote.  Who replaces the two retired commissioners (one of them always on the majority side) could make a massive difference in how (what) decisions are rendered.

 

+++

 

Secretary Frederick Go will head a delegation to Washington DC to negotiate a better deal following the announcement the United States is unilaterally imposing a 20% on imports from the Philippines. The 20% duty is a tad higher than the unilateral tariffs initially announced in April.

 

Note the US was the destination for 18% of Philippine exports last year, equivalent to 3% of GDP. However, around 1/3rd of PH exports to the US are electronics and semiconductors, which are exempt from the unilateral duty.

GAMING
PAGCOR orders removal of gambling billboard advertisements; DEPDev Secretary proposes higher tax
  • PAGCOR ordered the removal of billboard and other out-of-home advertisement related to gambling activities by August 15
  • Advertisements on vehicles such as trains, buses, etc. must also be removed
  • Separately, Department of Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev) Secretary Arsenio Balisacan proposed to tax online gambling through e-wallets
    • Online gaming firms currently pay 38% of their e-casino and e-bingo GGR to the government. They pay 24.25% of sports betting GGR to the government  
    • In 1Q25, the BSP issued draft regulations that would prohibit e-wallets from offering or presenting gambling  
PROPERTY
LPC: Net office demand improves in 2Q25; residential rents decline
  • During its 2Q25 briefing, Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) revealed that Metro Manila office vacancy was broadly flat QoQ at 18%
    • We estimate that rental rates in most parts of Metro Manila are still 10%-50% below pre-COVID levels   
    • Makati and Fort Bonifacio have lower vacancy rates of 10%-15%   
  • Net office take-up hit 182,000sqm nationwide in 2Q25 – from 88,000sqm in 1Q25
    • BPO net office take-up declined 21% QoQ (+3% YoY) to 107,000sqm
    • Net office take-up from traditional tenants (including government agencies) grew 10% QoQ (-38% YoY) to 91,000sqm
    • Meanwhile, POGO firms experienced net closures of 16,000sqm, from net closures of 132,000sqm in 1Q25
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 49% of Filinvest Land’s NAV - FLI is trading at 61% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 5.6X FY25E P/E
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV - MEG is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY25E P/B, and 3.4X FY25E P/E
    • 28% of Robinsons Land’s NAV - RLC is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY25E P/B, and 5.1X FY25E P/E
    • 75% of AREIT’s AUM – AREIT currently offers 5.9% FY25E dividend yield
  • Meanwhile, LPC estimates that residential condominium rental rates fell 0%-6% QoQ
    • It estimates that rental rates in Bonifacio are c. 5% above pre-COVID levels but rental rates in other parts of Metro Manila are generally 15%-50% below pre-COVID levels
  • Based on trailing 12M sales, the residential unsold inventory level rose from 39 months of sales in 1Q25 to 41 months in 2Q25
  • Residential sales fell 10% YoY (up 2% QoQ) to 6,643 units
  • Unsold inventory rose 31% YoY and 2% QoQ to 82,800 units
    • ALI is trading at 49% discount to NAV, 1.3X FY25E P/B, and 13.0X FY25E P/E
    • SMPH is trading at 41% discount to NAV, 1.6X FY25E P/B, and 15.8X FY25E P/E

 

 

 

 

 

 

POWER
New Dept. of Energy secretary appointed; two ERC Commissioners retire
  • Palace Press Officer Claire Castro announced in a press briefing the appointment of Sharon Garin as Secretary of the Dept. of Energy (DOE)
    • This move formally assigns her as the head of the DOE
    • She was assigned as the temporary OIC (officer-in-charge) of the DOE following former Secretary Raphael Lotilla's reassignment to the Dept. of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR)
    • Prior to being OIC, she served as an undersecretary under Lotilla
  • Garin is a lawyer and CPA with experience in public service, having served as a representative of Iloilo in Congress for over a decade through multiple terms
  • Meanwhile, two (2) out of the five (5) commissioners of the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) ended their seven-year terms yesterday, July 10
    • Commissioners Alexis Lumbatan and Catherine Maceda, appointed by former President Duterte, retired yesterday
    • The ERC can make decisions with just three commissioners so it can continue to function
MACRO
PH delegation to be sent to US to negotiate tariffs, possible FTA
  • A Philippine delegation led by Frederick Go, Special Assistant to the President for Investment and Economic Affairs (SAPIEA) will head to Washington D.C. next week in order to negotiate a better tariff deal with the US
    • Go will be accompanied by Trade Secretary Cristina Roque as well as Trade Undersecretaries Perry Rodolfo and Allan Gepty
    • The meetings were already scheduled to take place from July 14-18 even before the announcement of the new unilateral tariff rate of 20% (vs. 17% previously)
  • The delegation will also push for an FTA or bilateral comprehensive economic partnership agreement, in order to pursue specific tariff concessions on certain goods
  • Go highlighted that semiconductors and electronics, which are the country's top export products to the US, remain exempted from tariffs
    • This is given the US' specific tariff exemptions that have been put in place since April
      • i.e., pharmaceuticals, semiconductors, critical minerals, among others
    • Electronic products accounted for 53% of the Philippines' total exports in 2024
