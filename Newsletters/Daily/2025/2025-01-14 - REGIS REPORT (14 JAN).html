REGIS REPORT

14 January 2025

The Dept of Finance (DOF) is still hoping to get a few tax reform measures passed this year. This includes a bill on reforming capital market and investment taxes as well as one on taxation on mining activities.

We think it’ll be hard (we won’t say impossible) for Congress to pass any major pieces of legislation before it ends its session in June. With elections in May, legislators will be busy campaigning for reelection in their districts over the next few months.

+++

It is looking more likely the proposed SM Prime (SMPH PM) REIT might never push through. Hans Sy, one the Sy siblings who control the group, says cash flows are “strong” – which we interpret to mean there’s no urgency to raise money for its capex / reclamation and thus no need for a REIT spin-off.

While a REIT will create value for SMPH by providing tax incentives, it also the possibility of a de-rating of SMPH’s stock. We reckon the probability of a stock de-rating is what is holding back the REIT spin-off.

+++

The Dept of Energy (DOE) is predicting that power supply will be more than adequate this year, especially for the main grid of Luzon, is premised on the completion of a whopping 5,743MW of capacity this year (just for Luzon).

A quick check shows that many of the projects the DOE is counting on are part of the GEAP but are unlikely to deliver capacity by the peak months of 2025 or within 2025 at all.  For example, the DOE has penciled in almost 1,000MW from Solar Philippines (highly unlikely).  It has also assumed the operation of 530MW of Bugallon solar, a project that Meralco (MER PM) only expects by end of 2025 if it’s not delayed. The biggest project (in kwh), by far, however, is the 1,320MW LNG-powered plant of San Miguel (SMC PM) held under Excellent Energy (EERI). One 440MW unit of EERI began testing a few weeks ago.

MACRO
Department of Finance pushing for key tax reform measures this year
  • The Department of Finance is hoping several tax reform measures will be approved by Congress this year despite the upcoming midterm elections
    • 19th Congress resumes session on Jan 14 but will adjourn on Feb 8 for the start of campaign period; Elections are scheduled for May 12
    • Lawmakers will resume session on June 2 and will adjourn sine die on June 13
    • 20th Congress will open on July 28
  • Priority measures include:
    • Government Revenues Optimization through Wealth Tax Harmonization (GROWTH) bill, which is the new Passive Income and Financial Intermediary Taxation Act (PIFITA) – Approved by Lower House; Pending at Senate
    • Excise tax on single-use plastics (SUPs) – Pending in Lower House
    • Proposed mining fiscal regime reform - Approved by Lower House; Pending at Senate
    • Amendments to the Motor Vehicle Road User’s Charge - Approved by Lower House; Pending at Senate
  • Delay in passage of these measures will mean that the Department of Finance will need to explain these measures to a new Congress
PROPERTY
SM Prime might not pursue mall REIT
  • Hans Sy, Director of SM Prime, said that the company is unlikely to list a mall REIT this year
  • Furthermore, due to the company’s strong cash flows, it might never pursue a mall REIT IPO
  • SMPH is trading at 44% discount to NAV, 1.5X FY25E P/B, and 15.1X FY25E P/E
POWER
Energy Secretary sees 2025 peak demand rising by 5% vs. capacity increase of +30% for Luzon
  • Secretary of Energy Raphael Lotilla sees power demand rising by 5.4% to 14,769 megawatts (MW) in Luzon, 16% to 3,111 MW in the Visayas and 8.2% to 2,789 MW in Mindanao in 2025
    • He believes that the country will be in a much better situation this year than last year, given new projects that have come online
  • A total of 5,743 MW (+30%) of new power plant projects are expected to come online in 2025 in the Luzon grid, of which 3,923 MW are renewable energy projects, 1,320 MW natural gas, and 500 MW of coal
    • Given that projects are typically delayed, we reckon most of these projects could be pushed back to 2026
  • Meanwhile, the privatization of the 796.64 MW Caliraya-Botocan-Kalayaan (CBK) hydroelectric power plant complex could push through in April
    • Last year, Finance chief Ralph Recto said the state-run organization expects to generate P50bn to P100bn
    • Privatization was deferred due to the delay in the Energy Regulatory Commission’s issuance of pricing scheme for the third green energy auction

 

BANKS
BPI sees robust growth this year
  • Bank of the Philippine Islands (BPI PM) is optimistic about the company's growth for 2025 mainly driven by robust economic prospects and strategic investments
  • BPI president and CEO Jose Teodoro Limcaoco stated he remains optimistic about the country's economy and the company
    • Highlighting growth in key sectors i.e., logistics, domestic tourism, trade and imports, consumer lending
    • He also noted to look out for the impact of US trade policies and Fed decisions which could impact currency stability and inflation
    • One of the drivers of BPI's strong performance can be attributed to its loan portfolio strategy leaning more towards consumer loans as consumer lending outpaces institutional growth
    • The company will continue its commitment to digitization as it allocates approximately 10% of revenues to technology investments
  • BPI's net income for the 9M24 increased 24% YoY to a record P48bn while revenues rose 25% YoY to P125.8bn for the same period
    • For FY24, we expect BPI to post P64.4bn +25% YoY growth in net income while consensus expectations are pegged at P63bn +22% YoY
    • For FY25, we expect BPI to hit P70.5bn +9% YoY growth in net income while consensus expectation stands at P67.4bn +7% YoY
