REGIS REPORT

28 January 2025

The Senate approved its version of a bill reforming / adjusting tax rates and regulations covering (mostly) the capital markets.

We highlight two key proposals in the bill that would be of most interest to investors in the market.

  • A reduction in the stock transaction tax (STT) from 0.6% to 0.1%. The STT is the single biggest source of friction cost for market investors today. Its reduction would significantly cut the friction costs for high frequency (relatively) and day trading, which would hopefully spur more liquidity and market activity.
  • A reduction in the dividend tax on foreign entities from 25% to 10%. This change would reduce the tax disparity between foreign and domestic institutional investors as the latter do not pay dividend tax at all. This would be incrementally positive for stocks/firms that are relatively high dividend payers (e.g. REITS).

The final shape and form of this tax reform will be hammered out at the bicameral conference and it’s probable that there will be other provisions that could sneak through. For example, we note there is a provision in the Senate version that would remove the excise tax exemption for pick-up trucks.

News media are reporting that the country’s new sovereign wealth fund Maharlika Investment Corp (MIC) will be acquiring 20% of Synergy Grid (SGP PM), which controls National Grid Corp (NGCP), the operators of the country’s power transmission network. Media source/s MIC will subscriber to new convertible preferred shares in SGP and SGP will thus have new funds that can be infused into NGCP.

The suggests that the infusion would (1) give NGCP more funds to invest in the grid and (2) also dilute the ownership of State Grid of China, which currently owns ~40%.

Filinvest REIT (FILRT PM) will acquire Festival Supermall from its sponsor, Filinvest Land (FLI PM), for 1.626bn new FILRT shares. The FILRT shares will be issued at P3.85/share, a 30% premium to the 30-day VWAP. The transaction values Festival Supermall below our estimated value of P7.6bn. As a result, our initial estimates suggest that FLI’s NAV will drop by c. 5% to P2.05/share.

Ayala Corp (AC PM) is borrowing up to US$100m from the Asian Development Bank (ADB) to finance its EV initiatives which will involve setting up EV charging stations. AC, through AC Mobility, is the distributor of BYD vehicles in the Philippines.

MACRO/MARKET
Senate approves bill for lowering tax on stock transactions
  • Senate Bill No. 2865 or the Capital Markets Efficiency Promotion Act was approved on final reading
    • Rationale for the bill was to make the country's capital market more competitive relative to its regional peers as well as to encourage more Filipinos to invest in the stock market
    • A forecast by the PSE, states that a lowered 0.1% stock transaction tax would boost stock trading to P4.9tn by 2029
  • Key measures in the bill are as follows:
    • Reduction of stock transaction tax
      • Tax on stock transactions from 0.6% to 0.1%
    • Taxes on dividends
      • Tax on dividends for non-resident investors to be lowered from 25% currently to 10%, unified with the current 10% tax rate on dividends for residents
    • Minimum corporate income tax for foreign entities
      • Requires resident foreign corporations and their regional operating headquarters to pay a minimum corporate income tax of 10% on taxable income from 2% previously
    • Tax rates on capital gains and interest income remain unchanged at 15% and 20%, respectively
  • The bill shall proceed to a bicameral conference committee with the Lower House's version before the President signs the final version into law
  • The effectivity date of the bill was stated to be on or after January 1, 2025, as of the latest version of the reading but may likely be adjusted when it is signed into law
MACRO
Maharlika invests in Synergy Grid
  • Maharlika Investment Corp. (MIC) signed a binding agreement with Synergy Grid & Development Phils., Inc. (SGP PM) for MIC to invest an undisclosed amount (here) in SGP
    • MIC will subscribe to preferred shares offered by SGP and will be entitled to two (2) seats in SGP’s expanded nine-member board (currently 7 board seats)
    • At the NGCP level, MIC will also have two board seats, once total seats are increased to 15 from 10
  • MIC President and CEO Rafael D. Consing said that this flagship investment will “safeguard the nation’s power supply from external threats and disruptions, citing the need for the government to have a say in NGCP decisions”
  • A news outlet (here) wrote that the MIC has initially purchased 1.3bn preferred shares of SGP at P15/sh (P19.5bn)
    • MIC has the option to convert the preferred shares to common shares at an additional P7.50/sh (P9.75bn)
    • SGP last traded at P10.98/sh (total authorized and issued common shares at 5.266bn)
  • SGP indirectly controls 60% of National Grid Corp’s (NGCP) voting capital stock and has an effective equity interest of 45.726%
PROPERTY
Filinvest REIT to acquire Festival Mall from Filinvest Land
  • Fillinvest REIT will acquire Festival Mall from Filinvest Land via share-for-property swap
    • Festival Mall is a 122,000sqm mall in Alabang, Muntinlupa
    • The transaction will not include the expansion wing of Festival Mall
    • The acquisition will increase FILRT’s GLA by 37% to 452,000sqm
  • Filinvest REIT will issue 1.626bn new FILRT shares at P3.85/share – P6.26bn
    • This represents a 30% premium to the 30-day VWAP
    • This will increase FILRT’s shares outstanding by 35%
  • At the current price of P3.14/share, the total cost is effectively P5.1bn
    • This is lower than our estimated value of P7.6bn (for Festival Supermall)
    • Our initial estimates suggest that the transaction will reduce FLI’s NAV by c. 5% to P2.05/share
  • Upon completion of the transactions, FLI’s ownership in FILRT will rise from 51% to 63%
  • FILRT currently offers 8.3% FY25E dividend yield (based on consensus forecast)
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 6.3%
  • FLI currently trades at 67% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, 4.8X FY25E P/E
CONGLOS
ADB, Ayala sign deal to support electric mobility in the Philippines
  • Asian Development Bank (ADB) has signed a financing package of up to $100 million to support Ayala Corporation's efforts to develop an EV ecosystem in the Philippines.
    • The package includes a concessional loan from the Canadian Climate and Nature Fund for the Private Sector in Asia (CANPA), a trust fund managed by ADB and supported by a commitment of Can$360 million from the Government of Canada.
  • The blended financing features an innovative pricing structure aimed at accelerating the deployment of EV Charging Station (EVCS) infrastructure; and a portion of the ADB financing will be allocated to procure EV’s from manufacturers across the Philippines.
    • Ayala Corporation’s subsidiary AC Mobility Holdings, Inc. (AMHI) will be implementing the EVCS project, which would involve the installation of up to 1,700 EV Charging Stations around the country.
  • According to ADB, EV development is still nascent in the Philippines with high barriers to adoption- including high initial costs, limited charging infrastructure, and evolving technologies. However, the Philippines EV Industry Development Act and various tax incentives are helping promote the growth of the EV sector.
  • As of Dec 2024, data from the Department of Energy showed that the country has 833 charging points = 329 AC + 41 DC + 463 battery swapping stations
