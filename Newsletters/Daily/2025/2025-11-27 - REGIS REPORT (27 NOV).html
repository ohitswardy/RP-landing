REGIS REPORT

27 November 2025

The budget deficit numbers released by the government show a P11bn budget surplus (+76 %YoY) but what’s more interesting to us is that Oct showed the fourth straight month of YoY declines in primary spending.

 

Government expenditures in Oct were down 8% YoY and would be down 9% if debt service costs were excluded (i.e. primary spending). These recent data points show that fiscal policy is now seriously contractionary, albeit not by design. The corruption scandal is clearly having an impact on government expenditures, and this will have unavoidable effects on the broader economy.

 

Meanwhile, revenue collections were down 7% for the month with BIR collections up only 1%.

The Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE PM) elected two new directors to its board, including the daughter of San Miguel (SMC PM) head Ramon Ang, who replaces the long-time SMC representative on the PSE, Ferdinand Constantino. The SMC block is the largest among shareholders of the PSE, and the group has two seats on the PSE board.

MACRO
Government posts P11bn budget surplus in Oct; P1.11trn deficit as of 10M25
  • The Philippine government recorded a budget surplus of P11bn in Oct, 76% wider YoY as both revenues and gov't spending declined in the month
  • This brings the 10M25 deficit to P1.11trn, 15% wider YoY
    • The YTD budget deficit was said to be in line with the P1.56trn full-year program
    • The wider YTD deficit resulted from the faster growth in expenses vs revenues
  • Revenue collections in Oct declined 7% YoY, dragged by poor tax and customs collections
    • Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) collections were only up by 1%
      • Recall that former BIR commissioner Romeo Lumagui previously stated that there was a slowdown in revenue collection given the slowdown in economic activity following the ongoing probe on the Dept. of Public Works and Highways' (DPWH) flood control issues
    • Customs collections fell 5%, reversing from the 2% increase in Sep due to lower import volumes and fewer working days in the month
    • Non-tax revenues dipped 53% given the recognition of one-off remittances in 2024
  • Expenditures decreased by 8% YoY in Oct due to the decline in primary spending
    • Primary spending for the month was lower by 9%
      • Similar to the previous three months, the gov't spending decline in Oct was primarily caused by the ongoing flood control probe
    • Interest payments rose 4% from higher coupon payments for domestic and external debt
  • The gov't eyes to narrow the deficit to 5.5% of GDP this year from 5.7% in 2024

Two new directors elected to the PSE board
  • The Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE) has elected Cecile Ang and Jaime Bautista as nonbroker director and independent director, respectively, following the recent resignation of two directors
    • Cecile Ang, VP of New Naia Infrastructure Corp., replaces Ferdinand Constantino, former SMC CFO who retired earlier this year
    • Jaime Bautista, former Dept. of Transportation (DOTr) Secretary, replaces Andrew Gan, former director of Capital Markets Integrity Corp. (CMIC)
      • CMIC is the independent audit, surveillance and compliance arm of the PSE
MACRO
OSAPIEA to disband after Go's appointment to the DOF
  • President Marcos will disband the Office of the Special Assistant to the President for Investment and Economic Affairs (OSAPIEA) following the reassignment of Secretary Frederick Go to the Dept. of Finance (DOF)
  • Palace Press Officer Claire Castro elaborated that the dissolution of the OSAPIEA is to remove redundancies as the office was created to enhance inter-agency coordination and ensure effective integration and implementation of the gov't's investment and economic policies and programs
    • Go's transition as the Finance Secretary was said to still put him in a position to work closely with the Dept. of Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev) and the Dept. of Trade and Industry (DTI)
