REGIS REPORT

13 February 2025

The Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) Monetary Board holds its first meeting for the year today. The Street seems to have been expecting the BSP to cut rates by 25 bps today but we’re not certain how that will hold given the inflation news from the US overnight. The CPI figure in the US prompted the markets to further push back expectations of a rate cut to Dec this year.

 

We believe domestic conditions clearly favor a rate reduction. Not only is inflation benign and likely to soften, money supply growth is also low, suggesting that monetary conditions are tight. How the BSP intends to balance these domestic factors against international monetary conditions will be the key question today.

 

+++

 

Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) announced that it refinanced another P40bn of debt, on top of the P72bn refinanced in 4Q24. This refinancing will extend maturities to 2035 and come with floating rates that the company has the option to fix.  We estimate that this new deal would save the company around P700m in financing cost on the first year.

 

+++

 

Loan growth for commercial banks accelerated to 12.2% in Dec, based on preliminary data. Growth was 11.1% in Nov. Notably, M3 growth stayed at 7.7% (unchanged vs Nov), which we believe is too low for this economy.

 

+++

 

The government received 7,500MW of bids for 4,650MW of capacity being offered under its Green Energy Auction III (GEA-3) program. Under GEA-3, most of the capacity made available involves hydro power. First Gen (FGEN PM) and San Miguel (SMC PM) are among those shortlisted for GEAP-3.  The results of GEA-3 will be made public in June.

MACRO
BSP rate cut on the table later today
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) is expected to cut rates in its first out of six monetary board meetings slated for this year
    • Recall that BSP Governor Remolona previously stated two weeks ago that a rate cut is still "on the table" for the policy stance meeting later today
    • Consensus sees that the within-target inflation as well as the recent weaker-than-expected GDP results of 5.2% last 4Q24 and 5.6% FY24 should warrant the decision to cut rates as early as the first meeting
  • If this materializes, the benchmark rate would be set to 5.5% from 5.75% currently and would be the fourth straight meeting the BSP cut rates since the beginning of the easing cycle last Aug 2024
  • Consensus expectations currently estimate 50-75 bps worth of rate cuts this year
    • We expect the BSP to cut rates by 50 bps
GAMING
Bloomberry refinances P40bn of debt
  • Bloomberry refinanced P40bn worth of debt
    • This refinancing is separate from the P72bn of debt that was refinanced in 4Q24
  • The interest rate on the previous P40bn loan c. 8%
  • The new syndicated refinancing facility will have an interest rate of 3M PHP BVAL + 1 percentage point
    • This should mean annual savings of c. P700mn (equivalent to 8% of FY23 net profit)
    • BLOOM will also have the option to fix the interest rate within the next 12 months
  • If the loans had not been refinanced, BLOOM would have been required to pay P40bn over the next five years (by 2029)
  • The new syndicated refinancing facility has a term of 10 years (until February 2035) and the principal payments will be back-ended
  • This should slightly increase the likelihood of BLOOM paying dividends
    • The company’s recently announced dividend policy is to pay 35% of previous year’s earnings as dividends
  • However, we estimate that the refinancing will also lead to a one-time tax expense of c. P500mn

BLOOM is trading at 5X FY25E EV/EBITDA

MACRO
Commercial banks' loan growth picks up to 12.2% YoY in December
  • Preliminary data showed the outstanding commercial bank loans rose by 12.2% YoY in December, a tad faster than November's 11.6% rate
    • Loans to productive activities rose 10.8% in Dec from 9.8% the previous month
      • Due to faster loan growth in retail, motor vehicle repair, electricity/gas/steam supply industries, and construction
    • Consumer loans growth accelerated to 25.0% in Dec from 23.3% as growth momentum was driven by credit card, salary loans, and motor vehicle loans
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) growth was unchanged at 7.7% in Dec relative to Nov

 

 

 

POWER
Third Green Energy Auction (GEA-3) proceeds, contract awards by June 6
  • At the GEA-3 auction last Feb 11, the Department of Energy (DOE) received offers totaling 7,500 MW, exceeding its goal of 4,650 MW
    • It received offers of 6,700 MW for pumped-storage hydropower projects in Luzon (vs. target of 4,000 MW) and 250MW worth of pumped storage hydro in the Visayas region (in line with target)
    • It attracted 550MW worth of bids for impounding hydro projects (vs. target of 300MW)
    • Geothermal attracted bids for 30.9MW, well below the 100MW target
  • Overall, the auction received offers for 14 projects, with delivery periods between 2025-2035
  • There were 12 shortlisted bidders, including listed companies First Gen (FGEN PM) and San Miguel (SMC PM)
    • However, DOE said only five of the shortlisted bidders participated in the auction
    • DOE is hoping to award contracts to the winning bidders starting June 6
  • The GEA program aims to promote investments in renewable energy sources
    • GEA allows RE gencos to sell energy to the WESM at their winning (guaranteed) bid prices and any difference between the bid price and the WESM price will be settled through the Feed-in-Tariff system, which is a full pass-through to end users
