REGIS REPORT

01 September 2025

President Marcos replaced Public Works Secretary Manuel Bonoan with Vince Dizon, who was Transportation Secretary. This comes after widespread public outrage over corruption uncovered in the Dept of Public Works (DPWH) relating to flood control projects.

 

Dizon had only been in his post at the Dept of Transportation (DOTr) for six months. He made himself very visible tackling high profile issues relating to his department.

 

+++

 

The regulator granted a temporary five-month extension for the power supply agreement (PSA) between Meralco (MER PM) and First Gen’s (FGEN PM) Sta Rita power plant. The extension will be on the same terms as the 25-year contract that expired a few days ago.

 

The extension is as good a deal as FGEN can get for Sta Rita at this point, even if it is only for a few months. The final approved terms of the new deal (undisclosed so far) are what will drive the longer-term value for Sta Rita. FGEN, though, is already selling 60% of Sta Rita and the rest of its gas assets to the Razon group.

 

+++

 

ACEN (ACEN PM) secured another 900MW project in Tasmania in Australia. The wind project will cost US$3bn to build. Australia is a priority market for ACEN and is likely to be as big as its home market of the Philippines (if not bigger) for the company.

 

+++

 

Megaworld (MEG PM) subsidiary MREIT (MREIT PM) is seeking approval to issue 1.36bn new shares, equivalent to 37% of capital. We expect the new shares to be issued to MEG in exchange for leasable assets. We estimate MREIT offers a FY25E yield of 6.8%.

 

+++

 

Based on its draft offering circular, the IPO of water concessionaire Maynilad has now been pushed back to Oct, from July. At the maximum price, Maynilad’s market cap would be ~P151bn (US$2.6bn).

 

+++

 

The suspension of all rice imports for sixty days from Sept 1 is now official with Marcos signing an Executive Order on the issue. The order is meant to help rice farmers by keeping rice prices from falling further during the current harvest season.

MACRO
Dizon replaces Bonoan as Dept. of Public Works and Highways chief
  • Dept. of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) Secretary Manuel Bonoan's resignation has been accepted by President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. with Dept. of Transportation (DOTr) Secretary Vince Dizon appointed as the replacement, effective immediately
    • The agency has been under investigation and scrutiny given alleged irregularities in multibillion-peso flood control projects
    • Dizon has been ordered by the President "to conduct a full organizational sweep of the department"
  • The DPWH has one of the biggest allocations in terms of budget, ~P900bn for 2026 (13% of the proposed P6.8trn budget)
  • Giovanni Lopez, undersecretary for the DOTr, was assigned as acting DOTr secretary
    • Lopez was the chief of staff of Former DOTr Secretary Arthur Tugade from 2020-2022 during the Duterte administration
POWER
ERC extends Meralco, First Gen power purchase agreement
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission has granted a five-month extension to Meralco and First Gas Power Corp.’s (FGPC) power purchase agreement (PPA)
    • PPA extension to Jan 31, 2026 was implemented to avoid potential power outages
    • PPA, covering 1,000 MW of FGPC’s Santa Rita power plant’s capacity, was originally due to expire on Aug 28
  • Among the conditions set by the ERC are a minimum dispatch of the Santa Rita power plant and the use of the previously approved rate, which is equivalent to or computed at an 83% plant capacity factor
  • FGPC is a wholly-owned unit of First Gen (FGEN PM)
    • Last June, FGEN agreed to sell 60% of its natgas assets to Razon-led Prime Infrastructure
POWER
ACEN secures approval for 900MW US$3bn wind project in Australia
  • ACEN said that its 900MW Robbins Island Wind project in Tasmania has obtained regulatory approval from the Federal Government
    • According to ACEN Australia’s managing director David Pollington
    • Project is estimated to cost US$3bn; ACEN did not provide a completion timetable but noted that the facility may start generating electricity by 2030
  • ACEN previously said that it is planning to build another ~2GW of renewable energy project in Australia
    • ACEN currently has over 1,000 MW of operating plants in Australia, which we estimate contributes 9% to core att. EBITDA
  • ACEN has 7,053 GW of RE capacity, of which 3,598 MW are operating, 2,941 MW are under construction, and 514MW are “committed”
PROPERTY
MREIT to issue 1.36bn new shares
  • MREIT will seek shareholder approval for the issuance of 1.36bn primary shares
    • It will hold its stockholders’ meeting on September 29
  • This will likely be used for a share-for-property swap with its sponsor, Megaworld
  • MREIT is targeting to grow its leasable area to 600k sqm by end-2025 and to 1mn sqm by 2027
    • It currently has a leasable portfolio of 480k sqm
  • The share issuance will raise MREIT’s shares outstanding by 37%
  • The AGI group currently holds 62.2% of MREIT
  • If all of the 1.36bn new shares will be issued to the AGI group, then we estimate it will need to sell c. 300mn MREIT shares in order for the latter to meet minimum public ownership requirements
    • At the last closing price, this would be equivalent to c. P4.5bn
  • MEG is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY25E P/B, and 3.5X FY25E P/E
  • MREIT currently offers 6.8% FY25E dividend yield (based on consensus forecast)
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 6.0%
UTILITIES
Maynilad sets October listing date for IPO
  • In its updated preliminary prospectus, Maynilad said that its IPO will be priced on Oct 13
    • Offer period will run from Oct 16 to 22
    • Listing is targeted on Oct 30
  • Maynilad is offering 2.289bn (1.9bn primary and 355mn secondary) shares to the public at a maximum offer price of P20/sh
    • At the max. offer price and full subscription of offer shares, Maynilad’s market cap could be P151bn, and public float will be 30%
    • Under its franchise, Maynilad is required to offer at least 30% of its outstanding capital stock to Filipinos by Jan 21, 2027
MACRO
President Marcos issues EO on 60-day rice import ban
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. issued Executive Order (EO) 93, suspending the importation of rice from Sep 1 to Oct 30, unless shortened or extended
    • The Department of Agriculture (DA) has been ordered to directly lead and monitor the implementation of the import ban as well as to ensure a stable and sufficient supply of rice
  • The DA alongside the Dept. of Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev) and the Dept. of Trade and Industry (DTI) must convene within 30 days of the EO's effectivity to assess the impact and recommend to the President whether to lift or extend the suspension
  • Recall that the DA recommended the import ban on rice to protect local farmers as the ban duration will coincide with the peak of harvest season
MACRO
BSP projects August inflation to settle within 1.0-1.8%
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) in its month-ahead inflation forecast expects August inflation to fall within 1.0-1.8%
    • August inflation expectations fall under the BSP's 2-4% target range
  • Upward price pressures are higher prices of fruits and vegetables, elevated fuel and electricity prices as well as the depreciation of the peso
    • These may have been partially offset by lower prices of rice and meat costs
  • Inflation has averaged 1.7% so far for the first seven months of the year; inflation in July was 0.9%
  • For 2025, the BSP expects 2025 inflation to be 1.7%
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to average 1.8%
  • August inflation data will be released by the PSA (Philippine Statistics Authority) on Sep 5 (Friday)
