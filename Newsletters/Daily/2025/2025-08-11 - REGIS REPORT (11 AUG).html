REGIS REPORT

11 August 2025

An unusually busy Monday morning for news on AC, ACEN, PLUS, BLOOM, SM/Melco, PLDT and more!

Before that, a quick roundup of results:

  • Aboitiz Power (AP PM) (-) – AP reported 1H25 profit (ex-FX) fell 25% YoY to P12.8bn, which appears to lag consensus full-year forecast of P32.9bn. Profit for 2Q25 improved 74% QoQ to P8.1bn (-13% YoY), which management attributed to the full-quarter impact of the Chromite acquisition, better WESM prices, and better plant availability. We hope to get more detail at management's briefing tomorrow.

 

  • Manila Water (MWC PM) (+) – 1H25 profit after the share of participating prefs rose 15% YoY to P7.0bn, in line with consensus but ahead of REGIS estimates. Like prior periods, the earnings beat came from the disappointing pace of spending at the East Zone. Management is about to reach financial close on the P37.8bn acquisition of its affiliate and bulk water provider, WawaJV.

  • Robinsons Land (RLC PM) (=) – 1H25 recurring profit grew 5% to P6.9bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus. The mall and office segments are trending above our forecasts but the residential segment is trailing behind, despite the sequential improvement in 2Q25. Presales of standalone projects rose 178% QoQ to P2.4bn while 2Q25 presales of JV projects climbed from P47mn in 1Q25 to P524mn . Meanwhile, mall revenue growth accelerated to 11% in 2Q25, from 6% in 1Q25.

 

  • Universal Robina (+) – Although the 1H NPAT of P6.9nbn looks ahead of REGIS estimates, 2Q results look soft with the business segments posting mixed results. 2Q top line increased 6% YoY to P40.6bn, while operating profit inched down 1% YoY to P3.9bn. Core profit in the quarter was slightly up YoY (+1%) to P2.8bn. The commodity segment was the strong on the back of 10% YoY sales growth, while Domestic BCF sales grew a modest 5% YoY, and Int'l BCF saw revenue growth slow to 3% YoY from 10% YoY in 1Q25. More crucially, management offered what sounded like a “soft” downgrade to its guidance for the FY25E.

With results out of the way, some other news worth highlighting:

ACEN (ACEN PM) has deferred its proposed rights issue due to “revised scheduling” of its capex plans in its SEA investments. For now, we believe this could prolong the overhang/uncertainty/anxiety among investors over the capital call. However, it could also mean that the company is considering other financing options.

Ayala Corp (AC PM) announced that Temasek, through its ABC Impact fund, is taking a 16% stake in AC Health. The infusion of new money will help fund AC Health’s portfolio of hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies. No monetary values were disclosed.

Digiplus (PLUS PM) will officially enter the PSEi Index, replacing Bloomberry (BLOOM PM). Based on last Friday’s close, PLUS will have a weight of 1.0%, though this could obviously change between now and the official inclusion at the end of trading on Friday 15 Aug.

Speaking of gaming, SM Investments (SM PM) during its briefing suggested that there are one or two interested bidders to take over the City of Dreams Manila casino, following Melco’s earlier announcement of its intent to pull out of the Philippines.

PLDT’s (TEL PM) mobile arm Smart launched KiQ an “app-based” telco brand, aimed at younger users, offering much more personalized plans.

PROPERTY
Robinsons Land 1H25 recurring profit rises 5%
  • Robinsons Land’s 1H25 headline profit fell 5% to P6.9bn. Removing non-recurring gains in 1Q24, we estimate that recurring profit grew 5% to P6.9bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus
    • The mall and office segments are trending above our forecasts but the residential segment is trailing behind, despite the sequential improvement in 2Q25
  • Although reservation sales of the JV and standalone residential businesses improved sequentially, we would still consider both to be weak
    • Presales of standalone projects rose 178% QoQ (-57% YoY) to P2.4bn, only 50% of the 2019 average
    • 2Q25 presales of JV projects climbed from P47mn in 1Q25 to P524mn (-85% YoY). While this is a large improvement QoQ, it is only 10% of the 2019 average
  • Despite Holy Week falling in April this year (vs March in 2024), 2Q25 mall revenue growth accelerated to 11% YoY, from 6% in 1Q25, as mall occupancy improved 1ppt QoQ (and YoY) to 94%
    • We estimate that same-mall revenue growth stood at 6%, the midpoint of the historical average (of 4%-8%)
  • Office occupancy improved 1ppt QoQ (and YoY) to 87%, higher than the industry average of 82%
  • 2Q25 Industrial leasing revenue 32% QoQ and 5% YoY to P183mn
    • This could mean that a tenant vacated one of RLC’s warehouses 
  • RLC is currently trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY25E P/B, and 5.2X FY25E P/E

 

 

 
 

POWER
Aboitiz Power: 2Q25 EBITDA drops 4% YoY
  • In a press release, Aboitiz Power announced 2Q25 recurring income rose 74% QoQ to P8.1bn but was lower by 13% YoY
    • Cumulative 1H25 recurring profit dropped 25% YoY to P12.8bn, which trails consensus full-year estimate of P32.9bn
    • Headline 1H25 profit (includes FX) was 26% YoY lower at P12.7bn
  • Beneficial EBITDA stood at P19.1bn in 2Q25, a 27% QoQ improvement due to (a) the full-quarter impact of the Chromite acquisition, (b) better plant availability, higher margins at the generation segment, and (c) stronger energy sales at the distribution unit
    • On a YoY basis, EBITDA dropped 4% YoY due to the weak performance of its generation/RES unit
  • Management is holding a briefing on Aug 12, 3pm HKT (registration)

 

 

MARKET
PSE Index August rebalancing: BLOOM (-), PLUS (+)
  • The PSE announced that Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) will be removed from the PSE Index during the August 2025 rebalancing
    • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) will take its place
  • The rebalancing will take place at the close of August 15 (open of August 18)
  • Based on our estimates, there could be c. US$23.6mn of inflow into PLUS
    • There should also be inflow to JFC, JGS, and SMC due to higher public float
  • We also estimate c. US$8.1mn of outflow from BLOOM and about US$20mn of outflow from all other PSE Index constituents

TELCOS
Smart launches KiQ, app-based telco brand
  • Smart Communications Inc., wholly owned wireless communications and digital services subsidiary of PLDT Inc. (TEL PM), will be launching a new mobile brand which is said to be the country's first and only fully app-based service for a "personalized digital telco experience"
  • The KiQ brand targets the Gen Z market for a platform that enables personalized and flexible offers
    • Enabling users to customize data allocation, calls/texts, plan length validity, as well as choose their own number
    • Smart's COO Anastacio “Boy” Martirez emphasized that this is what the future of mobile looks like—no wasted data, unnecessary add-ons, total freedom and flexibility
  • The app is available on the Google Play Store and Apple App store where users can purchase a KiQ eSIM for P99
MACRO
DOTr's allocation more than doubles in proposed 2026 budget
  • The Dept. of Transportation's (DOTr) budget was recorded to have the largest percentage jump in the 2026 National Expenditure Program (NEP)
    • The DOTr's proposed budget of P197bn in 2026 will be more than double its 2025 budget of P87bn
  • Majority of the budget increase is due to additional funding for key projects such as the North-South Commuter Railway (NSCR), Metro Manila Subway Project Phase I, New Cebu International Container Port Project, New Dumaguete Airport Development Project, as well as other new regional airports
  • The proposed P6.8tn budget is equivalent to 22% of GDP and 7% higher than 2025's P6.33 budget
    • Priority allocations are the education, agriculture, and transportation sectors which will see an increase in budget for next year
    • The 2026 NEP is targeted to be submitted to Congress this month
PROPERTY
SEC to examine Villar Land revaluation gains
  • SEC Chair Francis Lim said the SEC would investigate the P1trn revaluation gains recorded by Villar Land (HVN PM) in 2024
  • Villar Land has been suspended since May 16 for failing to file its annual report
  • Villar Land is owned by the family of Manuel B. Villar, Jr., which also owns Vista Land, AllDay Marts (ALLDY PM), and AllHome (HOME PM)
