REGIS REPORT

04 July 2025

The CPI inflation figure for June came in a 1.4%, not much different from the May figure (1.3%). This would bolster the case for further monetary easing in 2H.

 

 

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) has already opened the possibility of two more rate reductions this year – more than what Regis is predicting. Two 25bp rate cuts would bring the BSP policy rate to 4.75% this year, which is likely to be lower than what most in the market are expecting.

 

We think further rates cuts are justified given the state of the domestic macro picture (e.g. inflation, money supply, GDP growth). The key obstacle to more easing is Fed action (or inaction) on US rates.

 

+++

 

Likely in response to increasing calls for restrictions on online gaming, the BSP also issued a statement that it was preparing a directive to institutions it regulates on that specific matter. The BSP has been circulating and seeking comments on a draft circular that would restrict direct access to online gaming apps.

 

We expect restrictions on online gaming access to be imposed in the coming months. We believe it is only a question of how and when, not if.

 

+++

 

Meralco (MER PM) electricity sales for 1H are just about flat YoY, according to management. This would imply that 2Q electricity sales are down just over 1% YoY.  Recall that electricity sales in 2Q24 were particularly strong due to the exceptionally high temperatures.

 

+++

 

Consortiums led by First Gen (FGEN PM) and Aboitiz Power (AP PM) were among the pre-qualified bidders for the nearly 800MW CBK hydro being privatized.  We expect to know the highest bidder for CBK in the coming weeks.

MACRO
BSP sees possibility of two more cuts this year
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) stated that there could be two more rate cuts this year (to 4.75% from 5.25% currently) given benign inflation and slower-than-expected growth
    • Inflation YT-May stands at 1.9%, lower than last year's 3.5% for the same period
    • 1Q25 GDP growth was at 5.4%, lower than consensus expectations of 5.7%
  • BSP Governor Eli M. Remolona, Jr. noted that they will remain data dependent prior to deciding if more rate cuts are needed to support economic growth
  • The next Monetary Board meeting (4th out of 6 this year) will be on Aug 28
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expects 75bp total worth of cuts to 5.00%
  • The Philippine gov't's GDP growth target for 2025 is 5.5-6.5% (lowered from 6-8%)
    • Consensus and Regis expects 2025 GDP to be at 5.5%

 

GAMING
BSP to issue circular restricting access to online gambling
  • The BSP said it would issue a circular that would require BSP-regulated institutions, such banks and electronic money issuers, to better protect users of their digital platforms from risks associated with online gambling
  • Protection may come in the form of various limits to gaming access
  • In 1Q25, the BSP issued draft regulations that would prohibit online marketplaces from offering or presenting gambling
  • Separately, the Department of Finance (DOF) said that it is proposing a tax on online gambling and studying potential policies to restrict access to gambling
    • Online gaming firms currently pay 38% of their e-casino and e-bingo GGR to the government. They pay 24.25% of sports betting GGR to the government  
  • DigiPlus Interactive is the market leader in e-games, with market share of about 45%
  • PLUS is trading at 9.8X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
POWER
Meralco energy sales flat in 1H25
  • In a media chat, Meralco Chief Revenue Officer Ferdinand Geluz said that energy sales may have risen by 0.5% YoY to 26,952 GWH in 1H25
    • For the month of June alone, energy sales fell 1% YoY
    • The data suggests that MER’s energy sales in 2Q25 alone may have dropped by 1.3% YoY (+15.7% QoQ) to 14,459 GWH
    • Recall that MER’s energy sales in 1Q25 rose 2% YoY to 12,493 GWH
    • MER cited cooler weather caused by the onset of rainy season, dampening household demand while demand from commercial and industrial sectors remained lackluster due to the exit of POGOs
  • Meralco still expects a rebound in power demand in 2H, as it anticipates various data centers to come online and construction activity to rebound
    • It also expects base effects from the exit of POGOs to diminish
    • Company will revisit its energy sales growth target of 4.5% for this year
POWER
First Gen, AP submit bids for CBK hydroelectric power plant
  • State-run Power Sector Assets and Liabilities Management Corp. (PSALM) said that it has received bids from two consortiums led by First Gen and Aboitiz Power:
    • FWKG Consortium, composed of First Gen Prime Energy Corp. and Korea Water Resources
    • Thunder Consortium, composed of Aboitiz Renewables, Inc., Sumitomo Corp., and Electric Power Development Co.
  • The opening of financial documents is set to take place “at a date, time, and venue to be determined by PSALM’s bids and awards committee
  • PSALM is bidding out the 796.64 MW Caliraya-Botocan-Kalayaan (CBK) hydroelectric power plant (HEPP) complex in Laguna
  • The government previously said that it aims to generate US$200-600mn from the CBK privatization, which puts the asking price at ~US$250-750k per MW
    • Price is below FGEN’s valuation for the privatization of the 165MW Casecnan hydro plant last year (US$526mn or >US$3mn per MW) due to absence of contracts
    • CBK’s power purchase agreement is set to expire in Feb 2026
PROPERTY
Ayala Land sells AREIT shares
  • Ayala Land sold 12mn AREIT shares at P40.78/share
    • This is a 3% discount to the previous closing price of P42/share
    • Total of P489mn
  • The sale was made for AREIT to meet public float requirements in anticipation of a share-for-property swap with Ayala Land
    • We estimate that the Ayala Group’s ownership in AREIT fell from 61.7% to 61.3%
  • AREIT will acquire P21bn worth of assets from Ayala Land in exchange for 505.9mn new AREIT shares
    • This will increase AREIT’s AUM by 18% and its shares outstanding by 16%
    • The mall, office, and hotel assets are located in Cebu, Davao, and Cagayan de Oro
  • ALI is trading at 48% discount to NAV, 1.3X FY25E P/B, and 13.2X FY25E P/E
  • AREIT currently offers 5.8% FY25E dividend yield
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 6.24%

SEC plans to increase investment options and revise REIT regulations

MACRO
Government debt inches up to P16.92tn in end-May
  • The government's total debt stock went up by 1% MoM to P16.92tn as of end-May
    • This was due to additional borrowings which was partially offset by the appreciation of the PHP against the USD (from P56.25 end-Apr to P55.70 end-May)
  • Domestic debt rose by 1.6% (P190bn) MoM to P11.78tn due to the impact of net borrowings
  • External debt decreased by 0.5% (P24bn) MoM to P5.14tn given a decrease in the peso value of foreign debt due to local currency appreciation and net repayments
  • The current debt mix of the country's total obligations has domestic debt accounting for 69.6% and 30.4% being external borrowings
  • The year-end debt target for 2025 is pegged at P17.35tn which is 8% higher YoY than end-Dec's P16.1tn (see here)
    • This 2025 target is equivalent to 60.4% of GDP vs. 2024's debt-to-GDP ratio of 60.7%
    • The ratio is expected to further decline below 60% by 2027 (58.4%); by the end of the Marcos admin's term in 2028, it expects the debt-to-GDP ratio to fall to 56.9%
