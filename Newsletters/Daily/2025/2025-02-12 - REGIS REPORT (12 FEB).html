REGIS REPORT

12 February 2025

Meralco (MER PM) is proposing a distribution tariff of ~P1.69/kwh for Regulatory Years 26-29 (RY26-29) under an application filed with the regulator. The proposed tariff/s (~P1.69/kwh) would represent an increase of >20% from the rate it’s currently charging (P1.35/kwh). This tariff proposal is apparently based on a regulatory WACC of 14.6% and capex of P215bn over four years.

 

Simplistically, such a tariff adjustment would immediately add close to P20bn in annual revenue (without considering higher costs). It’s unlikely, however, that Meralco will get the full adjustment it seeks. Note as well that Meralco is still setting aside provisions in its P&L as if its tariff was even lower than P1.35/kwh.

 

+++

 

PLDT (TEL PM) and its related companies are apparently interested in raising the group’s stake in Maya Innovations, the holding company for Maya Bank as well as the e-wallet and payments platform. Private equity investor KKR was reported to be selling its 20% interest in the former.

 

While PLDT increasing its stake in Maya may not necessarily be a bad thing, we have two concerns. First is valuation. The other is that PLDT could have better uses for the cash.

 

+++

 

Ayala Corp (AC PM) is selling its digital healthcare operation KonsultaMD. The business, which enables online medical consultations, was touted as one of the promising ventures of the group not so long ago but is now being divested for an undisclosed sum. We reckon this is part of AC’s overall asset rationalization initiatives, and should help reduce losses from its unlisted investments.

POWER/UTILITIES
Meralco proposes >20% tariff increase, P215bn capex for 2026-2029
  • Based on its filing with the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC), Meralco plans to invest P215bn for RY 2026-2029
    • P34bn will be spent in P2026, P60bn in 2027, P58bn in 2028, and P65bn in 2029
  • MER is also proposing an annual revenue requirement (ARR) of P393bn, of which P82bn will be generated in 2026 and will rise to P115bn by 2029
    • MER said that its proposed ARR is partly based on a proposed WACC of 14.6%
    • This would translate to a maximum average price per kwh of P1.687/kwh for 2026, P1.6899/kwh for 2027, P1.6894/kwh for 2028, and P1.6872/kwh for 2029
    • The above tariffs would be ~25% above the current tariff of P1.352/kwh
      • However, Meralco is setting aside provisions as if the tariff was lower than what’s being charged
  • Meralco confirmed its application filed with the ERC but clarified that the application has not yet been formally docketed and remains subject to approval

DIGITAL FINANCE/TELCOS
Pangilinan group looking to increase stake in Maya
  • PLDT chairman and president Manuel V. Pangilinan said his group is in discussions with KKR to increase its stake in fintech Maya
    • “I’m meeting them (global investment company KKR) next week. I’ve gotten some rumors about values, but I don’t know. We’re participating in the purchase of the total of 80%. We’ll probably pick 30%,”
    • “If we agree on the price and the process, because I don’t know who the bidders are. We’re picking up some noises, some names. Hopefully, middle of the year,”
  • It was previously reported that KKR (see here) is eyeing a valuation of US$2bn for 100% equity of Maya, implying a value of US$400m for KKR’s ~20% interest
  • PLDT is the single biggest largest shareholder of Maya, with a ~37% stake
  • The last widely announced funding round (Apr 2022) valued Maya at US$1.4bn and raised US$210m
  • Maya also raised more funding early last year (2024) but the valuations for that round were not publicly disclosed
CONGLOS
Metro Pacific’s mWell acquires Ayala Corp’s KonsultaMD
  • Metro Pacific Investments Corp.’s (MPIC) digital healthcare arm, mWell, is acquiring KonsultaMD from the Ayala Group for an undisclosed amount
    • mWell’s acquisition of KonsultaMD will integrate with its existing portfolio of services that already serves 3.1mn users and has 90,000 users globally
  • The valuation for the transaction was not disclosed and it is unclear if mWell is buying 100% of KonsultaMD
  • KonsultaMD is owned between AC Health, 917Ventures of Globe and Salud Interactiva
  • KonsultaMD was launched in 2015 as a health hotline under Globe’s 917Ventures and has evolved into a telehealth ‘super app’ providing 24/7 doctor consultations, diagnostics, medicine delivery, and homecare services.
    • The platform currently serves 2.7mn users and a broad network of partner doctors in mobile-based healthcare solutions
  • The integration of KonsultaMD into mWell’s digital health ecosystem will allow MPIC to expand its digital health offerings while leveraging its hospital infrastructure to improve patient access to medical services
