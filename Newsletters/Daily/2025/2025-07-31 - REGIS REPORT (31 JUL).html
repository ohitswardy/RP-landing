In its briefing, Colliers revealed that Metro Manila net take-up of both the office and residential segments improved sequentially in 2Q25. Net office take-up picked up thanks to lower POGO closures as well as demand from both BPO firms and traditional tenants. Meanwhile, net sales of residential units surged to nearly 3,000 units, from about 200 units in 1Q25. In addition to lower cancellations, the aggressive discounts and promotions on ready-for-occupancy units enticed buyers to purchase condominiums.

MACRO
Trade deficit narrows to $4bn in June
  • The June trade deficit narrowed to US$4bn, contracting 9% YoY, reflecting the faster growth in exports over imports
    • This brings the 6M25 trade deficit to US$24bn, 4% narrower YoY
  • Imports for the month increased by 11% YoY to US$11.0bn, a reversal from the 4% decrease in May
    • Primarily driven by the increase in capital and consumer goods imports
  • Exports surged by 26% YoY to US$7.0bn, the sixth consecutive month of growth and the fastest since posting 28% growth back in April 2024
    • Exports to the US jumped by 35% to US$1.2bn in the month of June, the highest rate of growth in recent years
    • Electronics exports climbed by 30% YoY to $3.8bn, an acceleration from the 8% increase in May
      • These made up the bulk of exports at 55% of the total
    • Meanwhile, non-electronics exports jumped by 22% YoY to US$3.1bn, a tad slower than the 24% growth in May
      • Growth in non-electronics exports was led by mineral products as well as machinery and transport equipment

 

 

 

 

 

 

PROPERTY
Colliers: Office and residential net take-up improve in 2Q25
  • Colliers reported that Metro Manila office vacancy remained flat QoQ at 20% in 2Q25        
    • Makati CBD vacancy rose 1ppt QoQ to 8.1%
    • BGC vacancy fell 1.5ppt QoQ to 14.4%
    • Vacancy in the Manila Bay area rose to 45% in 2Q25, from 41% in 1Q25
  • Net office take-up rose 67% QoQ to 60,000sqm
    • This is largely due to fewer POGO closures
  • Office rental rates in Makati CBD and BGC were generally flat QoQ
  • Colliers expects Metro Manila office vacancy to rise to 21% by end-2025
    • While it expects net take-up to hit 150,000sqm, it expects about 277,000sqm in new supply
  • Residential vacancy was also relatively flat QoQ at 24.5%
  • Residential net take-up jumped to 2,866 units in 2Q25, from only 175 units in 1Q25
  • Colliers said that aggressive promotions (such rent-to-own schemes of up to 10 years) and cash discounts of up to 50% on select ready-for-occupancy units “appear to be working”
  • It also said that residential cancellations fell sequentially
  • ALI is trading at 52% discount to NAV, 1.2X FY25E P/B, and 12.2X FY25E P/E
  • MEG is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 3.4X FY25E P/E
  • RLC is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY25E P/B, and 5.2X FY25E P/E
  • SMPH is trading at 45% discount to NAV, 1.5X FY25E P/E, and 14.7X FY25E P/E  

 

 

PROPERTY
SEC considering loosening of REIT regulations
  • SEC Chair Francis Lim is looking into revisions to REIT regulations
  • Among the changes being considered is allowing funds raised from REIT issuances to be spent on Philippine property/infrastructure within three years (vs the current one year requirement) 
  • Another proposal is to allow REIT proceeds to be used for debt repayment
  • We do not expect any major impact on the major developers since their proceeds from REIT asset infusions are lower than their annual capital expenditures

  • Senate President Francis Escudero announced that the Senate will decide on Aug 6 to decide whether or not to proceed with the impeachment of Vice President Sara Duterte given the recent decision by the Supreme Court (SC) that ruled the impeachment of Vice President Sara Duterte as unconstitutional

POWER
Meralco signs MOU with Vietnam-based EV charging firm
  • Meralco signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with V-Green Global Charging Station Development Corp. and ride-hailing company Green and Smart Mobility Joint Stock Co.
    • Partnership aims to identify and evaluate sites for EV charging stations and electric taxi hubs, as well as explore other opportunities to scale up EV adoption
  • In Vietnam, V-Green serves as the exclusive charging infrastructure partner for VinFast electric vehicles
  • Green GSM, meanwhile, is an innovative electric ride-hailing platform that operates a fleet powered entirely by VinFast EVs.
  • As of July, the country has a total of 622 AC/DC charging points, up 14% MoM from 544 in end-June
    • The government is targeting 7,300 EV charging stations by end-2028
POWER
Supreme Court rules in favor of Malampaya gas operator on income tax issue
  • The Supreme Court (SC) En Banc lifted the charges against the operator of the Malampaya gas field for unpaid income taxes
    • In a decision penned by Associate Justice Japar B. Dimaampao, the SC ruled that the contractors are still liable for income tax but the government pays it on their behalf as part of its share
    • SC cited the Oil Exploration and Development Act, which states that income taxes paid by or on behalf of contractors are included in the government’s guaranteed 60% share of net proceeds
    • Presidential Decrees 1206 and 1459 also confirm that the government’s share includes all taxes
  • In a dissenting opinion, Senior Associate Justice Marvic M.V.F. Leonen argued that Presidential Decree 87 does not expressly state that the government’s share includes income taxes
  • The case stemmed from the Commission on Audit’s claim that over P53bn in income taxes had been deducted from the state’s share
