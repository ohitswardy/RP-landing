REGIS REPORT

20 October 2025

Digital payments continue to surge, up 47% YoY in September to P2.2trn, a new monthly record high. More impressively, the adoption seems to be broadening out even faster, with smaller-ticket InstaPay transactions up 63% YoY to P1.0trn. Even more shocking is that the volume of transactions exploded, quadrupling YoY, indicating that digital payments are being used more and more often, for smaller and smaller amounts. As before, we note that the National Retail Payments System (NRPS) data only capture transactions between platforms, and does not include payments made within a wallet/bank ecosystem. This suggests a long runway for fintech, e-commerce, digital banking, etc.

 

+++

 

The Dept of Information and Communication Technology (DICT) intends to issue the implementing rules and regulations (IRR) for the Konektadong Pinoy Act (KPA) as soon as today. If this indeed proceeds, it means the awaited second consultation with the industry operators will not happen before the IRR is official.

 

Previous drafts of the IRR still appear vague, which hands the regulators (e.g. DICT, NTC) plenty of room to interpretation. This ambiguity, however, could lead to uncertainty over the regulatory environment and thus result in hesitation on business plans (including capex plans).

 

+++

 

The power industry regulator Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC), in a press release, announced that it has come up with revised rules for setting distribution utility rates under a “Rationalized Rules for Setting Distribution Wheeling Rates” (RRDWR). The ERC says new timelines for the regulatory periods for the DUs has been set, with the first group entering the first regulatory period July 1, 2026. Meralco (MER PM) will be part of the first entry group. Meralco has yet to have a completed full rate review since 2014.

 

+++

 

The Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE PM) says the current methodology to determine the composition of its main index, the PSEi (aka “PCOMP”) will be revised. The PSE solicited comments on possible revisions to PSEi and could likely implement changes by next year (probably Aug 2026).

 

+++

 

The Dept of Trade and Industry (DTI) is imposing a P14/bag (=P349 per MT) safeguard duty on cement imports after the expiry of the previous temporary duty of P400 per MT. The new duty represents approximately 11% of our estimated landed cost of imports. Among the listed companies with exposure to the cement sector are: (1) Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM), through its partnership with CRH, (2) San Miguel Corporation (SMC PM) and (3) DMCI Holdings (DMC PM), which recently took a controlling interest in what’s now called Concreat Holdings (CHP PM).

 

+++

 

The approval rating of President Marcos slipped to 33% in polls conducted in Sept, down from 42% in Jun. This comes after the explosive corruption scandal which is currently engulfing the government.

 

The large geographical divide in public opinions about Marcos and Vice President Sara Duterte-Carpio remains quite evident. Marcos’ approval rating in the southern island of Mindanao is virtually nil (4%), while Duterte-Carpio has near universal approval (97%).

BANKS/DIGITAL FINANCE
Digital payments +47% in Sept to new high; InstaPay surges 63%

Digital payments continued to surge in September, up 47.3% to P2,170bn, a new monthly record-high, up 5.5% MoM. (Note tho that the July/Aug data was revised down slightly, though the Sept figure would still be higher than the previously reported August data.) For the 9M25 period, the National Retail Payments System (NRPS) posted total transactions worth P17,458bn, up 41% YoY and already surpassing the P17,423bn recorded for all of 2024.

 

Larger, batched transactions over PesoNet were up to P1,153bn, a new high, up 36% YoY, faster than the 29.6% pace posted in August. Year-to-date, PesoNet lodged P9,513bn in transactions, up 32% YoY.

 

Meanwhile, smaller-ticket transactions (up to P50,000) over InstaPay continued to explode, up 63.3% YoY to P1,017bn crossing P1trn for the first time (the August figure was revised down to P997bn from P1,027as previously reported.) That’s the fastest monthly YoY growth since December 2021 at the height of the Covid pandemic. The 9-month cumulative figure was P7,945bn, up 55% YoY already well above the P7,347bn logged in FY2024.

 

Staggeringly, the volume of InstaPay payments quadrupled (+300.7%) YoY. This took the average ticket size of InstaPay payments down to P2,057 (just over $35). This continues to indicate that digital adoption continues to broaden and deepen with ever-increasing usage.

 

We note, too that the NRPS data only includes transactions between platforms (eg, Bank A to Bank B, or Bank to Wallat, or Wallet A to Wallet B, etc), and does not count payments made within an internal system (eg, Bank A to Bank A, or Wallet B to Wallet B), and thus likely vastly understates the true level of digital adoption in the PH economy.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

TELCOS
DICT eyes Konektadong Pinoy IRR to be published soon
  • Dept. of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) is set to finalize the IRR for the Konektadong Pinoy Act as DICT Secretary Henry Aguda stated that it is "ready for signing" with a target to publish it no later than Monday, Oct 20
    • Recall that the DICT previously set Oct 2 as the target date to finalize the IRR
  • After the IRR is signed, it will be transmitted to the National Telecommunications Commission (NTC), the Dept. of Education (DepEd), and other relevant agencies
  • Aguda highlighted that the new reforms will encourage more players to enter the data transmission space, which may drive down prices for better affordability
POWER
ERC announces plan to reset power rates for private distribution utilities
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission’s (ERC) Rationalized Rules for Setting Distribution Wheeling Rates (RRDWR) aims to streamline the rate setting process of private distribution utilities
    • RRDWR addresses past delays in resetting the distribution rates and the ERC shall render only one final decision covering the reset and rate translation
    • The first group includes the Manila Electric Company (MER PM), Dagupan Electric Corporation (DECORP), Cagayan Electric Power and Light Company (CEPALCO), and Cotabato Light and Power Company (CLPC)
    • The 1st RP (previously known as 5RP) for this group covers the period from July 1, 2026, to June 30, 2030
  • Meralco withdrew its 5RP application last August 5 and will re-apply in November
    • MER sought a tariff of P1.6884/kwh, higher than the current tariff of P1.35/kwh, underpinned by a P246bn 4-year capex proposal and 14.6% WACC
    • Capex program involves the installation of 1,500-circuit-km underground cable system within its franchise area; At present, about 97% of Meralco’s cable network is still composed of overhead lines
MARKET
PSE to change index methodology in 2026
  • PSE President and CEO Ramon Monzon said that the Exchange plans to revise its index policy
  • The effectivity will likely be in June 2026 [for the August rebalancing]
  • The PSE received comments on some of its proposed revisions
  • Among the proposed changes is a lower public float requirement (of 15%) for companies with market capitalizations above P120bn
    • The minimum public float requirement for PSE Index constituents is currently 20%
  • Mr. Monzon wants the PSE’s index policy to be “…benchmarked against the best: MSCI, FTSE, S&P Global…”
CONGLOS
DTI approves P14 per 40-kg bag safeguard duty on imported cement
  • The Department of Trade and Industry imposed a safeguard duty amounting to P14 per 40-kilogram bag or P349 per metric ton of imported cement for a period of three years
    • We estimate the tariff is equivalent to ~11% (~US$6 per MT) of the landed cost of imported bagged cement of US$54/MT
    • Prior to this ruling, the temporary safeguard duty was set at P400/MT
  • This should provide some relief to local manufacturers, as imports have grown to represent more than 30% of market share
    • Concreat Holdings Phils (CHP PM) is 51%-owned by DMCI (DMC PM) and 10%-owned by Semirara (SCC PM)
    • Aboitiz Equity (AEV PM) owns 45% of CRH Aboitiz (a.k.a. Republic Cement)
POLITICS
Marcos' approval rating dips 9ppt to 33% in Sep
  • In the latest Pulse Asia Research Survey, President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.'s approval rating deteriorated 9ppt to 33% from 42% in Jun
    • The drop in approval rating follows Marcos' order to investigate alleged corruption in flood control projects during his 2025 State of the Nation Address (SONA) on July 28 as well as the nationwide protests against flood control corruption that took place on Sep 21
    • Notably, this is still much lower than the 82% approval rating at the start of his term and a reversal from the 10ppt gain to 42% in Jun from 32% in May
    • Marcos' approval rating in Luzon stands at 50% in Jun unchanged from May, Visayas at 23% falling sharply from 41%, while his approval rating in Mindanao nosedived to 4% from 25%
  • Vice President Sara Duterte's approval rating saw a 4ppt decrease to 55% in Sep from 59% in Jun
    • VP Duterte's approval rating in Luzon was at 36% from 41%, Visayas at 56% from 58%, Mindanao at 97% from 96%
