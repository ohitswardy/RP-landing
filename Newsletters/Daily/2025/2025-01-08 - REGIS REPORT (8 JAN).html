REGIS REPORT

08 January 2025

The Dec CPI inflation print of 2.9% was higher than what the market (and us) were expecting. While we don’t think this single print necessarily derails our expectations of BSP rate cuts this year (75bp), it does raise the possibility of fewer cuts if the uptrend does continue into this year.

+++

The Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE PM) and the local SEC are considering easing rules to pave the way for the listing of some, potentially large, IPOs. Chief among these rules that could be eased is the requirement for a 20% public float for new listings.

Paring down the minimum % float will allow deals to push through, albeit at a smaller size. Large offerings, especially those >US$1bn, will be difficult to pull off given current market conditions and investor interest levels.  Mynt, the holding company for GCash, is among the companies considering an IPO. It’s likely that a Mynt IPO will be big, if not the biggest in the Philippines ever.

+++

Rice imports for FY24 amounted to 4.7m MT, +30% YoY and roughly 30% of the nationwide consumption of the grain, which is a record high %. The high level of imports has helped tame rice prices, but we reckon the impact has been far too small. We suspect anti-competitive bottlenecks have kept rice prices from falling another 10-15%.

+++

Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) announced it is in discussions to divest its entire 16% stake in the Coral Bay HPAL plant in Palawan to its shareholder and joint venture partner, Sumitomo. We welcome this exit, given that the Coral Bay plant has been a large drag to NIKL’s profits.

MACRO
Inflation rate up to 2.9% in December
  • Headline inflation rose to 2.9% in December from 2.5% in November
    • This is slightly higher than consensus forecast of 2.6% and came in at the higher end of BSP's forecast range of 2.3-3.1%
  • On a MoM basis, CPI rose 0.6%, primarily driven by higher food prices which increased by 0.8% MoM
    • The increase was mainly due to price upticks in vegetables and meat due to the several typhoons that hit the country in October and November
    • Rice CPI sequentially dipped for the sixth consecutive month by 0.7% bringing the cumulative decline to 3.7%

      • Looking into rice prices on Jan 6 vs Dec 6, price ranges of well-milled rice remained stable and did not move from P40-52/kg for local and P40-54 for imported based on the Dept of Agriculture’s data

    • Outside of food, increases in the price of rentals and LPG contributed the higher inflation print, rising 2.4% and 7.8% respectively
  • Core inflation increased slightly to 2.8% in December from 2.5% in November. While core non-food inflation inched up to 2.8% from 2.7% for the same periods
  • With this, the country's average inflation rate in 2024 is at 3.2% in-line with BSP expectations and lower than the 6% average for 2023

 

 

 

DIGITAL FINANCE/TELCOS
PSE weighing exemptions for GCash IPO's $8bn valuation target
  • Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE) with the help of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) is exploring the possibility of granting an exemption by lowering the 20% float requirement for the IPO of Globe Telecom's (GLO PM) mobile financial services app GCash
    • Rationale behind this would be because of the large size with an estimated IPO valuation of P465bn (US$8bn) implying ~P90bn needs to be raised—much larger than current record holder Monde Nissin, which raised ~P56bn in 2021
      • The P465bn potential valuation would make the company bigger than the market caps of Globe (GLO PM) P316bn and Ayala Corp. (AC PM) P377bn, also comparatively to banks it would be bigger than Metrobank (MBT PM) P339bn
  • PSE CEO Ramon Monzon stated that GCash and Ayala has inquired about possibly reducing the required float of 20% and he stated that he is not too dogmatic about it
    • Based on current rules, IPO candidates need to sell at least 20% of shares when listing and must also maintain a public float of at least 10%
    • On IPOs raising over P5bn, they are allowed to sell just 15% to the public and then raise the remaining 5% in the next 2-3 years
      • An SEC exemption could also allow selling below 15% while meeting the 10% minimum float requirement, without mandating a follow-on share offering
  • Previous reports stated that GCash plans to list in 2H25 in an exclusive PSE listing instead of a dual PH-US-IPO
    • Although the deal timing has yet to be finalized, Monzon states that it may happen this year

Rice imports reach record 4.7mn MT in 2024
  • The country’s rice imports in 2024 reached 4.68mn MT, nearly 30% higher than the 3.61mn MT recorded in 2023
    • According to the Bureau of Plant Industry (BPI)
  • Vietnam remained as the country’s top rice supplier at 3.56mn MT (76%), followed by Thailand (598k MT) and Pakistan (284k MT)
MACRO
Government debt inches up to P16.09tn in end-November
  • The government’s total debt stock rose by 0.4% (P71bn) MoM to P16.09tn as of end-November
    • Cited depreciation of the PHP against the USD
  • Domestic debt rose by 0.3% (P31.8bn) MoM to P10.9tn due to the net issuance of domestic securities and the impact of the depreciation PHP on retail dollar bonds
  • External debt rose by 0.8% (P39bn) MoM to P5.17tn on the back of the PHP’s depreciation and net foreign loan additions
  • The government is targeting its outstanding debt to end at P16.06tn in 2024 (60.6% of GDP) and P17.35tn (60.4% of GDP) in 2025 (see here)
    • As of end-September 2024, the government’s debt-to-GDP ratio stood at 61.3%
    • The ratio is expected to decline below 60% by 2027 (58.4%); By the end of the Marcos admin’s term in 2028, it expects the debt-to-GDP ratio to fall to 56.3%

 

 

 

MACRO
Dollar reserves drop 1.5% at end-December
  • The Philippines' gross international reserves (GIR) at end-December saw a 1.5% MoM decrease to $106.8bn from $108.5bn at the end-November
    • Primarily due to the BSP's net foreign exchange operations, payments for foreign debt obligations, downward valuation adjustments on gold holdings
    • The level of dollar reserves by end-Dec was enough to cover about 3.8x the country's short-term external debt and also equivalent to 7.5x months' worth of imports of goods and payments
  • Reserve position in the IMF increased 1.1% MoM to $676.2mn
  • Buffers in the form of gold were valued at $11bn, slightly down 0.2% MoM
  • Foreign investments declined by 1.3% MoM to $90bn
  • The end-December figure is in-line with the BSP's end-2024 target of $106bn. While the BSP expects $110bn by end-2025
