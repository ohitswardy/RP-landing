REGIS REPORT

13 June 2025

There’s a flurry of legislation passed by Congress right before it adjourned that media is now reporting on.

Among them is the bill on a rationalized, uniform taxation framework for mining activities.  Apart from retaining the 5% royalty rate on operations within mineral reservations, the bill also imposes a five-tiered royalty rate on mining operations outside mineral reservation.  The final bill does not include the provision on the ban of ore exports and windfall profits tax.

Congress also approved the “Accelerated Right-of-Way” (AROW) bill, which aims to expand the coverage of the previous laws on rights-of-way acquisition to all national infrastructure projects, including PPP projects.  The price to be paid for property acquisitions will henceforth be based on a standard valuation system and a schedule of market values.

As significant as the passage of the above bills was the non-passage of the bill mandating a P200/day increase in minimum wages. Enactment of that bill would have raised minimum wages by over 60% in some areas, leading to, we reckon, significant disruption if not damage to the economy.

Note that the legislation approved by Congress becomes law only with the President’s signature, assuming these are not vetoed.

+++

Jollibee (JFC PM) announced it has been named as the preferred buyer in the sale process for a Korean fried chicken chain named Norang Tongdak.  The deal value is estimated at KRW140bn (US$100m), which implies just under 14x trailing PER (FY24). Norang Tongdak has over 700 stores, which we suspect are mostly franchised outlets. Its revenue and EBIT are 2% and 3% of JFC’s and we estimate this transaction will be very marginally accretive to EPS.

The deal does not look expensive multiple-wise from a global perspective, but multiples in South Korea are generally lower.

+++

Global oil prices are spiking in response to Israeli strikes in Iran. The Philippines is exposed to gyrations in crude prices as we import nearly all of our oil requirements. We also do not subsidize fuel prices at the pump. Hence the pass-through of global oil prices (in PHP terms) is pretty direct into local inflation. Note, though that even with today’s blip, oil prices are still some 20% lower than where they were last year. One hopes that the conflict remains contained and does not spread throughout the Middle East.

 

+++

The DENR amended Semirara’s (SCC PM) Environmental Clearance Certificate (ECC), allowing the company to increase its annual production to 20mn MT from 16mn MT starting this year.  Higher production/sales volumes should help offset the impact of declining thermal coal prices.

MACRO
19th Congress adjourns without passing minimum wage hike bill
  • Both the House and Senate adjourned on Wednesday, ending the third regular session without either chamber adopting the other's bill or presenting a bicameral conference committee report after not being able to agree on the amount of increase
    • Rizal Rep. Juan Fidel Nograles, chair of the House committee on labor and employment, pushed for a bicameral conference committee for transparent discussions on the minimum wage hike. However, Sen. Joel Villanueva urged the House to adopt the Senate's P100 wage increase version, which would bypass the need for a bicameral conference, so it can be signed by the President. 
      • Villanueva expressed "strong reservations" against the House's proposed P200 hike, citing potential "adverse economic and social impacts" like increased unemployment and higher consumer prices
      • Admin’s economic managers also warned against the bill stating that a P200 increase in wages could raise inflation by about 2%, while a P100 may add 0.7%
  • Under the Congress’ rules, when there are different provisions in similar bills approved by the Senate and the House, a bicameral conference committee shall be convened, which will come up with a final version of the proposed measure
  • The final version, in a bicameral conference committee report, must be separately ratified by the House and Senate before being sent to the President for approval
  • Bills last only until the end of the congress they are filed in, but the bills that are not passed into law can be refiled after the next congress convenes
MACRO
Congress ratifies Accelerated Right-of-Way Act
  • Congress ratified the bicameral conference report on the “Accelerated and Reformed Right-of-Way (ROW) Act”
  • Among the bill’s salient provisions are:
    • Expansion on the ROW law to include all national government infrastructure projects undertaken through Public-Private Partnerships (PPP)
    • Revision of rules on negotiated sale, which now allows the price offer to be based on the standardized property valuation system and schedule of market values established (vs. BIR zonal values previously)
    • Payment terms are now clearly laid out to be: 50% of land price and 70% of structures upon execution of a deed of sale; Remainder will be paid upon transfer of title or annotation of deed of sale
CONSUMER
JFC acquires South Korean fried chicken chain, Norang Tongdak
  • Jollibee Foods Corp. (JFC PM) has been named as the preferred buyer of Norang Tongdak, in a deal estimated at KRW140bn (US$100mn or ~P5.7bn)
    • Norang Tongdak is a South Korean fried chicken chain that was founded back in 2009. Its signature dish is Norang Chicken which is made with a special batter mix using turmeric
    • JFC signed an MOU last Tuesday with Q Capital Partners and Corstone Asia to acquire the company from the two Korean private equity firms
    • Corstone and Q Capital acquired Norang Tongdak for KRW70bn back in 2020
  • In 2024, the company posted revenue of KRW106.7bn (+10% YoY), operating profit of KRW12.7bn (+10% YoY), and net profit of KRW10.3bn
    • Norang Tongdak’s revenue and EBIT (for FY24) are ~2% and ~3% of JFC’s
  • The KRW140bn acquisition price implies a PE ratio of 13.6x on Norang's 2024 net profit
    • Fried chicken chain Kyochon is on 8x 2025E PER; Korean fast-food chains trade at a median of 6.7x
  • Norang Tongdak has over 700 stores, up from 400 back in 2020
    • Some notable competitors would be bb.q chicken which has 3,500 stores, BHC Chicken 2,200, and Kyochon 1,400
    • JFC's current footprint in Korea is 2,700 Compose Coffee stores and around 200 CBTL stores
  • As of end-March 2025, JFC’s cash stood at P28bn while net debt stood at P65bn
    • Net debt-to-equity stood at 1.0x
MINING
DENR approves Semirara’s ECC, increases annual production limit to 20mn MT
  • The DENR has granted Semirara’s request to amend its environmental compliance certificate (ECC), which includes the following:
    • Increase in annual production limit to 20mn MT from 16mn MT from 2025-2027
    • Expanded coverage area of 5,222 hectares (from 4,370 hectares), allowing Semirara to operate the new Acacia pit once reserves in the Narra pit are depleted
  • We estimate the increase in production limit to 20mn MT could increase SCC’s FY25E profits and FCF by 27% and 16%, respectively, holding other things constant
  • SCC trades at 7.7x FY25E PER; Our FY25E DPS of P4.50/sh is equivalent to a div yield of 14%
    • Note that SCC already paid dividends of P2/sh in April
MINING
Congress ratifies bicam report on mining fiscal regime
  • The Senate ratified the bicameral conference committee report on the Enhanced Fiscal Regime for Large-Scale Metallic Mining Act
  • Disagreeing provisions of Senate Bill No. 2826 and House Bill No. 8937 were ironed out:
    • Five percent royalty based on gross output on mines operating within mineral reservation and a 5-tier margin-based royalty rate for mines operating outside mineral reservation
    • Removal of raw ore export ban under SB 2826
  • We estimate the approved Bicam version of the bill could decrease Nickel Asia’s (NIKL PM) profits by ~4%
