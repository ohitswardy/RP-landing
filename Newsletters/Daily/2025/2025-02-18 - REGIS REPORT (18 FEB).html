As the first property developer to release FY24 results, SM Prime (SMPH PM) offers us a glimpse of what might be happening across the sector

 

2024 net profit rose 14% to P45.6bn, in line consensus but ahead of REGIS forecast, as revenue of the residential, hotel, and office segments outperformed our estimates.

 

Once again, we focus our attention on the residential segment, which reported sequential improvements in both revenue and presales for 4Q. We estimate that reservation sales for the quarter grew about 90% QoQ (+2% YoY). Meanwhile, residential revenue grew 27% QoQ and 23% YoY to about P15bn, a record level. We suspect this was thanks to stronger sales of ready-for-occupancy (RFO) units, which are recognized as revenue more quickly. SM Prime has been offering promotions on RFO units, which appear to have successfully improved sales.

 

Meanwhile, systemwide mall revenue grew 7% in 4Q24 and FY24, in line with our estimates. This likely means that same-mall revenue growth was c. 5%, at the lower-end of the historical range (of 4%-8%).

Overseas remittances reached US$3.38bn in Dec, up +3% YoY. This brought cumulative FY24 remittances to US$34.5b, also +3%, in line with expectations.

Food prices seem to be softening further if the information being provided by the Dept of Agriculture (DA) is to be believed. Notably, the price of imported rice appears to have dropped markedly since Dec. Assuming these prices are representative of on-the-ground reality across the country, we should see reported CPI inflation drop and, more importantly, rising consumer spend on other non-food items.

OFW Remittances up 3.0% YoY in December
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks grew 3.0% YoY to US$3.38bn, a 20.3% MoM jump as Nov was the lowest monthly inflow in the past six months
    • Land-based remittances from the USA, Saudi Arabia, and Singapore were the primary drivers in the increase in remittances posting significant YoY and MoM growth
    • Sea-based remittances growth decelerated to 0.6% YoY from 1.0% in Nov
    • In PHP terms, remittances grew by 8.8%, marking the second-fastest increase of the year and surpassing the 8.5% growth recorded in Nov
  • Bringing FY24 remittances up 3.0% YoY to US$34.5bn
    • This is in-line with the BSP's target growth rate of +3.0% for the year

Prices of rice and vegetables on the decline
  • After conducting a recent inspection at the Commonwealth Public Market in Quezon City, Department of Agriculture (DA) Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. observed that the prices of rice and vegetables have gone down
  • Looking into the DA's price monitoring data, the prices of local well-milled rice remain sticky but prices of imported well-milled rice saw significant decline
    • With the price range for local well-milled rice narrowing slightly from P40-54/kg (last Dec 18) to P40-52/kg (as of Feb 15)
    • While prices for imported well-milled rice saw a bigger decline from P52-60/kg (last Dec 18) to P44-46/kg (as of Feb 15)
  • Notably, prices of tomatoes have also decreased from as much as P160-260/kg (last Dec 18) to P35-70/kg (as of Feb 15)
  • The DA is positive that rice prices have room to go down further to around P35/kg~ in the near term
    • Recall that since last month, the DA has set a price cap for imported rice from P58/kg on Jan. 20, P55/kg on Feb. 5, and P52/kg on Feb. 15
