REGIS REPORT

29 May 2025

We published a new report on Cebu Air (CEB PM), reducing our FY25E profit by 55% after the recent results.  We are nonetheless maintaining our BUY rating on a revised TP of P48/sh, as we believe the airline is now well positioned to harvest on the heavy investments it made last year and see CEB sustaining its market share gains amidst an underpenetrated and supply-constrained Philippine aviation market.

 

Meanwhile, the recent announcement of leasing two of its aircraft to a Saudi-based airline during the lean season should help maximize utilization of its assets. 

 

Trading at 5.5x/4.5x FY25E/FY26E EV/EBITDA, the shares are attractively valued relative to peers. We see CEB begin to de-lever this year and pay dividends on both convertible prefs and commons by next year

The SEC approved the latest preferred share issue of Ayala Corp (AC PM).  The issuance will raise up to P20bn and will be principally used for refinancing.  Given the absence of taxable income at the AC holding company level, there’s no tax advantage in issuing debt while for lenders (investors) there is a tax advantage to investing in prefs.

 

+++

 

ACEN (ACEN PM) signed an agreement to take a 25% stake in a wind project being developed by Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners (CIP).  The wind project will have a capacity of up to 1G (=1,000MW) and will be a likely participant in the government’s Green Energy Auction (GEA) program.  The program works like the feed-in-tariff program but with tariffs determined through an auction.

 

+++

 

The Dept of Agriculture (DA) is forecasting a 7% increase in rice output, which could, in theory, lead to lower price and cut rice imports by 20-25%. As always, the caveat is that it’s dependent on weather being cooperative. Experience has taught us that one shouldn’t pin one’s hopes on that.

SEC approves Ayala Corp.’s P20bn pref share offering
  • The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has approved Ayala Corp’s preferred share offering of up to P20bn
    • Offering consists of the re-issuance of 5mn preferred B shares, with an overallotment option of up to 5mn shares, at a price of P2,000/sh
    • Proceeds of up to P19.86bn will be used to repay short-term bank loans, general corporate purposes, and capex
  • AC’s outstanding pref shares amount to P28bn with average dividend rate of 5.5%
    • AC’s parent level net debt (inclusive of prefs) stood at P193.5bn in end-March 2025
    • Capex guidance (parent level) at P31bn for 2025, of which P15.9bn is earmarked for ACEN
  • AC is trading at a 27% discount to its market-based NAV of P805/sh
TELCOS
Globe targets single-digit growth in subscribers and revenues
  • Globe Telecom Inc. (GLO PM) is targeting single-digit growth in prepaid subscribers and revenues for 2025
    • Globe Head of Consumer Mobile Business Eric Leif Tanbauco stated that the company aims to expand network accessibility while still being affordable to take advantage of the increasing mobile data usage among Filipinos
    • Tanbauco noted that the rise of new content and apps besides the already popular Facebook, TikTok, and Mobile Legends will help drive and stimulate usage
  • Recall that Globe’s core income for 1Q dropped 22% to P4.5bn as revenue was soft across all segments, falling 3% YoY
    • Breaking it down per segment, mobile revenue dropped 3%, broadband fell 5%, and corporate data was down 2%
    • Headline reported income for 1Q25 was P6.98bn
POWER
ACEN acquiring a 25% stake in first large-scale offshore wind project in Camarines Sur
  • ACEN signed definitive agreements with Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners’ (CIP) Growth Markets Fund II to acquire a 25% minority stake in CIP’s proposed offshore wind project
    • Project could potentially have an installed capacity of up to 1GW and will be located near the cost of San Miguel Bay in Camarines Sur
    • Project is currently in its pre-development stage in anticipation of the Department of Energy’s 5th round of the Green Energy Auction (GEA-5) and will be subject to relevant regulatory approvals
  • Further information regarding the transaction and the project will be disclosed in due course
  • ACEN’s RE portfolio stands at 6.98GW, of which 3.6GW are operating, 2.6GW are under construction, and 823MW are committed
    • 2.4GW of 6.98GW are in the Philippines, of which 1.3GW are operating
TRANSPORT
Cebu Air to lease aircraft to Saudi-based Flyadeal during lean season
  • Cebu Air (CEB) disclosed that it signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Saudi-based LCC, Flyadeal, to explore joint strategic commercial initiatives, including:
    • Entering into wet lease agreements for two (2) of CEB’s A320s during Saudi Arabia’s upcoming summer peak flying season, which coincides with CEB’s lean season
    • Reciprocally, CEB will also explore opportunities of leasing Flyadeal’s A320s during the busy winter period of Southeast Asia at the end of the year
    • Flyadeal will also learn from CEB’s experience in flying A330s for long-haul
  • The two companies will map out joint commercial plans over the next few months
MACRO
Gov't expects higher domestic rice output; mulls floor price for rice
  • The Department of Agriculture (DA) stated that they expect the domestic output of palay (unmilled rice) to hit their target of 20.4 MMT (million metric tons), 7% higher than last year, barring any harsher than usual weather disturbances
    • This would yield approximately 12.65 MMT of rice
    • Note that the Philippines imported a record 4.68 MMT of rice in 2024, this filled the shortfall between domestic production and the estimated total consumption of 16.5 MMT that year
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. in a dialogue with farmers said that the gov't is studying the implementation of a floor price for rice
    • A floor price is being considered in order to protect farmers from losses and ensure stable supply
  • Marcos also eyes to return the role of the National Food Authority (NFA) as a rice buyer and price stabilizer
    • Recall that the Rice Tariffication Law in 2019 rescinded the NFA's ability to import rice and sell it directly to the public—effectively liberalizing the importation of rice
    • Currently the NFA can directly buy rice from local farmers but is limited to maintaining a buffer stock of around 555,000 MT or 15 days' worth of rice
      • The NFA at present, buys fresh palay (unhusked rice) for P19/kg and P24/kg for dried
      • Marcos states that they are trying to determine whether the current P19 and P24 prices are enough
