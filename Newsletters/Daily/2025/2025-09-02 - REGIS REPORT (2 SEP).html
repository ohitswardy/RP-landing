REGIS REPORT

02 September 2025

Alliance Global (AGI PM) will take control of the casino complex being built in the Entertainment City by the LET Group through Suntrust Resort (SUN PM). SUN will retain a 20% effective stake in the casino resort complex, which will also include a mall, a hotel, and a theatre.

 

SUN was initially expected to complete and operate the project on its own, through a sub-licensee arrangement with the AGI group (which holds the casino license from PAGCOR). Upon completion of this transaction, AGI will operate the casino itself.

 

This transaction would mean that funding for the casino would now likely be adequate, allowing the casino to be completed by 3Q next year.

 

+++

 

Dito Telecommunity (Dito Tel), which is controlled by Dito CME (DITO PM), says it aims to capture 30% market share in the mobile industry within two years. On our numbers, Dito Tel has ~8% market share today (based on revenue) and is barely breaking even on an EBITDA basis even while laboring under a massive amount of debt (US$4bn).

 

+++

 

Two previously disclosed transactions involving the Ayala (AC PM) group were just completed.

 

First, subsidiary AC Health completed the sale of a 16% stake in itself to a fund backed by Temasek. The deal valued AC Health at US$500m, post-money.

 

Second, ACEN (ACEN PM) sold 192MW of oil-fired plants to its parent company (a 100% subsidiary of AC). Despite the “sale”, we believe ACEN will retain most of the economics relating to the plants. 

 

+++

 

The new SEC chairman Francis Lim intends to have the SEC release revised rules governing REITs within the year. The rule changes are meant to increase the flexibility given the REITs and arguably make them more attractive. Our initial assessment is that the proposed changes will have little impact on most of the REITs already listed. Note that Lim used to the president of the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE PM) and was arguably one of the main proponents of the REIT Law.

GAMING
Alliance Global gains control of Manila Bay integrated resort project
  • In its disclosure to the PSE, Suntrust Resort Holdings (SUN PM) said it will pursue an agreement with Alliance Global
  • In the agreement, Suntrust Resort, which is controlled by the LET Group, will effectively hold a 20% indirect interest in Entertainment City Resorts Corporation (ECRC), a subsidiary of Alliance Global
  • ECRC will assume all rights and obligations related to the Westside Integrated Resorts Project, which consists of a 5-star hotel, a casino complex, a mall, and a theater complex
  • Suntrust Resort has spent c. US$700mn out of the US$1.25bn construction cost for the casino complex
  • Completion of the IR was previously moved from 4Q25 to 3Q26
  • The IR will have 280 gaming tables and 1,260 gaming machines
    • These will add 20% to table capacity and 13% to gaming machine capacity in Entertainment City
  • AGI is trading at 3.4X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
TELCOS
DITO targets 30% market share within the next two years driven by 5G
  • Dito Telecommunity Corp. (Dito Tel) President and CEO Eric Alberto stated that the company targets to secure 30% of the domestic telco market as it continues to expand its 5G network
    • Alberto stated that the timeline is "hopefully within the next two years" and that the company will not resort to a price war to grow its base
      • The company aims to increase market share with improved services, customer experiences, and through the DITO app
    • He claimed that Dito Tel has "over 15mn subscribers"
  • Capex allotted for this year is around P15bn (vs. P20bn in 2024) which is expected to lower next year given more focus on upgrades to 5G over rapid network expansion
  • Alberto noted that while total network coverage has already reached 86%, only 25% of the population is on 5G, underscoring why investments are now being directed toward 5G enhancements rather than expanding basic coverage
    • He believes that the company has "a good head start in stand-alone 5G" while others still use legacy, non-standalone 5G
CONGLOS
AC Health finalizes entry of new investor
  • Temasek-backed fund, ABC Impact, completed its capital infusion into AC Health, allowing it to own an initial ~16% economic stake in AC Health
    • ABC impact subscribed to 121mn common shares and 208mn redeemable preferred shares in AC Health
    • AC Health plans to use the funds to expand its portfolio to at least 10 hospitals (vs. 6 today), 300 clinics (vs. 270), and 1,150 pharmacies (vs. 879) by 2027
  • Transaction value was not disclosed
    • However, during AC’s 2Q briefing, management mentioned that the deal valued AC Health at a post-money EV of US$500mn, which is equivalent to “high-teens to low-20s” forward multiples
    • AC Health generated EBITDA of P586mn in 1H25 (+144% YoY) and trimmed net losses to P100mn
  • AC trades at a 30% discount to its market-based NAV of P773/sh and 7x FY25E PER
POWER
ACEN completes divestment of oil assets
  • ACEN Corp executed a deed of absolute sale, transferring ownership of a total 189MW oil-fired plants to its parent, AC Energy Infrastructure Corp. (ACEIC)
    • Sale covers diesel plants: 52MW in Bulacan, 116MW in Subic, 21MW in Luzon
    • ACEIC is a wholly-owned unit of Ayala Corp. (AC PM) and owns 58% of ACEN
  • Similar to its “exit” from the 246MW coal-fired plant SLTEC, we expect ACEN to maintain the contracts of these diesel plants so that revenue/income streams will still accrue to ACEN
PROPERTY
SEC to revise REIT regulations by 4Q25
  • SEC Chair Francis Lim plans to release revised REIT regulations by 4Q25
  • Among the changes being considered is allowing funds raised from REIT issuances to be reinvested within three years
    • The current requirement is to reinvest the funds into income-generating real estate or infrastructure within one year
  • Another proposal is to allow REIT proceeds to be used for debt repayment
  • Furthermore, Mr. Lim would like to expand the list of eligible assets that the sponsors can reinvest into
    • Currently, sponsors can only reinvest funds into income-generating real estate (or infrastructure) in the Philippines
  • We do not expect any major impact on the major developers since their proceeds from REIT asset infusions are lower than their annual capital expenditures
MACRO
Dept. of Finance expects to hit lower end of GDP goal
  • Finance Undersecretary and Chief Economist Domini Velasquez stated that they expect GDP growth to be faster in 2H25 to be able to meet the lower end of the 5.5%-6.5% growth target for 2025
    • Recall that 2Q GDP growth was 5.5%, slightly faster than 1Q's 5.4%
    • GDP growth in the 2H needs to average 5.6% in order to meet the minimum 5.5% growth target
  • Velasquez expects that household consumption (>70% of the economy) will continue to be the main growth driver alongside benign inflation and lower interest rates
    • She also noted a lower base for 2H25 given that GDP growth was 5.3% in 2H24 vs. 6.2% in 1H24
  • The gov't targets 5.5-6.5% growth for 2025 and 6.0-7.0% for 2026-2028
    • Consensus and Regis expect 2025 GDP growth to be 5.5%
      • 2026 GDP growth expectations are 5.7% and 5.8%, respectively
