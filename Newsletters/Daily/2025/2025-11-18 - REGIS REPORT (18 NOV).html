President Ferdinand Marcos Jr accepted the resignations of his Executive Secretary as well as his Budget Secretary yesterday.  Finance Secretary Ralph Recto will take over the post of Executive Secretary while Frederick Go will assume the post of Finance Secretary.  A Budget Undersecretary will be office-in-charge (OIC) at the Budget Department in the meantime,

MACRO
Budget, executive secretaries resign as President Marcos initiates Cabinet reshuffle
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. has appointed and reassigned new Cabinet Secretaries following the recent resignations of Budget Secretary Amenah Pangandaman and Executive Secretary Lucas Bersamin
    • Pangandaman and Bersamin resigned after their departments were cited to be allegedly involved in the flood control corruption scandal
  • This marks the second Cabinet reshuffle after the 'bold reset' back in May after the administration slate only won 5 out of 12 seats in the previous midterm elections
  • The new appointments/reassignments are as follows:
    • Finance Secretary Ralph Recto is the new executive secretary
    • Special Assistant to the President for Investment and Economic Affairs (SAPIEA) or economic czar Frederick Go is the new finance secretary
    • Budget Undersecretary Rolando Toledo is OIC at the Dept. of Budget and Management (DBM)
  • Recto as the newly appointed Executive Secretary emphasized focus on good governance and transparency—adding that "the president has guaranteed that cases will be filed, masterminds [be] named and accountability [will be] delivered"
CONGLOS
Vehicle sales (ex-BYD) flat in October; Toyota sales up 6% YoY
  • CAMPI reported vehicle sales for October was flat (+5.2% MoM) at 40,014 units
    • Note that CAMPI’s data does not capture BYD sales
    • Brings 10M25 vehicle sales to 383,424 units, down by 0.2% YoY
    • Assuming BYD’s market share of ~6%, we estimate industry car sales may be up 6% in 10M25
  • EV sales (ex-BYD) jumped 62% MoM to 3,603 units and accounted for 9% of the market
    • Brings 10M25 EV sales (ex-BYD) to 24,265 units (~6% of car sales)
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) sold 20,404 units, up 5.5% YoY, 10.7% MoM
    • Brings TMP’s 10M25 sales to 185k units, up 3.8% YoY
    • To reach TMP’s sales target of 235k units (+8%), sales should average 24.9k units per month for the rest of the year
    • To reach REGIS’ sales forecast of 231k units (+6%), sales should average 23k units per month for the rest of the year
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 31% of NAV (24% of gross asset value) and 35% of profit
  • GTCAP is trading at a 51% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,087/sh and at 4x PER

 

 

 

POWER
Prime Infra, FGEN natgas deal reaches financial close
  • Razon-led Prime Infrastructure Capital Inc. (Prime Infra) has reached financial close of its acquisition of a 60% stake in First Gen’s (FGEN PM) gas assets
    • Acquisition price is P50bn, subject to adjustments and additional earnout amounts that are payable based on the fulfillment of certain conditions
  • Prime Infra now owns a 60% controlling stake in FGEN’s 2,047 MW natgas power plants and the offshore LNG terminal
    • FGEN will retain a 40% stake in the natgas plants and a 20% stake in the LNG terminal
MACRO
OFW Remittances up 3.7% YoY in September
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks grew 3.7% YoY to US$3.1bn in Sep, accelerating from the 3.2% growth seen in Aug
    • Land-based remittances from the USA, Singapore, and Saudi Arabia were cited to be the primary drivers in the increase in remittances given their % contribution to the total
    • Sea-based remittances growth inched down to 3.7% YoY from 3.8% the previous month
    • In PHP terms, remittances climbed +7.9% YoY as the peso depreciated sharply from P57.08/USD end-Aug to P58.04/USD end-Sep

 

 

 

 

 

MACRO
DOF projects 2025 GDP growth to settle below 5.0%
  • The Dept. of Finance (DOF) projects GDP growth to be 4.7-4.8% for 2025, well-below the 5.5-6.5% gov't target range
    • This implies that 4Q GDP is expected to grow ~3.8-4.2%, below the consensus estimate of 4.8% for the quarter
    • The 4.7-4.8% FY25 growth will be the lowest since 2011 (ex-pandemic), where the economy grew 3.9% due to underspending during the Aquino administration's anticorruption campaign
  • For 2026, the DOF projects GDP growth to be around 5-6%, lower than the 6-8% gov't target set till 2028
  • 2025 GDP growth expectations for consensus and Regis are at 5.1% and 4.8%, respectively
    • 2026 GDP growth expectations are both at 5.5%
