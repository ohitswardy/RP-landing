REGIS REPORT

30 January 2025

Meralco (MER PM) and Aboitiz Power (AP PM) announced the closing of the transaction for them to take stakes in the LNG facilities owned by San Miguel (SMC PM). The transaction, dubbed Project Chromite, involves Meralco and AP buying 67% of two gas-fired power plants and the LNG terminal that will supply the fuel. Those two plants are the Ilijan facility (1,200MW) and Excellent Energy (EERI, 1,300MW). The former is a 20+-year old plant while EERI is a just-completed plant that began test runs a few weeks ago.  Both plants have agreements to supply power to Meralco.

 

We expect the investment to be accretive to profits for both Meralco and AP. For SMC, the completion of the deal would provide them the cash necessary to meet debt payments this year.

In an interview, BSP Governor Eli Remolona said that the commercial banks' regulatory reserve requirement (RRR) on PHP deposits could be cut by 200bps down to 5.0% "possibly this year". He noted that the current 7.0% was still quite high relative to our regional peers. Recall that the RRR was recently slashed by 250bps, effective late October 2024. We estimate that the further 200bps cut could improve the NIMs of banks we cover by 4.5-6% and increase our NPAT estimates by 2.6-3.3%. (See table below.) This simplistically assumes that the freed-up funds are placed with the BSP at the current policy rate of 5.75%. The impact would be higher if banks choose instead to lend more.

 

We remain Overweight the banks.

Meralco electricity sales for FY24 grew 6.4% over the prior year.  The growth figure is above MER’s long-term growth trend (~5%) due to the unusually high temperatures in 1H24. The growth figure also implies that 4Q growth was closer to ~4%.

 

+++

 

The Dept of Energy (DOE) released the list of 12 parties pre-qualified to bid under GEA-3 (Green Energy Auction Program). GEA-3 involves auction for 4,650MW capacity, mostly hydro.  Among the prominent operators named were the First Gen (FGEN PM) group, San Miguel (SMC PM) and the group of Enrique Razon.

POWER/UTILITIES
Meralco, Aboitiz Power completes acquisition of San Miguel’s LNG facilities
  • Meralco PowerGen and Aboitiz Power-owned unit, Therma NatGas Power Inc., reached financial close of the US$3.3bn LNG deal (aka “Chromite”) with San Miguel Global Power (SMGP)
  • Under the deal, Meralco and AP will jointly invest in SMGP’s 1,278-megawatt (MW) Ilijan power plant, its new 1,320-MW combined cycle power facility (Excellent Energy or EERI), and its LNG import and regasification terminal
    • MER will hold 60% of joint venture with AP, which will take a 67% in the LNG facilities
    • MER will thus hold a 40% economic interest in the gas-fired plants and LNG terminal; SMC’s stake will be diluted to 33% (from 100%) while AP will hold 27%
  • The three units (440MW each) of the new LNG power plant under EERI are now mechanically complete; two of those began testing a few weeks ago
POWER/UTILITIES
Meralco 2024 energy sales rise 6.4% YoY in FY24
  • Meralco said that energy sales reached 54,325 GWH in 2024, up 6.4% YoY and surpassing its target of 53,473 GWH
    • The growth was attributed to El Niño-induced warmer temperatures and sustained customer energizations
    • Note that MER’s energy sales grew by 7.1% in 9M24 and 4.1% YoY in 3Q24
    • This implies that 4Q24 energy sales grew by ~4% YoY
  • Residential energy sales volume grew by 9.4% (vs. 10.4% in 9M24)
    • Over 215,000 new customers were added, bringing the total to eight million as of October 2024
  • The commercial segment saw a 7.3% increase in energy sales volume, decelerating from the 8.1% YoY pace in 9M24
    • Growth was driven by strong performance in the hotel, restaurant, retail trade, education, and real estate sectors.
  • The industrial sector had a modest 1.5% growth (vs 1.6% YoY in 9M24)
    • The semiconductor, electronics, food and beverage, cement, and plastic products sectors contributed to growth, offsetting the decline in the steel sector

 

POWER
Department of Energy pre-qualifies 12 companies in GEAP-3
  • The Department of Energy said that 12 companies have been shortlisted for the third green energy auction round (GEA-3)
    • First Gen’s Energy Development Corp., Bac-Man Geothermal Inc., and First Gen Hydro Power Corp
    • Razon-led Ahunan Power Inc. and Olympia Violago Water and Power Inc.
    • SMC’s San Roque Hydropower
    • Yuchengco-led Maibarara Geothermal Inc.
    • Dexter Tiu-led Repower Energy Development Corp.
    • Pan Pacific Renewable Power Phils. Corp.
    • Pulangi Hydro Power Corp
    • United Hydro Power Builders Corp.
    • COHECO Badeo Corp
  • For GEA-3, the government is offering impounding hydro, pumped storage hydro and geothermal contracts with a combined capacity of 4,650MW
    • Auction will consist of 100 MW of geothermal, 300 MW of impounding hydro, and 4,250 MW of pumped-storage hydro
    • Auction is scheduled on Feb 11 and the notice of award is targeted in early May
    • The DOE previously said that the target start of deliveries is 2028-2030 for hydro while 2024-2030 for geothermal
