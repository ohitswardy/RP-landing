REGIS REPORT

11 June 2025

The impeachment of VP Sara Duterte has become a bit of a hot potato. The Senate yesterday convened itself into a trial court for the impeachment of the VP. However, within a few hours, the complaint was remanded (i.e. sent back) to the Lower House, ostensibly to remedy “infirmities” in the case. While the move to remand the case was filed by Duterte ally Senator Bong Go, the resolution was signed by 18 of the 24 sitting Senators. Senate President Francis Escudero – who voted in favor of remanding the case – also said that if the trial is not completed by the 30 June deadline, then the current 19th Congress “cannot bind the actions” of the incoming 20th Congress, should they choose to reject the proceedings.

 

In our view, the current case underscores the rapid decline in popularity of the Marcos administration, that even his allies in the Senate refuse to antagonize the Duterte supporters. This has blown the field wide open for the 2028 Presidential elections, with no faction having a clear mandate.

 

As we have pointed out, the current situation could see increased pressure to pursue more populist policies, including rice subsidies, food vouchers, and most recently, a push for a P200/day legislated minimum wage hike.

The IT-BPO Association of the Philippines said it expects the sector to grow revenue and employment by about 5% this year (to US$40bn in revenue and 1.9mn employees). This suggests that the sector continues to grow its employee base despite the presence of artificial intelligence.

 

+ + +

 

The new SEC Chairman, Mr Francisco Lim, said he will encourage government-owned and -controlled corporations (GOCCs), ie SOEs, to list on the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE) to further develop capital markets. As far as we can tell, though, the only GOCCs that might attract investor attention are the Land Bank of the Philippines (Landbank) and Development Bank of the Philippines (DBP). According to BSP filings, the Landbank was the second largest bank in the country after BDO Unibank, with assets of P3.4trn, just ahead of BPI and Metrobank. Landbank had an ROE of around 13% in 2024. DBP meanwhile, is much smaller, with an asset base of under P1trn, and an ROE of around 8%.

POLITICS
Senate sends VP Duterte impeachment case back to House
  • The Senate voted 18 in favor and 5 against, to remand the articles of impeachment against Vice President Sara Duterte back to the House of Representatives
    • This followed after a failed proposal to outright dismiss the articles of impeachment, which was proposed by Senator Dela Rosa and backed by other Duterte allied senators
    • The only five who voted against the motion to remand were Senators Pimentel, Hontiveros, Binay, Poe, and Gatchalian
  • Senator Alan Peter Cayetano moved to amend Senator Dela Rosa's motion, to instead return the articles of impeachment to the House, without dismissing or terminating the case until:
    • The 20th Congress will convene in July
    • The House can certify its non-violation of the Constitution
    • The House of the 20th Congress communicates to the Senate that it is willing and ready to pursue the impeachment complaint against the VP
  • Note that at least 16 votes (two thirds majority) are needed to impeach VP Sara Duterte, should the trial still proceed

 

PROPERTY
IT-BPO Association expects sector revenue to hit US$40bn this year
  • The IT-BPO Association of the Philippines (IBPAP) said that it would hit its US$40bn revenue target this year
    • This represents c. 5% growth from US$38bn in 2024
  • It is also projecting the sector to employ 1.9mn Filipinos by the end of the year
    • The sector had 1.82mn employees at the end of 2024
  • BPO firms account for 60%-80% of the occupied office space of the developers we cover
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 49% of Filinvest Land’s NAV - FLI is trading at 66% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 5.0X FY25E P/E
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV - MEG is trading at 72% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 3.0X FY25E P/E
    • 28% of Robinsons Land’s NAV - RLC is trading at 72% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY25E P/B, and 4.4X FY25E P/E
MARKET
SEC chief eyes GOCCs to list in the PSE
  • Francisco Ed. Lim, new chair of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), hopes to encourage Philippine gov't-owned and controlled corporations (GOCCs) to list on the stock exchange to spur investor activity
    • Currently there are no GOCCs listed on the PSE
  • Lim, who was PSE president from 2004-2010, stated that he will check the implementation of laws that require the public listing of companies that are availing of gov't incentives
    • Republic Act 11534 or the Corporate Recovery and Tax Incentives for Enterprises (CREATE) Act mandates that in order for certain incentives to be granted, companies must either list on the PSE or have committed to a specified timeframe to do so
    • Lim said that this provision has not been fully implemented as of late
  • The Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) in a report previously mentioned state-run banks such as Land Bank of the Philippines and Development Bank of the Philippines as potential candidates for listing
PROPERTY
Congress approves Bill allowing 99-year land leases to foreigners
  • The Senate and House of Representatives approved a bicameral conference committee report extending the land lease term to foreigners to a maximum of 99 years
    • The maximum of 99 years is in line with Singapore, Malaysia, and Indonesia
    • Currently, the Foreign Investors’ Long Term Lease Act allows foreigners to lease land for up to 50 years, renewable for another 25 years (total of 75 years)
  • Foreigners will be allowed to sublease properties unless their contracts prohibit them from doing so
  • The Bill should now be transmitted to the Office of the President for signing (into law)
POWER
Department of Energy awards over 6,600 MW worth of RE projects under GEA-3
  • The Department of Energy has issued notices of awards to 12 projects that will supply ~6,600 MW of hydro and 30MW of geothermal capacities
    • Committed capacities are scheduled for installation between 2028 and 2035
  • The winning bidders under Green Energy Auction (GEA-3) are:
    • Pan Pacific Renewable Power Phils. Corp. – 2,000MW pumped-storage hydro project at P3.50/kwh and 300MW of impounding hydro at P4.50-P4.75/kwh
    • San Miguel’s (SMC PM) San Roque Hydropower – 1,850 MW of pumped-storage hydro projects, with tariffs ranging from P3.25/kwh to P3.73/kwh
    • Prime Infra’s Ahunan Power – 1,400 MW pumped storage hydro project at P5.46/kwh
    • Olympia Violago Water Power – 600 MW pumped storage hydro at P5.36/kwh
    • Coheco Badeo Corp. – 500 MW pumped storage hydro at P2.58/kwh
    • First Gen’s (FGEN PM) EDC – 9.3MW geothermal capacity at P5.11/kwh to P7.64/kwh and 21.6MW at P7.64/kwh
  • The GEA program aims to promote investments in renewable energy sources
    • GEA allows RE gencos to sell energy to the WESM at their winning (guaranteed) bid prices
    • Any difference between the bid price and the WESM price will be settled through the Feed-in-Tariff system, which is a full pass-through to end users
