REGIS REPORT

17 September 2025

Vehicle sales, as reported by the auto industry association CAMPI, declined 7.6% YoY to 36,714 for the month of Aug. For Toyota, sales were down 5% for the month. Note the CAMPI figure does not include some brands, most notably BYD.

 

YT-Aug, unit sales for CAMPI members are flat YoY at 305k. Given our estimate of BYD sales numbers, we reckon sales of four-wheelers are up around 5%.

 

+++

 

Also on car sales: Regis hosted a chat with Vietnamese car maker Vinfast and its sister company Xanh SM. Vinfast is among the newest car brands to enter the Philippine market. Xanh SM, on the other hand, is the newly launched taxi operator whose vehicles are painted in a distinctly blue-green hue.  Both companies are part of the Vin Group, the largest private business group in Vietnam.

 

Vinfast and Xanh SM shared their plans, which include the rollout of a large taxi fleet and charging stations as well as the sale of EVs: a multi-pronged approach that will complement and sustain each separate business.

 

Check out notes and thoughts below.

 

+++

 

Online gambling has so far been significantly affected by the severing of the direct link between e-wallets to gambling apps and sites, based on statements from PAGCOR. At a Senate hearing, a PAGCOR executive said that revenues from online gambling are down by as much as half since the de-linking was mandated by the Bangko Sentral (BSP).

 

Meanwhile, in related news, online gambling firm Digiplus (PLUS PM) is set to launch its Brazil platform next week.

 

+++

 

Foreign ownership of the PSE-listed stocks dropped another 26bp to 18.5% in Aug. Our estimated foreign ownership of PSEi companies for the same period is 23.9%. Note the 23.9% figure is distorted by the change in composition of the PSEi, which saw the entry of Digiplus at the expense of Bloomberry (BLOOM PM).

 

+++

 

Finance Secretary Ralph Recto says he still expects another policy rate cut this year, notwithstanding recent comments by the BSP Governor about current policy rate being “in the sweet spot.” Recto is a member of BSP Monetary Board, which sets the BSP’s monetary policy, including policy rates.

 

Meanwhile, markets are expecting the US central bank (aka the Federal Reserve) to reduce rates tonight (US time).

Vehicle sales (ex-BYD) slump again in August
  • CAMPI reported vehicle sales fell 7.6% YoY, 5.5% MoM to 36,174 units in August, marking the second month of weakness
    • Note that CAMPI’s data does not capture BYD sales
    • Brings YT-August vehicle sales to 305,381 units, +0.2% YoY
    • Assuming BYD’s market share of ~5%, we estimate industry car sales may be up ~5% YoY YTD
  • EV sales totaled 2,244 units in August, down 17% MoM and accounting for 6.2% of overall sales
    • 8M25 EV sales reached 18,439 units, accounting for 6.04% of sales
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) sold 17,023 units, down 5% YoY, 5.7% MoM
    • Brings TMP’s 8M25 unit sales to 146,357, up 4.1% YoY
    • To reach TMP’s sales target of 235k units (+8%), sales should average 22k units per month for the rest of the year
    • To reach REGIS’ sales forecast of 231k units (+6%), sales should average 21k units for the rest of the year
    • We’ve baked in narrower margins for TMP, projecting GP margins/net margins of 10.8%/2.5% in 2H25 from 17.1%/9.2% in 1H25 to reflect expectations of a weaker PHP (vs. USD) and higher promotional activities
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 31% of NAV (24% of gross asset value) and 35% of profit
  • GTCAP is trading at a 40% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,110/sh and 5x FY25E PER

 

 

TRANSPORT
Key takeaways from meeting with Vinfast and Xanh SM (Green GSM)

Summary:

 

We hosted a group meeting with Vietnamese car manufacturer, Vinfast and its sister company, Xanh SM, operator of the Green GSM taxi and ride-hailing service.  The Vin group stands out as arguably the only foreign company pursuing large-scale investments to help accelerate EV adoption in the Philippines through a multi-pronged approach. With plans to invest as much as US$1bn over the next three years (see here), the group is positioning its EV ecosystem – taxis, charging infrastructure, and second-hand/car rental offerings – to replicate its success in Vietnam.  Although certain barriers to EV adoption have diminished (e.g. access to financing and grid connectivity), charging infrastructure and vehicle affordability continue to pose challenges for a more widespread adoption. The investments of the Vin group in EVs should help ease those barriers.

 

Key takeaways:

 

  • Ambitious taxi fleet expansion plans. The Vin group is making a strong push into the Philippine taxi market, through its ride-hailing/taxi unit, Green GSM Philippines. Launched in mid-June 2025 with an initial 300 units, GSM’s fleet has grown 4x to 1,200 units across Metro Manila today, accounting for about 1% of the country’s taxi population of over 100k units. The group is focused initially on SE Asia and has identified the Philippines and Indonesia as its first two priority markets.

 

  • Lower priced alternative to current ride-hailing apps. GSM differentiates itself from its competitors by an app-based service while offering prices similar to regular taxis (and thus lower than other app-based “ride-sharing” services).  The company buys its fleet from its sister company, Vinfast, and employs its own drivers, who are paid an estimated 20-25% more than their peers (inclusive of overtime + incentives). It offers fares that are ~30% cheaper than its competitors (e.g. Grab) through a no-surge pricing model, helped by lower operating and maintenance costs for its vehicles.  GSM believes it can sustain its growth path and is targeting to operate as many as 10k-20k taxis within the medium term.

 

  • Heavy investments in charging infra.  VinFast, through its sister company V-Green, is pursuing an ambitious charging network rollout – expanding from over 200 stations today to more than 1,000 within the year, which should support its ambitious taxi fleet target of 10k units.  Management noted that even though financial returns of charging stations are attractive (1 EV charging station will cost ~P1mn and the payback period is 25-30 months), obtaining approvals from local government units remain a major hurdle. It currently prices at P17/kWh, well below the >P30/kWh charged by competitors, though access to its charging stations is limited to Vinfast vehicles (which includes GSM taxis) only.

 

That said, the company recognizes that limited charging infrastructure remains a key barrier to EV adoption and is targeting the installation of 10k charging stations to help accelerate this, with the help of its partner, Meralco (MER PM). Management is reluctant to install charging stations in mall parking areas as users will need to pay additional parking fees. This appears in line with the group’s target market: the cost-conscious driver who has a utilitarian view of his vehicle.

 

  • Aggressive dealer network rollout. Since entering the Philippine market last year, Vinfast today has 22 dealerships/showrooms, which it plans to expand to over 70 by 1H26, which would be near Toyota’s current 75 dealer network and AC Mobility’s stated BYD dealer-network target of 77. 

 

While Vinfast cars make up <1% of new car sales today and <5% of all EV sales (BEV + HEV + PHEV), the company hopes to attract more buyers with its free two-year charging and 10-year battery warranty offers.  Priced at 10-20% higher than ICEs in the market, Vinfast cars are comparable to other EVs in the market. Management also noted that bank financing is easily accessible for its cars, as its used car/car rental sister company, Green Future, can offer to buy old Vinfast cars, mitigating risks related to resale values.

  • The Brazil market generated US$3.2bn in GGR in 1H25
    • 1H25 Philippine GGR grew 26% to US$3.8bn, US$2.0bn of which came from e-games
  • PLUS is trading at 5.4X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)

AGI to begin construction of Mactan and Boracay casinos next year
  • AGI’s gaming subsidiary, Travellers International, will begin construction of the US$400mn Mactan World Resorts and the US$300mn Boracay World Resorts next year
  • The upcoming integrated resorts will be in existing Megaworld/GERI townships (Mactan Newtown and Boracay Newcoast)

  • AGI is trading at 3.6X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
MARKET
Foreign ownership of Philippine market falls in August
  • We estimate that foreign ownership of the overall market fell 26bps MoM to 18.5% (in August) while foreign ownership of the PSE Index declined 3bps MoM to 23.9%
    • The more muted drop in the foreign ownership of PSE Index was partly due to the removal of BLOOM (which has foreign ownership of 12.5%) and the addition of PLUS (which has foreign ownership of 25.2%)
  • Net foreign selling increased to US$74mn, from US$29mn in July
  • Of the 29 PSEi constituents that were not replaced, 16 saw increases in foreign ownership while 12 experienced declines
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were:
    • GTCAP – saw the biggest increase in foreign ownership for the second consecutive month. Foreign ownership rose 39bps MoM to 23.4%, as net foreign buying hit US$12.5mn, the second highest for the month. 
    • ICT – foreign ownership climbed 34bps MoM to 34.9%, as it experienced net foreign buying of US$18.9mn, the highest for the month.   
    • AGI – foreign ownership increased 33bps MoM to 16.6%, as foreign buying amounted to US$4mn. 
    • ACEN – foreign ownership fell 1.03 percentage points MoM to 23.0%.
    • CNVRG – foreign ownership declined 37bps MoM to 15.6%. It saw net foreign selling of US$10.6mn, the fourth highest for the month.

Finance Secretary expects one more rate cut this year
  • Dept. of Finance Secretary Ralph Recto stated that there is room for "at least one more cut" but noted that the US Federal Reserve's stance and the Philippines' inflation trajectory are the key decision-making factors
    • The next FOMC meeting is scheduled for this Thursday, Sep 18, 2am PH time (Sep 17, 2pm ET)
      • Jefferies expects three rate cuts by the Fed this year
    • The YT-Aug inflation rate is 1.7%
      • The BSP expects 2025 inflation to be 1.7%, below their 2-4% target band
      • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to average 1.8%
  • Recall that BSP Governor Remolona during the last Monetary Board meeting in Aug mentioned that the current rate of 5.00% is the "sweet spot" given recent data but floated the possibility of one more cut this year depending on external factors
  • The last two Monetary Board meetings for the year will be held on Oct 9 and Dec 11, respectively
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect 100bp total worth of cuts to 4.75%
