REGIS REPORT

17 July 2025

BPI (BPI PM) kicked off reporting season, with 1H25 NPAT of P33.0bn up 7.8% that was behind Regis forecast (P70.7bn for FY25) but in line with consensus (P66.1bn). This implied a rather soft 2Q25 of P16.4bn, up 7% YoY and -2% QoQ. Revenues were strong, driven by accelerating loan growth of 14.1% YoY (vs 13.2% YoY in 1Q), and widening NIMs. PPOP for 2Q25 was up a remarkable 19% YoY. However, this was negated by a sharp +187% increase in 2Q25 provision expense to P4.3bn, for a credit cost of about 75bps in 2Q25, well above management guidance (1Q25 was in line at 53bps). The flat end-period NPL figure (2.25% vs 2.26% in 1Q) and lower NPL cover (97% vs 100% in 1Q) suggests a jump in write-offs, likely in the consumer/MSME segments.

 

The bank will host a briefing on Tuesday, 22 July, at 4pm. Expect lots of questions about their credit cost guidance going forward.

 

 

  

+++

 

Finance Secretary Ralph Recto said that 2Q25 GDP growth would be faster than the 5.4% recorded in 1Q25. He also said that full-year GDP growth would likely hit 5.7%-5.8%, in line with REGIS forecast (of 5.8%) and slightly faster than consensus forecast of 5.5%.

 

The Department of Finance also said it expects minimal impact from the 1% excise tax on remittances from the US.

 

+++

 

Due to the continued decline in international rice prices, the Department of Agriculture will reduce the maximum suggested retail price for imported rice to P43/kg, from P45/kg currently. Faling rice prices is one of the key reasons for our overweight rating on the consumer sector. For reference, rice export prices from Vietnam and Thailand have dropped about 35% YoY and 25% YTD.

 

  

+++

 

First Gen (FGEN PM) announced it has signed a share purchase agreement for the divestment of a 60% interest in its natural gas assets to Razon-led firm, Prime Infrastructure, moving a step closer to vertically integrate the assets of both parties. The deal size of P50bn is subject to certain additional payments based on achieving certain targets.

 

The transaction implies an equity value of at least P83bn for 100% of the natural gas assets, which would yield a NAV estimate for FGEN of P40/sh, more than double the yesterday’s close of P18.10/sh.

MACRO
Finance secretary expects below 6% GDP growth this year
  • Department of Finance (DOF) Secretary Ralph G. Recto stated that 2Q25 GDP growth will likely be better than the first (+5.4%) but guided that full-year growth may be below 6% given heightened uncertainties over US tariffs
    • Recall that 1Q25 GDP grew by 5.4%, weaker than consensus expectations (5.7%) and slower than the 5.9% growth posted in 1Q24
    • He highlighted that the key drivers for growth are gov't spending and household consumption, which comprise for over 70% of the economy
  • Recto said GDP growth may be around 5.7-5.8% for the full-year
    • In 2024, GDP grew by 5.6%
  • The gov't GDP growth target for 2025 was recently lowered to 5.5-6.5% from 6-8% previously, due to global headwinds
  • Consensus expects GDP growth for 2025 to be at 5.5%, while REGIS expects 2025 GDP to grow by 5.8%
MACRO
DOF downplays 1% remittance tax impact
  • The Department of Finance (DOF) stated that the 1% excise tax on remittances from the United States will have minimal impact on the Philippine economy next year
    • President Donald Trump signed the "One Big Beautiful Bill" into law on July 4, with the new remittance tax to take effect on Jan 1, 2026
    • The tax will apply to remittances sent via nonbank channels, including money sent by US citizens, green card holders, visa workers, int'l students, and other legal migrants
  • The BSP projects 2026 remittances to grow 3% to US$36.5bn with ~40% of the total (US$14.8bn) coming from the US
    • The US$14.8bn figure however may be overstated as remittances sent through US money transfer companies or banks are recorded as having US origin even if the sender is not American
    • DOF Chief Economist Domini SD. Velasquez elaborated that 12.8% (US$1.9bn) of the US$14.8bn is estimated to fall under the scope of the tax, assuming these are not routed through correspondent banks
    • Velasquez estimated that the remittance tax will affect 0.009% of the country's GDP in 2026
      • She commented that the overall impact of the 1% tax will be minimal and OFWs can compensate the taxed amount
MACRO
MSRP of imported rice to be cut to P43/kg
  • The Department of Agriculture (DA) stated that it will reduce the maximum suggested retail price (MSRP) for imported rice to P43/kg effective Jul 16
    • The current MSRP is P45/kg, which has been in effect since Mar 31
    • The reduction was due to the continued decline in global rice prices
  • Recall that the DA first implemented an MSRP on imported rice of P58/kg back in Jan 20 and staggered the reductions to the upcoming P43/kg MSRP
    • An MSRP was implemented due to stubbornly high retail prices as much as P64/kg "despite rice prices softening, tariff reductions, and a stronger peso."
  • According to the DA, well-milled rice prices as of Jul 14 for local and imported rice are at P37-50/kg and P38-45/kg, respectively, vs. P40-53/kg and P40-54/kg back in Jan 14
POWER
FGEN signs agreement to sell 60% of natgas assets to Prime Infrastructure
  • First Gen (FGEN PM) and Prime Infrastructure signed a Share Purchase Agreement for the sale of a 60% stake in First Gen’s natgas subsidiaries to Prime Infrastructure
    • Equity stake is valued at P50bn, subject to adjustments and modifications as may be agreed by the parties
    • FGEN will likewise be entitled to receive additional earnout amounts, subject to the fulfillment of certain conditions
  • Deal is subject to the approval of the Philippine Competition Commission and certain conditions precedent
  • First Gen’s natgas assets include the following:
    • 1,000-megawatt Santa Rita,
    • 500MW San Lorenzo,
    • 450MW San Gabriel,
    • 97MW Avion
    • Offshore LNG terminal
    • planned 1,200MW Santa Maria gas-fed plants
  • A valuation of P50bn for 60% would imply a valuation of P83bn for 100%. This compares with our valuation of US$1,779mn (P99.6bn)
    • At P83bn for 100% of its gas assets and assuming our valuation for all other assets, we estimate a NAV of P143bn or P40/share
  • Prime Infrastructure is the operator of the Malampaya natural gas field, which is a major source of gas for FGEN’s plants

Gambling ads to be regulated and pre-screened
  • PAGCOR signed an MOU with the Ads Standard Council (ASC) to pre-screen and regulate all gambling-related advertisements across media platforms
    • Gambling ads are now part of the ASC’s must-screen categories, which include alcoholic beverages, over-the-counter medicines, food supplements, and breast milk substitutes
  • Last week, PAGCOR ordered the removal of billboard and other out-of-home advertisements related to gambling activities by August 15
MACRO
President Marcos, Jr. approves privatization of North-South Commuter Railway O&M contract
  • The Economic and Development Council, chaired by President Marcos, Jr. has approved the North South Commuter Railway (NSCR) O&M contract under a PPP arrangement
    • O&M contract is valued at P229bn
    • Construction of the 147-km NSCR project is ongoing
    • Project will connect Central Luzon, Metro Manila, and CALABARZON and is expected to carry 800k daily passengers in its opening year
  • Pre-operations for the project will begin in March 2026 until July 2027
  • The concession period for the partial operations of Phase 1, which stretches from Clark International Airport (CIA) to Valenzuela (13 stations), will commence in December 2027 and continue until September 2028
  • Meanwhile, the concession period for the partial operations of Phase 2, which extends the services to Nichols with additional segment from Alabang to Calamba (32 stations) will run from October 2028 until December 2031
