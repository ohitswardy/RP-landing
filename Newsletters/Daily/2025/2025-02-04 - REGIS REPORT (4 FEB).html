04 February 2025

Two banks kicked off the FY24 earnings season, with both BPI and Union Bank extolling the virtues of their fast-growing high-margin consumer loan businesses

  • BPI (BPI PM) - FY24 NPAT came in at P62.0bn, up 20% YoY, but slightly lower than Regis (P64.4bn) and consensus (P63.1bn) estimates. NII was up 22%, on loan growth of 18.2% and wider NIMs (+22bps YoY by management estimates). Loans to the "institutional" segment slowed to 11% (from 13% in 3Q24), while "non-institutional" loans surged 42% YoY (vs 39% in 3Q). The slight "miss" in non-interest income was likely due to lower trading gains, as the yield curve turned up in 4Q24. Higher bond yields also likely contributed to the 100bp drop in CET-1 from 14.5% to 13.5% (typically the 4Q is down 50-60bps due to loan growth, and dividend payments). NPLs improved to 2.13%, though NPL cover fell to 106%. Maintain Buy, with the stock trading on 9x PER, 1.30x P/bk on ROE of 15.4% on our FY25E numbers.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

  • Union Bank (UBP PM)  - Net profit of P12.0bn was up 31% YoY, but fell short of consensus (P12.6bn) estimates. For 4Q24, NPAT surged, more than trebling to P3.55bn given a) the cleanup of consumer loans in 2H23, and b) the ending of the Transition Service Agreement (TSA) with the completion of the migration of Citi customers. UBP claimed NIMs of 6.0% (vs larger banks in the 4% range), due to its consumer loan book accounting for 61% of total. While the 4Q24 figures were impressive, we note that that would still indicate downside risk to consensus forecast of P17.2bn for FY25. The stock is trading on 7x PE, 0.6x P/bk on c9% ROE based on consensus estimates.

The bill granting Meralco (MER PM) a franchise renewal/extension was approved in the Senate, taking it forward one more step. Meralco’s current franchise does not expire until 2028 but firms have endeavored to extend / renew franchises early after the experience of ABS-CBN (ABS PM).

Meanwhile, the power regulator will be evaluating Meralco’s proposal to refund overcollections of P19bn (and counting) to consumers. The amount represents the difference between what Meralco has been collecting from consumers and the distribution tariff approved by the regulator. The difference arises from the variance in consumption mix between residential, commercial and industrial customers. Such a refund has been provisioned for and will not have a direct P&L impact on Meralco.

+++

The Senate passed its version of the bill rationalizing taxes on the mining sector, with a provision that could stop mining firms from selling unprocessed ore five years hence. We await what comes out in the reconciled bicameral version over the next few weeks.

+++

Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) agreed to sell its stake in one of the HPAL nickel processing facilities, Coral Bay, to Sumitomo for its carrying cost on its books. This would no loss or gain for NIKL for its stake.

+++

AC Mobility, a unit of Ayala Corp (AC PM), says BYD Cars Philippines sold 4,780 vehicles last year and accounted for 85% of New Energy Vehicles (NEV) sold in the country. The headline sales figure for BYD would imply a market share of 1% last year. Given the trajectory, it is very plausible that BYD could reach 3-4% market share this year.

POWER/UTILITIES
Senate approves Meralco franchise bill on final reading
  • The Senate approved on the final reading the proposed measure to extend Manila Electric Co.'s [Meralco] (MER PM) franchise for another 25 years
    • House Bill No. 10926, allows the power provider "to continue constructing, operating, and maintaining its electric distribution systems in areas such as Metro Manila, Bulacan, Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, and Rizal."
    • The bill also states that at least 30% of Meralco's outstanding capital stock must be owned by Filipinos and that the company must submit an annual report of its finances by April 30 after the measure takes effect
    • By law, all public utilities are required to be 60% Filipino owned and Meralco is classified as one
POWER/UTILITIES
ERC to assess Meralco's P19bn refund proposal by March
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) is set to decide on Meralco's proposed P19bn refund to its customers as announced by ERC Chairperson Mona Dimalanta
  • Meralco's refund accounts for the difference between its actual weighted average tariff and most recent approved distribution rate of P1.35/kWh from Jul 2022 to Dec 2024
    • Resulting in a refund of P0.19/kWh for residential customers over three years, based on Meralco's prevailing end-user rate of P11.74/kWh as of Jan 2025
  • ERC's technical and legal teams are currently evaluating the proposal on whether the refund is sufficient or should be higher given the overcollection and will make a decision on the application by March 1
MACRO/MINING
Senate passes mining tax regime measure
  • The Senate approved on the third reading the bill setting up a margin-based royalty and windfall profit system for the mining industry with expectations that it will raise the gov't's share of mining profits
    • The Lower House approved its version of the bill last Sep 2024 so a reconciled bicameral version will be needed
  • Senate Bill No. 2826 sets a five-tier margin-based royalty tiers ranging from 1 to 5%, while the five-tier windfall profit tax system ranges from 1 to 10%. It also requires large-scale miners to pay the gov't 5% of their gross output
  • Mining companies will be barred from exporting raw ore five years after the measure takes effect
    • Rationale behind the limitation is to encourage mining firms to establish processing plants for more value-adding processing vs. simply exporting the ore
  • The Chamber of Mines of the Philippines stated their support for the bill but disapproves of the raw ore export ban as it can lead to job losses
    • Given the low probability of finishing building plants within five years and disruptions caused in mineral trading
  • The Department of Finance estimates P6.26bn in additional revenue

 

MINING
Nickel Asia finalizes deal with Sumitomo Metal to sell Coral Bay stake
  • Nickel Asia Corporation (NIKL PM) signed a share purchase agreement (SPA) with SMM to sell the company's entire stake of 15.625% in in Coral Bay Nickel Corp. (CBNC) to Sumitomo Metal Mining Co., Ltd. (SMM)
    • The purchase price is based on the carrying amount of the CBNC shares on NIKL's financial statements as of Sept. 30, 2024 (P1.8bn)
  • CBNC is the operator of the Coral bay high-pressure acid leach (HPAL) processing plant in Palawan which processes metals to convert lateritic nickel ore to nickel and cobalt-mixed sulfide
    • Processed products are then shipped and refined in Japan to become components in electronics, chemical engineering, and battery components for EVs
CONGLOS
BYD PH sales accelerate in 2024
  • According to AC Mobility, the automotive arm of Ayala Corporation (AC PM), BYD Cars Philippines sold 4,780 BYD passenger vehicles in 2024, representing an 8,900% growth YoY from 2023 with an 82% market share in the new energy vehicle market.
    • BYD is ranked as the 11th best-selling automotive brand in the Philippines with a 1% market share.
  • Out of the total units sold, 2,078 were BYD Seagull, Dolphin, Atto 3, T3, Seal, Han and Tang models, which allowed the company to secure a 69% market share in the battery electric vehicle segment.
    • The company sold 2,669 units of the BYD Sealion 6 DM-i, which was BYD Cars Philippines top-selling model for the year.
  • Given its trajectory, we find it plausible that BYD can reach 3-4% market share in 2025
PROPERTY
Ayala Land and Department of Transportation break ground on new terminal
  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) and the Department of Transportation broke ground on the Taguig City Integrated Terminal Exchange (TCITX)
  • It will occupy c. 5 hectares in Ayala Land’s 70-hectare Arca South township and will begin operations in 2028
  • The multi-modal transport hub will serve buses and will be connected to the North-South Commuter Rail as well as the Metro Manila subway (when those are completed)
  • Arca South comprises 8% of our ALI NAV estimate
  • ALI is trading at 55% discount to NAV, 1.1X FY25E P/B, and 11.4X FY25E P/E
