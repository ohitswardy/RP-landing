REGIS REPORT

14 May 2025

A heavy day for results today, with a number of key large and mid-caps reporting. Notably, most names delivered disappointing results, in varying degrees.

 

  • Ayala Corp (AC PM) (-) – Headline profits came in at P12.6bn, -4% YoY, with core income at P11.3bn (-4%),  which would be slightly short of expectations.  We had disappointing numbers from two of its key listed units, Globe (GLO PM) and ACEN (ACEN PM).  Losses at the unlisted business narrowed, albeit not at AC Mobility.  Nonetheless, we are encouraged by the progress at AC Mobility, with market share of BYD likely now at over 3%.

 

  • Jollibee (JFC PM) (-) – Operating income grew 18% to P4.8bn but net profits dipped 8% to P2.3bn (after prefs) due to higher tax rates and financing costs. Like in previous periods, the domestic business continued its strong operating streak, but the overseas results were mixed.

  • Aboitiz Power (AP PM) (-) – At yesterday’s analyst briefing, management attributed the 40% YoY decline in 1Q25 profits to the timing of plant outages, which were mostly advanced in the first quarter, as well as lower spot prices. The full-quarter impact of non-cash and financing charges from GNPD, as well as additional financing costs from the Chromite acquisition also dragged earnings lower.  Management conveyed optimism about a rebound in 2H, as recently signed higher-margin contracts start to kick in and outages diminish.

 

  • Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) (-) – Profits for 1Q came in at P601m, down 6% YoY. Revenues rose 5% to P22bn while operating profit grew 9% to P1.0bn. The company says SSS declined 1.2% in the period but note this was affected by (1) the leap year last year and (2) the timing of this year’s Holy Week (Easter Week).  Regis will host a briefing on May 20 to discuss its 1Q numbers.

 

  • AREIT (AREIT PM) (=) – 1Q25 net profit (excluding revaluation gains) grew 43% YoY to P2.1bn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. Portfolio occupancy was flat QoQ at 99%. The company paid a DPS of P0.58/share in 1Q25 and declared that it would pay the same amount in 2Q25. This is also in line with our forecast

 

  • Filinvest Land (FLI PM) (=) – 1Q25 earnings rose 3% to P905mn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. Reservation sales fell 7% YoY but rebounded 52% QoQ to P5.3bn. Management said that provincial projects performed better than Metro Manila ones. Meanwhile, office occupancy rose 1ppt QoQ (to 62%) despite a 6% QoQ increase in the company’s floor area.

 

  • Globe (GLO PM) (-) – Following the soft 1Q top line (down 3%), management has yet to revise its guidance of low- to mid-single digit revenue growth. We reckon that guidance will be tough to meet. The saving graces for 1Q25 are (1) the strong wireline broadband adds and (2) the surprise jump in Mynt profits.  More discussion in the published note (link below).

CONGLOS
Ayala Corp: Globe, ACEN weigh on 1Q25 profit; Losses shrink at portfolio investments
  • 1Q25 core profit drops 4% YoY to P11.3bn.  Headline net income of P12.6bn (-4% YoY) included a one-time P1.7bn gain from the partial deemed divestment of AC Ventures in Mynt, which was offset by P400mn worth of write-offs and losses at LRMC, IMI, AC Logistics, and Gogoro. Core profit fell 4% YoY to P11.3bn, pulled down by lower earnings from Globe and ACEN.  Its portfolio investments recorded narrower losses due to better cost management at IMI and AC Logistics, as well as revenue improvements at AC Health. The results are tracking slightly behind consensus full-year forecast of P50bn and our forecast of P48bn.

 

  • AC Health EBITDA up 3x on revenue growth of 27%. AC Health’s revenues grew 27% to P2.75bn in 1Q25, boosted by higher patient count. EBITDA jumped 188% YoY to P265mn, on trend to meet management’s full-year EBITDA target of P1.25bn. Losses shrank 69% YoY to P59mn in 1Q25 but management reckons it may need a couple more years to reach profit breakeven.  

 

  • IMI turns a profit in 1Q25.  Despite a 14% YoY drop in revenues, IMI swung to a profit of US$3.3mn in 1Q25 from a loss of US$3.7mn the previous year, following last year’s cost rationalization initiatives (i.e. manpower reductions and facility shut downs).  On the impact of trade wars, management believes it has ample capacity in Mexico and the Philippines should supply chains be diverted out of China.   

 

  • AC Mobility unit sales jump 8% QoQ, 114% YoY but losses widen.  AC Mobility’s unit sales rose 8% QoQ and 114% YoY to 9,206 units in 1Q25, equivalent to a market share of 7.4%. We estimate sales of BYD may have hit 4k units in 1Q25 alone, which will put its market share at ~3%. However, higher marketing and manpower expenses largely to support the expansion of BYD pushed AC Mobility to record a wider net loss of P168mn in 1Q25 from P35mn in 1Q24.     
POWER
Aboitiz Power: Frontloaded plant outages, lower spot prices pull 1Q25 profit down 40% YoY
  • 1Q25 core income drops 40% YoY to P4.7bn.  Headline net profit fell 42% YoY to P4.6bn while core profit of P4.7bn was also lower by 40% YoY.  Both headline and core profit are trending behind consensus forecast of P35bn.  

 

  • Plant outages, lower spot prices lead to narrower genco/RES margins.  EBITDA for the generation and RES segment fell 10% YoY, after (a) the company advanced its scheduled maintenance activities to avoid outages during the May elections and (b) spot prices fell. Plant outage days (planned and unplanned) totaled 193 days during the quarter from only 75 the previous year, leading to higher power purchases. Genco GP margins fell slightly to P1.91/kwh from P1.92/kwh but combined with RES margins, overall GP margins dropped to P1.69/kwh from P1.75/kwh. This was further exacerbated by the full-quarter impact of the recognition of non-cash and interest expenses at GNPD, as well as the incremental financing costs from the Chromite acquisition.  

 

  • DU volume growth normalizes to 5% in 1Q25.  Its distribution business saw volumes rise by 5% in 1Q25, normalizing from last year’s warmer temperatures. By segment, residential grew 8% while commercial and industrial grew 4%.  EBITDA was slightly lower due to higher opex and a one-time refund of regulatory re-set fees.

 

  • Outlook: Fewer plant outages, larger exposure to higher-margin contract. Given the frontloaded maintenance activities, management expects minimal plant outages for the rest of the year. After having close to 30% of its baseload capacity exposed to the spot market in 1Q25, management expects to gradually lower its WESM exposure to 10% by year-end. Over 1GW worth of new contracts are set to start in the coming quarters, with 800MW of these contracted with Meralco.

 

 

CONSUMER
Philippine Seven - Initial Comments on 1Q25
  • Philippine Seven Corporation [PSC] (SEVN PM) reported net profit of P600.8mn, down 6% YoY
  • Operating profit increased 9% YoY to P1.0bn, while revenue is up 5% YoY to P21.8bn—trailing a bit behind REGIS but in line with consensus expectations
  • 1Q25 SSSG posted at -1.2%, a mild improvement from the -2.3% in 4Q24 but weak relative to the strong 8.7% in 1Q24
  • From a brief discussion on the results of operations in the quarterly report, the SSSG decline was said to be primarily due to the change in Holy Week timing (occurred in 1Q last year vs. 2Q this year) which created a high base
    • E-wallet partner system downtime incidents decreased and revenue share received by SEVN did not change, despite earlier indications that it will be reduced
    • The issue of regulatory compliance for a major vape brand was resolved and the product was restocked, but importation challenges hampered sales from returning to previous levels
    • The company also noted that spillover effects from the issues that negatively impacted sales in 4Q24 continued in 1Q25, but to lesser effect
  • More details after the briefing on May 20 (4:00pm), register here

 

PROPERTY
AREIT 1Q25 net profit rises 43% YoY, in line
  • AREIT’s 1Q25 net profit (excluding revaluation gains) grew 43% YoY to P2.1bn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts
    • The substantial YoY increase was due to the acquisition of assets in 3Q24
  • Occupancy of its portfolio was flat QoQ at 99%
  • The company paid P0.58/share in DPS in 1Q25 and declared that it would pay another P0.58/share in 2Q25
    • This is in line with our forecast
  • AREIT previously announced that it will acquire 306k sqm of provincial mall, office, and hotel assets from Ayala Land in exchange for 506mn AREIT shares (at P41.50/share)
    • After the acquisition, AREIT’s shares outstanding will expand by 16% while its AUM will increase by 18%

 

 

PROPERTY
Filinvest Land net profit rises 3% in 1Q25
  • Filinvest Land’s 1Q25 net profit rose 3% YoY to P905mn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts
  • Reservation sales fell 7% YoY but rebounded 52% QoQ to P5.3bn
    • Provincial projects performed better than Metro Manila ones
    • Provincial sales rose 24% YoY to P3.5bn
  • Residential GPM expanded 3.8ppt YoY (to 52.8%), thanks to cost control efforts and the higher proportion of sales from the middle-income segment (79% in 1Q25, from 71% in 1Q24)
  • Office occupancy rose 1ppt QoQ (to 62%) despite a 6% increase in the company’s floor area
    • Management said that it has been seeing more office demand recently
  • Mall occupancy improved 3ppt QoQ and 7ppt YoY (to 75%)
    • The company said that it had signed contracts for another 10% of its mall space, implying that mall occupancy could hit 85% in the coming quarters  
  • FLI is trading at 65% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 5.1X FY25E P/E

 

 

BANKS
Commercial banks’ loan growth moderates to 11.8% in March; M3 growth slows to 6.1%
  • Loans of commercial banks rose by 11.8% YoY in March, a tad slower than the 12.2% rate in February
    • Loans to productive activities grew 10.9%, moderating from 11.2% in February due to slower growth in retail (+11.6% from 13.7%) and real estate (+9.6% from 9.9%)
    • Consumer loans grew 23.6%, slightly slower than the 24.1% pace in February
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) grew by 6.1% in March, slightly slower than the 6.3% growth rate in February
