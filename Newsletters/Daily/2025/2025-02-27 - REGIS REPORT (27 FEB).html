REGIS REPORT

27 February 2025

Megaworld (MEG PM) announced that its FY24 net profit reached P21.7bn (including share of minorities), which would appear to be better than estimates.

 

Revenues for all three key segments (residential, office and mall/retail) outperformed our expectations. Residential revenue rose 19% to P51bn. We suspect MEG has been able to sell more ready-for-occupancy (RFO) units, which would mean a speedier recognition of revenue.

 

 

There was no mention of reservation sales (pre-sales) figures. We hope to get more information during the briefing next week.

 

+++

 

PAGCOR says gross gaming revenue (GGR) across the (legal) gaming industry it oversees grew +24% to P410bn in FY24. Approximately P200bn (49%) was attributable to the licensed casinos. This implies 4Q24 GGR of P51bn, roughly flattish YoY.

 

On the other hand, the implied GGR for the e-games sector would have been up +170% for the year to P155bn. This also suggests e-games GGR for 4Q alone was P52bn (around +30% higher QoQ), which would roughly match the GGR for the licensed physical casinos.

 

+++

 

ACEN (ACEN PM) began construction of its battery energy storage system (“BESS”) for its solar project in Australia, the New England Solar Farm. The 936MW solar farm coupled with the 400MWh BESS is likely to cost US$800-900m.

 

+++

 

Ayala Corp (AC PM) says it’s no longer selling Integrated Microelectronics (IMI PM). It’s unclear if this is because there are no buyers and/or valuations are too low. IMI appointed a new CEO last year to rationalize its operations and bring it into the black, presumably ahead of an eventual sale by AC.

 

The market cap of IMI has shrunk to a mere P3.7bn (US$64m), which means AC’s attributable ownership is nominally worth only US$34m. From a financial perspective, one has to ask if the value of the business is, or can be, meaningful.

 

+++

 

In a press release, Chinabank (CBC PM) reported FY2024 NPAT of P24.8bn, up 13% YoY. There is no consensus figure for the bank, as Bloomberg shows no recent coverage. Loan growth surged 18% YoY, a significant acceleration from the 14% pace in 3Q24. Net interest income was up 19% YoY, sustaining the growth from 9M2. Non-interest income jumped to P2bn; while there were no details given, the previous quarters were showing large-ish loan write-backs and write-ups from NPLs that became current. Note that 9M24 non-interest income was a P146mn loss, as the bank booked its interest rate swaps as trading/fx losses. Provision expense soared to P1.8bn in 4Q24 – more than the 9M24 figure) – as we believe the bank also did some cleanup of their NPLs. The bank ended 2024 with NPLs of 1.6% and NPL cover of 139%.

 

The stock is trading on trailing PER of 9.2x and P/Bk of 1.38x (on 2024E numbers), on a 2024 ROE of 15.6%.

 

The bank will hold a briefing on 04 March at 4pm.

PROPERTY
Megaworld FY24 net profit rises 12%
  • In a press release, Megaworld said that its FY24 net profit (before minority interest) rose 12% to P21.7bb
  • A corresponding 12% increase in net profit to parent equity holders would be equivalent to P19.4bn, above REGIS and consensus forecasts
  • Revenue of every segment outperformed our expectations
  • FY24 residential revenue grew 19% to P51bn, a record level
    • In 9M24, MEG sold about P40bn of RFO units, which can be recognized as revenue quickly. This trend might have continued in 4Q24
  • FY24 office revenue rose 7% to P13.4bn
    • 4Q24 office revenue increased 13% QoQ and 19% YoY, as new office buildings began contributing to revenue
  • FY24 mall revenue rose 19% YoY to P6.3bn, just slightly above the pre-COVID level
    • 4Q24 mall revenue climbed 19% QoQ and 27% YoY
  • FY24 hotel revenue grew 34% to P5.1bn, thanks to the completion of the 1,530 room Grand Westside Hotel, the largest hotel in the Philippines
  • MEG is trading at 72% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 3.0X FY25E P/E
  • The company plans to hold a briefing next week
GAMING
PAGCOR says Philippine GGR grew 24% in 2024
  • PAGCOR said that the country’s gross gaming revenue (GGR) rose 24% to P410bn in 2024
  • It expects GGR to grow to P450bn-P480bn in 2025
    • PAGCOR expects the growth to be led by e-games
    • It expects licensed casinos to comprise 50% of 2025 GGR and e-games to account for 45%
  • 49% of GGR came from licensed casinos
    • This would imply FY24 GGR of P200bn (-3% YoY) and 4Q24 GGR of P51bn (flattish to mildly up QoQ and YoY)
  • 38% of GGR came from e-games
    • This would imply FY24 GGR of P155bn (+170% YoY) and 4Q24 GGR of P52bn (+30% QoQ)

  • It will be dedicated to strategic partnerships, talent acquisition, and international growth
  • PLUS is trading at 10.2X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
POWER
ACEN starts construction of 400MWh BESS project in Australia
  • ACEN will begin construction on the 400MWh battery energy storage system (BESS), which will complement its 936MW dc (720MWac) New England Solar Farm (NESF) project
    • BESS will provide 200MWh of energy over a two-hour period, when required – day or night
  • BESS project is expected to be completed within the year
    • The first stage of NESF project (521MWdc) was completed in 2023
    • The second stage of NESF, 416MWdc (320MWac), will begin construction in 2026
    • We estimate the total cost of the NESF + BESS project could be ~US$800mn (or ~US$900k/MW)
    • ACEN owns 100% of the NESF
  • As of Oct 31, 2025, ACEN’s total attributable RE capacity stood at 6,754 MW
    • Its operating RE capacity stood at 3,051 MW, of which 1,067MW is in the Philippines
    • It is currently constructing 2,302 MW, of which 1,330MW is in the Philippines
    • Another 1,401 MW of new capacity is “committed”, which likely includes the second stage 416MWdc NESF project
