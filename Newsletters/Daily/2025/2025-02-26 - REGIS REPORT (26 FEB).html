REGIS REPORT

26 February 2025

The rapid growth in digital payment adoption continued into January. Digital transactions done through the National Retail Payments System (NRPS) rose 38% to P1.8trn (US$31bn) for the month, a pace similar to the last few months.

 

The transition to digital payments will be transformative of the model of how finance and even consumption will be done. As seen in these growth numbers, this transition is happening quite rapidly.

BANKS/DIGITAL BANKS
January digital payments jump 38% to P1.8tn
  • Digital transactions over the National Retail Payments System (NRPS) jumped 38.2% YoY in January, keeping pace from the 38% growth seen in Dec, and 41% in Nov
  • Combined PesoNet and InstaPay payments totaled to P1.8tn (roughly $30.8bn) much higher than P1.3tn last Jan 2024 but unchanged relative to the previous month of Dec
  • By volume, consolidated volume of transactions through InstaPay and PesoNet surged 58.4% YoY to 160.2mn from 101.2mn
    • It is of note that these datasets only reflect inter-platform transactions (e.g., BDO-to-BPI, GCash-to-MBT, Maya-to-GCash, etc.) and do not include transfers within the same platform. As a result, they significantly underestimate the total volume of digital transfers
  • Real-time, small-ticket transactions over InstaPay amounted to P751bn, up 47.8% YoY but down 7.3% MoM given seasonality from the holiday season last Dec. In terms of number of transactions, InstaPay volumes grew 62% YoY—slightly slower than Dec's 65% growth
  • For larger payments over PesoNet, the January figure increased 32% YoY and 6.2% MoM to P1.05tn. Aligned with the 31% growth in Dec and 32% in Nov. While PesoNet volumes are up 21% to 9.6mn.
  • The BSP targets online payments to comprise 60-70% of the country's total retail transaction volume by 2028
    • Bringing the fee to zero (from as high as P25 transfer fees per transaction) would encourage even lower-ticket payments and more transactions to be done digitally
TRANSPORT
ICTSI’s Mexico port volumes rise 11% YoY in January
  • Data from Mexico’s port regulator showed that volumes at ICTSI’s (ICT PM) port, Contecon Manzanillo (CMSA), grew by 11% YoY to 137k TEUs in January 2025
    • This was faster than the 4% YoY growth rate in Dec 2024
  • CMSA’s market share in January stood at 42% (up from a 40% share in Jan 2024 and 37% in FY24), making it the largest port by volume in Manzanillo
    • Volumes across the four ports in Manzanillo grew by 6% YoY to 329k TEUs
    • Industry growth was virtually unchanged from Dec 2024/FY24 (+6% pace), given that the port is already congested
    • Recall that CMSA increased its capacity in October 2024 by 250k TEUs (+15%) to 1.7mn TEUs
  • For FY25E, we’ve built in a 16% YoY increase in CMSA’s volumes to 1.69mn TEUs
    • To reach our forecast, CMSA’s volumes should average 141k TEUs for the rest of the year
    • We estimate CMSA accounts for 12% of FY25E consolidated volumes, 21% of revenues, and 25% of EBITDA
  • ICT trades at 7.2x Regis’ FY25E EV/EBITDA (consensus: 7.8x), lower than peer average of (11x) and historical average (11x)

CONSUMER
Monde remains open to unloading Figaro stake
  • Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) CEO Henry Soesanto stated that the company is selling its 15% stake in Figaro Coffee Group (FCG PM) is not definite but is still open to the possibility
    • FCG through its subsidiary Figaro Coffee Systems Inc., operates and franchises a retail restaurant network including brands such as Figaro Coffee, Angel's Pizza, Tien Ma's, and Café Portofino
  • He states that the problem was that they are not of much help there [in FCG] as well as that their stake did not help them as intended
    • Recall that MONDE acquired back in 2023—a 15% stake in FCG for about P820mn with synergies in mind for its foray into the food service business and overall growth strategy in the Philippine market
    • Soesanto first brought up the idea of selling the stake during its previous investor briefing, as he mentioned that they initially thought Figaro could have helped Monde enter a new category but the company found out that it was not so feasible
  • Note that as of late, the company in a disclosure last Feb 7 clarified that the CEO's statements were "preliminary and essentially exploratory in nature" and that since the disclosure posting date no plans, proposals, or discussions for divestment have been presented or taken up by MONDE's board
