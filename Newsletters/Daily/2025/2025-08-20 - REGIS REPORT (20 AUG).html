CONSUMER
SEVN 1H25 net profit +1% YoY

1H25 net profit +1% YoY

Philippine Seven reported a 1H25 profit of P1.8bn (+1% YoY). EBIT increased 8% to P2.8bn on revenue growth of 7% to P46.5bn. Results for 1H are overall broadly within REGIS expectations. For 2Q25 alone, net profit was up 5% YoY to P1.2bn. EBIT rose 8% YoY to P1.8bn, while top line grew 9% to P24.8bn. EBIT and net margin dipped 10bp and 20bp YoY, respectively, on store-expansion-related expenses from higher logistics costs and accretion of interest costs on lease liabilities.

SSSG sees mild improvement but remains negative

Same store sales contracted by 0.5%, marking the third consecutive quarter of negative SSSG. While this appears to be a slight improvement from -1.2% in 1Q, it remained down YoY despite the usual boost from the Holy Week timing this year (Apr 25’ vs Mar 24’) and election spending. Management noted that consumers cut back on discretionary spend (e.g., chips, instant noodles, etc.) and focused on essentials such as canned goods.

New payment system switch completed; store network expansion on track

The switch to the new payment system was said to have been completed mid-July, enabling better reliability of e-wallet payments and cash-ins. The company eyes further improvements with the implementation of card payment transactions in 1,000 stores by 4Q. SEVN is still aiming to open 500 new stores this year, with about 70% located in Visayas and Mindanao, where sales growth has been outpacing that of Metro Manila and Luzon. In 1H, the company has opened 218 stores so far, ending the period with 4,268 stores, 9% higher vs 2Q25.

Rough start to 3Q but optimistic for the rest of 2025

Management noted that July was a tough month due to strong monsoon rains causing flooding around Luzon including Metro Manila, while August at the very least was said to see some recovery. The company is optimistic for an improved 2H, but one should recall that the YoY comparisons will look flattering simply because of a more depressed base during 2H24. Improvement in store economics, especially SSS, will depend on execution (SKU assortment, new product and service offerings, store improvements) as well as a rebound in overall consumer spend.

 

 

TRANSPORT
Container volumes in Mexico drop in July amid customs delays
  • Data from the Mexican port authority showed that container volumes across Mexico’s ports fell 5% YoY in July, after growing 3% in 1H25
    • Based on a report (here), cargo dwell time has worsened across Mexico’s international ports due to longer customs processes and other bottlenecks in port operations
  • Recall that last week, we reported that ICTSI’s port in Mexico (CMSA) saw volumes drop 12% YoY in July, extending the 14% decline in June and reversing the +10% growth rate in 5M25
    • Management noted that the onboarding of new customs officials (previous officials were replaced after strike in May) has prolonged processing times at the port of Manzanillo
    • We anticipate the volume weakness was likely mitigated by higher storage revenues

 

PROPERTY
Ayala Land appoints new CFO
  • Ayala Land appointed Mr. Jose Eduardo A. Quimpo II as Chief Finance Officer (CFO) and Treasurer, in addition to his roles as Head of Corporate Finance and Chief Risk Officer
    • Mr. Quimpo joined Ayala Land in May 2024 and served as President of AREIT from August 2024 until last week
    • Prior to joining Ayala Land, he had been in various roles in corporate finance, capital markets, and risk management in Philippine banks for over 20 years 
  • He will replace Mr. Augusto D. Bengzon, who has been with Ayala Land for 21 years and served as CFO for 8 years
    • Mr. Bengzon will remain a member of Ayala Land’s management committee and a director of various subsidiaries, including AyalaLand Logistics Holdings Corp (ALLHC PM) and Avaland Berhad (AVALAND MK)
  • ALI is trading at 51% discount to NAV, 1.2X FY25E P/B, and 12.4X FY25E P/E  
MACRO
Finance Secretary says no new tax proposals in 20th Congress
  • Finance Secretary Ralph Recto stated that the gov't will not introduce new tax proposals in the 20th Congress
    • He also ruled out increases in existing tax rates, emphasizing that the Dept. of Finance will not back additional tax initiatives
  • Recto said that they will only push for the previously filed measures such as the single-use plastics excise tax and tax amnesty program
    • The new single-use plastics excise tax is projected to raise up to P34bn
    • The tax amnesty program proposes to implement a 2% amnesty tax dependent on the total assets of taxpayers up to 2024
  • However, Recto noted that if the lawmakers would newly propose and pass a "wealth tax" into law, he would support it
  • The gov't debt-to-GDP ratio stands at 63.1% as of end-June
    • This is up from 62% the prior quarter and 60.7% at end-2024
  • From 2025 to 2028, tax revenues are expected to rise by an average of 9.4% per year, bringing total collections close to P6trn by the end of the President’s term
