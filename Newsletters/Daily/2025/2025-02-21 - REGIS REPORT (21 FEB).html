REGIS REPORT

21 February 2025

Despite strong sales of some high-end projects in February, Ayala Land (ALI PM) remains cautious about the residential segment in 2025. It plans only P80bn of residential projects launches this year, lower than the FY24 presales figure (P127bn), suggesting that it intends to reduce unsold inventory levels. On the other hand, management has a more optimistic outlook on the commercial lot segment, as it intends to launch P20bn of such lots this year, higher than the P15bn of sales in FY24.

 

The residential market environment still looks challenging, but Ayala Land appears to be performing well on a relative basis, with FY24 presales growing 12% and commercial lot presales climbing 52%.

+++

 

The 4Q results of Metrobank (MBT PM) don’t look as strong as strong as they appeared at first glance. Although 4Q net profit rose 19% YoY (+2% QoQ), this was flattered by a tax reversal during the quarter. 4Q24 pre-provisioning operating profit actually fell 11% YoY (and 27% QoQ) due to the increase in operating expenses.

 

 

+++

 

Unit sales for vehicle brands under CAMPI saw 10% YoY growth in January. Of the units sold (37.6k) in the month, 1.6k were electrified vehicles (~4%). Note the CAMPI figures don’t include BYD, which should be a meaningful presence in the market by this year.  Unit sales for Toyota were 18.1k, +12% YoY.

 

+++

 

The country’s balance of payments (BOP) deficit ballooned to US$4.1bn for the month of January alone, the largest in over a decade. It’s unclear what led to such a large deficit, which is likely to have contributed to a big factor in the US$3bn drop in international reserves (now at US$103bn).

 

+++

 

Media reports say that the 600MW wind project in Laos where ACEN (ACEN PM) has a 24% stake is expected to begin operating in July. If things go according to timetable, ACEN will add 1,230MW of attributable RE capacity this year (vs 3,051MW today).

CONGLOS
Vehicle sales rise 10.4% YoY in January; Toyota sales up 12% YoY
  • Industry vehicle sales grew by 10.4% YoY to 37,604 units (-10.6% MoM)
    • According to the Chamber of Automotive Manufacturers (CAMPI) and Truck Manufacturers Assoc. (TMA)
    • CAMPI is targeting sales of 500k units for 2025 (+7% YoY)
  • A total of 1,600 EVs were sold in January, of which 1,445 units were hybrid EVs, 146s were battery EVs, and nine were plug-in hybrid EVs
    • Note that BYD is not a member of CAMPI/TMA
  • Toyota Motor Philippines’ (TMP) retail sales rose by 12.3% YoY to 18,078 units (-10.8% MoM)
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 32% of NAV (25% of gross asset value) and 26% of profit before parent expenses
    • GTCAP is trading at a 58% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,248/sh and 5x Regis’ FY25E PER (consensus: 3.7x FY25E PER)

MACRO
BOP deficit at US$4.1bn in January 2025; GIR drops to US$103bn
  • The country's overall balance of payments (BOP) for the month posted a deficit of US$4.1bn
    • This is higher than Dec 2024's $1.51bn deficit and the $740mn deficit in Jan 2024
      • It was also the widest monthly BOP deficit in 11 years
    • Primarily reflected the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas' (BSP) net foreign exchange operations and drawdown on the national government's deposits
  • Meanwhile, the final GIR level for end-Jan 2025 stood at US$103.3bn, down from the US$106.3bn in end-Dec 2024
    • GIR level is reflective of an external liquidity buffer equivalent to 7.3 months’ worth of imports of goods and payments of services and primary income

POWER
ACEN to fully energize 600 MW wind project in Laos by July
  • ACEN is set to fully energize its 600MW Monsoon Wind power project in Laos as early as July
    • ACEN holds a 24% economic stake in the project, which has an estimated investment cost of around US$1bn
    • Laos’ Vientiane Times reported that the wind project is set to transmit an initial 300 MW of power to Vietnam in 2Q25
  • If things go as planned, ACEN could be adding 1,230 MW of attributable RE capacity this year
    • Pangasinan Solar (100 MW by 4Q24)
    • Palauig 2 Solar (300MW by 2H25)
    • Stubbo Solar (520MW by 2H25)
    • Monsoon Wind (600 MW by 2H25)
    • Maharashtra Hybrid Solar-Wind (153MW by 3Q25)
    • Stockyard Wind (129 MW by 1Q25)
  • As of Oct 31, 2025, ACEN’s total attributable RE capacity stood at 6,754 MW
    • Its operating RE capacity stood at 3,051 MW, of which 1,067MW is in the Philippines
    • It is currently constructing 2,302 MW, of which 1,330MW is in the Philippines
    • Another 1,401 MW of new capacity is “committed”
