REGIS REPORT

01 December 2025

The country’s trade deficit narrowed 34% to US$3.8bn for the month of Oct, bringing the YT-Oct deficit to US$41bn (-9% YoY). What’s been interesting in recent months has been the uncharacteristic strength in exports (+14% YT-Oct), which is dominated by electronics exports.

 

+++

 

Digiplus (PLUS PM) is supposed to be in a legal dispute with the firm of former congressman Elizaldy Co over the sale of PLUS’ stake in the Midas Hotel. PLUS is seeking P2.5bn for its stake to be sold to other firm, but there is a dispute over the payment amount.

 

+++

 

Simultaneous indignation rallies were held across many locations calling for action against government corruption. Estimates of crowd size (~50,000 in one location) were much lower than organizers hoped, and there no reported issues of violence. Administration officials likely breathed sighs of relief.

 

+++

 

With market trading data for Nov already in, it is looking even more likely that RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM) will become a constituent of the benchmark PSE Index (PSEi aka PCOMP).  If RCR does indeed become part of the PSEi at the next scheduled rebalance date (Feb 2026), it would come three years after its parent firm Robinsons Land (RLC PM) was removed from the same index.

 

Based on our estimates as well, the component RCR will most likely replace would be Alliance Global (AGI PM).

MACRO
Trade deficit narrows to $3.8bn in Oct
  • The Oct trade deficit narrowed to US$3.8bn, contracting 34% YoY, reflecting the decline in imports the robust growth of exports
    • This brings the 10M25 trade deficit to US$41.3bn, 9% narrower YoY
  • Imports for the month fell by 7% YoY to US$11.2bn, a reversal from the 2% increase in Sep
    • Primarily driven by the significant decrease in consumer goods (-12%) and fuel imports (-20%)
  • Exports climbed by 19% YoY to US$7.4bn, the tenth consecutive month of growth and accelerating from the 16% increase in Sep
    • Electronics exports surged by 44% YoY to $4.2bn
      • These made up the bulk of exports at 56% of the total
    • Meanwhile, non-electronics exports were down 3% to US$3.2bn

 

 

 

 

 

 

GAMING
DigiPlus still in legal dispute with former congressman's firm
  • According to the Philippine Daily Inquirer, DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) remains in a legal dispute with a firm owned by former Congressman Elizaldy “Zaldy” Co
  • DigiPlus owns 51% of Hotel Enterprises of the Philippines, which owns Midas Hotel
    • The JV partner is Eco Leisure and Hospitality Holdings, which is reportedly owned by Zaldy Co
  • DigiPlus was in talks to sell its stake to a third party for P2.5bn but Eco Leisure invoked its right of first refusal
  • However, Eco Leisure was only willing to pay P1bn
  • The two parties went to court and DigiPlus won the case
  • Eco Leisure then filed an appeal before the RTC Pasig in 2022
  • We expect only minor impact to DigiPlus from this legal dispute
POLITICS
Second edition of anti-corruption rallies held in Quezon City and Manila
  • The second rally of the “Trillion Peso March” at the People Power Monument in Quezon City, along with the “Baha sa Luneta 2.0” protest at Luneta Park in Manila, was held on November 30, with both demonstrations calling for an end to corruption and greater accountability
  • It was reported that a small portion of left-leaning groups called for the resignation of President Marcos and impeachment of VP Sara Duterte given the allegations of both being involved in corruption
    • A "Manifesto of Unwavering Support and Solidarity" from retired and active generals of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, Philippine National Police, and other enforcement agencies was released
      • The statement iterated their stance "to condemn and reject any call for the AFP to engage in unconstitutional acts or military adventurism"
  • The Quezon City Police District reported no violent or chaotic incidents throughout the event as rallyists dispersed peacefully by end of day
  • The crowd estimates at Quezon City ranged from 33,000-55,000 protestors throughout the day while the crowd at Manila peaked at around 5,000 people
    • This is significantly lower than the 100,000 estimated participants in the first anti-corruption rally held last Sep 21
MARKET
RCR likely to be added to PSE Index during February 2026 rebalancing
  • Based on our estimates, RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM) will likely be added to the PSE Index during the February 2026 rebalancing
    • If this will be the case, Alliance Global (AGI PM) will likely be removed from the index
  • The PSE Index rebalancing in Feb 2026 will be based on the 12 months from Jan 2025 to Dec 2025
  • Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap
  • Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when
    • 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or
    • 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35 
  • Based on our estimates, RCR is ranked within the top 25
    • If this will remain the case by the end of 2025, then the lowest ranked PSE Index constituent (which is currently AGI) will be removed

CAMPI lowers 2025 sales target to 486k units (+4%)
  • The Chamber of Automotive Manufacturers of the Philippines Inc. (CAMPI) reduced its 2025 sales target to 486k units (+4%) from a previous target of 500k units (+7%)
    • According to CAMPI President Rommel Gutierrez
    • Even though CAMPI’s 10M25 sales only reached 383k units (-0.2% YoY), Mr. Gutierrez anticipates the target remains achievable once all manufacturers submit results by year-end
  • We reckon CAMPI can achieve its target only with the inclusion of BYD’s sales in the count
    • We estimate BYD accounted for ~5% of 9M25 industry sales
POWER
MGEN, Saudi-based ACWA Power to develop 125MW solar farm in Iloilo
  • Meralco Powergen (MGEN) and Saudi Arabia-based ACWA Power are moving forward with its first joint venture project, a 125MW solar plant in Concepcion, Iloilo
    • Project will rise on a 120-hectare site and MGEN is considering joining the government’s green energy auction to secure offtake
    • JV ownership structure was not disclosed
  • MGEN currently has 5,068MW (2,558 MW attributable) of operational generation assets and 6,327 MW (2,460 MW attributable) projects under construction
PROPERTY
SEC approves increase in MREIT’s authorized capital stock
  • The SEC approved the increase in MREIT’s authorized capital stock from P5bn to P8bn (at P1/share)
  • MREIT previously announced that it would acquire 200,000sqm sqm of office buildings from Megaworld in a share-for-property swap
    • 165,000sqm in McKinley Hill, Fort Bonifacio
    • 35,000sqm in Eastwood, Quezon City
    • This will expand MREIT’s portfolio by 41% to 680,000sqm
  • MREIT will issue up to 1.36bn new shares to Megaworld at a minimum price of P14.39/share
    • This implies a value of c. P19.6bn
    • The issuance will expand MREIT’s shares outstanding by 37%
    • The minimum price is 3% higher than the last closing price
  • If MREIT will issue 1.36bn new MREIT shares, then we estimate that the AGI Group would need to sell c. 100mn MREIT shares in order for MREIT to meet minimum public ownership requirements
  • MEG currently offers 4.5% dividend yield and is trading at 69% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY26E P/B, and 3.2X FY26E P/E
  • MREIT currently offers 7.6% FY26E dividend yield (based on consensus forecast)
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 5.9%
MACRO
BSP expects November inflation to settle within 1.1-1.9%
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) in its month-ahead inflation forecast expects Nov inflation to fall within 1.1-1.9%
    • BSP expectations for Nov inflation are below the lower-end of the BSP's 2-4% target range
    • Consensus expects Nov inflation to be 1.7%
  • Upward price pressures are higher prices of rice, fish, electricity as well as the depreciation of the peso
    • The peso averaged P58.9 per US dollar in Nov vs P58.3 in Oct
    • These may have been partially offset by lower prices of meat and vegetables
  • Inflation has averaged 1.7% so far for 10M25, while inflation in Oct alone was 1.7%
  • For 2025, the BSP expects 2025 inflation to be 1.7%
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to average 1.7% and 1.8%, respectively
  • Nov inflation data will be released by the PSA (Philippine Statistics Authority) on Dec 5 (Fri)
