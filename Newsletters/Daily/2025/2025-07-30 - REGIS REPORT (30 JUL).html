REGIS REPORT

30 July 2025

Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM) is first off the blocks for the consumer names this 2Q reporting season. The retailer reported 1H25 sales and EBIT rising 5% to P98.5bn and P4.3bn, respectively. There was a modest improvement in same-store sales, as SSSG rose to 4.8% for 2Q alone.

 

While better, the top line for the Food segment was still uninspiring. Revenues were up 4% for the quarter as SSSG reached 4.2%

 

More details below.

 

+++

 

Revenue from (legal) online gambling did indeed grow 23% QoQ to P63bn (US$1.1bn), which would put Philippine online gambling revenue on track to be the highest in the world. The very visible impact of this “industry” is already attracting plenty of attention from politicians. While President Marcos made no mention of online gambling in his major policy speech this week, we still expect significant measures to limit or restrict online gambling access soon.

 

+++

 

The Dept of Agriculture (DA) will seek to raise the tariff on rice on the next review in order to help local farmers. The government cut import tariffs to 15% from 35% last year in order to drive prices down. With regional rice prices down by ~40%, the DA seems to think the lower tariffs are no longer as necessary.

CONSUMER
RRHI 2Q25: SSSG improves
  • 1H25 sales and EBIT rise 5%. Robinsons Retail’s (RRHI) reported 1H25 headline net profit of P2.3bn, down 67% YoY with the absence of a P4.9bn one-time gain from the BPI-RBC merger in 1Q24. For 1H25, sales and EBIT rose 5% YoY to P98.5bn and P4.3bn, respectively, a tad short of expectations. For 2Q25 alone, net sales increased by 6% to P50.7bn while EBIT was up 7% to P2.4bn. Group SSSG for 2Q25 improved to +4.8% from +3.0% the previous quarter. The SSSG improvement was visible across each of the segments except specialty stores.
  • Food segment revenues up +4.0%. Food segment revenues were up 4.0% in 2Q25 to P30.1bn on SSSG of +4.2%, as basket sizes expanded. Within the food segment, convenience stores saw its 4th consecutive quarterly contraction in SSS (-3.5% in 2Q25). Drug stores saw 12% revenue growth with strong SSSG (+8.2%) and a boost from new store openings. The Food and Drugstore segments comprise 78% of revenue in 2Q25.
  • Department store revenue grew 12.9%, with SSSG (+8.1%) lifted up by changes in the school calendar. The DIY business, meanwhile, continues to languish with sales virtually unchanged YoY.
  • O!Save still growing briskly. O!Save sales in 2Q25 surged ~140% YoY to US$118mn. Store expansion continues to accelerate, and the firm ended the period with 560 stores, implying another 89 stores were opened in 2Q25. It aims to reach 800 stores by year-end. O!Save management commented that its results are tracking better than their expectations with 1H25 EBITDA margin at -0.23%.
  • Treasury share retirement excludes Dairy Farm shares. RRHI announced that it is retiring 158mn shares held in treasury, all of which were previously repurchased under the company’s buyback program. The share retirement does not include the 315mn shares bought back from the Dairy Farm group at P50/sh (P15.7bn). We note that the transaction will be accretive to EPS despite an increase in finance costs due to the reduction in share count. For more details on the DFI buyback see our note here.

GAMING
GGR of e-games grows 23% QoQ in 2Q25
  • PAGCOR reported that GGR of e-games (including bingo) hit P114.83bn in 1H25 (up 83% YoY)
  • This implies that 2Q25 GGR of e-games rose 23% QoQ and 78% YoY to P63.4bn

 

 

GAMING
Additional regulations on online gambling being discussed
  • Finance Secretary Ralph Recto said that President Marcos was concerned about online gambling
  • There are discussions about more regulations as well as the government possibly collecting more revenue
  • Secretary Recto said that fees on revenues could be raised by another 10ppt on revenue (from the current 38%) but they were still studying this
  • He noted that if fees were too high, illegal platforms might proliferate
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) is the market leader in e-games, with a market share of about 45%
  • PLUS is trading at 9.0x FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
MACRO
Dept. of Agriculture to push for rice tariff hike before harvest season
  • The Department of Agriculture (DA) will push the raise to current 15% tariff on imported rice during the periodic review of its implementation today
    • This marks the third review since the tariff was slashed from 35% to 15% back in July 2024
  • DA Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. stated that a tariff increase is timely to lift palay (unhusked rice) farmgate prices with harvest season in Sept
    • He did not guide for a specific increase but emphasized that it will not raise prices much given currently low international rice prices
  • Rice prices are estimated to increase between P1-1.5/kg if tariffs are raised to 25%, and increase around P2-3/kg if reverted back to 35%

 

 

BANKS
SECB CFO to retire 15 Sept
  • In a disclosure, Security Bank Corp. (SECB PM) announced the retirement of CFO Edu Olbes effective 15 Sept 2025
  • He will be replaced by Allen Alexander Reyes, who is the current CFO of SingLife Philippines, prior to which he was with Diageo
  • Mr Olbes will be Special Adviser to the President and CEO from 11 August, and will stay on to aid in the transition of the CFO role to Mr Reyes
  • Olbes has been with SECB since mid-2010, and was seen as a potential successor to Mr Sanjiv Vohra as the bank’s CEO

Metro Pacific group weighing options should San Miguel decide to sell its 3.9% in Meralco
  • Manuel V. Pangilinan revealed that his group is weighing options should San Miguel decide to sell its 3.9% stake in Meralco
