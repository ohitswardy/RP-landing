REGIS REPORT

16 January 2025

Asset quality improved nearly across the board in November, more so when we exclude inter-bank loans (IBL) and reverse repurchases (RRPs). NPL ratio ex-IBL/RRP fell 8bps MoM to 3.73% (vs the 6bps drop as reported) . More importantly, the nominal PHP value for NPLs were down 0.7% MoM to P520bn. Past Due ratio (PD) fell 11bps MoM to 4.55% (vs 8bps as reported), with the PHP value down 0.8% MoM to P636bn. Restructured Loans (RLs) improved 3bps to 2.10% (vs 1bp as reported), though the PHP value inched up 0.3% MoM.

Loan growth (ex-IBL/RRP) accelerated to 11.8% YoY, the fastest rate since December 2022. On a MoM basis, loans were up 1.5%, helping the ratios improve too.

+++

The enactment of a new law promoting domestic natural gas will help the prospects of those who produce and use it, and there’s really only one big source of domestic natgas (Malampaya) and only one big user (First Gen (FGEN PM)).

Among other things, this new law (1) provides exemption from VAT (12%) for domestic natgas and (2) mandates the government to provide priority to domestic natgas over other conventional power sources. The former should result in an immediate cost reduction for First Gen, allowing it to become more cost-competitive ahead of the expiry of its single biggest power supply agreement this Aug (1,000MW for Sta Rita).  How the latter will be implemented, however, will be left to the bureaucrats of the Dept of Energy (DOE) and we would withhold judgement until it becomes clearer where the bureaucrats are heading.

Apart from that, in the power sector, there’s also news that a solar power project involving Meralco (MER PM) just reached financial close.  The Bugallon project (550MW solar) in northern Luzon is one of many projects that the DOE is assuming to deliver over 3,000MW of incremental capacity to Luzon this year.  It doesn’t look like this one will be available for the 2025 peak season.

+++

Overseas remittances rose 3.3% YoY in Nov to US$2.81bn.  For the period YT-Nov, remittances reached US$31.1bn, up +3%.  The latest numbers have stayed consistent with longer-term trends as well as market expectations.

MACRO
Remittances rise 3.3% YoY in November
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks grew 3.3% YoY to US$2.81bn but dropped 8.8% MoM—marking the lowest monthly inflow in the past six months
    • The acceleration in land-based remittances from Saudi Arabia which was up 26.2% YoY and 2.0% MoM was offset by the slower remittances growth from the USA only up 0.6% YoY and down 18.6% MoM
    • Sea-based remittances growth picked up to 1.0% from 0.6% in Oct
    • In PHP terms, remittances grew by 8.5%, marking the second-fastest increase of the year and surpassing the 3.6% growth recorded in Oct
  • Bringing YT-November remittances up 3.0% YoY to US$31.1bn
    • Broadly in-line with the BSP's target growth rate of +3.0% for the year

BANKS
Banking sector NPL ratio falls in November
  • The NPL ratio of the banking sector fell to 3.54% in November, from 3.6% in October
    • It stood at 3.41% in November 2023
  • Nonperforming loans fell 0.7% MoM to P520.55bn in November (from 524.31bn in October)
    • NPL rose 14.6% YoY (from P454.28bn)
  • The total loan portfolio rose 1.2% MoM to P14.72trn (from P14.55trn in October)
    • It grew 10.4% YoY (from P13.34trn in November 2023)
  • Past due loans declined 0.8% YoY to P635.6bn November (from P640.9bn in October)
    • It increased 12.8% YoY (from P563.4bn in November 2023)
  • As a result, the past due ratio fell to 4.32% in November, from 4.4% in October
    • It stood at 4.22% in November 2023
  • Restructured loans rose 0.3% MoM to P293.7bn
    • However, this represents a 4% decline YoY
  • NPL coverage rose to 93.2% in November, from 92.3% in October
    • However, this is lower than the 101.5% figure in November 2023
POWER
President Marcos signs natural gas development bill into law
  • President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. has signed into law Republic Act 12120 or Philippine Natural Gas Industry Development Act on Jan. 8
  • Law seeks to establish the Philippine Downstream Natural Gas Industry (PDNGI) and increase the share of natural gas in the country’s energy mix; Salient provisions include:
    • 12% VAT exemption on the sale and purchase of indigenous natural gas, aggregated gas, and electricity from gas-fired power generation facilities
    • Certified PDNGI facilities will be entitled to fiscal incentives under CREATE (e.g. income tax holiday)
    • Power produced from indigenous natural gas shall have priority over other conventional energy sources
  • The law will become effective once the Department of Energy issues its implementing rules and regulations (IRR) within six months

Vena Energy, MGreen finalize funding for solar project

Maynilad prepares for US$1bn IPO
  • Maynilad Water aims to launch a US$1bn IPO within the year or latest by 2026 to meet the regulatory requirement of listing by January 2027
    • Maynilad plans to file for an IPO in Q1 2025 and target launch dates in April or July
    • According to Maynilad Chairman Manuel V. Pangilinan
  • Under its franchise, Maynilad is required to list at least 30% of its outstanding capital stock within five years from the grant of its franchise in Jan 2022
  • Maynilad plans to fund its 2025 capex of P40bn through additional debt and internal funds
  • Maynilad is a 25%-owned affiliate of DMCI (DMC PM) and accounts for 23% of its NAV; DMC trades at a 19% discount to its market-based NAV of P13.41/sh
    • Other shareholders of Maynilad include Metro Pacific (53%) and Marubeni (20%)
