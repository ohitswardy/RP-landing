REGIS REPORT

17 October 2025

Century Pacific (CNPF PM) is set to acquire and upgrade a coconut processing facility in Mindanao, its third one. CNPF will spend US$45m for the facility, which will augment its capacity in a business that’s been growing rapidly in recent periods. Coconut products account for ~10% of revenue, inclusive of the OEM export sales of coconut water.

 

+++

 

The government is banning imports of sugar until middle of next year in response to what’s happening to domestic sugar prices. The Dept of Agriculture (DA) has done the same for rice imports for essentially the same reason (low domestic prices which actually remain significantly higher than international prices).

 

 

The local sugar industry is highly regulated, and the government’s import policy is a main driver of profitability for local sugar firms (millers included). Only a select few are given import licenses and the DA determines who gets them. The lucky ones can import sugar at international prices (which are low) and sell them at artificially high domestic prices. 

 

Around 17% of the EBIT before corporate overhead of Universal Robina (URC PM) was from the sugar and renewables (SURE) segment.

 

+++

 

The government plans to expand its e-voucher program as part of its plan to reduce hunger. The Reducing Food Insecurity and Undernutrition with Electronic Vouchers (REFUEL) project will cost P74bn (US$1.3bn).

CONSUMER
CNPF acquires Mindanao coconut process facility for US$45mn
  • Century Pacific Food Inc. (CNPF PM) with an investment of US$45mn or P2.6bn is set to acquire and upgrade a coconut processing facility in South Cotabato, Mindanao from Roxas and Company, Inc.'s (RCI PM) agriventure subsidiary
  • The plant is CNPF's third coconut hub alongside its other Mindanao facilities in General Santos and Misamis Occidental
  • The new acquisitions aim to expand product lines to boost production for domestic and export markets
  • For the coconut OEM business, it contributed 6% of total revenue in FY24
  • Coco Mama, CNPF's fresh coconut cream product is part of Milk & Others, which contributed 29% of total revenue in FY24
CONSUMER
Gov't to impose sugar import ban until 2Q26
  • The Dept. of Agriculture (DA) Secretary Fransico Tiu Laurel Jr. stated that the gov't will not import sugar until mid-2026 given the low prices exhibited in the first sugar auction in Negros earlier this month
    • Future imports were said to only be classified as reserve sugar and will not be released into the domestic market
  • Sugar output according to the DA has hit 2.01 MMT, surpassing the 1.92 MMT in the previous crop year
    • This is the highest in four years, on track to approach the 2.14 MMT in 2020-2021
  • Note that in FY24, URC's net sales and operating income totaled P162bn and P20bn, respectively—with the SURE (Sugar and Renewables) segment contributing 18% of total revenues and 20% of EBIT
MACRO
Marcos expands e-voucher use to end hunger in the PH by 2027
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. stated that he will increase the use of electronic food vouchers in part of his plan to eradicate hunger among the poorest families in the country by 2027
  • The Reducing Food Insecurity and Undernutrition with Electronic Vouchers (REFUEL) project was recently launched in Manila City where each recipient will get P3,000 in monthly electronic food credits through "benefit transfer cards" which are usable at accredited partner retailers
    • The gov't plans to link electronic benefit transfer cards to mobile payment platforms like GCash to enhance transaction efficiency and minimize administrative expenses
  • Program participants are required to participate in productivity enhancement sessions which eye to prepare them for livelihood opportunities
  • The P74-billion REFUEL program is supported by the Asian Development Bank, Agence Française de Développement and the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC) Fund
PROPERTY
Ayala Land to redevelop more malls
  • Ayala Malls COO Paul Birkett said that ALI would redevelop an additional five malls over the next five years
  • In 2024, Ayala Land announced that it would spend P13bn to redevelop its four flagship malls – Glorietta, Greenbelt, TriNoma, and Ayala Center Cebu
    • Mr. Birkett said that the redevelopment of Glorietta, TriNoma, and Ayala Center Cebu will be completed this year
  • Earlier this year, the company said it would spend P4.5bn to redevelop Ayala Malls Abreeza, Ayala Malls Cloverleaf, Ayala Malls Fairview Terraces, and MarQuee Mall
  • ALI is trading at 58% discount to NAV, 1.0X FY26E P/B, and 10.8X FY26E P/E
MACRO
Finance Secretary expects faster GDP growth in next 3 years
  • The Dept. of Finance Secretary Ralph Recto expects GDP growth to pick up in the next three years to an average of 5.9%
    • This is below the gov't's target growth rate 6-7% for 2026-2028
    • 1H25 and FY24 GDP growth was at 5.4% and 5.7%, respectively
  • Recto highlighted that the acceleration in growth would be driven by "the gov't addressing the leakages" exposed by the recent flood control scandal through "stronger institutions and better governance"
    • He also emphasized other growth drivers such as low inflation, easing policy rates, strong consumer spending, and a robust labor market
  • The Finance Secretary also guided a debt-to-GDP ratio of 58% by 2030
