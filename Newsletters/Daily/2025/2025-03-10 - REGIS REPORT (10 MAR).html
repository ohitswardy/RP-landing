REGIS REPORT

10 March 2025

We have a few mid-cap names reporting results on Friday.  We note the results of Robinsons Land (RLC PM) in particular, as being symptomatic of what the property market is going through right now.

 

  • Robinsons Land (RLC PM) (-) – FY24 operating profit rose 2% to P17.6bn, behind REGIS and consensus forecasts. With the exception of the standalone residential business, which saw a 90% drop in operating profit, the operating profit of all segments outperformed our expectations. The residential segment continued to struggle in 4Q24: 1) the operating loss of the standalone residential business widened to P434mn, 2) presales of JV projects fell 33% QoQ and 68% YoY, 3) the unsold inventory of standalone projects represents c. 7X the full-year presales in FY24. Meanwhile, FY24 operating profit of the mall business grew 22% as revenue rose 11% and EBIT margin expanded 380 basis points, thanks in part to lower power costs.
    • For international investors>>Robinsons Land - Residential segment still struggling in 4Q24
    • For Philippine-based investors>>Robinsons Land - Residential segment still struggling in 4Q24

 

  • Aboitiz Power (AP PM) (-) – Core income grew 5% to P33.7bn as attributable EBITDA rose 13% to P73bn. Depreciation and financing charges from its GNPD began to be recognized in FY24.  Unit sales for generation/RES were flat YoY while volumes for its distribution utility (DU) business expanded ~8%. The company will hold its briefing on Friday.

 

Meanwhile, gaming firm Digiplus (PLUS PM) saw profits trebling to P12.6bn in FY24 as revenue jumped 176%. We note 4Q revenue was still up 23% sequentially to PHP23.6bn.

Meanwhile, there are a few more, new, data points regarding Philippine monetary conditions.

 

The BSP released the latest (January) data on loans at commercial banks.  Loans there grew 12.8% in January. M3 growth, however, remains a relatively anemic 6.8%.

 

In addition, the country’s international reserves rose US$3.4bn to US$106.7bn in Feb, following the two-tranche bond issue last month.  However, expect the reserves to come down over the next two months as two bond maturities come due.

POWER
Aboitiz Power FY24 core income up +5%
  • Core profits up 5% to P33.7bn with attributable EBITDA growing 13% to P73bn
    • Profit grew less than EBITDA as AP started to recognize depreciation and interest cost of GNPD
  • Headline income was up just 2% to P33.9bn
  • Energy sales for the generation business was flat YoY
  • For the distribution utility, residential volumes grew 13% while commercial/industrial volumes were up 5%
    • We estimate overall volumes rose +8%
  • AP will hold its briefing on March 14

 

 

 

GAMING
DigiPlus profit triples in FY24
  • FY24 net profit of DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) grew 208% to P12.6bn, ahead of consensus forecast
    • 4Q24 net profit rose 10% QoQ and 95% YoY to P3.9bn
  • FY24 revenue grew 176% YoY to P75.2bn – implying 45%-50% market share for the full-year
    • 4Q24 revenue rose 23% QoQ and 110% YoY to P23.6bn – implying 40%-45% market share in 4Q
  • PAGCOR fees on e-casino games, which comprise majority of PLUS’ revenue, fell from 41.25% in August 2023 to 35% in April 2024
    • They dropped further to 30% in January 2025 – this will likely cause online operators to increase spending for marketing, driving up revenue further
  • DigiPlus introduced several new games on its platform in 2024, including Pinoy Drop Ball in September 2024

 

BANKS
Commercial banks’ loan growth accelerates to 12.8% in January; M3 growth decelerates to 6.8%
  • Commercial bank loans rose by 12.8% YoY in January, mildly accelerating from 12.2% in December
    • Loans to productive activities rose by 11.8% (from 10.8%), led by utilities (+24%), trade (+14%), real estate (+10%), and transport/storage (+21%)
    • Consumer loans rose by 24% YoY, mildly decelerating from +25%
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) grew by 6.8% YoY, decelerating from the 7.7% pace in December

 

 

 

 

MACRO
PH Dollar reserves up 3.3% at end-February
  • The Philippines' gross international reserves (GIR) at end-Feb saw a 3.3% MoM increase to $106.7bn from $103.3bn at end-Jan
    • Primarily reflecting the BSP's net foreign exchange operations and proceeds from its recent global bond sale worth $3.3bn
    • The level of dollar reserves by end-Feb was enough to cover about 3.8x the country's short-term external debt and is equivalent to 7.5x months' worth of imports of goods and payments
  • Reserve position in the IMF decreased 0.2% MoM to $670mn
  • Buffers in the form of gold were valued at $12.1bn, which was up 2.5% MoM
  • Foreign investments increased by 3.5% MoM to $89.4bn
  • Foreign currency deposits went up 5.1% MoM to $787mn
  • Note that the BSP expects the country's GIR to be at $110bn by end-2025
