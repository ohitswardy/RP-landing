05 August 2025

We cover 2Q/1H results from two names this morning – SM Prime (SMPH PM) and Semirara (SCC PM).

 

  • SM Prime (SMPH PM) (+) – 1H25 net profit grew 11% to P24.5bn, ahead of REGIS and consensus forecasts, thanks to margin improvements from the company’s cost control efforts. Philippine mall operations remained healthy, with 2Q25 revenue growing 8% and same-mall revenue growth hitting c. 6%. More than the reported financial results, however, what we found more noteworthy was the sequential improvement in reservation sales. 2Q25 reservation sales grew 23% QoQ to P14.3bn, on stronger sales of provincial projects as well ready-for-occupancy units (RFO) in Metro Manila. Furthermore, sales generally remained strong in July.

  • Semirara (SCC PM) – Profits for 2Q alone dropped 34% to P4.0bn, bring 1H income to P8.3bn, also down 34%. It appears coal sales are unlikely to meet our estimates for the year even as electricity selling prices drop due to softer demand and lower energy prices.  We thus downgrade our profit forecasts for SCC by 20-30% and cut our TP by 5% to P21/sh, after the disappointing 1H25 results and management’s guidance. We maintain our Hold rating, as SCC’s capital returns remain attractive at our projected 2H25E DPS of P2/sh (~6% yield) and FY26E DPS of P4.50/sh (~14%).

The Dept of Agriculture (DA) submitted its recommendation to the President for an increase in the current rice import tariff (=15%) as well as a temporary freeze in rice imports in a bid to help domestic rice farmers.

 

The fall in rice prices over the past year has not only helped consumers, it has also been a key factor in the Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) monetary easing path because it has dragged down CPI. The flipside of the rice price decline is that local farmers face lower incomes.

 

+++

 

Speaking of CPI, the statistics bureau announced that CPI inflation for July was 0.9%. This is the lowest CPI inflation (YoY) print since Oct 2019.  Rice prices were down 15.9% YoY. Core inflation for the month was 2.3%, a slight increase from the 2.2% in June.

 

With this CPI print, the likelihood of a policy rate reduction this month (Aug 28) has now increased further.

Dept. of Agri proposes to reinstate 35% imported rice tariff, suspend rice importation
  • The Department of Agriculture (DA) has recommended to President Marcos for an increase in imported rice tariffs and a temporary freeze on rice imports to protect local farmers given declining farmgate prices
    • The average farmgate price of palay (unhusked rice) as of June 2025 is at P17/kg, vs. P23.2/kg back in Jan 2025, while production costs remain around P13.4/kg
  • DA Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. recommended a gradual increase in rice import duties back to the original 35% from the current 15%
    • Recall that Executive Order (EO) No. 62 was signed by President Marcos in June last year reducing imported rice tariffs to 15% until 2028, subject to a periodic review every four months
    • Either Congress must act swiftly on the proposal to restore the rice tariff, or the President can raise tariffs through an Executive Order once Congress goes on recess in October
  • For 2025, rice imports are projected to amount to over the record high 4.7mn MT imports this year given rising demand
    • 2.3mn MT of rice has been imported as of 1H25
    • 2025 rice demand is estimated to be at  17.3mn MT (+4% YoY)
  • The DA's proposal will still be discussed and finalized with the President over the next few days
