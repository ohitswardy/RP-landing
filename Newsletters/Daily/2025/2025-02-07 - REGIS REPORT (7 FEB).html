FY24 core net income of Globe Telecom (GLO PM) rose 13% to P21.5bn, behind REGIS forecast but in line with consensus. While EBITDA of P86.8bn (+7% YoY) was in line with both REGIS and consensus forecast, non-cash costs and profits from subsidiary Mynt, which holds GCash, disappointed.

 

To our surprise, Globe’s share of Mynt’s profit fell 74% QoQ and 55% YoY in the fourth quarter. Given the magnitude of the drop, we suspect there were non-recurring items involved.

RRHI: 4Q24 SSSG rebounds

FY24 recurring profit up 30% to P5.61bn – in line with REGIS but behind consensus  

RRHI posted 4Q24 net income of P2.7bn (+62% YoY), bringing FY24 net income to P10.3bn, up 1.5x YoY due to the one-off gain from the BPI-RBC merger of P4.66bn recognized in 1Q24. Recurring income as reported by the company grew 12% YoY to P6.3bn in FY24.  We estimate recurring profit (i.e. including FX adj, interest expenses and dividend income related to BPI) grew by 30% YoY to P5.61bn, broadly in line with REGIS but behind consensus estimates.  EBIT rose by +9% YoY to P9.8bn for the year, within Regis and consensus forecasts.   

 

For 4Q24 alone, recurring net profit increased 5% YoY to P2.3bn while EBIT improved to P2.3bn +22% YoY underpinned by better category mix and the rationalization of underperforming stores.

 

4Q SSSG recovers across most segments

Blended SSSG improved to 3.4% in 4Q24 from +0.7% for 9M, primarily due to faster growth rates in key segments, such as food (+4.1% in 4Q24 from 1.5% in 9M24) and specialty stores (+1.7% in 4Q24 from -8.6% in 3Q24). The DIY segment was still hampered by stiff competition, with SSSG dropping by 2% in 4Q, marking a sustained seven-quarter period of decline.

 

4Q operating margins improve

The company's consolidated EBIT margin for the 4Q expanded 130 bps QoQ and 80 bps YoY, with the food and specialty stores segments showing QoQ margin improvement of 30 bps and 340 bps, respectively. The food segment saw better margins on the back of higher demand of imported products while specialty store margins were boosted by the aforementioned optimized category mix and vendor support.

 

Less inflation worries, discretionary spending recovery as drivers for 2025 growth

Management is guiding for 2-4% consolidated SSSG in 2025 (Regis FY25E: +3.6%). Although expectations for primary growth drivers are still hinged on food and drug stores, with the bulk of net store additions in 2025 (c.90% of total)—less pressure on the consumer wallet as inflation continues to ease will increase the propensity for discretionary spending.

Colliers: Office vacancy rises in 4Q24
  • Colliers reported that Metro Manila office vacancy rose to 19.8% in 4Q24       
    • From 18.5% in 3Q24, 19.3% in 4Q23
    • Makati CBD vacancy stayed flat at 8%
    • Vacancy in the Manila Bay area rose to 35% in 4Q24, from 30% in 3Q24
  • Office transactions were negative in 4Q24, with net closures of 185,000sqm
    • Largely due to the POGO ban
    • From net closures of 33,000sqm in 3Q24, net take-up of 130,000sqm in 4Q23
  • Colliers expects Metro Manila office vacancy to rise to 22% by end-2025
    • While it expects net take-up to hit 150,000sqm, it expects about 600,000sqm in new supply
    • We estimate that there are still about 200,000sqm of offices occupied by POGO firms

Department of Energy prepares for GEA-4 auction
  • The Department of Energy said it will release the terms of reference for the fourth Green Energy Auction (GEA-4) this month
    • GEA-4 will be limited to solar power plants with Battery Energy Storage Systems
  • Recall that the bidding for GEA-3 is scheduled on Feb 11
    • GEA-3 will involve 100 MW of geothermal, 300 MW of impounding hydro, and 4,250 MW of pumped-storage hydro, with targeted deliveries in 2028-2030 for hydro and 2024-2030 for geothermal
    • Listed companies that have been shortlisted are: First Gen (FGEN PM) and San Miguel (SMC PM)
  • The Green Energy Auction program aims to promote investments in renewable energy sources
    • GEA allows RE gencos to sell energy to the WESM at their winning (guaranteed) bid prices and any difference between the bid price and the WESM price will be settled through the Feed-in-Tariff system, which is a full pass-through to end users
