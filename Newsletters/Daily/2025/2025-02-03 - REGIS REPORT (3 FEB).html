REGIS REPORT

03 February 2025

The PSE Index (PSEi) plunged 4.0% on Friday, crashing through the 6,000 level to close at 5,863, down 10% year-to-date, and more than 20% lower from the October peak.

Friday's plunge was due to the PSEi rebalancing, which saw the addition of Chinabank (CBC PM) and AREIT (ARIET PM), and the deletion of Wilcon (WLCON PM) and Nickel Asia (NIKL PM).

Why was this a problem? The PSEi is rebalanced every 6 months, based on various criteria including market cap, and stock liquidity. Most of the time, it's a non-event.

Why was this a problem this time? The PSEi weights are based on free float. Trouble is, free float is self-reported, and the PSE generally accepts the data provided by the company. CBC disclosed free float of 58%. However, we believe that does not fairly represent what the real float is. Based on news reports over the last two decades, we believe the bank's shares are much more tightly controlled than is disclosed. Despite the claimed float, the stock's average daily trading volume (by # of shares) before today was 550,000 shares, or about 0.02% of the 2,691mn shares outstanding. Before today, the most it traded by value in the last 12 months was under PHP200mn, averaging just PHP23.4mn (or about $400,000). The rebal had $72mn in projected inflow for the stock, equivalent to more than two months' worth of trading.

Unsurprisingly, CBC stock was up 38% on Friday as funds rushed to take what little liquidity was on offer.

Based on the 58% float and after considering its price action just before the PSEi inclusion, CBC's weight in the new PSEi Index would be around 4.3%, even more than PLDT (TEL PM). So passive investors (and many of the local institutional funds) had to sell down their shares in the other index names to fund their new weighting into CBC (4.3%) and AREIT (1.5%).

After last Friday's move, CBC is now trading at a valuation premium (based on Street estimates) to all the banks we cover, including BDO and BPI.

We would use the move to pick up high-quality names like Century Pacific (CNPF PM), Ayala Land (ALI PM), BPI (BPI PM), BDO (BDO PM) and GT Capital (GTCAP PM), all of whom were down 6-10%.

The big news globally is the imposition of 25% tariffs by the United States on goods coming from its North American neighbors Canada and Mexico.  Invoking an economic emergency, President Trump signed orders slapping the tariffs even though the free trade agreement between the three North American countries (USMCA, previously NAFTA) is still in force and not up for renegotiation until next year.  Apart from the tariffs on its neighbors, Trump also ordered the imposition of a 10pt increase in tariffs on goods from China.

There should not be many Philippine companies affected by this looming trade war. One of the few that come to mind would be ICTSI (ICT PM), whose port in Mexico contributes ~25% to its EBITDA.

The indirect (2nd and 3rd order and beyond) effects, however, are hard to fathom now and could be significant.

+++

The Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) official expectation is for Jan CPI inflation to settle between 2.5% to 3.3%. Consensus is, meanwhile, expecting 2.8%. This compares with the 2.9% print for Dec CPI inflation. We don’t expect the BSP to take any action on policy rates at next week’s Monetary Board meeting. BSP Governor Eli Remolona did, however, reiterate his view that policy will be cut by 50bp this year and RRR will be reduced by 2ppt.

In somewhat related news, the government approved the declaration of a food security emergency, allowing the National Food Authority (NFA) to import and sell rice in a bid to drive rice prices further down.  Rice prices have stayed stubbornly high despite the rice tariff cut in July and the continued fall in international export prices. If the government is successful, we believe retail rice prices can fall 15-20% from here.

+++

The president of Cebu Air (CEB PM) says the company is optimistic that it can fly 30m passengers this year. What’s remarkable about this target is that it represents a ~22% increase over FY24.

+++

Liquor firm Emperador (EMI PM) says it’s taking majority ownership of Mexican premium mezcal maker Distileria Los Danzantes. EMI’s overseas ventures have proven quite successful but the same cannot be said about its core domestic operations in recent years.

MACRO
Food emergency declaration approved to address high rice prices
  • The National Price Coordinating Council (NPCC) has approved the Department of Agriculture's (DA) proposal to declare a food security emergency
    • Recall that the DA is authorized to propose food emergency declarations under the recently amended Rice Tariffication Law, in response to a shortage or extraordinary prices increases
  • DA Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. stated last Friday the need to declare a food emergency on Feb. 4, Tuesday in order to bring down elevated rice prices
  • Upon declaration, the gov't will release around 300,000 MT of rice stocks within six months from the National Food Authority (NFA) which will be sold to local gov't units (LGUs) at a subsidized price of P36/kg to be passed on to consumers at P38/kg
    • The NFA will also be allowed to purchase rice from farmers this coming harvest season
  • This initiative is part of the push by the gov't as the DA Secretary reiterates its targets of reducing the MSRP of rice which is currently at P58/kg to be lowered to P55 on February 5, then to P52 on February 15, and P49 by March 1
