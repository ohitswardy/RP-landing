REGIS REPORT

05 January 2025

President Marcos is expected to sign the 2026 national budget today. The total amount expenditure earmarked under the 2026 General Appropriations bill approved by Congress is P6.79trn, which is 7% higher than the previous year. However, it is unclear how much of the budget will (or won’t) be vetoed but House Appropriations Committee chair Mikaela Suansing expressed confidence no items will be.

 

As it stands, the budget passed by Congress is not expansionary. Financial constraints are holding back the government from spending more, given where the national debt is.

ICTSI (ICT PM) officially took over operations of Durban Container Terminal 2 (DCT-2), putting an end to the legal dispute over the auction that saw ICTSI submit the highest bid.

The Lower House has approved, again, the bill creating the Department of Water Resources.  The bill is a copy of one previously approved by the Lower House that failed to become law under the previous Congress. The bill, if it becomes law, is meant to streamline the regulation of the water sector, which is plagued by complicated regulatory structures across many jurisdictions.

Singaporean banker Victor Lee officially takes over as CEO of Security Bank (SECB PM) today. Lee’s appointment was announced a few months ago. He was previously head of the operations of CIMB in Singapore.

MACRO
President Marcos expected to sign 2026 budget today
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. is expected to sign the proposed P6.79trn (+7% YoY) General Appropriations Act today (Jan 5, Monday)
  • House Committee on Appropriations Chairperson Mikaela Suansing is confident that the President will sign the proposed budget into law without vetoing any item
  • Suansing highlighted that there are special provisions in the budget that define and limit the authorized uses of funds from unprogrammed appropriations as well as a provision for a ban on political involvement in cash aid programs
  • However, some lawmakers flagged the bicam’s rejection of the Senate’s proposal to cut unprogrammed appropriations to P174bn, with the panel instead retaining the House's P243bn allocation
    • Unprogrammed appropriations are a standby fund which is intended for use if excess revenues or new collections arise
    • The effectivity of the provision banning political involvement in distribution of cash aid (~P138bn allocated) was also questioned
  • Recall that for the 2025 budget, Marcos vetoed P194bn worth of items (180 public works projects and 15 unprogrammed appropriations) but the move was criticized to be largely "cosmetic" as other budget anomalies (e.g., insertions for flood control projects) were not vetoed

UTILITIES
Lower House approves Department of Water Resources bill again
  • The Lower House of the 20th Congress approved House Bill 6789, which is a measure seeking to create the Department of Water Resources
    • Recall that the Lower House approved a similar proposal on third reading in the 19th Congress last December 2023 but the bill was not tackled by the Senate
  • The bill’s salient provisions are:
    • Creation of a “Department for Water Resources”, which will solely supervise the country’s water resources (except fisheries and aquaculture), sanitation, flood risk, and drought risk management
    • Creation of a “Water Regulatory Commission”, which will be an independent, quasi-judicial regulatory body with overall authority and powers on all public and private providers
    • Reconstitution of the National Water Resources Board as the “National Water Resource Allocation Board”, which would serve as the approval body for using water resources, including dam construction
    • Existing concession agreements will remain valid
  • We would view the passage of this bill positively, as more streamlined policies could help draw investments in the water sector
BANKS
Singaporean banker assumes role of new Security Bank CEO
  • Security Bank Corp. (SECB PM), in a disclosure to the PSE, has announced that Victor Lee Meng Teck will assume the roles as new President and CEO (effective Jan 5, today), replacing Sanjiv Vohra who was president for six years
    • Vohra stepped down and was stated to remain as senior advisor to the board
  • Lee has over 30 years of leadership experience throughout Asia, having been the former CEO of CIMB Singapore and CEO of Growth Markets for CIMB Bank Berhad
    • Security Bank in a previous disclosure highlighted Lee's contribution to the growth of CIMB Singapore, doubling the bank's revenue and raising its ROE to nearly 20%
CONGLOS
Government to release balance of CARS program incentives this year
  • The government plans to release tax payment certificates (TPC) to qualified participants of the CARS program within the year
    • According to Board of Investments Managing Head Ceferino Rodolfo
    • However, release of ~P4bn of obligation will depend on the government’s savings before TPCs can be issued
  • Recall that GT Capital’s (GTCAP PM) Toyota Motor Phils (TMP) still has P4.5bn worth of un-credited tax incentives under the CARS program, equivalent to ~30% of TMP’s FY26E profit
    • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GTCAP and accounts for 35% of profit
    • GTCAP trades at a 46% discount to its market-based NAV of P1132/sh and 4x PER
