REGIS REPORT

28 October 2025

Monetary Board (MB) member and former BSP Governor Benjamin Diokno says he sees room for another 25bps policy rate cut in December (to 4.50%), with more potential cuts in early 2026, citing concerns of a GDP growth slowdown. Our forecasts assume no cuts in December 2025, but one more cut in 2026 (in line with consensus expectations).

 

+ + +

 

BDO (BDO PM) reported NPAT of P63.1bn, broadly in line with Regis and consensus forecasts for FY25E. Loan growth of 14% was driven by consumers (+18%) which helped prop up loan yields. However, NIMs were still crimped by intensifying competition for deposits. Strong fee growth (+15%) was negated by opex growth (also +15%). Earlier we expressed concern over NPL cover which fell to 134% from 140% in 2Q. Management explained that this was due to “technical” NPLs in the mortgage book, as one large developer (whose buyers were in loan default) was experiencing delays in titling of its units. This in turn prevented BDO from foreclosing on, and disposing of the properties in a timely manner.

 

Despite the in-line profits, BDO stock fell 3.6% yesterday to a new 18-month low of P131/sh, taking valuations below 1x P/book and below 7.5x PER on our FY2026E estimates. Buy.

POWER
Meralco core profit up 14% in 9M25 on strong generation and provision reversals
  • 9M25 core profit of P40bn (+14%) beats estimates. Meralco reported 9M25 core profit (excluding gains and FX adj) of P40bn, on trend to beat consensus forecast of P49bn and management’s guidance of P50bn.  Derived 3Q25 core profit of P14.5bn improved 22% YoY, lifted by (a) the accretive contribution of the Chromite LNG deal, (b) provision reversals at the distribution unit following real property tax settlements, and (c) higher income from Global Business Power’s participation in the Visayas reserve market.  These helped counter soft distribution volumes and reduced RES trading gains. 

 

  • Weather pulls down 3Q25 distribution volume down by 2%.  Electricity demand contracted by 2% YoY in 3Q25, led by a 6% decline in residential sales, which management attributed to cooler temperatures and more frequent typhoons. Note that even commercial were unusually weak (-1%). With similar trends persisting early into 4Q, management expects full-year volume growth (-0.5% to -0.8%) to track close to the 9M25 decline of 0.4%. 

 

  • Target completion of rate review: June 2026. Following the ERC’s release of the Rationalized Rules for Setting Distribution Wheeling Rates (RRDWR), Meralco plans to re-file its reset application by January 19, 2026, which the ERC will review from February to April 2026.  If all goes as planned, the ERC could render a decision by June 25 and the new rates can be implemented by July 1.  

 

  • Natgas law implementation guidelines remain under deliberation.  Even after nine (9) months since RA 12012 (Natgas law) was enacted, the Department of Energy has yet to finalize the guidelines (i.e. implementing rules and regulations or IRR) needed to implement the law’s provision mandating priority use of domestic natural gas over other conventional power sources (e.g. coal).  It has determined, however, that indigenous natural gas suppliers should offer a uniform rate to all its customers, with supply allocation to be based on each customer’s contracted capacity. Given this, Meralco believes its unit, LNGPH (under Chromite), should have an allocation of 55% over Malampaya’s gas. Until the final IRR is released and interpreted, however, nothing is cast in stone.

 

  • Minor delay for Terra Solar? Although adverse weather has caused some construction setbacks, Terra Solar’s first phase (2.5GWdc) has reached 65% completion to-date. While project delays could result in opportunity losses, Meralco is not required to procure replacement power until Aug 2026.  Meanwhile, Meralco confirmed that the exchangeable note extended to SP New Energy (SPNEC PM) founder and previous owner, Leandro Leviste, matured in September with the share transfer executed yesterday. As a result, Meralco, through MGreen, now owns 60.86% of SPNEC’s common shares.

    • Perks include a 5% rebate on purchases (up to P500 within first 30 days), card protection insurance for 120% of the outstanding balance, P500 daily hospital cash benefit, 24/7 telemedicine services
GAMING
Bloomberry to sell Korean casino business
  • Bloomberry Resorts (through a subsidiary) signed a share purchase agreement to spin off its casino business in Jeju and then sell the new company to Gangwon Blue Mountain
  • Gangwon Blue Mountain has made a down payment of KRW500mn (c. P20.5mn)
  • Bloomberry will continue to operate the hotel business in Jeju
  • In 1H25, BLOOM’s Jeju operations recorded a net loss of P146mn
  • Bloomberry’s 1H25 recurring net loss was c. P960mn
  • Since BLOOM’s Korean gaming license has been fully impaired, the company could recognize a gain on the sale of its Korean gaming business
  • BLOOM is trading at 6.7X FY25E EV/EBITDA
