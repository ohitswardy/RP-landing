REGIS REPORT

11 November 2025

We’re now in the thick of things this results season. We have three property companies – ALI, SMPH and RLC – and food retailer PGOLD reporting. For property, the residential segment still seems to be challenged, though not as catastrophic as some had feared given the flood control corruption issues. Oddly, the office occupancy levels were flat to up, continuing to defy concerns over AI’s impact on BPOs.

 

Most interesting, in our view, was that mall revenue growth rates were steady or stronger in 3Q25. For comparison, PGOLD numbers showed a sharp slowdown in the main Puregold brand (SSSG of 1.6% vs 6.7% in 2Q) while its higher-end S&R brand saw SSSG accelerate to 6.8% from 5.4% in 2Q. Is the “K-shaped” economy still a thing? Did the GDP’s Personal Consumption weakness disproportionately affect lower income households?

 

A quick summary of yesterday’s results:

 

  • Ayala Land (ALI PM (-) – 9M25 earnings declined 1% to P21.4bn, slightly behind REGIS and consensus forecasts, due to a decline in development revenue. Despite the eruption of the flood control corruption scandal in 3Q25, reservation sales of both the core and premium residential segments rose 1%-2% QoQ (up a combined 9% YoY). However, the company lowered its full-year targets for reservation sales and launches, due to a more subdued outlook on Metro Manila condominiums. Read more from our report here:

Although GDP growth slowed in 3Q25, SM Prime’s mall revenue still grew 6% YoY, the same pace as 2Q25. The company also expanded its mall EBIT margin in 3Q25/9M25.   • Robinsons Land (RLC PM) (=) – 9M25 recurring profit rose 10% to P10.2bn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. 3Q25 mall operations outperformed our expectations, with revenue rising 7% QoQ (+14% YoY) and EBIT margin expanding 1.6ppt QoQ (+3.9ppt YoY). However, residential demand was weak. 3Q25 reservation sales of standalone projects fell 63% QoQ to P860mn, only 17% of the 2019 quarterly average. Meanwhile, reservation sales of JV projects rose 228% QoQ (down 48% YoY) to P1.7bn, about 35% of the 2019 quarterly average. See more below.

 

  • Puregold (PGOLD PM) (-) – Revenues rose +11% in 9M (to P186bn) and +9% in 3Q (to P58bn) as same-store sales (consolidated, including S&R) grew around 5% in the latest quarter.  The 3Q/9M income looks a little soft, however, with profit for the quarter at P2.0bn (+2%). Note, however, that 4Q income can be very lumpy depending on the rebates/discounts that the company receives from manufacturers. CONSUMER
Puregold 3Q Initial Comments

9M25 profit +6%, top line +11%

PGOLD’s 9M25 net profit grew 6% YoY to P7.3bn on top line growth of 11% to P168.1bn—profit is trailing behind forecasts, while top line is within expectations. In 3Q25 alone, profit increased by only 2% despite EBIT and net sales rising, 13% and 9%, respectively.

 

3Q SSSG: moderation in Puregold, acceleration in S&R

Consolidated sales in the quarter rose 9% to P58.2bn primarily driven by continued momentum from the S&R store brand as it offset the deceleration seen in the Puregold format. 3Q SSSG for Puregold moderated to +1.6% from +6.7% in 2Q, while S&R SSSG quickened to +6.8% from +5.4% in 2Q.

 

Despite the moderation in Puregold SSSG, its YTD growth of +4.8% in same-store sales as of 9M25 is still higher than the full-year SSSG figures seen for the format in the past three years.
