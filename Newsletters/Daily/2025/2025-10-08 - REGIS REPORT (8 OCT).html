REGIS REPORT

08 October 2025

The inflation data released yesterday looks unlikely to prompt the Bangko Sentral (BSP) to take action tomorrow, in our view. At 1.7%, the inflation figure for Sept is higher than Aug, but remains below the 2-4% long-term target of the BSP.

 

For the Sept CPI, there was a notable jump in vegetable prices which tempered the continued downtrend in rice prices. Rice prices, however, are likely to begin reversing their downtrend soon because of the rice import moratorium. Optically, however, rice price inflation (YoY) will stay low/negative until at least early 2026 because of the high base persisting until Jan/Feb 2025.

 

If CPI inflation stays around here (<2%) for the next two prints, we think the BSP is likely to reduce rates at its last Monetary Board meeting in Dec.

 

+++

 

The implementing rules and regulations (IRR) for the Konektadong Pinoy Act (KPA) will be released by next week, if the Dept of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) is to be believed.

 

The IRR is supposed to thresh out the details of the KPA and thus affect how the KPA is actually executed. A check of the latest draft IRR, however, shows there is still lots of ambiguity, which would give the government agencies involved in implementing the law (e.g. DICT and NTC) plenty of room to interpret it as they see fit.

 

Broadly, we are of the view that the new law will have greater impact on competition on the broadband industry. Notwithstanding this, we think the shares of the telcos all offer value, including Converge (CNVRG PM) which is on <7x PER (FY26E), 9% 3-year EPS CAGR and 3% yield. PLDT (TEL PM) is also on <7x PER with a yield of just under 9%. Globe’s (GLO PM) multiples are higher at 11x (yield of ~7%) but that’s skewed by its stake in its fintech associate.

 

+++

 

The country’s reserves rose 1.6% to US$108.8bn as of Sept, helped by soaring gold prices and despite BSP interventions in the FX markets.

MACRO
Inflation rate inches up to 1.7% in September
  • Headline inflation picked up to 1.7% in Sept from 1.5% in the previous month of Aug
    • This is lower than the consensus forecast of 1.9% but is within the BSP's forecast range of 1.5-2.3% for Sept
    • Inflation has fallen from 2.8% in Jan and has been hovering below BSP's 2-4% target for the past seven months
  • Primary drivers for the faster pace in inflation in Sept were food and transport
    • Food was up 1.0% YoY, a tad higher from 0.9% YoY in Aug, while transport was also up 1.0% YoY but reversing from a 0.3% decline the previous month
      • Food was driven by a sharp 19.4% YoY increase in vegetable prices given adverse weather while transport saw higher diesel prices
    • Notably, rice prices are on their fifteenth consecutive MoM price decline since Jul 2024
      • According to the Dept. of Agriculture (as of Oct 4), prices for local and imported well-milled rice range from P38-52/kg and P38-46/kg, respectively vs. P42-52/kg and P40-54/kg back in Jan 2
  • Core inflation inched down to 2.6% in Sept from 2.7% in Aug, while core non-food inflation climbed to 2.4% from 2.1% the previous month
  • This brings the YT-Sep headline inflation rate to 1.7%, below the lower end of the 2-4% BSP target for this year
  • For 2025, the BSP expects 2025 inflation to be 1.7%
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to average 1.8%
  • The next Monetary Board meeting (5th out of 6 this year) will be on Oct 9
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect 100bp total worth of cuts to 4.75%
      • The BSP has cut 75bp total this year so far from 5.75% in Jan

MACRO
PH dollar reserves at $108.8bn end-Sep
  • The Philippines' gross international reserves (GIR) at end-Sep increased 1.6% MoM to US$108.8bn from US$107.1bn at end-Aug
    • The increase primarily reflected the surge in global gold prices which offset the drop in foreign exchange holdings due to the BSP's intervention in the forex market
      • The peso depreciated against the US dollar from P57.04 on Sep 1 to P58.04 by end-Sep
    • The level of dollar reserves was enough to cover about 3.6x the country's short-term external debt and equivalent to 7.3x months' worth of imports of goods and payments
  • Note that the BSP expects the country's GIR to be at US$105bn by end-2025, slightly down from the end-2024 figure of US$106bn

 

 

 

POLITICS
Justice Secretary Remulla appointed as Ombudsman
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. has appointed Dept. of Justice (DOJ) Secretary Jesus Crispin "Boying" Remulla as the new Ombudsman, replacing Samuel Martires whose term ended July
    • The Office of the Ombudsman is responsible for investigating and prosecuting government officials
    • Martires was appointed by former President Rodrigo Duterte
  • Remulla stated that "the people can expect transparency and a lot of action" and emphasized that strict access to the Statement of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Worth (SALN) of gov’t officials will be lifted
    • Prior to being appointed as Ombudsman, Remulla was assigned as the DOJ Secretary back in June 2022
    • He previously served as Cavite Representative for three terms from 2004 to 2022 as well as Cavite Governor from 2016 to 2019
  • Appointment comes amidst one of the largest corruption scandals in the history of the Philippines
  • Among the first actions expected from the Remulla is the release of the Statements of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Worth (SALN) of public officials
    • All public officials are required to submit a SALN but the previous Ombudsman, Martires, blocked public access to the SALNs  
CONSUMER
Del Monte eyes to raise up to US$600mn through private placement
  • Del Monte Pacific Ltd. (DELM PM) intends to raise US$500-600mn through private placement at Del Monte Philippines Inc. (DMPI) to address the capital deficit following the recent deconsolidation of its US business
    • Del Monte seeks to reduce financing costs by replacing higher-interest debt with equity that carries a lower dividend yield
  • Recall that US subsidiary Del Monte Foods Holdings Ltd. initiated voluntary Chapter 11 proceedings in the US Bankruptcy Court last July 1
    • Management added that the placement will also ensure that uncertainties in the US business will not affect the company's continuing core operations
