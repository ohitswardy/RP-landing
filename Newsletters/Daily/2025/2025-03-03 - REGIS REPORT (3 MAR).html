Profits at SM Investments (SM PM) rose +6% to P82.4bn for FY24, lifted most by the banking businesses.  SM Retail provided some positive surprise with income growing +5% for the year and +31% for the quarter alone. There’s quite a bit of news on the gaming sector today.

 

First, Senate President Francis Escudero expressed his misgivings about social ills brought about by online gambling and wants this sector to be banned.

 

Second, the BSP is circulating draft guidelines that would prohibit banks and electronic money issuers from offering or presenting gambling on their platforms. While existing users would likely continue to play directly on gaming apps, the new guidelines could curtail the growth of e-games, which saw a 170% surge in gross gaming revenue (GGR) in 2024.   

 

While some new risks appear for the Philippine e-games sector, market leader DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) just appointed a country head for its Brazilian operations. PLUS recently received a license to operate in Brazil and plans to start offering sports betting by the 4Q25.  

 

Finally, Melco Resorts (200 HK) is looking to sell its contract to operate City of Dreams Manila, which will end in 2033. The fortunes of integrated resorts and e-games operators have been completely different. While FY24 GGR of the e-games segment grew 170% (to P155bn), GGR of licensed casinos remained flat (at P200bn). Furthermore, the POGO ban, which only became effective in December 2024, will likely weigh on integrated resorts further.

The country’s trade deficit jumped to US$5.09b (+17%) in Jan, despite a modest YoY improvement in exports (+6%). The highest annual trade deficit of the country was US$57.6bn recorded in 2022.  

 

+++

 

The Dept of Trade (DTI) announced it was imposing a safeguard duty on cement imports equivalent to P16 per 40kg bag. This translates to US$6.9/MT, roughly 13% of the landed cost of imported cement.  We expect this to help local manufacturers, even if temporarily, as imported cement takes a bigger share of the market (now >30%).

Senate President wants ban on online gambling

  • He believes that the social issues caused by POGO firms can also be attributed to PIGO companies
  • Senator Escudero asked PAGCOR to provide an in-depth review of the PIGO sector
  • FY24 GGR of e-games grew 170% to P155bn
GAMING
PAGCOR says that new BSP guidelines could curtail growth of e-games
  • PAGCOR Chairman Alejandro Tengco said that the BSP draft guidelines could curtail the growth of the e-casino sector
  • The BSP’s draft “Guidelines on the Adoption of a Digital Marketplace Model” seeks to prohibit online marketplaces (banks and electronic money issuers) from offering or presenting gambling
    • In addition to access, advertising of gaming platforms will likely be prohibited in online marketplaces
  • About 80% of the players of DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) access its platform through GCash
  • The draft guidelines would suggest that players will no longer be able to access the PLUS platform through GCash but will still be able to transfer funds via GCash or banks
  • Existing players would likely continue to play on the PLUS platform
  • However, PLUS and other online gaming operators might experience more difficulty growing their current user bases

MACRO
Trade deficit widens to $5.09bn in January
  • The January trade deficit widened to US$5.09bn, up 17% YoY and 23% MoM, reflecting a larger gap between imports and exports
  • Imports for the month rose 11% YoY to US$11.45bn
    • The increases came from all major types of goods but primarily driven by the 12% YoY increase in raw and intermediate goods as well as the 14% YoY increase in consumer goods
  • Exports grew 6% YoY to US$6.36bn, the first month of growth after four consecutive months of contraction
    • Electronics exports declined by 3% YoY but recovered +20% MoM to US$3.37bn
      • These made up the bulk of exports at 53% of the total
    • Meanwhile, non-electronics exports surged 19% YoY to US$2.99bn

 

 

 

 

 

MACRO/INDUSTRIALS
DTI imposes P16 per 40-kg bag safeguard duty on imported cement
  • The Department of Trade and Industry issued an order imposing a provisional safeguard duty of P400/MT or P16 per 40kg bag for 200 days
    • We estimate the tariff is equivalent to 13% (~US$6.9 per MT) of the landed cost of imported bagged cement of US$54/MT
    • The case has now been transmitted to the Tariff Commission which will determine whether or not there is a need to impose a definitive safeguard measure
  • DTI said that the volume of imported cement rose to 22% in 2019 from 32% in 2023 while overall cement demand contracted
    • The Cement Manufacturers Association of the Philippines (CEMAP) said that local production currently operates at only 55-60% of its installed capacity, increasing production costs and forced plant shutdowns

TELCOS
Competition authorities approve Summit Telco’s purchase of DITO CME stake
  • DITO CME Holdings (DITO PM), parent company of DITO Telecommunity announced in a disclosure that the Philippine Competition Commission (PCC) recently cleared the transaction of an issuance of up to 9bn new shares to Summit Telco
    • Assuming the shares are issued in full, Udenna Corp.'s overall stake will be reduced from 49.8% to 35%, while Summit Telco Holdings, alongside Summit Telco Corp. Pte. Ltd., will increase its stake to 45.6% from 22.8%
  • The fund-raising is meant to improve the company's equity position given continued concerns about DITO's ability to fund ongoing investments
  • It is of note however that DITO CME is subject to a 40% foreign ownership cap as it owns property assets, but the company can divest of its property before the deal is finalized
  • The Philippine Congress will still need to approve the deal because of a provision in DitoTel’s franchise that requires its approval for any change in control
  • The company targets to achieve profitability by 2028
