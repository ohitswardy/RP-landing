REGIS REPORT

06 February 2025

Headline CPI inflation for January came in at 2.9%, unchanged from the December figure. While the price of rice declined on both a YoY and MoM basis, prices of vegetables, seafood, and meat rose YoY and MoM.

 

However, since the increases were attributed to lingering effects of the usual seasonal bad weather on supply, we continue to believe that the BSP has sufficient room to cut policy rates at the next Monetary Board meeting next (February 13).  

 

+++

 

215 of the 306 members of the House of Representatives endorsed an impeachment compliant against Vice President Sara Duterte, citing graft and corruption, among other grounds. The impeachment complaint will now move to the Senate. However, since the Senate’s final session before the mid-term elections is tomorrow, the impeachment proceedings will not continue until June, when the next Congress convenes.

 

In order to remove Vice President Duterte from office, at least two-thirds of the 24 senators must vote to impeach her. Given the number of “independent” senators (vs Duterte or administration allies), it is still not a guarantee that 16 senators will vote to remove her from office.

 

While the impeachment might cause a bit of a distraction, we expect most of the economy to be “business as usual”.

 

+++

 

Congress ratified the Bicameral Conference Committee report on the Capital Markets Efficiency Promotions Bill. Among other items, the Bill seeks to lower the stock transaction tax from 0.6% to 0.1%. The stock transaction tax is the largest friction cost for market investors and its reduction should spur more market activity.

 

+++

 

Colliers reported that sales of Metro Manila condominiums fell 60% to 9,000 units in 2024. As a result, even though the number of unsold residential units fell slightly QoQ and YoY, 4Q24 unsold inventory now represents 8.2 years of reservation sales, from “only” 5.8 years in 3Q23 (3.3 years in 4Q23).

 

+++

 

Robinsons Land (RLC PM) President “Mybelle” Aragon-Gobio said that high-end residential demand remains strong and the company still has 22 hectares of unused land bank in Fort Bonifacio that would be suited for such projects. We reckon that RLC probably has the 2nd largest unused land bank in Fort Bonifacio, after Megaworld (MEG PM).

MACRO
Inflation rate unchanged at 2.9% in January
  • Headline inflation was unchanged at 2.9% in January vs. the previous month
    • This is slightly higher than consensus forecast of 2.8% and came in at the midpoint of BSP's forecast range of 2.5-3.3%
  • On a MoM basis, CPI rose 0.5%, primarily driven by higher food prices which increased by 1.1% MoM
    • The increase was mainly due to price upticks in vegetables and meat due to the lingering effects of the several typhoons that hit the country in October and November
    • Inflation was primarily driven by food—specifically meat, fish, and vegetables which rose 2.0%, 3.6%, and 3.9% respectively
      • The big uptick in vegetable prices was due to the recent spike in the price of tomatoes which ranged as high as P250-350/kg at the start of Jan according to the Department of Agriculture's price tracker
    • Rice CPI MoM sequentially dipped for the seventh consecutive month by 0.9% bringing the cumulative decline to 4.6%
      • Notably, rice prices on a YoY basis decreased for the first time since Dec 2021
  • Core inflation dipped slightly to 2.6% in Jan from 2.8% in Dec. While core non-food inflation also dropped slightly to 2.5% from 2.8% for the same periods
  • The gov't targets to keep inflation within 2-4% for this year
    • Bloomberg median consensus is for the full year average is 3.0%
    • We expect inflation to average 3.5%

 

 

 

 

 

POLITICS
Lower House impeaches VP Sara Duterte, Senate trial to be held
  • 215 of the 306 lawmakers have endorsed an impeachment compliant against Vice President Sara Duterte
    • Grounds for impeaching the Vice President cited: culpable violation of the Constitution, betrayal of public trust, graft and corruption, other high crimes
  • This implies that with the signatures of at least 1/3rd of all House members, the petition was fast-tracked directly to the Senate with no need for a deliberation by the House committee on justice
    • The impeachment bill will first undergo review by Senate Secretary Renato Bantug Jr., who will then report to the plenary which is a prerequisite for the complaint to be proceed
  • Given that there was no plenary action on the bill prior to Senate's adjournment of its final session, it will likely be tackled after elections as Congress will go on break till resuming on June 2
  • Senate Minority Leader Koko Pimentel III however stated that there is a possibility for the Senate to work on the impeach complaint while on break if there is a 100 percent unanimity or zero objections on proceeding
  • In the Senate trial, a two-thirds vote of the 24 senators is needed for impeachment to take place which will also result in a lifetime disqualification from holding office
    • If impeached, the President is granted authority by the Constitution to nominate a new Vice President from Congress, which needs confirmation from both the Lower House and the Senate
    • The trial date has yet to be finalized
MACRO/MARKET
Bicam approves capital markets reform bill, new DBP chapter
  • Congress ratified the Bicameral Conference Committee report, which reconciled the Senate and Lower House’s versions of the Capital Markets Efficiency Promotions bill
    • Reduction in the tax on stock transactions to 0.1% from 0.6%
    • Cut in the documentary stamp tax (DST) on original issue of shares of stock to 0.75% from 1% of the par value of the shares of stock
    • Exemption of DST on the original issuance, redemption or other disposition of shares or units of participation in a unit investment trust fund
    • Introduction of an allowable deduction of 50% of an employer’s contribution to their employees’ personal equity and retirement accounts
    • A copy of the bicameral conference committee report of the measure was not immediately available
    • The Philippine Stock Exchange previously forecasted that lowering the stock transaction tax to 0.1% would boost the stock market’s trading volume to P4.9tn by 2029
  • Meanwhile, the Senate also ratified the Bicam report on the new Development Bank of the Philippines (DBP) charter
    • The measure mandates that the National Government will always own 70% of the DBP’S capital stock, with P32bn or 10.67% being fully subscribed to and paid for by the state
    • It will also allow the state-run lender to conduct an initial public offering
PROPERTY
Colliers: residential demand falls in 4Q24
  • In its 4Q24 briefing, Colliers said that the number of condominium units sold in Metro Manila in FY24 fell to 9,000 units, from 23,000 units in 2023
    • In 3Q24, trailing 12M take-up stood at 13,000 units
  • The number of unsold residential units fell 1% QoQ and 4% YoY to 74,000 units
  • As a result, the level of unsold residential inventory in Metro Manila now represents 8.2 years of reservation sales
    • From 5.8 years in 3Q24, 3.3 years in 4Q23
  • Colliers said that pre-selling condominium units in Makati CBD were priced at an average of P400,000/sqm, 50% higher than the prices of ready-for-occupancy units in the area
    • During the pre-COVID period, prices of preselling projects in Makati were about 20%-30% higher than prices of RFO units
  • ALI is trading at 52% discount to NAV, 1.2X FY25E P/B, and 12.3X FY25E P/E
  • MEG is trading at 71% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 3.1X FY25E P/E
  • SMPH is trading at 39% discount to NAV, 1.6X FY25E P/E, and 16.4X FY25E P/E
