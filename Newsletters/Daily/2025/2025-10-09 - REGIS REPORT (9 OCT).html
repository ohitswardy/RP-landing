09 October 2025

Alfamart Philippines has just launched its franchising program more than ten years after it opened its first store. Alfamart Philippines is a joint venture between the SM (SM PM) group and PT Sumber Alfaria Trijaya Tbk (AMRT IJ). Alfamart Philippines opened its first store in 2014 and now has over 2,300.

 

The move to franchise, finally, comes as a natural progression of the Alfamart business. It will allow the business to grow with less of its own capital amidst a retail market that’s quickly expanding in the small format space. For perspective, Alfamart in Indonesia has over 23,000 stores of which around a quarter (24%) are franchised. In contrast, close to half of the 4,000+ stores of Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) are franchised.

 

Operators of small format groceries have all been expanding rapidly and this should accelerate the shift to modern retail. One near-term consequence of this, however, is that there will be greater competitive intensity in the space.

CONSUMER
Alfamart launches franchising program
  • Alfamart Philippines has launched its franchising program to ramp up its nationwide expansion with MSME-led (micro, small and medium enterprise) franchising
    • The official launch of franchising follows the pilot program of two franchise-owned stores in Laguna with the first having opened back in Oct 2024 and the second opened in Aug this year
  • Alfamart has also engaged in strategic partnerships with local entrepreneurs such as the co-locating of laundromat stores as well as food and beverage brands Coco Fresh Tea & Juice and Potato Corner located inside select Alfamart stores to drive foot traffic
  • The SM Food Retail segment posted SSSG of +4.7% in the 1H25, with Alfamart SSSG +0% for the same period
    • As of end-Sep, Alfamart has 2,337 stores nationwide, having opened 245 stores so far this year
      • This is in line with the company's target to open around 300 stores in 2025
  • Alfamart, part of the SM Group’s (SM PM) retail food segment, operates as a joint venture with Indonesia-based PT Sumber Alfaria Trijaya Tbk (AMRT IJ)
UTILITIES
Maynilad eyes expansion opportunities in Bulacan, Cavite, Pasay’s Harbor City
  • Maynilad is evaluating adjacent areas of its West Zone concession – Bulacan, Cavite, and reclamation areas in Manila Bay – for possible expansions
    • It plans to partner with local water districts in Bulacan and Cavite to leverage on its existing West Zone infrastructure
    • New reclamation activities along the coast of Manila Bay (i.e. Navotas and Pasay) could also be tapped given proximity to the West Zone’s infrastructure
  • The West Zone covers 11 cities in Metro Manila and three cities and three municipalities in Cavite Province, including Bacoor, Imus, Kawit, Rosario, Noveleta, and Cavite City
MACRO
DOF tax reforms expected to generate P170bn in the next five years
  • The Department of Finance (DOF) expects to raise P170.9bn in the next five years from its recent tax reforms, according to the DBCC’s 2025 Mid-Year Report
  • About P3.8bn is expected to be collected this year
  • The reforms include:
    • VAT on Digital Services (RA 12023) – estimated to generate P126.8bn from 2025–2030; requires both local and foreign platforms to remit VAT on local transactions
    • CREATE MORE Act (RA 12066) – projected to reduce revenues by P13.1bn from 2025–2030 due to lower corporate income tax (25% from 30%) and expanded deductions on power and trade fair expenses
    • Capital Market Efficiency Promotion Act (CMEPA, RA 2214) – expected to raise P35bn from 2025–2030 by rationalizing capital market taxes and adding an excise tax on pickup trucks
    • Mining Fiscal Regime (RA 12253) – will take effect January 2026, projected to generate P32.1bn in incremental revenues
  • The revenue-to-GDP ratio is projected to rise from 15.9% in 2025 to 16.8% by 2030
  • Overall tax revenues are seen to grow 10.3% annually until 2030, reaching P7.13tn by then (from P4.52tn in 2025)
