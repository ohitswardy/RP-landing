Consumer loans in 2Q25 jumped 21.2% YoY, the fastest quarterly YoY pace in 10 years, and now make up 22.3% of total loans, a new all-time high. Meanwhile, Consumer NPL ratio improved to 5.33%, down 9bps QoQ and down 49bps YoY. Noteworthy was the continued surge in credit card loans, up 29.9% YoY, marking the 12th consecutive quarter of >25% YoY growth. To put that into perspective, CC loans hit P1.04trn in 2Q25, fast catching up to Mortgages at P1.15trn, and will likely be the largest slice of consumer loans in the next quarter or two. The continued shift in loan mix has resulted in both a) higher loan yields, but also b) higher provision expense across the banking sector. It remains to be seen if banks will be able to manage credit costs well enough to continue to post higher risk-adjusted returns.

BANKS
Consumer loans jump 21% YoY in 2Q25; NPLs ease, but…

Consumer Loans for the Philippine banking system jumped 21.2% YoY in 2Q25 to P3.4trn, accelerating from the 18.1% YoY pace in 1Q25. That’s the fastest quarterly YoY growth rate clocked since 1Q 2015. Industry-wide, consumer loans now account for 22.3% of total loans, a new all-time high. Consumer NPL ratio stood at 5.33%, down 9bps QoQ from 5.42% in 1Q25, and down 49bps YoY from the 5.82% in 2Q24. However, we note that the NPL ratios for mortgages and auto loans ticked up QoQ.

 

  • Mortgages grew 10.9% YoY to P1,152bn, slightly faster than the 10.8% pace in 1Q. Mortgage NPL ratio edged up 8bps QoQ, but was down 29bps YoY.
  • Motor Vehicle (MV) loans expanded 17.6% YoY to P656bn, slowing from the 18.5% in 1Q25. Of this, Auto loans were up 17.9% YoY (vs 19.4% in 1Q) and Motorcycle loans were up 13.8% (accelerating sharply from +3.7% in 1Q). MV NPLs were up 6bps QoQ to 4.99% though were down 73bps YoY.
  • Credit Card (CC) loans continued to surge 29.9% YoY to P1,036bn, marking the 12th consecutive quarter of >25% growth. This has put CC loans just shy of total mortgages as the largest category of consumer loans in the banking system. NPL ratio for CCs dipped 9bps QoQ to 4.51%, and down 5bps YoY.
  • Salary Loans jumped 37.0% YoY to P501bn, accelerating sharply from +15% in 1Q, with NPL ratio improving dramatically to 4.65% from 5.23% in 1Q25, and from 5.94% in 2Q24.

 

The continued surge in consumer loans explains both a) the higher NIMs, and b) higher credit costs experienced by the banking system in recent quarters. While the credit costs / provision expense seem worrying, it can be argued that the significantly higher loan yields more than compensate, and result in higher risk-adjusted returns. Only time will tell if the banks – collectively and individually – are able to balance the risk/reward calculus.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

TRANSPORT
Mexico plans to raise import tariffs on China, others to up to 50%
  • Mexico’s Economic Minister Marcelo Ebrard is proposing to raise tariffs on a multiple of goods (e.g. autos, textiles, steel) to up to 50% from 20% today
    • Tariffs will affect countries that do not have trade treaties with Mexico, which includes China, South Korea, India, Indonesia, Russia, Thailand, and Turkey
    • Plan will impact 8.6% of the country’s imports, based on the proposed document
  • Congress, which is controlled by the current administration, will have to approve the proposal
  • ICTSI’s port in Mexico is estimated to generate ~30% of throughput from imports of Chinese goods (i.e. 48% of total throughput are imports, of which ~60% originate from China)
    • We estimate the port in Mexico accounts for ~11% of volumes and ~25% of EBITDA
GAMING
Suntrust and Alliance Global’s Travellers to spend another US$450mn for IR project
  • Suntrust Resort (SUN PM) and Alliance Global’s gaming arm, Travellers, will spend another US$450mn to complete the integrated resort project in Westside City
  • The total cost of the integrated resort is US$1.25bn
    • About US$800mn has already been spent by Suntrust
  • Travellers owns 80% of Entertainment City Resorts Corporation, which will develop and operate the Westside Integrated Resort Project
    • Suntrust holds 20% of ECRC
    • The project consists of consists of a 5-star hotel, a casino complex, a mall, and a theater complex
  • The IR will have 280 gaming tables and 1,260 gaming machines
    • These will add 20% to table capacity and 13% to gaming machine capacity in Entertainment City
  • The target completion date is still 3Q26
  • AGI is trading at 3.4X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
MACRO
August tourist arrivals -3% YoY, 8M25 -2% YoY to 3.96mn
  • Data from the Department of Tourism (DOT) exhibited that tourist arrivals for August dropped 3% YoY to 457,438—bringing the 8M25 down by 2% YoY to 3.96mn
    • The 3.96mn figure is ~67% of the FY24 arrivals
      • 2024 saw 5.9mn tourist arrivals for the year, short of the gov't target of 7.7mn
      • The DOT has yet to provide its tourist arrivals target for 2025
  • YTD, Tourist arrivals from the USA and Japan were up 8% YoY and 19% YoY, respectively, but these were not enough to offset the decline in arrivals from Korea and China (-19% and -24%)
    • Tourism Secretary Christina Frasco noted that the drop in South Korean tourist arrivals was also seen in other Southeast Asian destinations such as Singapore (-2%), Thailand (-17%), Cambodia (-7%), and Vietnam (-2%)
  • Frasco highlighted difficulties with funding as the DOT's branding and promotions budget steadily declined over the years from ~P1bn before the pandemic, P1.2bn in 2023, P200mn in 2024, and P100mn in 2025
  • Tourism's share to GDP in 2024 was 8.9% (P2.35trn, +10.3% YoY) (Tourism Direct Gross Value Added [TDGVA])
    • 8.9% of GDP was the highest in five years but still lower than 2019's 12.9%
    • The DOT expects TDGVA to amount to P2.7trn (+15% YoY) this year

 

MACRO
Customs' revenues flat in August
  • Bureau of Customs (BOC) collected P78bn in revenues for the month of August, unchanged from the same month last year
    • External economic pressures, lower global oil prices, inclement weather, and August being a seasonally slow month were cited as reasons for the absence of growth in collections
  • Customs Assistant Commissioner Vincent Maronilla expects a recovery in collections during the '-ber' months
    • Maronilla also stated that the BOC is pushing for more anti-smuggling measures to offset an estimated P150bn in foregone revenues this year due to illicit trade
  • Total customs collections as of 8M25 amounted to P622bn, 1% higher YoY
    • The figure is 65% of the BOC's P959bn (+4.5% YoY) revised full-year collection target
      • The BOC's initial target set for this year was P990bn (+8% YoY)

 

 

CONGLOS
MPTC targets 2026 CALAX completion with new contractors to finish remaining works
  • MPCALA Holdings, a unit of Metro Pacific Tollways Corp. (MPTC), tapped EEI Corp. (EEI PM) and CM Pancho Construction, Inc. to finish remaining Cavite-Laguna Expressway (CALAX) works
    • Full completion is targeted by the first half of 2026
    • EEI will build the P2.7B subsection from Open Canal to Kawit, Cavite; CMPCI will handle the stretch from Governor’s Drive to Open Canal
  • The 45-km, four-lane CALAX will feature eight interchanges and serve around 95,000 motorists daily
  • Property companies (e.g. GTCAP [GTCAP PM], Ayala Land [ALI PM]) with landbank in the surrounding areas of Cavite could see land values increase once CALAX is completed
    • GTCAP’s property unit accounts for ~20% of NAV (assumes Cavite land value at ~P6k per sqm); GTCAP trades at a 40% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,112/sh
    • Land in Cavite and Laguna comprise ~35% of ALI’s NAV. ALI is trading at 44% discount to NAV (of P53.56/share), 1.4X FY25E P/B, and 14.2X FY25E P/E
