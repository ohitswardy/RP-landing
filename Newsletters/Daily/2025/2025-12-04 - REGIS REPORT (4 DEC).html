REGIS REPORT

04 December 2025

One of the three members of the fact-finding commission investigating the corruption involving “flood control” funds, has resigned. Rogelio Singson, former Public Works Secretary and a former CEO of Maynilad Water (MYNLD PM), tendered his resignation from the Independent Commission for Infrastructure (ICI) effective Dec 15, only a few months after he was appointed.

 

The reasons for his resignation are unclear (and thus fodder for rumor), but we would view his departure as a negative. This development would, in our view, harm the credibility of the investigation/s.

 

+++

 

Port operator ICTSI (ICT PM) is applying to renew its contract to operate the Manila International Container Terminal (MICT) beyond 2038. The concession expiry is not until 2038, but ICT is already moving to have it extended this early.

 

There are legal issues against an extension, but we reckon ICT’s lawyers have likely prepared their case arguing in favor of such. We are inclined to believe that the regulator (Philippine Ports Authority or PPA) is friendly given ICT’s long-term presence and track record. However, a concession extension on the exact same terms seems unlikely. One could reasonably expect the PPA to (1) open it up to a 3rd party challenge or, at the very least, (2) make the financial terms less favorable for the operator in exchange for an extension.

 

The MICT accounts for ~20% of ICT’s EBITDA.

 

+++

 

President Marcos signed Executive Order #107 raising salaries the military and other uniformed personnel. The increase is a modest 15% over three years beginning next year. Since the adjustment involves an increase in base pay, it would also affect pension costs, which are indexed to base pay. The unfunded pension liability for uniformed personnel is enormous (>US$20bn) and could balloon further.

Rogelio Singson resigns as ICI commissioner

  • Singson was the former secretary of the Dept. of Public Works and Highways during the late President Noynoy Aquino's administration from 2010-2016
    • He also oversaw the Good Governance and Anti-Corruption Program at the time

  • ICT trades at 10x FY26E EV/EBITDA, below peer average of 12.6x (median: 11.7x) and historical average of 11x
MACRO
President Marcos announces base pay hike for military, uniformed personnel
  • President Marcos signed Executive Order No. 107, which hikes the base pay of military and uniformed personnel (MUP) by 15% over three years, beginning Jan 1, 2026
    • He also ordered an increase in MUP’s Subsistence Allowance to P350 per day from P150 starting on Jan 1, 2026
    • Move will also consequently increase the pay of regular and survivorship MUP pensioners, as pensions are indexed to the salary of active personnel
    • More than 470k active military personnel will reportedly benefit from the hike
  • Funds for the increase in the base pay and subsistence allowance will come from available appropriations in the 2026 national budget and other allowable sources
    • Future increases will form part of the national budget
  • Under EO 107, an Inter-Agency Technical Working Group (lATWG) will also be created to conduct a comprehensive review of the current MUP pension system
  • Recall that former President Duterte raised salaries for MUPs by an average of 59% in 2018 and 72% in 2019

BSP says rate cut next week more likely given slower growth outlook
  • Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona stated that the current slower growth outlook implies a higher chance for another rate cut during next week's Monetary Board meeting on Dec 11
    • Remolona added that GDP growth likely slowed to 4-5% for 2025, lower than the gov't target range of 5.5-6.5% for the year
  • For 2025 and 2026, consensus expects GDP growth to be 4.9% and 5.4%, respectively
    • Regis expects GDP growth to be 4.8% and 5.5%
  • On rate cut expectations, consensus and Regis expect 125bp total worth of cuts to 4.50% in 2025
