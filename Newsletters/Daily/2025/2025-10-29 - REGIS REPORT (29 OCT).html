REGIS REPORT

29 October 2025

The Philippine Peso depreciated to a record low yesterday, with the PHP:US$ rate closing at P59.13. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) said that the recent weakness reflected market concerns over economic growth prospects due to the infrastructure spending scandal as well as expectations of possible further easing by the BSP. It also said that it would "allow the exchange rate to be determined by market forces".

Mining firms, such as Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) and Semirara (SCC PM) are the main beneficiaries. On the other hand, Cebu Air (CEB PM), GT Capital (GTCAP PM)  as well as most of the consumer companies are losers in a weak peso scenario.   

 

For reference, we forecast the peso to depreciate to P61:US$ by the end of 2026.

US Senator Bernie Moreno, a Republican from Ohio, introduced the Halting International Relocation of Employment (HIRE) Bill, which would 1) prohibit outsourcing payments from being tax-deductible, and 2) impose a 25% excise tax on outsourcing payments. We would expect this bill to face opposition and lobbying from US firms so the bill might not advance further. In addition, we reckon that even if it were to become law, the cost savings of outsourcing from the US to the Philippines would still be substantial.

MACRO
BSP releases statement on recent peso depreciation
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) stated that it will "allow the exchange rate to be determined by market forces" as the USD-PHP rate closed at a record low, P59.13 by end of day, breaching the P59 level for the first time
    • The central bank also emphasized that intervention will be done "to dampen inflationary swings" in the exchange rate over time and not to address day-to-day volatility
  • Recent peso depreciation was said to reflect market concerns over dampened economic growth prospects due to the infra spending scandal as well as expectations of possible further easing by the BSP
    • Recall that the BSP cut earlier than expected in Oct and guided for another cut 25bp in Dec, with further cuts possible in 2026
  • Consensus and Regis expect USD-PHP to be at P57.25 and P59 by end-2025, and at P57.9 and P61 by end-2026, respectively

 

 

CONSUMER
Po family injects capital through parent holding firm of Century Pacific Food, Shakey's Pizza
  • The Po family injected capital into its primary holding company, Century Pacific Group Inc. (CPGI), through Zephryn Holdings which is reported to also be owned by the Po family
  • Zephryn Holdings, an entity under the Po family, secured indirect ownership in Century Pacific Food (CNPF PM) and Shakey’s Pizza (PIZZA PM) at 29.9% and 29.15%, respectively, through a 45.7% stake acquired via a new redeemable preferred share issuance in CPGI
  • The transaction made Zephryn Holdings, a Po family vehicle, a new beneficial owner of CNPF—but no listed shares were sold as the family’s overall ownership in CNPF and free float of the listed company remained unchanged following the internal restructuring

 

 

PROPERTY
US Senator introduces bill to penalize international outsourcing
  • In September 2025, US Senator Bernie Moreno (Republican from Ohio) filed the Halting International Relocation of Employment (HIRE) Bill
  • The bill would
    • 1) prohibit outsourcing payments from being tax-deductable, and
    • 2) impose a 25% excise tax on outsourcing payments
  • The IT-BPO Association previously estimated that outsourcing work to the Philippines (from the US) could result in cost savings of 70%-80%
  • If the HIRE Bill were to become law, we estimate the cost savings could drop to 60%-70%
  • About 70% of the Philippine BPO industry caters to North America
  • About 70% of BPO revenue is from voice operations
  • BPO firms account for 60%-80% of the occupied office space of the developers we cover
  • MEG is trading at 70% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY26E P/B, and 3.1X FY26E P/E
  • RLC is trading at 69% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY26E P/B, and 5.0X FY26E P/E
