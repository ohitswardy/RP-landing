REGIS REPORT

21 October 2025

Maynilad Water, the operator of the West Zone water concession in Metro Manila, has priced its IPO at P15/share. This price would place Maynilad’s market cap at P113bn, equivalent to a PER of ~8x, EV/EBITDA of 6x and a dividend yield of ~6%. More importantly, we estimate Maynilad’s EV/OCP at 1.1x, where OCP (opening cash position) is equivalent to the net present value (NPV) of the concession discounted at 12% (pretax).

 

For comparison, Manila Water (MWC PM) is is trading at ~5x EV/OCP, 7.5x PER, 7.6x EV/EBITDA, and div yield of ~5%.

 

+++

 

Semirara’s (SCC PM) declared dividend of P1.25/share fell short of our expectations (P2.00/share). Combined with the previous dividend declared earlier this year (P2.00/share), the dividend yield for this year would be ~10%, which is still better than most.

 

 

+++

 

The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) approved an increase to P0.2073/kwh of the Feed-in-Tariff Allowance (FIT-All) collected from consumers. The increased amount would allow the RE gencos selling to the FIT program to be paid in full.

 

+++

 

The Philippines generated a small BOP surplus in Sept, bringing the YT-Sept to a US$5.3bn deficit.

Maynilad IPO price set at P15/sh
  • Maynilad has set its IPO price at P15/sh, which would put its market capitalization at P113bn assuming full subscription of offer shares
    • Price is lower than the initial ceiling of P20/sh
    • Media sources say IPO is nearly two times oversubscribed
    • We estimate that at P15/sh, Maynilad’s valuation metrics are: 1.1x EV/OCP, ~8x PER, 6x EV/EBITDA, and div yield of ~6%
  • Retail sale period will be from Oct 23-29; Listing date on Nov 7
MINING
Semirara declares P1.25/sh special dividend
  • Semirara declared a special divided of P1.25/sh, payable to shareholders of record as of November 4 (ex-date on Nov 3)
    • The dividend fell short of consensus’ median estimate of P1.35/sh and Regis’ estimate of P2/sh
    • This dividend tranche is equivalent to a yield of 4%
    • Payment date on Nov 20
  • Together with the P2/sh dividend declared early this year, SCC has paid a total of P3.25/sh this year, equivalent to a yield of ~10%
    • Cumulative 2025 dividend of P3.25/sh is equivalent to a payout ratio of 70%, down from last year’s payout ratio of 91% and the lowest since 2020
    • Note that the company guided for capex of P6.9bn this year, up from P5.3bn in 2024 for re-fleeting and equipment acquisitions
    • In the event of a non-extension of its coal operating contract (contract expires in July 2027), Semirara will retain ownership of all its mining equipment, port facilities, and other infrastructure in the island
POWER
ERC approves 74% increase in FIT-All tariff to P0.2073/kwh
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) has increased the Feed-in Tariff Allowance (FIT-All) effective November 2025, to PHP0.2073 per kilowatt-hour (kWh)
    • The current rate under the Feed-in Tariff program is PHP0.1189/kWh
    • This new rate covers outstanding payments and maintains a small buffer fund to prevent delays in future payments to RE producers
    • It considers the required funds to settle a PHP19.06bn FIT differential and build a PHP3.74bn working capital allowance, which serves as buffer to guarantee timely payments to the RE producers
  • We anticipate the new rate should alleviate collection issues of RE companies under the FIT system, such as ACEN Corp. (ACEN PM)
MACRO
BOP posts US$82mn surplus in Sep; GIR settles at US$109.1bn
  • The country's overall balance of payments (BOP) for the month posted a surplus of US$82mn
    • Narrower than both the US$3.5bn surplus in Sep 2024 and Aug 2025's US$359mn surplus
    • The smaller surplus in Sep reflected lower forex inflows and a still-wide trade gap
  • This brings the YT-Sep deficit at US$5.3bn, reversing from the US$5.1bn surplus in the same period last year

    • The reversal to a deficit stems from the timing of gov’t debt issuances and the continued trade in goods gap

  • The BSP projection for the overall BOP position for 2025 is a US$6.9bn deficit (-1.4% of GDP)
    • The BOP projection for 2025 reflects expectations of slower global trade and subdued investor confidence given uncertainties from trade policy and geopolitical developments
  • Meanwhile, the final GIR level for end-Sep stood at US$109.1bn, up from the $107.1bn in end-Aug
    • The GIR level is reflective of an external liquidity buffer equivalent to 7.3 months’ worth of imports of goods and payments of services and primary income
    • GIR is expected to decrease from $106.3bn in 2024 to $105.0bn in 2025

 

 

TELCOS
Converge open to asset co-sharing with Konektadong Pinoy entrants
  • Converge ICT Solutions Inc. (CNVRG PM) expects to increase its fiber utilization by sharing the usage of its fiber infrastructure network with the new data transmission industry participants (DTIPs) after the Konektadong Pinoy Act (KPA) takes effect
    • Converge CEO Dennis Anthony Uy stated that the initiative can generate additional cash for the company
  • The law requires existing providers to co-share their infrastructure network with DTIPs, this provision can boost utilization of Converge's underutilized fiber network
    • As of June, Converge manages 9.05mn ports across its fiber network nationwide but is only currently serving 2.82mn customers
  • The KPA lapsed into law back in Aug and its IRR is pending finalization
PROPERTY
Megaworld to subscribe to MREIT shares at P14.39/share
  • MREIT seeks SEC approval to increase its authorized capital stock from P5bn to P8bn (at P1/share)
  • Its sponsor, Megaworld, will subscribe to 25% of this increase (750mn shares) at P14.39/share – P10.8bn
    • This is higher than the 30-day VWAP and 3% higher than the last closing price of P14.00/share
  • The share issuance will increase MREIT’s share outstanding by 20%
    • We estimate that the AGI Group’s ownership in MREIT will increase from 58% to 65%
  • This will likely be used for a share-for-property swap with its sponsor, Megaworld
  • MREIT is targeting to grow its leasable area to 600k sqm by end-2025 and to 1mn sqm by 2027
    • It currently has a leasable portfolio of 480k sqm
  • MEG is trading at 70% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 3.3X FY25E P/E
  • MREIT currently offers 7.3% FY25E dividend yield (based on consensus forecast)
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 5.9%
