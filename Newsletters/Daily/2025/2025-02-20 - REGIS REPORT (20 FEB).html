In what looks like a bid to give an “advance” warning to the market, noodle and biscuit maker Monde (MONDE PM) provided guidance regarding another impairment charge for its Quorn business ahead of its official FY24 results release. The good news though is that indications for its core APAC BFB business, however, look solid with top line growth accelerating to 8% in 4Q. We reckon we’re seeing the beginnings of a rebound not just for MONDE but also for the rest of the consumer space.

Metrobank (MBT PM) just announced FY24 results this morning, ahead of the earnings briefing this afternoon.

 

Net profits reached P48.1bn for the year, up 14% YoY, in line with Street and Regis estimates. Loan growth looked quite strong at 17.0%, even faster than the 15.6% as of 3Q. The NPL ratio ended the year at 1.43%, with NPL coverage staying at very comfortable 164%.

 

The bank also announced a P5/share dividend for this year (=7% yield), with the initial P3.5/share to be paid next month. 

 

We should have more information after the call later.

 

 

+++

 

Ayala Land (ALI PM) also announced their FY24 results, with their earnings briefing this afternoon at 2pm.

 

FY24 net profit of Ayala Land (ALI PM) rose 15% to P28.2bn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. FY24 reservation sales grew 12% to P127.1bn, also in line with REGIS forecast. However, some investors might be concerned that 4Q24 reservation sales fell 5% YoY and 17% QoQ.

 

We hope to find out more after the briefing later today.

AREIT (AREIT PM) will issue 506mn new shares to Ayala Land (ALI PM) in exchange for P21bn worth of mall, office, and hotel assets. We estimate that Ayala Land will need to sell about 75mn AREIT shares in order for the latter to meet the minimum float requirement.

The 20% investment of Tokyo Gas in First Gen’s (FGEN PM) LNG terminal was finally completed, six years after the initial announcement of cooperation between the two parties.  We understand the valuation for Tokyo Gas’ entry is roughly the project cost, which is likely to include cost of money.

  • AREIT will acquire P21bn worth of malls, offices, and hotels from Ayala Land in exchange for 505.9mn new AREIT shares

POWER
Tokyo Gas completes purchase of 20% stake in FGEN's LNG unit
  • Tokyo Gas Co., Ltd. (9531 JT), Japan's largest natural gas utility company, completed its purchase of a 20% stake in FGEN LNG Corp., the liquefied natural gas (LNG) terminal subsidiary of First Gen Corp. (FGEN PM)
    • FGEN LNG Holdings Corp., a wholly owned subsidiary of FGEN, will own the majority 80% stake
    • The transaction value was not disclosed
  • Recall that last May 2024, FGEN LNG Holdings inked a subscription agreement involving the purchase of a minority stake in FGEN LNG Corp.
    • Prior to the deal, Tokyo Gas partnered with First Gen back in Dec 2018 for the construction of the terminal
  • Notably, FGEN earlier this year secured a 25-year permit from the Department of Energy to operate and maintain the offshore gas terminal
  • As of March 2024, FGEN operates power plants that run on solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, natural gas with a capacity of 3,668MW

MACRO
NFA releases rice buffer stocks
  • The National Food Authority (NFA) started to release rice buffer stocks to local gov't units (LGUs) who will sell the rice at P33-P35/kg
    • This is in part of the Department of Agriculture's (DA) initiatives to lower rice prices as it also placed staggered price caps on rice since last month
      • The current imported rice price cap is at P52/kg since Feb. 15, set to go down to P49/kg by March
  • 25,000MT of rice per month from the NFA's total stock of 300,000MT will be released which will last up to a year, but total volume may be increased if deemed necessary given that P9bn was allocated for NFA's rice procurement
  • From the DA's latest price monitoring as of Feb 17, price for local well-milled rice is at P40-54/kg while imported well-milled rice is priced at P44-45/kg
MACRO
Government eyes incentives for four-wheeled ICE vehicle production
  • The government expects to complete a joint administrative order (JAO) for the implementation of the Revitalizing the Automotive Industry for Competitiveness Enhancement (RACE) program
    • Details of the JAO are expected to be released by mid-March and applications could be opened by April or May
  • A program briefer from Board of Investments (BOI) executive director Corazon Halili-Dichosa showed that the government would provide P9bn in fiscal support for the program
    • This will cover the production of three models of four-wheeled internal combustion engine (ICE) vehicles
    • Each participating car maker can get fiscal support worth up to P3bn for tooling and equipment, as well as fixed investment support (FIS), which will cover up to 40% of capex
    • Each participant will have to invest in vehicle manufacturing; produce 100k units; introduce the enrolled model to the market within two years
    • The fiscal support will be provided through the tax payment certificate scheme
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) plans to enroll the “Tamaraw” in the RACE program
    • TMP recently invested P5.5bn to produce the Tamaraw to produce about 20k units annually
