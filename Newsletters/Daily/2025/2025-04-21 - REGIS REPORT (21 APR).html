21 April 2025

President Ferdinand Marcos, Jr’s popularity cratered in March, according to a PulseAsia survey. The report showed a sharp drop in approvals (from 42% to 25% in February). But more crucially, it showed the majority of those surveyed 53% disapprove of the President, up from 32% the month before. While firm did not mention it, we reckon the only major event between the two survey dates was the arrest and extradition of former President Rodrigo Duterte, as seen in the surge in the Senate rankings of Duterte allies. Despite this, it seems likely that Marcos’s coalition will retain control of the Lower House, and will nominally have majority in the Senate. But the recent ratings could see shifts in allegiances in the Senate, which could play a role in the impeachment trial of VP Sara Duterte scheduled for July.

 

What’s this got to do with the markets? If the ratings stay where they are, the administration may be tempted to push more populist policies to bolster their standing. According to the PulseAsia survey, the top two concerns of respondents are inflation and wages. Subsidies, handouts, and minimum wage hikes would seem easy ways to improve sentiment short-term, but could have negative implications down the line

+++

 

Electricity sales volumes of Meralco (MER PM) grew 1.5% YoY for 1Q25. This is a notable slowdown from the historical ~5% average, though this is partly due to the high base in 1Q24 (up +9%).  The warmer-than-normal temperatures in 1Q24 contributed to the high base. We note that volume growth for the commercial segment was a mere 1% in 1Q25.

 

+++

 

The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) approved the revenue application of the transmission operator National Grid Corp (NGCP), allowing it to not only keep the revenue it collected over the prior regulatory period (up to 2022) without clawback, but also charge an extra P28bn worth of under-recoveries.  Further details have yet to be made available.  The decision has been pending since 2023, if not earlier.  We had in fact been expecting that NGCP could be ordered to retroactively reduce rates and pay consumers back a refund.

 

Note that the recently created sovereign wealth fund Maharlika announced it was acquiring 20% of NGCP in Jan 2025. Listed entity Synergy Grid (SGP PM) holds an indirect controlling interest in NGCP, with an economic interest of 40% as of end-2024.

 

This ERC ruling was apparently approved by three of the five commissioners.  Two of the commissioners, including the chair, voted against the ruling favoring NGCP.

 

+++

 

Suntrust Resort Holdings (SUN PM) said that its integrated resort in Entertainment City will open in 4Q25. Upon opening, the resort will have 280 gaming tables and 1,300 gaming machines, which would add 20% to Entertainment city’s table capacity and 13% to its gaming machine capacity. As we noted previously, Entertainment City already has a demand problem due to the POGO ban. Soon, it will also see an increase in supply. This will have repercussions for Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM), Belle Corp (BEL PM), and to a lesser extent, Alliance Global (AGI PM), which all have integrated casino resorts in Entertainment City.    

 

+++

 

DoubleDragon (DD PM) is acquiring a 35% interest in affiliate Merry Mart (MM PM) for P1.28bn, which equivalent to P0.48 per MM share.  DD will acquire shares from the common founder of both firms, Edgar “Injap” Sia. The consideration will be paid for half with cash and half with DD shares. DD will subsequently conduct a tender offer for the remaining shares of MM. It is curious that DD is only acquiring 35% of the 79% that Sia owns right now.

 

From around P7bn in 2024, MM was aiming to reach P500bn of revenue by 2035.

Meralco energy sales growth slows to 1.5% YoY in 1Q25
  • Meralco’s consolidated energy sales volume rose 1.5% to 12,493 GWH in 1Q25, moderating from the 4% pace in 4Q24 and 6% in FY24
    • According to Senior VP and Chief Revenue Officer Ferdinand O. Geluz
    • Residential sales volumes rose by 3% to 4,257 GWH (vs. +6% in 4Q24 and 9% in FY24)
    • Commercial sales posted a 1% YoY increase to 4,744 GWH, buoyed by robust demand from consumer-facing establishments but offset by slowdown in real estate
      • This compares with the +5% growth rate for the segment in 4Q24 and +7% in FY24
    • Industrial sales rose 0.2% YoY to 3,455 GWH, as gains from non-metallic, semicon, and plastic sectors were muted by the downturn in F&B and steel sectors
      • Sales to industrial customers remain lackluster (+1% in 4Q24/FY24)
  • Mr. Geluz is optimistic energy sales will pick up in 2Q25 and reach the company’s full-year target of 4.5%
POWER/UTILITIES
ERC allows NGCP to recover P28.3bn worth of under-recoveries
  • The ERC said in a statement that it approved a maximum allowable revenue of P336bn for NGCP for its fourth regulatory period (4th RP) rate reset spanning 2016 to 2022, allowing the utility to collect P28bn worth of under-recoveries
    • NGCP is allowed to collect an additional P0.1013 per kwh in transmission charges over the next 84 months from the issuance of the decision
    • Amount covers the P0.0629 per kWh average increase in the basic transmission charge and additional P0.0384 per kWh corresponding to the under-recovered portion of the increased MAR
  • Commissioners Alexis M. Lumbatan, Floresinda G. Baldo-Digal, and Marko Romeo L. Fuentes ruled in favor while ERC Chairperson and CEO Monalisa Dimalanta and Commissioner Catherine Maceda dissented
    • Majority of the commissioners used the “As spent” approach, which allows NGCP’s capital expenses to form part of the asset base regardless of if the project is completed or not, and adopted a WACC of 11.33%
  • The ruling, as well as the dissenting opinions of Ms. Dimalanta and Ms. Maceda, will be “issued in due course
  • Synergy Grid Corp. (SGP PM) indirectly controls 60% of National Grid Corp’s (NGCP) voting capital stock and has an effective equity interest of 40.2%
