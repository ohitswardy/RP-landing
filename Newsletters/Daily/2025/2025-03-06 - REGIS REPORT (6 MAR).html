REGIS REPORT

06 March 2025

With headline inflation easing to 2.1%, the inevitable question now is when (if) the Bangko Sentral (BSP) will cut rates.  This Feb inflation print is the last one the BSP Monetary Board will see before it meets on April 3.  The likelihood of a policy rate reduction at that meeting is now looking high – even the Dept of Finance (DOF) appears to be hinting at such.

+++

At Nickel Asia’s (NIKL PM) briefing yesterday, management explained that 4Q profits for the mining unit were affected by bad weather, which limited their ability to take advantage of higher ore export prices. Given (a) elevated ore export prices and (b) production ramp-up of new mines, which have higher saprolite ore reserves, management was upbeat about near-term earnings prospects and did not rule out the possibility of additional special dividends. They also discussed future investments in power and nickel processing.

+++

The Capital Markets Efficiency Promotions bill has been approved by the bicameral committee, which means it will now be up for final ratification before it is handed to Malacañang for the President’s signature.  Once enacted, the bill will reduce some of the friction costs related to the trading of shares at the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE PM). Among these costs is the stock transaction tax, which the bill will lower to 0.1% (from 0.6%).

+++

The preliminary numbers announced by San Miguel Food and Beverage (FB PM) showed relative resilience in 4Q top line, with growth in beer sales accelerating to +8% for 4Q alone.  The liquor business saw +15% growth in revenue for 4Q.

+++

Belle Corp (BEL PM) says that it is not pursuing a buyout of the Melco Resorts’ (200 HK) contract to operate City of Dreams Manila (CODM).  Melco was reportedly seeking to exit the contract for US$1bn, which is approximately 9x EBITDA.

MACRO
Inflation rate eases to 2.1% in February
  • Headline inflation eased to 2.1% in February from 2.9% in the previous month, marking the lowest print since Sept 2024
    • This is lower than consensus forecast of 2.6% and came in below the BSP's forecast range of 2.2-3.0%
  • On a MoM basis, CPI dropped 0.2%, primarily driven by lower food and diesel prices
    • Food & non-alcoholic beverages saw a drop in prices 0.9% MoM for the first time since Sept 2024
      • This is as vegetables dipped 12.4% MoM with prices starting to normalize after the onslaught of typhoons last 4Q24
      • Rice also saw its eighth consecutive month of price decline (-1.6% MoM), taking the cumulative decline to 6.2%
  • Core inflation dipped slightly to 2.4% in Feb from 2.6% in Jan, with core non-food inflation falling to 2.2% from 2.5% for the same periods
  • Notably, inflation for the bottom 30% income group dipped to 1.5%, down from 2.4% in Jan
  • This brings YT-Feb headline inflation rate to 2.5%
    • The gov't targets to keep inflation within 2-4% for this year
    • Bloomberg median consensus is for the full year average is 3.0%
    • We expect inflation to average 3.5%

MACRO
DOF: Slower inflation gives more leeway for rate cut
  • The Department of Finance in a statement said that the slower inflation figure in Feb is "creating more room for potential monetary policy rate easing."
  • The DOF also emphasized the gov't's commitment to mitigating price pressures and long-term price stability
  • Recall that the BSP last Monetary Board meeting left rates unchanged at 5.75% citing continued global macro uncertainty
    • The next MB meeting (second out of six this year) is scheduled on April 3
  • Bloomberg median consensus currently expects 75 bps worth of rate cuts this year
    • We expect the BSP to cut its policy rate by 50 bps to 5.25%
MACRO
Bicam approves bill on capital markets reform
  • The Bicameral Conference Committee ratified the Capital Markets Efficiency Promotions bill
    • A copy of the bicameral conference committee report of the measure was not immediately available
  • Key features of the bill:
    • Lowering the stock transaction tax from 0.6% to 0.1% to encourage stock market activity
    • The documentary stamp tax (DST) on the original issue of shares of stock will be lowered from 1% to 0.75% to reduce transaction costs for investors
    • The DST on unit investment trust funds (UITFs) will also be removed
    • A 50% tax deduction will be introduced for employers' contributions to their employees’ personal equity and retirement accounts to encourage businesses to help employees save for retirement
    • The authorized capital stock of DBP will be increased from P35 billion to P300 billion to strengthen its financial capacity.
    • The National Government will be required to maintain at least 70% ownership of DBP at all times
      • Of this, P32 billion (10.67%) will be fully subscribed and paid for by the government.
    • DBP will be allowed to conduct an Initial Public Offering (IPO) to raise additional capital
CONSUMER
San Miguel Food & Beverage FY24 net income up 7% to 40.9bn
  • In a press release, San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) stated that its FY24 net income went up 7% to P40.9bn
    • Figure implies that 4Q24 net profit is down 1% to P10.5bn
  • FY24 food segment revenue grew 3% to P185bn
    • The company noted that for FY24, there was strong demand for Purefoods Tender Juicy Hotdogs, Magnolia dairy, and San Mig Coffee
    • 4Q24 food sales grew 9% QoQ and 3% YoY
  • FY24 beer revenue rose 4% to P153.4bn
    • Implied 4Q24 beer sales surged 17% QoQ and 8% YoY, likely due to holiday seasonality
  • FY24 spirits revenue climbed 17% to 62.5bn
    • GSMI's sales for 2024 continued to grow given "strong demand and increased popularity among a wider range of consumers"
    • 4Q24 spirits sales grew 9% QoQ and 15% YoY
  • We hope to get more details after the briefing, although do note that there has been no scheduled date disclosed by the company as of late

GAMING
Melco reportedly asking for US$1bn for City of Dreams Manila contract
  • According to Bilyonaryo.com, Melco Resorts (200 HK) is looking to sell its contract in City of Dreams Manila for US$1bn
    • The contract with Belle Corp (BEL PM) is until 2033
  • GGR of City of Dreams Manila fell 1% to US$531mn
  • Adjusted EBITDA rose 2% to US$211mn
  • Melco’s share of the EBITDA was US$111mn
  • Assuming the US$1bn represents enterprise value, then it is equivalent to 9X FY24 EV/EBITDA
  • Belle said that “…any buy-out of Melco’s interest in COD Manila is not part of Belle’s plans for the immediate future”
POWER
ACEN investing US$13mn for solar projects in Malaysia
  • ACEN is investing US$13mn to build solar plants in Malaysia with a combined capacity of 80MW
    • Project will be under its joint venture company, IBV ACEN Renewables Asia, which is a partnership with ib vogt Singapore
  • As of Oct 31, 2025, ACEN’s total attributable RE capacity stood at 6,754 MW
    • Its operating RE capacity stood at 3,051 MW, of which 1,067MW is in the Philippines
    • It is currently constructing 2,302 MW, of which 1,330MW is in the Philippines
    • Another 1,401 MW of new capacity is “committed”

SM Prime to expand hotel portfolio
