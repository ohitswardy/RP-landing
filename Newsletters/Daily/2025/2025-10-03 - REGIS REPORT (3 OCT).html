03 October 2025

Loans of universal and commercial banks grew at a robust pace of 11.2% YoY in August, just a tad slower than the July figure (+11.8% YoY). Consumer loans continued to lead, with credit card growth accelerating slightly to 29.7% YoY.

 

In his latest GREED & fear report, Christopher Wood noted that the strong growth in credit card loans has made them nearly as large as mortgage loans and that “Clearly, the scale of this borrowing raises some potential risks…”

 

Check out his other comments on the Philippines in the attached note.

Ayala Corp. (AC PM) announced its intention to exit all its nine (9) Honda dealerships by Jan 1, 2026, in line with its ongoing efforts to rationalize its portfolio.  The recent exits from Volkswagen, Maxus, and Honda underscore the group’s focus on expanding its BYD operations, which has been experiencing strong sales momentum.

The Legislative-Executive Development Advisory Council (LEDAC) announced its list of 44 priority bills. The bills cover a swath of topics, including governance (requiring civil servants to waive bank secrecy), social services (universal healthcare and cash handouts to the poor), and energy security.

 

There is also a proposed “Law on Online Gambling” (as compared to an “Anti-online Gambling Law”) which suggests to us that there will likely be tighter regulation on online gambling but not an outright ban.

BANKS
Commercial banks’ loan growth 11.2% in Aug; M3 growth at 6.6%
  • Loans of commercial banks rose by 11.2% YoY in Aug, moderating slightly from the 11.8% growth in July
    • This marks the slowest growth rate in nine months since Nov 2024's +11.1%
    • Loans to productive activities slowed to 9.9% YoY, slowing down from the 10.8% in July due to decreased lending in key industries such as
      • Elec, gas, steam (+28.1% from +30.3%), trade (+8.1% from +8.5%), and finance, insurance (+6.9% from +13.1%)
      • Loans to manufacturers, declined for the fourth consecutive month, -5.5% in Aug from a -6.3% in July
    • Consumer loans grew 23.9%, inching up from the 23.6% pace in July
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) picked up to 6.6% in Aug, accelerating from the 6.2% growth in July

 

 

 

CONGLOS
ACMobility exits Honda dealerships
  • Ayala Corp. announced the transfer of ACMobility’s operations of Honda dealerships to “new dealer principals” by Jan 1, 2026
    • Transition aims to streamline portfolio and focus on new growth areas in the electric vehicle space
    • ACMobility, through Iconic Dealership Inc., operates nine (9) Honda dealerships in the country
    • AC first partnered with Honda in 1990
  • We understand that AC anticipates to book asset write-offs and employee separation costs but the final amount will be determined before year-end
  • This move follows recent exits from the Volkswagen and Maxus distribution business
    • By Jan 1, ACMobility will only carry the BYD, Kia, and Isuzu automobile brands
MACRO
44 priority bills to be pushed in the 20th Congress
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. and Congress leaders in a Legislative-Executive Development Advisory Council (LEDAC) meeting identified 44 priority bills under the Common Legislative Agenda of the 20th Congress
    • The bills are said to be focused on improving the investment climate, modernizing governance as well as the agri sector, expanding social services, and ensuring energy security
  • Some key measures in the list are as follows:
    • Proposed amendments to the Bank Deposits Secrecy Law and Anti-Money Laundering Act
    • A progressive budgeting bill and proposals which require civil servants to waive bank secrecy protections
    • Masterplan for Infrastructure and National Development
    • Amendments to a number of social programs such as the 4Ps, Universal Health Care Act, gov't assistance to students and teachers, free college education, among others
    • Digital Payments Act and legislation on online gambling which seek to secure online transactions and ensure responsible use of digital platforms
    • National Land Use Act which seeks to create a national level framework for land allocation to enforce sustainable land use to prevent environmental degradation, overdevelopment, conversion of farmland to non-agricultural uses, etc.
    • EPIRA Amendments which seeks to strengthen the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC)
    • The establishment of a Dept. of Water Resources
