REGIS REPORT

29 September 2025

With the SEC approval of another acquisition by AREIT (AREIT PM) of a number of assets of Ayala Land (ALI PM) -- mall, office and hotel assets in Cebu, Davao and Cagayan de Oro – the market cap of AREIT will rise by another 16% from the current P139bn (US$2.4bn).

 

AREIT and Robinsons Land’s (RLC PM) REIT subsidiary RL Commercial (RCR PM) have been growing steadily through asset injections. RCR is, today, only slightly larger than AREIT with a market cap of P150bn (US$2.6bn). AREIT is the 25th largest Philippine listed company by market cap while RCR is the 23rd.

 

Meanwhile, the SEC is planning amendments to the REIT Law that are likely to make things more enticing for potential REIT listings. The current SEC chairman Atty Francis Lim was a key proponent of the REIT Law and encouraging REITs appears to be an advocacy for him.

 

+++

 

Meralco (MER PM) is contemplating the injection next year of its renewable energy assets under MGREEN into the listed vehicle SP New Energy Corp (SPNEC PM), which it also controls. SPNEC has a number of small projects and one big one: the 3,500MW MTerra Solar project, which is supposed to be completed by 1H27.

 

This is an idea that’s been floated before and that’s waiting for the right time.

 

+++

 

The consumer confidence survey conducted by the Bangko Sentral (BSP) for 3Q showed a slight improvement (-9.8% from -14.0%) as consumers were slightly less pessimistic about the future in the latest quarter.

PROPERTY
SEC approves share-for-property swap between Ayala Land and AREIT
  • The SEC approved a previously announced share-for-property swap transaction between Ayala Land and AREIT
  • AREIT will acquire P21bn worth of assets from Ayala Land in exchange for 505.9mn new AREIT shares
    • This will increase AREIT’s AUM by 18% and its shares outstanding by 16%
    • The mall, office, and hotel assets are located in Cebu, Davao, and Cagayan de Oro
  • ALI is trading at 53% discount to NAV, 1.2X FY25E P/B, and 12.0X FY25E P/E
  • AREIT currently offers 5.5% FY25E dividend yield
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 6.0%
PROPERTY
Draft amendments on REIT regulations to be released in October
  • SEC Commissioner McJill Bryant Fernandez told the Philippine Daily Inquirer that the SEC was working on amendments to the REIT law’s provision on tax income deductibility
    • Commissioner Fernandez did not elaborate further
  • The draft regulations will be released for public comments in October
  • REITs are currently required to pay out at least 90% of their distributable income as dividends
    • REITs only need to pay income tax on the portion of distributable income that was not paid out as dividends
  • SEC Chair Francis Lim previously said that the changes being considered included:
    • Allowing funds raised from REIT issuances to be reinvested within three years (from one year currently)
    • Allowing REIT proceeds to be used for debt repayment
POWER
MGEN eyes backdoor listing of RE unit, MGreen, next year
  • Meralco PowerGen (MGEN) is studying the possibility of listing its renewable energy unit, MGen Renewable Energy Inc. (MGreen) via SP New Energy Corp. (SPNEC PM) next year
    • According to MGEN president and CEO, Emmanuel Rubio
    • MGEN will end up owning 60.86% of SPNEC once both (5.0bn/5.8bn) exchangeable notes are exchanged into common equity shares (vs. 35.7% today)
  • Mr. Rubio said that it is currently in the process of acquiring land for Terra Solar 2, which would require 2,200 hectares of land for a 1,500 MW of capacity
    • The 3,500 MW MTerra Solar project is 54% complete as of end-June (Phase 1 of 2,500 MW is expected to be operational by 1Q2026 and the balance will be operational by 1Q2027)
CONSUMER
Current consumer confidence and next 12 months outlook improves—BSP survey
  • The latest Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Consumer Expectations Survey (CES) results exhibited that current sentiment or the overall consumer index (CI) was less downbeat at -9.8% in 3Q25 from -14.0% the previous quarter
    • A negative CI implies more respondents are pessimistic than optimistic
    • Less pessimistic sentiment in 3Q stemmed from expectations of new income sources, higher income, better employment conditions and/or opportunities
  • For the next quarter, consumer sentiment jumped to 6.9% from 0.6% the previous quarter
  • For the upcoming 12 months, consumers expect economic prospects to improve, with a positive CI at 14.1%, up from 11.8% the previous quarter
    • The positive consumer outlook can be attributed to the expected improvement of wages, employment, and price stability
  • The CES results are taken into account in the BSP's decision-making for monetary policy
    • The last two Monetary Board meetings for the year will be held on Oct 9 and Dec 11, respectively
      • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect 100bp total worth of cuts to 4.75%
