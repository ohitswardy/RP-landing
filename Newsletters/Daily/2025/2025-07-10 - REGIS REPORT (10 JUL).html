REGIS REPORT

10 July 2025

The United States will be imposing a blanket 20% tariff on imports from the Philippines effective Aug. The surprise here is that’s actually higher than the 17%/18% that was previously announced (April). The Marcos administration seemed hopeful that the US would lower the planned tariffs but Trump dashed those hopes with a terse form letter citing unspecified trade grievances.

 

Around 18% of Philippine exports go to the United States, equivalent to 3% of GDP.

MACRO/TRANSPORT
Trump sets 20% tariff rate for Philippines
  • President Donald Trump set a tariff of 20% on imports from the Philippines effective Aug 1
    • This rate is higher than the 17%/18% rate issued in April
    • Tariff letter also stated that the rate can increase for every retaliatory tariff hike
  • Exports to the US account for ~3% of Philippine GDP
  • ICT trades at 9x FY25E EV/EBITDA (10x consensus) vs peer median of 9x

 

 

PROPERTY
SM Prime launches premium residential brand
  • SM Prime unveiled its Signature Series line 
  • Its first project will be an “ultra-luxury village” in Susana Heights, Muntinlupa
  • Signature Series Group Head Jose Juan Jugo said that lots would range from 750sqm to 1,000sqm and the entry level would be P100mn
    • This implies c. P130,000/sqm
  • The company is currently forging a partnership for a high-end project in Makati
  • SMPH is trading at 42% discount to NAV, 1.5X FY25E P/B, and 15.6X FY25E P/E
CONSUMER
URC appoints new chief marketing officer
  • Universal Robina Corp. (URC PM) has appointed Karen Ong as its new chief marketing officer
    • Ong prior to joining URC has had over two decades of marketing experience in Colgate-Palmolive, Johnson & Johnson, and Fonterra
    • She replaces former CMO Anna Milagros "Mian" D. David, who recently took on the role of President for the “Branded Consumer Foods” (BCF) segment earlier this year
  • Ong is expected to accelerate regional growth and strengthen local brand equities given her successful stint as the Senior Marketing Director for URMunchy's Malaysia
    • Under her direction, she bolstered the company's position as market leader in the biscuit category (Lexus, Munchy's, Cream-O) in Malaysia—raising market share to 25.4% in end-2024 from 23.2% end-2023
TRANSPORT
AirAsia Philippines plans to receive 20 new aircraft over the next five years
  • AirAsia Philippines (AAP) will likely receive 20 new aircraft over the next five years
    • According to its parent company’s CEO Tony Fernandes
    • We suspect these deliveries will largely be replacements of its aging fleet
  • It plans to increase its current fleet to 21 by year-end from 15 today
    • Note that AAP has been communicating this since 2023 (see here)
POWER
Meralco sees higher electricity rates in July
  • Meralco spokesman Joe Zaldarriaga said that is expects overall rates to increase this month due to potentially higher generation and transmission charges
    • Cited the PHP’s depreciation against the USD, given that suppliers’ costs are largely dollar-denominated; Transmission charges could rise due to pressure in reserve market prices
    • “We hope that the expected increase will be mitigated by lower WESM (Wholesale Electricity Spot Market) charges,”
  • Generation charges have been lifted in recent months by the higher cost of power from the LNG plants that Meralco bought stakes in
    • The cost of power from Excellent Energy (1,300MW) is notably higher than those from other plants
GAMING
Fintech firms to promote awareness of gambling risks
  • The country’s largest organization of financial technology (fintech) companies, Fintech Alliance, said its members would “adopt ethical marketing practices and promote awareness of gaming-related risks across digital and community platforms.”
  • Its members would also implement “enhanced due diligence” for all merchant accounts in their platforms that have online gaming licenses and employ “real-time detection, monitoring and blacklisting of illegal and unregulated platforms.”
