REGIS REPORT

17 February 2025

Electricity sales at Meralco (MER PM) were barely up YoY (+0.4%) in January, coming off a high base of comparison. Recall 1Q24 electricity volumes jumped +9% YoY, driven by higher temperatures induced by El Nino. A slowdown in unit sales growth for FY25 should not be surprising given the elevated base last year. At the peak, unusually high temperatures pushed power usage growth to over +10% in 2Q.

 

+++

 

Speaking of temperatures, Cebu Air (CEB PM) is seeing a blistering start to its 2025 operations, with passenger volumes jumping 33%. Seat load factor for the month rose 1.4pt to a rather tight 86.5%. The strong volumes and high load factors, if they continue, should translate to good operating numbers for 1Q/FY25.

 

+++

 

Online gaming firm Digiplus (PLUS PM) says it’s spending up to P3bn (~$52m) for capex this year. This amount is, frankly, a pittance relative to how much the offline operators spent on their integrated resorts (US$1bn+) and how much PLUS is making in gaming revenue (US$300m) every quarter.

 

Meanwhile, bricks-and-mortar casino operator of Okada Manila says it intends to improve its online offering to get in on the action. Okada Manila’s online gaming revenue was an inconsequential US$5m in FY24.

 

+++

 

Ayala Land (ALI PM) is said to have sold P4.7bn worth of lots in the first week of Feb -- a strong number and a testament to what is likely to be a strong and unique product. The jury is still out, however, on whether this can be sustained.

POWER
Meralco consolidated energy sales rise 0.4% YoY in January
  • Meralco (MER PM) stated that consolidated energy sales volume increased to 4,061 gigawatt-hours (GWh) in Jan 2025, up 0.4% YoY
    • The marginal increase in power sales was partly due to Jan 2024's high-base given last year's electricity usage surges due to El Niño
  • Residential sales increased to 1,425 GWh, up 0.2% YoY, while commercial sales increased to 1,564 GWh, up 1.0% YoY
    • Meralco Chief Revenue Officer Ferdinand Geluz cited that: “both sectors experienced slowdown in demand, with impact of long weekend holidays on household consumption and operations”
  • In the industrial segment, sales decreased to 1,059 GWh, down 0.4% YoY
    • This was due to intermittent shutdowns from key accounts in the steel and food & beverage industries
  • For the FY24, Meralco had energy sales of 54,325 GWh, a 6.4% YoY increase from 2023
    • Implying that for the 4Q24, energy sales were approximately 4% higher YoY at around 13,500 GWh~
  • Meralco has yet to report its FY24 earnings but the company expects to exceed its guidance of P43bn profit set back in 1H24
    • Consensus estimates MER to post a FY24 core net profit at P42.8bn a 15.3% YoY increase from FY23
TRANSPORT
CEB pax volumes jump 33% YoY in January
  • In its latest traffic report, Cebu Air carried 2.6mn passengers in January, up 33% YoY
    • Seat load factor rose 140bps to 86.5%, driven by holiday travel
  • Management guiding for seat growth of 20-25% in FY25 on a net fleet addition of one aircraft
  • CEB trades at 3x FY25E EV/EBITDA, below peer average (6x)

 

GAMING
DigiPlus to spend up to P3bn in capex in 2025
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) plans to spend P2.5bn to P3bn in capex in 2025
    • In 2024, the company had a capex target of P1.5bn to P2bn
  • This includes initial spending for its Brazil operations
    • DigiPlus allotted P660mn for its first three months of operations in Brazil
    • The company plans to offer online sports betting by 4Q25
  • In 3Q24, DigPlus generated P18.7bn in gross gaming revenue and P3.5bn in net profit
  • PLUS is trading at 9.9X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
GAMING
Okada Manila seeks to expand online business
  • Universal Entertainment, the parent of Okada Manila, seeks to increase the penetration of its “new online gaming platform” in the Philippines
  • In 2024, Okada’s online GGR fell 31% to P296mn
    • Okada’s total 2024 GGR was P34.9bn
  • Okada’s online platform is not currently available on GCash
  • Meanwhile, Okada Manila’s integrated resort will “conduct extra promotions” to improve its profitability
  • This will likely mean more intense competition within PAGCOR Entertainment City
PROPERTY
Ayala Land Premier sells P4.7bn of units in first week of February
  • Ayala Land Premier sold P4.7bn worth of lots in the first week of February
    • Enara in Nuvali – P2bn
    • Virendo in Davao – P2.7bn
  • This shows that demand for high-end lots in some provincial areas remains strong
  • Ayala Land’s 9M24 presales grew 17% to P100bn
  • ALI is trading at 57% discount to NAV, 1.1X FY25E P/B, and 11.0X FY25E P/E
