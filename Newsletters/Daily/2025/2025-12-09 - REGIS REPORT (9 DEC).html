REGIS REPORT

09 December 2025

The local port regulator (“PPA”) approved a 20% increase in cargo handling fees and passenger terminal charges for Manila North Harbour (North Port) in two equal tranches: one in Jan 2026 and the other in July 2026.

 

We had assumed a 20% increase for North Port spread over two years. ICTSI (ICT PM) owns 50% of North Port and the latter accounts for 4% of revenue and 5% of EBITDA.

 

+++

 

Notable in the Nov CPI data is that rice prices stopped declining MoM, likely because of the ban on rice imports. Food prices have been a key reason why headline CPI inflation has been low, and we expect base effects to keep reported inflation low until early next year.

 

This dip in Nov inflation certainly strengthens the case for another policy rate cut by the Bangko Sentral (BSP) this Thu. The BSP will announce its rate decision hours after the US Federal Reserve makes its own decision. The markets are expecting a >90% chance of a rate reduction by the Fed.

 

+++

 

Alliance Global (AGI PM) President Kevin Tan says subsidiary Megaworld (MEG PM) will inject 70,000sqm of mall space into MREIT (MREIT PM) in 2H of next year. This is on top of previously announced plans to inject 200,000sqm of space into MREIT, which would necessitate the sale of ~100m MREIT shares by the AGI group.

TRANSPORT
PPA approves 20% hike in cargo, passenger charges for Manila North Harbour
  • The Philippine Ports Authority (PPA) approved a 20% hike in cargo-handling tariff and passenger terminal fee at Manila North Harbour (a.k.a “North Port”)
    • Cargo-handling tariff will be raised in two equal tranches: Jan 1, 2026 and July 1, 2026
    • PPA also approved 10 new tariff items and raised tariffs on other charges (e.g. porterage, parking and storage)
  • Approved port charges were broadly the same as North Port’s petition (+20.32%)
  • We have modeled a 20% tariff hike over a two-year period
  • We estimate North Port accounts for 9% of consolidated volumes, 4% of revenues, and 5% of EBITDA
    • ICTSI owns 50% of North Port
MACRO
Inflation inches down to 1.5% in November
  • Headline inflation eased to 1.5% in Nov from Oct's 1.7%
    • This was a tad below with consensus expectations of 1.7% and within the BSP's forecast range of 1.1-1.9% for Nov
    • Inflation has fallen from 2.8% in Jan and has been hovering below BSP's 2-4% target for the past nine months
  • Inflation in Nov decelerated slightly given slower YoY increases in meat and vegetable prices
    • Food overall was only up 0.1% YoY, moderating from the 0.5% pace in Oct
    • Notably, rice prices were up MoM (+0.4%)—the first MoM price increase since Jun 2024
      • Rice prices are down 15.4% YoY in Nov, marking the eleventh consecutive YoY price decline since Jan 2025
      • According to the Dept. of Agriculture, average prices for local and imported well-milled rice are around P42.7/kg and P43.5/kg as of Nov vs. P46.6kg and P46.7/kg back in Jan, respectively
  • Core inflation inched down to 2.4% in Nov from 2.5% in Oct, core non-food inflation was unchanged at 2.3% relative to the previous month
  • This brings the YT-Nov headline inflation rate to 1.6%, below the lower end of the 2-4% BSP target
  • For 2025, the BSP expects inflation to be 1.7%
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to average 1.7% and 1.8%, respectively
  • The last Monetary Board meeting for the year will be held on Dec 11
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect 125bp total worth of cuts to 4.50%
    • For 2026, consensus expects one 25bp cut, while Regis expects 50bp cuts total

 

 

 

 

 

PROPERTY
Megaworld to infuse malls into MREIT in 2H26
  • Alliance Global President Kevin Tan said that Megaworld would infuse about 70,000sqm of malls into MREIT in 2H26
  • MREIT previously announced that it would acquire 200,000sqm of office buildings from Megaworld in a share-for-property swap
    • 165,000sqm in McKinley Hill, Fort Bonifacio
    • 35,000sqm in Eastwood, Quezon City
    • This will expand MREIT’s portfolio by 41% to 680,000sqm
  • MREIT will issue up to 1.36bn new shares to Megaworld at a minimum price of P14.39/share
    • This implies a value of c. P19.6bn
    • The issuance will expand MREIT’s shares outstanding by 37%
    • The minimum price is 3% higher than the last closing price
  • If MREIT will issue 1.36bn new MREIT shares, then we estimate that the AGI Group would need to sell c. 100mn MREIT shares in order for MREIT to meet minimum public ownership requirements
  • MEG currently offers 4.4% dividend yield and is trading at 69% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY26E P/B, and 3.2X FY26E P/E
  • MREIT currently offers 7.6% FY26E dividend yield (based on consensus forecast)
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 5.9%
