REGIS REPORT

05 November 2025

CPI inflation for Oct came in at 1.7%, unchanged from Sept (=1.7%) and just around Street expectations (=1.8%). The number could help pave the way for another Monetary Board action on policy rates should 3Q GDP numbers (due Nov 7, Friday) be weak, as many are already anticipating.

 

+++

 

We have 3Q results from the last of the Big 3 Banks, Metrobank (MBT PM), as well as from Megaworld (MEG PM) this morning.

 

Metrobank (MBT PM) (=) – Metrobank’s 9M earnings call revealed a more cautious stance compared to their peers, and compared to MBT itself from just 6 months ago. From aggressively buying corporate loan market share in 2023/24, the bank instead shifted funds into their portfolio of investment securities, which now account for 40% of total loans, an all-time high, and about double the ratio for BDO (19%) and BPI (21%). The upshot, though, is that CET-1 ratio jumped 60bps QoQ to 16.3%, which suggests that the current dividend (P3/sh regular and P2/sh special) might be sustained in 2026E. Who doesn’t want a 7.5% div yield?

Megaworld (MEG PM) (=) – 9M25 EBIT grew 8% to P22.6bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. Among the noteworthy items in 3Q25 were: 1) a 1.2ppt QoQ and YoY expansion in residential gross profit margin, and 2) a 6% QoQ increase in mall revenue, which implies strong consumer spending in Megaworld malls. 3Q25 office revenue was flat QoQ, which likely means that vacancy remained at 13%. The company will hold its briefing at 2PM on November 7.

 

+++

 

In what we’d describe as a long-hoped-for but still surprising move, the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) says it’s lowering the threshold to qualify for open access (OA). The threshold will be reduced to 100kw from the current 500kw effective June 2026. The 500kw threshold has been in effect since 2021.

Megaworld 9M25 net profit rises 16%, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus
  • Megaworld’s 3Q25 earnings fell 7% QoQ (+1% YoY) to P5.2bn, partly due to forex losses from its US$ debt
    • 9M25 net profit rose 16% to P15.9bn
  • 3Q25 EBIT grew 9% YoY (-2% QoQ) to P7.4bn, faster than revenue growth of 4% YoY
    • The faster EBIT growth was partly due to the expansion of residential GPM
    • 9M25 EBIT grew 8% YoY to P22.6bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts
  • 3Q25 residential revenue dropped 6% QoQ (+1% YoY) to P13.1bn
    • Residential GPM expanded 1.2ppt QoQ and YoY
  • 3Q25 office revenue was flat QoQ (+12% YoY) at P3.7bn
    • This likely means that office vacancy was flat QoQ at 13%
  • 3Q25 mall revenue grew 6% QoQ and 18% YoY (to P1.8bn), implying strong consumer spending in Megaworld malls
  • The company will hold a briefing at 2PM on November 7
  • MEG offers 4.5% dividend yield and is trading at 69% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY26E P/B, and 3.1X FY26E P/E

 

 

POWER
ERC reducing open access threshold to 100kw by June 2026
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) is reducing the threshold for power consumers to qualify for open access (OA)
    • The threshold will be cut to 100kw from the current 500kw beginning June 26, 2026
  • The reduction will allow more consumers to choose their own electricity suppliers
  • The move will expand opportunities for the retail electricity supply (RES) businesses of many power companies
    • Including Aboitiz Power (AP PM), ACEN (ACEN PM) and First Gen (FGEN PM)
  • We estimate that ~30% of kwh demand within Meralco’s (MER PM) franchise area is from consumers qualified under the 500kw threshold
    • We are uncertain how much larger the proportion will be with the lower threshold
MACRO
BSP projects inflation to accelerate until 1H26
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) has projected inflation to accelerate until 1H26 but within the 2-4% target
    • Inflation has averaged 1.7% so far for 10M25, while inflation in Oct alone was 1.7%
  • BSP Deputy Governor Zeno Abenoja stated that the increase in 4Q25 and 1H26 "could be mainly driven by statistical effects (low base)" as well as anticipated rate adjustments for electricity
    • He added that by 2H26, global oil prices are projected to stabilize which will help keep inflation pressures under control
  • Abenoja added that even if headline inflation may pick up due to some supply-side shocks (bad weather), the underlying pressures have cooled down, which has been "one of the factors that was considered in shifting to a more accommodative stance"
  • For 2025, the BSP expects 2025 inflation to be 1.7%
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to average 1.8% in 2025
  • For 2026, BSP expects 2026 inflation to be 3.1%
    • Consensus and Regis expect inflation to average 2.8% and 3.0%, respectively in 2026
  • The last Monetary Board meeting for the year will be held on Dec 11
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect 100bp total worth of cuts to 4.75%
    • For 2026, consensus and Regis expect one 25bp cut to 4.5%
