REGIS REPORT

28 August 2025

First Gen (FGEN PM) subsidiary Energy Development Corp (EDC) has signed up to do a joint venture with the Sinar Mas group for 440MW of geothermal projects. With FGEN’s exit (partial) from gas-fired generation, it’s now focusing efforts on renewable energy (RE), including geothermal power. If these are greenfield projects, then it will likely be many years before they are commercially operational.

 

+++

 

ACEN (ACEN PM) says it is investing P34.5bn (US$600m) for another wind farm in Quezon Province, this time in Tayabas. Capacity for the farm is 245MW, implying a capex cost of US$2.5m/MW. ACEN is already embarking on another wind farm project in Mauban, Quezon which will cost ~US$1bn and have capacity of 552MW.

 

Given the company’s project pipeline, we believe ACEN will need to raise capital soon, despite its decision to postpone its stock rights offer (SRO). We reckon the company could be considering other capital-raising options instead.

 

+++

 

Data from the electric vehicle association (“EVAP”) shows there are now 29,715 registered EVs (inclusive of, and mostly, hybrids) in the Philippines.  This represents less than ½ of 1% of all registered 4W vehicles (~7mn).

 

The share of EVs should grow sharply over the next few years. Around 10% of all 4W vehicles sold this year are now EVs (mostly hybrids). Nearly all these EVs are from Toyota and BYD, with the share of each close to even.  GT Capital (GTCAP PM) owns 51% of Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) while Ayala Corp (AC PM) is the distributor of BYD in the country through subsidiary AC Mobility.

 

+++

 

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) will make its monetary policy decision public a little earlier than usual beginning today. It will release its statement at 2:30pm through various online channels after the Monetary Board meeting today. This will be earlier than the previous 3:00pm schedules.

 

The BSP is widely expected to reduce policy rates from the current 5.25% today.

MACRO
BSP implements new monetary policy announcement format
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) will announce monetary policy decisions using a new format starting today, Aug 28—by releasing a monetary policy stance statement at 2:30pm through its website and official social media, followed by a livestreamed press conference at 3:00pm
    • Previously, the Monetary Board's stance was announced during the 3:00pm press conference
  • The BSP noted the change as part of its initiative to "strengthen transparency" and "reduce uncertainty and speculation" as the policy decision will be announced prior to the stock market close
  • The next Monetary Board meeting (4th out of 6 this year) will be held today, Aug 28
    • The fifth and sixth MB meeting will be held on Oct 9 and Dec 11, respectively
    • For 2025, consensus and Regis expect 100bp total worth of cuts to 4.75%

 

 

MACRO
Gov't to auction NFA rice stocks prior to and during rice importation ban
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. ordered the Dept. of Agriculture (DA) to auction 100,000 MT of rice from the National Food Authority (NFA) in an effort to stabilize prices before the rice importation ban that will take place for 60 days starting Sept 1
    • DA Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. stated that an initial 25,000 MT will be auctioned this week, while the remaining allocated stocks will be auctioned in the coming months
    • The suspension on rice importation was previously announced to protect farmers from declining farmgate prices
  • The gov't also cited other measures such as strict enforcement of the maximum SRP (P45/kg), coordination with farmers and retailers to prevent price manipulation, as well as expanding the implementation of the P20/kg rice program
  • According to the Dept. of Agriculture (as of Aug 23), prices for local and imported well-milled rice range from P38-52/kg and P40-50/kg, respectively vs. P42-52/kg and P40-54/kg back in Jan 2
