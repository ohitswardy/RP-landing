We take a look at a few more results today as this 3Q results season comes to its end (barring the few who missed the regulatory deadline).

  • GT Capital (GTCAP PM) (+) – GTCAP’s 9M21 profit reached P7.6bn, implying 3Q21 profit dropped QoQ to P2.1bn. While the 9M21 figure is trending ahead of forecast, the 3Q21 performance was quite poor for the auto business, which can be attributed to the lockdown. All other key segments fared well, including the bank. Click on the link for more.

  • Alliance Global (AGI PM) (+) – 3Q profit fell 41%QoQ due lower contributions from liquor (-26%QoQ) and casino (-P700mn loss). Brandy margins contracted sharply, worsening the impact of softer revenues. Despite the loss, the casino’s underlying operations showed a marked improvement on the back of a very strong VIP segment (+65%QoQ).  More below.
  • First Gen Corp (FGEN PM) (=) – 3Q recurring income declined 9%QoQ due to the impact of an outage of 1 unit in Avion, with attributable earnings from nat gas declining 9%QoQ. The 9M recurring income after pref divs appears broadly in line with our FY21E of $262mn.  Details in the section below.
  • Cosco Capital (COSCO PM) (=) – The liquor distribution segment (Keepers Holdings) saw a 27% jump in volumes in 3Q, led by the brandy segment. Revenues (+28% YoY) and EBITDA margin (24.3%) were strong in 3Q and could further improve as we move into the seasonally strong 4Q.  More below.
  • Vista Land (VLL PM) (-) – Despite a drop in revenue, 3Q21 earnings rose 6% to P2.0bn, thanks to lower interest expense. Management said that weather disruptions and stricter lockdowns caused residential revenue to fall 31% QoQ and 36% YoY. We believe that sales cancellations also contributed to the drop. Although 9M21 rental revenue jumped 25%, we suspect that receivables also saw a substantial increase and that rental revenue is overstated relative to cash rents.  More detail below.

Globe (GLO PM) reported its 3Q results Friday last week but held its earnings briefing yesterday. The latter yielded a few interesting takeaways. Among them: (1) Mynt (GCash) posted a net profit in 3Q, (2) Globe already laid down 1m fiber broadband lines YT-Sept and increased its target to 1.4m by end-FY21, (3) the company is in advanced stages of negotiations for a joint venture with ST Telemedia for its data center business. Check out the highlights below.

+++

The daily vaccination rate for the week ended Nov 14 (Sunday) was 788k, up from 697k the previous week. At this latest pace and assuming the 3-day vaccination holiday is implemented successfully, the country should be able to reach over 50% vaccination by year-end.

OFW remittances rose 5% to a healthy US$2.74bn in Sept. YT-Sept, remittances are up 4.2%.

+++

The deadline for substitutions in the May national elections lapsed yesterday and the line-up of candidates looks close to set. President Duterte opted not to run as Vice President but filed his candidacy for the Senate instead.

It appears that the “serious” candidates for the Presidency would be, in alphabetical order of their surnames: (1) Manila Mayor Francisco Moreno Domagoso, (2) Senator Christopher Go, (3) Senator Panfilo Lacson, (4) Former Senator Ferdinand Marcos, Jr., (5)  Senator Emmanuel Pacquiao and (6) Vice President Leonor Robredo.

Notwithstanding the fact that the substitution deadline has lapsed, there is still the possibility of change/s in the cast of candidates. It is within the realm of possibility that one or more candidates might eventually drop out (or even be disqualified) as the elections approach and alliances shift.

TELCOS
Highlights of Globe’s 3Q results and the briefing this afternoon
  • 3Q core earnings P5.1bn (+12%), taking 9M to P15.8bn (+1%). Globe reported core income (ex- exceptionals) of P5.08bn, which would take core income to P15.8bn (+1%) for 9M by our reckoning. The core income excludes adjustments on deferred tax arising from the reduction in tax rate booked in 1Q (included in company’s reported core income) which amounted to P2.5bn based on our estimate. Excluding the deferred tax adjustment, core income is below expectations.  If one were to include that item, Globe’s core income would be within expectations.
  • Mobile service revenue dips QoQ. Wireless service revenue was down 2% QoQ and 1% YoY. The absolute figure for 3Q implies a small (-0.3ppt) loss of revenue market share. The company blamed the lockdowns in 3Q for the softness in revenue.
  • Broadband finally picking up. Broadband revenue of P7.8bn for 3Q was up 10% QoQ (+12% YoY), the fastest % increase in six quarters. The company added 77k wired broadband subscribers in the quarter. While this is still well below PLDT’s (229k) and Converge’s (222k), it is the biggest such increase for Globe ever.

Globe has now laid out 1m fiber broadband lines as of end-Sept. It’s raising its target for FY21 to +1.4m lines by which time it would have migrated all its copper (DSL) subscribers. We also note that Globe reported a net churn of 561k subscribers for its Fixed Wireless (-17%) service as it removed those who have not been topping up.

  • Mynt in the black for 3Q21. Mynt achieved positive EBITDA and net profit for 3Q21. Globe’s share of Mynt’s profit in the quarter was P115m. This implies a net profit of ~P287m for the quarter for the entire company – over P1bn annualized.

The registered user base of GCash is now over 51m and it is on track to achieve P3.5trn GTV for FY21 (previous guidance was P3trn). Interestingly, GCash claims it has 4x more active users than its direct competitor (which we assume to mean Paymaya),

  • New growth avenues for Mynt. With US$300m in new cash from the latest funding round, Mynt will focus on three avenues for growth. The first will be lending. A buy now pay later (BNPL) product will be launched soon. Second is a platform for “invest-tech” to allow trading of crypto, among others. Third involves further expansion of its services to the “base of the pyramid” which we assume to mean the financially marginalized.
  • Joint venture on data centers. Globe also announced today that it was in discussions to form a joint venture (JV) for its data center business with Singapore firm ST Telemedia. Globe currently has six data centers with 1,500 racks and a maximum capacity of “up to 30MW”, with expansion potential of “up to 45MW”. Globe intends to remain a “significant shareholder” in the JV.

A transaction like this is almost certainly going to have an accretive (albeit modest) impact on Globe’s valuation. Revenue-wise, this is not a big business (for now) but could have huge upside potential if it can tap the market successfully. For perspective, PLDT’s data center business has a capacity of 9,000 racks (“up to 72MW”). Media reports suggested a valuation of US$500m in talks with potential investors.

CONGLOS
Briefing takeaways - AGI 3Q profit -41%QoQ to P3.5bn; 9M is nevertheless ahead of FY21E (Regis and consensus)
  • Alliance Global reported that its 9M12 income was P12bn (+106%YoY), tracking ahead of FY estimates. 3Q profit alone, however, fell 41%QoQ due to lower contributions from Emperador. RWM also swung back to a loss after a one-time gain boosted its 2Q.
  • Travellers swung back to a loss of P700mn, down from a profit of P3.7bn in 2Q. 2Q was boosted by a one-time gain of P4.8bn. Operations nevertheless continued to recover despite the strict lockdown in August. GGR increased 46%QoQ to P4.9bn, driven by a 65% jump in VIP GGR (higher absolute value vs BLOOM). We suspect that its VIP business was probably helped by 1) the partnership with SunCity and/or 2) higher hold rates.
  • The contribution of EMP also dipped, down 26%QoQ. Brandy revenues were up 4%QoQ, while whisky revenues declined 11%QoQ. While margins for whisky were largely stable, that of brandy fell to 28.8% in 3Q from 44.7% in 2Q. Management attributed the decline to product mix and cost pressures on logistics and certain raw materials. We think they may have discounted heavily as well to push volumes locally.
  • GADC posted a profit of P134mn in 3Q21, coming from a loss of P24mn in 2Q21. Underlying operations were not that rosy though. Systemwide sales dipped 2%QoQ while overall sales (company owned + royalties etc) declined 3%QoQ. GP (14.2% vs 23.4%) and EBIT (7.3% vs 9.4%) margins were both sharply lower due to cost pressures and an inability to raise prices in the current environment. The bottom line, was, however, salvaged by items below the EBIT line. We await more detail from the 17Q.
  • MEG’s 3Q profit amounted to P3.150bn, a 19%QoQ improvement, but short of estimates due. Office revenues rose 7%QoQ, helped possibly by rental escalations and the lapse of the rent-free period. Residential sales on the other hand is still lagging at just P17.1bn (<50% of the pre-Covid average).  
  • AGI trades at 7x FY22E PE and a 70% discount to market NAV

CONGLOS
Cosco Capital (COSCO PM) 9M21 net profit +13% YoY
  • COSCO reported net profit of P4.3bn, up 13% YoY. PGOLD’s results were slightly behind estimates. Meanwhile, while 9M21 profit of the liquor distribution segment (KEEPR PM) has exceeded our FY21E of P1.1bn. The real estate segment continues to lag with 9M profit -7% YoY.

 

  • Keepers Holdings’ net income in 3Q rose 23% YoY to P401mn, bringing the 9M figure to P1.1bn (+48% YoY). 3Q revenues were up sharply (+28% YoY) though coming from a low base. KEEPR sold 965k cases in 3Q, up 27% YoY, led by the brandy segment (+33% YoY). While management did not provide further detail, the EBITDA margin improvement during the quarter was attributed to a move favorable sales mix.

COSCO management indicated that it has some new investments in the pipeline, which will be among the main uses of cash in its balance sheet. While management did not elaborate, the investments will involve 1) the legacy mineral assets under COSCO and 2) a retail JV that could potentially be announced before the end of the year.

PROPERTY
Vista Land’s residential revenue falls for second consecutive quarter
  • Despite a sharp drop in residential revenue, VLL 3Q21 earnings rose 6% YoY to P2.0bn
    • Thanks to lower interest expense
  • 9M21 net profit of P5.7bn (+7%) is behind REGIS forecast
    • Residential revenue is down 20%
    • In line with consensus
  • 3Q21 residential revenue fell 31% QoQ (down 36% YoY) to P3.3bn
    • Management said this was due to weather disruptions and the hard lockdown
    • Residential cancellations likely contributed to the drop as well
  • 3Q21 reservation sales rose 9% QoQ (5% YoY) to P14.5bn
    • 74% of the 2019 average
  • 9M21 reservation sales grew 11% to P44bn, in line with REGIS forecast
  • 9M21 rental revenue jumped 25% to P6.2bn, 20% higher than the 9M19 figure
    • Most mall operators’ 9M21 mall revenues are 60% lower than 9M19
    • We suspect that there was also a substantial increase in receivables and that rental revenue is overstated relative to cash rents
  • VLL is currently trading at 57% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY22E P/B, and 5.3X FY22E P/E

POWER
FGEN 9M recurring income +11% to $212mn

  • Earnings from hydro doubled to $6mn in 9M21
