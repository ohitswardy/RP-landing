REGIS REPORT

31 March 2021

More results this morning, from Cebu Air (-), and Filinvest Land (=), and news on Bloomberry and its chairman Enrique Razon (also chair of ICTSI)

Filinvest Land (FLI PM) – in line – FLI’s FY20 net profit fell 41% to P3.7bn, in line with REGIS forecast (behind consensus). What was remarkable though was that 4Q20 office revenue fell 8% QoQ (-16% YoY), implying that office vacancy rose further in 4Q. For reference, FLI’s office vacancy already increased from 9% in 2019 to 13% in 3Q20. The company will hold its briefing later this morning.

Cebu Air (CEB PM) – behind – CEB’s FY20 net loss came in larger than expected at P22.2bn due to higher ARO provisions and aircraft/traffic servicing costs. The business continues to struggle but the P28.5bn of capital raised recently should tide it through the next 18 months. The issuance of a US$250mn convertible bond remains uncertain.

The CREATE Act’s tax cut from 30% to 25% sounds simple, right? In theory, NPAT should go from 70% of pre-tax profit to 75% of pre-tax profit, or an increase of 7.1%. Done and dusted.

But Filipinos never do things simple. Very few companies’ effective tax rates were even close to 30% prior to CREATE, because of interest income, offshore profit, and associates, etc. To estimate the impact, we simplistically took our forecast tax rates and reduced them proportionately. While the range varies, the majority of companies we cover would see a recurring uplift of 4-7% for all forecast years 2021 and beyond, with aggregate earnings up c5%. (See blue column in the table below.)

However, there are (at least) two more issues that could create lots of “noise”, particularly for 2021E. The combination of these two could swing profit 2021E forecasts by 10-20% in either direction for some companies. (See last two columns in the table below.)

  • First is the retroactivity of the tax cut to 2H2020. The deadline for filing of taxes is 15 April, and many companies have already submitted their audited financial statements. If companies are allowed to recognize the tax reduction as a credit to their 2021E, this could add another 1-3% to our 2021E profits, depending on how good 2H20 profitability was.
  • Second – and much more complex – is the treatment of Deferred Tax (DT) Assets and Liabilities, which arise from timing differences from financial and tax accounting. Provisions, for example are not tax deductible until they are actually written off. Hence banks and Meralco have large DT Assets that will likely have to be written down by 1/6th (ie they will now receive a tax shield of only 25%, not 30%). On the other end, property companies have chunky DT Liabilities, which will result in write-backs.  

We also discuss the good news / bad news for property companies in the attached report. To the upside, the impact of CREATE on BPOs is much more manageable than originally thought. To the downside, the President vetoed a proposed increase in the threshold for VAT exemption for low-cost housing, which would hurt the likes of Filinvest and Vista.

MACRO
Spending picks up in February, Budget deficit balloons to P116bn
  • The government’s budget deficit ballooned to P116bn in February largely due to a rise in spending
    • Revenues were up 6% YoY, of which tax revenues grew 7%; Non-tax revenues dropped due to lower collections from PAGCOR and investment income
    • Expenditures rose YoY and MoM due to a P45bn release of equity to government financial institutions for various COVID-19 support programs under Bayanihan II
  • This brings 2M21 budget deficit to P130bn

 

PROPERTY
Filinvest Land FY20 net profit falls 41%; office vacancy likely rose in 4Q20
  • Filinvest Land’s 2020 net profit fell 41% to P3.73bn, in line with REGIS (behind consensus)
    • 4Q20 earnings dropped 43% to P1.1bn
  • 4Q20 office revenue fell 8% QoQ (-16% YoY), implying that office vacancy increased further in 4Q
    • Office vacancy rose from 9% in 2019 to 13% in 3Q20
    • As of 3Q20, POGOs occupied c. 15% of the office portfolio
  • The company said that residential reservation sales saw a healthy rebound
  • FLI will hold a briefing at 10:30AM

GAMING
Razon sued by GGAM in NY federal court
  • Global Gaming Asset Management, LLC (GGAM) sued Enrique Razon in a federal court in New York for illegally terminating the deal to operate Solaire
  • The lawsuit seeks to confirm a $297mn arbitration award by a Singapore court
  • GGAM added that Razon managed to evade paying the arbitration award by “forming a vast network of shell companies in the US to conceal his assets”
  • Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) maintains that the arbitration award can only be enforced by a Philippine court
    • The Final Award issued in September 2019 ordered Bloomberry to pay GGAM about US$100mn in damages due to the termination of the management services agreement
    • It also ordered BLOOM to buy GGAM’s 921mn shares in BLOOM for P10.17bn@P11/sh (or allow the sale of the shares and make up the difference between market sale and P10.17bn)
