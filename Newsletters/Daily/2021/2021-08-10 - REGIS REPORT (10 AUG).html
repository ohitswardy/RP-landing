REGIS REPORT

10 August 2021

The government is set to release GDP numbers for 2Q this morning and the Street is expecting a YoY expansion of around 11%.

+++

Puregold (PGOLD PM) disclosed a net profit of P3.99bn for 1H21, which is up 17% YoY.  This would be in line with expectations despite a rather soft revenue number.  2Q sales revenue were down 6% YoY, with the Puregold brand down 12%.  An improvement in margins due to cost controls as well as a shift in mix in favor of S&R helped lift earnings.

More after the briefing tomorrow.

+++

The country administered 517k doses per day over the past week. This is a tad lower than the previous week but it would still be on track to reach 50% of population on two doses, or higher for one dose.

+++

Globe (GLO PM) held its earnings call yesterday and we share our key highlights here.

  • Cellular revenue market shares were just about unchanged in 2Q21.  What struck us, however, was that its yield on data (revenue/GB) is now lower than PLDT’s  
  • Broadband revenue was rather soft as the fixed wireless business seems to have peaked after the rush last year.
  • GCash is doing particularly well with active users up 2.8x YoY.  GCash now claims more active users in the Philippines than Instagram or Viber.

Philippine Airlines (PAL) is starting to return planes to its lessors ahead of its formal Chapter 11 filing.  What’s key for the industry and Cebu Air (CEB PM) is how much capacity it is giving up and where.

RETAIL
PGOLD 1H21 net profit in line at P4bn (+17% YoY) - margins ahead even as sales fall short
  • In a disclosure (link), PGOLD reported 1H21 net profit of P3.99bn, up 17% YoY. This is broadly in line with Regis and consensus estimates. Net sales (-7% YoY to P76.1bn) is falling short of expectations, though operating margin (8.1%) appears ahead.
  • For 2Q21 alone, net sales were down 6% YoY. The core Puregold brand saw a 12% decline in sales – which is largely expected given the high base from pantry stocking last year. S&R sales remain resilient (+6% YoY) despite the high base. SSSG was not disclosed.
  • Operating margin in 2Q21 expanded both QoQ and YoY. This was attributed to “strategic cost controls”, but we believe it is also likely due to the change in the revenue mix (eg higher-margin S&R).  
  • We hope to get further detail during the briefing on 11th Aug at 3pm.

 

INFRASTRUCTURE
Eagle Cement 1H21 profit rises 180% YoY to P3.69bn
  • Eagle Cement’s 2Q21 profit rises almost 16-fold YoY, +15% QoQ to P1.97bn
    • Revenues surged +317% YoY in 2Q21, with volumes up +334% YoY
    • Management said that Eagle’s ASPs now the lowest it’s ever been
    • 2Q21 EBITDA jumped 544% YoY to P2.48bn
  • Brings 1H21 profit up 180% YoY to P3.69bn

AIRLINES
PAL to start returning planes to lessors amid restructuring
  • PAL had sought “advanced clearance” from the Department of Finance (DOF) to return or re-export one of its Airbus A321-231 planes that was earlier brought into the country tax-free
    • According to a Bureau of Customs memorandum dated July 19, 2019 but posted on the Bureau of Custom’s website last Aug 5
  • As at end-March, PAL had 95 aircraft, of which 31 are widebodies and 64 are narrowbodies (incl. 18 Bombardiers)
  • PAL Holdings earlier confirmed it was in ongoing negotiations with creditors and lessors over its debts and that it was eyeing pre-negotiated court-rehabilitation in an overseas jurisdiction
