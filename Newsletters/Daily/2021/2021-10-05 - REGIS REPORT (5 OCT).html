REGIS REPORT

05 October 2021

The pace of vaccinations continues to creep down again, dipping below 350,000/day even as preparations are being made to begin inoculating adolescents. We reckon the deterioration is because the urban areas more able to distribute and administer the vaccines are close to fully inoculating their target populations.

Fifty-five percent (55%) of the total population of Metro Manila (“NCR”) and twenty percent (20%) of the country are now fully vaccinated. The disparity between NCR and the rest of the country could widen further in the coming weeks as vaccination of adolescents begins in NCR locations.

Meanwhile, despite the sluggish pace of vaccinations, new Covid-19 infections, however, are still falling. The latest data showed daily new infections of less than 11,000. Some skepticism over the daily count is understandable, given the government’s track record on data gathering (just look at the unemployment figures). However, even the hospitalizations figures are conforming to this trend.

+++

In a surprise move, the BSP capped the Digital Bank licenses to 6, saying that none of the 9 applicants (!!) for the 7th slot were able to submit the required documents. Among the applicants last slot was Russian digibank Tinkoff, which has publicly said it wanted to invest heavily in the Philippines. The limited number was meant to create a “sandbox”, allowing the BSP to monitor the performance, competition, etc of the players. There is, however, no official timetable for the moratorium on new licenses, and that new licenses could be given out if/when the Monetary Board deems it appropriate. The cap on licenses eases competitive pressure on the 6, and potentially increases the scarcity value of their Digibank licenses.

To recall, the six Digibank license holders are below. Of the six, only OF Bank and Tonik are currently operating.

  • OF Bank – 100% owned by state-owned Land Bank of the Philippines. While it was meant originally to cater primarily to Overseas Filipinos (OFs), it offers a broad suite of loan products and other services.
  • Tonik – Singapore-based principals. Tonik launched earlier this year, and has built a significant deposit base, offering TD rates of as high as 6%pa. It announced recently that it will launch its lending products, which charge as much as 7% per month.
  • UNO Bank – Also Singapore-based principals.
  • UnionDigital – wholly-owned by Union Bank of the Philippines (UBP PM), which is probably the most tech-focused of the local non-government banks. This is the only license so far owned by a private local bank.
  • GOTyme – a JV between the JG Summit (JGS PM) group and Tyme Bank of South Africa. Hopes to leverage off the massive customer base (individuals, suppliers, tenants, etc) of JGS’s subsidiaries including Cebu Air, URC, Robinsons Retail and Robinsons Land.
  • MayaBank – license granted to Voyager, part-owned by PLDT (TEL PM). Voyager also owns PayMaya, the 2nd largest e-wallet in the country.

Premium Warranty Services Philippines (PSWP) launched its warranty services business for used cars. This firm is 40% owned by GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and is one of two businesses of the group aimed at the secondhand vehicle market.

We think this business could be an important money-spinner for GTCAP if it is executed properly. The secondhand vehicle market is estimated to be larger than the new car market, with over 500,000 used cars changing hands annually (vs 400,000 for new vehicles). In addition, the industry is fragmented, and consolidation could lead to improved returns for the winner/s.

BANKS
BSP caps digital bank licenses at six
  • Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor Benjamin Diokno told investors at a Financial Executives Institute of the Philippines conference that the list of approved digital banks will remain at six after the nine entities with pending applications failed to submit documentary requirements
  • Gov. Diokno added that keeping the number of licensed digital banks at six for the meantime should allow regulators to closely monitor developments
  • The application period for digital banking licenses ended on August 31
  • “The MB reserves the right to reopen the application subject to the need for more and the impact of the six digital banks on competition in the banking sector”
  • The six licensees are Overseas Filipino Bank, Tonik Digital Bank, Inc., UNObank, Union Digital Bank, Gotyme and Maya Bank
  • Digital banks have a three year window to operate after they get the license
