REGIS REPORT

18 June 2021

Philippine Airlines (PAL) says it is about to finalize its restructuring plan which will be submitted to the relevant authorities as a Chapter 11 filing in a few weeks.

The plan will apparently involve new funding of US$630m (mostly from a ‘major stockholder’). While that is not a small amount, we still have to wonder if it would be enough, given the group has US$4bn of debt and leases and its equity position is deeply negative (US$1.5bn).

For Cebu Air (CEB PM), what is relevant is how this bankruptcy will affect PAL’s fleet and future operations. A much slimmed down PAL is the expected outcome, which would mean reduced capacity for the industry (a good thing for operators like CEB). The question is how capable will PAL be to compete with CEB where it matters, after it restructures.

While conditions will remain difficult for the airlines (including CEB) for a few more quarters, we believe a return to something close to “normal” is likely by the latter part of next year.

+++

It is looking increasingly probable that the long-awaited Executive Order on the mining industry will not give the green light for the massive projects that have been on hold. Without the lifting of the ban on open pit mining, the largest mining projects will not be able to proceed.

+++

Yesterday’s arrival of another 1.5m vaccines purchased from China means the country is on track to reach the 10.2m vaccine target (previously lowered) for the month. The pace of vaccinations is not ideal but there is progress.

TRANSPORT
PAL parent on 'final stages' of restructuring
  • PAL Holdings said it is now on the “final stages” of its restructuring plan to survive the pandemic
    • The group has not made principal and/or interest payments due in respect of the above long-term obligations since April 2020, resulting in breach of certain loan covenants and default provisions
  • It expects a funding of up to US$505mn from a “major stockholder”, plus an exit facility amounting to US$125mn
    • The US$505mn fund raising may involve sourcing of the fund by the major stockholder from the government and private financial institutions, estimated to be around US$250mn
    • Fundraising plan will be on top of the US$358mn already provided by the major stockholder from 4Q19-1Q21
  • In FY20, PAL’s after tax net loss to commons widened to P71.9bn from P10.3bn previously
  • The company remains optimistic that it will be able to continue operations in the next 12 months
    • It expects to generate “positive monthly operating cash flows” this year on the back of a gradual recovery in the travel industry
    • In 1Q21, operating cash outflow stood at P5.1bn (vs. CEB’s 1Q21 operating cash outflow of P2.4bn)
  • As at end March 2021, PAL’s total debt (incl. leases) stood at P197bn (~US$4bn)
MINING
EO silence on open-pit mining seen delaying DENR approval
  • The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) is still studying whether it can lift the open-pit mining ban
    • According to Presidential Spokesman Herminio L. Roque, Jr.
  • Executive Order (EO) No. 130, signed by Mr. Duterte on April 14, lifted the nine-year moratorium on new mineral agreements but did not have a provision lifting the ban on open-pit mining
  • The draft IRR was submitted to the DENR secretary early this month, which included a repealing clause that will lift the four-year ban on the use of open-pit mines to extract minerals
    • According to Wilfredo Moncano, director of the Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB)
  • Billions of dollars are expected to flow from the mining industry once the ban is lifted as this will allow two mines to resume operations
    • US$5.9bn Tampakan copper project of Sagittarius Mines
    • US$2bn King-king copper-gold project of St. Augustine Gold and Copper Ltd
    • US$750mn Silangan project of Philex Mining
TELCOS
PhilTower, Globe partner to deploy new cell sites in Luzon
  • Tower company Phil-Tower Consortium announced on Thursday that it is partnering with Globe Telecom for the deployment of new cell sites in Luzon
  • PhilTower president Devid Gubiani said the company is helping power critical digital network expansion needs through its cell site technologies
    • Introducing new solutions like micro cell poles that have low impact and small footprint with capability to load in radios, battery backup within the pole for network densification
  • PhilTower is part of a regional group with presence in Malaysia, Indonesia, Myanmar and the Philippines
  • Globe is targeting to put up 2,000 new cell sites this year and installed 318 new cell towers in the first three months
