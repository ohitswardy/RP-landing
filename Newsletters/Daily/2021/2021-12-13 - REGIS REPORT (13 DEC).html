REGIS REPORT

13 December 2021

We refresh our forecasts and ratings for the retailers under our coverage in a new report released over the weekend. Our BUY ratings on Puregold (PGOLD PM) and Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM) have been affirmed, but we downgrade Wilcon Depot (WLCON PM) to a HOLD after what has been a blistering year for its share price.

Our near-term bullishness on the sector is supported by improving mobility trends, coupled with “revenge” spending and election expenditures which should provide a near-term boost to the sector’s top line, particularly for non-essential retail formats (ex-groceries and drug stores). Admittedly, investors need to keep an eye out for the impact of rising inflation and even more lockdowns on household consumption, particularly those lower down on the income ladder.

Amidst this backdrop, our top pick is Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM). Its target market (mid- to upper-income consumers) is more insulated from inflationary headwinds, and around 1/3 of its portfolio is poised to see a sharp recovery in sales.

Over the weekend, news of the hacking of a number of bank accounts at BDO (BDO PM) trended on social media before hitting the news media. The breach was attributed to its “ten-year old web service” which is in the process of being decommissioned and replaced.  Funds from compromised accounts were sent to another bank (Union Bank (UBP PM) via Instapay (the digital retail payments platform initiated by the Bangko Sentral) and reportedly used to purchase bitcoin.

BDO CEO Nestor Tan assured customers that losses from the hacking will be reimbursed. The amount/s involved do not appear to be big but the question is how much will confidence be affected in the short term, not only in the institutions directly involved but also in digital transactions in general.

+++

ICTSI (ICT PM) secured a 15-year extension for its Madagascar port concession which was supposed to end in 2025. The port accounts for 5% of group EBITDA.  The extension should add to its share of group NAV (currently at only 2%).

+++

Ayala Corp (AC PM) approved the addition of another P10bn to its current buyback program. The current buyback program is also for P10bn, of which P7.2bn has been deployed.  With the discount to NAV of AC’s share price close to its widest (and its widest was when President Duterte himself was issuing personal threats), current levels do look enticing not only for the company itself but also to third party investors.

MACRO
Easing exports temper foreign trade growth in October
  • External trade rose 15% YoY to US$16.8bn in October, slower than the 17% YoY growth in September
    • Export growth slowed to 2% from +6% the previous month
    • Imports grew at a sustained rate of 25% YoY due to surging fuel imports and sustained improvement in consumer goods imports
  • With the import bill growing at a faster pace than export sales, the Philippines’ trade deficit stood at US$4.02bn in October, slightly bigger than US$4bn gap in September
  • Brings YT-October total trade and trade deficit to US$157bn and US$33bn, respectively

MACRO
End-November 2021 GIR Level Settles at US$107.67bn
  • The country’s gross international reserves (GIR) level, based on preliminary data, settled at US$107.67bn as of end-November 2021
    • Slightly lower than the end-October GIR level of US$107.89bn
    • Equivalent to 10.2 months’ worth of imports of goods
  • The BSP is targeting an end-2021 GIR level of US$111bn, lower than the previous forecast of US$114bn

BSP revises external sector targets
  • The BSP revised its end-2021 BOP surplus target to US$1.6bn (0.4% of GDP) from US$4.1bn (1.1% of GDP) previously
    • Reflects current account deficit of US$4bn (-1% of GDP) from a surplus of US$3.5bn (0.9% of GDP) due to widening of trade-in-goods deficit
  • OFW remittances growth target retained at +6% for FY21

BANKS
BDO depositors hacked
  • Social media over the weekend was rife with stories from BDO (BDO PM) depositors reporting unauthorized transfers from their accounts of up to P50,000 (roughly $1,000, or the maximum daily limit for online transfers over PesoNet). According to news reports, the funds were transferred to Union Bank (UBP PM) accounts, allegedly to a certain “Mark Nagoyo” – likely fictitious, name as “nagoyo” is a Filipino word for “fooled”. Some reports claimed the funds were then used to buy Bitcoin.
  • We reached out to BDO management, who said that the extent of the data breach and potential lost deposits was still unknown. Meanwhile, UBP says they have frozen several accounts that may have been used in the scam. UBP management claims some social media reports are exaggerated (including the “Mark Nagoyo” part of the story).
  • On Sunday, BDO President Nestor Tan was quoted by news outlets saying that customer credentials were indeed “compromised”, and that the bank would reimburse their clients’ losses.
  • Mr Tan was further quoted saying the breach “affects a 10-yr old web service” that is being phased out, and that the new system is currently in beta test and will be formally implemented in Jan/Feb 2022.
  • It is unclear at this stage whether the depositors’ data was acquired through phishing scams, or through a hack of BDO’s systems. Apart from the amounts lost, it is also unclear whether either bank will be assessed any fines by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) or other agencies for data privacy breaches. The BSP issued its own press release here.
TRANSPORT
ICTSI secures 15-year concession extension in Madagascar port
  • Madagascar International Container Terminal Services Ltd (MICTSL) signed a 15-year extension to its Concession Agreement with the Madagascar Port Authority
    • Concession will now expire in 2040 from 2025 previously
    • ICTSI has held the concession since 2005
  • Our forecasts and valuation do not assume an extension so this latest development will likely enhance our NAV
  • MICTSL is a wholly-owned unit of ICTSI (ICT PM) and accounts for c. 4% of group-wide revenues and 2% of NAV
