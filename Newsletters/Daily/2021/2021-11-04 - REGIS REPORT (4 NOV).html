REGIS REPORT

04 November 2021

Margin pressure from commodity prices is already looking like a key theme this early into the 3Q results season. The specter of rising costs is rearing its head in operations as varied as property development and food manufacturing.

  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) (-) – 3Q21 profit fell 22% to P2.55bn as the hard lockdown kept revenue muted and cost pressures weighed further on residential earnings. On a more positive note, reservation sales grew 11% QoQ, thanks to sales of high-end lots in provincial areas. Moreover, while 9M21 earnings of P8.6bn (+35% YoY) is below REGIS and consensus forecasts, the reopening of the economy should lead to demand recovery beginning 4Q21.

  • Century Pacific Foods (CNPF PM) (=) – 3Q revenues (+21% YoY) were boosted by the continued recovery of the non-branded OEM segment. Margins were crimped (GPM -320bps YoY) by higher input and freight cost even as skipjack tuna prices remained subdued. Cost pressure is likely to persist over the near term, but we remain positive on the company’s prospects over the medium term given its new product launches (chocolate malt drink, pet food). We raise our TP to P33/sh and maintain our Buy rating.

MACRO
Mobility pops to new Covid highs on 30 Oct
  • As we had predicted in our recent “Revenge travel” report, Google mobility data spiked on 30 October, the Friday before the long weekend (Monday, 01 Nov was a holiday). Every major metric we follow was at the highest level since the Covid-19 pandemic began – be it retail/recreation, transit stations, or workplace (which includes the malls, restos, and resorts). Interestingly, even visits to grocery/pharmacies were extremely high (+40% compared to pre-pandemic levels). People were eating out AND grocery shopping for the weekend getaway.
  • This is VERY good news as we head into the Christmas holidays, when economic activity tends to peak – including in 2020.
  • We have consistently held – since our first "Glass half-full" note – that the vaccine roll-out would allow for the gradual reopening of the economy. Macro challenges remain – inflation being the most immediate. But we believe the impact of further relaxation of mobility restrictions will be a more powerful macro / earnings driver, at least in the near-term.

AIRLINES
Airline capacity survey
  • Jefferies just published their latest airline capacity survey. 
  • Cebu Pacific’s weekly seat capacity in the week-ending November 1 surged to 52k, +54% higher than its 10-week rolling average and the biggest spike we’ve seen so far since the start of the pandemic.  While it’s still at 44% of pre-Covid levels (CEB likely very close to EBITDA break-even that week), Boracay has indicated to remove swab tests as a pre-travel requirement this month and hopefully more will follow.

POWER
Retirement of 10 out of PH’s 28 coal-fired power plants pushed
  • Consultants of the Asian Development Bank have recommended three coal-fired power plants to be retired first through the ADB’s Energy Transition Mechanism
    • DMCI group’s 600 MW SEM Calaca Power Corp. in Batangas
    • Palm Concepcion Power Corp.’s 135 MW plant in Iloilo
    • PSALM’s 232 MW plant in Misamis Oriental
  • Plants shortlisted will have an estimated market value of US$7bn
    • Valuations ranging from US$400k-US$2mn per MW, with plants with more profitable PPAs driving valuations higher
  • Other priority plants for retirement were the 1,294-MW Sual; 1,185-MW Pagbilao and 511-MW Quezon Power facilities
  • Finance Secretary Carlos Dominguez III said the government was studying the possibility of setting up a fund to acquire all coal-powered plants in Mindanao
  • We are doubtful about the success of this proposal, given ongoing power supply tightness and high power prices
CONSUMER
JFC to acquire 51% of Taiwanese milk tea chain
  • JFC will acquire a 51% stake in Milkshop International Co Ltd, a Taiwan-based operator of bubble tea shops under the trade names 'Milkshop' and 'Milksha'
  • Acquisition price is approx. USD12.8mn (P650mn)
  • Milkshop has over 250 outlets, of which 231 are in Taiwan
  • In 2020, Milkshop generated systemwide sales of USD75mn (cP3.8bn), equivalent to c2% of JFC's systemwide sales. Milkshop is generating 'modest' net income and is EBITDA-positive
  • In the Philippines, JFC will have exclusive rights to the 'Milksha' brand and will soon be sold in Chowking stores
