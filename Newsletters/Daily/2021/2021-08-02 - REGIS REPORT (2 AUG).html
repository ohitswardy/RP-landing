REGIS REPORT

02 August 2021

The government announced it will re-implement another round of stricter lockdowns (“ECQ”) beginning Aug 6 in anticipation of a Covid-19 surge due to the Delta variant. The tighter lockdown is being implemented with the support of business who reckon it is better to head off a surge by locking down now than during the holiday season in 4QQ. As a result, non-essential businesses and services will be shut for two weeks and travel outside homes will be restricted.

Covid-19 cases are already rising even before Delta has become dominant.  We expect cases to rise further in the coming weeks.

In a press statement released this morning, BDO (BDO PM) reported 1H21 NPAT of P21.4bn, about 60% of consensus full-year estimate and close to our FY estimate. The beat for us was nearly entirely due to provision expense, as PPOP was broadly in line with expectations. NPAT was 5x that of 1H20, as the bank put through massive provisions in 2Q20.

Some highlights 

  • Loans were flat YoY (vs -1% in 1Q21)
  • NII was -3% YoY, suggesting was still down YoY but flat-ish QoQ.
  • Other income was up 20%, driven by fees (+20%) and insurance premiums (+31%), which offset a 17% drop I trading/fix income
  • Opex was up 9%, largely due to insurance operations, without which opex would have been -1% YoY
  • Provision expense was P6.8bn, down 70% YoY. Recall that BDO put through P20.2bn in provisions in 2Q20 alone.
  • NPL ratio was up to 3.1% (was 2.8% in 1Q), while NPL ratio was 100% (was 107%)

 

The bank will hold a briefing at 4pm this afternoon. More then.

PLDT (TEL PM) is reportedly considering the sale / leaseback of its tower assets with an eye to fetching as much as US$1bn.  If true, it would come as a surprise as management previously said it considered the tower assets as “strategic”.

Office property vacancies in Metro Manila rose to 13% as of June, according to property consultancy Colliers.  This is a deterioration from the 11% in March, as supply rose and demand dropped.

Finally, total loans of the commercial banks contracted 2% YoY as of June.  While still down YoY, this latest figure is actually an improvement over the -4% figure the previous month.

MACRO
Gov’t places Metro Manila under ECQ from August 6-20
  • Metro Manila will be placed under the strictest enhanced community quarantine classification from August 6 to 20
    • Current status of General Community Quarantine with Heightened Restrictions (GCQ-HR) will remain until Aug 5
  • Indoor and al-fresco dining at restaurants prohibited from July 31 to August 20
  • Public transportation will not be affected
  • Personal care services (ie. beauty salons, barber shops) allowed to operate at 30% capacity until August 5 but will not be allowed to operate from Aug 6-20
  • Border closures outside NCR Plus bubble (i.e. NCR, Bulacan, Rizal, Laguna, Cavite) from August 1
  • The national government will distribute cash aid to poor families in Metro Manila once the National Capital Region is back in lockdown from August 6 to 20
    • According to Metropolitan Manila Development Authority Chairman Benhur Abalos
    • The government will release details on the amount, qualifications, and mode of distribution

MACRO
Hot money inflow hits US$334mn in June
  • Foreign portfolio investments (“hot money”) yielded a net inflow of US$334.51mn in June
    • A turnaround from the US$235mn net outflow a year earlier, but 20% less than the US$416.74mn in net inflow seen in May 2021
  • 1H21 hot money at a net outflow of US$106mn, smaller than the US$3.3bn net outflow in the same period last year

 

BANKS
Bank lending shrinks 2% in June; Domestic liquidity expands by 6.4% YoY
  • Outstanding loans of universal and commercial banks amounted to P9.09tn in June, down 2% YoY
    • A milder drop than the previous month (-4% YoY)
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) expanded by 6.4% YoY to P14.4tn in June
    • Faster than the +4.7% growth rate in May

 

 

TELCOS
PLDT reportedly seeking sale of telco tower assets
  • PLDT is supposedly considering the sale of its telecom tower assets for as much as US$1bn
    • Expected to come with a leaseback arrangement
  • If true, such a transaction would be a surprise since PLDT management did not seem keen in the past
    • Management considered its tower network a strategic asset
    • If the buyer allows a new operator (Dito) to lease the towers, it would give a significant boost to Dito’s rollout plans
  • A sale raising that amount (US$1bn) would be meaningful relative to PLDT’s market cap
  • Note PLDT’s market cap is only US$5.3bn and net debt US$3.9bn as of 1Q21

 

TELCOS
Globe cuts broadband package price, matching Converge
  • Globe reduced the monthly cost of one of its entry level fiber broadband packages to P1,499
    • This is supposedly being offered for a “limited” period only
  • The new plan is now the lowest priced fiber package for Globe without a data cap
    • Unlike its other plans, this new one does not come with a voice line
  • Globe has a cheaper package but that one has data usage capped at 500GB  per month
  • With this new package, Globe’s pricing now matches Converge’s
  • With pricing even, it’s now a race for coverage / availability

 

PROPERTY
Metro Manila 2Q21 office vacancy rises to 13%
  • Colliers estimates that Metro Manila office vacancy rose to 13% in 2Q21
    • 11% in 1Q21, 9% in 4Q20
    • These are similar to estimates of Leechiu Property consultants
  • The increase in vacancy was largely due to POGO closures and new office supply
    • POGOs vacated 388,000sqm of Metro Manila office space in 1H21 (93,000sqm in 2Q21)
  • Colliers forecasts office vacancy to rise further
    • 2021E: Colliers - 16%; REGIS – 15%
    • 2022E: Colliers - 18%; REGIS – 13%
    • 2023E: Colliers - 18%; REGIS – 13%
  • Metro Manila office rental rates fell an average of 4% QoQ, 8% HoH in 1H21
    • Office rental rates in most areas are 15%-25% lower than 2019 levels
    • Colliers estimates that rental rates will fall 20% in 2021 (REGIS: 10% drop)
  • Colliers estimates that Metro Manila condominium prices generally fell 3% QoQ, c. 5% HoH in 1H21
  • Colliers and REGIS estimate that by end-2021, Metro Manila condominium prices (excluding Manila Bay area) will generally be 15%-25% lower than 2019 levels (currently 10%-20% lower than 2019 levels)
  • Colliers and REGIS also both expect modest recovery in Metro Manila condominium prices by 2023E (c. +5% from end 2021E)
