REGIS REPORT

01 June 2021

President Duterte announced that NCR plus (Metro Manila and four adjacent provinces) would remain under the current quarantine status “GCQ with restrictions” until June 15. While the quarantine status is the same, recent mobility data suggest an improvement in activity. The increase in vaccinations might have provided a boost.  

Speaking of vaccines, while more delays in arrivals have been announced, we still expect 11.3mn vaccines to arrive in June and 15mn to 20mn a month starting July. This would be a vast improvement from the cumulative 8.3mn doses that we received over a 3-month period.  

+ + +

Loan of commercial banks shrank 5.0% YoY in April, worse than the -4.5% print in March. The contraction accelerated across nearly all major segments, notably for Trade (-10.2% in April vs -9.7% in March), and Manufacturing (-9.6% vs -5.5%). For consumer loans, the Auto segment was -12.7%, compared to -10.7% in March, while Credit Card receivables improved slightly to -9.9% from -10.7%. The weakness was likely due to the renewed lockdowns in Metro Manila and other provinces.

+ + +

NGCP warned of rotational blackouts as it raised red and yellow alert warnings yesterday, signaling power deficiency in the Luzon grid. Scheduled and unplanned outages led to 3,800MW of capacity being offline, including >400MW (out of 1,200MW) from Ilijan due to restricted gas output from Malampaya. In spite of lower demand from the commercial sector, WESM prices are likely to remain elevated. Among the generators, Semirara (SCC PM) is most exposed to spot, selling about 20% of capacity. The supply situation has also prompted a rethink of the contracting strategy of AC Energy (ACEN PM) which is gearing up >1,000MW of projects to sell to WESM beginning next year.  

+ + +

President Duterte certified a bill on POGO taxes as urgent. The bill would lower POGO taxes from 5% of bets/turnover to 5% of gross gaming revenue, which is a mere fraction of bets/turnover. Thus, the new bill, if passed, could slow down further POGO closures. This would be beneficial to developers whose operations relied heavily on POGOs, such as Filinvest Land (FLI PM) and Megaworld (MEG PM), as well as developers with large exposure to the Manila Bay area, such as SM Prime (SMPH PM).

+ + +

Philippine National Bank (PNB PM) announced that it will book a P34bn gain from the revaluation and transfer of its three property assets into PNB Holdings, and its eventual property dividend of PHC. That will come in handy, as it catches up on provisions for NPLs (current and future?). As of 1Q21, PNB’s NPL cover stood at a mere 43% despite having an NPL ratio of 10.7%.

We understand, though, that the BSP has not yet agreed to allow PNB to count the gains towards Qualifying Capital for its CAR/CET-1 calculations. The sticking point is whether the transfer/property div of PHC can be classified as a “true sale” when no cash changed hands.

+ + +

Leading instant noodle and biscuit maker Monde Nissin Corp (MONDE PM) has its maiden listing today.

BANKS
PNB to generate P34bn from monetization of real estate
  • Philippine National Bank (PNB PM) is set to unlock P34bn in capital through extraordinary gains from the spinoff of its interest of its three most valuable property assets into a real estate unit
  • PNB disclosed that the transaction would raise P10bn in fresh capital after the issuance of property dividends to stockholders
    • Plan earlier announced to exchange three prime properties for an equivalent value in shares of stock of PNB Holdings Corp
  • As a result, the bank will be able to record about P34bn in its books reflecting the difference between the fair value of P46.677bn and the book value of P12.6bn of the properties exchanged for shares
  • PNB president Wick Veloso said after generating P34bn in additional capital, property dividend declaration would cost around P24bn, leaving p10bn in fresh capital
  • Change will be reflected in PNB’s income statement as of May 28, 2021
PROPERTY
POGO tax bill certified as urgent
  • President Duterte certified Senate Bill (SB) 2232 as urgent
  • SB 2232 would require POGOs to pay 5% of gross gaming revenue (GGR) as tax to the Bureau of Internal Revenue
    • This would be in addition to the 2% of GGR paid to PAGCOR
  • POGO employees will also pay 25% income tax (with an assumed minimum annual salary of 600,000)
  • The government expects the passage of this law to raise P120bn in tax revenue over four years 
  • The Bayanihan 2 Act, which was passed in September 2020, imposed taxes based on 5% of gross bets or turnover on POGOs
  • This new bill would lower POGO taxes since gross revenue is just a fraction of gross bets/turnover
  • Thus, the new bill could slow down POGO closures
  • Office leasing comprises 74% of Filinvest Land’s NAV and 52% of Megaworld’s NAV
  • Manila Bay area land comprises c. 40% of SM Prime’s
  • FLI is trading at 5.1X FY21E P/E, 0.3X FY21E P/B and 65% discount to NAV
  • MEG is trading at 6.9X FY21E P/E, 0.5X FY21E P/B and 60% discount to NAV
  • SMPH is trading at 32.9X FY21E P/E, 3.1X FY21E P/B and 5% discount to NAV
POWER
National Grid Corp warns of rotational blackouts
  • The National Grid Corp of the Philippines (NGCP) raised the red alert warning on the Luzon power grid from 2pm to 4pm yesterday
  • Yellow alert notice was up from 11am to 2pm and 4pm to 5pm
    • A red alert means there is severe power deficiency which could lead to rotational power interruptions
    • A yellow alert means there were not enough reserves to cover the largest running generating unit but does not necessarily lead to power outages
  • Energy Secretary Alfonso Cusi said supply was severely affected by several power plants on outage despite demand being well within forecasted levels
  • Some 3,771MW were off the grid on Monday due to outages including scheduled maintenance
    • 647MW Sual unit 2
    • 345MW GNP Mariveles
    • 300MW Sem Calaca Unit 2
    • 484MW (out of 1,200MW) Ilijan due to limited gas supply
  • NGCP implemented manual load dropping to  maintain a balance system in several provinces during the red alert period
  • Meralco experienced supply deficiency of 163MW and 95MW from 1-2PM and 2-3PM, respectively, which led to manual load dropping within its franchise
TELCOS
PLDT prepares international capacity surge with submarine cable system
  • PLDT (TEL PM) plans to increase its international capacity by five times by end of the year through the Jupiter submarine telecommunications cable system
  • The project would increase its international capacity to almost 60 terabits from the current 10 terabit capacity running on its 15 major international cable systems
  • PLDT said it aims to hep make the Philippines a hub connecting Southeast Asia to United States as a draw for firms expanding loud and network offerings
  • In 2017, it said it would investment P7bn in a Trans-Pacific cable system
  • In April, Converge ICT Solutions (CNVRG PM) announced plans to invest P4.8bn in the Bifrost Cable System, a 15,000km undersea cable project from Singapore to the North American West Coast including a branch to the Philippines
