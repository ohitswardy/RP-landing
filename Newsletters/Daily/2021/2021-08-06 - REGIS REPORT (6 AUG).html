REGIS REPORT

06 August 2021

We have PLDT (TEL PM) and ICTSI (ICT PM) results recaps following their respective earnings calls yesterday. Both did not stray too much from our estimates.

Globe (GLO PM) and Robinsons Land (RLC PM) also just released their numbers this morning and we offer our initial take on them. We will take time to digest their 2Q results fully after their respective briefings (later for RLC and Monday for Globe).

  • PLDT (TEL PM) (=) – PLDT reported net income of P12.9bn for 1H and we estimate ‘core income’ was P14.3bn (+10% YoY), in line with estimates. Our take of 2Q does not differ markedly from 1Q: (1) broadband growth is still looking vigorous but (2) wireless is showing softness. PLDT’s attributable losses from Voyager (38.5%-owned) amounted to P1bn in 1H but that firm is aiming to turn positive by 2023.

  • ICTSI (ICT PM) (=) - 1H profit excluding one-offs rose 89% YoY to US$162mn, in line with Regis but trending ahead of consensus. Yields mildly disappointed but this was offset by higher EBITDA margins. 2Q profit improved by 21% QoQ, as volumes and yields rose and tax rates normalized, offsetting the mild drag from start-up costs at its new port in Nigeria. Management indicated volume growth in 3Q21 so far is still in the “double-digits”, which appears stronger than our forecast for the remainder of the year (+7% YoY).

For foreign investors >> ICTSI – 2Q21: Strong sequential rebound in Asia ports

For domestic investors >> ICTSI – 2Q21: Strong sequential rebound in Asia ports

  • Robinsons Land (RLC PM) (+) – 2Q21 net profit rose soared 391% to P2.55bn, ahead of REGIS and consensus forecasts, due to lots sold to JV companies. If not for the lot sales, we estimate that operating profit would have been in line with our forecast. Due to the hard lockdown, mall revenue fell 14% QoQ but still rose 43% YoY since restrictions were not as tight as in 2Q20. Meanwhile, reservation sales were flat QoQ (+247% YoY) to P2.8bn, also in line with our forecast. The company will provide more details during its briefing this afternoon.
  • Globe (GLO PM) (-) – Globe also just released its results this morning. Headline income for 1H was P13.0bn (+13% YoY). Stripping out exceptional items, including adjustments for the tax changes from the CREATE Law, we estimate core income was P10.7bn (-3% YoY) – which ought to be in line with consensus, albeit lower than our estimates. This implies a sequential QoQ improvement for 2Q. The segmental revenue numbers for Globe appear to show that broadband growth was sputtering in 2Q, unlike for PLDT. On the wireless side, we estimate practically no change in revenue market shares QoQ. Outside of core operations, the most important highlight seems to be the progress at Mynt (GCash). Mynt claims to be at EBITDA and profit breakeven in June and GCash is on track to see close to P3.4trn in transactions in its network this year (probably more).

Meanwhile, San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) provided some more detail on its 2Q results at the earnings call yesterday. Although alcohol volumes fell QoQ (-11% for beer, -25% for gin), the declines were less severe than expected considering the strict lockdowns and liquor restrictions in April. Food profits also moderated QoQ as prepared and packaged foods dipped by single digit, but segment contribution remained elevated due to high chicken prices. Click below for more.

+++

Headline inflation dipped to 4.0% in July, from 4.1% the previous month. This should ease pressure for the Bangko Sentral (BSP) to act on rising prices.

+++

The PSE announced the changes to the composition of the PSEi (“PCOMP”). AC Energy (ACEN PM) is being added to it and, in a surprise twist, Converge (CNVRG PM) will be as well. We did not expect CNVRG to be because the PSE’s own rules require a stock to be listed for at least a year before it qualified for inclusion. CNVRG was only listed 4Q20. DMCI (DMC PM) and Emperador (EMP PM) will be taken off the PSEi.

The PSE apparently decided to change its rules and immediately applied the changes for this month’s index re-composition.

Based on these latest policy revisions, recent listing Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) is now a likely candidate for PSEi inclusion in 1Q22. Among the likeliest to be removed is San Miguel (SMC PM). Given this latest action, however, we would not be surprised if the policies are revised or, worse, “re-interpreted”, differently again six months from now.

MACRO
Inflation eases to 4% in July
  • Inflation slowed to 4.0% in July from 4.1% the previous month
    • Due to decelerating transport inflation (fares dropped as capacity limits eased)
    • However, food inflation accelerated, as prices of fish and vegetables surged
    • Utilities inflation also accelerated due to elevated global crude prices
  • Year-to-date inflation averaging at 4.4%, above BSP’s target range of 2-4%

PROPERTY
Robinsons Land 2Q21 earnings nearly quintuple - ahead on lot sales
  • Robinsons Land’s 2Q21 net profit soared 391% to P2.55bn, ahead of REGIS and consensus forecasts
  • The company recognized revenue from lots sold to JV companies (Shang Properties and HK Land are JV partners)
  • We estimate that if not for these lot sales, operating profit would have been in line
  • Due to the hard lockdown, mall revenue fell 14% QoQ but still rose 43% YoY since the restrictions were not as tight as in 2Q20
  • 2Q21 office revenue rose 10% YoY on 4% floor area expansion
    • The 5% QoQ improvement in revenue might indicate better occupancy (from 93% in 1Q21)
  • Despite the hard lockdown, presales were flat QoQ (+247% YoY) at P2.8bn, 55% of the 2019 average
    • 1H21 reservation sales rose 19% to P5.6bn, in line with our forecast
  • The company will hold its briefing at 3PM on August 6
  • RLC is currently trading at 9.2X FY22E P/E, 0.8X FY22E P/B, and 59% discount to NAV

INDUSTRIALS
D&L delays Batangas plant operations to May 2022
  • D&L Industries said it will delay the earliest start of commercial operations for its Batangas plant to May 2022 after its subsidiary was granted an extension by PEZA

OTHERS
PSE Index August 2021 Rebal: ACEN (+), CNVRG (+), EMP (-), DMC (-)
  • The PSE announced that AC Energy (ACEN PM) and Converge ICT (CNRG PM) will be added to the PSE index while Emperador (EMP PM) and DMCI Holdings (DMC PM) will be removed
    • The PSE revised its Index policy – stocks that have been listed in 6 months of the 12-month period in review can be eligible for index inclusion, subject to liquidity, float, and market cap conditions
  • The rebalancing will take effect on the morning of August 16 (close of August 13)
  • We estimate inflows to:
    • AC Energy (ACEN PM) – US$43.3mn
    • Converge ICT (CNVRG PM) – US$29.1mn
    • Meralco (MER PM) – US$9.4mn
    • Security Bank (SECB PM) – US$3.0mn
  • We estimate outflows to:
    • Emperador (EMP PM) – US$30.7mn
    • DMCI Holdings (DMC PM) – US$17.9mn
    • Ayala Land (ALI PM) – US$9.9mn
    • Ayala Corp (AC PM) – US$5.4mn
    • SM Investments (SM PM) – US$4.6mn
  • Beginning the Feb 2022 rebalancing, the free float requirement for PSEi constituents will be 20%, from the current 15%
  • The PSEi constituents that have public float levels below 20% are:
    • AC Energy (ACEN PM) - 15.6%
    • First Gen (FGEN PM) – 18.7%
    • San Miguel Corp (SMC PM) - 15.8%
  • Note that the PSE index is free-float market cap weighted, so an increase in public float would increase the weighting in the PSE index, holding all other things constant
