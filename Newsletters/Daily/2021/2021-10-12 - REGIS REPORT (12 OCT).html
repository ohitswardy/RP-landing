REGIS REPORT

12 October 2021

Vehicle sales remained sluggish in September. Although unit sales popped up 36% MoM from the August lockdown lows, the September figure was still down 12% YoY, and down 32% against 2019 levels. For 9M21, vehicle sales were up 29% YoY, but are still down 28% from 2019 levels. This is consistent with banking sector data, which shows that loans for autos (4-wheels) were still down 16% YoY as of 2Q21. Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) continues to gain markets share, helped in no small part by its sister company Toyota Financial Services Inc (TFSI), which has grown substantially to now have a loan book equivalent to almost 20% of the total banking system’s auto loan book. The strength of both TMP and TFSI underscore our Buy call on parent GT Capital (GTCAP PM).

However, the fact that even Toyota’s 9M21 sales are still down 19% from 2019 levels suggests that it’s largely still a demand / affordability issue. A broader recovery in the economy – and employment – is likely needed to get the numbers back to pre-Covid levels.

===

Ayala Land’s REIT (AREIT PM) is now trading at a dividend yield of around 4.8%, based on our estimates. This basically offers no spread over the 10-year risk free rate (BVAL). Meanwhile, Megawide (MWIDE PM), whose airport business is in the doldrums due to Covid movement restrictions is offering prefs at 5.3%, just 50bps above the 10-year BVAL.

What gives? The 10-year bond yields seem to be reacting to inflation (4.8% in Sept). The 10-year BVAL is up nearly 100bps from the June levels. However, the short end of the curve has stayed remarkably flat. The 1-year BVAL is still down at 1.62%, which is actually 5bps lower than the similar rate in June. This to us demonstrates the extremely high levels of liquidity in the financial system, following all the BSP’s moves in 2020, including bond-buying (our version of QE), direct government lending, and RRR cuts.

The conclusion? Companies should sell as much assets into REITs as they can. We’re frankly surprised that telco towers, power plants, toll roads, etc aren’t yet jumping on the bandwagon…

CONSUMER
Vehicle sales drop 12% YoY in September
  • Industry car sales fell -12% YoY to 21,493 units in September
    • According to CAMPI/TMA
    • On a MoM basis, car sales recovered +36% after quarantine restrictions in Metro Manila were lifted
    • YT-September industry car sales are up 29% YoY to 191,606 units (vs CAMPI’s FY21E of 295k units, +32% YoY)
    • To reach CAMPI’s target, car sales should average 35k per month for the rest of the year
  • Toyota sold 9,929 units in September (-7% YoY, +27% MoM)
    • Brings YT-September sales to 92,318 units (+46% YoY)
    • To reach Toyota’s target of 130k units (+30% YoY), Toyota needs to sell 12.6k units per month for the rest of the year
    • To reach Regis’ target of 117k units (+17% YoY), Toyota needs to sell 8k units per month for the rest of the year
  • Toyota is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for c. 20% of GTCAP’s profit
  • GTCAP is trading at a 12% discount to its market-based NAV of P609/sh

  • PEZA accredited offices – 165,500sqm – 98% occupied

    • Bacolod – 11,300sqm – 100% occupied
    • North Point (Talisay, Negros Occidental) – 4,600sqm 100% occupied
    • Makati and Alabang – 1,600sqm – 100% occupied
  • Retail – Vertis North – 39,300sqm
    • Ayala Land will pay a guaranteed monthly building lease to AREIT for 36 years
  • We estimate that AREIT’s 2022E dividend yield is currently 4.8%
