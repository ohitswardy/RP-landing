REGIS REPORT

06 July 2021

Various local government units in Metro Manila have apparently slowed down, if not suspended, the administration of vaccines amidst a lack of available supply of doses. We suspect the dearth of supply in NCR is due to increasingly more doses being sent to provincial areas. Case count in NCR has fallen while infections in provincial areas have risen sharply.

The country has administered 11.7m doses as of July 4, and 8.8m (8% of the population) have had at least one dose.

+++

The government’s total outstanding debt reached P11.07 trillion as of May, which would out it at ~62% of projected FY21 GDP. Bulk of the borrowings (71%) are in local currency, with the government continuing to move away from FX debt.

+++

Regional coal prices are still soaring and are now almost 3x what they were a year ago. The share price of Semirara (SCC PM), however, has not done nearly as well: it’s up a “mere” 33% over the same period.

We are aware that many investors are allergic to coal, either because of their own ESG mandates or in self-perpetuating anticipation of even more investors avoiding it. Nonetheless, the stock looks very attractive, if not compelling today. It’s ‘officially’ trading at 10x PER and a dividend yield ~10%, but that it is on the assumption that coal prices are at US$85/MT. Regional coal prices are already at US$143.

For a good sense of where earnings and multiples may end up if coal prices settle closer to where they are today, check out this table of sensitivities for SCC.

+++

Headline inflation eased to 4.1% in June, from 4.5% in May.

AIRLINES
Airline regulator reimposes fuel surcharge
  • The Civil Aeronautics Board (CAB) has approved the reimposition of the passenger fuel surcharge following its suspension last year
  • The CAB board has approved a fuel surcharge rate under Level 1 of the passenger fuel surcharge matrix for the months of July and August
    • Airlines are allowed to impose an increase of P34 to P132 for domestic flights, and P163 to P1,557 for international flights
    • This is based on the average price of jet fuel in April/May 2021 of US$71.82 per barrel or P21.78 per liter
  • We estimate the Level 1 fuel surcharge will increase average domestic ticket fares by c. 5%
  • CAB reviews global jet fuel prices every two months and determines the allowable surcharge level for the next two months
    • Jet kero spot price is at US$77 per barrel
