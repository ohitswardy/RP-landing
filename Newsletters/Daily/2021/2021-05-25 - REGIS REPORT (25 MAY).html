We have two more rather late 1Q reports out yesterday, both delivering bottom line numbers below estimates:

  • Megawide (MWIDE PM) (-) – Net loss of P124mn in 1Q was wider than street forecast. Underlying operations remained soft for the airport and landport units and these offset the decent performance of the construction unit. The company finalized the debt restructuring agreement for the Cebu airport, which will allow them to preserve liquidity until 2023.

  • Vista Land (VLL PM) (-) – 1Q profits came below expectations at P2.0bn, down 15% YoY. While residential revenue was the reason for the shortfall (due to limitation on construction activity), what struck us most was the huge jump in rental receivables which are now approaching 2x annual  revenue.

We got a bit more detail on Alliance Global’s (AGI PM) 1Q21 results from their briefing yesterday. Vaccine resistance is high, if a recent poll is to be believed, and is one more obstacle the government will have to overcome to inoculate 70% of the population. Only 1/3rd of the country’s population are willing to be vaccinated today. It doesn’t help that bulk of the vaccines received so far are from Sinovac, which President Duterte himself was unwilling to be injected with.

MACRO
Filipinos evenly split between being willing / uncertain / unwilling to be vaccinated
  • Last week, the Social Weather Stations (SWS) released a survey that showed only 32% of Filipinos are “willing” to get vaccinated against Covid-19. The balance was roughly evenly split between those who were “uncertain” (35%) and those who were “unwilling” (33%) to be inoculated. Of the 33% unwilling, the vast majority (26%) replied “surely not”.
  • The main reason for the reluctance was fear of side effects (39% among “uncertain” and 30% among “unwilling”). Other reasons included doubts over the safety/effectivity, and (false) reports of fatalities as a result of taking the vaccine.
  • The same survey showed that only half of Filipinos are “confident” in the thoroughness of the government’s evaluation of the vaccines.
  • The figures are worrisome, as we believe a successful Covid-19 vaccination program is critical to the economy’s recovery. A stronger information / PR campaign will be needed to increase the population’s confidence in getting inoculated.

House committee approves bill creating 3-year development plan for domestic auto industry
  • The House Committee on Trade Industry approved an unnumbered substitute bill to House Bill 1833 or the proposed Philippine Motor Vehicle Manufacturing Industry Act
    • Bill authorizes three-year planning for the industry (National Motor Vehicle Manufacturing Development Plan) to be prepared by the Board of Investments (BoI)
  • The bill also provides for investment incentives for motor vehicle manufacturers registered with the BoI such as income tax holidays, deductions for training expenses, and the opportunity to carry over net operating losses, among others
