Today, we highlight the gradual improvement in the daily vaccinations tallied across the country over the past week. 

The average daily vaccinations over the past week climbed to over 200,000. While this pace is not nearly enough to bring the Philippines to herd immunity by year-end, it is still a visible improvement over the previous weeks.  The country will need to administer ~500,000 doses daily to hit 70% within 1Q22.

We expect the key economic centers, however, to achieve the 70% level earlier than the rest of the country.  A larger share of the vaccines arriving in 2H21 will be of the mRNA variety (Pfizer and Moderna).  Given the likely constraints relating to storing and administering these, these vaccines are likely to be disproportionately used in the large urban centers.

Consumer NPL data for 1Q21 was a bit of good-news-bad-news. The bad news is that consumer loan NPL ratio increased 42bps QoQ to 9.75%. The good news is that that’s the slowest QoQ increase since the pandemic started. The bad news is that auto (4-wheel) NPLs jumped 131bps to 12.26%. The good news is that mortgage NPLs were up just 21bps QoQ to 9.44%.

The deceleration in consumer NPLs is welcome news. However, it is unclear how the new lockdowns in April might have affected households’ finances. Anecdotally, banks we speak to have not noted a significant uptick. We’ll keep our fingers crossed, as the stress won’t be reflected in the NPL data until 3Q.

BANKS
Consumer loan NPLs up to 9.75% in 1Q21, but decelerating
  • Consumer loan asset quality deteriorated again QoQ in 1Q21, though the pace has decelerated nicely. Overall consumer loan NPL ratio was 9.75%, up 42bps from the 9.33% at end-2020. That’s the slowest QoQ increase since the Covid-19 pandemic started. That’s also slower than the 59bps QoQ increase in total NPLs in 1Q21.
  • Mortgage NPLs inched up 21bps QoQ to 9.44%. The biggest jump was seen in Motor Vehicle NPLs, which surged 142bps QoQ to 12.10% from 10.68% at end-2020. The MV NPLs were driven by a spike in Motorcycle NPLs to 9.60% from 6.20%. Auto loan (4-wheels) NPLs were still higher at 12.26%, up 129bps QoQ.
  • Credit card NPLs improved to 8.48%, down 37bps QoQ, while Salary loan NPLs were flat at 6.60%. As we have cited in the past, banks tend to write off unsecured loans much more quickly, which masks the new NPL formation (data that is not disclosed by banks or the BSP).
  • Overall consumer loans contracted 2.5% YoY and 1.7% QoQ. However, mortgages grew 7.9% YoY and 0.9% QoQ. Banks’ continued appetite for mortgage lending is surprising, considering the high NPL ratio, in what should be a low-risk asset (in theory). We also note that condo prices within Metro Manila are down 10-20% YoY. It is likely that the new mortgages are for landed property outside Metro Manila, and for leisure property, where demand is strong and prices are rising.
  • Motorcycle loans were also up 9.5% YoY and 2% QoQ. This is likely due to a) continued limited public transportation in Metro Manila, and b) the surge in demand for delivery services (eg Grab, Lalamove, etc) given the explosion in e-commerce.
  • The deceleration in consumer NPL ratios in 1Q21 is welcome news. However, note that Metro Manila went into another lockdown in April. That said, the mobility restrictions were nowhere near as bad as they were in 2Q2020. Still, any new NPLs as a result of the recent lockdowns will only hit the banks’ books in 3Q21.

 

CONSUMER
GrowSari raises over $30mn in funding from global investors
  • E-Commerce platform GrowSari said on Monday that it had raised over $30mn this year from two rounds of capital raising efforts
  • Series A investors include Robinsons Retail, JG Digital Equity Ventures and Wavemaker Partners LLC
  • Series B investors include Singapore-based Pavilion Capital Fund Holdings Pte. Ltd, Saison Capital, Tencent Holdings and International Finance Corp
  • Co-founder Reymund Rollan said in a statement Monday that the fresh funds will help to more than double GrowSari’s existing coverage and service to more than 300k sari-sari stores including those in Visayas and Mindanao
  • As of end-2020, the app had a user base of 50,000 stores in over 100 municipalities across Luzon

 

PROPERTY
Megaworld sees stronger domestic residential sales
  • Megaworld Chief Strategy Officer Kevin Andrew Tan said that the company was seeing “a lot” of domestic residential sales in the past two months
    • Not the same as 2019 levels 
  • In 1Q21, reservation sales were flat at P20.7bn, 56% of the 2019 average
  • MEG is currently trading at 6.9X FY21E P/E, 0.5X FY21E P/B, and 60% discount to NAV

 

TELCO
Globe 5G network now covers at least 88% of NCR
  • Globe Telecom said on Monday that its 5G network now covers at least 88% of the National Capital Region
  • Bonifacio Global City CBD and Ortigas CBD having the highest coverage at 97%, followed by Makati CBD at 96%
  • “We expect more installations of 5G cell sites for the remaining six months of the year, said Globe chief commercial officer Issa Guevarra-Cabreira
  • Globe earlier announced plans to spend a “record” P70bn capex for 2021 to support aggressive cell site builds, upgrade all sites to 4G/LTE, “fiberization” of homes nationwide and the roll out of 5G technology
