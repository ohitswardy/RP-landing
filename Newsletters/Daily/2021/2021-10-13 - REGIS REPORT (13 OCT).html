REGIS REPORT

13 October 2021

The August trade deficit ballooned 64% YoY to $3.58bn. Half of the $1.4bn increase was due to Fuel imports, which more than doubled from $603mn in August 2020 to $1.3bn this year in line with the spike in global energy prices. Year-to-August, the deficit was also up 64%to $25.7bn as export growth of 19% YoY was overwhelmed by the 31% growth in imports. Fuel imports for 8M21 were up 65%.

There’s some good news, though. Consumer goods imports were up 27% YoY, and capital goods imports grew 24% in 8M21. Granted, both were coming off a low base. But compared to 2019, consumer goods imports are just 4% off 8M2019 levels; most notably, non-durable goods are already up 7% compared to 8M2019. Capital goods imports are 7% below 2019 levels. The recovery bodes well for our “Glass half full” base case of a nascent recovery in demand.

The rebound in consumer goods imports, though, seems at odds with the continued low foot traffic at malls. We reckon the pandemic has rapidly shifted consumers to online platforms such as Shopee, Lazada, etc. How permanent might this shift be? Could malls (occupancy and rent) lag even after the economy fully reopens?

Semirara (SCC PM) declared a special dividend of P1.75/sh, higher than our forecast special div of P1.25/sh. This takes the stock’s dividend yield up to 7% -- even after the stock has more than doubled in the last 6 months alone.
