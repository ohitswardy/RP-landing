REGIS REPORT

19 August 2021

We are initiating coverage on AC Energy (ACEN PM) with a HOLD rating and a target price of P7.8/share. The stock has been one of the market darlings the past year and was a recent addition to the PSEi. In the global scramble for ESG-friendly plays, the stock has been a clear beneficiary and there is some justification for this given its exceptional track record.

However, a sober look puts its multiples at a daunting 47x PER and 23x EBITDA for FY22E and our estimate of the aggregate intrinsic value of its existing projects and visible pipeline is at only P3/share. For those who have not noticed, ACEN’s share of Ayala Corp’s (AC PM) value is now already bigger than Ayala Land (ALI PM) and BPI (BPI PM).

While we are upbeat about the prospects of the firm and the sector, we think the theme is now a little overplayed. Current levels are justifiable only with execution that is more-than-flawless not just on its existing pipeline but also beyond.
