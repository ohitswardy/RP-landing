REGIS REPORT

28 May 2021

Two Buy reports out today. The companies couldn’t be more different – Puregold (PGOLD PM) and Semirara (SCC PM). Both stocks have de-rated, but the investment case for each diverge. Many investors have shunned coal miners and coal power producers. But if Klyne is right, Semirara’s dividend yield could be as high as 17%. Trash to some, treasure to others. For Puregold, the core story – formalization, and premiumization of retail – hasn’t changed. But the stock now trades near all-time low valuations and is likely worth a second look. More on both below.

Meanwhile, Robinsons Land (RLC PM) released more details on its planned REIT listing, which will include 14 PEZA-accredited office building. The listing should highlight just how under-valued the stock is. At the maximum offer price, the listing would raise P26bn in cash for RLC. That’s almost a third of its P86bn market cap today.

In other news, ICTSI (ICT PM) raised its stake in its Congo port from 52% to 62%. Meanwhile, Philippine National Bank (PNB PM) announced the untimely passing of its CFO, Mr Nelson Reyes.

+ + +

The outlook for Semirara (SCC PM) has improved dramatically following the strong recovery in regional thermal coal prices. Newcastle thermal coal spot price is now at US$105 per MT, up from around $60 this time last year. Even at our more conservative coal price forecasts of US$80-85 per MT, we upgrade our NPAT forecasts by 12-43%. We expect SCC’s free cashflow to surge, with EBITDA set to increase from P10bn in 2020 to P16bn in 2022. The company paid its regular div last month but we believe there is ample room for them to pay special dividends at least until next year. With this, our div yield forecast is 17%, which is the highest amongst regional coal miners and Philippine stocks.

Despite the large earnings upgrades, we’ve maintained our TP at P14/sh, recognizing that ESG trends could limit the capital appreciation. We nonetheless expect that the combination of free cash flow growth and dividend growth will lead to SCC’s shares to outperform the market on a total return basis over the next 12 months.

It is worth noting that SCC accounts for c.50% of its parent DMCI’s (DMC PM) earnings and the recent upgrades could raise DMC’s earnings forecasts by 6-21%.

Puregold’s (PGOLD PM) share price has underperformed the PCOMP by over 25% over the past 12 months, due in part to its deletion from the MSCI Index. This has pushed down multiples to near all-time lows, and we believe current levels remain attractive, notwithstanding potential corporate governance concerns from a recent related party transaction.

We refresh our forecasts for PGOLD following the strong 1Q21 results, though we are adjusting our TP down to P42/sh (-11%), which implies 14x 2021E PE. We believe the reduced multiple (relative to its historical average) is reflective of a “normalized” EPS CAGR of +8-9% over the next 5 years vs the +18-19% EPS CAGR historically.
