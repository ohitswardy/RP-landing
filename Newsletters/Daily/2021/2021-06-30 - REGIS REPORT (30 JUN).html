REGIS REPORT

30 June 2021

The vaccines to be donated by the Japanese and US governments (2m in total) will arrive in July. Half of this will be AstraZeneca vaccines from Japan landing on July 8.

This bumped up the number of vaccines scheduled to come in July to over 13m, despite some delays elsewhere.

+++

Citicore Power is hoping to raise P8-10bn selling 49% of itself in an IPO structuring its business as a REIT. The company, controlled by the principal behind Megawide (MWIDE PM), is focusing on renewable energy, more specifically solar power. It claims to have 163MW of solar capacity and aims to have 1,500MW by 2025.

Beyond this specific offering in itself, this IPO is interesting from at least two other perspectives. 

First, it finally gives investors, especially domestic ones, a closer comparable to AC Energy (ACEN PM) because of its claimed renewable focus. ACEN has been on a tear, share price-wise, over the past couple of years. Another listed name doing something similar would provide a good sanity check.

Second, structuring solar farms as a REIT opens up a world of possibilities for other firms. If it is passes regulatory muster, it could lead to many other businesses being structured as REITs in order to take advantage of the very generous tax breaks. Among the ones we think are good candidates are toll roads and telecom towers.

+++

President Duterte signed an order directing the government-owned Landbank to take over and merge with the United Coconut Planters Bank (UCPB). The merge will create a bank with P2.7trn in assets, fortifying Landbank’s position as the second largest bank in the country. Notwithstanding its size, however, Landbank punches far below its weight in private sector lending and deposits because of the nature of its mandate. We don’t expect that to change.

BANKS
Landbank taking over UCPB
  • Land Bank of the Philippines and United Coconut Planters Bank are set to merge, with Landbank as the surviving entity
  • Under Executive Order No. 142 signed on June 25, President Duterte ordered the two banks to merge
  • The move is seen to solidify Landbank’s position as the second largest bank in the country in terms of resources, with combined assets totaling P2.7 trillion
  • The two banks were directed to work on the merger within six months after the signing of the EO

JFC to raise up to P12bn from preferred share issuance
