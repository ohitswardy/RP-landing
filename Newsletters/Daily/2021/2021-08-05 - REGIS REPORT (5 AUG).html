REGIS REPORT

05 August 2021

We are packed with 2Q/1H results today.

Of the six we officially cover, half are doing better than expected while the other half are doing worse,

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (+) – Net profit for 1H21 was P20.1bn, up almost three-fold from the previous year’s P7.1bn. The jump was most attributable to the reduced provisioning at its banking business. The other key businesses (property and retail) also rebounded, profit-wise, from the trough last year. For 2Q alone, net profit was P10.6bn – a reversal from the previous year’s net loss.

  • Metro Pacific (MPI PM) (-) – 1H core profit of P6.0bn (+14% YoY) trails our estimates, as volumes across key segments fell short. Profits were only partially lifted by lower taxes at the tollroad unit and lower interest expenses at the parent. While new lockdowns could mean downside risks near-term, we nonetheless believe MPI’s businesses stand to benefit once travel restrictions are eased and the economy recovers more meaningfully.

  • Century Pacific Foods (CNPF PM) (+) – Net income rose 21% to P2.7bn for the half, putting it comfortably on track to beat our FY21 estimate (P4.7bn). Branded revenue growth was notably resilient in 2Q (flat YoY) given the exceptionally high base last year. EBITDA margin was supported by tight cost control even amidst heightened pressure on input cost.

  • Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM) (-) – Profit for 1H was up only 2% to P1.7bn as the top line disappointed. Non-essential formats saw a sharp bounce in 2Q21, but underlying trends suggest sales are still well below pre-pandemic levels. On the flipside, Supermarket SSS declined 14% from a high base but was flattish QoQ. EBITDA margin for the segment improved due to increased supplier support and other income.

  • Megaworld (MEG PM) (-) – 2Q21 net profit rose 39% YoY to P2.6bn. However, 1H21 earnings of P5.0bn (-7%) is trailing behind both REGIS and consensus forecasts. 2Q21 office revenue fell 5% as vacancy increased from 4% in 1H20 to 10% as of 1Q21. Meanwhile, 2Q reservation sales fell 3% YoY to P16.5bn, only 44% of the 2019 average. Thus, 1H21 presales of P37.2bn (flat YoY), is also tracking behind our forecast. We have some more detail below, ahead of the company’s results call.
  • San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) (+) – Consolidated income was P17.36bn in 1H21 (+137%YoY), with 2Q declining 21%QoQ to an estimated P7.68bn. The brewery’s income dipped 27%QoQ, presumably due to the lockdown in April. This was partly offset by steady contributions from gin (2Q flat QoQ) and food (+20% revenue growth for protein).

AC Energy (ACEN PM) also reported 1H numbers yesterday with pro forma net income of P2.69bn, assuming the retroactive injection of its international assets. The bottom line figure appears to put it in on track to match consensus estimate of P5.28bn for the year.

+++

The REIT listings of both Megaworld (MEG PM) and Robinsons Land (RLC PM) were approved by the Philippine SEC. Notwithstanding the pandemic, the property firms are in a rush to take advantage of record low interest rates and a regulatory backdrop favorable to REITs. It is uncertain how long these conditions will hold when the economy starts recovering from the pandemic and future governments realize how much in taxes they are giving up. More detail below.
