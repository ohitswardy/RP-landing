REGIS REPORT

15 March 2021

The rise in reported Covid-19 cases continues to be the focus right now. The mayors of Metro Manila have already collectively announced a curfew that begins tonight, and a couple of cities have also introduced bans on the sale of liquor.

The total number of new cases reported continues to climb, reaching almost 5,000 yesterday. This is the highest in over six months and over double the pace in Jan and Feb, leading to a sense of alarm from the public and governments. The new cases pushed “active” cases to almost 50,000 nationwide.

Note that the spike in cases is concentrated in Metro Manila. The fact that population density plays a part should surprise no one. As a result, ICU bed occupancy there is now back at 68%. While that figure by itself is not at a panic-inducing level yet, the new case trajectory implies we could get there very quickly. A look at the charts below should convey that message visually.

PLDT (TEL PM) mobile subsidiary Smart introduced new all-data packages with no expiry.  The packages, dubbed “Magic Data” have denominations from P99 (US$2) and P399 (US$8). Globe (GLO PM), through an MVNO using its network called Gomo, has a 30GB all-data package with no expiry for P299.

We don’t think this is a sign of price deterioration but rather a shift in product towards those who no longer use legacy voice and SMS.  The effective yields for the data under these new packages range from P50/GB down to P17/GB. The highest denomination (P399), however, is probably not going to be that popular with the majority. We estimate the latest average mobile data yields for both PLDT (TEL PM) and Globe (GLO PM) are around P25/GB (US$0.50).

MACRO
Imports flat MoM, exports shrink in January
  • Preliminary trade data showed imports unchanged MoM to US$2.34bn in January 2020
    • Non-electronic imports dropped slightly while imports of electronics rose 2%
    • Fuel imports rose +6% MoM, Capital goods (-5% MoM), Raw material imports (+6% MoM), Consumer goods (-3% MoM)
  • Meanwhile, exports dipped slightly by 4% MoM, led by a 10% drop in electronic exports
  • Trade deficit stood at US$2.42bn in January

MACRO
Infrastructure spending surges MoM in December, exceeds FY20 target
  • The government spent P132.3bn in December 2020 (-25.6% YoY), excluding LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies
  • This brought FY20 infra spend to P681bn (-28% YoY), surpassing the government’s P609.3bn target
    • Cited better-than-expected accomplishments by the Dept. of Public Works
  • This took the government’s overall infra spend (including LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) in 2020 to P869.5bn (-17% YoY)
    • Surpassing the target of P825bn
    • For FY21, the government is targeting to spend P1.17tn on infrastructure (+35% YoY)

TELCOS
PLDT introduces all data package with no expiry
  • PLDT mobile subsidiary Smart introduced “Magic Data” with three denominations
    • P99 for 2GB (=P50/GB)
    • P199 for 5GB (=P40/GB)
    • P399 for 24Gb (=P16.6/GB)
  • The mobile data allocation is available for use on all sites and does not have an expiry
  • All previous offerings were time-bound
  • An associate of Globe, Gomo, currently offers a 30GB package for P299 (=P10/GB)
  • For perspective, the latest average yield per GB for both operators is ~P25/GB
