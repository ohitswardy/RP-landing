REGIS REPORT

09 March 2021

Two more companies reported results yesterday. While Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) reported a bigger loss than we expected (due to an impairment charge), underlying operations are performing well.

  • Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) (=) - The company reported FY20 loss of P8.3bn, exceeding our FY loss estimate, due to a c.P1bn impairment charge on its Jeju property. Excluding this, BLOOM did well operationally, with total GGR climbing to nearly 40% of pre-Covid levels despite current mobility restrictions in Manila. While VIP volumes dipped 18%qoq, we are encouraged by the strength of the domestic market as evidenced by >70% sequential improvement in mass table and slot GGR.  
  • Aboitiz Power’s (AP PM) (-) - 4Q20 core income jumped to P6.1bn (+106%QoQ, 106%YoY), boosted by business interruption insurance claims and better availability of coal plants. The implied energy sales for its generation & RES business in 4Q20 were higher by 25%YoY compared to a depressed 4Q19 base (outages at Therma South and GNP Mariveles). Volumes of its distribution business, however, were more telling. Demand in 4Q was still down 9% from pre-Covid, exhibiting no material improvement from the initial rebound in 3Q.

The Philippines’ “Vaccine Czar” said we could receive over 20mn COVID vaccine doses in 2Q20, sooner than expected. While the arrival of more vaccines is a positive development, the government has been slow to administer the ones that we already have. We started vaccinating Filipinos on March 1 and have so far only used 44,000 out of the 1.1mn doses that we have received.

GAMING
BLOOM EBITDA turns positive as GGR improves to near-40% of pre-Covid
  • Bloomberry reported a net loss of P2.5bn in 4Q, same as the net loss incurred in 3Q, despite a c.P1bn sequential improvement in GGR.
    • This brings the FY20 net loss to P8.3bn, larger than our FY20E of -P7.3bn, dragged by impairment charges recognized on its Jeju property. 
    • We estimate the impairment charge could have amounted to as much as P1bn.
    • Underlying operations are actually ok, with substantial improvements recorded by the local business. This is despite the current mobility restrictions
  • EBITDA turned positive to P129mn in 4Q, bringing the FY20 EBITDA to P1.4bn. Excluding Jeju Sun, BLOOM’s EBITDA would have amounted to P1.8bn for FY20.
  • 4Q GGR was P5.3bn (+21%QoQ, -63%YoY), implying an improved run-rate of P1.8bn/month (~40% of pre-Covid levels from 30% of pre-Covid in 3Q). Although the headline GGR for the year of P22.6bn (-62%YoY) is in line with our FY20E, there were differences in the revenue mix for the 4Q.
    • GGR from mass jumped 78%QoQ to P2.0bn while slot machines jumped 73%QoQ to P2.2bn
    • VIP GGR dropped 40%QoQ to P1.2bn

POWER
AP 4Q20 core income jumps 106%YoY on insurance claims
  • 4Q core income hit P6.1bn (+106%YoY), boosted by the recognition of business interruption insurance claims, better availability of coal plants and higher water inflow for Isabela hydro.
    • FY20 core income of P12.5bn is 25% lower YoY –  short of our FY20E despite the sharp rebound in the quarter
  • Generation & Res – energy sales for FY20 was flat at 77,754GWh, implying that 4Q20 energy sales came in at 6,065GWh (+25%YoY)
    • Implied 4Q20 EBITDA jumped more than 50%
    • Included recognition of BI claims
  • Distribution – distribution volumes were down 8% in FY, implying that 4Q20 vols were still lower by 9%YoY (same as 3Q)
    • Implied 4Q20 EBITDA down 26%YoY, worsening from -4% in 9M20

POWER
AC to divest from Mindanao coal plant
  • AC Energy and Infrastructure Corp (unlisted, under AC) is transferring its shares in GNPower Kauswagan to its partner and affiliates
    • AC Energy (unlisted) holds an 85% interest in GNPower Kauswagan
    • Transfer of indirect ownership interest in the project (4x135MW) for a base selling price of US$453.24mn
    • AC Energy signed the divestment agreement on March 5, 2021 with its partner Power Parters Ltd. Co and certain affiliates
  • AC Energy will also be selling the project site to the retirement company affiliated with the GNPower firms for U$15.9mn
