REGIS REPORT

29 April 2021

The government is extending the lockdown status of Metro Manila and the surrounding provinces (NCR plus) by another two more weeks to May 14. What’ll be changing, though, are the curfew hours. They have been shortened to 10pm-4am (from 8pm to 5am) to give businesses more elbow room.

The daily tally of new infections has dipped from its peak but remains high. The government is reluctant to ease the lockdown restrictions under the current conditions.

 

+++

Aboitiz Power (AP PM) claims that core income for 1Q21 amounted to P6.2bn, which is 3x the level last year and roughly the same as 4Q20.  Note that the core income figure is inclusive of liquidated damages from delays in the completion of GNPower Dinginin as well as business interruption insurance claims for GNPower Mariveles. Those total around P2.4bn.

Outside of the earnings performance, the big picture highlights include (1) the 8% decline in electricity demand for its distribution utilities and (2) the rising electricity spot prices. Normally, one would not expect these two (drop in power demand + rising power prices) to occur at the same time. Outages, however, played a large role.

One has to wonder what the situation would be if demand did not decline. It also should not take too much imagination to extrapolate the situation 3-4 years from now.

+++

Wilcon (WLCON PM) reported net income of P604m for 1Q21, up 84% from the previous year and looking within, if not slightly ahead, our expectations. Net  sales were up 19%, as  the company is lapping the initial phase of last year’s lockdown/s.  Gross margins were a source of positive surprise, climbing to 36.3%.

+++

Globe (GLO PM) President Ernest Cu says GCash will likely do more than P2trn in transactions this year, up from around P1trn in 2020.  Adoption and use of the GCash e-wallet platform has skyrocketed since the pandemic.

MACRO
MECQ in NCR Plus extended until May 14
  • President Duterte extended the Modified Enhanced Community Quarantine (MECQ) in Metro Manila and neighboring provinces until May 14
    • To ease pressure on hospitals that were at near breaking point
    • Quarantine status is subject to the appeals of the local government units
  • Meanwhile, Metro Manila mayors have agreed to shorten curfew hours to 10pm to 4am on May 1 (previously 8pm to 5am)
    • To give a “breathing spell” for businesses in Metro Manila, adding more time for malls and other establishments to operate

POWER
Aboitiz Power 1Q21 up 197% YoY
  • Aboitiz Power reported a core income of P6.2bn, up 197%YoY +2%QOQ. The “core” income included liquidated damages from the GNPD delay and business interruption claims from a GNP Mariveles outage last year totaling about P2.4bn after-tax. Excluding these, core income would have been 84% higher.
  • Core income from generation & RES jumped to P6.036bn (+214%YoY). Outside the boost from insurance claims, energy sales grew (rev +3%, volume +8%) while gross margin improved on lower generation costs (-3%) and purchased power (-31%).
    • Management said that weekly peak demand in Luzon and Visayas in 1Q21 was similar to 2019 levels, and that the impact from the restrictions on demand is much less than the original lockdowns in March 2020.
  • Core income from distribution also improved to P1.090bn (+6%YoY). While total power sales were still down 8% (commercial and industrial -13%, resi +3%), gross margin per kwh printed a little higher at P1.64/kwh vs P1.58/kwh in 1Q20 due to the difference in volume mix.
  • WESM – While mostly soft in the first two months, spot prices jumped to P3.87/kWh in March after hovering at close to P2 since October. Management said the GNPower Mariveles  is on outage and is targeted to be back only by 3Q22. He mentioned both units of Sual are on outage.
  • Big push into RE – Management discussed plans to increase re capacity to 3,900MW by 2030, including the 1,600MW already in the pipeline for the next few years. They are looking to partner with foreign operators including Scatec (new owner of SN Power) and a partner in a previous bid for a Vietnam project.

RETAIL
Wilcon 1Q21 profit rises 84% YoY
  • In a press release, WLCON reported 1Q21 net profit of P604mn, up 84% YoY, broadly within expectations
  • Net sales grew 19% on the back of 9.8% SSSG
    • 2 Depots were opened during the period, bringing total store count to 65
    • Strong top line growth was partly driven by base effects relating to the temporary store closures during ECQ in the last week of March 2020
  • Gross margin improved to 36.3%, tracking above forecasts
    • While contribution of in-house brands came down (49.5% of sales in 1Q21 vs 50.9% in 1Q20), the actual margin for these products expanded
  • On top of the GPM improvement, op margin expanded due to proportionately lower opex (23.2% of sales in 1Q21 vs 25.6% in 1Q20)
  • The company will be hosting a results briefing on 30th Apr at 4pm

 

TELCOS
GCash targets P2trillion transactions in 2021
  • In an online summit, Globe President and CEO Ernest Cu said that Globe is targeting gross transactions of GCash to reach more than P2trillion this year
    • Coming from more than P1trillion in transactions processed last year
  • Gcash saw 38 million registered users, over 1 million merchants and social sellers and 6.8mn daily transactions
  • In January, Globe Fintech Innovations Inc or Mynt raised more than $175mn in fresh capital from Bow Wave Capital Management
