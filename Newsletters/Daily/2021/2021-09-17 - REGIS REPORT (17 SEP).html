REGIS REPORT

17 September 2021

Emperador (EMP PM) disclosed yesterday that it officially submitted the requirements for its listing on the Singapore Exchange. This is part of its previously disclosed plan to have a secondary listing in Singapore to provide a “broader audience and greater access to international investors”. The stated rationale for the Singapore listing seems, frankly, a little too simple and motherhood. We are doubtful that a secondary listing in Singapore for a company already listed here will provide a significant boost in investor interest beyond retail punters there.

The stock has risen sharply in recent months and is now trading at over 30x PER, compared to its historical multiples of <20x.

+++

In a media report following the participation of Converge (CNVRG PM) in a public forum, the company was reported to have provided updated guidance on FY21 expectations. Based on the latest closing price and the guidance, CNVRG would be trading at 38-41x PER and ~20x EBITDA.

For perspective, after the recent 50% run-up, Globe (GLO PM) is on 16.3x and 6.3x PER and EV/EBITDA. PLDT (TEL PM), on the other hand, is on 10.8x and 5.0x. This is before giving any value to their fintech businesses (Mynt/GCash for Globe and Voyager/Paymaya for PLDT).

+++

The airline and tourism sectors are pushing for eased restrictions for those who are vaccinated in order to  begin the recovery process for these industries. However, given the Byzantine nature of local regulations, we reckon this is unlikely to happen until vaccination levels are much higher and case counts fall sharply.

Meanwhile, AC Energy (ACEN PM) is selling two small power barges that don’t quite fit in to its green energy profile.
