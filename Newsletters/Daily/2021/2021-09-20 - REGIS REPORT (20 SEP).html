REGIS REPORT

20 September 2021

Voyager, the fintech holding company partly owned by PLDT (TEL PM), was granted a digital banking license by the Bangko Sentral (BSP). It will be one of only seven entities receiving such a license from the regulator in this first round.

Among the new entrants to the digital banking space, Voyager has arguably the biggest head start as its e-wallet already has over 40m registered users, many of whom have been on the Paymaya platform for years. This user base gives Voyager a huge advantage, as it would have a name familiar to the public and massive amounts of user data to work with already.

PLDT owns ~38.5% of Voyager and the latest funding put a valuation of >US$600m (post-money) for the holding firm.

The commercial operation of GN Power Dinginin (GNPD) plant of Aboitiz Power (AP PM) is delayed again. The first unit of GNPD was supposed to begin commercial operations this 3Q but that’s been pushed to 4Q. The operation of the 2nd unit is now scheduled to begin operations in 3Q22.

The plants are over two years delayed and another quarter is unlikely to make much difference. However, the delays here and the impact they have on supply expectations highlight the precariousness of the demand-supply balance of the Luzon grid. 

Have a look at this chart, keeping in mind that there were already outages in 2020-2021 despite headline capacity being >40% higher than peak demand. In particular, keep an eye on the blue portion of the bars, which represents “average” capacity, net of estimated outages. It has to be said  that much of the capacity arriving after 2022 will be either solar or wind. The effective capacity factors for these plants are likely to be only <20%, a fraction of what coal and natgas plants can deliver.

+++

Boxer Manny Pacquiao declared his intention to run for the Presidency, accepting the nomination of the PDP-Laban, which has been hit by internal strife. His declaration is not a surprise given what’s been going on the past few months (even years). He will be one of what is likely to be a messy field of five or six candidates. Given that Philippine elections do not have run-offs and winners only need to secure a plurality, the race could be volatile and the dynamics can shift quickly, especially with the heavy use of social media trolls.

+++

We have a new note from Rafa (rafael.garchitorena@regis.ph) reminding us that, despite what are sometimes grim headlines, vaccinations are slowly but surely rising. This would hopefully lead to a subsequent decline in infections that would lead to a re-opening of the economy. We are maintaining our positive view of the market, anchored on that premise.
