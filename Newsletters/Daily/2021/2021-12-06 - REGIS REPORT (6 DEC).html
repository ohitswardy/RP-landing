REGIS REPORT

06 December 2021

Globe (GLO PM) released new mobile postpaid plans that essentially matched those of PLDT’s (TEL PM), following the latter’s adjustment of monthly data allocations at the end of Sept. The new plans (“GPlan Plus”) have double the data allocations of the previous ones. The new P999/month plan, for example, now has 20GB of mobile data (from 10GB) with unlimited SMS and voice.

These recent moves are a consequence of the introduction of mobile number portability (MNP), which raises the threat of competition for long-time postpaid customers. Postpaid subscribers account for less than 4% of total subscribers but account for over 20% of mobile revenue. Ironically, postpaid subscribers actually pay more for mobile data. For the P999/month plan, subscribers pay around P50 (US$1.0) per GB. Prepaid subscribers as a whole pay something closer to ~P20 (US$0.40) per GB.

The arrest of Suncity chairman Alvin Chau in Macau is sending reverberations across the entire gaming industry in the region given how big a role the firms he controls plays. The group has by far the largest junket operations not just in Macau, but also the Philippines. Pre-pandemic, it used to account for a quarter of all VIP gaming revenue in this country, and VIP revenue was half of revenue of the private casinos. The group is also now in the process of constructing a new casino under the umbrella of the Alliance Global (AGI PM) group’s gaming license.

What will the VIP business be like now when the pandemic subsides? Is the hope for a return to the pre-pandemic normal for casinos no longer feasible?

+++

The tax authorities are beginning to collect the revised taxes on offshore gaming companies (“POGOs”) operating in the country. The formalized taxation with more “reasonable” rates gives these firms the cloak of legitimacy even though the government of mainland China considers their operations illegal. The question now is if this cloak will hold back their exodus and entice those who left in the past two years to return. Their presence or absence has important implications on property demand and FX inflows, among other things.

BIR to start collecting taxes from POGOs
  • The BIR issued implementing rules on RA 11590 (POGO taxes)
  • This will require POGOs to pay 5% of gross gaming revenue (GGR) as tax to the Bureau of Internal Revenue
    • Non-gaming revenue will be subject to 25% corporate income tax
  • POGO employees will also pay 25% income tax (with an assumed minimum annual salary of 600,000)
  • The government expects the passage of this law to raise P145bn in tax revenue over five years
  • We assume that Metro Manila office take-up from POGOs will be:
    • 2020 - -175,000sqm (net vacancy)
    • 2021E - -280,000sqm (net vacancy)
    • 2022E – zero
    • 2023E - +130,000sqm
  • POGOs occupied c. 11% of Metro Manila office stock in 2019 but now occupy only c. 6%
  • Office leasing comprises 58% of Filinvest Land’s NAV and 46% of Megaworld’s NAV
  • Manila Bay area land comprises c. 35% of SM Prime’s
  • FLI is trading at 5.4X FY22E P/E, 0.3X FY22E P/B and 62% discount to NAV
  • MEG is trading at 6.8X FY22E P/E, 0.5X FY22E P/B and 59% discount to NAV
  • SMPH is trading at 26.8X FY22E P/E, 2.9X FY22E P/B and 10% discount to NAV

Suntrust: casino project not affected by Chau arrest
  • Suntrust Home Developers (SUN PM) clarified that the arrest of Suncity Group Holdings chairman Alvin Chau will not affect the construction of the hotel and casino in its co-development agreement with Westside City Resorts World
    • Suncity Holdings is the parent company of Fortune Noble which owns a 51% stake in Suntrust and Summit Ascent (holder of convertible bonds in Suntrust)
  • Suntrust said that current funds (P7.7bn as of Nov 15) will support the immediate construction and working capital requirements of the hotel and casino complex
  • Mr. Chau has tendered his resignation from the posts of Chairman and director of Suncity Holdings, Summit Ascent and other positions within the group
  • The disclosure also echoed Suncity Holdings (HK listed parent company of Fortune Noble) clarifications that the VIP business are under a different entity owned by Mr. Chau on a personal capacity
  • Tigre de Cristal, the group’s casino hotel in Russia is reportedly under investigation for illegal gambling/junket activities in China
  • Solaire confirmed that Sun City-operated rooms have been closed since November 30
    • At its peak, we estimate Suncity accounted for as much as 13% of total industry GGR. VIP as a whole, was about 50% of total industry GGR.
    • The VIP segment was predominantly driven by junket operators (90% of VIP).
  • While the recent recovery in GGR of Philippine operators has been driven by domestic, the development dents the potential recovery of VIP GGR expected for next year.
TELCOS
Globe doubles data allocations for mobile postpaid plans
  • Globe just doubled the data allocations for postpaid subscriber packages under its new GPlan Plus packages
  • For the P 999/month plan (SIM only), the monthly data allocation has been doubled to 20GB
    • The data allocation for the P1,499 plan was also doubled to 40GB, and so forth
  • The new plans include unlimited SMS and voice calls to all networks
  • These changes now put Globe’s postpaid packages at par with PLDT’s (Smart) following the latter’s move at the end of Sept
    - Simplistically, the new P999 plan puts the cost of mobile data for the consumer at P50/GB, assuming no value to the other services (SMS, voice)
    • We estimate cost/GB for prepaid packages is less than half that (closer to P20/GB)
