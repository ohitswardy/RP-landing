REGIS REPORT

11 November 2021

Lots of results out today but most of them are, surprisingly, better than our (admittedly tempered) expectations. With the economy re-opening, the prospects for 4Q are looking much better for many.

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (+) – Profits for 9M21 amounted to P27.2bn, up +79% YoY, which is on trend to exceed FY21E estimate. Earnings for 3Q alone (P7.1bn), however, were weaker than 2Q21 and also the previous year. The dip in 3Q was related to lower numbers for both SM Prime (SMPH PM) and SM Retail resulting from the lockdown restrictions imposed during the surge in Covid-19 infections in Aug-Sept.
  • Megaworld (MEG PM) (-) – 3Q21 earnings jumped 57% YoY (+19% QoQ) to P3.2bn, as higher construction activity propped up residential revenue. This is a contrast to some other developers, whose residential revenue dropped sharply due to sales cancellations. However, 9M21 net profit of P8.2bn (+10% YoY) is still behind REGIS and consensus forecasts. Office revenue rose 7% QoQ and outperformed our expectations but reservation sales were below 50% of 2019 levels.

  • D&L Industries (DNL PM) (+) – Amidst pressure on overall volumes from the 3Q lockdown, profits improved sequentially (+11%QoQ), driven by better margins for oleochemicals and plastics. Higher forex gains and a lower interest expense also helped the bottom line.

  • Metro Pacific (MPI PM) (+) – Lower interest costs at the parent and higher contributions from Meralco’s non-distribution subs saved MPI’s 3Q21 profit from collapsing QoQ. 3Q21 profit of P3.5bn surprised to the upside, falling just slightly QoQ despite the lockdown and typical seasonal softness in volumes. We anticipate 4Q21 should be better with mobility restrictions eased. Management also noted that an increase in the buyback program is still possible.

We have initial looks on the 3Q numbers of Puregold (PGOLD PM) and San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) pending their respective earnings briefings

  • Puregold (PGOLD PM) (=) - 9M21 net profit rose 14% YoY, broadly in line with expectations. Sales were flat YoY and QoQ. The Puregold brand’s sales were soft (-3% YoY / flat QoQ), but S&R continued to grow (+7% YoY / +5% QoQ) - again highlighting the K-shaped recovery of the sector. More after today’s briefing. 
  • San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) (+) – 9M21 consolidated income was P24.2bn (+68%YoY), with 2Q declining 11%QoQ to P6.84bn. Both beer (+12%QoQ) and gin (+6%QoQ) posted sequential improvements in profit despite the strict lockdowns imposed in August. The food segment (which typically has more volatile margins) likely dragged overall profits vs 2Q. We hope to get more detail in the call today at 4PM.

CONGLOS
SM Investments 3Q results comment
  • Net income P27.2bn (+79%). SM Investments reported net income of P27.2bn for 9M21, up 79% YoY. This implies net income of P7.1bn for 3Q alone, which is a drop from 2Q21 (P10.6bn) and also 3Q20 (P8.1bn). The dip in 3Q income was attributable largely to declines in contributions from both property and retail. Despite this, net income is on track to beat FY21E due to lower banking provisions.
  • Retail profits drop QoQ. Income at SM Retail amounted to P4.8bn for 9M21 (+125% YoY). This, however, implies 3Q income of P1.2bn (-26%). Management blamed the fall to the stricter lockdowns in Aug-Sept due to rising Covid cases in Metro Manila.

  • Focusing on small formats. The Alfamart business is seeing improvement with SSSG of +3% in 3Q (from negative 2-3% in 1H) and revenue growth of 19%. This segment is on track to add 200 stores for FY21 (from 1,012 at the start of the year). The Food Retail group also introduced a new format called Savemore Express, which is smaller than the Savemore supermarkets but still much larger than the Alfamarts. Savemore Express stores are typically ~1,000sqm (vs Alfamart’s 100-150sqm). To date, there are nine Savemore Express outlets.
  • “New channels” account for 11% of non-food retail sales and 2-3% of food retail. The new sales channels (online, delivery, etc) account 10.9% of sales of the non-food retail businesses but most of this is still accounted for by the “call-to-deliver service” (where the customer calls to order his purchases). The proportion for food retail (groceries) is still much smaller at 2-3%.

RETAIL
PGOLD 9M21 net profit +14% to P5.7bn - in line
  • In a very limited press release (link), PGOLD reported 9M21 net profit of P5.7bn +14% YoY – this is tracking broadly within expectations. Net sales of P115bn (-5% YoY) are slightly behind, but operating profit of P9.1bn (+7% YoY) is more or less in line.
  • For 3Q21 alone, net sales were flat YoY.  Puregold brand sales were down 3% YoY, while S&R saw +7% growth – again highlighting the K-shaped recovery within the sector. SSSG figures were not disclosed.
  • The company is hosting a briefing today (11 Nov) at 3pm.

TRANSPORT
ICTSI to raise US$300mn notes to fund perps tender offer
  • ICTSI announced it is raising US$300mn of new senior fixed rate notes with a 10-year tenor
    • Yield at 3.5%
  • Proceeds will be used to partly fund the buyback of US$775mn perps
    • US$400mn perps with a coupon of 5.875% (yield to call at 1.8%); Call date in May 2022 (no step-up if not called)
    • US$375mn perps with a coupon of 4.875% (yield to call at 3.3%); Call date in May 2024 (step-up: US treasuries +343bps + 250bps)
    • Tender offer will end on Nov 16
  • As at end-September 2021, ICTSI’s net debt (inc. perps/concession liab/leases) stood at US$4.3bn, equivalent to 3.9x net debt-to-EBITDA and 6.6x net debt-to-equity
