REGIS REPORT

14 October 2021

Our “Glass half full” strategy scenario continues to play out nicely. The Inter-Agency Task Force (IATF) will take Metro Manila down to “Alert Level 3” from 16 October, relaxing movement restrictions further. Nearly all businesses and services will now be allowed to operate, if to varying degrees – for example, cinemas are now allowed to open for the first time since the pandemic started (details in the table below).. Perhaps most crucially, universities can now have in-person schooling for the first time since March 2020. For primary/secondary levels, the government plans to start “pilot tests” from November.

The move to reopen comes as vaccination rates re-accelerate, daily Covid cases are down, and hospital occupancy is easing.

The sectors that will directly benefit would include restaurants, mall operators, discretionary retail, and domestic tourism. For those looking for a cyclical recovery, the banking sector is looking good too, as NPLs don’t seem to have deteriorated during the recent lockdowns.

Fundamentals and valuations would suggest top picks to include Robinsons Land (RLC PM), Metrobank (MBT PM), Cebu Air (CEB PM) and the like. The initial wave of buying, though, might favor larger, more liquid market proxies such as SM Prime (SMPH PM), Ayala Land (ALI PM), BDO (BDO PM), BPI (BPI PM), Jollibee (JFC PM), and Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM). Among the conglos, Ayala Corp (AC PM) stands out to us, as the stock has lagged big moves in its subsidiaries Globe (GLO PM) and AC Energy (ACEN PM), but also benefits from the reopening plays of ALI and BPI.

+ + +

The IATF also eased quarantine restrictions for Filipinos and foreigners arriving from overseas. Trouble is, the “green lane” list does not include the US or Singapore. Unsurprisingly, it does include China (including HK). Great news for the 1 or 2 Filipinos coming home from Djibouti, or Tuvalu. Or North Korea…

AllDay Supermarkets, the retail chain of the Villar group – which includes Vista Land (VLL PM) and AllHome (HOME PM) – has priced its IPO at P0.60/sh. This would put the IPO size at roughly $90mn, and a market cap of around $270mn.

MACRO
Metro Manila eases to Alert Level 3 until end-October
  • Metro Manila’s quarantine classification will be eased from Alert Level 4 to Alert Level 3 from October 16 to 31
    • Most business establishments, facilities, and services are allowed to open at varying degrees
    • Indoor venues allowed to operate at a max capacity of 30% for fully vaccinated; 50% outdoor
    • Cinemas now allowed to open
      • Pre-COVID, cinemas comprised c. 10% of mall revenue
  • The government also released quarantine classifications for the rest of the Philippines, with restrictions for most cities/provinces eased

TOURISM
Gov’t drops quarantine for fully vaccinated travelers from “green” countries
  • Fully vaccinated Filipinos and foreign nationals traveling from ‘green’ countries or territories tagged as low risk for COVID-19 will no longer be quarantined upon entry to the Philippines beginning Oct. 14
    • A negative RT-PCR test taken 72 hours before arrival is the only requirement; Filipinos can also choose to take the test upon arrival and quarantine until results are out
  • Unvaccinated or partially vaccinated individuals shall undergo a facility-based quarantine until the release of a negative RT-PCR test taken on the fifth day
  • Inbound tourism still not allowed
  • Green countries as of October 8 include Mainland China and New Zealand, among others
    • Other key countries are not included in the list: South Korea, UK, US, Japan
