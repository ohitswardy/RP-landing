REGIS REPORT

31 December 2021

Jefferies’s Chris Wood features the Philippines in his latest GREED&Fear (entitled “Musk and Marcos”) instalment this week. With the economic recovery, and with the BSP holding interest rates down, Chris reckons “equities still look much more attractive” compared to fixed income, at least for domestic investors. However, the PHP might be an issue for foreigners. Chris spends some time discussing the potential of a Ferdinand “Bongbong” Marcos, Jr presidency in the May 2022 elections. While domestic economic policy might be broadly orthodox, we are likely to see foreign policy make an even further shift towards China (vs the West). Chris also wonders how Marcos might regard families that opposed his father 35 years ago…

+ + +

President Duterte signed the 2022 budget into law yesterday. The P5.02trn appropriation is up 11% YoY, and is equivalent to roughly 22% of GDP. The big increases per department come from social spending, with the Department of Health (DOH) up 28%, and Social Welfare (DSWD) up 16%. Infrastructure spending also gets a boost, with the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) up 13%.

Duterte also signed a law carrying over the un-spent budget in 2021 over into 2022. These could provide a boost to GDP and employment as the economy recovers from the Covid crisis.

+ + +

Not quite the spike we were hoping to end the year with. Covid cases jumped to 1,623 yesterday, nearly double the previous day’s 889, and the highest daily count since mid-November. This is still way lower than the 22,000++ we were clocking in September. But with Omicron now in the country, and with all the traditional family gatherings over Christmas, we would expect case count to surge over the next few weeks.

The good news is that vaccination rates are up – 52% nationwide, and close to 80% in Metro Manila – and the government is already administering booster shots. Hospital occupancy is also still quite low. The hope is that the combination of vaccination rates, and (hopefully) milder Omicron severity will not over-burden the country’s healthcare system.

Despite Covid, a Social Weather Stations (SWS) survey, showed 93% of adults are entering 2022 with “hope”. That said, Filipinos do tend to be an optimistic lot. That ratio was only slightly higher than the 91% at the end of 2020, and 96% in 2019. Fingers crossed.

MACRO
Duterte signs P5tn 2022 budget into law; extends 2021 budget for another year
  • President Duterte signed into law the P5.024tn national budget for 2022
    • 2022 budget is 21.8% of GDP and c. 11% higher than 2021
  • The health sector’s budget saw the largest increase, with an allocation of P268bn (+28% YoY)
    • P80bn of this will be used to subsidize the health insurance premiums of 13mn indigent families and 7mn senior citizens
    • P56bn will be used to improve health facilities
  • The DPWH and the DOTr will receive P786.6bn and P75.8bn, respectively, to accelerate the Build, Build, Build program
    • Program includes Network Development Program (P127bn), Flood Management Program (P129bn), Rail Transport Program (P23bn) and the Land Public Transportation Program (P16bn)
  • Meanwhile, President Duterte also signed into law Republic Act No. 11640, which effectively extends the validity of the FY 2021 General Appropriations Act until December 31, 2022
    • Appropriations for maintenance and other operating expenses as well as for capital outlays are now available for release, obligation and disbursement until the end of the next fiscal year
    • However, the budget validity extension would not cover funds for Personnel Services and thus, this will expire by the end of 2021
    • As of end-November 2021, the government has released P4.166tn of the 2021 budget, leaving about P340bn of un-released allocations

MACRO
DOH reports 1,623 new Covid-19 cases
  • The Department of Health (DOH) reported an additional 1,623 additional cases of Covid-19 on December 30, the highest single-day tally since November 21 (2,227 new cases)
  • This brings the total case count to 2,841,260
  • 11,772 active cases
  • The positivity rate of 10% is also the highest since October
  • There were 133 reported deaths and 256 recoveries

MINING
Finance chief backs lifting of open pit mining ban
  • Finance Secretary Carlos Dominguez supports the Environment Secretary’s decision to lift the ban on open-pit mining
  • Dominguez cited DENR estimates wherein 11 pending projects could be immediately started to generate 22,880 jobs in remote towns, raise P11bn in annual tax and non-tax revenues for the government, plus jack up yearly mining exports by P36bn
