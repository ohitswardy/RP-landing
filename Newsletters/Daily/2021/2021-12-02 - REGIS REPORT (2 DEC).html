REGIS REPORT

02 December 2021

Bloomberg reported that Union Bank of the Philippines (UBP PM) has been selected as the preferred bidder for the consumer banking assets of Citibank in the Philippines.  If confirmed (and eventually consummated), it would mean UBP bested the Big Three banks in what could be a transformative deal for itself. Acquiring the Citibank assets would be a big step away from being a trading-centric institution and catapult it to the top 3 in the credit card business.

While the potential acquisition price remains under wraps, UBP is very likely to need new capital to fund this purchase.  It’s a good thing that its largest shareholder Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM) recently announced a deal to sell part of Aboitiz Power (AP PM) for over US$1.4bn.

Mobility as measured by Google reached another pandemic period high, up another 2-4 ppt WoW.  New daily Covid-19 infections are now down to ~500 but the threat of the Omicron variant looms.

Government debt rose to just a hair below P12trn as of Oct.  This is equivalent to ~63% of GDP and is also higher than government target of P11.7bn for this year.

We take another preliminary look at the upcoming rebalancing of the PSE Index (PSEi or PCOMP) in Feb today.  Our analysis suggests Monde (MONDE PM) is almost certainly going to get into the PSEi come February.  There is one other likely new (or returning entrant).  Have a look below to see who we think the other entrant might be and who are the likely candidates to be removed from the PSEi.

MACRO
Mobility at new highs
  • Another week, another new Covid-era high for mobility indicators across the board. Visits to retail/recreation are up 2-4 percentage points WoW, while visits to transit stations are up 3 percentage points WoW. To be fair, it’s possible that this was because of the not-quite-long-weekend (Tuesday 30 Nov holiday). But still. We think the trend will continue into the December holiday season. Anecdotally, the traffic in Metro Manila has built up enough that the mayors have partially reinstated the “coding” for vehicles during rush hour.
  • While the Omicron variant may be an issue for international news, the case count in the Philippines has hovered below 1,000/day for the last week or two. All of which points to a continued uptick in December. Will this lead to a new spike in Covid cases in January?

MACRO
Senate approves 2022 budget on final reading
  • Senators unanimously passed the 2022 General Appropriations Bill worth P5.024tn (+11% YoY)
  • Bulk of the budget will go to the government’s pandemic response, including benefits to healthcare workers, hiring of health professionals, development of COVID-19 treatments, among others
  • The Senate increased the Department of Health’s budget by 26% to P230bn from the original P182bn approved by the Lower House
    • Additional funds for the health sector were taken from the controversial National Task Force Against Local Communist Armed Conflict (NTF-ELCAC)
  • Senators also increased the budget for education to keep face-to-face classes safe
    • The Dept of Education saw a P6.74bn increase in its budget
    • The budget for State Universities also increased by P26.57bn
  • Bicameral Conference Committee will convene next. Congress targeting to approve the budget before Dec. 17
MACRO
NG debt swells to P12tn in October
  • The National Government’s outstanding debt rose to P11.97tn in October
    • 29% was sourced externally, 71% were domestic borrowings
    • Equivalent to c. 63% of GDP (estimated at P18.9tn)
  • Government has accumulated P2.18tn of additional debt so far this year and has also breached its end-2021 program of P11.73tn

BANKS
UBP wins Citi assets?
  • Bloomberg reported that Union Bank (UBP PM) is the “preferred bidder” for Citigroup’s consumer business in the Philippines, citing unnamed sources. If correct, UBP would have bested the Big Three, all of which had expressed interest in the portfolio.
  • If true, the transaction could be hugely transformative for UBP. We wrote in a report in April that Citi’s credit card portfolio was estimated at around P70bn at end-2020, making it among the Top 3 in the country. We estimate that UBP’s total credit card receivables are less than P8bn. It could also fit in nicely with the group’s digital push, particularly with their digital banking license.

  • On valuations, there are lots of rumors swirling in the market, with some investors claiming to have heard a price of 4x P/bk. Bloomberg itself says that the sale “could value the assets at $1bn”. Trouble is, there is not enough publicly available data to make many conclusions. For example, we do not know whether the transaction will include liabilities (deposits), which could materially change the “book value”, and hence, the price-to-book valuation.
  • We can estimate, though, how it might impact UBP’s capital. At 9M21, UBP’s total equity was P110.4bn. At end-2020, its risk-weighted assets (RWA) was P428bn at the parent bank level. At 9M21, its solo/parent CET-1 was 15.7%. Assuming the addition of the P70bn credit card portfolio alone would increase RWA by about 15%, and take CET-1 down to around 13.5%.
  • Now, the question will be where the portfolio will be housed. If it is kept at the parent bank, then the only additional reduction to CET-1 would be the premium paid over book (ie goodwill). However, if it is moved to another entity – e.g. at the digibank or another subsidiary – then that entity would have to be capitalized heavily to take the portfolio. Even at 10%, that would require P7bn in capital. That could take solo CET-1 down to 12% or lower.
  • A few weeks ago, UBP applied to increase its authorized capital, signaling a potential capital call.
  • As we mentioned though, this is all conjecture that is dependent on a) the veracity of UBP’s win, b) the pricing and structure of the sale, and c) where UBP lodges the assets.
OTHERS
MONDE and EMP could be added to the PSEi in Feb 2022
  • Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) and Emperador (EMP PM) could be added to the PSE Index during the mid-Feb 2022 rebalancing
    • MONDE has already met the liquidity requirements and will almost certainly be added to the PSE Index
    • EMP would have to meet liquidity requirements in December
  • If both stocks will be added to the PSEi in February, then the two PSEi constituents most likely to be removed are Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) and Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM)
  • In order for a stock to be added to the PSE Index, it must meet certain criteria:
    • Free float must be at least 15% (20% beginning December 2022)
    • Its median value traded must be among the top 25% for 9 of the 12 months in review (minimum of 6 months for new IPOs)
    • Its 12-month average market cap must be among the highest   
  • Stocks that meet the first criteria can enter the PSEi if they are within the top 25 in terms of 12-month average market cap
  • MONDE has 31% public float while EMP has 15% public float
  • MONDE and EMP are currently in the top 20 in terms of 12-month average market cap
  • An existing PSE index constituent can also be removed if it falls to the 36th position (and below) in terms of 12-month average market cap (from the list of companies that pass the public float and liquidity requirements)
    • BLOOM is currently ranked 36th
