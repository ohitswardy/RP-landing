REGIS REPORT

07 May 2021

We have another day full of results and the standout today is ICTSI (ICT PM), whose results blew estimates out of the water. The telcos Globe (GLO PM) and PLDT (TEL PM) offered contrasting bottom line results but the rapid growth in their broadband businesses was what they had in common.

Check out our brief summaries as well as links to the published comments below.

  • PLDT (TEL PM) (=) – Core income for 1Q21 (including Voyager losses) was P7.1bn, which was in line with our estimates but likely better than the Street’s. Service revenue was up 7% YoY led by a robust 31% growth in broadband. For the rest of the year, we should expect growth in the wireless business to taper off but that for the broadband business to be maintained.

For foreign investors >> PLDT, Inc – Solid 1Q with broadband leading the way

For local investors >> PLDT, Inc – Solid 1Q with broadband leading the way

  • Globe (GLO PM) (-) – Service revenue was up +3% as expected, and that was lifted by a 27% expansion in broadband revenue. EBITDA, however, dropped 11% as marketing expenditures and provisions both soared by over 50% YoY. The company reported net income of P7.3bn (+11% YoY) and core income of P7.4bn (+13%) but both include the tax adjustments due to the CREATE Law. Without that, we estimate core income would have been P5.0bn (-24% YoY). We ought to have more after the briefing today.
  • ICTSI (ICT PM) (+) – 1Q21 profit of US$73mn (+61%) outperformed on better-than-expected volumes and yields, as well as lower losses from associates and tax rate. Volume growth accelerated to +8% and yields jumped +7%, buoyed by sustained volume improvements across its portfolio and the new cargo handling fee in Manila. EBITDA margin reached an all-time high of 61%, with EBITDA at US$265mn (+25%). We expect Street upgrades to follow.

  • Semirara (SCC PM) (+) – Core profit (ex-FX) surged to P2.24bn in 1Q21, tracking well ahead of expectations as both coal mining and power benefited from higher thermal coal prices, better plant availability, and higher power prices. While the coal mining unit should sustain its recovery, unexpected outages at the power unit could dampen overall earnings in 2Q.

We receive more detail from the briefing of San Miguel Food & Beverage yesterday (FB PM). Alcohol volumes remained mixed, with beer still down 11%yoy, but gin up by 29%yoy to a new quarterly record. Food (profit +107%) benefited from the inflationary environment, driven by higher prices of chicken and pork. The lower tax rates stemming from CREATE also added about 11% to the bottom-line.

PLDT (TEL PM) CEO Manny Pangilinan publicly acknowledged the plan to hand over the reins of PLDT to his successor (as CEO), Al Panlilio at the shareholders’ meeting in a few weeks.  Panlilio is currently Chief Revenue Officer and also CEO of PLDT subsidiary Smart.  The move has been widely anticipated given the pronouncements made by Pangilinan more than a year ago, including at our online Zoom event last year.

MACRO
Unemployment rate eases to 7.1% in March
  • Preliminary results of the Philippine Statistics Authority’s March 2021 round of the Labor Force Survey (LFS) showed around 3.441mn unemployed Filipinos
    • Down from 4.187mn in February and 3.953mn in January
    • March unemployment rate at 7.1%, the lowest since the 5.3% in January 2020, as well as the record-high 17.6% posted in April 2020
  • Labor force participation rate improved to 65% in March
    • The country added 3.6mn jobs since January 2021 and 3.8mn jobs since January 2020

TELCOS
Globe 1Q initial comment
  • Revenue numbers are decent, growing 3% YoY.  As is the case with PLDT, this is led by broadband (+27%)
  • Mobile service revenue was down 2% YoY but +1% QoQ.  This actually implies a market share gain for Globe (for wireless) but the company restated its revenue numbers so the basis of comparison has now changed. I hesitate to make a firm conclusion at this point but I still expect Globe to have gained some share in 1Q21.
  • EBITDA and EBITDA margins disappointed.  EBITDA dropped 11% and the margin fell to 48% (from 55%).  The primary reason is a huge jump in marketing and subsidy expenses (+57%) and provisions (+53%).  The latter is understandable because of the pandemi6c.  The former is probably a reaction to almost two years of market share losses.
  • Net income for 1Q was P7.34bn which is up 11% YoY.  This looks like a very good number but includes a rather large positive tax adjustment due to CREATE. Perplexingly, Globe included this tax adjustment in what was considered core income of P7.4bn.  We are, however, excluding that in the table below. Core income, we estimate, was P4.97bn, -24% YoY.  On that basis, the core profit is below expectations.

INFRASTRUCTURE
Megawide airport unit signs debt restructuring agreement with lenders
  • GMR Megawide Cebu Airports Corp. (GMCAC) and its lenders signed an agreement for the rebalancing of its debt servicing commitments and relaxation of debt covenants
    • Outstanding loan at P23.9bn as of end-2020
  • The exercise will temporarily free up approximately P3.6bn in cash from 2021-2023
  • Principal payments will be postponed to 2027-2029
  • GMCAC is a 60%-owned unit of Megawide (MWIDE PM)
  • MWIDE is trading at 17x FY21E EV/EBITDA, at par with historical valuations
