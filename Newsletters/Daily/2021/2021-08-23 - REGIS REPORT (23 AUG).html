REGIS REPORT

23 August 2021

Japanese carmaker Toyota Motor Corp (TMC) announced it was reducing auto production by 40% globally for the month of Sept due to shortages at a number of factories.  Output in other countries will also be reduced.  The carmaker, however, is still maintaining its global full-year production target of 9.3m, which suggests it is still hoping to ramp up production later in the year.

Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) sources bulk of its inventory from Indonesia and Thailand with some models from Japan.  While TMP says its operations will not be affected, we suspect this will not be the case if Toyota’s production cuts persist.  TMP is 51% owned by GT Capital (GTCAP PM).

+++

RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM), the REIT being spun off by Robinsons Land (RLC PM), was priced at P6.45/share.  At that price, RCR would have a yield of 6% and would raise P23.6bn (US$470m) in its IPO.  Its market cap would be P64bn (US$1.3bn).

Based on current assets, RCR is arguably among the more attractive REITs already listed or about to be listed.  All buildings are PEZA-accredited and a mere 3% of space is occupied by POGOs.  This should give investors greater confidence about its lease portfolio.

+++

We published a 2Q recap for the consumer sector over the weekend outlining our key thoughts about the recent quarter.

Among the key highlights was the still-anemic consumption demand evident across nearly every segment.  Investors should not be misled by the flattering YoY comparisons: consumers (and those who sell to them) are still hurt by weak incomes, rising prices and mobility restrictions.  Restrictions on dining made it doubly hard for restaurant operators.  Despite expectation to the contrary, even grocery retailers were not unscathed.

A lot of the year’s hopes are now pinned on a successful reduction of Covid infections that could usher in a 4Q rebound.

Toyota Japan to cut September global output by 40%
  • Toyota Motor Corp. of Japan will suspend output for several days in September, which would result in a 40% cut (c. 360k units) in production globally
    • Out of the 360k reduction, 140k will be at 14 Japanese plants, with the rest in US, China, Europe, and other Asian countries
    • In Japan, production of Rav4, Corolla, Prius, Camry, and Lexus RX will be affected, among others
    • Cited spread of Covid-19 in Southeast Asia affecting suppliers in Vietnam and Malaysia
  • Toyota has maintained its 2021 global production target of 9.3mn vehicles
  • Meanwhile, Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) said that the reduction will not affect the local business
    • According to TMP First VP Rommel Gutierrez
    • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for c. 22% of FY21E profit
    • GTCAP is trading at a 15% discount to its market-based NAV of P615/sh
