REGIS REPORT

12 May 2021

The 1Q GDP numbers released by the government yesterday showed a contraction of 4.2% compared to the previous year. The drop was steeper than Street predictions as the economy continues to reel from the impact of the pandemic and lockdowns. Drops in investment (i.e. capital formation) are leading the contraction in GDP throughout the lockdowns while consumption is holding up (at least relatively). The urgencies of today are understandably trumping the spending for tomorrow.

We think it’s also worth highlighting that the GDP report for 1Q20 (last year) was revised to a contraction of 0.7%. This was +1.5% growth when it was first reported a year ago. This means that the GDP decline this 1Q21 is against a base even lower than previously thought.

We have the usual roundup of 1Q results today. Despite the gloomy GDP figures and the looming impact of the new Covid-19 lockdowns, the results seem to be tracking ahead of estimates for the most part.

  • Puregold (PGOLD PM) (+) – 1Q21 net profit (+15% YoY to P2bn) was boosted by a reduced tax rate arising from CREATE. What is remarkable, however, is that operating profit grew +11% YoY (to P3.3bn, trending above expectations) even if sales were down 8% YoY. We hope to get more detail after tomorrow’s briefing.
  • Jollibee (JFC PM) (=) – The 1Q21 bottom line just about broke even (P153mn) due to tax-related adjustments. Operating profit (+36% QoQ) was broadly in line even as revenues (-12% YoY) are behind. The Philippines continues to be the main underperformer, with SSS down 26% in 1Q. Most of JFC’s international businesses have reportedly recovered to pre-pandemic levels. We expect to get further detail during the briefing (hosted by Regis) on 18 May at 8pm HKT.
  • LT Group (LTG PM ) (+) – Profit grew to P6.487bn (+32%QoQ, +4%YoY), ahead on better performances from its “sin” segments. PMFTC reported flat profit despite a 24% decline in cigarette volumes because of pricing. Bank profits, on the other hand, grew 33%YoY to P1.792bn, with lower provisions (-38%YoY) masking the worsening NPL situation.

  • Emperador (EMP PM) (+) – A limited press release said that 1Q21 income jumped 43% to P2.1bn. Management attributed the 13% revenue growth to the continued strength of its international businesses. All hard-liquor companies in the Philippines sustained growth throughout the pandemic as consumers shifted to cheaper products and increased home-consumption of alcohol.
  • DMCI Holdings (DMC PM) (+) – Headline profit surged 7-fold to P4.25bn. Recurring profit doubled QoQ to P4.019bn, tracking well ahead of Regis and consensus estimates.  Except for Maynilad, all segments outperformed, aided by faster completions, better margins, higher commodity prices, and better power demand.   

We received more detail from the Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) earnings call yesterday. The roll-out of its online initiative is taking longer than expected, and tweaks are still being made to the platform and payment gateways to attract more players. Also, it appears that they continue to serve local patrons, averaging 3k foot traffic/day (14% of pre-Covid), despite being technically closed to the public.

MACRO
1Q21 GDP disappoints at -4.2%
  • GDP fell -4.2% YoY in 1Q21, a larger drop than anticipated (-3.2%)
  • On the demand side, all GDP components contracted at a slower pace than in previous quarters
    • Household consumption and capital formation still weak
    • Government consumption was the only item that expanded, thanks to stimulus packages (Bayanihan II)
  • On the supply side, all major sectors of the economy remained in the red
    • Public construction improved by +26%, after two quarters of declines
    • Private construction, however, remained a drag (-40% YoY)

RETAIL
PGOLD 1Q21 net profit +15% YoY to P2bn - ahead
  • PGOLD reported 1Q21 net profit of P2bn, up 15% YoY, tracking ahead of Regis and consensus expectations. The outperformance was largely driven by a lower effective tax rate (24% in 1Q21 vs 29% in 1Q20).
  • Operating profit (+11% YoY to P3.3bn), however, is also trending mildly above forecasts on a sharp YoY improvement in margins. We believe that this was likely driven by the strong PHP, which typically benefits S&R. SSSG for the brand was also strong at +8.8%.
  • For the core Puregold brand, SSS declined 15.3% in 1Q21 mainly due to the high base last year. This will likely normalize in the coming quarters.
  • We expect to get more granular detail during the briefing on Thursday, 13 May.

CONSUMER
JFC 1Q21 operating income +36% QoQ to P1.5bn (in line); to issue PHP prefs
  • In a press release, JFC reported 1Q21 net profit of P153mn. Excluding perp coupons, we estimate a net loss of P140mn, trending behind estimates
    • The underperformance was due to tax-related adjustments
  • Operating profit was P1.5bn, up 36% YoY, tracking in line with our estimates even if revenues (-12% YoY) are falling short
    • Operating loss for Smashburger was a third of 1Q20 (we estimate around P150mn in 1Q21)
  • SSS for the Philippine business declined 26% YoY, while the international business posted SSSG of +7.5%
    • Latter partly driven by the recovery in China, which was locked down for much of 1Q20
    • However, management noted that most businesses abroad are already at pre-pandemic levels
  • JFC also announced that it plans to issue PHP preferred shares and to buy back some of its USD perpetual bonds through a cash tender offer within 2021
    • The amount of the pref issuance is estimated to be P8bn, and the company intends to buy back c.USD250mn worth of perps (USD600mn total)

CONSUMER
Industry car sales fall 14% MoM in April; Toyota sales drop 6% MoM
  • Vehicle sales reported by CAMPI/TMA dropped 14% MoM to 17,843 units in April
    • Cited the reimposition of tighter quarantine measures, as well as tighter bank lending and the safeguard duty
    • Brings 4M21 industry car sales to 88,155 units (+36% YoY)
    • CAMPI/TMA is forecasting industry sales to grow by +20-25% (with provisional duty) this year
  • Toyota sold 8,642 units in April, down 6% MoM and brings 4M21 retail sales to 41,737 units (+62% YoY)
    • Our forecasts assume vehicle sales to rise by 17% YoY to 117k units this year
    • To reach our forecast, Toyota will need to sell c. 9,400 units per month
  • With the safeguard duty, Toyota expects unit sales to reach on 104k-108k units this year (+4% to +8%)
    • To reach Toyota’s target of 104k units, monthly sales should average 7,800 units
  • Assuming Toyota’s forecast, we estimate our forecast for Toyota and GTCAP could fall by 10-14% and 4-5%, respectively
  • GT Capital is trading at a 12% discount to its market-based NAV of P599/sh

CONSUMER
EMP 1Q21 +43% to P2.1bn
  • In a limited press release, Emperador said that 1Q21 net income jumped 43%YoY to P2.1bn
  • Revenues increased 13% to P12.1bn on the “continued robust performance of Emperador’s global business”
CONGLOS
DMCI 1Q21 headline profit rises 7-fold
  • Headline 1Q21 profit rose almost 7-fold to P4.25bn; Headline number included a one-time gain on lot sale of P179mn
    • Removing one-offs and FX adjustments, we estimate 1Q21 core profit stood at P4.02bn, accounting for 43% of Regis FY21E and 40% of street estimates
    • All segments outperformed, except for Maynilad
  • DMCI Homes’ profit improved to P1.58bn in 1Q21, on faster revenue bookings and better margins
    • Reservation sales was flat QoQ at P6bn (-14% YoY), broadly in line with forecast
  • Construction also recovered on higher project accomplishments and better margins
    • Order book stood at P55bn (-6% QoQ)
  • Nickel profits soared on the back of higher sales volumes and ASPs
  • DMCI Power also recovered on better demand
  • DMCI is trading at a 24% discount to market NAV of P7.10/sh; 7.6x FY21E PER
  • The company will hold a briefing on Friday, May 14 (1pm HKT); Registration details

PROPERTY
Megaworld to launch REIT in 2H21
  • Megaworld Chief Strategy Officer Kevin Tan said that the company would launch its REIT in 2H21
  • “…it’s going to be big”
  • As of end-2020, Megaworld’s 1.4mn sqm office portfolio was 90% occupied
    • BPO – 73%
    • Traditional – 18%
    • POGO – 9%
  • We estimate that a one percentage point reduction in our office cap rate (7% currently) would increase Megaworld’s NAV by 9%
  • Mr. Tan said the company has been seeing strong demand in Pampanga, Cavite, Bacolod, and Iloilo
    • Combination of residential and office demand
  • MEG is trading at 6.7X FY21E P/E, 0.5X FY21E P/B, and 61% discount to NAV
