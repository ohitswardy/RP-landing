REGIS REPORT

12 August 2021

An estimated twenty-nine percent (29%) of the ~14m population of Metro Manila has now been fully vaccinated and another 13% have received at least one dose. The government is aiming to take the 29% figure to 45% by next week. Nationally, 11% have been fully vaccinated while 13% have received at least one dose.

The need to speed up vaccinations has taken on an increased urgency as infections continue to climb. The number of new infections climbed to over 12,000 yesterday.

We have a quite a few results releases over the past 24 hours and here’s our round-up of the latest:

  • Jollibee (JFC PM) (-) – 1H21 net profit after perp coupons is estimated at P543mn. The profit underperformance was mainly due to non-operating items (taxes, interest expense), and operating profit (P2.9bn) appears broadly in line. Same-store sales trends in 2Q21 were mixed given the various lockdowns, with only China and North America already back to pre-pandemic levels. Philippines is still tracking 25-30% below pre-pandemic sales given the lockdowns in Mar-Apr. Meanwhile, margins were pressured by higher input cost despite price increases. We should get further detail at the briefing on Monday.

  • Puregold (PGOLD PM) (=) – 1H21 net profit (+17% YoY to P3.9bn) is mildly ahead of expectations due to CREATE-related adjustments, but operating profit (+8% YoY to P6.1bn) is in line. Sales are trending behind expectations given continued pressure for key customers of the core Puregold brand (lower-income consumers). Margins, however, received a boost from a rebound in supplier support.

  • LT Group (LTG PM) (-) – LTG incurred a loss of P2.8bn in 2Q, dragged by P16.9bn in provisions booked by PNB. Although PNB booked a P33.6bn gain (and headline profit of P11.5bn) from the revaluation of properties under PNB Holdings, LTG eliminated this on consolidation.   • Cebu Air (CEB PM) (-) - 1H21 net loss of P13.86bn was wider than anticipated because of the slower-than-expected pax volume recovery. The good news is the company continued to exert effort to lower costs amidst a challenging environment. Thus, net losses have shrunk QoQ. We hope to get more detail after the briefing today.
  • D&L Industries (DNL PM) (+) – 2Q profits dipped just 3%QoQ, taking out the retroactive tax impact of CREATE in 1Q. Overall volumes dipped by just mid-single digit, despite the return to a stricter lockdown in April. This was offset by generally better margins across food and oleochemical segments.
  • Emperador (EMP PM) (+) – EMP’s 2Q income jumped 44% QoQ to P3.0bn, buoyed by +30%QoQ growth in international revenues and a more than 15ppt increase in margins of the domestic business. We estimate that EMP’s domestic volumes fell by low double-digits, compared to GSMI’s 25%QoQ decline and Tanduay’s +7%QoQ growth.

The company held back on price adjustments given the fragile consumer environment, leading to a QoQ contraction in margins.

+++

Broadband service provider Converge (CNVRG PM) announced results this morning. The company added 173,000 subscribers (net) in 2Q, slightly more than PLDT (TEL PM) and ~47% of the combined numbers of the three top operators. Note the numbers are net of disconnections and does not take into account the migration of subscribers from copper to fiber. Both PLDT and Globe (GLO PM) each added close to 500k fiber broadband subscribers in 1H, though bulk were “migrations” from copper.

Market foreign ownership falls in July
  • We estimate total market foreign ownership fell to 21.1% (-25bps MoM) while PSEi foreign ownership was practically flat at 24.7% (-1bp MoM) in July
  • 21 out of 30 PSEi constituents saw declines in their foreign ownership MoM
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were
    • ALI – foreign ownership fell 79 basis points MoM to 32.8%. Net foreign outflow in July was US$20.6mn, the second highest in the market.
    • BLOOM – foreign ownership declined 73 basis points MoM to 23.0%.
    • RLC – foreign ownership rose 26 basis points MoM to 18.0%. This was the highest increase among PSEi constituents. Net foreign inflow was US$2.86mn, among the highest for the month.
