REGIS REPORT

31 August 2021

New Covid-19 infections are still hitting new highs with the new infection rates above 20,000 for the first time.  Hospitals nationally are packed, with occupancy already matching the previous peak in April.

+++

Meanwhile, the head of the gaming regulator had a few things to say over the past couple of days that were interesting.

The first was that close to half of all POGOs have left the Philippines and re-located to other, nearby countries. Andrea Domingo, chair of PAGCOR, is merely confirming what has been quite apparent already on the ground.  Office vacancies are in the double-digits

The second was that the moratorium on new casinos will be lifted. This was in apparent response to a statement from President Duterte that he wants to allow new casinos to raise money due to the pandemic.  Even if the new casinos will really only start to generate revenue for the government after they begin operations, which could be 3-4 years down the road.  Certainly not before Duterte’s term of office theoretically ends in June 2022.

Among the firms/parties who were previously on the verge of being allowed to build new casinos are: Leisure Resorts (LR PM) in partnership with Galaxy, Alliance Global (AGI PM) and companies controlled by the Villar family and also the Davao-based Dennis Uy.

MACRO
Metro Manila, 15 other areas under MECQ until September 7
  • Metro Manila will remain under modified enhanced community quarantine (MECQ) until September 7
    • Other areas under MECQ are: Apayao, Ilocos Norte, Bulacan, Bataan, Cavite, Lucena City, Rizal, Laguna, Aklan, Iloilo province, Iloilo City, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu City, Mandaue City, and Cagayan de Oro City
  • Metro Manila, Bataan and Laguna shall have added restrictions on dining, personal care services and religious activities
  • Curfew hours in Metro Manila under the prevailing MECQ is from 8 pm to 4 am

 

MACRO
New Covid-19 cases hit record high of 22,366
  • The Department of Health reported 22,366 new Covid-19 infections on Monday, bringing the country’s total case count to 1,972,202
  • This is the highest number of infections recorded after the 19,441 cases reported last August 28

 

PROPERTY
POGOs leave for other countries
  • PAGCOR said that nearly half of the country's POGOs left the Philippines to set up operations in Cambodia, Vietnam, and Laos
  • There are now only 41 approved POGOs, from 60 as of end-2019
  • Leechiu Property Consultants estimates that POGOs now occupy c. 1mn sqm of Metro Manila office space (from 1.4mn sqm as of end-2019)
  • We estimate that Metro Manila office vacancy will rise to 15% by end-2021 before recovering to 13% in 2022-2023
    • From 9% in 4Q20, 13% in 2Q21

 

GAMING
Gov’t lifts moratorium on new casinos
  • Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corp chair Andrea Domingo said the agency was ready to execute the President’s new directive to lift the moratorium on new casinos to help raise government revenues
    • This comes three years after Duterte imposed the moratorium on new casinos
  • Projects that were granted provisional gaming licenses before the moratorium include the LRWC-Galaxy and Alliance Global projects in Boracay, Clark Grand Leisure Corp of Dennis Uy and Villar family’s Prime Asset Ventures
  • Despite the lifting, Domingo said no new casinos would be allowed in Pagcor Entertainment City in Paranaque since there was no more space for new locators

 

POWER
ACEN to launch Green Bond
  • AC Energy Corporation’s board authorized its subsidiary ACEN Finance Limited to issue senior guaranteed undated notes to be listed on the Singapore Exchange
  • Size and terms of which shall be fixed at a later date
  • The board also authorized ACEN to guarantee the notes
  • The notes will be part of a US$1.5bn medium-term note program, which was granted an approval in principle by the SGX on August 26, 2021
  • Target launch date of the notes is on August 31, 2021

 

POWER
KKR-controlled Philippines Clean Energy Holdings Inc submits Tender offer report – offering P33 for 3.0%-5.7% of FGEN
  • Philippines Clean Energy Holdings has submitted a tender offer report detailing its offer
  • 897mn – 205mn shares (3.0-5.7% of total outstanding common shares) at P33.0/share
  • The offer price represents a 17% premium over last close as of Aug 26 and over the one month VWAP
  • The TO will start at 9am on September 1 and end at 12pm on September 29, 2021
  • According to the TO report, Philippines Clean Energy Holdings Ic is controlled by Valorous Asia Holdings
    • Both are managed and/or advised by KKR
  • Valorous  already holds 12.59% of FGEN
  • A successful tender offer will increase ownership of Valorous in FGEN to 15.59-18.29%, making it eligible to nominate and elect a director to the board
  • At P33/share, we estimate FGEN would trade at 8.3x PE and 4.2x EV/EBITDA for FY22E
    • Valorous’s initial stake in FGEN was acquired through a tender at P22.50/share in June 2020
      • At that time, the price was equivalent to around 6x PE and 3.7x EV/EBITDA for FY21E
    • DCF estimate is P37/share
