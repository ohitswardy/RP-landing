Stock idea for the day – Semirara (SCC PM). Yes, yes. Being mega-ESG conscious is de reigueur these days. But how does a potential 20% dividend yield sound?

Coal prices (Newcastle) were already rising from $50/MT for most of last year, to around $85/MT in Jan/Feb on the back of the global economic recovery/reflation theme. But the recent flooding in Australia caused spot prices to spike to $105/MT. Our JEF partners raised their coal price forecast to $85/MT average for 2021E, with risks skewed to the upside. For reference, our forecasts still assume an average of $50/MT for FY2021.

Klyne’s base case is for dividends of P1.25/sh for 2021E, for a 10% dividend yield. But her sensitivity shows that at $85/MT, SCC’s NPAT could more than double. If that happens, could SCC double dividends too? Well, in 2017, when Newcastle spot prices averaged $87/MT, SCC paid out a cash dividend of P2.50/sh.

Results! Results!

Century Food (CNPF PM) – in line – FY20 net profit (+24% YoY to P3.9bn) was in line with estimates. The branded business continued to drive much of the top line growth in 4Q20 (+15% YoY) even as overall consumption softened. Input cost pressure was evident, with gross margins contracting 150bps YoY during the quarter, but this was mitigated by tight cost controls. CNPF also announced the acquisition of Pacific Meats from the Po family, formally marking its entry into the refrigerated food segment.

Puregold (PGOLD PM) – ahead – The company finished the year strong, with FY20 net profit jumping 19% YoY to P8.1bn, surpassing Street estimates. While the disclosure was thin on detail, S&R appeared to be the key driver of top line growth in the 4th quarter with SSSG of c.13%. We hope to get more detail after the briefing on Monday, 29th March.

Senator Manny Pacquiao filed a bill proposing a P335bn ($6.9bn) stimulus package, which would include P100bn in direct cash payments to low-income families, and P100bn in wage subsidies for employers. Last month, a bill was filed in Congress for a Bayanihan 3 stimulus. Out of the P420bn proposed budget, P108bn was in direct payments to households, and P52bn was for wage subsidies. The Cabinet, though, seems resistant to a third stimulus package. Given the populist/popular nature of the cash handouts, expect more noise ahead of the 2022 elections.

A-REIT gave more detail on the P15bn worth of assets it plans to acquire from Ayala Land (ALI PM) via a share for asset swap. We estimate that the transaction could increase the stock’s dividend yield from 5.1% to 5.4%.
