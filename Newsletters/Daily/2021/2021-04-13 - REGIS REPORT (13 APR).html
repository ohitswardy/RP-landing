REGIS REPORT

13 April 2021

Property consultancy firm Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) just released its quarterly recap of the real estate market. While there is positive spin being applied to the data, there is no denying that the big numbers are deteriorating.

Chief among them is the vacancy rate for office space, which climbed 2ppt to 11% as of 1Q21. Around 130,000 sqm of space was let go (net) within the quarter. With the deterioration in vacancy rates, rents cannot be far behind. LPC estimates office rents dropped 12% with lease rates for POGOs (for those who are still around) falling as much as 30%. This is looking like a slow motion train wreck for owners of office property in specific areas (e.g. near Manila Bay) as they are likely to be severely hit even if their financials don’t show it yet.

Also according to LPC, capital values for residential condominiums are supposed to be down by as much as 10% (even for the more resilient high-end segment), but anecdotal evidence points to an even steeper decline.

Clearly, the pandemic has severely affected demand and the property market, and only favorable monetary policy is helping to keep the latter from getting worse.

PROPERTY
Metro Manila office vacancy rises to 11%
  • Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) estimates that Metro Manila office vacancy rose from 9% in 4Q20 to 11% in 1Q21
  • It estimates that 130,000sqm were vacated in 1Q21 (on a net basis)
    • BPO – net incremental take-up of 25,000sqm
    • POGO – net incremental vacancy of 118,000sqm
    • Others (traditional) – net incremental vacancy of 37,000sqm
  • LPC also said that office rental rates are mostly 12% lower than 2019 levels while rental rates to POGOs are down 30%
  • We forecast Metro Manila office vacancy to rise to 12% in 2021E (and stay at 12% in 2022E)
    • We also expect office rental rates for PEZA accredited office space to drop c. 10% this year, bringing rents c. 20% below 2019 levels
    • We expect rental rates in the Manila Bay area to drop c. 15% this year, bringing rents c. 35% below 2019 levels
  • Office leasing accounts for 74% of Filinvest Land’s NAV and 52% of Megaworld’s NAV
  • FLI is trading at 5.4X FY21E P/E and 63% discount to NAV
  • MEG is trading at 8.4X FY21E P/E and 51% discount to NAV
