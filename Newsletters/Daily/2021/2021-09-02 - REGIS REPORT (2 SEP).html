OpenSignal released its initial assessment of the mobile network of Dito, around six months after it first began taking on subscribers.

This is the snapshot of the results on download speeds for the three main metropolitan areas of Metro Manila, Cebu and Davao. As can be seen, the chart below shows Dito’s latest download speeds to be 17.5mbps in Manila, 10.7mbps in Davao and 11.3mbps in Cebu.

OpenSignal says “these initial results are impressive and bode well for the market’s long-term competitiveness — particularly if DITO can increase capacity to keep up with the influx of new users.”  Dito’s network is still relatively empty of subscribers as the company reached 2m subscribers only in early Aug. Both PLDT (TEL PM) and Globe (GLO PM) had >30x more subscribers.

It worth remembering that Dito committed to deliver minimum average mobile data speeds of 55mbps by the second anniversary of the granting of its licenses (which would have been July 2021) – and that was the basis for it securing the license and spectrum in the first place.

If you were wondering about what to make of all the REITs rushing to list, we have something for you.

Our property analyst Carl (carl.sy@regis.ph) released a new report providing a background on Philippine REITs and comparing the first five Philippine REITs across various metrics (such as dividend yields, land ownership, etc.)

The message here is that spinning off the REITs will be significantly value-accretive for the property company sponsors. Not only are the REITs going to enjoy tax incentives, the sponsors are likely to see NAV enhancement and also receive additional cash flows from land leases and management fees.

Carl reckons that, among the property company sponsors, Robinsons Land (RLC PM) offers the best risk-reward in the sector. Its mall operations are poised to rebound (as vaccinations increase) and it has the highest proportion of REIT-able assets among developers we cover. The stock is also trading at 60% discount to NAV, 0.7X FY22E P/B, and 9.1X FY22E P/E, all of which are well below historical averages

TELCOS
OpenSignal gives first assessment of DITO’s network
  • OpenSignal released its initial assessment of Dito’s network experience (link)
  • Among the key highlights/excerpts:
    • “In the last two 30-day periods — commencing June 6, 2021 and July 6, 2021 — there was no significant difference between the speeds seen by our DITO users and the overall speeds in Central Metro Cebu, but in Davao City the overall speeds were significantly faster than those seen by our DITO users.”
    • “With Upload Speed Experience we saw a different story between the three areas — in Central Metro Cebu, DITO users observed statistically higher upload speeds than the overall average in all but the data collection period starting June 6, 2021, and in that period DITO’s score statistically tied with the overall score.”
    • “As with speed, the Video Experience of DITO’s users will likely change further over time as DITO’s network matures, becomes available in many more locations, and as DITO grows its customer base and the network becomes busier.”
    • “The analysis in this insight indicates that so far DITO’s entry into the Filipino mobile market is evolutionary, not revolutionary, for mobile experience because DITO’s scores were mostly similar to — or below — the overall scores of all operators, with the exception of Upload Speed Experience in both Central Metro Cebu and Metro Manila.”
    • “However, given that DITO’s competitors – Globe and Smart – have had far longer to roll out their own 4G networks, these initial results are impressive and bode well for the market’s long-term competitiveness — particularly if DITO can increase capacity to keep up with the influx of new users”

Here are the results in charts. Note that Dito is the red bar. The average for the operators is blue.

  • A couple of things to remember:
    • Dito reached 2m subscribers around Aug 3. So for most of the review period, they would have had less than 2m subscribers. The incumbents had 30-40x more subscribers.
    • According to the terms of the bidding under which the Duterte administration granted its licenses and spectrum, Dito is required to provide minimum average data speed of 55mbps by the second anniversary (July 2021). You can see for yourself what the results are in the charts above. Theoretically, Dito’s spectrum can be revoked as it is clearly well below the commitment which was the basis for the awarding. But, hey, who’s checking?

Dito CME moves forward with takeover of Dito Telecommunity
  • Dito CME disclosed that the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) had approved on August 27 its application for a capital increase that would allow it to complete a previously announced asset swap deal with Udenna Corp
  • Under the transaction, Dito CME will issue 11.2bn new shares to Udenna for 100% of Udenna Communications Media and Entertainment Holdings Corp
    • This will give Dito CME an indirect majority stake in Dito Telecommunity
  • Apart from the Udenna, Dito CME said it also issued 35mn shares to an “unrelated party”
  • Following the said transactions, Dito CME said its public ownership level stood at 20.02% as of August 27
  • Dito CME will end up owning 53% of the operating company Dito Telecommunity
OTHERS
FGEN could be removed from the PSE index before the quarterly review
  • First Gen’s current public float is 18.74%
  • Valorous Asia, which currently owns 12.59% of FGEN, made a tender offer and seeks to increase its stake by another 3.0% to 5.7% of shares outstanding by end-September
  • This could lower the public float below 15%, the minimum requirement to be a PSE index constituent
  • If this were to occur, FGEN would be deleted from the PSE index in the mid-November index review
    • The mid-May and mid-November index reviews would only trigger revisions when public float changes by at least 5ppt or when public float goes below the minimum threshold
  • The PSE would also have the discretion to remove FGEN from the PSE Index earlier than that
    • Deletion would be implemented within five trading days of an announcement
  • At this point, we estimate that DMCI Holdings would be the candidate most likely to replace FGEN in the PSE index
