REGIS REPORT

16 December 2021

The Senate approved, on third reading, the amendments to the Commonwealth-era Public Service Act. These amendments would remove the classification of telecoms, airlines and domestic shipping from the list of “public utilities”, thus enabling them to be 100% foreign owned.

The proposed legislation now moves to bicameral reconciliation where the presence/absence of a number of provisions could prove crucial.

A previous version of the Senate bill contained provisions which would still restrict foreign state-owned entities to no more than 40% ownership of critical infrastructure. Another provision requiring reciprocity on foreign ownership limits between the Philippines and the counterpart states was also in the bill. These provisions, if present in the final law, could limit the avenues for the full takeover by a firm like China Telecom of something like Dito Telecommunity.

+++

The 2022 national budget amounting to P5.02trn has been approved by Congress and is now up for signature by the President. Given the holiday season and the need to review for items that may/not be vetoed, it is likely that the final budget will only be signed into law early next year. The proposed budget shows YoY increases in the allocation for the Dept of Public Works but reductions in the budgets for Education and Transportation.

+++

Overseas remittances for the month of Oct amounted to US$2.81bn, which is up 2.4% YoY. For YT-Oct, cumulative remittances are up 5.3%.

+++

ICTSI (ICT PM) is allocating US$230m to expand its port operations in Mexico by over 20%. The port in Manzanilla is one of the largest in ICTSI’s portfolio, accounting for 11% of group revenues and 14% of NAV by our estimates.

MACRO
Remittances hit 3-month high in October
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks rose 2.4% YoY to US$2.812bn in October
    • Highest amount since July
    • In PHP terms, October remittances rose 7.1% YoY
    • 3-month moving average growth rate steady at 4.2%
  • Land-based remittances up 4% MoM but this was offset by a 3% contraction in sea-based remittances
  • YT-October remittances reached US$25.9bn, up 5.3% YoY
    • BSP is targeting remittances to grow by +6% YoY in 2021 and +4% in 2022

MACRO
Senate approves amendments to Public Service Act
  • The Senate approved on third reading the bill amending the Commonwealth era Public Service Act
  • These amendments are supposed to remove foreign ownership limits on a number of industries, most significantly in Telecoms, Airlines and Domestic Shipping
  • It’s unclear how the latest Senate version looks but a previous version of the bill (or at least one of them) has a number of crucial limitations
    • First is the foreign ownership limit stays at 40% for foreign state-owned entities
    • Second, it requires reciprocity for the country of the foreign entity/entities
    • We reckon these clauses, if present in the final law, could still prevent an entity like China Telecom from taking over Dito completely.
  • The industries that are still considered public utilities are:
    • Electricity distribution, electricity transmission, water pipeline distribution and sewerage pipeline systems, airports, seaports, and public utility vehicles
  • Under this version, foreign ownership limits will still apply to Megawide (and its airport) and its hope that this bill will change that are likely to be dashed
  • Without foreign ownership limits, the telcos could be 100% foreign-owned if they spin off their property assets. From a technical point of view, it would have implications on MSCI weightings et al.
  • The incumbent telcos, PLDT (TEL PM) and Globe (GLO PM) have gone around foreign ownership limits by having local entities carry disproportionate voting rights
MACRO
Congress ratifies bicameral report on 2022 budget
  • Congress ratified the Bicameral Conference Committee report for next year’s P5.024tn budget
    • The General Appropriations Bill will be sent to President Duterte for his final approval, but this would depend on the completion of printing
    • President Duterte must sign the measure before Dec 31 to ensure the government does not operate on a reenacted budget
  • The Department of Education’s budget went up by P2.5bn to P592.7bn and state universities and colleges got an additional P32.47bn to bring its total budget to P104.18bn
    • Cited higher allocation to accommodate face-to-face classes
  • Bicam increased the budget for DPWH by P88bn to P786bn but slashed the Transport Department’s budget by P42bn to P75bn
  • Department of Health’s budget went up by P1.2bn to P184bn
    • Around P45-50bn in programmed and unprogrammed funds were allocated for COVID vaccines and boosters

TRANSPORT
ICTSI unit in Mexico allotting US$230mn for terminal expansion
  • ICTSI’s port in Manzanillo, Mexico is investing US$230mn to increase the port’s capacity by 300k TEUs to 1.7mn TEUs annually
    • Construction of berths, yards, and acquisition of quay cranes/other port equipment will begin in 2H2022
    • Expansion will complement the Mexican government’s expansion of land access to the terminal
  • ICT’s port in Mexico is operating at over 80% utilization and is the fastest growing port in the company’s portfolio
    • We estimate the port accounts for 10%/11% of group volumes/revenues and 14% of NAV

PNOC-EC withholds consent on sale of Shell’s stake in Malampaya
  • During a Senate committee hearing on energy, the Philippine National Oil Company-Exploration Corporation head Rozzano Briguez said it is withholding its consent on the sale of Shell’s stake in the Malampaya gas project
  • PNOC-EC has communicated to SPEX (Shell Philippines Exploration B.V.) as of December 13
  • According to Shell, the deal to sell its 45% stake in Malampaya will not be able to proceed for now
  • PNOC-EC holds a 10% stake in the Malampaya project
