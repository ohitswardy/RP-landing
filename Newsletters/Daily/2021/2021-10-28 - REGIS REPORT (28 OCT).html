REGIS REPORT

28 October 2021

Wilcon (WLCON PM) reported another quarter of firm results, albeit marred slightly by the tighter lockdowns imposed in August which affected store operating hours in Metro Manila (NCR).

For 3Q alone, sales revenue was down 2% but the continued climb in margins helped drive net profits up 17%. Margins for the quarter are now at 38%, up another 110bp.  It’s hard to imagine how much further they can rise. More on the results below.

WLCON has been one of the most resilient names this year. With the additional lift from its inclusion in the PSEi, the stock is up a 87% YTD and is now on 44x PER and 19x EBITDA for FY22. For holders, it might be time to take some money off the table.

+++

The government is expanding the vaccination of minors nationwide by next week, after allowing vaccination of the general adult population and minors in specific areas. This move could increase the pace of vaccinations, which has not improved much from three months ago (down, in fact).

Meanwhile, the Dept of Tourism is proposing to subsidize Covid-19 tests in order to jump-start tourism and travel, which have been in a virtual coma since the breakout of the pandemic.

++

Century Pacific Foods (CNPF PM) introduced a new item to its fast-growing dairy product line. “Choco Hero” is a powdered chocolate malt drink, so whose market it’s aiming for is quite obvious. Those who have not been watching CNPF closely over the past couple of years would be surprised to find out that dairy now accounts for a quarter of revenue. We reckon this segment has plenty of room to grow not just with rising general consumption but also from eating someone else’s proverbial lunch.

RETAIL
Wilcon Depot – strong margins drive 3Q21 performance; cost/supply issues loom
  • WLCON reported 9M21 net profit of P1.87bn, up 111% YoY, broadly within estimates (Regis FY21E: P2.6bn). Net sales (+27% YoY to P20bn) and gross margin (37.1%) are tracking ahead of expectations, while EBIT (+86% YoY to P2.8bn) is in line. For 3Q21 alone, net profit grew +17% YoY to P623mn, lifted by a lower tax rate.
  • 3Q21 net sales were down slightly (-2% YoY) on the back of -9% SSSG. NCR stores (25% of total network) posted double digit SSS decline due to the ECQ/MECQ imposed in Metro Manila in August.
  • Margins, however, remained healthy with GPM +110bps QoQ to 38% in 3Q. There was minimal change in the contribution of in-house merchandise (49% of total) and management attributed the margin expansion to 1) price increases to pass on additional freight-related cost, and 2) proportionately lower sales from volume buyers (e.g. contractors).
  • WLCON saw a sharp increase in receivable days (23 days as of 9M21 vs 6-7 days historically) due to a jump in advances to suppliers. Management has decided to ramp orders to proactively manage any potential issues from suppliers in China, coupled with the anticipation of increased demand as the economy reopens.
  • Management observed a marked improvement in store visitation in the last few weekends as Metro Manila Alert levels have eased. While this bodes well for the company’s top line recovery, they admitted that growth could come at the expense of margins as volume buyers return. There is also looming cost pressure given potential supply issues from China, though the company is confident that they can pass this on through price increases.
  • WLCON is trading at 2022E multiples of 44x PE and 19x EV/EBITDA, vs regional peers of 31x PE and 18x EV/EBITDA
