REGIS REPORT

22 December 2021

President Duterte declared a “state of calamity” in the 6 regions worst hit by Typhoon Rai/Odette. The main features include the government’s ability to impose price caps on basic goods, and the expedited release of government funds. Relief efforts continue to be hampered by damage to infrastructure, be it transport (airports), power, water, or telecommunication. Bear in mind that most of the hardest hit areas are on different islands, further complicating the delivery of relief goods and services. Some areas will likely not see electricity fully restored even by year-end. Collectively, the 6 regions account for roughly 22% of Philippine GDP.

+ + +

In Covid-19 news, boosters will now be available to fully vaccinated individuals after 3 months from the last jab, down from 6 months previously. The relaxation is partly driven by fears over the Omicron variant. But we believe part of the reason is the growing stockpile of vaccine doses that could go off soon. By our latest estimates, there are close to 90mn doses in inventory across the country, with only about 1mn jabs done per day. The reduction in the gap means up to 21.4mn Filipinos are now eligible for the booster (ie those fully vaccinated as of end-Sept).

+ + +

Macau junket operator Suncity said its major shareholder Alvin Chau could lose control over the listco, over an unpaid loan with some of his shares as collateral. It is unclear how this development will directly affect locally-listed Suntrust (SUN PM), which is building a casino in partnership with Alliance Global (AGI PM). The building is being constructed by Megawide (MWIDE PM), which claims the P20bn project is fully funded.

More broadly, the crackdown in China bodes ill for the future of junket operations in the Philippines. Pre-pandemic, we estimate that as much as 40% of GGR of the casino operators came from VIPs brought in by the junkets. Even assuming the world reopens post-Covid, gaming companies like AGI and Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) will likely have to do with much lower patronage.

GAMING
Alvin Chau to lose control of Suncity listco
  • Suncity Group said in a filing that it had been informed that Alvin Chau’s privately-controlled company had been served with a letter claiming his 4.99mn shares of Suncity Group Holdings over an unpaid loan
    • Mr. Chau pledged the Suncity shares as guarantee
  • The letter stated that a group of lenders together with Wooco Secretarial Services will have the power to sell, transfer or dispose all or any part of the stake previously owned by Mr. Chau
  • Suncity Group said that enforcement by Wooco may lead to a change in control of the company
  • As of this announcement, no information is available to Philippine subsidiary Suntrust Home Developers (SUN PM) as to how Wooco will enforce the securities
  • Suncity is the parent company of Fortune Noble which holds 51% of the outstanding capital of SUN PM
