REGIS REPORT

04 June 2021

President Duterte signed an Executive Order (EO) to lay the ground work for passing on more responsibilities and more funds to local governments. This move is in compliance with a previous Supreme Court ruling that ordered the national government to hand over more money to local governments beginning 2022.

The EO sets a gradual transfer of some basic services and function from the national government to local governments (LGU) beginning next year until 2024. This will come with a chunky increase in LGU budgets through a hike in transfers (called internal revenue allotments (IRA)) from the national government.

These changes are part of the three-decade old Local Government Code, whose basic rationale is for local governments to be more self-sustaining and autonomous, which will hopefully improve the delivery of basic services.

We suspect, however, that there will be short-term hiccups. We will not be surprised to see LGUs struggle to handle the “windfall” of more funds and the increased responsibilities that come with it. Longer-term projects are probably going to suffer, at least initially.

Enrique Razon has instituted changes in the management / board following his takeover of Manila Water (MWC PM). Razon will assume the CEO position and a number of his close aides are joining the team. Given the demands of the position, we imagine that the CEO post will only stay with Razon for a short transition period before someone else takes over on more permanent basis.

+++

SM Investments (SM PM) has completed its purchase of 22% of shipping and logistics firm 2GO (2GO PM) from Dennis Uy for P4.7bn.

A few years back, SM made its initial investment into 2GO with the stated goal of using it as its platform for its push into logistics, a push which has yet to see very visible progress. This new purchase, however, represents a mere ½ of 1% of SM’s value.

Dennis Uy’s sale, on the other hand, will help shore up his group’s finances, which were already appeared to be stretched even before the announced deal to spend US$1bn to buy the Malampaya operation.

MACRO
Duterte orders nat'l gov't, LGUs: Prepare for full devolution of basic services
  • President Rodrigo Duterte issued an executive order (no. 138) telling national government agencies to fully transfer the task of delivering basic services to local governments by the year 2024
  • EO will provide local governments with more funds (at least 50% more) starting 2022
    • To prepare for the “phased” devolution, all national government agencies and local governments will prepare “Devolution Transition Plans” within 120 days from the effectivity of the EO
    • A Growth Equity Fund will be included in the 2022 Budget and proposed by the Committee on Devolution to Congress
UTILITIES
Razon undertakes board, management changes at Manila Water
  • Enrique Razon is now the Chairman, President and CEO of Manila Water (MWC PM)
    • Fernando Zobel stepped down as Chairman but will remain as a board director
    • Jaime Augusto Zobel de Ayala has fully exited his previous vice chairman and board director positions
    • Rene Almendras also stepped down from his role of President but will remain as a non-executive director
  • Razon brought his own team including nephew, Global Corporate Head of ICTSI Group Christian Gonzalez and ICTSI CFO Rafael Consing
    • Details on respective positions were not disclosed
  • Mr. Razon now has a 34% economic stake and 51% voting stake in Manila Water
    • Ayala Corp.’s economic stake is now down to 31% (from 52% previously) while its voting stake is now at 32% (from 81% previously)

MEDIA
ABS-CBN gets reprieve from lenders
  • ABS-CBN Corp said it signed a standstill agreement with lenders, not to declare any event of default or to exercise any rights or remedies under existing loan agreements
  • This is contingent on the company’s compliance with the lenders’ condition of the creation of a mortgage and security interest over certain assets of the company
  • ABS-CBN had total debt of over P21bn as of 2020, including debt of subsidiaries SkyCable and ABS-CBN International
