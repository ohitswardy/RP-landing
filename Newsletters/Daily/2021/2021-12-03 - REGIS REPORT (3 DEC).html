REGIS REPORT

03 December 2021

The government was able to administer 7.6m Covid-19 vaccine doses during its special 3-day campaign (Nov 29 - Dec 1), based on preliminary figures. This is better than the “usual” pace but is also below the reduced target of 3m doses/day.  There’s practically no chance the Philippines will hit the government target of 54m individuals (almost 50% of total population) fully vaccinated by year-end as less than 50m individuals received at least one dose by end-Nov (and one needs to wait at least a month for the second dose). More likely, we will end the year somewhere above but closer to 40%.

Meanwhile, the government is now allowing all adults who’ve been fully vaccinated against Covid-19 to be given booster shots, effective today. These will be limited to those who were fully vaccinated at least six months prior (three months for the Janssen vaccine) and, on that basis, we expect around 6m individuals will be eligible within this month.

+++

In response to the global concern over the Omnicron variant, the government tightened restrictions for inbound international passengers again, effective immediately. Obviously, this would affect the hoped-for rebound in international tourism. Nonetheless, it looks like Cebu Air (CEB PM) passenger volumes continue to rise with demand from domestic travelers picking up sharply with the low case counts and eased regional restrictions.

    • 54mn Filipinos by end-2021
    • 77mn by end-1Q22
    • 90mn by end-2Q22

  • As of November 25, 69% of the Metro Manila population (9.3mn people) has been fully vaccinated
  • The Philippines has received a total of 145mn COVID vaccine doses and has administered 89mn doses (as of December 1)
  • Beginning December 3, all fully-vaccinated Filipinos are eligible to receive booster shots
    • If they received their second dose at least 6 months ago
MACRO
Gov’t implements new testing, quarantine rules for inbound international travelers today
  • The Inter-Agency Task Force approved new testing and quarantine protocols for international travelers coming from countries outside the red list
    • Fully vaccinated travelers will be required to present a negative RT-PCR test conducted 72 hours prior to leaving the country of origin
    • They will also have to undergo facility-based quarantine and take another test on the fifth day of arrival and quarantine at home until the 14th day
  • New rules will take effect beginning Dec 3
  • Previously, vaccinated travelers from non-red list countries were only encouraged to conduct self-monitoring after receiving a negative swab test result
AIRLINES
CEB seat capacity rebounds strongly; More domestic destinations remove COVID tests
  • Capacity surges for the week of November 29. The latest airline capacity survey from Jefferies shows CEB’s seat capacity jumped to 56k for the week of Nov 29, which is 46% higher than its 10-week rolling average and about 48% of pre-Covid capacity. This spike is likely partly due to the November 30 holiday, as well as the reopening of domestic tourism.
  • 32 domestic destinations remove COVID tests as pre-travel requirement. More importantly, there are now 32 domestic destinations (from 12 in mid-November) that do not require swab tests. Note that CEB used to fly to 37 domestic destinations pre-Covid. 
  • NAIA slot constrained again? On a side note, CEB is already flying its 459-seater A330 NEO aircraft on short-haul routes (e.g. Davao and General Santos), which suggests NAIA could be slot constrained again. However, it is also possible that CEB is using this particular aircraft because it was just delivered and is available. Note that NEOs are more fuel efficient than older CEOs. We believe CEB is well-positioned to rebound faster than its competitors in a slot-constrained environment.
