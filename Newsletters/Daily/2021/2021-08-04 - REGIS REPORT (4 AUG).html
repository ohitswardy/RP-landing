REGIS REPORT

04 August 2021

The national government will be allocating another 4m Covid-19 vaccine doses for Metro Manila to be administered over the next few weeks while the area is under a ‘hard’ lockdown (“ECQ”). The Palace spokesman says the allocation would be enough to take the vaccination levels in Metro Manila to 45% by Aug 20 if these are fully utilized.

With another shipment of 3m Moderna vaccine doses arriving yesterday (part of 20m expected this month), we don’t expect vaccine availability will be a constraint in the near-term. It will now be up to the logistics and administration of the vaccines in this race against the clock.

Meanwhile, the government has set aside P13bn (US$260m) as cash handouts for residents, equivalent to roughly PhP1,000 (US$20) per individual for the two-week period. This should help cushion the impact of the lockdown on spending.

Here’s today’s round-up of 2Q results:

  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) (-) – 2Q21 net profit jumped to P3.3bn from P197mn in 2Q20. However, 1H21 profit of P6.0bn (+34%) is tracking behind both REGIS and consensus forecasts. As it prepares to shut for another two weeks, management expects the experience in 3Q to be similar to 2Q (closed for a few weeks, local visitation to bounce back quickly in succeeding months). Management has also been hard at work developing the online platform and payment gateway for a meaningful roll-out of its online casino next year.

BANKS
MBT jacks up NPL cover to 179%, drags 2Q21 profit -50% QoQ

Metrobank (MBT PM) reported NPAT of P11.7bn, up 28% YoY. While headline profit is c80% of our full-year forecasts, PPOP is slightly behind on soft NII. For 2Q21 alone, NPAT of P3.9bn was up 30% YoY, but down 50% QoQ. MBT kicked up provision expense in 2Q to P4.5bn, from just P2.5bn in 1Q21. This was despite NPL ratio improving to 2.3% from 2.4% in 1Q21. As a result, NPL cover jumped to 179% from 166% in 1Q. It is unclear why management chosen to raise provision expense at this time – we suspect it would be partly write-offs, and partly sand-bagging in case asset quality deteriorates further in 2H21. The bank will hold a briefing this afternoon at 4pm.

Some highlights from the results (table and charts, below):

  • Loans were down 13% YoY (vs -19% in 1Q), and flat QoQ. Deposits were up 6.8% YoY, and up 4.5% QoQ, as the bank built up its trading book.
  • NII was down 16% YoY in 1H21, partly on lower loan balances. NIMs were at 3.18%, flattening out (-12bps QoQ); they were still -94bps YoY but 2Q20 was a high base. Loan yields and deposit costs were flat QoQ.
  • Fee incomes were up 16% YoY in 1H21, and are broadly back to pre-Covid levels. Trust fees were up 21% YoY, as AUMs jumped 17%. Trading/fx gains dropped 69% YoY from exceptionally high levels in 2020.
  • Opex was flat (-1% YoY) in 1H21 likely on lower taxes/licenses on lower revenues (esp trading)
  • Provision expense was down 69% in 1H21, due to the large pre-emptive provisioning done in 1H20. Surprisingly, though, 2Q21 provisions jumped 80% QoQ to P4.5bn. This was despite NPL ratio falling to 2.3% from 2.4% in 1Q21. These combined to bring NPL cover up to 179% in 2Q21, up from 166% in 1Q.

  • Solar accounts for 60% of the company’s pipeline projects in the development stage
