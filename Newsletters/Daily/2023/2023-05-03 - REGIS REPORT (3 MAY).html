03 May 2023

We received a little bit more color from Aboitiz Power’s (AP PM) earnings call yesterday. The 6%QoQ decline in earnings was largely a result of lower power prices in the period (spot -8%QoQ, BCQ -3%QoQ). GNPower Dinginin’s beneficial contribution in particular fell 16%QoQ when it contracted 300MW to Meralco P5.95/kWh early in the quarter. We nevertheless expect profits to improve in succeeding quarters as prices are expected to climb in 2Q/3Q.  More below from Aaron (aaron.salvador@regis.ph) here:

Meanwhile, Semirara (SCC PM) announced in a press release that 1Q23 profit of P9.03bn, down 40% YoY but up 122% QoQ. Stripping out FX adjustments, we estimate core profit doubled QoQ to P9.46bn (-36% YoY), which is 26% of Regis FY23E, 29% of Bloomberg consensus estimates, and 31% of consensus estimates. The results appear to be mildly better than our expectations due to (a) lower coal mining costs and (b) higher power ASPs, which more than compensated for lower-than-expected coal mining ASPs and sales volumes. 

The company will hold a briefing tomorrow (May 4) at 4:30pm (link).

ACEN Corp (ACEN PM) announced that it signed deals to sell some of the output of from its two solar power projects in Australia. The projects, with a combined capacity of 1,120MW, are only about to be completed. The deals would supposedly protect it from lower prices long-term. ACEN did not disclose details of pricing and quantity, so we suspect the terms are too complex to be discussed in a mere press release. 

+++

The reported revenue of the government for March dropped 12% to P221bn but the figure/s are not alarming as the headline might suggest. Changes in VAT collection schedules caused the drop in March, which we expect to normalize in succeeding periods. For the entire 1Q, revenue for the government was up +4% -- not a great figure but not as bad as the reported March figure might tempt one to think.

The budget deficit for 1Q was P271bn, down 14%.

MACRO
Budget deficit widens in March
  • The government recorded a budget deficit of P210bn in March, up 12% YoY and 98% MoM
    • Revenues dropped at a faster YoY rate than expenditures
    • Tax revenues fell 8.2% YoY due to the shift to quarterly filing of VAT returns from monthly filing previously
    • Other revenues fell 29% YoY due to high base effects of dividend remittances
    • Expenditures fell 2.6% YoY due to the lower national tax allocation shares of LGUs and timing of significant releases for the fuel subsidy program
  • In 1Q23, the budget deficit narrowed by 14.5% YoY to P270.9bn
    • Revenues should catch up in succeeding periods to reach the government’s end-2023 deficit target of P1.5tn (6.1% of GDP)

POWER
ACEN secures contracts for Australia solar farms
  • ACEN Corp (ACEN PM) disclosed that its Australian units secured long-term energy service agreements for its two solar projects at the New South Wales government’s first renewable energy and storage auction
  • The energy service agreements are for ACEN Australia’s 720MW New England solar project and the 400MW Stubbo solar project
  • The LTESAs offer the rights to access a minimum price for generation projects over a 20-year time frame, which protects investors like us from the risk of unexpectedly low wholesale electricity prices”
  • ACEN Australia has around 1GW capacity under construction and around 8GW capacity in the development pipeline
  • In March, management said it was in advanced stages of contracting output and was considering 8-10 year contracts at prices that were then lower than spot
