REGIS REPORT

19 May 2023

Despite the Fed’s rate hike earlier this month, the Bangko Sentral (BSP) opted to take a “prudent pause” on its rate hike trajectory, keeping the policy rate at 6.25%. It was an unsurprising decision, given the pronouncements made in the days leading up to the Monetary Board meeting yesterday. With the “pause”, the gap between the BSP policy rate and the lower bound of the target US Fed rate stays at 1.25%, lower than it’s been for a long time and well below the historical gap (~300bp).

At the same time, the BSP also lowered its inflation forecast for this year and next. Inflation forecasts by the BSP are often its way of signaling the direction of monetary policy. Thus, unless the Fed raises rates much more (something the markets don’t expect), the BSP’s rate actions for the next twelve months are likely to be biased in favor of reductions.

+++

The joint venture between Globe (GLO PM), ST Telemedia GDC and Ayala Corp (AC PM) is building a new data center in Quezon City. The facility has the potential to be a big as 124MW but the initial capacity by next year (2024) will be 28MW. The JV is 50% owned by Globe and 10% owned by AC. Competitor PLDT (TEL PM) is also in the process of building a data center with capacity of over 100MW.

Meanwhile, Globe disclosed it completed the sale of another 431 towers to Frontier Towers. Globe will receive P5.5bn in proceeds, taking total proceeds from completed tower sales so far to approximately P48bn.

+++

Statements made by a First Pacific (142 HK) representative suggests that the tender offer price for Metro Pacific (MPI PM) are subject to change. The consortium making the offer, which includes First Pacific, put up a P4.63/share price. While we believe there will be adjustments to the final tender offer price, we expect these adjustments to be ‘tweaks’.

MACRO
BSP pauses monetary tightening
  • The Monetary Board kept the interest rate on the overnight reverse repurchase facility at 6.25%
    • This is in line with consensus expectations
    • The gap between the BSP’s policy rate and the Fed funds rate has narrowed to 1.25% from 1.5% previously
  • Cited (a) lower inflation expectations and (b) potential moderation in demand indicators in recent months
    • The BSP lowered its average inflation targets for 2023/2024 to 5.5%/2.8% from 6.0%/2.9% previously
  • BSP Governor Medalla said that they may continue to pause in the next 2-3 policy meetings
    • However, they will be mindful of the potential impact of El Nino and food prices and utility rates, as well as the soft global economy

CONGLOS
First Pacific (142 HK) suggests MPI tender offer price not final
  • First Pacific (142 HK) said that the offer price as based on a “preliminary” report by an accredited third-party valuation provider and was not yet final
    • According to Stanley Yan, First Pacific executive vice president for group corporate development
    • “The final valuation report has not been released yet, it’s a work in progress by the third party”
    • Final report was still subject to the approval of the Philippine Stock Exchange
  • The current tender offer price of P4.63/sh did not take into account MPI’s 1Q23 financials
    • The valuation provider will have to update their analysis to reflect the 1Q performance
    • Final report will be released once the tender offer is formally launched after MPI’s annual stockholder’s meeting on June 6
CONGLOS
Ayala Corp putting up USD1bn data center, biggest in Philippines
  • STT GDC Philippines, a joint venture between Globe, ST Telemedia Global Data Centres and Ayala Corporation, will spend about USD1bn to put up STT Fairview, a 124-megawatt data center in Quezon City, to support the country in its pursuit to become a hyperscale hub.
  • The STT Fairview data center campus has over 83,000 square meters of gross floor area and 4 buildings, and will cater to both hyperscalers and enterprises.
  •  STT Fairview would start running by 2025 with an initial capacity of 28 MW that can be scaled up depending on incoming demand.
BANKS
BPI declares P1.68/sh cash div
  • BPI declared a cash div of P1.68/sh for 1H23. Assuming they pay the same for 2H23 (scheduled for November declaration / December payout), the annual dividend would be P3.36/sh. That would be equivalent to a payout about 42% of NPAT for 2022, and a dividend yield of 3.2% on today’s close of P105/sh.
  • Recall that in 2022, BPI revised its dividend policy from a fixed PHP amount, to a range of 35-50% of prior year’s profit, putting this year’s payout smack in the middle of that range. In 2022, the bank paid out just over 40% of 2021’s profits in cash dividends.

  • Completion brings the total number of towers sold to ~3,700
