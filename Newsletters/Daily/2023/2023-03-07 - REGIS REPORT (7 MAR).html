REGIS REPORT

07 March 2023

The results released yesterday still skew to the positive, with both ICTSI (ICT PM) and Aboitiz Power (AP PM) handily beating expectations. For Universal Robina (URC PM), the bottom line may have just been within expectations, but the top line was well ahead – suggesting demand remains robust, auguring well for FY23.

  • ICTSI (ICT PM) (+) – Recurring profit to common jumped 57% YoY to US$601mn in FY22, well above Regis and consensus expectations, boosted by (a) a higher than expected yield per TEU and (b) a lower tax rate.  While there was a mild deceleration of profit growth to 25% YoY in 4Q22, as organic volume growth moderated to +4% YoY from +6% YoY in 9M22 amidst an increase in operating and non-operating costs, management however remained constructive on its 2023 outlook, with tariff hikes continuing to take hold.

  • Universal Robina (URC PM) (=) – EBIT jumped 51% YoY in 4Q22, bringing the FY22 figure to P15.2bn (+20% YoY), broadly in line with expectations. The 4Q22 performance was mostly driven by the non-BCF segment (4Q EBIT up 2x YoY) as domestic sugar prices surged. But more importantly, EBIT margin for the Philippine BCF segment was unchanged QoQ, which implies (1) cost inflation has stabilized, and (2) price increases have been sufficient to cover cost inflation. The strong top line momentum across the BCF segment (+22% YoY) is expected to continue into 2023, though the growth could temper as the YoY base normalizes. EBIT margin recovery will be the main driver of earnings in 2023 and we believe there could be upside risk to earnings estimates on the back of the results.

  • Aboitiz Power (AP PM) (+) - Aboitiz Power’s printed another stellar quarter with a core profit of P8.2bn in 4Q22, taking full year core profit to P26.5bn (+27%). Although lower QoQ, the latest quarter run-rate suggests that consensus estimates for FY23 (P25bn) would have to rise by at least 20%. On the operational side, energy sales from the generation business grew another 3%QoQ, while EBITDA from distribution jumped to P3.2bn (78%QoQ, 129%YoY) on the recovery of high generation charges in prior periods.

Megaworld (MEG PM) held the briefing for its FY22 results (previously reported) yesterday and the key takeaway was management’s optimism about 2023 operations. The company is guiding P130bn in reservation sales and P20bn in rental revenue, both of which are higher than REGIS forecasts. SM Investments (SM PM) is offering to buy the 15.4% public float of 2Go (2GO PM) for P14.64/share, 25% higher than the latest close. This purchase will cost the group around P5.6bn (US$100m).

+++

The CPI inflation print for Feb came in at 8.6%, a tad lower than Jan’s 8.7% and also below Street expectations of 8.9%. The lower inflation number should relieve pressure for the Bangko Sentral (BSP) to hike policy rates. BSP Governor Felipe Medalla had hinted that rates may be raised by as much as 50bp if headline inflation crossed 9%.
