REGIS REPORT

07 July 2023

President Marcos signed an Executive Order (EO) reducing red tape in the process of securing permits for telecom infrastructure.

This new EO should mitigate the impact of the expiry of a regulation temporarily reducing permits for telco towers. We had concerns that the expiry would again slow down the rollout of towers, which ramped up sharply over the peak pandemic period due to the temporary reduction of required permits. It remains to be seen whether this EO will have a practical impact on the ground.

The Dept of Finance is hoping to collect around P100bn (US$1.8bn) of revenue from the imposition of VAT on digital services over a five-year period. MACRO
Additional P100bn revenues seen from digital transaction tax
  • The Department of Finance (DOF) expects to generate nearly P100bn in revenues over the next five years from the proposed value-added tax (VAT) on digital transactions
    • The imposition of a 12% VAT on digital service providers aims to level the playing field between traditional and digital businesses
    • Digital advertising, subscription-based services, and other services using information and communication technology-enabled infrastructure included in the plan
  • The DOF's estimate assumes a 50% collection efficiency in the first year, improving to 70% by 2025 until 2028
  • Revenues are projected to reach P72.69bn with 50% collection efficiency over the five-year period and up to P145.37bn with 100% collection efficiency.
  • The tax system currently covers only traditional businesses, missing substantial revenues from the digital economy
  • Taxing non-resident digital service providers is seen as fair and equal treatment in taxation, ensuring competition and preventing erosion of the VAT base.
  • The government aims to collect taxes from the growing digital economy to fund public services, infrastructure, and socioeconomic programs
  • Neighbouring Asian countries have also updated their tax frameworks to capture revenue from digital transactions, with VAT rates ranging from 5% to 11% on digital transactions
TELCOS
Telcos welcome EO cutting red tape in infra permit process
  • The signing of Executive Order (EO) No. 32 by the President of the Philippines has been hailed by local telecommunications companies.
    • The EO aims to streamline the permitting process for the construction of telecommunications and internet infrastructure in the country
    • The issuance of the EO is expected to pave the way for greater digital transformation, bridging the digital divide, and increasing access to digital resources and opportunities
    • The EO covers national government agencies, government-owned or -controlled corporations, and LGUs involved in the issuance of permits, licenses, clearances, certifications, and authorizations for telecommunications and internet infrastructure
    • Order encourages transparency and efficiency across government agencies and local government units (LGUs) involved in the permitting process.
  • Telcos had campaigned for the extension of the streamlined procedure and the institutionalization of the joint memorandum circular (JMC) of the Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA)
  • The streamlined permitting process will benefit infrastructure rollout plans and ensure faster, more affordable, and reliable services for the Filipino people.
  • The EO exempts certain permits and clearances, and mandates the establishment of one-stop shops for construction permits in cities and municipalities
  • Covered agencies and LGUs are required to implement a zero backlog policy and comply with the Anti-Red Tape Authority
  • A Technical Working Group (TWG) on Telecommunications and Internet Infrastructure will be created to ensure efficient implementation of the EO, chaired by the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT)
CONSUMER
Century Pacific allots P3.5bn for expansion
  • CNPF is allocating up to P3.5bn for this year's capital expenditure (capex) to expand its business segments.
    • The capex is higher than the company's usual allocation of P2 - P2.5bn
    • Funds will be mainly allocated to expand capacity in the meat, pet food, coconut, and other businesses, including packaging manufacturing
  • CNPF expects to achieve double-digit top-line growth this year
    • Although it may be on the lower end of the range (10%-11%) due to consumer headwinds in the first half
    • Also anticipates mid to high-level single-digit growth in net income this year 
    • Regis FY23E: revenues +10% / NPAT +19%
  • CNPF currently trades at ~15x 2023E PE
