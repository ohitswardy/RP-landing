26 January 2023

Ayala Corp (AC PM) subsidiary ACEN (ACEN PM) revealed yesterday that it has taken a 5% stake in SP New Energy Corp (SPNEC PM), the listed company controlled by entrepreneur Lean Leviste investing in renewables. It is unclear what prompted the deal but ACEN did say the acquisition of the 5% stake (equivalent to 500m shares) from SPNEC’s parent Solar Philippines will be treated as a prepayment of a loan ACEN previously extended to the latter. AC and Solar Philippines previously signed a deal to undertake a joint venture to develop a solar power facility. There appears to be little visible sign of progress on the JV.

For Solar Philippines, the transaction appears to be a simple exercise to raise funding for its various projects, given its limited financial resources. What’s less clear is the motivation behind ACEN agreeing to receive SPNEC shares as payment for a loan. Is ACEN taking the 5% stake immediately in exchange for the loan (rather than agree to be paid years from now) because prospects for the JV have become dim? Or could it actually be the reverse: ACEN is taking a stake in SPNEC as a prelude to a more meaningful ownership interest?

Yesterday, the majority shareholder of Cemex Philippines (CHP PM), Cemex Asia South East Corp, launched a tender offer to buy up to 12% of the CHP. The tender offer, if fully transacted, will bring the public float to just above 10%. This would keep CHP listed for now but doesn’t preclude another tender (ahead of a delisting) in the future.

The tender offer price of P1.30/share appears high relative to near-term multiples (around 7x EBITDA) but multiples can turn quickly when conditions change, especially because of the operating and financial leverage in the business. What is clear is the valuation at the tender offer price, while significantly above the undisturbed share price prior to the deal announcement, is well below replacement cost (US$90/MT vs US$150/MT).

We note that the share price of CHP already skyrocketed 63% to P1.03/share in the days before the tender offer plan was disclosed.

+++

Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) announced today that it was acquiring 15% of restaurant operator Figaro Coffee Group (FGC PM) for P820m through the purchase of 820m new shares at P1.00/share --  a modest 12% premium to the market price. FCG has a network of 124 stores and despite its name, over 90% of its revenue is derived from its pizza restaurant brand, Angel’s Pizza.

Frankly, we don’t see a lot of synergies that can be derived from the transaction, which doesn’t look like a deal motivated by broad strategic reasons.
