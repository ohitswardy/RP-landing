REGIS REPORT

10 May 2023

First Gen (FGEN PM) reported a core income of $89.1mn for 1Q23, up 58%YoY, and coming in much better than expectations. Improved gas availability and higher electricity prices lifted profits for its natural gas and geothermal units.  

Its LNG facility is currently in the commissioning stage and is scheduled for commercial operations by 3Q23. The ability to secure LNG supply is a risk, but the output from Malampaya appears sufficient in the near-term. More on First Gen’s results here:

Apart from First Gen, we also have a few more names whose results were just announced via press release (with the more detailed briefings still pending):

Jollibee (JFC PM) reported headline net profit of P2.1bn for 1Q23, down 11% YoY. Excluding pref divs and coupons to perps, we estimate net profit to common was at P1.8bn, behind our FY23 estimates. The underperformance was driven by a higher-than-expected effective tax rate vs 1Q22. Operating income jumped 81% YoY to P3.6bn, tracking ahead of FY23 expectations as the rise was magnified by operating leverage.  This was evident despite proportionately higher raw material cost as a % of revenues (+80bps YoY).

Revenues rose +29% YoY to P55.1bn, trending ahead of estimates. SSS in the Philippines grew 31.6%, though still coming from a low YoY base. Same-store transactions grew 17%, while average checks increased by 12%, with the latter primarily driven by price increases. Meanwhile, China SSS rebounded to +13%, now tracking in line with pre-pandemic levels by our estimate.

The briefing for JFC will be held on May 12.

DMCI Holdings (DMC PM) reported headline profit for 1Q was down 32% YoY to P7.62bn. Removing FX adjustments and one-offs, we estimate core income was lower by 30% YoY to P7.86bn. We’d characterize the 1Q results as trending behind Regis estimates (P29.9bn) but in line with consensus (P27.4bn). 

Profit from the property segment fell 22% YoY to P1.1bn, outperforming our estimates likely due to items below EBITDA. EBITDA dropped 34% YoY to P870mn (behind forecast), as revenues fell 18% YoY (behind) and margins contracted by 4ppts YoY to 18%. Reservation sales rebounded 70% QoQ and 37% YoY to P11.14bn due to higher launches and ASPs (2 vs. 1). The construction unit recorded a 26% YoY drop in 1Q23 profits at P273mn (behind), as revenues fell 24% YoY. After removing infra projects that are super delayed from its order in 4Q, the order book is 20% QoQ higher at P42bn, though this is down 10% YoY. 

DMCI management is holding a briefing at 4pm today.

Puregold (PGOLD PM), through parent company Cosco Capital (COSCO PM), reported 1Q23 net profit of P2.4bn, up 12% YoY. This is broadly in line with Regis FY23E (P10.5bn), but mildly ahead of consensus. Total net sales rose 15% YoY to P44.4bn, trending within expectations. SSSG for the core Puregold brand was 8.8%, while S&R posted SSSG of 16.8%. We should get further detail when the full quarterly report is released.

Finally, consolidated profit of San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) came in at P9.9bn (8%YoY), mildly ahead of Regis estimates. Income from its brewery jumped 38% to P6.8bn on the back of a 26% increase in local beer volumes, while Ginebra logged another 81% growth in profits (to P2.5bn – ahead). Growth from the beverage units more than offset the decline from food whose EBITDA fell 38% vs last year. We hope to get more detail during the earnings call next week (May 15).

+++

The country’s trade deficit for March came in at US$4.9bn, continuing its monthly print of being around US$5bn despite declining commodity prices. Exports continue to drop, falling 9% YoY in March. YT-March, the cumulative trade deficit was US$14.6bn.

+++

The country’s leading e-wallet GCash was hit by “unauthorized” transactions that led to as much as P37m (US$700k) being transferred to two local banks. As a result, GCash was down for a large part of Tuesday. Management says “no hacking occurred” and “customers’ funds are intact”, which sort of suggests that this series of incidents was a result of ‘phishing’. Confidence in the e-wallet is paramount and critical for its continued adoption and use.

GCash is a subsidiary of Mynt, which itself is partly owned by Globe (GLO PM).

MACRO
Trade deficit widens in March
  • Preliminary data showed the trade deficit ballooned to US$4.93bn in March (+7% YoY, 26% MoM)
    • Due to imports dropping by 3% YoY vis-à-vis a larger decline in exports (-9%)
  • Merchandise exports fell 9% YoY in March, slower than in Feb (-18% YoY) and January (-13% YoY)
    • By commodity, electronics saw the largest decline (-15% YoY), followed by coconut oil (-26% YoY)
    • Exports to China jumped 20% YoY, reversing 25% YoY monthly declines in the past two months
  • Merchandise imports declined by 2.7% YoY in March, slower than in February (-11.8% YoY)
    • Decline due to lower fuel imports and raw materials; Offset by higher imports of consumer goods
  • Year-to-March trade deficit at US$14.58bn, up 11% YoY
