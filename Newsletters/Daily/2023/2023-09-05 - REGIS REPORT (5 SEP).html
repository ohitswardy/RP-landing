REGIS REPORT

05 September 2023

The Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE) is set to revise its delisting rules, paving the way for clearer guidelines for companies wishing to delist. The current rules have two public ownership thresholds to delist. Companies wishing to “voluntarily” delist much reduce the public ownership to below 5%. Those whose public ownership falls between 5% and 10% will be involuntarily delisted.

The proposed revision will provide for a uniform threshold for minimum public ownership (MPO), currently pegged at 10%. Companies whose MPO falls below the threshold are required to undertake a tender offer for all remaining shares, essentially providing an exit for minority investors who may have not participated in any previous tender exercise/s.

These rule changes, when approved, are very likely going to apply to Metro Pacific’s (MPI PM) delisting plans.

Ayala Land (ALI PM) disclosed that its CEO Bernard Dy will be retiring by the end of the month after nine years at the helm of ALI. Dy will be replaced by Anna Margarita Bautista-Dy (no relation) who is currently COO, and also heads the residential and mall business of the group.

+++

Aug CPI inflation came in at 5.3%, a surprisingly sharp acceleration from July’s 4.7% and well above Street expectations (4.7%). As we feared, rice prices were the key driver of inflation. Rice price inflation was 8.7% for the month, a sharp jump from 4.2% the previous month. This CPI print (and succeeding ones) will likely prompt the BSP to hold any rate cuts in abeyance over the succeeding months.

Meanwhile, the administration said it will be providing subsidies to rice retailers in order for them to comply with the rice price cap. The President signed EO 39 last week which capped rice prices at P41-P45/kg (equivalent to US$730 to $800/MT). Retailers are unable to comply with the cap without suffering losses because wholesale prices are already above the price cap.

This proposal to provide subsidies to retailers is not only unwieldy and difficult to implement (e.g., how does one the determine the subsidy for each retailer?), it also corruption-prone. And, worse of all, we reckon it will ultimately be ineffective at addressing the one problem it’s meant to help solve: rising rice prices.

+++

Metro Pacific (MPI PM) says it is expanding its investment in the rail sector and is considering the purchase of Ayala Corp’s (AC PM) 35% stake in Light Rail Manila Corp (LRMC), which operates LRT-1. AC has not hidden its desire to exit LRMC after bruising encounters with the Duterte government on regulatory issues. AC is aiming to divest US$1bn of assets by end of the year and it’s raised ~$600m so far since the plan was first announced.

OTHERS
PSE proposes amendments to delisting rules
  • The PSE published draft amendments to its delisting rules in order for stakeholders to provide comments
  • Under the current rules, the delisting proponent is required to obtain at least 95% of the issued and outstanding shares of the listed company after a tender offer (in order to qualify for a voluntary delisting)
  • Under the proposed amendments, if the public ownership falls below the minimum requirement (currently at 10%), then a petition for voluntary delisting can be filed
  • Furthermore, if at the time the petition for delisting is filed, the minimum public ownership is already below the minimum requirement, then the persons proposing the delisting shall still be required to make a tender offer to all other stockholders and accept all tender shares
