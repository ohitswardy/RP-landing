REGIS REPORT

15 September 2023

Two of the most economically advanced regions outside Metro Manila have raised minimum wages this year. CALABARZON and Central Visayas (where Cebu is) raised their minimum wages between 8% and 11%. The wage hikes approved are a little higher than CPI inflation and would thus likely feed into better consumption spending. On the flipside, it also translates to higher costs for employers and is a contributing factor in core inflation creeping up.

+++

Vehicle unit sales are up 22% in Aug, taking YT-Aug sales to +30%, based on CAMPI data. Toyota unit sales were up 13% for the month and +17% cumulative to Aug.

Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) is 51% owned by GT Capital (GTCAP PM), and accounts for 28% of its gross assets and income. Our usual measure of value in the past has been its discount/premium to NAV and, on that measure, it is already quite cheap at just around half of its NAV. Alternatively, looking at GTCAP on an earnings basis puts it at a compelling 6x PER this year, sliding down to 5x in 2024.  

+++

Assets under management (AUM) of trust departments of banks and non-banks stood at P5.85trn, jumping 6% QoQ and 10% YTD. Banks are all looking to grow their wealth management businesses as a source of fees. In 2022, we estimate that banks earned on average 26bps worth of fee income from their AUMs. According to banks’ disclosures, BDO dominates the industry with just under P2trn in Trust AUMs as of 2Q23, with BPI at P1.1trn, and MBT at P0.5trn. Outside of potential fees, Trust clients are a potential source of funding for banks. For perspective, the total AUMs are equivalent to roughly 33% of total banking sector deposits in 2Q23. However, with system-wide LDRs at 72%, there is no urgency for banks to bring these funds “on-books”.

The AUM growth is almost certainly going largely into fixed income and money market funds, given high interest rates. Once interest rates start to fall, we could see some AUM shifting into risk assets (equities).

MACRO
Wage board approves hike in minimum daily wages for CALABARZON, Central Visayas
  • Regional wage board approved wage hikes for Regions IV-A (CALABARZON) and VII (Central Visayas - Cebu)
    • Region IV-A increased the minimum wage by 10%-11% to P385 to P520 (effective Sep 24)
    • Region VII increased the minimum wage by 8-9% to P420 to P468 (effective Oct 1)
  • Note that the minimum wage in NCR was hiked by 7-8% last July 16
  • In 2022, the government implemented wage hikes of +12%-14% throughout the country

CONSUMER
Vehicle sales jump 22% YoY in August
  • CAMPI reported that vehicle sales for the month of August rose 22% YoY to 36,714 units
    • On a MoM basis, vehicle sales slipped by 1%
    • Brings YT-August vehicle sales to 276,215 units (+30% YoY)
  • Toyota’s 8M23 sales stood at 126,795 units, up 17% YoY
    • Implies sales for August 2023 alone were 16,637 units, up 13% YoY and flat MoM
    • To reach our FY23E target of 192k units (+10% YoY), sales for the rest of the year only need to average 16.3k units
  • Toyota is a 51%-owned unit of GTCAP and accounts for 28% of net profit and gross asset value (36% of NAV)
  • GTCAP is trading at a 47% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,117/sh and 6x/5x PER in FY23E/FY24E

BANKS
Trust assets up again in 2Q23
  • Trust Assets of banks and non-banks jumped to P5.85trn as of 2Q23, up 6% QoQ, and 10% YTD.
    • Part of the QoQ jump may have been due to some mutual funds being transferred to trusts (2-3%)
  • Wealth management has been identified as a growth driver for banks
    • We estimate the average fee income in 2022 was around 26bps of AUM
  • Given the high interest rate environment, the growth was likely in fixed income / money market instruments, as opposed to equities.
    • A cut in local / global interest rates could see asset allocation into risk assets
  • Based on their disclosures, BDO had just under P2trn in consolidated AUM, BPI had P1.1trn, and MBT had close to P0.5trn, with the Big Three combining for more than 60% of the market.
