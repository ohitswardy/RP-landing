REGIS REPORT

26 July 2023

BPI (BPI PM) held its 2Q earnings call yesterday, which we believe pointed to even more positive trends than we had reported last week. Management reiterated its views that the current 15% ROE is a sustainable level. Beyond just policy rates – which are unlikely to be cut anytime soon – NIMs could still be supported by the rapid growth in higher margin consumer loans, particularly in the auto, credit card, and personal loan segments. The bank also stressed its (admittedly lofty) goal of 50mn customers by 2026 (from c9mn today) will be a major driver of ROEs. Its digitalization and agency banking initiatives are already bearing early fruit, now accounting for about half of BPI’s new-to-bank depositors, and about 40% of new credit card customers.

The aggressive push into consumer loans explained the apparent “deterioration” in asset quality. Management explained that the majority of the new NPL formation came from credit cards and personal loans. In fact, BPI argues that the NPLs are still “too low”, and that the bank can take on more risk, given the high yields on both products. Management also felt comfortable bringing NPL cover down to the 150-160% range (from 167.4% at 2Q23), suggesting that provision expense could stay low for a few more quarters.

After the call, we feel that risks to our forecasts might even be tilted to the upside. Considering, we’re already near the top of consensus estimates, we’d expect Street upgrades soon.

The deadline for SIM registration lapsed at midnight this morning, and all SIMs that had yet to be registered will cease to receive service.

Two days before the deadline, the government said that almost 106m SIM had already been registered, equivalent to 63% of the active reported SIMs.  Of the number, PLDT (TEL PM) accounted for 50m (75%) while Globe (GLO PM) accounted for 48m (56%).  DitoTel’s registered SIM count was 7.5m (50%).

Note that Globe’s proportion of SIMs registered is lower because it kept SIMs “active” longer before they are churned out due to inactivity.  In addition, PLDT was also more aggressive in providing freebies for those who register their SIMs. We’ll see if this freebie spree will have a lasting impact on the market.

Given the number/proportion of SIMs registered, the impact on mobile revenue is likely to minimal.  Moving forward, this SIM clean-up should give a more accurate picture of the true subscriber count. It would also result in a cosmetic increase in ARPUs (since the denominator is suddenly lower).

Philippine government revenues hit P1.9trn as of June, up 7.7%.  This is on track to meet its (modest) goal of increase revenue by 5%.

Meanwhile, the IMF adjusted its growth forecasts for the country, raising it to 6.2% (vs 6.0%) for FY23 and trimming it to 5.5% (vs. 5.8%) for FY24.

IMF raises Philippine growth forecast
  • IMF revised its Philippine growth forecasts for 2023 to 6.2% (from 6.0%) and 2024 to 5.5% (from 5.8%)
    • Revised outlook is still within the Philippine government's goal of 6.0-7.0% growth for 2023 but below its 2024-2028 GDP estimate of 6.5%-8%
    • Cited global headwinds and the effects of monetary policy tightening in advanced economies
  • We are forecasting 2023/2024 GDP growth rates of 5.5%/6%, broadly in line with Bloomberg consensus median estimates
    • Our 2023 GDP growth forecast implies growth will slow down to 5.2% from 6.4% in 1Q23
    • IMF’s 2023 GDP forecast implies GDP will moderate to 6.1% for the rest of the year
  • The government is set to release 2Q23 GDP on Aug 10

 

MACRO
Government revenues hit P1.9tn in 1H23
  • The National Government's revenues grew by 7.7% YoY to P1.9tn in the first six months of the year, driven by higher economic activity and digital transformation in revenue agencies
    • Tax collections in 1H23 rose by 7.5% YoY while nontax revenues grew by 9.1%
    • 1H23 revenues of P1.9tn exceeded the government’s 1H23 target of P1.81tn (+4.8% YoY)
    • For the full year of 2023, the government is targeting revenues to hit P3.7tn, up 5.2% YoY and equivalent to 15.2% of GDP
  • BIR Commissioner expressed hope in meeting the P2.6tn collection target for this year, emphasizing improved services for taxpayers
  • The proposed 2024 national budget of P5.768tn (+9.5% YoY) will be submitted to the House of Representatives on August 2, with priorities on infrastructure, food security, digital transformation, and human capital development

 

TELCOS
Registered SIMs hit 63% of total before deadline
  • The number of registered SIM cards reached 105.92mn, meeting the target before the July 25 deadline, according to the National Telecommunications Commission (NTC)
    • The registration represents 63.04% of the total 168.02mn subscribers across the three public telecommunication entities
    • The NTC stated that there will be no further extension in the deadline for registering active SIM cards
    • Smart Communications, Inc. registered 49.99mn subscribers, representing 75.39% of its total subscribers
    • Globe Telecom, Inc. recorded 48.38mn registrants, accounting for 55.77% of its total subscribers
