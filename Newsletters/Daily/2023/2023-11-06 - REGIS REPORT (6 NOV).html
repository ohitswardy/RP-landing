REGIS REPORT

06 November 2023

Globe (GLO PM) disclosed its 3Q results last Friday, ahead of the briefing to be held this afternoon. While the bottom line numbers do not look inspiring, we are actually quite encouraged by the results.  Core income for 9M was down 8% to P14.8bn (below trend to meet our FY23E estimate) but the light at the end of the tunnel is already visible. Mobile revenue picked up meaningfully and the broadband segment now appears to have bottomed out.  Contributions from Mynt, meanwhile, continue to improve.  Check out our initial comments below, ahead of the briefing this afternoon.

Meanwhile, Semirara (SCC PM) held its briefing last Friday, with management reiterating its expectation of a strong earnings rebound in 4Q.  While the company may not meet our 23E net profit forecasts due to higher non-cash costs, we reckon they should still be able to meet our EBITDA forecast. We maintain our 24E dividend forecast of P4.50/sh, which is equivalent to a yield of ~15%.

President Marcos appointed fishing tycoon Francisco Tiu-Laurel as his Secretary (Minister) of Agriculture last Friday.  Tiu-Laurel effectively replaces Marcos himself, who took over the portfolio directly. There could be questions raised, however, over Tiu-Laurel’s business interests, which could raise conflicts-of-interest concerns.

The appointment of a full-time Agriculture Secretary is itself a welcome development. A number of the biggest issues facing the administration today are arguably a result of the failure of agricultural policy.  The most pressing such issue is the soaring price of food, especially rice.

+++

Japanese Prime Kishida Fumio concluded his official visit to the Philippines after giving a rare address to a joint session of Congress on Saturday.  Among the other “fruits” of his visit were a few deals on the transfer of military equipment (radar and boats) and on tourism and mining.

More than these agreements themselves, the visit and the address underscore what we see as Japanese efforts to improve relations between the two countries. Japan is providing funding for the country’s single biggest infrastructure project (the Metro Manila subway) and Japanese firms are the partners of many of the biggest business groups in the country (PLDT (TEL PM), GT Capital (GTCAP PM), DMCI (DMC PM), et al).

+++

The Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE) is officially allowing short selling today in a process that has taken decades to accomplish, just as South Korea temporarily banned it.  Short selling is allowed for stocks that are part of the PSEi, the Mid Cap Index and the Dividend Yield Index of the PSE.  We don’t expect this to have much of an immediate impact but we view this development as an incremental step in the right direction.

TELCOS
Globe 3Q/9M – Profits mildly behind estimates but top line signs encouraging
  • 9M core profits P14.8bn, down 8% YoY. Globe’s 3Q core income (excluding one-offs) amounted to P4.8bn, down 3% YoY, taking 9M core income to P14.8bn. down 8% YoY.  While the results could be described as below trend to match our FY23E estimate, we are encouraged by positive signs. Overall service revenue is up +1% QoQ and +4% YoY, with  mobile revenue up +2% QoQ and +7% YoY.  Non-telco revenues grew 44% in 9M and now account for 3% of total revenue.
  • Mobile revenue picks up. 3Q saw the initial impact from SIM registration and the subscriber cull it resulted in (late July). Earlier in the year, we were concerned about its possible negative short-term impact but our fears did not materialize. Despite the subscriber cull (subscribers down 34% QoQ to 54.7m as of Sept), mobile revenue was up a surprising 2% QoQ (and +7% YoY) even if 3Q is usually a seasonally weak period. We believe “market repair” is happening and mobile data pricing is slowly rising.
  • Home broadband still down but turn is in sight.  Home broadband revenue is still down 1% QoQ but we believe 3Q is likely to be the bottom for this segment.  Globe launched its prepaid fiber product in July and added a net 23k wired subscribers in the quarter. Note Globe has shed wired broadband subs every quarter since 4Q22.

  • Mynt’s share of earnings continues to rise.  Mynt contributed P648m in profit for the quarter, equivalent to 13% of 3Q’s core income.  Mynt’s share of Globe’s core profit has grown steadily since last year.
