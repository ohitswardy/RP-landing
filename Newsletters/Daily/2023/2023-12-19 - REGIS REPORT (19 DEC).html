REGIS REPORT

19 December 2023

President Marcos created a new Cabinet Secretary-level position for Frederick Go, who is currently the CEO of Robinsons Land (RLC PM) and Presidential Adviser on Investment and Economic Affairs.

Mr. Go will serve as the Chairperson of the Economic Development Group, which was previously chaired by the Finance Secretary, and will supervise the following agencies:

    • Department of Finance (DOF)
    • National Economic Development Authority (NEDA)
    • Department of Trade and Industry (DTI)
    • Other agencies, such as Board of Investments (BOI), Philippine Economic Zone Authority (PEZA), and Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)

It is clearly a position with substantial authority. Thus, Mr. Go will likely be required to step down as CEO of Robinsons Land.

The Marcos administration has made efforts to be more collaborative with the business community and it has welcomed members of the Razon, Aboitiz, and Gokongwei groups into its fold.

+ + +

The Philippine port regulator approved the proposal to increase storage charges on foreign containers by 32%, as well as the 150% surcharge on storage fees for reefer containers. These new storage charges will take effect on January 6, 2024 and will apply to ICTSI’s Philippine-based ports, which account for 35% of group revenues.  

Assuming importers will not avoid paying storage fees, we estimate the tariff hike could raise our revenue and profit forecasts by 1-2% and 3-5%, respectively. 

+ + +

The BSP has approved the merger between BPI and Robinsons Bank. The merger is expected to increase BPI's asset base by 6-7%, making it the 2nd largest bank. It also gives BPI access to the vast ecosystem of customers, suppliers, etc of the Gokongwei Group.

INFRASTRUCTURE
Transport Department sticks to bid submission deadline for NAIA PPP
  • The Department of Transportation will not extend the current bid submission deadline of Dec 27 for the NAIA rehab PPP
    • According to Transportation Secretary Jaime J. Bautista
    • Government wants to implement the program as soon as possible
  • This comes after its transaction advisor, ADB, recommended to push back the deadline by a month (to Jan 29) to give potential bidders more time to prepare and submit their bids
    • While there are four bidders who requested for an extension, Secretary Bautista understands that they will still submit bids on the original deadline
  • To date, there are eight prospective bidders for the NAIA rehabilitation
    • Incheon International Airport
    • San Miguel Holdings Corp.
    • Manila International Airport Consortium (AEV, AC, LTG, AGI, FDC, JGS – and US-based Global Infrastructure Partners)
    • Turkish firm Cengiz Insaat Sanayi ve Ticaret AS
    • Turkish firm Limak Group
    • GMR Airports International
    • Spark 888 Management
    • Asia Airport Consortium
  • The P170.6bn project aims to raise passenger capacity to 62mn annually from 32mn today over the 15-year concession period (extendable by 10 years)
    • According to the draft concession agreement, the winning bidder is required to pay an upfront amount of P30bn, an annual payment of P2bn plus a share of revenues (excluding passenger terminal fees)
INFRASTRUCTURE
PPA approves higher storage fees for foreign containerized cargoes
  • The Philippine Ports Authority (PPA) approved a 32% increase in storage charges for foreign container shipments after the five-day free storage period
    • It also approved the 150% surcharge on storage of reefer containers
    • Both charges will take effect on January 6, 2024
    • The PPA last adjusted storage fees in 2014
  • Our forecasts do not assume this tariff hike
    • Storage fees would typically account c. 15% of ICT’s consolidated revenues while Philippine-based ports account for 35% consolidated revenues
    • We estimate this could lift our revenue forecasts by c. 1-2% and profit forecasts by 3-5%
    • ICTSI is trading at 7.9x FY24E EV/EBITDA and 12x FY24E PER, well below peers and historical valuation
BANKS
Merger of BPI and Robinsons Bank receives BSP approval
  • The BSP approved the merger between BPI and Robinsons Bank Corp (RBC)
  • JG Summit (JGS PM) owns 60% of RBC while Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM) owns the remaining 40%
  • BPI will be the surviving entity after the merger
  • Following the BPI-RBC merger and the subsequent purchase of an indirect stake in BPI by RRHI, the Gokongwei Group will own 10.1% of BPI (6.5% RRHI / 3.6% JGS)
  • The merger is expected to increase BPI's asset base by 6-7%, making it the 2nd largest bank. It also gives BPI access to the vast ecosystem of customers, suppliers, etc of the Gokongwei Group
