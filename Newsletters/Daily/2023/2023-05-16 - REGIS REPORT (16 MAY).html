REGIS REPORT

16 May 2023

We have more results today:

  • Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) (-) – Core net profit (-12% YoY) underperformed mainly to wider-than-expected losses at Quorn, which was hit by inflationary pressures both on the revenue and cost side. The core APAC BFB segment (EBITDA +5% YoY), however, performed in line with expectations as it continued to recover market share in instant noodles and lower cost inventory had started to come through. APAC BFB margins should continue to improve sequentially, though the near-term outlook for profitability at Quorn is less certain, and the impact of the business restructuring is not likely to be felt until next year.

  • GT Capital (GTCAP PM) (+) – GTCAP’s 1Q23 core profit after pref dividends jumped 46% QoQ and 67% YoY to P6.42bn, trending ahead of Regis FY23E (P16.7bn) and consensus estimates (P18.6bn). The auto unit outperformed, along with Metrobank (MBT PM, see results comment) and Metro Pacific (MPI PM, see here). Toyota’s 1Q23 standalone profit reached a record of P4.51bn, up substantially from P1.45bn in 4Q22 and P2.07bn in 1Q22. Revenues rose 28% YoY, underpinned by 21% YoY improvement in sales volumes and price increases. Gross profit margins jumped 410bps YoY to 14.5%, not quite the peak but higher than historical average (12.5%). The firm’s briefing will be held this afternoon.
  • San Miguel Food and Beverage (FB PM) (+) – The company saw income dip 8% YoY to P5.65bn, which is still better than our expectations. Underlying operations are trending below estimates, but a one-off gain boosted profits. Beer sales rebounded sharply (+26% YoY) as drinkers went back to bars and restaurants. Gin sales, however, were down.

  • Alliance Global (AGI PM) (=) – AGI reported income of P4.7bn for 1Q, up 20% YoY and looking in line with estimates. Megaworld’s (MEG PM) income rose 34% while Emperador’s (EMI PM) rose 10%. AGI holds its briefing this morning.
  • Vista Land (VLL PM) (+) – 1Q23 net profit was flat YoY at P2.2bn. While this looks technically “ahead” of REGIS and consensus forecasts, underlying operations are weaker than they appear. Although rental revenue is 85% above the 2019 average, we consider Vista’s rental revenue to be overstated relative to cash rent. Furthermore, residential revenue fell YoY for the 14th consecutive quarter.

  • Filinvest Land (FLI PM) (-) – 1Q23 earnings rose 9% to P741mn, behind REGIS and consensus forecasts. The reason for the underperformance was the tepid 4% growth of residential revenue. Meanwhile, office revenue was flat YoY and QoQ, suggesting that office vacancy is still close to the 4Q22 level of 32%. The company will hold a briefing on May 22.
  • Shakey’s (PIZZA PM) (=) – 1Q23 net profit surged 2.6x YoY, on track to meet our FY23 estimates. While revenues (+94% YoY) were boosted by the consolidation of Potato Corner, SSSG for the core brand was still a healthy 29%, albeit coming from a low YoY base. Margins, however, were weighed by higher input cost. The company will be holding a briefing tomorrow to discuss the results in detail.
  • Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) – Revenues jumped 36% YoY on the back of 23% SSSG. We understand that same-store foot traffic during the quarter was still slightly below pre-pandemic levels, but should improve in the coming quarters. Margins continue to improve, highlighting operating leverage from the top line improvement, as well as operating efficiencies.

+++

President Marcos signed the deal extending the service contracting covering Malampaya by another 15 years. Service contract 38 (SC38) was supposed to end next year and will now end in 2039. Its operator is now controlled by Enrique Razon, the principal behind Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) and ICTSI (ICT PM).

The extension means there will be more gas from Malampaya over the medium term, helping diversify the country’s fuel supplies and provide energy security. At its peak, Malampaya supplied fuel to over 3,000MW of power generation capacity. That figure is down to ~2,000MW due to diminished output. With the operator’s contract to supply gas to First Gen (FGEN PM) also ending next year, the gas from Malampaya will have to find buyers.

+++

March overseas remittances amounted to US$2.67bn, which is 3% higher YoY. This is in line with the government’s prediction for the year.

+++

Unit sales for CAMPI/TMA were 30,643 in April, up 22% YoY but down 17% QoQ. Toyota sales for April were 14,123, implying no change in its relative market share MoM.

MACRO
Remittances up 3.0% YoY in March
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks rose 3% YoY to US$2.67bn in March
    • 3-month average growth rate slowed down to 3.0% YoY in March from 4.0% YoY in February
    • In PHP terms, remittances rose 8.3% YoY
  • Land-based remittances rose 3.3% YoY, buoyed by remittances from Asia and the Middle East
  • Sea-based remittances remains tepid, rising by 1.8% YoY
  • Brings 1Q23 remittances up 3% YoY to US$8.0bn
    • This is broadly in line with the BSP’s target of a +3% YoY improvement

POWER
President Marcos signs deal extending Malampaya service contract
  • President Marcos Jr. signed an agreement renewing the service contract for the Malampaya gas field by 15 years or until 2039
    • Service Contract (SC) 38, which covers the Malampaya gas field in offshore Palawan, was originally scheduled to expire on Feb 22, 2024
  • “We optimistically look forward to the continued production and utilization of the remaining reserves of the Malampaya gas field, as well as further exploration and development of its untapped potential”
    • The Malampaya gas field is the country’s only indigenous source of natural gas and is expected to be commercially depleted by 2027
    • It supplies over 20% of the electricity requirements of the Luzon grid
  • SC 38 is owned by the Malampaya consortium: Razon-led Prime Infrastructure (45% stake), UC38 LLC of the Udenna Group (45%), and PNOC-EC (10%)
    • The consortium is mandated to conduct geological and geophysical studies and drilling of at least two deep water wells from 2024 to 2029
    • The consortium is also required to conduct exploratory drillings outside the Malampaya production area but still with the Service Contract to retain the exploration areas
  • SC38 has fueled gas plants owned by First Gen (FGEN PM) and San Miguel Global Power (1,200MW Ilijan, expired in 2022)
CONSUMER
Vehicle sales drop 17% MoM in April
  • Vehicle sales, as reported by CAMPI, fell by 17% MoM to 30,643 units in April
    • On a YoY basis, vehicle sales were up 22%
    • Note that CAMPI began including Chery in Sep 2022
    • Brings 4M23 sales to 127,927 units, up 28% YoY (vs 2023E target of 395k, +12% YoY)
  • Toyota sold 59,328 units in 4M23 (+16% YoY), implying that it sold 14,123 units in April (+3% YoY, -16% MoM)
    • To reach Toyota’s FY23 target of 188k units (+8% YoY), Toyota needs to sell 16k units per month for the rest of the year
    • Toyota is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for c. 20% of profit/NAV
    • GTCAP is trading at a 53% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,014/sh

CONSUMER
PIZZA 1Q23 NPAT surges 2.6x to P201mn as revenues jump 94% YoY - in line
  • PIZZA’s 1Q23 surged to P201mn from P76mn in the same period last year. This is tracking broadly in line with our FY23 estimates.
  • Revenues jumped 94% YoY to P3.1bn (ahead), boosted by the consolidation of Potato Corner. SSS (ex-PC) grew 29%, though coming from a low YoY base. We estimate same-store sales are already tracking above pre-pandemic levels.
  • Gross margins contracted 400bps YoY, pressured by higher input cost.

RETAIL
Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) 1Q23 net profit jumps to P528mn
  • Revenues +36% YoY on the back of +23% SSSG. The strong SSSG is likely partly due to the low YoY base (Omicron in 1Q22)
  • Price inflation also likely contributed to the strong SSSG. We understand that same store traffic is still tracking slightly below pre-pandemic levels as offices and schools are still on a hybrid model. Schools are mandated to implement full face-to-face classes when the new school year begins in 3Q.
  • Margins continue to improve, highlighting operating leverage from the top line improvement, as well as operating efficiencies.
