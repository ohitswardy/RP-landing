REGIS REPORT

09 November 2023

We have lots of names on today’s results round-up.  Among the more notable beats were ICTSI (ICT PM), SM Investments (SM PM) and San Miguel Food and Beverage (FB PM). While the consumer names Century Pacific (CNPF PM) and Monde (MONDE PM) underperformed a bit, the big miss would be from D&L Industries (DNL PM). Check out our summaries:

  • ICTSI (ICT PM) (+) - Recurring 3Q23 profit after perps distributions came in flat at US$163mn, as revenue growth at their ports in Asia, particularly Philippines and Melbourne, dropped and offset the strong growth rates in Latin America and EMEA. Management also flagged there could be a large one-off provision in 4Q23 related to the remaining receivable in Sudan, which has a carrying value of Euro 140mn.  Core 9M23 profits of US$473mn (+8%) are trending well ahead of forecasts, which should also translate to better 24E dividends, as ICT’s payout is based on recurring profit.

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (+) – A good print for SM this quarter, with profits rising 30% to P56bn for 9M, putting it ahead of trend to meet Regis and consensus estimates.  The key listed subsidiaries previously reported results that were within/slightly ahead of expectations while SM Retail delivered fairly robust numbers. Revenue for the latter was up 12% to P291bn, while income rose 19% to P13.7bn.  Some more detail below.
  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) (=) – 9M23 net profit rose 38% to P18.4bn. While this is tracking ahead of REGIS and consensus forecasts, 3Q23 development revenue was weak and we suspect that the unusually strong 3Q23 EBIT margin will not be sustained. More importantly, the company’s new CEO, Anna Ma. Margarita Dy, announced her plan to double earnings by 2028 (relative to 2023). Her plan entails expanding the company’s rental portfolios and increasing the residential segment’s focus on high-end projects.

  • Century Pacific (CNPF PM) (-) – The branded business continued to grow strongly in 3Q23 (+14% YoY) mainly driven by the dairy segment, which benefitted from downtrading. GPM expanded both QoQ and YoY on favorable input costs, but this did not translate to meaningful NPAT growth (+13% YoY in 3Q) as the company increased A&P spending.

For international investors >> Century Pacific - 3Q23: Margins stabilize as input costs ease

For Philippine-based investors >> Century Pacific - 3Q23: Margins stabilize as input costs ease

  • Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) (-) – 3Q23 performance was driven by the recovery of the instant noodle business, which saw record volumes during the quarter. Favorable input cost for wheat and palm oil helped push margins back to “normal” levels, leading to a 73% YoY jump in segment EBITDA. Meanwhile, Quorn continues to struggle in a challenged environment but restructuring efforts led to EBITDA breakeven. More importantly, MONDE’s principal shareholders are extending a backstop for Quorn to cover further losses until 2032.

For international investors >> Monde Nissin - 3Q23: Noodle business rebounds; shareholders extend backstop for Quorn

For Philippine-based investors >> Monde Nissin - 3Q23: Noodle business rebounds; shareholders extend backstop for Quorn

  • San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) (+) – Consolidated profits in 3Q softened to P8.7bn (-2%QoQ, +15%YoY), but 9M23 nevertheless appears ahead of estimates. Across beer and gin units, profits dipped 12%QoQ and 13%QoQ. It declared another special dividend of P0.55/sh (on top of a regular P0.40/sh), with YTD payout now yielding >5%.
  • D&L Industries (DNL PM) (-) – An underutilized Batangas facility dragged DNL’s performance even lower. Profits from food (-25%QoQ) and oleochemicals (-24%) continued to dip on lower volumes, and in the case of oleochemicals, worse margins. Given the limited orders for the new plant, we expect a drag on earnings from Batangas to persist over several quarters.

SM Investments 9M profits rise 30% to P56bn

    • Implied 3Q income of P19.4bn, +27% YoY
  • Earnings are trending better than forecast for FY23
  • Banking accounted for 47% of earnings, property 26%, retail 18% and portfolio investments 9%
  • Key listed units BDO and SM Prime reported slightly better than expected profits for 9M
  • SM Retail’s results are also looking quite strong with profit of P13.7bn for 9M23 (+19%)
    • Revenue growth of 12% in 9M and income growth of 19%
    • Food retail saw SSSG of 5% as transaction count grew more than the decline in basket sizes
      • Implying tighter purse strings for consumers
    • Profits were uncharacteristically higher in 3Q vs 2Q, driven by Non-Food segments, due to school-related purchases
