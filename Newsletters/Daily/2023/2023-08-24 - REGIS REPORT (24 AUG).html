REGIS REPORT

24 August 2023

A newspaper reported that BSP Governor Eli Remolona would like to see banks’ Peso deposit regulatory reserve requirements (RRR) drop to 5% by the end of his term (mid 2029), further adding that the “ideal is zero”. However, he also said that any cut will have to happen once inflationary pressures ease, and the BSP is no longer in hawkish mode. The earliest would probably be next year (2024). Recall that the RRR was dopped to 9.5% in June, from 11.0% previously, and down from 20% in 2018

A RRR cut would not be a major monetary decision, since the BSP already has other tools to mop up liquidity, since its new charter was approved in 2018.

Any cut would also not induce banks to lend, given system-wide LDRs are in the low-70% range.

The main benefit would be to banks’ profits, given that funds locked in RRR do not earn any interest. Assuming banks place the released funds in bonds at 6.5%, we estimate that the impact of every 100bps cut in RRR would add about 1.5% to pre-tax profits, based on our FY2023E forecasts.

+++

The administration’s economic team is apparently pushing to retain lower tariffs on a number of import commodities in order to help restrain inflation. Given the country’s dependence on food from other countries, the landed cost of imports is a major determinant of local prices of food items, most critically rice. Rice has been in the headlines recently due to feared supply issues amidst predictions of reduced harvests. Retail rice prices in the country are up ~17% YTD and rice is 9% of the CPI basket.

+++

Dito CME (DITO PM) supposedly disclosed the entities that acquired 2.2bn new shares but the disclosure shed little light on the true identities of the buyers. One has to wonder about the rationale of investing US$40m of new money into an entity whose operating loss (before interest costs and FX gains/loss) is on track to reach P20bn this year. Unless the new subscriptions are a prelude to the entry of a new strategic partner, we doubt it will mean much to the overall mobile/telecom market.

+++

The government has officially launched the bidding for the takeover and rehabilitation of Manila’s airport NAIA with the aim of getting final bids submitted by end of the year. Given the possibility of failed biddings, we’d be happy to see the project awarded by middle of next year.

    • RRR is currently at 9.5%
  • The timing of when to cut RRR is crucial and not while the BSP is still in a hawkish policy stance amid a still elevated inflation expectations
    • The BSP may reconsider the cut by end-2023
  • We estimate every 100bps RRR cut could increase NPAT for banks under our coverage by ~1.5%
MACRO
Economic team prefers extension of reduced tariffs on key commodities
  • National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) Secretary Arsenio Balisacan said that there is a need to address elevated inflation to achieve its 6-7% GDP growth target
  • Mr. Balisacan said there is a need to extend the executive order that reduced tariffs on key commodities, which is set to expire in December
    • He expressed support for extending the reduced tariffs for pork, rice, corn, and coal to keep inflation contained
  • Meanwhile, Finance Secretary Benjamin Diokno said that the economic team will have another meeting next month to review if there is a need to extend tariffs across all commodities
  • Listed below are tariffs for each commodity:
    • In-quota imports of pork – 15%
    • Out-quota imports of pork – 25%
    • In-quota imports of rice – 35%
    • Out-quota imports of rice – 35%
    • In-quota imports of corn – 5%
    • Out-quota imports of corn – 15%
    • Coal imports have zero tariff
INFRASTRUCTURE
Government opens bidding for NAIA privatization
  • The Department of Transportation (DOTr) has officially launched the bidding for the P170.6bn contract to operate and maintain the NAIA
    • Operator will need to raise passenger capacity to 62mn per year from 32mn today over the 15-year concession period (extendable by 10 years)
  • Based on the bidding schedule, the DOTr will issue the draft concession agreement on Sept. 8 and will host site visits for interested parties between Aug. 28 and Nov. 14
    • Pre-bid conference on Sept. 22
    • Release the final bid bulletin and concession agreement on Dec. 4
    • Submission of bids set on Dec. 27
INFRASTRUCTURE
Infrastructure spending up 2% YoY in 1H23
  • Overall infrastructure spending (i.e. including LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) rose by 2% YoY to P607bn in 1H23
    • Implies spending in June 2023 alone fell 15% YoY on high base effects from the lifting of last year’s election ban
    • 2Q23 infra spend rose 8% YoY to P363bn
  • The government is targeting to spend P1.29tn (+1% YoY) in FY23, equivalent to 5.3% of GDP

TELCOS
Third-party subscribers that took 13.55% of DITO CME named
  • Two unrelated third-party subscribers to shares in DITO CME Holdings have cornered as much as 13.55% of DITO CME
  • In a regulatory filing, DITO CME said one of the subscribers, Xterra Ventures Pte. Ltd., make up 3.76% or 610mn of the firm’s 16.235bn issued and outstanding common shares
  • Before the share issuance to Xterra, DITO CME issued 1.59bn new shares to Summit Telco Corp. Pte. Ltd, which then brought the firm’s outstanding shares to 15.625bn
  • Summit Telco now accounts for 9.79% of DITO while Udenna has 68.99%
  • Xterra Ventures is a Singapore-based entity with an” investment fund engaged in investment activities in the Asia Pacific region”
