REGIS REPORT

17 July 2023

As of July 13, the total number of SIMs that have been registered reached 103.1m. This is equivalent to 61% of the total active SIMs in the market as of Dec 2022. We reckon that SIMs registered account for almost all (>97%) of the mobile revenue of the operators. The short-term dip we had been expecting after the deadline (now July 25) is likely to be smaller than we anticipated, especially following the 90-day extension of the SIM registration period.

Of the total, PLDT (TEL PM) accounted for 48.8m, implying 74% of its SIMs have been registered. For Globe (GLO PM), the number of registered SIMs was 47.0m, equivalent to 54% registered. The absolute number of SIMs registered reflect, in our view, the true active SIM base – PLDT has slightly more but Globe has higher ARPU subscribers (thus higher revenue share). DitoTel’s registered SIM base, on the other hand, was 7.3m – 49% of its claimed subscriber base.

+++

New Bangko Sentral (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona gave the market a glimpse of his monetary leanings, reminding the markets not to count their chickens before they’re hatched. While the domestic financial market is already trying to guess when the BSP will cut rates, he says “we’re contemplating whether to hike or not to hike…not thinking about whether to cut or not to cut.”

Remolona, however, did say he’d like to lower the bank reserve requirement ratio (RRR) down further from 9.5%.

+++

San Miguel (SMC PM) and Metro Pacific (MPI PM) are apparently teaming up to build a new 96-km toll road traversing the provinces of Cavite and Batangas and connecting the STAR and CALAX toll roads. Cooperation between the two main toll road operators will likely speed up the process and hopefully limit delays.

Meanwhile, also in infrastructure news, the government is seeking private sector funding for four major rail projects. These are mammoth projects that local business groups cannot just fund that easily. These rail projects were previously supposed to be funded by the Chinese government, but plans have since fallen through.

+++

Cosco Capital (COSCO PM) is reportedly contemplating a REIT listing that would be spun off from its leasing operations. COSCO owns a real estate leasing business as well as Pure Petroleum, which holds its fuel terminal facility (also leased out) in Subic. The combined EBITDA of its leasing operations amounted to P1.2bn last year.

MACRO
BSP Governor keeps door open to rate tightening, plans to lower RRR further
  • BSP Governor Eli Remolona said that the BSP remains “more on the tightening side”
    • “For now, we’re contemplating whether to hike or not to hike…not thinking about whether to cut or not to cut”
    • It must bring inflation back to within the BSP’s 2-4% target
    • He described the BSP as “structurally hawkish” and emphasized its inflation-targeting role
    • Price gains have slowed in the past five months and are on course to return to within the target band next quarter
  • The governor also said that the wants to lower banks’ reserve requirement ratio further from the current 9.5%
    • Reducing the RRR, along with improving digitalization, would make the banking system more efficient
TELCOS
Registered sims reach 61% of total subscribers
  • As of July 13, there are 103.1mn registered SIMs in public telecommunication entities, which is 61.36% of the total number of subscribers as of December 2022
    • PLDT Inc.'s Smart Communications leads with 48.8mn registered SIMs - 73.6% of its 66.3mn subscribers
    • Globe Telecom with 47.04mn registered SIMs - 54.21% of its 86.75mn subscribers
    • DITO Telecommunity Corp with 7.28mn registered SIMs - 48.64% of its 14.97mn users
  • The SIM Registration Act requires all SIM users to register their SIMs under their name to combat text scams and mobile phone-aided criminal activities
  • The Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) Secretary Ivan John E. Uy previously hinted that there will be no further extension of the registration deadline on July 25
INFRASTRUCTURE
San Miguel, Metro Pacific team up for new tollroad connecting Batangas and Cavite
  • Metro Pacific and San Miguel are teaming up to build a new 96-km tollroad that will connect Nasugbu, Batangas, STAR tollway, Tagaytay, and CALAX
    • According to San Miguel’s President and CEO, Ramon Ang
    • The two companies are ironing out the details of the project, which could be completed as early as 2028
    • It will be submitted to the government as a new unsolicited proposal
    • Targeting to start working on securing the right-of-way as early as this year or 2024
  • San Miguel previously signed a JV deal with the Cavite local government to build a P27bn expressway linking Silang, Cavite and Nasugbu Batangas
  • Separately, Metro Pacific Tollways Corp. submitted an unsolicited proposal for a 50.4-km tollroad called, Cavite-Tagaytay-Batangas Expressway
INFRASTRUCTURE
Government seeks private sector funding for four railway projects
  • The Department of Transportation is looking at the private sector to bankroll four railway projects
    • P175bn PNR south long-haul railway (or PNR Bicol)
    • 800-km North Long Haul railway covering Ilocos Region, Cagayan Valley, Central Luzon, and Metro Manila
    • P50bn Subic-Clark railway
    • P83bn Mindanao Railway
  • The projects were originally supposed to be financed by China, but it failed to act on the loan proposals
CONGLOS
Cosco Capital plans industrial REIT IPO
  • Cosco Capital (COSCO) is reportedly planning an REIT listing for its industrial real estate business worth P15-30bn
  • COSCO’s real estate business currently operates commercial real estate buildings and community malls, most of which are locations of Puregold and S&R stores. It also operates Pure Petroleum, which is operates a fuel terminal facility in Subic.
  • In 2022, the real estate segment generated revenues of P1.8bn and EBITDA of P1.2bn
  • COSCO currently trades at a 58% discount to market-based NAV
    • Real estate accounts for 22% of NAV
