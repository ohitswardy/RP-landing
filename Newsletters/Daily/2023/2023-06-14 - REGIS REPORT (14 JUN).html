REGIS REPORT

14 June 2023

ICTSI (ICT PM) disclosed that the port authority of Pakistan is allowing ICT’s contract for a port there to lapse this week.

The end of the contract would mean ICT should hand over operations back to the government there pending a new concession agreement. A new agreement, with either ICT or any other parties, has not been finalized but ICT claims to have a right of first refusal over the operations of the terminal.

This terminal in Karachi was to account for 1% of ICT’s profit this year and next (FY23-24), but it comprises a negligible share of our NAV estimate for ICT because we are assuming it terminates after next year.

+++

Voyager Innovations, the fintech holding firm of PLDT (TEL PM), is aiming to raise up to US$150m within this year.  Voyager is the holding company for e-wallet Maya and digital bank Maya Bank.

The move does not come as a surprise given how much Voyager raised in April 2022 (US$210m or ~P11bn) and the level of its quarterly losses.  Based on PLDT’s disclosed share of Voyager’s P&L, we estimate Voyager has been losing P2bn every quarter.

+++

Ayala Land (ALI PM) is raising up to P19bn from fixed rate bonds with tenors of five (5) and ten (10) years. The funds will partly be for refinancing and partly for capex.

It’s not often that we see corporates raising funds from a 10-year PHP bond issue, but it makes sense to do so at this time given the shape of the PHP yield curve (see chart below, red line).  With the PHP yield curve today virtually flat (from the overnight to the 30-year tenors), it makes sense to stretch out maturities as long as possible.

 

+++

Vehicle sales for May, as reported by CAMPI/TMA, were quite robust.  Units sold in the month reached 38k units, up 45% YoY and 25% MoM.  Toyota sales for the month were also quite strong at almost 18k units.

TRANSPORTATION
Government of Pakistan terminates contract of ICTSI’s Pakistan port
  • Karachi Port Trust (KPT) is letting the contract of Pakistan International Container Terminal (PICT PK) expire as scheduled on June 17, 2023
    • All concession assets including equipment and IT systems will remain property of PICT
  • PICT has offered O&M services when and if required by KPT beyond June 16 but it is unclear at this time if KPT will take this offer
  • Meantime, PICT will wait for the terms of a new agreement that will be tendered, wherein PICT has right of first refusal
  • PICT has the right to operate Berths 6-9 under a 21-year concession agreement, which expires on June 17
    • The Build-Operate-Transfer agreement means that PICT is bound to transfer concession assets to KPT once conditions are met
    • All concession and non-concession assets will be fully depreciated by June 17, even though these assets may continue to be used beyond this period
  • While PICT’s value is inconsequential to our NAV (US$5mn vs. NAV of US$8.5bn), we assumed it will continue to contribute to ICTSI’s profits until 2024
    • We estimate PICT accounts for 4% of FY23E volumes, 2% of FY23E revenues, 1% of FY23E profit
    • It is unclear at this stage if ICT will need to impair the US$13mn of goodwill related to PICT
CONGLOMERATES
Vehicle sales rebound strongly in May
  • CAMPI reported vehicle sales jumped by 45% YoY and 25% MoM to 38,177 units in May
    • Recall that April sales volumes were affected by public holidays
    • Brings YT-May sales to 166,104 units, up 32% YoY and on track to beat CAMPI’s target of 395k (+12%)
  • Toyota’s sales volumes rose 21% YoY, 27% MoM to 17,866 units
    • Brings 5M23 sales to 77,194 units, up 18% YoY
    • To reach Toyota’s 188k FY23 target (+8% YoY), monthly sales should reach 15,800 units for the rest of the year
  • Toyota is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for c. 20% of GTCAP’s profit/NAV
  • GTCAP is trading at a 50% discount to its market-based NAV of P1009/sh

 

CONGLOMERATES
AGI approves buy-back extension, increases budget by P2.0bn
  • Alliance Global Group (AGI PM) disclosed that its board approved to extend the present buy-back program up to April 8, 2025 (from the original ending period of April 8, 2024) and increase the amount allocated for the program by P2.0bn
  • A total of P6.928bn (596.852mn shares or 6.6% of outstanding common shares) has been acquired by AGI under a buy-back program that originally started in 2017
    • Amount appropriated under the current program is P7.0bn
  • AGI is trading at a 62% discount to market NAV and 6.7x PE on FY23E
TELCOS
PLDT unit to raise US$150mn fresh funds for Maya
  • PLDT’s Voyager Innovations, Inc. is looking to raise US$100-150mn in fresh funds this year to strengthen its integrated money platform, Maya
    • According to PLDT Chairman Manuel V. Pangilinan
    • Voyager is currently negotiating with potential foreign investors to raise the funds, while PLDT is also expected to participate
    • Voyager last raised new funds (US$210m) in April 2022, at a valuation of around US$1.4bn
  • PLDT President and CEO Alfredo Panlilio also said that the market listing of Maya is being considered but it needs to improve fundamentals of the bank first
    • Maya has been increasing loan disbursements in recent months and management expects the bank’s EBITDA to be positive by year-end
  • For FY22, PLDT’s attributable losses from 36.8%-owned Voyager amounted to P3bn, which implies Voyager’s losses reached P8bn (US$150m)
PROPERTY
Ayala Land to raise up to P19bn in 5-, 10-year bonds
  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) plans to raise P14bn in fixed-rate bonds with an oversubscription option of up to P5bn (see bond prospectus here)
    • Series A 5-year notes will pay 6.0253% p.a.
    • Series B 10-year notes will pay 6.2948% p.a.

  • Net proceeds of up to P18.77bn will be used on the following:
    • P8.37bn will be used to partially refinance short-term loans, which have interest rates bet. 6.05% - 6.25%
    • P10.4bn will be used to fund capex
