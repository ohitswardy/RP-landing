REGIS REPORT

14 November 2023

Here’s the roster of results for today.

  • GT Capital (GTCAP PM) (+) – GT Capital reported its 9M23 core income doubled to P23.25bn. Excluding the P3.2bn of gain from lot sales, core profit grew 80% YoY to P19.61bn after the share of prefs, trending ahead of Regis FY23E (P21.8bn) and consensus estimates (P22.87bn). The Toyota business beat expectations, with its 9M23 standalone profit surging 158% YoY to P10.88bn, already 90% of FY23E. Implied 3Q23 profit for Toyota grew 3x YoY to P2.87bn on the back of a +13% YoY jump in retail sales and 462bps improvement in GPM to 12.5%. More after the briefing later at 2:30pm – Registration link
  • Emperador (EMI PM) (-) – Emperador’s 9M23 profit dipped 6%YoY to P6.8bn, short of estimates for the full year. 3Q23 profit came in at P2.1bn (-15%QoQ, 5%YoY), its lowest quarterly print for the year, with profits from the brandy segment remaining weak at just P867mn (-10%QoQ, 1%YoY). Its share of domestic spirits volumes has fallen to 17%.
  • San Miguel Food & Bev (+) – 3Q23 profit rose to P5.2bn (1%QoQ, 16%YoY) on the back of a steady contribution from gin and a recovery in the food segment. Within beverages, however, beer volumes trended lower (-8%QoQ, -4%YoY and now back to below 70% of pre-Covid levels due to downtrading.

  • Filinvest Land (FLI PM) (=) – 9M23 net profit rose 22% to P2.4bn, in line with REGIS forecast. While residential revenue is trailing behind our forecast, residential GPM was stronger than expected. Meanwhile, office revenue rose 3% QoQ, which might indicate an improvement in occupancy (from 60% in 2Q23).
  • Manila Water (MWC PM) (+) – 9M23 net income after the share of participating prefs reached P6.35bn, up 61% YoY and trending well ahead of forecasts.  The outperformance, however, came from (a) higher effective tariffs and (b) lower cash costs at its core East zone concession, which will most likely be clawed back in the next rate review.

The loan books of commercial banks grew 6.5% YoY in Sept, a further deceleration from the 7.2% in Aug. The one key segment that’s accelerating is consumer lending, which is up +23.5%.

+++

Malacañang Palace announced the appointment of Rafael Consing, Jr as the president and CEO of the newly formed sovereign investment  firm, Maharlika Investment Corp (MIC). For those who might recall, Consing was the CFO of port operator ICTSI (ICT PM) until recently. Before that, he was affiliated with the Aboitiz (AEV PM) group and was also a banker with a number of global financial institutions.

MACRO
Commercial banks loan growth slows to 6.5% in September; M3 growth picks up to 7.9%
  • Preliminary data showed loans of commercial banks rose by 6.5% in September, slowing from 7.2% in August
    • Loans to productive activities slowed to 4.9% from 5.5%
    • Consumer loans mildly picked up, rising by 23.5% in September from 23.1% in August
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) grew by 7.9% in September, accelerating from 6.8% in August

 

MACRO
Rafael Consing Jr. named Maharlika PCEO
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. appointed Rafael Consing Jr. as the President and CEO (PCEO) of the Maharlika Investment Corporation (MIC)
  • Consing, who is the executive director of the executive director of the Office of the Presidential Adviser for Investment & Economic Affairs, will now supervise the operations and internal administration of the MIC
  • In the revised IRR, the president has the power to accept or reject nominees for director positions and PCEO shortlisted by the advisory body
    • The revised IRR also stripped the requirement of the PCEO of having additional educational requirements
TRANSPORT
Cebu Air – 9M23 core profit and EBITDA trending ahead of estimates on higher fares
  • Excluding one-off gains, FX adjustments, and adding back interest expenses on CBs, we estimate core income to common stood at P1.82bn in 3Q23, up 8% QoQ.
    • 9M23 core profit of P4.85bn exceeded our full-year estimates due to (a) higher-than-expected fares and (b) the absence of fuel hedging losses
  • Revenues inched up by 3% QoQ to P23.35bn in 3Q23, as pax fares (excluding ancillary) jumped 5% QoQ, offsetting the 4% sequential dip in pax volumes to 5.2mn
    • Pax fares of P3k are higher by 26% YoY and are 11% higher than 2019 average
    • Ancillary revenues also rose 7% QoQ, with ancillary income per pax now at P1.2k, 57% above 2019 levels
  • EBITDA in 3Q23 was up 3% QoQ to P5.65bn, taking 9M23 EBITDA to P15.24bn
    • Given that 4Q is typically a strong quarter, we reckon CEB could beat our full-year EBITDA forecast (P20.77bn) and consensus (P18.72bn)
  • Management is holding a briefing at 2PM – Registration link

 

CONSUMER
EMI 9M23 -6%YoY to P6.8bn – behind
  • In press release, Emperador Inc (EMI PM) disclosed that net income for 9M23 amounted to P6.8bn (-6%YoY), falling short of estimates for FY23E. In 3Q23 alone, net income was P2.1bn (-15%QoQ, 5%YoY) – its lowest quarterly print for the year with a net margin of 12.9%, but just mildly higher vs last year.
  • 9M23 revenues increased 10%YoY to P47.1bn, which implies revenue for 3Q posted a modest growth of 3%QoQ and 10%YoY.
  • It said that its international business “continued to experience double-digit growth” due to strong single malt sales in Asia and North America.
  • Profits from whisky increased 8%YoY to P1.2bn, bringing 9M23 segment profit 16% higher to P3.9bn
  • Income from brandy was flat at P867mn (1%YoY), with 9M23 profit of the segment down 25%YoY to P2.8bn
    • Based on competitor data, Emperador’s volume share as of the domestic spirits industry has fallen to 17% - its lowest in more than a decade.

 

PROPERTY
Filinvest Land 9M23 profit rises 22%, in line
  • Filinvest Land’s 9M23 earnings grew 22% to P2.4bn, in line with REGIS forecast (ahead of consensus)
  • Although residential revenue is trailing our forecast, residential GPM is stronger than we expected
    • 3Q23 residential GPM expanded 3ppt QoQ and 6ppt YoY to 45.9%
  • Office revenue rose 3% QoQ, potentially indicating that office occupancy improved (from 60% in 2Q23)
  • The company opened its first dorm facility in 3Q23 (located in Clark)
  • Filinvest will hold its earnings briefing on November 15
  • FLI is currently trading at 74% discount to NAV, 0.1X FY24E P/B, and 4.0X FY24E P/E
