REGIS REPORT

01 March 2023

We have 4Q/FY22 results on three names today. While all of them delivered net profits better than estimates, the quality of the outperformance certainly varied.

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (+) – The group reported record profits of P61.7bn for FY22 (+53% YoY), lifted by contributions from banking and retail. The acquisition of the geothermal operations helped to pad earnings. Also worth highlighting is the fact that SM Retail, too, generated record profit of P17.9bn, much higher (>40%) than 2019 despite revenues being only 3% more.  Have a look at additional comments and detail below.
  • Manila Water (MWC PM) (+) – Headline profit to common jumped 65% YoY to P5.17bn, boosted by one-offs. Underlying operations of its investments in the Philippines were stronger than anticipated but this was offset by the poor performance of its overseas investments. Notably, capex for the East zone reached a record of P21bn in 2022.

  • Semirara (SCC PM) (+) – Core income of P39.01bn (+147% YoY) in FY22 outperformed, buoyed by lower than expected non-cash expenses. EBITDA of P47.65bn fell short of our forecast (P49.08bn), as coal mining ASPs disappointed. While underlying operations were a tad softer than anticipated, SCC still managed to end 2022 with a better-than-expected cash level of P20bn (P5/sh), suggesting a chunky dividend could be announced in April.

CONGLOS
SM Investments 4Q22 results comment
  • Net income rises 53% to P61.7bn.  SM Investments reported net income of P61.7bn for FY22, which is 53% above the P40.4bn profit (restated) for FY21.  The results are ahead of Street estimates, with record earnings from the banking as well as retail businesses, a rapidly recovering property segment as well as the retroactive inclusion of the geothermal production operation.
  • Banking and property deliver.  BDO reported record earnings of P57bn for FY22, +33% YoY, ahead of Street expectations. While loan growth was a tad short of estimates (+9% YoY), net interest margins expanded.  Trading gains also contributed to PPOP. Meanwhile. SM Prime’s FY22 net profit rose 38% to P30bn, as mall operations rebounded to near pre-pandemic levels.
  • SM Retail also produces record profit.  Net profit for SM Retail reached P17.9bn.  That profit level is a record and is over 40% higher than 2019 despite revenue being only 3% higher.  Management attributed the improved margins to efficiency improvements through headcount reduction and right-sizing of their retail space.  The improved margins / profitability is most evident with the department store segment (“SM Store”).
  • Portfolio investments contributed 11% of earnings. SM says “portfolio investments” accounted for 11% of FY22 profits.  The biggest contributor here was geothermal field operator PGPC, which provided P4bn of income for the year.  PGPC was acquired mid-2022 but SM’s results were restated to retroactively include PGPC to 2021.
  • Alfamart now at >1400 stores.  Alfamart added 205 stores during 2022 and now has 1412 outlets.  Without giving specific numbers, management said this operation is now profitable with net margin of around 1%.
  • Tender offer for 2GO.  SM also announced that it intends to launch a tender offer for the 15% public float of shipping and logistics firm 2Go (2GO PM). 2Go reported a profit of P312m for FY22, from a net loss the prior year.  SM will engage a 3rd party to come up with a fair price for the tender offer. SM’s disclosure of its intention at this time is curious, as usually a tender offer is launched with a specific price already set. 2GO’s latest market cap is only P19bn.
BANKS
Commercial bank lending grows 10.4% YoY in January
  • U/KB loan growth (net of RRP) moderated to 10.4% in January
    • While this appears to have moderated sharply from 13.7% in Dec 2022, data on U/KB loans from Jan 2022-Dec 2022 were distorted by the merger of BPI/BPI Family Savings
    • Recall that loan growth for the entire banking system rose by 11.4% YoY in Dec 2022
    • We are forecasting loans to grow by 12-15% for banks under our coverage in 2022E-24E
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) growth slowed to 5.5% in January from 6.7% in December

PROPERTY
IT-BPM sector meets 2022 targets
  • The IT-BPO sector’s 2022 revenue grew 10% to US$32.5bn while its employment rose 8% to 1.57mn 
  • These are in line with the association’s 2022 targets
  • The BPO sector is one of the major drivers of office demand
    • However, we expect continued implementation of work-from-home policies to keep office demand below pre-COVID levels
    • We forecast Metro Manila office vacancy to rise to 20% in 2023E (from 18% currently)
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 51% of Filinvest Land’s NAV
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV
    • 30% of Robinsons Land’s NAV
  • FLI is trading at 62% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY23E P/B, and 6.1X FY23E P/E
  • MEG is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY23E P/B, and 4.1X FY23E P/E
  • RLC is trading at 61% discount to NAV, 0.6X FY22E P/B, and 8.2X FY23E P/E
