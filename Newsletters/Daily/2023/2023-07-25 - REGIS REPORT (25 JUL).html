REGIS REPORT

25 July 2023

President Marcos delivered his second State of the Nation Address (SONA) yesterday following the resumption of Congress’ session. Like his previous SONA (and unlike his predecessor’s), yesterday’s speech outlined quite a few legislative priorities the current administration wants to pursue over the course of Marcos’ term.

Among the most notable involve (1) the creation of a Dept of Water Resources, (2) taxes on single use plastics, (3) VAT on digital services, (4) reform of the pension system for uniformed personnel, which has unfunded liabilities of ~P10trn, (5) reform of the immigration system, and (6) the rationalization of taxes on mining. Notable by its absence was the proposal by the Dept of Finance to tax “junk” food and raise taxes on sweetened beverages.

House Speaker Martin Romualdez separately mentioned other legislative proposal not specifically mentioned by Marcos. These include (1) amendments to the BOT Law, (2) reform of financial markets taxes, (3) valuation reform for real property, (4) amendments to the legal framework of the power reform and (5) changes to the Bank Secrecy Law.

Outside of legislative priorities, Marcos discussed plans to expand road and bridge infrastructure to connect the country. Very interestingly, he also brought up the power sector, talking about the push for renewable energy and the issues with the power grid operator National Grid, which is controlled by Synergy Grid (SGP PM).

+++

Puregold (PGOLD PM) is the first among the consumer names to report results this quarter. The company saw net sales rise by 11% in 1H, with net profit up by 5% to P4.4bn. We view the bottom-line figure as being on track to meet our full-year estimate of P10bn. Margin compression in 2Q is noticeable, following the big annual sales event for its Puregold customers and the competitive pressure being addressed by S&R.

MACRO
Highlights of 2023 SONA
  • Achievements in first year of administration
    • Agrarian Emancipation Act – condonation of P57bn worth of farmers’ debts
    • Creation of Water Resources Management Office through an EO whilst coordinating with Congress to pass a law forming a Department of Water Resource Management
  • Laws to be passed for the next five years
    • Enactment of a new Government Procurement Law and new Government Auditing Code
    • Excise tax on single-use plastics
    • VAT on digital services
    • Rationalization of mining fiscal regime
    • Motor vehicle user’s charge/road user’s tax
    • Military and Uniformed personnel pension
    • Philippine Immigration Act
    • Amendment of the Anti-Agricultural Smuggling Act
  • Boost local agricultural production via consolidation, modernization, mechanization, and improvement of value chains
    • Called to Fisheries code to incorporate and strengthen science-based analysis and determination of fishing areas
    • Amend Cooperative code to speed up formation of cooperatives, which today covers 300 clusters consisting of 900 cooperatives with 200k hectares
  • Continue Build-Better-More program, which covers 194 infrastructure projects worth P8.3tn
    • Infrastructure spending will stay at 5-6% of GDP
    • Road network plans to connect 3 major islands: 1200-km Luzon Spine Expressway Network program to cut travel time from Ilocos to Bicol from 20 hours to 9hours
    • Mega-bridge program: 12 bridges totaling 90km: Bataan-Cavite bridge; Panay-Guimaras-Negros Island bridges; Samal Island-Davao City Connector bridge
    • Maharlika Fund established for strategic financing without added debt burden
  • Emphasized renewable energy targets: 35% share in power mix by 2030 and then 50% by 2040
    • 126 renewable energy contracts with potential capacity of 31,000 MW have been awarded
    • Renewal of Malampaya contract guarantees continued revenues and energy production for another 15 years
  • Conduct performance review of National Grid Corporation of the Philippines
    • 68 grid connections are much delayed
    • Wants deliverables completed: Mindanao-Visayas and Cebu-Negros-Panay interconnections

RETAIL
PGOLD 1H23 NPAT +5% YoY to P4.4bn – in line
  • PGOLD’s net profit rose 5% YoY to P4.4bn in 1H23, up 5% YoY – this is trending broadly in line with expectations
  • Net sales +11% YoY, though op profit is flat at P6.7bn
  • For 2Q23 alone, net sales rose 7% YoY as SSSG slowed to low-single digit by our estimate. The YoY deceleration is largely driven by base effects; we estimate SSSG was flattish QoQ.
  • Operating profit during the quarter was down 7% YoY, mainly driven by lower GPM
    • The GPM contraction is expected given seasonality due to Puregold’s Tindahan ni Aling Puring convention, which was held in May 2023
    • S&R also likely saw GPM contraction, partly driven by more competitive pricing
  • PGOLD currently trades at 2024E multiples of 8x PE and 4.4x EV/EBITDA with a dividend yield of 3.5%

Megaworld set to open ‘biggest’ hotel by year end
  • MEGAWORLD Corp. plans to open its 1,500-room hotel in Parañaque City by the end of the year.
    • The hotel, named Grand Westside Hotel, will become the largest hotel property in Megaworld's portfolio and in the entire country
    • Will be the 19th hotel property launched by Megaworld Hotels & Resorts, with 12 currently operational and seven more in the pipeline
  • Located within the 31-hectare Westside City township in Entertainment City, Parañaque, the 19-storey property will be the biggest hotel development in the Philippines in terms of total room keys
    • The first tower of the hotel will open this year, and the second tower is scheduled for 2024.
    • Megaworld has invested around P64 billion to build Westside City, where the hotel is situated.
  • Hotel details:
    • Various unit types, including queen suites, twin suites, and executive suites, with specific options for specially-abled guests
    • Two levels of retail and commercial space, as well as amenities such as pools, a gazebo, a zen garden, function rooms, a gym, and massage rooms.
    • Four dining outlets, including the Grand Westside Café.
  • Megaworld’s hotel portfolio accounts for about 2% of operating profit
  • It currently operates about 5,000 hotel rooms (including condotels)
  • MEG is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY23E P/B, and 4.0X FY23E P/E
