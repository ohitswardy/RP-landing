+++

Vehicle sales slowed unexpectedly in November, up just 7.6% YoY – the slowest YoY pace since early 2022 – with the monthly sales down 1% MoM to 37,683 units. The slowdown is surprising, considering bank data shows auto loans were still growing in the mid-teens into October. One caveat though – the CAMPI data does not include many Chinese car brands (only Geely and Chery are counted). For the 11M23, vehicle sales were up 24% YoY.

Toyota bucked the trend, though, gaining market share by growing 11% YoY and 1% MoM. We maintain our Buy call on GT Capital (GTCAP PM), which trades at just 5.1x PER on our 2024E estimates.

+ + +

ICTSI (ICT PM) was the lone bidder for the management of the Iloilo Commercial Port Complex (ICPC). However, it was still declared a “failed” bid, as there were some missing clerical requirements. The company has until tomorrow (21 December) to ask for a reconsideration of their bid. The 25-year contract limits the port to just foreign cargo for the first 5 years. ICTSI already operates 9 ports domestically; if it is awarded the Iloilo bit, it will be ICT’s first in the Visayas. The investments required to upgrade the facilities were not disclosed.

+ + +

The country’s BOP swung into a small deficit of $216mn in November, following the $1.5bn surplus recorded in October. This takes the 11M23 BOP to a surplus of $3.0bn

PROPERTY
Lance Gokongwei to take over as RLC President and CEO
  • Lance Gokongwei will replace Frederick Go as President and CEO of Robinsons Land beginning 8 January 2024
  • Frederick Go was appointed as Special Assistant to the President for Investment and Economic Affairs (SAPIEA)
  • RLC is trading at 62% discount to NAV, 0.5X FY24E P/B, and 7.1X FY24E P/E

 

AUTOS
Vehicle sales growth slows to 7.6% YoY in November
  • CAMPI reported vehicle sales for the month of November stood at 37,683 units, up 7.6% YoY but down 1.2% MoM
    • This was the weakest sales growth in 21 months or since the 7.3% contraction recorded in February 2022
    • Cited slowdown in consumer spending in recent months, reflecting the impact of soaring interest rates
    • Brings YT-November industry sales to 390,654 units, up 24% YoY
  • Toyota gained market share for the second straight month, selling 18,251 units in November, up 11% YoY and 1% MoM
    • Cumulative 11M23 sales are up 15% YoY to 180,480 units, ahead of Regis FY23E of 192k (+10% YoY)
  • Toyota is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 28% of profit and gross asset value (37% of NAV)
  • GTCAP is trading at a 48% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,082/sh and 5.5x/5.1x FY23E/FY24E PER

 

 

MACRO
Balance of payments swings to US$216mn deficit in November
  • Preliminary data showed the country’s balance of payments swung to a deficit of US$216mn in November after recording a US$1.5bn surplus in October
    • Recall October BOP surplus was boosted by the government’s issuance of US$1.24bn global bonds
    • BOP deficit in November, however, is lower than the US$756mn deficit in November 2022
  • Brings 11M23 BOP position to surplus of US$3.0bn, a reversal of the US$7.9bn deficit in the same period a year ago
    • BSP is forecasting a BOP surplus of US$1.1bn by year-end, a reversal of the US$7.3bn BOP deficit in 2022

 

TRANSPORT
ICTSI submits lone bid for Iloilo port but falls short of requirements
  • ICTSI emerged as the sole bidder for the 25-year O&M contract of the Iloilo Commercial Port Complex (ICPC)
  • Its bid was, however, declared “failed” due to the lack of a notarized Secretary’s Certificate or any document showing proof of authorization
    • Other eligibility criteria, including technical and financial requirements, were met
    • It may seek for reconsideration until December 21
  • 25-year contract involves the exclusive handling of foreign containers for the first five years with an undisclosed investment requirement to improve the port’s facilities
    • ICPC currently only handles domestic containers but will become one of two foreign container ports in the Visayas region once ICT takes over
    • Iloilo is part of the Western Visayas region, which in turn accounts for 5% of the country’s GDP
  • ICTSI currently operates 32 ports in 19 countries. In the Philippines alone, ICTSI owns and operates 9 ports
    • The Iloilo port will be the company’s first port in the Visayas region
    • ICTSI trades at 7.9x FY24E EV/EBITDA and 12x FY24E PER, below peers (10x/15x) and historical average (11x/23x)
