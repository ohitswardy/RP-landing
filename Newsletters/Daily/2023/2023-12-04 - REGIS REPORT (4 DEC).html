REGIS REPORT

04 December 2023

The government is allowing the accreditation of a number of economic zones within Metro Manila to proceed, even after the Duterte period order issued stopping such. Under the new government ruling, PEZA applications that were already in process but were halted because of the Duterte order will now be allowed to re-submit their applications.

We estimate that around 5% of Ayala Land’s (ALI PM) and 10% of Filinvest Land’s (FLI PM) office portfolio will benefit from this change. PEZA accreditations confer an advantage since they come with fiscal incentives that are accessible through an administratively simpler process (vs. BOI). The office property market is currently laboring under high vacancy rates (close to 20%) and any advantage would be welcome.

Meralco (MER PM) announced it has paid an initial P7bn to SPNEC (SPNEC PM) as down payment for the subscription of new SPNEC shares. After closing, MER will have 31.4% economic stake and 50.5% voting interest in SPNEC. The parties do not expect that MER will need to undertake a tender offer.

MACRO
BSP forecasting November inflation to settle at 4.0-4.8%
  • The BSP projects November 2023 inflation to settle within the range of 4.0 to 4.8%
    • Consensus forecast at 4.4%
    • Slower than in October (+4.9% YoY, -0.2% MoM)
    • BSP forecast implies MoM change in headline CPI will be greater than or equal to +0.05%
  • Forecast incorporates higher prices for most agricultural commodities (i.e. rice, fruit, fish, and meat), as well as electricity, LPG, and toll rates
    • To be partially offset by lower prices of vegetables and petroleum products, along with the appreciation of the PHP against the USD
  • Government set to release inflation for November on Dec 5
POWER
Meralco subscribes to new shares of SPNEC
  • Meralco subsidiary MGen Renewable Energy Inc. (MGreen) has signed a subscription agreement to acquire 15.7bn of solar power developer SPNEC’s (SPNEC PM) common shares and 19.4bn preferred shares
    • The agreement is in line with a previous announcement of Meralco taking over control of SPNEC
    • MGreen paid an initial amount of P7bn on Nov 30 and will pay another P8.9bn on the closing date
    • Closing date is likely to at least mid-2024, if not later
  • Upon closing, MGreen will have a total voting interest of 50.5% in SPNEC
    • Meanwhile, the 15.7bn common shares is equivalent to a 31.4% economic stake in SPNEC’s enlarged outstanding shares
    • The parties do not expect that a tender offer will be necessary
PROPERTY
Government to allow approvals of new Metro Manila ecozones
  • The Marcos administration amended provisions of the previous administration’s Administrative Order (AO) 18, which halted the processing of new ecozones in Metro Manila
  • The new order states that those who obtained “Pre-qualification clearances” before AO 18 was implemented in 2019 will be allowed to resubmit their application with certain conditions
  • In line with this, PEZA will submit a list of applicants with the said clearances to the Office of the President
    • We estimate that nearly 5% of Ayala Land’s office stock and 10% of Filinvest Land’s office portfolio could be affected by the revisions  
  • BPO firms still have a preference for PEZA-accredited space
  • We estimate that about half of the 2.5mn sqm of vacant Metro Manila office space is PEZA-accredited
    • We estimate there is sufficient PEZA-accredited office space for about three years of take-up from BPO firms
  • Thus, we believe the revision to AO 18 is unlikely to have a near-term impact on the office market
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 49% of Filinvest Land’s NAV
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV
    • 28% of Robinsons Land’s NAV
  • FLI is trading at 74% discount to NAV, 0.1X FY24E P/B, and 4.0X FY24E P/E
  • MEG is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY23E P/B, and 3.7X FY24E P/E
  • RLC is trading at 64% discount to NAV, 0.5X FY24E P/B, and 6.6X FY24E P/E
