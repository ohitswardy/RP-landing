10 March 2023

We have a new consumer sector report out, covering F&B manufacturers Century Pacific (CNPF PM), Monde Nissin (MONDE PM), and Universal Robina (URC PM). We adjust our 2023-24E EPS estimates by 5-9% and our target prices by as much as 19%. Our BUY ratings on CNPF and URC have, however, been maintained, as has our HOLD rating on MONDE.

For the consumer food manufacturers, margin recovery is on the horizon and should provide some much-needed relief. The past 12-18 months have been a balancing act for them – from navigating cost headwinds to struggling to keep up with the demand surge following the post-lockdown reopening. The tides are now shifting, with demand now settling down and cost pressures abating. Consequently, we expect earnings growth over the medium term to be driven by margin recovery as (1) soft commodity prices moderate from 2022 levels, and (2) upward price adjustments (>10%) over the past year take full effect. Even with revenge spend subsiding, demand continues to be surprisingly healthy despite the impact of high inflation on consumer wallets, highlighting the defensiveness of the sector.

URC is our top pick in the space, which we expect will see the fastest 2022-24E EPS CAGR (+20%) among the three companies. At 22x 2023E PE, the stock trades below its historical average of 29x.

The FY22/4Q results ACEN (ACEN PM) were a disappointment with recurring profit plunging in the final quarter. Excluding the net impact of P8.6bn in one-time adjustments, we estimate that ACEN’s 4Q22 fell to just P334mn (-83%QoQ, -66%YoY). This took ACEN’s FY22 net income to P4.455 (-15%), coming in well under consensus and Regis estimates for the year. Its short spot exposure dragged profits once again, as SLTEC went on outage in the quarter and it had to buy expensive replacement power. For the year, the negative impact arising from its short spot exposure and the mismatch between fuel cost and tariffs amounted to P2.4bn.

Meanwhile San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) reported a consolidated income of P34.7bn for FY22, in line with estimates, after 4Q22 consolidated profit improved to P8.4bn (10%QoQ, 15%YoY). EBIT from both gin and food segments improved sequentially as margins held steady. Operating income for beer, on the other hand, dipped 3% despite the revenue surge, as margins dipped 2.6ppts in the quarter.

MACRO
Labor force participation deteriorates in Jan 2023 vs Dec 2022
  • Preliminary Labor Force Survey data for January 2023 showed a MoM decline in the labor force participate rate to 64.5% from 66.4% in December
    • It is unclear if this is due to seasonality, given that the government only began providing monthly data beginning Jan 2021
    • On a YoY basis, 3.5mn more people entered the labor force, with the labor force participation rate improving from 60.5% in Jan 2022
  • The number of employed decreased by 1.65mn MoM while the number of unemployed increased by 153k MoM
    • Employment across sectors dropped MoM, with the agri sector alone losing 1.2mn MoM

INFRASTRUCTURE
Marcos admin bares P9tn flagship infrastructure projects
  • The National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) announced that it has approved a new list of Infrastructure Flagship Projects (IFP), which includes 194 projects worth P9tn
    • Of which 45 projects will be financed through public-private partnerships (PPPs)
    • Majority will prioritize physical connectivity and water resources
    • As of today, NEDA has not uploaded the list in its website
  • Recall that close to the end of President Duterte’s term, its IFP still included 112 projects worth P4.69tn
    • Of which only 12 projects were funded under “solicited” PPPs (P355bn) while 20 projects were “unsolicited” PPPs (1.50tn)
CONSUMER
FB reports a consolidated income of P34.7bn in FY22 (+10%YoY)
  • In a press release, San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) said that its consolidated net income for FY22 grew 10%YoY to P34.7bn
  • Against our own estimate for consolidated income (P34.6bn), the result appears in line
  • The result implies 4Q22 consolidated income grew to P8.353bn (+10%QoQ, +15%YoY) on back of improved contributions from food and gin
  • Among the things we can derive from the press release include –
    • Beer EBIT dipped 3%QoQ and -17%YoY on lower margins (19.5% in 4Q22 vs 22.1% in 3Q22, 25.5% in 4Q21)
    • Gin EBIT increased 8%QoQ and 17%YoY to P1.4bn; margins were stable at 11.1%
    • Food EBITDA grew to P2.488bn (13%QoQ, 161%YoY); margins were steady QoQ at 5.3%
  • FB is trading at 15.1x PE on FY23E
