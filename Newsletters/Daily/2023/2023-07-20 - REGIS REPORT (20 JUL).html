REGIS REPORT

20 July 2023

In a press release, BPI (BPI PM) reported NPAT of P25.1bn for 1H23, up 23% YoY, bang in line with REGIS full-year forecast of P50.5bn, but well ahead of consensus (P48.4bn). For 2Q23 alone, profit was P13.0bn, up 4% YoY, though recall that 2Q22 included the chunky sale of a land asset (P5bn gross gain, P3bn net gain). Excluding that gain, NPAT would have been up around 44% YoY in 1H23 and 38% in 2Q23.

Loan growth slowed to 10.7% YoY (from 13.6% in 1Q23), as the bank lapped some big-ticket loans booked in 2Q/3Q last year (telco tower sales). This was more than made up for by a 56bps YoY expansion in NIMs, by management’s estimate, in 1H23. Corporate loans were up just 8%, with much faster growth in higher-margin auto loans (+20%) and credit cards (+43%). Other income dropped 15% YoY in 1H23, but excluding the property sale, would have been up 16% YoY.  Opex jumped 21% YoY due to continued IT spend, as well as salary hikes. Asset quality deteriorated slightly, with NPLs ticking up 6bps QoQ to 1.88%, and NPL coverage down to 167% from 177% in 1Q23, as provision expense fell 60% to just P2bn in 1H23. This is in line with the bank’s guidance of gradually bringing NPL coverage.

Overall, a solid set of results. We expect consensus to upgrade closer to (or above) our current estimates. Maintain Buy. The bank will host an earnings call on Monday, 25 July.

The board of the National Economic Development Authority (NEDA), which is chaired by the President, approved the plans to rehabilitate Manila’s airport NAIA through solicited bid. This effectively rejects the Manila International Airport Consortium’s (MIAC) unsolicited proposal to upgrade the airport and operate it for 25 years.

The NEDA-approved bidding for the NAIA rehabilitation will involve a 15-year concession, extendable by another 10-year period. NAIA has been operating at (or even above) its stated capacity for many years and a rehabilitation / upgrade has long been planned (and overdue). Due to financial constraints, the government is pursuing this endeavor via public private partnership (PPP).

Apart from the NAIA PPP plan, the NEDA board also approved a major road project in eastern Visayas and, interestingly, an unsolicited proposal from Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM) to upgrade the Laguindingan Airport outside Cagayan de Oro in northern Mindanao.

SM Prime (SMPH PM) will defer the planned IPO of its REIT to early next year, according to the president of the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE). The deferral is likely due to expectations of an environment of lower interest rates by then. REIT valuations are naturally affected by prevailing interest rates.

INFRASTRUCTURE
NEDA Board approves P170.6bn solicited NAIA PPP project, 2 other infrastructure projects
  • The National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) approved the P170.6bn solicited proposal to privatize the operations of the Ninoy Aquino International Airport (NAIA)
    • The project was proposed by the Department of Transportation and the Manila International Airport Authority
    • The winning bidder will be able to operate NAIA for a period of 15 years, with an option to extend by another 10 years in the event that construction for the Bulacan airport and Sangley airports are delayed
    • Project aim to increase the pax volume capacity of the airport to 62mn pax (from 35mn today) and boost air traffic movements to 48 per hour (from 40 today)
  • The NEDA Board also rejected the P267bn unsolicited proposal of the Manila International Airport Consortium (MIAC)
    • MIAC proposed to pay an upfront payment of P57bn and capex of P210bn over a 25-year concession period
    • Capex proposal aimed to increase the capacity of the airport to 62.5mn by 2028 and 70mn by 2048
  • The government believes project implementation under the solicited route will be faster
    • It aims to publish the bid documents by August and auction the project before year-end
  • The NEDA Board also approved two other infrastructure projects:
    • P7.48bn Samar Pacific Coastal Road 2 Project
    • P12.75bn unsolicited proposal for the upgrade, expansion, and O&M of the Laguindingan airport (Proponent: Aboitiz InfraCapital, a unit of Aboitiz Equity Ventures, AEV PM)
PROPERTY
SM Prime defers REIT IPO to 2024
  • PSE President Ramon Monzon said that SM Prime deferred its planned REIT IPO to 2024
    • There were reports that the IPO could take place in 4Q23
    • We suspect that SM Prime is deferring the IPO in order to take advantage of lower interest rates next year
  • SM Prime plans to raise up to US$1bn from the IPO of a mall REIT
    • This would imply a market cap of up to US$3bn
  • The Philippine Daily Inquirer previously reported that the REIT would hold 12-15 Philippine malls 
  • A 1ppt reduction in our mall cap rate (currently 8%) would raise our SMPH NAV (currently P48.27/share) by 7%
  • SMPH is currently trading at 32% discount to NAV, 2.4X FY23E P/B, and 25.4X FY23E P/E
TELCOS
PLDT gets NTC nod for two cable landing stations
  • PLDT Inc. received provisional authority from the National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) to construct two new cable stations.
  • The two stations will host the Apricot cable system, an intra-Asia submarine cable facing the Pacific side of the Philippines.
    • The new cable landing stations will be in Baler, Aurora, and Digos, Davao del Sur.
    • The Apricot cable system will provide telecommunication companies with alternate routes that avoid the usual West Philippine Sea waters.
    • New facilities and cable systems are expected to be completed by 2025 and will connect the Philippines to Japan, Singapore, Indonesia, Taiwan, and Guam.
  • PLDT's international capacity will increase to over 140 terabytes per second with the fully equipped cable system.
    • Improving internet speeds, lower latency, and add resiliency to existing landing stations in Batangas, La Union, and Camarines Norte.
