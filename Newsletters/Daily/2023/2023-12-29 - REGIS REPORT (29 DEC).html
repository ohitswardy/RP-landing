REGIS REPORT

29 December 2023

The government's budget deficit fell 25% to P93bn in November, largely because of the 10% YoY drop in primary spending. The good news is that there is typically a seasonal spike in primary spending in December. However, even if the government achieves its full-year target, this would still imply a 9% YoY drop in 4Q23 primary spending. For reference, in 2Q23, when primary spending "only" fell 1% YoY, GDP growth was a disappointing 4.3%.

 

+++

Perhaps the most interesting revelation from the FY23 presentation of Leechiu Property Consultants is the spike in POGO office demand. In addition to the office space leased, we would expect the POGO firms to bring more foreign employees into the country, leading to stronger residential demand in the Metro Manila business districts, particularly the Manila Bay area. 

Key beneficiaries of the POGO sector include: SM Prime (SMPH PM), Megaworld (MEG PM), and Filinvest Land (FLI PM).

MACRO
Budget deficit narrows 25% YoY to P93bn in November as primary spending falls
  • The government recorded a budget deficit of P93bn in November, down 25% YoY
    • Due largely to a 10% drop in primary spending
  • Revenues rose 2.8% YoY on higher dividend remittances and government share from PAGCOR
    • Tax revenues dropped 9% YoY due to shift in the deadline for VAT collections (i.e. from monthly to quarterly beginning Jan 2023)
  • Expenses fell 4.7% YoY due to a 10% YoY drop in primary spending
  • Brings 11M23 budget deficit to P1.11tn, down 10% YoY

MACRO
BIR slaps 1% withholding tax on online merchants
  • The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) has officially imposed a 1% creditable withholding tax on one-half of the gross remittances of online platform providers to their partner sellers or merchants
    • Withholding tax imposed, however, will only apply to online merchants and online sellers with cumulative gross remittances of more than P500,000 for the past taxable year
    • Tax will apply to online shopping, food delivery platforms, platforms for booking of resort, hotel, motel, inn, house, condominium unit, bed space, room for rent and other similar lodging accommodations, and other service or product marketplaces
    • BIR said the withholding tax imposed shall be in addition to the existing withholding tax obligations being imposed on e-marketplace operators
  • Last year, the digital economy contributed P2.08tn, equivalent to 9.4% of GDP
    • Of this, e-commerce had the highest growth at 26.5%, with its share to the economy reaching 20% or P416.12bn
    • Data showed that there are roughly two million entities involved in online selling as of last year

PROPERTY
LPC: POGO office demand rebounds in 4Q23 but BPO office take-up drops
  • During its 4Q23 briefing, Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) revealed that Metro Manila office vacancy fell 1ppt QoQ to 17%
    • We estimate that rental rates in most parts of Metro Manila are still 20%-50% below pre-COVID levels      
  • Metro Manila net office take-up was about 425,000sqm, up 50% YoY but behind our FY23E forecast of 450,000sqm
    • Prior to the POGO industry entering the country, Metro Manila annual office take-up was 470,000sqm to 530,000sqm
  • Nationwide net office take-up rose 22% QoQ to 157,000sqm
    • POGO office demand picked up but BPO office take-up dropped to its lowest level since 1Q22
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 49% of Filinvest Land’s NAV - FLI is trading at 73% discount to NAV, 0.1X FY24E P/B, and 4.0X FY24E P/E
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV - MEG is trading at 69% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY24E P/B, and 3.6X FY24E P/E
    • 28% of Robinsons Land’s NAV - RLC is trading at 61% discount to NAV, 0.5X FY23E P/B, and 7.2X FY24E P/E
  • LPC also said that secondary market prices of high-end Makati and Fort Bonifcacio condominium prices generally rose 5-15% YoY
  • ALI is trading at 33% discount to NAV, 1.8X FY24E P/B, and 18.7X FY24E P/E
