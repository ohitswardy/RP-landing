REGIS REPORT

20 September 2023

In this new report, we reiterate our Buy rating on Jollibee Foods (JFC PM) on our continued optimism about the firm’s growth prospects. While the company should see near term volatility from input cost inflation and other macro headwinds, we believe a sharper focus on its four core segments, a more tempered appetite for acquisitions, and improving profitability of international operations should continue to support margins. Regis consumer analyst Carissa (carissa.mangubat@regis.ph) forecasts 15% 2023-25E EPS CAGR on the back of continued store expansion and healthy SSSG. Her TP of P300/sh implies 2024E multiples of 30x PE and 12x EV/EBITDA, a discount to the stock’s own historical average.

Following the disclosures that Aboitiz Power (AP PM) and one of its directors bought AP shares in the market, it looks very likely that the company’s public float is now below 20%. Twenty percent is the minimum threshold for any stock to be part of the PSEi (“PCOMP”) so this development will almost certainly mean AP will be dropped from it.

Meanwhile, Metro Pacific (MPI PM) today disclosed that, following the tender offer deadline, the total non-public shares would constitute 97.2% of the company. Even before today’s announcement, it was already a foregone conclusion that the consortium proposing to take MPI private would have enough shares to delist it. The extension of the tender offer period allowed some more minority shareholders to sell their shares (just under 1% of total) through the Exchange rather than outside it.

The above developments mean that there would an impending replacement of two of the PSEi constituents. Based on the latest available data, we believe the two likeliest candidates to become part of the PSEi would be Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) and Century Pacific (CNPF PM). We should expect an announcement from the PSE in a few days.

+++

GT Capital (GTCAP PM) disclosed it was redeeming P4.8bn worth of its 4.63% preferred shares. This is indicative of the continued improvement of its cash flows, which is enabling it to retire debt without needing to refinance.

Finally, Ayala Corp (AC PM) says the 96% of the dividend shares of ACEN (ACEN PM) that it declared as property dividend last year would have been distributed already by tomorrow. The distribution of this property dividend has proven to be a long tortuous process for investors. Many have had to wait over a year for the property dividend of ACEN shares to be received.

Consortium acquires 97.2% of MPI shares
  • At the end of the tender offer period on September 19, a total of 5.46bn Metro Pacific Investments (MPI PM) shares were tendered to the consortium composed of Metro Pacific Holdings, GT Capital Holdings (GTCAP PM), Mit-Pacific Infrastructure Holdings Corp, and MIG Holdings Incorporated
    • This represents 19.04% of MPI shares outstanding 
  • The shares will be crossed on September 26
    • Settlement date on September 28
  • The consortium will hold 97.22% of MPI common shares outstanding, above the 95% threshold for voluntary delisting
  • The consortium anticipates the voluntary delisting of MPI around October 2023
  • MPI share trading will be suspended from September 26, upon crossing of the tendered shares
  • We expect BLOOM to replace MPI in the PSE Index on September 26, the cross date
CONGLOS
GTCAP to redeem series “A” perpetual prefs
  • GT Capital (GTCAP PM) will redeem P4.8bn worth of series “A” perpetual prefs, which has a dividend rate of 4.6299%
    • The perpetual pref’s redemption date is on October 27
  • Our profit/cash flow forecasts assume debt payments will not be refinanced
    • Earlier this year, GTCAP also paid P6bn worth of bonds that matured
  • As of end-June 2023, GTCAP’s parent-level gross debt (incl. prefs) stood at P86.4bn and cash was at P17.6bn
    • Net debt-to-equity ratio at the parent stood at a record low level of 51%
  • GTCAP is trading at a 49% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,103/sh and at 5.6x/5.1x FY23E/FY24E PER
POWER
AP buyback disclosures suggest public float is now below 20%
  • Aboitiz Power disclosed to the exchange yesterday that it bought back a total of 2.5075mn AP shares from Sep 15 – 18
  • It also disclosed that director Danel Aboitiz bought 1.37mn shares on September 15
  • Based on these disclosed transactions yesterday, we estimate that AP’s public float has gone below 20% and now stands at 19.9835%
  • A minimum public float of 20% is required for a stock to remain part of the PSE Index
  • The company has expressed its interest to continue buying back shares and is aware of the repercussions of its float going below 20%
  • At the close yesterday, we estimate AP trades at 5.3x PE with a dividend yield of 7.8% for FY24E

    • Ayala Corp (AC PM) said that it has distributed 92% of the common shares of ACEN Corp that it has declared as property dividends as of September 1, 2023

  • “Dividend distribution will continue as and when eCARs are released by the BIR”
