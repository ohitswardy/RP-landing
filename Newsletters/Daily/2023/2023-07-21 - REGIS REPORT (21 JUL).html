Globe (GLO PM) is eyeing to have 200,000 prepaid fiber subscribers by the end of the year, which is a fairly ambitious target. 

For perspective, Globe had just 1.1m postpaid wired subscribers and 1.2m fixed wireless broadband subscribers as of March 2023. Adding 200,000 in just over half a year, if accomplished, would be quite a feat. Note that Converge’s (CNVRG PM) prepaid service Surf2Sawa had ~30k subscribers as of March, after being available for around half a year.

We reckon this prepaid wired service could be a growth engine in the broadband sector, which had seen a slowdown when the economy began re-opening.

Household penetration of wired broadband is only just over 25%. We believe penetration can quickly go to 50-60% with a more affordable, flexibly priced service.

Gaming agency PAGCOR hiked its 2023 gaming revenue target to P273bn, implying a 27% growth vs. 2022, and 11% higher than the target it set in March. The growth figure is higher than our estimates for the integrated resorts this year (+17-20% rev growth) and suggests improved trends on the ground based on what the regulator is seeing. We expect Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) to do well this year, notwithstanding the relative absence of Chinese gaming patrons.

+++

The government is allowing local sugar earmarked for exports to be sold domestically, which would free up more supply for local consumption and help keep a lid on prices. Under the industry’s antiquated and byzantine regulatory regime, a portion of the country’s sugar output is set aside for exports, mainly to the U.S. at favorable (high) prices.

Allowing this “diversion” from the export market to domestic supply, however, is not going to be detrimental for millers. Domestic sugar prices today are far above prices under the U.S. export quota system.

Universal Robina (URC PM) is a key beneficiary of higher domestic sugar prices as it is one of the largest sugar millers.

BOP posts US$606mn deficit in June
  • The balance of payments posted a deficit of US$606mn in June 2023
    • This is lower by 61% YoY vs. June 2022’s deficit of US$1.57bn
    • But higher by 38% MoM vs. May 2023’s deficit of US$439mn
    • Cited foreign currency debt payments
  • Brings 1H23 BOP to a surplus of US$2.26bn, a reversal of the US$3.1bn deficit in 1H22
    • The BSP is targeting a balance of payments deficit of US$1.2bn by year-end (equivalent to -0.3% of GDP)
    • In 2022, the BOP deficit stood at US$7.3bn, equivalent to 1.8% of GDP

MACRO
Sugar for export may now be diverted for local use
  • The Sugar Regulatory Administration (SRA) allows the reallocation of unused sugar originally intended for the global market for domestic use to meet the growing local demand.
    • SRA issued Sugar Order (SO) No. 8 and SO No. 9 to reinstate homeless "A" (sugar for export to the US) and "D" quedans (for the world market) and reclassify them for the local market, subject to conditions
    • Move aims to stabilize the supply and selling prices in the local sugar market and alleviate the perceived tightness in domestic sugar balance for crop year 2022-2023
    • Domestic sugar prices 
  • Requests for the conversion of unshipped "A" and "D" sugar quedans have been received, with around 11,432 MT for "A" sugar and 5,478 MT for "D" sugar remaining outstanding
  • The Philippines typically exports ~100k MT of raw sugar annually to the US

TELCOS
Globe eyes 200,000 prepaid fibre users this year
  • Globe Telecom aims to reach at least 200,000 customers for its prepaid fibre internet by the end of 2023, targeting less saturated D and E markets in the Philippines.
    • Company launched GFiber Prepaid, offering high-speed internet connection through pay-per-use promos starting from P299 for seven days and P999 for 30 days.
  • Prepaid fibre penetration geared towards A, B and C markets which are highly saturated at around 78% but only making up 30% of total
    • Aim to reach the other 70% - markets D and E – currently penetrated at 26%
  • The GFiber Prepaid is available nationwide, not limited to Metro Manila.
  • Globe, Ayala group, and ST Telemedia Global Data Centres (STT GDC) joint venture partner with three local universities to build a data center talent pool.
  • Collaboration with De La Salle University, University of Santo Tomas, and National University aims to enhance awareness of the data center industry, integrate industry-relevant content into engineering curricula, and provide hands-on internship programs.
  • STT GDC Philippines plans to scale up the program by inviting more universities and training institutions to participate and create a robust talent pipeline for the country's data center industry.
