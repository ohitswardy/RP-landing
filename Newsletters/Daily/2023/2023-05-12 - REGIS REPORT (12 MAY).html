12 May 2023

More results today as we approach the tail-end of the earnings season.

  • Converge ICT (CNVRG PM) (-) – Headline profit was P2.17bn, +10% YoY.  While this looks on trend to exceed full-year Street estimates, we note that costs were unusually low, partly due to accounting policy changes.  Revenue growth was 12%, with the enterprise segment providing a boost (+32%).  The core residential segment added a 43k subscribers in the quarter, 2/3rds of which were prepaid. The success of the prepaid product is key to the longer-term outlook for the company, in our view.

  • Manila Water (MWC PM) (+) – Net income doubled YoY to P1.99bn, buoyed by the chunky (+30%) tariff hike at the East zone and a rebound in billed volumes for its PH and Vietnam assets.  While the 1Q figure is tracking ahead of our forecast, we note that the beat largely came from lower cash opex at the East zone. Given that the East zone’s capex of P2.5bn (-56% YoY) in 1Q23 is trailing our full-year estimate, we reckon 1Q margins may not be sustainable.

+++

The government reported that the country’s GDP growth for the first quarter was 6.4%, a tad higher than the 6.2% average estimates in the market.  On the supply side, the services sector provided the boost to growth.  On the demand side, private consumption continues to grow largely in line (+6.3%) with the overall GDP.

We don’t expect the 1Q GDP figure to figure much in how the Bangko Sentral (BSP) decides on its next steps on monetary policy matters.  The BSP’s Monetary Board meets later this month.

+++

The water concessionaires, Manila Water (MWC PM) and Maynilad, finally signed the Revised Concession Agreement (RCA) last May 10 and the Republic also issued the Letter of Undertaking on the same day.  Some provisions were revised  in the final version of the RCA, including modifications to the FCDA mechanism. Given that FX movements arising from future foreign currency loans can no longer be passed on to end users, unless these movements are deemed “extraordinary” (>20%), we view the change as marginally negative to neutral to the value of the water concessions, as it can potentially raise their funding costs in the future.

MACRO
1Q23 GDP positively surprises at 6.4%
  • 1Q23 GDP came in at +6.4% YoY, higher than street estimates (6.2%) and decelerating from a revised 4Q22 GDP of 7.1% (previously 7.2%)
    • This is well within the government’s target of 6-7% for FY23
  • On the demand side, consumption grew 6.3% YoY, slower than in 4Q (7.0%) but normalizing to historical trends
    • Private investment (ie. capital formation) rebounded, up 12.2% YoY (from 3.8% in 4%) and contributing 2.6ppts to GDP growth due to strong public construction
    • However, with export growth slowing sharply, net imports dragged GDP growth by 1.6ppts.
  • On the supply side, all major economic sectors recorded positive growth
  • To reach Regis’ FY23E GDP growth target of 5.5%, GDP for the balance of the year should grow by 5.2%

 

 

UTILITIES
MWSS, concessionaires sign revised concession agreement
  • Maynilad and Manila Water (MWC PM) signed the seventh amendment to the Revised Concession Agreement (RCA) on May 10, 2023
    • Along with the Amendments to the RCA, the Republic issued on 10 May 2023 the Undertaking Letter in the form agreed on by the Parties
    • The RCA retroacts to 29 June 2022
  • Key highlights:
    • CPI adjustments revised to ¾ from 2/3 of the percentage change in the CPI
    • FCDA will be reinstated for foreign currency loans obtained as of June 29, 2022
    • FCDA will be modified for loans obtained after June 29, 2022, such that it will only apply to events of “extraordinary” (i.e. >20%) inflation or deflation in the value of the peso
    • MAGA provisions to be in line with PPP guidelines; The most significant of which is MAGA will now cover actions of the legislative and executive branch of government
    • Regulatory Office decisions are also now arbitrable

 

POWER
PSALM qualifies 8 firms for Casecnan bid
  • The Power Sector Assets and Liabilities Management Corporation announced that it has qualified eight firms to proceed in the bidding for the sale of the 165-MW Casecnan Hydroelectric Power Plant
  • These entities include Fresh River Lakes Corporation, Neptune Hydro Inc, Global Hydro Power Corporation, Panasia Energy inc, GigAce 11 Inc, Belgrove Power Corporation, a consortium comprising EEI Power Corporation, Soosan ENS Co. Ltd., Soosan Industries Co. Ltd, Mapalad Power Corporation and Semirara Mining And Power Corporation
  • GigaAce 11 is a unit of ACEN Corp
  • The Bidding for the Casecnan Plant is scheduled on May 16
  • Casescnan was turned over to the government in 2021 after the 20-year BOT between CE Casecnan Water and Energy Co and the National Irrigation Administration lapsed on December 11, 2021
  • It is a run-of-river type of plant with limited impounding area situated in Pantabangan, Nueva Ecija
