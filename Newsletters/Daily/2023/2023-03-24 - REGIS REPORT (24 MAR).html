REGIS REPORT

24 March 2023

The Monetary Board (MB) of the Bangko Sentral (BSP) took the cue from the Fed and raised policy rates by 25bp yesterday, taking it to 6.25%. The gap between US and Philippine policy rates was thus kept at 1.0% to 1.25%, which is still lower than it has been historically.

Also among the more notable actions of the BSP yesterday was the reduction of its inflation forecast to 6.0% (from 6.1%) and 2.9% (3.1%) for this year and next. We believe this is a signal that the BSP is increasing leaning towards reducing rates once the worst of the headline CPI prints are behind us.

+++

PLDT (TEL PM) released its FY22 results yesterday, with net income of P10.5bn (-60%) marred by massive write-offs of fixed assets. Core income, as we reckon it, was P30.0bn (+7%). The latter figure looks above estimates but only because non-cash charges were lower. Otherwise, the results of core operations would have been a miss.

Both mobile and broadband are facing issues of an industry-wide slowdown. PLDT ceded ~30bp of revenue share on mobile while net additions to the broadband segment were pretty much negligible in 4Q.

Beyond the operating results, PLDT management also took the time to discuss the latest developments on its “capex overrun” issue, with regard to the results of the third party forensic audit as well as negotiations with its vendors. Check out the report from Gio (gio.delarosa@regis.ph) here:

In a press release, GT Capital (GTCAP PM) reported headline FY22 net income of P18.4bn (+67%YoY) and core profit of P15.9bn (+45% YoY). After pref divs, core profit stood at P15.3bn (+47% YoY), which is well behind Street forecasts (P16.4bn). Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) recorded an income of P5.7bn (-1% YoY), below our forecast of P6.9bn likely due to disappointing margins.

+++

President Marcos signed an order creating a Water Resource Management Office to help streamline policy across many regulatory agencies in the sector. This is a first step towards creating an environment that will attract investment to improve the water resource sector.

MACRO
Monetary Board raises policy rates by 25bps
  • The Monetary Board decided to raise the interest rate on the BSP’s overnight repurchase facility by 25bps to 6.25%
    • Decision was based on new information and its assessment of the effects of past policy actions
    • Also cited the steady increase of core inflation in Feb, despite the modest decline in headline inflation
  • The BSP tempered its 2023 average inflation forecast slightly to 6.0% from 6.1% previously
    • It also lowered its inflation forecast for 2024 to 2.9% from 3.1% previously

UTILITIES
Government creates Water Resource Management Office
  • President Marcos Jr. announced he has signed an Executive Order for the creation of a Water Resource Management Office
    • Creating the said office was necessary to form a cohesive policy among water supply agencies
    • Creating a “Department of Water Resources” was one of the President’s priorities during his first State of the Nation Address
  • Water regulation in the country is currently de-centralized, having different governing bodies for provincial cities/municipalities and Metro Manila
  • This development is a step in the right direction, given that a centralized governing body should encourage more integrated planning and policy formulation for the water sector
