REGIS REPORT

22 March 2023

First Gen (FGEN PM) says it has secured an agreement with the government to develop a new 120MW pumped storage hydroelectric facility at the Pantabangan Dam. First Gen currently already operates the existing hydroelectric facilities (132MW) at the Pantabangan and Masiway dams.

In terms of headline numbers, the incremental 120MW will not be a small but arguably meaningful addition to First Gen’s capacity (~2,000MW + a controlling interest in geothermal operator Energy Development Corp (EDC)).  We don’t expect commercial operations, however, within the next three years.

+++

Robinsons Land (RLC PM) is expanding its share buyback program further to P9bn (from P6bn), making it arguably the largest relative to market cap among the names we cover. The unspent portion (P4.9bn) of the revised buyback program represents 7% of RLC’s market cap.

+++

Gaming regulator and operator PAGCOR is hoping to raise as much as P80bn from the sale of its 41 casinos. A sale will mean a significant change for the gaming industry as it would usher in the potential entry of new players, the possible expansion of the main private operators today (e.g. Bloomberry (BLOOM PM)) via acquisition of these PAGCOR casinos, or both.

MACRO
BOP deficit widens to US$895mn in February; GIR falls to US$98.2bn
  • Preliminary data showed  the Balance of Payments widened to an US$895mn deficit in February, up 5.7x YoY (vs Feb 2022: -US$157mn)
    • A reversal of the US$3.08bn BOP surplus in January 2023 (note: the government raised US$3bn in fixed rate global bonds in Jan)
    • Cited government debt payments and a larger trade deficit
    • Brings YT-Feb BOP to a US$2.2bn surplus, a reversal of the US$259mn deficit in the same period a year ago
  • The BSP also revised the end-February GIR to US$98.2bn (previously US$99.3bn)
    • This is down from US$100.7bn in January 2023 and is equivalent to 7.4 months’ worth of imports
GAMING
PAGCOR eyes US$1.47bn from sale of 41 casinos
  • PAGCOR is considering selling all 41 casinos it operates to raise P80bn (US$1.47bn) in government revenue and in order to focus on its regulatory functions
    • According to Pagcor Chairman Alejandro Tengco
  • Systemwide GGR will likely hit the P256bn pre-pandemic level by 2024 versus P214bn last year
    • Out of the P214bn GGR in 2022, PAGCOR-operated casinos generated P15.9bn
POWER
First Gen unit gets DENR nod on 120-MW hydro facility
  • First Gen Corp. said its unit had signed an agreement with the DENR for the development of a 120-megawatt (MW) Aya pumped-storage hydroelectric facility in Pantabangan, Nueva Ecija.
  • The signed deal will enable the FGEN to use a 36-hectare portion of the 84,000-hectare Pantabangan-Carranglan watershed forest reserve for the Aya pumped-storage hydroelectric power project.
  • The agreement is valid for 25 years and subject to renewal for another 25 years
PROPERTY
RLC expands share buyback program again
  • Robinsons Land expanded its share buyback program to P9bn from P6bn
    • The original P3bn buyback program was announced in November 2021
    • It was then expanded to P6bn in November 2022
  • The company has spent P4.1bn to purchase 238mn RLC shares
    • We estimate that the average price was P17.10/share
  • The remaining P4.9bn represents c. 7% of current market cap
  • The decision to expand the buyback program signals management’s confidence in the company’s operations and its view that RLC shares are at compelling valuations
  • RLC trading at 64% discount to NAV, 0.5X FY23E P/B, and 7.6X FY23E P/E
