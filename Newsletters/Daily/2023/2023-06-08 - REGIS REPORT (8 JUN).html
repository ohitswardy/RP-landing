08 June 2023

Globe (GLO PM) has finally introduced its prepaid fiber broadband product, GFiber Prepaid.  The prepaid fiber service comes over six months after Converge (CNVRG PM) quietly introduced its prepaid service, Surf2Sawa.

 

We believe the introduction of prepaid fiber will reshape the broadband sector and restart growth, just as prepaid services did to mobile.  We won’t be surprised to see the operators resume adding hundreds of thousands of new broadband subscribers, even if they’re lower ARPU users.

 

Globe’s prepaid fiber service distinguishes itself from Converge’s in two key ways.  First, it is significantly more expensive than Converge’s.  For perspective, GFiber Prepaid will cost P299 for seven days. This compares with Surf2Sawa’s P200. Second, the subscriber can pay the upfront installation cost (P1,499) for GFiber Prepaid using the installment schemes of GCash.  This reduces the initial cost of subscription, a key obstacle for many, and also subjects the potential subscriber to the implied credit checks embedded in GCash’s credit scoring scheme GScore.

The process to privatize the Manila airport’s (NAIA) operations, possibly the first major PPP project of this administration, could begin by early 2024, if the Dept of Transportation (DOTr) is to be believed. The DOTr is planning a process different from the unsolicited proposal presented by the consortium of six big business groups.  The choice between going down the solicited or unsolicited path is the first step for any privatization process.

Dollar reserves slip to US$101.3bn in May
  • Gross international reserves (GIR) as of end-May fell by US$464mn (-0.5% MoM) to US$101.3bn
    • Due to debt servicing, lower value of gold holdings, and exchange rate market operations
  • Represents a more-than-adequate external liquidity buffer equivalent to 7.6 months’ worth of imports
  • BSP expects the GIR level to end at US$100bn by Dec 2023, up from US$82bn in Dec 2022 but lower than the peak of US$110bn in end-Dec 2020

Gov’t could boost infra spending to 12% of GDP
  • The country could boost infrastructure spending to as much as 12% of GDP as early as next year if the Maharlika Investment Fund (MIF) and PPP go as planned
    • According to Finance Secretary Benjamin Diokno
    • 5-6% will come from the national budget and the rest will come from PPP and MIF

  • From 2022-2028, infrastructure spending is expected to rise at a +10% CAGR, slower than 10-year historical CAGR of +18%

 

MACRO
NAIA privatization doable by early 2024
  • The privatization of NAIA could kick off by 1Q24
    • According to Undersecretary for Aviation and Airports Roberto Lim
  • Winning bidder must commit to invest P141bn for the expansion and improvement of NAIA within a 15-year concession period
    • In addition, the private concessionaire will be asked to pay an upfront cost of P30bn and an annuity payment of P2bn
    • The government will also have a share in revenues from commercial and non-commercial operations
  • Last May, the NAIA consortium comprised of 6 local conglomerates submitted an unsolicited proposal valued at P100bn
    • Consortium includes six Filipino conglomerates + US-based Global Infrastructure Partners:
      • Aboitiz InfraCapital –AEV PM
      • AC Infrastructure – AC PM
      • Asia’s Emerging Dragon Corp. – LTG PM
      • Alliance Global – Infracorp Dev’t – AGI PM
      • Filinvest Development Corp. - FDC PM
      • JG Summit – JGS PM
  • Based on its 2019 annual report, NAIA recorded a net income of P5.0bn, operating profit of P6.8bn, and revenues of P15.2bn
    • Revenue breakdown: Passenger service charge (36%), landing/parking fees (34%), rent income and others (30%)
    • NAIA handled 48.1mn passengers and 306k aircraft movements
TELCOS
Globe launches prepaid fiber broadband
  • Globe launched prepaid broadband fiber under the brand “GFiber Prepaid”
    • This prepaid service comes after Converge began offering its prepaid fiber product latter part of 2022
  • The service is being offered at a premium to Converge’s product.
    • GFiber Prepaid is being priced at P299 for seven (7) days to as much as P999 for 30 days
    • This compares with the P200 for seven (7) days and P700 for 30 days for Converge’s Surf2Sawa prepaid broadband
  • An upfront installation fee of P1,499 for subscribers is required
    • Subscribers can pay the upfront fee using GCash’s buy now pay later (BNPL) or installment schemes (“GGives” or “GCredit”)
  • Subscribers can apply either through the Globe One app or through select Puregold stores
CONGLOMERATES
DMCI Mining aims to ship 1.5 WMT of nickel ore
  • DMCI Mining is targeting to ship 1.5mn WMT of nickel ore this year after securing environmental compliance certificate
    • Production will largely be in its Zambales mine
    • DMCI Mining shipped 1.45mn WMT in 2022; Peak shipment was in 2021: 1.95mn WMT
    • As of 1Q23, shipments declined by 21% YoY to 487k WMT
  • DMCI Mining is currently in the process of mineral exploration at its Zambales and Palawan mines to beef up its nickel reserves
  • DMCI Mining is a wholly-owned unit of DMCI (DMC PM) and accounts for 3% of NAV and 6% of profit
  • DMCI is trading at an 11% discount to its market-based NAV of P11/sh
