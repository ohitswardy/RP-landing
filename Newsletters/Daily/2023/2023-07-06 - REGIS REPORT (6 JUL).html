REGIS REPORT

06 July 2023

Arguably the biggest highlight from the 2Q23 briefing of property consultancy firm Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) was its claim that net office take-up for the quarter was +205ksqm, of which +75k was from the POGO “industry”. This is the first significant pick-up in net demand for office space from POGOs since the pandemic. Office vacancy, as estimated by LPC, dipped to 17% from 18% the previous quarter.

Is this a one-off blip? Or is it the first sign that the sector is turning?

A return of the POGOs could usher in a resurgence of demand not just for office property but also residential property and other adjacent sectors (e.g., consumption, including the conspicuous kind). Note, however, that there’s been high profile opposition to POGOs continuing presence. Among the most notable has been opposition from Finance Secretary Benjamin Diokno.

More detail below.

+++

The headline decline in June CPI inflation to 5.4% is another data point to bolster the case for a rate cut by the Bangko Sentral (BSP). Even the core inflation figure released by the government, while still high at 7.4%, dipped from 7.7%.

It will be another six weeks before the BSP’s Monetary Board meets to act on monetary policy. Between now and then, the two big events that should influence BSP policy action would be the US Fed’s FOMC meeting (July 27) and the release of Philippine CPI data for July (likely Aug 4).

+++

Megawide (MWIDE PM) is paying P5.2bn to acquire a set of property assets from its controlling shareholders. With net debt at P22bn and equity of P10bn (treating prefs as debt), gearing will become even higher post-transaction. Also note that the purchase price is equivalent to ~80% of MWIDE’s market cap of P6.6bn.

+++

The European Union is extending GSP+ privileges to the Philippines for another four years. This is a welcome development for exporters, among them Century Pacific (CNPF PM).

MACRO
Inflation eases to 5.4% YoY but MoM trend accelerates
  • Headline CPI rose 5.4% YoY in June, moderating further from 6.1% the previous month
    • Came in slightly below consensus estimates (+5.5%) and at the lower-end of the BSP’s forecast range (+5.3%-6.1%)
    • Same drivers of easing we have seen in previous months: base effects of food and fuel-related items such as transport and utilities
  • On a MoM basis, however, headline CPI rose 0.2%, accelerating further from 0.0% in May
    • Due to higher prices of food items (+0.3% MoM vs 0.2% in May), mainly rice (+0.6% MoM, rising for four consecutive months) and vegetables
    • Prices of alcohol were up +0.5% MoM in June, though decelerating from a +2.0% MoM increase in March 2023
    • Non-food items were muted (+0.0%), with electricity prices and fuel falling 1.3% MoM and -0.5% MoM, respectively
    • These offset rising prices for passenger transport services (air and sea)
  • Core CPI remained stubbornly high, rising by 0.2% MoM, 7.4% YoY
    • Due to higher prices of fish and vegetables; Non food items: passenger transport (toll fees, air, and sea fares) and housing rental
  • 1H23 inflation averaged at 7.2%, still above the BSP’s 2-4% target range
    • Assuming CPI rises +0.2% MoM for the rest of the year, Dec 2023 inflation should already be c. 2%
  • Newly appointed BSP Governor Eli Remolona said it will be “dangerous” to cut rates ahead of the Fed
    • The next BSP policy meeting is on Aug 17 while the Fed’s next rate decision will be on July 27 (consensus: +25bps)

PROPERTY
LPC: Office demand from POGO sector rebounds in 2Q23
  • During its 2Q23 briefing, Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) revealed that total nationwide net office take-up jumped 27% QoQ (+140% YoY) to 205,000sqm
  • While BPO office demand also picked up, the rebound in POGO office take-up was more eye-catching, from -10,000sqm in 1Q23 to +75,000sqm in 2Q23
    • POGO office take-up was predominantly in the Manila Bay area  
  • Metro Manila office vacancy fell 1ppt QoQ to 17%
    • Vacancy in the Manila Bay area fell from 30% in 1Q23 to 23% in 2Q23
    • Vacancy in Fort Bonifacio was flat at only 9%
    • We estimate that rental rates in most parts of Metro Manila are still 20%-50% below pre-COVID levels
  • We estimate that Metro Manila net office take-up was likely 250,000sqm-300,000sqm, tracking ahead of our FY23E forecast of 400,000sqm
    • Prior to the POGO industry entering the country, Metro Manila annual office take-up was 470,000sqm to 530,000sqm
  • We forecast Metro Manila office vacancy to rise to 20% in 2023E
    • Supply will increase due to new office completions
    • We also expect demand to be muted due to WFH policies – several BPO firms have transferred their registration from PEZA to BOI, which does not set limits on WFH
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 51% of Filinvest Land’s NAV - FLI is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY23E P/B, and 5.1X FY23E P/E
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV - MEG is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY22E P/B, and 4.1X FY23E P/E
    • 30% of Robinsons Land’s NAV - RLC is trading at 64% discount to NAV, 0.5X FY23E P/B, and 7.6X FY23E P/E
  • Should office demand from the POGO sector remain strong, then property in the Manila Bay area (the preferred location of POGO firms) should benefit more
    • 45% of our SMPH NAV estimate (of P46.67/share) is comprised of Manila Bay reclamation projects – the stock is trading at 28% discount to NAV, 2.5X FY23E P/B, and 24.6X FY23E P/E
  • LPC also said that Metro Manila condominium prices in the primary market are generally up 5-10% YoY
  • However, the volume of Metro Manila condominiums dropped 27% QoQ to 8,741 units
    • This was largely attributed to 1) tighter payment schemes in 2Q and 2) stronger sales of horizontal projects in provinces adjacent to Metro Manila (which was brought about by infrastructure improvement)
    • In 1Q23, reservation sales of the developers we cover grew 29% YoY (+24% QoQ) to P133bn, a record level
  • ALI is trading at 50% discount to NAV, 1.4X FY23E P/B, and 15.7X FY23E P/E

TELCOS
Dito CME aims for profitability by end-2028
  • Dito CME (DITO PM) said that it aims to achieve profitability by end of 2028
    • DITO is the holding company that has a controlling interest in Dito Telecommunity (Dito Tel)
    • Outside of Dito Tel, Dito CME has interests in small, IT-related businesses

  • It would be difficult for China Telecom, which owns 40% of Dito Tel, to buy more equity in Dito Tel given the prohibitions against foreign state-owned entities
INFRASTRUCTURE
Megawide acquiring property firm from related party
  • Megawide’s Board approved the acquisition of PH1 World Developers for P5.2bn (100% equity value)
    • Purchase is large relative to MWIDE’s market cap of P6.6bn
    • Recall that MWIDE’s net proceeds from the sale of the Mactan airport last year was c. P14bn
  • PH1 World Developers is owned by its principal, Edgar Saavedra’s Citicore Holdings
    • Projects include The Hive, My Enso Lofts, Modan Lofts Pasco, and Modan Lofts Ortigas Hills
    • A valuation report from a third party, FTI Consulting Philippines, valued PH1 World at P3.82bn-P6.2bn
  • Purchase is up for shareholder approval during the company’s ASM on July 12
  • Based on the 2022 FS of PH1 World Developers, the company generated an EBITDA of P76mn and profit of P67mn
    • We estimate the deal is valued at 75x EV/EBITDA and 78x PER
  • As of end-March 2023, MWIDE’s net debt (inclusive of prefs) and equity stood at P22bn and P10bn, respectively
    • Treating prefs as equity, gross debt-to-equity stood at 1.5x (vs. covenant: max 2.3x)
    • Debt service coverage ratio at 1.51x (vs. covenant min. of 1.1x)
CONSUMER
EC proposes extension of GSP+ scheme for PHL
  • European Commission (EC) proposed the renewal of the Philippines’ participation in the EU Generalised Scheme of Preference Plus (GSP+) for another 4 years
  • The GSP+ extension opens up opportunities for the Philippines to strengthen trade relations with the EU and maximize benefits for exporters and the country's overall economic development.
    • GSP+ allows the Philippines to enjoy zero tariffs on 6,274 products or 66% of all EU tariff lines
    • In 2022, Philippine products exported to the EU under GSP+ rates amounted to EUR2.93bn, with a utilization rate of 77%
    • EC proposed extending until December 31, 2027, pending approval from the European Parliament and the European Council
  • The Philippines participates in the GSP+ as a low and lower middle-income country and must uphold commitments to 27 international conventions on human rights, labour, good governance, and climate action.
    • Faced potential loss of GSP+ status due to concerns over the war on drugs and alleged human rights violations during the Duterte administration
  • The GSP+ extension is seen as a significant boost for Philippine exporters, maintaining their competitive edge in the EU market and potentially expanding trade volumes.
  • The extension would be mildly positive for Century Pacific (CNPF PM), which currently has GSP+ status for some of its tuna OEM products for export to the EU
    • Total tuna OEM accounts for <10% of consolidated sales
