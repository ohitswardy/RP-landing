REGIS REPORT

04 August 2023

We have results from PLDT (TEL PM) and ACEN (ACEN PM) this morning, as well as the initial results release from San Miguel Food and Beverage (FB PM).

Core income for PLDT (TEL PM), inclusive of Voyager losses, came in at P16.3bn (+5%). While this appears to on track to meet (if not slightly exceed) our FY23 estimates, the underlying operations don’t look particularly strong, with revenue growth of 2% (mobile revenue was actually down 2%).  PLDT will face the challenge of keeping 2H revenue growth in positive territory with the slowdown in broadband subscriber adds and the impact of subscriber attrition due to SIM registration.

Among the more interesting takeaways from ACEN Corp’s (ACEN PM) earnings call yesterday include the higher range of tariffs for retail customers and the expected quantum of merchant exposure by year end. Averaged contracted tariffs are up 14% to P5.80/sh, lifted by new RES contracts now being signed in the P6-7/kWh range. Net merchant exposure is also expected to double over the next 12 months as it rolls out more than 500MW in new capacity in the Philippines.

Consolidated profit of San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) came in slightly lower at P8.9bn in 2Q23 (-10%QoQ, -7%YoY). The press release notes that 1H beer volumes increased 9%, while Ginebra’s profit jumped 45%YoY. We hope to get more detail during the earnings call next week (Aug. 8).

+++

July CPI inflation came in at 4.7%, down from 5.4% the previous month.  This appears a tad lower than Street expectations and should provide room for the Bangko Sentral (BSP) not to match the Fed’s recent rate hike.

 

Jollibee (JFC PM) will operate two new niche brands, Tiong Bahru Bakery and Common Man Coffee Roasters, that are held by Titan Dining. JFC owns 90% interest in Titan Dining.

+++

The PSE Index rebalancing will take place at the close today. While there won’t be any changes to the constituents, we estimate inflow to BPI (US$5.6mn), ACEN (US$3.4mn), and UBP (US$0.9mn), due to increases in their public float. The other PSEi members should see modest amounts of outflow, with TEL seeing the highest (at US$1.8mn).

JFC to operate Tiong Bahru Bakery and Common Man Coffee Roasters in the Philippines
  • JFC announced it is entering a JV with Food Collective Pte. Ltd. (FCPL) to operate Tiong Bahru Bakery and Common Man Coffee Roasters in the Philippines
    • JFC to own 60% of the JV; both parties have committed to invest up to P250mn
  • The JV will franchise the brands, which are ultimately owned by Titan Dining LP, in which JFC has a 90% participating interest
    • FCPL is effectively a majority-owned subsidiary under Singapore-based Titan Dining
    • Titan Dining owns and operates various lifestyle brands, including Tim Ho Wan
  • Tiong Bahru Bakery is famous for its baked goods, while Common Man Coffee Roasters is an all-day dining restaurant
  • There are currently 16 Tiong Bahru Bakery and 5 Common Man Coffee Roasters around Singapore and Malaysia. The first Common Man Coffee Roasters in the Phils will begin operating in 2023.
  • JFC’s foreign franchised brands in the Phils currently account for ~3% of systemwide sales
    • Burger King (127 stores), Panda Express (18 stores), Yoshinoya (7 stores)
