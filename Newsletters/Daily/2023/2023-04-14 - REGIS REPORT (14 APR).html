REGIS REPORT

14 April 2023

The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) has revised down its inflation target for 2023E to an average of 6.0%, from 6.1% previously. For 2024E, the BSP forecasts average inflation at 2.9%, from their previous 3.1% estimate, well within the target band of 2-4%. This comes after the surprisingly sharp deceleration in the March CPI print down to 7.6%.

In an interview, BSP Governor Felipe Medalla said, “If April turns to be a very low inflation month, so that’s three good points in a row, we are in the position to pause” policy rate hikes. The next Monetary Board (MB) meeting is set for 16 May. The April CPI comes out around 5 or 6 May.

+++

We got more color on the results of Emperador (EMI PM) and Alliance Global (AGI PM) from their respective briefings yesterday.

EMI’s brandy segment jumped in 4Q22 on seasonally strong sales. Overall margins appear to be off the lows, but other costs are starting to creep up as markets reopen internationally. Sales of whisky, on the other hand, continued to climb on strong single malt sales in Asia and Europe. The recent trends affirm a growth outlook of c.20% for whisky and single-digit for brandy.

AGI, on the other hand, posted a 4Q22/FY22 income of P4.182bn (14%QoQ, -14%YoY)/P16.1bn (-5%YoY), ahead of Regis’ estimates on better-than-expected contributions across most segments. Golden Arches 4Q22 jumped 18%YoY to P800mn on the back of the cumulative impact of prices increases and input costs easing. Meanwhile, Travellers’ posted a 4Q22 profit of P1.3bn (from losses in previous quarters) as GGR jumped 163% YoY due to VIP.

The discount level is likely to remain for AGI, as is the case for other mid-size holdcos with listed subsidiaries. A more important takeaway from the casino numbers (UE/Okada just reported 1Q23 – Total GGR +9%QoQ, VIP +25%QoQ) is that the industry appears to be firing on all cylinders (locals, low-end vs high-end, VIP). Against consensus, there is probably scope for Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) to surprise in 1Q23.

++

Philippine Seven’s (SEVN PH) FY22 operating margins rebounded to pre-pandemic levels, mainly driven by the reopening. SSSG jumped 27% in 4Q22, albeit coming from a higher YoY base relative to previous quarters. Underlying trends suggest that average daily traffic at the store level is still below pre-pandemic levels as schools and offices continue to operate on a hybrid setup. Customer traffic, however, continues to improve on a QoQ basis, which should further drive operating leverage in the coming quarters.

RETAIL
Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) FY22 NPAT rebounds to P2.1bn; margins recover to pre-pandemic levels
  • Philippine Seven reported FY22 NPAT of P2.06bn, rebounding from the net loss in FY21. FY22 operating margin of 5.6% is already above FY19 level.
  • For 4Q22 alone, net profit surged 6x YoY on operating leverage as same-store sales recovered.
  • Revenues in 4Q22 jumped 41% YoY as SSSG moderated to 27%, though coming from a higher YoY base
    • SSS was largely boosted by price inflation, which led to higher average basket sizes (+25-30%). Average customer traffic, however, was still ~20% below pre-pandemic levels as many schools and offices were still on a hybrid set-up.
    • We understand SSSG in 1Q23 is trending 15-20%, partly due to the low base from the Omicron surge in 1Q22
  • SEVN has earmarked P4bn in capex for 2023 primarily for store expansion. The company plans to open 400 stores this year, of which half will be franchised.
