REGIS REPORT

19 September 2023

The bill on a revised fiscal (tax / royalty) regime for mining has hurdled 2nd reading at the House of Representatives. There appears to be no change in the current royalty rate (4%) but the House bill is proposing to impose a graduated windfall tax.

From what’s been disclosed so far, we don’t see how this new mining tax law will encourage investments in the sector. But, as they say, the devil is in the details. We’ll only find out when the bill advances further.

+++

SM Prime’s (SMPH PM) residential reservation sales are likely to reach record levels in 2023, according to the head of SMPH’s residential business. Despite this, however, reported revenue is nowhere near the record despite pre-sales having recovered nicely.

Having reservation sales hit a record high is certainly more positive than not. However, the reported value of headline presales (which is gross of backouts or cancellations) as an indicator of demand is not what it used to be. Looser payment schemes and, more importantly, higher cancellation rates make comparisons with historical data misleading. It’s akin to saying a telco secured 200,000 in gross subscriber additions without regard for churn, when what’s more relevant is the net subscriber addition.

+++

Manila Water (MWC PM) is applying to extend its concession agreement by ten years to 2047. Note the latest approved business plan and rate review by the regulator already assumes the concession will last until 2047.

MINING
Lower House approves mining fiscal regime bill
  • The House of Representatives approved on second reading House Bill no 8937, which seeks to establish a new fiscal regime for the mining sector
    • The bill seeks to maintain the royalty rate of large-scale miners within mineral reservations at 4%
    • For large-scale miner outside mineral reservations, a margin-based royalty scheme will be applied (i.e. a tax rate of up to 5% depending on pre-tax income margins)
    • A windfall profit tax of 1-10% will also apply to miners with net income margins of more than 35% to over 80%
  • The government projects to collect an additional P1.93bn in royalties and taxes every year
    • In 2022, taxes, fees and royalties from mining amounted to P44.8bn
PROPERTY
SM Prime residential sales could hit record level in 2023
  • Jose Mari Banzon, head of SM Prime’s primary residential business, said that sales from January to August are “hitting record levels” and the company could achieve record sales in in FY23
  • We forecast residential reservation sales to rise 10% to P112bn, a record level
  • 1H23 reservation sales grew 15% to P68.5bn
  • SM Prime previously said that 3Q23 mall revenue could be higher than 2Q23 mall revenue, which would imply that mall revenue would also hit a record level
  • SMPH is trading at 39% discount to NAV, 2.0X FY24E P/B, and 20.1X FY24E P/E
UTILITIES
Manila Water proposes to extend expiration date of concession agreement to 2047
  • East zone concessionaire, Manila Water, submitted an application to extend the expiration date of its Revised Concession Agreement (RCA) to July 31, 2047 from July 31, 2037
    • Application for an extension was made to coincide the RCA’s term with the company’s 25-year legislative franchise
  • The approval of the extension will not affect our valuation for MWC (see report here)
    • Our valuation already assumed an expiration date of 2047, which is the same date assumed under the Approved Business Plan during the most recent rebasing
  • West zone concessionaire, Maynilad, has not applied for an extension as of this writing
