REGIS REPORT

18 September 2023

Overseas remittances amounted to just under US$3bn in July, 2.6% higher YoY.  There were no big outliers (by geography) this month in terms of YoY growth.  Most regions saw growth of 2-4%.

 

Meanwhile, in a related development, the Bangko Sentral (BSP) reported that the current account deficit narrowed to US$3.6bn in 2Q23, from US$4.6bn in 1Q23 and US$3.2bn in 2Q22.  The decline is principally because of a lower trade deficit, which itself was due to lower commodity prices.

 

The lower CA deficit prompted the BSP to reduce its CA deficit forecast to US$11bn, equivalent to 2.5% of GDP.  For FY24, its forecast is for the CA deficit to dip marginally to US$10.3bn (2.1% of GDP).

President Marcos says that British firm Dyson will be investing PhP11bn (US$190m) in the Philippines over the next two years. If completed, it would create 1,250 jobs and add to the meager FDI that the country receives.

 

+++

 

Budget carrier Cebu Air (CEB PM) will need to reduce its capacity growth guidance for FY24 following issues with Pratt & Whitney, which supplies engines for the Airbus 320neo.  CEB’s latest guidance was for its fleet size to rise to 91 by end-2024 from 78 by end-2023. It’s unclear how much capacity will need to be reduced.

MACRO
Remittances rise 2.6% YoY in July
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks rose 2.6% YoY to US$2.99bn in July
    • In PHP terms, remittances were up just 0.8% YoY
    • 3-month moving average growth rate for July was at 2.5% YoY
  • Brings 7M23 remittances to US$18.79bn, up 2.9% YoY
    • Tracking within the BSP’s FY23 growth target of +3% YoY (US$33.5bn)

 

 

 

MACRO
Current account deficit narrows in 2Q23, 1H23; BSP lowers BOP deficit targets
  • The BSP reported the current account stood at a deficit of US$3.6bn in 2Q23
    • Lower than the US$4.6bn deficit in 1Q23 and US$4.2bn in 2Q22
    • Cited (a) narrowing trade deficit of US$16bn (vs. US$17bn in 1Q23 and US$19bn in 2Q22) and (b) higher receipts from trade services (e.g travel) and secondary income accounts
    • Brings 1H23 current account deficit to US$8.2bn, lower than the US$12.1bn deficit in the same period last year
  • Following its quarterly review, the BSP lowered its FY23E BOP deficit target to US$100mn due to a lower current account deficit forecast
    • Lower current account due to a smaller trade deficit forecast and higher forecasts for travel receipts
    • Offset by lower FDI and foreign portfolio investments forecasts

TRANSPORT
Cebu Air to cut 2024 capacity growth guidance
  • Cebu Air (CEB PM) announced that it will need to lower its capacity growth guidance for 2024
    • This follows Pratt&Whitney’s (P&W) announcement of accelerated engine inspections of 650 A320/A321 NEO jets in 2024 (see here)
    • The recall will affect the number of CEB's active fleet for 2024 but will not affect 2023 operations 
  • Prior to this announcement, the company guided its fleet size will rise to 91 by end-2024 from 78 in end-2023
    • CEB did not provide new guidance
    • Approximately 35% of CEB's 2024 fleet are A320/321 NEOs but we are not certain how much of these will be affected by the recall
  • Our forecasts assume 2024E pax volumes and ASKs will rise by 12% YoY and 24%, respectively
    • Every 5% cut in pax volumes could reduce FY24E EBITDA/profit forecasts by 23%/71%, respectively, ceteris paribus
    • CEB is trading at 6.2x/4.5x FY23E/FY24E EV/EBITDA
