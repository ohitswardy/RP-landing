REGIS REPORT

07 November 2023

We have more results today.

  • SM Prime (SMPH PM) (=) – 9M23 operating profit grew 29% to P44.5bn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. In contrast to the soft 3Q sales figures of some of the retailers, SM Prime’s 3Q23 mall revenue was resilient.  We estimate that Philippine mall revenue inched up 1%-2% QoQ (up over 20% YoY), which is unusually strong since 3Q is seasonally weaker than 2Q. Meanwhile, 3Q23 residential reservation sales fell 15% YoY and 36% QoQ. While this was a weak performance, we do not consider it a cause for alarm since large quarterly swings are common for this business.

  • Meralco (MER PM) (+) – Meralco delivered 3Q core income of P10.8bn, up +90% YoY.  This takes 9M core income to P30bn, +53% YoY.  The primary source of outperformance is still the generation business, but we note electricity usage growth was +6.3% YoY in the quarter.  A key highlight of yesterday’s briefing was the company’s proposal to withdraw and re-file its tariff application. Meralco is hoping to have the two-year period from July 2022 declared a lapsed period and have its 5th Regulatory Period (5RP) begin in 2024 instead.  Read more below.
  • Globe (GLO PM) (-) – Globe held its earnings call yesterday.  As noted yesterday, core income for 9M came in at P14.8bn (-8% YoY). Despite core income falling below expectations, we see this glass as half full, given positive signs from (1) improved mobile revenue which rose 2% QoQ, (2) positive broadband net adds and (3) continued improvement in the profitability of Mynt.  More detail in the report (link below).

The government reported CPI inflation to be 4.9% in Oct, below consensus expectations (5.6%).  Food inflation moderated to 7.3% from 10% in Sept.  Rice inflation came down to 13.2% (vs. 17.9%) while vegetable price inflation slowed down to 11.9%.

This inflation print likely will hold back any move by the Bangko Sentral (BSP) to hike rates this month.  We, however, remain wary about the near-term direction of rice prices given where Vietnam export prices are (Vietnam accounts for >80% of rice imports).

UTILITIES
Meralco – another strong quarter
  • Another quarter, another surprise.  Meralco delivered another strong quarter with 3Q core profits soaring 90% to P10.8bn.  As a result, cumulative core income for 9M is now P30.0bn, up +53% YoY.  This is well ahead of expectations (both ours and consensus) on the back of strong generation profits and improved core distribution volumes. For 9M, the distribution business was 58% of core income, while generation was 34%.
  • Generation provides the lift again. From being a money loser, this segment has seen a massive turnaround. For 3Q and 9M, this business, particularly Pacific Light, has continued to surprise to the upside. Pacific Light has been benefiting from the tight supply situation in Singapore but management seems to hint that period of exceptional earnings will taper off next year.

 

  • Distribution business volumes rise 6.3% in 3Q. The regulated distribution business saw volumes rise 6.3% in 3Q, led by commercial usage (+9.4%). This lifted 9M volume growth to 4.4%. Industrial volumes are still lagging (+1%).
  • Planning to re-file tariff application. Meralco has asked the regulator (ERC) to allow it to withdraw its pending tariff review papers for the Fifth Regulatory Period (5RP). It plans to ask the ERC to allow it to treat the first two  years from July 2022 as a “lapsed period” instead and apply 5RP from July 2024 to June 2028. This will mean the current tariff of P1.352/kwh will apply as a final rate for the two years from July 2022 to July 2024.

We reckon Meralco management thinks it will get better tariffs for 5RP with its new application given (1) higher interest rates today, and (2) a higher asset base, among other things.

  • Core income guidance of only P37bn.  Management is guiding for core income of P37bn this year, despite already reaching P30bn as of 9M. We are uncertain how much of this guidance figure is just management attempting to low-ball expectations or there’s something to it (a sharp drop in Pacific Light profits or some write-offs in 4Q).  The last one would be ironic, given that we believe Meralco has huge provisions that it can and ought to reverse or write back already.
