REGIS REPORT

29 August 2023

Digital payments growth shows no signs of slowing down. Payments made through the BSP’s two systems – InstaPay and PesoNet – hit a combined P1.09trn in July, up 31.5% YoY, a new all-time high. Year-to-date, the figure is P7.02trn, up 30.7% YoY. It’s important to note that the BSP’s National Retail Payments System (NRPS) only records transactions made from one financial entity to another, and does NOT include payments made within a platform (e.g., GCash-to-GCash, BDO-to-BDO, etc.), and thus vastly understates the actual value of digital payments in the entire system.

We were most interested in the data for InstaPay, the platform for real-time transfers of up to P50,000 (c$880). For the month of July, P427bn went through the system, up 40.3% YoY. But the number of transactions jumped even higher, up 53.7% YoY. This to us suggests either a) penetration is increasing down the economic ladder, and/or b) people are using digital payments more often, even for smaller transactions. Most likely, it’s a combination of the two.

Of the banks we cover, BPI (BPI PM) is leaning the hardest into customers’ digital adoption. Globe’s (GLO PM) GCash is by far the dominant e-wallet, while PLDT’s (TEL PM) Maya leads in merchant acquisition.

 

+++

Globe (GLO PM) and Ayala Corp (AC PM) disclosed more detail regarding their partnership with Gogoro Inc after their earlier announcement a few months ago.  They previously announced a tie-up for an electric scooter venture in the Philippines.

Globe will own 49% of the joint venture, AC will own 21% and Gogoro Inc will own 30% of the company to be called Gogoro PH.  Gogoro PH will be capitalized at US$8.5m and will engage in the importation and sale of electric scooters.  It will also develop a battery-swapping network for these e-scooters in order to do away with the lengthy charging waits.

This is the initial foray of the Ayala group into electric vehicles.  AC also recently announced it was, through AC Motors, taking over the distribution of BYD vehicles in the Philippines.

BANKS/TELCOS
Digital payments hit new highs in July; smaller-ticket transactions still surging
  • Payments made over the BSP’s National Retail Payments System (NRPS) continued to surge in July, up 31.5% YoY to a new all-time monthly high of P1,094bn. For 7M23, total payments stood at P7,021bn, up 307% YoY. The data only captures payments made between financial institutions (eg BDO-to-Metrobank, BPI-to-Maya, etc). It does NOT include payments made between two parties on the same platform (eg BDO-to-BDO, GCash-to-GCash, etc), and thus likely vastly under-estimates the true value of digital payments in the financial system.
  • Most notably, in our view, is the data on InstaPay, ie for real-time transfers of up to P50,000 (roughly $880). The value of InstaPay payment grew 40.3% YoY in July to P426.9bn, with the volume of transactions up an eye-popping 53.7% to 72.2mn. This to us suggests wider adoption of digital payments by either a) more individuals and/or b) individuals using digital payments more often, even for smaller items. Most likely it’s a combination of the two. Year-to-date, InstaPay transactions were up 40.7% YoY to P2,689bn.
  • The story was different for PesoNet, which is used for larger, batched payments. Payments here grew 26.5% YoY to P666.9bn in July, while the number of transactions were up just 4.3%. For 7M23, PesoNet logged P4,332bn in transactions, up 25.1% YoY, with volumes up 9.3%.
  • The July value figures for both PesoNet and InstaPay were record highs.
  • Of the banks we cover, BPI (BPI PM) appears to be the most ardent in embracing the digitalization trend. Among the e-wallets, Globe’s (GLO PM) GCash is by far the dominant player, while rival PLDT’s (TEL PM) Maya leads in merchant acquisition.
