REGIS REPORT

19 January 2023

The release of the “official” Dec 2022 public float numbers for the listed companies confirms our view that DMCI Holdings (DMC PM) will very likely be added to the PSEi (“PCOMP”) effective next month. We expect DMC’s entry to be at the expense of Megaworld (MEG PM).

Meanwhile, the other probable entrant into the PSEi next month is Union Bank (UBP PM), whose official float as of Dec was 35.7%. If UBP does enter the index, it will likely replace Robinsons Land (RLC PM).

Expect an announcement from PSE on the index changes sometime in Feb.

+++

Finance Secretary Benjamin Diokno says the government intends to tap revenues from the mining sector to support the Maharlika sovereign wealth fund. Frankly, the mining sector contributes little to the national coffers today and his statement suggests that the administration is still trying to find funding sources for the proposed fund after initial plans to use the government-run pension funds (SSS, GSIS) were derailed.

+++

The CFO of ICTSI (ICT PM), Joel Consing, has been appointed as Presidential Adviser and Investment and Economic Affairs, and will thus be resigning his current post.

The Philippine SEC has finally approved the share-for-asset swap relating to AREIT’s (AREIT PM) purchase of 124,000 sqm of office property from mother firm Ayala Land (ALI PM). The previously announced deal will involve AREIT issuing new shares equivalent to 17% of its capital.

PROPERTY
SEC approves share-for-asset swap between AREIT and Ayala Land
  • AREIT will acquire 124,000sqm of Cebu offices from Ayala Land
    • This will expand AREIT’s rental portfolio by 23% to 673,000sqm
    • The offices have a combined occupancy rate of 96%
    • eBloc Towers 1 to 4 at Cebu IT Park, ACC Tower and Tech Tower at Ayala Center Cebu
  • The offices are valued at P11.257bn
    • AREIT’s AUM will grow 21% to P64bn
  • AREIT will issue 252.1mn AREIT shares to Ayala Land
    • The AREIT shares would effectively be priced at P44.7/share
    • AREIT’s shares outstanding will expand 17%
    • AREIT said that the yield on the Cebu offices was 6%
  • This transaction was first announced in March 2022 and has been factored into our ALI estimates/forecasts
  • We estimate that ALI’s ownership in AREIT would rise from 60% to 66%
  • AREIT currently offers a dividend yield of 6.5%
    • Based on consensus forecast
    • The interest rate on the 10-year government bond is 6.3%
  • ALI is trading at 35% discount to NAV, 1.9X FY23E P/B, 20.6X FY23E P/E
OTHERS
DMC and UBP could enter PSE Index during the February 2023 rebalancing
  • Based on our estimates, DMC is highly likely to enter the PSE Index during the February 2023 rebalancing
    • We expect this to lead to the removal of MEG
  • Meanwhile, we consider the likelihood of UBP entering the PSE Index to be moderate
    • This should lead to the removal of RLC from the index
  • The PSEi rebalancing in February will be based on the 12 months from January 2022 to December 2022
  • Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap
  • Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when
    • 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or
    • 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35 
  • Based on our estimates, DMC and UBP have reached the top 25
  • In addition to DMC and UBP, we also expect relatively large inflow to SMC and EMI due to increases in their public float
  • In addition to the PSEi constituents to be removed, we also expect relatively large outflow from ALI due to the reduction in its public float
  • We estimate US$126mn in total flows (US$63mn in each direction)
  • Notes:
    • EMI disclosed that its public float increased to 20%.
    • The SSS stake in UBP is about 19%. We assume that the SSS stake in excess of two board seats will be treated as public float.

OTHERS
Philippine market foreign ownership drops in December
  • We estimate that market foreign ownership fell 6bps MoM to 21.0% while PSEi foreign ownership dropped 29bps MoM to 25.0% (in December)
    • If the BDO foreign ownership had remained unchanged MoM, then market and PSEi foreign ownership would have been 21.2% and 25.2% respectively
  • Market net foreign selling reached US$109mn in December (from US$99mn of net foreign buying in November)
  • 18 PSEi constituents saw declines in foreign ownership while 11 experienced increases
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were:
    • BDO – saw the biggest drop in foreign ownership, which fell 3.77 percentage points MoM to 20.7%. However, this appears inconsistent with trading data, which show that BDO was the top foreign bought stock in November and December.  
    • GTCAP – foreign ownership fell 2.61 percentage points to 16.2%. The stock was deleted from the MSCI Index in late November but the reduction in foreign ownership was only reflected in the December data. 
    • MPI – foreign ownership fell 1.88 percentage points to 12.3%. The stock was deleted from the MSCI Index in late November but the drop in foreign ownership was only reflected in the December data.
    • AGI – saw the biggest increase in foreign ownership, which rose 36 basis points to 17.8%.
