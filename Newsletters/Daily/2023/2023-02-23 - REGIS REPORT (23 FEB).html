REGIS REPORT

23 February 2023

Metrobank (MBT PM) issued a press release announcing FY22 results. Net income for the year rose 48% to P32.8bn, bang in line with Regis estimates and just slightly (4%) above Street expectations. While net interest income was slightly lower than forecast, non-interest income and opex made up for the difference.

Metrobank will hold its earnings briefing on Monday.

+++

Bloomberg News reported that Maya is considering a fund raise of as much as US$150m. Maya is the brand under Voyager Innovations that covers a number of fintech operations, including an e-wallet, a payments platform and a digital bank. Voyager is ~36% owned by PLDT (TEL PM). It last raised funding (US$210m) in April 2022, at a post-money valuation of US$1.4bn.

If true, the fund-raising exercise should not come as a surprise. Voyager’s contribution to PLDT’s 9M22 bottom line was a loss of P2.0bn, which implies Voyager lost ~P5.5bn (>US$100m) over the period. At that rate (assuming the loss corresponds to cash burn), Voyager would have needed an infusion by 2H23.

The contrasting situations of Voyager (Maya) and Globe’s (GLO PM) GCash are one reason why we reckon Globe’s shares will do better over the near-term, despite the deflation of the fintech bubble. We view the upcoming MSCI rebalancing (which will lead to the removal of Globe) on Feb 28 to be an opportunity.

+++

The banks have begun implementing higher credit card interest charges following the Bangko Sentral’s (BSP) circular raising the maximum interest on such to 3% (from 2%) per month. We estimate the impact on the bottom line to be as high as 10% for the banks.

+++

SpaceX’s Starlink service is now available in the Philippines. The monthly fee of ~US$50 is surprisingly low but the high upfront cost of almost P30,000 (US$530) is likely to make it a niche service.

BANKS
Metrobank 2022 net profit jumps 48%, in line with REGIS forecast 
  • Metrobank’s 2022 earnings rose 48% to P32.8bn
    • In line with REGIS forecast, 4% above consensus forecast
  • Net interest income came in slightly behind but this was compensated for by slightly higher non-interest income and slightly lower opex
  • FY22 net interest income grew 14% as loan growth accelerated to 14% (-1% in 2021, +13% in 3Q22) and net interest margin expanded to 3.6% (from 3.2% in 2021)
    • The BSP increased its policy rates by 350 basis points in 2022
    • Deposits grew 15% to P2.2trn (CASA ratio of 68%)
  • Meanwhile, Opex only rose 3% for the full-year, bringing the cost-to-income ratio to 54% (from 59% in 2021)
  • Pre-provisioning operating profit (PPOP) climbed 24% to P52.0bn
  • NPL ratio eased to 1.9% (from 2.2% in 2021) while NPL cover stood at 172%
  • 4Q22 net profit surged 55% YoY (+18% QoQ) to P9.3bn
    • 4Q22 net interest income grew 5% QoQ (+25% YoY) to P23.5bn
  • CET1 ratio of 16.8% is well above the minimum regulatory requirement
  • Thus, the bank declared a P3/share cash dividend for the year, representing a dividend yield of 5.0%
TELCOS
Starlink starts PH operations
  • Starlink is now available in the Philippines
  • The monthly service fee appears to be much lower than we thought it would be at only P2,700 per month
    • That’s only US$50, half of what it is elsewhere and what we expected (US$100+)
    • Starlink is supposed to provide speed of ~200mbps.  For perspective, the Converge P1500 plan is for a similar speed.
  • Despite the lower monthly cost, it would still primarily be a niche service given its high upfront cost of P29,320 (~US$530)
