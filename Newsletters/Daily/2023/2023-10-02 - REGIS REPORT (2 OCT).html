REGIS REPORT

02 October 2023

It has been a struggle for the Finance Department to get any of its many measures advanced and the tax on “junk food” is but one prime example. To date, not a single lawmaker has dared to sponsor the DOF’s measure to impose a tax on “food lacking nutritional value”. Early on, we were of the view that this tax was unlikely to be passed. Its current orphaned status only strengthens our view further.

Apart from the “junk food” tax, the DOF has made little or no headway on many of its proposals or plans. Its proposal to reduce tariffs on rice imports was flatly rejected by President Marcos. Its plan to reform generous pensions for the military has been so watered down by the House that Finance Secretary Diokno himself remarked that it was better to not even pass the measure. Even Diokno’s publicly espoused views against free tuition in state colleges have attracted no visible support. Amidst all this, government agencies have been “underspending” – a factor in the weaker-than-expected GDP in 2Q.

+++

Tourist arrivals to the Philippines are looking weak in Sept, with arrivals likely to be down >20% MoM. While Sept is indeed a seasonally weak month, the comparison with 2019 figures also shows a visible drop-off. Sept 2023 tourists are likely to be only half of what they were in 2019, vs 65% for YT-Sept period.

+++

The placement of 559m shares of Bloomberry (BLOOM PM), 5.1% of outstanding shares, in a top-up placement last week will likely result in an increase in the PSEi weighting of the stock. Assuming this increase in its public float meets the PSE’s Sept cut-off, the increased index weighting for this stock (which only rejoined the PSEi last week too) could happen as soon as November.

+++

PLDT (TEL PM) is already looking to build an even bigger 13th data center facility even before its 11th facility is completed. Globe (GLO PM) is itself building its largest data center facility in Quezon City, to be completed by 2025. All these are demonstrative of the explosive demand for data center capacity as hyperscalers rush to locate in the country.

Tourist arrivals reach 4mn as of Sep 29
  • International arrivals have breached the 4mn mark as of Sep 29
    • This is 35% below 2019 levels
    • The government is targeting 4.8mn tourist arrivals this year or about 58% of 2019 arrivals (8.26mn)
    • The government expects tourist arrivals to reach pre-pandemic levels in 2025
  • This suggests that tourist arrivals dropped MoM to 344k from Sep 1-29 (~50% of 2019 levels)
    • September is seasonally a lean month

MACRO
Finance Secretary Diokno disappointed over watered down MUP bill
  • Finance Secretary Benjamin Diokno expressed disappointment over the watered-down version of the Military and Uniformed Personnel (MUP) Pension System Act (House Bill 8969)
    • The Lower House approved the bill on third and final reading last week
    • Diokno said that “Kung ganun, parang walang reform. 'Wag mo na lang ipasa kung ganun,” [If there’s no reform, then might as well not let it pass]
  • MUP Pension System Act seeks to address the financially unsustainable military pension system in the country
    • Under the current system, the government needs to spend P164bn in 2024 to fund MUP pensions and gratuity benefits, equivalent to 3% of 2024 budget
    • Data showed that a guaranteed 3% annual salary increase for 10 years with full indexation of benefits will require P11.8bn in 2024, rising to P165bn by 2033
    • Unfunded military pension liabilities currently stands at P9.6tn

BLOOM placement will likely result to PSE Index rebalancing in November
  • Quasar Holdings, one of the controlling shareholders of Bloomberry Resorts, sold 559mn BLOOM shares to institutional investors
  • This will raise the public ownership of BLOOM from 34% to 39%
  • In turn, we expect this to lead to a rebalancing of the PSE Index in November
    • When the end-1Q or 3Q public float level of a PSE Index constituent has changed by at least 5ppt from the end-4Q or 2Q level, then there will be a rebalancing in May or November, respectively
  • We estimate inflow of US$4mn to BLOOM
  • All other PSE Index constituents should see modest amounts of outflow
