REGIS REPORT

31 July 2023

The government reported a budget deficit of P225b in June, mildly (5%) higher YoY.  Tax revenues were down YoY for June due to some timing issues but what’s arguably most notable item is the decline in expenditures.  Expenditures for June were down 3% despite a 44% jump in interest payments.  For 1H23, expenditures are barely up (+0.4%), with primary spending actually down 0.7%.

The slow pace of expenditure is manifesting itself in weak cement demand as reported by Cemex Philippines (CHP PM) on Friday.  CHP’s cement volumes fell 17% in 1H.

The anemic government spending is itself partly a symptom of the state of government finances.  Over the past seven years, government debt has ballooned to over P14trn (US$250bn), equivalent to over 60% of GDP (from just below 40%). Notwithstanding this, the Dept of Finance has been pushing government departments and agencies to spend more quickly to revive the economy’s animal spirits.

MACRO
Fiscal deficit widens in June
  • The government recorded a budget deficit of P225bn in June, higher by 5% YoY
  • Revenues dropped 8% YoY in June, with tax collections lower by 4% YoY
    • Note that the government started collecting VAT on a quarterly basis this year vs. monthly previously
  • Expenditures fell 2.6% YoY, despite a 44% YoY jump in interest payments
    • Cited lower National Tax Allotment shares of local governments and slow payment or encashment of suppliers/creditors in various agencies
  • Brings 1H23 budget deific to P552bn, lower by 18% YoY
    • The government is targeting a budget deficit of P1.5tn by year end, equivalent to 6.1% of GDP

 

 

MATERIALS
Cemex Philippines: Sales volumes collapse by 17% YoY in 2Q/1H23
  • Cemex Philippines (CHP PM) reported revenues contracted by 4% QoQ, 18% YoY in 2Q23, as construction activity remained weak amidst a challenging competitive environment. 
  • Sales volumes fell by 17% YoY and 1% QoQ during the quarter.  CHP further lost market share, as management estimates industry volumes fell by “mid-single digits”.  Management has not seen a meaningful recovery of demand yet from both private and government sectors.  It remains hopeful about a recovery by 4Q23, as it expects to end the year with its full-year volumes down by just “mid-single-digit to high-single-digit”, after the 17% YoY drop in 1H23.
  • Recall that a new player, Century Peak, started operating its 1mn MT cement production line in Cebu early this year.  Due to intense competition, ASPs fell by 2% YoY and 4% QoQ in the quarter.
