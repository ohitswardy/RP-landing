REGIS REPORT

14 July 2023

The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) says its revenue collections for the first five months of the year reached P1.06trn, which is 10% higher vs the same period. While May collections are down slightly (due to changes in the timing of VAT collections), the YT-May collections are on track to meet the full-year target of 11% growth.

+++

GT Capital’s (GTCAP PM) property subsidiary Federal Land revealed its master plans for ten multi-use developments across its portfolio. The projects, in BGC, Pasay, Marikina and Cavite, signal the rising contributions from the property segment of GTCAP, which today accounts for just 5% of earnings.

+++

ACEN (ACEN PM) disclosed it sold just under 5% of its interest in the two Indonesian geothermal assets Salak and Dajarat to its partner there. No price was disclosed but the company says the sale achieves “highly attractive value realization”.

Prior to this transaction, ACEN effectively owned 19.8% of the two projects, which have a combined capacity of 655MW.

+++

Security Bank (SECB PM) raised P18.5bn from the sale of 1.5-year bonds yesterday, well above the original P8bn target. A look at the yield shows why demand was strong. At 6.425%, it was not far from the yield of the government bond (6.5%) with a remaining life of 10 years.

That Security Bank is raising 1.5=year money at that price is an illustration of how mid-sized banks are being squeezed because they don’t have the CASA franchise of the Big Three.

+++

Three contracts with a combined value of P52bn (US$950m) for the construction of the North-South Commuter Rail (NSCR) were awarded yesterday. What’s interesting is that two of the three contracts were won by Indonesian firms. The NSCR, which the government aims to complete by 2029, will run 147 km from Calamba, Laguna to Clark, Pampanga.

INFRASTRUCTURE
Multinational JVs secure P52bn contracts for NSCR project
  • The government has awarded 3 contracts for the NSCR project to joint ventures of multinational firms for P52bn
    • Contract packages encompass the 14.9km of the NSCR’s rail viaducts and stations
  • Two contracts, Packages 1 and 3c, were awarded to the joint venture of Indonesian firms PT Adhi Karya (Persero) Tbk. and PT PP (Persero) Tbk. (Adhi-PT PP JV)
    • Package 1 includes the construction of a 1.2-km railway viaduct structure and the Blumentritt Station which will be connected to the existing Blumentritt Station of the Light Rail Transit Line
    • Contract price: P11.29bn
  • Package 3c involves the construction of 5.8 km of railway track structure with elevated stations at Bicutan and Sucat
    • Contract Price: P15.75bn
  • Package 3a was awarded to Leighton Contractors (Asia) Ltd. in partnership with Philippine company First Balfour, Inc
    • Comprises the construction of 7.9 km of railway track structure with elevated stations at Buendia, and at-grade stations at EDSA and Senate
    • Contract Price: P21.39bn
  • Civil works for the contracts are expected to begin between Q4 2023 and Q1 2024
  • The NSCR project is a 147-km commuter railway that will connect Calamba, Laguna and Clark, Pampanga; Project cost is P870bn
    • Entire system is expected to be completed in 2029
CONGLOS/PROPERTY

  • Federal Land plans to develop 10 master-planned, multiuse properties as part of its Federal Land Communities product line
    • Grand Central Park in BGC
    • Met Park in Pasay

    • Federal Land has a joint development agreement with SM, wherein it will sell 100 hectares of land in Cavite (only 39 hectares has been sold so far)
  • Federal Land is a wholly-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 5% of profit and 22% of gross asset value
  • GTCAP is trading at a 53% discount to its market-based NAV of P1118/sh and at 5.1x FY23E PER

POWER
ACEN pares down stake in 655MW Salak and Darajat geothermal plants in Indonesia
  • ACEN disclosed that it has reduced its stake in the 655MW Salak and Darajat geothermal plants in Indonesia
    • It currently owns a 19.8% stake in the plant through a holding company called ACEHI Netherlands B.V.
    • The disclosure says the ACEN subsidiary sold a 24.2% stake in ACEHI Netherlands B.V. to Star Energy Oil & Gas Pte. Ltd. (SEOG)
    • Effectively sold a 4.8% stake in the Salak and Darajat geothermal plant
  • SEOG is part of the Star Energy Group, which is ACEN’s current partner for the Salak and Darajat geothermal plants
  • With this sale by ACRI of shares in ACEHI Netherlands, ACEHI Netherlands has become a jointly controlled entity by ACRI and SEOG
  • Salak and Darajat Geothermal is a 655MW power plant in West Java and accounts for 13% of ACEN’s NAV and 8% of its operating capacity
    • Under our numbers, ACEN’s 19.8% stake in Salak and Darajat geothermal plants is worth P17.5bn or 13% of our NAV estimate of P139bn (P3.5/share)
    • It also accounts for 8% of ACEN’s operating attributable capacity of 1,600MW
  • “Modest selldown of our position to achieve highly attractive value realization, while still retaining a meaningful ownership”
  • ACEN acquired the stake in the Indonesian asset in 2017 as part of a consortium that bid for Chevron’s geothermal assets and operations in Indonesia
