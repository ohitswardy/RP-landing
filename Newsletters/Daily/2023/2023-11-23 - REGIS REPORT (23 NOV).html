REGIS REPORT

23 November 2023

Ayala Land (ALI PM) and San Miguel (SMC PM) signed a deal for the latter to build ramps in/out of Arca South for Skyway 4, which SMC is constructing. 

Skyway 4 is a new elevated road connecting southern and northern Metro Manila on its eastern side (along Laguna Lake).  Arca South is the mixed-use estate being developed by ALI in what used to be the Food Terminal Inc (FTI) property.  Access to and from Arca South has been an issue so the road link will help boost its long-term attraction and viability.  We expect ALI to pay for the incremental cost of these road links.

+++

The PSE says it expects at least four IPOs next year, from its list of six IPO candidates.  Likely the biggest on the list would be the REIT listing of SM Prime (SMPH PM), followed by Prime Infrastructure, controlled by ICTSI (ICT PM) chairman Enrique Razon. What’s not on the list for now is Mynt, the fintech arm of Globe (GLO PM) which operates GCash.

Clearly, the main thing holding back any of these new listings is the softness of market conditions.  With even companies like Ayala Corp (AC PM) and GT Capital (GTCAP PM) on single-digit PERs and others at below 5x, new listings will only happen when the equity market turns.

+++

The Dept of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) is planning to offer 135 new water projects for private investors.  We view this as an acknowledgement by the DENR of the need to develop more water sources and also the need to get private sector funding and expertise involved.

INFRA
Investors to be offered 135 water projects
  • The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) said it will offer 135 water projects to private investors next year
    • Projects will involve assignment of water rights held by the National Irrigation Administration and repurpose surplus irrigation water for other uses (i.e. hydropower, bulk water, aquaculture, and tourism)
    • Volume of the 135 water rights to be offered amount to 12,000 cubic meters per second (cms)
  • DENR will start seeking unsolicited proposals from the private sector by Dec 2023

    • EO will aim to “enable the expeditious processing of licenses, clearances, permits, certifications and authorizations”
