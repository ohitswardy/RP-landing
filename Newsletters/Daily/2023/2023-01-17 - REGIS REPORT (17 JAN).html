REGIS REPORT

17 January 2023

Rafa (Rafael.garchitorena@regis.ph) published a new strategy report last night and it is bullish!

  • Valuations are cheap – currently at 12.7X vs the historical average of 15.8X
  • Earnings growth is strong – we’re forecasting 19% growth this year after an already robust 39% in 2022E
  • Foreign ownership is close to record lows – the Philippines has seen five consecutive years of net foreign selling

In terms of sectors, we are overweight on the banks (BDO (BDO PM) and BPI (BPI PM)), consumer (Universal Robina (URC PM), Jollibee (JFC PM), Century Pacific Food (CNPF PM), and Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM)), and power (Aboitiz Power (AP PM)) on continued demand and widening margins. Outside of these themes, our stock picks would also include ICTSI (ICT PM), as well as Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM).

MACRO
Cash remittances up 5.7% YoY in November
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks rose 5.7% YoY to US$2.64bn in November
    • Amount lowest in six months due to seasonality; Note that remittances grew by +5.1% YoY in November 2021
    • In PHP terms, remittances were up +21% YoY
    • 3-month moving average growth rate accelerates to +4.3% YoY
  • Faster YoY growth rates across regions for land-based and sea-based remittances
  • YT-November remittances are up 3.3% to US$29.38bn, trending behind the BSP’s 2022 target (+4%)

PROPERTY
Makati land parcel sold at P1.12mn/sqm
  • Chemical Industries of the Philippines (CIP PM) sold its 1,009 sqm property along Arnaiz Avenue, Makati for P1.13bn
    • The property was sold to Keyland Makati Properties
  • We assign P1.5mn/sqm on Ayala Land’s Makati CBD land bank, which is located in more central areas than the land parcel that was recently sold
  • We believe this transaction implies that land prices are comparable (or slightly higher) than pre-COVID levels
PROPERTY
SEC approves share-for-asset swap between ALI and AC/Mermac
  • Ayala Land issued 312mn new ALI shares (at P55.80/share) in exchange for property assets of its parent, Ayala Corp, and Mermac (which owns 48% of AC)
    • This will increase Ayala Land’s shares outstanding by 2%
  • This transaction was announced in January 2022 but was only approved by the SEC recently
  • It is already in our forecasts/estimates
  • The property assets are:
    • AC’s 50% ownership in Ayala Hotels – this owns the ALP project Park Central Towers and the land on which Manila Peninsula is built
    • 633 hectares of agricultural land in Darong, Sta. Cruz, Davao del Sur
    • AC and Mermac’s office units and parking slots
    • AC’s Honda Pasig Property – which will be converted into an Avida residential project
    • AC’s Caluan Property – which will be turned into a BellaVita (socialized housing) project
  • ALI is trading at 37% discount to NAV, 1.8X FY23E P/B, and 20.0X FY22E P/E
CONGLOS
GT Capital interested in raising stake in Metro Pacific
  • GT Capital (GTCAP PM) is “open to discuss (opportunities)”, according to chairman Alfred Ty
    • Mr. Ty was asked if GT Capital was keen on expanding its 15.6% stake in Metro Pacific (MPI PM)
  • There is nothing definite yet although MPIC has always been in talks with interested investors
  • As at end-Sep 2022, GTCAP had P14.2bn in cash at the parent and P93bn in gross debt (including prefs)
    • GTCAP receives c. P8-9bn of dividend income annually, sufficient to cover debt service (c. P5bn)
