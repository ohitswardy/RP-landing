REGIS REPORT

12 July 2023

The May trade deficit amounted to US$4.4bn, an improvement from Apr as imports dropped 9%. The cumulative trade deficit to May now stands at US$24bn (US$4.8bn average per month). For the year, the government is forecasting a trade deficit of US$60bn.

+++

Vehicle sales reported by CAMPI for June reached 36,311 units, 27% higher YoY. Sales of the Toyota brand were 16,381 for the month, 14% higher YoY. This takes the 1H unit sales figure to 93,575 (+17%).

The relative market share of Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) within CAMPI was 45% for the month, a slight dip from May. Nonetheless, the firm appears on track to meet our full-year assumption of 192k units.

+++

The 13th Division of the Court of Appeals (CA) has made permanent the injunction allowing San Miguel’s (SMC PM) two subsidiaries (South Premier (SPPC) and San Miguel Energy Corp (SMEC)) to charge higher power rates. The 13th Division issued the order that curiously overrode one a separate Division (16th) issued against SMEC even if the case was heard by the 16th.

The CA order will technically allow SMC to renege on two of its fixed price contracts (which became loss-making when fuel prices soared). In essence, the 13th Division gives SMC free rein to charge higher power prices that will ultimately be paid by consumers. Note, however, that the regulator (ERC) already has a pending petition with the Supreme Court to annul the decision of the CA 13th Division.

+++

The parent company of Holcim Philippines (HLCM PM) has initiated a tender offer for the remaining 5% public float of HLCM. The float of HLCM is now down to just a tad over 5% following Holderfin’s acquisition of 9% of HLCM from Sumitomo.

The tender offer price of P5.33/share looks low, frankly, at an EV/MT of just around US$90. Nonetheless, Holderfin only needs to get the float below 5.0% from the current 5.05%. HLCM minorities likely have little choice but to participate in the tender.

MACRO
Trade deficit narrows to US$4.4bn in May
  • The country’s trade deficit shrunk to US$4.4bn in May, down 21% YoY and 9% MoM
    • Due to a rebound in exports (+2% YoY) and a sustained drop in imports
  • Imports fell 9% YoY due largely to a drop in the value of fuel imports (-32% YoY) and raw materials (-12%)
    • Regional gasoline and diesel prices were down by 39% YoY in May
    • Consumer goods imports surged by 25% YoY buoyed by passenger cars, home appliances, and food
    • Capital goods imports declined by 6% YoY on lower telecom equipment and electrical machinery
  • Exports rose 1.9% YoY in May, snapping a consecutive five-month decline
    • Due to a recovery of exports to China (+23% YoY)
  • For 5M23, the trade deficit is unchanged YoY at US$23.99bn
    • The government is forecasting a trade deficit of c. US$60bn for 2023 (~14% of GDP)
    • The trade deficit in 2022 stood at US$58.244bn (14% of GDP)

CONSUMER
Vehicle sales rise 27% YoY in June
  • Vehicle sales, as reported by CAMPI, jumped 27% YoY in June (-5% MoM) to 36,311 units
    • Brings 1H23 sales to 202,415 units, up 31% YoY
  • Toyota sold 16,381 units, up 14% YoY but down 8% MoM
    • We estimate Toyota’s market share dipped further to 45.1% in June from 46.8% in May
    • Brings 1H23 sales to 93,575 units, up 17% YoY
    • To reach our target of 192k units for FY23 (+10% YoY), Toyota only needs to sell 16.4k units per month for the rest of the year
  • Toyota is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP) and accounts for 28% of profit/NAV
  • GTCAP is trading at a 54% discount to its market-based NAV of P1095/sh and 5x 2023E PER

POWER
Yellow alert in Luzon grid triggered by tripping of San Lorenzo plant
  • The National Grid Corporation of the Philippines (NGCP) issued a yellow alert in the Luzon grid on Tuesday from 1pm to 4pm due to a tripping of the San Lorenzo Natural Gas Power Plant Modules 50 and 60 worth 267MW, according to the DOE
  • Data from NGCP says the available capacity stood at 12,705MW while peak demand in Luzon was 12,222MW
  • A yellow alert is issued when the operating margin is insufficient to meet the transmission grid’s regulating and contingency requirements
  • Generators on forced outage total 1,897MW while generators with de-rated capacities total 787MW
POWER
ACEN, Norfund approve JV to provide solar power to telco towers in the Philippines
  • ACEN Corp. (ACEN PM) plans to enter a joint venture with Norway's investment fund Norfund to provide solar power to telecommunications towers in the Philippines.
    • Proposed joint venture aims to fund projects by YMP Telecom Power, Inc. to offer sustainable and affordable telecom tower solarization solutions in the country.
    • Norfund is Norway's investment fund for developing countries, with a government commitment of 26.9 billion Norwegian krone ($2.5 billion).
    • YMP is a leading Southeast Asian company in distributed renewable energy, focusing on decarbonizing telecom operators and tower companies by designing, installing, and maintaining solar and solar-hybrid power plants
  • ACEN and Norfund are finalizing the terms of the joint venture, with definitive agreements to be signed later
  • ACEN aims to achieve 20 gigawatts of attributable renewable energy capacity by 2030 and currently has around 4,200 megawatts of attributable capacity across several countries
POWER
Aboitiz Power subsidiary APX ties up with UGEP for solar projects
  • Aboitiz Power Corp. (AP PM), through its subsidiary Aboitiz Power Distributed Renewables, Inc. (APX), has partnered with Upgrade Energy Philippines, Inc. (UGEP) to pursue solar rooftop projects in the Philippines
  • UGEP is focused on the development, engineering, procurement, and construction of renewable energy projects
    • Committed to developing 500 MW of utility-scale and 100 MW of commercial-scale renewable energy by 2030
  • AP and UGEP aim to expand solar rooftop and ground-mounted solar projects for commercial and industrial businesses in the country.
  • Partnership aims to provide sustainable and customer-focused solar energy solutions that meet the unique requirements of power consumers
  • APX is involved in the development and operations of AP’s distributed energy resources and facilities
    • APX operates under AP's renewables brand Cleanergy, which has a target of reaching 4,600 MW of renewable energy capacities by 2030.
  • Joint venture will leverage UGEP's capabilities to encompass the development, construction, and long-term operations and maintenance aspects of solar projects. The partnership aims to accelerate customer-centricity and support the country's energy transition goals.
  • UGEP sees the joint venture as an opportunity to promote the widespread adoption of renewable energy and provide customized solutions for business efficiency and environmental stewardship
INFRASTRUCUTRE
Holcim parent sets tender offer price at P5.33/sh
  • Holderfin BV is offering to buy the public float of Holcim Philippines (HLCM PM) for P5.33/sh
    • 325.6mn HLCM common shares, equivalent to 5.05% of its issued and outstanding shares, are currently owned by the public
    • Together with its affiliates, Union Cement and Cemco Holdings, Holderfin owns 94.95% of HLCM today
    • Under the tender offer rules for voluntary delisting, it only needs to hold at least 95% of HLCM’s total issued/outstanding shares to be delisted
  • The tender offer price of P5.33/sh is higher than the price (P3.92/sh) at which Holderfin bought Sumitomo’s 9.22% stake in HLCM last June 29
    • We estimate the tender offer price is equivalent to US$87 EV/MT, lower than replacement cost (US$100-150 per MT)
    • While the valuation is higher than the valuation given to Sumitomo (US$64 EV/MT), it is broadly in line with Cemex Philippines’ (CHP PM) tender offer (~US$94/MT) last Feb 2023
  • Tender offer period: 10 July 2023 to 30 August 2023
