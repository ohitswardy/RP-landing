REGIS REPORT

11 December 2023

Universal Entertainment, the firm which controls the integrated resort Okada Manila, disclosed that it has signed a preliminary deal to acquire the Cebu assets of PH Resorts (PHR PM). These assets pertain to the properties as well as the gaming licenses of what was supposed to be Emerald Bay Resort and Casino. Emerald Bay was/is supposed to be the casino resort of the Udenna group, controlled by the Davao-based Dennis Uy.

The parties are aiming to close the transaction by June 2024, with the prospective new owner hoping to operate by 2026. We don’t expect the operation of Emerald Bay (or whatever it might be called) in Cebu to have a material impact on the competitive dynamics of the existing operations in Entertainment City (in Metro Manila).

Completion of a deal is also by no means certain. There have been two previous announcements of a buyer for the gaming assets. The first potential buyer in 2021, Bloomberry (BLOOM PM), provided a P1bn (US$18m) deposit but backed out after conducting due diligence. The deal with the next potential buyer, a local Cebu-based property / hotel developer, also fell through.

For Udenna, it’s unlikely to receive much from the asset sale, in our view. A sale is likely to be barely enough (if at all) to pay PHR’s creditors. PHR has ~P7bn (US$130m) in bank debt and the group has also yet to return the P1bn deposit given by BLOOM.

MACRO
Tourist arrivals revert to 64% of pre-Covid in November
  • Tourist arrivals in November reached 440k, equivalent to 64% of 2019 average
    • This is up from 407k in October (59% of pre-Covid)
    • Due to higher visitor arrivals from Korea, USA, and Japan, which were 71%, 75%, 46% of pre-covid levels, respectively
    • Chinese tourists still lagging, at just 15% of pre-Covid levels
  • As of 11M23, tourist arrivals reached 4.89mn, equivalent to 65% of 11M2019

 

GAMING
Okada operator signs deal for majority stake in Emerald Bay Resort in Cebu
  • Universal Entertainment Corp said its subsidiary Tiger Resort, Leisure and Entertainmet, Incorporated (TRLEI), which manages the Okada Manila resort has reached a preliminary deal with a subsidiary of Dennis Uy’s PH Resorts Group Holdings
  • The agreement is for buying stakes in two of PH Trvel and Leisure Holdings’ subsidiaries – Lapulapu Lesiure and Lapulapu Land, which are working on the Emerald Bay Resort project in Cebu
  • Uy previously signed agreements with potential buyers, namely Bloomberry Resorts in 2022 and Cebu-based developer AppleOne in 2023
  • The definitive agreement between the two companies could be executed by July 2024, with the goal of having the Emerald Bay Resort and Casino open in 2026
  • A new casino resort in Cebu may not materially impact the competitive landscape (primarily Entertainment City operators) given the much smaller market and niche product offering of the location
  • However, a sale could improve the gearing situation of PH Resorts and the Udenna Group
