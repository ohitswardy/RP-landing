  • Security Bank (SECB PM) (-) - Headline NPAT of P4.9bn, was down 21% YoY and was behind Regis and consensus. BANKS
SECB 1H23 - strong NIMs, but trading/fx losses drag NPAT
  • Security Bank (SECB PM) reported 1H23 NPAT of P4.9bn, down 21% YoY, and tracking behind Regis (FYE P10.7bn) and consensus (P11.2bn). Most P&L line items were broadly in line, but trading/fx losses in 2Q23 dragged profitability. Loans contracted 1% YoY, as corporate loans shrank 9% YoY; we suspect the bank shied away from deals offering thin spreads. Meanwhile, consumer/MSME loans jumped 19% YoY, now accounting for 28% of total loans, nearing the 29% peak in 2019. As a result, NIMs surged 56bps QoQ, driven by loan yields that spiked 70bps QoQ, and a seeming moderation in deposit competition. NPLs were down 6bps QoQ to 3.06%, while coverage inched up to 100% (was 99% in 1Q). We are encouraged by the strong core numbers, but remain wary of trading operations as the yield curve has shifted up in recent weeks.

Some highlights:

  • Loans contracted 1.1% YoY and 0.8% QoQ, as corporate loans shrank 9% YoY, likely as SECB did not participate in aggressive loan pricing competition. Meanwhile, consumer/MSME loans jumped 19% YoY, driven by MSMEs (+62%), credit cards (+33%), auto loans (+17%), and mortgages (+15%). Loan yields spiked 70bps QoQ on the improved loan mix, and on the full-quarter impact of the higher credit card interest rate cap. Deposits shrank 8% YoY, with TDs down 15% YoY. This helped deposit costs moderate, down 9bps QoQ.
  • NIMs expanded 56bps QoQ as a result. The NIM gains were tempered by an increase in SECB’s Treasury portfolio (+28% YoY and +11% QoQ), largely in the AFS/FVOCI book.
  • Fees were up 7% YoY in 1H23, with 2Q alone up 13% YoY and QoQ.
  • Trading/fx gains halved in 1H23, as the bank booked forex losses in 2Q23, offsetting trading gains.
  • Opex was up 16% in 1H23, accelerating to +20% YoY in 2Q23 on higher IT and staffing costs as the bank beefs up its tech platform, and broadens its physical branch network.
  • Provision expense was P1.7bn, coming off a very low base in 1H22. NPLs improved slightly to 3.06%, down 6bps QoQ. We suspect that this was due in large part to aggressive write-offs in the quarter.

Management will hold a briefing (hosted by Regis) on Wednesday, 16 August, at 5pm Manila/HK/SG time. To register, send an email to investorrelationsoffice@securitybank.com.ph

 

TELCOS
Globe 2Q/1H initial comments
  • Core income down 10% in 1H.  Globe’s headline income of P14.4bn was down 27% YoY, due to the presence of gains from the partial sale of its data center business last year. Stripping out one-offs (including tower sales gains), core income for 1H was P9.9bn, down 10% YoY.  Service revenue was up +2%, as mobile revenue barely grew still (+1%). We’d characterize the results as below expectations.
  • For 2Q alone, core income of P4.8bn is 19% lower YoY.

 

  • Mynt profitability rises.  Mynt’s contribution to Globe was P972m for 1H and P567m for 2Q alone (see table above). That’s close to 12% of core income for the quarter. It is reasonable to suspect some of that profit might be due to the increased presence of gambling use cases on GCash.
  • Broadband subscribers decline.  Globe lost a net 158k broadband subscribers in 2Q, with 13k of that loss from the wired side.  Globe has now posted a net decline in wired broadband subs for three straight quarters.  Note Globe launched its prepaid fiber service in June.  See table for comparison of wired broadband subscriber additions quarterly.
