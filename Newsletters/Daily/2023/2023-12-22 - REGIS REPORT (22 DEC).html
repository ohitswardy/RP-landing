REGIS REPORT

22 December 2023

First off, wishing everyone a Merry Christmas ahead of the long 4-day weekend!

Not meaning to spoil the cheerful mood, but rice prices are again on the rise, with export prices out of Thailand bouncing back and those out of Vietnam steadily climbing to new highs. The country’s Bureau of Plant Industry reports that the Philippines has imported 3.2mn tons of rice so far this year, down from the 3.8mn tons for the full year 2022. El Niňo is forecast to bring droughts in 1Q24 or even into early 2Q24, according to government agencies. This could be a key risk to inflation, and consequently a reason that interest rate cuts might be delayed.

The good news is that we now have a full-time Secretary of Agriculture. The timely importation of rice and other food commodities could temper the impact of rising global prices. We also believe that a temporary reduction in the 35% tariff on rice imports from Vietnam and Thailand could help alleviate the pain.

 

+ + +

ICTSI’s (ICT PM) bid for the Iloilo port was considered “failed” due to incomplete documents. The Philippine Ports Authority will re-bid the contract, with the submission deadline and opening of bids set for 11 January. Considering a) ICT was the only bidder, and b) the short lead time to the next bid, we reckon chances are high that ICT will win the contract. The capex required for the port has not been disclosed. If they win, this will be ICT’s first port in the Visayas, and will add to their current portfolio of 32 ports globally, of which 9 are in the Philippines.

Ayala Corp (AC PM) disclosed that it is taking a significant minority stake in a chain of pharmacies in Northern Luzon. St Joseph Drug has 112 stores, which will add to AC Health’s Generika chain of 742, and bring it closer to its goal of 1,000 stores by 2025.

Meralco (MER PM) revised the terms for the upcoming CSP for the 1,800MW of supply intended to replace the terminated contract with San Miguel (SMC PM). Among the changes are a lower cap on the maximum offered capacity (1,200MW vs. 1,800MW) and the limitation on plant age. While the changes could encourage a better bid outcome, we think the supply tightness over the medium term will remain.

MACRO
Philippine rice imports as of mid-December hits 3.2mn MT
  • The country’s rice imports as of mid-December have reached 3.22mn MT
    • According to the Bureau of Plant Industry (BPI)
    • In 2022, total rice imports reached 3.826mn MT
  • Vietnam remained the country’s top source of imported rice, accounting for 87% (2.813mn MT) of the total import volume as of December 14
  • Vietnam FOB rice spot price has climbed to US$666/MT (vs. average of US$652/MT in November)
INFRASTRUCTURE
PPA rebids Iloilo port contract
  • The Philippine Ports Authority (PPA) decided to re-bid the 25-year contract to manage and operate Iloilo Commercial Port Complex (ICPC) in Iloilo City
    • This followed after the lone bid submitted by ICTSI (ICT PM) was declared “failed” due to the absence of a notarized Secretary’s Certificate or any document showing proof of authorization
    • ICTSI met all other eligibility criteria
  • A new invitation to bid was published on December 21
    • A pre-bid conference is set for December 29, with the deadline for bid submission and opening on January 11, 2024
    • We anticipate ICT to participate in the re-bid.  If awarded, ICPC will be its first foray in the Visayas region and will add to its current global portfolio of 32 ports
CONGLOS
Ayala Corp acquires stake in North Luzon-based pharma, St Joseph Drug
  • Ayala Corp’s healthcare unit, AC Health, acquired a “significant minority stake” in retail pharmacy, St. Joseph Drug (Joleco Resources, Inc.)
    • The definitive agreements were signed last December 15 between St. Joseph Drug and AC Health’s pharmaceutical arm, AHCHI Pharma Ventures Inc. (APV)
    • APV owns Generika Drugstore, the pioneer in generic retail pharmacies, and I.E. Medica and MedEthix, its pharma importation and distribution arms
    • Acquisition enables AC Health to strengthen its pharma footprint and reach more Filipinos throughout the country
  • St. Joseph Drug has 112 stores across Northern Luzon
    • AC Health currently has 742 Generika stores; It is targeting to have 1,000 stores by 2025
  • AC Health is a wholly owned unit of Ayala Corp (AC PM); AC Health incurred P304mn net loss in 9M23
  • AC is trading at a 19% discount to its market-based NAV of P874/sh and 7x FY24E PER
POWER
Meralco heeds ERC, revises terms of CSP for 1,800MW
  • According to Meralco, they heeded the recommendation of the Energy Regulatory Commission to revise the terms of reference of the upcoming competitive selection process for 1,800MW of baseload capacity
    • The only major change was capping the maximum offered capacity at 1,200MW vs a maximum of 1,800MW previously
  • The ERC earlier raised the limitation on power plants to allow operational plants vs plants that started operations in 2020-2025
  • The CSP for the 1,800MW involves a 15-year contract beginning Dec 2024
    • This is meant to replace the capacity covered by Meralco’s 2024 PSA with San Miguel (SMC PM) which was terminated earlier this year
  • Firms that have expressed interest include GNPD, First NatGas, SP New Energy, Mariveles Power Generation, Excellent Energy Resources, Inc. and Masinloc Power Partners
OTHERS
PSE allowed to take over PDS
  • The SEC has approved the application of the PSE for exemptive relief, allowing it to acquire additional shares in the Philippine Dealing System Holdings Corp (PDS)
    • The PSE currently owns 21% of PDS and wants to increase its stake to 100%
  • This will allow the integration of the equity and fixed-income exchanges
  • The SEC directed the PSE to submit a detailed and time-bound operational plan, which shall be subject to a separate review and approval by the Commission
