REGIS REPORT

12 December 2024

The bicameral committee for the budget approved the budget appropriations for 2025 yesterday. Total budget appropriations for next year will amount to P6.35tn, which is 10% higher than this year’s (2024) budget. 

 

Among the key items, the Public Works (+11%) and Transportation (+122%) departments will see the biggest % increases in allocation outside of the transfers to local government units (+17%).

 

Overall, the allocation for primary expenditures (i.e. excluding debt services) is up 8% over the current year. The increase is very likely to be lower than nominal GDP growth, which means the 2025 budget won’t be an expansionary one. The government is still undergoing fiscal consolidation following the sharp increase in debt incurred during the pandemic.

 

+++

 

System-wide non-performing loan ratio (ex-interbank) of the domestic banks rose 14bp to 3.7% in Oct, from 3.56% the prior month. Media is highlighting that this ratio is the highest since May 2022 (=3.85%). The larger commercial/universal banks reported an NPL ratio of 3.31%, implying that the smaller banks (thrift banks, rural banks and digital banks) had significantly higher NPL ratios.

 

+++

 

Foreign ownership of the local stock market dropped 44bp to 19.7% in Nov, reflecting the exodus of international funds during the past month. Foreign ownership of PSEi firms was also down (-34bp) to 24.9%.

 

+++

 

SP New Energy (SPNEC PM) subsidiaries will borrow a combined P180bn to complete Terra Solar’s massive 3,500MW solar project.  Terra Solar (60% owned) will borrow P160bn from local banks to build the project while Terra Nueva (100% owned) will borrow P20bn to buy the land where the project will be built.  Meralco (MER PM) is the controlling shareholder of SPNEC.

 

+++

 

Filinvest Land (FLI PM) accepted 1.866bn of the 4.2bn FLI shares that were tendered and exchanged them for 597mn Filinvest REIT (FILRT PM) shares. The tender offer arguably accomplished two goals: 1) buying back FLI shares at cheap valuations, and 2) raising FILRT’s public float from 34.5% to 46.7%, giving it room for the next asset injection from Filinvest Land.

MACRO
Bicameral committee approves final version of P6.35tn budget for 2025
  • The Bicameral Conference Committee approved the final version of House Bill 1080 or the proposed 2025 budget of P6.35tn, a 10% increase from this year’s budget
  • Although details of the approved budget will still be released by around 1Q25, key notable remarks from press releases were as follows:
    • The budget for the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) rose to P1.11tn, a 23% increase from the proposed budget and a 11% increase from the 2024 budget
    • Bicam reduced the budgets for other departments: Social Welfare, Health, Agriculture, Labor, and Transportation
  • The approved Bicam version will still need to be ratified by Congress, before it can be transmitted to the President
    • The enactment of the 2025 budget is expected to take place on Dec 20
    • According to the Presidential Communications Office (PCO) Secretary Cesar Chavez

BANKS
Banks’ NPL ratio rises to 3.7% in October
  • Data from the BSP showed that the NPL ratio (ex-interbank) of all Philippine banks rose to 3.70% in October 2024 from 3.56% in September 2024
    • This was the highest level since May 2022
  • Gross NPL (ex-interbank) climbed by 1.3% MoM to P524bn while gross loans (ex-interbank) dipped by 2.5% MoM to P14.17tn (+11.1% YoY)
  • Excluding interbank and RRP, systemwide loans grew by 11.0% YoY in October from 11.3% YoY in September
    • Loan growth decelerated to productive activities such as manufacturing, trade, and real estate
    • This was offset by sustained growth in consumer loans, which accelerated to 24% YoY from 23% YoY

 

PROPERTY
POGO firms to cease operations by end-December
  • PAGCOR Chairman Alejandro Tengco said that POGO licenses will be canceled by the end of the year and the industry is on track to cease operations by end-December
  • During his State of the Nation Address in July 2024, President Marcos announced that a ban on offshore gaming would be effective by the end of the year
  • Mr. Tengco said there are now only 17 POGO firms operating, from 48 companies earlier this year
  • We estimate that the POGO industry occupies about 4% of Metro Manila office stock (equivalent to about 600,000sqm)
  • POGO firms are predominantly located in the Manila Bay area
  • Thus, we expect Manila Bay area property to be the most negatively affected by the ban
  • Colliers estimates that after the ban is fully implemented, Manila Bay area office and residential vacancies will rise from c. 30% currently to 50%
  • Although the developers we cover generally have minimal direct exposure to POGO office tenants or mainland Chinese residential buyers, a few of them have meaningful exposure to Manila Bay area property
  • 27% of SM Prime’s NAV is from its coastal redevelopment project in the Manila Bay area
    • SMPH is trading at 37% discount to NAV, 1.7X FY25E P/B, and 16.8X FY25E P/E
  • Land in the Manila Bay area comprises c. 10% of Megaworld’s NAV
    • MEG is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY25E P/B, and 3.6X FY25E P/E
POWER
SPNEC borrows P180bn to fund 3,500MW Terra Solar project; seeks ERC approval for transmission facility
  • SPNEC is reportedly borrowing a total of P180bn from local banks to fund the solar project under Terra Solar Philippines (TSPI) (see here)
    • Terra Solar project is a 3,500MW solar + 4,500MWh BESS facility, which is estimated to cost P200bn
  • Up to P160bn will be borrowed from a syndicate of banks: BDO, Security Bank, China Bank, PNB, BPI, and Metrobank
    • Proceeds will be used to fund the Terra Solar project (including transmission), pay off bridge loans, and cover other costs
  • Terra Nueva, a wholly-owned subsidiary of SPNEC, will borrow up to P20bn from Security Bank, Chinabank, and PNB
    • Terra Nueva will use the proceeds to fund land acquisition costs, meet capital commitments to the project, and repay bridge loans
    • Terra Nueva will own the project site, which will be leased to Terra Solar
  • Meanwhile, TSPI is seeking approval from the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) to establish a transmission facility for the same project (see here)
    • TSPI has appointed Meralco Industrial Engineering Services Corp. (MIESCOR) to design and construct the transmission facility
    • The total contract price amounts to P7.8bn, while about US$116.9mn (approximately P6.8bn) was allocated for offshore equipment
  • Terra Solar is 60%-owned by SPNEC; SPNEC is 50.5%-owned by Meralco (MER PM), through its MGen stake of 47.3% and Metro Pacific stake of 3.2%
    • Project is estimated to be completed in phases: Phase 1: 2,500MW by 1Q26 and Phase 2: 1,000 MW by 2027
OTHERS
Foreign ownership of Philippine market falls in November
  • We estimate that foreign ownership of the overall market fell 44bps MoM to 19.7% (in November) while foreign ownership of the PSE Index dropped 34bps MoM to 24.9%
  • Net foreign selling hit US$349mn, a reversal from US$22mn of net foreign buying in October
  • Of the 30 PSEi constituents, 3 saw increases in foreign ownership while 27 experienced declines
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were:
    • ALI – foreign ownership fell 1.36 percentage points MoM to 26.7%. Net foreign selling was the second highest for the month, at US$62mn.  
    • URC – foreign ownership dropped 58bps MoM to 22.6% as net foreign selling hit U$16.4mn, the seventh highest for the month.
    • CNVRG – foreign ownership declined 58bps MoM to 17.4%.
    • JFC – foreign ownership increased 5bps MoM to 24.1% as net foreign buying hit US$0.8mn, the third highest for the month.

 

PROPERTY
Filinvest Land concludes tender offer
  • Filinvest Land accepted 1.866bn tendered FLI shares, the full amount of its capped tender offer
    • This represents 7.7% of FLI’s outstanding shares and 24% of float shares
    • There were 4.2bn FLI shares offered
  • FLI shares were exchanged for FILRT shares at a ratio of 0.32 FILRT shares for every 1 FLI share tendered
    • 597mn FILRT shares were exchanged for 1.866bn FLI shares – this is equivalent to 12.2% of FILRT shares outstanding
  • After the transaction:
    • FLI’s public float fell from 32.1% to 26.4%
    • FILRT’s public float rose from 34.5% to 46.7%
  • We estimate that the tender offer will result in a c. 1% increase in FLI’s FY25E EPS and a c. 5% increase in its NAV/share
  • FILRT currently offers 9.4% dividend yield (based on consensus forecast)
  • FLI is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 4.8X FY25E P/E
