REGIS REPORT

02 April 2024

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) says it expects the CPI inflation print for March to be in the range of 1.7% to 2.5%, compared to the Feb print of 2.1%. Should reported inflation indeed be in the low 2s, the BSP would very likely be cutting policy rates next week. We expect the BSP to make the first of two 25bp policy rate cuts for the year next week. The March inflation figure will be released this Friday (April 4).

 

+++

 

The national government’s total debt rose by P319bn (US$5bn) in Feb, +2% MoM, to P16.63trn (US$290bn). A large part of the MoM increase was due to the two-tranche US$3.3bn bond issue executed in the month. The bond proceeds will be mainly used to pay down over US$2bn in bond maturities over the subsequent two months.

 

+++

 

The City of Makati is cutting real property taxes retroactive to the start of 2025, benefiting Makati property owners. Our expectation is that Ayala Land (ALI PM) would be a beneficiary of the cut as it remains the single largest landowner in the city. The share of Makati property in ALI’s NAV has already been diluted to ~15% over the years but the tax cut could lift earnings by probably 1% or more.

  

+++

The total sales of hard discount chain O!Save reportedly rose 128% YoY to US$237m in FY24. O!Save, which is partly owned by Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM), now has over 400 stores, less than half of the other big hard discount chain, DALI.

 

+++

 

Online gaming firm Digiplus (PLUS PM) is said to be in talks to bring the national lottery operator, PCSO, to the online world. Based on the press reports, PCSO generated revenue of over P60bn last year.

 

+++

 

Listed firm Golden MV (HVN PM) which has, on paper, a market capitalization of P1,546bn (US$27bn) reported a net profit of P999.7bn (US$17bn) for FY24. Nearly all of the “profit” came from paper gains on the revaluation of land assets. For perspective, our estimate of the NAV of Ayala Land (ALI PM) is P803bn.

MACRO
BSP guides for March inflation to settle within 1.7 to 2.5%
  • The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) forecasts March inflation to be between 1.7% to 2.5%
    • Consensus estimates March inflation to be at 2.0%, relatively unchanged vs Feb's 2.1%
  • Upward price pressures for the month reflect higher electricity rates as well as higher prices for fish and meat
    • But this is expected to be offset by easing prices of rice, fruits, and vegetables from improving domestic supply conditions alongside peso appreciation
  • The gov't targets to keep inflation within 2-4% in 2025
    • Bloomberg median consensus for the full year average is at 3.0%
    • We expect inflation to average 3.0%
  • The Philippine Statistics Authority will announce March inflation figures on April 4, Friday
  • The next Monetary Board meeting will be on April 10, Thursday
MACRO
Government debt inches up to P16.63tn in end-Feb
  • The government's total debt stock went up by 1.96% MoM (+P319.3bn) to P16.63tn as of end-Feb
    • This was due to the net issuance of new domestic and external debt which was slightly offset by the appreciation of the PHP against the USD (from P58.375 end-Jan to P57.990 end-Feb)
  • Domestic debt rose by 1.26% (+P139.6bn) MoM to P11.22tn due to the impact of net borrowings (P140.7bn)
  • External debt increased by 3.44% (+P179.64bn) MoM to P5.41tn given net foreign debt additions (P193.7bn)
    • This includes the previous global bond offering of $2.25bn USD for the 10- and 25- USD bonds as well as the $1.08bn USD for the 25-year EUR bonds
  • The current debt mix of the country's total obligations has domestic debt accounting for 67.5% and 32.5% being external borrowings
  • The year-end debt target for 2025 is pegged at P17.35tn which is 8% higher YoY than end-Dec's P16.1tn (see here)
    • This 2025 target is equivalent to 60.4% of GDP vs. 2024's debt-to-GDP ratio of 60.7%
    • The ratio is expected to further decline below 60% by 2027 (58.4%); by the end of the Marcos admin's term in 2028, it expects the debt-to-GDP ratio to fall to 56.3%

 

 

PROPERTY
Makati City government lowers real property taxes
  • The Makati City government lowered the tax rate and assessment levels for the computation of real property taxes
    • The Makati City government said that it could lower real property taxes because it no longer needed to subsidize certain districts, which are now part of Taguig City 
  • The assessed value is equivalent to the assessment level multiplied by the market value
  • Based on our initial estimates, real property taxes in Makati could fall over 95%
  • We estimate this could lead to a 1%-5% increase in Ayala Land’s net profit
  • ALI is trading at 59% discount to NAV, 1.0X FY25E P/B, and 10.5X FY25E P/E

 

 

CONSUMER
O!Save doubles sales in 2024 to P13.6bn but net loss up 72%
  • Hard discount retailer O!Save reported that net sales surged 128% to US$237mn (P13.6bn) in 2024, but losses were up 72% from 2023 as it amounted to US$32.5mn (P1.86bn)
    • Costs jumped 110% to US$266.8mn (P15.2bn)
  • Recall that Robinsons Retail Holdings (RRHI PM) entered the segment back in 2021 through a 23% stake in HD Retail Holdings (operator of O!Save stores)
  • O!Save's store count in 2024 doubled to 401 stores from 207 and it operates four distribution centers located in Luzon (Pampanga, Rizal, Bulacan, Pangasinan)
    • The direct competitor of O!Save, DALI, has over 800 stores
GAMING
DigiPlus reportedly in talks with government for online lottery operations
  • According to Bilyonaryo.com, DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) is negotiating with the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO) to bring the latter’s lotto business online
  • In 2024, PLUS’ GGR grew 175% to P75bn
  • In 2023, PCSO’s total receipts grew 7% to P61bn
    • It paid P5bn in jackpot prizes
  • PLUS is trading at 9.7X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
PROPERTY
Golden MV Holdings FY24 profit nearly hits P1trn
  • FY24 net profit of the Villar Group’s Golden MV Holdings (HVN PM) grew to P999.7bn, from P1.5bn in FY23
    • This was due to P1.33trn in fair value gains on investment properties
  • For reference, this exceeds the SM Investment net profit of P83bn and even the market capitalizations of SM Investments (P970bn) and SM Prime (P700bn)
  • The company’s revenue fell 25% to P3.6bn, as real estate sales declined 33% to P3.3bn

SM Prime to launch high-end residential project this year

  • SM Prime sees better opportunities in the high-end market
  • Mr. Jugo was previously EVP at Shang Properties (SHNG PM) and Managing Director of Ayala Land Premier

POWER
ACEN signs P34bn loan for 345MW Quezon North wind project
  • ACEN’s wholly-owned subsidiaries ACEN Global Dev’t Corp. and GIGA Ace 6 signed an Omnibus Loan and Security Agreement worth P34.4bn
    • Senior Lenders include BPI, BDO, and RCBC while BDO Trust Investment Group acted as facility agent, paying agent, and security trustee
    • Loan will partially finance the 345MWp Quezon North Onshore Wind Power Project (Isla Wind)
  • ACEN’s attributable net debt obligations (excluding P25bn prefs and P14.5bn of lease liabilities) stood at P173.5bn in end-2024 while net gearing stood at 1.2x
  • ACEN is looking to spend P70bn on capex this year, up from P44bn in 2024
  • As of end-2024, ACEN had 6,978MW of att. RE capacity, of which 3,284 MW are operating, 2,315 MW are under construction, and 1,379 MW are committed
UTILITIES
Manila Water says it's on track with P95bn capex program
  • Manila Water is moving forward with its RY2023-2027 capex program of P95bn
    • Company said that it will be undertaking fundraising activities to support its operating and capital expenditures from 2023-2047
  • Note that MWC has so far spent P58bn on opex and capex (i.e. “spending”) from 2023-2024
    • The company is lagging its RY2023-2027 committed spending of P175bn
  • As of end-2024, MWC’s consolidated gross debt stood at P106.4bn while cash stood at P7.4bn
    • Consolidated gross debt-to-equity stood at P1.21x (covenant of 3.1x applies to stand alone FS)
    • Last Oct 2024, the company’s lenders consented to the removal of debt service coverage ratio (1.2x) among the parent’s loan covenants
  • MWC has 289m shares in treasury, which could be sold to raise cash/capital to allow it to spend to meet its commitments
