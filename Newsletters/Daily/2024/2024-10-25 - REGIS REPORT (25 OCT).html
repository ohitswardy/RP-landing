REGIS REPORT

25 October 2024

Aboitiz Power (AP PM) announced headline profit of P27.3bn (+2%) for 9M24, with core profit also rising +2% to P27.2bn.  Beneficial EBITDA was up 12% but higher depreciation charges from the official operation of GNPD trimmed profit.  Volumes for its generation business (including RES) rose 2% while the unit sales for the DU segment was up 8% for 9M.

AP will hold its briefing on Nov 4.

+++

The government’s budget deficit for Sept widened to P273bn (+9%) as expenditures rose 17%. YT-Sept, the deficit is now at P970bn, down by 1%.

+++

Jefferies global strategist Christopher Wood (christopher.wood@jefferies.com) raises his weighting for Philippine equities and takes us through his thoughts on the bond markets, Chinese stimulus and its property markets as well as the opaque world private credit / lending.

Wood’s weighting for PH equities is now up by another 50bp to 2.5%.

MACRO
Budget deficit widens to P273bn in September
  • The government ran a budget deficit of P273bn in September, 8.9% YoY wider
    • Due to a rebound in expenditures
  • Revenues grew 17.3% YoY, with tax collections up 8.5% and non-tax revenues doubling
    • BIR collections rose 15% due to the higher personal income taxes from government workers’ salary hike and higher collections from documentary stamp tax
    • BOC collections fell 3%, which was attributed to the double-digit negative growth in import duties partly due to tariff reduction of some commodities, as well as smuggling
    • Non-tax revenues doubled to P49bn due to the upfront fee from the new concessionaire of NAIA
  • Expenditures rose 13% YoY, as primary spending rose 15% YoY
    • Interest expenses rose by just 3% YoY
  • Brings YT-September deficit to P970bn, down by 1.3% YoY
    • The government is targeting the deficit to fall by 1.8% YoY to P1.48tn, equivalent to 5.6% of GDP




POWER
SPNEC defers start of Nueva Ecija solar farm project to 2025-2027
  • SPNEC announced that it is targeting to start commercial operations of the Phase 1A 50 MW solar farm by 4Q2025
    • Project has been installed but was not yet operational due to delays in the construction of the connection asset due to right-of-way challenges
    • It began construction of Phase 1A in December 2021 and originally targeted to complete by mid-2024
  • Project is part of its 500MW Sta. Rosa Nueva Ecija 2 Solar Power project (NE 2)
    • The first phase will have a capacity of 225 MW. We suspect Phase 1B (175 MW), originally targeted for 4Q2025, could also be delayed
    • The second phase involves a 275-MW solar power plant
POWER
Aboitiz Power 9M core income up 2% to P27bn
  • AP’s core income (ex- one-offs) for 9M24 rose 2% to P27.2bn from P26.7bn
  • Core income trending below Regis estimates but ahead of Street’s
  • Headline income for 9M was P27.3bn and “beneficial” EBITDA rose 12% to P56bn
  • Generation business saw unit sales rise by 2% with the addition of two solar farms
  • Volumes for distribution business grew 8%
