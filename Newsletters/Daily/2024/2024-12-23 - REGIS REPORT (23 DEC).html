BSP Governor Remolona said that the Central Bank could cut its policy rate during the first Monetary Board Meeting in February. We forecast the BSP to cut its policy rate by a total of 75 basis points next year, the same level telegraphed by Finance Secretary Recto last week.

+++

We published a report on Security Bank (SECB PM), attempting to assess its recently announced purchase of a 25% stake in Home Credit Philippines (HCPH), given the absence of detail in the official disclosures. Based on HCPH’s SEC filings, the company posted impressive figures – loan growth of c40% YoY in both 2022 and 2023 and NIMs of 30-34%. And despite high credit cost, HCPH booked ROE of 25% in 2022 and 20% in 2023. Assuming a base case that HCPH sustained its 20% ROE, we calculate that SECB paid around 2.34x P/B and 13x P/E for 2024E. These seem fair, given the high-growth, high-ROE business of HCPH.

To the upside, HCPH deepens SECB’s penetration into the high-margin, under-penetrated mass consumer finance segment. To the downside, though, we estimate that the transaction will knock c100bps off SECB’s CET-1 ratios (c13% as of 9M24), which could limit loan growth potential at the parent bank.  Overall, we have a neutral view the deal – pending firmer details, of course. We maintain our Buy recommendation, and P160/sh target price on the stock.

Vehicle sales of CAMPI members 9% YoY in November (to 41k units) and the YT-November period (to 425k units). Meanwhile, sales of Toyota Motor Philippines grew 6% YoY in November (to 19k units). This brought the YT-November sales to 198k units, up 10% YoY and tracking ahead of both management guidance and REGIS forecast.

+++

ACEN (ACEN PM) plans to spend at least P70bn in capex next year (from c. P50bn this year) and we expect this to rise even further in succeeding years. We reckon that the company will need to spend US$2bn annually in order to achieve its 2030 target 20GW capacity. This will likely necessitate equity raising as early as next year. ACEN President Eric Francia previously said that the company could raise up to US$6bn over the coming years.

MACRO
BSP remains open to more rate cuts in 2025
  • BSP Governor Remolona stated that they are open to delivering policy rate cuts as early as its first meeting in 2025
    • The BSP has finalized its calendar for 2025, with six monetary policy meetings scheduled but exact dates yet to be disclosed
    • To note, first monetary policy meeting is usually held on the 3rd week of February
  • He emphasized BSP's reliance on data and will continue to monitor inflation which are expected to ease in 2025 (3.4%) with a slight pickup in 2026 (3.7%) based on BSP's risk-adjusted inflation forecasts
  • Remolona also stated that the BSP does not directly track US policy rate changes but instead assesses the broader implication of Fed decisions on exchange rate and inflationary pressures
    • He commented that the Fed is less dovish than before but states that the BSP is on the same trajectory neither more nor less dovish
  • We expect the BSP to cut its policy rate by 75bps in 2025
    • Jefferies expects the Fed to cut rates by another 100bps for 2025
