REGIS REPORT

08 July 2024

The statements by Bangko Sentral (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona following the latest CPI print leave the door open for a policy rate reduction in Aug.  June CPI inflation of 3.7% was lower than expected, as energy prices dropped.  Notable was the MoM and YoY decline in electricity (utility) prices due to the regulator’s order to defer some charges.  This is likely to bounce back sharply in the coming months.

Nonetheless, the BSP seems to be leaning towards a rate cut in mid-Aug, which would be before the full impact of the projected drop in food prices (this month, July).

+++

The provincial government of Bulacan passed an ordinance banning the operation of POGOs in the province.  This follows the brouhaha over the discovery of illegal gaming (fraud) operations in two other provinces in Central Luzon (Tarlac and Pampanga). Bulacan itself is not a significant location for POGOs. The question is whether the prevaiing backlash against them will eventually spill over to local governments elsewhere (i.e. within Metro Manila).

+++

According to news media, First Gen (FGEN PM) has deferred the delivery of its LNG cargo.  The apparent reason for the deferral was sufficiency of its other fuel source (Malampaya).  FGEN’s ability to pass on the cost of LNG has also been held up due to regulatory bottlenecks relating to the cost of re-gasification of the LNG. We expect these bottlenecks to eventually be resolved,

Meanwhile, it was also disclosed that FGEN subsidiary Energy Development Corp (EDC) began operating its Palayan geothermal plant, a modest 29MW addition to the Bacon-Manito (BacMan) complex.

MACRO
Lower energy prices drive June inflation lower to 3.7%
  • Headline inflation eased to 3.7% in June from 3.9% in May
    • Came in lower than Street estimates of 3.9% and at the lower-end of the BSP’s forecast range of 3.4%-4.2%
    • On a MoM basis, CPI was flat, moderating slightly after rising +0.1% in May
    • Attributed to lower energy costs
      • Electricity CPI down by 13% YoY, 6.3% MoM due to implementation of staggered payment scheme to temper impact of higher generation costs in May
      • Operation of transport equipment eased to 3.5% YoY from 5.2% YoY on lower gasoline prices
    • Food inflation rose to 6.1% from 5.8% due to faster increases in vegetable and meat prices, with rice inflation remained elevated at 22.5% YoY (+0.1% MoM)
  • Core inflation was steady at 3.1%, with non-food “core” CPI also steady at 3.3%
  • Brings 1H24 average inflation rate to 3.6%
    • BSP’s baseline inflation forecast for 2024 at 3.3%
  • BSP Governor Remolona said that even if inflation for the upcoming month of July breaches its target band of 2-4%, it won’t derail the monetary board’s planned rate cut on Aug 15
    • Governor Remolona previously said that the BSP could cut rates by 50bps this year, 25bps in August and another 25bps in 4Q
    • Next monetary board meeting on August 15

 

MACRO
GIR settles at US$104.7bn in end-June
  • Preliminary data showed the country’s GIR settled at US$104.7bn in end-June, down by 0.3% MoM (-US$315mn)
  • Represents 7.7 months’ worth of imports
  • MoM decline attributed to national government’s foreign currency payments and lower gold prices

 

POWER
First Gen defers LNG purchase; EDC completes capacity expansion in Albay
  • First Gen (FGEN) has postponed the delivery of its fifth LNG cargo due to sufficient supply
    • Another reason for the deferment is the uncertainty over the ERC’s decision to allow full cost recovery over its LNG cost
  • Meanwhile, its renewable energy subsidiary, Energy Development Corp. turned on its 28.9 MW geothermal plant in Manito, Albay
    • This increased the capacity of its existing 140 MW Bacon-Manito (BacMan) facility
    • The P7bn project was synchronized to the grid in January and is one of the four geothermal projects to be operational this year
    • EDC’s expansion projects totaled P28bn and includes other projects such as the 28MW Mahanagdong Binary in Leyte, 20MW Tanawon Binary in BacMan, and 5.6MW Binary in Negros Occidental
RETAIL
DALI ordered to remove three products after trademark complaint
  • DALI Everyday Grocer pulled out three products off its shelves following the filing of a complaint for trademark infringement, unfair competition, and copyright infringement by Nutri-Asia Inc before the Intellectual Property Office of the Philippines (IPOPHL)
    • DALI sells KULINA banana ketchup, Raja Puro vinegar, and soy sauce
    • These products are packaged similarly to Datu Puti and UFC products which are manufactured and popularized by Nutri-Asia
PROPERTY/GAMING
Bulacan province bans POGO firms
  • The Bulacan Provincial Board passed an ordinance banning POGO firms in the province
    • In Metro Manila, Pasig City and Valenzuela City have passed similar ordinances
  • There appear to be limited POGO operations in Bulacan
  • POGO firms are predominantly in the Manila Bay area (Pasay City and Paranaque City)
  • We estimate that offshore gaming firms, now called Internet Gaming Licensees (IGL), occupy about 900,000sqm of Metro Manila office space (about 6% of Metro Manila office stock)
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 49% of Filinvest Land’s NAV – POGOs occupy c. 5% of office space
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV – POGOs occupy c. 5% of office space
    • 28% of Robinsons Land’s NAV – POGOs occupy 1% of office space
  • FLI is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 5.0X FY24E P/E
  • MEG is trading at 71% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 3.3X FY24E P/E
  • RLC is trading at 65% discount to NAV, 0.5X FY24E P/B, and 5.8X FY24E P/E
