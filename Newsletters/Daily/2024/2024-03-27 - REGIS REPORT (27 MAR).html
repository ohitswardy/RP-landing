REGIS REPORT

27 March 2024

SM Investments (SM PM) is buying 29% of Citicore REIT (CREIT PM) for P5bn, equivalent to P2.65/share. The acquisition price is roughly equivalent to the close yesterday (P2.70/share). CREIT is a subsidiary of renewable power developer Citicore Renewable Energy Corp (CREC). CREIT collects lease revenue from some of CREC’s solar farms.

It is a curious transaction for SM as there doesn’t appear to be any strategic or synergistic rationale (beyond greater adoption of renewable energy by the group). We are thus inclined to believe it is an opportunistic deal (notwithstanding the tiny discount) that involves other parts of both groups. CREIT and CREC are controlled by Edgar Saavedra, who is also the principal behind Megawide (MWIDE PM). MWIDE has had extensive dealings with SM and banking unit BDO (BDO PM).

+++

Globe (GLO PM) closed the sale of some more telecom towers over the last few days. On our tally, Globe’s completed the sale of ~P62bn worth of towers, out of the P96bn it signed in 2022. The company has been moving to reduce debt and produce positive free cash flow after enormous capex spend in the last three years. Globe aims to become free cash positive again by next year.

+++

Electricity spot prices climbed 32% in March to P5.26/kwh, a consequence of rising demand as well as outages during the period. Demand during the period was up 6.1% in Luzon, 6.4% in the Visayas and 8.4% in Mindanao. We expect spot prices to remain elevated over the next few months until the rains come in June (possible amplified by La Nina).

Aboitiz Power (AP PM) and Semirara (SCC PM) are proportionately more exposed to the spot market. More than half of SCC’s capacity is currently available for spot sales. Around 16% of Aboitiz Power’s capacity was available for spot sales last year. 

+++

Meanwhile, JG Summit (JGS PM) and airline subsidiary Cebu Air (CEB PM) both reported FY23 results. JG Summit’s income tripled to P19.6bn (vs. P6.2bn) for the year, due in no small part to the turnaround at Cebu Air. Cebu Air reported a profit of P7.9bn from a net loss of P14bn in FY22.
