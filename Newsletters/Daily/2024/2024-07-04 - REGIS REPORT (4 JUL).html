REGIS REPORT

04 July 2024

Malacañang Palace disclosed that the resignations of two members of the Monetary Board of the Bangko Sentral (BSP) were accepted.  Both were appointed by the Duterte administration. The departures create two vacancies on the seven-member MB, which will still be able to function as only four votes are needed to carry any MB decisions.

+++

Tourist arrivals were up only 2% in June and still well below (-33%) the levels pre-pandemic (2019). For 1H24, tourist arrivals numbered 3.03m, up 12%.

+++

The Speaker of the House Martin Romualdez has made known his views on proposals to prohibit online gaming firms (“POGOs”).  He believes banning them would only drive them underground and create more problems.  He is, instead, advocating “strict regulation”.

Proposals to ban POGOs have been gaining support in recent weeks following the discovery of illegal activities being conducted by some and the rising geopolitical tensions in the South China Sea.

MACRO
Palace accepts resignations of two Monetary Board members
  • The resignations of Monetary Board members Anita Linda Aquino and Bruce Tolentino were effective on June 30, 2024
  • Aquino and Tolentino were embroiled in a ghost employee scandal
    • The ghost employees, often political appointees, were included in the BSP payroll despite not performing substantive work
    • Investigations by the BSP found that there were 4 employees involved and 2 supervisors who had vouched for them
  • Malacañang has yet to issue a statement regarding the resignations or potential replacements
  • The Central Bank said the policymaking body would still be able to fulfill its function with the current 5 members
MACRO
Delays stall Maharlika’s organizational structure
  • State-run Maharlika Investment Corp. (MIC) still has not received approval for its organizational committee
    • According to MIC president and CEO Rafael Consing
    • This means that the company still has no manpower to jumpstart its investment portfolio
    • Mr. Consing initially targeted to complete its organizational structure in 1Q but he hopes the matter to be resolve within four weeks
  • The Philippine Star reported that the delay is coming from the Department of Finance and the Governance Commission for GOCCs (GCG)
  • An approval from the GCG and Office of the President is necessary for the paygrade and benefits, among others
MACRO
Tourist arrivals rise 2% YoY in June; 1H24 arrivals up 12% YoY
  • Data from the Department of Tourism showed that tourist arrivals for the month of June grew 2% YoY, 3% MoM to 459k
    • This was 67% of 2019 average
    • Korean tourist remained the top source of tourists but saw arrivals dip by 5% YoY in June
    • Chinese tourists grew 4% YoY, sharply slowing down after rising 94% YoY in 5M24 (likely due to the recent PH imposition of stricter visa requirements and tensions in the West Philippine Sea)
  • Brings 1H24 tourist arrivals to 3.03mn, up 12% YoY or 73% of 2019 average
    • The government is targeting 7.7mn tourist arrivals in 2024, up 41% YoY or 93% of 2019 levels
    • In 2023, tourist arrivals reached 5.5mn or 66% of pre-Covid

 

PROPERTY/GAMING
House Speaker favors strict regulation of POGO rather than total ban
  • House Speaker Martin Romualdez said that a total ban of the POGO industry would only lead to operators going underground
    • He prefers strict regulation of the industry instead
    • These sentiments were previously expressed by PAGCOR Chairman Alejandro Tengco
  • Earlier this year, the House Games and Amusements panel approved a bill banning Philippine Offshore Gaming Operators due to crimes linked to the sector
  • We estimate that offshore gaming firms, now called Internet Gaming Licensees (IGL), occupy about 900,000sqm of Metro Manila office space (about 6% of Metro Manila office stock)
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 49% of Filinvest Land’s NAV – POGOs occupy c. 5% of office space
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV – POGOs occupy c. 5% of office space
    • 28% of Robinsons Land’s NAV – POGOs occupy 1% of office space
  • FLI is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 5.0X FY24E P/E
  • MEG is trading at 72% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 3.3X FY24E P/E
  • RLC is trading at 65% discount to NAV, 0.5X FY24E P/B, and 5.8X FY24E P/E
PROPERTY
DoubleDragon: Ongoing Madrid hotel project tops $10M in sales
  • DoubleDragon Corp. (DD) said its ongoing hotel project in Madrid, Spain, has achieved sales exceeding USD10mn
    • According to DD, majority of these sales were recorded in 2Q24
  • DD anticipates securing over USD100mn (~P5.8bn) in contracted sales over the next 12 months driven by the momentum of its international projects
  • Hotel101-Madrid commenced construction in March and is set to become one of Madrid’s largest hotels
    • Will feature 680 rooms on a 6,593-square meter property along Avenida Fuerzas Armadas, Valdebebas
    • Ferrovial Construcción, one of Spain’s largest construction firms, is executing the project, with completion scheduled for 4Q25
  • The property is surrounded by major landmark buildings and rests nearby train stations and notable establishments including the Madrid Barajas International Airport
  • Hotel101 is eyeing to have presence in 25 countries by 2026
TELCOS
Converge taps US firm for data center sustainability
  • Converge ICT Solutions, Inc. announced a partnership with US-based Super Micro Computer, Inc. (Supermicro) to develop energy-efficient data centers aimed at reducing costs and environmental impact
    • Supermicro’s servers are said to provide exceptional AI computing capabilities and intensive deep learning tasks
    • With this tie-up, all of Converge’s data centers will be developed in line with Supermicro’s design and process
  • Converge announced in January its plan to allocate up to P5bn over the next 3 years to build data centers hosting planned digital platforms and store applications and information
    • Said data centers are intended to be constructed in Pampanga, Laguna, and Caloocan
  • The two companies recently signed a memorandum of understanding for the development of an AI-powered, “green” data center in the country
  • Supermicro is an IT solutions provider specializing in data centers and designing energy-saving servers, storage systems, and software
