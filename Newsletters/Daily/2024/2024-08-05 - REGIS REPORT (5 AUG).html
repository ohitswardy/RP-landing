REGIS REPORT

05 August 2024

North Asian markets are melting down this morning, after soft labor data came out of the US, sparking renewed worries of a hard landing. Markets adjusted expectations quickly, and are now forecasting a 50bp rate but by the September FOMC meeting (possible off-cycle cut, even?), and 125bps of cuts by end-2024 (from 50-75bps last week). The USD also weakened sharply. Check out the GREED&fear FLASH report from Jefferies’s strategist Chris Wood, attached.

Interest rate cuts and a weak dollar are potential positive catalysts for EM, and the Philippines. We remain positive on the banks, consumer, and power generation sectors. While potential interest rate cuts could see property stocks move up, we are less comfortable with the sector’s fundamentals.

 

+++

We have results from Universal Robina (URC PM) and Semirara (SCC PM) today:

 

  • Universal Robina (URC PM) (-) – Recurring profit rose 8% to P7.2bn, which appears below our expectations but in line with the Street’s. The top line is looking weak, however, with 2Q revenue largely unchanged YoY and Philippine BCF (branded) down 4% YoY in 2Q. Margins are holding up well due to lower input costs. 

For international investors>>Universal Robina - 2Q24: Top line challenged amidst persistent downtrading

For Philippine-based investors>>Universal Robina - 2Q24: Top line challenged amidst persistent downtrading

 

  • Semirara (SCC PM) (=) – Semirara held its management briefing last Friday and while the 1H24 profit of P12.59bn (-35% YoY) was largely expected, we were however surprised by two key announcements: (1) the possible 2-year extension of the mining unit’s income tax holiday, which ended last July 2024 and (2) the higher-than-expected plant availability factor guidance of 83%, which is higher than our 64% estimate. Our forecasts do not assume both developments; hence, a successful execution could materially boost our FY24E-25E profit, and consequently, dividend forecasts by 12%-25%, respectively.

Meralco (MER PM) is expecting the US$3.3bn deal to buy the LNG plants of San Miguel (SMC PM) to close within Sept.   Given the hurdles and conditions precedent (approval by the PCC, etc.), the target date does look a little optimistic.

 

+++

Ayala Corp (AC PM) is supposedly considering selling part of its very recently acquired stake in Mynt in favor of a strategic investor. We reckon such a move would make sense only if the new investor can add significant value and/or the new investor really pays up.

MGen targets to seal US$3.3bn LNG deal by September
  • Meralco PowerGen Corp. (MGen) is hoping to achieve “financial close” on its planned US$3.3bn LNG deal with Aboitiz Power (AP PM) and San Miguel Power in September
    • According to MGen President and CEO Emmanuel Rubio

  • Deal involves the buy-in of MGen and AP into SMC’s 1,278-megawatt (MW) Ilijan power plant and the 1,320-MW combined cycle power facility expected to start operations by the end of the year
  • Rubio noted that the regulatory approval could be pivotal in deciding whether they would pursue expansion of the Ilijan Phase 2 plant or if “the first phase is actually enough
    • The Ilijan Phase 2 plant, eyed for up to 1,800-MW of capacity, is operated by San Miguel’s Excellent Energy Resources Inc

TRO vs Meralco power supply bidding extended to 20 days
  • The Taguig Regional Trial Court extended the Temporary Restraining Order (TRO) issued against Meralco’s (MER PM) competitive selection process (CSP) to 20 days from 72 hours previously
