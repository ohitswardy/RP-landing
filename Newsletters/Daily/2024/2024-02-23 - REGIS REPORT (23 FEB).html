REGIS REPORT

23 February 2024

One of the positive surprises from Metrobank’s (MBT PM) results announcement (some detail below, briefing on Monday, Feb 26) yesterday was its dividend/s and the single biggest beneficiary of this is GT Capital (GTCAP PM).

MBT raised its regular dividend to P1.50/share every half year (P3.00/year) and also declared a special dividend of P2.00/share this time. This puts GTCAP on track to receive P8bn from Metrobank this 2024. Combined with expected dividends from its 51% interest in Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP), GTCAP should receive P14bn in cash.

The quantum of its cash receipts this year would put it in a comfortable position relative to its debt (net debt of P72bn as of Sept) and is likely to prompt a big jump in GTCAP’s own dividend. Our expectation for GTCAP’s dividend this year is a consensus-topping P30/share, which translates to a total of P6.5bn

BANKS
MBT surprise div P5/sh for FY24 (8.5% yield); FY24 NPAT P42.24bn in line

Metrobank (MBT PM) FY23 NPAT came in at P42.24bn, up 29% YoY, bang in line with consensus P42.2bn) and with REGIS (P43.1bn) estimates. Strong NII and fee incomes offset weak trading/fx gains. Loan growth accelerated to 7.6%, from 7.1% in 3Q. Notably, net loans were up 7.1% QoQ, signaling robust demand heading into 2024E. NIMs held steady QoQ, as did asset quality.

The big surprise was MBT’s declaration of a P5.00/sh cash dividend for FY24. The bank increased its regular dividend to P3.00 from P1.80 last year (half to be paid now, the other half in 2H24), and gave out a special P2.00/sh dividend. This is equivalent to a payout ratio to just over 50% of 2023 profits, and dividend yield of 8.5% for FY2024.

The bank will hold its earnings call on Monday, 26 February, at 4:30pm Ahead of that, some highlights from the results:

  • Loan growth (gross) accelerated to 7.6% YoY, vs 7.1% in 3Q (net loans +8.4% from 7.9%). While consumer loans remained robust, the growth eased a tad to 15.9% from 16.5% in 3Q. Commercial loans, meanwhile, sped up to 5.5% YoY, from 4.8% in 3Q23. Notably, overall loans grew 7.1% QoQ, signaling strong momentum into 2024E. Deposits grew 7.3% YoY, slowing sharply from 14.5% in 3Q23 as the bank eased off on TDs (-1% QoQ). CASA ratio improved slightly to 60% from 59% in 3Q.
  • NIMs were flat QoQ at 3.80% in 4Q23, but up 30bps YoY. The quarterly loan yield crashed 115bps QoQ; however, we believe this is likely due to a late surge in corporate loans skewing the ratio. Overall asset yields were broadly flat (-2bps QoQ). Encouragingly, deposit costs have also started to ease, down 3bps QoQ, helped by MBT’s letting go of some TDs.
  • Non-interest income disappointed, due largely to trading/fx gains (the bank booked P94mn in trading losses for FY23). Fee incomes were solid though, up 9% YoY.
  • Opex was up 14% YoY in FY23, though it did moderate to just +11% in 4Q23. Expenses were driven by manpower and IT expenses.
  • Asset quality remained robust. NPL ratio was flat QoQ at 1.70%. NPL cover slipped from 187% in 3Q23 to a still-impressive 180%, by far the highest among banks we cover.

Overall it was a solid set of results. We maintain our BUY call on Metrobank, trading at 0.67x P/bk and 5.9x PER, on an upgraded dividend yield of 8.5%.

 

POWER
DOE, DENR sign MOA to speed up offshore wind projects
  • The Department of Energy (DOE) and the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) signed a memorandum of agreement (MOA) that will simplify the issuance of an environmental compliance certificate (ECC) for offshore wind energy projects
    • MOA covers pre-development and exploration activities
    • Once DENR issues an interim ECC, developers can push through pre-development activities and will not need to undergo consultations with LGUs and communities yet
  • Other renewable technologies do not have interim ECC, and the ECC is only required during the development stage
POWER
Aboitiz Power increases equity interest in coal-fired power plant
  • AP announced yesterday its purchase of an additional 15.6% equity stake in STEAG State Power, Inc. (SPI), bringing its equity interest to 85%
    • Company purchased 48.22mn common shares and 25.76mn preferred shares for a total $11mn
    • Remaining shares are held by STEAG Power GmbH and La Filipina Uy Gongco Corp.
  • SPI operates a 210MW coal-fired thermal power plant in Mindanao, and with this investment, AP increases its attributable net sellable capacity
    • Based on their website, AP holds a total 3,962.25MW net sellable capacity
