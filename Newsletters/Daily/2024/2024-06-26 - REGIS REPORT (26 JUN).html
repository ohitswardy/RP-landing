REGIS REPORT

26 June 2024

Even before the rice tariff cut takes effect, the Secretary of Agriculture is already talking about possibly increasing the tariff rate if prices fall more sharply than anticipated (to P42 to P45/kg).

Floating the possibility of a policy reversal this early does not send a good signal about government policy stability.

+++

The Marcos administration is aiming to prioritize 28 pieces of legislation, of which 18 are considered “top priority”.

Within the “top priority” list, we would consider four as the most relevant or important to the equity markets:

  • Capital markets reform, to reduce investment friction costs and rationalize taxes
  • Amendments to the law/s on “rights of way”, to improve implementation of the state’s power of eminent domain for infrastructure projects
  • Amendments to laws on special ecozones, to adapt to the remote work environment
  • Mining laws / taxation reform, to rationalize taxes and regulation over the mining sector

The Marcos administration has been able to push through a few legislated reforms, including those on the Public-Private Partnership Code and, most recently, the Property Valuation Reform.

+++

The founder and principal behind Megaworld (MEG PM), Andrew Tan, has stepped down as president of the company and a long-time lieutenant will take his place.  The move appears to be part of the transfer of the reins to Tan’s son, Kevin, who has gradually taken over or appointed his own people to the leadership positions within the Alliance Global (AGI PM) / Megaworld group.

+++

Vice President Sara Duterte says her father and two brothers intend to run for the Senate next year, in what appears a move to protect themselves from perceived threats. If anyone needed further confirmation that the Duterte family sees the Marcos administration as inimical to their interests, this is it.

MACRO
Department of Agriculture to recommend raising rice tariffs if retail prices drop to P42-45/kg
  • Agriculture Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel, Jr. floated the idea of raising the rice import tariff if retail prices go down to as much a P42-P45 per kg in the next four months
    • Currently, the prices of local well-milled rice range between P48-55 per kg while imported well-milled rice are at P52-55 per kg
  • EO 62 reduced import tariffs on rice to 15% from 35% by July 6 until 2028, with a provision for a periodic review every four months
    • Traders recently indicated that the tariff cut could lower retail prices to P45-46/kg by July
INFRASTRUCTURE
LEDAC eyes approval of 28 priority bills by June 2025
  • National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) said the Legislative-Executive Development Advisory Council (LEDAC) agreed to prioritize the passage of 28 bills before the end of the 19th Congress in June 2025
  • 18 bills are considered “top priority” including the following:
    • Proposed changes to the Foreign Investors’ Long-Term Lease Act of 2018, which seeks to allow foreigners to lease private land (excluding agricultural land) for up to 99 years from 75 years
    • Amendments to the Rice Tariffication Act of 2020, which aims to empower the National Food Authority to buy local well milled rice or directly import rice on some occasions
    • Reforms in Philippine capital markets, which seeks to reduce taxes on stock transactions and equalizing dividend tax
    • Archipelagic Sea Lanes Act, which will “strengthen the country’s sovereignty over its archipelagic waters and maritime resources”
    • Bill amending the “Right-of-Way” Act to hasten the implementation of infrastructure projects
    • Imposition of excise taxes on single-use plastics
    • Rationalized mining fiscal regime, which seeks to impose margin-based royalties and a windfall profit tax on mining companies
    • Amendments to the Electric Power Industry Reform Act (EPIRA), which aims to give the Energy Regulatory Commission the authority to revoke franchises of underperforming concession holders
    • CREATE MORE bill, which seeks to allow the IT and BPO sectors to implement full work-from-home
    • Creation of a Department for Water Resources, which aims to centralize regulation of the water sector
  • 10 bills are considered “second priority” including the following:
    • Amendments to the 1998 Comprehensive Agrarian Reform Law by lifting restrictions on the ownership and transfer of land awarded under the program
    • Philippine Immigration Act amendments
INFRASTRUCTURE
SMC to build P323bn of new Luzon tollways
  • SMC to start construction of over P300bn in tollways within the next 12 months
  • The TRB expects SMC to start the civil works for the Northern Access Link Expressway (NALEX) this year
    • SMC has laid the groundwork for the Balintawak section of NALEX and can proceed with the toll road construction once the engineering design is approved
    • NALEX will cost SMC at least P148.3bn; Project will stretch 136km and will link Skyway Stage 3 and San Miguel’s New Manila International Airport in Bulacan
  • SMC also aims to complete the final engineering design for Southern Access Link Expressway (SALEX) within June
    • SALEX’s project cost is P152.39bn; It is 40.65-km elevated tollroad that will connect NAIA airport, Roxas Boulevard, and Skyway Stage 3
  • SMC is scheduled to push through with the first phase of the SLEX TR5, which will span 60 kilometers and cost P23bn
    • Entire SLEX T5 project will cover 417 kilometers
