  • Jollibee (JFC PM) (-) – Headline profit rose 29% to P5.7bn for 1H.  Stripping out distributions to perps / prefs, income was P5bn, +34%.  While this is a little short of our expectations, we are encouraged by the direction of revenue.  SSSG was +7.4% for 2Q, led by PH operations (+9%).

MACRO
BSP cuts policy rate by 25bps to 6.25%
  • The BSP monetary board decided to reduce its policy rate (target reverse repurchase rate) by 25bps to 6.25%
    • This was in line with consensus estimates
    • Cited weak consumption in 2Q24 GDP, as well as expectations of inflation declining in the coming months
    • Headline inflation is projected to trend downward to within the government’s 2–4% target range despite the uptick in July
    • BSP Governor Eli Remolona said the cut may only be felt next year, given lag effects
  • BSP said that the balance of risks to the inflation outlook continues to lean toward the downside for 2024 and 2025 with a modest tilt to the upside for 2026
    • See changes to inflation forecasts below
    • 7M24 average inflation rate stood at 3.7%
  • BSP Governor Remolona signaled another 25-bp cut for the remainder of the year
    • The Monetary Board will next meet on Oct. 17 and Dec. 19

MACRO
Remittances rise 2.5% YoY in June
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks grew 2.5% YoY to US$2.88bn in June
    • In PHP terms, remittances grew by 7.6% YoY
    • Strong growth rates seen from land-based remittances coming from Singapore (+4.6%), Saudi Arabia (+5.2%), UAE (+5.4%), Qatar (+6%), UK (+3.3%), and Canada (+3.3%)
    • Sea-based remittances rose 2.1% YoY
  • Brings 1H24 remittances up 2.9% YoY to US$16.25bn, broadly in line with the BSP’s target of +3% YoY

TELCOS
Follow-on offering of Dito CME Holdings Corp. (DITO PM) gets SEC nod
  • The agency approved Dito’s registration statement covering a total of 1.95 billion common shares priced at P1 to P2.15 per share
    • DITO PM closing price on 15 Aug is at PHP 1.960
  • The offer will run from September 5 to 12, in time for the listing of the shares on the main board of the Philippine Stock Exchange on September 20, according to the latest timeline submitted by the company.
POWER
First Gen gets ERC approval for cost recovery
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) allowed First Gen to recover the difference between the previously approved pass-through costs and the landed cost for LNG and the New Gas Sale Purchase Agreement
  • ERC said it allowed FGEN to recover the landed cost of the re-gasified LNG incurred during fuel supply force majeure events
REITS
AREIT appoints new President
  • Carol Mills will step down as President and CEO of AREIT
    • She will focus on her role as President and CEO of Ayala Land Offices
  • Jose Eduardo A. Quimpo II will take over as President and CEO  
    • He is concurrently Vice President and Treasurer of Ayala Land
    • Prior to joining Ayala Land this year, Mr. Quimpo held various positions in BDO Private Bank, BDO Securities, and BDO Capital
  • Mr. Quimpo plans to increase AREIT’s AUM to c. US$3bn over the next few years
    • AREIT’s AUM is currently c. US$1.5bn and is set to rise to c. US$2bn by end-2024 when the announced property-for-share swap is consummated
  • He is targeting to have AREIT included in the PSE Index as well as regional indices over the next few years
  • AREIT currently offers FY24E dividend yield of 6.0% (based on consensus forecast)
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 6.1%
