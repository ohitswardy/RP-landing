REGIS REPORT

26 July 2024

Digital payments over PesoNet and Instapay grew 26% YoY in June to P1,317bn, slower than the 33% growth in May. This was due largely to a sharp deceleration in PesoNet, where transactions of P738bn were up just 14% YoY, compared to 25% in May. To the upside, smaller-ticket payments over InstaPay still surged 45% YoY to P579bn, similar to the pace in May (46%). For the 1H24, total digital payments were up 34% to P7,796bn.

Earlier in the week, the BSP announced that, as of 2023, digital transactions made up 53% of all retail payments, exceeding the BSP’s target of 50% by 2023. More crucially, on our view, the BSP estimates that 65% of all personal payments, whether to individuals or merchants (P2X) were already digital.

The continued adoption of digital payments, particularly at the individual level, is best played through Globe (GLO PM), given its stake in GCash/Mynt, by far the country’s dominant e-wallet and payments platform.

Ayala Corp (AC PM) is raising up to P15bn from a preferred share issuance.  Funds raised are expected to be primarily for refinancing.

+++

Double Dragon (DD PM) could get some relief if reports that it secured the Dept of Foreign Affairs (DFA) as the main tenant in one of its newest office buildings is accurate.  DD is highly leveraged and disproportionately exposed to the POGO sector, so a tenant taking up as much as ~10% of its office portfolio would be welcome.

+++

Jollibee (JFC PM) says it expects the payback period for its latest acquisition, Compose Coffee, to be around 5-6 years.  This implies a return of ~15-20%, presumably on equity.

BANKS
Digital payments - growth moderates to 26% in June due to sharp PesoNet slowdown
  • Digital transactions done over the National Retail Payments System grew 26% YoY to P1,317bn. This was slower than the 33% YoY growth in May, and the 37% average over the previous 5 months. The June figure was also down 8% MoM; while June is typically a slow month, the drop was larger than usual. Still, the 1H2024 figure of P7,976bn was up a respectable 35% YoY.
  • The June deceleration was driven nearly entirely by PesoNet (large, batched transactions),  where transactions only grew by 14% YoY to P738bn, sharply slower than the 25% in May. On a MoM basis, PesoNet payments were down 11% compared to May. This takes the 1H24 total to P4,690, up 28% YoY.
  • To the upside, payments over InstaPay (up to P50,000 per transaction) continued to surge, up 45% YoY to P579bn (was +46% in May). The June print was down 4% MoM, in line with previous years’ seasonality. For the first half of the year, InstaPay clocked P3,286bn, up 45% YoY.
  • We view the continued adoption of digital payments, particularly at the smaller-ticket level, as a positive long term theme.

  • Stanley Co, who is currently COO, will take over as President and CEO beginning 1 January 2025
  • Mr. Co has been with the Robinsons Retail Group for 21 years
  • He joined the Group's DIY Segment as Division Merchandise Manager in 2003 and became its Group General Manager in 2008.
  • He was appointed as the Managing Director of the Food Segment on 1 September 2020 and became the Chief Operating Officer of RRHI effective 1 August 2023.
  • RRHI also expanded its buyback program by another P1bn
    • It has spent P6.9bn out of its existing P7bn buyback program

Globe completes sale of another P13.2bn of towers

  • This brings total gross proceeds from the tower sales to ~P85.2bn
    • Out of the total P96bn announced since 2022

CONGLOS
Ayala Corp’s planned divestment of LRT-1 stake hits snag
  • Metro Pacific is reconsidering plans to acquire Ayala Corp’s stake in Light Rail Manila Corp. (LRMC), operator of LRT-1, due to unresolved valuation issues
    • According to MPI Chairman, President, CEO Manuel V. Pangilinan
    • In 2019, MPI sold a 19% stake in LRMC to Sumitomo for P3bn
  • MPI owns a 35.8% stake in LRMC while Ayala Corp owns 35%; Other shareholders are Sumitomo (19.2%) and Macquarie Infra (10%)
  • Note that AC already achieved is “value realization” goal of US$1bn after it sold its remaining stake in Manila Water (MWC PM) in May (see here)
CONGLOS
Ayala Corp. raising P15bn from preferred share issuance
  • Ayala Corp. is looking to offer and re-issue up to P15bn worth of preferred B shares, consisting of a base amount of P10bn with an oversubscription option of up to P5bn
    • Existing preferred B1 and B2 shares pay coupons of 5.3% and 4.8%, respectively
  • As of end-March 2024, Ayala Corp.’s parent-level net debt (including prefs) stood at P181bn while its net debt-to-equity stood at 1.42x
PROPERTY
DFA headquarters to move to new DoubleDragon building
  • According to the Philippine Daily Inquirer’s Biz Buzz column, DoubleDragon won the contract to provide the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA) with its new headquarters
  • The DFA will take over almost the entire 40,000sqm DoubleDragon Tower in Pasay City
  • DoubleDragon has an office leasing portfolio of approximately 300,000sqm
CONSUMER
JFC expects payback on Compose Coffee investment in 5-6 years
  • According to JFC Chief Financial Officer Richard Shin, JFC expects that opening roughly 30 to 40 new franchisees per month could support a modest growth rate for Compose Coffee, allowing JFC to hit payback in 5-6 years.
  • Compose Coffee had 2,612 stores as of June and is ranked 2nd in the value coffee segment industry in terms of the total number of franchisees.
  • Compose Coffee currently has an 8% market share.
  • JFC will be focusing on the growth opportunities in South Korea for at least the next 5 years
