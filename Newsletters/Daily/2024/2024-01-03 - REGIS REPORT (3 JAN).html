REGIS REPORT

03 January 2024

We update our numbers for BPI (BPI PM) to reflect the completion of its merger with Robinsons Bank (RBC), which became effective on 01 Jan. To recall, BPI issued 314mn new shares – c6% of the merged entity – to the JG Summit (JGS PM) Group, which will end up with a 10% stake in the bank. The merger takes BPI to within a hair of Metrobank (MBT PM) for the #2 spot in terms of assets. Stand-alone, RBC’s attraction is its high-yielding assets, including its motorcycle and personal loan business. Beyond that, we believe the “holy grail” for BPI will be gaining access to the vast ecosystem of the JG Group, including the suppliers, tenants, and customers of sector powerhouses such as Universal Robina (URC PM), Robinsons Land (RLC PM), Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM), Cebu Air (CEB PM), and even other private businesses in the family stable. The loyalty programs alone of RRHI and CEB are likely to yield millions of potential customers that can be offered credit cards, insurance products, etc.

However, there will be some short-term pain. RBC’s profits were 3-5% of BPI’s from 2020-22, and likely lower in 2023. While there are clear upsides from lower funding cost, and improved efficiency, we forecast integration will mean EPS dilution of 3.3% in 2024E and 1.2% in 2025E. The merger will also generate some goodwill, which will reduce ROEs by 30-40bps from our previous forecasts. We trim our target price by 3.6% to P135/sh, but maintain our Buy call.

International tourist arrivals for the year 2023 reached 5.45m.  While the figure is only 66% of what it was pre-pandemic (in 2019), the figures are improving with Dec numbers at 82%.  In fact, if  one were to exclude arrivals from China, we’d already roughly match Dec 2019. Arrivals from China remain only a fraction (15%) of the official figures for 2019 but the pre-pandemic figures were likely very much padded by POGO-related arrivals.

Jollibee (JFC PM) is infusing more money into the vehicle that holds the international operations of Tim Ho Wan, the dimsum chain founded in HK. The fund size of the vehicle, Titan Dining, will be raised to SGD450m from SGD350m and JFC’s stake will rise to 92%. JFC aims to have 100 Tim Ho Wan stores in China, from the current 19.

BANKS
FX deposits jump, loans fall in 3Q23
  • The Foreign Currency Deposit Units (FCDU) of banks showed a divergence in loans and deposits, given the sharp depreciation of the PHP in recent years, particularly in 2022.
  • As of 3Q2023, FX deposits jumped to $51.8bn, up 13.2% YoY and 5.7% QoQ. This is the fastest YoY growth since the taper tantrum in 2014. In PHP terms, FX deposits grew a milder 9.3% as the PHP was particularly weak in 3Q2022. As a percentage of total deposits, FX deposits took up a 16.3% share, up more than 100bps since end-2022, and the highest level since 2018.
  • In contrast, FX loans contracted 1% YoY in USD terms to $15.5bn; in PHP terms, foreign currency loans fell a steeper 4.5% YoY. FX loans now account for just 6.9% of total loans, the first time it’s fallen below 7% since 2007.
  • FX LDRs stood at 29.9% dipping below 30% for the first time since 2011. (Banks invest a much larger portion of FX funds in sovereigns, mostly USD-denominated Philippine bonds.) With total LDRs at 69.8%, we estimate that PHP LDRs were at 77.5%. Given the reserve requirement (RRR) on PHP deposits was cut to 9.5%, banks have lots of capacity for loan growth, in either currency.

 

 

 

 

 

MACRO
FY23 tourist arrivals reach 5.45mn (66% of FY19)
  • The Department of Tourism reported that there were 5.45mn international visitor arrivals in 2023, which is 66% of 2019 levels
    • Tourist arrivals in 2023 more than doubled from the 2.6mn reported in 2022
    • South Korea remained the top source (26% of total) of tourist arrivals at 1.44mn, reverting to 72% of 2019 levels
    • USA came in second at 903k (17% of total and about 85% of pre-Covid levels)
    • Chinese tourists only reached 264k, or 15% of pre-Covid levels
  • Implied December 2023 tourist arrivals at 565k, 82% of pre-Covid levels
  • The government is targeting 7.7mn tourist arrivals in 2024, up 41% YoY and about 93% of 2019 levels

 

CONSUMER
Jollibee to expand Tim Ho Wan store network in China
  • Jollibee will increase its stake in Titan Dining from 90% to 92%
    • It will purchase the 2% stake of another partner for S$7.7mn
  • Furthermore, the fund size of Titan will increase from the current S$350mn to S$450mn in order to fund the store expansion and working capital requirements of Tim Ho Wan and the completion of other projects
  • Jollibee has joint ventures with Titan for Tim Ho Wan in China, and for operations of Tiong Bahru Bakery and Common Man Coffee Roasters in the Philippines
  • Jollibee is targeting to have 100 Tim Ho Wan stores in mainland China in the medium term
    • There are currently 19 stores in mainland China, most of which are in Shanghai
  • At the end of Nov 2023, Jollibee’s store network stood at 6,805 stores worldwide
