REGIS REPORT

23 January 2024

In a press release, Monde (MONDE PM), curiously, offered a glimpse into its operating performance in the just-ended 2023 financial year. The core income of MONDE implied in the statement would be ~P7.5bn for FY23, which is just a shade above Street estimates. The implied profit, though, has yet to include the impact of any valuation test on its alternative protein business, Quorn. More below.

+++

The competition authorities approved the purchase by PLDT (TEL PM) of Sky Cable from the Lopez group for P6.75bn (US$120m). The deal adds over 300k subscribers to PLDT’s 3.3m wired broadband count. Even before the acquisition, PLDT had just around 50% of the wired broadband market.

+++

The Department of Finance (DOF) says it expects potential cumulative revenue losses of P126bn over five years from the PIFITA tax reform proposal. PIFITA is the Passive Income and Financial Intermediary Taxation Act (PIFITA) being proposed to rationalize and reform taxation in the capital markets.

Despite the revenue loss, the authorities are hoping it will help spur more activity and deepen the capital markets.

+++

ICTSI (ICT PM) is selling a small listed Indonesian subsidiary for US$2m to rationalize its portfolio. That leaves it with one operation in that country.

MACRO
DOF expects P126bn revenue loss from PIFITA (Tax Package 4) over 5 years
  • During the hearing of the Senate Committee on Ways and Means, the DOF said that the Passive Income and Financial Intermediary Taxation Act (PIFITA) could result in lower tax revenue of P126bn from 2025 to 2029 (average of P21bn annually)
  • PIFITA seeks to:
    • Lower the transaction tax on the sale of listed stocks to 0.1% from 0.6%
    • Lower tax on interest income from 20% to 15% and raise dividend tax from 10% to 15%
    • Lower Documentary Stamp Taxes (DST) for original shares of stock from 1% to 0.75%
    • Harmonize the gross receipts tax on lending income and other income of banks to 5% (from a range of 1% to 7% currently)
    • Impose a uniform 2% premium tax on life, health, and pre-need insurance (Pre-need contributions are currently taxed at 12% VAT)
    • Remove tax exemptions on pick-up trucks
  • Many of the changes will be gradual (lowered by 1ppt annually or 0.1% annually in the case of the stock transaction tax)

TELCOS/MEDIA
PCC approves sale of Sky to PLDT
  • PLDT (TEL PM) and ABS-CBN (ABS PM) disclosed that the transaction for the former to buy Sky Cable from the Lopez group, including ABS-CBN, has been cleared by the Philippine Competition Commission (PCC). Sky Cable has a broadband business as well as pay TV (i.e., cable TV) business.
  • The deal was first announced last year.  PLDT (TEL PM) agreed to buy 100% of Sky from ABS-CBN and affiliates for P6.75bn. The deal has apparently been approved by the competition authorities even if it would result in PLDT further increasing its market share (to over 50%) in wired broadband (as opposed to all “broadband” segments, including fixed wireless).
  • Sky has around 300k to 350k broadband subscribers and a similar number of pay TV subs. PLDT had 3.28m broadband subs as of Sept 2023.
  • It’s unclear how much debt/cash Sky has, but assuming negligible levels (i.e., enterprise value is similar to equity value), the deal would translate to EV/broadband sub of around US$400. In contrast, Converge (CNVRG PM) is on US$800. That, however, is an admittedly simplistic comparison, since CNVRG has a much larger footprint (and more capable of growing subs) and Sky also has cable TV business.
CONSUMER
MONDE sees mid-teens growth in core net income in 2023
  • Based on unaudited preliminary results for FY23, MONDE expects core net income to grow by mid-teens in 2023, this implies core net income of c.P7.5bn
    • Regis FY23E: P9bn / Consensus FY23: P7.4bn
    • This implies core net income in 4Q23 alone grew 2x
  • FY23 sales grew high single-digit YoY (Regis FY23: +4%), while gross margin is in line with 2022 (31.3%)
  • The APAC BFB business continued to perform strongly in 4Q23
    • The segment posted all-time high volumes during the quarter, rising 5% YoY / 2% QoQ
    • Market share in instant noodles was 67.5% in 4Q23 up slightly vs 3Q23 (67.3%)
    • GPM improved 400bps YoY due to favorable prices of wheat and palm oil, which have been locked in until 2Q24 and 3Q24, respectively
  • Meanwhile, Quorn’s top line continues to be challenged, but still EBITDA-neutral in 4Q23
  • MONDE currently trades at 17x 2024E consensus PE
TRANSPORT
ICTSI fully divests stake in Indonesian unit
  • ICTSI agreed to sell its entire 80.19% stake in PT ICTSI Jasa Prima Tbk (KARW IJ) to PT Sarana Kelola Investa for US$2mn
    • KARW wholly owns PT PBM Olah Jasa Andal (OJA), which has concession with state-run PT Pelabuhan Indonesia II (Pelindo) at the port of Tanjung Priok in Jakarta, valid until 2028
  • Company cited rationalizing operations and redirect resources in Indonesia to focus on full concession contracts, where ICTSI controls all aspects of the business, including regulatory and commercial activities
    • ICT now only has one port in Indonesia, located in Lamongan Regency, East Java
  • Transaction is subject to approval of change of ownership application in the Indonesian Stock Exchange, among other conditions
  • We estimate KARW accounted for c. 1% of consolidated volume and <0.5% of revenues/profit
  • ICTSI trades at 7.9x FY24E EV/EBITDA and 12.3x PER
