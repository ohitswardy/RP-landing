REGIS REPORT

10 December 2024

The law amending the Rice Tariffication Act was signed, extending the life, and increasing the fund allocation of the Rice Competitive Enhancement Fund (RCEF) to support domestic rice producers. The revised law will also allow the Dept of Agriculture (DA) to again import rice during periods of inadequate domestic supply.

Meanwhile, rice prices are finally (!) dropping more meaningfully. Based on the data from the DA website, the price of well-milled rice just dropped to P40/kg as of Friday, 6 December. This is down 5% MoM from P42/kg in November, and down 15% QoQ from P47/kg in September. In theory, the price drop should have a) come earlier, and b) fallen faster as export prices of rice are already down more than 20% YTD in USD terms (~17% in PHP terms), and the import tariff was reduced from 35% to 15%.

The fact that prices took this long to fall hint at the existence of what we can euphemistically call “bureaucratic bottlenecks”. As long as these exist to a large degree, the disparity between import prices and domestic prices will stay unreasonably wide.

In any case, the continued fall in the price of the staple will come as a huge relief to lower-income households. Remember that rice is 18% of the CPI basket for the “bottom 30%” of households, according to the PSA. This supports our bullish view on consumer staples heading into 2025. Other factors contributing to our view include:

  • rising minimum wages,
  • the traditional holiday spending in 4Q,
  • election spending into 2Q25
  • weak PHP boosting the local purchasing power of remittances

Consumer firms most likely to benefit from mass-market consumption rebound would be Puregold (PGOLD PM), URC (URC PM), Monde (MONDE PM), and Century Pacific (CNPF PM).

SM Prime (SMPH PM) announced that its Board approved a share buyback program of up to P10bn, the first such program for the mall operator in over two decades. While the program is not large relative to SMPH (1.3% of market cap, 3.6% of free float), we view it as an incontrovertible sign that management views the stock as very undervalued (and is willing to put money where its mouth is).

+++

Udenna, the holding company for the business interests of the Davao-based Dennis Uy, signed an MOU with construction firm EEI (EEI PM), which is partly owned by the family of House Speaker Martin Romualdez, for the former’s Emerald Bay Casino in Mactan, Cebu. This might signal that construction of the casino complex will resume after years of inactivity but there have been at least three false starts for this casino project before.

+++

The power regulator Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) has given provisional approval for 500MW of power supply agreements (PSA) between Meralco (MER PM) as buyer, and San Miguel (SMC PM) and ACEN (ACEN PM) as suppliers.  What’s worth highlighting is that the ERC only approved a price of P5.19/kwh, for now, pending further review. SMC won the auction for this PSA at a price of P7.10/kwh (for 340MW) while ACEN secured 160MW at a price of ~P8.2/kwh.  We believe the tariffs secured at the auction are relatively high, and would result in above-average returns for the suppliers.

+++

ICTSI’s (ICT PM) announced it has secured additional clients for its port in Ecuador, which we estimate accounts for 4% of 25E consolidated revenues.

MACRO
Agri policy amendments to help PH manage rice woes
  • President Marcos signed on Monday Republic Act (RA) 12078, which will have amendments made to the Agricultural Tariffication Law of 2019 in an effort to increase the nation's rice output
    • Rice farmers will receive provisions of farm machinery and equipment, free distribution of high quality inbred certified seeds, and other interventions
  • The law also extends the life of the Rice Competitiveness Enhancement Fund (RCEF), which is sourced from the tariffs collected from rice importation until 2031
    • Annual allocation to the RCEF will be increased from P10bn currently to P30bn
  • The new law mandates that a buffer stock of rice will be maintained, equivalent to 30 days at any given time to address food security emergency situations on rice
  • Other initiatives of the law include
    • More stringent inspections and monitoring of warehouses and agricultural facilities
    • Empowering the Secretary of the Dept of Agriculture to designate entities within the DA to import rice to stabilize supply and prices when domestic supply is inadequate
MACRO
VAT refund program for non-resident tourists signed into law
  • Republic Act (RA) 12079 will establish a VAT Refund System for goods purchased at accredited retail outlets, provided that these goods are taken out of the country within 60 days and meet a minimum transaction requirement of P3,000
  • An estimated 30% increase in tourist spending is projected with the new law in place, this is set to benefit both large-scale industries and SMEs
  • The National Economic Development Authority (NEDA) projected that the law will raise P3.3bn to P5.7bn in additional revenues as well as create 4,400-7,100 jobs a year from 2024 to 2028
TRANSPORT
ICTSI secures new clients in Ecuador
  • ICTSI’s Contecon Guayaquil SA (CGSA) or its Ecuadorian business unit, has signed a new commercial agreement with five shipping lines: Wan Hai, Pacific International Lines, Cosco, Evergreen, and Yang Ming
    • The agreement covers the Asia-South America (ASA) service, which stopped in 2016, but recommenced in the first week of November
  • Together with the recently signed three-year contract with Cosco Shipping Specialized Carriers’ last September, CGSA should see its revenues rebound strongly beginning 4Q24
    • Note that CGSA was the key drag to ICT’s revenues in 9M24
    • Our forecasts assume CGSA’s revenues will rebound by 68% in 2025, after forecasting a 61% decrease in 2024
    • We estimate CGSA will account for 6% of ICT’s 25E volumes and 4% of revenues/EBITDA
  • We have a HOLD rating on ICTSI (ICT PM), as we believe uncertainties surrounding Trump’s trade policies for Mexico could overshadow strong positive earnings momentum near-term

GAMING
Udenna signs MOU with EEI for Mactan casino
  • Udenna Corp. signed an MOU EEI Corporation (EEI PM) regarding the Emerald Bay Resort and Casino Project in Mactan, Cebu
  • Emerald Bay is intended to have 140 gaming tables and 700 gaming machines
    • For reference, Solaire Entertainment City has about 400 gaming tables and 2,700 gaming machines
  • Udenna’s subsidiary, PH Resorts Group (PHR PM), holds the license for the said integrated resort
  • According to the PHR disclosure, the MOU paves the way for EEI to execute an agreement with PHR to finance, construct, and complete the Emerald Bay project
    • PHR was previously in talks with Bloomberry, Okada Manila, and Cebu-based developer Apple One
  • Travellers is also spending US$300mn for a casino in Mactan, Cebu
POWER
ERC approves power supply deals between Meralco, SMC, ACEN
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) grated provisional authority to Meralco (MER PM) to procure a total of 500MW 10-year mid-merit supply from San Miguel (SMC PM) and ACEN Corp. (ACEN PM)
    • PSAs will cover MER’s 350MW mid-merit requirement starting Feb 2025 and will increase by 150 MW by Feb 2026
  • The ERC, however, noted the applicable rate for these PSAs “shall be fixed at P5.1908 per kWh, without any escalation or adjustment”.
    • The approved fixed rate were based on average rate of other mid-merit supply to Meralco
    • ERC will need to further evaluate the higher rates, as well as the reserve price set by Meralco (P8.23/kwh)
    • Recall that San Miguel’s San Roque Hydropower’s (SRHI) offered a headline price of P7.10/kwh for 340MW
    • ACEN’s Gigasol3 unit’s offered a headline price of P8.1819/kwh for 139 MW
    • ACEN’s Santa Cruz Solar Energy Inc.’s PSA involves 21 MW at a rate of P8.1998/kwh for 21 MW (ERC has yet to release its decision on this PSA)
POWER
CREC agrees to contract 1.5GWh of BESS to Sungrow
  • Citicore Renewable Energy Corporation (CREC PM) has advanced its renewable development with a supply and services agreement of 1500MWh of Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) to Sungrow Power, a leading PV inverter and energy storage system provider
  • Sungrow will provide CREC with its Power Titan series, capable of supporting both DC and AC coupling, with its lower leverage cost of electricity and higher performance
    • These BESS systems will be used for CREC’s solar projects nationwide
      • The new systems should help in terms of grid stability, energy efficiency, and RE integration for consistent delivery of electricity
  • CREC is reportedly en route to completing its 1GW of renewable energy capacity as they currently stand on total aggregate capacity of 285MW spanning 10 plants
