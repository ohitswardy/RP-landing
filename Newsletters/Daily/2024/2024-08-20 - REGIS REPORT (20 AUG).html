REGIS REPORT

20 August 2024

The country’s gross international reserves (GIR) rose to US$106.7bn as of July, up US$1.5bn June. The GIR typically rises by that much only when there is borrowing activity, there does not appear to have been any such conducted by the government during the month (that’s publicly disclosed).

 

+++

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           

ICTSI (ICT PM) filed an arbitration case with the International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes (ICSID) contesting the move by the Honduras government to withdraw from ICSID as a venue for dispute resolution. The government of Honduras is trying to get any future disputes resolved within its local court system.  Understandably, ICTSI would want such to be resolved by a “neutral” international party.

 

+++

 

DigiPlus (PLUS PM) revealed it is applying for a license to operate gaming activities in Brazil. Seventeen other parties are applying for such a license so competition is likely to be fierce.

 

+++

 

SM Investments (SM PM) is buying a 184-hectare lot via a property-for-share swap.  Given that the lot is owned by a 96.75% subsidiary, we should see minimal practical impact from the increase in issued shares of SM. We reckon the deal is being done this way for tax reasons.

 

+++

 

Foreign ownership of Philippine listed stocks inched up by 2p to 20.1% in July.  For the PSEi components, foreign ownership rose 15bp to 24.9%. For the month, we estimate net foreign inflow into the market was US$60m.

MACRO
Balance of payments position swings to a US$62mn surplus in July; End-July GIR level rises to US$106.7bn
  • The country’s balance of payments registered a US$62mn surplus in July,
    • This was a reversal of the US$53mn deficit recorded in July 2023 and the US$155mn deficit in June 2024
    • Cited inflows from the BSP’s investments abroad and the national government’s net foreign currency deposits with the BSP
    • Brings YT-July BOP level to US$1.5bn surplus, lower than the US$2.2bn surplus recorded in the same period last year 
  • Meanwhile, the final GIR level for end-July stood at US$106.7bn, up from US$105.2bn in end-June
    • This is equivalent to 7.9 months’ worth of imports of goods
MACRO
Finance Department lowers revenue projections from mining fiscal reform bill
  • The Department of Finance lowered its projected annual revenue estimates to P6.3bn from P10.2bn previously from the mining fiscal reform bill
    • Cited lower metal prices
    • Broken down: it expects P4.5bn in incremental revenue from royalty tax inside mineral reservations, P1.1bn from outside reservations and P730mn from windfall profit tax
  • Latest proposal imposes five-tiered margin-based royalty rate and windfall profit tax with five tiers (vs. four tiers previously)
  • DoF’s proposal also includes a provision treating mining contractors as separate taxable entities under law “with respect to each and every mineral agreement it holds or operates”

 

TRANSPORT
ICTSI files ‘Request for Arbitration’ against Republic of Honduras to retain investment protections
  • ICTSI and its wholly-owned unit in Honduras, Operadora Portuaria Centroamericana, S.A. de C.V., filed a “request for arbitration” before the World Bank’s International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes (ICSID)
    • This filing should (in theory) allow the company to retain its investment protections, even after the withdrawal of the government of Honduras from the ICSID takes effect on Aug 2
    • Note that the Republic of Honduras denounced the ICSID Convention last Feb 24 under President Xiomara Castro
    • Withdrawal from ICSID did not have legislative authorization and has been questioned at local courts
  • This development will have no impact on ICTSI’s operations in Honduras and fundamentals should remain intact
    • However, it is unclear if the government of Honduras will continue to adhere to international arbitration rules should there be contractual disputes in the future
    • We estimate ICT’s port in Honduras accounts for 6% of volumes and 7% of revenues/EBITDA
GAMING
DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) applies for online gaming license in Brazil
  • Following the recent legalization of gaming and betting activities in Brazil in June 2024, DigiPlus applied for an online gaming operation license
    • The results of the license application are expected to be released by November 2024.
  • 17 other competitors also applied for a license
    • MGM Resorts (MGM US) formed a new venture with Grupo Globo, the largest media group in Latin America. Their new venture also applied for the online gaming operation license. This venture will allow MGM to tap into Grupo Global’s current 70 million daily users
  • Brazil has a population of over 200 million
    • MGM estimates there are 20 million active bettors, representing an estimated market size of more than USD$3 billion
    • Brazil has a GDP per capita of US$ 9,612 in 2022 and a median age of 34.4
    • Internet penetration is at 87%
    • The dominant online payment app, PIX, is owned by Brazil’s Central Bank and is used in 36% of all transactions in Brazil
  • PLUS is trading at 5.5X FY25E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
GAMING
Solaire patrons to receive BLOOM shares
  • Bloomberry’s Board of Directors approved the purchase of 1.94mn BLOOM shares “through the Stock Market at market price”
    • This represents 0.02% of shares outstanding
    • At the last closing price P7.70/share, the value of 1.94mn shares is P15mn
    • Bloomberry purchased 1.18mn BLOOM shares from the open market on August 19
  • These shares shall be given as a reward to Solaire’s loyal patrons as part of Solaire’s marketing program

 

CONGLOMERATES
SM acquiring property from subsidiary
  • SM Investments will be acquiring 184 hectares of land in Muntinlupa City (Susana Heights)
    • Property will be acquired from 96.75% subsidiary of SM called Intercontinental Development Corp (ICDC)
  • Acquisition will be done through a property-for-share swap
    • Number of shares to be issued was not disclosed
  • However, since ICDC is almost wholly owned, there should be almost no change in the outstanding shares
MARKET
Foreign ownership of Philippine market inches up in July
  • We estimate that foreign ownership of the overall market rose 2bps MoM to 20.1% (in July) while foreign ownership of the PSE Index expanded 15bps MoM to 24.9%
  • Net foreign buying amounted to US$60mn, a reversal from net foreign selling of US$104mn in June
  • Of the 30 PSEi constituents, 17 saw increases in foreign ownership while 13 experienced declines
  • The notable movements among PSEi constituents were:
    • PGOLD – foreign ownership rose 28bps MoM to 18.5%.  
    • URC – foreign ownership increased 26bps MoM to 22.3%. Net foreign buying hit US$13.8mn (the fourth highest for the month).
    • WLCON – foreign ownership fell 70bps MoM to 15.8%. Net foreign selling amounted to US$3.1mn (the seventh highest for the month).
    • JFC - foreign ownership declined 32bps MoM to 23.2%. It also experienced the highest net foreign selling for the month (US$12.1mn).
