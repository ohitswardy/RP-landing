REGIS REPORT

15 April 2024

Convenience store operator Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) announced its FY23 results, which emphatically showed the benefits of efforts made since the pandemic.  Profits leaped 69% to P3.5bn on the back of a 25% increase in revenue and 43% expansion in operating profit.

For 4Q alone, profit reached an unprecedented P1.2bn (+64%). Same-store sales grew 9.5% on the back of increased customer traffic.

The company will hold its earnings briefing on April 23.

+++

Jefferies’ rate cuts call of “All or Nothing” just went from “All” to “Nothing”. In December, Jefferies was among the most aggressive, forecasting 225bps worth of rate cuts for 2024E. As late as March, they were still in the “All” camp, though expecting fewer cuts (100bps) for the year. However, recent US macro data continues to show strong consumption, low unemployment and persistently high inflation. The “recession” / “hard landing” narrative seems off the table now. Jefferies has thus moved in the “Nothing” camp, now forecasting just one 25bp rate cut in 4Q24… and even that comes with a fairly large “IF” starts moderating.  

The bull case for the Philippine equity market depended heavily on the US rate cuts, particularly from a funds flow perspective. The fundamental story remains solid. Earnings growth was in the mid-20% range in 2023, and we expect another 10-15% increase this year. Valuations are still near historic lows, and companies are paying out larger and larger dividend yields.

After starting the year strong, the PSEi has given up much of its gains, up just 3.3% ytd. To be fair, that still puts us in the upper half of our regional peers – if only just. Is the Groundhog out to defeat the Dragon?

RETAIL
Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) FY23 net profit +69% YoY to P3.5bn
  • Philippine Seven’s net profit in FY23 jumped 69% YoY to P3.5bn, ahead of Regis estimates. The profit outperformance was primarily due to higher-than-expected interest income. Core operations are in line with revenues +25% YoY to P78bn and operating profit +43% YoY to P5bn.
  • 4Q23 net profit grew 64% YoY to P1.2bn boosted by higher interest income. Revenues rose 20% YoY on the back of +9.5% SSSG. While SSSG was slower vs previous quarters, the YoY base is also higher. SSSG continued to be driven by growth in customer traffic.
  • Operating profit was higher by 36% YoY during the quarter, driven by gross margin improvements and operating leverage from the ramp up of new stores. SEVN ended the year with 3,768 new stores, a net addition of 375 stores vs end-2022.
