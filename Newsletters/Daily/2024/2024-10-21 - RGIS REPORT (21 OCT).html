REGIS REPORT

21 October 2024

Following BPI’s (BPI PM) strong 9M results last week’s, we raise our forecasts by 4-9%, and upgrade our target price to P160/sh, with both forecasts and TP at or near the top of the Street. BPI reported record NPAT of P17.4bn in 3Q24, driven mostly by trading gains of over P2bn. But NIMs were better than expected, too, driven by explosive growth in high-margin, unsecured products like personal loans (+129%), business banking (+88%) and microfinance (+67%). We have increased our NIM forecasts by c20bps across our forecast range. The impressive growth though comes at a cost. NPL coverage dropped sharply to 111% as of 3Q, prompting us to increase our provision expense assumptions, particularly so in 2026E. We believe, though, that the consumer push is net accretive, and will be crucial in the bank’s goal of sustaining ROEs in the mid-teens. Maintain Buy.

Speaking of BPI, a news article on Friday claimed that listed sugar miller Roxas Holdings (ROX PM) defaulted on its loans to BPI and BDO (BDO PM). According to management of both banks, their loan exposures were a) relatively small, and b) fully collateralized. BPI had about P1.8bn worth of loans to ROX, equivalent to less than 0.1% of total loans (P2.0trn at 1H24). For BDO the figure is just over P1bn, compared to its P3.0trn loan book. BDO said that the loan was already part of its NPLs, for which they had provided around 70%.

The National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) has approved tjh P37bn (about $640mn) Mindanao Transport Connectivity Project, a vast system of new roads, and upgrades of existing roads totaling almost 560kms. The project will be financed through the World Bank, and is expected to be completed by 2030.

The country posted a BOP surplus of $3.5bn in September, due in large part to the government’s recent $2.5bn global bond issue. This brings 9M24 surplus to $5.1bn.

MACRO
BOP posts US$3.5bn surplus in September
  • The country’s overall balance of payments posted a surplus of US$3.5bn in September
    • This was a reversal of the US$414mn deficit in Sep 2023 and higher than the US$88mn surplus recorded in August 2024
    • Due to the US$2.5bn global bond issuance
  • Brings YT-September BOP surplus to US$5.1bn, higher than the US$1.7bn surplus in the same period last year
  • The BSP is targeting a BOP surplus of US$1.6bn this year, lower than the 2023 BOP surplus of US$3.7bn
BANKS
Roxas Holdings (ROX PM) debt default; BPI, BDO have modest exposures
  • Roxas Holdings said that it defaulted on its loans with BDO and BPI, as well as state-owned Land Bank of the Philippines, in March
    • The default came shortly after ROX shut down permanently the refinery operations of Central Azucarera Don Pedro which was founded in 1927
    • ROX did not meet its covenant, specifically the DSCR requirement of at least 1.1x and debt-to-equity ratio of not more than 2.33x
    • ROX had P4.29bn worth of loans as of September 2023
  • BPI and Land Bank granted an extension on payments but BDO declined
    • BDO’s exposure is about P1bn, 70% of which have been provided for
    • BPI’s exposure was P1.8bn
    • ROX’s property, plant, and equipment was valued at P8.36bn, which were mortgaged to secure the loans
POWER
Department of Energy preparing to terminate 105 RE projects
  • The Department of Energy is working on canceling at least 105 renewable energy (RE) projects due to failure to meet project timelines
    • 88 are either delayed in the predevelopment stage or making no progress. These include 53 solar, 17 hydropower, 10 wind, 5 geothermal, and 3 biomass projects.
    • Most of the contracts were awarded in 2017 and 2019, and delays were caused by failure to secure rights or system impact studies indicating an inability to connect to the grid.
  • At least 1,435 service contracts were awarded as of June, the renewable energy industry is poised to introduce more than 156,700 megawatts (MW) of capacity. About 6,100 MW has been installed.
  • The government hopes to increase the share of RE in the generation mix to 35% by 2030 and 50% by 2040
INFRA
NEDA approves P37bn Mindanao transport project
  • The National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) has approved the P37bn Mindanao Transport Connectivity Project (MTCIP)
    • Consisting of five major components, the MTCIP involves upgrading the Cagayan de Oro-Davao-General Santos Corridor: Regions 10. 11, and 12
    • The project will sum up to 277.4km from Cagayan de Oro to Davao and 150.8k from Davao to General Santos
    • Will also involve the upgrade of three local roads adding up to 129.86km
  • Completion is to be expected in 2030
  • It will be financed through an official development assistance loan from the World Bank
