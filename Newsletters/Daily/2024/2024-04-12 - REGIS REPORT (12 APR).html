REGIS REPORT

12 April 2024

Monde (MONDE PM) officially announced full year 2023 results after providing preliminary numbers already earlier this year.  Core net profit was P7.6bn in FY23, 16% higher YoY.  For 4Q alone, core income jumped 2x driven largely by the sustained margin recovery of the core APAC BFB business on favorable input cost (wheat, palm oil). MONDE’s instant noodle business continued to perform well, with volumes +9% during the quarter and market share up QoQ and YoY.  Meanwhile, Quorn’s top line (-4% YoY in 4Q) remains challenged but management was able to maintain EBITDA breakeven due to cost controls.  The company took another P10bn impairment charge for its stake there after another valuation review was conducted.

VIA optronics, an indirect subsidiary of Ayala Corp (AC PM), is seeking to delist from the NYSE.  The cost of maintaining the listing doesn’t appear justified anymore and a look at its market cap (US$14m) provides us an indication why.  VIA is 50% owned by Integrated Microelectronics (IMI PM), which itself is a 52% subsidiary of AC.  We view the “clean-up” at/of VIA as part of the ongoing restructuring efforts of AC.

+++

The country’s trade deficit narrowed to US$3.65bn in Feb (vs US$3.88bn last year) due to a bounce (+16%) in exports, led by electronics (+27%).

+++

Loan growth at the country’s commercial banks accelerated to 8.6% in Feb.  Notably, consumer loans grew 25% with credit cards (+30%) giving it a boost.

MACRO
Trade deficit narrows to US$3.6bn in February
  • Preliminary data showed the trade deficit narrowed further to US$3.65bn in February
    • This is down from US$4.39bn in January 2024 and US$3.88bn in Feb 2023 underpinned by a sustained recovery in exports
  • Imports rose 6.3% YoY in February from -6% in January
    • Due to higher imports of (a) fuel (+8%), (b) raw materials (+12%), and (c) consumer goods (+9%)
    • Decline in capital goods imports persisted (-3% YoY)
  • Exports grew at a faster rate of +16% in February
    • Electronic exports rose 27% YoY
    • Exports to top trading partners jumped: USA (+23%), HK (+46%), and China (+14%)
  • Brings YT-Feb trade deficit to US$8.0bn, down 15% YoY

 

 

 

 

 

BANKS
Commercial banks’ loan growth accelerates to 8.6% in February; M3 growth slows to 5.0%
  • Preliminary data showed commercial bank loans expanded by 8.6% YoY in February from 7.8% in January
    • This is consistent with all banks’ loan growth of 10% in February, up from 8.8% in January
    • Due to faster lending to real estate activities (11.6%), electricity, gas, steam, and airconditioning supply (11.2%); transportation and storage (21.1%); and manufacturing (5.9%)
    • Consumer loan growth was steady at 25% on sustained increases in credit card and auto loans
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) grew by 5.0% YoY in February from 6.0% in January

 

 

 

MACRO
Philippines, US, Japan, to invest in “Luzon corridor”
  • The Philippines, US, and Japan have agreed to accelerate high-impact projects in Luzon
  • The Luzon corridor will be part of the United States’ Partnership for Global Infrastructure and Investment and will connect Subic Bay, Clark, Manila, and Batangas
  • It will include investments in ports, rail, clean energy, semiconductors, supply chains, and other forms of connectivity
  • Philippine Ambassador to the United States Jose Manuel Romualdez said that Tokyo and Washington were expected to invest at least US$100bn in the Philippines over the next 5 to 10 years
