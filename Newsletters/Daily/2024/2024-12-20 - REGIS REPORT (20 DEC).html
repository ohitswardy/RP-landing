REGIS REPORT

20 December 2024

As widely anticipated, the Bangko Sentral (BSP) proceeded to reduce policy rates by 25bp to 5.75% yesterday.  The move comes hours after the US Federal Reserve reduced its target rate by a similar magnitude.

We note the BSP, like the Fed, now seems a tad more cautious about cutting rates over the near future, even if easing remains the direction.  Rate reductions worth 100bp next year are now deemed “too much”.  Note the BSP also tweaked its inflation forecast, raising it to 3.4% for next year (vs 3.3%) – a small but unmistakable signal about the perceived direction of inflation risk.

+++

Jollibee (JFC PM) announced it was redeeming the US$ perpetual securities that are still outstanding and callable next month. There are still US$396m worth of perps (vs initial US$600m) still outstanding after a tender offer conducted in 2021. The securities, which carry a coupon of 3.9%, will see a step-up in January if not redeemed.

+++

ACEN (ACEN PM) won a bid to build 936MW of wind capacity in Australia, through the government’s capacity investment scheme agreement (CISA) program. The project, to be completed by 2030, will be 95% owned by ACEN and is likely to cost ~US$1.5bn.

We reckon ACEN will need to raise a significant amount of capital as early as next year to fund its capex goals. To achieve its target of 20GW capacity by 2030, ACEN would need to spend an average of ~US$2bn annually until then. Its current market cap is US$2.4bn.

+++

Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) appointed Mr. Gregory Francis Hawkins, who currently heads Solaire North, as Acting COO. While he only joined the company in November 2023, he is a veteran casino executive. Prior to joining BLOOM, he was Chief Casino Officer at Star Entertainment Group (SGR AU). He was also previously CEO of City of Dreams and was part of the founding executive team at Crown Melbourne. Mr. Hawkins’ appointment comes after the rather sudden retirement of Thomas Arasi as President and COO on December 17.

MACRO
Monetary board cuts policy rate 25bps as expected
  • The BSP's Monetary board reduced its policy rate by 25bps to 5.75%, in-line with consensus expectations
  • But it raised its risk-adjusted inflation forecast for 2025 slightly higher to 3.4% from 3.3%, while 2026 remains unchanged at 3.7%
    • On the basis of the inflation outlook continuing to lean to the upside due to potential upward adjustments in transport fares and electricity but may be offset by the impact of lower import tariffs on rice
  • Given the within-target inflation outlook, Governor Remolona states that further cuts are expected next year giving guidance by the statements of 100 bps being "too much" and 0 bps being "too little"
    • He also stated that barring any big surprises, the BSP will continue its easing cycle as any small surprises will not warrant a change in current policy direction
  • We expect the BSP to cut its policy rate by 75bps in 2025
    • Jefferies expects the Fed to cut rates by another 100bps for 2025

MACRO
November balance of payments deficit hits US$2.3bn
  • The country's overall balance of payments (BOP) for the month posted a deficit of US$2.3bn
    • Higher than both of the deficits of US$724mn in Oct 2024 and the US$216mn deficit in Nov 2023
    • Bringing YT-November registered a surplus of US$2.1bn, lower than the US$3.0bn surplus recorded the same period last year
  • Meanwhile, the final GIR level for end-November stood at US$108.5bn, a slight drop from the US$111.1bn in end-October
    • GIR level is equivalent to 7.7 months’ worth of imports
    • The BSP is forecasting a GIR level of US$106bn in end-2024
CONSUMER
Jollibee to redeem remainder of US$600mn perpetual securities
  • Jollibee Foods Corp. (JFC PM) is set to fully redeem its US$600mn (P30.6bn) guaranteed senior perpetual capital on Jan 23, 2025
    • The perps had a coupon of 3.9%, payable semi-annually, noncallable for five years after the issuance back in Jan 23, 2020
    • As of Sep 2024, the remaining balance amounted to US$396.5mn or P20.3bn after previous repayments back in 2021
  • When JFC redeems the securities, the company will
    • Pay back the principal amount
    • Include any unpaid interest or distributions owed to investors
    • Cancel and delist the securities from the Singapore Exchange
  • Recall that the securities were issued on Jan 23, 2020 through its subsidiary Jollibee Worldwide Pte. Ltd. (JWPL) and was listed in the Singapore Exchange Securities Trading Limited (SGX-ST)
    • The securities are treated as equity as part of non-controlling interests
    • The proceeds from the issuance were used to repay debt from acquiring The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf (CBTL) which the company bought for $350mn back in Sept 2019
POWER
ACEN wins bid for a 936MW greenfield wind project in Australia
  • ACEN won the capacity investment scheme agreement (CISA) from the Australian government to build a 936MW wind farm (“Valley of the Winds”) in New South Wales
    • Valley of the Winds is 95%-owned by ACEN and is expected to be completed by 2030
    • According to ACEN President and Chief Executive Officer Eric T. Francia
    • The government bid out 3.6GW worth of new wind projects under the CISA program, which is similar to the Philippines’ Green Energy Auction Program
  • If completed, this project will be the single largest project of ACEN to date and will add to the company’s attributable RE portfolio of 6,745MW (3,051MW operational, 2,302 MW under construction, 1,401MW “committed”)
    • At an estimated replacement cost of US$1.6mn per MW, we estimate the project could cost US$1.5bn
    • ACEN’s international RE portfolio (attributable) stands at 4,357 MW, of which 2,004 MW (46%) is operational, ~22% is under construction, and ~32% is “committed”
