REGIS REPORT

01 October 2024

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) is considering two more regulatory tweaks that could have significant impact on the banks.

First is a further cut in bank RRR, to as low as zero, within the term of current BSP Governor Eli Remolona.  The BSP already announced a 250bp cut in RRR to 7.0% effective late this month. All other things held constant, we reckon every 1ppt cut in RRR will raise profits of our covered banks by over 1%.

Second, the BSP also released a draft circular mandating the elimination of fees on electronic fund transfers for personal transaction. Fees will be removed for small payments, within specified thresholds on the number of transactions and gross receipts for micro merchants.

The removal of such fees will certainly help accelerate adoption of digital payments. Those who have better digital infrastructure (and thus more equipped to capitalize on the shift to digital payments) should benefit as they will be able to attract more deposits.

+++

More than three years after PNB (PNB PM) declared a dividend covering 51% of its ownership in three prime pieces of property (held through a corporation, PNB Holdings), shareholders will finally receive it on Oct 25. PNB’s biggest shareholder LT Group (LTG PM) should receive ~30% of PNB Holdings, which could add P1.2/share to LTG’s NAV (12% of its share price) if valued based on estimated property prices.

+++

DMCI (DMC PM) is launching a tender offer for Cemex PH (CHP PM) after purchasing close to 90% of the latter from its Mexican principal.  The estimated tender offer price is P1.42/share, which will be subject to final adjustments.  This price is lower than the latest traded price.

+++

It is now looking very likely that China Bank (CBC PM) will become part of the PSEi (aka PCOMP) during the next review, which will determine the PSEi changes in Feb 2025.  CBC is likely to replace Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) based on the criteria of the exchange.

We also believe there is a chance that AREIT (AREIT PM) will be included in the PSEi, but this possibility is dependent on how a specific rule of the PSE will (or will not) be applied. If AREIT does get in, we believe it will be in place of Wilcon (WLCON PM).

MACRO
BSP Governor eyes lowering RRR to zero within term; BSP removing fees on small transfers
  • BSP Governor Remolona, Jr. hinted at the possibility of lowering banks’ reserve requirement ratio down to zero before his terms ends in 2029
    • Discussions are ongoing to bring the RRR down further to 5%, but timing is still undetermined
    • The BSP is reducing the RRR by 250bps to 7% for commercial banks by October 25, which could raise NPAT by c. 4% (see report)
  • In a draft circular, the BSP is eyeing to amend the Manual of Regulations for Payment Systems (MORPS) to eliminate transaction fees on electronic fund transfers for personal transactions, up to a specified threshold on the number of transactions, and on payments for micro-merchants
    • Micro-merchants are described to have monthly aggregate gross receipts that do not exceed P250k (c. US$4.5k)
    • Draft circular also proposes to lift the moratorium on the increase of fee for InstaPay and PESONet transactions for payment service providers (PSPs) upon the implementation of the circular
    • If the circular is approved, payment service providers (PSPs) will need to comply with the amended rules by April 1, 2025
    • Data from the BSP showed that the value of transactions done through InstaPay and PESONet climbed by 34.6% YoY to P9.45tn in 7M24
MACRO
BSP sees September inflation at 2.0%-2.8%
  • The BSP is projecting September 2024 inflation to settle within the range of 2.0%-2.8%
    • Consensus at 2.5%
    • August 2024 inflation rate stood at 3.3%
    • Cited negative base effects for rice, meat, and vegetables, as well as lower domestic oil prices, and the appreciation of the PHP against the USD
    • These are seen to offset the higher prices of fish, fruits, and electricity
  • The Philippine Statistics Authority is set to release September CPI on Oct 4
CONGLOMERATES
DMCI group launches tender offer for Cemex (CHP PM)
  • CHP has received a copy of the Mandatory Tender Offer Report of Consunji family-owned Dacon Corp. (“bidder”) to conduct a mandatory tender offer to acquire up to 1.367bn shares (10.14%) of CHP  
    • Shares will be acquired at an estimated price of P1.42/sh, which is the same price the group is paying to acquire 100% of CHP’s parent, Cemex Asian South East Corp. (CASEC), which in turn owns 89.86% of CHP
    • CHP traded at P1.74/sh on Sep 10 and P1.55/sh on Sep 30
    • Final purchase price is subject to customary closing adjustments based on CHP’s estimated working capital, cash and debt levels
    • If final price exceeds estimated price, tendering shareholders may receive an upward adjustment; In case of a downward adjustment, bidder will not claim a refund
  • Note that the bidder has agreed to purchase 100% of CASEC for an enterprise value of US$660mn and equity value of US$306mn
    • DMCI Holdings (DMC PM) will own 56.75% of CASEC; Dacon Corp. will own 32.12%; Semirara (SCC PM) will own 11.13%
    • Assuming no one participates in the tender offer, DMCI will end up effectively owning 51% of CHP while SCC will own 10%
    • Recall that the Consunji family indicated that it intends to keep CHP listed
  • Tender offer shall commence at 9AM on Oct 23 to 12noon on November 21
    • Cross date will be on November 26 ad Settlement date will be on November 28
BANKS
PNB approves and confirms property dividend declaration; PNB to sell 9.18% stake in PHC
  • PNB’s property dividend will be paid on October 25
  • On April 23, 2021, PNB’s Board of Directors approved and confirmed the property dividend declaration of up to 239,353,710 common shares of PNB Holdings Corporation (“PHC”) with a par value of P100 per share, to all stockholders of record as of May 18, 2021
    • This represents 51% of PHC, which owns three pieces of prime property in Makati and Pasay
  • The property dividend shall be paid at a ratio of 0.156886919 shares of PHC for every 1 share of PNB
  • Since LT Group (LTG PM) owns c. 60% of PNB, it will receive c. 30% of PHC
  • Separately, PNB also said it would sell at least 9.18% of its stake in PHC
MARKET
China Bank likely to enter PSE Index in Feb 2025
  • Based on our preliminary estimates, China Banking Corporation (CBC PM) will likely enter the PSE Index during the Feb 2025 rebalancing
  • Meanwhile, Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) is likely to be removed from the Index during the said rebalancing
  • Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap
  • Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when
    • 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or
    • 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35 
  • Based on our estimates, both criteria have been met
