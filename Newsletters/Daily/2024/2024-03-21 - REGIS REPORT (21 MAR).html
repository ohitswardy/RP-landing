REGIS REPORT

21 March 2024

BPI (BPI PM) is selling its 15% stake in digital bank GoTyme for P902m to JG Summit (JGS PM). BPI became the owner of the stake when it acquired Robinsons Bank from the JG Summit group. GoTyme, like the other digital banks, has been posting losses, so the sale should resolve concerns over those losses.

+++

First Gen (FGEN PM) reported recurring net income of US$277m for FY23, a modest (4%) increase from the previous year.  The figure does appear to be just a tad over our estimates.

Note, however, that the implied 4Q23 recurring income of US$28m is 60% lower YoY. 4Q earnings appear to have been down due to (1) losses at its newly completed LNG terminal and (2) higher financing charges at its two big natgas plants, San Lorenzo and Sta Rita.

+++

Belle Corp (BEL PM) says it is making a tender offer to buy out the 20% of Premium Leisure Corp (PLC PM) it does not own.  The offer price of P0.85/share, however, is 8% lower than the last traded price of P0.92/share.  The price rose from P0.74/share after BEL announced it was planning to do a tender offer at an undisclosed price.  At the tender offer price, PLC would be on 12x PER for FY23.

MACRO
February BoP deficit eases to US$196mn
  • Data showed that the balance of payments narrowed sharply to US$196mn in February, lower than the US$895mn recorded in Feb 2023 and the US$740mn deficit in Jan 2024
    • Reversed surpluses of US$2.2bn in Jan-Feb 2023
    • Reflected outflows primarily from the National Government's payments of foreign currency debt obligations
  • GIR dipped 1.26% MoM to US$102bn in February
GAMING
PLC tender offer price set at P0.85/share
  • The tender offer price for Premium Leisure Corp (PLC PM) was set at P0.85/share
    • The tender offer price was based on a Fairness Opinion by an independent appraiser (First Metro Investments Corp)
  • Belle Corp (BEL PM) previously disclosed that it would make a tender offer for all outstanding shares of PLC
    • BEL currently owns 80% of PLC
  • The tender offer price is 8% lower than the last traded price (of P0.92/share)
  • At P0.85/share, PLC would trade at 12.1X FY23 P/E
  • BEL would have to spend P5.3bn to acquire the PLC shares held by minority shareholders
  • The tender offer period will be from March 22 to April 24
  • PLC holds the license for City of Dreams Manila 
    • It has an EBITDA sharing arrangement with Melco Resorts Philippines
    • PLC’s FY23 gaming revenue share grew 50% to P2.3bn
  • BEL is trading at 9.3X annualized 9M23 P/E
