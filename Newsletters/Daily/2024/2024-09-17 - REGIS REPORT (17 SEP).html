REGIS REPORT

17 September 2024

The government wage boards approved increased minimum wages in CALABARZON and Central Visayas (Cebu) with effect one to two weeks from now.

The minimum wages in Region IVA (CALABARZON) will be increased by 6-18%, with agricultural workers receiving higher raises.  For Region VII (where Cebu is located), the increase will be just around 7%.

The wage hikes for non-agricultural workers have generally just kept in line with CPI inflation.  Over the past ten years, CPI has risen 38% while wage increases in the capital (NCR) rose a cumulative 39% (including this year’s +6%).

+++

Meralco (MER PM) announced its telco tower subsidiary MIDC is pooling its tower assets with the Philtower consortium to create a firm with >3,300 towers. Meralco will hold a 25% interest in the combined entity through MIESCOR.

We view the deal positively. The combination will provide geographical dispersion, heft and bargaining power for future tower lease deals (existing leases are already set for the next decade). In addition, the merged entity (with ~P50bn worth of tower assets) would be better positioned to be listed as a REIT.

Remittances rose 3% in July to US$3.1bn, with the YoY growth generally in line with what it has been practically every month.

+++

Digital payments coursed through the National Retail Payments System (NRPS) rose 29% YoY in August to P1,441bn, still a rapid pace despite a modest slowdown for PesoNet (+20%).  Growth in this data is reflective of increased digital adoption by consumers.

Globe (GLO PM) is appointing a new CFO or, as one headline put it, it appointed a “Millennial” as CFO.  Carlo Puno, currently SVP – Corporate Finance, will take over from Rizza Eala, who is retiring mid-October.

BANKS
Digital Payments +29% in August; driven by InstaPay +45%

Digital transactions done over the National Retail Payments System (NRPS) grew 29% YoY in August to P1,441bn. Growth slowed from the 35% pace in July, and the August figure dipped slightly (-3%) MoM. Despite the slowdown, the 8-month total of P10,896bn, up 34% YoY, still faster than the 29% growth for FY2023.

 

The deceleration was due to PesoNet – used for larger transactions – which saw August values at P817bn, still up a respectable 20% YoY. However, this was slower than the 30% clip in July, and was down 6% MoM. Given the chunky nature of the payments, the monthly variability in growth rates has been quite wide. Year-to-date, though, payments over PesoNet were still up 27% YoY (vs 22% for FY23) to P6,374bn.

 

To the upsidde, smaller-ticket, real-time payments over InstaPay continued to power on, rising 45% YoY to P624bn. This was slightly faster than the 43% YoY recorded in July, and in line with the 8-month trajectory, up 45% to P4,521bn (vs 42% in 2023). Once again, the volume of transactions over InstaPay surged, growing 69% YoY in August, accelerating from the 62% in July (and +71% YoY in 8M24).

 

Digital adoption, particularly at the retail/individual level seems to be picking up steam. Among stocks we cover, Globe (GLO PM) still offers the most direct exposure via its affiliate GCash/Mynt. Meanwhile, banks continue to invest heavily in their IT infrastructure, with BPI (BPI PM) seemingly ahead of the curve among the banks we cover.

  • Meralco subsidiary MIESCOR is combining its telco tower operations under MIDC with those of the Philtower Consortium

  • MIESCOR will hold 25% interest in the combined telco tower entity
