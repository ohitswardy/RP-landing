20 May 2024

Ayala Corp (AC PM) announced it was selling the last of its remaining holdings in Manila Water (MWC PM) for P14.5bn.  The sale places AC over its “value realization” goal of US$1bn (P50bn when it was announced). It also completes the exit from a business created after AC prevailed in the water privatization auction in 1997.

Despite the sale being transacted at a slight discount to market price, we believe AC secured good value. We also believe this should not be that last of AC’s divestments. More in the link below.

Gaming agency PAGCOR recently “upgraded” provisional licenses granted to a number Internet Gaming Licensees (IGL).  In total, there are now 40 IGLs will regular licenses and nine with provisional licenses.  As of Jan, there were 51 firms with licenses, most of whom (44) had only provisional licenses.  “IGLs” are what PAGCOR used to call “POGOs” – firms running online gambling operations targeting players overseas.

The granting of regular licenses has apparently led to the operators expanding their operations and office footprint. Over the twelve months to March 2024, we estimate IGLs / POGOs increased their office footprint from 700k sqm to 900 sqm.

GAMING/PROPERTY
More offshore gaming firms become regular Internet Gaming Licensees
  • Several offshore gaming firms that were previously granted provisional Internet Gaming Licenses (IGL) have been converted to regular Internet Gaming Licensees
    • The firms that have received regular licenses might expand their operations soon  
  • As of May 15, PAGCOR has issued regular licenses to 40 Internet Gaming Licensees
    • 9 provisional licenses have also been issued
  • As of January 15, there were only 7 regular IGL firms and 44 provisional licensees
  • In 2H23, PAGCOR required all POGO firms to reapply for licenses under the new nomenclature of IGL
  • From 2020 to 1Q23, offshore gaming firms vacated about 600,000sqm of Metro Manila office space
  • Since then, they have taken-up an incremental c. 200,000sqm of office space
    • We would partly attribute this expansion to the “re-legitimization” of the industry. The offshore gaming firms generally do not expand until they become regular Internet Gaming Licensees
  • We estimate that offshore gaming firms currently occupy c. 900,000sqm of Metro Manila office space
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 45% of Filinvest Land’s NAV – POGOs occupy c. 5% of office space
    • 44% of Megaworld’s NAV – POGOs occupy c. 5% of office space
    • 25% of Robinsons Land’s NAV – POGOs occupy 1% of office space
  • FLI is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 5.1X FY24E P/E
  • MEG is trading at 71% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 3.3X FY24E P/E
  • RLC is trading at 61% discount to NAV, 0.5X FY24E P/B, and 6.6X FY24E P/E
TRANSPORT
PH foreign container volumes down 1% YoY in 1Q24
  • The country recorded a 1% YoY decline in foreign container volumes to 1.27mn TEUs in 1Q24
    • Based on data from the Philippine Ports Authority and Cebu Port Authority
    • Volumes at ICTSI’s (ICT PM) Manila International Container Terminal (MICT) were flat at 664k TEUs while ATI’s (ATI PM) South Harbor recorded a 7% YoY drop in volumes to 281k TEUs
    • Nationwide import volumes rose 2% YoY while export volumes fell 4% YoY
  • Meanwhile, domestic container volumes also dipped by 1% YoY, with ICT’s MNHPI recording a 4% YoY drop
  • During ICT’s 1Q24 briefing, management said that volumes in Manila so far have turned “mildly positive” in 2Q24
  • ICT trades at 16.5x FY24E PER and 9.7x FY24E EV/EBITDA vs. peer median at 16.5x/10.7x and historical average of (22x/11x)
