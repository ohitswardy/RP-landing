REGIS REPORT

21 May 2024

There was a shake-up in the Senate leadership yesterday, with Senator Miguel Zubiri being replaced by Senator Francis Escudero as leader of the chamber.  The key question, as with every other time such leadership changes occur, is how this will affect the business of Congress.  And the most important such business, we reckon, is how the Senate will deal with proposals to change the Constitution (aka “Cha-Cha”), possibly as soon as next year. 

Speaking of the Senate, the chamber approved, on third reading and before the leadership re-shuffle, a bill imposing VAT on digital services of companies with no physical presence in the country.  Netflix, Spotify, and possibly Amazon and Lazada purchases could be more expensive. The Dept of Finance (DOF) expects the bill to generate P84bn in revenue over five years once implemented.

+++

Ayala Land (ALI PM) sold 98m shares of AREIT (AREIT PM), equivalent to 4% of the latter’s outstanding capital.  This share sale would complete the need for ALI to sell down so AREIT can comply with the 33% minimum public float even after all its announced asset-for-shares injections are completed.  The pricing would translate to yield of just over 7.0%.

+++

Finally, Security Bank’s (SECB PM) 1Q24 earnings were difficult to slog through, between NPL cleanups, one-off gains/losses, and accounting changes. It would appear that, after all the adjustments, SECB’s has turned a corner in 1Q24. The P2.6bn NPAT looks behind our P12.0bn FY24E forecast. But management claims that 1Q was the end of the cleanup of its problematic credit card book, which means credit costs will fall from c110bps in 1Q24 to a more “normalized” c60bps starting 2Q24 (roughly P500-600mn lower provisions per quarter). Meanwhile, the bank’s heavy IT spend in recent years should start paying off, with the launch of their new retail and corporate apps from 2H24. The stock has been shunned by investors, now trading at just 0.35x P/bk and 4.5x PER on our FY24E estimates, and even pays a 4.1% dividend yield.

MACRO
Senate OKs VAT on digital services
  • The Senate approved on third and final reading a bill that seeks to impose a 12% VAT on digital services provided by companies with no physical presence in the Philippines
    • Digital services include, but are not limited to: online search engines, online/e-marketplaces, cloud services, online media and advertising, online platforms, digital goods
    • BIR commissioner can order the blocking or suspension of digital providers if they fail to withhold and remit the 12% VAT
  • Bill exempts online education platforms, as well as services of banks/nonbank financial intermediaries
  • Department of Finance expects the bill to bring in P83.8bn in revenues from 2024-2028

Cagayan de Oro lawmaker backs Meralco franchise renewal
  • Renewing Meralco’s franchise for another 25 years will show potential investors that capital-heavy investments can benefit from long-term regulatory stability
    • According to Cagayan de Oro City 2nd District Representative Rufus Rodriguez
    • Previously, Albay 2nd District Rep. Joey Salceda filed bills seeking to renew MER’s franchise
  • Meralco’s franchise is set to expire in 2028
INFRASTRUCTURE
San Miguel nearing TPLEX Extension PPP contract signing
  • San Miguel (SMC PM) may soon sign the contract for the Tarlac-Pangasinan-La Union Expressway (TPLEx) Extension
    • According to PPP Center Executive Director Jeffrey Manalo
    • No parties have challenged the unsolicited proposal
  • TPLEX Extension Project is a 59.4 km 4-lane toll road which will start from the TPLEX in Rosario, La Union and will terminate at San Juan, La Union
    • Project is estimated to cost P27.5bn and will cut travel time from Rosario to San Juan from 1.5 hours to 40 mins
    • Civil works could be completed after five years
PROPERTY
Ayala Land sells 98mn AREIT shares
  • Ayala Land sold 98mn AREIT shares
    • This is equivalent to 4% of shares outstanding
  • The shares were sold at P31.45/share to P32.45/share, representing a 5%-8% discount to the last closing price
    • This implies that the total amount was raised was P3.1bn-P3.2bn
  • In 4Q23, AREIT announced that it would acquire properties from Ayala Land and ACEN in a series of transactions
    • AREIT will purchase a hotel from Ayala Land for P1.2bn in cash
    • AREIT will acquire P21.8bn worth of office, mall, and hotel assets by issuing 642mn shares to Ayala Land
    • AREIT will acquire P6.8bn worth of industrial land by issuing 199mn shares to ACEN
  • After all of these transactions are consummated (4Q24 to 1Q25), AREIT’s AUM will increase by 34% (to P117bn) and its shares outstanding will expand by 35% (to 3.2bn)
  • In order to meet minimum public ownership requirements, Ayala Land would have to sell 279mn AREIT shares
  • In January 2024, Ayala Land sold 181mn AREIT shares at P31.10/share (total of P5.63bn)
  • ALI is trading at 42% discount to NAV, 1.5X FY24E P/B, and 16.3X FY24E P/E
  • AREIT currently offers FY24E dividend yield of 6.7% (based on consensus forecast)
    • The 10-year sovereign bond yield is 6.6%
