REGIS REPORT

13 June 2024

Data center hype has indeed come to the Philippines.

PLDT (TEL PM) says it has agreed to a valuation of over US$1bn for its data center business with its potential, unnamed, partner.  PLDT intends to sell 49% of the business and a deal could be announced as soon as next month. The DC business operates 20+MW of capacity today and is in the process of expanding it to more than 100MW.

We don’t have a separate valuation for the DC operation, but it generates no more than 2% of PLDT’s revenues today (for perspective, PLDT’s market cap is US$5.3bn). The entry of a strategic partner at what appears to be a lofty valuation should be good for the business as well as for PLDT.  Recall Globe (GLO PM) sold part of its DC business at an estimated pre-money valuation of US$275m.

Meanwhile, the government announced that an investor will be spending as much as US$2.7bn to build a 300MW DC meant for hyperscalers in Tarlac province (Central Luzon).  The project will be completed in phases, with the first one targeting completion by late next year.

+++

Auto sales growth reported by CAMPI decelerated to +5.5% in May. This brought YT-May sales to 187k units, +13% YoY.  Sales for the Toyota brand grew 4.5% YoY in May.  This takes them to 86k units YT-May.  Despite the deceleration in growth, we reckon our FY24E assumption of 206k units is still quite achievable.

+++

The country’s trade deficit for April was US$4.8bn, from US$3.4bn in March.  Compared to a year ago, the deficit was largely unchanged.  Imports rose 13% YoY to US$11bn while exports grew a surprising 26% YoY.

YT-April, the trade deficit was US$16bn, 16% lower YoY.

MACRO
April trade deficit mildly lower YoY at US$4.76bn
  • The country recorded a trade deficit of US$4.76bn in April, slightly (-1%) down YoY but was much larger than the US$3.44bn deficit recorded in March
    • We suspect the sequential rebound in the trade deficit figure was largely due to timing of Holy Week
    • Note that while most ports are generally open year-round, customs offices are often shut during the holidays
  • Imports grew 13% YoY and 15% MoM to US$11bn
    • Imports of most major type of goods accelerated – raw materials (+14%), capital goods (+10%), consumer goods (+16%), and fuel (+8%)
    • Electronic imports also rose by 9% YoY
  • Exports also grew by 26% YoY and 1.4% MoM to US$6.2bn
    • Electronic exports grew by 33% YoY
  • Brings YT-April trade deficit to US$16.3bn, lower by 16% YoY

 

CONSUMER
Vehicle sales rise 5.5% YoY, 7.9% MoM in May
  • CAMPI reported car sales rose 5.5% YoY, 7.9% MoM to 40,271 units in May
    • Brings 5M24 car sales up 13% YoY to 187,191 units
    • CAMPI targets industry car sales to reach 468,300 units (+8% YoY) but also expressed optimism that car sales could reach 500k units this year (+16% YoY)
  • Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP) sold 86,257 units in 5M24, up 12% YoY
    • This implies that for the month of May alone, TMP sold 18,677 units (+4.5% YoY, +4.3% MoM)
    • To reach TMP’s FY24E target of 215k units (+7% YoY), monthly sales should average 18.4k units for the rest of the year
    • To reach Regis’ target of 206k units (+3%), monthly sales should average 17k units for the rest of the year
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 33% of NAV (25% of gross asset value) and 26% of gross profit (before parent expenses)
  • GTCAP trades at a 53% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,226 and 5.3x FY24E PER

BANKS
Trust AUMs grow 16% YoY 1Q24, with BPI the big gainer
  • Trust assets under management (AUMs) of banks and non-banks regulated by the BSP stood at P6,403bn (roughly $110bn) as of 1Q24, up 16% YoY, sustaining the pace set in 4Q23. AUMs were up 3% QoQ. The composition of AUMs changed dramatically, though, as both Rizal Commercial Bank (RCB PM) and Union Bank (UBP PM) spun off their trust departments into “Stand-Alone Trusts”, similar to BPI’s, effectively reclassifying their AUMs as “non-banks”.
  • BPI (BPI PM) was the big winner in 1Q24, with AUMs surging 38% YoY and 8% QoQ to P1,315bn, bringing market share up to 20.5% from 19.7% in 4Q23. In 1Q24, BPI accounted for just under 50% of incremental AUMs of the whole sector. This further narrowed the gap to BDO (BDO PM) Parent, which saw AUMs dip 1% QoQ to P1,385bn. On a consolidated basis (including BDO Private Bank), BDO was flat QoQ but continues to hold a commanding market share lead at 31.2%. Metrobank (MBT PM) was a far third at P503.5bn, up 1% QoQ.
  • The Big Three account for roughly 60% of total AUMs, with BDO and BPI already more than half the industry at a combined 51.8% market share.
  • Of the smaller banks, RCBC and East West (EW PM) took market share, posting QoQ growth of 5% and 6% respectively

 

TELCOS
PLDT agrees to valuation of over US$1bn for data center business with partner
  • PLDT has agreed to a valuation of over US$1bn for its data center business, according to CEO Manuel Pangilinan
    • Relating to the entry of a foreign company into the operation
  • For perspective, PLDT has a market capitalization of P312bn (US$5.3bn)
  • Foreign entity will take a 49% stake in the DC operation
  • Pangilinan previously said Japanese telco NTT was agreeable to a valuation of over US$1bn but wanted a majority interest
  • PLDT was supposed to be considering other bids to take less than 50% but at lower valuations
    • A possible REIT for the assets was also considered
  • The data center business today contributes less than 2% of revenue
  • PLDT is in the process of building its 11th data center which will be bigger than all its existing DCs combined
    • The first phase will be completed next month
  • Proceeds from a secondary sale will be used to reduce debt at PLDT

OTHERS
Group investing US$2.7bn for hyperscale data center in Tarlac
  • The group of construction-firm ENDECGROUP and Narra Technology Park Development Inc. (NTPDI) plans to invest US$2.7bn to build a 300MW hyperscale data center hub in Capas, Tarlac
    • Project will be done in three phases of 100 MW costing US$890mn per phase, with the first 100MW phase likely to be completed by 4Q 2025
    • On top of this, US$35mn has been allotted for site development
    • According to NTPDI managing director, William Johnson
  • The group signed a lease contract with the Provincial Government of Tarlac for a 47-hectare site within the New Clark City (NCC)
    • The site, which will be called the “Narra Technology Park, will use 100% clean energy
    • The project also envisions to create more than 1,500 direct jobs, especially in IT operations, maintenance, and cybersecurity for the local communities
