REGIS REPORT

22 August 2024

Data from the government showed air traffic volumes continue to recover but remain just shy of pre-Covid peak.  Air passenger volumes rose 26% in 1H to 29.5m, which is still 3% off the level just before the pandemic.  Passenger volumes for Cebu Air (CEB PM) were up 27% in the same period.

+++

ACEN (ACEN PM) expects to complete its five floating solar projects in Laguna Lake.  The projects will have a capacity of 1,120MW and will cost P48bn (~US$0.8bn). They are part of ACEN’s project pipeline to reach 20,000MW of capacity by 2030.

+++

Majority of senators have signed on to the proposed bill supporting and promoting the development of the natural gas industry in the country.  The country has only one domestic source of gas (the Malampaya field, operated by Prime Infra) and the gas from there has only one buyer: First Gen (FGEN PM). Malampaya, however, is already in terminal decline and more investment is needed to secure more resources around or near the existing field

It’s unclear what the proposed law can or will do.  However, incentives to develop, promote and favor domestic natural gas will likely provide advantages not just to the operator/s but also to the users (First Gen).

+++

Government infrastructure spending (including transfers to local governments) was up 18% in 1H to P720bn.  Given the appropriated budget and the relative base in 2H23, infrastructure spend in 2H is likely to be lower YoY.

MACRO
Infrastructure spending rises 18% YoY to P721bn in 1H24
  • Overall infrastructure spending (including LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) rose by 18.4% YoY to P720.5bn in 1H24
    • This exceeded the government’s 1H24 program of P671.3bn by 7.3%
    • This is equivalent to 5.7% of GDP
  • Implies that infra spend for 2Q24 alone grew by 24% YoY to P454bn
  • The government aims to spend P1.47tn (+4% YoY) on infrastructure for FY24 (5.6% of GDP)
    • However, this implies that infrastructure spending in 2H24 could drop by 7% YoY to P752bn
  • For 2025, the government plans to spend P1.5tn of infrastructure, up 4% YoY and equivalent to 5.4% of GDP
    • The government aims to spend 5-6% of GDP annually for infrastructure through 2028
TRANSPORT
Air passenger volumes rise 26% YoY to 29.5mn in 1H24, 3% below pre-Covid levels
  • Data from the Civil Aeronautics Board showed that nationwide air pax volumes rose by 26% YoY to 29.5mn in 1H24
    • 1H24 pax volumes is about 3% below pre-Covid levels
    • Cebu Air (CEB PM) sustained market share gains, with pax volumes up 27% YoY to 10.9mn, exceeding 1H19 levels by 3%
  • International pax volumes grew 54% YoY to 13.8mn in 1H24, 90% of 1H19 levels
  • Domestic pax volumes grew 8% YoY to 15.8mn in 1H24, surpassing 1H19 levels by 4%

POWER
ACEN to complete floating solar projects by 2027
  • ACEN will spend P48bn to complete five floating solar projects in Laguna Lake by 2027
  • They will have a combined capacity of 1,120 megawatts peak (MWp)
  • ACEN currently has a capacity of 4.8GW of attributable renewable energy (RE) capacity across the Philippines, Australia, Vietnam, Indonesia, and Vietnam
  • It has a target of quadrupling its capacity to 20 GW by 2030
POWER
Senate backs Natural Gas Industry Development bill
  • 17 senators have supported the proposed Philippine Natural Gas Industry Development Act (Senate Bill No. 2793), which seeks to revitalize indigenous gas exploration and develop natural gas infrastructure that covers all aspects of the natural gas industry, including transportation, transmission, and storage
  • Prioritizing the use of indigenous gas over imported LNG ensures lesser dependence on foreign suppliers for this critical resource
  • The natural gas operations will adhere to both the international and local best practices
CONGLOMERATES
Aboitiz Group consolidating food and agribusiness units
  • Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM) is consolidating Pilmico (flour and feed manufacturer) and Gold Coin Management Holdings Inc. (animal feed manufacturer) into a unified corporate brand named "Aboitiz Foods"
  • By combining expertise in trading, feed, specialty nutrition, and food production, and with facilities spanning across the region in the Philippines, China, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, and Brunei, Aboitiz Foods said it is strategically positioned to become a dominant force in the region
