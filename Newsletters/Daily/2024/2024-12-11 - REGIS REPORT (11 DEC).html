REGIS REPORT

11 December 2024

The country’s trade deficit hit US$5.8b for Oct, the largest monthly gap in over two years. The deficit expanded as imports jumped (+11%) and exports slumped (-5%).

YT-Oct the trade deficit is now at US$45bn, +4% YoY.

+++

Ayala Corp (AC PM) increased its stake in education holding firm iPeople (IPO PM) by 2.8% to 36%. There seems to be little business / strategic rationale for this investment – the silver lining here is that’s “only” P351m (US$6m).

+++

Ayala Land (ALI PM) raised P2.8bn by selling 75mn AREIT (AREIT PM) shares at P37.00/share. While AREIT has not yet announced its acquisition plans for 2025, we estimate that the recent share sale brought public float to 38.3% (from 36.0%) and gives AREIT enough headroom to acquire c. P18bn worth of rental assets from Ayala Land.

+++

Gaming regulator PAGCOR upsized its 2024E industry GGR target to P350bn, which represents a 23% increase from P285bn in 2023. The increase was likely due to better-than-expected expansion of online gaming GGR, which grew 218% in 9M24.

+++

The Lower House approved a bill that would formalize and extend the tariff and tax advantages of electrified vehicles. There should be no immediate impact from the bill, even if passed, as these advantages are already existing and would only be extended to 2030 (from 2028). Toyota Motor Philippines (TMP), which is 51% owned by GT Capital (GTCAP PM), is the largest seller of hybrid vehicles, while Ayala Corp (AC PM), through AC Mobility, is the distributor of BYD in the country.

+++

Consumer loans of the Philippine banks grew 16.5% YoY to P2,916.8bn. While this was slower than the 19.7% pace in 2Q24, it was still up a respectable 3.9% QoQ. Also, the 16.5% growth was faster than the 11.2% pace for total loans. That said, non-consumer loans accelerated to 9.8% YoY, consistent with banks’ strong corporate loan growth in 3Q. Consumer loans now account for 22.0% of total loans, a new record high. Encouragingly, NPL ratio for the sector improved to 5.67%, down 15bps QoQ

 

The slowdown was seen across all segments, but was largely driven by mortgages, which saw growth decelerate sharply from 13.5% in 2Q24 to 8.1% YoY in 3Q24. Balances were still up 2.2% QoQ though. NPLs for the segment were broadly stable at 6.78% up a tad from the 6.72% in the previous quarter.

 

Motor Vehicle (MV) loans grew 17.3% YoY (was 18.7% in 2Q). Car loans were still up 18.7% YoY (vs 20.3%), while motorcycle loans were down 2.6% YoY, the 7th consecutive quarter of YoY contraction. MV NPLs improved to 5.36%, down 36bps QoQ, thanks largely to 4-wheelers, whose NPLs dropped 38bps QoQ to 4.70%. Motorcycle NPLs were still high at 16.97%.

 

Credit card growth stayed elevated, growing 27.7% YoY, though slowing a bit from the 28.8% in 2Q. NPLs here improved to 4.35%, down 21bps QoQ.

 

While the slowdown was unexpected, we are comforted by the stable / improving asset quality. Of the banks we cover, BPI and SECB are the most exposed to the consumer loan segment.

MACRO
Trade deficit expands to $5.80bn in October
  • The October trade deficit is the largest in 26 months since the US$5.99bn gap in August 2022 and was wider 37% YoY and 14% MoM
  • Imports rose 11% YoY, 5% MoM to US$11.96bn
    • Primarily driven by increased imports of consumer goods (+29% YoY), as well as electronics and capital goods (both up +21% YoY)
    • These overshadowed the 10% drop in fuel imports and other goods
  • Exports dropped 5% YoY, 1.5% MoM to US$6.16bn
    • Electronics exports declined 23% YoY to US$2.86bn
  • Bringing the YT-October trade deficit to US$45.21bn, +4% YoY

Ayala Corp increases stake in iPeople
  • Ayala Corp disclosed that it agreed to buy 29.2m shares of iPeople (=2.8%)
    • Acquisition price is P12/share, total consideration is P351mn
    • AC’s stake would rise to 36.3%
    • Shares were acquired from Anscor

    • ~20x trailing (FY23) PER and 16x annualized FY24 PER
    • Also 7% discount to book value per share
PROPERTY
Ayala Land sells AREIT shares
  • Ayala Land sold 75mn AREIT shares at P37.00/share in an overnight placement on December 9
    • Total of P2.78bn
    • This represents a 7% discount to the previous closing price
    •  At P37.00/share, AREIT offers a dividend yield of 6.5% (based on consensus forecast)
  • AREIT currently offers a dividend yield of 6.4% (based on consensus forecast)
    • The 10-year government bond yield is currently 5.94%
  • After this transaction, we estimate that Ayala Land’s ownership in AREIT fell from 57.7% to 55.4%
  • We estimate that public ownership increased from 36.0% to c. 38.3%
GAMING
PAGCOR raises 2024 GGR target to P350bn
  • The Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corp (PAGCOR) raised its 2024 gross gaming revenue (GGR) target from P336bn to P350bn
    • This would represent an increase of 23% from the 2023 figure (P285bn)
  • 9M24 GGR grew 29% to P265bn and represents 76% of the P350bn GGR target set by PAGCOR for the full year
  • The 9M24 GGR breakdown is as follows:
    • PAGCOR operated: +16% YoY to P12bn
    • Licensed casino: -5% YoY to P149bn
    • Online gaming: +218% to P103bn
MACRO
Lower House approves electric vehicle zero-tariff measure
  • The House of Representatives approved on third reading House Bill 10960, which will remove tariffs on imported two-, three-, and four-wheeled EVs and their charging equipment from 2025-2030
    • Exemption covers BEV (battery), HEV (hybrid), and PHEV (plug-in hybrid)
  • Recall that President Marcos issued EO 12 in 2023, which eliminated tariffs for EVs (battery/hybrid) and their components until 2028
    • Thus, the passage of HB 10960 will not disrupt the status quo today, as EVs already enjoy duty-free importation
  • Together with the EVIDA Law, which exempted BEVs from excise taxes and provided 50% excise tax discounts for HEVs/PHEVs, these measures aim to accelerate EV adoption in the country
    • EV car sales rose by 133% YoY to 14,178 units in 9M24, accounting for c. 4% of new vehicle sales
