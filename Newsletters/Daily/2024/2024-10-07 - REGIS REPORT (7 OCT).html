REGIS REPORT

07 October 2024

The drop of headline CPI inflation in Sept below the psychological 2.0% threshold will provide a further nudge for the Bangko Sentral (BSP) to trim rates again next week.  The headline inflation rate is likely to stay low for a few more months, with the print for Oct likely to be even lower than Sept’s 1.9%.  We expect the BSP to cut policy rates another 50bp between now and year-end.

Notwithstanding the drop in CPI inflation to its lowest in four years, the price of rice has remained stubbornly high.  The fall in Vietnam export prices (down ~15% YTD) coupled with the 20ppt cut in import tariffs, should have driven the price of imported rice down 20-25% already. Such a fall would have led to overall rice prices (including domestic rice) dropping as well.  Rice prices, however, are down <5%. This suggests that “restrictions” are still hindering the entry of imports, preventing market forces from functioning properly to drive prices down.

+++

Commercial bank loans were up 10.7% YoY in Aug, accelerating from 10.4% in July.  Growth in M3 is still quite low at just 5.5%.  The non-performing loan (NPL) ratio, meanwhile, stayed unchanged at 3.68%.

With the BSP just commencing a cycle of monetary easing, the bank loan numbers should pick up over the next few quarters.

Cebu Air (CEB PM) is reportedly buying AirSwift for P1.7bn. AirSwift is the airline operation of Ayala Land (ALI PM) that services select tourist destinations like El Nido (Palawan). Boracay, Panglao (Bohol), et al.

MACRO
Inflation eases further to 1.9% in September
  • Headline CPI in September eased to its slowest pace since April 2020, declining to 1.9% YoY from 3.3% in August
    • This came in lower than consensus forecast of 2.5% and the BSP’s target range of 2.0-2.8%
    • On a MoM basis, CPI fell by 0.2%, as food and fuel prices fell
    • Food inflation slowed to 1.4% from 3.9% in August due to negative base effects on rice, vegetables, and corn
      • Note that as of October 3, the retail price of imported well-milled rice in NCR stood at P45-55/kg while local well-milled rice stood at P47-55/kg
        • Retail prices of well-milled rice in NCR stood at P52.90/kg in September, just slightly lower than P53.20/kg in June
        • Note that rice traders previously predicted that well-milled rice could fall to P45-46/kg due to the cut in rice tariffs in July
    • Lower fuel prices drove transport inflation lower
    • Brings YT-Sep average inflation rate to 3.4%, which is already within the BSP’s target band of 2-4% and at Regis’ FY24 forecast of 3.4%
  • Core inflation in September moderated to 2.4% in September from 2.6% in August
    • Non-food “core” CPI decelerated to 2.8% from 3.1%
  • BSP monetary board meeting was rescheduled to Oct 16 from Oct 17 previously

BANKS
Commercial banks’ loan growth accelerates to 10.7% in August
  • Loans of universal/commercial banks grew 10.7% YoY in August, slightly faster than the 10.4% pace in July
    • Due to faster growth in manufacturing, trade, utilities, and real estate
    • Consumer loan growth moderated to 23.7% from 24.3%
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) growth slowed to 5.5% in August from 7.3% in July

BANKS
Banks’ bad loan ratio flat at 3.68% in August
  • Preliminary data showed that the NPL ratio of Philippine banks (net of interbank) was steady at 3.68% in August
    • NPLs (net of interbank) rose to P512bn in August, up 15.8% YoY and 0.9% MoM
    • Total loans (net of interbank) rose by 10.3% YoY, 0.8% MoM to P13.91tn in August (+10.3% pace in July)
  • NPL coverage ratio was at 94.11% in August from 94.32% in July
  • Past due ratio climbed to 4.42% in August from 4.40% in July
    • Past due loans rose by 0.9% MoM to P531bn
  • Restructured loan ratio was steady at 2.05%, with restructured loans up 0.7% MoM to P293bn
TRANSPORT
Cebu Air reportedly buys Ayala Land’s AirSWIFT for P1.75bn
  • Cebu Air (CEB PM) and Ayala Land’s (ALI PM) ALI Capital are reportedly scheduled to sign a share purchase agreement, which will allow CEB to acquire ALI’s shares in AirSWIFT for P1.75bn
  • Acquisition will reportedly boost CEB’s turboprop fleet to 20 from 16; Deal also opens a new market for CEB in El Nido, Palawan
    • AirSWIFT operates flights from Manila and Clark to El Nido, as well as provides inter-island connectivity to Boracay, Cebu, Coron, and Panglao from El Nido
  • ALI Capital will remain the owner and operator of the Lio Airport in El Nido 
  • We anticipate CEB will rationalize AirSwift’s operations and remove duplicate routes upon takeover
