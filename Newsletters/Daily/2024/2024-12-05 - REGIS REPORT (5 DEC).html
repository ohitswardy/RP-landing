REGIS REPORT

05 December 2024

Despite a “moratorium” imposed four years ago, the Department of Energy (DOE) says as many as 4,000MW to 6,000MW of new coal fired power may push through.  This capacity is from coal-fired power projects that secured approval before the moratorium was announced.

This move by the DOE is contrary to the existing push to increase the share of renewables (RE) in the generation mix.  Current rules mandate distribution utilities (DU) must increase procurement of RE power every year in order to reach the “aspirational” target of 35% by 2030.  The fact that the DOE is even considering this is a sign that the supply outlook could be inadequate over the medium-term if the government does not allow new coal plants.  Power companies have been recently lobbying for new coal plants to be allowed.

Among the coal-fired projects that may be revived are the long delayed 1,200MW Atimonan plant of Meralco (MER PM). For those who remember, this plant was proposed as early as 2015 but plans changed a few times (between being coal-fired or LNG-fired). If revived, this plant could realistically be operational by late this decade.

Ironically, companies like ACEN (ACEN PM) are doing the exact opposite. As mentioned in the news recently, ACEN is accelerating the retirement of the 246MW SLTEC coal plant (to 2030) in order to replace it with a solar farm.

Policy-wise, though, we will not be surprised if the DOE eventually abandons or significantly alters its targets for RE (under the RPS or renewable portfolio standards) in the face of realities on the ground.

Many office tenants are considering increasing the office space they occupy, if a survey conducted by a real estate consultancy is to be believed.

The survey conducted by Santos Knight Frank (SKF) says that 64% of respondents are planning to expand office space in the next 3-5 years.

The CPI inflation for Nov was just reported to be 2.5%.  This would be in line with Street expectations.  Food inflation (3.4%) crept up as non-rice components rose, tempered by the continuing, albeit slow, decline in rice prices.

The latest inflation print is likely to mean the Bangko Sentral (BSP) will cut policy rates again two weeks from now. What may derail such an action would be events overseas (e.g. the Fed).

POWER
Department of Energy (DOE) sees 4,000-6,000 MW of new coal-fired power plants may push through
  • Development of 4,000-6,000 MW coal-fired power plants could be pursued over the next few years
    • According to DOE Undersecretary Rowena Guevarra
    • These projects are among the 20,000 MW coal-fired projects that are not covered by the coal moratorium
    • Other coal-fired projects may no longer push through due to financing issues
  • This includes the following projects:
    • Meralco’s (MER PM) 1,200MW ultra-super critical coal-fired power plant in Atimonan, Quezon, as well as Global Business Power’s 80 MW coal plant
    • San Miguel’s (SMC PM) Masinloc 700 MW and Mariveles 600 MW greenfield plants
  • Coal-fired plants (11,730 MW) account for 45% of the country’s dependable capacity (25,876 MW) today
    • In the Luzon grid alone, coal plants total 8,457 MW, accounting for 46% of the grid’s total capacity of 18,559 MW

Aboitiz Power sees strong 2024 finish

PROPERTY
Survey shows majority of office occupants plan to expand in 3-5 years
  • Based on a survey by property consultancy firm Santos Knight Frank, 64% of respondents plan to expand their office space over the next 3-5 years
  • We suspect that very few (if any) POGO firms were part of the survey
  • Based on our estimates, POGO firms still occupy about 4% of Metro Manila office stock (equivalent to 600,000sqm)
  • Thus, Metro Manila office vacancy is likely to increase to 20% (from the current 16%) once the POGO ban becomes effective (at the end of the year)
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 49% of Filinvest Land’s NAV - FLI is trading at 66% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY25E P/B, and 5.0X FY25E P/E
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV - MEG is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.3X FY25E P/B, and 3.6X FY25E P/E
    • 28% of Robinsons Land’s NAV - RLC is trading at 69% discount to NAV, 0.4X FY25E P/B, and 4.9X FY25E P/E
