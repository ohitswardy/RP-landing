REGIS REPORT

30 January 2024

Union Bank’s (UBP PM) slew of disclosures yesterday leaves us doubly, triply confused. First, the bank reported headline NPAT of P9.2bn, down 27% YoY, with 4Q23 down 57%. Core operations seemed strong – NIMs were up 70bps, NII was up 34%, fees were up 54%, and PPOP was up 25%. However, the bank put through a staggering P14bn worth of provisions, more than triple YoY. It is unclear at this stage whether the provisions were for the Citi portfolio, for UnionDigital, or something else.

The bank also declared a P0.80/sh cash dividend, equivalent to P2.4bn. But then UBP also announced a P10bn rights issue, to pump more capital into UnionDigital. This will be the third rights issue in as many years. That said, their parent bank (solo) capital ratios were quite healthy as of 3Q23, with CAR of 15.3% and CET-1 of 13.2%, which seem to leave enough room for a UnionDigital capital infusion.

UBP has a compelling narrative of digital-heavy expansion into the high-margin consumer loan segment. Hopefully, this is just a case of “birthing pains”, and that the provisions and capital calls set the stage for strong (profitable) growth going forward.

+++

After a re-bid, ICTSI (ICT PM) was awarded the concession for a port in the province of Iloilo in the western Visayas. This new, 25-year concession would add to its nine concessions in the Philippines and over 30 globally.  On a full year basis, we estimate the Iloilo port would add 1% to group volumes and revenues.

+++

The Dept of Finance (DOF) is proposing to remove tax exemptions for pickup trucks.  The exemptions for pickup trucks were a last minute insertion under the TRAIN Law’s provisions on auto taxes.  The DOF estimates that the removal of the exemptions would raise P43bn (~US$760m) in revenue.

BANKS
UBP - FY23 profits down sharply; announces P10bn rights
  • Union Bank (UBP PM) disclosed at least three things yesterday: 1) Headline 2023 NPAT fell 27% YoY to P9.2bn; 2) The bank declared a P0.80/sh cash dividend, equivalent to P2.4bn; and 3) a P10bn rights issue.
  • UBP disclosed headline 2023 NPAT of P9.2bn, down c27% YoY, and well behind consensus of P11bn (was as high as P21bn in March 2023). This would suggest that 4Q23 NPAT alone was P1.1bn, plunging 57% YoY and 33% QoQ by our estimate. Loans grew 10% YoY, slowing from the 14.5% pace in 3Q23. That said, NII jumped 34% YoY, as the high-yielding Citi assets too NIMs up 70bps YoY to 5.5% by the bank’s estimate. Despite the high opex associated with the Citi transition agreement, PPOP still grew 25% YoY. However, profits were crushed by outsized provision expense more than tripling to P14bn. It is unclear at this stage whether the provisions were for Citi or for UnionDigital assets.
  • The bank also sent mixed signals to the market. On the one hand, they declared a P0.80/sh dividend, equivalent to P2.4bn. At the same time, it announced a P10bn rights issue to infuse more capital into UnionDigital, among other things. Terms and timing of the rights are still TBD. This will be the bank’s third rights issue in as many years. As of 3Q23, the bank had parent bank (solo) CAR of 15.3% and CET-1 of 13.2%, well above the minimum 10.5%.

 

INFRA
ICTSI awarded 25-year contract for Iloilo port
  • The Philippine Ports Authority awarded ICTSI (ICT PM) the 25-year cargo-handling contract for the Iloilo Commercial Port Complex (ICPC)
    • ICPC will be renamed “Visayas Container Terminal” (VCT)
    • ICTSI won the contract after it was rebid last month due to lack of documents showing proof of authorization
  • VCT will be ICTSI’s first foray in the Visayas region
    • The port will handle domestic and foreign containers in the first 6 years of operations and will eventually handle foreign containers solely after
    • ICTSI will pay the PPA 20% of revenues on the first five years of operations but this will revert to a fixed P750mn (c.US$13mn) concession fee payment over a 5-year period
    • We estimate the port could add c. 1% to consolidated volumes and revenues
  • ICTSI trades at 7.6x FY24E EV/EBITDA and 11.9x PER, below peers (11x/15x) and historical valuations (11x/23x)

 

AUTOS
DOF pushes for limited tax exemption for pickup trucks
  • In a Senate hearing, Finance Undersecretary Karlo Adriano said that the government wants to remove tax exemptions on pickup trucks, except for single-cab chassis pickup trucks
    • Other types of vehicles such as the Toyota Hilux, Ford Ranger, and Nissan Navara will no longer be granted special tax treatment
    • Proposal is under Tax Package 4 of the Comprehensive Tax Reform Program
  • The DOF estimates additional government revenues of up to P42.6bn should this push through
    • Pickup trucks comprised 16% of total industry sales in 2023
    • We estimate price hikes of c. 20% if the tax exemption is removed
