13 May 2024

Banking sector NPL (ex-interbank) ratios improved 5bps MoM to 3.47% in March, while Past Due (PD) ratios eked out a 1bp MoM dip to 4.30%. Intriguingly, the improvement was seen by larger universal/commercial banks (UKBs), whose NPLs fell by a larger 7bps MoM to 3.14%. In contrast, asset quality for smaller thrift banks (TBs) and digibanks deteriorated sharply in March. NPLs for TBs jumped 38bps MoM to 7.13%, while those of Digibanks spiked up 369bps MoM. Are larger banks just that much better at loan underwriting? Or do the TB/Digibank data point to increased stress in the SME and consumer loan portfolios that could work its way into the UKB numbers down the road?

Airline Cebu Air (CEB PM) held its earnings briefing last Friday. The 47% YoY improvement in core profit to P1.98bn was attributed to strong demand in both domestic and international sectors, as well as favorable competitive dynamics. This backdrop has been sustained so far, with forward bookings trending better for 2Q24 vs 1Q24. The more disappointing news is that, while the planned capital restructuring is underway, management deferred the possibility of declaring dividends in 4Q24 to next year, as it wants to maintain capital flexibility once ongoing negotiations with Airbus and Boeing are finalized.

FDI net inflows rise to US$1.36bn in February
  • FDI net inflows rose 29% YoY to US$1.36bn in February
    • Cited 927% YoY expansion in net investments in equity capital (other than reinvestment of earnings) to US$764mn
    • Tempered by 42% YoY contraction in net investments in debt instruments and 3.8% drop in reinvestment of earnings
    • Bulk of the equity capital placements during the reference month came from the Netherlands with investments directed mostly to the financial and insurance industry
  • Brings YT-Feb FDI inflows up 48% YoY to US$2.3bn
BANKS
NPLs improve, -5bps MoM in March; big banks perform much better
  • Asset quality (all banks) saw slight improvement across all metrics in March, while loan growth inched up. NPL ratio (ex-interbank) dropped 5bps MoM to 3.47% from 3.52% in February. More importantly, in our view, the absolute PHP value of NPLs fell 0.3% MoM. Past Due (PD) rates, meanwhile, edged down 1bp MoM to 4.30%, as did Restructured Loans (RL), to 2.15%. NPL Cover, also improved a hair, up to 100.7% from 100.1% in February. Meanwhile, loan growth (ex-interbank) inched up to 10.2% YoY in March, compared to 10.0% in February. On a MoM basis, loans were up 1.1%.

 

  • Intriguingly, the asset quality data was much better for the larger universal/commercial banks (UKBs), which saw PD ratios down 7bps, and NPL ratios down 8bps MoM. For Thrift Banks (TBs), PD rates jumped 77bps and NPLs increased 38bps MoM. The biggest jump by far was for Digibanks, whose PDs spiked 588bps MoM while NPLs jumped 369bps MoM.

 

  • On the one hand, the stability / improvement in UKB asset quality is comforting for investors in the larger listed banks. On the other, the sharp deterioration in TB/Digibank data suggests heightened stress among smaller borrowers, likely consumers and/or SMEs. The BSP has yet to release consumer loan data for 1Q24.

Lance Gokongwei joins SPNEC Board
  • JG Summit President and CEO Lance Gokongwei was elected as a member of the Board of Directors of SP New Energy Corp (SPNEC PM) during the company’s stockholders’ meeting
  • Manila Electric (MER PM) currently owns 55% of SPNEC 
  • JG Summit holds a 26% stake in Meralco
  • SPNEC, through subsidiary Terra Solar Philippines Inc., is developing the massive solar farm in Nueva Ecija and Bulacan which will consist of 3,500 megawatts (MW) of solar panels and 4,000 megawatt-hours of battery storage
  • Project is set to be completed in two phases:
    • First phase will have a capacity of 2500 MW and is slated for completion by 2026

MINING
DENR to shorten permit process for mining
  • The DENR plans to implement “parallel” processing of mining permits this year to shorten bureaucratic approval of prospective mining operations to just 1-3 years from 6 years
    • Mining permits that are not prerequisites for securing another government permit will be simultaneously processed by concerned government agencies
