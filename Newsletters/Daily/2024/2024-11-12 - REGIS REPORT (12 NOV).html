REGIS REPORT

12 November 2024

Results are coming in thick and fast, as we near the deadline for 3Q reporting. But before that, a few items that shouldn’t get lost in the shuffle:

  • The CREATE MORE Act was signed into law yesterday, that gives tax and non-tax perks to promote small businesses, and new investments, particularly to export manufacturers. It is important to note that the incentives, including the cut in corporate income tax from 25% to 20% do NOT apply to all companies, as some had hoped. While we view the move as positive for future investments (and FDIs), the positive impact to our market earnings will be miniscule. There could be some negative impact to office landlords as PEZA-registered BPOs are now allowed to have 50% of staff working remotely (up from 30% previously).

  • Speaking of negative for office… As discussed yesterday, President Marcos issued Executive Order 74 (EO 74), which formalizes the ban on the offshore gaming sector. We quantify some of the impact of the ban on the property sector.

Philippine Property - President Marcos formalizes POGO ban

  • The Senate passed on third reading the bill promoting the use of indigenous gas, taking it very close to final approval.  The Lower House already passed the counterpart bill on third reading.  The proposed law would grant advantages for those using indigenous natural gas, including on tax (i.e. 12% VAT).  This bill would allow natgas users like First Gen (FGEN PM) to become more price competitive against other gencos who use LNG

 

+++

And now, back to the earnings roundup:

  • SM Prime (SMPH PM) (=) – SM Prime’s 9M24 operating profit grew 7% to P47.7bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. However, reservation sales were incredibly weak. After falling 48% QoQ in 2Q24, presales dropped another 50% QoQ (67% YoY) to P6.8bn, the lowest quarterly figure in the past 10 years. 3Q24 mall revenue growth also slowed to 6% YoY, from 8% in 1H24.

    SM Prime - 3Q24 presales fall 50% QoQ and 67% YoY

    SM Prime - 3Q24 presales fall 50% QoQ and 67% YoY

  • Globe (GLO PM) (=) – Core profits for 9M grew 19% to P17.6bn, in line with expectations. Mobile revenue growth, however, decelerated to +3% and we are not quite certain if this is a blip (due to the natural disasters that affected many areas in 3Q).  What’s clear, however, is that the non-telco businesses continue to perform, with Mynt alone contributing 22% of 3Q core income.

Globe - 3Q: Revenue growth slows, non-telco operations compensate

Globe - 3Q: Revenue growth slows, non-telco operations compensate

  • First Gen (FGEN PM) (-) – Recurring profit for 9M came in at US$206m, -17% YoY, and a tad below expectations.  For 3Q alone, recurring profit was US$56m (-19% YoY).  The core natgas power business remained resilient but contributions from its RE business (i.e. Energy Development Corp) declined due to lower output at both its geothermal and wind operations.
