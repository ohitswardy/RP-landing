REGIS REPORT

12 September 2024

It seems that PAGCOR chairman Alejandro Tengco has had several speaking engagements over the past few days.

Two days ago, he said that Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) would open an integrated casino resort in Cavite in 2028. However, the company issued a statement clarifying that the said project would be completed in 5 to 10 years, well past the opening date that Mr. Tengco envisioned.

Yesterday, Mr. Tengco said that PAGCOR would focus on its role as a regulator and that it was looking to raise P50bn from the sale of its 45 casinos in 2026.  

+++

Another section of the Cavite-Laguna Expressway (“CALAx”) will be opened to the public by early next year.  This should improve access to those areas (western side of Cavite) and beyond. The entire CALAx will be completed by 3Q25.   Better access should help property values, and a few firms have land bank around there, including GT Capital (GTCAP PM), Ayala Land (ALI PM) and to a lesser extent even SM Prime (SMPH PM).

+++

Surprising the market, Converge (CNVRG PM) declared its first dividend – P0.18/share, record date Sept 24 – ahead of its promised dividend in 2025.  The company also revealed its new policy of paying out 25-30% of audited earnings (effectively prior year’s).

MACRO
NEDA sees faster GDP growth in 2H
  • The National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) sees GDP growth for 2H24 to be faster than the 6.0% print in 1H24
    • According to NEDA Secretary Arsenio M. Balisacan
    • Secretary Balisacan thinks the likelihood of the government achieving its 6-7% GDP growth goal this year is now very high, citing easing inflation
  • To reach the high-end of the government’s target range, GDP growth in 2H will need to average 7.9%
INFRASTRUCTURE
Cavite segment of the Cavite-Laguna Expressway (CALAX) to partially open by 1Q25
  • MPCALA Holdings is set to open the Governor’s Drive Interchange of the CALAX project by 1Q25
    • Segment is currently at a 25% completion rate
    • It is anticipated to help traffic congestion in Aguinaldo Highway in Silang, Cavite and other major roads in Cavite (Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, and Quezon)
    • Segment spans 7.8km from Silang (Aguinaldo) Interchange to Governor’s drive, will be the longest operational section of CALAX
  • To date, the overall completion rate of CALAX reached 64%, it said, adding that the expressway is now serving about 45,000 motorists per day
    • CALAX is a 45-kilometer four-lane expressway with eight interchanges. Once fully operational, it will serve at least 95,000 motorists daily
    • The entire expressway is set to be fully operational by 3Q25
  • Property companies (e.g. GTCAP's Federal Land, Ayala Land) with landbank in the surrounding areas of Cavite could see land values increase once CALAX is completed
    • GTCAP’s property unit accounts for 25% of GTCAP’s NAV (assumes Cavite land value at ~P6k per sqm); GTCAP trades at a 44% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,268/sh
    • 30% of ALI's NAV is comprised of land in Cavite and Laguna; ALI trades at 35% discount to NAV, 1.8x FY24E P/B, and 18.8x FY24E P/E
POWER/UTILITIES
Meralco household rates inch up in September
  • Meralco’s overall household rate will climb by P0.1543/kwh (+1.3%) MoM to P11.7882/kwh
    • Cited 50% higher transmission charges due to the doubling of ancillary service charges
    • This offset the 2.2% MoM drop in generation charges
  • On a YoY basis, the overall rate is up 3.4%, slightly slower than the 6.7% pace in August
  • Electricity prices account for 4.5% of the CPI basket
    • Recall that headline inflation decelerated to 3.3% in August from 4.4% in July, even as electricity CPI rose 3.1% YoY, 3.4% MoM in August due to slower increases in rice, other food, and fuel-related transport CPI
CONGLOMERATES
Cosco Capital buys 2nd power plant in Nueva Vizcaya
  • Cosco Capital (COSCO PM) bought 100% of Matuno River Development Corp. (MRDC) for P1.02bn
    • MRCD is the developer of the 8.66 MW Matuno River hydroelectric plant in Nueva Vizcaya
    • The run-of-river facility is covered by a hydro power service contract with the Department of Energy
  • In March, COSCO bought a 60% stake in Catuiran Hydropower Corp. for P552mn
    • Catuiran is the operator of an 8MW hydropower plant in Oriental Mindoro
GAMING
PAGCOR targets P50bn from privatization of casinos
  • PAGCOR is targeting P50bn from the privatization of its 45 casinos
  • It is currently upgrading its facilities ahead of the privatization, which will begin in early 2026
  • PAGCOR also said that it expects GGR of e-games to triple YoY to P100bn this year
    • 1H24 GGR of e-games grew 530% to P53.4bn
  • It also expects GGR of licensed casinos to grow 33% to P275bn this year
    • 1H24 GGR of licensed casinos fell 6% to P100bn
