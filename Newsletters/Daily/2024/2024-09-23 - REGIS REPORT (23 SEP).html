REGIS REPORT

23 September 2024

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) surprised the market with a chunky reserve requirement ratio (RRR) cut last Friday.  The 250bp reduction to 7.0% is a surprise both in terms of timing and magnitude, coming only a few weeks after the recent policy rate cut.  BSP Governor Eli Remolona had voiced his intention to slash bank RRRs “substantially” only a few days prior.  The Fed had also just reduced its policy rate range by 50bp to 4.75% to 5.0%.

This action takes effect on Oct 25 and will release over P300bn (~US$6bn) of liquidity into the system, providing another boost to the economy. It’s a clear signal that the BSP is comfortable about where inflation is heading.  What’s less clear is whether this might mean a trade-off for smaller policy rate reductions.

In any case, the immediate impact will be a decline in interest rates, particularly PH Treasury yields and deposit rates. For banks, the RRR cut (unlike a policy rate cut) is unambiguously positive, even if the net profit impact is not huge (close to +4%, annualized).

Malacanang announced the appointment of Jesse Andres, an Undersecretary of the Dept of Justice (DOJ) as OIC-Chair of the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC). The appointment is a surprise as we had expected the appointment of an ERC insider to the position.  The leanings of Andres on the issues involving the energy sector are unknown. Prior to his appointment with the DOJ, Andres was a director at mining firm Benguet Corp (BC PM), which is controlled by the Romualdez family, cousins to President Marcos.

+++

In a “detail” some might have missed in his note released last Friday, Jefferies Global Strategist Christopher Wood (christopher.wood@jefferies.com) raised his weighting for the Philippines in his model portfolio from 1.0% to 1.5%.

+++

The Philippine telcos are asking the government to reduce payments for spectrum that telcos make in the form of spectrum user fees (SUF). The telcos argue the SUF payments (P5bn or <US$100m industrywide) increase their costs, which would have otherwise made it easier to improve their infrastructure. Frankly, this lobbying seems a little rich, considering the Philippines is among the few countries where telcos don’t have to pay for spectrum upfront.

+++

Ayala Land (ALI PM) sold 75m shares of AREIT (AREIT PM) worth P2.7bn on Friday, equivalent to ~3% of the latter’s shares. The sale reduced ALI’s stake to 51% prior to the completion of the last announced asset injection, or 64% post-injection.

MACRO
BSP reduces reserve requirements
  • The BSP is reducing the reserve requirement ratios (RRR) by October 25
    • Universal/commercial banks’ and non-bank financial institutions with quasi-banking functions’ RRR will be cut by 250bps to 7%
    • Digital banks’ RRR will be cut by 200bps to 4%
    • Thrift banks’ and rural banks’ RRR will be cut by 100bps to 1.0% and 0.0%, respectively
MACRO
BSP revises BOP forecasts; sees wider current account deficits but larger BOP surpluses in 2024/2025
  • The BSP is projecting higher BOP surpluses to US$2.3bn in 2024 and US$1.7bn in 2025, higher than its previous projections of US$1.6bn and US$1.5bn, respectively
    • Cited higher-than-expected non-resident inflows or financial account in 1H24, driven by higher foreign portfolio investments, which emanated from higher withdrawals of placements in foreign-issued debt securities
    • This offset higher current account deficit projections of US$6.8bn in 2024 and US$5.5bn in 2025 due to lower projections in merchandise exports

POWER/UTILITIES
DOJ Undersecretary Jesse Andres named OIC chairman and CEO of ERC
  • Executive Secretary Lucas Bersamin appointed Department of Justice Undersecretary Jesse Hermogenes T. Andres as OIC Chairman and CEO of the ERC
    • Position will be in effect until a replacement is designated or until otherwise directed
    • This comes after the 6-month suspension of previous ERC Chairperson Monalisa Dimalanta last Sep 9
    • This move reportedly breaks from tradition, as typically the most senior commissioner of the ERC would be named OIC
  • Mr. Andres is a former director of Benguet Corp. and was previously the chief of staff of former Vice President Noli de Castro and was the previous head of the litigation department at Ponce Enrile Cayetano Bautista Reyes & Manalastas Law Offices (PECABAR) before establishing his own law firm
TELCOS
Telcos seek reductions in spectrum user fees
  • Telco industry players are asking for a reduction in cost on the spectrum user fees (SUF) that the government charges on telcos, which amount to P5.39bn a year
  • Philippine Chamber of Telecommunication Operators (PCTO) Vice President Roy Ibay said additional SUF costs make it harder for telcos to expand their networks
    • The PCTO hopes that the government can consider reducing the SUF so providers can allocate more capital for infrastructure buildup.
  • The SUF funds the P2.5bn requirement of the Free Internet Access Program, which puts internet sites in public spaces, such as government offices and schools.
