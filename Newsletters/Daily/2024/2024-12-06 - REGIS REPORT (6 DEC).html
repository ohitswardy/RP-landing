REGIS REPORT

06 December 2024

With Nov CPI inflation inching up to 2.5%, the case for further monetary easing domestically is on track. We still believe the Bangko Sentral (BSP) will cut rates two weeks from now. What could derail this are events abroad, including a more hawkish (less dovish) turn for the Fed. The FOMC meeting will be hours before the BSP’s Monetary Board meeting this month. So far, markets are pricing in a ~70% chance of a Fed rate cut that day.

+++

P3.05bn worth of deferred charges relating to the electricity reserve market will be collected beginning the next billing cycle. This would raise end-user rates by ~1% or less, depending on the grid. Energy prices remain a source of upside risk for CPI inflation. Electricity is 4.6% of CPI.

+++

SM Prime (SMPH PM) plans to open three malls next year (2025), after the planned two malls this year. For 2026 and 2027, the company will be adding five each year, which will push their Philippine mall count to 100. Apart from the new malls, SMPH will also be renovating ten of their malls to keep them fresh and expand space.

Ayala Land (ALI PM), meanwhile, opened two new malls this year and is in the midst of renovating four of its “flagship” malls.

MACRO
Inflation inches up to 2.5% in November
  • Headline inflation inched up to 2.5% in November from 2.3% in October
    • This is in-line with consensus forecast of 2.5% and came in at the midpoint of the BSP's forecast range of 2.2-3.0%
  • On a MoM basis, CPI rose 0.4%, primarily due to higher food prices which increased by 0.7% MoM
    • The increase was mainly driven by price upticks in vegetables and fish as several typhoons hit the country in the month of November
    • Rice CPI sequentially dipped for the fifth consecutive month by 1.5%, which brings the cumulative decline to 3.0%
      • Prices of well-milled rice stood at P42-52/kg for local and P45-54 for imported on Dec 3 (vs. P43-54 for local and P45-55 for imported rice on Oct 31) based on the Dept of Agriculture’s data
    • Outside of food, petrol prices which saw a slight decrease of 0.2% last month—saw an increase of 1.2% in November due to the weaker peso and global oil supply concerns
  • Core inflation and core non-food inflation both remained relatively stable at 2.5% and 2.7% respectively, as both saw a small increase of 0.1% from last month
  • Bringing average YT-November inflation rate to 3.2%, on track to meet the BSP's risk adjusted inflation forecast of 3.1%—in-line with Regis' FY24E of 3.1%
  • This latest print increases the likelihood that the Bangko Sentral (BSP) will cut policy rates again two weeks from now
    • Although what may derail such an action would be events overseas (e.g. the Fed)
    • To note, the next FOMC meeting will be on Dec 18 (EST), while the next BSP Monetary Board meeting is on Dec. 19

 

 

 

 

 

 

POWER
Electricity prices set to rise in January after ERC approves WESM charges worth P3.1bn
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission approved the collection of the remaining reserve trading amounts in the WESM
    • Un-collected amount of P3.05bn will be charged to consumers over a three to six-month period
    • Deferred payments stemmed from the moratorium imposed on March 26, which suspended the billing for the WESM’s co-optimized energy and reserve due to concerns with the ERC-approved Price Determination Methodology
    • On May 9, the ERC allowed energy providers to collect only a portion of the P8.8bn during the moratorium
  • Consumers in Luzon and Mindanao will see an additional P0.124/kwh increase in rates for three months
    • Consumers in Visayas will see an increase of P0.033/kwh for six months
  • Electricity accounts for 4.6% of the CPI basket
POWER
DOE resets Green Energy Auction to early 2025
  • The Department of Energy (DOE) is rescheduling the third green energy auction (GEA-3) for geothermal and hydro technologies to January 2025
    • According to DOE Undersecretary Felix William Fuentabella at the DOE Investment Forum last week
    • It is currently waiting for the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) to release the green energy auction reserve (GEAR) prices, which could be released within the next two weeks
  • GEA-3 will cover non- feed-in-tariff (FIT) technologies:
    • 399 MW of impounding hydro, 3,120MW of pumped storage hydro and 380 MW of geothermal, as well as FIT-eligible 200 MW run-of-river hydro
    • Target start of deliveries is 2028-2030 for hydro while 2024-2030 for geothermal
PROPERTY
SM Prime to open three malls in 2025
  • SM Prime said it would open three new Philippine malls in 2025
    • It currently has 87 malls in the Philippines
  • The company will also redevelop 10 Philippine malls next year
  • It also plans to complete around five malls annually in 2026 and 2027
    • This will bring the total number of Philippine malls to 100
  • These plans are broadly within our expectations
  • SMPH is trading at 38% discount to NAV, 1.7X FY25E P/B, and 16.6X FY25E P/E
