REGIS REPORT

16 October 2024

Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) announced it completed the refinancing of P72bn of debt, an exercise which would extend its debt maturities and also reduce its cost. The principal payments of the ten-year debt facility will be back-ended, and the financing cost will likely be 70-80bp lower (leading to interest savings of >P500m annually).

+++

Container volumes at the Manzanillo port of ICTSI (ICT PM) in Mexico are showing a sharp slowdown in growth for 3Q.  Before one gets too concerned about it, this sharp deceleration appears to be a consequence of the port being at full capacity already, an issue that’s expected to be resolved this month (Oct). ICTSI opened its new container yard a few weeks ago, and the new facilities will raise capacity by 17%.

+++

Overseas remittances rose 3.2% to US$2.89bn in Aug, in line with growth expectations.

+++

The renewable energy unit of First Gen (FGEN PM), Energy Development Corp (EDC), says it may spend P25bn (US$430m) to boost the output of its geothermal facilities in the southern part of Negros island. The amount does look large relative to EDC’s current capacity there (222MW, dependable). This suggests a meaningful increase in output if the activities are successful.

+++

The Monetary Board of the Bangko Sentral (BSP) will meet to decide on monetary policy later today. It is widely expected to announce another 25bp reduction in policy rates.

MACRO
Remittances rise 3.2% YoY in August
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks rose by 3.2% YoY to US$2.89bn in August, mildly accelerating from 3.1% rate in July
    • In PHP terms, remittances rose by 5% YoY, moderating from the 6.5% pace in YT-July due to PHP appreciation
    • The change in school calendar (start of school year varied between July/August this year vs Aug/Sep last year) could have still distorted the growth rate
  • Brings YT-August remittances up by 2.9% YoY to US$22.2bn
  • The BSP expects remittances to grow by 3% YoY in 2024 (to ~US$34bn)

GAMING
Bloomberry refinances P72bn of debt
  • Bloomberry refinanced P72bn worth of debt
  • The previous loans had an interest rate of c. 7.5%
  • The new syndicated refinancing facility will be priced at a spread that is 75 basis points lower than the previous facilities
    • This should mean annual savings of c. P540mn (equivalent to 5% of FY23 net profit)
    • BLOOM will also have the option to fix the interest rate within the next 12 months
  • If the loans had not been refinanced, BLOOM would have been required to pay P72bn over the next four years (2028)
  • The new syndicated refinancing facility has a term of 10 years (until October 2034) and the principal payments will be back-ended
  • This should slightly increase the likelihood of BLOOM paying dividends
    • The company’s recently announced dividend policy is to pay 35% of previous year’s earnings as dividends
TRANSPORT
Congestion in Manzanillo, Mexico causes 3Q24 volume slowdown in ICTSI’s Mexico port
  • Throughput volumes in ICTSI’s Contecon Manzanillo (CMSA) port rose by 3% YoY to 120k TEUs in September on lower transshipment volumes
    • Industry volumes in Manzanillo fell 6% YoY during the month of September due to lower transshipment volumes
  • This brings 3Q24 volumes in CMSA up by 2% YoY, 8% QoQ to 367k TEUS; growth rate slowed sharply from +20% YoY pace in 1H24
    • Industry-wide volumes fell 2% YoY and was flat QoQ to 976k TEUs
  • Brings 9M24 volumes in CMSA up by 13% YoY to 1.065mn TEUs
    • We forecast CMSA’s volumes will grow by 12% YoY to 1.43mn TEUs in 2024, suggesting that volumes for the rest of the year only need to average 120k TEUs per month to reach our forecast
    • We estimate CMSA to be ICT’s largest port today, accounting for 11% of volumes, 20% of revenues, and 24% of EBITDA
  • Note that ICTSI opened its new container yard in Mexico in end-September (https://www.ictsi.com/news/contecon-manzanillo-commissions-new-container-yards)
    • This should prompt a rebound in CMSA’s volume growth in 4Q, given that the new container yard boosts the port’s annual capacity by 17% to 1.7mn TEUs from 1.45mn TEUs
    • At full build-out by end-2025, ICT estimates the port’s capacity could increase by 40-45% from 1.45mn TEUs in September 2024
    • Note that ICT is the only port in Manzanillo that has available land for an expansion
  • ICTSI trades at 8.3x FY25E EV/EBITDA and 13.4x PER, below peer average (11x/16x) and historical average (11x/21x)

POWER
EDC eyes P25bn expansion of Negros steam field project
  • Energy Development Corp. (EDC) plans to allocate P25bn for the expansion of its 282.5-megawatt (MW) Southern Negros Geothermal Project (SNGP) in Negros Oriental
    • Covers the cost of drilling new wells, the expansion of existing pads, and the construction of new pads, road networks, pipeline routes and other support structures, as necessary
    • The estimated amount also includes the budget for emerging technologies
    • SNGP currently has a total dependable capacity of 221.8 MW due to steam availability constraints of one unit (Nasuji geothermal plant)
  • The company is currently applying for an Environment Compliance Certificate amendment for the steamfields of the SNGP
    • It is proposing a change in the boundary/shape of the development block and a reduction from 5,163 hectares to 4,027.59 hectares
  • At present, EDC has an installed capacity of 1,480.19 MW, accounting for about 20% of the country’s total installed renewable energy capacity
POWER
SCC declares special dividend of P2.50/sh
  • Semirara (SCC PM) declared a special dividend of P2.50/sh
    • This is higher than REGIS' forecast of P2/sh and consensus estimate of P1.80/sh
    • Ex-dividend date: Oct 28
    • Record date: Oct 29
    • Payment date: Nov 14
  • This takes cumulative 2024 dividends to P6.00/sh, equivalent to a yield of ~17%
