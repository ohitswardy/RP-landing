BPI’s (BPI PM) headline profit for 2023 was P51.7bn, up 31% YoY, in line with Regis (P51.55bn) and ahead of consensus (P50.33bn). Loan growth jumped to 10.5% YoY, compared to 8.8% in 3Q23, while NIMs expanded 50bps YoY. Higher opex was offset by strong trading gains. Provisions fell 56% to 4bn, as NPLs improved to 1.84%, from 1.97% in 3Q23. Its NPL cover dipped to 154% from 159% in 3Q, as the bank has been guiding a run-down of its over-provisioning levels.

Overall, a pretty solid set of results. The bank will host an earnings call on Tuesday, 6 February at 4:30pm.

BANKS
BPI FY23 NPAT P51.7bn in line; loan growth speeds up to 10.5%

BPI (BPI PM), in a press release, reported headline FY23 NPAT of P51.7bn, up 31% YoY, bang in line with Regis estimates of P51.5bn, and slightly ahead of consensus. Loan growth accelerated to 10.5% (in line with Regis 10.3%), up from the 8.8% pace in 3Q23. Opex was slightly elevated in 4Q, but this was offset by higher trading gains, and lower provisions than we expected. Overall a pretty solid set of results. The bank will host an earnings call on Tuesday, 6 February at 4:30pm.

Some highlights from the press release:

  • Loan growth sped up to 10.5% YoY, from 8.8% in 3Q23, with expansion seen across the board. We suspect the acceleration was led by late big-ticket corporate loans, while consumer stayed robust. Deposits grew 9.5%, driven by TDs, which took the bank’s CASA ratio down to 67%, from 69.3% in 3Q, and from 74% at end-2022.
  • Full-year NIMs were up 50bps YoY, by the company’s estimates, in line with Regis forecast (51bps).
  • Fee incomes were down 3% YoY on a headline basis. However, recall that FY22 included a P5bn sale of a property asset, without which fees would have been up 16.6% YoY. Trading/fx gains also jumped strongly to P5.2bn.
  • Opex was up 19% YoY for FY23, higher than we forecast. The 4Q23 was particularly surprising, up 19% QoQ. We suspect some of the jump was due to performance and/or milestone payments for the bank’s tech contractors.
  • Provision expense was P4bn, down 40% YoY. NPL ratio improved to 1.84% (from 1.97% in 3Q) while NPL cover dipped to 154% (from 159% in 3Q). The bank has been guiding that they intended to bring down NPL coverage, as they (and their auditors) believed they were over-provided.

More after the briefing tomorrow.

 

TRANSPORT
ICTSI reaches settlement with labor group in Portland, Oregon
  • ICTSI and International Longshore Warehouse Union (ILWU) reached a settlement on all of ICTSI’s claims against the latter
    • Pursuant to the settlement, ICTSI will receive a payment of US$20.5mn
    • Settlement was agreed on during ILWU’s chapter 11 bankruptcy case, which will be voluntarily dismissed as part of the terms of the settlement
  • Recall that ICTSI was forced to pre-terminate its contract in Portland due to the work stoppage of ILWU in 2017, even though it still had 20 years left on its contract
    • ICT paid US$11.5mn as a pre-termination fee to the Port of Portland in 2017
    • ICTSI will book a gain from this settlement
  • ICT trades at 8.1x FY24E EV/EBITDA and 12.7x PER, below peers (11x/15x) and historical valuations (11x/23x)
UTILITIES
Meralco sees electricity sales +10% in January
  • According to preliminary data, Meralco says it recorded over 4,000GWh sales in Jan, around 10% higher vs Jan 2023
    • The growth comes off a relatively soft base in Jan 2023
  • All segments are “performing better”
  • Meralco also noted that the country experienced warmer temperatures in Dec up until the 3rd week of January, which likely contributed to the growth
  • For 2024, the company is expecting +4.5% volume growth
POWER
SN Aboitiz Power's Magat BESS Project gets the go signal from ERC
  • In a disclosure dated Feb 2, Aboitiz discloses that its 24-MW Magat BESS project with Scatec received provisional authority to operate last Jan 17
  • Timeline of the project is as follows
    • Began construction in April 2022
    • Completed construction in July 2023
    • Concluded testing in December 2023
    • Started commercial operation in the reserve market on January 26 2024
  • Magat BESS is connected to the grid via a 230-kV power transformer and is co-located with SNAP's Magat hydroelectric power plant in Ramon, Isabela
