REGIS REPORT

11 April 2024

As was widely expected, the Bangko Sentral (BSP) kept policy rates steady at 6.5%.The inflation print last week was unlikely to have prompted any moves from the BSP (and also any changes in the market’s view for future rate decisions). The CPI prints for April and May are likely to set the tone for the rate moves (cuts or not). The BSP is still stressing that the inflation risks are still tilted to the upside.

This comes against the backdrop of new U.S economic data moving against the idea of any short-term rate cuts. The market is now expecting only around 50bp of cuts by the U.S. Federal Reserve this year and the Fed’s moves will certainly have an influence on how the BSP acts.

+++

Century Pacific Food (CNPF PM) reported net income of P5.6bn, up 12% YoY.  This is a bit short of our estimates but in line with the consensus numbers. Gross margins were up sharply YoY in 4Q23 as the company reaped the benefits of (1) price adjustments earlier in the year and (2) favorable input costs (skipjack tuna, milk). Heavy A&P spend in 4Q trimmed the margin gains, however. The branded business grew 7% during the quarter, which was largely volume driven. Market shares in core branded categories were sustained despite persistent downtrading by consumers. The company is guiding to grow net profit by low double digit this year, which does not imply much upside from where estimates are currently (Regis FY24E: +14%). At ~20x 2024E PE, the stock trades broadly in line with regional branded consumer food peers.

+++

In a sign of the times, President Marcos arrived in the U.S. today for a trilateral summit among the leaders of the U.S., Japan, and the Philippines. While it is claimed that the meeting is not aimed at any outside party, it’s obvious what the meeting is all about.

It’s arguable that no Philippine leader has been accorded such importance by the White House in the thirty years since the US bases were vacated in the early 1990s.

+++

One of the losing bidders in the auction for the Pier 2 concession of the Durban port in South Africa has filed a case with the courts to contest its loss. APM Terminals, a unit of Maersk, filed a suit with the Durban High Court challenging the government-owned Transnet’s decision to pick ICTSI (ICT PM) as the winner in the port auction process. It’s unclear what the basis for the suit is.

The Durban port privatization is one of the initiatives of the South African government to revitalize its infrastructure. This privatization will involve at least 2m TEUs of annual capacity. For perspective, ICTSI’s capacity today is ~20m TEUs annually.

+++

Hard discount retailer DALI says it intends to add ~300 more stores this year on top of the ~630 it already has. The expansion of DALI has led to pressure on the groceries targeting lower income consumers, including Puregold (PGOLD PM). The emergence of hard discounters has prompted PGOLD to reduce prices (and thus margins) for some key SKUs.

CONSUMER
Century Pacific - Margins recover sharply in 4Q23
  • CNPF reported FY23 net profit of P5.6bn, up 12% YoY, behind Regis estimates but in line with consensus. Consolidated revenues rose 8% YoY to P67bn (in line), while EBITDA grew 15% YoY to P8.7bn (behind) on higher-than-expected opex. For 4Q23 alone, net profit grew 25% YoY to P948mn. Margins recovered sharply during the quarter, driving EBITDA +47% YoY.
  • 4Q23 revenues rose 9% YoY to P16.7bn as the OEM segment recovered, growing 17% YoY during the quarter. The branded segment slowed to +7% YoY (+10% in 9M23), driven mainly by double-digit growth in milk (29% of revenues). While downtrading persisted, volumes across all the branded categories continued to be healthy (+mid single digit). CNPF maintained market share in the core marine (85% market share), meat (52%) and milk (28%) categories.
  • Gross profit expanded nearly 500bps YoY in 4Q23 due to favorable input cost, particularly for skipjack tuna and milk. The gross margin gains were partly offset by higher A&P and trade spending to help drive volumes.
  • Management is guiding to grow top line and net profit by low double digits in 2024 (Regis FY24E: +14%) driven by the continued recovery in OEM and growth of the branded business. Input prices of most raw materials continue to move favorably and CNPF has locked in requirements for these until 1H24, which should buoy margins in the coming quarters.
