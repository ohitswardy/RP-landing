REGIS REPORT

17 April 2024

Bangko Sentral (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona is signaling that the window for policy rate reductions this year is narrowing. Not only are the prospects for US rate cuts fading because inflation in the U.S. appears to be staying doggedly high, a further push from rising oil prices looms. Some comfort can be taken that the near-term outlook for rice prices seems to be improving.

Only a couple of months ago, there was a wide consensus that policy rate cuts of 100bp (or more) by the BSP for the year were almost certain. Now, it appears that consensus has crumbled, replaced by views that there are unlikely to be more than 50bp of cuts, if it all. 

+++

We have two more results today, both of which are beats. The quality of the 4Q/FY23 outperformance of AGI (AGI PM) and Vista Land (VLL PM), however, is not ideal.

  • Alliance Global (AGI PM) (+) – FY23 net profit rose 22% to P19.6bn, ahead of REGIS and consensus forecasts. While operations of three of the key subsidiaries (Megaworld, Travellers, and Golden Arches) did improve, lower forex losses also appeared to have been a key contributor to the increase in profits.
  • Vista Land (VLL PM) (+) – We estimate that FY23 recurring profit grew 19% to P7.3bn, as rental revenue grew 17% to P16bn, double the FY19 figure. However, rental receivables now represent two years’ worth of rental revenue, which implies that the figure is vastly overstated relative to cash rent.

+++

Property consultancy firm and broker Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) says office vacancies stayed at 18% in 1Q24, as positive net office take-up helped keep vacancies (%) stable despite more inventory. For the residential condominium segment, signals were conflicting. Primary (i.e. new) sales of condominiums dropped sharply but prices of secondary market units rose 5-10%.

+++

The power grids for Luzon and Visayas were placed on yellow and red alerts yesterday, following forced outages at power plants that pushed supply to just below demand for certain times. Situations like these bolster our view that the demand-supply balance remains tenuous, particularly in Luzon during peak demand periods. Those gencos with available, uncontracted capacity should benefit from the high spot prices. Unfortunately for some of the listed firms (Aboitiz Power (AP PM), First Gen (FGEN PM) and Semirara (SCC PM)), it was the outage/s at their plants which led to yesterday’s supply deficiency.

The influx of renewables in the next two years could ease the situation, but only during certain hours of the day and only if the transmission grid is able to accommodate the RE capacities.

PROPERTY
LPC: Office vacancy stays flat in 1Q24; high-end residential prices rise
  • During its 1Q24 briefing, Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) revealed that Philippine office vacancy stayed flat at 18%
    • We estimate that rental rates in most parts of Metro Manila are still 10%-50% below pre-COVID levels      
  • Nationwide net office take-up rose 17% QoQ (14% YoY) to 184,000sqm
    • BPO net office take-up increased 61% QoQ (-15% YoY) to 66,000sqm
    • Meanwhile POGO net office take-up fell 54% QoQ to 34,000sqm
  • Office leasing (including stakes in REITs) accounts for:
    • 49% of Filinvest Land’s NAV - FLI is trading at 69% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 4.9X FY24E P/E
    • 43% of Megaworld’s NAV - MEG is trading at 72% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 3.2X FY24E P/E
    • 28% of Robinsons Land’s NAV - RLC is trading at 64% discount to NAV, 0.5X FY23E P/B, and 6.1X FY24E P/E
  • LPC expects office vacancy to rise to 19% in FY24 before falling to 17% in FY25 (14% in FY26)
  • It said that primary residential condominium units sold fell 29% QoQ and 42% YoY to 6,928 units
  • However, it also said that secondary market prices of high-end Makati CBD and Fort Bonifcacio condominium prices generally rose 5-10% YoY
  • ALI is trading at 47% discount to NAV, 1.4X FY24E P/B, and 15.0X FY24E P/E
PROPERTY
Vista Land FY23 recurring profit rises 19%, ahead of REGIS 
  • Vista Land’s headline profit grew 41% to P8.7bn
    • Stripping off gains from insurance claims, we estimate that recurring profit rose 19% to P7.3bn, ahead of REGIS forecast (in line with consensus)
  • FY23 reservation sales rose 10% to P71.9bn, ahead of our forecast
    • 4Q23 reservation sales increased 7% QoQ (10% YoY) to P18.8bn
    • Similar to previous years, OFWs comprised 55%-60% of residential sales
  • FY23 residential revenue rose 19% to P15.3bn, in line
    • This is 47% of the FY19 figure
    • FY23 gross profit margin expanded 320 basis points to 60%
    • Only 5% of residential receivables are over 90 days past due, from 37% in 2022
  • FY23 rental revenue grew 17% to P16.0bn
    • This is double the FY19 figure, even though the rental portfolio is only about 15% larger
    • Rental receivables amounted to P32.5bn, representing two years’ worth of rental revenue

POWER
Luzon, Visayas grids placed on red and yellow alerts
  • In an announcement yesterday, the National Grid Corporation of the Philippines (NGCP) raised red and yellow alerts for Luzon and Visayas grids in the following periods
  • Luzon grid
    • Yellow alert from 1-2 PM
    • Red alert from 2-11 PM
    • As of 6 PM yesterday, the grid held an available capacity of 12,031MW and a peak demand of 11,995MW, with 2,477MW unavailable to the grid as a result of 21 power plants on forced outage and three plants running on derated capacities
    • Pagbilao units 1 and 2, Masinloc 1, Sta. Rita 40, Southwest Luzon Power Generation Corp. 2, Bakun 1 and 2, Ambuklao 1 and 2, Binga 1 and 4, Bineng, BT2020, the generating unit of the National Irrigation Administration, VS Gripal Power Corp., Maris 1 and 2, NMH, Irisan, and CBK Power's Kalayaan 1 and 2 Pumped storage plants were among the power plants on forced outage
  • Visayas grid
    • Yellow alert from 2-4 PM and 9-11 PM
    • Red alert from 5-9 PM
    • As of 6 PM yesterday, the grid held an available capacity of 2,253MW and a peak demand of 2,242MW, with 780MW unavailable to the grid as a result of 13 power plants on forced outage and five plants running on derated capacities
    • KEPCO SPC Power Corp's KSPC 1 is among the plants on forced outage with a capacity of 103MW
  • Yellow alerts are issued when the operating margin falls below a set threshold and red alerts are raised when the power supply is insufficient to meet consumer demand
  • NGCP responded with manual load dropping (MLD) with schedules distributed from 2-11 PM across different areas in Luzon and Visayas to maintain power system integrity
