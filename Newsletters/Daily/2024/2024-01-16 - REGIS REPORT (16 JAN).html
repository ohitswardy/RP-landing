REGIS REPORT

16 January 2024

Gaming agency PAGCOR said that it expects 2024 gaming revenues for the industry to reach P336bn in 2024, a growth of 17% from a record-setting 2023 performance. GGR jumped 33% in 2023 on the back of strong contributions from integrated resorts (P207bn) and, more notably, electronic games (P58bn). The regulator’s outlook for 2024 aligns with our revenue estimates of integrated resorts this year (+15-20%), and we expect Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) to extend its revenue streak on the back of increased play from existing players, the return of tourists and the new property.

Speaking of Bloomberry, we released a new report where we reiterate our BUY call on the stock with a 50% upside to our P15.0/share target. Although the opening of Solaire North in 2Q24 could weigh on near-term earnings due to the overhead, we expect its first full-year of operations to drive EBITDA/income 26%/23% higher than the 2023 peak. Our 3-year outlook is for an 18% GGR CAGR, a 21% EBITDA CAGR and a 36% profit CAGR.

At 7.5x EV/EBITDA for FY24E, BLOOM is trading below both historical levels and comps. Our target of P15.0/share would place it only at par with its mean, despite much improved operations and outlook.

GAMING
PAGCOR eyes P336.4bn GGR in 2024; readies privatization program
  • In a session with the media, PAGCOR Chairman Al Tengco said that the gaming agency projects P336.38bn (+17%) in gross gaming revenue in 2024, banking on the sustained growth in the e-gaming sector and new integrated casino resorts
  • This comes after GGR reached P285.27bn (+33%YoY), surpassing both the P272.74bn target and the previous record of P256.49bn in 2019
    • The integrated resorts contributed P207.48bn while electronic games generated P58.16bn
    • PAGCOR-operated casinos chipped in P19.62bn
  • For 2025, it will ramp up its drive to privatize assets
  • In its bid to privatize all of its 41 casinos, Tengco said they would sell all licenses through a bidding process

 

PROPERTY
Ayala Land’s Park Villas reportedly sells well
  • According to the Philippine Daily Inquirer’s “Biz Buzz” section, Ayala Land’s Park Villas has received several unit reservations (reservation sales)
    • Buyers are reportedly empty-nesters from exclusive gated communities in Metro Manila, such as Forbes Park and Dasmarinas
  • Park Villas offers 600sqm units at P500mn each
    • There are 45 units in total (implying total sales value of P22.5bn)
    • This is the first project under the ALP Signature brand
  • We are forecasting 2024E reservation sales to grow 20% to P144.7bn
  • ALI is trading at 34% discount to NAV, 1.7X FY24E P/B, and 18.5X FY24E P/E

 

MACRO
Remittances rise by 2.8% YoY in November
  • Remittances in November grew by 2.8% YoY to US$2.72bn
    • Remittances were down 9.3% MoM due to seasonality
    • In PHP terms, remittances dipped by 0.3% YoY
    • Land-based remittances rose 2.9%, lifted by higher remittances from Saudi Arabia (5.6%) and UAE (9.3%); Sluggish growth from the USA (+1%) persisted
    • Sea-based remittances continued to gain pace, rising by 2.6% YoY in Nov, fastest growth rate since April 2023’s +2.7%
  • Brings YT-November remittances are up by 2.8% YoY to US$30.2bn
    • The BSP is forecasting remittances to rise by 3% in 2023/2024
