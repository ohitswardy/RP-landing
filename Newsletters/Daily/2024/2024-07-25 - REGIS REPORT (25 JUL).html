REGIS REPORT

25 July 2024

Heavy monsoon rains intensified by Typhoon Gaemi (local name Carina) inundated Metro Manila and surrounding areas yesterday.  Flooding in many areas led to the suspension of work for government offices and many private organizations. Trading in the domestic financial markets, including the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE), was also suspended.

+++

The government’s budget deficit for June was P209bn, down 7%.  Revenues were 11% higher, driven by non-tax sources (+74%).  Tax revenues were up only 3.5%.  Primary spending for the month grew 2%.

For 1H, the budget deficit stands at P614bn, up 11%. The FY24 target is P1.48trn.

+++

Twenty-seven airports are being evaluated for potential privatization via the PPP route. Under the Marcos administration so far, the operation of the country’s biggest airport (NAIA) has been privatized.  Two others are undergoing Swiss challenges.

+++

We refresh our near-term estimates for Semirara (SCC PM), raising our near-term profit forecasts by 15-19% to incorporate higher coal price assumptions. However, given the uncertainties surrounding the renewal of its mining contract, which is due to expire in 2027, we adjusted our TP to P22/sh from P28/sh after a DCF roll-over, reflecting the remaining three-year life of the existing mining contract and the potential increase in fuel costs for the power unit after 2027.  The prospects for a contract renewal are uncertain and, frankly, the outcome will be binary.

Having said the above, we reckon SCC’s shares reflect much optimism on a contract renewal, as we estimate the company could be worth P37/sh should it successfully renew its contract. This is only ~10% above the current price (P33.2/sh).  In the meantime, ahead of the Department of Energy’s final decision, we estimate SCC could pay dividends of P6.50/sh in the next sixteen months, equivalent to a yield of 18%.

MACRO
Typhoon Carina damages schools, agriculture, and infrastructure: latest tally at almost P500mn
  • The latest data from various government agencies showed that at least P500mn worth of agriculture and infrastructure were damaged from typhoon Carina
    • Over 880k people affected
  • The Department of Education (DepEd) reported an estimated damage of P308.5mn in school infrastructure
    • This amount is equivalent to the funds needed for the reconstruction and rehabilitation of 45 damaged schools
  • Meanwhile, the Department of Agriculture estimates the agri sector has incurred damage and losses of more than P156mn
  • Infrastructure damage was reported at P700k, mostly in Luzon and Visayas regions
MACRO
Budget deficit narrows in June; 1H24 deficit widens by 11% YoY to P614bn
  • The national government recorded a lower budget deficit of P209bn in June, down 7% YoY
    • Revenues grew 11% YoY due to 74% YoY increase in non-tax revenues while expenditures rose by 3% YoY
    • Tax revenues grew 3.5% YoY, with BIR collections up 4.7% YoY and Customs collections up 0.7% YoY
    • The surge in non-tax revenues were due to undisclosed items unrelated to GOCCs dividends (-31% YoY) and privatization proceeds
    • Primary spending (excluding interest payments) grew 2.3% YoY
  • In 2Q24 alone, the budget deficit grew 22% YoY due to 18% YoY jump in spending (primary spending up 16% YoY)
    • Prelude to 2Q24 GDP; favorable base effects have likely boosted GDP for the quarter
  • Brings 1H24 budget deficit up 11% YoY to P614bn; Primary deficit in 1H down 12% YoY

 

INFRASTRUCTURE
27 airports being considered for PPP
  • The Department of Transportation (DoTr) said 27 other airports are undergoing public-private partnerships (PPP) screening by the Asian Development Bank (ADB) and International Finance Corp
  • It is looking to revive previous plans to privatize the Davao airport (4th largest airport in the country)
  • It is also seeking PPP-led upgrades for Iloilo, Bohol, Puerto Princesa, and Siargao
  • The government has so far bid out the country’s largest gateway, NAIA
    • It is currently in the process of bidding out, under a Swiss challenge, the Laguindingan airport and Bohol-Panglao Airport
    • Aboitiz InfraCapital, Inc., a wholly-owned unit of Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM), is the original proponent for the two projects and has the right to match the best bids
    • Negotiations are also ongoing with Prime Asset Ventures, Inc. which secured OPS for the upgrade, operation and maintenance of Iloilo International Airport

GAMING
DigiPlus clarifies that it is not covered by POGO ban
  • DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) issued a statement that its operations are not covered by the recently announced POGO ban
  • Philippine Offshore Gaming Operators (POGO) can only service a foreign internet protocol
  • PLUS’ gaming license requires it to have physical sites and its online platform can only cater to players within the Philippines
  • PLUS is trading at 8.6X FY24E P/E (based on consensus forecast)
