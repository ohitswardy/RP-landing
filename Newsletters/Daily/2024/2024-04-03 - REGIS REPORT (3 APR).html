REGIS REPORT

03 April 2024

The latest approval ratings poll by Pulse Asia poll revealed a sharp drop for President Marcos.  Marcos’ approval fell to 55% for the March polling period, down 13ppt from Dec.

A look at the income class as well as the geographical splits shows an emerging divergence in opinions across the population.

Marcos’ approval with respondents in the ABC income classes was virtually unchanged but dropped sharply for those in the poorer D and E income classes. This divergence is likely reflective of what’s been often described as the K-shaped trajectory of economic expansion. The upper income classes are doing fine but the lower income classes are much more badly affected by inflation. Operating results of the listed companies conform with this thesis.

Geographically, the drop in Marcos’ approval rating was most pronounced in Mindanao.  While this is partly a function of economic disparity (Mindanao is less affluent), the apparent rupture between Marcos and the camp of ex-President Duterte (Mindanao-based) likely had some impact as well.

Meanwhile, Vice President Sara Duterte’s own approval ratings also slid, albeit not as sharply, to 67% (vs. 74%).

Following Cebu Air’s (CEB PM) 4Q23 results briefing a few days ago, we discuss management’s latest 2024 outlook given improvements on its fleet situation. Management raised its 2024 seat growth guidance to +11-15% from +5-8% previously and reiterated confidence in it grabbing more market share from competitors, who are still struggling to return to their pre-pandemic capacities. In addition, the completion of the quasi-reorganization of capital position (within the year) is a looming positive catalyst, as this will finally allow the company to pay dividends. The listed preferred shares of the firm (CEBCP PM), which are convertible into common shares, have cumulative unpaid dividends of P6/share.

POLITICS
Marcos, Sara Durterte ratings fall in latest Pulse Asia survey
  • The approval and trust ratings of President Marcos fell by 13ppts to 55% in the March 6-10 survey conducted by Pulse Asia
    • The decline was evident across areas, with the lowest in Mindanao at 40% (from 62%), followed by those in Metro Manila at 47% (from 64%), Visayas at 54% (from 66%) and the rest of Luzon at 66% (from 74%)
    • President Marcos’ rating also fell the most in class E at 48% (from 68%), followed by D at 56% (from 69%) and class ABC at 62% (from 63%)
    • President Marcos’ trust ratings also dropped from 73% in Dec to 57% in March
  • Meanwhile, Vice President Sara Duterte’s approval ratings also dropped by 7ppts to 67%

 

INFRASTRUCTURE
Panel to address right-of-way issues hounding railway projects
  • President Marcos Jr. created the Inter-Agency Committee for Right-of-Way (RoW) Activities for National Railway Projects to streamline land acquisitions for railway projects
    • Panel will ensure “effective completion” of programs related to land acquisition and other right-of-way activities such as livelihood programs, income restoration, and resettlement
  • The panel will be chaired by the secretary of the Department of Transportation (DoTr) and co-chaired by the secretary of the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development
  • The government’s infrastructure projects have been hampered by RoW issues that have delayed their completion
    • 45% of the P488bn Metro Manila Subway Phase 1 project’s RoW has not yet been secured, which threatens the project’s 2029 completion
