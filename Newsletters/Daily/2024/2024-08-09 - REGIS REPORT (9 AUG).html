Here’s today’s round-up of results released yesterday:

  • ACEN (ACEN PM) (-) – 1H headline profit was P6.3bn, which is +49% YoY.  Stripping out divestment gains, management said income was P4.9bn (+21%) which would be below expectations.  The implied 2Q core income appears to be P2.2bn (from P2.7bn in 1Q24) but we are waiting to confirm this with ACEN management. It appears that seasonally weaker wind output exacerbated by El Nino led to the QoQ drop.  We await further clarification from management.  We have a short comment below pending the receipt of the clarification.

 

  • Megaworld (MEG PM) (-) – Profits for 1H came in at P8.55bn, up 9%. 2Q profit was P4.15bn, also +9% even though it was weighed down by FX losses.  We note that 2Q reservation sales amounted to P32bn, which is 12% lower YoY and QoQ.

Meanwhile, its non-East zone Philippine assets are continuing to benefit from tariff hikes secured early this year.

MACRO
2Q24 GDP accelerates to 6.3%
  • GDP expanded by 6.3% in 2Q24, accelerating from the 5.8% (revised from 5.7%) pace in 1Q24 and 4.3% in 2Q23
    • Bang in line with the consensus forecast of 6.3%
    • Brings 1H24 GDP to 6.0% (vs. Regis FY24E: 6% and government’s 6-7%)
  • On the demand side, domestic demand grew 7.9%, the fastest pace since 1Q23 following a strong rebound in government spending (+10.7%) and capital formation (+11.5%)
    • Private consumption grew at a steady pace of 4.6%, but remained below pre-pandemic trends (5.4%)
    • Government spending rebounded by 10.7% in 2Q24, after declining by 7.1% in 2Q23
    • Capital formation went up by 11.5%, after construction activity rebounded (+16% YoY in 2Q24 vs 6.9% in 1Q24 and 2.5% in 2Q23)
      • Public construction grew 22% in 2Q24 from 12% in 2Q23; Private construction rose 10% from 5%; Commercial construction grew 14% 
      • Strong fixed capital formation was also aided by slower growth in inventory withdrawals
    • Net imports turned positive, reversing last year’s net exports and weighing on overall GDP, albeit mildly (-0.9ppts)
  • On the supply side, the industry sector grew at a faster rate of 7.7%, boosted by strong construction growth while service sector grew a solid 6.8%
    • Agriculture sector declined by 2.3%, primarily driven by the decline of Palay by 9.5%
  • National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) Secretary Arsenio M. Balisacan described household consumption growth of 4.6% as “anemic”
  • Monetary Board meeting on Aug 15

    • To reach Regis’ target of 206k units (+3%), monthly sales should average 16.6k units for the rest of the year
  • TMP is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for 33% of NAV (25% of gross asset value) and 26% of gross profit (before parent expenses)
  • GTCAP trades at a 50% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,186 (vs. historical discount of 14%) and 5.4x FY24E PER

MACRO
BSP to lift ban on new digital banks in 2025
  • BSP will lift the moratorium imposed on the granting of digital banking licenses starting Jan. 1, 2025, but will only permit a maximum of 10 digital banks to operate in the country
    • The issuance of new digital bank licenses includes converting an existing bank’s license to a digital bank license
  • Six digital banks are currently operating in the country
    • Lifting the moratorium will only accommodate four additional licenses, coming from either new or converting banks
    • The BSP previously granted digital banking licenses to Maya Bank, Overseas Filipino Bank Inc., Tonik Digital Bank Inc., UNObank Inc., UnionDigital Bank and GOtyme Bank Corp
POWER
ACEN 1H24 recurring profit rises 21% YoY to P4.9bn – Below expectations
  • Core profit below expectations. ACEN reported headline income of P6.3bn for 1H, +49% YoY.  Excluding ‘value realization’ gains, net income was P4.9bn (+21%) in 1H, with ‘core attributable EBITDA’ of P10.6bn. In 2Q, ACEN recorded P1.4bn of net gain from the sale of its stake in the Sidrap wind farm in Indonesia as well as a gain from the receipt of a loan repayment.

 

We view the results as being below expectations. Implied 2Q core income of P2.2bn was lower sequentially compared to 1Q24 (P2.7bn) due to lower electricity output.

 

  • Renewable output down QoQ.  Total attributable renewable output in 2Q was 1,308GWH, down 16% QoQ.  Output from the wind farms was already seasonally down and this was aggravated further by exceptionally low wind regimes likely due to El Nino.  Electricity sales at its Australian business was also affected by grid issues in 2Q. The sale of Sidrap (April) also affected “core”results. Higher spot prices in the Philippine spot market could not offset the decline.

 

  • Equity raise next year.  The company says it is unlikely to raise equity in the next six months but indicated capital raising is coming soon (ideally middle of 2025). This could be done at various levels. Not necessarily at the ACEN holding company level.,

 

  • Guiding for seasonally weak 3Q. Management is also guiding for a seasonally weak 3Q due to lower wind and solar output.  We will need to review our FY24 profit forecast of P10.9bn.
