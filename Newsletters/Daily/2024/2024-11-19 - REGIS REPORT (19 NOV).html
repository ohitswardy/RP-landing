REGIS REPORT

19 November 2024

A bill to extend Meralco’s (MER PM) franchise (currently expiring in 2028) has been filed in the Senate, after a similar bill was approved by the House of Representatives. We think it likely that this bill will be enacted into law in a couple of months.

Meanwhile, the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) kicked the can down the road on Meralco’s Fifth Regulatory Period (5RP) rate review. Meralco was seeking to have 5RP treated as a lapsed period, where tariffs will be fixed according to what they were at the end of 4RP (= P1.35/kwh). The ERC is instead ordering the deferment of 5RP and deliberations before a definitive, final decision.

+++

Volume growth at ICTSI’s (ICT PM) Mexico port jumped to 25% in Oct after the completion of its capacity expansion.  Growth at Manzanillo (CMSA) was +2% in 3Q due to capacity constraints.

+++

ACEN (ACEN PM) is spending P26bn (<US$440m) for the expanded capacity of its Palauig, Zambales solar facility.  The facility will now be 420MW and will include 1,200MWh of battery storage.

+++

Demand for air travel grew by 21% YoY to 44mn in 9M24, just 2% shy of pre-Covid levels.  Cebu Air (CEB PM) is the only Philippine-based airline that's gaining share, with its own pax volumes already 6% above pre-Covid levels.  CEB is poised to sustain its market share gains and take advantage of the country’s underpenetrated air travel demand, as its competitors continue to struggle to return to pre-Covid capacity. 

+++

In the attached published note, we offer some more color on the 3Q results of convenience store operator Philippine Seven (SEVN PM), which were released late last week. Among the notable items was SSSG declining to +2.7% in 3Q, which management blamed on weather reasons.

A media website claims DigiPlus (PLUS PM) is considering the acquisition of another e-gaming operator, CasinoPlus. The acquisition could increase PLUS’ revenue by half. DigiPlus says “there are no definitive plans” at this time for such an acquisition.

TRANSPORT
ICTSI Mexico port volumes rebound in October on new capacity
  • Volumes at ICTSI’s port in Mexico (CMSA) rose by 25% YoY and 16% MoM to 139k TEUs in October
    • This follows the completion of Phase 3A expansion, which added 17% to the port’s capacity to ~1.7mn TEUs
    • ICT took market share, as industry volumes grew by 6% YoY and 10% MoM to 340k TEUs
  • Brings YT-October volumes up by 15% YoY to 1.2mn TEUs
    • This is on track to exceed our FY24E forecast of 1.43mn TEUs (+12%), given that volumes only need to average 112k TEUs per month for Nov/Dec
  • We estimate CMSA accounts for 11% of ICTSI’s volumes, 20% of revenues, 25% of EBITDA
  • ICT trades at 7.8x FY25E EV/EBITDA, below peer average (10.6x) and historical average (11x)

Meralco franchise bill filed in the Senate; non-ruling issued for its lapsed period
  • The bill extending Meralco’s franchise has been filed in the Senate
    • The bill would extend the franchise by another 25 years (from 2028)
  • A similar bill had already been approved on 3rd reading in the lower House
  • Meanwhile, the regulator (ERC) issued a ruling that effectively deferred a final decision on its 5RP (5th Regulatory Period)
    • Meralco sought to treat the 5RP as a lapsed period, which would mean fixing the tariff at P1.3522/kwh
    • The ERC instead will hold hearings / deliberations before such a decision on 5RP and push back its implementation to 2025
POWER
ACEN spending P26bn for integrated solar project
  • ACEN is spending P26bn to build a 420 MWp solar plant and 1,238.5MWh battery energy storage system (BESS) in Palauig, Zambales
    • Project is under its wholly-owned
    • subsidiary, Giga Ace 8
  • It has seeking clearance from the Department of Environment and Natural Resources to increase the capacity and land area of its solar project to 370 has from 275 has
    • Project originally had a target capacity of 346-megawatt peak (MWp)
  • ACEN will start the project in early 2026; Completion set in 2030
  • ACEN has 6,754MW of attributable RE capacity, of which 3,051MW is operational
    • In the Philippines alone, ACEN has a total of 2,397MW of capacity, of which 1,330 MW are under construction
    • Its international att. RE capacity totals 4,357 MW, of which c. 2,400 MW are under construction

  • The combined entity will reportedly have quarterly revenue of P30bn

TRANSPORT
Air pax volumes rise 21% YoY in 9M24 but still a shade below pre-Covid levels
  • Data from the Civil Aeronautics Board (CAB) showed that systemwide passenger volumes grew by 21% YoY to 44.1mn in 9M24
    • This is still a tad below 9M19 levels of 44.9mn
  • This implies that in 3Q24 alone, systemwide pax volumes rose 13% YoY to 14.6mn pax
    • Cebu Air (CEB PM) sustained market share gains, with pax volumes up 23% YoY to 5.6mn, exceeding 3Q19 levels by 12%
    • CEB trades at 3.5x FY25E EV/EBITDA, below peer average of 6x
