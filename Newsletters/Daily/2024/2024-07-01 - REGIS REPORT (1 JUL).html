REGIS REPORT

01 July 2024

Meralco (MER PM) has appointed Emmanuel “Manny” Rubio as CEO of MGEN, its power generation subsidiary.  Rubio just retired from his post as CEO of Aboitiz Power (AP PM), replaced by Danel Aboitiz just yesterday.

It is curious that Rubio is assuming his new position immediately, with no between-tenure vacations, no gardening leaves, and no non-compete restrictions.

Outside of its purchase of Pacific Light in Singapore, MGEN’s investment track record has been less than stellar. This is despite massive resources and the huge advantage of its captive market at its disposal. The appointment of Rubio could hopefully change that.

Meanwhile, in another change-of-guard announcement, Union Bank (UBP PM) disclosed that Ana Aboitiz-Delgado is taking over as CEO effective Jan 2025.  Like in the case of Aboitiz Power, the Aboitiz family seems to be taking management of their group companies directly (personally).

+++

A couple of newspapers reported the 2023 financial highlights of hard discount retailer DALI. DALI has made its presence felt with its rapid store growth and the threat it is posing to other retailers.

Among the most interesting highlights:

  • DALI reported revenue of P22.3bn (~US$400mn) for FY23, up 141% YoY.  Assuming its revenue is almost entirely from store-level merchandise sales, its sales level is already not far from Alfamart’s.  In addition, it also implies sales per store is 50% higher than Philippine Seven’s (SEVN PM) 7-11 stores.
  • The company posted a net loss of P1.9bn (US$32m) for last year.  Assuming no exceptional items, this implies pretax margin that’s worse than negative 10%. Operating margins will have to improve by over 10ppt for the company to break even, ceteris paribus.
  • The group’s losses and announced expansion plans (300 new stores this year) suggest it needs to raise more money. Recent reports already have DALI raising US$25m from private equity. We think it will need more than that.

+++

Local mayors in the province of Batangas are apparently supporting a “joint venture” between Meralco and the two electricity cooperatives there (BATELEC 1 and 2).  This could pave the way for Meralco to take over their operations, adding ~3% to Meralco’s electricity sales. A takeover (even one disguised as a “joint venture”), however, is bound to raise fears that Meralco will become even more dominant.

+++

Finally, our preliminary assessment suggests that there will no additions / deletions for the PSEi this coming August (which is based on data as of end-June). Bloomberry (BLOOM PM), however, could see a weight reduction arising from a smaller public float due to its share buyback.

POWER/UTILITIES
Meralco-Batelec JV gains support from local mayors
  • 18 mayors from the province of Batangas passed a resolution supporting the partnership between Meralco and Batangas Electric Cooperative Inc (Batelec) 1 and 2
    • The partnership aims to address the province’s prolonged power outrages and recurring brownouts
    • The joint venture is expected to improve consumer services and ensure reliable and affordable electricity
  • Batelec 1 covers one city and 11 municipalities in the western part of Batangas
  • Batelec 2 provides service to 2 cities and 15 municipalities in the eastern part of Batangas
  • Batelec 1 and 2 have more than 500,000 consumer members and around 1,671 gigawatt-hours in energy sales

INFRASTRUCTURE
Bulacan special ecozone bill lapses into law
  • Republic Act 11999 (RA 11999) or the Bulacan Special Economic Zone and Freeport bill lapsed into law on June 13
    • Said bill creates the Bulacan EcoZone (BuZ) that covers the airport city project of San Miguel Aerocity (subsidiary of San Miguel Holdings) in Bulacan
    • The Bulacan Special Economic Zone and Freeport Authority (BEZA) will manage the BuZ
  • The newly-enacted law is seen to create US$200 billion in exports, according to Rep. Joey Salceda, chairman of the House ways and means committee
    • The project “will create massive revenue gains in income taxes and property taxes, so the forgone revenue from tax incentives will get offset”
    • The country’s domestic product could be boosted by no less than P130bn annually, land value gains will be over P226bn through the RA
  • President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. had vetoed the measure creating BuZ in July 2022
    • Cited defects such as the low tax base and lack of coherence with existing laws, etc.
    • Salceda filed a new version in the 19th Congress to correct the deficiencies
RETAIL
DALI Everyday Grocery operator widens net loss
  • Hard Discount Philippines, Inc. (HDPI), the corporate entity behind the DALI discount grocery chain, reported a net loss of P1.88bn in 2023, a wider loss from 2022’s net loss of P894.68mn
  • Revenues +141% YoY to P22.31bn while opex +255% YoY to P3.01bn
    • Opex increase was mainly attributed to increased expenditures on salaries, wages, and benefits
  • As of end-2023, HDPI accumulated losses totaling P3.26bn, resulting in a capital deficit of P1.29bn for the same period
  • HDPI also disclosed that P4.67bn worth of additional capital was infused by stockholders as of end-2023
  • The company forecasts that profit margins will improve over the next 5 years resulting from the implementation of cost efficiency measures
    • The planned increase in equity, commitment of and continue financial support from parent company, and projected improvement in net margin is seen to generate sufficient cash flows from operations to meet its obligations as and when they fall due
  • In March, Singapore-based growth equity firm Venturi Partners announced a USD25mn investment to fund the expansion of DALI
    • DALI seeks to have up to 950 stores across the Philippines by end-2024, a net addition of ~300 stores vs end-2023
OTHERS
PSE Index Aug 2024 rebalancing predictions – no additions/deletions
  • We expect no additions/deletions during the August 2024 PSE Index rebalancing
  • The PSE Index rebalancing in August 2024 will be based on the 12 months from July 2023 to June 2024
  • Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap
  • Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when
    • 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or
    • 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35 
  • Based on our estimates, neither of the two criteria has been met
