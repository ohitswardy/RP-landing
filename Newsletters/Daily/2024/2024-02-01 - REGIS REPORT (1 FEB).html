REGIS REPORT

01 February 2024

The government statistics office reported that the Philippine economy grew 5.6% in both 4Q23 and for full year 2023.  This was a slowdown from the 3Q23 growth (+6.0%, revised) but is better than Street expectations (5.2%).

Private Consumption grew a respectable 5.3% while Capital Formation rose more sharply (11.2%).  Unsurprisingly, Government Consumption contracted (-1.8%) in the last quarter due to the budget constraints.  With continued belt-tightening, sluggish government consumption growth in FY24 is likely to be drag even up to this year.  Meanwhile,  Net imports (exports) were a drag (+2.3%) as Exports contracted again.

 

+++

Based on preliminary data (and the announced GDP figures), total debt/GDP for the country was 60.2% as of end of last year.   This is a modest improvement from the 60.9% in 2022.  The government is aiming to trim this down further to 51% by 2028.  Going forward, the country’s economic managers will have to tread, carefully, the interplay between higher interest rates (and debt service), the public sector deficit and debt, and economic growth.

+++

PLDT (TEL PM) announced it was acquiring 35% of the broadband service provider Radius Telecom, a subsidiary of Meralco (MER PM).  We understand Radius has around 48,000 subscribers, but its fiber network and the potential for it to tap more enterprise customers is likely the key attraction.

The deal would strengthen PLDT’s position in the consumer broadband and enterprise businesses. PLDT is adding to its lead in broadband by acquisition, with the just-approved purchase of Sky Cable expected to add over 300k broadband subscribers.

+++

The Philippine Competition Commission approved the acquisition by Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV PM) and Coca-Cola Europacific Partners of 100% of the local Coca-Cola bottling operation.  The reported valuation for the transaction was US$1.8bn.

MACRO
4Q23 and FY23 GDP up 5.6%
  • 4Q23 GDP settled at +5.6% YoY, slowing down from +6.0% in 3Q23 and +7.1% in 4Q22
    • This was mildly better than consensus forecast of +5.2% for the quarter
  • Majority of the heavy lifting was done by private consumption and a strong rebound in capital formation
    • Private Consumption grew slightly to +5.3% in 4Q from +5.1% in 3Q
      • This was propelled by sustained strong growth in discretionary spending: restaurants/hotels (+16% in 4Q), transport (+12%), and recreation (+7%)
      • However, food spending remained soft for the fourth straight quarter, up by +0.5% YoY (below historical trend: +5.1%)
    • Capital formation rose sharply to +11.2%, after being lethargic for the past two quarters
      • Due to the (a) turnaround in durable equipment, which expanded 14.6% YoY in the quarter, as well as (b) inventory re-stocking
    • Government consumption, however, contracted -1.8% from +6.7% in 3Q
    • Domestic demand was strong, accelerating to 6.1% in 4Q after posting below trend growth rates in 2Q/3Q
    • Net imports dragged overall GDP growth by 1.2ppts, largely due to a 12% YoY drop in goods exports
  • On the supply side, growth accelerated for the services and agriculture sectors, which more than compensated for the slowdown in the industrial sector
    • Industrial sector growth moderated to +3.2% from +5.6% in 3Q, as the manufacturing sector barely grew during the quarter
  • FY23 GDP growth averaged +5.6% YoY, falling below the government-set target of 6-7% but was broadly in line with consensus estimates (5.5%)
    • For 2024, we expect GDP growth to accelerate to 6.0% (Street: 5.7%)

 

MACRO
Government debt reaches P14.62tn in end-2023, equivalent to 60.2% of GDP
  • The national government’s debt reached P14.62tn in end-Dec, up by P108bn (+0.74%) MoM and by P1.2tn (+8.9%) from end-2022 levels
    • Debt-to-GDP ratio mildly improved to 60.2% from 60.9% in end-2022
  • Domestic debt dropped by P6.5bn (-0.06%) MoM to P10.0tn
  • External debt rose by P114bn (+2.54%) MoM to P4.6tn, following issuances of US$1bn maiden Sukuk bonds and disbursement of US$300mn loan from ADB
  • By end-2024, the government is targeting debt levels to reach P15.84tn, equivalent to c. 60% of GDP
    • Under its medium-term fiscal framework, the government plans to bring down the debt-to-GDP ratio to less than 60% by 2025 and 51% by 2028

 

TELCOS
PLDT buys 35% of Radius Telecoms
  • PLDT disclosed it is subscribing to 34.9% of Radius Telecoms
  • Radius is a broadband service provider with a congressional franchise
  • Radius is presently a 100% subsidiary of Meralco
  • Purchase price for the 35% interest is P2.116bn
    • Implies post-money valuation of P6.06bn and pre-money valuation of P3.95bn
  • Radius has a subscriber base of ~48k
CONGLOS
Aboitiz group and partner receive approval for Coca-Cola PHL joint acquisition
  • Aboitiz Equity Ventures and Coca-Cola Europacific Partners (CCEP) received the go signal from the Philippine Competition Commission to acquire Coca-Cola Beverages Philippines, Inc. for US$1.8bn
    • Through CCEP Aboitiz Beverages Philippines, Inc., incorporated in December, Aboitiz will have a 40% beneficial ownership while CCEP will hold a 60% stake in Coca-Cola
  • AEV and CCEP will acquire 2.45mn shares of class A common stake with a par value of P2 and 1k shares of class B common stock with a par value of P1
  • All parties expect to close the transaction in late February 2024
