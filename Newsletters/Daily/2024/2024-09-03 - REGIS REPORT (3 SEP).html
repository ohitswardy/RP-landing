REGIS REPORT

03 September 2024

The country’s output of raw sugar this coming crop year (2024-25) is projected to fall below 1.8m MT, the lowest in 25 years.  This may lead to another surge in prices unless the government steps in quickly to allow more imports.  There are quantitative restrictions on the importation of sugar, similar to the case with many agricultural commodities. 

Unlike rice prices, the price of domestic sugar has been diverging from international prices due to (1) the shortfall in local production and (2) quantitative restrictions that allow only certain parties to import legally. The premium of domestic sugar prices over international prices has now widened to ~150%.

In contrast, the gap between international and domestic rice prices has narrowed considerably since the Rice Tariffication Law, even if the absolute price of rice is higher today.

In “news” that has yet to be officially disclosed, PLDT (TEL PM) is reported to be re-hiring one of its senior executives from over a decade ago.  Anastacio (“Boy”) Martirez is supposedly joining PLDT subsidiary Smart Communications as COO. Martirez was heading the consumer mobile business of Smart when its market share was at its peak (before the acquisition of Digitel/Sun) in the 2000s.

The mobile industry is in the midst of what’s euphemistically called “market repair” as both major players gradually tweak packages to lift revenue / price.  Big management changes create, in our view, the risk of re-igniting competitive conflagrations. There’s great temptation for new management to produce results (market share gains) and price is always the easiest lever to pull.

+++

The Bifrost cable system will be completed by early 2025, according to Converge (CNVRG PM) COO Jesus Romero.  Bifrost will provide Converge with connectivity to areas on the other side of the South China Sea as well as increased trans-Pacific capacity.  Bifrost was previously expected to be completed in 2024.
