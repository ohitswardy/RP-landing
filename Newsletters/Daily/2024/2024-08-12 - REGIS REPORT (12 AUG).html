REGIS REPORT

12 August 2024

We have a few more results today:

  • Puregold (PGOLD PM) (-) – Profit for 1H rose 12% to P4.9bn, implying 2Q profit of P2.47bn (+24%). The bottom line figures are trending below Regis estimates (P11.1bn for FY24) but likely within consensus expectations.  Nevertheless, PGOLD’s operations seem to be doing relatively well vis-à-vis some of the other retailers in 2Q.  Revenue growth in 2Q was a respectable +9%, compared to +3-6% for its two biggest competitors in grocery retail.

 

  • Robinsons Land (RLC PM) (=) – 1H24 recurring profit rose 11% to P6.5bn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. After plummeting in 1Q24 (due to an internal restructuring), reservation sales of RLC’s standalone projects jumped 8X QoQ to P5.45bn, equivalent to the FY23 average and c. 10% higher than 2019 levels. Meanwhile, 2Q24 same-mall revenue growth remained healthy at 9%, near the top of the historical range of 5%-8%.

Meanwhile, online gaming firm DigiPlus (PLUS PM) reported 1H profit of P5.2bn (up 5x) on revenue of P32bn (+269% YoY).  What’s interesting here is that PLUS’ gaming revenue in 2Q (=P18.9bn) is already higher than the peak quarterly gaming revenue of Bloomberry (BLOOM PM).

Banking sector NPLs (ex-interbank) improved in June, down 7bps MoM to 3.59% from 3.66% in May (though still up 12bps QoQ). More encouraging was that Past Due ratio came in at 4.29%, down 9bps MoM and 1bp QoQ. The improvement in the ratios was partly due to strong loan growth (ex-interbank) in June, up 12.5% YoY and 3.4% MoM. This was the fastest monthly YoY loan growth print since February 2019. The data were broadly consistent with results out of the Big Three banks, reporting loan growth of 11-16%, and broadly stable NPLs QoQ. We remain overweight the sector.

BANKS
Bank asset quality improves in June; loan growth accelerates to 12.5% YoY
  • Asset quality ratios for the Philippine banking system improved nearly across the board in June. NPL ratio (ex-interbank) dipped 7bps MoM to 3.59% from 3.66% in May. In PHP terms, NPLs were up 1.4% MoM to P502bn. Past Due (PD) ratio fell 9bps MoM to 4.29%, with the nominal amounts up 1.0% to P614bn. Restructured Loans (RL) improved to 2.05%, with absolute RLs down 0.8% MoM to P294bn. NPL cover was flat-ish MoM, with June coming in at 95.41%, 40bps lower than the 95.81% in May.

 

  • The better ratios were seen across all the different banking classifications. NPL ratios for universal/commercial banks (UKBs) were down 6bps MoM to 3.29%. For Thrift Banks (TBs), NPL ratio dropped 24bps MoM to 6.79%. Most notably, Digibank NPLs plummeted 245bps MoM, though this was still quite high at 18.19% (we also suspect there may have been large write-offs).

 

  • The ratios were partly helped by strong loan (ex-interbank) data, growing 3.4% MoM. On a YoY basis, loan growth accelerated sharply, up 12.5% YoY, the fastest pace since February 2019 (12.7%).

Puregold 1H24 net profit +12% YoY to P4.9bn

  • Net sales rose 8% YoY to P98.5bn (behind) while operating profit grew 11% YoY to P7.4bn

  • SSSG for 1H24 was 1.9% for the core Puregold brand and 2.4% for S&R
    • This suggests SSSG accelerated in 2Q24
    • 1Q24: +1.5% Puregold / -1.2% S&R
  • The company will be having its results briefing on 16 August
  • PGOLD currently trades at 7x 2024E PE
