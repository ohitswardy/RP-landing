REGIS REPORT

31 January 2024

The Philippine government signed an MOU for a 5-year rice supply agreement with Vietnam, part of the deals announced in conjunction with the visit of President Marcos to Vietnam.

Assuming the MOU becomes a more definitive deal, it would provide the Philippines with rice supply of 1.5m to 2.0m MT annually.   The Philippines consumes ~16m MT of rice annually, but production has only been around 12m or so.  Vietnam has been the biggest supplier of rice to the Philippines, accounting for over 80%.

Also among the other “fruits” of the Marcos visit was a supposed deal for Vietnamese firm Vinfast to establish dealership for EVs in the Philippines. The Philippines is a natural target market for Vinfast because of the proximity of the two countries and with both being part of ASEAN (AFTA).

+++

The Philippine Statistics Authority tweaked the 3Q23 GDP growth numbers upwards slightly to 6.0% (vs 5.9%), ahead of the release of FY23 and 4Q23 GDP data later this morning.

Consensus expects 5.2% growth in 4Q and 5.3% for the full year 2023.  This implies expectations of a slowdown in 4Q23. Our expectation for GDP growth for 2023 is 5.5%.

+++

Manila Water (MWC PM) announced it signed, or is seeking, new contracts to replace ones with the local governments of Pangasinan province and Cebu City that were recently terminated for various reasons. While each deal is likely to be small, acquiring such concessions are likely the only way to expand within the country.

MACRO
Government revises 3Q23 GDP to 6%
  • The Philippine Statistics Authority revised the real GDP growth rate for 3Q23 to 6.0% from 5.9%
    • Brings 9M23 GDP growth to 5.6%
  • Cited upward revision in private consumption (5.1% from 5%)
  • On supply side, upward revisions came from manufacturing (+5.1% from 4.5%), financial and insurance (9.6% from 9.5%), and accommodation and food service activities (21% from 20%)

 

MACRO
Philippines secures 5-year rice supply deal with Vietnam
  • During President Marcos' two-day visit to Vietnam, among the deals signed was a memorandum of understanding on Rice Trade Cooperation
    • The agreement entails a steady supply of 1.5 to 2mn metric tons of white rice at "a competitive and affordable price" from Vietnam's private sector to the Philippine private sector
  • The country sources 90% of its rice imports from Vietnam, importing 2.4mn tons of rice worth US$1.5bn in the nine months to September 2023
  • Recent monitoring by the Department of Agriculture shows the following retail prices per kilo
    • Local milled rice at P53
    • Local well-milled rice at P55
    • Local premium rice at P60
    • Local special rice at P68
    • Imported well-milled rice at P56
    • Imported premium rice at P62
    • Imported special rice at P65

 

UTILITIES
Manila Water to revive contract with Provincial Government of Pangasinan
  • In a press release, Manila Water said that it reached out to the Provincial Government of Pangasinan (PGP) to explore the revival of the botched concession agreement
    • Recall that MWC and PGP mutually terminated its 25-year contract for a bulk water project due to “non-fulfillment of conditions” last December
    • Under the canceled contract signed in Jan 2022, the project had an estimated capital expenditure program of P8bn to deliver 200MLD to 1.4mn Pangasinan residents by the 25th year

 

UTILITIES
Manila Water signs 10-year bulk water contract with Metropolitan Cebu Water District
  • Manila Water signed a 10-year contract with the Metropolitan Cebu Water District for the supply and delivery of potable surface water
    • No other details disclosed
  • Recall that last month, MWC terminated its 30-year bulk water supply contract with the Provincial Government of Cebu
    • Previous contract involved the construction of a 35MLD bulk water facility that will supply water to the Metropolitan Cebu Water District
    • The project only accounted for <1% of our SOTP NAV

 

POWER
ERC sets hearings for new rates in off-grid power areas
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission is set to conduct public hearings on the proposed new rates filed by the National Power Corp. for off-grid areas
    • Hearings will be conducted from March to October
  • ERC Chairperson Monalisa C. Dimalanta said that subsidized approved generation rates (SAGR) for the 14 first-wave areas was approved in 2005, while for the remaining small areas, it was approved in 2011
  • DMCI (DMC PM) has c. 160 MW of off-grid plants in Masbate, Palawan, and Oriental Mindoro
    • DMCI’s off-grid power unit accounts for 5% of NAV and 3% of profit
    • DMCI trades at a 19% discount to its market-based NAV of P12.9/sh (vs. historical average discount of 15%)

AUTOS
Vietnam’s Vingroup to invest in EV dealerships, battery production
  • Vietnam’s EV maker Vinfast said it will start to establish a network of electric car and motorcycle dealerships in the Philippines by April
    • Scope of investment may span across potential cooperation in developing public services such as buses and taxis
    • The company also mentioned investments to train students in artificial intelligence, as well as investments in medicine
    • Vinfast is a unit of real estate firm Vingroup
  • Vingroup also expressed interest in investing in the production of electric vehicle (EV) batteries in the Philippines
    • According to Trade Secretary Alfredo Pascual
  • President Marcos, Jr. had a two-day state visit in Vietnam recently

 

PROPERTY
AREIT receives EDGE Zero Carbon Certification for eight office buildings
  • Ayala Land’s REIT subsidiary, AREIT (AREIT PM), received EDGE Zero Carbon Certification for eight office buildings with a combined leasable area of 354,000sqm
    • This represents 60% of AREIT's office space
  • Excellence in Design for Greater Efficiencies (EDGE) is an international green building certification from the International Finance Corp (IFC)
  • EDGE Zero Carbon is the highest EDGE certification
    • It requires a minimum of 40% energy savings as well as 20% savings in water and embodied carbon in materials compared to the base case
  • The eight AREIT buildings showed a 45% improvement in energy efficiency, 49% in water, and 62% in embodied carbon material reduction
  • AREIT currently offers 2024E dividend yield of 7.3% (based on consensus forecast)
  • ALI is trading at 36% discount to NAV, 1.7X FY24E P/B, and 18.0X FY24E P/E
