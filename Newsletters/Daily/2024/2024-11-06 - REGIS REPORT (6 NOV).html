REGIS REPORT

06 November 2024

ICTSI’s (ICT PM) released 3Q24 results yesterday and recurring profit for the quarter rose 28% YoY to US$208mn, which fell short of our expectations but was in line with consensus estimates.

Operations were a tad softer than we anticipated during the quarter as bad weather in Rio, Brazil and the port congestion in Mexico resulted in slower organic volume growth and lower storage revenues. Despite the earnings shortfall, we remain upbeat on ICT’s near-to medium-term prospects and are maintaining our BUY call.  On consensus forecasts, ICT trades at 9x FY25E EV/EBITDA, lower than peer average of 11x and historical average of 11x.

The 9M24 net profit of e-casino operator DigiPlus Interactive (PLUS PM) grew 314% to P8.75bn, ahead of consensus forecast. While 3Q24 GGR growth slowed to 2% QoQ, this could pick up meaningfully in 1Q24, as PAGCOR will lower the fees it charges to e-casino operators beginning January 2025. This was the experience in 2Q24, when PLUS’ GGR grew 41% QoQ, coinciding with the drop in PAGCOR fees in April 2024.

Jollibee (JFC PM) is taking full control of the Tim Ho Wan (THW) business, bringing it from under the fold of Titan Dining (a PE fund). THW is a dim sum restaurant chain founded in HK.   Since JFC holds 92% of the Titan fund that owns the THW operation anyway, the true cash out for JFC will only be SGD20.2bn for 8%.  This transaction will allow JFC to now manage the business and consolidate its accounts.  We understand THW has over 60 stores and is profitable.

MACRO
Inflation picks up to 2.3% in October
  • Headline inflation rose to 2.3% in October from 1.9% in September
    • This is in line with consensus forecast of 2.3% and came in at the lower end of the BSP’s forecast range of 2.0-2.8%
  • On a MoM basis, CPI rose by 0.2%, largely due to higher food prices, which rose by 0.5% MoM
    • This increase was largely driven by upticks in the prices of vegetables, corn, fruits/nuts, and meat, which were partly attributed to weather disturbances
    • Rice CPI sequentially dipped for the fourth consecutive month by 0.5%, bringing the cumulative decline to 1.4%
    • Low base effects from the implementation of price ceilings on rice in Oct 2023 pushed up the YoY growth rate to 9.6% in October from 5.7% in September
    • Outside of food, inflation was tempered by lower electricity prices (-0.7% MoM) and petrol prices (-0.2%); Admittedly, these two items could pick up again in succeeding periods
  • Core inflation remained stable at 2.4%, the same as in September, with core non-food inflation easing to 2.6% from 2.8%
  • Brings average YT-October inflation rate to 3.3%, on track to meet the BSP’s risk-adjusted inflation forecast of 3.1% – in line with Regis’ FY24E of 3.1%
  • We don’t expect this to derail our expectations of a policy rate reduction (25bps) by the Bangko Sentral (BSP) in Dec

MACRO
Rice - regional prices continue to fall, but...

Rice prices in the region continue to fall, with export prices out of Vietnam at $511/MT as of yesterday, down from $516/MT at the end of last week, and down 17.5% year-to-date (peak was end-2023 at $620/MT. The collapse is even more pronounced for rice coming out of Thailand, with prices down 25% ytd. On the one hand, the Philippines sources far more out of Vietnam than we do from Thailand. On the other hand, it’s a global market…

The bad news is that, because of the strong USD, the movement in the PHP equivalent price of VN and TH exported rice has been much milder over the last 6 weeks or so. In fact, the PHP equivalent for VN rice is up slightly from where it was in August/September.

Domestically, rice prices have stabilized over the last 3 weeks. According to the DA website, the price of well-milled rice is currently at P43-54/kg for local rice and P45-55/kg for imported rice (the chart below uses the low end of the ranges).

With the wild gyrations in rice prices last year, we could see rice inflation (YoY) show odd prints (as we did this month). However, once we get past December, rice inflation will be consistently in negative territory for at least 9 months in 2025 (barring any weather- or tariff-related issues).

From a consumer standpoint, the sequential drop in rice prices is more important. More so given the recently granted minimum wage increases.

This news (link) included estimates of palay production falling 3.2% YoY this year due to El Niňo (late 2023) and due to the recent storms and floods (Kristine). Assuming milled rice production falls at the same rate, and assuming that consumption is flat vs 2023, then we could see a record rice deficit this year of 4.3mn MT. That would mean record-level imports to fill the gap. Thankfully, regional prices are down, and import tariffs were lowered.

House Committee approves bill seeking to extend terms of land leases to foreigners
  • The House Committee on Trade and Industry approved a bill extending land leases to foreigners to (up to) 99 years
  • The bill would amend Republic Act 7652, The Foreign Investors’ Long Term Lease Act, which currently allows foreigners to lease land for up to 50 years, renewable for another 25 years (total of 75 years)   
  • The Senate has also filed a counterpart bill
CONSUMER
Jollibee takes full ownership of Tim Ho Wan business
  • Jollibee will buy out the other investors in Titan Dining, which owns the Tim Ho Wan (THW)
    • JFC already holds 92% of Titan Fund and will buy the remaining 8% for SGD20m
  • The transaction will allow JFC to take full control of the business
    • Prior to this deal, it was only an investor (albeit 92% ownership) in the fund that owned the business so did not manage it
  • JFC first acquired an indirect interest in Tim Ho Wan (through Titan Dining) in 2018, buying 45% for SGD45m
  • Tim Ho Wan is a Chinese-style dimsum restaurant chain with over 60 branches globally
  • We understand the THW business was profitable last year
GAMING
DigiPlus 9M24 net profit rises 314%; ahead of consensus
  • DigiPlus 9M24 net profit surged 314% to P8.75bn, ahead of consensus FY24E forecast
    • Although 9M24 profit only represents 76% of the consensus FY forecast, note that the 4Q is seasonally the strongest quarter 
  • After growing 41% QoQ in 2Q24, 3Q24 GGR growth slowed to 2%
    • PAGCOR lowered the fees on e-casino by 6ppt in April (to 35%)
    • It will lower fees by another 5ppt (to 30%) in January 2025  
  • 3Q24 EBITDA margin expanded 190 basis points QoQ while net margin increased 120 basis points
    • As a result, 3Q24 EBITDA grew 12% (to P3.83bn), while 3Q24 net profit rose 9% (to P3.52bn)
  • PLUS is trading at 8.0X consensus FY24E P/E and 6.2X consensus FY25E P/E
