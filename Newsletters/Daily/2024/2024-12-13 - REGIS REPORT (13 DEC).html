REGIS REPORT

13 December 2024

As we had predicted, the banking industry asset quality data was distorted by the wild fluctuations in a) interbank loans, and b) reverse repos (RRPs), which the BSP includes in the “loans” denominator. Headline data shows October NPL and Past Due (PD) ratio deteriorating sharply – reported NPL ratio jumped 13bps MoM from 3.47% to 3.60%, while PD ratio increased 15bps MoM from 4.25% to 4.40%. However, this was only because the RRPs collapsed from P994.7bn in September to P503.5bn in October. That P491bn was equivalent to 3.5% of total loans (ex-interbank and RR). Note that the October RRP was much more similar to the P528.4bn in August.

 

Using the “clean” denominator of loans, ex-interbank and RRPs, all the asset quality data was much, much more stable.

 

NPL ratio inched up 1bp MoM to 3.81%, as gross NPLs were up 1.3% MoM vs loans up 0.9%  MoM. Similarly, PD ratio was up 1bp MoM to 4.66%, as PDs were also up 1.3% MoM. Restructured Loans (RL) ratio actually improved, down 3bps MoM to 2.13%, with nominal RLs down 0.6% MoM. NPL cover fell 33bps MoM to 92.98%

 

Meanwhile, loan growth slowed a touch to 10.9% YoY, from 11.0% YoY in September (but still +0.9% MoM, as above).

 

We are encouraged by the relative stability in asset quality ratios, despite the increasing share of consumer loans.

CONSUMER
Jollibee ramping up expansion in Vietnam and eyes entry into Australia
  • JFC is accelerating the expansion of its flagship brand internationally as it continues to open up new stores in Vietnam and aims to make its entry into the Australian market
  • The 200th Jollibee store in Vietnam was opened on Dec. 12, solidifying their number two spot in terms number of fast-food restaurants just behind Lotteria which has 210 restaurants.
    • Jollibee Group CEO Ernesto Tanmantiong stated that Jollibee Vietnam is committed to reaching the 300-store mark as soon as possible as they eye to be the number one quick service restaurant in the country
    • He also remains very bullish with the Vietnam market adding that "Vietnam can even be similar to Jollibee in the Philippines in the near future as we continue to grow aggressively"
  • For Australia, the company now eyes New South Wales as the primary market to be the location for its first potential Jollibee store in Australia
    • Jollibee Group regional business head for Europe, Middle East, Asia and Australia Dennis Flores states that it is highly possible that the company will be able to open the first store within the next two years
  • As of end-September 2024, JFC's global store network is currently at a total of 9,568
    • 3,340 stores in the Philippines, 6,258 worldwide
    • For the Jollibee brand specifically, it totals at 1,712 with 1,259 in the Philippines, then 453 spread across other parts of Asia, North America, Europe, and the Middle East
UTILITIES
Metro Manila water utilities to implement third tranche of tariff hike in January
  • The water regulator, MWSS, approved the increases of P5.96 per cubic meter (+11%) for Manila Water (MWC PM) and P7.32 per cubic meter (+13%) for Maynilad beginning January 1, 2025 (see here)
    • These adjustments cover the (a) third tranche of the approved “R” tariff hike for RY2023-2027, (b) three fourths of CPI, and (c) foreign currency differential adjustment (FCDA)
    • Recall that in the 2022 rate rebasing, MWC and Maynilad were granted “R” tariff adjustments of +73% and +38%, respectively, which will be spread out over five years
    • Our valuation/profit estimates assume these tariff hikes
  • MWC is owned by Razon-led Prime Infrastructure (62% economic)
  • Maynilad is a 25%-owned affiliate of DMCI (DMC PM) and accounts for 25% of its NAV; DMC trades at a 15% discount to its market-based NAV of P13.20/sh
POWER
ACEN unit acquires 49% stake in Bangladesh solar project
  • IBV ACEN Renewables Asia Pte. Ltd. (IBV ACEN) finalized the acquisition of a 49% stake in a 70MW greenfield solar project in Bangladesh from Sonagazi Sun Hldgs. Pte. Ltd.
    • The company said that the acquisition price is “less than 10% of the book value of ACEN as of 30 September 2024”
  • IBV ACEN is a joint venture between ACEN’s international unit, ACEN Renewables International and ib vogt (Singapore) Pte. Ltd, an affiliate of ib vogt GmbH
    • Recall that last October, ACEN said that it plans to invest as much as US$18mn for the construction of a 70MW solar project in Bangladesh (see disclosure)
    • Investment is part of ACEN’s expected contribution of up to US$200mn equity investment as IBV ACEN targets a minimum operational capacity of 1,000MW across Asia pacific
  • ACEN has 6,754MW of attributable RE capacity, of which 3,051MW is operational
    • In the Philippines alone, ACEN has a total of 2,397MW of capacity, of which 1,330 MW are under construction
    • Its international att. RE capacity totals 4,357 MW, of which c. 2,400 MW are under construction
