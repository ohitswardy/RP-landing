REGIS REPORT

18 July 2024

Our property analyst Carl (carl.sy@regis.ph) has a new report on the sector out.

 

As the title “Residential challenges in Metro Manila” suggests, he’s turned incrementally more cautious on the Metro Manila residential segment, specifically the primary market for middle-income condominiums.

 

There are two key reasons for this:

 

  • Unsold condominium inventory is high at about three years of presales, potentially leading to balance sheet issues (if the units remain unsold)
  • Prices of preselling condominiums are up to 80% more expensive than units in the secondary market, which may dissuade buyers from purchasing in the primary market (crimping presales)

 

Because of these concerns, we are widening our target discounts to NAV by 10ppt across the board and lower our target prices by 7%-25%.

 

However, we caution against being overly bearish on the sector as there mitigating developments.  Cancellation rates have been moderating, high-end and provincial sales have generally been robust, and valuations of most developers are compelling (see table below).

 

Robinsons Land (RLC PM) is, in our view, the most attractive stock on the basis of fundamental value. We forecast the company to show the fastest earnings growth in the sector (relative to 2019) and we expect future asset injections into RL Commercial REIT (RCR PM) to further highlight how undervalued RLC is.  

 

Between the two property big caps, SMPH appears to offer better value at this point. It’s poised to continue hitting record level profits. In addition, a mall REIT IPO could raise NAV by 5%-20% and the sale of lots in the Pasay Coastal Development could enhance NAV by another 10%-20%. Based on our estimates, the current SMPH share price does not place any value of the company’s coastal development project, which represents 27% of NAV.

President Marcos’ approval rating dipped 2ppt to 53% in the latest Pulse Asia poll.  Geographically, the decline came from Luzon outside of Metro Manila. His approval ratings are lower, by far, in Mindanao (38%). Meanwhile, VP Sara Duterte-Carpio’s approval inched up 2ppt to 69%.  The biggest disparity between the rating of two arises from the Mindanao region (Duterte-Carpio’s home area) where she has an exceptionally high approval rating of 92%.

Gaming (gambling) has become the hot new thing again.  Belle Corp (BEL PM), subsidiary of SM Investments (SM PM), is applying for a license to operate a casino in Clark. Media says Belle could invest US$300m in the venture.  Belle is the partner of Melco in the City of Dreams Manila (CODM) casino in Entertainment City.

 

+++

 

Airline Cebu Air (CEB PM) approved a restructuring plan for its equity that will eliminate its retained losses and thus allow it to pay a dividend as soon as 1Q25.  We can expect CEB to pay a dividend on its convertible preferred shares first as these have to be paid before any dividends can be distributed to common shares. We estimate the preferred shares have accrued cumulative dividends of P6.4/share.

POWER
NGCP declares yellow alert over Luzon grid
  • The National Grid Corp. of the Philippines (NGCP) declared a yellow alert over the Luzon grid yesterday (July 17), the first such declaration in over a month
    • This followed after the San Gabriel gas-fired power plant in Batangas which has a capacity of 417.4 megawatts (MW) tripped
    • Available capacity was 13,198 MW while peak demand was estimated at 12,028 MW
    • A total of 1,652.7 MW was unavailable to the grid, including the output of 15 power plants on forced outage and 6 running below their rated capacity
  • The Luzon grid was last placed on yellow alert on June 5 while red alert on June 2
    • To date, the grid was under red and yellow alerts for 11 and 32 days, respectively
  • Manila Electric Co. (Meralco) said it has advised the participants of its Interruptible Load Program to be on standby “in case the situation escalates to red alert”
    • Meralco and Department of Energy also urges the public to implement energy conservation and efficiency practices to help manage overall demand
  • Energy Undersecretary Rowena Cristina L. Guevara said that the department expects the power situation to improve with the addition of new capacity
  • Note that the Luzon grid’s LWAP stood at P8.15/kwh on July 16, up from the July1-7 weekly average of P6.35/kwh

TRANSPORT
Cebu Air initiates process for equity restructuring
  • Cebu Air’s Board of Directors approved a restructuring plan to eliminate the company’s retained earnings deficit, which stood at P16.3bn in end-2023
    • Will be offset from APIC of P20.7bn
    • Equity restructuring plan will go through SEC approval
  • Once approved, we reckon CEB should be able to declare dividends by 1Q25
    • Convertible pref shares (CEBCP PM) accrue an semi-annual dividend of P1.15/sh and accumulated unpaid dividends have reached P6.4/sh as of end-2023
    • CEBCP last traded at P35/sh, a P5.75/sh premium to the P29.25/sh of CEB commons
    • 1 CEBCP share is convertible to 1 common share
