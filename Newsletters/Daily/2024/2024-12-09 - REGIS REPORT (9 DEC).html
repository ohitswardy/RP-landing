REGIS REPORT

09 December 2024

The country’s gross international reserves declined US$2.6bn (-2.4%) MoM to US$108.5bn in Nov with the payment of FX debt by the government.

+++

SM Prime (SMPH PM) will be expanding its mall presence in China over the next few years. It will add one new mall in Xiamen in Fujian to its existing eight in China, including two in Fujian. Its existing mall in Xiamen will be expanded and a new mall in Fuzhou will be completed by 2027. The China malls presently account for 5% of EBIT for SMPH.

+++

Dito CME (DITO PM) raised P2bn from the sale of 1.95bn new shares (19.5bn outstanding shares before the transaction). The amount raised will not be enough to cover even one quarter’s financing costs. These periodic fund-raising exercises appear aimed at tiding DITO over until it can find a more permanent solution (e.g. secure a strategic investor with deep pockets).

Note DITO already disclosed a new investor (“Summit Telecom”) who may buy up to 9bn new shares in the company. Summit will become the controlling shareholder of DITO and subsidiary Dito Telecommunity (DitoTel) should the transaction be consummated. A change in control of DitoTel would require congressional approval.

+++

First Gen (FGEN PM) intends to spend up to P2bn for a new 50MW solar power facility in Batangas province.

+++

Jefferies’s US Economist Thomas Simons seems pretty bullish about Trump 2.0. Oddly, he’s bullish on the US economy, but also dovish on interest rates. He forecasts a 25bps cut at the next Fed meeting on 17/18 December, and then another 100bps in 2025E, for a total of 125bps. He also assumes inflation to head downwards (but not linear), as he expects the labor market to grow “more and more weary”. Check out his slide deck, attached.

To be fair, the market seems to have turned less hawkish on the Fed. At the low on 22 Nov, market expectations were of just 66bps worth of cuts until end-2025E. As of Friday the market is now pricing in almost 90bps as of Friday. Given the tight correlation of the Philippine market / currency, could there still be hope for a Santa Clause rally?

MACRO
Dollar reserves slip 2% at end-November
  • The Philippines' gross international reserves (GIR) saw a 2.4% MoM decrease to $108.5bn from $111.1bn at the end of October
    • Primarily due to net foreign currency withdrawals to settle foreign currency debt obligations and payment of expenditures
    • The level of dollar reserves by end-Nov was enough to cover about 4.3x the country's short-term external debt and also equivalent to 7.8x months' worth of imports of goods and payments
    • The BSP expects the country's GIR to settle at $106bn by end-2024
  • Reserve position in the IMF declined 2.3% MoM to $668.2mn from $683.9mn end-Oct
  • Buffers in the form of gold were valued at $11.03bn, down 2.9% MoM from $11.35bn at end-Oct
  • Foreign investments dipped by 2% MoM to $91.2bn as of end-Nov from 93.1bn end-Oct
  • The government's borrowing plan this year is set at a 75:25 mix, in favor of domestic sources
    • While for 2025-2027, the mix is seen to be at 80:20, the majority still from domestic sources

POWER
First Gen to build 50MW solar facility in Batangas
  • First Gen plans to begin construction of a 50MW solar facility in Batangas next year
    • It will spend P1bn-P2bn and will finish construction in two years
  • First Gen President and COO Francis Giles Puno said that the company “is very serious about expanding solar installation across the country”
  • FGEN has 3,668MW of installed capacity
POWER
Huawei secures contract for Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) in SPNEC’s Terra Solar
  • SPNEC, a subsidiary of Meralco (MER PM), has secured an agreement with Huawei to deliver the BESS component of their Terra Solar megaproject
    • Huawei will further provide step-up transformers to connect to the 34.5kV busbar at the main substation
    • Terra Solar intends to have a 4500MWh battery storage paired with a 3500MW solar plant, set to be fully operational by February 2027.
  • These BESS systems are intended to store excess electricity generated during low-demand periods and supply it back to the grid during peak demand
    • BESS systems are intended for optimizing grid operations, and ensuring a consistent and uninterrupted power supply

Globe expects 11mn new subscribers over the next five years

  • Globe’s mobile segment currently has 60 million customers
