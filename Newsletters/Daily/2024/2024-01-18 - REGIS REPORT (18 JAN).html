REGIS REPORT

18 January 2024

The market could see its first new listing this year from Citicore Renewable Energy Corporation (CREC). After securing SEC approval, CREC plans to raise as much as P12.9bn by selling 29-33% of its shares to the public in March. Bulk (83%) of the proceeds will be used to fund its capex pipeline of 1.4GW solar projects in the medium term. Longer term, the company has ambitions to grow its renewable energy portfolio to over 7GW from only 285MW today. 

CREC is owned by Citicore Holdings Investments, the parent company of construction firm, Megawide (MWIDE PM). CREC also owns a 48% stake in Citicore REIT (CREIT PM), which derives lease payments from three properties leased by CREC’s parent, Citicore Power.

AREIT (AREIT PM) acquired the 153-room Seda Lio hotel from Ayala Land (ALI PM) for P1.2bn in cash. This is the first leg of three transactions announced in 4Q23. The other two transactions are: 1) AREIT will acquire P21.8bn worth of office, mall and hotel assets by issuing 642mn shares to Ayala Land, and 2) AREIT will acquire P6.77bn worth of industrial land by issuing 199mn shares to ACEN Corporation (ACEN PM).

After all of these transactions are consummated, AREIT’s AUM will increase by 34% to P117bn and its shares outstanding will expand by 35% to 3.2bn shares. Since AREIT will issue 841mn new shares to Ayala Land and ACEN, we estimate that Ayala Land would have to sell about 280mn AREIT shares (worth c. P9.5bn at yesterday’s closing price) in order to meet minimum public float requirements.

+++

The PSE Index rebalancing will take place in mid-February and while we don’t expect any additions or deletions, it still won’t be a straightforward affair.

The big question is how the PSE will compute BPI’s float. This is more complicated than one might expect because BPI’s merger with Robinsons Savings Bank took place on January 1, the day after the 12-month review period for the Feb 2024 rebalancing. Furthermore, JG Summit (JGS PM) and Robinsons Retail (RRHI PM) now hold a combined 10% stake, which the PSE may or may not consider as strategic stakes.

From our perspective, there are three main scenarios:

            Scenario 1 - The PSE will use the BPI public float as of December 31 – 51% float

            Scenario 2 - The PSE will use the public float provided by BPI as of January 1 (which considers JG Summit and Robinsons Retail as part of public float)  - 54% float

            Scenario 3 – The PSE will consider JG Summit and Robinsons Retail as strategic shareholders of BPI – 44% public float

The implications can be massive. Under Scenario 2, BPI could receive inflow of US$11mn while under Scenario 3, it would see outflow of US$26mn.  

Where we have more conviction is that in all three scenarios, we expect moderate amounts of inflow to Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) and ACEN Corporation (ACEN PM) due to increases in their public ownership.
