REGIS REPORT

16 July 2024

Overseas remittances grew 3.6% in May to US$2.58bn.  This brings YT-May remittances to US$13.4bn (+3%), on track to match the government’s “target” and well within historical trends.

+++

On the gaming (gambling) front, there were two pieces of news that we have to highlight:

First, the disclosure by Okada Manila’s parent company showed its gaming revenue for 2Q was P8.8bn, practically flat QoQ. Recall that PAGCOR’s press statement implied that 2Q gaming revenue for its casino licensees (which includes the integrated resorts) was also flat QoQ.  This suggests softness in the GGR of Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) in the just-concluded quarter.

Second, Manny Villar, head of the Villar group of companies, says the casino his group is developing will be operational by latter part of next year (2025).  While we are not convinced that this property can be completed within that time frame, the addition of new gaming supply may pose increased competition risk for the rest of the operators in the future.

+++

The rates of Meralco (MER PM) customers jumped ~20% MoM for July.  This will have a perceptible impact on the reported CPI numbers for the month, eroding some of the gains we expect on rice prices.  Electricity accounts for 4.6% of the CPI.

+++

After a succession of tough quarters, we slash our forecasts for Wilcon Depot (WLCON PM) by 28-30% and cut our target price by 35% to P22/sh. Weak demand, heightened competition, and expansion-related costs contributed to slower top line growth and margin compression, leading the stock to fall another 15% YTD.   Nevertheless, we believe the worst is behind us and maintain our BUY rating.  Recovery of discretionary spending and moderation of store expansion following the post-pandemic slowdown should lead to more meaningful earnings growth (+14%) in 2025E. Our target price implies 21x 2025E PE, broadly in line with regional home improvement retail peers.

MACRO
Remittances rise 3.6% YoY in May
  • Cash remittances coursed through banks rose 3.6% YoY to US$2.58bn in May
    • In PHP terms, remittances expanded by 7.4% YoY
    • Driven by higher remittances from Singapore (+5%), USA (+5%), Saudi Arabia (+4%), and UAE (+5%)
  • Brings 5M24 remittances up 3% YoY to US$13.37bn
    • BSP is targeting remittances to grow by 3% YoY this year (suggests FY24E: US$34.5bn)

 

UTILITIES
Meralco rates up by P2 per kWh in July as power costs ‘normalize’
  • Meralco’s overall rate would increase by P2.1496 per kilowatt-hour (kWh) to P11.6012 per kWh in July (from P9.4516 per kWh in June)
    • Cited “normalizing power costs following artificially low rates last month”
    • Lower demand in the Luzon grid mitigated the increase in further charges
  • The release of Meralco bills will again be delayed as the utility firm “sought guidance from the ERC on the implementation of this month’s rate adjustment”
  • Electricity has a weight of 4.6% in the CPI basket
GAMING
Villar Group to open casino in 2025
  • Manuel Villar Jr. said his group is on track to open an integrated casino resort as early as mid-2025
    • The planned investment is approximately US$1bn
  • It will open on the redeveloped 18,000sqm Vista Mall Global South along C5 extension in Las Pinas
    • Mr. Villar expects it to perform well since it is closer to NAIA Terminal 1 than the existing integrated casino resorts
  • The number of hotel rooms and gaming tables has not been decided
GAMING
Okada Manila 2Q24 GGR flat QoQ, down 22% YoY 
  • Okada Manila’s 2Q24 GGR hit P8.8bn, flat QoQ but down 22% YoY
    • Volume and GGR of every category fell on a YoY basis
  • The slight QoQ increase in mass table and slot machine volumes suggest that the opening of Solaire Resorts North has not caused any weakness for Okada Manila
  • PAGCOR reported that the 2Q24 GGR of licensed casinos (including integrated resorts) was flat QoQ (down 4% YoY)
