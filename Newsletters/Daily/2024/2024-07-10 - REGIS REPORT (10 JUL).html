10 July 2024

We hosted a call with Jollibee (JFC PM) CFO Richard Shin to discuss its latest acquisition: Compose Coffee in Korea.

Purely on valuation, the deal looks attractive for JFC.  If the numbers pan out as expected, the acquisition will not only be accretive to EPS (+6-7%) but also enhancing to multiples (7x EBITDA vs JFC’s 9.5x). The question is whether the business can still grow and whether it might become an added distraction to the group’s large (and growing) portfolio of brands.  Check out the note and see what else management has to say:

SM Investments (SM PM) received approval from the SEC to increase the number of its board directors to nine (9), from eight (8).  Its shareholders already approved the election of Lily Gruba, a noted tax lawyer and former Finance Undersecretary, to the seat pending SEC approval.

+++

Bits of news reinforce our upbeat view on ICTSI’s (ICT PM) near-term outlook.  First, the local port regulator says it expects port throughput to rebound nicely in 2H.  Second, data from Mexico shows that volumes at ICTSI’s port there are on track to meet, if not slightly beat, our expectations for the year.

+++

There is media chatter that Trade Secretary Alfredo Pascual will be replaced by Frederick Go, the economic “czar” whose official title is Special Assistant to the President for Investment and Economic Affairs”. Go’s role as the overseer of the economic cluster of departments is arguably big enough as is. Adding direct control of the Trade Department could mean too much on his plate.

TRANSPORT
Port regulator sees cargo volume rebound in 2H; eyes 10-13% growth for 2024
  • The Philippine Ports Authority (PPA) sees cargo volumes rising by 10-13% YoY this year
    • While January to May 2024 cargo volume growth was flat, PPA expects a rebound by the 2H due to the holiday season
    • According to PPA general manager Jay Daniel Santiago
  • Foreign cargo volumes are currently experiencing delays due to congestion at regional transshipment hubs arising from the conflict at the Red Sea
  • Recall that container volumes at ICTSI’s (ICT PM) Manila International Container Terminal (MICT) was flat in 1Q24 (664k TEUs)
    • We forecast MICT’s volumes will grow by 5% YoY in 2024 to 2.9mn TEUs
    • We estimate MICT accounts for 23% of ICT’s consolidated volumes
TRANSPORT
ICTSI (ICT PM) Update - Port in Mexico rebounds to new high in June
  • After a brief interruption in May to make way for the arrival of new cranes, volumes at ICTSI’s port in Mexico, Contecon Manzanillo (CMSA), rebounded by 14% YoY and 23% MoM to a new monthly record of 128k TEUs in June.  CMSA’s market share surged to 39% for the month of June, higher than the typical 34%-ish level for the port in the past five years. 
  • While CMSA’s 2Q24 volume growth sharply decelerated to 7% YoY from the 37% pace in 1Q24, we note that this was only temporary and was largely due to a drop in lower-yielding transshipment volumes.  We reckon volume growth should re-accelerate once the company fully completes its expansion in August. CMSA’s volumes in 1H24 grew 20% YoY to 698k TEUs, on track to reach or even exceed our full-year volume forecast of 1.43mn TEUs (+12% YoY).  The latest data suggests that monthly volumes for the rest of the year should only average 122k TEUs to reach our forecast.  We estimate CMSA to be ICT’s second largest port, accounting for 11% of ICT’s consolidated volumes and 18% of consolidated revenues. 
  • We believe the cycle of earnings upgrades is far from over and current valuations of 9x/8x FY24E/FY25E EV/EBITDA and 15x/12x FY24E/FY25E PER remain attractive relative to history and peers.  Reiterate BUY.
