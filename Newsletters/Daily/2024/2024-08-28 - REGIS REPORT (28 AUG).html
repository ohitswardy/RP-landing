REGIS REPORT

28 August 2024

San Miguel Corp (SMC PM) and Aboitiz Power (AP PM) submitted the lowest bids in the auction to supply 600MW of baseload power to Meralco (MER PM).  SMC’s bid for 500MW (under Masinloc Power) was the lowest priced, while AP’s GNP-Dinginin was the next for the remaining 100MW.  Pricing for both was just above the P5.6/kwh (US$0.10) level.

Notably, Semirara (SCC PM), which runs one of the lowest cost plants in the grid, submitted a bid that was almost 40% higher.

The bidding for 400MW mid-merit will happen next month.

+++

ACEN (ACEN PM) issued a press statement announcing an “exclusive” partnership with Indonesian firm Barito Renewables (BREN IJ) for the development of wind farms in Indonesia.  BREN is not a new partner for ACEN as they have jointly owned RE projects in Indonesia.

+++

The national budget deficit for the month July was P26bn, down by ~45%.  YT-July, the budget deficit rose 7% to P643bn.  The government is aiming to have budget deficit of P1.48trn this year (5.6% of GDP) and it appears probable that it will come in below that.

+++

A new article on AI and the BPO industry in the Philippines just appeared on Bloomberg News. The article brings up what’s been on the minds of many: the potential implications of AI on the US$38bn BPO industry which employs ~1.8m people.

The piece breaks no new ground but is a reminder that the implications of AI, including the threat to many jobs in the Philippines and everywhere else, have yet to be fully understood and appreciated.

Budget deficit widens to P643bn in YT-July; Implies July budget deficit narrows to P26bn
  • During a Senate finance committee hearing yesterday, Finance Secretary Ralph Recto revealed that the government’s budget deficit widened by 7% YoY to P642.8bn in 7M24
    • Revenues rose 15% to P2.6tn while expenditures grew 13% YoY to P3.25tn during the same period
    • The government is targeting a budget deficit of P1.48tn for 2024, lower by 2% YoY and equivalent to 5.6% of GDP
  • Implies that for the month of July alone, the government’s budget deficit narrowed to P26bn
    • Implied revenues for July improved by 12% YoY to P461bn, after tax revenues jumped 15% YoY
    • Implied government expenditures for July grew by 6% YoY to P487bn
  • Official budget deficit data for July is scheduled to be released today

POWER
San Miguel, Aboitiz submit lowest bids for Meralco’s 600MW CSP
  • San Miguel (SMC PM) and Aboitiz Power (AP PM) submitted the lowest bids for Meralco’s (MER PM) competitive selection process (CSP) of 600MW baseload power supply agreement (PSA)
    • SMC’s Masinloc Power Co. Ltd. submitted the lowest bid of P5.6015/kWh for the 500 MW PSA
    • AP’s GNPower Dinginin Power Ltd. Co. submitted the lowest bid of P5.7392/kwh for the 100 MW PSA
  • Other bidders were:
    • SMC’s Mariveles Power Generation Corporation – P6.4017/kwh
    • EGCO group’s Quezon Power – P6.5487/kwh
    • Semirara’s (SCC PM) Southwest Luzon Power Generation Corp. – P7.7303/kwh
    • AP’s Therma Luzon Inc.- P8.3388/kwh
  • Winning offers had been lower than the reserve price set by Meralco –with a headline rate of P6.9537/kwh; and a levelized cost of electricity (LCOE) at P7.2609/kwh
  • The PSAs to be sealed for the winning offers will have a term of 15 years – with a delivery period commencing on August 26, 2025, or upon the approval of the contract by the Energy Regulatory Commission
  • The next CSP round will be for MER’s 400MW mid-merit capacity requirement, with bid submissions targeted by October 1
POWER
ACEN, Indonesian firm partner to develop wind renewable energy projects across Indonesia
  • ACEN Corp. is tying up with Jakarta-based PT Barito Renewables Energy to develop wind energy projects in the provinces of South Sulawesi, Sukabumi, and Lombok
    • Details about the partnership were not revealed
    • However, recall that ACEN acquired three late-stage wind development assets with a combined potential capacity of 320MW in the same provinces seven months ago
  • ACEN currently has a portfolio of 99MW of geothermal assets (operating and under construction) in Indonesia
    • In aggregate, ACEN’s current total attributable RE capacity stands at 4.8GW across the Philippines, Australia, Vietnam, Indonesia, and India
    • ACEN has a target of quadrupling its renewable energy capacity to 20 gigawatts (GW) by 2030
