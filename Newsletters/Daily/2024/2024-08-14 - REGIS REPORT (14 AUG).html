REGIS REPORT

14 August 2024

Bangko Sentral (BSP) Governor Eli Remolona appears to be dousing rate cut expectations for tomorrow’s Monetary Board meeting. After signaling a rate reduction a few weeks back, Remolona’s latest pronouncement that “there is more room to stay tight” could dash hopes for a move tomorrow.  Until yesterday, consensus seems to be expecting a 25bp policy rate reduction.

+++

Check out today’s round-up of results, which looks a bit skewed to misses than beats:

 

  • PLDT (TEL PM) (=) – Core income for 1H (inclusive of Maya losses) amounted to P17.4bn (+6% YoY).  While the bottom line appears within expectations, the underlying operations are a little softer than we anticipated.  Mobile revenue was up +3% YoY in 2Q -- less than we hoped. PLDT also added 35k wired broadband subscribers, but revenue was virtually unchanged QoQ.

  • Philippine Seven (SEVN PM) (=) – Profits for 1H rose 14% to P1.76bn, with 2Q earnings up +10%. SSSG moderated slightly in 2Q but remained quite healthy at +7.4%, despite the “Holy Week effect”. Overall, the results are within our expectations, if not slightly better.

For international investors>>Philippine Seven - 2Q24: Margins dragged by frontloaded opex; adjusting forecasts and TP

For Philippine-based investors>>Philippine Seven - 2Q24: Margins dragged by frontloaded opex; adjusting forecasts and TP

 

  • Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) (-) – 1H24 net profit fell 38% to P4.0bn, behind REGIS and consensus forecasts. Despite the opening of Solaire Resort North, 2Q24 GGR fell 2% QoQ and 4% YoY to P14.5bn. 2Q24 GGR of Solaire Entertainment City fell 10% QoQ and 12% YoY to P13.3bn, largely due to lower VIP volumes (though mass and slot GGR fell as well). Bloomberry will hold a briefing later today.

 

  • Jollibee (JFC PM) (-) – Profits for 1H rose 29% YoY to P5.7bn, which appears to be a tad short of Regis estimates. The underlying operations, however, do look healthy.  Revenues rose 11% and operating profit grew 29%. For 2Q, group-wide SSSG was a solid +7.4%, with PH operations leading the way at +9.1%. Management will hold a briefing tomorrow (Aug 15) and we should get more “granularity” then.

 

  • Filinvest Land (FLI PM) (=) – 1H24 reservation sales expanded 16% to P12.8bn, thanks to: a strong push to liquidate ready-for-occupancy units, new sales offices in more countries, and the government raising the VAT-free ceiling (from P.3.2mn to P3.6mn). While 2Q24 office occupancy improved c. 5ppt QoQ (to 63%), this will likely come down in 2H24, as POGO firms close and new untenanted office space is completed.

+++

The Lower House approved Meralco’s (MER PM) franchise renewal on 2nd reading, crossing a major hurdle. The final approval, however, is likely to be many months away.  Meralco’s franchise is supposed to expire in 2028, and the renewal will allow Meralco to operate another 25 years.

+++

The competition authorities have cleared the way for DMCI (DMC PM) to acquire the Philippine operations of Cemex. A tender offer for Cemex PH (CHP PM) shares is expected to follow.

MACRO
BSP Governor: “More room to stay tight”
  • BSP Governor Eli Remolona, Jr. said that there is “more room to stay tight”, after the better-than-expected GDP growth in 2Q
    • Statements made on the sidelines of a budget committee hearing at the Senate
    • BSP said the latest GDP performance “helps” the case for keeping rates steady as the risk of a hard landing is lessened
  • During the Senate hearing, Governor Remolona told senators that the 6.5% benchmark rate is “tight”, given that they are trying to tame inflation
    • BSP does not want to keep rates high for an “unnecessarily” long time because this could lead to loss of output
    • As soon as inflation is on the way to our target range (of 2-4%), BSP will have room to ease the policy rate
    • Note that the latest inflation print for July stood at 4.4%; Ceteris paribus, if rice CPI stays flat MoM in August, headline inflation for August could drop to 3.3%
  • Monetary Board is set to meet tomorrow (Aug 15); Consensus expects the BSP to cut policy rates by 25bps
