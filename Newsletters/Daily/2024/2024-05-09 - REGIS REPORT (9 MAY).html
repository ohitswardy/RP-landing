REGIS REPORT

09 May 2024

We have a deluge of 1Q results today.  Arguably the most keenly watched would be Ayala Land’s (ALI PM) given recent concerns about industry property sales.  The numbers from SM Investment (SM PM) are also noteworthy due to an uncommon disappointment from the retail segment.  Monde’s (MONDE PM) results, meanwhile, are a refreshing positive after the tough periods over the past year.

  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) (=) – 1Q24 net profit rose 39% to P6.3bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. Reservation sales rose 20% YoY (+19% QoQ) to P33.3bn, on track to meet management’s 15% growth target for the full-year. Management credits this to the 25% increase in the number of premium segment sales agents (last year), which led to a corresponding 34% jump in 1Q24 premium segment reservation sales.

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (-) – Profits for 1Q24 rose 6% to P18.4bn, which look weak relative to consensus expectations and the unlisted operations were the reason for the disappointment. Contributions from SM Retail dropped 23% YoY, as revenues grew 3%.  The non-Food segments saw big declines in contributions, with earnings fall far more than revenues.

  • Monde Nissin (MONDE PM) (+) – A sharp jump in APAC BFB margins (>400bps QoQ / >700bps YoY) led drove consolidated net profit up 53% YoY during the quarter. Management believes current gross margin levels can be sustained, with majority of wheat and palm oil input requirements secured for the year.

  • Century Pacific Food (CNPF PM) (=) – 1Q24 net profit grew 15% YoY on a strong top line driven by the recovery of the OEM segment (sales +49%). Branded sales grew 10% amidst downtrading, while gross margin improved on favorable input cost. The gross margin gains, however, are to be reinvested in A&P spending to help further drive volume growth.
  • ACEN Corp (ACEN PM) (+) – Profits rose 34% YoY to P2.7bn as newly completed projects started delivering output. The progression of project completions this year should help keep output growing sequentially, especially during the peak months for its domestic operations. We reckon the Street is likely to upgrade estimates meaningfully after these results.

  • San Miguel Food and Beverage (FB PM) (=) – Headline net profit was flattish YoY. While the Food and Liquor segments saw strong operating profit growth, the Beer business saw a slight decline, partly due to a high base last year.

  • DMCI (DMC PM) (+) – 1Q24 recurring profit of P5.38bn is trending ahead of Regis estimates due to better-than-expected profits at Semirara, Maynilad, and the off-grid units. However, market and regulatory issues have hampered operations of its key segments, leading to profit declines. Management also revealed it plan to fund its acquisition of Cemex (CHP PM) entirely through debt to be able to maintain paying dividends.

  • First Gen (FGEN PM) (-) – Recurring profit for 1Q24 dipped 9% because of lower contributions from its geothermal unit EDC and the end of San Gabriel’s contract with Meralco (MER PM), which was followed by a maintenance shutdown. Notwithstanding the weaker-than-expected profits, we are optimistic about the rest of the year, especially 2Q24.

  • Cebu Air (CEB PM) (+) - Better-than-expected fares and lower-than-expected costs boosted CEB’s 1Q24 recurring profit up 47% YoY to P1.98bn, trending ahead of Regis and Street estimates.  We hope to get more detail during management’s 1Q24 briefing tomorrow, May 10 at 3PM – Registration link.

MACRO
Trade deficit narrows to US$3.2bn in March
  • Preliminary data showed the country’s trade deficit shrunk 37% YoY, 13% MoM to US$3.18bn in March
    • Due to a 20% drop in imports
  • Imports dropped 20% YoY to US$9.3bn, the sharpest drop since July 2020
    • Raw material imports dropped 25% and capital goods imports dropped 15%
    • Consumer goods imports fell 19% and fuel imports dropped 18%
  • Exports fell 7% to US$6.13bn
    • Exports to the US and HK rose 9% and 60%, respectively
  • Brings 1Q24 trade deficit down by 22% to US$11.25bn

 

 

CONSUMER
Century Pacific – 1Q24 margins buoyed by favorable input cost
  • CNPF’s net profit grew 15% YoY to P1.7bn in 1Q24, broadly in line with Regis and consensus expectations. Revenues rose 16% YoY to P18.2bn (in line), driven by the rebound of the non-branded OEM segment, which jumped 49% YoY. EBITDA grew 13% YoY to P2.6bn, trending within forecasts.
  • Branded revenues rose 10% YoY during the quarter, driven largely by higher volumes. Management noted that downtrading persisted amidst high inflation, and much of the volume growth was driven by value brands/SKUs. By segment, the core marine and meat segments grew mid single digit, while milk grew in the mid teens.
  • Gross margin expanded 250bps YoY due to the combined impact of (1) favorable inventory cost relative to 1Q23 and (2) price increases from the previous year. EBITDA margin, however, saw a mild contraction (-40bps YoY) as the gross margin gains were reinvested into A&P and additional trade spending.
  • Management expects to achieve 100-200bps gross margin improvement for the full year (Regis FY24E: +60bps)  as it has locked in prices of key inputs (skipjack tuna, meat, milk) for most of the year. However, the gains will continue to be reinvested into A&P to help drive volumes amidst a challenging consumer environment.
  • CNPF currently trades at 22x 2024E PE

 

CONSUMER
San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) 1Q24 headline net profit at P10bn – in line
  • In a press release, FB reported headline net income of P10bn, up 1% YoY, broadly in line with expectations
  • Strong results from Purefoods and Ginebra San Miguel, with operating profit +78% YoY and +40% YoY, respectively
  • Meanwhile, San Miguel Brewery’s operating profit dipped 4% YoY. Volumes were down vs last year, partly due to trade loading ahead of price increases in March 2023
  • FB currently trades at 12x 2024E PE
POWER
First Gen 1Q recurring profit dips 9%
  • First Gen reported recurring net income of US$81m for 1Q24, down 9% from US$89m in 1Q23
    • Decline was blamed on lower profits at geothermal unit Energy Development Corp (EDC)
    • San Gabriel also saw a drop due to the end of its contract with Meralco
    • First Gen also completed the deal to buy the 165MW hydro Casecnan
  • The gas-fired plants saw a 4% increase in recurring earnings to US$45m
    • San Gabriel went on maintenance after the end of its contract with Meralco in Feb
  • EDC saw a 33% contraction in earnings due to lower output and reduced WESM prices
  • The hydro contributions rose 12% to US$8m

DigiPlus 1Q24 net profit rises 370%

TRANSPORT
Cebu Air 1Q24 core profit up 47% YoY to P1.98bn – Ahead of estimates
  • Headline net income more than doubled to P2.24bn, with core profit up 47% YoY to P1.98bn
    • This is trending AHEAD of Regis estimates and consensus due to higher fares and lower cash costs
  • Revenues rose 21% to P25.3bn
    • Pax volumes rose 14% to 5.5mn (in line) while average fares jumped further to P3.2k (ahead)
  • EBITDA rose 61% to P6.6bn, just 5% below pre-Covid levels and is on track to beat our FY24E (P27bn), as well as consensus FY24E (P24.1bn)
    • Cash costs of P18.7bn (+11% YoY) primarily on lower fuel and maintenance costs
  • More after the management briefing on May 10, 3pm – Registration link
  • CEB trades at 4.6x FY24E EV/EBITDA, below regional peer average of 7x
