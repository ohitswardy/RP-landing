REGIS REPORT

20 March 2024

We estimate that the US$300mn settlement between Bloomberry Resorts (BLOOM PM) and GGAM will result in BLOOM’s net debt/equity level rising from 1.2X to 2.0X. While the company would still be well within its debt covenants, the likelihood of an equity-raising at some point in the future has arguably increased.

Furthermore, the purchase of GGAM’s stake will lower BLOOM’s public float by about 8ppt. All else held constant, this should result in a reduction in its weighting in the PSE Index. We estimate that outflow could hit c. US$6mn (during the rebalancing after the shares are purchased)

In other gaming news, FY23 revenue of online gaming firm DigiPlus (PLUS PM) rose 300% to P27.3bn while net profit grew nearly 600% to P4.1bn. Remarkably, this young company’s revenue and net profit are nearly half of the respective figures of Bloomberry, the most profitable integrated resort. PLUS is scheduled to get a further boost as PAGCOR will be rationalizing the fees it collects from online gaming firms, which will result in a reduction in payments from PLUS.

The Real Property Valuation and Assessment Reform Act took one step further to becoming law. Property valuations used for property tax purposes are 10-20 years out of date in many areas. Understandably, the government wants these values updated more regularly (by forming a new national government agency). Thus, the Bill could raise real property taxes and would likely be most negative for developers with large land banks, such as Ayala Land (ALI PM), Filinvest Land (FLI PM), and Vista Land (VLL PM).

PROPERTY
Senate ratifies bicameral report on property valuation reform
  • The Senate ratified a bicameral report on the Real Property Valuation and Assessment Reform Act (RPVARA)
    • Bill seeks to standardize the property valuation used by the national government and local government units
    • Assessed valued used for computing real property taxes can be 10-20 years out of date in some areas
    • The measure sets a two-year amnesty on interests and penalties for taxpayers with unpaid real property tax
  • We estimate that real property taxes account for 3%-7% of pretax profit of the developers we cover
  • This law would likely have a large impact on developers with large land banks, such as Ayala Land, Filinvest Land, and Vista Land
GAMING
PAGCOR expects industry GGR to hit P450bn-P500bn by 2027; to lower fees on e-games operators
  • PAGCOR, the gaming regulator, expects Philippine GGR to hit P450-P500bn by 2027, from P285bn in 2023
    • This would represent 13%-16% CAGR
  • Due to opening of about six new casinos over the next few years – in Manila, Clark, Cebu, Boracay, and other areas
  • It also expects online gaming to grow during the period
  • Meanwhile, PAGCOR will lower the fees that it charges online gaming operators by 2.5ppt to 7.5ppt depending on the game
  • We estimate that revenue of DigiPlus would likely increase c. 10%
  • Holding all other things constant, EBITDA could increase up to 40%

 

GAMING
Initial estimates on financial impact of BLOOM settlement, impact to PSE index
  • The settlement between BLOOM and GGAM will raise net debt (by P16.9bn), reduce equity (by P16.9bn), and (effectively) lower shares outstanding (by 921mn shares or 8%). As a result:
  • Gearing - We estimate that FY23 consolidated net debt/equity will rise from 1.2X to 2.0X. Management said that this would not breach debt covenants, which are a function of gross debt at the operating subsidiary level.
  • EPS – If BLOOM were to raise debt at an interest rate of 7%, we estimate that 2025E EPS would only drop by c. 1% (due to the reduction in shares outstanding).
  • Target price – Our initial estimates suggest that if we were to maintain our target EV/EBITDA multiple of 10.1X (as well as account for the higher net debt and lower shares outstanding), then our target price would drop by c. 3% to P14.60/share
  • Meanwhile, our preliminary estimates suggest that the BLOOM settlement will result in a lower PSE Index weighting
  • Our initial estimates suggest an outflow of US$6.3mn from BLOOM (and minor amounts of inflow to all other PSEi constituents)
  • Note that this can only be triggered after a transaction actually occurs (after BLOOM subsidiary buys the GGAM shares)
  • According to BLOOM management, since it is a BLOOM subsidiary (not BLOOM parent) that will buy the BLOOM shares, shares outstanding will not drop but the acquired shares will appear as non-public (vs public currently)

 

GAMING
DigiPlus (PLUS PM) FY23 net profit rises nearly 600%
  • FY23 earnings of digital gaming company DigiPlus grew 596% to P4.1bn, higher than consensus forecast of P3.1bn
    • FY23 revenue rose 306% to P27.3bn on higher user traffic in its flagship bingo platform, BingoPlus, and sportsbook ArenaPlus
    • FY23 EBITDA grew fivefold to P4.8bn
  • In 4Q23, net income grew 392% to P2.0bn, due to the full quarter impact of the Color Game, which was launched in September 2023
    • 4Q23 revenue rose 331% to P11.3bn while EBITDA expanded 460% to P2.3bn
  • The stock is trading at 10X FY24E consensus P/E (consensus target of P4.3bn)
POWER
First Gen may shut down Pantabangan plant sooner than expected
  • First Gen may need to close its 132MW Pantabangan-Masiway hydroelectric plant earlier than expected due to decreasing water levels caused by El Niño
    • According to Richard P. Difuntorum, complex head of the Pantabangan-Masiway and Casecnan hydro plants
    • Shutdown typically starts in third week of April
  • Data from the National Irrigation Administration (NIA) showed that the water elevation at the Pantabangan Dam is projected to reach 177.80 meters by March 27
    • As of Tuesday morning, water elevation at the dam was at 179.9 meters, below the 207 meters required for the plant to operate at its full capacity
    • Currently, the Pantabangan plant produces 40 MW, which is c. 30% of its installed capacity
  • Data from the National Grid Corp. of the Philippines showed that the Luzon grid has an available generating capacity of 14,709 MW and a system peak demand of 11,284 MW
POWER
Citicore Energy REIT Corp declares 10% hike in cash dividend
  • In a report to the PSE, Citicore Energy REIT Corp. (CREIT) declared dividends of P0.054/sh for 4Q23, 10% higher than 3Q23 at P0.049/sh
    • Driven by income generated from guaranteed and variable lease contracts in the quarter
    • 4Q23 dividend is equivalent to a yield of 1.9%
    • Record - April 18, 2024
    • Payment - May 15, 2024
  • This translates to total dividends of P0.199/sh for FY23, a 9% increase from P0.1833/sh in FY22 
    • Using the 12-month average price of CREIT, its FY23 dividend is equivalent to a yield of 7.7%
