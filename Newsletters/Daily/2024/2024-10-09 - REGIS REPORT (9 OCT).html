REGIS REPORT

09 October 2024

Banks’ foreign currency loans and deposits as of 2Q24 declined sharply QoQ, with loans down 2.7% and deposits falling 5.9% compared to the 1Q24 levels. On a YoY basis, FX loans were up just 1.6% to $15.6bn, while FX deposits were up 12.6% to $55.2bn (slowing sharply from the +20.3% in 1Q). As a share of totals, FX loans dipped to just 6.6% of total (was 6.8% in 1Q), while FX deposits dropped to 16.9% of total (from 17.5% in 1Q). The banking system’s FX LDR stayed extremely low at 28%, compared to the 71% total, suggesting that PHP LDRs were just shy of 80%.

We believe that the sharp drop in FX deposits – following the sharp jump in 4Q23/1Q24 – includes some measure of funds shifting away from USD given the prevailing “weak dollar” outlook. Recall that the calls for a US Fed rate cut began growing in early- to mid-June. A continued shift away from USD into PHP assets bodes well for financial instruments, including property and the stock market.

ICTSI (ICT PM) cleared one hurdle in its bid to operate one of two terminals in South Africa’s Durban port with the approval by the competition authorities there.  The significant unresolved issue is the lawsuit filed by Maersk which is pending with the Durban High Court.

While this acquisition is unlikely to be meaningfully accretive initially, it would be an important deal for ICTSI given the status of Durban for South Africa and South Africa’s position in the continent.

Property broker Leechiu Property Consultants estimates that the number of Metro Manila condo units available for sale is equivalent to twenty-nine (29) months of sales.  This would be much higher than what LPC says is the typical level (12 months).  We believe the rise in “inventory days” is a result of both a jump in supply and a dip in monthly take-up.

Digiplus (PLUS PM) is one of 93 firms to receive approval to operate an online betting platform in Brazil.  This appears to be a temporary approval, as it can only operate until year-end 2024. The Brazilian government will subsequently decide who will be allowed to operate beyond that.

BANKS
FX loans, deposits drop QoQ

Last week, the BSP released its latest data for banks’ foreign currency deposit units (FCDUs), which showed 2Q2024 FCDU loans at $15.6bn, up 1.6% YoY, slowing from the 4.0% pace in 1Q24. The figure was also down 2.7% QoQ from 1Q24 levels ($16.1bn).

More striking, in our view, was that FX deposits dropped 5.9% QoQ to $55.2bn. On a YoY basis, growth slowed sharply from 20.3% YoY in 1Q24 to just 12.6% YoY in 2Q24. We have been watching this closely as it could indicate sentiment towards the PHP assets. Recall that by mid-June, the talk of a Fed rate cut had begun to re-intensify, signaling the start of the “weak dollar” narrative. It also marked the start of the PSEi rally. We would be surprised if the FCDU deposit trend did not continue into 3Q, considering the USD’s softness.

As a result of the FCDU contraction, FX loans as a share of total loans dipped to 6.6% in 2Q24 from 6.8% in the previous quarter. FX deposits accounted for 16.9% of total, compared to 17.5% in 1Q24. On an LDR basis,  The drop would have been more dramatic, had it not been for the weak PHP which depreciated 4% QoQ in 2Q24.

FX LDRs climbed to 28.3% from 27.4% in 1Q24. This compares to overall LDR of 70.9%, suggesting that the PHP LDR was 79.4%.

  • South Africa’s Competition Commission conditionally greenlighted the 25-year concession state-owned Transnet is granting to ICTSI (ICT PM)
    • Approval was given on the condition that Transnet does not retrench any employees for a period of three years following the merger implementation date
    • A 50:50 SPV will be formed, which will attain a Level 4 B-BBEE contributor status within one year (Level 4 B-BBEE means black ownership is below 51%)
  • Meanwhile, Transnet extended the validation period of the Durban privatization to March 25, 2025 from the initial September 30 deadline
    • Due to ongoing legal challenge launched by losing bidder, Maersk
    • The Durban High Court has heard the arguments last month and parties are awaiting judgement
  • ICT trades at 8.8x FY25E EV/EBITDA and 14.5x PER, lower than peer average (11x/16x) and historical average (11x/21x)

PROPERTY
Filinvest Land tender offer to begin on October 7
  • Filinvest Land provided the relevant dates for its tender offer
  • Tender offer period – October 7 to November 27
  • Cross date – December 9
  • Settlement Date – December 11
  • Filinvest Land’s Board of Directors approved a tender offer for up to 1.866bn FLI shares
    • This represents 7.7% of FLI’s outstanding shares and 24% of float shares 
  • FLI shares will be exchanged for FILRT shares at a ratio of 0.32 FILRT shares for every 1 FLI share tendered
    • 597mn FILRT shares will be exchanged for 1.866bn FLI shares – this is equivalent to 12.2% of FILRT shares outstanding
  • FILRT is trading at P3.05/share while FLI is trading at P0.81/share
    • Based on the swap ratio, FLI should be worth P0.976/share (when FILRT is at P3.05/share)
  • The crossing prices will be:
    • FLI – P1.00/share
    • FILRT – P3.11/share
  • After the transaction:
    • FLI’s public float will fall from 32.1% to 24.4%
    • FILRT’s public float will rise from 34.5% to 46.7%
  • FILRT currently offers 10.5% dividend yield (based on consensus forecast)
  • FLI is trading at 62% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 5.8X FY24E P/E

CONSUMER
Sari-sari stores continue to dominate business registrations in the country
  • Businesses engaged in wholesale and retail trade, many of which are small neighborhood shops or “sari-sari stores”, made up 55.43% of a total 923k business name registrations and renewals filed as of October 6
    • According to data from the Department of Trade and Industry
    • New business registrations and renewals are up 2% from the 903k recorded in 10M23
  • This was followed by accommodation and food service (14%), manufacturing (5%), and real estate (4%)
  • Data also showed that Region IV-A continued to lead the highest number of registrations at 175k (18% of total), followed by NCR (16%)
