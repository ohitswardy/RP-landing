REGIS REPORT

12 March 2024

Jollibee Foods (JFC PM) reported headline net profit of P8.8bn (+16% YoY), behind our estimates but in line with consensus expectations. SSSG decelerated in 4Q23 across most markets, except China and CBTL. This may have contributed to the contraction in operating margins during the quarter, despite gross margin expansion. Meanwhile, the company released FY24 guidance, which would put our FY24 forecasts on the upper end of the guidance range. We hope to get further detail during the briefing on March 15. 

Meanwhile, the key highlights from the briefing of Robinsons Land (RLC PM) were: 1) management appears cautious on demand for its standalone residential projects, and 2) the company will jointly develop a 33-hectare property in Fort Bonifacio with SM Prime (SMPH PM). The new property should allow RLC to continue launching high-end residential JV projects when its existing projects are nearly sold out.

Century Pacific (CNPF PM) entered into a new coconut water supply aggreement with Vita Coco, its largest customer for coconut OEM. The deal is expected to augment CNPF's coconut water production by 30-40% over the next 5 years.

+++

Belle Corp (BEL PM) made a tender offer for all outstanding shares of its 80%-owned subsidiary, Premium Leisure Corp (PLC PM). PLC holds the license for City of Dream Manila and has an EBITDA sharing arrangement with Melco Resorts Philippines.

+++

The Senate passed on third reading the Real Property Valuation and Assessment Reform Act. Property valuations used for property tax purposes are 10-20 years out of date in many areas. Understandably, the government wants these values updated more regularly (by forming a new national government agency). Thus, the Bill could raise real property taxes and would likely be most negative for developers with large land banks, such as Ayala Land (ALI PM), Filinvest Land (FLI PM), and Vista Land (VLL PM).

MACRO
FDI net inflows rise 30% YoY in December; FY23 inflows dip 7% YoY to US$8.9bn
  • Foreign direct investments posted a net inflow of US$826mn in December, up 30% YoY
    • Due to an 86% YoY jump in nonresidents’ investments in debt instruments to US$527mn
    • Reinvestment of earnings rose 4% to US$91mn while investments in equity capital dropped 22% YoY to US$208mn
  • Brings FY23 FDI net inflows to US$8.9bn, down 6.6% YoY and equivalent to 2.0% of GDP
    • This is above the BSP’s target of US$8bn for the year
    • For 2024, the BSP is targeting FDI net inflows of US$10bn

 

MANUFACTURING
Marcos wants more US semiconductor firms to expand in PHL
  • President Marcos is calling for US companies to set up wafer fabrication facilities in the country
    • Upskilling of workforce would help the Philippines expand its role in the semiconductor industry beyond assembly and packaging
  • US Trade Secretary Gina Raimondo reiterated a plan to crank out more than 120k engineers and process technicians by 2028 to boost output
  • The Philippines is one of seven countries that the US is partnering with to diversify its semiconductor supply chain under the CHIPS and Science Act
    • Law provides US$52.7bn in federal subsidies to support chip manufacturing and persuade chipmakers with operations in China to relocate to the US or other friendly countries
PROPERTY
Senate approves Bill on property valuation reform
  • The Senate approved on third reading Senate Bill 2386, the Real Property Valuation and Assessment Reform Act
    • The law will take effect two years after it is passed
  • This seeks to standardize the property valuation used by the national government and local government units
  • Assessed valued used for computing real property taxes can be 10-20 years out of date in some areas
  • We estimate that real property taxes account for 3%-7% of pretax profit of the developers we cover
  • This law would likely have a large impact on developers with large land banks, such as Ayala Land, Filinvest Land, and Vista Land
  • ALI is trading at 37% discount to NAV, 1.6X FY24E P/B, and 17.8X FY24E P/E
  • FLI is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 5.1X FY24E P/E
  • VLL is trading at 68% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 2.7X FY24E P/E
GAMING
Belle Corp makes tender offer for Premium Leisure
  • Belle Corp (BEL PM) disclosed that it would make a tender offer for all outstanding shares of Premium Leisure Corp (PLC PM)
    • BEL currently owns 80% of PLC
  • The tender offer price will be based on a Fairness Opinion by an independent appraiser (First Metro Investments Corp)
  • At the last price of P0.74/share, PLC traded at 10.6X FY23 P/E
  • At the PLC market cap of P23.1bn, BEL would have to spend P4.6bn to acquire the PLC shares held by minority shareholders
  • PLC holds the license for City of Dreams Manila 
    • It has an EBITDA sharing arrangement with Melco Resorts Philippines
    • PLC’s FY23 gaming revenue share grew 50% to P2.3bn
  • BEL is trading at 9.1X annualized 9M23 P/E
CONSUMER
Jollibee Foods FY23 headline net profit +16% YoY to P8.8bn – behind
  • JFC reported FY23 headline NPAT of P8.8bn, up 16% YoY, behind Regis but broadly in line with consensus expectations
    • After deducting perp coupons and pref dividends, we estimate NPAT to common was P7.5bn, 4x vs P1.9bn in FY22
  • FY23 revenues of P244bn (+15% YoY) were in line while operating profit of P14.4bn (+36% YoY) fell short of estimates
  • For 4Q23 alone, we estimate recurring operating profit was flat at P2.5bn
    • Recall that the company incurred a one-time provision expense in 4Q22 relating to the closure of Pho24 stores in the Philippines
  • Revenues grew 8% YoY during the quarter while consolidated SSSG slowed to 5.4%
    • SSSG slowed across all formats except for China (4Q SSS +22% vs +2% in 3Q) and CBTL (+2.4% vs -1.5%)
    • Philippines SSS +5.4% from +13% in 3Q
    • North America SSS flat vs +6% in 3Q
  • Operating margin in 4Q23 contracted QoQ and YoY despite GPM expansion due to proportionately higher opex
  • The company also provided FY24 guidance
    • +10-14% systemwide sales growth and +10-15% operating profit growth
    • 700-750 new stores with capex of P20-23bn
    • Regis FY24 operating profit (P16.9bn) is within the upper end of the guidance range
  • We hope to get more detail during the results briefing on March 15 – Registration link

 

CONSUMER
Jollibee to raise up to P8bn from preferred share issuance
  • JFC plans to raise P5-8bn through the issuance of a 2nd tranche of PHP-denominated preferred shares
    • 5mn preferred shares with an oversubscription option of up to 3mn preferred shares at a subscription price of P1,000/sh
  • The prefs will be cumulative, non-voting, non-participating, non-convertible, and redeemable
  • The dividend rate will be determined at the time of the pricing of the offering
  • Net proceeds will be used to refinance obligations including:
    • Series A preferred shares due in October 2024
    • Other general business purposes
  • JFC had P12bn worth preferred shares as of end-Sep (issued in 2021)
    • P3bn Series A: 3mn shares at a dividend rate of 3.2821%
    • P9bn Series B: 9mn shares at a dividend rate of 4.2405%
  • We estimate that the preferred share issuance should not materially impact our 2024E net profit forecasts
  • JFC currently trades at 26x 2024E PE
CONSUMER
Century Pacific forges new supply contract with Vita Coco
  • CNPF entered into a new contract with The Vita Coco Company
    • Vita Coco is CNPF’s largest customer for its coconut OEM business
    • The agreement is an addition to the previous agreement signed in 2020, which is up for renewal in 2025
  • The new contract covers additional volume requirements by Vita Coco of approximately 90m liters of coconut water over the next five years
    • This can potentially add 30-40% to CNPF’s coconut OEM volumes
    • It will also entail additional capex of USD40mn (~P2.3bn) for the incremental capacity requirements
  • The coconut OEM segment contributes 8-10% of CNPF’s annual revenue and approximately 8-12% of operating profit (estimated)
  • CNPF currently trades at 18x 2024E PE
POWER
Meralco to complete Atimonan gas conversion project by 2029
  • Meralco expects to complete conversion of its coal-fired power plant in Quezon province into a gas-fired plant by 2029
  • Meralco Powergen Corp. (MGen), through its subsidiary Atimonan One Energy, Inc. (A1E), is developing a 2,400MW natural gas-fired combined cycle gas turbine power plant
  • About P175bn is being invested into the project which is targeted for commercial operations by 2029
    • Project includes building an LNG storage unit, LNG unloading facility, and an LNG re-gasification system
