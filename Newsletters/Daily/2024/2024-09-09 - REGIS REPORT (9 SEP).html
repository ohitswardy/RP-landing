Meralco (MER PM) found a partner for SPNEC (SPNEC PM) in Terra Solar, which is developing a huge RE project in Central Luzon.  UK-based investor Actis will be investing US$600m in the project company which is developing a US$3.3bn integrated solar-cum-battery, supposedly the largest such project in the world. SPNEC has now effectively been taken over by Meralco / Metro Pacific.

The transaction valuation is not particularly rich, implying total EV of around US$3.7bn.  This compares to the project’s capacity (3,500MW solar capacity + 4,000MWh battery).  Note Terra Solar has a contract with Meralco to supply it with 850MW of mid-merit power beginning Feb 2026 (initially for 600MW) so Meralco is the one providing a lot of value to the project (like the LNG deal with San Miguel (SMC PM) dubbed “Chromite”). We also note that the timeline/s do appear a little tight, though not impossible.

The transaction is significant given its size, with implications beyond just the companies involved. Apart from the incremental RE capacity in the Luzon grid, US$600m of FDI is significant for the Philippines’ capital account.  Banks would also see a nice bump in loan demand from the debt funding needed for this project.

Looking for a mid-cap, off-index, laggard, turnaround story? Rafa thinks Security Bank (SECB PM) might fit the bill. We noted that SECB turned the corner in 1Q24, and the trend seems to have continued into 2Q24. The bank’s consumer/MSME loan surged 37% in 2Q24, accounting for 32% of its total loans, which could make the bank a beneficiary of falling interest rates in the sector, especially given their reliance on rate-sensitive time deposits. The main challenge, in our view, will be managing asset quality.

The bank’s ROE bottomed in 2023, and management seems confident it will break back above 10% in 2025E, with a medium-term goal of returning to the mid-teens. Even with our wariness over credit costs, our new numbers are 20-25% above the Street, which clearly suggests the turnaround is under-appreciated. Despite the stock’s 15-20% bounce from the bottom last week, SECB is still well below its long-term peak. And at 0.34x P/bk and 3.5x PER on our 2025E forecasts, the risk/reward still seems extremely compelling. Our new target price of P160/sh may sound overly bullish (+127% total return, including a 4.2% yield). But at that price, the stock would still just be at 0.77x P/bk and 7.9x PER.

UK-based Actis to infuse P34bn into SPNEC’s Terra Solar project
  • UK-based Actis will inject P34bn (US$600m) for a 40% stake in the Terra Solar project of SP New Energy (SPNEC PM)
    • It is the largest single site solar-battery project in the world; total project cost is ~US$3.3bn
  • The project site will cover areas in Nueva Ecija and Bulacan provinces
  • The project will have 3,500 MW of solar panels and 4,000MWh of battery storage capacity
    • The Terra Solar project has an 850MW contract to supply power to Meralco
      • 600MW by Feb 2026 and 250MW by Feb 2027
      • Headline tariff was P5.70/kwh but we understand that the effective price is likely above P7.00/kwh
  • Meralco (MER PM) currently has a 36% economic interest in SPNEC
    • This could rise to 47% when its loan to SPNEC’s parent can be exchanged for shares of SPNEC
