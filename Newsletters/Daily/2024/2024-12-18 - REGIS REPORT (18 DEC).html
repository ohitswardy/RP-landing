Bloomberry (BLOOM PM) president / COO Tom Arasi made a rather unexpected departure from the firm yesterday. BLOOM disclosed that Arasi, who had been with the firm for eleven years, retired.  Whatever the long-term implications, the abruptness of Arasi’s retirement could cause some anxiety for investors.

The mammoth data center (DC) of STT GDC Philippines (50% owned by Globe (GLO PM)) in Quezon City will begin operations by 2Q25, according to the business head, suggesting a slight delay.  The DC in Fairview QC will eventually ramp up to 124MW IT load, approximately 5x the current capacity.

ICTSI’s (ICT PM) port in Argentina has been given the certification to service neo-Panamax vessels, the only port in the country to have received such. Whether this will matter is still up in the air as ICTSI’s port there is barely operating (<1% utilization) following years of economic turmoil there.

MACRO
BIR confident in hitting downgraded P2.85tn collection target this year
  • The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) stated it is on track to meet its downgraded P2.85tn collection target (+13% YoY)
    • Recall that the agency’s target was downgraded from the initial P3tn target last July to account for unpassed legislation and weaker-than-expected GDP growth
    • Laws that were expected to be passed this year were estimated to have generated P107bn
      • i.e., taxes on single use plastics, pre-mixed alcoholic beverages, sweetened beverages and junk food, as well as the mining fiscal regime rationalization
  • For YT-November, the BIR collected P2.67tn (+14% YoY)
    • Income tax collections rose 9% to P1.41tn
    • VAT collections are up 37% to P622.2bn
    • Excise taxes totaled P284.bn, an increase of 9%
      • With alcoholic beverages and tobacco accounting for P103bn and P131bn respectively
    • Other taxes category amounted to P214.7bn, posting 11% growth
POWER/UTILITIES
ERC denies early start for Meralco, San Miguel’s Masinloc power supply deal
  • The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) has denied the request of Meralco and San Miguel-led Masinloc Power Co. Ltd. (MPCL) to begin their Power Supply Agreement (PSA) ahead of the original Aug 26, 2025 schedule
    • ERC determined that awarding provisional authority for their PSA is “premature”, despite the successful competitive selection process (CSP)
  • Recall that MPCL secured 500 MW of the 600 MW 15-year baseload power requirement that Meralco offered in a bidding last August
    • MPCL offered P5.6015 per kilowatt hour (kWh)
    • MPCL owns the 659-MW coal-fired thermal power plant in Zambales
DATA CENTER
Globe on track to complete first phase of data center in 2Q25
  • ST Telemedia Global Data Centres Philippines (STT PH) is on track to activate the first phase of its STT Fairview 1 data center by 2Q25
    • According to Carlo Malana, President and CEO of STT PH
    • STT PH is owned by Globe (GLO PM, 50%), Singapore firm STT GDC (40%), and Ayala Corp. (AC PM, 10%)
  • Once fully completed by 2026, STT Fairview will have a capacity of 124MW
    • STT PH’s nationwide capacity is expected to reach 150 MW
TRANSPORT
ICTSI’s port in Argentina receives certification to handle neo-Panamax vessels
  • ICTSI’s Tecplata port in Argentina received certification from the Argentine Naval Prefecture to handle docking maneuvers for neo-Panamax vessels
    • Accreditation positions TecPlata as the first terminal in Buenos Aires—and in the entire country—capable of accommodating these large ships
  • Tecplata only handled 8k TEUs in 2023 against Buenos Aires’ total throughput of 634k TEUs
  • Our valuation assumes a negative equity value of P8/sh for Tecplata over its remaining 14-year concession life
