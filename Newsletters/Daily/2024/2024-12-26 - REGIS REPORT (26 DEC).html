REGIS REPORT

26 December 2024

President Marcos’ approval rating fell by 2ppt to 48% based on the latest survey of Pulse Asia (in November). While the decline was modest on a nationwide basis, the drops were sharper among the lowest socioeconomic class (Class E). Meanwhile, Vice President Sare Duterte’s November approval rating saw a sharper drop of 10ppt to 50%, from 60% in September. This was amidst allegations of graft and corruption, as well as death threats against the President. The approval ratings of the President and Vice President could have implications for their political allies in the upcoming mid-term elections next year.  

+++

After some delay, President Marcos is set to sign the 2025 national budget on December 30. It appears that President Marcos will apply line-item vetoes, so among the things to watch out for are:

1) the budget for Education, which was lowered

2) the budget for the Department of Public Works and Highways, which was given an 11% YoY increase in allocation (vs the 2024 budget).

3) the removal of subsidies for Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth)  

+++

The Philippine Competition Commission approved the acquisition of two gas-fired power plants and an LNG terminal of San Miguel Global Power Holdings (SMGP) by Chromite Gas Holdings, which is a JV between Meralco (MER PM) and Aboitiz Power (AP PM). After the transactions, Meralco will effectively own 40% of the assets, SMGP’s stake will be diluted to 33% (from 100%), while AP will own 27%.

+++

Manila Electric (MER PM) said that its electricity sales volume in the 11M period rose 6.6%. This would imply that volumes grew c. 4.3% in October/November, similar to the 4.1% growth in 3Q4.

POLITICS
Marcos, Sara Duterte ratings continue to fall in latest Pulse Asia survey
  • The approval ratings of President Marcos saw a decline by 2ppts to 48% in November from 50% last September
    • The decrease mainly stems from Visayas at 48% (from 52%) and Mindanao at 14% (from 26%). While his approval ratings in Luzon and NCR both stayed relatively stable to 65% (from 61%) and 51% (From 52%) respectively.
    • In terms of approval rating by socioeconomic classes, Class E declined the most to 35% (from 47%), Class D slightly to 51% (from 52%), while Class ABC rose to 39% (from 35%)
  • Vice President Sara Duterte's approval ratings slumped 10ppts to 50% from 60%
    • The significant drop were driven by all three regions from Visayas to 51% (from 71%), Mindanao to 80% (from 93%), Luzon to 40% (from 46%), and NCR to 34% (from 36%)

POWER
PCC approves Aboitiz-Meralco-San Miguel US$3.3bn LNG deal
  • The Philippine Competition Commission (PCC) approved Chromite Gas Holdings Inc. (Chromite) acquisition of two gas-fired power plants and an LNG terminal of San Miguel Global Power Holdings Corp. (SMGP)
    • Chromite is a joint venture of Meralco (MER PM) and Aboitiz Power (AP PM)
    • Approval is subject to certain conditions, including PCC oversight of the Competitive Selection Process (CSP)
    • Other conditions were: appointment of a competition compliance officer; submission of reports on unplanned outages and Competitive Retail Electricity Market (CREM) reports to the PCC; election of independent members on boards of directors
    • Conditions will be in place for five years
  • US$3.3bn deal will allow Meralco to own a 40% stake in the gas-fired plants and LNG terminal; SMC’s stake will be diluted to 33% (from 100%) while AP will own 27%
    • Transaction will be funded through a leveraged buyout, wherein 50% of the funds will come from debt that will be injected into the operating companies

 

 

POWER
Meralco's power sales increased by 6.6% in 11 months
  • Power sales rose to 49,953 GWh, +6.6% YoY given high demand primarily from the residential and commercial sectors
    • Residential YTD sales hit 17,962 GWh, +9.8% YoY, driven by consistent energization of new accounts and organic growth
    • Commercial sales reached 18,719 GWh, +7.6% YoY, given retail, real estate, and hotel accounts expansion
    • Industrial sales are at 13,138 GWh, +1.2% YoY, slightly up from increased demand in semiconductor, F&B, and plastics
  • Meralco’s volumes grew 7.1% in 9M24 (4.1% in 3Q24)
    • This implies that volumes grew c. 4.3% in October/November
  • SVP and Chief Revenue Officer Ferdinand Geluz guided around 53,350~ GWh for the full year, representing 6% growth from 2023
