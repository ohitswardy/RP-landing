REGIS REPORT

07 August 2024

Here’s our results summary for today:

  • Globe (GLO PM) (=) – 1H core income came in at P11.7bn, +18% YoY, which is within Regis expectations but ahead of the Street’s.  Mobile revenue growth in 2Q remains healthy, up +6% YoY but the broadband business is reverting to weakness. Mynt is still showing good numbers, with its 2Q profit contribution now at P1.2bn. This implies Mynt’s 2Q profit was P3.3bn. Some initial detail below and more after today’s briefing.

 

  • DMCI Holdings (DMC PM) (=) – DMCI continued to face difficulties in most of its key markets, causing 2Q24 profit to fall by another 30% YoY to P5.59bn. The disappointing performance, however, appears to be largely expected by the Street.  During yesterday’s management briefing, the company said that it is on track to complete the acquisition of Cemex Philippines (CHP PM) this year, as it is confident about securing PCC approval within the month. Funding for the acquisition will now be sourced from a private placement of convertible prefs, with management reiterating its determination to maintain dividend payments.

  • Emperador (EMI PM) (-) – 1H profits tumbled 20% to P3.8bn as the domestic (Philippine) business continued to be under pressure. Management noted that there was improvement in its international business, however.

 

+++

 

The rise in headline CPI inflation to 4.4% for July is apparently giving the Bangko Sentral (BSP) a reason to hesitate on the rate cut path it was hinting on.  BSP Governor Remolona now says the BSP “is a little bit less likely” to reduce rates this month. He did not rule out an off-cycle adjustment afterwards given that the BSP Monetary Board will not be meeting in Sept.

 

We had already been expecting the CPI inflation number to remain high in July and decline meaningfully only in Aug (and onwards).  The Aug CPI number will only be available in early Sept.

 

+++

 

The country’s trade deficit rose by 9% to US$4.3bn in June.  Imports were down by 7% but exports collapsed 17%.  YT-June, the trade deficit now amounts to US$25bn (down 10%).

MACRO
Headline inflation accelerates to 4.4% in July
  • The Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) reported that headline CPI rose 4.4% in July, accelerating from 3.7% in June
    • Above consensus estimate of 4.1% but came in the lower end of the BSP’s forecast range of 4.0%-4.8%
    • Key drivers were electricity prices (-5.4% YoY in July vs. -13.7% in June), food prices (+6.4% YoY vs. 6.1%), and fuel prices for transport (+6.4% YoY vs. 3.8%)
    • While rice inflation moderated to 20.9% in July from 22.5% in June, it remained elevated; inflation of other food items quickened (meat, fruits, vegetables, and corn)
  • However, unfavorable base effects on rice CPI should start fading by August due to falling Vietnam rice prices and the rice tariff cut last July 5 (see here)
    • As of Aug 6, the Department of Agriculture indicated that prices of imported well-milled rice ranged P51-53/kg and regular milled rice ranged P46-48/kg in NCR
    • The PSA reported that NCR retail prices of well-milled rice averaged at P53.1/kg while regular milled rice averaged P45.88/kg in July 2024
    • In Aug 2023: NCR retail prices averaged P47.06/kg (well-milled) and P42.70/kg (regular)
  • Core inflation eased to 2.9% in July from 3.1% in June, with non-food “core” inflation steady at 3.3%
  • The average 7M24 headline inflation rate stood at 3.7%, within the BSP’s 2-4% target band
  • The BSP said that the latest inflation outturn is consistent with the BSP’s projections that inflation will temporarily rise above target in July but could follow a general downtrend beginning in August
    • Monetary Board will consider the latest inflation outturn and 2Q24 GDP (to be released on Aug 8) in its policy meeting on Aug 15
  • However, BSP Governor Eli Remolona Jr. said the BSP "is a little bit less likely [to cut rates] kasi mataas ng inflation (because inflation is high).” (see here)
    • Statements were made at the sidelines of the official launch of the Financial Services Cyber Resilience Plan yesterday
    • BSP is also "always open" to the possibility of an off-cycle easing, particularly if (GDP) growth is unexpectedly weak

MACRO
Trade deficit widens by 9% YoY to US$4.3bn in June; 1H24 trade deficit down 10% YoY
  • Preliminary data showed that the trade deficit widened to US$4.3bn in June, up by 9% YoY
    • On a MoM basis, the trade deficit was lower than the US$4.71bn deficit in May
  • Exports slumped by 17% YoY to US$5.57bn due to a 24% decline in electronic product exports
  • Imports were at US$9.87bn, down 7% YoY
    • Fuel-related imports increased by 2%
    • However, imports of raw/intermediate goods declined by 10%, while capital goods and consumer goods imports fell by 9% and 7%, respectively
  • Brings 1H24 trade deficit down 10% YoY to US$25bn

TELCOS
Globe 2Q/1H initial comments
  • Net income of P14.5bn for 1H was up1%.  Core income for 1H was P11.7bn, +18%. This is line (or slightly behind) Regis expectations.
  • Mobile service revenue for 2Q was up still up a pretty respectable 6%.
  • Overall service revenue was up only 2%. Apart from continued decline in broadband, Globe also sold a subsidiary (causing a drop in non-telco revenue)
  • Mynt profits are still climbing sharply with 2Q contribution at P1.18bn. This is 20% of post-tax core income.
  • Capex for 1H at P28bn, which puts it in line with guidance of US$1bn this year
  • The Fairview data center is scheduled to be completed by April 2025 (slight delay).  This DC would more than double the current capacity of 22MW

CONSUMER
Emperador 1H24 earnings fall 20% YoY; below estimates
  • Emperador’s 2Q24 net profit rose 19% QoQ (-15% YoY) to P2.1bn
    • This brought 1H24 earnings down 20% to P3.8bn, behind REGIS and consensus forecasts  
  • 2Q24 revenue rose 18% QoQ (flat YoY) to P15.5bn
    • This brought 1H24 revenue down 8% to P28.6bn 
  • Emperador said that QoQ improvements were seen in Asia, Europe, and Latin America
  • However, the Philippine market remains challenging
  • The company will hold a briefing at 10AM today
