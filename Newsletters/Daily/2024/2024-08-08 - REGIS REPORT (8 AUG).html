REGIS REPORT

08 August 2024

We have long list in our results round-up today.  Arguably the most watched names on this list are SM Investments (SM PM) and Ayala Land (ALI PM), both of whom reported respectable numbers.

On the consumer front, Monde (MONDE PM) and Century Pacific (CNPF PM) are seeing margin expansion at the GP level, aided by much improved input costs,  The top line growths of the two, though, provide a stark contrast. We also have some additional detail on Globe (GLO PM), particularly following the deals affecting the ownership of Mynt.

Check out some of those who released numbers yesterday below:

  • SM Investments (SM PM) (+) – SM’s 1H profits rose 10% to P40bn, which is trending ahead of Regis FY24 estimates.  Profits from the two biggest segments (banking and property) were in line with/ahead of expectations. Notable was the drop in residential reservation sales in 2Q, though. The retail business, on the other hand, is seeing a better 2Q after a rather weak 1Q.

For Philippine-based investors>>SM Investments - 1H24: Banking and property lift profits; retail segment improves

  • Ayala Land (ALI PM) (=) – 1H24 net profit grew 15% to P13.1bn, broadly in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. Although operating revenue grew by a much faster 28%, the operating margin fell c. 2ppt due to discounts on selected residential units and quality improvement initiatives in its leasing businesses. Meanwhile, reservation sales were healthy, rising 15% YoY and 5% QoQ in 2Q. For the first half, reservation sales increased 17%, led by the premium segment, which grew 23%.

  • Globe (GLO PM) (=) – Beyond what was already mentioned yesterday, we highlight the net subscriber adds for the wired broadband segment (+23k), likely to be mostly prepaid. The additions, however, were not able to offset the fall in the fixed wireless (FW) business. Globe also revealed that the two latest transactions on Mynt would dilute its ownership to 34.3% (from 36%) and result in a gain of P2.7bn upon completion.

  • Monde (MONDE PM) (+) – Core profit for 1H grew 46% (ahead of expectations) as noodle volumes jumped and gross margins remained wide due to lower commodity inputs. Management, however, is investing the gross margin gains into A&P, limiting the improvement in EBITDA. The consumer still seems to be struggling in 2Q/1H as evidenced by the rather tepid revenue expansion (+3%) in the half.

  • Century Pacific (CNPF PM) (-) – Profit for 1H rose 14% to P3.6bn, a tad behind estimates. Revenues jumped 13% in 1H (much better than most consumer names) but EBITDA rose slightly less (+11%) due to higher A&P spend.  The favorable input cost outlook should keep gross margins high over the near-term.

  • Cebu Air (CEB PM) (=) – Even though 1H core profit of P3.17bn (+4% YoY) is tracking mildly behind Regis estimates due to higher-than-expected financing costs, EBITDA of P13.3bn (+39% YoY) is on trend to exceed our full-year forecast. In 2Q24 alone, revenues grew 15% YoY while EBITDA rose 22% YoY, boosted by pax volumes rising by 10% YoY and cargo revenues jumping 59%. We hope to get more detail during the briefing later at 5pm.

 

  • Megaworld (MEG PM) (-) – 1H24 operating revenue rose 24%, with residential revenue showing particularly growth of 30%. However, 1H24 net profit grew by a slower 9% to P8.55bn, slightly behind REGIS and consensus forecasts. We suspect that the disappointment at the profit level was due to forex losses from its US$ debt.

 

  • Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) (-) – 1H24 profit fell 36% YoY to P1.12bn, trending well behind Regis and consensus forecasts. In 2Q24 alone, profit grew 18% YoY, largely on the back of the spectacular performance of the power unit (power profits up 4x QoQ). The mining and HPAL plants continued to post YoY profit declines and net losses, respectively, during the quarter.  Management is holding a briefing at 3pm today.

MACRO
Commercial banks’ loan growth steady at 10.1% YoY in June; M3 growth mildly accelerates to 6.6%
  • The BSP reported that commercial bank loans grew by 10.1% YoY in June, based on preliminary data
    • This is the same rate of increase in May 2024
    • Loans to production activities decelerated slightly to 8.3% YoY from 8.4%
    • Consumer loans expanded by 25% YoY from 25.6% YoY in May
    • Loans to non-residents grew by 9.8% YoY in June from 8.1% YoY in May
  • Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3 growth) grew by 6.6% in June, mildly faster than the 6.5% in May

MACRO
End-July GIR level rises to US$105.7bn
  • Preliminary data showed the country’s GIR level rose to US$105.65bn in end-July from US$105.19bn in June
    • MoM increase attributed to higher gold prices, higher net income from the BSP’s investments abroad, and higher national government foreign currency deposits with the BSP
  • GIR represents 7.8 months’ worth of imports
PROPERTY
Megaworld 1H24 net profit rises 9%; slightly behind
  • Megaworld’s 2Q24 net profit rose 9% YoY (-6% QoQ) to P4.1bn
    • This brought 1H24 earnings up 9% to P8.55bn, slightly behind REGIS and consensus forecasts  
  • Since the revenue figure was slightly ahead of our forecast, we suspect that the cause of the earnings underperformance was forex losses from US$ debt
  • Residential revenue 30% YoY in 2Q and 1H, outperforming our estimates
  • Office revenue was flat QoQ and YoY
    • Office occupancy was also flat QoQ at 87%
  • Mall revenue grew 18% YoY to P1.5bn, slightly higher than the pre-COVID level
  • The company will hold a briefing at 2PM on August 8 

TRANSPORT
Cebu Air: 2Q24 profit drops 30% YoY but EBITDA up 22% YoY
  • Headline 1H24 profit dipped 5% YoY to P3.55bn. Excluding FX and other one-offs, core profit rose 4% YoY to P3.17bn
    • The profit figure is trending behind Regis estimates on higher-than-expected financing costs
    • Given demand seasonality, 1H24 revenues of P51bn (+18%) appears to be mildly behind forecast while EBITDA of P13.3bn (+39% YoY) is well ahead of forecast
  • 2Q24 core profit dropped 40% QoQ, 30% YoY to P1.19bn, as financing costs jumped 30% QoQ, 68% YoY
    • Revenues rose 3% QoQ, 15% YoY, largely due to higher pax volumes (+10%) and cargo revenues (+59%)
    • EBITDA margin dipped 80bps QoQ to 25.5%, taking EBITDA for the quarter flat QoQ but higher by 22% YoY to P6.67bn
    • Given that the number of aircraft was unchanged QoQ at 87, we suspect the increase in financing costs was driven by other factors
  • Management is holding a briefing at 5pm

MINING
NIKL 1H24 profit drops 36% YoY to P1.12bn, well behind Regis and Street
  • Nickel Asia (NIKL) reported 1H24 profit fell 36% YoY to P1.12bn, accounting for just 27% of Regis and 25% of consensus full-year estimates
    • The disappointment came from lower-than-expected mining ASPs and sales volumes, which more than offset the massive outperformance of its power unit
  • In 2Q alone, profit rose 18% YoY to P914mn, after profits from the power segment jumped 4x QoQ to P279mn, reversing the P65mn net loss recorded in 2Q23
    • The mining unit recorded a 29% YoY drop in 2Q profit, with revenues flat
    • Equitized losses from the HPAL plants were lower by 41% YoY
  • More after management’s briefing at 3pm

CONSUMER
FB 1H24 profit up 6% YoY to P20bn
  • In a limited PR, FB reported its 1H24 profit rose 6% YoY to P20bn
    • We suspect the profit figure provided is before minorities
    • 1H24 consolidated revenues rose 4% to P192.9bn, accounting for 46% of consensus FY24E
    • 1H24 EBITDA grew 5% YoY to P33.9bn, accounting for 48% of consensus FY24E
  • By segment, food and gin drove profits higher but beer margins were lower
  • Management is holding a briefing on August 12, 4pm

CONSUMER
EMI 2Q24 profit falls 15% YoY; 1H24 profit down 20% - Below estimates
  • Emperador’s 2Q24 net profit rose 19% QoQ but was down 15% YoY to P2.1bn
    • Brings 1H24 profit down 20% YoY to P3.81bn, trending well behind Regis and consensus forecasts
  • Revenues were flat YoY for both whisky and brandy segments in 2Q24, after posting negative growth rates for most of 2023 and up to 1Q24
    • Management said that the QoQ improvements should be sustained with 2H likely to be better than 1H; However, we reckon a meaningful YoY recovery could still be elusive
    • Philippine market remains challenging, as it is inundated with cheap products; But long-term premiumization strategy remains compelling both internationally and within the Philippines
    • Company said it is on track with its target of a “balanced portfolio” of 50:50 split between whisky and brandy business by 2025 (vs. 43% in whisky in 2Q24)
  • Gross margins improved by 100bps QoQ to 33% in 2Q but was still down 260bps YoY
  • 2Q24 EBITDA margins were down QoQ and YoY, which could have been due to marketing costs
