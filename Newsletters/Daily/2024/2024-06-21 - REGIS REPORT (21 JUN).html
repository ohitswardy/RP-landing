REGIS REPORT

21 June 2024

The regional wage board for the National Capital Region (NCR), which sets minimum wages for Metro Manila, is set to render its decision on wage petitions by next month. The current official minimum wage for Metro Manila is P610 (US$10.3) per day.

A few months ago, there was concern over a mandatory P100/day wage hike across the country being proposed by the Senate. That initiative seems to have died a quiet death, as we had predicted.

In the case of this proposed hike at the wage board, we expect a more reasonable increase to be the outcome – possibly in the ~5% range.

+++

The law on property valuation reform, signed into law a couple of weeks ago, has been released and its final enacted form is now publicly available.

We took a look at the law (a.k.a. Republic Act 12001), and the key “new” thing is that it imposes a 6% cap on property taxes increases on the first year of implementation (possibly next year or even 2026).  This should limit the impact on property companies (and all other property owners) in the short term. All other major provisions from previous bills were otherwise the same. This should calm the worries relating to this issue.

The balance of payments was in a US$2bn surplus in May, reflecting the US$ bond issuance of the government last month.  As a result, the country’s gross international reserves (GIR) rose 2% to US$105bn.

MACRO
NCR Wage Board to decide on minimum wage hike on or before July 20
  • The NCR wage board is set to release its decision on or before July 20
    • The NCR wage board is expected to set the tone for the wage increases in other regions
  • During its public hearing, the Confederation of Wearable Exporters of the Philippines asked the NCR wage board to delay a proposed P100-750 wage hike
    • Group asked for a one-year moratorium on wage hikes to allow exporters to fully recover and preserve jobs until orders stabilize by 2025
    • NCR’s minimum wage for non-agricultural sector is currently at P610
  • In 2022 and 2023, the government has implemented cumulative wage hikes of +20-22% across regions
    • In NCR alone, the cumulative wage hike was 13% over the same period
    • The cumulative headline inflation rate was 11.8% over the same period

MACRO
BOP reverses to US$2bn surplus
  • The Philippine’s BOP position shifted to a surplus in May
    • Hitting US$2bn and reversing the US$439mn deficit recorded in May 2023
    • A turnaround from the US$639mn shortfall in April
    • Biggest surplus since January 2023 (US$3.08bn)
  • Reflected inflows mainly from the national government’s net foreign currency deposits with BSP
    • Includes proceeds from ROP Global Bonds and net income from BSP’s foreign investments
  • Cumulative BOP position reverted to a surplus of US$1.6bn
    • However, it is 44.3% lower than the surplus in the same period last year
  • In the same period last year, the country’s trade deficit went from US$19.29bn to US$16.27bn from January to April (15.7% decline)
  • Personal remittances rose to US$12.01bn from January to April (2.8% increase)
    • Cash remittances increased by 2.8%
  • BOP position reflects increase in GIR level to US$105bn
    • GIR level is 6.1 times the country’s short term external debt and 3.8 times based on residual maturity
  • Foreign exchange buffer represents an external liquidity equivalent to 7.7 months’ worth of imports
  • BSP raised its BOP surplus forecast to US$1.6bn for this year
  • Projected BOP surplus stands at US$1.5bn
MACRO
BSP official says restrictive policy stance appropriate
  • PH central bank will keep monetary policy settings restrictive to keep inflation in check as peso currency weakens in line with global conditions
  • BSP remains optimistic that macroeconomic conditions would be favorable in 2024 and 2025
    • Inflation would stay within the 2% to 4% target of BSP
  • Central bank’s key policy rate is at a 17-year high of 6.5% after a series of rate hikes last year to tame inflation (14-high peak 8.7%)
    • Inflation fell to 2.8% in January 2024, rose to 3.9% in May
    • Average inflation YTD in May was 3.5%
  • Peso remains to be influenced by the strength of US dollar
    • Said to be largely based on market expectations that the Fed will keep interest rates higher for longer
  • Peso closed at P58.78 a dollar yesterday, down 2.5 centavos from the day prior (P58.755)
    • YTD peso is down by P3.41 from end-2023 close (P55.37) against USD
MINING
Nickel production to remain flat in 2024
  • The Philippine Nickel Industry Association (PNIA) expects the country’s nickel production to remain flat this year due to limited capacity in ore-supplying regions
  • Thus, the industry is pushing for the government to fast-track the approval of mining permits by establishing a “one-stop shop” application process
    • PNIA believes streamlining approvals will attract more investment in mining
  • About 470 applications are currently awaiting approval for exploration of copper, chromite, nickel, and cobalt, according to the Mines Geosciences Bureau
    • Phase 1 of the MGB’s priority list consists of metallic mines, with about 12 projects expected to start operations in the next six months
    • Projects include magnetite sand (Iron) site in Region 2, nickel laterite in Region 3, gold in Region 5, four nickel, copper and gold sites in Region 11, and five nickel, copper and gold sites in Caraga
  • We estimate Nickel Asia’s (NIKL PM) sales volumes will grow by 7% this year and 5% annually in succeeding years
GAMING
Suntrust Resort Holdings secures US$17mn loan for casino project
  • Suntrust Resort Holdings (SUN PM) secured a US$17mn loan from LET Group Holdings
    • SUN is the Philippine unit of Hong Kong’s LET Group  
  • The loan will mature in 10 years
  • The proceeds will be used for the construction of a casino complex in Alliance Global’s section of PAGCOR Entertainment City
  • The Main Hotel Casino will have 475 rooms and a ballroom that can accommodate 550 people
  • It will also have a performing arts theatre and a grand opera house
  • Structural works and the construction of the tower façade up to roof-level has been completed
  • SUN is targeting to open the complex in 1Q25
  • At that time, the casino will have 281 gaming tables, 1,126 slot machines, and 134 electronic gaming tables
    • For comparison, Solaire Entertainment City has about 350 gaming tables and 2,700 slot machines
  • SUN borrowed US$38mn from LET Group in April 2024
