REGIS REPORT

25 June 2024

The government has apparently persuaded rice traders to begin reducing prices by next month, soon after the tariff cut on the commodity takes effect. The reduction in tariffs on imported rice from 35% to 15% will take effect on July 6.

Retailers are reportedly committing to sell rice at no more than P45/kg (equivalent to US$770 / MT).  Depending on the variety and quality, rice currently retails for over P50/kg.

 

The importance of rice cannot be overstated. We estimate retail rice prices will drop ~15% with the tariff reduction and this should result in a ~1.3pt reduction in CPI inflation, all else held equal. Don’t be surprised if headline inflation falls to 2% or even lower by Aug or Sept. When that happens, the case for monetary loosening (policy rate reduction) will be strengthened. The Bangko Sentral (BSP) policy rate today is 6.5%.

Beyond that fact that rice is, mathematically, the single most important item in the Philippine CPI (=8.9%), there’s also no question that it is the largest source of sustenance (calories) for the average person. A drop in rice prices will mean money is freed up for other, more discretionary, expenditures. A rebound in consumer confidence and a re-acceleration in consumer expenditures is likely to follow.

Bloomberg reported that the Indonesian government is blocking local access to gambling websites based in Cambodia and the Philippines, as concerns over their impact grows.  As the online gambling epidemic spreads, the possibility of more governments in neighboring countries lobbying the Philippine government to stop such activities also increases. The POGO “industry”, which specifically targets gamblers outside the Philippines, currently employs hundreds of thousands of workers.

MACRO
Traders commit to lowering rice prices starting July
  • Rice retailers have committed to selling rice at P45 per kilo starting in July following the issuance of EO 62 on June 20
    • EO 62 formally reduced tariffs to 15% from 35% until 2028
  • The effects of the tariff cuts are expected to be felt as soon as possible, ideally before the third State of the Nation Address happening on July 22
  • Rice traders prediction for rice prices in July and August
    • Well-milled rice at P45-46/kg / Premium rice at P47-46/kg
    • This translates to a 12-14% reduction based on the current retail price of P48-55/kg for well-milled rice
  • Before EO 62 takes effect, however, a farmers’ group plans to file a temporary restraining order before the Supreme Court
    • Citied that 500,000 farmers would be displaced amid flooding of imported rice
    • NFA will continue procuring palay from farmers at higher farmgate prices (P23-P30)
    • Unless high court issues a TRO, the DA will abide by the EO 62
  • Foregone revenues resulting from the reduced tariff is projected to reach P80bn
    • Revenues collected from rice tariffs should pay for RCEF’s yearly P10bn requirement based on the Rice Tariffication Law
