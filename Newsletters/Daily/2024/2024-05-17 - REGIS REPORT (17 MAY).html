REGIS REPORT

17 May 2024

The Bangko Sentral (BSP) surprised no one when it held policy rates steady yesterday.  The more interesting takeaway from yesterday’s press briefing was BSP Governor Eli Remolona’s expressed dovishness. The monetary authorities are now looking at possible two rate cuts by year-end, even if the US Federal Reserve does not move before then.  A rate reduction at the Monetary Board meeting in August is now in the cards.

+++

We have a few more 1Q results today, and the tally for our coverage is now almost complete.

 

  • GT Capital (GTCAP PM) (+) – 1Q24 core profit, after removing a P400mn gain from a lot sale and pref divs, stood at P6.57bn, up 2% YoY and looks on track to beat our FY24E but appears slightly behind consensus. Profit contributions from Metrobank and Metro Pacific grew 14% and 52%, respectively, but these gains were offset by base effects at Toyota and lower margins at the property unit. Adjusting for the nuances of the timing of Holy week, vehicle sales for March and April are up a decent +11% YoY but the recent depreciation of the PHP against the USD could drive profitability lower in 2Q. While there could be knee-jerk reaction to the results in 2Q, it is worth noting that GTCAP’s valuations remain inexpensive, trading at a 45% discount to market-based NAV of P1,150/sh and 6x FY24E PER.

  • Nickel Asia (NIKL PM) (-) - Lower global nickel prices drove profits for its nickel ore mining unit and HPAL units lower, resulting in a 79% YoY drop in profit to P202mn. The only bright spot was the power unit, which grew profits by 83% to P67mn, after it completed a 72MW solar plant expansion and benefited from high WESM prices.

  • Filinvest Land (FLI PM) (=) – 1Q24 net profit rose 14% to P845mn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts. Reservation sales rose 10% YoY and 44% QoQ to P5.7bn, thanks to stronger sales of Metro Manila mid-rise buildings. On the other hand, office revenue fell 7% YoY and 10% QoQ due to lower occupancy at Filinvest REIT (FILRT PM). Looking ahead, office occupancy could improve by up to 7ppt in 2Q24 (from 61% in 1Q24) thanks to take-up by government agencies and BPO firms.   

 

  • San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) (=) - The 1Q24 results of San Miguel Food & Beverage show profit to common shareholders rising 6%. Beer sales dipped but hard liquor sales were sharply up.  This suggests consumer wallets are under pressure. The rise in hard liquor sales (+8% by volume, +16% by revenue) stands in contrast to the numbers of the two other operators.

 

With nearly all companies done releasing 1Q results, we can safely say there are more beats than misses this quarter. Compared to Street numbers, we estimate 27% of the PSEi constituents reported results below expectations, compared to 40% which beat them.

Overall, the companies comprising the PSEi (“PCOMP”) delivered an estimated earnings growth of 6% for the quarter.  This looks low relative to our growth estimate (9% for FY24), but we note that it was dragged down by San Miguel Corp (SMC PM).  Without the latter, PSEi earnings growth would have been 12%.

+++

Alliance Global (AGI PM) is subscribing to 1.375bn new shares of Megaworld (MEG PM) in a transaction that could raise eyebrows.  While AGI is buying the new shares (=4% of MEG’s expanded capital) at a modest premium (5%), the fact that it dilutes other shareholders at ~70% discount to NAV is not likely to be seen kindly. Especially when MEG is not under distress.

MACRO
BSP maintains policy rate, signals possible rate cut in August
  • The BSP kept its policy rate unchanged at 6.5%, as expected
  • BSP Governor Remolona, Jr. however said “We are actually somewhat less hawkish than before, which means we could ease (or) cut rates by the third or fourth quarter this year”
    • BSP could deliver a 25bp rate cut at its Aug 15 meeting
    • They are looking at one or two rate cuts in 2H, ahead of the Fed, but are not too worried about the potential impact on the peso
  • BSP lowered its inflation forecast to 3.5% from 3.8% for this year
    • For 2025, it increased its inflation forecast to 3.3% from 3.2%
    • Cited better-than-expected inflation in April (3.8%); YT-April average inflation rate at 3.4%
  • BSP may also consider cutting banks’ reserve requirement ratios once the Monetary Board starts easing interest rates this year
CONSUMER
San Miguel Food & Beverage (FB PM) 1Q24 net profit +6% YoY to P6bn – in line
  • FB reported net profit to common of P6bn in 1Q24, up 6% YoY, broadly in line with estimates. By segment, Beer is broadly in line while Spirits and Food are ahead.
  • San Miguel brewery posted a 4% decline in net income on lower domestic volumes (-6% YoY). Management attributed this to the high YoY base due to trade loading ahead of price increases in March 2023. No price increase was implemented as of 1Q24 but prices may be raised in the latter part of the year.
  • Ginebra saw a 17% YoY growth in sales, driven by an 8% increase in volumes and a single-digit price increase in February. Operating profit grew 40% YoY on lower costs.
  • Purefoods operating profit jumped 79% YoY due to favorable input cost. Segment sales were up only 2% YoY as higher chicken volumes were offset by lower selling prices.
  • FB currently trades at 12x 2024E PE

 

CONGLOS/PROPERTY
AGI to subscribe to 1.375bn new MEG shares
  • Alliance Global will subscribe to 1.375bn new Megaworld shares at P1.90/share
    • 5% premium over the 30-day VWAP
    • Total cost of P2.6bn
  • To “support growth and future business expansions” of Megaworld
  • Megaworld’s shares outstanding will increase by 4%
  • In March 2024, Megaworld’s Board approved an increase in the authorized capital stock from P40.2bn to P45.7bn
    • Previous: P40.14bn common shares (40.14bn common shares at P1/share par value) and P60mn voting preferred shares
    • Amended: P45.64 common shares (45.64bn common shares at P1/share par value) and P60mn voting preferred shares
  • As of 1Q24, Megaworld’s net debt/equity was only 28%
  • We estimate that the transaction will reduce Megaworld’s NAV by 3% to P6.12/share
  • MEG is trading at 71% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 3.3X FY24E P/E
PROPERTY
Filinvest Land 1Q24 net profit rises 14%, in line
  • Filinvest Land’s 1Q24 net profit rose 14% YoY to P845mn, in line with REGIS and consensus forecasts
    • Although pretax profit was flat YoY, the company benefitted from a 6ppt drop in effective tax rate (due to higher contribution from low-cost housing)
  • Residential revenue grew 22% YoY thanks to higher contributions from low-cost housing
    • 4ppt of the 6ppt YoY GPM expansion was due to an accounting change – interest can no longer be capitalized as part of inventory/COGS
  • Reservation sales grew 10% to P5.7bn on higher sales of mid-rise buildings in Metro Manila
  • Meanwhile, office revenue fell 7% YoY (down 10% QoQ) as office occupancy at Filinvest REIT (FILRT PM) fell to 79%, from 83% in 1Q23 and 4Q23
  • At the consolidated FLI level, office occupancy stood at 61%, down 1ppt QoQ
    • The company believes this could rise by up to 7ppt in 2Q24 due to new take-up by government agencies and BPO firms
  • FLI is trading at 67% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY24E P/B, and 5.1X FY24E P/E

MACRO
Gov’t mulls sale of stake in SCTEx
  • The government is looking to sell its 50% stake in the Subic-Clark-Tarlac Expressway (SCTEX)
    • Apart from private firms, such as Metro Pacific, it is also looking at state pension funds as potential buyers
    • Finance Undersecretary Catherine Fong said a government-to-government transaction would be much faster than selling the asset to a private entity
  • Last week, BCDA President said it received an offer of roughly P20bn from Metro Pacific Tollways Corp. (MPTC) for the buyout of its stake in SCTEX
  • SCTEX is currently being operated by MPTC under an O&M contract that will expire in 2043
