REGIS REPORT

17 July 2026

Philippine shares rallied for a third straight week, benefitting from flows rotating out of AI and tech, and despite the sharp rise in oil prices as tensions persisted in the Middle East. For the month so far, the PCOMP along with most Asean benchmarks are outperforming counterparts in developed markets amid mounting scrutiny over elevated valuations on chipmakers.

 

The PCOMP climbed 1.9% or 117.41 pts WoW to 6,404.11, its strongest close since early March, backed by higher trading activity and positive index breadth that saw advancers outnumber decliners by three-to-one.

 

Property led all sector indices higher, as bargain hunting lifted shares of ALI (+6.3% WoW), RLC (+6.1% WoW), and SMPH (+4.6% WoW). Bottom-fishers also bought up recent laggards CNVRG (+7.7% WoW), TEL (+7.5% WoW), and AC (+6.8% WoW), which were the biggest outperformers in the PCOMP this week.

 

Banks were also broadly positive. BPI (+1.0% WoW) kicked off the 2Q earnings season with results that were in-line with forecasts, although concerns over declining asset quality overshadowed strong loan growth and sustained NIMs, ultimately capping gains for the stock this week.

 

Consumer names were more mixed for the week as investors recalibrated inflation expectations with the rebound in oil prices, while defensive plays relatively lagged as the rally broadened out into recent underperformers.

 

Certain corporate stories also stood out: FGEN (+16.4% WoW) surged following reports that Indonesia's Barito Renewables Energy (BREN IJ) is in talks to acquire its subsidiary Energy Development (EDC) in a deal valuing the company at more than $5bn, while SCC (-7.1% WoW) fell amid continued uncertainty over the rebidding of the Semirara coal mine operations. Late in the week, SCC disclosed it filed a petition asking a Makati court to block the DOE's request to disclose detailed asset lists and proprietary equipment data that would be shared with bidders for the Semirara mine.

 

Foreign inflows persisted for a second straight week, though slightly tempered to $14m. ADTO improved to $129m. The peso slightly weakened by 0.1% WoW to 61.588:$1, while the PH10Y yield blipped up 1.9bps to 7.2826%.

Throughput at ICTSI’s Mexico port rises 24% YoY in June; 2Q26 volumes up 23%

Data from Mexico’s port authority showed that throughput at ICTSI’s port, Contecon Manzanillo SA (CMSA), rose 24% YoY to 137k TEUs in June. Brings CMSA’s 2Q26 volumes up 23% YoY to 437k TEUs and 1H26 volumes up 15% YoY to 854k TEUs.

AC Health targets acquisitions, plans to open 3-5 clinics annually

AC Health said it has several hospital acquisition opportunities in the pipeline, with at least one transaction expected to close within the year. The company is targeting Level 2 hospitals with at least 100 beds located in densely populated urban areas. According to AC Health Chief Strategy and Investment Officer Rafael Jaime V. Recio. The company also plans to expand its clinic network by opening three to five new clinics annually over the next three to five years. While its clinics have traditionally been located in shopping malls, the company is also exploring alternative formats, including standalone roadside locations. Over the medium term, AC Health aims to grow its network to 1,150 retail pharmacies, 300 clinics, and 10 hospitals. As of end-2025, AC Health had a network of 900+ pharmacies, 18 multi-specialty clinics and 300 corporate clinics, as well as 7 hospitals (including 1 cancer center). AC Health generated revenues of P12.8bn, EBITDA of P1.5bn and net income of P34mn in 2025. AC hold an 84% economic stake in AC Health, with the balance owned by Temasek-backed fund, ABC Impact.

DigiPlus joins Brazil gaming association

DigiPlus joined the Brazilian Institute for Responsible Gaming (IBJR). Other members include A2FBR, BandBet, bet365, BetBoom, BetMGM, Betsson Group, and Entain. DigiPlus previously said that it was preparing to relaunch its online gaming platform in Brazil.

Bloomberry relaunches online gaming platform

Bloomberry Resorts relaunched its online gaming platform, FUNaloMAX (formerly called MegaFUNalo). FUNaloMAX targets mainstream online players. FUNaloMAX can be accessed via browser (an app is not yet available). BLOOM’s Chairman previously said that FUNaloMAX could hit breakeven in 2027, at the earliest. The company’s Solaire Online remains focused on the group’s luxury casino clientele.

Converge expands existing share buyback program by an additional P5bn

Converge ICT in a disclosure announced that it expanded its ongoing share buyback program by an additional P5bn to P6.5bn. After accounting for shares repurchased since the program began in Sept 2022, P6.1bn remains available for buybacks, equivalent to 9% of CNVRG's current market capitalization. The original P1.5bn share buyback program was first announced in Sept 2022.

SEC approves RLC-RCR share-for-property swap

The SEC approved a share-for-property swap between RCR and Robinsons Land. This was first announced in June 2026. RCR will acquire P10.6bn worth of malls from Robinsons Land in exchange for 1.29bn new AREIT shares. This will increase RCR’s floor area by 7% to 1.3mn sqm. RCR’s shares outstanding will increase by 7% to 20.8bn shares. RCR will acquire six provincial malls with a combined GLA of 160,000sqm. RCR currently offers 6.3% FY26E dividend yield.

Megaworld sells MREIT shares

Megaworld sold 38mn MREIT shares at P13.70/share. Total proceeds of P516mn. This represents a 1.4% discount to the previous closing price. At that price, MREIT offers an FY26E dividend yield of 7.7% (based on consensus forecast). MREIT previously announced that it would acquire 300k sqm of mall, office, and hotel assets from related parties. The acquisition will increase its portfolio by 47% to 950,000sqm. MREIT will issue up to 1.8bn new MREIT shares. This would expand shares outstanding by up to 38%. MREIT said that the acquisition will be DPS accretive.

AGI takes full control of Travellers International

Alliance Global purchased the 40% stake of Travellers International held by the Genting Group. With this acquisition, AGI now owns 100% of its gaming arm, Travellers International. 1Q26 net profit of Travellers fell about 95% QoQ and 70% YoY to c. P30mn.

Philippine Seven more optimistic for 2H26

Philippine Seven Corp (SEVN PM) chairman Victor Paterno, in the company's annual stockholders' meeting, stated that PSC expects a better second half this year on continued momentum from 2Q. He noted that the company's 2Q performance has made management more optimistic, with growth supported by the continued expansion of its payment terminals. Card/QR payment terminal sales accounted for 8% of sales. Paterno also cited expectations of less weather-related disruptions with drier conditions compared to last year as flooding and torrential rains dampened sales in 2H25. Slowing economic growth and rising inflation, however, were cited as concerns that may impact the business. The company's continued expansion in VisMin may provide support, given the region's stronger sales performance relative to Luzon and Metro Manila. SEVN in 1Q26 saw revenue grow 14% YoY to P24.8bn on +4.4% SSSG with profit up 5% YoY to P629mn. The company targets to hit 5,000 stores by year-end from 4,575 as of 1Q. 25% of stores are located in Metro Manila, 45% in Luzon ex-MM, 17% in Visayas, and 13% in Mindanao.

Indonesia’s PT Barito Renewables Energy offers US$5bn to takeover EDC

Indonesia’s PT Barito Renewables Energy has reportedly made a non-binding cash offer to buy geothermal company, Energy Development Corp. (EDC). Offer values EDC at an equity value of more than US$5bn and an enterprise value of US$7bn. "Barito Renewables wants to expand its presence in the country," a company spokesperson said. The talks are ongoing, and there is no guarantee a final deal gets done. The US$5bn valuation is equivalent to a trailing PER of ~40x. EDC (ex-FG Hydro) generated core net income of US$116mn in FY25. FGEN holds a 45.7% economic stake and 60% voting stake in EDC.

Cebu Air’s pax volumes up 3% in June but load factors still soft

Cebu Air reported its pax volumes rose 3% YoY to 2.3mn in June, the first positive growth since the Iran conflict. Domestic pax volumes climbed 6% YoY to 1.8mn on an 18% increase in seat capacity, with seat load factor down 9ppts YoY to 83.1%. International pax volumes declined 9% YoY to 510k as capacity was cut by 19%. Jet kero prices fell ~40% in June from the peak in April but remain materially above pre-conflict levels. Cost pressures remain, limiting the pace of margin recovery, particularly during the lean travel season.

Okada Manila 2Q26 GGR falls QoQ and YoY

Okada Manila’s 2Q26 GGR fell 6% QoQ and 15% YoY to P6.1bn. The decline was led by the VIP business. VIP GGR fell 30% QoQ and 56% YoY to P1.0bn. VIP rolling chip shrank 30% QoQ and 23% YoY to P40.6bn. On the other hand, the gaming machine segment saw an improvement. Gaming machine GGR rose 5% QoQ and 3% YoY to P2.86bn. Gaming machine handle rose 9% QoQ and YoY to P50.7bn.

Remittances rise by 2.0% in May

Cash remittances amounted US$2.7bn in May, 2.0% higher YoY—steady relative to the 2.0% growth in May 2026, which was the lowest YoY growth figure since May 2022 (+1.8%). Remittance growth was primarily driven by Japan (+4.0%), Saudi Arabia (+3.6%), and the UAE (+4.0%).

Gov't mulls measures to cushion impact of rising fuel prices

Palace Press Officer Claire Castro stated that the President alongside the concerned agencies are assessing possible measures to mitigate the impact of rising oil prices given renewed tensions in the Middle East. The Dept. of Energy (DOE) recently said that it is weighing the implementation of fixed weekly fuel price adjustments over the current prescribed fuel price ranges as oil firms were consistently implementing adjustments at the higher end of the range. The Dept. of Transportation (DoTr) is evaluating potential assistance for affected sectors as well as studying pending fare hike petitions. Recall that the DoTr alongside other gov't agencies previously issued fuel subsidies and cash aid during the height of the war. The gov't is also still studying the removal/suspension of excise taxes for gasoline and diesel. If the suspension of the excise tax on gasoline and diesel is approved, previous government estimates suggest it would result in P43.6bn in foregone revenues. Excise taxes are at P10/liter for gas and P6/liter for diesel.

Gov't eyes three major asset sales to meet P101bn privatization target

The Privatization and Management Office (PMO) plans to dispose of three additional major assets before year-end, namely the FTI property (valued at P20bn), the Mile Long property (P10bn), and the government's 20% stake in SLEX, which is currently undergoing valuation. This follows the completed sale of state-owned properties at the Atrium of Makati with 24 condo units and 21 parking slots sold for about P1bn. The PMO also noted that the revised guidelines issued in Mar 2025 spurred more interest in public assets as the gov't was permitted to receive unsolicited offers from the private sector and accredit brokers for privatization transactions. The gov't eyes P101bn in revenues from the privatization of gov't assets.

IT-BPO Association revises 2028 targets

The IT-BPO Association of the Philippines (IBPAP) revised its 2028 BPO sector targets. Revised 2028 revenue targets: US$43.3bn to US$50.5bn. These represent CAGR of 3% to 8% from the US$40bn in 2025. Previous 2028 revenue targets: US$38.5mn to US$58.9mn. Revised 2028 employee targets: 1.85mn to 2.14mn. These represent CAGR of -1% to 4% from the 1.9mn employees in 2025. Previous 2028 employee targets: 1.8mn to 2.5mn. Growth is expected to be driven by global capability centers (GCCs), healthcare, banking, and financial services. IBPAP said revenue generated per worker will become a more important indicator of competitiveness as companies increasingly demand specialized, knowledge-intensive services.

Digital payments up 45.4% YoY in June; PesoNet reaches record high

Digital transactions rose to a record P2,909bn ($47.2bn) in June, up 45.4% YoY, accelerating from 41.0% growth in May. This brought 1H26 digital transactions to P16,090bn, up 44.6% YoY, faster than the 42.0% growth recorded in FY2025. PesoNet transactions climbed 37.5% YoY to a record P1,491bn in June, while its 1H26 total reached P8,113bn, up 31.9% YoY. InstaPay transactions increased 54.8% YoY to P1,419bn in June, bringing its 1H26 total to P7,977bn, up 60.3% YoY. InstaPay transaction volumes surged 96% YoY, while the average ticket size in 1H26 stood at P1,927. Several banks have recently reduced person-to-person transfer fees to zero, while the two major e-wallets lowered transfer fees from P15 to P10. Prior to the fee reductions, banks and e-wallets generally charged P10-15 per InstaPay transaction, with some charging as much as P25-35.

DOE suspends green energy auction for offshore wind projects

The Department of Energy (DOE) has temporarily put on hold the fifth round of the Green Energy Auction Program (GEA-5) to recalibrate the auction framework. GEA-5 involves offshore wind projects. The recalibration seeks to better match auction requirements with actual project development conditions, including infrastructure readiness, regulatory processes, transmission capability, and overall project deliverability.

ERC to tighten oversight on power supply auctions

Under its proposed regulatory framework, the ERC intends to review the terms of reference (TORs) for competitive selection processes (CSPs) prior to bidding launch. The proposed rules would mandate power distribution utilities and the system operator to first submit their TORs to the ERC before publishing invitations to bid. Power utilities will be required to submit a sworn least-cost justification statement, attesting that the TOR parameters neither exclude legitimate bidders nor favor certain suppliers. All concerned energy stakeholders may submit their comments on the proposed rules until July 24, ahead of a virtual public consultation on July 31.

Marcos signs order on Data Sovereignty

President Marcos signed Executive Order (EO) 119 that establishes a “data residency framework” for all government data. The EO mandates that some of the government’s data will need to be stored within the Philippines. Such data includes those classified as “Secret” or “Top Secret”. “Confidential” data should also be stored domestically, with exceptions. Other data can be stored in the cloud. Total government data currently housed in data centers/cloud is estimated to be equivalent to 200-300MW worth of IT load. Domestic data center capacity is currently only around ~130MW. Government agencies (and thus third-party providers) will have three years to transition and comply. This EO will ultimately mean greater demand for local data centers.

Trade Undersecretary Ceferino Rodolfo said the two proposed incentive programs could provide a combined P69bn in tax incentives for qualified vehicle manufacturers. The proposed Electric Vehicle Incentive Strategy (EVIS) would allocate up to P60bn in incentives to as many as four qualified EV manufacturers. The Board of Investments (BOI) is currently drafting the implementing rules for EVIS before opening a three-month application period. According to BOI executive director Ma. Corazon Dichosa, two local vehicle manufacturers are also in talks with the agency and are planning to partner with foreign manufacturers to qualify for the program. Meanwhile, the proposed Revitalizing the Automotive Industry for Competitiveness Enhancement (RACE) program would provide P9bn in incentives to three internal combustion engine (ICE) manufacturers over three years.

10 firms keen on North-South Commuter Rail O&M contract

The Department of Transportation (DoTr) said that ~10 companies are expected to submit bids for the P229bn North-South Commuter Railway (NSCR) 15-year O&M contract. Domestic firms such as San Miguel (SMC PM) and Lopez-owned, First Balfour, attended the pre-bid conference. Japanese firms: Tokyo Metro Co. Ltd (operator of Tokyo Metro) and East Japan Railway (operator of bullet train Shinkansen) are also interested. France’s state-run operator, RATP Group, which manages Paris Metro, is also a contender. The bid submission deadline has been pushed back a couple of times from the original May 2026 target and is now scheduled for end-September. It expects to announce the winning bidder before the year-end. The NSCR is expected to be partially completed by Dec 2027 (Malolos-Valenzuela), with full operation targeted by Jan 2032. NSCR will connect Clark International Airport in the north to Calamba, Laguna in the south, spanning 147km and 35 stations. Construction of NSCR is costing the government P874bn, with funding from Japan.

President Marcos’ net satisfaction rating rebounds from record low in June SWS survey

President Marcos’ net satisfaction rating (% satisfied minus % dissatisfied) in the latest Social Weather Stations Survey (SWS) rebounded from his record low "poor" -15 rating in March to "neutral -7" in June. Marcos’ net satisfaction rating has remained negative since Sep 2025 (-5) after his flood control scandal expose during his State of the Nation Address last Jul 2025. Marcos' gross satisfaction rating rose from 33% in Mar to 38% in Jun. Gross dissatisfaction fell from 49% to 45% for the same period. SWS cited the following movements in Marcos’ net satisfaction rating in all four major areas. +7 points in Metro Manila (from “bad” -31 to "poor" -24). +9 points in Balance Luzon (from “neutral” +2 to "moderate" +11). +12 points in the Visayas (from “poor” -15 to "neutral" -3). +5 points in Mindanao (from “bad” -40 to "bad" -35). Marcos' average net satisfaction deteriorated to -11 in 2026 YTD, continuing its steady decline from zero in 2025, +25 in 2024, +51 in 2023, and +66 in 2022. Survey was conducted via face-to-face interviews with 1,200 adults from Jun 20-29, 2026.
