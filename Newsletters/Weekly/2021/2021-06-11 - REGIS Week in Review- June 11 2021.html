REGIS REPORT

11 June 2021

The Phils rose for a third straight week, gaining 1.6% WoW to close at 6907.79. News flow was generally thin but the direction of fund flows continued to be supportive. 

Foreigners were net buyers, with US$18m flowing through this week. This marked the first successive weekly inflow since October. Top inflows were seen in Jollibee (US$9.6m), Ayala Land (US$9.3m), and BDO (US$5.6m).  ICTSI (US$3.1m) and CNPF (US$2.85m) were also among those with top inflows.  

Meanwhile, the acceleration of vaccine arrivals in the coming months as well as the recent increase in the pace of inoculation kept sentiment buoyant.

Based on the latest data breakdown available, around 2.2m of the 6m doses administered nationwide were in Metro Manila, in line with government’s pronouncements of focusing its vaccination efforts in urban areas.  This translates to around 15% of the Metro Manila population having received at least 1 dose, compared to just 5% nationwide.  As a consequence, new cases in Metro Manila are down to about a 7-day average of 1,000/day or 15% of the nationwide cases from over 50% of nationwide cases a few months ago (as much as 5,500/day). 

Index gainers outnumbered losers 22 to 7. Leading the pack were Aboitiz Equity (+6.1% WoW) and  Ayala Land (+5.2% WoW). Consumer names also led this week, with Jollibee (+4.7% WoW), Universal Robina (+4.4% WoW),  Emperador (+4.0% WoW), and Puregold (+3.6% WoW) all ending with gains. 

Semirara (+2.6% WoW) gained for the fourth straight week as it is seen to benefit the most from the near-term spike in spot prices, selling about 20% of capacity to WESM.  Aboitiz Power (+2.6% WoW) also gained.   

Ayala Land is infusing P15.5bn worth of assets into AREIT (+0.1% WoW) in exchange for new shares

Meanwhile, Al Panlilio officially took over as CEO of PLDT (-1.4% WoW) from Manny Pangilinan.  Panlilio was Chief Revenue Officer of PLDT immediately before taking the CEO role.

Corporate News

Panlilio officially takes helm of PLDT

PLDT Chairman Manual V. Pangilinan has officially relinquished his position as PLDT president and chief executive officer to Alfredo Panlilio at the company’s AGM. Panlilio was Chief Revenue Officer and CEO of PLDT subsidiary Smart. He was previously Meralco senior vice president for customer retail services group prior to returning to PLDT in 2019.

Gokongwei, Gotianun-led venture seals shareholders’ agreement for Clark Airport

In disclosures to the PSE, JG Summit Holdings and Filinvest Development Corp outlined their ownership under special purpose company Luzon International Premiere Airport Development (Lipad). FDC will take a 42.5% stake while JG Summit Holdings will get 33%. Meanwhile, Changi Airports Philippines Pte. Ltd. will get 15%. Jefferson Cheng’s Philippine Airport Ground Support Solutions will get 9.5%. The consortium won the 25-year contract to maintain and operate the Clark Airport in 2018 and assumed operations of the facility in August 2019. The Clark International Airport expansion involved the construction of a new passenger terminal with a capacity of 8.2mn passengers annually, doubling its existing limit.

ICTSI’s Brazil unit to run eco-friendly rail operations

ICTSI Rio Brasil, a wholly-owned unit of ICTSI, formed a new logistics company, IRB Logistica. IRB Logistica will take over the operations of Multitex Logistica starting 1 July. The intermodal facility is located some 150km away from the port of Rio and can accommodate up to 70 train wagons and has a yard for storage and container stuffing. The logistics arm aims to connect Rio, Minas Gerais, and Sao Paolo.

Banks’ NPL ratio hits 4.35%

Preliminary data from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) showed the gross NPL ratio of the banking industry hit a fresh 12-year high of 4.35% in April from 4.21% in March. The NPL ratio was the highest since the 4.37% recorded in May 2009 and higher than the 2.31% recorded in the same period last year. NPLs surged 83.7% to P463bn in April while bank loans slipped by 2.5% to P10.649tn in end-April. Meanwhile, past due loans jumped by 38% to P574.128bn in April from P415.619bn a year ago. The industry restructured loans amounted to P242bn in April, more than 5x the P47.35bn booked in the same month last year. NPL cover ratio at 81.48% while NPA rose by 5.85% to P581.21bn.

AREIT and Ayala Land execute property-for-share swap

Ayala Land will infuse P15.46bn worth of assets into AREIT which would be 206,000sqm leasable area – P75,435/sqm and  in exchange for 483.25mn new shares at P32/share. This will expand AREIT’s shares outstanding by 47%. Ayala Land’s ownership in AREIT will increase from 50% to 66%. This will grow AREIT’s leasable portfolio from 344,000sqm to 550,000sqm and increase its deposited property from P38bn to P53bn.Ayala Land will pay a guaranteed monthly building lease to AREIT for 36 years. We estimate that AREIT’s dividend yield is currently 5.0%.

Citicore eyes P10bn listing of solar plants through REIT IPO

Citicore Power Corp may list its power assets via the country’s first ever energy-focused Real Estate Investment Trust offering by October. Citicore president Oliver Tan said the ipo offering under Citicore Energy REIT could raise P8-10bn. It operates 8 solar plants with an installed capacity of 163MW. The goal is to expand portfolio to 1,500MW by 2025.

 
 

Macro News

May inflation steady at 4.5%

Headline inflation remained at 4.5% for the third consecutive month, within consensus estimates and the BSP’s forecast range of 4-4.8%. Food inflation eased slightly in May as rice prices , fruits, and vegetables declined, offsetting still elevated meat inflation. Non-food inflation (e.g. utilities) accelerated slightly due to higher oil prices. The 5M21 average inflation stood at 4.4%, above the government’s target range of 2-4%.

Another 1mn Sinovac doses arrive

The Philippines received another 1mn doses of Sinovac COVID vaccine doses. The country expects to receive 10.2mn doses in June, including the 1mn doses that just arrived. The government previously expected 11.3mn doses to arrive this month. The country has now received a total of 9.3mn COVID vaccine doses and has administered 5.4mn doses. Shipments of COVID vaccines to the Philippines are scheduled to increase to 15 to 20mn doses/month starting July. The government aims to vaccinate 50%-60% of the population by end-2021.

Around 4% of Metro Manila residents fully vaccinated vs COVID

Roughly 600,000 Metro Manila residents have been fully vaccinated against COVID-19 and around 1.6mn other have received their first dose. The country has now received a total of 9.3mn COVID vaccine doses and has administered 6.0mn doses. The 7-day average vaccinations dropped to 112,000/day, from 144,000/day the previous week. The Philippines expects to receive 10mn COVID vaccine doses in June and 15 to 20mn doses/month starting July. The government aims to vaccinate 50%-60% of the population by end-2021.

ERC looks into outages, high power prices

The Energy Regulatory Commission said it was keeping an eye on generation companies’ unplanned outages and recent spikes in the Wholesale Electricity Spot Market (WESM) amid ongoing rotational brownouts implemented by the National Grid Corporation of the Philippines (NGCP) in Luzon. The ERC has identified generation companies that are not compliant with the reporting requirement – of the 2,083 incidents of unplanned outages this year, only 1,288 were reported. For the 236 incidents of planned outages, 220 were reported.

Unemployment rate inches up in April

Preliminary results of the PSA’s April 2021 round of the Labor Force Survey showed an unemployment rate of 8.7%, inching up from 7.1% in March and cited “temporary reversal” of gains made in previous months due to the lockdown, according to a joint statement of economic managers. The total jobs in April remained above pre-pandemic levels. While the economy lost 3.9mn jobs last April 2020, total jobs generated since was 6.3mn, suggesting net addition of +2.5mn jobs. It could also be lower paying jobs vs pre-pandemic.

Lifting of ban on open-pit mining seen

The ban on open-pit mining is expected to be lifted soon as the draft implementing rules and regulations (IRR) of Executive Order No. 130 have been finalized according to Wilfredo Moncano, director of the Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB). MGB submitted the proposed IRR to Environment Secretary Roy Cimatu. A repealing clause in the draft IRR will lift the four-year ban on the use of open-pit mines to extract minerals. Billions of dollars are expected to flow from the mining industry once the ban is lifted as this will allow two mines to resume operations. US$5.9bn Tampakan copper project of Sagittarius Mines; US$2bn King-king copper-gold project of St. Augustine Gold and Copper Ltd; US$750mn Silangan project of Philex Mining.

Trade in April moderates MoM

Preliminary trade data showed a deficit of US$2.7bn in April, broadly unchanged MoM. The deficit was larger than the US$187mn recorded in the same period last year during the hard lockdown. MoM, exports and imports dropped 16% and 11%, respectively, following the renewed strict lockdown in Metro Manila. The total exports amounted to US$5.7bn in April vs. monthly average of US$5.9bn in 2019 while imports amounted to US$8.5bn vs. monthly average of US$9.2bn in 2019. The 4M21 trade deficit at US$11.1bn vs. US$8.6bn in 4M20 and US$14.1bn in 4M19.

3.2mn COVID vaccine doses to arrive

3.2mn COVID vaccine doses are scheduled to arrive today. 1mn will be Sinovac and 2.2mn will be Pfizer. The Philippines has received a total of 9.3mn COVID vaccine doses and has administered 6.3mn doses. The country is scheduled to receive 10mn doses in June including 1mn doses that already arrived. The Philippines expects to receive 12mn doses in July and 17mn doses in August. The government previously said that 15-20mn doses would arrive monthly beginning July. The government aims to vaccinate 50%-60% of the population by end-2021.

Infra spend drops MoM in April

Gov’t infrastructure and other capital outlays (excluding LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) rose to P58.2bn in April (+45% YoY, -34% MoM). Metro Manila and its adjacent provinces were placed under a stricter lockdown in April, although construction work both in public and private sectors were allowed to continue. This brings 4M21 infra spend stood at P253bn, up 29% YoY. The government is targeting to spend P1.02tn on infrastructure (including LGU transfers and GOCC subsidies) this year, up 17% YoY.
