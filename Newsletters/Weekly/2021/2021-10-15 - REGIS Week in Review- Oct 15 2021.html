REGIS REPORT

15 October 2021

Finally, after more than several attempts, the Philippines managed to close above 7000 this week. The market gained 4.4% to close firmly at 7213.46.

Foreigners were sellers for US$11.3m but ended the last two sessions as buyers as the market broke key 7,000 levels. 

The story this week: buying into banks and property and profit taking in recent market favorites, including consumers. Wilcon (-15.7% WoW) suffered the biggest loss after its recent surge,  followed by AC Energy (-10.3% WoW), Converge (-5.4% WoW), and ICTSI (-1.8% WoW).  Most of the index gained however. Large cap consumer play Jollibee (+12.1% WoW) was the top gainer. The leaderboard was peppered with cyclicals with SM Prime (+9.8% WoW), Ayala Land  (+9.5% WoW), Security Bank (+9.2% WoW),  BDO (+9.1% WoW), Metrobank (+8.2% WoW), and BPI  (+4.9% WoW) all gaining markedly.  Conglos also jumped, led by laggard mid-caps LT Group (+8.5% WoW), GT Capital (+8.5% WoW) , MPI (+8.4% WoW). 

Semirara (+20.9% WoW) shot up again, after recent massive gains fueled by surging coal prices, following its declaration of a special div (P1.75/sh). Parent DMC (+15.3% WoW) surged as well. 

Economic reopening was obviously in play.  Metro Manila’s lockdown classification will be eased from Alert Level 4 to Alert Level 3 from October 16 to 31.  Most business and services will be allowed to operate and max capacities for venues will be increased further. Universities can now have in-person schooling for the first time since March 2020 and “pilot tests”  for primary/secondary levels will start in November.

Meantime. the government is easing travel restrictions for those from Metro Manila, allowing vaccinated individuals to travel on point-to-point basis, regardless of age. The IATF also eased quarantine restrictions for Filipinos and foreigners arriving from the “green lane” countries.

Vaccination rates are once again improving, reaching 469,092/day as of October 10, from 349,511/day as of October 3.  Some 21% of the Philippine population has been fully vaccinated. For Metro Manila, the number is 56%. Daily new case counts have also fallen dramatically --  now at around 9400 (7-day moving ave) vs  the peak of ~21k in September.  It is also down a lot from the 11900 figure last week.

Cebu Air (+11.7% WoW) also gained as an offshoot of the easing.  The airline announced that 10 domestic destinations no longer require expensive RT-PCR or Antigen swab tests for fully vaccinated passengers. 

The Department of Justice (DOJ) plans to indict Megawide’s (-0.2% WoW) senior officers, alleging violation of the Anti-Dummy Law. The charges claim that MWIDE allowed foreign nationals to intervene in the “management, operations, administration of control” of the Mactan Airport. It is unclear what the consequences of the DOJ case might be, and whether it could mean the forfeiture of MWIDE’s contract. Foreigners are not (technically) allowed in management of public utilities, which would include airports. Beyond MWIDE, though, this could have potentially far-reaching implications on other public utilities.

Corporate News

AGI renews buyback program

The board of Alliance Global has authorized a new buy-back program for up to P4bn worth of common shares. The program shall for a term of two and a half years starting October 8, 2021 up to April 8, 2024. A total of 90mn shares worth P883mn were repurchased under the program that just ended (P2.5bn buyback, September 2020-September 2021). A total of 620mn shares (~6% of outstanding shares) are held in treasury arising from prior buyback programs.

Manila Water, partners bag second management contract in Saudi Arabia

The consortium of Manila Water, France-based Saur Group, and Saudi Arabian Miahona Co. sealed the 7-year management contract for the eastern cluster of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The East cluster covers cities of Dammam, Al Hofuf, Al Jubail, Al Khobar, Al Qatif and Hafar Al Bati. Additionally, the East cluster covers a population of 5.27mn and a supply demand of about 1,800 MLD (comparable to East Zone). This is the second management contract secured by the consortium; First contract covers the northwest cluster. MWC holds a 30% interest in the consortium. The contract is a performance-based service agreement (capex requirement will likely be small); revenues will depend on the consortium’s performance against certain KPIs. The impact will be minimal but contract could give MWC an opportunity to participate in a possible auction of water concessions in Saudi Arabia after 5-7 years.

 

Megawide sets 5.3% dividend rate for preferred shares offering

Megawide set the dividend rate for its follow-on offering of Series 4 preferred shares to 5.3%. The dividend rate is slightly lower than our forecast (5.5%) and the dividend rate (5.5%) of its Series 2 prefs issued last November 2020. The offer aims to raise P4bn to refinance existing Series 1 prefs with a coupon rate of 7.025% p.a. The Series 1 prefs has a step-up rate of 487.5bps this year. The offer period will be from Oct 13-19. MWIDE’s parent-level net debt (inc. prefs) stood at P21.9bn in end-June; net debt-to-equity at 2.65x while consolidated net debt (inc. prefs) stood at P46.8bn in end-June; net debt-to-equity at 4.08x.

SEC approves property-for-share swap between Ayala Land and AREIT

Ayala Land will infuse P15.46bn worth of assets into AREIT; 206,000sqm leasable area at P75,435/sqm in exchange for 483.25mn new shares at P32/share. This will expand AREIT’s shares outstanding by 47%. Ayala Land’s ownership in AREIT will increase from 50% to 66%. This transaction was announced in 2Q21 but did not receive SEC approval until this month. ALI and AREIT agreed that income (from these assets) for the entire 4Q21 will accrue to AREIT. This will grow AREIT’s leasable portfolio from 344,000sqm to 550,000sqm and increase its deposited property from P37bn to P52bn. PEZA accredited offices – 165,500sqm – 98% occupied. Vertis North (Quezon City) – 125,500sqm – 97% occupied. Nuvali (Laguna) – 23,700sqm – 100% occupied. Bacolod – 11,300sqm – 100% occupied. North Point (Talisay, Negros Occidental) – 4,600sqm 100% occupied. Makati and Alabang – 1,600sqm – 100% occupied. Retail – Vertis North – 39,300sqm. Ayala Land will pay a guaranteed monthly building lease to AREIT for 36 years. We estimate that AREIT’s 2022E dividend yield is currently 4.8%. The 10-year Philippine government bond yield is 4.8%.

Vehicle sales drop 12% YoY in September

Industry car sales fell -12% YoY to 21,493 units in September according to CAMPI/TMA. On a MoM basis, car sales recovered +36% after quarantine restrictions in Metro Manila were lifted. YT-September industry car sales are up 29% YoY to 191,606 units (vs CAMPI’s FY21E of 295k units, +32% YoY). To reach CAMPI’s target, car sales should average 35k per month for the rest of the year. Toyota sold 9,929 units in September (-7% YoY, +27% MoM). This brings YT-September sales to 92,318 units (+46% YoY). To reach Toyota’s target of 130k units (+30% YoY), Toyota needs to sell 12.6k units per month for the rest of the year. To reach Regis’ target of 117k units (+17% YoY), Toyota needs to sell 8k units per month for the rest of the year. Toyota is a 51%-owned unit of GT Capital (GTCAP PM) and accounts for c. 20% of GTCAP’s profit.

Semirara declares P1.75/sh special dividend; DMCI declares P0.48/sh special div

Semirara declared a special dividend of P1.75/sh, higher than Regis forecast (P1.25/sh) and equivalent to a yield of 7%. The ex-div will be on Oct 20 while payment date is November 9. DMCI also declared a special dividned of P0.48/sh, higher than forecast P0.22/sh

SEC approves Sta. Lucia follow-on offering

The SEC approved the follow-on offering of Sta. Lucia (SLI PM). The deal will have 2.5bn new common shares with an overallotment option of 500mn shares. The price range will be from P2.38/share to P3.29/share. The total gross proceeds could be up to P9.9bn. SLI’s last traded price was P2.88/share and the company has a current market cap of P24bn. As of 1H21, book value per share was P2.36/share. The offer period will run from November 10 to 19 and the listing date will be on November 26. 

AllDay supermarket IPO priced at P0.60/share

The IPO of the Villar Group’s AllDay Supermarket was priced at P0.60/share, lower than the maximum price of P0.80/share. The IPO could raise as much as P4.5bn with the breakdown as follows: 6.86bn primary shares – P4.1bn and the overallotment option – 686mn shares - up to P411mn. As of 1H21, AllDay had 33 supermarkets with net selling area of 55,881 and is targeting 45 stores by end-2022 and 100 stores by 2026. Listing date is set to November 3.

DOJ indicts Megawide, Indian partner execs over anti-dummy law

The Department of Justice (DOJ) has indicted top officials of GMR-Megawide Cebu Airport Corporation (GMCAC) and former Mactan-Cebu International Airport Authority (MCIAA) general manager Steve Dicdican. The DOJ is accusing top officials for violating the anti-dummy law. The case briefer named Megawide chief executive officer Edgar Saavedra, executive director for infrastructure Manuel Louie Ferrer, GMR Group chairman Srinivas Bommidala, along with two other Filipino officials and 10 foreign nationals. Megawide reiterates it followed applicable laws.

10 destinations in Cebu Pacific network no longer require swab tests prior to travel

Bacolod City, Cagayan de Oro, Iloilo City, and Maguindanao are now included in the destinations in Cebu Air’s (CEB PM) network that have simplified travel requirements and do not require RT-PCR or Antigen testing. The other six destinations were Butuan, Cebu province, Guimaras, Negros Occidental, Pagadian, and Tacloban. These destinations only require vaccination cards. Pre-covid, CEB flew to 37 domestic destinations.

 
 

Macro News

Gov’t eases domestic, international travel requirements

The government is allowing fully vaccinated individuals regardless of age from Metro Manila to travel on a point-to-point (P2P) basis. The Department of Tourism will be releasing guidelines for P2P travel. The RT-PCR test requirements would depend on the protocols imposed by the destination. Meanwhile, the government reduced the quarantine days for fully vaccinated incoming travelers from “green” and “yellow” countries.

Pace of vaccinations picks up

As of October 10, the 7-day average vaccinations stood at 469,092/day from 349,511/day as of October 3. Around 21% of the Philippine population (23.2mn Filipinos) has been fully vaccinated against COVID. As of October 7, 56% of the Metro Manila population (7.5mn people) has been fully vaccinated. The Philippines has received a total of 86.5mn COVID vaccine doses and has administered 49.7mn doses (as of October 10).

Trade deficit expands further in August

Preliminary data showed that total external trade rose 25% YoY to US$16.51bn in August, faster than the 23% growth rate in July and a turnaround from last year’s contraction. Exports rose 18% YoY, marking its sixth straight month of growth. Meanwhile imports rose 31% YoY, slightly faster than the previous month (+30%). Trade deficit expanded to US$3.58bn. The 8M21 trade deficit rises 64% YoY to US$26bn.

Metro Manila eases to Alert Level 3 until end-October

Metro Manila’s quarantine classification will be eased from Alert Level 4 to Alert Level 3 from October 16 to 31. Most business establishments, facilities, and services are allowed to open at varying degrees. Indoor venues allowed to operate at a max capacity of 30% for fully vaccinated; 50% outdoor. Additionally, cinemas are now allowed to open. Pre-COVID, cinemas comprised c. 10% of mall revenue. The government also released quarantine classifications for the rest of the Philippines, with restrictions for most cities/provinces eased.

Gov’t drops quarantine for fully vaccinated travelers from “green” countries

Fully vaccinated Filipinos and foreign nationals traveling from ‘green’ countries or territories tagged as low risk for COVID-19 will no longer be quarantined upon entry to the Philippines beginning Oct. 14. A negative RT-PCR test taken 72 hours before arrival is the only requirement; Filipinos can also choose to take the test upon arrival and quarantine until results are out. Unvaccinated or partially vaccinated individuals shall undergo a facility-based quarantine until the release of a negative RT-PCR test taken on the fifth day. However, inbound tourism still not allowed. Green countries as of October 8 include Mainland China and New Zealand, among others. Other key countries are not included in the list: South Korea, UK, US, Japan.
