REGIS REPORT

02 July 2021

The PSEi gained 0.7 % this week to close above the 7,000 level for the for the first time since Feb 16. 

Underpinning the positive sentiment was the continued improvement in vaccinations.  The country has crossed the 10m mark in administered doses as of last weekend and the government is now aiming to administer 8-10m jabs within July.  This translates to roughly 300k doses per day and at that pace, the country would hit ~65m doses by year-end (vs population ~110m).  The pace of inoculation has scope to pick up however with the government expecting to receive 13.3m doses in July and 13.6m doses in August.

The government announced minor changes in the lockdown statuses for the various regions across the country for the period July 1-15. Metro Manila (NCR) will stay under the same lockdown classification (“GCQ”).

Gainers outnumbered decliners 19:11. 

Large caps SM (+2.3% WoW) and URC (+3.6% WoW) helped push the market higher – contributing 26 and 10 index points to the market’s overall 52-point gain for the week.

Taal Volcano elicited some concern after the government raised the alert level for the volcano to level three in the wake of a small eruption. Taal is located around 60km south of Manila, and its eruption last led to  temporary disruption in operations of many industries and also sales of properties around the area. Calabarzon region, where Taal is located, accounts for ~15% of Philippine GDP.

A couple of news developments came from the banks.

Japan’s second largest bank Sumitumo Mitsui is taking a 5% stake in Rizal Commercial Bank (+26.9% WoW), acquiring the stake for ~P44/share (book value).

Meanwhile, President Duterte signed an order directing the government-owned Landbank to take over and merge with the United Coconut Planters Bank (UCPB). The merge will create a bank with P2.7trn in assets, fortifying Landbank’s position as the second largest bank in the country.

The steady  newsflow  for REITS continued.  Nine  groups are reportedly planning to spin off assets into REITs. Among them, Citicore plans to spin off its solar farm as a REIT. The company is controlled by the principal behind Megawide (+4.2% WoW). As potentially the first non-property REIT, its listing would be significant and could pave the way for telecom towers, toll roads, and even power plants to follow suit.

PLDT’s (+3.0% WoW) digital innovation arm Voyager is raising US$167m in new funds from both existing investors and one new one. The company intends to use the funds to further grow its fintech business to include credit, investments and insurance, similar to its rival Mynt (GCash).

It was another week of foreign selling, with net outflows at US$82m. Outflows were lighter WoW stripping out the Monde (+10.8% WoW) greenshoe. The market saw inflows on two out of the five trading sessions.

PUBLISHED RESEARCH
Monde Nissin - Seeking ‘alpha’; initiating with a Buy
 
 
by Carissa Mangubat +632 8894 6647 carissa.mangubat@regis.ph
 

Seeking ‘alpha’

We initiate coverage of Monde Nissin (MONDE) with a Buy and target price of P18.50/sh. MONDE is a unique company with two distinct businesses: (1) a solid, highly cash-generative domestic consumer business, and (2) an international operation aiming to capture the exponential growth in meat alternatives. The first of these is APAC Branded Food and Beverage (BFB). It is the market leader in instant noodles and biscuits and is, in our view, an attractive play on the Philippine consumption story with healthy growth and steady free cash flow. The second is a business that is likely to become a key player in the rapidly-growing global meat alternatives industry: Quorn, which is already profitable today. At 27x PER and 16x EV/EBITDA for 2022E, we believe MONDE is attractively valued relative to its peers in the broader consumer space and the meat alternatives sector.

APAC BFB: Market leader in a resilient segment

MONDE’s APAC BFB business offers attractive exposure to the growing Philippine consumer sector. This operation produces high returns on capital (c.40% from 2018-20) on the back of its leading position in instant noodles and biscuits. We expect the current, fragile consumer environment to favor market leaders with familiar brands, leading to market share consolidation.

Meat Alternatives: Investing in growth

Increased environmental and health awareness globally is feeding the rapid growth of the meat alternatives industry. MONDE’s 100%-owned Quorn is looking to take on the challenges of capturing this growth with several key strategic initiatives. These include strengthening its position in its home market (UK), tapping the faster-growing US market, and expanding its presence in food service. Amidst an industry attracting new entrants, we believe the short-term cost of additional investments in marketing and capacity expansion will yield the fruit of a greater share of a global market growing by 35% CAGR over 2020-29F.

18% net profit CAGR for 2020-23E

We are forecasting 18% earnings CAGR for 2020-23E for MONDE. For top line, we expect Quorn to drive most of the growth (+27% CAGR), though this will come at the expense of margins as it invests in additional capacity and marketing. Meanwhile, we expect the APAC BFB business to grow operating profit by 7% CAGR in 2020- 23E.

Initiating with a Buy, TP of P18.50/sh; key downside risks

Our target price is P18.50/sh, which is based on a sum-of-the-parts valuation of MONDE’s two distinct business units. APAC BFB is valued using a DCF methodology (12% COE) implying 27x 2022E PE, broadly in line with peers. For Quorn, we apply a 2022E EV/sales multiple of 3x, at a discount to Beyond Meat (BYND US). Key downside risks relate to competition and execution

 
PUBLISHED RESEARCH
ICTSI - All aboard! (part 2)
 
 
by Klyne Resullar +632 8894 6645 klyne.resullar@regis.ph
 

Raising TP to P200/sh, Buy

We upgrade our price target by 38% to P200/sh after lifting our earnings forecasts sharply (+26-27%), following the company’s strong 1Q21 operational performance, optimism on the global vaccine rollout, and the accretive impact of the CREATE Law. The shares are up 31% YTD and are at all-time highs, but at 9.7x/8.6x FY21E/FY22E EV/EBITDA, multiples remain severely discounted relative to peers (15x/13x) and historical average (13x). Maintain Buy.

Another round of upgrades; Consensus estimates still too low

Our revised estimates assume volumes will grow by +11% over the rest of 2021, faster than the +8% growth rate in 1Q21. We also hiked our yield per TEU assumptions by 5-8% and now expect yields to further rise to US$164 in the last three quarters of 2021 from US$161 in 1Q21. Our profit estimates are now the highest on the Street and we believe consensus upgrades will follow.

Free cash flow rising; Potential dividend hike

We estimate free cash flow (after payment of leases and concession fees) will rise to c.US$700mn by 2023 from US$395mn in 2020, as capex remains low relative to history. The CREATE Law also removed the 30% tax on repatriated capital from overseas investments, which could potentially raise its payout. Our forecasts conservatively assume a payout ratio of 80%, which translates to a low div yield of 3-5% relative to its FCF yield of 7-10%.

Revised price target of P200/sh offers 28% total return

We upgrade our SOTP NAV-based TP by 38% after (a) raising yield per TEU assumptions, (b) mildly increasing volume forecasts, (c) lowering the effective tax rate for its Philippine ports, (d) incorporating newly-acquired ports in Nigeria and Manila, and (e) rolling over our DCF to 2022. Our revised TP of P200/sh offers a 28% shareholder return and implies an FY22E EV/EBITDA multiple of 10x, still below historical average of 13x. Downside risks relate to trade, dilutive acquisitions, and labor issues.

 
 

Corporate News

Megawide eyes 2 contracts, P100bn order book

Megawide expects to secure one contract worth P20bn by December and another by early next year, bringing its order book to P100bn according to Megawide Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Edgar B. Saavedra. The company intends to participate in 3-4 packages of the Metro Manila Subway project and another 3-4 packages of the North-South Commuter Rail-South Line project. MWIDE’s order book stood at P66.1bn in end-March 2021.

ALI to spend P90bn for South Coast City development

Ayala Land plans to spend almost P90bn for the development of a mixed-used estate a the South Road Properties in Cebu City. In a press briefing, ALI said that land development of South Coast City is underway and is set to be completed in May 2022. The 26-hectare waterside project is being developed by a consortium of SM Prime, Ayala Land and Cebu Holdings. The P90bn would be spent by ALI alone.

Landbank taking over UCPB

Land Bank of the Philippines and United Coconut Planters Bank are set to merge, with Landbank as the surviving entity. Under Executive Order No. 142 signed on June 25, President Duterte ordered the two banks to merge. The move is seen to solidify Landbank’s position as the second largest bank in the country in terms of resources, with combined assets totaling P2.7 trillion. The two banks were directed to work on the merger within six months after the signing of the EO.

Sumitomo Mitsui to take 5% stake in RCB

According to Nasdaq, Japanese banking group Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group (SMFG) will invest JPY10bn (roughly PHP4.4bn ) to take a 5% stake in Rizal Commercial Banking Corp (RCB PM). Assuming this will be in the form of new shares, we estimate the price of the transaction to be about P43.19 per share, or just shy of RCB’s BVPS of P45.46/sh at 1Q21. That is more than double the current share price of P19/sh. If the purchase will indeed be new shares, then the transaction will increase RCB’s capital by 5% to 92bn, which would take its Solo CET-1 from 11.6% to around 12.2%.

Voyager raises US$167m, applies for digital banking license

Voyager raised US$167m in new funding from existing shareholders, including PLDT (TEL PM), as well as a new investor (a fund managed by IFC Asset Management). Voyager is the holding company for a number of investments of PLDT in the digital/fintech space. It holds Paymaya, which is the e-wallet and payment solutions platform of the group. PLDT’s stake will be diluted to 38.5% following this latest funding round. Funds raised will be used to expand its Paymaya services, which will include credit, insurance, savings and investments to be done through a new digital bank under Voyager. Paymaya’s main competitor on e-wallets, Mynt/GCash, currently has no digital banking license. The latter only has remittance and e-money licenses. GCash offers lending, investments and deposit accounts through various partners.

9 firms eye REIT offering

A total of nine companies are looking to do a real estate investment trust (REIT) offering in a bid to maximize the value of real estate assets according to Leechiu Property Consulstants. During the Villar Group Convention, David Leechiu said some companies looking to do their REIT offerings have yet to announce their plans, but some of these firms already have sponsors that are publicly listed. Among the nine companies are Filinvest Land, Villar Group, Megaworld, Robinsons Land, SM Prime Holdings and Megawide sister company Citicorp. Ayala Land and Double Dragon already have REITS.

 
 

Macro News

BSP records US$417mn ‘hot money’ inflow in May

Foreign portfolio investments (FPI) or ‘hot money’ yielded a US$416.74mn net inflow in May. This is a reversal of the US$1.006bn net outflow in May 2020 and US$373.95mn net outflow in April 2021. Gross inflows in May ballooned to US$1.458bn (vs. US$651mn in April 2021 and US$486mn in May 2020). Meanwhile, gross outflows dropped 30% YoY to US$1.041bn (vs. US$1.025bn in April 2021). Around 67.9% of the for FPIs during the month went mainly to securities listed in the Philippine Stock Exchange while the remaining 32.1% went into investments in government securities. For 5M21 ‘hot money’ outflow at US$441mn from US$3.1bn outflow last year.

First batch of Moderna vaccines arrive

Around 249,600 doses of Moderna vaccines arrived recently and another 1mn doses of Sinovac vaccines also arrived. This batch appears to be ahead of schedule. The Philippines has now received a total of 17.5mn COVID vaccine doses and has administered 8.9mn doses. The country has so far received 9.2mn vaccine doses in June and is still scheduled to receive another 2mn AstraZeneca doses within the month. The Philippines expects to receive 11.7mn doses in July and 17mn doses in August. The government aims to vaccinate 50%-60% of the population by end-2021.

NCR Plus: Metro Manila, 6 neighboring provinces stay under GCQ till July 15

Metro Manila and its close provinces will remain under general community quarantine with restrictions starting July 1 till July 15. Laguna and Cavite will be under “GCQ with heightened restrictions” until July 15.

Government targeting to administer 8mn to 10mn doses in July

The government aims to administered 8mn to 10mn COVID vaccine doses in July. This would be equivalent to 258,000/day to 323,000/day and faster than the current 237,000/day. The Philippines has now received a total of 17.5mn COVID vaccine doses and has administered 10.1mn doses. The country has so far received 9.2mn vaccine doses in June and is still scheduled to receive another 2mn AstraZeneca doses within the month. The Philippines now expects to receive 13.3mn doses in July and 13.6mn doses in August from the previously expected 11.7mn in July and 17mn in August. The government aims to vaccinate 50%-60% of the population by end-2021.

Philippines to launch dollar bond issue

The Bureau of Treasury will be offering dollar bonds with tenors of 10.5 years and 25 years at benchmark sizes, or around US$500mn for each tenor. Initial price guidance is set around the level of Treasuries plus 90 basis points and 3.55% for the 10.5-year and 25-year bonds. The government is planning to raise a total of US$7bn (P340.5bn) from the international debt market this year. So far, they have raised US$500mn in a yen-denominated Samurai bond issue in March and US$2.5bn worth of euro-denominated notes in April. As at end-March, the national government’s debt-to-GDP stood at 60.4%, up from 54.6% in end-2020 (vs FY21E target of 57.8%).

Bank lending contracts by 4% in May

Outstanding loans of universal and commercial banks (U/KBs), net of reverse repurchase (RRP) placements with the BSP, fell by 4.0% YoY in May. Mildly improving from the -5% in April and marginally up (+0.2%) MoM. Most loans “for productive purposes” were up MoM, with construction loans already up 2.8% YoY. Notably, though, auto loans were still down 2% MoM, and the YoY decline accelerated to -13.8% YoY from -12.4% in April. Meanwhile, domestic liquidity (M3) rose by 4.7% in May, slower than the 5.2% recorded in April.

PSE Index August 2021 Rebal Forecast: ACEN (+), EMP (-)

We forecast the addition of AC Energy (ACEN PM) into the PSE Index for the mid-August 2021 rebalancing. Among the eligible stocks, ACEN is within the top 24 in terms of full market cap (based on 12-month VWAP). Typically, the PSEi constituent with the lowest market cap would be removed – DMCI Holdings (DMC PM). However, we expect Emperador (EMP PM) to be removed instead because it did not meet the liquidity requirements. In order to be eligible, a stock must rank among the top 25% in terms of median daily trade per month in 9 out of the 12-month period in review. We estimate that Emperador only ranked within the top 25% in 8 of the 12 months. Official announcements for the rebalancing results will be made by the PSE a few days prior to implementation.

Unemployment rate sustains drop in May

Unemployment rate fell to 7.7% in May from 8.7% in April but is still higher than in Jan ’20 (5.3%). The size of the labor force increased by 1mn MoM and 3.5mn since Jan ’20. Employment in most sectors have improved since Jan ’20, except for manufacturing.
