REGIS REPORT

18 June 2021

The Phils broke a three-week winning streak, inching down 0.8% WoW to close at 6851.38. 

The national government extended the quarantine status (GCQ with restrictions) of Metro Manila and surrounding provinces but it did reduce some restrictions specifically for Metro Manila and Bulacan. Curfew hours have been shortened (12mn-4am from 10pm-4am previously).

The pace of vaccinations is now at around 1m jabs a week and around 7.6m doses have so far been administered. The Phils has now received a total of 14.2m vaccine doses.  In total, around 10.2m doses are expected in June, accelerating to 11.7m does in July and 17m doses in August.

While new daily COVID  cases continue to stay in the 6,000-7,000 range, the 7-day average case count in the capital has dropped to below 1,000/day, around 15% of the country total. This has come off from around 60% of total cases at the height of the recent surge in March.  The lower case counts in the capital points has raised hopes of potentially faster reopening of the economy.

The FTSE rebal this week had the market expecting US$40m in outflows but net selling related to the rebal saw lower outflows of US$18m. Foreigners were sellers for US$139m for the week, driven mostly by outflows from Ayala Land (-6.0% WoW) blocks. Excluding those blocks, foreigners were marginal seller for around $3m -- primarily selling out telcos, power, and consumers, while buying banks.

Corporate news continued to be light.

GT Capital’s (+1.8% WoW) auto unit Toyota saw a nice 25% MoM (+344% YoY) rebound in  unit sales in May. Sales for the first five months are up 87% YoY to over 52536 units. 

Cebu Air (+4.6 WoW) gained amidst news that rival Philippine Airlines (PAL) is about to finalize its restructuring plan with regards to its Chapter 11 filing. The plan will apparently involve new funding of US$630m (mostly from a ‘major stockholder’). A rival with potentially reduced capacity would benefit operators like Cebu Air.

In the meantime, a couple of potential listings hit the news. Megaworld (-3.1% WoW)  is planning a to REIT a portion of it office property assets. Robinsons Land (-3.3% WoW) and Filinvest Land (unch) have also announced plans to list REITs.

Meanwhile,  Globe’s (-1.4% WoW) 917Ventures said it is considering listing GCash.  GCash is operated by Globe Fintech innovations (Mynt), a venture led by Globe and Ant Financial. The mobile wallet is one of the largest in the country with over 40 million users and targeting to handle P2trillion transactions this year.

PUBLISHED RESEARCH
Philippine Strategy - Glass half full – buying the recovery story
 
 
by Rafael Garchitorena +632 8894 6644 rafael.garchitorena@regis.ph // Gio Dela Rosa, CFA +632 8894 6642 gio.delarosa@regis.ph
 

Vaccine roll-out accelerating, Metro Manila already at c12%

Despite the bounce, the PSEi remains among the worst performing markets in APAC, dragged by recent lockdowns, and inflation concerns. But after a fitful start, the country’s Covid-19 vaccination effort is picking up. As of 08 June, 6.3mn doses had been administered, and the pace has risen to about 1mn jabs a week. While just 4% of the country has received at least one shot, we reckon Metro Manila is already at c12%. And vaccine arrivals should rise sharply from June. While risks remain, investors seem to have discounted the worst. We see the glass half-full and would begin positioning for the eventual recovery in 2022.

PSEi bounces 12% from lows; but still down 3% ytd

The Philippines has been “un-loved” for much of 1H21. As recently as 24 May, the index was down 14% ytd, among the world’s worst performing markets. Despite a recent surge, the PSEi is still lagging the region. Mobility slumped post-holiday season due to continued restrictions. Inflation spiked, pushing 10-year yields up as much as 150bps. Then a spike in Covid-19 cases saw Metro Manila go into lockdown in April. All of which put 2021E GDP and earnings expectations at risk.

 

Vaccine rollout gaining momentum, to accelerate in June and 2H21

The Philippines got off to a slow start in its Covid vaccination drive, with delays in ordering, and seeming lack of a distribution plan. For March and April, for example, a total of just 3.5mn doses arrived, and less than 2mn doses were administered. As of 08 June, those numbers shot up to 9.3mn arrivals, and 6.3mn doses given, with the pace rising to 1mn jabs per week. Crucially, about 1.6mn (nearly 12%) of Metro Manila residents have received at least one shot of a vaccine as of 06 June. The pace should accelerate further, as the government expects 10mn more doses to arrive in June, rising to 12-20mn/month in 2H21.

Position for 2022 recovery – restaurants, cyclicals, tourism

Granted, inflation and mobility risks remain, and vaccinations will likely fall far short of the 70% goal by end-2021. But we see the glass as half full. Atthe current pace of inoculation, it is increasingly likely that the economy can gradually reopen, making the 2022 recovery more apparent. We would build positions in “reopening plays” like restaurants (JFC, PIZZA), consumer discretionary (WLCON, RRHI, GTCAP), cyclicals (banks/property), and tourism (CEB, BLOOM). Outside of these, we like stock-specific themes at ICT, CNPF and AC.

Foreign positioning is extremely light; a sustained turn could provide a kick

Is the market bounce sustainable? Foreigners have been net sellers for $1.4bn so far this year, for a running total of $6.2bn since 2018. Foreign ownership of PSE-listed stocks is at a decade-low 22% as of May. But in a market like the Philippines, a sustained turn in sentiment could be a powerful market catalyst. The PSEi is already up 12% in the last 3 weeks on a few days of foreign buying.

 
 

Corporate News

MPI remains keen on Sangley airport

MPI bought bid documents for the re-bid of the US$10bn Sangley airport project. MPI is “interested in principle but no commitments at this time”, according to MPI chairman, Manuel Pangilinan. MPI also purchased bid documents in the first auction of the project but did not submit a bid. The Cavite government has moved the deadline for purchasing bid documents to June 28 (previously March 30). The consortium of Macroasia (MAC PM) and China Communications Co. Ltd. (CCCC) won the bid but the project was canceled last January 2021 due to “infirmities” in the required documentations.

Dito racing to complete 2nd year audit targets

In a briefing last Friday, Dito Telecommunity chief technology officer Rodolfo Santiago said the company had over 3,000 cell sites and opening in 23 new areas by Saturday. By end of June, it claims it will be in approximately 150 cities and municipalities. It is aiming to reach a million subscribers after connecting about 890k as of Thursday last week. Dito has committed to cover 51% of the population and offer minimum after internet speeds of 55mbps for its next annual review in July.

PAL’s Chapter 11 filing seen pushing through this month

PAL’s Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing may push through this month, after the expected filing for bankruptcy protection by the end of May did not take place. The negotiations with its lessors continue; Lessors are now at the documentation stage ahead of the filing. According to PAL lessors interviewed by Cirium, PAL is eyeing to raise debtor-in-possession financing of US$505mn, broken down into US$250mn of debt and US$255mn of convertible debt. An exit financing of about US$150mn is also being sought by the airline.

Globe warns against modems sold as DITO’s

Globe Telecom said on Monday that some establishments are illegally reselling and passing off its Globe WiFi modems as DITO Telecommunity modems. Globe said it is prepared to take the necessary legal actions against these establishments and individuals carrying out these deceptive business practices. DITO chief administrative officer Adel Tamano said that there are no official DITO modems or pocket WIFI devices that have been launched at this time. He added that the unauthorized use of DITO branding in any form shall result in the possibility of prosecution for intellectual property infringement.

EastWest eyes Citi’s retail banking assets

EastWest Banking Corp is interested to join the bidding for the Philippine consumer and retail banking assets of Citi. Josephine Gotianun-Yap, President of Filinvest Development Corp, said in a forum that the bank is always on the lookout for M&A opportunities to break into the top 10 largest banks in the country. EastWest had P386bn in total assets as of end-2020 (11th) while Citi was 12th largest with P331bn, total loan book of P154bn and deposits of P215bn

Citi has about 1 million credit card customers.

Megaworld to raise up to P27.3bn from REIT listing

Megaworld plans to raise up to P27.3bn from the sale of secondary shares in MREIT from 1.24bn shares with a price up to P22/share which includes an overallotment option. This also represents 49% of shares outstanding. The assets in the REIT will be 10 office buildings with GLA of 225,000sqm. Properties in Eastwood (Quezon City), McKinley Hill (Fort Bonifacio, Taguig), and Iloilo Business Park. Kevin Tan, Megaworld Chief Strategy Officer and MREIT President, said the tenants were “superior” BPOs, no POGOs. At the maximum price, market cap of MREIT would reach P55.7bn. We estimate that at the maximum price, MREIT would offer a dividend yield below 5%. This could raise our MEG NAV estimate by c. 5%-10%. If we were to lower our assigned cap rate on Megaworld’s entire 1.4mn sqm office portfolio by 1ppt (to 6%), then our MEG NAV estimate would rise by c. 10%. MEG is currently trading at 7.4X FY21E P/E, 0.6X FY21E P/B, and 57% discount to NAV

GCash eyes stock market debut

Managing Director Vince Yamat of Globe Telecom’s 917Ventures said in a media briefing it is considering listing GCash on the stock exchange through an initial public offering. GCash is operated by Globe Fintech innovations (Mynt), a venture led by Globe and Ant Financial. The mobile wallet is one of the largest in the country with over 40 million users and targeting to handle P2trillion transactions this year. Mynt raised $175mn at the start of the year from new and existing shareholders in a deal valuing it at approximately $900mn.

PAL parent on 'final stages' of restructuring

PAL Holdings said it is now on the “final stages” of its restructuring plan to survive the pandemic. The group has not made principal and/or interest payments due in respect of the above long-term obligations since April 2020, resulting in breach of certain loan covenants and default provisions.

It expects a funding of up to US$505mn from a “major stockholder”, plus an exit facility amounting to US$125mn. The US$505mn fund raising may involve sourcing of the fund by the major stockholder from the government and private financial institutions, estimated to be around US$250mn. Fundraising plan will be on top of the US$358mn already provided by the major stockholder from 4Q19-1Q21

In FY20, PAL’s after tax net loss to commons widened to P71.9bn from P10.3bn previously. The company remains optimistic that it will be able to continue operations in the next 12 months.

It expects to generate “positive monthly operating cash flows” this year on the back of a gradual recovery in the travel industry. In 1Q21, operating cash outflow stood at P5.1bn (vs. CEB’s 1Q21 operating cash outflow of P2.4bn).

As at end March 2021, PAL’s total debt (incl. leases) stood at P197bn (~US$4bn).

EO silence on open-pit mining seen delaying DENR approval

The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) is still studying whether it can lift the open-pit mining ban, according to Presidential Spokesman Herminio L. Roque, Jr.

Executive Order (EO) No. 130, signed by Mr. Duterte on April 14, lifted the nine-year moratorium on new mineral agreements but did not have a provision lifting the ban on open-pit mining. The draft IRR was submitted to the DENR secretary early this month, which included a repealing clause that will lift the four-year ban on the use of open-pit mines to extract minerals. According to Wilfredo Moncano, director of the Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB)

Billions of dollars are expected to flow from the mining industry once the ban is lifted as this will allow two mines to resume operations:

US$5.9bn Tampakan copper project of Sagittarius Mines

US$2bn King-king copper-gold project of St. Augustine Gold and Copper Ltd

US$750mn Silangan project of Philex Mining

PhilTower, Globe partner to deploy new cell sites in Luzon

Tower company Phil-Tower Consortium announced on Thursday that it is partnering with Globe Telecom for the deployment of new cell sites in Luzon. PhilTower president Devid Gubiani said the company is helping power critical digital network expansion needs through its cell site technologies. 

PhilTower is part of a regional group with presence in Malaysia, Indonesia, Myanmar and the Philippines.

Globe is targeting to put up 2,000 new cell sites this year and installed 318 new cell towers in the first three months.

 
 

Macro News

'NCR Plus' under GCQ with restrictions until end-June

President Rodrigo Duterte extended the quarantine status (i.e. GCQ with restrictions) of Metro Manila and four nearby provinces until end-June. Metro Manila and Bulacan will be under GCQ with some restrictions. Calabarzon provinces of Rizal, Laguna and Cavite will be under the same status but with heightened restrictions. Metro Manila mayors have shortened the curfew to 12mn-4am from 10pm-4am previously.

Vehicle sales in May jump +24% MoM; Toyota sales up +25% MoM

Data from CAMPI/TMA showed that industry sales rose +24% MoM, +361% YoY to 22,062 units in May. This brings 5M21 industry car sales up 59% YoY to 110,217 units. Toyota sold 10,799 units, up 25% MoM and 344% YoY and brings 5M21 sales to 52,5236 units (+87% YoY). We’ve modeled a +17% YoY jump in Toyota’s unit sales to 117k units and to reach our forecast, Toyota will need to sell c. 9,200 units per month. With the safeguard duty, Toyota expects unit sales to reach on 104k-108k units this year (+4% to +8%). To reach Toyota’s target of 104k units, monthly sales should average 7,400 units. Assuming Toyota’s forecast, we estimate our forecast for Toyota and GTCAP could fall by 10-14% and 4-5%, respectively. GT Capital is trading at an 8% discount to its market-based NAV of P649/sh.

Dollar reserves dip in May

The country’s gross international reserves, based on preliminary data, settled at US$106.98bn in end-May from US$107.71bn in end-April (-US$727mn). The decline was due to the foreign currency withdrawals of the national government from its deposits with the BSP to pay its foreign currency debt obligations and various expenditures. The GIR level represented a “more-than-adequate external liquidity buffer” equivalent to 12.2 months’ worth of imports of goods and payments of services and primary income. The BSP projects the GIR to reach US$114bn this year from US$110.117bn in end-Dec ’20.

100,000 Sputnik V doses arrive

100,000 Sputnik V arrived on Friday night. The Philippines has now received a total of 12.7mn of various COVID vaccine doses and has administered 6.3mn doses. The country is scheduled to receive 10mn doses in June, including 4.4mn doses that already arrived. The Philippines expects to receive 12mn doses in July and 17mn doses in August. The government aims to vaccinate 50%-60% of the population by end-2021.

NCR Covid cases plunge - further reopening likely?

Our bullish Strategy report focused on vaccine rollout in Metro Manila (aka National Capital Region, or NCR). Adding to that is the rapid decline in Covid-19 cases within NCR. The headline nation-wide figure has stayed stubbornly high, in the 6,000-7,000 range. However, the 7-day average case count in the capital has dropped to below 1,000/day in the last week. At the height of the recent surge in March, Metro Manila accounted as much as 60% of total cases in the country. That figure is down to 10-15% of total. This bolsters our view of a potentially faster reopening of the economy within Metro Manila, its surrounding provinces, and major economic centers such as Cebu and Davao. The downside risk to our theme is humanitarian/political. If Covid cases do not start to fall, there could be pressure to de-prioritize NCR, and send more doses to provinces. This pressure could increase, particularly as politicians begin posturing for the 2022 elections. Congressmen have criticized the vaccination drive’s focus on Metro Manila. President Duterte recently directed that the vaccine supplies be increased to Covid hot spots. 

Remittances at US$2.3bn in April

OFW remittances grew 12.7% YoY to US$2.3bn in April (-8% MoM). This brings the 3-month moving average growth rate at +7.3% YoY. In PHP terms, remittances rose 7.6% YoY. Remittances from land-based workers rose by 15.2% YoY to US$1.779bn and sea-based workers by 4.9% to US$526mn. To date, remittances have risen by 4.8% to US$9.9bn from a year earlier. The BSP expects FY21 remittances to rise by 4% YoY to US$31bn.

Finance chief firm on keeping stimulus at P173bn

Finance Secretary Carlos G. Dominguez III said the government can only provide P173bn in stimulus funds this year. Dept. of Finance (DoF) will remain open to Bayanihan III if lawmakers can identify other potential revenue-generating measures. He believes it is unsustainable to have the fiscal deficit exceed 9.3% of GDP. The DoF is not proposing any tax hikes to boost revenues, but instead looked for savings from the 2020 budget and asked state-run firms to increase their dividend remittances to P47bn this year. The Bayanihan III bill aims to provide more than P400bn in stimulus funds. The Bill already passed the Lower House and the Senate will tackle the bill when it resumes session on July 26. Bayanihan I and II provided funds of P275bn and P160bn, respectively.

1.5mn more Sinovac doses arrive

1.5mn more Sinovac vaccine doses arrived yesterday.  The Philippines has now received a total of 14.2mn COVID vaccine doses and has administered 7.6mn doses.

The country is scheduled to receive 10.2mn doses in June, including 5.9mn doses that already arrived.

The Philippines expects to receive 11.7mn doses in July and 17mn doses in August. The government aims to vaccinate 50%-60% of the population by end-2021.
