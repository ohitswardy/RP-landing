REGIS REPORT

03 September 2021

The Philippines gained for a third straight week, this time advancing 1.6% to close at 6897.13. The market extended its gains, supported by continued net inflows into conglos and consumers.

Big cap conglomerates Ayala Corp (-0.4% WoW) and SM (+4.6% WoW) saw biggest inflows alongside retailer Puregold (+8.0% WoW) and Universal Robina (+0.8% WoW). Meanwhile, telcos continued to see foreign buying albeit at a  slower pace than last week. Net foreign inflow for the week stood at US$9m.

Gains were broad based with 2/3 of the PSEi ending the week up. Power names dominated, however.

First Gen (+12.7% WoW) led the gainers this week after KKR-controlled Philippines Clean Energy Holdings submitted a tender offer for between 3.0% to 5.7% of the shares of First Gen at P33.0/share. The offer price represents a 17% premium over last close as of Aug 26 and over the one month VWAP.  Philippines Clean Energy Holdings is controlled by KKR unit Valorous Asia Holdings which already holds 12.59% of First Gen.

Following news on the tender offer, market chatter of another group buying into into Aboitiz Power (11.1% WoW) sent it and parent Aboitiz Equity Ventures (11.4% WoW) sharply higher.  Meanwhile, renewable energy name AC Energy (+8.5% WoW) continued to hit new highs.

The week saw some profit-taking in Globe (-5.1% WoW) after surging 32% last week.  PLDT (+0.6% WoW) though, which rose 17.45% in the same period, managed a slight gain.

On the ground, COVID infections remained high, with between 14k-over 22k new daily case counts this week.  Based on the lastet tally, Eighteen percent (18%) of the Philippines’ total population has now received at least one dose, with 13% already fully vaccinated.

PUBLISHED RESEARCH
Philippine Property - Guidebook to Philippine REITs
 
 
by Carl Sy, CFA +632 8894 6646 Carl.sy@regis.ph
 

Comparing the first five Philippine REITs

Since REIT regulations were revised in 1Q20, three REITs have been listed on the PSE and two more are about to be listed by the end of September 2021. We compare these five REITs across various metrics and present them on to our readers on pages 4-6.

Some history on Philippine REITs – biggest hurdles removed

The REIT Act was passed in 2009 but for over 10 years, no Philippine REITs had been listed. This was due to prohibitive regulations which imposed 1) 12% VAT on the initial transfer of assets from sponsor to REIT and 2) minimum public ownership requirement of 67%. Both these provisions have since been relaxed. Since then, several developers (and even non-developers) have announced plans to spin off their rental assets into REITs.

Tax advantages

The main incentive to spin off assets into a REIT is lower income tax. Philippine REITs are required to pay out at least 90% of distributable income (pretax profit) as dividends and that portion paid out will not be subject to income tax. Thus, the post-tax earnings of the rental asset could rise as much as 33% (see Figure 2 on page 3). We estimate that by spinning off rental projects into a REIT and selling the minimum requirement of 33% to the public, a REIT sponsor could realize a large one-off windfall and lose only a minor amount of annual recurring cash flows from the rental assets despite much lower ownership. The drop in cash flow to the sponsor could be reduced further if the REIT pays land leases or fund/property management fees to the sponsor.

RLC offers the best risk-reward; biggest beneficiary of REITs

In our view, RLC offers the best risk-reward in the sector. Its mall operations look poised to rebound (as vaccinations increase) and it has the highest proportion of REIT-able assets among developers we cover.

 
 

Corporate News

FGEN could be removed from the PSE index before the quarterly review

First Gen’s current public float is 18.74%. Valorous Asia, which currently owns 12.59% of FGEN, made a tender offer and seeks to increase its stake by another 3.0% to 5.7% of shares outstanding by end-September. This could lower the public float below 15%, the minimum requirement to be a PSE index constituent. If this were to occur, FGEN would be deleted from the PSE index in the mid-November index review. The mid-May and mid-November index reviews would only trigger revisions when public float changes by at least 5ppt or when public float goes below the minimum threshold. The PSE would also have the discretion to remove FGEN from the PSE Index earlier than that. The deletion would be implemented within five trading days of an announcement. At this point, we estimate that DMCI Holdings would be the candidate most likely to replace FGEN in the PSE index.

Open Signal on DITO

Some key highlights/excerpts for those who don’t want to be bothered to read the whole report;

“In the last two 30-day periods — commencing June 6, 2021 and July 6, 2021 — there was no significant difference between the speeds seen by our DITO users and the overall speeds in Central Metro Cebu, but in Davao City the overall speeds were significantly faster than those seen by our DITO users.”

“With Upload Speed Experience we saw a different story between the three areas — in Central Metro Cebu, DITO users observed statistically higher upload speeds than the overall average in all but the data collection period starting June 6, 2021, and in that period DITO’s score statistically tied with the overall score.”

“As with speed, the Video Experience of DITO’s users will likely change further over time as DITO’s network matures, becomes available in many more locations, and as DITO grows its customer base and the network becomes busier.”

“The analysis in this insight indicates that so far DITO’s entry into the Filipino mobile market is evolutionary, not revolutionary, for mobile experience because DITO’s scores were mostly similar to — or below — the overall scores of all operators, with the exception of Upload Speed Experience in both Central Metro Cebu and Metro Manila.”

“However, given that DITO’s competitors – Globe and Smart – have had far longer to roll out their own 4G networks, these initial results are impressive and bode well for the market’s long-term competitiveness — particularly if DITO can increase capacity to keep up with the influx of new users”

Under the terms of the bidding under which the Duterte administration granted its licenses and spectrum, Dito is required to provide minimum average data speed of 55mbps by the second anniversary (July 2021 – yes two months ago).  You can see for yourself what the results are in the charts above. Dito CME moves forward with takeover of Dito Telecommunity

Dito CME disclosed that the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) had approved on August 27 its application for a capital increase that would allow it to complete a previously announced asset swap deal with Udenna Corp. Under the transaction, Dito CME will issue 11.2bn new shares to Udenna for 100% of Udenna Communications Media and Entertainment Holdings Corp. This will give Dito CME an indirect majority stake in Dito Telecommunity

Apart from the Udenna, Dito CME said it also issued 35mn shares to an “unrelated party”. Following the said transactions, Dito CME said its public ownership level stood at 20.02% as of August 27.

Manila Water elects new President, CEO

Manila Water has elected J.V. Emmanuel De Dios as its president and CEO. In September last year, De Dios was appointed as the CEO of Prime Metro BMD Corp., a joint venture between the Razon group’s Prime Infra and Australia’s BMD Group. De Dios previously served as CEO of GE Philippines for eight years and was a former undersecretary of the Department of Energy (DOE).

FGEN’s Avion unit 2 goes offline due to damage

The second unit of First Gen’s 97MW Avion gas-fired plant will be offline for the time being due to damage found in its gas turbine. The damage to the 48.5MW unit was found during routine inspection and will require further offsite assessment at a GE service depot abroad to determine extent of the damage and effect repairs. We estimate Avion contributes <2% of FGEN’s income.

 

Megawide audited 1H21 profit at P138mn

In a press release, Megawide reported audited 1H21 net profit after minorities of P138mn. The unaudited numbers initially was a net loss of P93mn. 1H20 net loss stood at P290mn while 1H21 EBITDA rose 16% YoY to P1.7bn (from P1.60bn unaudited).

 

MREIT office portfolio is 100% occupied

Management of Megaworld’s REIT, MREIT, said that office occupancy improved from 93% in March 2021 to 100% in June 2021. Thus, dividends could be higher than the projections in the prospectus. FY June 2022 (based on prospectus) had a DPS of P0.91/share and at the maximum offer price of P22/share, this would represent a 4.1% dividend yield. Meanwhile, FY June 2023 (based on prospectus) – DPS of P0.98/share and at the maximum offer price, this would represent a dividend yield of 4.5%.  The REIT will have 10 office buildings with GLA of 225,000sqm. MREIT plans to acquire 44,000sqm of office space in January 2022 is targeting to have a portfolio of 500,000sqm by 2024. The pricing date was set on September 9 while the offer period will run from September 14 to 20. Listing date is on September 30. If MREIT were priced at the maximum, our MEG NAV estimate could rise by 9%. If we were to lower our assigned cap rate on Megaworld’s entire 1.4mn sqm office portfolio by 1ppt (to 6%), then our MEG NAV estimate would rise by 8%. MEG is currently trading at 62% discount to NAV, 0.5X FY22E P/B, and 6.4X FY22E P/E.

ACEN raises $400mn from green bonds

AC Energy Corp (ACEN) disclosed that its unit ACEN Finance Ltd successfully issued a senior guaranteed undated fixed-for-life green bonds (perp) worth $400mn. Pricing for the bonds was at 4%, 45 bps tighter than the initial price guidance with no step-up and no reset. The green bonds will be issued by ACEN Finance Ltd under its $1.5bn medium-term note program, which will be guaranteed by ACEN and will be listed on the Singapore Exchange Securities Trading platform.

 
 

Macro News

Vaccinations pick up slightly

As of August 29, the 7-day average vaccinations figure was 387,176/day from 368,897/day the week before. Around 18mn vaccine doses arrived in August and 25mn doses are expected to arrive in September. The Philippines has now received a total of 51.9mn COVID vaccine doses and has administered 33.1mn doses (as of August 29). The government aims to vaccinate 58 Filipinos by end-2021. 

Bank lending drops 0.7% in July

Preliminary data show that outstanding loans of universal and commercial banks (U/KBs), net of reverse repurchase (RRP) placements with the BSP, declined by 0.7% YoY in July. This is a smaller drop than in previous months; Loans up 0.2% MoM. Loans for production activities expanded by 0.8% YoY, reversing contractions since December 2020. Consumer loans dropped 8.2% YoY with auto loans contracting at a faster rate of 15.4% than in June. Domestic liquidity (M3) expanded by 5.9% YoY in July, slower than the 6.5% (revised) growth recorded in June.

Gov’t lifts moratorium on new casinos

Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corp chair Andrea Domingo said the agency was ready to execute the President’s new directive to lift the moratorium on new casino applications to help raise government revenues. This comes three years after Duterte imposed the moratorium on new licenses. Projects that were seeking a casino licenses before the moratorium include the LRWC-Galaxy project in Boracay and Landing International’s proposed Entertainment City project. Despite the lifting, Domingo said no new casinos would be allowed in Pagcor Entertainment City in Paranaque since there was no more space for new locators.

New Covid-19 cases hit record high of 22,366

The Department of Health reported 22,366 new Covid-19 infections on Monday, bringing the country’s total case count to 1,972,202. This is the highest number of infections recorded after the 19,441 cases reported last August 28.

 

Metro Manila, 15 other areas under MECQ until September 7

Metro Manila will remain under modified enhanced community quarantine (MECQ) until September 7. Other areas under MECQ are: Apayao, Ilocos Norte, Bulacan, Bataan, Cavite, Lucena City, Rizal, Laguna, Aklan, Iloilo province, Iloilo City, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu City, Mandaue City, and Cagayan de Oro City. Metro Manila, Bataan and Laguna shall have added restrictions on dining, personal care services and religious activities. Curfew hours in Metro Manila under the prevailing MECQ is from 8 pm to 4 am.

Customs beats collection goal in August

The Bureau of Customs collected P54.05bn in August and exceeded the agency’s target for the month by 1.86%. This is up 22% YoY but down 7% MoM and the government cited improved valuation and volume of importations. This also brings YT-August collections to P413bn, up 19% YoY and accounting for 67% of the govt’s FY21E.
