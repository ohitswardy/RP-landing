REGIS REPORT

10 December 2021

The PSEi gained +1.9%, recovering from last week’s Omicron triggered drop of 3.1%.  The PSEi managed to climb on its first week of longer trading hours since the pandemic commenced, even as volumes thinned WoW. News was fairly light as well.

On the ground, mobility continued to rise to new pandemic period highs.  New cases are down to 499 (7-day moving ave) and progress continues to be made on the vaccination front. Vaccinations for the week ended Dec 5 reached 1.5m/day -- the fastest pace outside of the three-day national vaccination drive (Nov 29-Dec 1). Some 35% of the total population has been vaccinated.  For Metro Manila, 69% of the population has been vaccinated, equivalent to its entire adult population.

Gains were across the board. AEV (+12.5% WoW) led the index this week, as speculation continued on banking unit UBP’s (+8.4% WoW) and the Citi retail assets that are for sale. AGI (+8.1% WoW), PLDT (+6.5% WoW), and Wilcon (+6.45% WoW) gained.

Ayala Corp (+5.5% WoW) also gained. AC finalized its agreement to sell another 2.69bn more shares of AC Energy (-4.1% WoW) to Singapore sovereign wealth fund Government Investment Corp (GIC), completing the agreement to sell the shares signed last year. This transaction was initially to be consummated at P2.97/share, but the price was raised to P3.47/share after adjustments. Following this deal and the upcoming share issuances relating to ACEN’s purchase of increased stakes in JV projects with its partner, Ayala Corp will own 62.5% of ACEN while GIC, through “Arran”, will own 16.9%.

Cebu Air (+4.35% WoW) gained.  Cebu Air is now running at >60% of pre-pandemic domestic capacity and expects to hit 84% in 2022.

Headline net foreign inflow stood at US$158m this week. There were multiple blocks that skewed net foreign flows -- among them was the sale of a stake in AC Energy by parent ACEIC to Arran investments which registered US$190m in inflows and an RCB block which clocked in US$21mn in outflows. Excluding these, foreigners would have been net sellers for US$11mn. Biggest outflows were seen in consumer stocks Universal Robina (-0.4% WoW) and Monde Nissin (-4.3% WoW). Meanwhile, foreigners were seen consistently buying BDO (+2.9% WoW, BPI (-0.2% WoW), and  Ayala Corp.

CORPORATE NEWS
 
 
 
 

AC Energy and Infrastructure (ACEIC) to sell additional 2.690bn ACEN shares to Arran.

The controlling shareholder of AC Energy Corp (ACEN PM) signed a share purchase agreement to sell 2,689,521,681 shares to Arran Investment at P3.4678/share for a total consideration is P9.327bn. The completion of the share sale is scheduled for December 10 this year subject to regulatory approvals. The sale of secondary ACEN shares to Arran is meant to implement the investment agreement signed last December 30, 2020 to enable to Arran to own 17.5% of the outstanding capital stock of ACEN post completion of the follow-on offering and infusion of international assets. Arran initially acquired 4bn shares in ACEN at P2.97/share for a total of P11.88bn. The transaction was completed in March 2021. ACEIC’s stake in ACEN will go down to 62.5% of the enlarged capital base of 39.648bn shares after factoring in the issuance of 1.4422bn new shares to UPC in exchange for the additional stakes in several plant.

 

Cebu Pacific targeting to operate 84% of pre-Covid domestic capacity in 2022

Cebu Pacific remains “cautiously optimistic” about its 2022 prospects and expects to operate 84% of its pre-pandemic domestic capacity. The company is currently operating above 60% of its pre-pandemic domestic network and capacity according to Head of Strategy Alex Reyes. Regis is forecasting CEB’s FY22 domestic seat capacity to reach 90% of pre-pandemic levels. The company expects a slower recovery on the international side, targeting restoring 39% of pre-pandemic seat capacity in 2022. Additionally, Regis expects CEB’s international capacity to reach 22% of pre-pandemic levels in 2022. CEB is trading at 5.3x FY22E EV/EBITDA, below peers (14.7x).

Cemex terminates construction contract for expansion

Solid Cement Corp. canceled its construction contract with CBMI Construction Co., Ltd. for its 1.5mn MT new production line due to delays. Solid Cement is a wholly-owned unit of Cemex Philippines (CHP PM). The new line would have increased CHP’s annual clinker capacity (in cement terms) to 6.5mn MT (Capex: US$235mn). The company is currently reviewing its completion timetable guidance of end-June 2022 (Regis: end-2022). CHP is in talks with its lender, BDO, to defer scheduled debt payments. CHP’s 9M21 EBITDA stood at P3.3bn (+2% YoY). In FY19, CHP’s EBITDA stood at P4.3bn.

Suntrust: casino project not affected by Chau arrest

Suntrust Home Developers (SUN PM) clarified that the arrest of Suncity Group Holdings chairman Alvin Chau will not affect the construction of the hotel and casino in its co-development agreement with Westside City Resorts World. Suncity Holdings is the parent company of Fortune Noble which owns a 51% stake in Suntrust and Summit Ascent (holder of convertible bonds in Suntrust). Suntrust said that current funds (P7.7bn as of Nov 15) will support the immediate construction and working capital requirements of the hotel and casino complex. Mr. Chau has resigned from the posts of Chairman and director of Suncity Holdings, Summit Ascent and other positions within the group. The disclosure also echoed Suncity Holdings (HK listed parent company of Fortunate Noble) clarifications that the VIP business are under a different entity owned by Mr. Chau on a personal capacity. Tigre de Cristal, the group’s casino hotel in Russia is reportedly under investigation for illegal gambling/junket activities in China. Solaire confirmed that Sun City-operated rooms have been closed since November 30. At its peak, we estimate Suncity accounted for as much as 13% of total industry GGR. VIP, was about 50% of total industry GGR. The VIP segment was predominantly driven by junket operators (90% of VIP). While the recent recovery in GGR of Philippine operators has been driven by domestic, the development dents the potential recovery of VIP GGR expected for next year.

Globe doubles data allocations for mobile postpaid plans

Globe just doubled the data allocations for postpaid subscriber packages under its new GPlan Plus packages. For the P 999/month plan (SIM only), the monthly data allocation has been doubled to 20GB. The data allocation for the P1,499 plan was also doubled to 40GB, and so forth. The new plans include unlimited SMS and voice calls to all networks. These changes now put Globe’s postpaid packages at par with PLDT’s (Smart) following the latter’s move at the end of Sept. Simplistically, the new plans put the cost of mobile data for the consumer at P50/GB, assuming no value to the other services (SMS, voice). Regis estimates cost/GB for prepaid packages is less than half that (closer to P20/GB).

Bank of Commerce plans P4bn IPO

San Miguel Corp affiliate Bank of Commerce is eyeing to raise as much as P4.03bn through an initial public offering from March 7-18 next year. The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) said it received the bank’s registration statement for its IPO on December 7. The bank is eyeing to offer 280,602,800 common shares for up to P12.50/share, with an overallotment option of up to 42,090,400 common shares. The company plans to use P1.8bn to expand it loans portfolio, primarily to corporate borrowers and some housing and auto loans. Around P522mn for “ATM” refresh project to replace 273 existing automated teller machines with 144 cash recycling machines and P400mn to replace Silverlake Integrated Banking Solution core banking system.

 

Tonik attracts P5bn deposits in 8 months

Tonik chief executive officer and founder Greg Krasnov said the country’s first digital-only neobank has secured P5bn worth of deposits within 8 months since going live in the Philippines. The deposit products offer interest rates ranging from 4-6%. It also recently launched its first lending product called Quick Loan – an all-digital loan product designed to serve the underbanked with a quick and affordable loan proposition in less than 15 minutes.

BIR to start collecting taxes from POGOs

The BIR issued implementing rules on RA 11590 (POGO taxes). This will require POGOs to pay 5% of gross gaming revenue (GGR) as tax to the Bureau of Internal Revenue. Meanwhile. non-gaming revenue will be subject to 25% corporate income tax. POGO employees will also pay 25% income tax (with an assumed minimum annual salary of 600,000). The government expects the passage of this law to raise P145bn in tax revenue over five years.

We assume that Metro Manila office take-up from POGOs will be:

2020 - -175,000sqm (net vacancy)

2021E - -280,000sqm (net vacancy)

2022E – zero

2023E - +130,000sqm

POGOs occupied c. 11% of Metro Manila office stock in 2019 but now occupy only c. 6%. Office leasing comprises 58% of Filinvest Land’s NAV and 46% of Megaworld’s NAV. Manila Bay area land comprises c. 35% of SM Prime’s.

Inflation eases further to 4.2% in November

The country’s Inflation rate decelerated for the third straight month to 4.2% in November. This is slightly higher than the BSP’s forecast range for the month of 3.3%-4.1% and also higher than consensus (4.0%). The lower CPI is due to moderating food inflation (e.g. vegetable, fish, and meat). Meanwhile, non-food inflation rose to 4.1% in November from 3.8% the previous month due to higher crude oil prices (transport and electricity). The YT-November average inflation is at 4.5%. The slowdown in November inflation supports the BSP’s assessment that inflation would decelerate to within the target range of 2-4% over the policy horizon.

Pace of vaccinations surges

As of December 5, the 7-day average vaccinations figure jumped to 1,497,212/day from 813,734/day the previous week. The government held a major vaccination drive from Nov 29 to Dec 1. It intends to conduct another vaccination drive from Dec 15 to 17. Around 35% of the Philippine population (38.2mn Filipinos) has been fully vaccinated against COVID. As of November 25, 69% of the Metro Manila population (9.3mn people) has been fully vaccinated. The Philippines has received a total of 148mn COVID vaccine doses and has administered 92mn doses (as of December 5). Beginning December 1, on-site workers for both the private and public sectors must be fully vaccinated against COVID-19. This rule will only apply to areas with “stable” supply of vaccines. The government is targeting to inoculate 54mn Filipinos by end-2021; 77mn by end-1Q22; 90mn by end-2Q22.

Lower House approves bill on SIM card registration

The House of Representatives approved on 3rd reading a bill mandating the registration of SIM cards, including prepaid SIMs. Buyers of SIM cards will be required to provide proof of identity and must sign a registration form. The mandatory registration will enable law enforcement to track down/identify mobile phone users and reduce fraud. The telcos, historically, have opposed this move. One likely positive impact of the mandatory registration is the reduction of churn as prepaid subscribers find it easy to buy new SIMs and throw the old ones away.

 

DTI slaps anti-dumping duty on cement imports from Vietnam

The Department of Trade and Industry slapped a provisional anti-dumping duty on specific Portland cement brands from Vietnam. The duties will apply to 28 cement exporters from Vietnam. The provisional anti-dumping duties will be imposed for four (4) months once the Bureau of Customs issues the order. For Type-1 cement, the provisional anti-dumping duty will range from US$1.02 per metric ton (MT) to US$10.53/MT, which is equivalent to 2.69% to 31.87% of the export price. For Type-1P cement, the duties will range from US$1.16 per MT to US$12.79 per MT, equivalent to 3.8% to 29.2% of the export price. Provisional duties are expected to increase the import cost of a 40-kg bag of cement by P2.01 to P25.08 but the DTI does not expect the duty to be passed on to users due to strong competition from local and other imported brands.

Mobility at new highs (4-5 Dec)

Mobility continued to improve last week. For some indicators, there was a spike on the 30 Nov holiday. However, the latest data point (Sunday, 05 Dec), was a new Covid-era high for Retail in Metro Manila, and for Transit stations in the capital and the country at large.

 

This data is consistent with news that mall operators have ended some/all their rental support for its tenants as foot traffic has returned. It also jives with the airline/airport data, and reports that more and more local governments are relaxing restrictions for domestic tourists, particularly for vaccinated travelers.

 

As Covid case counts remain extremely low, Regis expects mobility data to continue to increase into the holiday season.
