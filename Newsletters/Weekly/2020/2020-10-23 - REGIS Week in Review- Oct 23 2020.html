REGIS REPORT

23 October 2020

 

The PSEi surged 9.9% this week, after a two-week slump.  For the first time in a while, foreign selling did not weigh on the market, with the last three days of the week recording net inflows.

Foreign net inflow stood at US$0.4m this week. Largest inflows were seen in BDO (US$12.1m), Jollibee (+US$10.8m), and SM (+US$10.1m). 

While the extension of the current quarantine level (GCQ) to year-end has been recommended by Metro Manila mayors and is looking likely, easing measure continue to be gradually introduced.  Age restrictions are being loosened, curfew hours are being shortened, the ban on non-essential foreign travel is being lifted and hotels will soon be allowed to operate at 100% capacity.

Virtually all index stocks were higher this week, as value and reopening themes played out.   Defensives generally underperformed this week.

LT Group (+25.6% WoW) was the top index gainer. Cigarette volumes for 49.6%-owned PMFTC reached 11.7bn (-6% YoY) sticks in 3Q, showing a nice 21% rebound from 2Q. 

Jollibee (+21.8% WoW) also rose sharply.  Jollibee is raising its stake in the fund holding the master franchise for the Tim Ho Wan restaurant chain across much of Asia. JFC will pay SGD36m to lift its stake from 60% to 85%. 

Alliance Global (+16.1% WoW) gained, as did GT Capital (+16.1% WoW), with Toyota Motor Philippines this week modestly increasing its FY20 sales forecast to 100k units (from 94-95k units previously).  Toyota accounts for 23% of GTCAP’s FY20 profit estimate and 30% of NAV.

Other holding companies also climbed, with Ayala  Corp (+12.3% WoW) and SM Investments (+12.3% WoW) not far behind.

Property and banks also populated the leaderboard. Ayala Land (+15.9% WoW), Robinsons Land (+14.2% WoW) and SM Prime (+13.3% WoW) all registered increases, while banks BPI (+11.5% WoW) and BDO (+10.9% WoW) also rose in the double digits.

Meanwhile, Robinsons Retail (+1.0% WoW) is acquiring the leading pharmacy chain in the Visayas region, Rose Pharmacy. RRHI will acquire 100% of Rose Pharmacy from Dairy Farm (D01 SP) for P4.5bn (US$93m) in cash. Dairy Farm owns 12% of RRHI.

The transaction will add 300 stores to RRHI’s network, concentrated mostly in the Visayas region, with some in Mindanao. The transaction adds 4-5% of revenue to RRHI’s pharmacy business  We view the transaction as a significant one for RRHI’s pharmacy business, and narrows the gap between RRHI and the market leaders. At 0.5x sales, the deal appears reasonably priced, assuming it can reach 4% net margin soon.

The 3Q20 results season kicked off this week.

BPI reported that its NPL ratio jumped to 2.98% in 3Q20, 115bps higher than the 1.83% in 2Q. As a result, provision expense for the quarter stayed high at P6.1bn (+453% YoY), dragging 3Q20 NPAT down 34% YoY (9M20 down 22% YoY). The increase in NPLs was not a surprise but the magnitude of the increase was higher than expected,  with Regis forecasting year-end NPL of 2.80%. The 3Q NPL also ran counter to the less-bearish guidance given by management in recent meetings. Meanwhile, NIMs were up 18bps YoY (+4bps QoQ) as the bank grew CASA deposits 15% YoY.

Meanwhile, URC’s (+6.9% WoW) 3Q20 net profit and EBIT rose +6% YoY and +8% YoY, respectively. Revenues for the quarter were flattish across all  business segments.  Demand in the Phils was weakened in the absence of government dole outs and pantry stocking, while recovery trends on the international side were mixed. The 9M result is tracking ahead of expectations largely on margin outperformance.

Philippine Strategy - Conference takeaways; and the “Roadshow Popularity Index”
 
 
by Rafael Garchitorena +632 8894 6644 rafael.garchitorena@regis.ph & Gio Dela Rosa, CFA +632 8894 6642 gio.delarosa@regis.ph
 

Lessons from the maiden JEF-Regis conference

We held our first Philippine conference under the Jefferies-Regis partnership, with 263 investors meeting 24 companies. Consensus across sectors was that business is steadily, sequentially improving as the economy slowly reopens. But some worry that unemployment and lack of fiscal stimulus could stall momentum in the crucial 4Q. Consumers, telcos, and reopening plays were the belles of this (virtual) ball; but contrarians may find the less “popular” ones just as attractive.

And… we’re back

After last year’s break, the first Jefferies-Regis Philippine conference was held on 14-15 October. Despite having only 24 listcos, the virtual event drew 263 investors. Foreigners were just under half (129) of the crowd, logging on from Asia, Australia, Europe, the UK, the US, Canada, and the Middle East. Foreign interest was surprising, given the constant net foreign selling so far this year.

“Cautious optimism” on reopening, but wary of recovery losing steam

Revenue trends have improved sequentially from 2Q to 3Q across all sectors. Consumer firms are “cautiously optimistic”, wary that Covid-19 could hurt demand in 4Q, traditionally a year’s strongest quarter. A POGO exodus could put a damper on the property sector, just as residential and BPO segments are rebounding. To the upside, the telecom sector is having the best year in a very long time. Reviving the Roadshow Popularity Index

We bring back our Roadshow Popularity Index (Figure 1), looking for trends in investors’ meeting preferences. Unsurprisingly, the consumer sector took the most requests. And after years of being ignored, the telcos were again in vogue. Among “reopening plays”, property seemed to be much preferred over banks. PUBLISHED RESEARCH
Universal Robina - 3Q20: margin expansion drives profitability despite challenged top line
 
 
by Carissa Mangubat +632 8894 6647 carissa.mangubat@regis.ph
 

9M20 net profit (+7% YoY to P7.5bn) ahead on better-than-expected margins

URC’s 9M20 net profit rose 7% YoY to P7.5bn, while EBIT grew +8% YoY to P11.9bn – both are tracking mildly ahead of our FY20 estimates. Consolidated revenues (flat YoY) were broadly in line, with the profit outperformance being largely driven by margin expansion. For 3Q20 alone, net profit and EBIT rose +6% YoY and +8% YoY, respectively.

PH branded consumer food (BCF): EBIT margin expansion despite soft top line

Revenues for the PH BCF segment were subdued, declining 2% YoY and 3% QoQ. The softness was evident across its key categories – including staples, such as coffee and instant noodles – in the absence of pantry stocking and the boost from government handouts. Despite the challenged top line, EBIT margin for the segment expanded 150bps YoY due to 1) favorable sales mix and 2) subdued input cost. Management, however, also attributed some of the gains to its business transformation strategy and expects these cost savings to be sustained longer term.

Int’l BCF: VN recovers, pantry stocking boosts

Oceania Int’l BCF revenues were flattish in 3Q20, with Vietnam showing signs of recovery (+11% in local currency terms) as lockdown measures were eased as early as late-2Q. Oceania (+3%) continued to be boosted by pantry stocking, while Thailand (-2%) remained challenged.

Strong position amidst challenging environment

Notwithstanding the seemingly positive margin trends, management is cautious given the challenging environment. Consumer spending in the PH remains weak, and management said it is willing to reinvest some of the gains into further brand building and product innovations, as well as improvements in distribution to the trade. This should help solidify market share gains achieved over this period.

 

 

Corp News

Robinsons Retail acquires leading Visayas drug store chain Rose Pharmacy

RRHI is acquiring 100% of leading Visayas drug store chain, Rose Pharmacy, from Dairy Farm

Dairy Farm owns 12% of RRHI. The total cash consideration of P4.5bn, imply a valuation of 0.5x EV/sales. Rose Pharmacy generated P9bn sales in 2019 and had 300 stores strategically located in Visayas and Mindanao. We estimate that Rose Pharmacy will add approx. 4-5% to RRHI’s consolidated sales and may be mildly margin dilutive near term. However, the acquisition gives RRHI an instant footprint in Visayas and Mindanao. As of 1H20, RRHI had 520 South Star Drug stores, of which only 72 were in VisMin.

Toyota raises 2020 sales target to 100k units

Toyota Motor Philippines upgraded its 2020 sales forecast to 100k units previously 94-95k units. In comparison, 2019 unit sales was at 162k. Our forecasts assume FY20 car sales will drop by 44% to 90,372 units. As of 9M20 Toyota’s retail sales stood at to 63,182 units, down 45% YoY. Toyota accounts for 23% of GTCAP’s FY20 profit estimate and 30% of NAV.

Megawide signs new contracts with Century Properties unit

Megawide bagged three contracts to build more than 4,700 row housing units for PHirst Park Homes, Inc. PHirst Park Homes is the affordable housing unit of Century Properties. As at end-June, Megawide’s order book stood at P47.7bn and since then, the company has bagged a P10-12bn contract to build a section of the PNR Malolos-Clark railway. Around 98% of orderbook comprises residential/office/retail.

PH Resorts sets offering at P1.68/sh

PH Resorts sets the final price of its follow-on offering at P1.68/sh. It will raise up to P756mn from the sale of 300mn common shares and an over-allotment option for another 150mn shares. Proceeds would be used to partially fund the construction of the first phase of Emerald Bay in Mactan, Cebu with a target completion of the first phase by 2022. The plan is to have 122 gaming tables, 600 electronic gaming machines and 270 hotel rooms. The implied market capitalization post-offering is P8.8bn. The offering would raise the float from 10% to 19%. The offer period runs from October 19 to 26 with a target listing date of November 3.

JFC increases investment in Tim Ho Wan holdco to 85%

JFC is increasing its investment in Titan Dining LP by another 25%, raising its stake to 85%. Titan is the holding company of the Tim Ho Wan brand of restaurants. The total cash consideration was around SGD36mn for the additional 25%. JFC is currently the master franchise holder for the Tim Ho Wan brand in Shanghai and opened its first store in Sep 2020.

AC Energy & partner to build US$36mn solar farm in India

UPC-AC Energy Solar said that it is developing a 70MW solar facility in Gujarat state’s Amreli district in India worth US$36mn. It won the 25-year power supply contract through a competitive bid. The project is expected to begin power generation in 1H2021. The JV has another US$68mn solar facility project in Rajasthan and is expected to come online by next year. Apart from India, AC and UPC are partners in the development of two wind projects in Vietnam with a combined capacity in of 60MW.

PMFTC cigarette volumes rebound 21%qoq in 3Q

Philip Morris International (PM US) disclosed last night that cigarette volumes in the Philippines (PMFTC) came in at 11.7bn in 3Q20, down 6% yoy but higher by 21% from the low in 2Q. Volumes for the 9M20 were lower by 14% to 32.1bn sticks vs our FY20E of 45.7bn sticks (-8%). In comparison, industry volumes were flat in 3Q20; so the unit market share of PMFTC dipped to 66.9%. Our existing forecasts have PMFTC earnings declining by 5% this year to P29.1bn, and LTG by 13% to P20.1bn. LTG is trading at 5.8x PE for 2021 and a 58% discount to NAV.

Hotels allowed to operate at 100%

Tourism Secretary Bernadette Puyat announced that all hotels in areas classified under general community quarantine (GCQ) and modified GCQ may now be allowed to operate at full capacity. The decision to open at 100% operational capacity would be subject to hotel management’s compliance with safety guidelines.

FGEN issues bid invitations for natgas transfer vessel

First Gen Corp has issued bid invitations to its three preferred providers of a Floating Storage and Regasification Unit to be used in its short-term import terminal in Batangas. The bidders are BW Gas ltd, New York-listed GasLog and Hoegh LNG Asia Pte Ltd and the bids have to be submitted in mid-November.

Cebu Air includes other financial instruments in fundraising plan

Cebu Air (CEB PM) updated its US$500mn fundraising plan to include the following instruments. Tey plan to have a private placement of warrants, bonds with detachable warrants or other similar security instruments (apart from the private placement of convertible bonds). These will have an aggregate subscription price of up to US$250mn which will be on top of the US$250mn convertible pref share offering. The Convertible Preferred Shares and the Convertible Bonds/Warrants/Bonds plus warrants is expected to be set within P38 to P45 range, representing 2% to 21% conversion premium over 30-day VWAP.

PLDT drops Sun Prepaid

PLDT is now re-classifying Sun prepaid subscribers as Smart prepaid and dropping the Sun prepaid brand.  Henceforth, the prepaid subscribers under Sun will be consolidated under Smart prepaid.  From a consumer perspective, they can now subscribe to packages and promos applicable to the latter. This exercise should streamline PLDT’s mobile brands, reducing them to two on prepaid (Smart and TNT, formerly Talk N Text) and two on postpaid (Smart and Sun).  This is likely to result in more rationalized marketing efforts (i.e. no need to produce ads or marketing material for Sun Prepaid, maintain separate apps or websites).  We don’t know yet if PLDT will plan to drop Sun on the postpaid side, though we think there could be a more distinct identity for the Sun postpaid business (vs Smart postpaid). Sun Prepaid accounted for 5.7m of PLDT’s 70.2m subscribers  (67.7m of whom are prepaid).  PLDT acquired Digitel, which used to operate Sun, in 2011.

BEL’s losses narrow to P46mn in 3Q

Belle Corp incurred a net loss of P46mn in 3Q, smaller than the P238mn loss in 2Q, as restrictions on gambling activities eased. This brings the 9M20 net income to P236mn, -90%yoy. From a loss in 2Q, gaming revenue share improved to P77mn in 3Q20 vs P496mn in  3Q19 (-85%yoy), reflecting the restart of operations at City of Dreams Manila. The equipment rental and scratch ticket sales recovered from 15mn in 2Q20 to P44mn (-79%yoy).

 

 

Macro News

Lower House passes P4.5tn 2021 national budget

The House of Representatives passed Friday the P4.5-tn national budget for next year on final reading. This paves the way for the Senate to tackle the spending bill in the plenary. The Senate plans to start debates by the week of Nov 9, a week earlier than previous.

Gov’t to lift ban on non-essential foreign travel on Oct 21; All rail lines hike capacity

The government is lifting the ban on non-essential travel of Filipinos by Oct 21, subject to strict requirements. This means that Filipino travelers must have a confirmed round trip ticket and travel health insurance. They also must have negative antigen test result taken within 24 hours before departure and a swab test and quarantine upon return. The government is also allowing travel agencies and tour operators to reopen even in Metro Manila and other areas under general community quarantine effective October 16. The IATF lifted a standing ban for people traveling from a GCQ to an MGCQ area even if not for official or work purposes. Meanwhile, all of Metro Manila’s rail lines will increase capacity up to 30% with capacity gradually expanded to 50%. Rail lines have been operating at 13-18% of capacity since June.

GCQ in Metro Manila eyed until year-end

The mayors of Metro Manila want to remain under general community quarantine up to the end of the year according to Presidential spokesman Harry Roque. Protocols on economic activity can be relaxed without downgrading the quarantine classification. It is assumed that President Duterte is expected to make a decision by month-end. Metro Manila mayors have recommended the following to the Inter-Agency Task Force: (1) Broaden the age group allowed to go out of their houses to 15-18 to 65 years old (vs. 21-59 years old currently) and (2) Shorten curfew hours. The Department of Trade and Industry has also asked companies to adopt staggered work hours to decongest the still-limited public transportation. The gov’t has also allowed mall-wide sales in GCQ and MGCQ areas.

Treasury sells P30bn 5-year bond on lower yield

The Philippine Bureau of the Treasury raised P30bn from the sale of 5-year bond. The average yield on its 5-year bond at auction was 2.782% with a coupon of 3.625%. The yield was 40bps lower than June 23 offer (MART: 2.672%). Auction was oversubscribed, with bids reaching P68.7bn.

Finance chief sees worse GDP slump

GDP is expected to shrink by 6% this year according to Department of Finance Secretary Carlos Dominguez III. This compares with the government’s July projection of 5.5%. The govt cited the reimposition of the modified enhanced community quarantine (MECQ) in Metro Manila and some areas of Calabarzon in August.

More jeeps, UV Express to return to Metro Manila roads on Oct. 25

Another batch of traditional public utility jeepneys (PUJs) and UV Express (UVE) units will return to the streets of Metro Manila according to the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB). A total of 30 routes with 1,704 PUJs and 22 routes with 1,064 UVEs will be activated on Sunday.
