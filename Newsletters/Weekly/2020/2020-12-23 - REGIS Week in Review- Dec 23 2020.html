REGIS REPORT

23 December 2020

Happy Holidays!

It’s a short, quiet week as Phils goes on Christmas break starting tomorrow (resumes on Monday).

The Phils inched down 0.9% for the week to close at 7204.38. Volumes were quieter than in recent weeks, with average turnover at US$179m. 

Focus was on second liners, with local retail active in DITO (+9.7% WTD), MM (+3.25% WTD) and AC Energy (+11.7% WTD). 

AC Energy released details of its upcoming stock rights offering. ACEN will be offering 2.268bn new shares to shareholders at a ratio of 1 rights share for every 1.11 shares held. ACEN’s parent (AC Energy, Inc (ACEI) will not participate in the rights offer and will have itself diluted. The price of the new shares will be set at P2.37/share; the shares will go ex-rights on Jan 8.

This exercise is part of a series of transactions that will also involve the purchase of shares by Singapore sovereign wealth fund GIC, the injection of ACEI’s international assets and an eventual follow-on offering.

Meanwhile, Ayala Corp (+0.85% WTD) is acquiring 70% of Mercado General Hospital.  Mercado General Hospital runs four hospitals and a number of other health facilities some of which operate under the Qualimed brand. Some of the 70% stake will be purchased from subsidiary Ayala Land (-0.4% WTD).

Foreigners were very light this week, accounting for only 29% of market activity. Foreigners also continued to sell the market – with net outflows this week reaching US$32m.  Sentiment was weak, with global markets again fearing COVID mutations and more lockdowns.

Losses were broad-based as 22 of the 30 index names ended the week in the red. Some profit-taking in recent outperformers was apparent. Among the top decliners for the week were conglos GTCAP (-6.1% WTD), SMC (-6.0% WTD), LTG (-5.0% WTD), and MPI (-4.8% WTD). URC (-5.7% WTD) also declined.

Meanwhile, among those making it to the winners list were First Gen (+3.0% WTD), Metrobank (+2.4% WTD) SM Prime (+1.7% WTD) and Puregold (+1.4% WTD).

Meralco (+0.4% WTD) is extending its “no-disconnection” policy to end of January for small users. Customers with monthly consumption below 200kwh will not be disconnected even if their payments are overdue. These customers comprise 47% of total customers and account for <10% by kwh usage. The grace period extended by Meralco since the pandemic has resulted in a rise in receivables by as much as P25bn as of September.

In the meantime,  Bloomberry (-2.4% WTD) creditors agreed to waive the financial covenant  testing relating to its debts until Jun 2023.

Meanwhile, PLDT (-1.5% WTD) and Globe (-4.3% WTD) are aiming to build as many as 2,000 telecom towers each next year, adding to some 10,000 they each now have.  PLDT has already upped its capex guidance to P88bn – P92bn for next year, up from P70bn this year.  This comes ahead of the impending launch of new operator Dito which says it has 1900 towers.

Corp News

AC Energy Philippines to conduct stock rights offering

AC Energy Philippines (ACEN PM) will conduct a P5.4bn stock rights offering to partially fund its power projects as well as potential “inorganic growth opportunities”. A total of 2.27bn rights shares to be offered (1 rights share for every 1.11 ACEN shares held) . The rights offering will increase shares outstanding by 17%.  

The offer price is at P2.37/share. The ex-date will be January 8.

ACEN’s parent, AC Energy Inc (ACEI), will not participate in the rights offering until after minority shareholders’ orders have been filled. 

Ayala Corp to acquire 70% stake in Mercado General Hospital

Ayala Corp will acquire a 70% stake in Mercado General Hospital Inc (MGHI). The stakes will be purchased from Ayala Land (39%), Mercado Group (15%). 16% will be from new shares. 

MGHI operates 4 hospitals, 5 clinics, and 2 ambulatory facilities, some of which are under the “Qualimed” brand. 

Bloomberry creditors grant deferment of covenant testing

Bloomberry signed with a syndicate of banks an amendment to the P73.5bn Omnibus Loan and Security Agreement for additional principal amount of P20bn. 

The syndicate of banks also granted the deferment of financial covenant testing on the existing term loan facilities from 30 September 2020 to 30 June 2023. The syndicate banks are: BDO, China Bank (CHIB PM), PNB, Robinsons Bank, and United Coconut Planters Bank (UCPB).

 

Phoenix Petroleum (PNX PM) to redeem preferred shares

PNCX disclosed that it plans to redeem all outstanding preferred shares PNX3A at the redemption price of P100/sh. The prefs were issued on 18 Dec 2015 and have an annual interest rate of 7.427% (option to redeem on the 3rd year, subject to a step-up on the 5th year if not redeemed).  

 

PLDT and Globe to roll our 4,000 cell sites next year

PLDT and Globe will each install 2,000 cell towers next year. Globe said that several local government units in Palawan, Nueva Ecija, Negros Oriental, Oriental Mindoro, Occidental Mindoro, Cebu, Quezon, Batangas, Bulacan, and Ifugao have set-up their respective one-stop shops.  The company has installed 1,050 new cell sites and upgraded 10,876 sites this year. Meanwhile, PLDT’s Smart Communications said that it has over 10,000 sites, including over 700 new ones installed this year. 

 

Megawide seeks OPS reinstatement; wins P20bn worth of contract with Suntrust

Megawide submitted a motion for reconsideration to the Manila International Airport Authority (MIAA) on the OPS it was previously awarded for its P109bn unsolicited proposal. 

Meanwhile, Megawide secured a P20bn construction contract from Suntrust Home Developers to build the latter’s Main Hotel Casino.

As at end-September, Megawide’s order book stood at P40.7bn

 

First Gen selects three preferred tenderers for floating storage regasification unit

First Gen has selected three preferred tenderers for the charter of a Floating Storage Regasification Unit (FSRU) in FGEN’s Interim Offshore LNG Terminal. These would be BW Gas Limited, Dynagas Ltd., and Hoegh LNG Asia Pte. Ltd. 

The three companies will proceed to the next stage of the binding tender process. The Offshore LNG Terminal is expected to be operational by 3Q22

Meralco to begin disconnections at the end of Jan 2021

Meralco has extended the no-disconnection policy until the end of January 2021. Meralco President Ray Espinosa said that the extended grace period would benefit more than three million Meralco customers with consumption of 200 kilowatt hours and below during the billing month of December 2020. Around 47% of Meralco’s customer base could benefit from the extension. 

Emperador to expand to more markets next year

Emperador plans to expand to more countries in Africa and South America next year. The company’s brandy and whisky products are currently available in over 100 countries.

 
 

Macro News

BSP to keep rates low until end-2022

The BSP is committed to keeping interest rates low until the end of 2022, according to BSP Governor Benjamin Diokno.  Low rates will be kept until the economy recovers to its previous 6.5-7.5% growth and unemployment is down to 5% range. The BSP still has room for further monetary easing amid a benign inflation environment (2-4%) until 2024. The BSP’s Monetary Board is set to meet again on Feb 11. 

BIR collections for November exceed goal

The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) collected P182bn in November, exceeding the P145.4bn program. This was +19.7% higher MoM but lower by -21.6% YoY.  This brings YT-November collections to P1.78tn, down 12% YoY. This already exceeds the government’s FY20E of P1.69tn. For FY21, the BIR is tasked to collect P2tn. 

House of Representatives approves bill granting fee discounts on OFW remittances

The House of Representatives approved (on third reading) a bill which seeks to reduce fees on OFW remittances by up to 50%. The bill would also prohibit financial and non-financial intermediaries from raising their current remittance fees without prior consultation with the DOF, BSP, or Philippine Overseas Employment Administration (POEA). However, the measure would also grant tax discounts to intermediaries for the cost of services to OFWs.

ERC issues new rules on plant outages

The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) has issued new rules on plant outages to ensure ample supply in the grid.  The rules also seek to set a reliability performance benchmark per technology for all generating units. 

 

Gov’t allows more provincial buses, UV express to operate in Metro Manila

The Land Transportation Franchising Regulatory Board has opened more provincial bus routes from Dec 21. The routes will connect the provinces of Pampanga and Batangas to Metro Manila. The government also allowed more UV express units to operate. 

Treasury to borrow P140bn from domestic mart in January

The Bureau of Treasury has set a P140bn borrowing program for January. The Treasury is planning to borrow P80bn via T-bills and P60bn via T-bonds. This is higher than the P120bn program for December. 

 

Gov’t borrowings have reached P3.224tn in 10M20, exceeding the full-year program. This is due to a new round of cash advances from the BSP in October worth P540bn. 

 

The government has a  budget deficit target at 7.6% of GDP in 2020.  For FY21, the government plans to borrow another P3tn to plug a deficit of 8.9% of GDP.
