REGIS REPORT

29 December 2022

The PSEi closed out the year with a 0.4% gain in the final week of the year Global markets were weak, as renewed concerns on the COVID spread from China resulted in risk off sentiment. This week’s performance took the PSEi’s YTD performance to -7.8% – marking its third straight year of closing with declines.

It was a short, 3-session week, with investors generally quiet amidst the holidays. Average daily turnover stood at  a mere US$76m.

Foreigners ended the week as buyers for US$729k, with inflows in Ayala Land and PLDT were offset by outflows in Monde and SM. For year-end 2022, foreigners were net sellers for $1.025bn

As expected, things on the ground were quiet, with a dearth of corporate news this week.  Nonetheless, index gainers outnumbered losers 15:13.

Leading this week was Converge (+14.1% WoW), which posted a recovery after recent weakness.

PLDT (+7.1% WoW) also gained, after falling 14.6% and 16.8% in two weeks prior, on selling related to its admission of P48bn in capex budget overruns in the past four years. The company says it is now in talks with its suppliers to negotiate down the P48bn. While the results of these negotiations could help mitigate the financial impact, concerns on  of this incident, the hit to credibility could take longer to fade.

Other leading gainers were Ayala Land (+5.5% WoW) and Meralco (+5.4% WoW), and Puregold (+3.0% WoW).

PSEi rebal addition candidate Union Bank (+2.5% WoW) continued to rise. Union Bank said it aims to raise P12bn from its stock rights offering, down from the initial P20bn. This is equivalent to 6.7% of market cap. In conversations with management, the bank reckons that the P12bn will be enough to support growth. An offering would help the bank rectify its low free float, which has been an obstacle to inclusion in local and foreign indices.

Today is PSE’s final trading day for the year. The market will resume on Tuesday Jan 3,2023. Wishing you all the best in the New Year!

CORPORATE NEWS
 
 
 
 

PLDT to replace some projects to reduce overrun

PLDT is in talks with suppliers for discounts in order to reduce its P48bn budget overrun. The discussions also include the possible replacement of certain projects with new projects that will improve revenue growth and customer experience.

 

ICT acquires remaining 20% stake in Bauan port

ICT acquired an additional 20% stake in Bauan International Port for P507mn from Marubeni Corp. ICT now owns 100% of Bauan International Port. The port is a 20-hectare facility in Bauan, Batangas. It supports cargo movements in and out of Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, and Quezon.

RRHI to merge Supermarket and CVS business units

RRHI will be merging its Supermarket and CVS business units effective July 2023. This is aimed at reducing overhead costs and maximizing synergies (e.g. procurement, distribution) between the two formats. Supermarkets and CVS accounted for 57% and 4% of 9M22 consolidated sales, respectively. The transaction should not have a material impact to our estimates and valuation for RRHI.

UBP lowers size of stock rights offering

UnionBank reduced the size of its stock rights from P20bn to P12bn. This is 6.7% of market cap, 8.2% of 3Q22 book value. UBP is currently trading at 1.2X 3Q22 book value.

 

 
 
MACRO NEWS
 
 
 
 

Government meets revenue targets

Collections by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) and Bureau of Customs (BoC) reached P3.2trn this year, 2.2% higher than the target. In 2023, the two agencies have been tasked to collect P3.436trn. This is 7% higher than collections in 2022. The BIR’s 2023 target is P2.67trn, 11.6% higher than the 2022 target of P2.39trn while the BoC’s 2023 target is P765.4bn, 6.1% higher than the P721.5bn target in 2022. Government revenue as a percentage of GDP is slated to decline from 16.1% in 2022 to 15.4% in 2023.

NGCP files application for rate reset

The National Grid Corp. of the Philippines (NGCP) has officially filed its application for the resetting of the transmission wheeling rates (RTWR) on Dec 22. The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) said NGCP also filed a petition to change the coverage of the review period to cover 2016-2020 (4RY). To recall, the ERC on Nov. 28, promulgated Resolution 08 (the "Amended RTWR"), covering years 2016 to 2022 (4RY) and years 2023 to 2027 (5RY). The last transmission reset completed by the ERC was in 2010 for the regulatory period covering the years 2011 to 2015. The ERC said that the rate reset is expected to result in lower transmission charges for the benefit of electricity consumers. Hearings on the application and the process of verification of the completeness and accuracy of the information submitted by NGCP will commence next month.

House Bill reforming property tax assessment transmitted to Senate

The House of Representatives approved on third reading a bill “Instituting reforms in real property valuation and assessment…”. The bill has been transmitted to the Senate. The bill seeks to transfer the responsibility of determining market values (for purposes of real property taxes) from Local Government Units (LGU) to the national government (specifically, a division of the Department of Finance). LGUs (such as city governments) have generally been reluctant to raise market values/property taxes because the elected officials might lose their reelections. Some LGUs have not updated their values in over 10 years. However, local government units will still determine the tax rate to be applied for real property taxes. Thus, local governments might lower the tax rate on real property in order to limit the impact on their constituents. Pre-COVID, real property taxes comprised 3%-7% of the pretax profits of the developers we cover.

Bureau of Customs increases 2023 revenue target

The Bureau of Customs raised its 2023 revenue target to P901bn, from P765bn. The higher target is partly due to higher exchange rate assumptions.

GDP growth to exceed 7.5% in 2022

Finance Secretary Benjamin Diokno said that the Philippines will exceed the government’s full-year GDP growth target of 6.5%-7.5%. This implies that 4Q22 GDP will grow over 6.9%.
