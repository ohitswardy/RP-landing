REGIS REPORT

02 December 2023

The market dropped -0.4% WoW to 6,245.30 following its sharp rally the previous week.

Turnover jumped to US$105.6m (+75.7% WoW) on the back of the MSCI rebalancing last Thursday. Although there were no additions or deletions to the index, the Phils saw outflows as most local stocks saw downweights across the board.

Biggest drags to the index were URC  (-6.6% WoW) as the stock saw heavy foreign outflows for US$3.5m. Earnings results for the quarter were also soft as inflation continues to pressure consumer wallets.  

Conglomerates likewise dampened the index as foreigners let go of GTCAP (-4.5% WoW), SM (-1.1% WoW) and AEV (-3.4% WoW) for US$2.8m, US$1.3m, and US$1.2m respectively.

Only property names outperformed the market. SMPH gained (+1.5% WoW) after the reclamation projects were given approval to resume. The lifting of the suspension removes the overhang on arguably the single most important project of SMPH -- a project accounting for P9/share (23%) of its P40/share NAV. ALI (+3.7% WoW) followed suit as foreign demand worth US$7.0m lifted the name.  SMPH also saw inflows worth US$2.7m.

AREIT (+4.3% WoW) also announced another asset injection into the Ayala Land in exchange for new shares. AREIT will issue 642m new shares to ALI in exchange for properties what are arguably among the poshest properties in Makati (and the country). For perspective, AREIT today has 2.4bn shares before the recently announced 199m share issuance to ACEN (ACEN PM) is completed.

Headline foreign inflows were strong at US$34.9m, mostly skewed into ICT for US$16.7m.

Philippine Property - 3Q23 recap: residential presales decline; office vacancy rises
 
 
by Carl Sy, CFA +632 8894 6646 Carl.sy@regis.ph
 

Weaker residential and office segments

Two of the three main property segments showed a worsening in 3Q23, as residential reservation sales dropped while office vacancy rates increased. On the other hand, the mall business remained the pillar of strength for the sector, with revenue rising on a QoQ and YoY basis.

Residential reservation sales fall 16% QoQ and 4% YoY

Aggregate reservation sales of the developers we cover fell 16% QoQ and 4% YoY to P108bn. Thus, after exceeding pre-COVID peaks for two consecutive quarters, reservation sales are back down to just 86% of the 2019 average. While this is not a positive development, we do not consider it a cause for alarm either since large quarterly swings are common for this account.

Residential revenue also remains far below the pre-COVID levels, likely due to a combination of: 1) looser payment schemes delaying revenue recognition, and 2) cancellation rates remaining high. Thus, relative to the pre-COVID period, reported reservation sales alone would be an insufficient gauge of the true strength of residential demand.

Meanwhile, Colliers estimates that capital values of Metro Manila condominiums in the secondary market inched up for the eighth consecutive quarter, up 4% YoY. Prices of most units are still 5%-20% below 2019 levels.

Office vacancy rises; new work-from-home Bill a risk to the sector

Office vacancy rose 1ppt QoQ to 18%, largely due to new buildings completed in 3Q23. While the surge in POGO office take-up in 2Q23 was head-turning, demand from this segment quickly fizzled.

Some office tenants have renewed office contracts at lower rental rates. We expect more such cases in 2024, since 1) office rental rates are currently 20%- 50% below 2019 levels, and 2) many of the contracts up for renewal next year were previously signed in 2019, when rental rates were close to the peak.

A key risk to the office segment is the proposed legislation in the House of Representatives seeking to allow BPO firms to avail of tax incentives even if employees are fully work-from-home. The impact to the sector could be large since BPO companies occupy 50%-80% of the office space of the developers we cover.

Mall revenue still grows QoQ and YoY

Aggregate mall revenue of the top 3 mall operators grew another 2% QoQ (+22% YoY) in 3Q23 to P25.8bn, 11% higher than the 3Q19 level. While 4Q23 revenue should be even stronger due to the holiday season, the YoY growth will likely drop sharply, as base effects from the removal of rental concessions dissipate.

 
SM Prime - Pasay reclamation project to resume
 
 
by Carl Sy, CFA +632 8894 6646 carl.sy@regis.ph
 

SMPH Pasay reclamation project exempted from ban

The Pasay City government announced that President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. exempted its coastal redevelopment project with SM Prime from the ban on Manila Bay reclamation. The Department of Environment and Natural Resources and the Philippine Reclamation Authority deemed the project to be compliant with all regulations.

What previously transpired?

On August 10, Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) Secretary Yulo-Loyzaga said that all Manila Bay reclamation projects were deemed suspended. The DENR would review all Manila Bay reclamation projects, citing that several individual projects were approved [by the previous administration] without considering the combined impact on the environment.

What are the terms of SM Prime’s Pasay reclamation project?

The 360-hectare Pasay reclamation is a joint project between the Pasay City government and SM Prime. The company will shoulder all the costs and will own 49% (176.4 hectares) of the reclaimed land. SM Prime’s share of the land will all be developable/sellable. The roads and other public areas will be part of Pasay City’s 51% share.

What is the status of the project?

The Notice to Proceed for the 360-hectare Pasay reclamation was granted in 2019. Due to the pandemic, however, SM Prime did not start reclamation work until 2021. A 60-hectare island was just completed in July 2023 and two 150- hectare islands were still in the process of reclamation. Naturally, there has been no physical work done since the suspension was first announced by the DENR.

SM Prime also signed an agreement for a 300-hectare reclamation project with the Paranaque city government. However, this has not been granted national government approval and we believe it is unlikely to ever receive such approval. Thus, we do not include it in our forecasts/NAV estimate.

How important is the Pasay project to SM Prime?

The Pasay reclamation project comprises 23% (or P9.04/share) of our SMPH NAV estimate. In short, if approval of the project were revoked, then our NAV estimate would drop by 23%. The reclamation project would also grant SM Prime large parcels of developable land, which are increasingly scarce in Metro Manila. We estimate that the reclaimed land would take over 20 years to fully develop (after reclamation). SMPH is trading at 19% discount to NAV (of P40.00/share), 2.2X FY24E P/B and 21.8X FY24E P/E.

 
 
CORPORATE NEWS
 
 
 
 

Court orders Megaworld to post P873mn counterbond

The Quezon City Regional Trial Court (RTC) ordered Megworld to post an P873mn counterbond to cover the potential liability claimed by Datem. Since the counterbond was sufficient to cover the potential liabilities, the RTC also discharged the Writ of Preliminary Attachment (WPA) on Megaworld’s assets. Datem filed a lawsuit against Megaworld because the latter refused to honor a September 2022 agreement in which Megaworld would pay P873mn to the contractor. The P873mn represents 5% of Megaworld FY23E net profit, as well as 0.4% of its NAV and book value. These recent events will likely make it more difficult for Megaworld to attract contractors in the future.

SPNEC moves to comply with minimum public float

Solar Philippines Power Project Holdings, Inc. (SPPPHI)  has donated additional shares of its listed subsidiary to Asia Pacific Institute for Green Development, Inc. (Green Development) to comply with the stock exchange’s minimum public ownership requirement. SP New Energy Corp (SPNEC) disclosed that its parent firm executed a deed of donation for an additional 1.58bn unlisted and fully paid common shares of SPNEC to Green Development. “The shares donated by SPPPHI to Green Development in total represent 6.2% of the issued and outstanding shares of SPNEC, and SPNEC would thereby comply with the MPO requirement”. As of November, SPPHI held 74.38% ownership of SPNEC’s outstanding common shares. Separately, SPNEC said that it plans to pursue several transactions to increase its public float further after the lifting of its trading suspension. The quoted ownership numbers do not yet factor in the new common shares to be issued to Mgen Renewable Energy (15.7bn shares ~ 31.4% of enlarged outstanding shares).

ADB: Potential NAIA bidders seek extension

The Asian Development Bank (ADB) is requesting the Department of Transportation (DOTr) to extend the deadline for the submission of bids for the NAIA PPP by a month. ADB is the transaction adviser of the government and cited that four prospective bidders have requested for an extension; Warned that if deadline is not moved, only two prospective bidders would be able to submit bids. ADB is proposing to move the bid submission date to Jan 29, 2024 from the original Dec 27, 2023 deadline. There are currently six “active” prospective bidders which have been participating in pre-bid activities. The P170.6bn project aims to raise passenger capacity to 62mn annually from 32mn today over the 15-year concession period (extendable by 10 years).

AREIT reveals more information regarding upcoming property acquisitions

AREIT will issue 642mn new shares to Ayala Land in a property-for-share swap.

The properties to be acquired are:

Ayala Triangle Tower Two office building (in Makati)

Greenbelt mall 3 and 5 (in Makati)

Holiday Inn and Suites Makati

SEDA (hotel) Ayala Center Cebu

The properties are valued at P21.8bn

This implies that the AREIT shares will be priced at P34/share. AREIT said that the cap rate on the transaction is 6.8% (on the first year). Note that only buildings will be acquired (no land). The hotel and mall assets will be leased by Ayala Land for 25 years. Ayala Land will pay a fixed rent with escalation of 2.5% per annum, subject to renegotiation every 5 years. AREIT will also acquire SEDA Lio in El Nido, Palawan from Ayala Land subsidiary Econorth Resort Ventures for P1.19bn. This transaction is also valued at 6.8% cap rate.

 

AREIT will also acquire 276 hectares of industrial land by issuing 199mn new shares to ACEN. The property is valued at P6.77bn, implying that the AREIT shares to be issued will also be priced at P34/share. ACEN will subsequently lease the land from AREIT for 25 years at a fixed rental rate with escalation of 1.5% per annum. AREIT said that the cap rate for this transaction is 6.9% (on the first year). Due to the two property-for-share transactions, Ayala Land will have to sell 280mn AREIT shares to the public in order for AREIT to meet public float requirements. At the last closing price of P29.85/share, this would be equivalent to P8.4bn. AREIT currently offers 7.6% FY24E dividend yield (based on consensus forecast). Regis estimates that the transactions will be approximately DPS neutral. All of AREIT’s mall, hotel, and land assets are under fixed-rent agreements. To an extent, AREIT will not benefit from better occupancy/revenue of the assets. The rent agreements have escalation rates of 1.5% to 3% per annum.

Meralco starts bidding for 1,200MW power supply

Manila Electric Co (MER PM) said it is calling on interested power generation companies to participate in a competitive selection process (CSP) for 1,200MW baseload capacity. The deadline to submit an expression of interest is on December 11 while a pre-bid conference will be held on December 18. Submission of bids is set for January 23, 2024. The 1,200MW baseload supply seeks to replace the terminated power supply agreements with South Premiere Power Corp (SPPC) covering 670MW; San Miguel Energy Corp covering 330MW and the 200MW unsolicited proposal from Solar Philippines Batangas Baseload Corp. Meralco has a 300MW emergency power supply agreement with SPPC until March 25, 2024. The Department of Energy (DOE) issued a certificate of conformity on the terms of reference (TOR) for the 15-year PSAs which will be effective once approved by the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC).

ICTSI to complete Melbourne expansion by December

Victoria International Container Terminal (VICT) operating at the Port of Melbourne in Australia is set to complete Phase 3A of its expansion project in December. The capacity expansion will allow VICT to service neo-Panamax ships with capacities of up to 14,000 TEUs and introduce economies of scale across the supply chain. Phase 3 will cost AUD235mn will be carried out in two phases, with Phase 3A expected to increase the capacity of the port by 25% to 1.25mn TEUs. Meanwhile, Phase 3B will increase the port’s capacity by 250k TEUs to 1.5mn TEUs by 2025. Regis valuation assumes the entire capex for Phases 3A/3B. The expansion is timely, as VICT secured a major client that will allow the port to recover all the volumes it lost this year. Regis did not assume a volume recovery. The Regis 24E EPS growth forecast of +10% assumes group-wide volumes will rise by just +1% YoY. VICT is a wholly owned unit of ICTSI (ICT PM); Regis estimates it accounts for c. 6% of volumes and 8% of revenues. ICT is trading at 8.4x/7.6x FY23E/FY24E EV/EBITDA, below peers (11x) and historical average (11x).

Supreme Court rules against Kazuo Okada in leadership row

The Supreme Court lifted the status quo ante order which temporarily reinstated Kazuo Okada as the CEO of casino resort Okada Manila. The order effectively removed Okada as the shareholder, director, chairman and CEO of Okada Manila after the High Court dismissed Kazuo Okada’s complaint against one of the hotel’s parent companies, Tiger Resort, Leisure and Entertainment Incorporated (TRLEI), which the court said was filed beyond the prescribed period. “The Court, after careful consideration of the records, found no merit in Kazuo’s petition. It found that the allegations and reliefs sought in Kazuo’s complaint clearly show that it is an action for election contest and was, thus, subject to the prescriptive period under the Interim Rules”. The SC also said that Okada was properly removed as the TRLEI official, saying that he is just a nominal shareholder and that he is not the controlling shareholder of Okada Holdings Limited.

Government raises US$1bn from sukuk bonds

The Bureau of the Treasury said the government raised US$1bn after it tapped the global Islamic financial market for the first time. The Bonds have a 5.5-year tenor with a coupon rate of 5.045%. The maiden issue was met with strong demand as the order book reached US$2.45bn, oversubscribing the auction by 4.9x and was upsized from an initial target of US$500mn. The government has raised a total of US$5.26bn from global bonds this year.

Budget deficit shrinks to P34bn in October

 

The budget deficit narrowed to P34bn in October, down from P99bn a year ago and P251bn in Sep 2023 due largely to (a) timing differences from VAT collections and (b) moderation in primary spending. Revenues jumped 34% YoY and 51% MoM due to the timing of VAT collections. Meanwhile, customs collections rose +3.8% YoY during the month, after posting declines since June. Expenses rose 8% YoY on the back of a +78% jump in interest expenses. Primary spending growth slowed to +1.8% YoY in October after rising +9% YoY in 3Q23. This brings YT-October budget deficit to P1.018tn, down 8.5% YoY.

DPWH announces Swiss Challenge for TPLEX Extension project

The Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) is inviting interested parties to submit their comparative proposals for the Tarlac-Pangasinan-La Union Expressway (TPLEX) Extension Project. The Instructions to Comparative Proponents and other relevant qualifications and tender documents will be available from December 6-20, 2023. TPLEX Extension Project, which will be implemented through public-private partnership (PPP), is a 59.4 km 4-lane toll road which will start from the TPLEX in Rosario, La Union and will terminate at San Juan, La Union. The project proponent is San Miguel (SMC PM) and is estimated to cost P27.5bn.

Senate approves 2024 P5.768tn budget

The Senate approved on final reading its version of the bill on the proposed P5.768tn national budget. The Senate version had minor tweaks on defense and education budgets, according to Senator Juan Edgardo M. Angara. He also mentioned a “Significant increase in allocation for the Dept. of Defense, Armed Forces of the Philippines, and Philippine Coast Guard”. There is also a P1bn hike in the budget of the Department of Science and Technology and an additional P50mn for the Department of Education. The Senate approved a request from the National Economic and Development Authority to establish an innovations revolving fund to provide grants for innovation programs and projects. Representatives from the Senate and the House will now meet in a Bicameral Conference Committee to reconcile conflicting provisions of their respective budget bills. President Marcos Jr. is likely to sign the 2024 national budget before he leaves for Japan in mid-December, according to Finance Secretary Benjamin Diokno.

NWPC approves pay hike for Mimaropa workers

The National Wages and Productivity Commission (NWPC) has approved the daily minimum wage hike of P40 (+11-12%) for MIMAROPA region (IV-B). The wage increase will take effect on Dec 7. The government has approved wage hikes in 13 out of 17 regions. The average wage hike so far is c. 8%. In 2022, the government implemented wage hikes of +12%-14% throughout the country.
