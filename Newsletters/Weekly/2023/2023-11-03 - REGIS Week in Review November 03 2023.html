REGIS REPORT

03 November 2023

The PSEi ends this 2-day trading week up +0.5% and closed at 5,989 still below the 6,000 technical support level which, hopefully not, could start becoming a resistance level if the market does not boost out of this. The PSEi was trading as high as 6,080 early on Friday but faded steadily towards the close. AGI (+6.1%), LTG (+3.4%) and WLCON (+3.0%) were this week’s biggest gainers. Meanwhile, MONDE (-7.2%), GTCAP (-4.4%) and SCC (-3.5%) were this week’s index big losers.

Average daily market turnover this week stood at $69m, up +20% WoW. Foreigners continue to be net sellers this week and sold for a total net outflow of $16.2m. Top net foreign sold stocks were SMPH $2.6m, GTCAP $1.8m, SM $1.7m, MONDE $1.4m and BDO $1.4m. Meanwhile, ICT $1.1m was the only one that saw a decent inflow for the week.

Earnings continue to trickle in, Aboitiz Power reported 3Q23 core income of P8.8bn (-15%QoQ, -5%YoY) but still meaningfully higher than typical profits, buoyed by a new normal for its generation business. Operationally, volumes for both generation (8%YoY) and distribution (5%YoY) jumped. The timing of refunds, however, dragged EBITDA from its distribution utilities 61% lower QoQ. AP’s 9M23 income of P26.7bn (45%YoY) implies it is well on its way to exceed Regis (P34.8bn) and consensus (P30.8bn) estimates for the full year.

Meanwhile, Semirara 3Q23 core profit fell 71% QoQ, 69% YoY to P2.95bn, as heavy rains affected coal sales volumes. This brought 9M23 profit down 10% YoY to P22.4bn, lagging Regis’ and consensus forecasts. Even though the biggest source of disappointment was lower coal sales volumes, the higher inventory level and recovery of the Indonesian Coal Price Index (ICI) so far in 4Q23 suggest the company can still catch up in 4Q. 

DMCI following Semirara’s disappointing 3Q23 results also reported lower-than-expected 3Q23 profit of P3.84bn, down 53% QoQ and 44% YoY. The nickel mining and construction units also fell short of expectations but was compensated by higher-than-expected contributions from the property and water segments.

RESEARCH
Wilcon Depot - 3Q23: Reopening momentum fades on weaker consumer sentiment
 
 
by Carissa Mangubat +632 8894 6647 carissa.mangubat@regis.ph
 

9M23 net profit falls short of expectations as SSS contract in 3Q

WLCON’s 3Q23 net profit fell 18% YoY to P908mn, bringing the 9M23 figure to P2.7bn (-8% YoY), behind Regis and consensus. 9M23 net sales (+5% YoY to P25.9bn) are trending behind estimates mainly due to weaker-than-expected same-store sales (SSS). In 3Q23 alone, SSS contracted by 5.2%. Operating profit (-5% YoY in 9M / -14% YoY in 3Q) was dragged lower by higher opex.

SSS decline continues as reopening momentum fades 3Q23

SSS declined at a faster pace (-5.2%) vs 2Q23 (-3.4%). While same-store tickets managed to inch up (+2% YoY), same-store transaction amounts fell 7.5%. The drop in SSS was partly attributed to a high YoY base but it was also a result of increased competition (particularly in Metro Manila), and soft demand.

Pre-operating expenses drag margins

Gross margin was resilient and rose ~20bps, in line with higher contribution from in-house and exclusive brands. However, operating profit fell 14% YoY to P1.4bn in 3Q as the company continued to incur proportionately higher opex for its new stores. WLCON has another 13 stores in the pipeline until 2024, when it expects to reach its 100-store target. WLCON had 87 stores as of end-Sep.

Macro headwinds to weigh on top line

While sales should see some sequential improvement heading into the seasonally strong 4Q, underlying demand continues to be soft because of inflationary pressures. High inflation is constraining consumer budgets and customers are delaying major home improvement plans. We believe top line softness could persist in the near term until inflationary pressures ease.

 
 
CORPORATE NEWS
 
 
 
 

Meralco starts CSP for 1,800MW supply needs

Meralco has commenced the competitive selection process (CSP) for 1,800 MW of power supply. This will involve a 15-year contract beginning Dec 2024. This is meant to replace the capacity covered by Meralco’s 2021 PSA with San Mgiuel (SMC PM), which was terminated earlier this year. The submission of interest for interested power generation first is due on Nov 13 and the bid submission deadline is set on Dec 26.

 

Semirara 3Q23 core income of P2.95bn (-71% QoQ, 69% YoY) disappoints on lower coal volumes, power ASPs

Semirara reported headline 3Q23 profit fell 66% YoY to P3.40bn. Stripping out FX gains and a one-off refund, Regis estimates core income fell 71% QoQ, 69% YoY to P2.95bn. The 9M23 core profit stood at P22.4bn, down 10% YoY and trailing Regis’ FY23E: P33.3bn and consensus estimates: P32.7bn. Regis attributes the underperformance to lower-than-expected coal mining sales volumes and lower power ASPs. Contributions from mining fell 74% QoQ/YoY to P2.0bn in 3Q23, as coal shipments dropped 22% YoY to 2.5mn MT and ASPs fell 20% QoQ (36% YoY) to P3,315/MT. Meanwhile, coal shipments fell due to heavier rainfall and lower exports of high-grade coal to South Korea.

 

This takes 9M23 coal sales volumes to 10.5mn MT, down 13% YoY and trending behind management’s guidance (15-16mn MT). Coal inventory rose to 3.0mn MT in end-Sept, up from 2.8mn MT in end-2022, suggesting ample inventory to catch-up in 4Q. The 9M23 ASP per MT stood at P3,964 (-24% YoY) vs FY23E: P3,895; ICI4 spot at US$62/MT vs 3Q23 average of US$52/MT. The contributions from power fell 54% QoQ and 35% YoY to P997mn in 3Q23 due to lower plant availability and spot prices. Recall that SPLGC Unit 2 went on extended outage from mid-June to end-Aug; Other plants also suffered from occasional deration. Power ASPs fell 22% QoQ/23% YoY to P4.82/kwh in 3Q23, bringing 9M23 ASP to P5.75/kwh (+2% YoY) – trending below forecast (P6.14/kwh). As of end-Sep 2023, net cash stood at P19bn (P4.5/sh); Recall that SCC declared a special div of P3.5/sh (ex-div: Oct 20; Payment: Nov 8).

DMCI 9M23 profit falls short at P19.87bn (-25%)

DMCI’s headline 3Q23 profit fell 44% YoY to P4.09bn. Excluding FX adj and one-offs, Regis estimates core income fell 53% QoQ and 44% YoY to P3.84bn. The 9M23 core profit at P19.87bn, down 25% YoY and trailing Regis’ FY23E: P27.6bn and consensus: P28.3bn. The disappointment largely came from Semirara, nickel mining and construction. These offset the higher-than-expected contributions from property and Maynilad. The property unit recorded profits of P1.37bn (+21% YoY) in 3Q23 and P3.86bn (flat) in 9M23. This is trending better than forecast due to higher-than-expected income from forfeitures. Reservation sales surged 122% QoQ to P11.9bn (net of cancellations), thanks to new launches. This brings 9M23 reservation sales to 28.4bn (+11% YoY), trending well ahead of forecast (P30bn).

 

Contributions from Maynilad rose 70% YoY, 28% QoQ to P605mn in 3Q23 due likely to better water supply; Billed volume growth accelerated to 3% in 3Q23 from 1% in 2Q23. Meanwhile, construction profits fell 32% YoY to P459mn in 9M23, trending behind forecast due to slower completions and lower net margins. The nickel mining unit recorded a P154mn net loss in 3Q23 after ASPs plunged 59% QoQ, 52% YoY to US$20 per MT due to lower global prices and nickel grade. The 3Q23 profit from the off-grid power unit improved by 16% QoQ and 26% YoY due to better margins from its new plants in Palawan and Masbate.

AP 3Q23 softer QoQ as distrib biz corrects; 9M23 core income of P26.7bn (45%YoY) still tracking ahead of est

AP reported a 3Q23 core income of P8.8bn (-15%QoQ, -5%YoY). It was a soft quarter even compared to last year, which management attributed to timing of refunds for distribution utilities (3Q distrib EBITDA was down 33%YoY). Regis notes that 3Q23 is seasonally lower QoQ due to the trajectory of energy demand and prices after the summer peak season. This nevertheless brings core income for 9M23 to P26.7bn, (45%YoY), mildly ahead of Regis (P34.8bn) but well ahead of consensus (P30.8bn). AP’s generation and RES EBITDA for 9M23 was P46.7bn, an increase of 20%YoY and implied 3Q EBITDA of P16.5bn (-3%QoQ, 4%YoY). Meanwhile, the energy sold in 9M was up 21%YoY and implied 3Q was 8,757GWh (-3%QoQ, 8%YoY). Distribution EBITDA amounted to P7.2bn in 9M23 (50%YoY) and implied 3Q EBITDA was P1.2bn (-61%QoQ, -33%YoY). Energy sales hit 1,586GWh (0%QoQ, 5%YoY).

SLEX, MCX to increase toll next month

The Toll Regulatory Board approved the implementation of higher toll fee charges at South Luzon Expressway (SLEX) and Muntinlupa-Cavite Expressway (MCX). The adjustments were approved in September, and this was the first periodic toll rate adjustments for SLEX since 2011. Class 1 motorists traversing Alabang to Calamba will see an additional P10 fee (+10%) from the current fee of P96. While class 1 motorists traveling from Calamba to Santo Tomas Batangas will see a P4 hike (+16%) from the current P25 toll fee. The new toll fees will become effective by Nov 3. The TRB said the adjustments will be implemented in two tranches: in 2023 and in 2024.

 
 
MACRO NEWS
 
 
 
 

Debt service swells by 57% YoY to P1.4tn in 9M23

Data from the Bureau of Treasury showed the government’s debt service rose by 57% YoY to P1.4tn in 9M23. Amortization payments rose 92% YoY to P940bn while interest payments rose 15% YoY to P460bn. As of end-Aug 2023, the government’s debt stock stood at P14.35tn, up 6.9% from end-Dec 2022 levels of P13.4tn.

EV subsidy pilot program set in 3Q24

The Department of Trade and Industry plans to begin the pilot phase of the subsidy program for buyers of electric vehicles. The subsidies are initially set at P500k per unit for public utility vehicles, P20k per unit for three-wheelers and 10k for e-bikes. The subsidy program will be funded by the United Nationals Industrial Development Organization and the Global Environmental Facility. To date, there are more the 16k EV units in the country (data includes hybrids). The sales of EVs grew to 2,536 units in 1Q23, up 495% YoY. The EV Association of the Philippines (EVAP) targets to grow EV sales to a cumulative 6.61mn units by 2030, majority of which will be two-wheeled vehicles such as e-motorcycles.
