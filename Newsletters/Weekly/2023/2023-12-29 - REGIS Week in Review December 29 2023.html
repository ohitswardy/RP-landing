REGIS REPORT

29 December 2023

The PSEi dropped -1.1% WoW to close at 6450 just below the PSEi’s 6,500 resistance level. This brought the index's full year 2023 performance to -1.8%. Additioannly, the market saw a net foreign inflow of $6m this week mainly due to net inflows in ALI $6.1m, JFC $2.9m, ICT $2.4m and BDO $2.1m. On the other hand, URC -$4m, MBT -$1.5m and SMPH -$1m were the top net foreign sold stocks. Meanwhile, the biggest index gainers for the week were AGI +11.9%, ACEN +8.7%, NIKL +7.5%, CNVRG +6.9% and SCC +6.7%. Top laggards were all conglomerates, SMC -10.2%, AEV -9.4% and JGS -9.2%, which seems to suggest that there was some type of passive fund redemptions on the last trading day of the week and year. Average daily market turn over dropped 31% WoW from $101m last week to roughly $70m this week.

Pretty light on news for this short week and there were just a few milestone headlines that came out. Four consortiums, which included most of the influential business groups in the country, submitted bids for the NAIA PPP project. The government aims to complete the review of the qualification documents within 10 days and thereafter proceed with the review of technical and financial proposals.  Assuming all goes well, the government aims to award the concession agreement within 1Q24. This is the first big-ticket PPP project of the Marcos administration.

Meanwhile, Prime Infrastructure, potential IPO for 2024, signed a 15-year Terminal Lease Agreement with First Gen for the latter’s LNG storage and regasification terminal in Batangas City. The deal would reduce FGEN’s risk relating to the terminal, both financial and operating while allow Prime infrastructure to expand on its utilities business. Prime Infrastructure has control of the Malampaya gas field, which supplies gas to First Gen.

Lastly, the government's budget deficit fell 25% YoY to P93bn in November, largely because of the 10% YoY drop in primary spending. The good news is that there is typically a seasonal spike in primary spending in December. However, even if the government achieves its full-year target, this would still imply a 9% YoY drop in 4Q23 primary spending. For reference, in 2Q23, when primary spending "only" fell 1% YoY, GDP growth was a disappointing 4.3%.

This ends this week’s summary. Catch you all in 2024!

CORPORATE NEWS
 
 
 
 

FGEN, Prime Infrastructure ink 15-year lease for LNG terminal

Prime Infrastructure signed a 15-year Terminal Lease Agreement (TLA) with First Gen for the latter’s LNG storage and regasification terminal in Batangas City. Effectivity of the TLA is conditioned upon the satisfaction of a number of conditions precedent. The lease will form part of Prime Infrastructure’s proposed gas aggregation strategy that will enable it to deploy a tolling business model, which will in turn allow it to leverage on its existing Malampaya project facilities and its expertise in the natural gas market. Recall that Prime Infrastructure holds a 45% stake in the Malampaya gas field, the country’s sole natural gas source, which is expected to be depleted by 2027. Prime Infrastructure’s wholly-owned power subsidiary, Prime Energy, plans to undertake a US$187mn capex for the trilling of at least two deepwater wells in Camago and Malampaya East fields. Last week, FGEN awarded an LNG supply contract to TotalEnergies Gas & Power Asia Pte. Ltd (TEGPA), with its first delivery of 154,500 cubic meters slated for early Feb 2024. FGEN has four gas-fired power plants, totaling 2,000 MW.

MVP becomes Chairman, President, and CEO of SPNEC

Meralco (MER PM) subsidiary MGen Renewable Energy (MGreen) has paid the remaining balance of P8.9bn to acquire common and preferred shares of solar power developer SP New Energy Corporation (SPNEC PM). In October 2023, MGreen agreed to pay P15.9bn for 15.7bn common shares and 19.4bn preferred shares of SPNEC. MGreen made a downpayment of P7bn on 30 November 2023. This gives MGreen a 31.4% economic stake and 50.5% voting stake in SPNEC. Manuel V. Pangilinan was elected as Chairman, President, and CEO of SPNEC. Mr. Pangilinan is also CEO of PLDT (TEL PM), Meralco (MER PM), and Metro Pacific. Meanwhile, Leandro Leviste, who was previously Chairman, President, and CEO, will remain as Vice Chairman.

ACEN acquires two firms under Yoma Micro Power

Belenos Energy Corp. acquired YMP Telecom Power Inc. and its affiliate YMP Industrial Power on Dec 22 by purchasing 100% of the outstanding shares held by Yoma Micro Power Pte. Ltd

Belenos Energy is a subsidiary of AC Energy (ACEN PM). YMP is a leading company in Southeast Asia engaged in distributed renewable energy that encourages telecom operators and tower companies to decarbonize. Shares were bought at a price “less than 10% of the book value of ACEN”. As of end-Sep 2023, ACEN’s book value stood at P144.6bn. ACEN will also lend up to P200mn to YMP Telecom to fund the latter’s development costs of certain energy efficiency and renewable energy projects.

LPC: POGO office demand rebounds in 4Q23 but BPO office take-up drops

During its 4Q23 briefing, Leechiu Property Consultants (LPC) revealed that Metro Manila office vacancy fell 1ppt QoQ to 17%. Regis estimates that rental rates in most parts of Metro Manila are still 20%-50% below pre-COVID levels. Metro Manila net office take-up was about 425,000sqm, up 50% YoY but behind our FY23E forecast of 450,000sqm. Prior to the POGO industry entering the country, Metro Manila annual office take-up was 470,000sqm to 530,000sqm. The nationwide net office take-up rose 22% QoQ to 157,000sqm. LPC also said that secondary market prices of high-end Makati and Fort Bonifcacio condominium prices generally rose 5-15% YoY.

 
 
MACRO NEWS
 
 
 
 

Budget deficit narrows 25% YoY to P93bn in November as primary spending falls

The government recorded a budget deficit of P93bn in November, down 25% YoY due largely to (a) 10% drop in primary spending and (b) timing differences of VAT collections. Revenues rose 2.8% YoY on higher dividend remittances and government share from PAGCOR. Meanwhile, tax revenues dropped 9% YoY due to shift in the deadline for VAT collections (i.e. from monthly to quarterly beginning Jan 2023). Expenses fell 4.7% YoY due to a 10% YoY drop in primary spending. This brings 11M23 budget deficit to P1.11tn, down 10% YoY.

BIR slaps 1% withholding tax on online merchants

The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) has officially imposed a 1% creditable withholding tax on one-half of the gross remittances of online platform providers to their partner sellers or merchants. The Withholding tax imposed, however, will only apply to online merchants and online sellers with cumulative gross remittances of more than P500,000 for the past taxable year. The tax will apply to online shopping, food delivery platforms, platforms for booking of resort, hotel, motel, inn, house, condominium unit, bed space, room for rent and other similar lodging accommodations, and other service or product marketplaces. The BIR said the withholding tax imposed shall be in addition to the existing withholding tax obligations being imposed on e-marketplace operators. Last year, the digital economy contributed P2.08tn, equivalent to 9.4% of GDP. Of this, e-commerce had the highest growth at 26.5%, with its share to the economy reaching 20% or P416.12bn. Data showed that there are roughly two million entities involved in online selling as of last year.

President Marcos signs EO to extend lower import tariffs on rice, pork, corn

President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. has approved the extension of reduced tariffs on rice, corn, and meat products until the end of December 2024. The government cited looming negative impact of dry weather on the price and production of rice and corn and the ongoing prevalence of African Swine Fever that restricts meat trade in some exporting countries.

Investment pledges rise to record P1.16tn in 2023

Investments approved by the Board of Investments (BOI) rose to an all-time high of P1.16tn as of Dec. 18. This is the highest level recorded in BOI’s history. The previous peak was in 2019, when investment pledges reached P1.14tn. There are three more projects worth about P350bn “that are currently being assessed. Approvals cover 303 projects, which are expected to generate 47,195 jobs. Renewable energy and power sector investments reached P968bn (83% of total). Meanwhile, the information and communication sector amounted to P96.16bn (8% of total). Thee the manufacturing sector was at P22bn, infrastructure (toll roads) sector attracted investments worth P20bn and real estate sector, specifically mass housing, received P15.63bn.

State agencies’ cash usage down in November

Data from Department of Budget and Management (DBM) showed that notices of cash allocation (NCAs) inched up by just under one percent to reach P4.03tn as of the end of November. Despite the increase, government agencies recorded a lower utilization rate of 93%, compared to 94% last year. This implies that cash utilization fell 6% YoY in November alone to P366bn.
