REGIS REPORT

15 September 2023

The market fell 1.6% WoW to 6,126.34 to mark another year-to-date low as selling pressure accelerated after the index broke below its 6200 medium term support.

Investors continue to watch out for further inflation risks as the BSP will only consider policy easing once inflation remains steady within the 2-4% band. Based on estimates, rate cuts are unlikely until 1Q24, if not even later.

Losers outnumbered gainers 23:7.

Daily turnover was muted at just US$90.0m, with most of the activity happening last Friday, September 15 in light of the FTSE rebalancing.

AP (-8.1% WoW) and RRHI (-10.0% WoW) were excluded from the FTSE mid and small cap indices respectively. AP saw substantial outflows worth US$20.7m for the week.

Meanwhile, BPI (+2.8% WoW) saw the most inflows worth US$1.7m as it saw an increase in FTSE weightings.

Outside the rebalancing, sectors ended mixed as the index continued to consolidate.

Energy names outperformed. SCC (+7.8% WoW) on the back of rising coal prices while Meralco (+5.1% WoW) gained as investors flock back into defensive names amid market uncertainty.

Recent underperformers MONDE (+5.1% WoW) and Converge (+9.2% WoW) bounced back as bargain hunters finally stepped in.

Otherwise, headline outflows stood high at US$78.4m.

AP on track to build LNG facility
  • In a summit, Aboitiz Power CEO Emmanuel Rubio said that is still on track to build a liquefied natural gas (LNG) facility and complete it by 2028
  • In June, AP said it had partnered with JERA to explore the feasibility of co-firing ammonia in coal-fired plants and hydrogen in LNG facilities
  • It is targeting a 50:50 split between renewable and thermal portfolios by 2030 by building 3,700MW worth of RE projects
  • To date, the company has RE projects with a combined capacity of about 1,000MW
FGEN takes first shipment of imported LNG
  • First Gen President Giles Puno said in an interview that the first imported shipment of LNG arrived last month
  • The shipment was meant for the commissioning of the company’s interim offshore LNG terminal
  • In July, FGEN said an LNG carrier would be bringing in 154,500cm3 of LNG
  • We estimate it was enough to supply the requirements of FGEN’s plants for <20 days
  • In June, FGEN disclosed that it was in talks with Prime Infra for the lease and operations of its LNG terminal

 

ACEN forges new partnership for RE ventures in Indonesia
  • ACEN disclosed that its Indonesian unit had signed a shareholders agreement with PT Dewata Megaenergi on September 8 to look into possible RE projects in Indonesia
  • Earlier on August 18, ACEN Indonesia’s agreement with PT Trisuya Mitra was declared effective
    • The partnership focuses on large-scale solar plants, battery storage and green hydrogen
  • ACEN has 4,400MW in attributable capacity from operating and under construction facilities and is aiming to expand its RE portfolio to 20GW by 2030
Vehicle sales jump 22% YoY in August
  • CAMPI reported that vehicle sales for the month of August rose 22% YoY to 36,714 units
  • On a MoM basis, vehicle sales slipped by 1%
  • Brings YT-August vehicle sales to 276,215 units (+30% YoY)
  • Toyota’s 8M23 sales stood at 126,795 units, up 17% YoY
  • Implies sales for August 2023 alone were 16,637 units, up 13% YoY and flat MoM
  • To reach our FY23E target of 192k units (+10% YoY), sales for the rest of the year only need to average 16.3k units
  • Toyota is a 51%-owned unit of GTCAP and accounts for 28% of net profit and gross asset value (36% of NAV)
  • GTCAP is trading at a 47% discount to its market-based NAV of P1,117/sh and 6x/5x PER in FY23E/FY24E

Dept. of Finance eyes temporary zero tariffs on rice
  • The Department of Finance is proposing to temporarily slash the tariff rates for rice imports to zero or a maximum of 10% from 35% currently
  • A comprehensive approach is needed to ensure that rice supply remains sufficient and to arrest surge in rice prices
  • According to Finance Secretary Benjamin Diokno
  • Mr. Diokno said this can only be approved when Congress is in recess. Congress is set to adjourn on Sept. 30 and resume session on Nov. 6
  • Secretary Diokno said that the price ceiling on rice would likely last only for a mont
  • The Finance chief admitted he was “surprised” when Mr. Marcos first announced the price cap on rice on Sept. 1
Trade deficit narrows to US$4.2bn in July
  • Preliminary data showed the trade deficit shrunk by 30% YoY to US$4.2bn in July
  • On a MoM basis, the trade deficit in July was higher by 7% (vs. June 2023: US$3.94bn)
  • Imports fell 15% YoY in July, showing similar trends as in June
  • Due to base effects from last year’s high commodity prices, with fuel and raw material imports falling 34% YoY and 22% YoY, respectively
  • Consumer goods imports rose 6% while capital goods imports dipped 4% YoY
  • Exports fell 1% YoY, reversing positive YoY growth rates in May and June
  • Decline due to “other manufactured goods” (-50% YoY), mineral products (-79% YoY), chemicals (-34% YoY), and coconut oil (-86% YoY)
  • Electronic exports actually rose 8% YoY
  • Brings YT-July trade deficit to US$32.18bn, down 10% YoY
  • The government is forecasting exports and imports to grow by 1%/2% YoY, respectively, suggesting a trade deficit of US$60bn (~14% of GDP) in FY23
  • The trade deficit in 2022 stood at US$58.24bn (14% of GDP)
Government debt stock rises to P14.24tn in end-July
  • The national government’s total outstanding debt stood at P14.24tn in end-July, up by P96.4bn (+0.7%) MoM
  • Domestic debt accounted for 69% of total and rose 1.1% MoM
  • External debt accounted for 31% of total and declined by 0.3% MoM in PHP terms (in USD terms, foreign debt was up 0.7% MoM)
  • The government is targeting a debt-to-GDP ratio of 61% this year
  • As of end-June, the debt-to-GDP ratio stood at 61%
Government may launch $2bn dollar RTB offer this month
  • According to National Treasurer Rosalia de Leon, the government may push through with the launch of its US dollar retail Treasury bond offer this month
  • “We’re conducting financial literary seminars including in Doha and Dubai. So, we’re looking at the interest of overseas Filipino workers,” added Ms. De Leon
  • The government earlier said it was targeting an offer size of $2bn
  • In January, the government raised $3bn from its second global bond offering under the Marcos administration
  • The government plants to borrow P2.207trn this year, of which 75% will be sourced domestically
  • As of end-July, the National Government’s outstanding debt stood at P14.24trn
  • External debt rose by 9.3% to P4.43trn at the end of July, which consisted of P2.42trn in global bonds and P2.02trn in loans
DTI says rice price cap may be lifted in 2 weeks
  • Accdg to Dept of Trade (DTI) Secretary Alfredo Pascual, the rice price cap may be lifted in 2 weeks when local harvest begins and more imports arrive
  • The govt is looking to import 2mn MT of rice in September, while the local harvest is expected to amount to 5mn MT
  • The price ceiling was set in Sep 5 at P41/kg for regular milled rice and P45/kg for well-milled rice
  • Meanwhile, the Dept of Finance has proposed to temporarily slash the 35% rice import tariff rate to 0% or maximum of 10% to arrest the surge in rice prices
Hawkish monetary policy stance stays until inflation reverts to BSP’s 2-4% target
  • The BSP would like to see inflation go back within the 2-4% target band before any policy easing is considered due to the need to anchor inflation expectations
    • According to BSP Deputy Governor Francisco Dakila, Jr during the Philippine Economic Briefing in Dubai
  • The BSP sees inflation falling within the target range in 4Q23, barring unprecedented supply shocks
    • Inflation accelerated to 5.3% in August due to higher food and transport costs
    • YT-August inflation rate averaged 6.6%, still above the BSP’s 5.6% full-year forecast
  • The Monetary Board is set to convene and decide on the policy rate on Sep 21

It will take into consideration the US Federal Reserve’s next move (meeting on Sep 20)

Government plans another US$1bn bond sale this year
  • The Philippine government is looking to return to the international bond market before year end or in 1Q24 with a US$1bn Islamic/sukuk bond offering
    • According to National Treasurer Rosalia de Leon
  • It also plans to issue US$2bn through retail bonds this month
  • This is in line with its programmed US$5bn overseas bond sales for the year
BoI-approved investments hit P800bn
  • The Board of Investments (BoI) has already approved P800bn-worth of investments as of early September
    • According to Trade Secretary Alfredo E. Pascual
    • This has already exceed the P729bn in investments it approved in the same period last year
  • The government is targeting to approve P1.5tn of investments this year, which was upgraded from an initial target of P1.0tn
  • No other details disclosed
Wage board approves hike in minimum daily wages for CALABARZON, Central Visayas
  • Regional wage board approved wage hikes for Regions IV-A (CALABARZON) and VII (Central Visayas - Cebu)
    • Region IV-A increased the minimum wage by 10%-11% to P385 to P520 (effective Sep 24)
    • Region VII increased the minimum wage by 8-9% to P420 to P468 (effective Oct 1)
  • Note that the minimum wage in NCR was hiked by 7-8% last July 16
  • In 2022, the government implemented wage hikes of +12%-14% throughout the country
