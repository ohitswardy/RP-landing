REGIS REPORT

06 January 2023

The market started the year strong, rising  +1.5% WoW this week, pushed up by foreign inflows. Foreigners were net buyers this week for $19.7m with inflows mostly in BDO (+$13m), JFC ($3.55m), and ALI ($3.47m). Average daily turnover improved to $101m, from a mere $76m in the last trading week of 2022 amid the holiday season.  
 
Biggest movers were large caps SMPH (+3.5% WoW) and BDO (+5.01% WoW) as risk-on sentiment lifted the market. 
Converge (+10.2% WoW) and Monde Nissin (+11.6% WoW) surged after lagging last year. End 2022, share prices of Converge and Monde Nissin dropped -50% and -31.6% respectively, greatly underperforming the index (PSEi -7.8% FY22). Converge disclosed that they are set to offer wholesale connectivity services in Singapore, although this is unlikely to significantly impact revenues based on our estimates.
 
ACEN (-1.7% WoW) saw a late sell off ahead of the payment date of Ayala Corp’s (-1.6% WoW) property dividend (January 9, Monday).
 
Outside of index names, BLOOM (+16.3% WoW) and CEB (+3.9% WoW) gained heftily on the back of the China reopening theme.
 
Meanwhile, Unionbank’s (+4.0% WoW) stock rights offering has been priced at P56.88 per share with an entitlement ratio of 1:10.1536. Offer period is on January 16-27.
 
RRHI (-2.2% WoW) is acquiring an additional 4.4% interest in BPI. Following this transaction and the previously-announced merger between BPI and Robinsons Bank, RRHI will have ~6.5% equity interest in BPI.
 
Inflation figures for December came out at 8.1% driven mostly by food prices. The elevated inflation print however was largely ignored by the market.

CORPORATE NEWS
 
 
 
 

MREIT on track to reach 500,000sqm target by 2024

MREIT said it was on track to increase its leasable area to 500,000sqm by end-2024. It currently has a portfolio of 280,000sqm (predominantly offices). It is waiting for SEC approval for its acquisition of 45,000sqm of offices from its sponsor, Megaworld. This will increase MREIT’s portfolio to 325,000sqm. As of 3Q22, MREIT’s office occupancy was 95% (MEG – 92% occupancy).

Filinvest REIT to acquire more office buildings from Filinvest Land

Filinvest REIT is looking to acquire eight office buildings from its sponsor, Filinvest Land, from 2023 to 2025. The office buildings have a combined leasable area of 164,000sqm. Six of the buildings are in Clark, Makati, and Pasay. Two of the buildings are in existing FILRT areas (Alabang or Cebu). Regis estimates that Filinvest Land’s non-REIT portfolio has a vacancy rate of about 60%. FLI is trading at 66% discount to NAV, 0.2X FY23E P/B, and 6.0X FY23E P/E.

Headline inflation jumps to 8.1% YoY in December

Headline CPI rose by 8.1% YoY and 0.3% MoM in December 2022 vs consensus +8.4% and BSP expectation of +7.8%-8.6%. Core inflation (ex-fuel, food) accelerated further to +6.9% in Dec from +6.5% in Nov. By category, headline inflation was driven by an acceleration in food, utilities, and other service-related categories; tempered by decelerating pump prices. Food prices rose at a faster rate due to higher prices of rice, fruits, vegetables, sugar; but offset by deceleration in other items (e.g. meat, fish) due to favorable base effects. Utilities grew at a slightly faster rate of +14.2% from 13.8% due to higher electricity prices and gas. 2022 average headline inflation rate was at +5.8% (in line with BSP latest upgrade). Assuming the headline CPI rises at a similar +0.3% MoM in the next 12 months, inflation for 2023 could average +5.9%. This implies two things: (1) inflation will unlikely revert to the BSP’s 2-4% target band by 2H23 and (2) it will also unlikely meet the BSP’s 2023 average inflation target of 4.5%. Monetary board meeting on Feb 16; Fed meeting on Feb 2.

 

Mixed results at T-bill auction

The Bureau of Treasury (BTr) decided to fully award bids for the 91-day, 182-day T-bills while partially awarding the 364-day T-bill. The 91-day T-bill fetched a yield of 4.155%, up by 7bps from Dec 5’s auction (BVAL: 4.25%). The 182-day T-bill fetched a yield of 4.903%, down by 5bps from Dec 5 auction (BVAL: 4.88%). The 364-day T-bill fetched a yield of 5.24%, up by 9bps from last auction in Nov (BVAL: 5.24%). BTr raised P13.7bn of the P15.0bn offering. The auction was twice oversubscribed, with total tenders reaching P30.3bn.

Government debt rises to P13.6tn in end-November

The National Government’s total outstanding debt stood at P13.64tn as of end-November 2022. This is up 0.02% (P3.15bn) MoM due to the PHP’s appreciation against the USD. Compared with end-Dec 2021, the government’s debt level is higher by 16.3% (P1.92tn). This is equivalent to 62.5% of GDP (2023E nominal GDP: P21.82tn). Domestic debt rose by 0.78% (P72.7bn) MoM to P9.43tn. External debt declined by 1.62% (P69.58bn) MoM to P4.22tn. The PHP appreciation resulted in a P106.98bn MoM decrease in debt levels, tempered by the impact of third-currency fluctuations against the USD (P50.78bn).

Hot money nets US$489mn inflow in November

Foreign portfolio investments (i.e. hot money) recorded a net inflow of US$489mn in November. This is higher than the US$83mn net inflow in October 2022 and US$110mn net inflow in November 2021. Note that the Philippine Stock Exchange recorded foreign net outflows of US$99.4mn in November 2022. The gross inflows amounted to US$1.1bn, up 64% MoM, while gross outflows were up 0.8% MoM to US$566mn. The 11M22 foreign portfolio investments yielded net inflows of US$794mn, a turnaround from the US$570mn net outflow in 11M21.

Tourist arrivals hit 2.65mn in 2022

The Philippines received 2.65mn international tourist arrivals in 2022 of which, 2.02mn were foreigners while 628k were returning Filipinos (or Philippine passport holders who are permanently residing abroad). Top markets by volume were US (19%), Korea (16%), Australia (5%), Canada (5%). For 2023, the government is targeting 4.8mn international tourist arrivals. In 2019, tourist arrivals hit 8.26mn. Top markets by volume were Korea (24%), China (21%), USA (13%) and Japan (8%).

Rice, pork shortages expected in 2023

The country will be in short supply of rice by more than 3mn MT this year due to high fertilizer and fuel costs according to Agriculture Undersecretary Fermin Adriano. Apart from rice, Mr. Adriano warned there will also be a shortage of pork due to continuing disruptions to grower operations posed by the African Swine Fever. We lost three million head of swine to ASF from our hog population of 12 million. Assuming repopulation 400,000 hogs per year, it will take more than five years to repopulate our stock to pre-ASF levels. Rice and meat account for 8.9% and 6.4%, respectively, of the CPI basket.

Deadline for BPO firms to transfer to BOI extended

The deadline for BPO firms to transfer their registration from PEZA to BOI was extended to 31 January 2023. The previous deadline was December 2022. Only 786 out of 1,100 applications have been endorsed by PEZA to BOI. BOI does not set limits on work-from-home but PEZA sets a 30% WFH limit.

Beginning 1 January 2023, PEZA only allows 10% of employees to work from home. The number of applications suggest that most BPO firms would like to flexibility to fully implement work-from-home policies. BPO firms, which are predominantly registered with PEZA, occupy 60%-90% of office space of the developers Reigs covers.

41% of MEG’s NAV
