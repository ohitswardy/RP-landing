REGIS REPORT

04 October 2024

The PSEi showed some signs of easing in its strong upward momentum, which has now extended for over a month, but still managed to continue its winning streak to 5 consecutive weeks as shares were boosted by a dovish CPI print at the tailend of the week. The benchmark advanced +0.53% WoW to a new 52-week high of 7467. However the pace of gains were evidently much slower this time (compared to 2%-3% in past 2 weeks) as we saw some bouts of volatility earlier in the week with focus in the broader region largely turning to China, where shares have been rallying sharply as of late amid the PBOC's busy round of new stimulus measures.

On the ground, key focus was the latest CPI data released on Friday morning which showed that inflation eased to 1.9% in September from 3.3% in August, significantly lower than 2.5% forecast and falling outside of the BSP’s target band of 2%-4%. This further boosted confidence that the BSP may cut rates twice more (by 25bp each) in their remaining October and December policy meetings before the year ends. Furthermore, breakdown of the data showed that food inflation stood at only 1.4% vs 3.9% in August, which should bode well for consumer spending leading into 4Q.

Along with lighter pace of gains came lightened activity as well with an average daily turnover of only $111m, -35% WoW (but inline with the YTD average), notwithstanding the quarter-end session at the start of the week. Foreign investors continued to account for the slightly bigger share of volumes with their 53% participation rate, same as in the previous week, while their net inflow streak remained unbroken at 25 consecutive days. However, the pace of inflows eased as well to a net of $36m this week vs $190m last week (which included AC blocks).

Top index gainer in the PSEi this week was smaller mid-cap name NIKL +8.04% WoW which extended recovery from September lows amid an improving outlook on demand following stimulus measures in China. The gains in NIKL were tracked by its non-PSEi nickel mining peer as well, FNI +5% WoW. Of the bigger cap names GLO +6.29% was notable outperformer, supported by an uptick in foreign buying, while its peers TEL +2.97% and CNVRG -2.98%. In terms of sectors, the best performing one YTD, Banks, was mixed this time with BPI +5.69% WoW rising to a new high, while in contrast widely-held BDO -2.11% came off highs, dragged down by foreign selling. Another sector that was mixed was consumer with MONDE +4.45% and CNPF +2.47% outperforming peers PGOLD -2.65% and URC -0.96% this time. On the losing end, WLCON -7.93% was the top laggard this, falling to its lowest level in 2 months, likely weighed down by a still tempered outlook on upcoming 3Q earnings.

PSE Index Feb 2025 rebalancing predictions – CBC (+), NIKL (-)

Regis expects CBC to enter the PSE Index during the Feb 2025 rebalancing. Regis also expects NIKL to be removed from the Index during the said rebalancing. Stocks that meet the free-float and liquidity requirements will be ranked by 12M market cap. Changes to the constituents of the PSE will occur when: 1) a non-index constituent is ranked within the top 25 or 2) a PSEi constituent falls outside the top 35. Based on our estimates, both criteria have been met.

DMCI group launches tender offer for Cemex (CHP PM)

CHP has received a copy of the Mandatory Tender Offer Report of Consunji family-owned Dacon Corp. (“bidder”) to conduct a mandatory tender offer to acquire up to 1.367bn shares (10.14%) of CHP. The shares will be acquired at an estimated price of P1.42/sh, which is the same price the group is paying to acquire 100% of CHP’s parent, Cemex Asian South East Corp. (CASEC), which in turn owns 89.86% of CHP.  The final purchase price is subject to customary closing adjustments based on CHP’s estimated working capital, cash and debt levels. If final price exceeds estimated price, tendering shareholders may receive an upward adjustment; In case of a downward adjustment, bidder will not claim a refund. Note that the bidder has agreed to purchase 100% of CASEC for an enterprise value of US$660mn and equity value of US$306mn. DMCI Holdings (DMC PM) will own 56.75% of CASEC, while Dacon Corp. will own 32.12%; Semirara (SCC PM) will own 11.13%. Assuming no one participates in the tender offer, DMCI will end up effectively owning 51% of CHP while SCC will own 10%.

Consunji Group to keep Cemex listed

The Consunji Group will sell a small stake in Cemex Holdings Philippines (CHP PM) to ensure that the latter complies with the minimum public ownership requirement of 10% and stays listed. The Group is acquiring 100% of Cemex Asian South East Corp. (CASEC), which in turn owns 89.9% of Cemex Philippines. The completion of the transaction is subject to the satisfaction of various conditions, including, but not limited to, completion for the sale and purchase of shares in each of APO Land & Quarry Corporation and Island Quarry and Aggregates Corporation, and the execution of the mandatory tender offer requirement by the Purchasers to the minority shareholders of CHP (which hold 10.1% of CHP).

GSIS eyes DOF stake in Semirara

State-run Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) is interested in buying the Department of Finance’s 3.4% stake in Semirara (SCC PM), according to SCC Chairman Isidro Consunji.

AREIT executes share-for-property swaps with ALI and ACEN

AREIT received SEC approval for its share-for-property transactions with Ayala Land and ACEN. AREIT will acquire P21.8bn worth of office, mall, and hotel assets from Ayala Land by issuing 642mn new AREIT shares (to ALI). AREIT will also acquire a 275-hectare property in Zambales province from ACEN by issuing 199mn new AREIT shares (to ACEN). The AREIT shares were issued at a price of P34/share. The transactions were first announced in 4Q23 and will increase AREIT’s shares outstanding by 35% (841mn shares). AREIT will recognize the income from the assets beginning July 1.

NAIA operator hikes fees for airlines starting this month

San Miguel-led New NAIA Infrastructure Corp. will start collecting higher landing and take-off fees from airlines this month. Minimum rates for international movements will increase to US$794 from US$248. Minimum rates for domestic operations will increase to P154 per 500kg from P48. NAIA Infrastructure Corp. took over operations of NAIA last month and the new rates were only published then. Airport fees fall under “Aircraft and traffic servicing fees”, which accounts for 14% of Cebu Air’s (CEB PM) cash costs. Regis anticipates some near-term margin erosion for CEB, given the sudden increase in fees. Over the medium-term, we reckon these fees will ultimately be passed on to end users.

ACEN to Infuse Additional Capital into Belenos Clean Energy Holdings

ACEN Corporation to inject an additional P1.5bn into its subsidiary Belenos Clean Energy Holdings In attempts to plan to subscribe to 50 million redeemable referable shares, at a par value of P1 each. The shares represent 6.22% of total outstanding shares of Belenos, will be issued out to increase its authorized capital stock. Upon the deal closing, ACEN will own 100% of Belenos. ACEN plans to quadruple its attributable RE capacity to 20GW over the next six years, from current level of around 4.8GW.

PNB approves and confirms property dividend declaration; PNB to sell 9.18% stake in PHC

PNB’s property dividend will be paid on October 25. On April 23, 2021, the PNB Board of Directors approved and confirmed the property dividend declaration of up to 239,353,710 common shares of PNB Holdings Corporation (“PHC”) with a par value of P100 per share, to all stockholders of record as of May 18, 2021. This represents 51% of PHC, which owns three pieces of prime property in Makati and Pasay. The property dividend shall be paid at a ratio of 0.156886919 shares of PHC for every 1 share of PNB. Since LT Group (LTG PM) owns c. 60% of PNB, it will receive c. 30% of PHC. Separately, PNB also said it would sell at least 9.18% of its stake in PHC.

Lopez-Led EDC Plans Expansion into Indonesia’s Renewable Energy Market

Energy Development Corporation (EDC) aims to establish a foundation in Indonesia by investing roughly US$456mn in developing two greenfield geothermal projects. Projects are expected to produce a combined capacity of 80MW. First Gen group plans to expand its low-carbon energy portfolio to 13000MW in the next six years, 9000MW of that attributable to RE. In 2018, EDC subsidiary PT EDC Indonesia was granted a decree by Indonesia’s ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources. EDC is investing around P24bn to develop four geothermal projects within the year. Its portfolio includes 1380MW of installed capacity, which is 20% of the Philippine’s installed RE capacity. It generates 11185.4MW, which equates to 61% of the Philippine’s total installed geothermal capacity.

Aboitiz group to take over Laguindingan airport in 2025

The infrastructure arm of the Aboitiz group is set to take over the operations and maintenance of the Laguindingan International Airport located in Misamis Oriental next year. The concession agreement for the airport will be signed within the month. The takeover will happen within the next 4-6 months. The company stated that it plans to expand the airport's operating capacity. The Transportation Department aims to implement the first phase of the project from 2024-2026. The airport will have a capacity of 1.6mn passengers a year, which will increase to 3.9mn by the end of the first phase and to 6.1mn by the end of the second phase. The contract for the Laguindingan airport will run for a period of 35 years. The company has also submitted proposals for the New Bohol-Panglao International Airport, Bicol International Airport, and Iloilo International Airport.

8990 Holdings signs MOA with Quezon City government

The project has a total of 3,000 units. This is the first partnership between the company and a Local Government Unit.

Shakey’s Pizza incorporate US subsidiary

Shakey’s Pizza Asia Ventures (PIZZA PM) will incorporate a US subsidiary which will be wholly owned by PIZZA. The intention is to own and operate stores, and franchises as well as market Shakey’s Group’s Products and brands in the US. This is expected to grow systemwide sales and revenues. Japan’s SBI invests in Aboitiz fintech unit UBX

SBI Holdings Inc. has made a strategic investment in UBX, the fintech and digital transformation arm UnionBank. UBX stated that SBI took in a “minority stake” in the Filipino fintech, “allowing both parties to collaborate in developing more impactful digital transformation solutions for the needs of the enterprises.” SBI Holdings President (Yoshitaka Kitao) stated that the Japanese company invested in UBX as it sees “immense potential” in the Philippines as it is one of the fastest-growing economies in the region.

Robinsons Land to spend P10bn for hotel projects

Robinsons Land will spend P10bn over the next few years to develop four hotels with 990 room keys: Ultra-luxury “NuStar” in Cebu, 5-star “Fili” in Bridgetowne, and Lifestyle brand “Grand Summit” in Cebu and Pangasinan. At the end of 2Q24, RLC had 26 hotels with 4,243 room keys. Hotels comprised 5% of RLC’s 1H24 EBIT.

Cebu Air signs purchase agreement with Airbus; Up to 152 aircraft to be delivered from 2029 to 2033

Cebu Air signed the purchase agreement for 152 aircraft from Airbus valued at US$24bn. The order covers firm order of 102 narrowbodies, with purchase rights for 50 more. The initial batch will be delivered in 2029 and all “firm” deliveries are expected to be completed by 2033. CEB has the option to order 50 more beyond 2033.

Note that CEB’s deliveries of CEB’s previous purchase orders will end in 2028. Majority of the aircraft will be assigned to serve flights from NAIA. Pre-delivery payments will be sourced from its own equity and once deliveries begin, the airline plans to undertake sale-and-leaseback deals. Note that CEB will also be retiring and returning aircraft to lessors over this period.

Swiss challenge for Villar group’s unsolicited airport proposals to be launched soon

The Department of Transportation (DOTr) said it plans to award the concession for the rehabilitation of the Iloilo International Airport and Puerto Princesa International Airport next year. The Villar group holds the “original proponent status” for these airports and the contracts are currently under “negotiation.” Prime Asset Ventures Inc. BSP Governor eyes lowering RRR to zero within term; BSP removing fees on small transfers

BSP Governor Remolona, Jr. hinted at the possibility of lowering bank’ reserve requirement ratio down to zero before his terms ends in 2029. Discussions are ongoing to bring the RRR down further to 5%, but timing is still undetermined. The BSP is reducing the RRR by 250bps to 7% for commercial banks by October 25, which could raise NPAT by c. 4%.

In a draft circular, the BSP is eyeing to ament the Manual of Regulations for Payment Systems (MORPS) to eliminate transaction fees on electronic fund transfers for personal transactions, up to a specified threshold on the number of transactions, and on payments for micro-merchants. Micro-merchants are described to have monthly aggregate gross receipts that do not exceed P250k (c. US$4.5k). The draft circular also proposes to lift the moratorium on the increase of fee for InstaPay and PESONet. If the circular is approved, payment service providers (PSPs) will need to comply with the amended rules by April 1, 2025. Data from the BSP showed that the value of transactions done through InstaPay and PESONet climbed by 34.6% YoY to P9.45tn in 7M24.

The Consumer sentiment improves for 3Q 2024 and the next quarter

The consumer sentiment for Q3 2024 improved as the overall confidence index (CI) turned less negative at -15.6 percent from -20.5 percent in the Q2 2024 survey results. This is reflective of the decrease in the percentage of pessimists, which outweighed the slight decrease in the percentage of optimists. According to the respondents, their less pessimistic sentiment for Q3 2024 was attributed to expectations of: Higher income from wages/salaries, remittances, and other sources, additional sources of income, permanent employment and more available jobs, and additional working family members. For the next quarter (Q4 2024), the consumer outlook turned optimistic as the CI reverted to positive territory at 0.7 percent from -0.4 percent in Q2 2024.

President Marcos signs law imposing 12% VAT on digital services

President Marcos, Jr. signed into law Republic Act 12023, which imposes a 12% VAT on foreign digital service providers and a 5% VAT on registered foreign players providing services to the government. Law covers online search engines, online marketplaces, cloud services, online media and advertising, online platforms, and any digital content providers. The law exempts digital services sold to educational institutions and requires nonresident digital service providers to register for VAT payments if their gross sales or receipts for the past year have exceeded P3mn. The government expects to collect P105bn in additional revenues over the next five years from this measure. Senate President Francis G. Escudero said there are currently no pending revenue-generating bills except the measure imposing an excise tax on single-use plastic.

Regional wage boards in three regions hike minimum wages

Regional wage boards have approved increases in minimum wages across three regions: Region 2 increased wages by 7% to P460-480, Region 3 granted ~10-13% hike in minimum wages to be implemented in two tranches: Oct 17, 2024 and April 16, 2025, while Region 12 hiked minimum wages by ~7% to be implemented in four tranches: Oct 17, Jan 1, April 1, June 1.

67% of BPO firms implement AI initiatives

67% of IT-BPO firms survey by the IT-BPO Association of the Philippines (IBPAP) said that they are using AI in their operations, including customer support, data entry and process, workforce management, and sales and marketing. 8% have reported declines in their workforce due to the use of AI, while 13% have reported gains. IT-BPM industry is expected to end the year with US$38bn in revenues and a workforce count of 1.82mn, representing 7% growth from 2023. IBPAP President Jack Madrid expects growth to slow down in 2025. BPO firms occupy 50%-80% of the office portfolios of the developers Regis covers.

Government debt dips to P15.55tn in end-Aug

The government’s total outstanding debt decreased by P140bn (-0.9%) MoM to P15.55tn in end-August. The decline is due to the PHP appreciation against the USD and the net repayment of external debt. Domestic debt increased by P38bn (+0.4%) MoM to P10.79tn due to net issuance of government securities. External debt decreased by P178bn (-3.6%) MoM to P4.76tn due to the PHP’s appreciation (-P195bn) and net repayments of P4bn. The government is targeting a debt-to-GDP ratio of ~60% by year-end and is aiming to reduce the ratio further to 51% by 2028. As of end-June 2024, the government’s debt-to-GDP ratio stood at 60.9%.

House mulls overhaul of ERC charter in EPIRA reforms

The House of Representatives energy committee is considering a major overhaul of the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) as part of reforms to the Electric Power Industry Reform Act (EPIRA). Some changes include increased administrative and regulatory procedures. This includes benchmarking generation rate-setting and to publish benchmark rates at least annually. Officials are also considering a provision to rule on administrative cases within 30 days, with summary and regular quasi-judicial cases given 60 to 180 days. Considering doubling the commissions seats to 8, whilst requiring people in the commission to have ‘extensive familiarity’ in the energy sector. The draft substitute bill also included the possibility of the ERC to work with the Philippine Competition Commission in times of suspected anti-competitive behavior.
