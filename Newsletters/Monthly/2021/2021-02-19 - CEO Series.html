The worst of the Covid-19 crisis is (hopefully) behind us. As the country – and the world – takes its tentative steps to reopen, we sit down with the leaders of Philippines Inc to find out what challenges remain, and who, if any, might emerge even stronger than before. 

We ask the CEOs – what was the most significant learning during the pandemic and how they will run things differently when the economy reboots. Are there any lasting changes to their customers’ behavior  that will shape their outlook for the future?

We are pleased to invite you to attend this year’s REGIS CEO Roundtable Series. As was the case last year, our CEO Roundtable Series will feature foremost industry and thought leaders providing insight into their respective companies and industries.

The Series will run from March to May this year, with the first event on the 1st of March.
