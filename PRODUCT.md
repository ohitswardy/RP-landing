# Regis Partners

register: brand

## Product purpose

Marketing site + client portal + CMS for Regis Partners, a pure-play Philippine
institutional brokerage and research house (PSE member since 1999). The public
site sells credibility to global allocators; the portal serves research to
entitled clients; the CMS is the staff back office.

## Users

- Fund managers and analysts at global institutions evaluating a first call.
- Existing institutional clients reaching the research portal.
- Regis staff running content through the CMS.

## Brand voice

Sovereign, patient, precise. Think GIC and Ariel Investments, not fintech SaaS.
Anti-references: crypto neon, navy-and-gold cliché, editorial-magazine serif
affectation, hero-metric SaaS templates.

## Committed design system

- Drenched corporate navy (oklch 260°) as the dominant dark surface; warm paper
  for reading surfaces; amber (oklch 62°) as the single conviction accent,
  expressed as the "amber tick" before eyebrows and numerals. Bronze is the
  secondary drench for story panels.
- Geist (sans) + Geist Mono (labels, numerals, tabular figures). No new families.
- Hairline rules, ruled listings, uppercase mono eyebrows, animated amber arrow
  as CTA grammar.
- Ease: cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1). No bounce.

## Strategic principles

- Design reads as a sovereign-wealth annual report brought to the web, not a
  startup landing page.
- Photography of real people, floors, and skylines carries warmth; color blocks
  carry authority.
- The CMS uses a separate Vercel x Nothing utility language (see DESIGN notes in
  memory); do not leak marketing flourishes into it.
